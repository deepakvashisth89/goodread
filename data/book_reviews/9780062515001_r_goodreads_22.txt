February has been a very slow month, reading-wise. Some books I DNF'd cause I couldn't feel their magic and cause I didnt feel like reading much. Thus I moved back to my comfort zone and decided to read an autobiography of a very famous singer: Johnny Cash. Like Springsteen's, which I read last year, this is a very blunt, emotional, enjoyable read about an artist who changed the face of (country) music.You should give it a try.
