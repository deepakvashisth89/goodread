My Review: Another one down in the Roswell series! I hate that it took more than halfway through the series for me to get into it, but the plot is getting so good! I like the twists and surprises, although I’m not loving how sudden some are (although that just seems to be how the books are written). Could be worse, could be better. Overall a quick and easy fun read.My Overall Rating: ⭐️⭐️⭐️/5Tropes: forbidden love, alien romance, best friends brother-ish
