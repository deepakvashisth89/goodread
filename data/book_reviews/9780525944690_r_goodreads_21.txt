My favorite character in the AprilWoo series is her mother, Skinny Dragon. Skinny is constantly in fear that her daughter will never marry a rich Chinese doctor or businessman. She squawks at April whenever she comes home about her Hispanic lover and former partner at work. By plying April with unpalatable herbal concoctions, Skinny hopes to adjust April's yin and yang so that April will want to marry, have children, and stop embarrassing her mother by running around NYC solving crimes.This mystery was not one of my favorites in the series because the criminals' identities were revealed at the beginning. Plus it's really annoying to me when spoiled rich kids commit crimes because they have no conscience. I think the book was supposed to generate compassion for the teens because their parents were lousy, but I just wanted to give them a good kick in the pants.
