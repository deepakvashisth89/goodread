11/2014When I tried to read this in my mid twenties, I decided I didn't like it, and stopped. When I read it at, like, thirty-one, I thought I really loved it (I was going through a Continental Op hysteria - hey, he's short, fat, forty and nameless - dreamy - but, truly, the pulp hero's humble unattractiveness charms me). This time, I didn't love it so much. The Dain Curse was serialized in Black Mask in 1928-29, and I guess it's obvious. Part One and Part Two are just okay and, oh yeah, horribly racist, egregiously so. Part three is almost great, I guess. It has some really good scenes. But all in all, rather dull and convoluted.
