Little Mouse is busy playing games and daydreaming. Big Mouse is digging, planting and weeding the flower garden. Big Mouse would like for Little Mouse to help, but each time Big Mouse asks for help, Little Mouse says, "No." As the morning sun gets hotter toward afternoon, Big Mouse becomes grumpier and, finally, frustrated with Little Mouse. Little Mouse proves to Big Mouse he was not goofing off or being lazy when he gives Big Mouse a sun hat he made for him. They enjoy a picnic and find time to take a nap during the sunny afternoon.This book is about friendship, about helping, and about planting a garden. The delightful pictures engage the reader with the text. The by play of the Big Mouse and the Little Mouse will keep readers guessing why Little Mouse might not be helping and the reasons why Big Mouse is upset and frustrated. It could be a good story about planting a garden and watching it grow. This book has many possibilities for child and for class interaction.
