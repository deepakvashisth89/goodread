I knew I would enjoy this novel since I’m a huge fan of the 1977 film adaptation, The Haunting of Julia. But I wasn’t prepared for how different it would be from the film, which made it quite interesting to read. There are many similarities, and the film certainly got the vibe of the book down perfectly, but there are also some major differences. It took me a little while to get used to Straub’s wordiness (he sure did love a long paragraph!) but once I got into this book, I REALLY got into it. It really is, for lack of a better word, haunting. The only thing I did not like about the novel was the ending. I won’t spoil anything, but let’s just say that’s one aspect that the movie most definitely improved on.
