Junie B Jones, the bratty first grader upstart created by Barbara Park, is totally BOSS when she runs her first grade class ragged, and yes it's a question of WHEN! So it makes sense when she runs into her favorite lunch lady from kindergarten, the one who gives out sugar cookies for school lunch, who this year gives out fruit bars. Someone must've told her that first graders outgrow unhealthy sugar cookies. Somebody lied. Still Junie's teacher, the one she snarkily calls Mr Scary (not in any way related to author Richard Scarry, who's not scary and much nicer to children!), lets Junie see lunch lady Ms Grabowitz for one reason and one reason alone: it gets her out of his hair, though the relief is only temporary. Ms Grabowitz thus offers Junie a job, that of helping out in the cafeteria greeting the kids and handing out cookies. Junie opts to name herself Food Boss, and orders (orders!) the kitchen staff to provide a fun time for lunch, Junie's way! Can Ms Grabowitz smooth things out before Junie gets a big head, as she usually does. Junie's 19th adventure promises to create intense laughs. Junie herself has grown into her own smart cookie. And she ain't gonna crumble! Yet!
Four stars
