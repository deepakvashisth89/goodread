Akenson offers a fairly straight forward Christian premise, but one coming from a secular liberal tradition that is quite astounding: Paul offers a better glimpse of the historical Jesus than the gospels or other writings. Because Paul is writing from a pre-70 (i.e. before the destruction of the temple) time period, he has not been effected by the theological cataclysm that all other New Testament writers have. Akenson's book is a fantastic secular or historical critique of the Quest for the Historical Jesus, even if a few of his claims are fairly unstable. I do find it interesting, however, that, while engaging a great deal of moderate and liberal religious scholars, there is no mention of N. T. Wright. Still, a very interesting work of history.
