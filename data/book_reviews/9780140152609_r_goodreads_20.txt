I listened to the audiobook version—an incredibly long 33 hours! At first, I wasn’t sure I’d finish before my library loan expired, but I managed. The biography is extremely detailed, covering Franklin’s early life in Boston, his apprenticeship to his older brother, his bold move to Philadelphia where he founded his own print shop, and continuing through his later years as he aided the Revolutionary War, helped shape the new government, and even contributed to the birth of the abolitionist movement.

If you want a thorough, comprehensive account of Benjamin Franklin’s life and times, this is it. Next up on my list: The Loyal Son (about his relationship with William Franklin) and a biography of Benjamin Franklin Bache.
