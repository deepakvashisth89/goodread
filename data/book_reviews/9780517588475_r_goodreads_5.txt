A wonderful story of intrigue. I truly enjoyed the unraveling of the complicated strands of this case, or is it cases? The writing is so good and the wordplay most enjoyable. It's wonderfully narrated by Samuel West, one of my favourites.I love how Morse and Lewis collaborate as a team and seem on the same wavelength for the most part, or play off of one another, as they grow increasingly accustomed to working together. Here is an example of their dialogue:Lewis: “We’re getting plenty of suspects, sir.”Morse: “Mm, we’re getting pretty high on content, but pretty low on analysis wouldn’t you say.”Then, I loved this analogy of horses on a racecourse:Lewis regarding what he admires about Morse: “That ability to leap ahead of the field almost from the starting stalls, all be it occasionally finding himself completely on completely the wrong racecourse."Lewis goes on to add that what he admires most of all about Morse is that he "appeared to believe that Lewis was not only usually up with him in the race galloping happily abreast but that Lewis could sometimes spot something in the stretches ahead that Morse himself had missed as the pair of them race on towards the winning post.”
