Even for someone who's not American, this book adds immense value. It is in a way, a riveting and enlightening account of human-animal relationships in general.
My biggest takeaway is the humbling realisation that even today there's a cloud of mystery around the creatures with which we share this planet. I am never to lose sight of this.
It's important to remember that even our current understanding is sort us arranged in disparate elements of science, folklore, metaphors etc. And the first step to furthering this understanding is to find and link these elements together.
