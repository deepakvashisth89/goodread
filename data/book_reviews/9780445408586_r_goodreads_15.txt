Better than the first in this series, as Bertie is a bit less annoying in his self-important narration, and as the tongue-in-cheek Agatha Christie allusions abound. Given the title, the reader (unlike the characters) knows that it's not over 'til it's over. Set at a shooting party at a country house, the bloodshed is enormous, especially among the poor pheasants. I had no idea the toll was so great at one of these things.
