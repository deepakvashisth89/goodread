This was very insightful, I learned a lot about operas, Mozart, and Freemasonry. Never read anything about operas or classical music. I am not classically trained altogether, but I try to educate myself. This work was made even more interesting because it gives a peek behind the curtain of a Master at work; how does a symbolic story come together and in what manner are the symbolic meanings cleverly though visibly disguised. It is a good lesson for an aspiring writer of symbolic fiction, such as myself.My one problem with this book doesn't have anything to do with the content, but with the printing. Specifically the version that I own, the 1992 Inner Traditions publication. Merely opening the book makes the pages snap loose. I have to handle the book with great care lest the whole things falls apart. The spine is unbent, so that's not the problem. I guess it's the thickness of the paper they used, the book is American-fat even though it's just over 300 pages. I own books that have twice the amount of pages but are thinner.
