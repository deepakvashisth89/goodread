4-solid stars.Tom Radigan isn't looking for a war, but the harsh, wind-scoured ridges of New Mexico don't offer compromises.InRadigan, Louis L'Amour delivers a lean, high-stakes Western that distills the genre down to its absolute essentials: land, grit, and survival.When a ruthless cattle baroness tries to freeze Radigan off his hard-won winter range, the conflict evolves into a brilliant, tactical chess match fought with repeaters and raw endurance.What sets this book apart is its atmospheric claustrophobia. L'Amour traps his characters in a brutal winter landscape where the elements are just as deadly as the bullets.Radigan himself is classic L'Amour—patient, fiercely independent, and driven by a quiet moral compass.It's a blistering, fast-paced read that proves security is never given on the frontier; it is carved out of the rock. Excellent, stripped-down storytelling.Highly recommend.
