William Manchester's "American Caesar" is the definite biography of Douglas MacArthur. Lots of information in a book that is over 700 pages long. The reader will read more than you probably ever want to know about MacArthur. Its all here and will satisfy any reader who wants to know more about the General's professional and private life. Pay special attention to how much his mother played in advancing his career. This was very interesting and I wonder how far up the military ladder he would have gotten without her help. Finding the words to cover every aspect and period of MacArthur's life had to be daunting and yet Manchester's writing is very inspiring, informative and well researched. A very well worth read that I recommend highly.
