This book has got to be one of my favorites, if not the favorite. The way Cook sows horror so fragile like a spider web into the empty fields and lonely houses. How he describes horror so terrifying you cannot even face it.The horrors in this book are not monsters lurking from under the bed, or come by as blood covered axes. The real horrors, as Cook so expertly goes on to show are in the mind. And there is no corner in your mind you can escape yourself.You can very well run, but you cannot hide.This book has stayed with me all these years and I doubt it will never let go. It's haunting in the truest form.
