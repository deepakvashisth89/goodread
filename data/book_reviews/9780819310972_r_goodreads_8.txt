Got this book as a Christmas present when I was 2 years old. Also was the first book I learned to read on my own. Born in Florida, I was surprised to find out that real snow is more "crunchy" than "fluffy" as the book described. I made it to my mid-twenties thinking that snow felt like cotton or pillows!The story itself has great lessons on empathy, friendship, and sharing. The illustrations are wonderfully handcrafted and vibrant. Highly recommended to all children!Show more
