A neighbor of my mom gave her this book to read and she passed it to me before she read it. It was written in 1929 by Anne and tells her life from childhood to adult growing up in the mining fields of Colorado. Very interesting as I know some of these areas, but her descriptions of their cabins and living conditions gives you a view into yester year.She kind of skips around so you might get confused a little but still a very worthwhile read.
