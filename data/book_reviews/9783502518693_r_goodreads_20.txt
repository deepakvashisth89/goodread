I adore stories with vivid descriptions and rich dialogue—even though this book was a fairly long endeavour, it was worth every moment. The story grabs your attention and holds it until it's 1 AM and you're whispering, "just one more page..." to yourself because you know you're going to say up way past your bedtime and dream of opening the tomb of King Tut or smelling spice, oud, oils, and hot sand as you watch ancient objects touch light for the first time in centuries.If you like long and evocative stories with dynamic dialogues that weave both history and modernity together, find a copy (preferably, a ragged and worn one) brew a cup of tea and settle in for a long night of reading.
