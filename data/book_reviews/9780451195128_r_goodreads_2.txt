Read: 3/27/26Setting: Regency EnglandTrope: switched identities/twin, forced marriageThis book was a joy to read!plot: Maeve has lived in the shadow of her notorious courtesan mother her whole life. Now, her mother is dead and she's finally meeting her father. He reveals that that she has an identical twin. When her parents separated, each one took a twin. Well, Maeve got the short end of that stick!. Now, her father needs her help. Her extremely shy sister has runaway from her impending marriage and he needs Maeve to masquerade as Meg until he can find her. Maeve agrees to carrying out this deception, but only if her father pays off her mother's enormous debts. He also has only a fortnight to find her sister.It turns out that playing a shy girl is harder than Maeve realized. She barely last an hour before her quirky personality comes through, to the dismay of her Meg's future MIL.The Earl of Linley is marrying Meg for her money. It's a business arrangement, plain and simple! He plans to marry, but continue his liaisons. Linley can't hope for more with a fiancee who's scared of him. So, he is shocked when his shy fiancee challenges him. Who would have thought she was hiding such a witty personality underneath a timid shell? It doesn't take long for this rake to fall in love. He can't wait to make her his wife!Maeve finds herself falling in love with her sister's fiancee. It gets harder and harder to lie to him. Another problem is the Reverend, who seems to care a lot about her sister. Maeve tries to avoid him in fear he'll recognize that she's not Meg. The man is probably the only person who truly saw her sister. Unfortunately, she can't confide her secret to him. She needs help, because her father doesn't seem to be looking for her sister. Maeve going to have to find Meg herself!Another problem is with Meg's future MIL. She was happy when Meg was a shy girl that posed no threat. Now, she's furious with this new version of Meg and she won't stand for it! She'll get rid of that girl on way or another!So what has happened to Meg and why isn't her father looking for her? The guilt is driving Maeve crazy! She has to tell Linley the truth! What will he do? Little does Maeve know that Linley has his own secret, one that could destroy her love for him.liked:1. I adored Maeve! She's intelligent and has a fabulous personality. She's no beauty, but she doesn't let that bother her. No pity party for this h!2. Linley was a bit of a rake in the beginning, but that all changes when meets Maeve. He falls hard and quick.3. These two were perfect for each other. Their conversations were hilarious. Though, what I loved best was that their romance was based on personality and not on looks.4. The h is plain. I want to thank the author for not stating that fact on every page like other authors tend to do. I think her looks are only mentioned twice5. The real Meg was pretty brave to run away instead of obeying her manipulative father. I was happy when she got her HEA with a certain character.5. Well written with light, humorous moments.dislike:1. How could a father allow his daughter to be raised by a courtesan? I guess the same father who wouldn't bother to look for the daughter he raised. Still, this was a little too farfetched for me.2. Clean book. This doesn't bother me, but I thought I'd mention it.3. Maeve couldn't even last an hour as shy Meg. With all that money on the line, you'd think she could have tried harder than that!4. An illegitimate son bring legitimized as the heir to a peerage was idiotic. It couldn't happen!Conclusion: This was a charming story! I had a smile on my face the whole time reading this book. It was far from perfect, but I enjoyed it!
