Apparently, there was a documentary made of the life of Mardy Murie, whose memoir, Two in the Far North, I just finished. Although I have not searched for the documentary yet, I was delighted to stumble upon this companion book filled with all the photos of 101 years of a life well lived that I wished I'd had as I read her book. Marty Murie has been known as the mother of the conservation movement and is a role model for all with a passion for protecting the land.
