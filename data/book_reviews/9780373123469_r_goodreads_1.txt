Second chance story of H/h who have a child together but have never married. The h turned down the hero’s proposal because she thought he didn’t love her. The H let her go and has had regular contact with his 4 year-old son. Sounds a little bloodless, doesn’t it?And for the most part it is. I felt no chemistry between the H/h. The heroine was a shrew, constantly railing about how independent she was, how much she hated the hero and how great she was at her job and juggling work with a child. She also throws an OM in his face, although they are just friends.The hero has decided to push his luck and again try to get the heroine to marry him. The h takes this as a threat that he is going to take her son away from her when he goes back to Italy. The hero lets her think this for awhile, just as he has let her think she can afford the house she is renting (fully furnished) in central London. (It’s his mother’s family home and the son’s bedroom was his when he was a boy,)Her mother - thank goodness – spoke for me and told the h to get over herself and to take the chip off of her shoulder and see that hero has been nothing but kind since she met him.The heroine can’t see it because she believed the lies the hero’s brother told her before he died in a car accident. The hero’s brother wanted the h for himself, so he told the h that hero was going to marry a family friend. The pregnant heroine then decided she wanted nothing to do with the hero. (Although she never tells him why.) The OW helped give that impression as well.The author gets the H/h together by using the cheap trick of hospitalizing the son for a mystery virus. The heroine realizes that life is short and her son needs both parents in his life. She proudly tells the hero she will marry him for her son’s sake. (At this point I wanted to slap her and tell her that she is no martyr – see about 100 Lynne Graham stories to see how it’s done)They marry in Italy and the hero’s family warmly welcomes her. Unfortunately family friend OW is there to tell the heroine that the hero visited her on his last night of freedom. Heroine is cold to the hero (what’s new) and won’t tell him about the OW.The hero uses his sexual wiles so they do have a happy wedding night. But the next morning the OW calls to upset the apple cart yet again. The heroine lies and says the phone call was from OM.Hero finally gets angry with the heroine and that’s when they figure out what went wrong 4 years before.Seems the brother told the hero that the heroine was in love with him and was only sleeping with the hero to get a job at one of his publishing companies. Heroine is offended he would think that of her, and then finally brings up the OW. The hero sets her straight – he was never interested in the OW. The OW was obsessed with him, but they never had a relationship. The reason he didn’t pursue the marriage proposal the first time was that he felt guilty for the brother’s death and thought that heroine was still mourning him. Heroine also felt guilty about the brother’s death. (He was upset when he left the heroine and drove recklessly.)That they both believed the brother lies and never spoke to each other about it is the cause of their separation for all of these years.I liked the hero well enough. It was obvious he was in love with the heroine from the beginning. I didn't like the way the author used a child's illness to bring about a reconciliation, but I could have lived with that if the heroine had been more sympathetic.
