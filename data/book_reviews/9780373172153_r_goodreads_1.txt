Second chance, secret baby story that hinges on a huge misunderstanding that continues until the very end of the story. There are lots of hints that the hero got it wrong, wrong, wrong - but we don't find out what the hero actually heard until the last quarter of the book.
Hero heard a tape of the heroine and his half-brother reading the script of an old play.

The story opens with the hero replaying in his mind how the heroine betrayed him with his half-brother the night before his half brother's wedding. It had only been a few days before when he (at 30) had taken the 18 year-old heroine's virginity. But apparently she loved sex so much she had to jump in bed with her best friend's fiance.

Now, five years later, the heroine is going to be godmother to his half-brother's infant. The hero is enraged by this and wants to keep the heroine away from his half-brother. Heroine's bff promised that the hero would not be in attendance at the christening. So heroine left her secret 4 year-old son with her father and returned to the manor house where she had loved and lost.

Hero is cruel to the heroine and she doesn't know why because he broke up with her. Hero eventually finds she has a son when she is called home because her father is ill. Hero thinks the child is his half-brother's.

Then there is the hero's father's will that he changed right before he died. Whomever has a son first will inherit the manor house. Heroine lets everyone know the hero has four year old son, hero doesn't dispute it and announces their engagement.

They do marry, have sex on their wedding night - and then it is deja vu all over again as the hero cruelly rejects her. Hero eventually spells out how he knew she cheated on him and that he thinks her son is his half brother's.
