I have mixed feelings about this organizing book. I didn't finish it because many of the chapters didn't offer new advise. On one hand the first few chapters dealt with organizing for a creative person, on the other hand, about 85% of the book seemed to just rehash what all organizing books state and to tell the "creative" person that they needed to just bite the bullet and make a plan.I read it to help one of my "creative" sons and did not find it very enlightening except for the first few chapters. Also I feel organizing books need to have visual pictures and this book had almost none.Now it would be interesting to see if I was a "creative" person if this would connect more with me.
