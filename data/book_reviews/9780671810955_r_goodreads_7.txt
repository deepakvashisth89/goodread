I have known about this book for many years and finally got around to reading. I was interested because it all took place near my hometown of LaGrange, GA. Well-told factual account of the events of the highly publicized 1948 murder of a poor tenant farmer by powerful local man -- John Wallace.Sheriff Lamar Potts does brilliant investigative work to bring justice to a man who believed he was above the law. My parents were from Heard County, GA and Mother used to tell about Mayhayley Lancaster, a local legend. Lancaster was consulted on the case. Very interesting page-turner.
