This completes the third book I’ve read from or about Malcolm X. The first being The Autobiography and the second being Malcolm X Speaks. This book in particular has the speeches and correspondence of Malcolm after he left the Nation of Islam up to the month of his assassination. This very capable man was in motion to organize the African masses around the world. Which is the reason why he was murdered by the American government. I will focus on two rallies and an interview.

The first rally is the founding rally of the Organization of Afro-American Unity (OAAU). Malcolm reads the “Statement of Basic Aims and Objectives of the OAAU”. After this speech and collections he discusses the structure of the organization. The charter, which he comments through, has elements of Pan-Africanism, Black nationalism, and self-improvement. In the speech about structure he explains the necessity to be independent financially through the funding of members and he describes the departments of the organization. It stood out to me that Muhammad Speaks, the Nation of Islam’s newspaper, was started by Malcolm and another person instead of a team of editors.

The second speech is from the second rally of the OAAU. It’s in a question and answer format. Two points of importance are Malcolm’s analysis that if you put your life at risk for an action, you must be willing to take lives as well. The other point was that in order to stop the American military interventions abroad, in this case in the Congo, the people must be organized at the city, state, and country level. In passing he discusses the need to have a long-range and short-range program in relation to the destiny of the Afro-American people, similar to the Marxist minimum and maximum program concept.

Lastly the interview was granted to representatives of the Young Socialist Alliance. In this short interview the most important take away for me was that white people should organize themselves and their communities. This approach, Malcolm states, has never been done and it’s what he recommends whites do if they want to fight against racism. He recognized that he was alienating white revolutionaries by excluding them from the revolutionary movement but maintained the position that they were most effective in their own communities. He also condemned both the Republican and Democratic parties for being responsible for racism in the USA.

Out of the three books I’ve read, this is the most useful for organizers. Especially the chapters discussing the ideas of the OAAU. With the inclusion of the first chapter, this book shows Malcolm’s political growth and what ultimately made him a danger to the ruling class. There is no bibliography or further reading sections in this book, there are other books advertised at the end that are in relation to the Socialist Workers Party. This completes for me the twelve book study recommended by the Chicago chapter of the Black Panther Party.
