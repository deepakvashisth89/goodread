This book is brilliant! The narrator's voice is so gentle and kind, talking straight to children to comment on and explain some details that may be too old or too harsh for their sweet minds. The morals are clear. The language is simple and sweet. The emotions are vivid, strong, and relatable.It's definitely a book I would read to my own children. There were plenty of moments when I felt I could just melt into a puddle of warm and fuzzy emotions.
