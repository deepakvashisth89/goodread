I just finished reading NOTES FROM A LIAR AND HER DOG. I was flipping through overdrive looking for something to fill a couple of hours and found this. HOW could I turn down a book about a child and her dog. In some ways, this was the saddest book I have ever read and in other ways, I think it is the plight of every child who has sibs to believe that their parent loves the other more than them.

I remember that I always believed my sister was better, prettier, got more and was loved more than I. With five years between us I was surprised when after our parents passed to find out that I was right! No, not really... I found out hat she believed the same thing. The book offers a strong lesson in life that we are not all the same and that parents are really only people trying to learn as they go. Parenthood is something you have to learn, you must just try your best.

I liked the little book very much. I adored Harrison and admired Just Carol... Mom was just harried with three kids and a job-hoping her husband and trying to get ahead in life. Well Done.

Happy Reading.
