Fantastic book on moral choices for children. Yes it was created in an earlier simpler time, but the values described transcend any cultural era. This book made a significant impact on me as a 7 year old, who had just discovered books. I could have written much of the plot from memory, and this is now 51 years later, it had that much of an impact.Children, Grandchildren, yes, they need books like this rather than the type stuff typically available now.Recommended for ages 5 through 9.
