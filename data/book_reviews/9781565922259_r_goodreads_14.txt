This is fun as a vintage programming textbook. But in 2026 I don't know if there's a practical reason to read this. After learning more about sed and awk, I feel like they're really useful for simple changes. But sed feels a little esoteric. Awk felt like it can be useful for more complex changes, but it's a whole new language to learn. If you're doing anything even mildly complex, you're probably better off using a language you're more comfortable with. Both sed and awk's commands seem to have many side effects that could make them confusing to use.
