As America's sole representative in the Oulipo group, Mathews shocked me by not being terribly Oulipian in this novel. The primary Oulipo element is Mathews' writing each chapter as an encounter between two people, and he recombines these encounters. But the chapters are really only ostensibly about the encounters, and each one contains quite a bit of other stuff going on. What you get in the end is a very fun novel about entangled interpersonal relationships yo-yoing across multiple decades and states, including the most hilarious story about sadomasochism I've ever heard.Show more
