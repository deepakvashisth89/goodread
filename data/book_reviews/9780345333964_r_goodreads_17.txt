Baum is back in form with this one, with some of my favorite characters - practical Trot, caring Cap'n Bill, lackadaisical Button Bright, and the loyal Ork, a flying creature who uses a propeller to fly and looks down on ordinary birds. Baum put a lot of care into the writing and the adventures, and this is one of my favorites.Trot, Cap'n Bill and Button Bright also show up in two non-Oz books, theThe Sea FairiesandSky Island. They are worth a read.
