Wolfe did create a full portrait of John Muir. She showed him as a conservationist, but also as a caring father.She told stories of his early years that will be new to some readers, and you can watch his character develop through his childhood experiences.She covers his mechanical genius, his subsequent injury, and his walk to Florida and eventual trip west.Wolfe shows Muir as more than just a conservationist, but also as inventor, husband and father, geologist, naturalist and writer. And a presidential persuader.She rounded out the portrait I had of Muir, and added depth to my understanding of my hero as a young man.
