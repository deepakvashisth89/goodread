Sometimes I think authors get a bit carried away with themselves and try to make story lines as they think life should be rather than similar to how it really is. I just couldn't buy that the principal male character hates that his wife loses her extra weight and has plastic surgery to look younger and instead prefers a late middle aged woman who, in his words, "acts like a man". On a more positive note it did deal interestingly with story of the Jews of Germany who fled to England during the Nazi regime.
