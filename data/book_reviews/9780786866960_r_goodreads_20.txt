I first read this book back when I was in high school (probably 2004 or 2005). I've been a massive Simpsons fan my whole life, and I have a fond appreciation of the business of voice-over work and the art of animation. Nancy's book provided insight into the process of how the episodes were created back in the 1990s and early 2000s. This book came out in 2000, right around the time the show was starting to become mediocre in quality after the absolute brilliance it held through the 90s. It's an interesting slice of time, and I loved the details! Nancy mentions several times about "One day when the show ends." It's insane that it's still going... for multiple reasons, lol.I enjoyed her diary entries describing what happened when a few specific guests were on the show. The part about Phil Hartman was very touching. Her enthusiasm and love of her job, her audience/fans, and the business of animation & VO, and of Bart himself made the book a fun quick read.
