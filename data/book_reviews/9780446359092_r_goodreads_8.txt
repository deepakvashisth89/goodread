Charming, non Miss Silver entry in Wentworth's long career. Broke, down on his luck Car Fairfax suddenly gets a mysterious job that involves lots of pay and mainly requires him to dress up and go to nightclubs. As he reenters society after a long time away, he encounters the girl he loves as well as the girl he doesn't, and he finds himself in danger before all is said and done. A combination adventure, mystery and romance, this is a delightful read.
