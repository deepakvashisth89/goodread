Generally speaking this is a selection of passages from the Zohar: the book of Splendor, the primary source text of Jewish mysticism (kabbalah). The editor is Gershom Scholem, the professor who brought kabbalah into serious academic study. While the average person could get something out of this book, it would be better to have some background in Hebrew. At least one should know the Hebrew alphabet and vowels. This is not absolutely necessary but it will help a lot. Also some familiarity with Jewish Torah Study would be beneficial. (FYI, I am Jewish.) There is a certain way that Jews study the Bible, especially the first 5 books of the Bible (The Torah). If you are not used to the thinking, the presumptions, the flow of study then this is all going to feel really weird. Just so you know.This book makes be want to read more of the Zohar.
