I'm not sure how I missed Sally Wright, but when I put down Publish and Perish, I was astonished at the intelligence behind the plot and characters. The book takes place in 1960/61, just after Kennedy was elected president. The protagonist, Ben Reese is an archivist at a small Midwestern college - wounded war veteran of the intelligence department, widower and researcher. While in England on a research sabbatical, he hears of the death of his dear friend, Richard West, curmudgeon and Chair of the English Department of their University. Ben flies home as executor and is quickly persuaded that Richard was murdered. Another professor dies mysteriously and when Ben himself is left for dead in an accident during a blizzard, he becomes convinced of the murderer's identity.Now he just has to prove it.This was an entertaining, erudite and gripping mystery. I've already downloaded the next in the series.
