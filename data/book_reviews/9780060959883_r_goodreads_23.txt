Pressfield is a talented wordsmith, but if I had never seen the film, I would have been thoroughly lost and confused.Most of the book is the replay of a fictional golf match, so anyone who loves the game and understands all the mechanics of the sport will be hugely entertained. This is quite cerebral and there is much to ponder here about discovering our true authentic self. There are references to the holiness of sport and some fascinating dialogue about the "mysterious current of consciousness" as well. There are some very inspired, and may I say heartwarming, moments that spoke to me.
