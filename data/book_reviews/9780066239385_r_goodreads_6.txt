I suppose I should begin by saying that I love Chris Lynch as a person. He's gracious and smart and lovely and I kind of adore him. But one of the things that is so fascinating about him is that his writing is all over the place for me. Sometimes (Slot Machine, the Blue-Eyed Son trilogy) it is so perfect and brilliant that I'm filled with sadness because I know I'll never be able to create anything even half as divine. And other times (He-Man Woman-Haters series, this book) where the characters are so reprehensible and the story so annoying and the narrative voice so irksome that I want to set the book on fire so that it will no longer exist in my sphere.I am curious, though, as to whether he picked up the phrase "moms" (to refer to his mother) from Adam Rapp. Hmmm . . . .
