Dead Water (Roderick Alleyn, #23) by Ngaio Marsh.Another excellent addition to the Golden Age of Mysteries. This story finds Inspector Alleyn on the coastal village of Portcarrow. His long time friend Miss Emily Pride has inherited the island from her deceased sister. Unfortunately, Miss Pride has taken it upon herself to bring the way of life as the villagers know it to a halt. She has further announced her intentions to those in charge of the miraculous springs now known as Pixie Falls. This abrupt transition has caused an uproar against her approaching arrival. Inspector Alleyn has warned her against going to the village at this time, but she is bound and determined to not heed his request.Upon her arrival Miss Pride makes her wishes known in no uncertain terms bringing Alleyn to the village for her own protection. There he finds out about the goings on at Pixie Falls and the miraculous cures that have taken place.Excellent book and has its place in My Favorites file. This author is above reproach in details. 5 stars *****
