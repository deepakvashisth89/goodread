I did not read the first book in this series but now I need to go back and do so. This was a good book and I liked the character of Korine McFaile. She attends a landscaping convention in Savannah and is assigned a room with Dodie Halloran, a woman who, for some reason, hates her. When Dodie is found murdered, Korine is the prime suspect. She also meets up with an old boyfriend who seems to be looking to rekindle their romance. There are references to the first book that made no sense to me so I needs to read that one but this was a good book.
