This is a very successful and coherent short story collection, in that each story elaborated and expanded upon Gaitskill's thesis, like chapters in a book. I understood completely and obliquely what she wanted to say. Intensely compelling to read, convincingly and strangely sexual, and with a kind of post-feminist and post-queer understanding about the desperation of gender (especially pathetic Maleness). However, I think I know everything I need to know about Gaitskill now, I've seen right through her beautiful, hollow men and her much more various inchoate girls. While this collection is not a 5 stars as it can repeat, be overwritten, use the same metaphors and ways of structuring arguments, I'm sure if she saw me she'd see right through my attempt at tension and power and write my micro-expressions, pretense of sexuality, in another of her little books. What an observer of character!
This is definitely to say that I loved this collection! The first couple of stories I absolutely got my shit rocked. I thought, is this my father and I? - with a terrible pain (No, for our story is stranger than fiction: we love each other now). I thought, I didn't know that anyone else could have such complicated feelings about other people's children. I felt actually nauseous and transparent, with strange water drops of emotion falling on my rubber surface.

I need to rest a bit after this! And I need to write a bit more about it too !
