This mail-order bride story is OK, and I'm not sorry I read it, but some of the dialogue felt a little bit forced, awkward, or unreal, and their was one situation that the main character, Linda, went through where I found her reaction to be really understated.When she finds out she has been married to a criminal, and not the wonderful man she fell in love with through letters, after everything else she's been through, I would have expected her to react much more strongly than is portrayed here. It just didn't seem believable.The historical time period and other interesting elements of the story did keep me reading until the end.
