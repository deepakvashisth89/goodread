Incredible… and also incredibly funny. Martha writes with such precision and confident splendor, but apart from some great tips on how to plan a party and deal with a caterer, her menus (even the “gracious” and “simple” ones) seem both wildly out-of-reach to even those with plenty of disposable income (you really need TIME and also scads of talent to pull them off) and even, to a 21st century eye, gross. . . Think “heart-shaped cream cheese dessert” for Valentine’s Day and “lime wedge on a pile of prosciutto” and “midnight omelette brunch for sixty.” You will be surprised at how often she pushes the omelette idea.
