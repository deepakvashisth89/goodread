This book is a compilation of the most common problems that occupied the attention of the major European philosophers during the span of nearly three centuries. To name some of the philosophers included, you'll find Descartes' Discourse on Method, Spinoza's Nature of Evil, Leibniz' Relation Between Soul and Body, Rousseau's Social Contract, Kant's Critique of Pure Reason, Hegel'sIntroduction to the Philosophy of History, Nietzsche's Beyond Good and Evil, etc.
