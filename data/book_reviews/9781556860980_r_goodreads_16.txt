Book #9: Tanglewood Tales

Sequel to "A Wonder-Book," "Tanglewood Tales" relates six more of the classic myths, retold for children. Included are:

-The Minotaur (story of Theseus)
-The Pygmies (the giant Antaeus)
-The Dragon's Teeth (Cadmus, Phoenix, Cilix, Europa)
-Circe's Palace (Ulysses/Odysseus)
-The Pomegranate Seeds (Proserpina/Persephone, Ceres/Demeter, Pluto/Hades)
-The Golden Fleece (Jason, the Argonauts)

Quite enjoyable, though the addresses to the reader that were sprinkled throughout the narrative by the chatty storyteller were not exactly necessary, nor did they even help the story, really. I found them to be a bit patronizing. Aside from that, this is one worth reading and re-reading.
