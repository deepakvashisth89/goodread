Having read Alleyn 3 before this one, Enter a Murderer was a bit of a let-down. I thought I had wandered into an episode of "Murder, She Wrote", stilted dialogue, unbelieveable wrap, and all. (How much did she actually know about heroin addicts?) Yes, I know, it's a vintage cosy. But if I had read this before "The Nursing Home Murder" I would have given up on Marsh without a backward glance. Fortunately in vol. 3 there is a decided improvement in voice, devices and plotting.ETA: I just listened to this on audiobook, and had completely forgotten everything except the name and MC of the play!
