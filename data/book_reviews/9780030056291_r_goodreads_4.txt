I liked this book and even though I didn't hate the ending, it still seemed to me like too much was left out for the reader to piece it together herself. I liked Vandike a lot but the other characters didn't really pop for me. As always, I love Emmy and Henry but it occurred to me while I read this that Emmy could have found out more information just by being a woman and a wife.I do wish Moyes was still alive and writing books... I think she and Christie are both two of mystery's big geniuses.
