I picked this up as a Library discard because of the intriguing title. Is a "light" read. Lightly engaging, lightly engrossing, lightly entertaining and lightly memorable. I would term this: a "cotton candy" book. It is sweet, but quickly forgotten and then you are still hungry for something more substantial. It doesn't leave a bad taste in your mouth, so its not a total waste of time, but there are more memorable, satisfying fare to be had.I hate giving this kind of review, when I know the author labored quite a bit on delivering the goods. So, sorry Ms. Jones. But readers have the right to know what kind of book they are biting into.Vh1
