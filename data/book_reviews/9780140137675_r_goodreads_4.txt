"S.T. Bindoff has enjoyed a career remarkable chiefly for its uneventfulness." How can you not like an author who introduces himself like that? Typical British understatement which shows up every now and then in the text, too. I took a course on Tudor England from Bindoff when I was a student at Stanford (long ago). I bought his book then ($1.25) and just got around to reading it. And it's good! Dry, but much more of a social and economic history of an era than a kings and queens melodrama. Some beautiful writing and some wry humor here and there.
