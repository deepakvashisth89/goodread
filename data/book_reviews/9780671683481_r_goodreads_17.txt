One of the girls that he asked to marry him kind of teased him and many girls took advantage of him as he gave them free goodies/drugs from his store to win their approval.He bought an insurance policy on the "tease" and then asked a fellow to kill her. The fellow got desperately drunk and the three teenagers were killed - mistaken identity.
