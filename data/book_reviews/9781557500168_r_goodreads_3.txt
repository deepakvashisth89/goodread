A book that tries to set the record straight about American astronomers of the 1800s who made lasting contributions to their field, yet whose work and reputation were downplayed by various European astronomers. If you've ever heard of the Chandler wobble, this book is for you! :)

The book is directed more at astronomers than the general public; I think it would be a tough read for people unfamiliar with the jargon. They don't do much in terms of defining terms or explaining the significance of different efforts they describe, until very late in the book. I found the pacing frustrating.
