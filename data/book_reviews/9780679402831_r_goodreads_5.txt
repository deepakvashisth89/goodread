Vachss books are such tough reads sometimes. This one was especially dark - where the worst of humanity sometimes dwells. Burke (PI or more of a vigilante for hire) is back again in book #6 attempting to save those who fall through the cracks of the legal system in New York and be their salvation. Child crimes are tied deep to his past and this one touches part of who he is or was - a victim of child abuse.Nevertheless, this plot was difficult to follow - too many side subplots and characters. I found it hard to keep track and felt a bit nauseated from the subject matter of cults and children - probably a bad choice this time of the year. The final third of the book picked up a bit which pushed me through to the end. 3 stars. Not recommended for the faint of heart.
