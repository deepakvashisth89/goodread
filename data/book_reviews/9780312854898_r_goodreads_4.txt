After reading such glowing reviews I ordered this book but was disappointed. Yes, it does turn classic D&D style books on its ear, but I thought that Costikyan's Cups and Sorcery (also uncompleted series) did it better: "Another Day, Another Dungeon" and "One Quest, Hold the Dragons".Also, the main character's sudden change from young tribesman to thinking barbarian reminded me too much of Dave Duncan's The Reluctant Swordsman (The Seventh Sword Book 1), which at least has an explanation for the transformation.
