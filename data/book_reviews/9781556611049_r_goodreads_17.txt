Sue, North River patron, June 2016, 5 stars:

Series I read Book 1 and 2 this week, The Journals of Corrie Belle Holister. Book 1, My Fathers' World. Corrie is traveling with a wagon train to CA with Mother and 4 siblings to start new life with her uncle Nick. Her Father and Uncle Nick left the family years before, they thought the father dead. Mother dies before arriving in CA, wagon master looks after kids, Corrie the oldest at 15 has a big surprise while looking for Uncle Nick. Just discovered author Michael Phillips recently and love his writing. Book 2, , tells about Corrie's surprise, the Gold Rush in Ca., living in a little 2 room cabin. Getting a Preacher, a church building, a school for the first time. Corrie dreams of writing for a news paper. Gets to meet Ulysses Grant, write article with picture of Grant she has drawn. Finds her father is still alive. Book is about history, family and finding out about God's love.
