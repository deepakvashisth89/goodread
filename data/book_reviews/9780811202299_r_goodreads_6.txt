Here is a book I read many over the years. And when I am teaching a
poetry class for children or adults I use it as a nice
reference book to what Williams accomplished as a modern "American" poet.

Spring and All is my favorite section. Imaginations! Hmm. Great and
so simple but great poems by a true American English speaking poet
from Rutherford, New Jersey. I glad he stayed in Rutherford he mined
the language of Polish, Italian and Jewish immigrant voices of
Paterson's silk mills and factories. which are now warehouses.
