Another solid entry in the series. I’m a sucker for a hardboiled-noir detective novel (or film), and this series scratches that itch.

Kaminsky layers in plenty of WW2 era American cultural references, and the plot is decently but not overly complicated. This one is told mostly in flashback after a dramatic shootout opening.

The nutty dream sequences, featuring Koko the Clown among others, are hilarious—an homage to Raymond Chandler’s poetic riffs on Marlowe’s often being knocked unconscious or drugged and having similar dreams.

Workmanlike with a dash of panache.
