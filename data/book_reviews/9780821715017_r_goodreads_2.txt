It may seem I am trolling, when I give Amber Passion a 5-star review. Or being ironic. Both are false, and though it does not TOUCH the "real 5-stars," I give other, sometimes "more serious works" the place I hold for it in my heart is large, and the thrill upon my first readings --then loaning out to all my sexually confused/frustrated girlfriends-- was priceless. The star ratings are weird, anyway. I LOVED IT!!! It was my Twilight, I s'pose. Amber Passion is the absolute FIRST "bodice ripper" (so hate that expression, it's the 80's version of "mommy porn") that I ever read. I bought it at Annie's Book Stop in Wiscasset, Maine with hard-earned babysitting money. Was it terrible? Hells to the yes! Purple? Purpler than Barney the dinosaur. Trashy? Oscar the Grouch would have grabbed it out of my 12-year-old hands. BUT....did it get me to start writing erotica as an adolescent? Totally. Help me comprehend the fact that the funny feeling in my Sedgefield jeans might not be a problem, but kind of a good thing? Absolutely. Did it make me think being a redhead wasn't the worst thing that could have happened? Well it IS Amber Passion. Did it make me find pirates even hotter? Blah, blah, blah. When my book was released, I found this on Amazon and ordered it. This was back in December. I still haven't re-read it, because I am saving it for....I don't know what, but will continue this review when I do reconnect with my 12-year-old self, looking at the 80's nymphet's version of Playboy. But I will never change my five stars. Because once upon a time, i loved this book. It changed my life.
