This one's about a serial killer murdering men with AIDS, so you know right away it's going to be a laugh riot.Genuinely though: starts out extremely strong; Dave Brandstetter's need to uncover what's going on clearly goes far beyond his usual work as an insurance investigator. His meeting with friends and loved ones of AIDS victims is wrenching and plainly and powerfully depicted by Hansen.Then there's a twist about 3/4ths of the way through and the rest is fairly silly. But overall, I remain impressed and fascinated by this series, even though no single book has blown me away.
