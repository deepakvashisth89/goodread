Irish Eyes by Andrew M. Greeley is book 5 of the Nuala Anne McGrail mystery series set in contemporary Chicago. Dermot and Nuala are the proud parents of Nelliecoyne, 7 months old, winning everyone's heart with her adorable ways. She's a "good baby", as in: sleeps all night so her parents can too. But one night when they're vacationing at the lakeshore, she wakes them screaming. Nuala tells Dermot, "It's the five-masted ship in the lake that's bothering her." Dermot knows there is no such vessel in the lake, hasn't been for a hundred years. It's the first time they knew Nellie is "fey" just like her mother.

Of course Dermot and Nuala must solve the mystery of why the ship appeared to Nellie and Nuala, and why it upsets them.

They learn of a shipwreck at the spot one hundred years ago, then begin tracing what became of the few survivors.

Dermot's father needs to pull strings to arrange for Dermot and Nuala to meet with an oh-so-busy-and-self-important socialite, descendant of a survivor.

Meanwhile they must deflect troublesome journalist Nick Farmer, who once attacked Dermot in the press. Now he insults Nuala for her popular Irish lullaby performances and recordings.

Then there's Maybelline, neurotic sister-in-law with a psychiatrist husband, who is vicious to Nuala.







Dermot, Nuala and Nellie take a break from mystery and troublesome people, to spend Christmas in Ireland. Alas, they must leave Fiona the Irish wolfhound at home.

Back to the shipwreck...

Dermot's resourceful, assertive sister Cindy once again is their attorney when police try strong-arm tactics.

Dermot

Dermot and Nuala are terrified when Nellie contracts a high fever. Luckily they only have to call Dermot's physician father for help.

Eventually they learn all that happened from century-old diaries.







Fiona the Irish wolfhound is the shining star of the story. Nellie's constant guardian, she risks her life to save Nellie from armed intruders.
