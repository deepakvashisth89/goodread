I was immediately drawn to the main character, Iliana, and the questions she seemed to be exploring. Why is my family broken when others are normal? What long past incident can I pin current failings to? Is there some supernatural force that drives the evil I can’t escape?There are whispers of magical realism - or perhaps magical thinking - that lift the narrative and seem to offer hope of redemption. The story is carried fluidly through the darkest month of a year, while slipping through different points of view and occasional memories.I found the struggle of hope and redemption through mental illness, violence, poverty, domestic abuse, and self-doubt to be at once chilling and captivating. The writing is exquisite as it is heartbreaking.
