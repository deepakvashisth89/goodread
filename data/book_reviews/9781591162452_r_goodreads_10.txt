I saw the Angel Sanctuary anime OVA on a whim back in 2001 and became completely obsessed. Back then I had a serious thing for twisted religious material, so the fact that in Angel Sanctuary the angels are portrayed as the villains while the demons are the heroes really piqued my interest.I know people really hate on the incestuous relationships in this series, but to me, it's just fiction and it doesn't bother me. Quite the opposite; I love a forbidden romance. There are at least two brother/sister pairings in this manga but they're not treated like they're just normal couples. They fight their feelings, they fight each other, they fear society's reaction, they are willing to kill and die to be together.Volume 1 of this manga is packed with so much info it's almost overwhelming - in a good way. You get a lot of bang for your buck, basically. There's a lot going on, there are a lot of characters, and there are several interesting side panels that explain more of the story and how the author came up with it. There's a war being waged between heaven and hell, and humanity is about to be dragged into it. There's a guy who wants more than anything to be normal, but he has unnatural strength and discovers he is the reincarnation of a powerful angel. There's a demon trying to reunite with this hell-sympathizing, reincarnated angel so they can take on heaven's warrior angels together. There's a wicked angel infecting the world with a brain-frying video game. And there are forbidden romances and unrequited love and love triangles galore.Kaori Yuki's art, to me, is god tier. There are so few comparable in my mind. She draws in such exquisite detail, and all her characters are so beautiful you can't tell which are male and which are female. But that's part of the point in this series; androgyny plays a huge role. There are guys in this manga who thrive on being called beautiful. There's a demon who has the body of a man and the beauty of a woman and screams to the heavens, "Don't underestimate the power of the third gender!"Both the art and the story are amazingly complex. If you're looking for a manga that's more meat and less fluff, this is it.Fingers crossed that VIS will republish Angel Sanctuary in omnibus format in English like they have in German, because I badly need it in my life!
