This guide to two of the most commonly used and powerful *nix command-line tools was written back in the 1990s, but it has remained in print (at least as an ebook) simply because these tools have remained broadly the same for decades now. However, resources for learning sed and awk have evolved over the years and, faced with that competition, this book is no longer very important.Thus, while this book is a convenient introduction to sed, there isn’t much here that won’t be readily covered in free introductions on the web. With regard to awk, Arnold Robbin’sGAWK: Effective AWK Programming(a revised and expanded version of this old book) is freely provided along with GNU Awk as a PDF and covers some significant new features that have appeared in GNU Awk in recent years. So, don’t go out of your way to find this old O’Reilly manual.
