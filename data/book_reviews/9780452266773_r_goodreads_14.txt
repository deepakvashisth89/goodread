Sometimes people tell me that the book they’re looking for has to be easy to read and mustn’t ask any difficult questions at all, because they really don’t want anything that makes them think (too much?). This always strikes me as very odd — to say the least — because that’s exactly what I don’t want my books to be or do. I want to be challenged. I want to step out of my comfort zone. I want different perspectives and, most importantly of all, I want to be triggered.

Yes, you read that right: I don’t want and don’t need trigger warnings. If I did, I wouldn’t keep opening one book after the other, after the other.

, published in 1990, is my first novel by Jamaica Kincaid and represents everything I long for in fiction (and more). It’s deceptively simply written, yet nuanced, layered, and poignant. Because of how real she is, not everyone is going to like Lucy — but I seriously doubt anyone could possibly forget her. I know I won’t.
