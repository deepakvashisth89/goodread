A pretty astonishing book. Picked it up for $3 at a library book sale because of the title. Ever since the second law of thermodynamics became clear to me after it was applied to heat exchange between a building and the outdoors, it has changed how I see the world. It’s everywhere. And can be used as an explanation for so much phenomena. I keep wanting to read more about it.

Written in the late 80s, but incredibly prescient in its predictions. And timely, given how the world’s resources are constrained by a 35 mile strait. Plus, I’m a sucker for applying fundamental physical laws to explain how human social systems work. And energy and entropy are a pretty great frame for understanding the state of the world. Certainly does a better job than the social sciences, and should be used as a foundation for those fields.

Rifkin says we’re moving into a Solar Age. But it’s impressive that even back then he predicted how people would make the mistake of thinking we can make a 1:1 replacement of non-renewables with renewables, like the green growth and abundance adherents, and keep the train moving at increasing speed. Up, up, up. He even mentioned the mining problems these new energies have, causing more entropy.

Definitely re-readable. And I plan to do so in the future. Can be heavy though, and took me a while to read because of that. But I came out of it with more reverence for life and a general outlook that lowering entropy is a good barometer to judge individual and societal options.
