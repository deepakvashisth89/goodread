There are just some books that seem like the author is talking down to the audience. The lead character is set to be smarter than the entire town but the plot seems to think the readers are natives. It just didn't work for me. It almost seemed like she wrote an outline and then filled in a word count the quickest, easiest way possible. I thought I've read other books in this series (a LONG time ago) but I was either mistaken or something went seriously wrong with this one.

The mystery isn't. When the cast had a bit of a sharp turn, it was almost an after thought and not developed further. The humorous scenes were truncated just before the punchline. The holes were just assumed to be spackled with an ending monologue.

This book looked like fun and just wasn't.
