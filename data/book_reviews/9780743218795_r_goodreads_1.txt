I don’t know how what’s clearly a pitch for a Green Acres style sitcom winds up being condescending to both city folks and rural people, but Jim Mullen manages it. For someone who’s theoretically a humorist, he seems to think that the existence of gay people is funny, even as he notes that they have AIDS. Also weirdly racist in a very old man way (was anyone saying “mulatto” in 2001)? And he can’t decide on a verb sense within any given paragraph? And he thinks that saying “Yanni” is inherently hilarious?
Just talked myself down another star. Yeesh.
