This book was a very well researched and written history of Stalin's initial years and involvement in the Revolution led by Lenin. If you have any interest in Stalin and the Marxist/Leninist movement of the early 20th century, this is a book you'll want to read.Sadly, the author planned a three volume series, but did not live long enough to publish the third volume. I have the second volume on my shelf and intend to give it a perusal sometime soon, though.
