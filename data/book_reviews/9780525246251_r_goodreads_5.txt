Chasing a psychopathDonato and his daughter are both cops. At the beginning of the story they are estranged from each other due to an earlier family trauma. Over course of the book in the hunt for a min murdering serial killer many revelations of family secrets both their own and the killers give clues to a resolution but by bit, revelation by revelation. Will they get their killer? Will they mellow towards each other? You'll need to read the book!
