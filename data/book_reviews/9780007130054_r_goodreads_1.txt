I think this is the perfect general access biography that one could ask for. It could easily have become bogged down in the minutiae of the technology or side-tracked with any of the mini-stories linked to the development of wireless communications. Instead however, we hear of a polarising family man and serial adulter who develops an incredible system of which he has next to no understanding of. The use of Marconi systems on transatlantic ocean liners such as the Republic, Titanic Volturno and Lusitania underscores the importance of the system and makes you wonder where the world would be without this technology.
