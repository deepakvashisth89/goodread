Movies take the Discworld by storm.The magic of moving pictures altering reality in a way so that it opens an ancient portal to the Dungeon Dimensions is an interesting concept. The plot isn’t exactly the strongest you’ll find in the Discworld series though. The strength of the plot lies more in poking fun at the film industry, with lots of witty references to pop culture and the early days of cinema in particular.There’s a reason why the main characters never return in other Discworld novels. But there are other interesting characters in this novel who do return later on like Detritus the troll, Cut-me-own-Throat Dibbler and the real star of this novel: Gaspode the talking dog.While I would recommend starting with other books if you’re new to the Discworld series, this is quite a fun and interesting stand alone novel with a cool concept.
