Okay so I liked this book, it had its moments I just didnt think overall it was as good as it could have been..okay so this is the story of Henry Slater, a married man and Mayor of a town in California with a perfect life who seems to have it all, all of it as well as a huge obsession with seventeen year old Sheila..It starts off innocently enough as the older man makes more and more of an effort to buy her things, see her, treat her special and her grandmother is not having any of it. It then becomes Henry's mission in life to see her, to have her and if her grandmother is the only obstacle well he will just have to find her way around that, and he does...the book picks up from there as Henry will stop at NOTHING to make sure no one knows about him and Sheila and that no one ever finds out..It is thriling and suspenseful I did enjoy that however I didnt feel a big reveal with the ending and I wish it was a little more exciting, less drawn out..good read though
