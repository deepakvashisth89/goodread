i’ve been wanting to read this one for ages. it was a quick read, as it was written for readers much younger than myself, but it was startling and complex in a way that is rare in middle-grade books. this book narrates a 13-year-old girl’s experience of grief during and after the loss of her older sister. but it’s more than a story about death—also about unlikely intergenerational friendship, pursuing art, gender roles, and healing. would have absolutely destroyed me if i had read it earlier in life. especially poignant now.
