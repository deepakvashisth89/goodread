“If you do one good deed your reward usually is to be set to do another and harder and better one.” 🏇⚔️🦁

another amazing book that takes place in the beautiful world of narnia! thoroughly enjoyed it!

the plot!! i loved this book! it isn’t my favorite of the series, but it’s definitely up there. the characters were cute and i loved the idea of a boy befriending a horse. i thought that was the cutest thing. i want to be friends with a horse too. no fair!

the characters!! i loved both shasta and bree so much. they were adorable! instant besties. they were both so easy to like. you’ll be rooting for them on their exciting journey!

the pevensies!! the rumors are true, the pevensies are in this book! sure, they aren’t the main characters in this book, but they do pop in from time-to-time. even mr. tumnus has a cameo! seeing these beloved characters back made me so happy. i missed them so much!

the ending!! i finished this book with a smile on my face. it truly was a good one. i loved seeing the characters all get their happy endings!

would i recommend this book? i would!
would i read it again? definitely!
