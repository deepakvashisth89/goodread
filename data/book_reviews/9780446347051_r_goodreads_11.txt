Thought I was reading The November Man, based off the large title. Turns out was reading There Are No Spies. Great book. But movies getting big and changing the titles in a series to the same title as the first book while in reality this is still the seventh is REALLY DUMB.That confusion aside, you can totally jump in and read this series anywhere.This was a fast paced simple thriller. Politics, ruthless spies, government cover ups. It’s got a little bit of everything which does come together nicely in the end. Deveraux is a compelling character, very cold and calculating but fascinating to observe.Great read for people looking for something fast and easy. If you are here to try to follow along with the movie, don’t expect much. Very different stories but still an interesting plot.
