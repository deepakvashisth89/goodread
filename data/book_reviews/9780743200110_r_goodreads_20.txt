I thought I'd a reasonable handle on the living world, then along came this fabulous book. MIND BLOWN! My thanks to Carl Zimmer and all who research parasites because this is far more than the big jar of preserved tapeworm at the back of the biology lab. This is an indepth, readable (utterly fascinating) look at what may be the largest and most influential part of life on this planet.And, not going to lie, as an SF writer--so many more options!
