Richard Russo’s books tend to a familiar pattern. Decaying upstate New York town with the gravitational pull of a black hole and where dreams die. A cast of characters consisting of the good guy, the bad guy and of course the utterly feckless but probably good guy and strong women who endure.

It is wonderfully done compassionate and moving throughout. Mohawk is darker than most of his books but as enjoyable and touching as them all.
