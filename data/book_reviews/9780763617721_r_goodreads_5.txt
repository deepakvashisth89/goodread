This is an interesting book about the imagination and power of youthful wonder and belief. While Annabelle's mother and father are too old and busy to believe in and look for fairies, Annabelle is not.

The story is entertaining and the characters reminded me of those from "Let's Get a Pup!" Said Kate and "The Trouble with Dogs..." Said Dad, also by Bob Graham.

Overall, it was an entertaining story and we enjoyed reading it together.
