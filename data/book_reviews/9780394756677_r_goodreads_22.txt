A novel that relies heavily on visual representation and page layout. Dialogue appears untagged - and appears almost like a screenplay. Pauses within speech are shown in space, the ship's manifest is reproduced on page, and there is a diagram of the ship (rather than a verbal description). Days, and time of day, are shown by graphic depiction of the sun and moon. What words compose a man? Matthessien arranges his description of Vemon in the shape of a man late in the novel.Overall it is a fun book - a little difficult to follow before you know the characters - a book that took me back to the days when I eagerly read Robert Louis Stevenson and his adventure tales. Definitely recommended.
