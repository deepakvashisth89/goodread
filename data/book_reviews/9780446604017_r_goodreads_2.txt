A good book with a bummer ending. Not surprising, I suppose, since the theme of this novel is the near impossibility of surviving as an outsider in a world governed by the corporate state.Cat's a sweet character who somehow manages to be a bridge between peoples, but also manages to find himself alone & disregarded.This is a book filled with longing - for community, for change, for love, for companionship, for a way to be whole. Vinge writes interesting characters & the plot here is nicely character driven, although she doesn't really do anything with the cloud whales and their dreamfall - odd to set up such a cool premise & then just sort of leave it there.This is the third book of a trilogy and I think I like the second one, Catspaw, best.
