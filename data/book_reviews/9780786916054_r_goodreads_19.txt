A marked step back from the first entry in the series and from the usual Salvatore fare. It's possible that reading two of Salvatore's books in relatively short succession was a mistake. The D&D tropes and stereotypes became a bit grating and overused. The story advances relatively little, and there's a heavier focus on combat. Maybe that's the problem, his action scenes are not particularly strong; so if there's a lot of them then they really start to grate on the nerves.Still planning on continuing in the series, but I'll leave a longer gap before I pick it up again.
