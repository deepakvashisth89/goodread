I found this book at work and decided to give it a shot. The cover has retro RL Stine vibes and anything involving dangerous quicksand sounds exciting but.....Exciting is not exactly how I found it. The story feels like a badly written Hallmark mystery. I found the plot to be a bit dull and the writing could use some *pizazz* too. It took me about a week to finish the last 60 pages because I was just bored with it.I also want to note that I realize the book was published in 1989 but at one point, the boys literally slaughter an alligator. It was a sad moment for me. I was just wondering why they didn't catch a fish? AND when they catch Kenny, Ben contemplates killing him!
