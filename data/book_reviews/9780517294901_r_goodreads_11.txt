I got this book on my 6th birthday and have treasured it ever since. I hadn’t actually read it in years, but since I’ve been too ill to read serious novels lately, I decided it would be a good time to revisit this. I still love the first book so much, especially because it tells of many more adventures than the movie could hold. The second is not quite as good as I remembered. It seems to sag in the middle but still has many entertaining parts. I might try to find the next book online. It would be totally new to me, as my parents never bought me the rest of the series.
