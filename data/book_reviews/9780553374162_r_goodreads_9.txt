I do not know why it took me so long to read this. I admire McDonald immensely. The lyric poet of SF. Amazing imagination, inventiveness of language, the immersion of history and culture and speculation, stirred and twisted into night-shapes of surpassing exoticism. And the basic concept! (Possibly the only zombie novel I will ever recommend, though it isn't quite that, no...)A feast. Sit down, bring you steely knives, and check in.
