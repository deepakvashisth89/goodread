Great book for identifying gemstones and crystals, as well as for learning about how they affect us. I use this book often as a reference for my jewelry designs, that I make and sell online for the purpose of alternative healing. It gives the history of the stone, healing properties, and magical properties. It also has a section on the zodiac, and which stones work well for each sign and why, and is a very good introduction to stone work for chakra healing. It's hard bound, and I like that each stone has a picture associated with it.I would recommend this book to anyone interested in stones, and alternative healing. It's a great bridge to other fields of knowledge. Enjoy!!
