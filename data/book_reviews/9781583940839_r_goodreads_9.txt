Cosmic Thoughts from Phyllis:

Evidence of the forthcoming energetic transformation of not only humanity and the earth, but the whole solar system. Wilcox’s credible and well researched treatise is presented to the reader by Wynn Free and later by David Wilcock himself: The imminent evolution of our planet and all whom live on it. This work is targeted to those that want to understand the significance of the changes that are now taking place. With so many prophesies through the years of death, doom and destruction for this planet, this extraordinary compilation turns them all on their head and provides evidence of a rare opportunity for everyone who reads this book and wishes to take steps to awaken to their inner self.

Wilcox’s incredible story of awakening will resonate most highly with those who are ‘wanderers’ – those from higher ‘densities’ or vibrations who have chosen to incarnate here on earth. Also presented is Wilcox’s extraordinary gift of the ability to channel information from higher sources, both the struggles and the incredible awe of it all. If as a reader you can indeed ‘step back’ from the maelstrom of everyday living and look at a higher level view of our life here on earth, the information presented is most incredibly uplifting and inspiring !

Ra, the higher source of David Wilcox, conveys incredible wisdom through David. Awareness of how we are limited by negative forces and how this awareness can move you past these artificial limitations – how we can move past our finite physical bodies to a realization of the greater cosmos and the Oneness inherent in our universe. The inherent wisdom in the Ra readings stand on their own. Healing – how you (yes, you) can help our evolution through focused thought/prayer/intention to send positive healing energy to the earth and all it’s inhabitants.

A plethora of links to other researchers – current research presented here has literature references and web links, a TON of work has gone into Wilcox’s effort. Most remarkable of the lot of researchers is the non-profit organization LL Research – a resource for wanderers – those reincarnated visitors who have care and concern for the earth, it’s transformation, and the evolution of souls that is taking place ( ).

This work resonates positively with me and I give this book a most cosmic 5+++++. A must read for those who are already awake and are seeking to know more !
