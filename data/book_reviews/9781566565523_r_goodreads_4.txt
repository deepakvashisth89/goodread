David Ray Griffin has written a number of books on the subject of 9/11. I have read most of them. All are well written, entertaining, and scary. If you, like I, tend to think of conspiracy theorists as nut cases, the books on 9/11 written by Griffin might cure you. Before you read any of them, take a quick peek at any YouTube video of World Trade Center Building 7 falling. Note the little plumes of smoke that just precede the drop. Note how they are each exactly one above the other floor after floor.Then note how perfectly symmetrical is the collapse itself. And fast! Without any resistance by floors below.Then read the book. The key to understanding 9/11 is to focus on building 7. It was never hit by anything other than debris. It was a block removed from the Twin Towers. A few fires started on several floors, but these were being controlled by firemen, who are on record in their belief that the fires were under control and could be extinguished. They were ordered out several hours before the building collapsed. A reporter for CNN reported that the building had collapsed--30 minutes BEFORE it did!Once you understand what must have happened to building 7, it makes it much easier to accept that maybe the same thing happened to the Twin towers. That makes you then wonder about the Pentagon with its lack of plane parts and bodies, and the Pennsylvania crash, with similar missing pieces.This book is very disturbing. Do not read it if you are a martini sipping patriot who refuses to accept that our own government just might have participated in the murder of its own citizens on 9/11.
