This novel doesn't get nearly the amount of love that 'Notre Dame' and 'Les Mis' receives, and while I have yet to get to those two, I can't imagine them being much better than this. Gilliatt might be the most likeable character in literature, and Hugo's style reminded me of Melville, which was a pleasant surprise. Apparently some people are put off by the opening section titled 'The Archipelago of the Channel', but I thought that was where Hugo's descriptive prose really shines. I loved it. No complaints whatsoever and I hope people continue to read this for many years to come. Easy five stars.
