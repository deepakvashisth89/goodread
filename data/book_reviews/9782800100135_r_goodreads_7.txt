What a better place to go on Safari than the wild and unknown African Continent? I too always wanted to go visit the silver back gorillas. In fact, at one point I happily contemplated studying them in Africa. My dad's response was to go show me in his private library a book that was written by someone in our family, an aunt who now lived in Tasmania, and who had done just that, together with either Dian Fossey or Jane Goodall. One more of our illustrious family members, and though I wish so hard to meet her and see if I could follow on her footsteps, that dream never conjured enough strength to become a reality. My mother's reaction was to say that she too had always wished to go to Africa, and that if I ever made it, I must bring her and if I must study gorillas, or chimps, or any other primate, that her contribution would be a series of knitted sweaters for them... Perhaps that is why it never came to pass...

Le gorille a bon mine is none other than Fantasio disguised as one of them in order to study them from closer quarters! It is a charming story and has a lot of funny scenes that are very out dated, though very true to the spirit of those times. I hope one day i will get my chance to go visit, if not stay for a while.
