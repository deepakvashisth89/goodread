Alexa's fiance jilted her for her bff and she's totally devastated. She attends the wedding because otherwise the gossip will be unbearable, besides her bff's mother had written and specially asked her to attend because her bff feels so bad and guilty.At the reception she meets her ex's brother Scott and he looks so much like Marc that she can almost pretend he is. She gets very drunk and ends up in bed with him and would have given her virginity to him if she hadn't called him by his brother's name. She then locks herself in the bathroom and attempts suicide via swallowing as many aspirin as she can on top of a great deal of alcohol. Scott breaks down the door and rescues her from her folly and won't let her out of his sight. He's in a total rage about her behavior and accuses her of trying to ruin Marc's life and marriage. He also insists that he's taking her to Brazil with him because he can't trust her not to do something else to ruin poor Marc's life. by the way he also insists that they marry as His job won't allow irregular relationships and marriage is the only way he can take her with him.Alexa actually is not as bad as she sounds. She is actually immature and a little egocentric but this is probably due to the fact that her parents died three years previously when she was 19. After that she kind of drifted until she met Marc and then he became her anchor and she was very dependent on him. She has all kinds of grief issues and after Marc dumped her she's in a state of shock and mental breakdown. Also anyone who feels Alexa is tstl should really question the parents of her ex-fiancee and of her bff. They all claim to care a great deal for her but no one seemed to see that she was totally without a support system and was truly too fragile to deal with the final blow of being dumped by the love her life.This oldie is a classic, full of angst, misunderstandings and obstacles to a HEA and it's truly a miracle that the author manages to pull off a believable HEA.
