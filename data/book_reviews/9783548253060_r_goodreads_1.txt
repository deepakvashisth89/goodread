Disappointing. I’ve read the previous books in this series years ago and was really looking forward to returning to it. I finished it but more out of loyalty to the series and the fact that there are a few more and didn’t want to miss a storyline trend. It’s too long, too narrative and boring. Can I say that about a book that takes place in the beautiful city of Venice? Well sadly I did and it’s true. By the end I just didn’t care anymore and the mysteries here were not that clever or interesting to resolve. Here’s hoping the rest in the series are able to redeem themselves.
