Ben Carlton is a reclusive artist. His aunt Destiny is determined to find him a wife, like she did his 2 brothers (which she did perfectly!). So she invites Kathleen, who owns a successful art gallery, to his house for Thanksgiving dinner. They soon realize she has set them up, and due to past relationships, they vow to ignore the sparks and and keep things friendly. She wants to show his pictures in Her gallery but he is adamant that he’ll never show them. Destiny is not deterred, gloating any time they spend time together. And of course, she’s right about them. But it takes a while for them to see it. Loved all the Destiny stories, in fact all her books.
