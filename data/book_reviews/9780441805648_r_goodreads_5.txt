The ultimate coming-of-age story combined with the strangest school trip, and it all started with an extra charge on a Springer when theStarschooltried to land at a specific spaceport.Carl Bok might be a poor country boybut he has his pride, and he thinks he can use his unusual size to earn back the money fighting professionally (albeit illegally). Size isn't everything, however, and he quickly learns that he must rely on his wits and friends if he wants to be successful.But this isn't about weird cagematches, or even about space travel. It's an exploration of several planets, with different customs, different aliens, and different dangers, and the similiarities that are discovered.
