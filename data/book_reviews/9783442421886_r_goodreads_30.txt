So action packed. Very exciting. Fascinating tech. Dirk and Al a hoot as usual, though honestly, I still didn't find him as entertaining as later books.I think the main thing that bothered me about this book was a major part of the plot.Japanese nationalists ready to wrack havoc with nuclear bombs.Okay okay, fanatical nationalists are obviously completely cuckoo, so I get that. But I just don't buy Japanese people at all being okay with nukes like this.I'm not Japanese and I'm no expert.....but I have been consuming Japanese media in one form or another for more than half my life.....and the amount of terror, regret and the sheer amount of devastation that they had in 1945.....like, it just doesn't make sense. anyone, even a fanatic, deeming it a good idea to process nukes on their soil and unleash them in the world....? no, just don't buy it, even in fiction. I cannot suspend my disbelief that much.
