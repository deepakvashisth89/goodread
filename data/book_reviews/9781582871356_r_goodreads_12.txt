Thanks to Hawthorne, Transcendentalism officially gives me the creeps. This along with Ethan Frome is making me feel really uneasy about Massachusetts....

Casting off the shackles of society, the members of the Blithesdale commune seek to embody the incorruptible cosmic harmony they imagine to be Nature. Their acting out of an "Arcadian affectation" brings them face-to-face with Nature after all, but in her bleak and tragic aspect. The convoluted love triangle (square?) that unfolds allows for a pitiless exploration of the ancient curse placed on womankind: "thy desire shall be to thy husband, and he shall rule over thee."
