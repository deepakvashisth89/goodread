One of the most trivial and useless book ever. The plot is both far-fetched and paper-thin. Rafael Molinet is an aging gay guy who has squandered all his money. To end his life with panache, he decides to check into aa remote and exclusive Morrocan resort, where he'll eventually commit suicide. By "coincidence", one of the other guests turns out to be a recently widowed Madrid socialite whose husband died in mysterious circumstances. Was he having sex with his new mistress at the time, or did Mercedes choose to let him choke to death on a almond? As a narrator Molinet is neither engaging nor fun, and there's so much padding in this book you could use it to insulate your roof anew.
