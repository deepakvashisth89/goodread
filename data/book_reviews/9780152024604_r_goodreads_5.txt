I will forever be glad I was warned of the subject matter of this particular book in the series in advance, in as spoiler-less a way as possible. It didn't ruin the book for me, and I don't think it made it hit less hard either.The title of this book is truly fitting. When you struggle to find any way but destroying life to save other life, there's not always a clean and happy way out. It may take you places you'd never imagined going.I went through a very similar situation - minus the wizardry, and I honestly don't know what my choice would have been either - when I was just two years older than Nita is here, and reading this, I cried buckets. Perhaps that was a given. But have tissues ready, just in case...
