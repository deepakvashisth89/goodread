I am primarily a reader of genre fiction (mostly science fiction and horror), but picked up Empire Falls out of curiosity and a desire for a change of pace about two years ago and absolutely loved it. I ran across a copy of Mohawk and decided to give that a whirl, since I really liked Empire Falls. Going back to an author's first work after reading something later, it's always interesting to find instances of them finding their own voice. I found the first 100 or so pages of Mohawk to be a bit difficult to get into, as it was largely Russo spinning stories about the characters past to establish them, rather than letting details unfold through the characters' actions. I was still entirely engrossed, though.
Part Two of the novel was gripping and very well written. I'm sort of a sucker for a small town yarn, and Russo seems to be one of the best in the game. He ably handles a large cast of characters and myriad story threads that weave in and out with each other.
I very much enjoyed this book and will probably be picking up other Russo books when I need a reprieve from genre fiction.
