Seriously, this book made me cry my eyes out inside by the end of it 😭 This is such a beautifully written, heartbreaking story... It begins as a love story and family drama, and ends on such a bittersweet note I ache inside for these characters and the injustice they were dealt ;;;;-;;;;!! I am so glad I happened upon it secondhand 😭!!I despise her father Hayato, he was a stubborn old packmule of a man who forced his own ideals upon his daughter and feigned his stupid actions with a thin veil of righteousness. Completely despicable. Sending Nagayuki to America made me weep for his character because he just wanted to do his 'father' proud 🥲 the ending was awful, the things these evil people did to Tomiko UGHI REALLY recommend reading it, it is so beautifully written and is a classic Historical Fiction, but be ready with tissues SOBS
