I really liked this book. It was a fun quick read that held my attention from page 1. I liked both the main characters River James (Joe’s Foster son) and Sophie (Joe’s daughter). Sophie always followed River when she was younger and always loved him. River always loved Sophie too, but he sent her away. After Sophie is mugged and moves back to the ranch, their feelings take over. You have the continued story about Meredith(Joe’s wife that has amnesia).
