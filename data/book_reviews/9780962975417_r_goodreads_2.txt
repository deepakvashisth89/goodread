Yet another "lying around the house" book. I remember this one vividly. A true story of a man who ends up answering unanswered letters to Santa written by children in need. My child self loved the sense of love and inclusion, and the ending-- when he confides in a girl, who is unsure if she is allowed to receive a present because she is Jewish, that he is too. It's a real "Santa is the spirit of giving in all of us" type of read.
