This is a good mystery novel, set near Wellfleet, MA on Cape Cod. It moves along quickly, although as with the other mysteries by this author, there tends to be a lot of characters that blend together. In addition, these books have NOT been "updated for the modern reader," so certain phrases and sayings come across as extremely crude to our present-day ears and eyes.With all of these disclaimers noted, I highly encourage other mystery-lovers and Cape Cod lovers to try this series out!
