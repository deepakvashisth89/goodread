This title was free on audible for a while. The movie with Jack Black was on Netflix. My idea was to listen to the free book off audible then watch the movie, but like always my plans failed. audible took the book off audible plus. so, i watched the movie, that was not bad. then bought the audible on a discount audible sale for under 3 dollars!

This novel was written in 1973. The author John Bellairs wrote the story about a house (the Cronin House) in Marshall Michigan. All that being said i did not know any of this. i was attracted by the 2018 move with jack black directed by Eli Roth. The book is actually worth the read in my opinion fast paced you learn alot in about 4 hours. ancient magic breathes through the house. Jonathan Barnavelt is a average magician who has been trying to figure the main clock and the curse laid on it by former owner Isaac Izard. Izard is a heavy-duty evil wizard who passed away in the house. Lewis is thrown into this mess after his parents die. Lewis learns the craft from his odd uncle and his neighbor Florence Zimmermann. of course, something goes wrong and the clock in the walls reaches Zero!

this seems like a good spooky series my only problem is it has 14 books and not all are on audible!
