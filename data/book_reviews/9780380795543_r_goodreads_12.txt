I liked the premise of the book - a young woman has a crush on a glamorous, handsome star from the 1930's and somehow he travels 60 years through time to her. The execution of the premise didn't quite make it for me. Instalove or at last instalust takes hold amidst a murder mystery, two protagonists who each have major psychological issues, a side plot of a pair of blackmailers that seems unnecessary, and other secondary characters that don't seem to know what role they're supposed to play.It took me days to read this when it should have only taken 2-3 hours.
