I read Iris Johansen's first book in the Eve Duncan Series, "The Face of Deception" and I was immediately hooked! I loved this book, so of course I had to read #2 in the series, "The Killing Game" which also was a big hit with me! Eve Duncan is a forensic sculptor who helps to identify the dead from their skulls. Eve's own daughter, Bonnie was murdered, but the body was never found. A merciless killer is hunting for an innocent child. The killer knows Eve's pain for her murdered daughter and she can't resist believing that one of the skeletons found on a bluff in Georgia could be Bonnie. The killer won't stop playing with Eve until he gets what he want's: her life.These are incredible suspense filled books that I highly recommend!
