Showing it's ageThis Heinlein's first "Juvenile" science fiction book, written in 1947. It appears to be set in the mid-to-late 1950s, which is about the same era I read it for the first time.Three boys just out of high school team up with an eccentric atomic scientist to build an atomic-powered moon rocket on the cheap.It's a rip-roarin' adventure with an interesting plot complication near the end, but the science and engineering haven't aged well.Not one of Heinlein's best.
