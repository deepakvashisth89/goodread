A Satisfying Conclusion to Jack's JourneyIn the final book of the trilogy, Jack faces significant challenges—financial struggles, eviction, and the loss of his mother. True to his character, he rises to the occasion, dedicating himself to solving the case while finding new strength in his life and relationships. The story wraps up nicely, leaving no lingering cliffhangers, yet I find myself eager for more of Jack's adventures. A satisfying conclusion to a fantastic trilogy!
