This is not an easy book to find, and the movie version is difficult to find as well! I finally got the book from a great site that I would recommend for any items that are hard to find, out of print or rare called AbeBooks.
The reason for my wanting to read the book stems from my recollection of a scene in the movie, which I saw as a child. The scene involved Sooner and her pet "Bird". Reflecting upon that scene as an adult, it still brings back that sick in the gut feeling you get when you know you have made a mistake, a very big mistake. I only had a visual in my mind, which was still vivid after so many years, and was curious to see how it had been captured in print form.
The scene in print form was not as elaborated upon but combined with my visual recollection, it is still powerful none the less.
An additional joy from reading the book that I did not pick up on as a child was the parallel, or simultaneous, personality development of Sooner and Elizabeth. Pertinent to us all is the self inflicted journey that Elizabeth undertakes, prompted by Sooner's arrival, which is necessary to reach self-actualization. Elizabeth undertakes it in much the same way all of us do, kicking and screaming because it is not something one wants to do, until finally the light bulb goes on, and you are left wondering why you fought it at all in the first place.
