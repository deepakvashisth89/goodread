I read this book as part of a novel study for a new curriculum. At first, I totally judged the book by the cover.. I thought, well this doesn’t look interesting to me… I can’t imagine being a kid reading this book.Fast forward 10 weeks of really diving deep into this book and my kids LOVED it. Even the most reluctant readers couldn’t stop themselves from reading ahead.This book tells the story of how the Nez Perce were forced off their homeland and their journey to find freedom. To my class it was so much more than that. We studied the characters words and actions to determine what each character values. Then we were able to conclude that differing values, specifically cultural values are the reason for conflicts and that we have the choice to control our own actions.My kids became so attached to the characters in this story and their journey that they are genuinely upset to move on to our next novel.
