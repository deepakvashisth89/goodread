A very weak "loved you forever theme". Both heroine and hero left a lot to be desired, particularly the heroine who not only lacked common sense, moral integrity, but recklessly damaged her father's business and livelihood. A disaster all the way around.The hero was somewhat okay - except I could't forgive him for the friends he kept, or for allowing his friends to malign her and for believing them.This is my very first and last HP by Lillian Peake. With so many great HP authors out there, I don't want to waste my time reading romance like this.
