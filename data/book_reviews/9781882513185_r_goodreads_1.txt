What a delightful find! I was looking for audio presentations of classics and stumbled into this selection of 3 stories that are an amazing presentation. I believe the stories are told by Jim Weiss.

The first is: The Canterville Ghost written by Oscar Wilde.
This is hilarious and so well done. The story takes place Britain where castles are often haunted. An American family takes up residence in the castle house and notices the blood stain on the floor. The older son launches into an advertisement for stain remover that will remove any stain which it promptly does. Only the next morning the stain is back. Remove, reappear, again and again. Along the way we meet the Ghost of Canterville, who does not understand these Americans who seem unafraid of him. He tries the moaning and chain rattling to which the father tells him he needs to oil his chains as his family needs a good night's sleep. The ghost feels he is losing his touch and tries even harder with more elaborate schemes and various moaning and chain dragging. All to no avail. I will not give you the twist as that would ruin the fun.

The Second is: Dr. Heidegger's Experiment written by Nathaniel Hawthorne
Several old acquaintances of Dr. Heidegger are invited to his house for an evening. This collection of old friends are all very aged. Surprised by the tale of the doctor tells, he offers them a carafe of liquid that he claims is from the Fountain of Youth. They are welcome to drink as much as they want. The results are true to his word, but not at all what they expected.

The Third is: The Sending of Dana Da written by Rudyard Kipling. This is one story that I had never read. A gentleman has been badly treated by another man who he wishes on him the worst things, but short of dying. The events play out in a very interesting way. The India Magician puts a "sending" on the targeted man, but it is not at all what you expect. Very Kipling and with kittens. If you are sharing this with children please be aware it is "magic to harm".
