THE classic Western, arguably Ohio-born Zane Grey's best work and certainly his best- known Western. No one could bring the American Southwest to life as he did at his best--with some memorable characters and his depictions of the beauty and ruggedness of the landscape.This one was published in 1912--and, at first, was rejected as being too anti-Mormon! Grey presents us with the quintessential laconic and lethal gunman, Lassiter. At the heart of the story is the growing romance between him and the Mormon woman Jane Withersteen, who inherited a great ranch in southern Utah from her father. The Mormon elders put pressure on her to marry one of their number--and to have nothing to do with a Gentile like Lassiter. There's also another romance and that is between a young cowboy named Venters and a girl named Bess. With all the romancing going on, there is still plenty of action and it all ends with one of the most satisfying conclusions ever!
