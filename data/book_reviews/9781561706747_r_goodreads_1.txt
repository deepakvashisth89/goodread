I have not yet read this book, however, I did meet Bob Du Pre, the father who inspired it, in Tucson, on a street corner, one beautiful November morning in 2000. He explained to me that he'd been a lawyer, married several times, been divorced and disbarred, was homeless and seemingly happy about it. He invited me out for coffee to discuss the possibility of me writing his rebuttal to his son's book. He was gracious, revealed to me that he had just smoked a joint, and sang me a medley of big band hits, to entice me to come dancing with him once he received his pension check. "Are you embarrassed?" he asked me, between phrases, and answered his own question: "I'm not!"I did not collaborate with him, but I very much enjoyed the encounter.
