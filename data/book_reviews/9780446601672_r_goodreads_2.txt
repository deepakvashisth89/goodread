I read the setup for this story and I was sold: The Americans discover some kind of stargate on the moon, but after a while they flee back to Earth and use new technology to lock themselves in their own country. How cool is that? There's mystery, science and the rest of the world struggling to repeat what the USA did a century before. But that's as far as the cool goes. When the stargate is rediscovered, the author sadly loses his sanity and starts to write gibberish.In short, the stargate is really a doorway to a lot of fictional universes, because everything, including the real world, is fiction. A weird group of people, constantly changing because new fictional characters are added or old ones are killed, jump from one book to another. Maybe it would have made more sense if I had read the books in question. Riverworld by P.J. Farmer is the only one I recognized and I haven't even read that one yet.Anyway, if you do plan on reading the book in spite of my warning, be prepared for an incoherent story and sex. Lots of sex. And not the fun kind. Green ants raping red ants. A human married to an ant. They have sex of course, but no details of how that would actually work. Thank God. And then there's the moving story of a young woman who changed her body through surgery so she looks like a spider and a man with the cute nickname of Brucie Big Dick who fall in love and get it on.In short: yuck!
