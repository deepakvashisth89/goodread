I miss this brand of Sci-Fi. It hits the sweet spot in-between the old school stuff, much of which hasn't aged super well and the new school of Sci-Fi that is just so stupid 3/4 of the time.

A group of Christians (sort of) travel around evangelizing and find a planet with an eternal secret. They attempt to spread the Gospel (again, sort of) to the planet but things are not what they seem.

Balshazzar's Serpent was a very easy, interesting read that didn't wear out its welcome. It's the first in a trilogy, I believe, and if I'm not mistaken, it was the last series Chalker wrote before his death.

I recommend it if you like the Baen style from the late 90's and early 2000's.
