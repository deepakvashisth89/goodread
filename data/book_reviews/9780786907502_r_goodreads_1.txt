⭐ My Review of Passage to DawnI liked this book a lot, in fact, like every Drizzt novel, it feels like soulfood for my brain. There’s something comforting about returning to this world: the cold winds of Icewind Dale, the clash of blades, the moral clarity of Drizzt’s heart.Those who know me will understand why these stories speak to me deeply.Drizzt was born into an evil family, lost everything, and still built a new life based on who he chose to be, not who the world told him he was. He found friends, love, purpose, and courage far away from the shadows of his past.Without sounding too dramatic… yes, this is soulfood for me.The book balances heavy themes with humor, and Salvatore’s timing is perfect as always. The scene with the two mages battling enemies on the beach,while simultaneously trying to outdo each other , had me laughing out loud. Harpell-chaos at its finest.And then there’s the return of a certain old favorite of mine.A character who was… well, dead.Apparently not anymore.Good for him, I guess. But I’m still deciding how I feel about that plot twist. Let’s say I’m cautiously optimistic and mildly confused.In the end, Passage to Dawn gave me exactly what I needed: adventure, reflection, warmth, and a world that feels like home.Onward to the next adventure.
