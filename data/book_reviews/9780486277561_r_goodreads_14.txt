It is really not something you'll want your child to read/hear/see. I am reading my daughter the Internet Archive's gorgeous scanned edition in PDF format, and I actually edited the two offending pages, by replacing the Tottenhot with a Hopper, and changing the line about "a lower form of man." I also removed an extraneous and racist reference to Romani people. If you want a copy of it, you candownload this copy from my Google Drive. It does not change anything substantive. The racist bit was egregious and in no way important to the story.Beware, though! Once Ruth Plumly Thompson takes over the series, the racism goes into overdrive.
