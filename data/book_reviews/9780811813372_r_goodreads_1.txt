While there is quite a variety of flavors of herbal teas which are currently available in supermarkets, health food stores, tea shops as well as from various websites, some of us are curious to learn how to create our own homemade blends.In "The Book of Herbal Teas: A Guide to Gathering, Brewing and Drinking," Sara Perry and provides step by step instructions for creating our own unique homemade blends of herbal teas from various leaves, flowers, and herbs which you can purchase in supermarkets and health food stores, and some leaves and petals which you can cultivate in your home garden or find growing wild.You don't need to have any previous culinary experience to enjoy this book, Ms. Perry describes the processes which are involved in blending herbal teas so that people who are complete beginners will easily be able to fully comprehend and follow the instructions which Ms. Perry presents in this book.If you're curious to experiment with crafting your own unique flavors of herbal teas, you will enjoy this book.
