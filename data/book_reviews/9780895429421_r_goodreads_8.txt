4 stars mostly for nostalgia purposes. The family had this in our box of Christmas books (if you didn't grow up with a cardboard box wrapped in Christmas wrapping paper that held only Christmas-themed books and was only brought out between Thanksgiving and New Year's, I am sorry for you) starting in 1980, and in reducing the amount of "stuff" she had in storage my mom gave it to me, along with a couple other books from that box.I've not read the book as an adult, but it's a sweet story and has a nice ending, as one would expect from a book about a bear who goes looking for Christmas because he thinks it's a destination. In scrolling Amazon streaming shortly after Thanksgiving this year I discovered that there is a tv-movie version of the book, but I've not seen it to know how closely it adheres to the story here.
