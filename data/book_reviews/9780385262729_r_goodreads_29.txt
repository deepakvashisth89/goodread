Scandalously good read!I don’t normally write reviews but this book is a Mr. Toad’s wild ride.I was a freshman in high school on a too early lunch hour the day Ray Ryan’s car exploded 4 blocks from school. Four of us were walking to McDonald’s. We witnessed the obliterated remains and massive police & fire department response. It was incomprehensible all the same. Word from adults & local news was about the millions of dollars Ryan had embezzled from members of the “Evansville 100.” Their money? 💸 💨“Lots of people probably wanted Ray Ryan dead.” FBI instigative records traced all clues to the Lexington Police Department. What?!I went to grad school at UofL. We often ate at John Y’s restaurant, once sitting in a booth next to Phyllis George & John Y Brown during their political heyday. Great fried chicken, btw.Loved this book. Great investigative reporting from Sally Denton. 🍿 There are a lot of players , so my Kindle X-Ray feature got some good use.
