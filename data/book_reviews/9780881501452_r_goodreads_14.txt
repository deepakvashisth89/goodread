I'm a big fan of the Asey Mayo series. Actually I think of them more as Prue Whitsby books. Maybe she isn't in all of them. But she's the one I like. Obviously, takes place on Cape Cod. A Golden Age mystery from 1932. A follow-up fromThe Cape Cod Mysteryin 1931. Taylor keeps the locale to rural and gives Asey a dialect. Per the back cover, Margaret Mitchell was a big fan.Bert Stires is holding a house party (aren't they all?) but he is late to his own party. One of Prue's neighbors, Rowena, at the shore invites both herself and Prue to the party. It is March and weather is changeable. There are about 5 men already at the party. All anxiously awaiting Bert in his "'lectric" automobile. They get snowed in. Sometime the next day Bert arrives. A girl has also arrived saying that she is his niece, who was expected.Apparently the men all went to Harvard together and the ladies knew them in those days. So these people have a long history together.Bert's house is new and he wants to show it off which is the purpose of the party. There are a lot of candles in the house. And there is a fair amount of arsenic in the house, in various forms. When Bert arrives he is exhausted and a couple of the men need to talk to him. Come morning, Bert is dead. They put in an emergency call but the phone isn't working. Somehow, Asey and, eventually, the doctor come to call. Arsenic poisoning is the verdict. But how, and by who?It is dated but interesting.
