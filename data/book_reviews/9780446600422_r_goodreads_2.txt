The book, completed in 1994, is the typical Big Strong Man sort of mystery where the man has two very different sides to his character. Is he good? Is he bad?Pretty run of the mill, although several characters do make a strong case for how Domination and Submission sexual games actually free a woman to be more of who she is. Actually, that's save for the submissives who find freedom in surrender. And dog collars. And whips.The little girl isn't very believable. And there were a few things about Morrow....Still, an OK summer read.
