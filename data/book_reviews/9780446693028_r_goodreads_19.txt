Every so often I have to dip back into the comic crime capers of Donald Westlake's character Dortmunder and his gang. In an introduction to this collection, Westlake tells how he usually writes novels, but occasionally he'd get an idea that really only worked as a short story. So he'd write it. And Alice Turner at Playboy Magazine usually bought it.

As the title implies, there are twelve very funny short stories here. None of them include the entire gang, usually just Dortmunder's pal Andy Kelp. Doesn't matter. These capers are hilarious. I was giggling so hard yesterday that my wife demanded I tell her what was so funny. So I read her the story 'Too Many Crooks' and we both chuckled our way through it.

Need a little pick-me-up at shorter lengths? Try this one.
