It is the height of abhorrence that we treat our dying pets with more dignity and compassion than our dying loved ones!Final Exit, published in 1991 is a dated, yet an historic expose of euthanasia alternatives at that time. Alternatives often violent, gruesome, and fraught with error, all due to spineless lawmakers and medical providers unwilling to respond with compassion.Now, twenty-five years later, we have advanced little. An entire industry of prolonging death continues to evolve, with society complacently standing by.It is shameful that the dying should be treated so inhumanly.
