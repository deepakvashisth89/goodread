This didn't do much for me. It reminded me ofThe Girl's Guide to Hunting and Fishing, which I tried at lest twice to like and just ended up hating it more. I think it's the format--a book composed of short stories that intertwine. Most of the sections focused on Rachel, a college professor in her thirties that I pretty much hated. I did enjoy the parts about Emma and Georgia, but not enough to make me really like this book. Not for me.
