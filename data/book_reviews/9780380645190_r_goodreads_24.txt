God breaks his promise to the human race and decides to flood the world and destroy everyone again due to us engaging in a nuclear war, but forget about Calvin Cohn, who is diving off a boat when it happens. Consequently, Cohn then has to deal with God, who makes further promises to him which turn out to be completely empty. There is a chimp called Buz on the boat who becomes capable of speech but turns out to be Christian, unlike Cohn who is Jewish. They later find an island with other chimps. In the end, Cohn destroys the voice box which gave Buz the power of speech and Buz kills him by cutting his throat. As he dies, Cohn becomes an old man in order that God's promise that he would die an old man be fulfilled.God is not shown in a good light by this novel.
