2.5 maybe. Nothing too special. I like senseless revenge plots, but nothing about this one to stand out from the pack. It's the old 'your male relative harmed my female relative, and you're dude's female relative I'd most like to get in bed, so obviously you must be the one to pay by tolerating my dickish behavior and multiple orgasms.'

Why me? The heroine may ask. Well, because this is an HP and the straight tycoon hero has no desire to literally screw your brother, so buck up sister and don't waste time on logic. There lies the path of madness and all that.

Don't get me wrong. This can be a fun plot (sometimes wish my bro would tee off a hot oversexed billionaire who deep down just needs love and wants to buy me a mansion.... for revenge). I love Penny Jordan's classic of this genre - Response. This one just missed the mark with me. Perhaps it was all too convoluted for HP length.
