I was given an advanced copy of this book for an honest review. This is the Book 2 with the characters of Roman Cantrell and Nikki Holden. If you enjoy a really puzzling mystery with some out of this world elements, this will not disappoint you. It has a little of everything, UFOs, robberies, strange, strange, strange characters and a little bit of romance. There are some very weird turns and a rather surprising and satisfying ending.

While this is a stand alone book, you might want to read Book 1, Dangerous Places, before this one. While the stories are different, you would get an understanding of the relationship between Roman and Nikki that will make book two even more enjoyable. I do not usually read mysteries, but have been totally engrossed in both of these books and truly enjoyed them.
