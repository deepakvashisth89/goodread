Dove into the deeper end of my tbr to find this oldie but goodie. This book very sneakily went from "Hmm, I don't know", to "Wait, this is actually pretty good", to "Oh my god, this shit is hilarious".So the story is about Sophie and her sister coming to a small town to make a short movie about a local star returning home from Hollywood and facing her past. Sophie just wants to get in and get out as fast as possible and keep her sister away from trouble that their family is prone to get into. And Phin is the golden boy of Temptation, a mayor from the family of mayors, with expectations and small-town politics that he's getting tired of. He and Sophie couldn't be more different and pretty much everyone is against them coming together but they can't seem to stay away from each other. The banter between the main couple is top notch. It's basically Nobody Wants This with a lot more sex.Honestly, two main characters were not an instant yes for me, they worked for it. Sophie was a bit rigid and paranoid at first, only when she let go a little did her real personality come through and I came to like her a lot. And from the first time we meet Phin I was already rolling my eyes and thinking he's a pig. Turns out he's a bear, lol. IYKYK...It's a fun, a little out there story that doesn't take itself all that seriously. I mean they're filming porn in a small town for fun's sake. Yes, porn. Imagine all the pearl clutching. Also it's somewhat of it's time, it came out in 2000, I think, so I gave it a break for some things but, honestly, it didn't bother me much. I had a great time and smiled a lot and giggled some and that's all I wanted from this. So mission accomplished.Over and out
