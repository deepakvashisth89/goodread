I started this last night, and was pleasantly surprised at how easy it was to read. I ended up reading more than half in one go, and finished the rest today.

I've actually been eyeing the TV show for years, so with season 13 currently airing, I figured maybe I should finally take the plunge and get through the books, so I could give it a watch. I know it's going to be different, but I have a feeling I'm going to love it, just like I loved this.

The story is so sweet, and the romance adorable, I just wish it wasn't so rushed at the end.

I spoiled myself a little by reading the blurb of book two before I was done, and I was like there's absolutely no way these two are getting together in this book, no way...it was so slow, until it wasn't.

They needed a few more scenes together so that love confession wouldn't have seemed so out of the blue.

Also, Wynn could've used a bit more development. I got to know Elizabeth, and all her neighbours, so much better than I got to know him.

Now, something that bugged me was the way Elizabeth originally acted when she found out her mother wanted to send her westward to join her brother. I know the fear she felt would've been accurate to the time, but her being all like "oh I'm going to get scalped" rubbed me the wrong way.

This wasn't present later on in the book, and hopefully nothing like it will appear in the rest of the series, but that's the one and only reason why I'd be hesitant to recommend this, despite how much I enjoyed the rest of the story.

P. S. Justice for Napoleon the mouse, he did nothing wrong!!!
