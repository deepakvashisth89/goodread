Here's a peek at France from an American family living there as WW II erupts and sends them back to the States. Their French cook elects to come with them. You see life before the family leaves with them enjoying food, wine and daily village life and then acclimating back to a small New England town and introducing their French cook, Clementine to American food and ways. The drawings of buildings mentioned throughout are charming.Nostalgia is high in this, and it comes with recipes.
