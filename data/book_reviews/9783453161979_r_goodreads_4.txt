The blurb on the back sounds promising, but the story fails to deliver.The Eridani Light Horse regiment are sent to Milos, which has been taken over by Sun Tzu Liao as part of his Xin Sheng movement.The populace seem quite happy with that state of affairs, ensure the ELH know about it...and that's about it.There's a few mech battles, some fairly tense moments with a sniper on the loose, but as a novel this doesn't really go anywhere or achieve anything.
