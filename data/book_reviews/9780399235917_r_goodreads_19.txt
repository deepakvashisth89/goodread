I’m inclined to pick up any book with a dog on the cover or in the title so this book was a no brainer. Ant feels all alone, as she is a part of a family she doesn’t think cares for her. She also has few friends, and most have more than 2 legs. Ant believes that her real parents will come and save her from this miserable life of hers if she continues volunteering at her local animal shelter, and maaaaybe starts to bend the truth. I remember in a point in time where I felt like my only companion is my dog, which is not uncommon for people of all ages. This book was easy to read because the diction used was not that hard. The book also tackles topics older readers might be struggling with in real life like the feelings of abandonment and the battle between telling lies vs the truth. This book is a wonderful transitional book for readers interested in a fun story about a girl who just wants to be accepted, which is a feeling we all encounter at some point in our lives.
