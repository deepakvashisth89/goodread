Let us suppose, for a minute, that the author was successful with the series thus far. He has accomplished everything he set out to achieve. We found the plot engrossing. The new twists and dead-ends kept us in suspense. The development of the setting fascinated us, and like the worst of the Trekkies we jotted down whatever bits of trivia he let slip about the world of Amber and desperately tried to reconcile it with the Amber we knew. And, above all, we loved the characters. Luke was cool and hilarious. Mandor was suave and seductive. And Merlin was the hero we dreamed of, rather than a whiny brat whom we've been visualising in the same airlock as Weseley for four books now.What better way to start the finale, then, than have Merlin have a whine at his mother."Moooom. Did you send that demon to take care of me?""Yes dear.""But moooom. I can take care of myself.""The demon saved your life, didn't it?""Yes, but...""So obviously you needed mother's help after all.""But mooooom!"Great. So even if the first four books had us fooled, heading into the fifth we know exactly where we are. We're stuck with the (second) most obnoxious character in the Amber universe, and we have to watch him win. Somehow, at something. Neither the means nor the stakes are clear yet, presumably to the author as well as the reader.The means Zelazny solves quickly enough. In the last book, Merlin found a magic ring which boosted his already incredibly vague powers into the ability to do whatever the plot required of him, but at the price of a devious curse. In this book, we even forget about the curse. The ring Merlin wears, which is never explained, gives him the power to defeat two chaos lords and the Logrus itself, simultaneously, in the last few pages of the book, despite admitting that he went into the battle without preparing anything. He just wanted the book to end so bad that, mercifully, it did.And the stakes? Well, fuck me if I have any idea what this series was about. Merlin set out to stop a rogue AI that was threatening the world, until it got bored of being threatening and stopped. Then he set out to stop a killer after the blood of Amber, until the killer got bored of killing Amberites and stopped. Then he set out to stop Dasra who was out to kill Merlin, and Merlin in particular, until Dasra got bored of that quest and stopped. Then he set out to stop Mask-and-or-Julia, who wanted to prove herself worthy of Merlin, until she got bored of that and stopped. Then his half-brother Jurt, who wanted to kill Merlin to take his place in the succession, until Jurt got bored of that and stopped. Finally it is revealed that the Pattern and the Logrus are battling over control over the universe and they need Merlin for unspecified reasons. Merlin refuses to be a pawn in their game... until he gets bored of that and takes his place as the King of Chaos, as the Logrus had wanted him to do all along. Great. I'm sure we achieved something, somewhere, I just have no idea what it is.I can only presume the author was as bored of the series as his characters, and couldn't figure out for the life of him why people kept reading. So to punish them for wasting his time, he decided to waste theirs. Well done, Zelazny. You showed us good.
