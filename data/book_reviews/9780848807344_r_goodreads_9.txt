An interesting story by the author of the Oz books. Similar to the Oz books, this story features a child/youth protagonist, and much of the plot centers around him having various disconnected adventures while traveling. There's also a strong moral message at the end.The most fascinating part of the story are the fantastical electrical devices which the "demon of electricity" gives to the young protagonist. Many of these inventions are commonplace in our world today, 100+ years after this book was written. It's classic science fiction in that regard, as the story predicts the future and suggests that even in an "advanced" civilization, humanity and human nature remain the same.Many aspects of the story are quite dated by today's standards and might be considered culturally insensitive. However, Baum was a skilled and popular writer in his time, and this book weaves a thought-provoking yarn that can still be enjoyed today.
