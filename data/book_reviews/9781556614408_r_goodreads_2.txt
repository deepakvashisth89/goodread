As much as I enjoyed all the other seven books, I have to say I was greatly disappointed in this book, especially it being the last book of the series. I am a Christian who loves learning about God in whatever I read, make no mistake. However, I chose to read this book because it was supposed to be a NOVEL. I felt 90% of this book was one theological discussion after another, with only 10% of a story line! I feel there should have been a greater balance as there were in the other books.
