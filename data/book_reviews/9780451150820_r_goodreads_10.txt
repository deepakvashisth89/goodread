★★★I enjoyed Lady Killer. The race-against-the-clock plot was a fun change of pace, keeping me hooked as the detectives scrambled to prevent a murder. McBain’s knack for quick dialogue and snappy procedural detail shines through, but the time limit meant the characters didn’t feel as fleshed out as usual. The ending worked, but after all the suspense, it felt a bit too tidy.Not my favourite in the series, but a solid, entertaining read.
