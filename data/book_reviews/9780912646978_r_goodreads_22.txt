Good Old Boys is on par with what I've already read by Elmer Kelton and it's good news 'cause he's an awesome author!Kelton had the talent to tell a western story without being a classic western story as you could expect when reading the genre. No robbery, no outlaw, no unfriendly indians. Kelton renders a novel that matches all the markers of realism. Hewey's life at the turn of the century and the renewal of his world, restraining his liberty with fences and his job with automobiles could have been a testimony of a real Good Old Boy. It almost seems like it could be a biography of sort.Kelton had all my attention when reading this, even though the genre isn't at all what I usually like to read and keep me interested till the end.You clearly should read Kelton once, just to get a sense of what makes him the most rewarded western author.⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
