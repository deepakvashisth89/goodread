When Dixie Parish delivers a horse to Rhett Brown for his daughter and has instant rapport with his 2 children, he offers her a temporary job as their nanny, while he tries to get his life organized. Rhett, a lawyer is investigating an option to run for State Attorney General, when his wife runs off with another man. Dixie has a secret in her past that is not good for a politician, so as they are drawn together conflict is inevitable. Then his soon to be ex starts fighting for custody and playing dirty. An average series romance.
