Enjoyed this one

This was a quick read, simple but entertaining. Elias Donovan was an attorney who was up for partnership at the law firm where he worked. He'd poured his heart into his job at the expense of everything else. It was part of his plan. Once he'd achieved his goal then he'd focus on finding a wife and starting a family. Unfortunately, his boss, Leland Wagner, doesn't see things that way. He knows how much time Donovan puts in and he's concerned. He learned a long time ago that work wasn't everything. He had a wonderful wife and three beautiful daughters to prove it. He suggests to Donovan that it might be better to put the partnership on hold until he gets married. This is when Donovan panics and tells the man he's engaged.

Sarah Madison owns the shop next door to Donovan's law firm. She pays him a visit and asks him to help her with a client who refuses to pay. She's an interior decorator, working to build her business up while living out of her shop. She's stunned when Donovan asks her to pose as his fiancee for one night. His firm is hosting an event. All he has to do is show up with Sarah, prove he's engaged, and the partnership would be his. Sarah has her misgivings but Donovan manages to convince her to help him. Then Leland's family corners Sarah at the party and before Sarah knows it, she's knee deep in wedding plans. Things escalate to the point Donovan finally suggests they get married for real. It would be a marriage of convenience just until his partnership goes through. In return, he'll give her a place to live and pay the bills as well as pride her with some valuable contacts for her business.

While the marriage of convenience is nothing new, these characters were and I enjoyed reading their story. Sarah had a sweet nature and I liked the way she got Donovan to loosen up around her. They started spending time together and the man known as the Iceman began to melt. What he doesn't count on is falling in love.
