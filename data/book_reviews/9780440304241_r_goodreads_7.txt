This book was long and drawn out and at times tedious. Chapters were over 100 pages long. Parts of this book were riveting. I enjoyed the escape from Corregidor and the recapture of the Philippines. I also enjoyed reading about MacArthur’s brilliant Governorship of the Japan.

I’m a fan of Manchester. The Last Lion – Manchester’s trilogy on Churchill - was the best biography I’ve ever read. This one I didn’t enjoy as much. However, if you want to learn more about MacArthur, this is very comprehensive, even handed book on the General.

I must admit, I still get chills up and down my spine every time I read it.
