3 1/2 stars. The author writes about places in San Antonio and the surrounding areas that are allegedly haunted. I found it interesting although I take everything, of course, with a grain of salt. I like the fact that she included photos of the places and information about them that is historical. Part of what interests me in this book is just reading about the folklore and stories, whether or not there's any truth to them. Overall, an enjoyable book, with interesting stories about the possibility of the supernatural.
