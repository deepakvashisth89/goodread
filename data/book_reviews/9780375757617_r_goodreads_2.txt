A sweet quick read. I mainly read this because of my daughter and I will draw a name of a country or continent out of a bowl and then cook for an entire month recipes from that country. So happens that Russia is our month for December. So, Katish: Our Russian Cook was a good read to compliment our Russian cooking month. In this little book were many recipes and the family stories based around their experiences with their Russian cook. It wasn't exactly a great piece of writing but special in it's own right.
