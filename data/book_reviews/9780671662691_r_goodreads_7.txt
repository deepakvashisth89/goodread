A lovely story about a pebble-collector who finds a beautiful magic red pebble but also discovers that some things you already have are better than what you wish for.I like pebble-collectors. My mother used to collect them from her woods. She'd take a walk and she'd pick up a pretty pebble or unusual small rock and hold it in her hand while she'd think of something she was grateful for. She'd then bring it home and put her "gratitude rock" in a homemade yucca basket I made for her.When she died, the basket was full.
