Looking back to my youth:
I remember, now, reading Henry Reed, Inc. back in about the seventh grade. Awesome fun then awesome fun now. No space battles, no Divergents or Hunger Games or Maze, just an eighth-grader spending the summer with his aunt and uncle in a small New Jersey town, who somehow manages to regularly and repeatedly attract a charming, 1950's sort of trouble almost daily. Hats off to Mr. Robertson for a clean, fun story
