Time for Yesterday.... I read this shortly after finishing Yesterdays Son. This was definitely my favorite of the two. I felt like yesterday's son should have had a bit of a "flashback" at the end so following up with this felt natural. Not sure it it was always meant to be a duology style story but it felt perfect.It really fleshed out the Zar character and brought him from a seemingly hollow character made to fill a story-line to more of a real part of Spocks life that while hidden, seemed to be more believable than in the previous Zar plot.I really enjoyed this novel and the only hangup I had was the old "Kirk is unhappy behind a desk" sub plot. I'm so over it. I think it would have been better for the story to have placed this novels time to be closer to the end of the original 5 year mission. Leaving out the V'ger/Spock event wouldn't have changed the story that much. I know as fans we love some continuality and pushing the story beyond the series original air time, myself I gobble up the DS9 reboots like treats, but we have so much opportunity to expand of the adventures from that original mission.Overall I really loved it and felt like it gave some closure to Yesterdays Son.
