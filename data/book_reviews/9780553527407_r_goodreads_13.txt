Quick thoughts:What can I say. A collection of some of the finest horror shorts I have read. The wide range of genres and excellent story telling will have you devouring each one.Here are all the individual updates:Story 1 of 20:"Jerusalem's Lot" - What a fun, brooding, spooky tale featuring a family home and a town with a dark past. LOVED the letter writing perspective and the church sequences. (⭐⭐⭐⭐)Story 2 of 20:"Graveyard Shift" - A deceptively fun story that quite literally creeps up on you at the end. Stay out of the basement! 🐀🦇 (⭐⭐⭐⭐)Story 3 of 20:"Night Surf" - A deadly virus has broken out and we follow a couple of college kids as they try to cope with the battle of staying alive for as long as possible. (⭐⭐⭐)Story 4 of 20:"I Am The Doorway" - I really do not want to say too much because this story is just so good! Arthur was a part of the space program and might have contracted something. He will have something interesting to talk about the next time he sees his ophthalmologist. (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)Story 5 of 20:"The Mangler" - Funny, detailed, spooky, and so well written. I think I might avoid laundry day for a few more hours. 😅 Also, I just found out there was a movie directed by Tobe Hooper. Where have I been? Note to self: Watch this in the near future!! (⭐⭐⭐⭐)Story 6 of 20:"The Boogeyman" - Lester loses a son to something in the closet. Will he listen to his other children's concerns before it's too late? Only his psychiatrist has the answer to that. This one is for all of us who had to have a nightlight as children so we could make sure that closet door never opened. 😐 (⭐⭐⭐⭐)Story 7 of 20:"Gray Matter" - This one was fantastic! Total B-movie creature feature vibes. Lesson: If you have a friend that insists on drinking warm natty light or PBR and you have not seen them in awhile, you might want to go check on them. (⭐⭐⭐⭐)Story 8 of 20:"Battleground" - This short captures the feel of one of my fave Twilight Zone episodes, so naturally this is a five star-er for me. Do shorts get more fun than this? I think not. **An absolute must read** (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)Story 9 of 20:"Trucks" - What would you do if you were holled up in a fuel station while seemingly autonomous trucks try to mow down everyone in sight? This sort of sounds like a trip to Bucees, but worse. This short was fantastic!!! (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)Story 10 of 20:"Sometimes They Comeback" - Jim is flabbergasted to find out that his brothers killer is back after all these years. One of the strangest bits is that he has not aged a day in almost 20 years. How could that be? A bonkers story. Loved it! (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)Story 11 of 20:"Strawberry Spring" - People are going missing in a local college town. The gossip rises and more and more people start to turn up. Who could be the culprit? A few too many red herrings, but fun and exciting nonetheless. (⭐⭐⭐)Story 12 of 20:"The Ledge" - Cressner has crossed a criminal overlord. He must walk the ledge of his penthouse to obtain money and the person he desires most. Will he be able to do it? This was a fun one. It has Die Hard vibes. (⭐⭐⭐⭐)Story 13 of 20:"The Lawnmower Man" - Harold hires a company to mow his lawn. The prices are reasonable, they do a good job, but never comment on how they accomplish their task. This one was SO great. Totally bonkers. Loved it. (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)Story 14 of 20:"Quitters, Inc" - Richard employes a service to help him stop smoking. They will go to any lengths to make sure you do not fall off the wagon. Another tense and thought provoking short. Loads of fun satire. (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)Story 15 of 20:"I Know What You Need" - Elizabeth meets a guy who seems to know quite a bit about her. As time passes, strange things start to happen to those in Liz's life. This one was tense! The ending was just perfect. It ramps up the craziness pretty quick. (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)Story 16 of 20:"The Last Rung on the Ladder" - A brother saves his sister from a fall in a barn. Can he save her again as she gets older? Gosh. This one was SAD!! (⭐⭐⭐⭐)Story 17 of 20:"Children Of The Corn" - A road trip is haunted by something that walks in the local cornfields. I'm kind of torn on this one. Not sure if I'm alone on this, but it was just okay. (⭐⭐⭐)Story 18 of 20:"The Man Who Loved Flowers" - Someone is going to meet someone they love with flowers and hope. This one was extremely dark, but it was probably one of my favorites. The twist threw me for a loop!!! (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)Story 19 of 20:"One For The Road" - Sequel to Salem's lot! Amazing!! (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)Story 20 of 20:"The Woman In The Room" - Talk about ending with a sad and one. Ugh! (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)⭐ |Rating| ⭐❖ 5 out of 5 ❖
