A book from it's time, heavily influenced by movies and theater. Lots of slang and drinking, and not a whole lot of detecting. There are several murders, but no one seems interested in figuring out who-done-it. Of course there is one guy, a lawyer, off in the wings most of the time who ends up figuring it all out.It's more of people doing "crazy things" and usually all the wrong things, having a drink a laugh and telling the lawyer to get things fixed up.So, while it has a few wonderful lines here and there, for me it the sum of it didn't add up to a great mystery or a great comedy.
