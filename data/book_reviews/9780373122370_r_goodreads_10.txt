Great read for angst junkies. I loved the first half of the book, but the cheating misunderstanding resolved too early, and left me feeling pretty bored for the rest of the book. I get that the wife is bored and wants a compromise with the hub, but by the end, I felt like she wanted it all her way and she did get it all her way. I actually ended up feeling bad for the hero.4 stars when I first read it years ago, 3 stars when I re read it today.
