This is my first Ringo book. Apparently, this is his first book too.It is also the most realistic depiction of how the military actually works (circa 1990) I have ever read.Many of the things that other reviews complain about:- Characters split up and separated.- Having to learn esoteric specification of your weapons- So much emphasis on training- Casual misogynyAre what gives it verisimilitude. It's a slice-of-life military style.Having said that, a book shouldn't feel like an entire enlistment.The pacing could have been improved, and judging by other reviews, the author has learned that lesson in future books.
