I like the setting. And it struck me in this one that I definitely have a better sense of Alleyn as a character now. One could argue that 13 books in is too late - I don't disagree. I will only say that it probably happened a few books ago, and I didn't notice until now, but I definitely have a better sense than in the early novels.Also, no case of insta-love. There is an engaged couple, but they've known each other for a year or so, so y'know, fair.And, I'm pleased that Ngaio Marsh was clearly planning on finally developing something with Alleyn/Troy in the next book.
