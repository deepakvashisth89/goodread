Decent idea is turned into a pretty dry book.Piers channels Philip Jose Farmer in this story about a warrior from the times of the mongul hordes is brought through time to be used in a future war game that is based on his time period.He soon realizes his that his only way to survive is to help his patrons, but also ensure that his minor 'character' in the game progresses through the various levels and eventually 'wins'.Some cool stuff, but pretty dry and too much over explained and everyone but the hero stays fairly one dimensional.
