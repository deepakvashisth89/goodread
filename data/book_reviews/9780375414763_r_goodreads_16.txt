2.5⭐️

Another literature class read. I thought that this was an interesting take on one of the nights at the brown table. I personally don’t know much about King Arthur and his legend other than who he is on a basic level, his wife, Guinevere, Lancelot, and the Knights of the round table. I enjoyed certain aspects of this book/epic poem, but I found the language to be slightly hard to understand. There were certain parts of the book that I had to keep going back to reread to make sure I understood what was going on correctly. There was a slight twist at the end, but that was one of those moments where I had to go back and try to figure out if I misread something because I didn’t comprehend what had happened.
