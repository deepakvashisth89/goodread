Another guilty quick read in ASDA. The book is bright and colourful, couldn't fault it. There may be some difficulties for certain neurodivergent children, but I believe parents will know if this is something that will impact your child. Same as the colours may be something that might be difficult to digest for some kids.

I really like the art style, the story was bland but simplistic- perfectly catered for a newborn - 4 year old children. I loved these sorts of books as a kid, it's great to see new ones still coming out. It's a matter of preference, although in the same breath check your local charity shops as these will be in abundance usually. As well as local libraries (I see you mamas and papas.)

If I had a kid I would of paid for it- on the basis I just like having bits and bats. Again it comes down to preferences and kids preferences too. I loved this little book.
