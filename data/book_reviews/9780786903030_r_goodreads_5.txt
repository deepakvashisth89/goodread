The antagonist felt really different and interesting, it was very refreshing.I really enjoyed it overall.The Magic Thief By Mark AnthonyI have enjoyed Mark Anthony’s writing thus far, the title of this story is intriguing. The antagonist was interesting, I suppose he was meant to be portrayed as pitiful and it really showed his desperation.I definitely hung on to every word and the ending was sad but well-deserved for what it was.I love it when characters are humbled one way or the other but take it in strides and learn from it.The Quiet Place By Christie GoldenI’ve been waiting to read this story for a long time and am so delighted to do so finally.
