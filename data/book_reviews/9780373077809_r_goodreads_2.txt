4 Stars! ~ Karen Anders' debut is a wonderful read. There's suspense, danger, precocious 13 year old, feisty but cautious heroine and the most yummy tortured hero. If you are a tortured hero fan, you'll love this book. Corey is haunted both from an abusive childhood the feeds his fear that he'll be like his father, and from a horrific rodeo accident that wounded his body and shaken his confidence. The attraction between them is instant. Jennifer seems to see all the pain hidden deep inside him and Corey is torn between the need to run and the need to stay. This book was published in 1997, and there is a budding crush between Ellie and a boy, Tucker, who is her protector. I'd love to see their story and a look at how Corey and Jennifer are doing
