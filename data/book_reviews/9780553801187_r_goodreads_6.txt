From the back cover: It is 1943-and the unthinkable happened: a local spotter on the remote English coastline has witnessed American pilots, returning from a bombing run, turning their guns on one of their own. The victim and his plane are downed, and the spotter and his wife are the next targets as the rouge American planes return to strafe their home, their livestock, with such force and purpose that it is a miracle anyone survives.What could cause decorated leaders of men to kill their brother-in arms and then viciously attack allied civilians?Review:This was an engaging tale of military blunder and cover up in World War Two. I found it to be an interesting read.
