My expectations for this were admittedly very high considering how amazing Pt. 3 is, and this certainly delivered for the most part. The first three stories are absolutely epic but unfortunately it slowed down and dragged in the middle. There were probably three stories in a row that were complete letdowns. As it kept going however, it improved a lot. The story called 'Trick or Treat' and another story based on the creepy urban legend about the hitchhiker at the mall helped redeem a very lackluster stretch and make this a 4/5 stars from me.
