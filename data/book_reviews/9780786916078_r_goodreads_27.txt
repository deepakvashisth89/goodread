The Fallen Fortress follows Cadderly and gang as they attack the Castle Trinity and attempt to defeat the followers of Talona once and for all. They also need to make pit stop to destroy an evil magical object from book 3.This is another one of the books in this series I'm not sure fond on. While in book 2 Cadderly was whiny and timid, in this book hes overconfident and on a power trip. He constantly says/thinks that he is correct and refuses to listen to anyone who disagrees with him or his method. He goes so far as to magically overpower anyone who disagrees with him. Hes cocky and kind of an asshole. I know this is character progression and the events in book 5 will take away his cockiness.
