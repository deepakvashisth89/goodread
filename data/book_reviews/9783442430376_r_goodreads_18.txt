One of the best books I have ever read and a long time favorite of mine. I try to read it every year and still cry every time. Danielle Steel pulls you in and you can’t help but fall in love with the tragedy, the heartbreak and the beauty of Vietnam.These Vietnam veterans did not get a welcoming at home & I mourn that often for them. My heart breaks for the trials they endured stateside just as much as what they experienced in country. It’s crazy. I’m thankful for this story!!!
