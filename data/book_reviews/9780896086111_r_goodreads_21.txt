Originally didn’t finish this book because it, at times, just reads like a catalogue of evidence and examples of the US doing dodgy things. The most interesting part is probably it’s opening chapter, where Chomsky lays out how rogue states aren’t just the “traditional” group that we think violate international laws, but the superpowers and their allies who can use international law to control others and flout it themselves when it suits.There are some really interesting examples in here but it can get repetitive and dry at times. Had to skim some bits where it just was the same argument over and over but with a slightly different example used.He has written a lot of stuff and whilst it is interesting, and important, it isn’t the best read throughout. Honestly could have been written as a long essay rather than a book.
