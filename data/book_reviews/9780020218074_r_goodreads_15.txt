08/04/20252025 reads book 20“I cannot make you understand. I cannot make anyone understand what is happening inside me. I cannot even explain it to myself.”I think this was a great first introduction to Kafka and his works! I will not lie and say that I fully understood everything he was trying to convey in his stories (especially those really short ones), but I think I got the main gist of it. The ones that stood out to me were (of course) the title story, in the penal colony and the fever dream that was a country doctor. For sure reading more Kafka after this, since I really enjoyed the writing style and the constant "what?" that came out of me after finishing a story.
