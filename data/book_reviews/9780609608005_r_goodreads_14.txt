Picked this one up because it was referrenced a couple of times in Never Split the Difference by Chris Voss. And I’m glad I did so. Jim Camp adds another dimension to the methods of negotiation from
Chris Voss. Camp’s negotatiation centers largely around business negotiation, and strongly captures the behavioural and psychological qualities required of a great negotiator. I liked that Camp also went into significant detail and dialogs of the negotiations he sampled in the book. This was something I really missed from Never Split the Difference. Camp also adds in a higher meaning of mission, vision and even philanthropic habits that makes his teaching method deeper beyond just cold negotiation. I would recommend this book as a starting point for anyone looking to understand and better their negotiation skills.
