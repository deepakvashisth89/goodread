I really liked this book. Yes there is a triggering part around the beginning of the book, but you will come around to the story when you keep reading. I really liked Gabrielle and the twist near the end. I didn’t really care for Ruarke much.. I know the author wrote his action as something that could be forgiven in this story, so it doesn’t bother me much. He feels remorse and is extremely ashamed (he also doesn’t remember it).
