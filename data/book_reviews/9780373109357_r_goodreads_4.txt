RE the Impossible Woman - ED brings us a very sweet romance this time around and we get bonus scenes with the H and h from her last HP A World Apart. The h in this one is 22 and a landscape designer - except she can't get any jobs cause she is 5'2, blonde, blue eyed and perky with cuteness. She almost wishes she looks like Julia Childs, ( but in the garden,) or was an Amazon so people would take her seriously.She is very involved in the family owned nursery and has pretty much given up on dating. Since she moved to the big city with her family, she has had one relationship with a guy who came from a rich family. She thought she was in love and they would marry and he thought she was fine as the side of dip on a bag of chips, but anything further was just not on -guys like him don't marry women like her. Needless to say the h was very hurt, but she hadn't done anything she couldn't tell her mum about, so she just gave up on the dating game - most guys don't interest her.The h has to deliver sod to a new house being built by a famous architect. She gets there and is unloading when this Nordic God appears next to her without his shirt and carrying a big hammer-- wait, there was no hammer really, I was daydreaming again--- but the rest is fairly accurate. The h is agog at all the manliness on display and since there is no landscaping whatsoever to look at, well a girl has to have something to occupy her eyesight.The man offers to help her, seeing as she is so small and cuddly an' all, and the h is a bit offended that he thinks she can't do it (and there is some words about that,) but srsly what sane woman in her right mind is going to miss the chance to ogle all that manly muscle while he totes her sod? So he helps her and the h is thinking he is really nice, there is a lot of teasing banter and since the guy introduced himself as the handyman, she is thinking "Maybe my dad can give him a job.."She still doesn't know his name, he calls himself Punch to her Judy, but he does offer to show her around the architecturally stunning home and let her jump in the pool, if she will bring her bikini when she comes back later that afternoon to deliver the rest of the sod. The h happily agrees and bikini in hand, she returns after a fortifying lunch. They swim and tour the house, teasing each other with risque banter the whole time.They finish the tour of the house in the man's bedroom and the kissing begins. The h is totally overwhelmed by the roofie kiss and almost faints from the fireworks. Passion is burning high and the h has lost all self-control. Then a woman calls out the man's name and he grabs a towel to go answer her. The h realizes that she has almost surrendered her most cherished wedding night gift and that the man is no common handyman, he is the famous architect that is building the house. She is outraged that he thought he could seduce the hired help and then toss her away, just like her ex.She decides to beat him at his own game. When he returns, ready to resume the firework festivities, the h demands a medical certificate - she isn't the kind of girl not to consider her health don't you know.The H is a bit flabbergasted and then the h tells him off for lying to her about who he is and trying to make it with a girl he considers to be easy and makes to leave. The H waylays her and says he was actually going to ask her to go to a dinner party with him. She says she can't as she has nothing to wear. He tells her they will hit the beach boutique and he buys her a dress, but he lies about the price - which she finds out about later.They arrive at the dinner party, it is the H's solicitor, his predatory wife who is trying to make it with the H, another couple with the male half being a lech and the couple from A World Apart. The H built their house. The wife is very obvious in her moves and the H implies that the h is his lover and the h doesn't like that, or the speculation from the male lech or the catty remarks of the wife and her crony. She does like the h from the first book and they get along well.Then the h finds out the H lied about the cost of the dress and it really bugs her. She wants to leave but the H is playing snooker and is stuck in a very bad spot. The men teasingly tell the h to save the day and she shocks all of them when she does. Her dad is the amateur state snooker champion and he taught her everything she knows.Finally the H and h get to leave, but the h avoids his attempts to get her into bed and accuses him of using her as a sub for the solicitor's wife - she thinks they are having an affair- and she is still mad about the dress and the lover implications. She tells the H off and he calls her an impossible woman and she storms off for home.The H shows up a week later and wants her to design the landscape for his fabulous house, the h agrees if it is business only and the H says ok. Then she is kissing him and her dad walks in. They wind up touring the nursery and the h introduces the H to her mentally challenged brother who is 19, but meningitis left him with limited capacity. The H is very nice to her brother, when most people aren't and the h is taking all of this as a very good sign.The H invites her over to review her landscaping plan and sign a contract. She shows up with her plans, he tells her to do whatever she wants and then roofie kisses happen again and they wind up in bed. The h is making wedding plans and the H freaks out. He just wants her to live with him, he doesn't do marriage. They have a huge fight and she vows to get his landscaping done as fast as humanly possible. Her dad and her brother help her out and she gets it done in under a month.The H tries to bribe her to live with him by getting her the landscape design contract for a big city center park he just built but the h doesn't want any further contact and tells the H no. He tells her to think about it, but the h is really torn.She wants the job - it is very prestigious and could jump start her career, but she knows the H is using the job to get her live in sin and she just can't do it. Then the solicitor's wife shows up again and she is really mean to the h's brother. There is another big telling off and the H swears he isn't having an affair with the woman, it is too hard to get a good solicitor. The h believes him, but she still can't go against what she believes is right - the H questions the depth of her love and the h goes off to think.The h has a few days of angsting, cause she loves him so much - but he can't love her as much because he won't commit to marriage. She thinks about just shacking up - her dad tells her the family will love her no matter what, and then the time the H has given her to think is up. She has to give the H an answer, and she is about to fling her bonnet over the windmill when the H and h from A World Apart show up and the h see's how much they love and support each other and they are married. The previous h tells this h to fight for what she thinks is right, her own road to marriage was very rocky, and the h sees the light of HPlandia wisdom.The H is thinking he is getting what he wants when the h abruptly decides to leave. She turns down the job and tells him to look at his friends - they are married and committed and they make all his arguments against commitment and marriage look pathetic. She wants marriage and a steady commitment and if he can't do that, she is done. The h leaves and the H follows her right away. The h tries to get her dad to kick the H out, but the H is determined, so the dad gives him a shot at the h.He avows eternal love and devotion in the kitchen in front of all her family and tells her he will marry her at the registry office the next day. The h's mum isn't having any of that, and so a big wedding (ED LOVES big weddings) is planned and everyone is happy and HEA with the H delighted that he finally gets to keep his impossible woman.This was a quick fluffy one with some good banter and it was nice to see the prior couple from the other book all lurved up. Nothing big happens, but there is some funny moments and the H's aversion to marriage isn't really explained. Who cares tho?
