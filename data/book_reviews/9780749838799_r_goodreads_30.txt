Right from the start, this is a book designed to please young readers and jaded adults, alike.The narrator, Roger Hargreaves, calls attention to the house of Miss Curious. He asks, "It's a very curious shape. Isn't it?"Fact is, after reading that, I took a quick glance. And I didn't get it.On second glance, though, I got it. What a hilarious visual joke! (Not that I'll reveal it and spoil it for you, Goodreaders. Just be on the lookout.)I can give you'all a sample of her curiosity, though.One day, Little Miss Curious headed off to town."Why do doors squeak, yet they are not small and furry with pink ears and long tails?" she asked her door as she went out."Delightful book -- often LOL funny. FIVE STARS delightful, actually.
