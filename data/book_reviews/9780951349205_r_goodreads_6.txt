Heartstone Odyssey is an okay book, and is slightly interesting.
This book contains splotches of racist comments spoken by the bad people, but it seems as if the author just randomly added those comments in, as if he had to do it. The format of this book is quite annoying as the words are all squished up together and is therefore hard to read. As well as this, the storyline of this book is a bit confusing in the beginning, but might become clearer as you progress through the story. Overall, I would give this book three stars, as there are some good and bad things in this book.
