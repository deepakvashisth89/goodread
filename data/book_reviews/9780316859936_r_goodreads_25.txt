I'm not hugely inspired to write a review of this book. It was ok, not a page turner, I wasn't desperate to know what happened in the end and it was fairly predictable anyway, I didn't like the characters very much and I felt a lot of the story was implausible, though some of the more factual bits were interesting. Ultimately, it was an ok 'holiday read'. Not a book I would recommend, but I wouldn't say don't read it either.
