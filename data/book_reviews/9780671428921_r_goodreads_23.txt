I read this book often when I was young, which probably helped to cement my love of truly scary ghost stories. This is a scary, poignant ghost story of two haunted lost girls in two different centuries, yet somehow their stories connect. As an adult, I see the whole story differently, and while I still didn't like the antagonist, I found myself sympathizing with her a bit more than when I was young. I also get a lot more of the more subtle points the author made. Very cleverly written.
