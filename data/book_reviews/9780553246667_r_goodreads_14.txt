This is the best Trek book I've read from Bantam so far - but that's very faint praise considering how poor the earlier novels from this publisher have been. The book is better because the author gets the cast. I think that's because Ms. Lawrence was able to review actual scripts from the series while the other Bantam writers apparently based their depictions on half-remembered prime time viewings in the years before VHS/DVD recordings were available. And like Alan Dean Foster did with his later novelizations of the animated Trek show, Lawrence provides us with an original adventure to supplement the two TOS Harry Mudd episodes. It's a serviceable enough plot idea but the real appeal here is that the crew of the Enterprise act like themselves.

3 stars
