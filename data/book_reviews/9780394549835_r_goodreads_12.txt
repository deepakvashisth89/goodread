Memoir of a WWII American bomber pilot based in Italy. Muirhead would appear to be a solitary man, not a loner, I think there's a difference. He's not at all concerned with his fellow airmen or crew members, in fact he doesn't seem to recall too many of them specifically saying that the crews changed so often on his plane that he never really got to know too many of his fellow crew members at all. What he remembers most is flying, and he writes about that with a poetic ease that I found almost spiritual. His love of the planes, the sound of the engines, the weather, everything about it was kind of a mystical experience for him.His description of how his bomber was shot down and how he got out of the plane is one of the most exciting and harrowing WWII stories I have ever read. Not bad for a library book sale find!
