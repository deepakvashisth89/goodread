This is a fairly comprehensive guide to this period. But I mean hell, what do I know. It's all castles and serfs to me.

As a rough guide for reference or timeline etc, it's good, but for the deeper stuff, probably not so much. And the author has a terrible habit of introducing someone important, say the new king or queen, with 'well, there was heaps of work for Mary to do once she ascended the throne.' Does that mean the last king died? Is that how you're gonna kill him off? Something obviously happened to the guy if his daughter's currently facing the trials of rule. I just spent pages reading how he got the magnates in control (spoiler: liberal execution, some exiles, a ton of imprisonment) and instead of a lavishly detailed funeral with appropriately grieving thanes you give me a segue?

Oh, and I learnt that England used to fight France for pretty much no reason at all. The wife's turned into a crone by 22, the thralls are restless and I've now got to export wool due to the plummeting price of oats. May as well skip town for a while and pester the crapauds.

Although I think as a derogatory appellation crapauds came much later.
