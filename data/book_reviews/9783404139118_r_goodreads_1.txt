Another Fast Action Book!The author is known as the Queen of Spy Thrillers, and indeed she is. After reading ‘The Book of Spies’ my first book by Gayle Lynds I was eager to read another. Masquerade was difficult to find but locating a copy is well worth the effort. It is fast action with never a dull moment throughout the entire book. The characters are exceptionally strong along with a suspenseful story line. An engrossing book and highly recommended.
