✍🏻 One of the things I’ve always appreciated about Nikos’s writing is its lyricism, his use of fascinating similes and metaphors, the poetic way of his style. This can be quite startling. I probably enjoy that about his fiction as much or more than his storylines and characterization. So, that alone is a reason for reading this famous work of his.

✨ It seems to me there was some fuss over this novel. It’s fiction. It’s a story. Chill. Take it, leave it, but let an author explore and imagine. Let them create worlds that might be or might never be, but that take your mind and spirit into other realms and other ways of looking at the reality that sprawls around us: past, present, future, infinity.
