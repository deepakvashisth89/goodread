This is an older harlequin first published back in 1983 I’ve not read anything by the late Sally Wentworth before. This was an interesting read quite tame and a little dated.Alexa is engaged to Mark she’s crazy about him, she takes him to meet her best friend Elaine. Big mistake as he dumps her and promptly marries Elaine. Alexa is even invited to the wedding and weirdly decides to go, forcing herself to watch the man she loves marry another. It’s at the reception she meets Marks brother Scott, again it’s odd that Scott has no idea that Alexa is his brothers jilted fiancée. Scott offers Alexa a lift back to London.He drops her at her flat and she invites him in for a drink,(these two certainly drink a lot of alcohol in this, they’re quite the boozers) from here things go crazy. Alexa encourages Scott to have sex with her, he looks a lot like his brother she intends to fantasise that it’s Mark. But she makes the mistake of calling Scott by his brothers name. It’s then that Scott discovers who she is. She locks herself in the bathroom and swallows a bottle of Aspirin, again it’s odd he doesn’t seek medical assistance. After this he forces her to marry him and go back to Brazil where he works in the jungle.When they return home to England he expects her to play happy families with Mark and Elaine. A very odd story all round the characters were okay I didn’t like Alexa much she was a bit of a whinger but she did have just cause most of the time but it was annoying. Scott was a mystery and was difficult to connect with. But most of all I didn’t believe that Scott and Alexa were in love and were going to have their happy ever after.
