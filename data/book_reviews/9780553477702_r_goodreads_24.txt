Lights, camera, action !

Allegra comes from a family engrossed in Hollywood and is an attorney to the stars. After her work sends her to New York, she meets Jeff. As the title suggests they decide to get married. The Wedding is so much more though. Not only is it about Allegra and Jeff, it is also about her family.

The Wedding was trashy yet engrossing. Danielle Steel's depiction of Allegra's Hollywood was over the top and beautiful. I found myself rolling my eyes but unable to put down the book.

Danielle Steel possesses a talent for writing contemporary romance. While there were several scenes that had me rolling my eyes and cringing, I absolutely fell in love with this book.

I did kind of have an issue with the romance though as it seemed a little bit quick.

I gained a bit of respect for this novel when it's hard to discuss real life issues.

At first I didn't think I was going to connect very much with Allegra or any of the other characters, but that was so not the case. I ended up really caring about a whole Steinberg family. They we're so involved with each other and really loved one and other. Love and loyalty are strong in the Steinberg family. Danielle Steel did a great job depicting the whole family dynamic.

This is a slice of life story and all the characters many stories connecting with each other was really need. I felt like all the characters mattered in the story and had a place. The story just wouldn't have been as good if one of them was excluded.

Cringe-worthy and at times ridiculous, I really enjoyed The Wedding. I think I found my new guilty pleasure author!
