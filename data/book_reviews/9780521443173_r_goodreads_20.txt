A mammoth history of the war (the real length is close to 2,000 pages), focusing a great deal on the (geo)political, diplomatic and strategic aspects, rather than only on the tactical developments on the battlefields, emphasizing, as the title suggests, its global aspect. As if writing this enormous history was not enough, the author finishes with a 25-page bibliographical essay in an even smaller font, listing the most important books which he used in the writing of the book, also providing recommendations for the best ones in each of the various topics, highlighting just how much effort was invested in this monster of a book. This at the end made me to award it five stars. As a bonus, an interesting feature is that occasionally the author references WWII films (Das Boot is mentioned several times) and puts them in the context of the relevant campaigns. All in all, a fantastic achievement.
