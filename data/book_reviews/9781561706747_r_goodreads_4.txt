I met Jon recently when he joined my TV station in San Diego as an anchor / reporter. As we began to work together I learned of this book he had written 13 years ago. Back in 2000 when the book was published he appeared on Oprah and subsequently the book sold about 150K copies. The story is about his life growing up as the middle of three boys to volatile southern parents with a father who is a federal prosecutor, later becoming a civil rights lawyer. But then his family story takes an unexpected turn when they urgently leave the South and head West, to San Diego County. Within a few years, Jon's father vanishes, leaving three teenage boys and an ex-wife to fend for themselves. Jon's life story could have turned horribly tragic accept for one thing, he didn't crumble under the weight of that devastating blow. He found his way, went to college, and became a respected TV reporter. But these kinds of hard, emotional life events don't just go away. When his dad left them, the former lawyer wound up homeless, living the rest of his life on the streets from the East coast to the West. His father's abandonment and mysterious transient life stayed with Jon throughout adulthood, manifesting in an anger resting beneath the surface of his skin, affecting his wife and children. But there came a point of reckoning, a moment Jon would be forced to find his dad and confront him. Jon's 4-year-old son would plant the seed that grew into a street odyssey where Jon and his dad would have the final showdown. The book is well written with good visual story-telling. Overall, an easy read and I enjoyed it.
