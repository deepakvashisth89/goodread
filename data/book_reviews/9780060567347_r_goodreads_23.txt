Although not a memoir, this novel by Adeline Yen Mah does incorporate the basic parts of her life such as being abused by her stepmother but in Chinese Cinderella and the Secret Dragon Society, she was able to run away. After running away she runs into Grandma Wu who takes her in and introduces her to the Chinese Resistance during the 1940s. There she gains a new family and learns martial arts and saves 5 injured Americans. But her favorite aunt is killed after an attack from the Japanese and suspects her stepmother.
