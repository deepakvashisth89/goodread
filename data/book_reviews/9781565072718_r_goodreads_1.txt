This book did a great job at clarifying what the Bible says about the spiritual realm and what we as believers need to be looking out for in terms of what the culture says about angels and demons. While there wasn't a huge presence of practical application, this was an insightful read that was a great reminder that there is a very real spiritual battle going on that we need to be on guard against! It was more educational with small bits of application, but I would definitely recommend it to anyone who wants to learn more about what God's word says about the spiritual realm.
