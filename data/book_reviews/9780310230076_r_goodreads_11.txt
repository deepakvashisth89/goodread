Certainly, a book worth reading again and again. More practically encouragement and exhortation from the pastor of the Brooklyn Tabernacle church. At the end of the book are 33 Treasures or scriptures about faith. He defines and illustrates what it is to have genuine life-changing faith no matter what you have gone through or done with your life. More stories like in his first book about people that God has transformed and rescued from the dead, some truly amazing testimonies. And we all could take a look at our daily walk with God and look to find a renewal and revival of our faith in God and the work and life of Jesus Christ.
