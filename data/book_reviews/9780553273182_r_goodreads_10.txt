It's a solid adventure story about a well realized and quite not our culture going on a quest to get the magic treasure.It's billed as hard science fiction, but I think that Benford is cheating. He may not technically deviate from "science" at any point, but everything about the book is great examples of what happens when a culture is so far removed from the "science" behind their science that it may as well be magic. We get results without causes, and the explanations may be scientifically consistent but it hardly matters for the story.Which is fun.Really great fantasy novel.
