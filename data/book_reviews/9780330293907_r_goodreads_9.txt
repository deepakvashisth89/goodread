This is a great BIG book. The kind they don't sell anymore, nor write. This is the kind of book a travel writer loves to have around. I'd never heard of Nearchus (325 bc) traveling and writing about being in India! Or Lucy Atkinson, who was married to an architect who was a traveler too, and she wrote wonderful.
My only quibble is that he leaves out Mountain explorers, or claims to in the intro, then he includes them selectively. Newby skips over the biggest travel writer of his period too, Edmund Hillary, including himself instead. I will keep this book around though- it has sources of regions that I won't see anywhere else.
