This early Asey Mayo mystery features a snowbound, and uncongenial, group of people, until the host dies suddenly of arsenic poisoning. Boston spinster Prue Whitsby and her oldest friend, Rowena Fible, have been drafted to chaperone a young lady newly arrived from life in France for her half-uncle, and Rowena's long-time bete noire, Adelbert Stires. When Asey Mayo arrives on the scene, Prue--his old acquaintance--becomes his Watson, and Rowena becomes one of the main suspects. Another suspect is Denny James, Prue's one-time beau, who keeps trying to propose to her all over again.
