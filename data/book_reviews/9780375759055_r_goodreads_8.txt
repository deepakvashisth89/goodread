Eh this novel is a bit too...Victorianfor its own good. It's basically a couple of white English guys go to Africa and say the most racist things they possibly can. Apart from the blatant and offensive racism, the story is enjoyable. This isn't a novel that takes itself seriously. It's a light, fun read, nothing more.Since this is one of the most influential and best-selling novels of all time (it's sold 100 million copies, the same asThe Hobbitand double the amount of copiesDeathly Hallowshas sold) it is kinda disappointing that it is overall so-so. Oh well.
