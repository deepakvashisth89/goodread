As usual, Mary Roberts Rinehart’sThe Wallproves equal parts suspenseful mystery and sly social satire. Although first published in 1938, today’s readers will thoroughly enjoy it.Arthur Lloyd has suffered a lot. He married “a cheap girl” who now goes by Juliette Ransom; when she divorced him, she took him to the cleaners, and the hefty alimony payments make his life with new wife Mary Lou and their 5-year-old son Junior very circumscribed, indeed. So when Juliette leaves New York to impose on Arthur’s 28-year-old little sister Marcia Lloyd and is murdered soon after, suspicion immediately falls on Arthur.Rinehart littersThe Wallwith lots of twists and surprises, and I never, ever suspected the true culprit. Another wonderful Rinehart novel!
