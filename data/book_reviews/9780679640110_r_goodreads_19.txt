An absolute classic text.

These lecturers were written and delivered in 1901.

That’s only 40 years post Marx, Darwin, Freud and Nietzsche.

And over a decade before WWI.

Modernity was just hatched.

Indoor plumbing and electricity were not common features.

There were only 15,000 motor vehicles registered in the US.

When understood in historical context.

This book is literally astounding in its prescience.

James presaged our current trans cultural, pan religious science informed psycho-spiritual landscape by 100 years.

Interestingly, James writes about the spiritual value of drug experiences approximately 35 years before LSD was discovered. And about 60 years before Leary and Alpert did their (infamous) experiments at Harvard Divinity School.

James also writes about the spiritual value of addiction recovery approximately 35 years before AA was founded.

Negative GR reviews complain that the language is formal, and ornate, (as per the oratory style of the day) and as such tedious. Other negative reviews complain that there is nothing new to be found here. While these types of criticisms are somewhat valuable in that they might spare a would-be reader similar disappointments. These sentiments also reflect the critics inability to appreciate this text in historical context.

Personally.

I think the writing is simply wonderful.

Some say William James wrote better than his brother Henry.

And when taken in historical context.

This text is astonishing in its impact on the fields of psychology, religious studies and on our culture at large.

Not only did I LOVE it.

But (IMO) it’s pretentious/ignorant to give a historical text like this less than 5/5 stars ⭐️. I understand that GR is a social media platform. And not an academic journal. But a legitimate critique of this text needs to consider it in historical context.

So…

5/5 stars ⭐️
