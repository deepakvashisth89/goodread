Elmore Leonard books are always so much fun! This one was pretty cool. As always, the characters were great. The dialogue was smart and snappy. I can't ever get enough of Elmore Leonard's writing style! His characters are always so easy to root for. This one was kind of dated, written in the early 80s. I still enjoyed it. An easy 5 stars for an easy and entertaining book. Elmore Leonard has become one of my very favorites. I will definitely be reading many more of his books!
