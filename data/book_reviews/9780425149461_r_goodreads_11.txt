Oy vey! This was a tough one ... boy, Jack Ketchum is definitely not for the faint of heart. He pulls no puncheseverand this one is no exception. Just like his other infamous, traumatizing novel --The Girl Next Door--Only Child(previously published asStranglehold) is inspired by true events -- and what a horrible and depressing story! Give me supernatural horror any day because at least then I don't have to brood about the real consequences for the real people involved. This one does not have a happy ending and left me feeling really shitty (hence, three stars). But it was compelling and well-written.
