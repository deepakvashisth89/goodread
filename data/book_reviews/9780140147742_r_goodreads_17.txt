After carrying this around for thirty years I finally got around to reading this collection and stopped after about 200 pages. I've read some of this before, I realized. Perhaps some of these are stand-alone stories once published in magazines, but many are excerpts from his novels, and I don't consider them "short stories." Much of this is sentimental essay, not short story. I can see why Stegner left the form once he was done using it to establish his name. His novels are far superior, IMO. Angle of Repose is still one of my favorites. Donation to little library 6.10.26
