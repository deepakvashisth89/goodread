A very compelling quick read of the life of Vann Nath, one of only 4 survivors of the S-21 prison camp, also known as Tuol Sleng. It's incredibly gripping to hear the first hand account the extreme Maoist Khmer Rouge rise to power, and systematic torture and mass killings of anyone with even the slightest inclination to the old regime. While Nath is saved because the prison guards were ordered to find artists to paint pictures of Pol Pott, Nath is constantly surrounded by mass killings, forced confinement, torture and fear. This book is hardly a tell-all of the Khmer Rouge and Pol Pott's rise to power, and independent knowledge of the regime and the history behind their coming to power and fall is really necessary to fully appreciate this book, which is unfortunately very sparse on detail and information
