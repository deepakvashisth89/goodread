Not a crazy complex book, which I liked. Definitely a kids book (I got this from the little library with Saraea) and I thought it was a good story about a teenager gaining perspective. Disability is hard to understand if you’re not directly exposed to it (esp pre-social media) and I thought this book was an honest account of what that exposure can look like. It’s marked as a true story, so I’m giving it more leeway than if it was fiction. Also, I liked the illustrations.
