I’m surprised (and also not) at the overall rating of this book. I really enjoyed it, although it doesn’t seem to constitue five stars. The story starts off strong, gets a little slow in the middle and end. During the slower story parts, we learn more about Schweitzer and his hospital which is interesting, so it keeps the pages turning. I felt uplifted by this book and read it while traveling in a 3rd world country, so it was easy to feel akin to the author. For people that like adventure books or are interested in humans with many talents, I’d recommend this book.
