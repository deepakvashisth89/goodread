This was an odd book. There was much about it that didn't appeal, a surprise because they were some of the very things that drew me to the book in the picking-it-out stages. It was about a librarian. I usually love librarians. This one, not so much. It was about a giant, a conspicuous outlier. I usually love those - the bigger, the better, the more out there, even yummier. This one, hmm.I thought about putting it down - the story wasn't holding me. Just these two characters flitting about each other sadly, not about each other but about their own self-contained, sealed up sadnesses. One of the best ways to dilute sadness is to share it, where it will fade and change into something different on many levels. Seal it up, and it stays, it strengthens and convinces itself of its own special virtues. There was a whole book of this, until 90% in and *poof* stuff happens you'd never believe. After you reach the end, you still don't believe. You realize you've been fairy-taled.And you still don't believe. No one would do that.
