My neighbor lent me the book, thinking I would be interested in reading a local author. It was an enjoyable read, moving quickly from one topic to another, a lot of which you can identify with if you're an older middle class woman living near Cape Cod. I enjoyed her sense of humor and insight into common daily rituals. Apparently, the author is an NPR commentator on "All Things Considered." I will have to listen for her next time I'm in the car.
