Despite it’s massive size, the tone and structure ofTo Believe in Women: What Lesbians Have Done for America — A Historymade it an incredibly readable history of the wide-ranging accomplishments of lesbians in the early feminist movement. The author approached the topic thoughtfully, maintaining an awareness of the issues associated with applying the lesbian label to women who wouldn’t have been familiar with the term without downplaying the evidence of their relationships.The women that Lillian Faderman discussed came from a range of different backgrounds and took on a range of different roles in the fledging feminist movement and, although some of the names were familiar, this aspect of their stories was not. It was wonderful to see so many examples of women supporting each other and thriving in fulfilling same-sex relationships. There were times when I was furious on their behalf, and the knowledge that so many of the same battles are still being fought across the United Statues could be draining at times, but I was truly inspired by the courage, the ferocity, and, above all, the love of these pioneering women.
