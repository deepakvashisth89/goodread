This is a book I read more than twenty years ago, and it has stayed with me. His thesis is that the writers of scripture, for whatever reason (he has ideas), wrote God out of the story, bit by bit, until humans are on their own. This refers to the Hebrew Bible of course, which begins with Adam and Eve walking and talking with God and ends with the books like Esther, where God doesn't even make a cameo appearance. Yes, Daniel is an exception, but Friedman can explain that. He goes on to work out this theory via comments on Christianity, Nietzsche, and the Death of God movement. If you want a book that will leave you scratching your head in curiosity--a book to challenge pedestrian piety--this is it.
