If you are a follower of Doreen you will definitely love this book. If you surround yourself with healing and 'light' and call yourself a 'lightworker' you will definitely love this book. I found it to be beneficial with techniques and it allowed me to understand more about how certain people perceive Angels and their own experiences with them. I enjoyed it for that but at the same time I personally feel that angels are not all Happy go lucky I'll fluff you if you call me baby ;) But that's just my own perception. Again if you enjoy the lighter workings and feel this is a place for you to look, I'm sure you'll enjoy the book and the techniques provided.I do like reading about inspiration and I do enjoy the upbeat/positive aspect of it all I just wonder if it does a disservice not honoring the other aspect of Angels? All in all good for those who need this!
