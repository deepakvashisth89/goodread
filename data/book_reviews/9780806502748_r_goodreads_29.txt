The Procession is a short collection of poems by khalil jibran.These poems were made or created while khalil jibran was in US after migrating from his native town in lebanon at the end of nineteenth century.Being a great painter too ,Jibran has adorned The procession with beautiful paintings.The procession seems sequel of his an other great work, The Prophet.
Through his poems ,Jibran his expressed his agonies and pains and the every day dilemmas faced by man in life.His vision seems equally primitive and modern and he seems to transcend the boundries of religion,culture and state.He belongs to the whole community and his untimely death is a loss for the entire mankind and not only for lebanon or the Arab world.He has in fact not found his real place in history and he is not available to a large number of people who would be his natural lovers.
