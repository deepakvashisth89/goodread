Pretty solid as far as introductory books to Catholicism goes. I helped lead an adult Confirmation group at a local parish and we followed through this book for discussions. Fr. Lukefahr's book starts with a belief in a deity, then looks at the Jewish story, then the life, death, and Resurrection of Jesus. From there it's early Church history, looking at the Sacraments, and then living as a Catholic with contemporary issues.

While this is good, I think Trent Horn's Why We're Catholic: Our Reasons for Faith, Hope, and Love is a little more approachable in terms of modern language and also easier to get a copy of since this one is out of print I believe.
