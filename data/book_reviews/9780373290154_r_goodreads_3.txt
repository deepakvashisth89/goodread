The 2nd book in this Knight SeriesThis is Brandon Cavendish's story (the first book was his younger brother Guy). Brandon is 31 but has no plans of marrying. His father has bandied him about it often with no results so it seems Sir Thomas had enlisted the help of the King in the matter. Whilst playing tennis the King announces to Brandon he has arranged a marriage for him. Brandon assumes it a ply to distract, as it did. He finds it to be truth.Kat has had two husbands and desires no more. Her first marriage was to an 80 year old man who needed a nurse maid not a wife and the second to an abusive beast who she was glad when he died.Kat receives the letter from the King that she will marry. A plan comes to mind. Miranda becomes Kat. That way Kat can watch and see what this man is truly like.Brandon and his best friend, Jack, also decide to switch places.Fenton, the nephew of Kat, spends his allowance ahead of time and plans on being the soul heir to her lands and money. No worries. He then hears of the marriage that could change everything for him and sets out to stop it.Many turns and twists in this story but it all ends up together as a great tale.This entire review has been hidden because of spoilers.Show full review
