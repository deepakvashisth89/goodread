This is the second time I have read this collection of short stories that were later developed into Chandler’s Philip Marlowe novels.Killer in the Rain and The Curtain became the iconic Big Sleep and it’s fascinating to see how the Lady in The Lake was developed and expanded upon to become such a perfect novel. To quote the man himself ‘A good story cannot be devised; it has to be distilled‘Chandler is the master of Los Angeles Noir and his Philip Marlowe along with Dashiell Hammett’s Sam Spade is a simply wonderful character..
