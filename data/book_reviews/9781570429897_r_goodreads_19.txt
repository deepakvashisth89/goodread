MAY CONTAIN SPOILERS.Michael Garrick, a junior atty. in White House counsel's office, is the main character. A self-described 'Boy Scout," Garrick starts dating First Daughter, Nora Hartson, which is where the adventure also begins.This falls into the category of mystery in which the central detective is not a detective by trade.This is a thriller that takes place mainly in the workplace -- where the usual principles apply (rivalry, alliances, turfdom, rank). Because it's the White House, though, everything is amped up.An interesting twist -- Garrick has a low I.Q. father who resides in a facility since he can't live independently (without being taken advantage of).The "mother" of all twists, though, Nora Hartson turns out to be under the sway of an individual that the author has set us up to least suspect -- i.e., a key advisor to the president, with a friendship that dates back to the men's high school years.Wish Nora hadn't ended up dead.
