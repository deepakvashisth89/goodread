In just the first few pages I felt some empathy with Lodge’s main character, Laurence Passmore, he had similar thoughts to me and I looked forward to how the story would evolve. My anticipation rapidly declined and after five or so pages I just felt bored and wondered where this “story” about a paranoid, hypochondriac and depressed scriptwriter was going and whether to bother continuing. It is just a boring diary with no main storyline and boring characters, followed by a series of one-sided conversations with characters with some relationship to the main protagonist. I found I still didn’t care… it’s a book describing the minutia of his everyday boring and depressing life… these chapters made the narrative sound really repetitive. To be fair, things picked up a little at the end when he drove the Camino de Santiago, looking for his childhood sweetheart, Maureen.
