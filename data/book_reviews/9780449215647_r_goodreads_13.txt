I haven’t read a Mary Stewart book in a very long time. I picked this one up because it took place in Vienna, Austria, a place I will be visiting in September.

This book reminded me of a grown up version of Nancy Drew mysteries. Vanessa, our “spunky” heroine, her cool, spy husband, Lewis and 17 year old Timothy, happy to be part of this sleuthing adventure.

I found this to be a very average light read but it did give me a feel for Austria, with the beautiful descriptions of the place.
