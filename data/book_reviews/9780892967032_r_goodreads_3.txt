I usually enjoy Lansdale and Freezer Burn was a fun read, but one that lacked much umph at the end. The dark humor animates the tale, one of Billy the loser in South Texas; the novel starts with this as the opening line:
Bill Roberts decided to rob the firecracker stand on account he didn't have a job and not a nickel's worth of money and his mother was dead an kind of freeze-dried in her bedroom.
So, Bill Roberts, still living at home jobless even after his mother died, needs some cash and a life; why not rob the firecracker stand across the street after all? What could go wrong? Well, two dead accomplices later, drifting in 'the bottoms' swamp, Bill is about to find out! Without going further into the plot, Billy washes up just outside a travelling freakshow camp, and one thing leads to another...

The title refers to one of the 'attractions' of the show-- a dead man ("The Ice Man!") kept in a freezer; Billy eventually ends up living in the trailer of the Ice Man and does a heap of contemplating...

If you like Landsdale, you should enjoy this. Not too big on plot, but Bill is quite the character and the language Landsdale deploys here makes it a fun, light read. 3 frozen stars!!
