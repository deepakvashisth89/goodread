Hands down, this is one of my favorite books. And though I'm not usually a fan of villain origin stories (and I'll die on this cross, Raistlin IS a villain), it's such a full and immersive story that makes sense as to how and why he ended up how he did without it being a sob story. It's sympathetic, colorfully and warmly written (imo). I just love Weis's writing style, you just don't find many omniscient POVs anymore, and I appreciate that it's just a wonderful, classic fantasy that isn't romantasy, YA, or "representing" something. Both books in this duology are great. Then if you want more of the twins, the Legends Trilogy is equally great, but that is set after the Dragonlance Chronicles.
There's no real spice, but a series of adult situations do come up. Just as a warning 🙂.
