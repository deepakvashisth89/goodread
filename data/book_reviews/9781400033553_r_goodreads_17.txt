This may be the hardest book to review I’ve ever read. It’s a collection of stories that when taken altogether present a compelling, stretching, and at times riveting portrayal of life in America over the latter part of the twentieth, and early portion of the 21st century. In totality, it’s a replica of living in America. Compelling at times, a bit monotonous at times, but will have you balling your eyes out at other times. With no warning. I’m not really sure how to describe it other than, it’s a collection of stories about America that pace out in a way that America does. As a young adult that grew up in during 9/11 and the Iraq war, those particular essays were incredibly poignant, as it showed an up close and personal look at tragic and monumental events I was too young to understand at the time, and at the time of understanding, they were too far in the collective rearview for normalized exposure. It’s not a quick read as it’s not a linear page-turner, but each essay shows a unique aspect of American society. Some meaningful, some really not, but the ones that hit you make reading the whole book worth it.
