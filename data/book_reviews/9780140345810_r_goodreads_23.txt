I first read this when I was 9 or 10 and re-reading it now, it’s clear why I liked it so much as a kid. There’s the easy to relate to put upon protagonist, the cool time travel gimmick, and ultimately hints at the horror genre that I’d become obsessed with just a couple years later.The four stars here are an average of my 3 star take now and the 5 star take I had 35 years ago.
