Both Morse and I are poorly: me in bed getting over a cold; himself in the JR2 with a perforated ulcer. And both of us given reading to do: me the book; himself a short pamphlet from the recently widowed wife of a patient. And both of us working out how Joanna Franks was murdered back in the 1800s.This is a goodie, like solving a cryptic crossword. And the solution, coming on the heels of patient research, several lucky finds (including a carpet on sawdust in Ireland, and some height marks in a soon-to-be-demolished house in Derby), and paralleling Morse's own climb back to health, is elegant.One small point is how often Morse falls in love with women, and how often he is frustrated, or says the wrong thing, or.... At least in this one he is able to 'stay the night'...
