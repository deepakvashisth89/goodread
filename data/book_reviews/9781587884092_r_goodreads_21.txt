Penn Cage goes home, to Natchez, Mississippi, after the death of his wife to find his father is being blackmailed. Being Houston prosecutor and familiar with the law he tries to protect his father, but he then becomes wrapped up in the 30 yr old murder of a black veteran. Along with Caitlin Masters, a newspaper publisher, he gets involved with the FBI, the memory of an old girlfriend, and a judge who threatened to ruin him and his family years ago.I found it a bit hard to get into the swing of Greg Iles' writing. However once settled in, found the story and plot to be one that induced me to turn the next page.
