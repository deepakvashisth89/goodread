A classic never gets old, and I enjoy detox with this kind of book from time to time.The "Junior Classics for Young Readers" version makes it also very smooth to read, so I recommend that if like me you're not native and want to read a book without having to use the dictionary. Other than a few technical words (cutlass, coracle, ...) the book can be read aidlessly from cover to cover.Other than that specific note on the junior version of the book, not much to add to the story itself that wasn't said before. I read the full version as a child in my native language and it's always nice to revisit such an adventure.For some reason, I never realized until now that "Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde" was also written by R.L.Stevenson who in my mind was the author of only "Treasure Island". The nice time I had reading this one makes me want to read the other now :-)
