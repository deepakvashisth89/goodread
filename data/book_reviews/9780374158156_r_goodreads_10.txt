A whole lot of graphic sex scenes in this rather strange novel by Pulitzer Prize-winning author Oscar Hijuelos. On sale for my Kindle. I found the endless tales about the 14 sisters and their love lives rather boring, but not boring enough not to finish it. THe author's sentences read like my Spanish teacher tries to get me to write: long, flowery sentences, which sometimes seem endless.The parents of these 15 children seem rather implausible: a Cuban woman who never really learns to speak English and writes her memoirs but won't let her daughters read them and her Irish American photographer husband, who brings her to the U.S. They have a lot of sex, but seemingly little other communication while he gradually drinks himself to death and she mainly spends time either lonely without her adult children or having too many visitors as they come back to see her or take her on trips.There is little plot. I had trouble relating to this book.
