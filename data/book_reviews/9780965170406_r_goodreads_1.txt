A remarkable history of Scandal in American from Hollywood to all of the robber barons of industry, politics, and crime plus the horrid religious fanatics (Jim and Tammy Faye, Jimmy Swaggart, Salem Witchcraft, Too bad it was published to late to record the derelicts of our rich society these past 10 years to add another 200 to the somewhat 600 scandals to date. Rita Jenrette, The title basin bombshell, Billy James Harris, Henry Ward Beecher and more. Boy, and to compare the Nixon Scandals to today's along with Spiro Agnew and the nattering nabobs of negativism. What a way to relive my past with all of these creepy characters.Seems like many today in politics, film, and religion have just picked up where they left off. Highly recommended as a table top book to reflect on for the winter months.
