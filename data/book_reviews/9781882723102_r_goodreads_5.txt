I’ve had this book for years, but it wasn’t until I watched the movieBeyond the Blackboard,based on this book, that I finally decided to read it. If I had known it took place in Salt Lake City, I would have gotten to it sooner. Stacey Bess teaches at the homeless shelter school, the “School With No Name,” and she encounters a world completely opposite from the safe, nurturing, caring atmosphere she has provided for her own children. Her stories are completely heart-wrenching, but sometimes heart-warming, too.
