hats off to LG! she has done it again! another awesome and intense book! full of angst and tension! rafael used to think the world of georgie but circumstances got ugly and things took a turn for the worst! he was really as much a victim as georgie! it was heartbreaking the way they got separated when they were so much in love. it was such an innocent and untainted love! beautiful with the throes of first love! but steve had to come and spoil things! this is a story about a second chance at love; how two lovers found each other again among betrayal, lies and cheating. i was never happier than when they finally got their HEA as they so richly deserve:-)
