If this book had not been about the adventures of Henry and Emmy Tibbett it would be a one star (did not like it) book. Their presence brings it to two stars (it was OK).The Tibbett books set in Europe or the United States have been so good that I keep reading the ones with the Caribbean settings. This one, even more than the others, is an attempt at a drug runner thriller where it is unclear who is on the side of justice and who is not. A lot of the characters are cartoonish caricatures which made it very hard for me to suspend disbelief and just roll with the story.
