Reading a biography of Sir Arthur Conan Doyle revealed that he was perfecting the detective genre by incorporating standard medical practices. The art of medicine does rely on the investigative powers of the physician to determine the cause and the cure of the disease. In reading Louisiana Fever, one is invited to step into the world of forensic science and solve the puzzle. The characters are interesting and engender empathy for their various plights and struggles. I appreciated the factual information regarding the disease and didn't mind that there were some portions of the plot that were a bit too dramatic to be part of reality. I could suspend my reality and step into theirs with abandonment so the book was quite enjoyable.
