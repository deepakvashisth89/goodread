Stevie used to be the boy with a boat who took me to the bay. While he rowed, I baled water with a tin can. At twelve, it was the height of romance. Now, a new Stevie has my heart. He is older in years yet acts even younger. He's quiet, too. But this Stevie doesn't live in a small town with lawns and beaches. His home is above a dingy London shop. It sells pictures of women in their underwear or nothing! The shop is dark and always looks shut.

If he had a boat, even one with holes, we could go out. All he has is a compass and paper. Instead of going around a bay, he draws circles all day. Isn't that sad enough?

What happens to Stevie in this book upsets me. Mr Conrad, couldn't you write another ending? I can't sleep without thinking of your Stevie. It hurts almost as much as love.
