I’m going to leave a three star review out of respect for this books potential, but I did not enjoy reading it.

I think if I were a historian trying to focus in on more specific parts of Rome A.D., this would be a valuable resource. Unfortunately, despite having an (at the very least) basic understanding of Roman history, this account felt much more like a news report reel rather than a cohesive and comprehensive breakdown of events and people.

The timeline felt scattered and while it took a mostly chronological view, there were frequent dips into past or future events that made it hard to track at times. I think the biggest strike against it was the lack of context for the characters that centered on. It felt as though Tacitus was writing for the benefit of (at his time) present day Rome, and his account did not feel like one that was targeted at being future proofed.

Overall, it felt too specific to be a Birds Eye view of Roman history, but too vague to feel that it was providing any specific information that would make sense to a future reader who didn’t know the subjects of its narrative already.

3.0 🤩
