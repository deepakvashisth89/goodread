Bound for Oregon is an engaging account of a young girl's and her family's journey on the Oregon Trail. It's sweet and suspenseful--hard to put down. I really enjoy the perseverance in times of trials and hardships. The 'one foot in front of the other' mentality is quite thought-provoking. This book highlights good and bad times in that era and on the journey westward, and leaves the reader with a deep look into an important time in American history.*one use of a derogatory term by a man in the wagon train and several mentions of death and dead bodies of people and animals.
