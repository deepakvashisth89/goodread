I’m giving Lady of Light three and a half stars. Within the first fifty pages I almost quit reading but forced my self to finish just so I can go on to Child of Promise. Plus the story wasn’t as bad once I got further into it. The writing was fine and the characters were good. Well except for Mary Sue who just annoyed me. Had I not wanted to read Beth’s story in Child of Promise I definitely wouldn’t have finished this.I only recommend this if you’re looking to continue on with the Brides of Culdee Creek series.
