I am very interested in what Cross writes about, and what the characters think about, and you would think that a literary mystery is just my speed. But this is very, very dull. What do you call wit without humor? Archness? Academia? There’s plenty of that in here.I’d love to read this story by someone who is better at people (like Sayers), or mysteries (like Christie), or wit WITH humor (like Christianna Brand or Sarah Caudwell). I understand that she gets better at writing later. I will give the later ones a chance.
