The atmosphere felt more bittersweet and soulful & the introduction of Caspian added a layer of political intrigue that kept me much more engaged than the White Witch's reign.

I know that most people consider the first book the gold standard, but for me, Prince Caspian is where the series truly found its heart. While the first book is a charming introduction to Narnia, this one felt more grown-up with all the ache of returning to a place you love.

Caspian is a fantastic protagonist- vulnerable yet brave. And his journey to unite the "Old Narnians" against his uncle Miraz was much more compelling to me than the linear journey of the first book. Oh, and the scene between Susan and Caspian was- something!

There is a sense of history and old magic here that feels so much richer. The characters felt more seasoned as they are to fight harder for their faith in Narnia this time around. Seeing the Pevensies return as ancient kings and queens in children’s bodies is such a brilliant concept. Between the swashbuckling Reepicheep (my new favorite character!) and the thrilling and dangerous battle for Narnia’s soul, I couldn't put this down.
