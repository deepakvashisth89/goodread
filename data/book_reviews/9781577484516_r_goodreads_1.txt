It is now 2020, and we’re in our own epidemic. I read this a few years ago when I lived in the Middle East, and I would have never imagined that I would live through a pandemic. Pandemics seemed like an outdated thing that never happened anymore. Reading this book is eerie, as it’s so similar to the modern day. What this book gives is hope. The world got through a epidemic before (a much deadlier and actually scary one), and we will get through another one. The story itself is really good and it’s hard to put down! 4/5 stars.
