Pasternak, who was more remembered forDoctor Zhivago, also wrote a short autobiography regarding his flight from different humanities until he finally stayed with poetry. His autobiography starts off with his experience with music and mentorship with Scriabin which, despite his solid competence, is hindered because he himself admits that he does not possess absolute pitch. To him, absolute pitch is a must as a musician, and so he moves to Marburg and philosophy. While competent with neo-Kantianism through his mentorship with Cohen, he breaks away from philosophy after having his heart broken. Eventually, he is led to poetry at first through Rilke, which was among his ideals for a poet, then a real life friendship and kinship with the prodigious Mayakovsky congeals this passion for poetry. His admiration for Mayakovsky in the final part of the autobiography also fosters his poetry: the final part is his exordium and elegy to him, which is why Mayakovsky's suicide at the end heavily affects him.To Pasternak, art is something that happens to you as a product of reality. It is not a smooth, uneventful process, but rather an explosive sequence of pain and birthing. Reality interpolates in the creation of one's art, and he places his own evolution as an artist vis-a-vis this violent, incendiary process.
