2.5 stars/C-Too long! This book is in need of some serious cutting. Say about 100 - 200 pages. But then you have to consider that this was written in 1993. I loved the hero - not crazy about the heroine. The plot seemed to wander all over the place - needed to be tighted up - lots of holes. I think I deserve a medal for reading all 480 pages. And the ending just sort of ended abruptly like the author finally gave up the ghost but the H/h did get their story resolved.
