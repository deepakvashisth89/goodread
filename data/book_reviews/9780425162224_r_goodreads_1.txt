Good story. Good characters. But the book is terribly marred by many comma splices and other marks of careless editing--wrong verb tenses, bad punctuation, misspellings, occasional hard-to-follow dialogue. It's hard to believe this is the fourth in a series and that Wings has won a Lambda Award. I liked Emma (the detective), but I won't read the other three Emma Victor mysteries because the technical flaws are too flagrant and annoying. This is discouraging. Writing this unpolished should not win awards just because it addresses a gay/lesbian audience.
