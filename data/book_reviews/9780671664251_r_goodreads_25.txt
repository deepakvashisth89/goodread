This was my first Clegg novel, and I am very very pleased. Contrary to popular belief, there IS an art to writing good horror. King certainly has the chops for it, as does Dan Simmons and I am happy to add Clegg to my list.This is a well crafted story from begining to end, I liked the characters, even though some were deeply flawed. Good writing and scares all around.The key is enough horror without the kitchen sink feeling, and this novel delivered. Eerie, evocative and visceral in perfect measure.
