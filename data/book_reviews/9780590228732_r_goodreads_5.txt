My entire readthrough I've been eagerly awaiting this book: Abby's here! She's one of my favourite characters, and though Kristy paints her in a semi-obnoxious light, she's still tons of fun.
Though I am bummed that Anna didn't join the Club, even as an associate officer. It worked for Shannon! And I actually enjoyed the "sporty" main story, though it wasn't really about the baseball, per se. All in all, this was a solid BSC book, and I'm excited to keep reading because I haven't read this half of the series nearly as much as the first, so it still feels fresh!
