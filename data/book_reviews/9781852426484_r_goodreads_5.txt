I am a fan of Bello’s work.Having said that I have to say that this story was the darkest I’ve read from him. His writing is as always brilliant and the story is harder to follow than his other books. The choice of clues as parts of so many different types of text and the width of information given in them is staggering and difficult to compute as the subject is obscure even though the puzzle is the worlds most “familiar” game.I would suggest reading even if only to admire his complex way of writing and thinking.
