First of all, I would like to apologize to all authors of books I have rated 3 stars. Your book ismileslightyears better than this one. Ratings of a book do not just go in a line from terrible to terrific, they are in a circle. Sometimes, a book is so bad that it comes all the way around and becomes, well... entertaining (ever watched Troll 2?)The plot of this book is outrageous and its characters despicable. I spend so much of my time enjoying how ridiculous the male character was that I totally missed the twist at the end. Great fun.Definitely judge this book by its cover. In fact, the cover is so crazy-awesome that the publisher were considerate enough to put a little picture of the guy on the spine (!), just in case you had misgivings about shelving the book and missing that face.The guy is Emmett, he exudes sex appeal but is a little too impetuous and irresponsible (he is floating in mid-air with his motorcycle on the cover). His twin brother, Yale, has the same physique but is cultured and no woman in the book can resist his charms. In fact, two of them (his fiancee and mistress)hadto have him.He was everything she had always wanted in a man - daring and passionate in bed, yet urbane and conservative everywhere else.-BTW the guy always makespassionate love.The story starts with Lexi having Emmett's baby and the same night, learning that Emmet got killed. Struggling for money, she turns to Yale for help, the very one who broke her heart back when she was in high school. Thanks to this, we get awesome lines such asShe forced her eyes up, away from his clothes, to the face of the man she had grown to loathe.But it was also the face of the man she'd love, because Yale Bradigan had Emmett's sharply sculpted features...So the plot in a few words:Yale fiancee's is a crime boss daughter, his mistress is blackmailing him for selling fake art provided by crime boss. Mistress used to sleep with twin brother Emmett before sleeping with Yale - that created a riff between brothers. Emmett was blackmailing Yale for money for his kid. Yale decides to hire hitman to kill Emmett. Hitman shot wrong target in two different events.Shot Yale instead of Emmett, then mistress instead of Lexi. Emmett pretends to be Yale, gathers all the evidence and gets crime boss in jail. HEA with Emmett and Lexi.What would have made is more enjoyable? I wished the writing wasworse. There is a little bit in the middle when the authors actually try to sell a relationship - that part was boring.And well, it was refreshing to have a too stupid to love male character (Yale). And it is interesting that the only thing Fabio seems to get right is to ask consent before sex.
