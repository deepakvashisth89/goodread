The heroine is an heiress who doesn't know she's an heiress; her fiance is a snake who's in it for the money (while sleeping with her best friend, which I refuse to spoiler-tag because if you've ever read a Harlequin Presents novel you know this); the hero fell in love with her at first sight and is determined to save her from herself... with seduction! The heroine is spunky and in many ways as dumb as a pile of rocks but the hero is unbothered by this because the sex is good and he wants to run her life for her anyway; I feel like these two crazy kids will make it.
