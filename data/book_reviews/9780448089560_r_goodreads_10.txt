I know this book isn't for me in either time period or my age when reading it, but even giving it allowances for that, it comes off feeling like a parody of an old Scooby Doo episode where young folk stumble into some insane scheme that would never have worked in reality but somehow the villain ends up cursing *those meddling kids* at the end when they are foiled.There was a tone or atmosphere in the book where things just are as they appear and the author is just telling you plainly everything. It reminds me of other old books of my parents generation that I read growing up, and while it is dated I guess it holds some nostalgia points for me.
