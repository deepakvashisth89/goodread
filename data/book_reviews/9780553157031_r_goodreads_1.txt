I had collected and read almost every single Saddle Club book out, up to when I was 18 or so, even though I was getting a bit old for them. I started reading them at the public library in Amherst when I was 12-ish, and never could believe that such a thing had existed, and I hadn't known about it.I was so very Stevie, but wished I was Carole with her 'own' horse. I rode at Bellamy Farms, my real-life Pine Hollow, and dreamed every day of living the dream.Dreams sometimes become reality, but they are never as picture perfect as in the storybooks, but these books will remain in my memory as the hopes of one little girl, and a time-capsule of memories.Good times, good times.
