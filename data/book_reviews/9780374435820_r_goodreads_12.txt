The Incredible Painting of Felix Clousseau by Jon Agee is a book all about a famous painter, Felix Clousseau who becomes famous because his artwork comes to life. There was a Grant Contest of Art held in Paris at the Royal Palace. Well known artists, Gaston du Stroganoff, Felicien CaffayOllay, and Alphonse LeCamembair, all came to this art contest, showing off their greatest paintings. When Clousseau displayed his painting of a duck, everyone laughed. However, when the painting “quacked”, all of the judges turned their heads because they were stunned! Clousseau was then awarded the Grand Prize. However, his paintings started to get out of hand everywhere in the town, causing trouble. Clousseau was thrown into jail. However, when a thief broke into the castle one night, one of Clousseau’s paintings of a dog grabbing the thief by the shirt, and the painting saved the day. Cliusseau was released from jail, and he was even awarded the Medal of Honor. Clousseau returned home, back to his painting.The Incredible Painting of Felix Clousseau has great illustrations throughout the book. Specifically, all of Clousseau’s paintings come to life, and the illustrations show the animals going from paintings, to more realistic. In addition, some of the paintings are of nature, and when they “come alive”, the illustrations are beautiful, flowing through the page. The cover of the book is of the town from a bird eye point of view, showing all of the neighbors waving at Clousseau. The front cover looks almost like a painting, which I think is great at foreshadowing the art competition in the beginning of the story. The back cover is of rooftops of the houses with cats and birds. I love the simplicity of the back cover, since the illustrations are complicated throughout. The first page in the book shows a worker hanging up a sign for the art competition, which I love how this illustration sets the scene for the book. Overall, I highly recommend this book for ages 4-8 years, and this book is a great read aloud for the classroom.
