Patricia Wentworth (Dora Amy Elles) was a prolific mystery writer in the same era as Agatha Christie. This series began in 1928. Christie had Miss Marple and Wentworth had Maude Silver as the heroine, solving complicated crimes. She wrote many other mystery books besides the Miss Silver series.Maude Silver was a former governess who was as devoted to her former charges as they were to her. Often she helped her friend Inspector Frank Abbott of Scotland Yard to solve murders. Miss Silver was his former governess and her wide circle of friends was very large. She was a major influence in the lives of many children and their families which gave her many connections she used in unraveling the whodunit mysteries she came to solve.In this particular story, The Summer House (The Gazebo), Althea Graham's mother has claimed her health will be destroyed if her daughter marries her longtime love, Nicholas Carey. He has gone away for 5 years but returns to claim Althea again and they plan to marry this time but make plans for her mother to have a companion to care for her.Miss Silver and Althea met at a cocktail party of a common friend and became a close confidant and protector. Miss Silver is a brilliant crime solver and though her appearance may be modest and unpretentious, she is quite a strong character to be admired.There is at least one murder to be solved and a dastardly villain to outwit and expose while also protecting an innocent person who falsely stands accused. This makes for a satisfying happy ending.Miss Silver sees twists and turns that the police seem to miss at first inspection. If you read this series you will come to love her, as I do.
