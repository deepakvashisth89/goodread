A beautiful story about childhood.Through the voice of a five-year-old girl, I traveled to Morocco of the 1970's. I saw beauty, and friendliness, but also cruelty, and brutality of that world (and people).In this (untypical) background (and with a hippie mother) a kid was still a kid. It was obvious in what way Lucia and Bea profited from such childhood. Yet, there were also (sadly) failures of such growing up.The child's point of view isn't easy to narrate. For a time, I found a bit annoying this kind of wandering, erratic narration. But at some point, I got, that it was as truthful as possible (while writing by an adult). I appreciate the reality of a child's voice.I don't agree with many choices of (adult) characters, but I value this story.[4.5 stars]
