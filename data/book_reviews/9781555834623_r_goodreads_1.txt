Theo, a 40-something gay man who is a very successful plastic surgeon, has to deal with his lover leaving, his biological child being dumped on him for a week (he helped a friend get pregnant, but wanted no parenting responsibilities), and a jerk from his past showing up as a competitor to the chief of staff position at his hospital. There's lots of domestic drama with family and friends, and much of it should be interesting. But Theo the character is bland and unappealing, and I've seen the "learn to become an instant parent" plot done so much better in other books and movies. Plus, I have to admit, I wasn't too enraptured by the writing style.
