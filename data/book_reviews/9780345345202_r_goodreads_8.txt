I tried ten times to get into this, but it just wouldn't work. Rendell wanted to give the investigating team some personality by adding weird personal ambitions, arrogance and romantic lust. But just as I couldn't get interested in the murdered people and the suspects, the plot line of the policemen was even more boring.To be honest, I couldn't finish the book, so this review isn't representative. The only thing I can say is that I often read pages automatically, only to find out after after a few minutes that I had no idea what I had been reading. It just didn't register. This sometimes happens to me when I am drunk or preoccupied with personal problems. In the case ofWolf to the slaughter, I was neither.
