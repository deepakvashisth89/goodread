Dortmunder humor in small doses (3 stars)

I enjoyed the first Dortmunder novel, “The Hot Rock”, quite a bit, but future books in the series proved disappointing, often having a slow start, and afflicted with too many instances of profanity or innuendo for my liking. While this collection of around a dozen short stories still includes some of the shortcomings with language, at least the brevity of the individual stories means there’s no painfully slow buildup, and all the stories get quickly into the action and humour.

All the stories revolve around the misadventures of the unfortunate crook Dortmunder, who despite his skills in thievery invariably runs into some kind of bad luck or unexpected surprise to thwart his best efforts. This formula seems to work even better in short story form than in a full-length novel, and most of the tales here are quite enjoyable.

Like most readers, for me the favourite was “Too Many Crooks”, a hilarious story where our thief ends up mixed up in a simultaneous bank robbery attempt being made by another group of thieves, and becomes an accidental hero. Dortmunder often has to think quickly on his feet, and his innovative and creative thinking when faced with disaster is one of his more endearing qualities. Many stories feature a pleasant plot twist, or entertain us with our luckless thief in the most unlikely of situations. Given my disappointment with most of the Dortmunder novels, this short story collection was a welcome surprise.
