Beautiful, emotional, and charming! The Parent Trap or Lisa and Lottie is one of a kind. The writing is so powerful that it really moved me. It is one of the few children's classic that has made me so emotional. I admire Kästner for achieving that feat.

Two estranged identical twins meet for the first time in a summer camp. Imagine their initial surprise and the state of their feelings when they learn the truth about themselves! They may be only nine years old. But they know what they want; they want their family united once again.

Narrated from the children's perspective, the story addresses the issue of the effects on children when their parents go their separate ways. As a lawyer, divorce and custody have always been the most painful legal proceedings for me because of the emotional strain that it puts me through. My colleagues have teased me saying that I'm oversensitive. But I never could stop feeling keenly the children's suffering because of the actions of the adults. Of course, there are justifiable circumstances for divorce and custody, but more times than not people take the easy way without trying to work out their differences. These decisions affect children more than parents realize. Consequently, the children suffer emotionally. I don't think any reader reading this book can resist loving their sweet innocence. The story was entertaining. It made me both laugh and cry. Undoubtedly, it is one of the best children's classics I have read.
