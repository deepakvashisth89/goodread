3.5 stars rounded down*In this story, Corrie starts writing for the newspaper, there is a town election where Mrs. Parrish runs against a man, and then Corrie covers the presidential election with the new Republican party and the long standing Democrats.This series does a lot of what is called moralizing, something that always brings the enjoyment level down for me. I want to be shown, not told.Corrie really grew on me in the last part of this book. It's like she grew up right before our eyes and that was enjoyable to read about. I am looking forward to reading the next book in the series.
