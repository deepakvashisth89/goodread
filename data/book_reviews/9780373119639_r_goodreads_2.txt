Read this one for all the evil side characters – the romance is fairly pedestrian. Jilted h goes on vacation in Italy and head-of-a-wealthy-banking-family hero falls her at first sight, even though he thinks she is as free and easy as her companions. Their conflict is the hero thinks she’s still in love with her former fiancé and she thinks he’s just using her until he marries his stepmother’s niece.It all works out don’t worry.What is more interesting is the roll call of all the characters committing bad behavior in this story:1.Nephew of housekeeper rents out the hero’s villa to a group of rowdy British tourists.2.Heroine is part of the group and is belittled by them for her caution - and they leave her behind when they’re thrown out of the villa.3.There’s a rapey guy who keeps trying to pick the heroine up – first trying to rip her dress off at the party and later when she is out with the hero.4.The hero’s stepsister is useless and doesn’t protect her children from her toxic mother.5.Hero’s stepmother is evil – she ignores or belittles the granddaughter and spoils the grandson. Tries to set the heroine up as a thief, won’t give the hero the family ring, etc . .6.Hero’s stepmother’s niece brings the heroine’s ex to the villa (small world) to make the hero jealous. She is garden variety OW – promiscuous and bitchy and ordering the heroine around.7.Heroine’s ex belittles her as frigid, won’t loan her money to go back home unless she has sex with him.8. The grandson tries to get his sister in trouble so she’ll have to go to boarding school9. The granddaughter knows she is unloved and lashes out at her grandmother and tries to steal her ring, etc. . .10. So by the time I get to the hero – his slut-shaming for the heroine looking hot in her dress (that he rips) looks quaint.If you enjoy OTT antics, you’ll enjoy this one. Heroine is ridiculously paralyzed by her love for the hero. I’m assuming that will continue to be her coping strategy once she marries into this crazy family. The granddaughter would make a great next generation heroine.Boogenhagen has all the details in her excellent review.
