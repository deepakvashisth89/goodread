This is in my Top 3 favorite books of all time.I'd put something by Nassim Taleb on that list (he recommended this one). Yuval Harari's Sapiens and Bryan Magee.It is (as the sub-title says) a tour through Western Philosophy but it is also deeply personal and perhaps most importantly, readable.I don't know of another book that will teach you as much about the philosophical underpinnings of Wesstern society and which will at the same time be as thoroughly enjoyable a read.
