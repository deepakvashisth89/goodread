Wade met Pascal in remote China, and they hatched a plan to travel to a remote valley to meet and document a minority people (the Drung) that Westerners has not seen for over a century. Brackenbury is a good storyteller, and this was a quick read. I enjoyed reading about the people and beautiful landscapes of these remote areas of Tibet. It wasn't quite clear to me why Wade wanted to meet the Drung other than to be the first to do so. While traveling, they depended on the kindness and hospitality of people with very few resources and put themselves and their benefactors in danger. Nonetheless, it was quite the tale of adventure, and I appreciated the stick-it-to-the-man attitude of defying authority to travel in an unauthorized area.
