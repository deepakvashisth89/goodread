"Another great read in the Grijpstra and de Gier series, this one without Grijpstra however. The Commisaris goes to Maine to help his newly-widowed sister. De Gier goes along (stealthily) to help, and meets the new Sheriff. Things proceed from there in the usual manner. A drawn-out series of killings is unraveled and his sister gets a good price for her house so she can move back to Amsterdam. The story unfolds with the usual mix of Dutch pragmatism and dreamy Buddhist imagery. Wonderful. "
