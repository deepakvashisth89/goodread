A 1994 category romance that involves a Navy SEAL about to be drummed out of the service due to a injury that could eventually take his life. He pays a visit secretly to the town where his ex-wife and daughters now live. He finds that the ex is now a widow with an additional child and is in serious trouble with an entire town wanting her head on a platter.

In order to help he reveals himself to his ex-wife but request that his daughters never know he is their father. Author Paula Detmer Riggs is known for her ability to write stories with riveting angst and this one is no exception. It is a tear-jerker and full of emotional what-ifs. I read it in one-sitting because it is an super outstanding must read.
