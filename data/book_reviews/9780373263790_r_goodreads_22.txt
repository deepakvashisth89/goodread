Absolutely loved this murder mystery, it also had a missing person mixed into the fray. The story is well written and beautifully paced.
The characters are marvelous and keep the story moving and the reader guessing as to the "who done it". Along with the characters the setting and atmosphere are well detailed without being over done, just enough to give the reader a real sense of them.
Dorothy Simpson (the author) is no longer with us, having passed away in 2020. She left us with 15 books in this series (Inspector Thanet), this one being the last one.
For me hopefully finding the other 14 won't be too difficult....
