This is the first in the Kovac/Liska series. I read the third in this series some time ago and am reading the books out of sequence.I enjoyed the story, at 570 pages it's by no means a short novel but for me it was a page turner. With plenty of twists and turns I was pretty certain I knew who the Cremator was but I was wrong.I found the novel well written and felt that the characters were very believable. With a good mix between the narrative and the dialogue driven parts.I'm looking forward to reading the next in this series before long.
