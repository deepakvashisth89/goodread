I accidentally stumbled across these when I was about Alba's age, and I remember being fascinated by the cool, stylish world of the books. Alba was so relatable to me. A wildly inappropriately older suave silver fox was #lifegoals, and her insecurities and combination of precociousness and cluelessness are exactly what it's like to be that age. Ditto the raging hormones. It was so obvious it was written by a man with one hand in his pants, but it was also weirdly nuanced and real.

I have not reread the series as an adult. At 40-something, I suspect their relationship would just feel pathetic.

If you love a stylish thriller and you can stomach 70s cultural values, give it a read. Just realize that it's full of land mines. The constant lusting over the 13-year-old is but the tip of the iceberg.
