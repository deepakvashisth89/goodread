Reread on 2/25/25I originally gave this book 3 stars but I'm bumping my rating up to 4 this time. In some ways I like this book better then the others in the series. I think the writing improved and the characters felt like they had more depth to them. Tasslehoff is definitely the best character. I love him in this! There were some very poignant moments in this book that had me crying all over again. There are also some things that frustrated me about the story.Tanis dies in the most anticlimactic way. He should have died in a bigger way than being hit from behind.Raistlin is too easily forgiven by Caramon.Steel Brightblade was a fool for far too long in this book. Even after he saw his god abandon him he went back and forth with which side he should be on. Also, it wasn't really explained why he finally took the gem off and how that stopped both voices in his head. It seemed like it was a good thing for him to have it and that Takhisis wanted him to get rid of it so he would completely join the darkside, but then when he takes it off it seems like something that was hindering him. It was confusing.Also, I thought Kitiara was doomed to be with Lord Soth after she died? Why wasn't she? Did I miss something? It bothers me that Kitiara is still somehow ok even after death. She's an evil woman that should be living her afterlife in torment.There was one interesting thing that I discovered on this reread. At one point in the book Tasslehoff finds something with the name Haplo written on it. Haplo is a character in the Deathgate Cycle. This got me thinking...when Fizban / Paladine says he has to leave Kryn and he's going to create another world was he going to create the world in the Deathgate Cycle?
