I listened to a recording of the book being read as I traveled to Denver daily to teach. Boy, it took a while getting to the root and plot of the story, but I guess the plot wasn't really the point. Hawthorne has such a beautiful way with words. It was the descriptions of the people and places, the discussion of human nature and just the beauty of the writing that was most enjoyable. Without giving away any plot points, it was a lot of fun to be in on the "secret" of what was happening and then have what was going on drawn out for such a long time. Hawthorne's vigil with Judge Pyncheon through the day and the night was almost to the point of being both laugh-out-loud funny and pretty creepy and macabre!! Definitely worth the work through the entire novel to see the joy of the family at the end!
