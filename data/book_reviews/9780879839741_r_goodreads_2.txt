Although this book is geared toward those at risk for developing heart disease (or who have already suffered a heart attack), it is also a fitting guide for healthy emotional living in general. Cardiologist Sinatra touches on nutrition and exercise, but his main guidelines deal with the emotions, relationships, and the mind-body techniques that so many patients overlook. This book has a 1996 copyright; despite that, most of the information and techniques provided are equally useful today.
