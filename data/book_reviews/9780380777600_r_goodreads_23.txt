Ah, the joy of used bookstore finds. The Silent Strength of Stones is a wonderland of a book, one that manages to describe its rural setting, its mundane characters, and its magical inventions with the same delightful wit and curiosity. This is a grounded fantasy, not a story about escape to another world but about finding one's place in this one. And while it was a comforting, cozy read, Hoffman has the dramatic chops to unsettle with one unexpected sting after another in many tense and memorable scenes. How much damage can be done to one person purely with a magical power of suggestion? Quite a bit, as it turns out.
Easily one of my favorite reads of this year.
