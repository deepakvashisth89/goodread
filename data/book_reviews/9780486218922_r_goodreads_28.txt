I’ve realized I’m behind on my “short reads” (at least according to my reading plan, I am) and so I’m continuing my journey alongside Frank Baum’s and Lewis Carrol creations.While I’m generally not a big fan of short stories, this read pretty well. It felt like a book of fairy tales for children set in a Fairyland of Mo. While they’re all short stories, there are themes that hold them together, so it does feel like we’re in the same kingdom.The stories themselves are total nonsense. But it was so absurd that I wanted to keep on reading because I had no idea what was to happen next. Also…stories were pretty violent, a whole lot more violent than his Oz books. Since things are taking place in a fairyland no one can die, per se, but still when a person gets their two arms chopped off, and then both legs and then their head…that certainly makes for a violent fairy tale. And that’s just one example…Overall, while it is certainly not Oz, it was an amusing light read nonetheless, if you like Baum and Fairytales, naturally.Roman
