I could say it was a fun read to travel you into a different world. I planned to read the whole series because David Eddings is one of the most important fantasy novel writers, at least that was what I heard.
Although, I got curious while reading it and found it enjoyable, there were way too many things to ignore for me to keep reading the last few pages and following books. I read a lot of discussions online to see if I was the only one who sees certain problems, and I was not alone.
I believe fantasy novels are important assets of literature and they need to be taken seriously. But I think books like these make it a bit difficult. I have a huge respect to these writers and their creativity, also the hardwork they put in to create a new world. But David Eddings had the goods and the bads. There were no grey or more bluntly they were not "characters", they were "types", and that is okay. It was just way too shallow for me. I am okay with a easy-read fantasy, but this was way too predictable and the characters did not feel like they could be real, honestly.
And the main thing that made me leave the book, series, and probably the writer, was how he created the races and characters based on vague stereotypes. It was way too much for me, and I could not stop thinking that this whole world was based on a one-sided and partially racist point of view. I will not go much in depth, but this needed to be said.
I am sure lots of people would disagree with me, and will have different arguments for it. I am not saying that the writer was racist or anything. It is just that his built world was based on some racist stereotypes.
Also, I am not saying that one can not use reality in these books, but it was a bit disappointing to see that most of that world had that much one- sided and one-dimensional ideas in it. You don't need to be from the referenced communities to feel uncomfortable.
Those were my humble opinions. I am anly sharing this so the ones have the similar thoughts while or after reading it would not feel like they are alone.
I want to clarify that my 2 stars was not only because of these reasons, it is a combination of: wording, world and character building, fluency, and the lack of strong messages throughout the book.
