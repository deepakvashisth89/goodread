This book feels like a subreddit forum on legal humor.First published in 1992, most of the material in it is much older. And, while it is aging pretty well, some of the elements went over my head and thus missed the mark. There's also some casual racism and plenty of sexism, (think "mad Men").On the whole, it is an easy and quick read. If you're into [supposedly] real-life stories then this should be satisfying.
