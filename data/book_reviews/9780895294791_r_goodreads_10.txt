On the back of this book is a warning. I quote:"Warning: This book may change the way you look at doctors, the great American diets, and health forever."Well, it didn't.Let my preface by saying that I went into this book with the concreted beliefs that doctors are quacks, only good for mending bones and what not, that the food Americans consume is among some of the worst in the world, and that health is the responsibility of the individual. So basically, all the same things Mr. Benedict says in his book.I could've read this book in a day if I wanted, but alas, I hadn't the time, so it took me about a week. But in this past week, I've been doing me some thinking. I've been looking, albeit not super hard, at a better way of eating, of a better was of fueling my body (I say as I sit here about to go get a cup of sweet tea,) and this diet sounds pretty darn good to me.Ten days, thats all Mr. Benedict asks of us. Ten days to potentially change your life. And you know what? I think I'm gonna go for it. I'm going to do a little more research, find the things I need, learn the things I need, but like Mr. Benedict tells us, it takes time, it takes commitment, and it takes an awful lot of hard work. But I've always wanted to be the healthiest I can be, to be the best version of myself, both physically, mentally, emotionally, spiritually, and if this is the first step, then let it be a leap.If you do research on the macrobiotic diet, you'll read a lot of things saying, "Its not scientifically backed," "doctors don't approve of it," "it might make it harder to eat at family events." And, well, my reaction if of course it isn't scientifically backed, and doctors don't approve of it! Because doctors specialize in treating symptoms, not the cause, they specialize in getting you to come back to them, not in getting you well. The drug industry is just a cash grab used to keep the American people hooked on drugs that don't do anything but give you more problems that they put you on more drugs for. And of course they'd tug at your heartstrings and tell you to think of your family. Thats exactly what I'm thinking of. Just think of it. Maybe, if you better yourself first, you can better others. And maybe, just maybe, America can become, instead of the unhealthiest and most obese country in the world, it can become the healthiest, and the longest lived. We can't be the greatest country if we're dying in droves.
