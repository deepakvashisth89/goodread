Set in a small town with a host of credible characters this book focuses on Lyon, his friend Justin, & Justin's sister Sylvie. These three, while out frogging, come across something very unexpected near the pond. What they do with it causes a host of change for them and their community members. It's a wonderful story of good intentions, self-fixation, parental challenges, honesty & more.I'd recommend it to a grade 5-6 & up with a heads up about lanaguage/idioms etc.
