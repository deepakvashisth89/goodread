A linked collection of short stories set in a small Kentucky town. The format works so very well here; brief mentions of characters in one story gain deeper meanings once those characters have their turn in the spotlight, and by the end, the entire project thrums with a level of subtle emotional intensity that'd've been hard to develop and maintain in a novel.Wilkinson writes with unshowy ease and empathy about the intimate, small-time dramas that shape people's lives, and while these stories often feel more like inconclusive vignettes at time, they're all very compelling and add up to something more than the sum of their parts. I'm really eager to read Wilkinson's other collection and her novel now.
