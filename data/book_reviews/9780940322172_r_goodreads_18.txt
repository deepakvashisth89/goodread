I am really not sure if I have read this one before or not! I have read the third volume in the trilogy,The Horse’s Mouth, about a dozen times, and I suspect I readHerself SurprisedandTo Be A Pilgrimin the pre-comprehensive exam summer of 1995. It is wonderfully interesting to see the Sara Monday/Gulley Jimson relationship from Sara’s perspective. Cary’s humour is a continuing mix of the hilarious and the poignant. As a first person narrator, Sara Monday is unreliable to say the least and yet somehow lovable. This is one I’ll probably read again, if I’m lucky.
