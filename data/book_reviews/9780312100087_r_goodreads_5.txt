Due to understandable cultural and linguistic challenges, the author’s story is likely told with the help of other writers. There’s flowery language and a high sounding, idealistic tone at times. Yet, that doesn’t diminish the heart of his experience nor it’s import. It’s a compelling story in need of hearing and remembrance clearly identifying the evils of communism acted out against human freedom and relationship. According to polls, many today don’t understand the dangers. In a personal, relatable way, he gives the world a concrete example of the harms done and the hope that can be found in freedom and love - even amidst imperfection. The stories of his own escape from Cuba as well as his return to rescue and liberate his wife and children are exciting and hard to put down. I highly recommend this book. (For full transparency: While a police officer, I met the author prior to the rescue of his family. Some suspected foreign agents had reportedly tried to enter his apartment but left the scene when he and an associate arrived. The apartment building in Alexandria, Virginia, where this occurred, is mentioned in the book but the incident is not.)
