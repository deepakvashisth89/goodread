A collection of five stories from the 50s and early 60s, featuring Father John Carmody, criminal-turned-monk- and- then- priest. It's by Philip J. Farmer, who has long been one of my favorite science fiction writers. Not the best of his work--I didn't care that much for Carmody but a couple of the stories were intriguing--so I give it 3.5 stars, rounded up to 4.
