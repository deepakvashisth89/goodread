All kinds of wonderful.stories here. While the stories can be fabulous in nature, the fabulous news is toned down. The stories rather than the emotions or the wonder shine through.Some stories show what what happening among Americans at the time. Houses had attics. Children were given time and space. Curiosity educated. As much as middle-class children had certain freedoms, so did wealthy women wanting titles too have the freedom to buy coronets. Here we have one terrible and good story about such buying of coronets.However much things have changed, some things have remained the same. Wealthy easily received will too often easily go. A lesson those who want to win the lottery are warned about all the time.Well-crafted stories by a master writer.
