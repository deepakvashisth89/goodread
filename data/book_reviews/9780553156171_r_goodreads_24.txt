This booklet consists of six short stories — or, perhaps it would be more accurate to call them tiny stories (not even "little stories", as the title of the booklet says). Alas, all the stories are very boring, and they don't even have any point to them. For this reason, I wouldn't recommend this booklet to anybody, not even children. Children should be given entertaining and fun stories to read, so that they would learn to enjoy reading and to love books. Boring, pointless stories like the ones in this booklet too easily tend to make children think that reading is boring, and turns them away from books and reading, and that is something that would be very unfortunate.The only funny thing in all the six stories in this book is the names of three imps mentioned in one story: Olite, Udent and Ertinent, i.e. imp Olite, imp Udent and imp Ertinent. So, if in a booklet of six stories the only funny and slightly entertaining thing is three puns, I just don't see any incentive to read such an uninteresting and pointless booklet. One's time would be much better spent by reading something that's worth reading, whether one is a child or an adult.
