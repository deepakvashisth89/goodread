My first ever James Herbert read. This was good but not great this just didn’t capture me as much as I hoped it would – the descriptive qualities of this book where amazing especially the description of the storm it really made me feel like I was there.I won’t lie I did find myself skimming pages and a little bored and it didn’t help that I had high hopes for James Herbert as people keep telling me to read his work – maybe I picked the wrong one? If anyone does want to suggest one of his books PLEASE do.
