This book exists in two printings: the original printing was of five Campion short stories followed by five standalone stories; the later printing replaced the latter with five more Campion stories. Managing to track down both I read both second halves.

The standalone short story is a curious thing in detective writing: it can be a hard sell to put down a favourite character like a Campion or Poirot and give your attention to characters you’ve never met and are only going to spend 20 minutes with - hence the replacement, probably - and the standalone stories weren’t all crime stories, or where they were, were just as much about love or marriage (categorically not the same thing in Allingham’s world 😉), and specifically the psychological condition of women in conjugal arrangements - somewhat of a favourite subject for her. Still, I love her writing, so I enjoyed them all.
