I’m not sure if my copy of Georgie and the Robbers came from my childhood bookshelf or my step-mother’s classroom library. But, wherever its origin, I’m glad I got to revisit this sweet story starring a small, shy ghost and its animal friends. The story’s moral of working together to accomplish a task is subtle while the story itself is 100% fun. During the Halloween season, this is a perfect “scary read” for the younger readers: it features a ghost without much, if any, “scare.”
