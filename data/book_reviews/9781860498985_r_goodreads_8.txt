I thought this was a good story. I couldn't get over just how selfish the daughters were the whole way through the novel. All they cared about was how much money they would each get when their father died and when he wanted to remarry they tried every thing they could to prevent it. Including trying to make a judge think that their father was no longer capable of making his own decison. It was sort of sickening really. They weren't happy for him or anything they would have rather seen him live out his last days in a small cell with padded walls then see him be happy. All so they would get a straight 25% of his inheritance instead of spliting half between the four of them.The only thing that I would say was weak about the story or that I didn't enjoy was the ending. They let the sisters off everything. Sure they had evidence and shit to condemn them if they ever tried to do anything again but still they just let them walk away! After all that they did and were trying to do. We are not talking about stealing shit from the house were talking trying to commit murder for personal gain.
