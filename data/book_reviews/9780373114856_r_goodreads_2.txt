Re Irresistible Enemy - Lilian Peake is making an increasingly rare HP appearance. This time with an h from the wrong side of town who gets involved with the local Lord of the Manor H.Warning: The tart shaming in this is massive and LP lets the old skool shine thru. While the H draws the line at rape, LP had to ramp up the almost lurve mojo moments to meet editorial requirements and so there is lot of nasty implications about the h in this one that will probably put non-vintage fans off as they are never corrected and the h just has to get over it.The basic premise of this one is that the h and her father are running a little local village shop. The h's supposed BFF is the local daughter of the manor and the girl was a frequent childhood visitor to the h's happy and loving household but it was made clear to the h that she wouldn't be welcome in the girl's own much more luxurious surroundings.The H in this one is the upper class BFF's brother, he has wanted to slum it for a long time as the h is beautiful and according to him and his friends a bit on the tarty side. The h is pretty happy with her life but she and her father are poor, their little store doesn't pay much and making ends meet is difficult. The H runs a big supermarket conglomerate and via a series of events, the h winds up working for him as his housekeeper and running a tiny little section of a mini store in the H's bigger one.This starts with the h being invited to her wealthy BFF's engagement party. The BFF tells her to bring a date and the h has two local auto mechanic brother childhood friends that she goes out with - in a very platonic way.However the BFF AND the H AND all their upper class friends instantly assume that the h is sleeping with both of them and that she can be had for whatever offers they care to make. They loudly and clearly state this IN FRONT of the H and all the guests at the engagement party and all the H does is refuse to let the h stop dancing with him, force a kiss on her and then have a wobbly later that the h tried to leave him on the dance floor and go home. The h's wealthy BFF has nothing to say about it, she is busy getting toasted.So the tone is quickly established that the H is going to treat the h like a cheap tart and that is fine, but if the h complains or tries to avoid that behavior, then she is a conniving and evil woman and even more of a tart.Throw in h half seductions and roofie kisses, plus the h harboring an unwilling attraction for the H when he molests her as she does her job as his housekeeper, then show the H going off with his upper class girlfriend when the h protests his gropey treatment and we have ourselves the typical old skool LP story with more graphic lurve club attempts and more frequent tart shaming.Eventually the H's rich male BFF, who wants the h, but she hates him and turns him down, manages to get the h's family store condemned for health violations and the h is forced into servitude with the H because she resisted his offer to buy the father's store out and of course now H live in servitude is the only job in town.There is the usual LP back and forth bickering and semi seductions and H girlfriend drama with some h OM childhood friend drama thrown in. The tart accusations get thicker and louder every time the H almost scores but misses.Eventually we have the dramatic climatic moment where the h is acting as the maid for a big garden fete of the H's and the marquee that the H hired from his sewer swiller male BFF collapses, trapping the h. The inevitable H rescue of the h leads to the big explosion of very purple LP passion and then the H goes off to the OW and to hire a new housekeeper.The h, instead of finding a solicitor and starting a harassment and bodily injury suit against the H and his BFF, merely mopes about and sulks until the H returns, accuses her of more sexual shenanigans and then declares true lurve for the next week at least, as they throw themselves passionately at each other for the HEA with extra tower of passions climbs.Overall this is an extremely typical LP day at the HP office, with more detailed lurve scenes. But it should be read concurrently with either the other August 1992 offerings of Miranda Lee'sThe Reluctant Loveror Susan Napier'sDevil to Payfor a really good comparison of the old skool HP vs the newer and more modern HP take on things.Lilian Peake had been writing for HQN for twenty years at this point, and her writing is always highly dramatic and angsty, but LP also has those iron clad rules of good girl h's in HPlandia firmly embedded in her writing output.When HPLandia demands more detailed passion, LP does comply and she writes a decent love scene. However it seems in LP's writer mind, heroine's that go that far before getting the ring on their finger are to be shamed and doubted and her writing really hammers that point home. LP will write to comply with editorial requests, but she obviously doesn't like doing it and punishes her h severely for it by having every single character in the H's world condemn the h for being a slut on NO evidence whatsoever, all the while their own behavior is arguably worse. Sex for the h is shameful if the h isn't married, in Lil's HPLandia.Contrast that attitude to every other offering in the line up for August 1992 and you will be shocked at the entirely different tone the other books take towards sex. The H's in every other book this month, besides the Karen van der Zee which has an entirely different trope, are enthusiastic and encouraging and extremely supportive about the h's exploring their own sexuality and desire. Every single H in this month's line up goes out of his way to be nurturing and supportive of the h's budding lurve mojo and explain that there is nothing wrong with getting your groove on in a healthy relationship and the contrast is startling and kinda illuminating.HPlandia is moving forward again and August 1992's offering of books shows that in rather extraordinary ways.
