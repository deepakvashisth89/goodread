Fascinating book about an incredibly talented President whose deep personal and political flaws left so much more to be desired from his tenure.“His immense talents invited higher standards and expectations than were needed at so placid a moment in history; he had the misfortune to serve at a time when greatness wasn’t required. There was always the sneaking suspicion that Clinton was a bit bored, that he needed the thrill of a crisis—and that if the world didn’t present him with a challenge, he’d create one for himself”Only criticism is that I wish this book hadn’t been released so early (only about a year after Clinton left office). Being so close in time to its subject matter leaves it lacking the broader context and analysis a book released later could have provided.
