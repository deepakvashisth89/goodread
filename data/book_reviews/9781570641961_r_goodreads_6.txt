I have a few of these Wishbones laying around instead of the actual classics and so with part of my goals being to move books out this may be a good start.The Prince and the Poochis based off the episode of the same name so readers get the chance to read the unfolding of Joe, Wishbone's boy, learning the same lesson as Prince Edward and the pauper Tom Canty although in a modern background. As a result the story switches between the two main Twain characters as well as the modern events all while Wishbone narrates or offers commentary throughout.The book ends with a note on both Mark Twain and the original story for those who are interested to learn more as this is most definitely an abridged version.Since this is an adaptation of a TV series there isn't much character development even between the main characters. As a result even though Wishbone does keep his tone, the story does feel slow and repetitive.And honestly if you get a chance it's just better to watch the series than read it.
