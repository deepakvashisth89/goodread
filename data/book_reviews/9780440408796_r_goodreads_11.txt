*read for school- wit and wisdom grade 5 module 1*Tragically eye-opening. Experiencing the removal of the Nez Perce for their lands through the eyes of the chief’s daughter is powerful. This story is filled with violence, death, and, at times, uncomfortable topics. However, it is the perfect book to show the other side of a historical event. Will be a challenging book for my young readers, but with start tough discussions no doubt!
