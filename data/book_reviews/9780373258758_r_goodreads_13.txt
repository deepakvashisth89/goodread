Simply simple is book one in the re-written Simply series by Carly Phillips which has been updated and released.

Kayla takes over the “Charmed” etiquette school/business her late aunt and uncle were running before their untimely death. When Kane, an undercover cop shows up for classes, sparks fly between them ! Kane is investigating a prostitution ring that the police believe was being run from the business… Upon further investigation Kane believes that Kayla has no idea about it but is now in danger and he is determined to keep her safe and discover who is behind the ring…

Mystery, suspense, chemistry, laughter, kidnapping, a quick read and looking forward to the other books in this series!
