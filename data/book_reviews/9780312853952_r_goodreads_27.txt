I'm torn on what to rate this. I was told by many internet people to stop reading after Speaker for the Dead, and I almost did because I loved that book so much on its own.

Buuuuut I didn't want to leave the world. And if all you want to do is stay in the world, then the last two books in the quartet are good (though this is the weakest, I think).

But if you're more interested in a satisfying conclusion, then you should know the writing and storytelling does get progressively weaker after Speaker—never bad, but weaker. Stopping with Speaker is what I would advise if I was recommending the series to friends, but I'm glad I continued, and the flaws of the last two books don't ruin my experience of Speaker.

This is a pretty negative review so far, but I actually did enjoy Children of the Mind! I loved spending more time with the characters, and apparently I can't get through any book in this series without crying, so if that's your jam, then godspeed.
