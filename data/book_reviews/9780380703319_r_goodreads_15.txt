Alisa Craig a.k.a. Charlotte MacLeod does it again. She is the queen of the quirky mystery. I have yet to read another author capable of drawing such absurd but totally lovable characters. They are zany, humanly faulted, but mostly pure of heart. Except the bad guys, of course. This debut to the Grub and Stalkers series did not disappoint. I loved all the unique characters and their little town. The mystery was unique and maybe a tad predictable but still very entertaining. Wish this series had been longer but I am looking forward to reading each one.
