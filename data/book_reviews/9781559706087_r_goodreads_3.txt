An interesting story. There is no definite proof because personal papers of the royal family were destroyed by Tsar Nicholas I, the successor to Alexander I. Alexander was involved in the death of his father, Paul, to take over the throne. He was horrified at what he had done and was depressed and worried afterwards. He became very religious and felt he had committed an unpardonable sin for which he needed to atone - which he could not have done while Tsar. There are many unexplained circumstances surrounding his sudden death from an illness which no one can name. Also, there were many delays in the burial and autopsy which is contradicted in personal diaries and letters.Also, the starets, Feodor Kuzmich was visited by Tsarevitch Alexander II, as well as many cultured and educated people. He also spoke 3 or 4 languages and was very cultured himself. He refused to take communion, supposedly because he would have had to go to confession.One of the most interesting reasons for supposing Alexander I faked his death to become a religious recluse and wanderer, was that several close members of the Imperial household refused to attend the memorial services for him every year until they received word that the starets had died.Quite an interesting tale - and, if true, a very interesting story of a troubled ruler.
