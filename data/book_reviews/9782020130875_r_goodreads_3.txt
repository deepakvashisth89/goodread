A mildly interesting book, I find it fascinating how something as common place today as stripes was actually such a big deal in the past and how this colors our use without us even realizing it.I did feel however that even for a small book , it was rather light in content.He spends more time listing the different ways stripes have been used in the past rather then explaining exactly why stripes were considered so dangerous.Also, there could have been more images of what he was describing to make his concepts somewhat easier to understand.I would only recommend this to someone who is a history buff, someone studying fabric and it's uses or someone who is fascinated by useless facts from the past.
