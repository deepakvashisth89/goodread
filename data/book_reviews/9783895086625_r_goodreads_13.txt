This is such a beautifully written book! I love how thoughtful and philosophical it is.

The way the story is told—through letters and then through “the editor”—is very interesting. Werther is writing to a close friend of his, and because of this the reader gets an intimate glimpse into his honest thoughts and feelings. Once the narrative style changes, the impact of what has happened is even more powerful.

I felt terribly for what Werther was going through, but (at the same time) part of me felt slightly detached from him and his emotional turmoil. To be honest, I can’t put my finger on why exactly I felt/feel this way. I’ve been wondering if it’s because of how dramatically he expressed his feelings, which made him feel more like a caricature than a “real” person.

The ending was heartbreaking and left me feeling (as the title says) very sorrowful.
