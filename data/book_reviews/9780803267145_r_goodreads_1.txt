Really wish there was a new edition of this taking us up to the present. Provides a fabulous overview particularly from the 1960s to 1999, along with some compelling themes. Nearly 20 years after publication it still has valuable insights. You can evaluate for yourself how well the arguments carry forward to the present, but I would love to read how the authors - or similar informed experts - would put the more recent decades into perspective. Particularly since (as they predicted) Ventura was a one-off, yet arguably the political culture shifted nonetheless.
