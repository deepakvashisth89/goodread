I am torn on this book. On the one hand, it's not a bad book, I guess? It's fine. It's competently written except for a few spelling mistakes here and there, a few characters that briefly came back to life (the author wrote the wrong name, I guess?) and a few other, minor nitpicks. Only the kind of mistakes that made me chuckle but not get upset.But, I feel like this book has strayed so far from what made the first book,The Engines of God, so appealing to me, namely the space archaeology bit. In this book, we get practically none of that and instead we're completely swamped by a philosophical debate that goes, more or less, nowhere. And a lot of action, a lot of "We have to leave quickly!" as they somehow keep getting into trouble of the deadly variety. All of which can solidly be blamed on people making really... REALLY dumb decisions consistently throughout.Priscilla "Hutch" Hutchins is back in the pilot chair and could, at any point in the plot if we believe her, have stopped it all but chose not to for... reasons. Love, duty, curiosity, sheer stupidity, who knows? But she's definitely horny, there's not a man she won't ogle (more power to you) but her flipflopping is absolutely maddening. At some point she accepts that she's in love with someone but never for a second does she stop considering any of the other men around as potential love interests. If a man so much as smiles at her, she starts sizing them up like a shark in shallow waters.The last 100 pages or so were just an absolute bore to read through as well, there was never any doubt how things would resolve (because love!) and the only worry I ever felt was for the side-characters who for some reason, again, chose to go along with it all.But I also can't deny that it kept me up way past bedtime, thinking "Just one more chapter" and then there just wasn't that much left of the book so I might as well finish it despite not particularly enjoying myself. This is not about exploring alien cultures or about coming to some kind of peace with your place in the universe, this is a space soap opera, where emotions run high, egos clash and people die because of someone's dumb choices. I'm just shocked there wasn't a steamy sex scene.I still intend to read the others in the series eventually but I think I'm just gonna have to calibrate what I'm expecting from these books now. They may have started as one thing but they've certainly become something else.
