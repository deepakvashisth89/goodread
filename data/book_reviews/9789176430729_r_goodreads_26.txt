One of the best crime novels I’ve read.However I’m note sure that is the correct classification - there are murders and there is suspense, but I would classify Blackwater as literary fiction. It is incomparable in depth and quality to some of the easier reads of nordic noir, such as Nesbø or Nesser, but it is also not a quality police procedural, e.g Leif GW Persson.No, this is a novel that happens to have a crime as its central theme, but that for me worked as a deeply moving story about the lives and relationships of a number of characters, most of them people, but nature and weather definitely also among the main cast.
