There are still some amusing bits in this eighth Oz epic, but the plot is made from 90% recycled materials and there are WAY too many characters for any of them to shine properly. The titular Tik-Tok is maybe the 12th most prominent or consequential persona in another quest to the Nome King's domain to deal with the devilish sorcerer once and for all.The pictures are still good fun though:Six more in the series and I'll see it through to the end. They're too light and quick to give up just because of a ho-hum entry.
