Oh what a tangled web we weave...Abandoned by their suddenly wealthy mother, Shylo and her baby sister Cassie were left with their alcoholic father. The girls spent their childhood miserable, always cold, always hungry. At a very young age Shylo became like a mother to her sister and they clung to each other, two discarded orphans in an unspeakably cruel world.Even today an abused child, if taken from the situation, craves the love they desperately hope their faulty parent has denied them. And so, as soon as the sisters are released from their Orphan Train adoption gone bad, big sister drags Cassie on a wild chase to find a mother that Cassie remembered nothing about.Problem: traveling to chase down this neglectful mother takes money. She'll had learned to lie and steal on the New York City streets as a 7-8 year old just to have a morsel to stave off hunger. Now, no longer a child, she resorts to lies and trickery to travel after that mother.Enter the Greeks, Ari and Demitri, who are short on funds, who hope they can find a wealthy debutante to glean funds from.The story gets interesting when several characters keep building on their lies, trying to not get tangled up!Their cross nation journey is packed with action, outlaws, scheming and perhaps a hint of romance. None of the schemers know what twisted adventure lies in California, but you can count on the story shaking them all off balance as they seek that elusive happy ending that may not be in the cards for them.If some some what graphic sex scenes bother you may want to pass by this twisty tale, or at least quit about 3/4 of the way into this novel. Otherwise, hold this book tight as you read...sharp twists and turns ahead!
