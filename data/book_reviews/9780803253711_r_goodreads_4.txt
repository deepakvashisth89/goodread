If some works are above criticism, this one is unquestionably too low. As the editor of the edition that I read notes: "In truth, Wycherley's play is protected against the critics as a skunk is protected against the hunters. It is safe, because it is too filthy to handle and too noisome even to approach."I read this book because I was seeking an encounter with the zeitgeist of Charles II's restoration and was well served. "The Country Wife" is a work remarkable for its occasional wit and consistent stench.
