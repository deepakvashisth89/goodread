Cartoonist Peter van Straaten gently skewers the pettiness and vanity of that strange group of people who have chosen to call themselves "writers". Etched in these few dozen cartoons is the story of the 99% that no one reads: loneliness, insecurity, and a terrible mediocrity that is only matched by their delusional ambition. Van Straaten is Dutch, so I assume the texts have been modified to suit an Anglophone audience. Plenty of nudge-nudge, wink-wink; it helps to know your Serpent's Tail from your Melvyn Bragg. Cute little nods too to Simenon's whoring (!!!) and the #metoo movement, 30 years before its time.
