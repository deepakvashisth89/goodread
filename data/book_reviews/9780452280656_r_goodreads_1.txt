The Reagan Rules: pp. 248-249
*Reality is determined by image and anecdote. Reagan was master of transforming a single example, however far removed from any representative situation, into proof of a sweeping generality. When he was finished putting his spin on a situation, reality didn't get a hearing. A black woman who had been convicted of fraud in a case involving $8,000 was transformed into a "welfare queen" with a fleet of Cadillacs and a tax-free income of $150,000, proving that the poor were getting too much help. It sounded good; we bought it.
*Too much money is never enough. The rich don't share.
*Violence rules. People were sick of being pushed around by little countries like Vietnam and Iran and they were ready to prove they weren't going to take it anymore.
