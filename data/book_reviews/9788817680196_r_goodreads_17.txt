I really like the writing style, it is certainly fluent and clear, without too many turns of phrase. With just a few stories I became fond of the character of Adele Sofia. The themes covered made me cry, I love how Dacia Maraini managed to deal with these topics and develop them in the stories. My favorite story was that of Tano which I enjoyed accompanying with the song "Occhi lucidi" by Ultimo, an Italian singer. This story best represents the tenacity of children, the fear of confessing and Dacia Maraini managed to frame the situation perfectly. I cried a lot when the grandfather-grandson relationship was discussed, because I think it is one of the most beautiful relationships in the world and one that makes one very fragile. Although the main theme is "darkness", I liked the friendship between Adele Sofia and Marra (every time I eat licorice I will think of Adele Sofia)3.15
