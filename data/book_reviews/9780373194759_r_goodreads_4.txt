This is Jessie's story. Jessie is the middle sister, the responsible one who is good in business. She has loved Matt for years and so has Matt, but Matt thought she was a career woman so he and Maddie decided to get married(he realized his mistake soon enough). So, 2 months have passed since Maddie married Joe instead of Matt and Jess is gone and we later find out why. She's pregnant with Matt's baby. Matt goes to chase her and woo her and well he puts his foot in the mouth so many times. We get to see the little baby talking as well. The book was funny, these two people were nuts about each other and didn't know how the other felt. In the same vein as the other book. Crazy, quirky, funny.
