To the south of Narnia lays a vast desert, and beyond that the Calormen Empire (obviously based on the Middle Eastern Muslim world). Deep within Calormen the bedraggled and misused Thasta the 'son' of a fisherman, and Aravis, who is due to be forced into a terrible arranged marriage find themselves unlikely travel companions, on the road to the fabled (in their country) Narnia; they get caught up in a big Narnian adventure!

This book, the fifth published, but third chronologically, is set during the reign of the Pevensies as briefly referred at the end of , and is a full-out classic fairy tale adventure with a long journey, an 'evil' empire, kings, queens, battles, giants and more, as well as your Narnian talking animals and fabled creatures. Although it doesn't particularly sit well with the other tales, it is one of the biggest Narnian reality building stories as there is so much detail about life in the Narnian world, especially its neighbours. Fab read 8 out of 12 Four Stars.
