Madeira holiday book 4‘I lose my memory, and it turns out that an old friend of mine is a world expert on memory. Isn’t that a hell of a coincidence.’ er yes it flaming well is🥴The whole book is a collection of wild coincidences and pure hokum. It reminded me of Hitchcock’s Vertigo or similar. Action packed it roars along. If you can withhold any semblance of reality it works …and it worked for me😉I only really know the author from his ‘Pillars of the Earth.’ And he had certainly honed his skills by then.But for a rollicking holiday read as the booze invades the brain it was great👍
