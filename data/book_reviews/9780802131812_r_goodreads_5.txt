One Hundred Dollar Misunderstandingis structured as alternating monologues by two characters: J.C. Holland (a white male college student) and Kitten (a black female prostitute). With the voice of J.C. Holland, Gover expresses “the ignorance and arrogance that is at the heart of racism”. And with the voice of Kitten,Robert Governot only captures nuances of the then current “black slang” (like one of my favorite words: ofay), but conveys a paradigm shift that brings both characters to life.Read the full review on my blog.
