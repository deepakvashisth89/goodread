Corpses show up with regularity in seedy LA hotel rooms.Most of the action takes place in the dark. And it’s usually raining.Handguns seem to be everywhere, so shoot-outs are not infrequent.Characters are described with detail. Corruption abounds.These are regular features of Chandler’s writing, and shared by all the four stories here.The title story is a little different though, in that it takes place in the African American area of LA, and its seedier side plays a key role.Though there is no Marlowe here, these stories are classic Chandler.
