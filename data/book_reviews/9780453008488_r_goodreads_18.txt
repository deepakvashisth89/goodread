In pursuit of one of my reading goals for 2023, I decided to give this collection of short stories a reread. However, I didn't realize that this was just 4 of the stories, not the complete collection. I will have to get a copy of the whole book in order to finish reading this one.The four stories in this "selection" were The Raft, Mrs. Todd's Shortcut, The Monkey and Gramma. The Raft was a true creature feature and it was creepy, tension filled and horrific. I enjoyed Mrs. Todd and this story had more of a fantasy vibe with an unexpected and enjoyable ending.I remember The Monkey from my first reading and I found it to be creepy and memorable. I never want to see a stuff monkey with clacking cymbals IRL. And, Gramma was probably the most horrific of the four stories. Full of tension, great build-up and ending, it is the perfect short story to make you cringe.The audiobook featured a different for each of the stories and they all did a great job (even SK himself!). They were perfect and built the tension/horror at just the right pacing.
