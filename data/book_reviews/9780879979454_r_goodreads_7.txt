Jul 15, 1030am ~~ I've been a little busy lately and also wrapped up in book 2 of this trilogy, so I sort of forgot that I had this review pending. Oops.

I'm still a bit pressed for time so I am not going to babble on very long here. I enjoyed the book. I thought it was interesting and complex. I was fascinated by the linguistic aspect and information.

I could rant forever about how so many things mentioned in this book are happening today....just look at the Taliban in Afghanistan, or at the Supreme Court in the United States.

Men and their need to control everything around them. Women and their need to survive, and the choices they must make in order to do so. Will this never end? When will humanity learn to work together? Not just between countries, but between all the sexes as well?

I suppose if that happened we would not have books like this to read, would we.

But I would like to see what such a world could be.
