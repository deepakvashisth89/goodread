This is just the kind of book I like. Four generations of women and the men they loved. It has everything - romance, a mystery, disasters and near-disasters.Ari McAlister, just graduated from college, is trying to figure out how she will support herself and pay her debts after losing the part time job she has had during college when she a) is offered a high price for a mountain in Telluride Colorado that has been handed down in her family for the past hundred years and b) wins the 'sweepstakes' - apparently some kind of lottery from which she will get 25 million dollars. She doesn't want to sell the mountain and thinks the 25 million will allow her to keep it, but it turns out to be more work than she expected even with the money.The book goes back to tell the story of her great-grandmother, for whom her great-grandfather first purchased the rights to the mountain. There is a mystery to the great-grandmother's life which is never unraveled until Ari and a woman she meets who is a genealogist start looking into the family history. It also details the story of her grandmother and her mother and the men they loved but were unable for various reasons to marry.Pivotal to the story is the recurrent interference of several prominent families from Denver's high society. Although a few of them have been good friends, they have mostly been meddling in unhelpful ways over the years.Early in the book, Ari meets Rivers Alexander, the grandson of one of the families whose grandfather was good friends with Ari's great-grandfather. She doesn't realize when they first meet that he is the man who called (on behalf of his boss, the scion of another of the old families, who mostly didn't do so much direct meddling in Ari's family's affairs, but nevertheless seemed to be there to scoop up all their assets every time things went wrong) offering to buy the mountain. And Rivers doesn't realize when they first meet that she has just won the sweepstakes.As soon as Ari claims the money and the publicity about her is out, another man comes into her life - Caley Sutherland, the grandson of another of the old families, and one of the prostitutes from the brothel where Ari's great-grandmother took refuge when she first came to Telluride (a woman who was intimately involved in betraying Morna, the great-grandmother, to the husband she had run away from) comes into her life. Caley attempted to rape Ari's mother and was the direct cause of her father's death back in the day, but when he talks to Ari, he acts like they were all best friends. He tells her that Rivers Alexander is just after her money, and that he thinks just like his boss. At first, Caley appears to be only interested in trying to manage Ari's money (although he doesn't let on to her about that), but eventually he decides he wants to control her as well and decides he is going to marry her.Fortunately, Ari is distracted for a while by her search for the truth of Morna's story, and by her campaign to save her mountain, but it's a near thing that she doesn't wind up marrying the wrong man.
