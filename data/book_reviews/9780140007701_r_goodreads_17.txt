This is the first book in the series, and apparently they get much better. It felt like the author wasn't too sure what she wanted from this story. AlbertChampion wasn't her detective, and at one point he seemed like one of her suspects. It read more like a thriller, though started off as a country house mystery. First books are often weaker than others in a series, so I will be carrying on with this one and see where we go from here.
