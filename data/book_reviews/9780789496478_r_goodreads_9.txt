I read this book a number of years ago, and while some of its concepts are now becoming dated, it was certainly a big fat corporate kick in the ass when it was written.One thing you'll notice about Tom's books is their unique and somewhat anarchistic design style. At first this takes some getting used to, but I find it is very appropriate for the subject matter. It's a nice break from the typical boring corporate drivel.Mostly, I love reading Tom's books because they are a great source of inspiration. I could read a couple of pages from his book and suddenly a brilliant idea for a product or service would pop into my head. It's funny how his books pull this stuff out of me.
