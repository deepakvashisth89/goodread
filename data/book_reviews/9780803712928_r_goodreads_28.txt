This book details the origins, language, and daily themes of Kwanzaa, which takes place over seven days (hence, seven candles). The book also has suggestions for ways to celebrate certain days, such as refinishing a wooden chair in the house or using coins saved throughout the year to purchase something for the home.I checked this one out because Kwanzaa is often mentioned during the Christmas and Chanukah times of year as an important alternate holiday and I wanted to know more about what it really was. It sounds like a lovely way for family to be together and be respectful of one another and others. The only issue I had with this book was the frequent use of the African terms, which my tongue continued to trip over and which interrupted the flow of my reading - but only because they were unfamiliar to me and I didn't know how to pronounce them, and had to stop to slowly say the word out loud before I could continue reading. :)Recommended for ages 7 and older
