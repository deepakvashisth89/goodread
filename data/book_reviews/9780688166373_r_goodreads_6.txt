This is a collection of approximately ninety obituaries plus additional articles of many different kinds of people. Most are positive, leaving the reader with a feeling that the person had a good run or accomplished something. Some are heart-wrenching, such as the obituary for seven-year-old pilot Jessica Dubroff, who died in a plane crash with her father and flight instructor after taking off in bad weather while trying to fly across the country.The selection may be somewhat limited in scope and time, but the book also presents a slice of humanity that gives pause for thought.Please read the full reviewhere.
