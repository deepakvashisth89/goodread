I wish I could give this book 10 stars because it was amazing and touched every part of my being in every chapter. This is a book that makes you really think about who you are and what you can become as a human being and a person of God.I found myself questioning my own character throughout this book and wondering how I can become more of what is expected of me from God.This is a book that I will not give away just yet and may read it again or maybe even three times.
