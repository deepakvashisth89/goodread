Recommended from my Mom... just got into Chapter 2. I'm having difficulty reading it because of all the asides the author puts in mid-sentence. Sometimes, asides within and aside. It makes me have to re-read without the aside just to figure out what she's trying to say.Now that I've finished, it was an intriguing read because I've got a friend who is currently going through an adoption and I know they'll be on one side of this conversation in the distant future. I wish them the best as their children go out and search for the biological mothers. I'd rather talk about my friends than this book, which helps explain the 2-star rating.
