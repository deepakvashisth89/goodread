An excellent follow up to William Marshall’s “Yellowthread Street” - it’s a much darker and ‘more serious’ crime thriller than the first book which was more of a crime/comedy in a way.Marshall still has plenty of fun with the pacing and descriptions and his characters are richly drawn. I’m really starting to get a real sense of who these people are - the tensions between them, the respect and love between them.Really looking forward to getting stuck in to the third book in the series - Gelignite.
