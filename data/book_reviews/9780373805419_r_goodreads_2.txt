4 Stars ~ This is the first I've read by Ms. Power, and it certainly packs a few unexpected punches. The emotional conflict is high from the first page to the last, which for some readers may be too intense. Outside of the conflict, we didn't get to see very much of the softer side of the hero, though there are moments where it's obviously that he has one. As a relief break from the battle between hero and heroine, Ms Power give us the hero's twelve your old son, adjusting to a recent blindness.The story opens with Lydia arriving in Bermuda to fulfill a three week contract for a nanny to a rebellious 12 yr old. Not only is she in for a shock but so is her new employer. Thirteen years ago, Lydia (18) had been engaged to Blaine (25). It was a two month whirlwind affair. Lydia in the lime light as a super model and Blaine, the handsome son and partner of a financial tycoon. Though in the focus of the world, Lydia held onto her innocence with a now deceased notorious actress for a mother. Blaine's father played onto that innocence and put an end to their engagement in the most brutal way, taking Lydia aside and claiming that around the time of her conception, he'd had an affair with her mother. He'd begged Lydia not to ruin Blaine with this horrendous secret, and instead pretend to be a gold digger and betray him with a media mogul willing to play the role. The devastation was complete. Lydia acted out with very public relationships and parties, earning the press nickname, Super-Tramp. Blaine agreed to the arranged marriage in order to merge their families financial empires.So in the present, we learn that Blaine's marriage was only one of convenience and that the only thing they had in common was the love they held for their son. Lydia, on the other hand, had to come to terms with the horror of her own feelings of desire and love for her own brother. This trauma lead to breakdowns and a hatred for her inability to lose those feelings. A trauma so strong that she continues to have horrible grotesque nightmares. Giving up modeling for a private quieter life, she partnered with a friend in the employment agency. A trick of coincidence and a matchmaking friend, brought Lydia to Bermuda and all her trauma and fears of her own desires must be faced all over again, but this time she has to endure the bitter hatred of the man she loves with all her being.This story is not for the faint of heart. Ms Power masterly portrays Lydia's inner torment. She holds a secret that has destroyed her and bares Blaine's ignorant contempt. Blaine totally in the dark about the real reason Lydia brutally ended their engagement, battles his own demons of self-loathing for still loving and desiring her. When the Lydia reveals their blood relationship, it's an anguish for both of them.
