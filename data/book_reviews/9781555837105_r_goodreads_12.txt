Insurance investigator Dave Brandstetter is checking out a death by gunshot that appears to be a homicide, but he has to make sure it's not suicide, or the insurance company couldn't pay the death benefit to the victim's mother. A young man has been arrested, but he claims someone else shot Rick Wendell. Tracking down the associates of Wendell leads Dave into many different places. There are so many characters in the book, I had to stop reading after a few chapters to go back and list all of them, with a description of who they were. Most of the characters, as well as the protagonist, are gay, which is unusual for a detective novel written in the mid-1970's.
