I've sd it before & I'll say it again: KEdswuump, Pure KEdswuump. Ok, as far as I know, I've never actually sd that before. What I have sd before is: This is a serial. B/c it's a serial it's repetitious. The author's challenge is to keep consistency but to also up the ante so that each new installment is more exciting. This excitement is usually generated by having the main character's challenges get even more over-the-top. Tubb is good at that but the repetitions get more than a little bit tedious. Every novel has a new woman falling madly in love with Earl Dumarest of Terra & trying to dissuade him from his search for his birthplanet, Earth. She never succeeds.

I have to say that this one seemed to up the ante even more than usual. For fuck's sake, Dumarest is on a spaceship, forever trying to escape capture by the Cyclan & their agents. Waddya know? There's plague on the ship, how's that for extremity?

""Plague," he said. "It was in the city. Maybe the steward carried it or maybe he picked it up, either way he fell sick and died. The dead needed to be disposed of but with the city at war that wasn't too easy. A soldier could have seen something, put two and two together, and there would have been a riot. As it was the news must have leaked out."" - p 43

"Fren Harmond provided it. He sat in his cabin, his bandaged leg supported before him, his face seamed and graven with lines of pain. A stubborn man, refusing the drugs Dumarest offered which would have eased his anguish. A fanatic who believed that the body would heal itself if left alone. - p 48

Ha ha! That's me. In general, I like the Dumarest hero, he has strength & integrity, but here's where I part from him, he's a believer in medical drugs. The plague ravages on.

""Earl!" Delphine stood beside the door, turning as Dumarest entered the compartment. "He's crazy. Raving mad. Do something."

""What?"

""Knock him out. Drug him. Anything."

""He's a man," said Dumarest. "And he knows what he's doing."

""Burning himself?"

Dumarest's philosophy is always a main point of interest. The navigator is resorting to an extreme to try to combat the plague w/in himself. Dumarest respects this & doesn't take it upon himself to interfere.

Welp, Dumarest survives the plague & ends up on yet-another-planet-where-the social-system-is-somewhart-medieval & fights in an arena, an experience that Tubb revels in describing.

""Slow down!" yelled Galbrene. "Stand and fight like a man!"

"A demand echoed from the stands where men and women leaned forward, shouting, faces avid, eyes reflecting their hunger. The lust for blood and pain and death, the primeval desire always to be found when the veneer of civilization was torn away and the true nature of the beast was revealed.

""Kill! Kill! Kill!"" - p 101

"[T]rue nature of the beast"? Really?! Since when do any animals other than humans create a stadium in wch 2 creatures fight each other to the death in front of gathered thousands?!

ANYWAY, I didn't take any reviewer's notes from this point forward to the end at p 157. Plenty happens, it's action-packed, the woman who 'loves' Dumarest turns out to be a bit differently motivated than she proclaims & Dumarest survives triumphant once again to resume his search for Earth & his fleeing from the Cyclan.
