Vol 8 of Mars is crucial. Here's where we learn why Kira has intimacy issues with Rei. I won't go into detail except that what you're thinking is probably the reason why. You most likely guessed correctly. So that's an uphill battle for Rei and Kira and then circumstances crop up that make the situation far, far worse. I can see Rei's point in what happens at the end of this volume. He can't just sit by and watch Kira repeat history, and he has no faith whatsoever that things will be different this time. I sort of get Kira's logic, but I understand Rei and his choice a lot more.We've also got some Masao back in this volume, and he says something that I think is very telling, and probably true. Psychopaths are brutal, but sometimes they do tell the honest truth. He says, "Do you know why people in this world wish for peace or no more discrimination? That's because people aren't peaceful creatures by nature. They love to discriminate." And that discrimination can take any shape or form.Anyway, I love this volume and I love how the story ramps up again from this point on.On to Vol 9.
