I love historical fiction. I think this is the first book I've read that focuses on Native Americans, and I found it very interesting. It was very well-written, and delved deep into the life of the Native Americans. I loved that it focused on two tribes. I wish it had been longer, because I think there is a lot more detail that could have been included. That being said, it was the perfect book for me to read over a weekend while I was on the lake with no internet. I would love to see a follow-up.
