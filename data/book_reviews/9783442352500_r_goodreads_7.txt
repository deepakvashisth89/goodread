This is a pretty good adaptation of the screenplay by Lawrence Kasdan and George Lucas for a film that I'll bet anyone who might think of reading this book has seen multiple times. There are a few minor differences in some details and background, but no major additions or deletions. I preferred Foster's novelization of the original, but Kahn did what they hired him to do, so it's all good. (And yes, I did read it with the soundtrack playing on the stereo!)
