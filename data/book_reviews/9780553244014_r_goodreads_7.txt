My dad read this book out loud to my siblings and I growing up and while I generally retained positive vibes, I forgot howfunnyit was. This is a delightful, wholesome, joyful look at life with 12 kids in the early 1900s. If you're only familiar with the Steve Martin film, you're missing out.I definitely recommend this one. I laughed out loud often while reading it, and nearly teared up there at the end. It is a good time.
