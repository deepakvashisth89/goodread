2.5Jesse Stuart first published this story in 1954. So yes, as may reviewers have said, it is dated. However, the teaching for morals is not often done in this day and time. (I haven't found an app for that.) This simple story (remember 1954, written for CHILDREN not adults) tells the value of honesty. With many descriptions of wildlife (rarely see that anymore either) this short story is just right for little boys and girls today to learn the value of doing the right thing.
