Norman Rockwell's Christmas Bookwas so much more than I expected and that's delightful! I thought I was requesting a book full of Norman Rockwell artwork to enjoy during this holiday season. I received a book full of Norman Rockwell artANDfull of stories, poems, and carols! This book, originally published in 1977, is a collection edited by Norman Rockwell to add to your Christmas season. He groups the selections by passages (some Bible passages and religions poems at the start); short stories; poems; and carols. It was such fun to read them all!
