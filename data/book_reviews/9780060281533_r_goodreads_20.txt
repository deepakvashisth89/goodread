2.5 stars. There are things I like about this book and things I didn't. I liked that the boys in the orphanage called each other buddy and treated each other well.I was bothered by the all night parties, and wondered if those were based on fact. I can't imagine a household with a young child having parties that lasted all night. In my research of this topic, it appears that rent parties did indeed last all night. I also didn't like that at the end of the book, the people helping Dave only seemed to care about the 11s. I can't imagine not caring about the other boys who were being abused.I liked Dave's relationship with Solly, but I felt his reasons for leaving him in the home were contrived.I also wonder if Dave as an orphan in pajamas really would have been welcomed into high society parties. Over all the book was okay, and I learned some things, but it isn't my favorite by Levine.
