Because he owe the guy (about something not mentioned), Rafe went undercover as a security personnel, bringing his twins with him. Coincidentally, Megan also transferred there at the same time for a month from another branch of the company to review a promotion of some kind. Since Rafe didn't know anything about her, he was immediately suspicious. A series of events ensued that cleared that up. Unfortunately, it seems the issue of the heart was not that easy to clear up.THE COVER:It was really great! I love the twins so them being on the cover was cool. I wish they're facing the audience though so I could gawk at their cuteness. The color was warm and had a chance of standing out among other books. Other than the title of the book being small, I really liked it.THE CHARACTERS:Based on what I have read,Rafefeels like he have some issues with domineering, confident women. The way he reacted about Megan's snobbishness projects a seriousness I was itching to tease out of him. Of course, he was on the security business so it was understandable, but the guy really needs to lighten up. He was also suspicious about everything (again, he was perfect for the job). I couldn't seem to think about any other layers to his character other than his seriousness, except that he was very loving and protective of the twins. He was okay, I guess."She was a snobbish, arrogant woman" is what I thought whenMeganfirst met the twins. Being harsh toward kids about dirt on your pants was unforgivable. I was surprised that I didn't totally hate her because she was so guilty afterwards and so alone then. She's also judgmental and tend to stereotype people, but hey! Don't we all. She also knew her own mind when she broke up with her fiance because she was in love with Rafe. She also showed that she won't cheat on him (Ryan) while they are in a relationship when she did that and that says something good about her character. I was inclined to take her side because of the way she acted all high-and-mighty when things don't go as she had expected to hide a soft, vulnerable, clumsy side. She's not perfect as a person, which I appreciate. She was more like the villain at times, and I like it.GabeandGregare adorable and so vulnerable (specially when Gabe went looking for his mama) that they made my heart squeeze in a good way. I really like their antics in the story.Ryandidn't come out as a villain, so I don't hate him for being involved with Megan. He seems to prioritize his work more though and he didn't even fight for his (ex)fiance when she broke things up with them. It's just sad that Megan broke things up with him while on another state and on the phone. I pity him, but I think it's good that they didn't wed because their feelings are not truly engaged.Trig, the biker who's really a genius and CEO was really interesting that he earned a spot in my review. He feels really warm, a big bear of a man, that I wish to have met him (in books or in true life) someday (can't he be the main character?).THE PLOT, THE WRITING, THE WHOLE SHEBANG:The plot was good, if a little paranoid and questionable in real life (on Rafe's part). I mean, I didn't feel the sense of urgency Zane (Rafe's friend who asked him to investigate) was obviously having a dose of for Rafe to go undercover himself when Rafe's employee's could do it.Anyway, as I was saying, the plot was good and did the author gave it justice? Some. I mean the writing was good. It was easy to understand and the events that happened was at least given thought. The flow of the story was good. The dialogue and scenes between Rafe and Megan was great. It was smile worthy.However, I do feel that Rafe and Megan's acquaintance feels kind of new and the love, like a flower, was on its first blush. It was not enough to declare a love for life with each other, I think. Rafe even thought of Megan as someone who cared more about status in life and that she was a snob. Megan, on the other hand, was saying "I love you" to Ryan before and after she met Rafe, so excuse me for doubting her feelings.The mystery was not intense. It was something there in the background, yes, but the information about it was kept under wraps that no clue was forthcoming. It was just something that enabled the characters to meet and for Rafe to have the status dilemma (which I don't think was really an issue at all). It was sad because it had some potential (the mystery part). The hum is there so why not give the reader's the whole music? The villain was unexpected, simply because no clue was given. The ending with catching the villain was not even a great climax because I was not really invested in it. It was an extra event, is all.The story was filled with the company leak and mostly Megan and Rafe's encounters. No sex, and it's not something I'd miss here because the whole book needs every ounce of wholesome encounter from the main characters to have a poignant development with their relationship. The attraction was there, but it was not sizzling. It was more like a simmer, and I was hoping for something to happen to fan the flames but it didn't come.The wonder was on how the twins took to Megan even when she's rude to them on the first encounter. It was unbelievable, specially when she did not do anything to warrant some affection from the twins. She doesn't look like their mom so I really wonder how that came about. Because of the twins affection, I kept wondering if there's a small part of Rafe that fell for Megan because he wants someone to care for them.The ending was not satisfying for me. Well, they cleared up the misunderstanding about the bet, so good. The wedding of Mary and Robert was a good development, but I wish the epilogue was more about Rafe and Megan and even the twins.OVERALL:It was good I guess.I would recommend itfor a light read, but I probably won't rave about it.
