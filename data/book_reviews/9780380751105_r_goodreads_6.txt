Charles Paysuer's 5-star reviewhereis very good and much, much kinder to the book than I am. I can see and understand everything that he says, but in the end, I still didn't enjoy this book, despite some of Arnason's unique touches. There's a lot in this book that relies on randomness (and, as Payseur suggests, part of the author's subversive satire), but as a story to follow, the different elements just felt cut off from each other.
