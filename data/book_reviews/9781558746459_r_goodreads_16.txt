Stories about love from the general public, famous people, contributors and reprints from other sources.While most of the stories are touching, some of the stories about illness and loss are depressing. Memories are fickle, in that we tend to forget some of the negative things that happened to us, when asked to tell stories about our loves.Some of the stories read more like works of fiction. There was also an abundance of stories where love was assisted with the aid of God and angels.
