Say before male-dominated capitalism there was a better society that was matrilineal and wise and successful. Vicki Noble finds archeological evidence that this was so, but direct lines and specific information on how to live this way has been lost, destroyed, discredited, retold, and perverted. But, there are still ways we can tap into this power, and Noble, co-creator of the Motherpeace Tarot, explains some of these in this book. They include trance, sex, astrology, and dreaming. Note that men and masculinity are not the problem, but global dis-ease of taking power, rape and murder, that have spread like an alien life-form in the last 5000 years or so.

This book was more interesting than I originally imagined. It's a little dated (we have made some progress since 1991) and very menstrual (being post-menopausal I notice that the author is not very inclusive of non-bleeding females).
