I remember this one feeling like the beginning of adult ennui in my readership. I read it 30-some years ago but I remember the disillusionment. I felt like it had a buffet of different ideas I'd seen done more interestingly elsewhere all swirled into something new. I know now that this is what art is supposed to be, but at the time I encountered this it felt needlessly derivative. That probably says a lot more about me than the content, I admit. A stale plot emerges amid characters that spoke in ways that never felt genuine to me, felt like cutouts of advertisements speaking stiffly and strangely. No offense to anyone who likes it or the author.
