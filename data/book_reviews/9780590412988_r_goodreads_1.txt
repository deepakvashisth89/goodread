If this were a 10 star rating system, I would have given 7. I decided to round up instead of down because I do like the book series.
This is my least favorite of the three books in the series I have read so far. It was an entertaining read. I personally was not the biggest fan of reading the Southern drawls in this book, but that part was written well. The story itself was interesting, although I did find Sarah's feelings toward Robbie a bit strange considering the situation.
The book cover is cool, although not from a scene in the book, but a clever use of representing the past and present (as of when it was written).
I really like the title in consideration to the story.
