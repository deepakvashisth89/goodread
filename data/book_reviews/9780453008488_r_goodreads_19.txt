Overall the "Selections" version of "Skeleton Crew" was okay; however, I'd still like to read the full version that has a great deal more of King's short stories. Overall "The Raft" and "The Monkey" was pretty good, "Gramma" was okay, and "Mrs. Todd's Shortcut" was so-so.I have seen "The Raft" featured in Creepshow 2 multiple times and thoroughly enjoyed reading it as a short story. This story translated to film very well and this story was perfect as a short story. I haven't seen the recent film version of "The Monkey" so I can't say the same, but as as short story it was pretty good."Mrs. Todd's Shortcut" never seemed to really take off and I didn't find it very engaging. "Gramma" was a nice, self-contained horror story with an unexpected ending.Pros: interesting how so many of King's stories work better as short story.Cons: there always bound to be some duds.Bottom line: I'm looking forward to reading the full volume of "Skeleton Crew".
