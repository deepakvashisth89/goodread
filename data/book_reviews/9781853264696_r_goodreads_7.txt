Epic🐫 Now at last I’ve read it and have a book to recommend equal to The Iliad in Greek, War and Peace in Russian, All Quiet in German. The writing is amazing, the use of the English language exceptional. I savored every page as I listened to Ralph Lister’s narration. This, even though every war story must also be a story of pain, horror and loss.🐫 Churchill puts it correctly and succinctly:🐫 “It ranks with the greatest books ever written in the English language. As a narrative of war and adventure it is unsurpassable.”
