There are more new characters who time travel with Andrea in this book. I can easily tell the difference between the personality of the characters in this book compared with the same character's personality in the first and second books, which is a good thing. One thing I don't really like about this book is how Kami and Eddie, despite being the whole reason Andrea and Tony are in the past again, don't really do very much except for when they are first captured. Another thing that I don't particularly like is how easy the ending was for them:Andrea's haircut and Tony's broken backpack strap were automatically fixed as soon as they got the present, which bothers me how easy it was for them to pretend that nothing had happened.
