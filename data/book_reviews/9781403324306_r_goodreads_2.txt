I wanted to like this book better. The outline is there. However, the storyline needs "fleshing out" and it was in dire need of a proof reader. The various underlined words were distracting, I assume it was to emphasize the words? The end of it felt quite rushed as well. If the storyline had been expanded a bit more, some of the simplistic writing improved, and a proofreader/editor to catch the various mistakes, it would have been much improved.
