The title is a poke of USA Today, which had just recently started coming out. The "News mcnugget" version of information apparently really bothered Trudeau. Artistically, he seems to want to evolve. He is trying new ideas here in panels and art, and it seems a very experimental time for him. For example we have a "Waiting for Mario" segment that parodies "Waiting for Godot", poking fun at Mario Cuomo's considered run for the Democratic nomination (which eventually went to Dukakis). George Bush is also crucified, showing how he had no real convictions beyond wanting to be president, often calling him a wimp. This volume is more character heavy, rather than political.
