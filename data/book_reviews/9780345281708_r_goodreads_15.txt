Pretty much a combination of Don Quixote and Candide, with quite a bit of the likes of Ivanhoe, Dunsany, Mencken, and chivalric romance thrown in.I'm pretty sure a lot of the intent behind this was to satirize classical romance, but a lot of that won't be so obvious to modern readers (if you haven't read the subject of a satire, it's tough to get the satire...).In any case, it turns a lot of heroic conventions and cliches on their head, to generally interesting effect. I wouldn't say it's better than Don Quixote, et al, but still well worth a read.
