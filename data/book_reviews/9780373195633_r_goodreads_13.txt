Nicole Burnham became a favorite a author several years ago. She writes such vivid pictures of her world that it feels like I'm there. I absolutely love her books and audiobooks.GOING TO THE CASTLE was Prince Antony and (American) Jennifer's story. It was a beautiful story of putting others first, even when it brings heartbreak and knowing what is truly important in life. Hard choices were being made, but in the end their is a true HEA.I love seeing characters from one book to another, building the world. I look forward to meeting more of the royals from San Rimini.I read the newer version of this book. 2020
