this was by far my favorite in the book versions of the original trilogy 😌 it was SO incredible. the writing was fantastic, and it made me way more emotional than i thought it would. the other original trilogy books struggled a bit with writing conflict/combat scenes, they just gave way too much detail and it was a bit of a drag to try and follow it all. this book handled combat beautifully, it wasn’t too much detail, but it wasn’t too generalized either; you could follow along easily and the weight of it wasn’t lost. this book also felt like it added a lot more than what you get from the movie, it added interesting internal dialogue and really made you care about the different relationships in the story. i only wish it went a little more into vaders internal struggle, but they still did a great job with him! i loved this book, it was so so so so good! especially the ending, but i think i’d like the ending of this story no matter what lmao
