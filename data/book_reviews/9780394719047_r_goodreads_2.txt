Some essays here are good, most just fair-to-middling, but all are worth the little bit of time they take to read. My favorite by a wide margin was "Instinct, Intelligence, and Anxiety." Though quaintly dated and stamped with a heavy 1950s vibe, that's still a 5 star essay for modern folk burdened by a Capital-A Anxiety Disorder like yours truly.
