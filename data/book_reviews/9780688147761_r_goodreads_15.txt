Lots of fun! Beano X. Bates, an infamous conman/gambler, plays a high-stakes poker game against the gangster Joe Rina. Unfortunately, Beano gets caught cheating and gets the heck kicked out of him and left for dead. A witness steps up, identifying Joe's brother, Tommy, as the killer. A few days later, Carol Sesnick is dead. Turns out that Carol is one of Beano's hundreds of cousins, and he wants revenge his way, the way of the Long Con ala "The Sting."Some of it is silly, some of it is disturbing, but it's all entertainment! Great characters, intricate plotting, and lots of twists.
