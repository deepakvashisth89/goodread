This man saw things so clearly and explained so eloquently the "colored problem" in his words, or the veil of color. The tragedy of this book, published in 1903, is so very little has changed. Outward situations have changed, but not attitudes."Herein lies the tragedy of the age: not that men are poor,--all men know something of poverty; not that men are wicked,--who is good? Not that men are ignorant, --what is truth? Nay, but that men know so little of men.""Would America have been America without her Negro people?"I think that says it all.
