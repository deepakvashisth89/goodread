Code to Zerois my first Ken Follett book and I can't wait to read more of his books!I was captivated from the very beginning. A man wakes up in a public restroom and has severe amnesia. His pockets don't contain a wallet or any information (or money). He slowly picks up pieces of his identity as he is being followed.Plenty of intrigue, betrayal, and espionage along with a rocket launch from Cape Canaveral.Highly recommend!
