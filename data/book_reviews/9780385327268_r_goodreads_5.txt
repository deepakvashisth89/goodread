I have looked forward to picking up the second book in this series...and I wasn't disappointed with Akiko in the Sprubly Islands. Akiko is a perfect character for children to read about. Never one to be a leader back in her hometown on Earth, she is thrust into the role of leading the rescue of a young prince.I quite enjoy seeing her make decisions and become a leader. The band of misfits she travels with are fun and seem to find themselves in all kinds of trouble everywhere they go. But they compliment each other well, and they are able to escape unharmed from all their adventures.Mark Crilley has done a wonderful job in creating characters that are easy for a younger audience to relate to. The wondrous journey that he has created will leave the reading craving for more . . . and more. I wonder what will happen when this series continues in Akiko and the Great Wall of Trudd?I rated this book an 8 out of 10.
