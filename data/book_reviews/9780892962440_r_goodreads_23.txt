I'd had this on my bookshelf for a few years and was looking for something light to read before bed. I finished it in a bubble bath, which seemed somehow appropriate. I'm not sure these are all that memorable, and I had that experience again of discomfort and admiration at Highsmith's writing all rolled into one. I can't decide if she's brilliant or frustrating, perhaps both.Misogyny usually means the hatred or dislike of women, but in this case, Patricia Highsmith is writing short little stories about women she wants you to hate. Or maybe they are typical of the types of women everyone loves to hate, from the perfectionist to the mother-in-law to the prude. Most of them die lonely deaths or live forgotten lives, some get the pleasure of both in only a few pages. Their brevity will translate to me not remembering them for long, which seems to only be what the author wants of me. Okay Highsmith, you win.
