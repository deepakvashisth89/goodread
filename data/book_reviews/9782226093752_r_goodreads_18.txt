I've been a huge fan of Robin Cook's books but this one didn't land the mark.

Contagion was very confusing from the start and as the story progressed no real plot was being uncovered and everything just became boring to read, I mean if you don't give substance to the reader which unravels the story a bit from time to time and just let it all out 20 pages before the end the rest 400 pages just become a drag and hard to read.
The story had potential but was lacklustre, everything felt like it was being repeated thus failing to invoke any real intrigue in me.
Only the fact that the year is ending and my goodreads goal (even tho it's not completed at least would add one more book to it) made me finish it today.
