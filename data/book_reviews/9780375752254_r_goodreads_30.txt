This book has tons of useful tips to help one be frugal. I think that I would have enjoyed the newsletter more. I found the format of the book sufficiently unattractive that it was hard for me to read through each section; in a much shorter newsletter format, I think I’d have found it easier to absorb what is offered. There’s plenty of helpful advice in here, although most of it was not applicable to me; I’m sure everyone would find enough here to interest them though. Each newsletter was, I’m sure, very readable. In this format, it’s more of a reference book, in my opinion.
