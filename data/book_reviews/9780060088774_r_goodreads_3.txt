I loved this history book on Fitzroy and Darwin and the Beagle by Nichols.This long read was quite riveting and took many unanticipated turns and pursued numerous but informative tangents to round out the characters. This felt like a passion project by a well respected author and I say that in a positive and honored way. I did not expect so much insight on the Native Americans in Tierra del Fuego and three of them play a major role in the story.I am currently on a trekking trip in Sur Patagonia and was able to see the Straits of Magellan. This book's events mainly take place a hundred miles south along the Beagle Straits which virtually no one sails today because of the foul weather.5 stars. Highly recommended.
