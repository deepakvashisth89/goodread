Hans Kung admits from the very beginning 'the Catholic Church, this fellowship of believers, has remained my spiritual home to the present day.'

This book is an attempt by this author to answer his own question: did Jesus of Nazareth found a church at all?

The chapter headings are: The Beginnings of the Church | The Early Catholic Church | The Imperial Catholic Church | The Papal Church | The Church is Split |Reform, Reformation, Or Counter-Reformation | The Catholic Church VS Modernity | The Catholic Church - Present and Future.

This book left me hopeful, as in highlighting the reality of the Catholic Church, it gives the readers an opportunity to grasp the church's stumbles while it grew.

Kung wants and very skillfully brings the focus for active, attentive, listening church that does not divide on 'rite', or gender, or culture, or class.
