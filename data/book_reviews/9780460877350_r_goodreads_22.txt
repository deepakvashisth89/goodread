Nature never appeals to intelligence until habit and instinct are useless. There is no intelligence where there is no need of change.The Time Machineis one of my favorite books. It’s a great story, and very well-written. It has the first use of time travel as plot device, used to tell a thought-provoking critique of modern society. It is one of the foundational stories of science fiction, but completely readable today. A must-read if you never have.P.S.
