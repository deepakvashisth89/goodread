I probably should have liked this more than I did. But I didn’t. I thought the first story here about the murder was perhaps the most interesting in the book, but if I had thought it was going to be the best of what was to come, I probably would have stopped reading. I’ve never really been all that interested in drugs and so I found most of that dull more than anything else. There was a chapter on Alcatraz that started by describing the wild flowers on the island – I don’t know any of these flowers and so had no image at all in my head of what was being described, other than some vague idea of lots of different flowers. I think you would have to be American to really enjoy these. Or maybe not, I really don’t know. I’m a great fan of the poem – I know it by heart – but even that wasn’t enough to sustain my interest here. I do know I’m in a minority on this – I read it because at Readings Bookshop they had a card under the book where they raved about it. I’m probably just displaying my ignorance, but wouldn't recommend this to anyone who doesn't live in the US with an interest in the 1960s.
