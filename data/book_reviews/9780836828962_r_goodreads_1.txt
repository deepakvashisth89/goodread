Schnitzel von Krumm's family are going camping, there is so much to pack and remember and Schnitzel is getting under everyone's feet. You can guess from the title what's going to happen!

This is such a fun read, the rhyming text is face paced and funny, the illustrations are fun and the story is good fun too. I have read this story to so many children who have all totally loved hearing about Schnitzel's adventures!
