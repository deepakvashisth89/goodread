I am a staunch fan of The Simpsons and I truly was excited to read more about them, especially to hear about Bart Simpson, and by extension, Nancy Cartwright; however, her writing style was too stream-of-consciousness and too “dorky”, IMO. The consistent narrative asides got annoying since they usually weren’t funny.I realize she’s telling her story, but it got grating having all of the asides and lame jokes thrown in. I tried to finish the book after making it over halfway, yet I started getting a headache with all the jumping back-and-forth and the attempted bon mots.She seems like a nice person, which is a plus…
