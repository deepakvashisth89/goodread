Great sister book. If you have sisters or wish you did this is an interesting read. If you thought your mother was/is something to deal with this story will make you rethink that. Ansa writes a sensitive, revealing, story about the closeness of sisters, the strained relationships between family, and how living with someone that you know has mental problems affects how one functions in their own life. Much of Tina McElroy Ansa's phraseology reminded me of growing up, interactions with family and how communicating with family is different from communicating with any one else in your life.My only problem with this novel was not understanding why their mother was the way she was.
