I picked up this book because of my interest in nuclear semiotics.If I were to judge this book by its cover, I would have assumed it was cheesy sci-fi.This book should have really been two separate books. The first two thirds pertained to nuclear semiotics and communication with beings on other worlds, but the last third could have been a different book entirely. I can see how environmentalism is relevant to communicating with future generations, if I squint a bit, but it doesn't fit well with the first part of the book.
