Annette Funicello?This comment, from the very last page of the book, sealed the deal for me—I shrieked with laughter and howled so loudly that my husband came running from three rooms away, wanting to know, what was so funny?!? My hysteria was partially from relief, because my reading experience of this novel up to this point had been extremely bumpy—not being raised Roman Catholic,I just didn’t get it. What’s with all these feelings of self-loathing, shameful guilt and desperate desire for penitence? A completely foreign terrain. What I could understand was Isabel’s delayed mourning responses after the death of her remaining parent, having undergone that same experience, but unlike poor Isabel, I never felt alone or broken in my grief. This book was a real learning experience.
