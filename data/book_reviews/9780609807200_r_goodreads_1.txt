A book to be read by every parent of girls (Casey) & teachers, grandparents - anyone who deals with girls (boys too, but I think the things they talk about in this book most boys get). The introduction kind of summarizes the lessons the author learned from the women interviewed for the book. The main part of the book is the actual interviews & the women tell you the things that they think influenced them to be successful in their lives. One of the things I like is that the author takes the lessons the women give & puts them in bold print. I also think that older girls should read the book; some of the things talked about in the book are things parents etc. can do to help their daughters; but it also tells the girls things they need to do to succeed.
