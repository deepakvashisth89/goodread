I’m deeply ashamed that I’ve only recently learned about this award-winning play while browsing lists of the best African-American literature.My library had a copy, and I started reading as soon as I got home.While this was a very quick read, there are so many universal themes. Anyone who has been an outsider, has experienced prejudice, or struggled with financial hardship will be able to relate. The family interactions and problems were realistic and no different than those faced by modern families.The 1989 TV adaptation can be found here:https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jzfgw...The library also has Lorraine Hansberry’sTo Be Young, Gifted, and Black: An Informal Autobiography, which I hope to read soon.
