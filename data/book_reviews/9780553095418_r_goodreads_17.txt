It was OK.This was one of the first Star Wars books to come out after the Thrawn Trilogy. I think they were hoping to ride on those books' successes. I tried to read this years ago but never got through it. The abridged audio version was easy to listen to and follow. The story was fairly interesting and well-constructed, and the narration was excellent. It just was a bit bland compared to some of the other Star Wars books I've read or listened to.
