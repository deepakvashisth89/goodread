This is a pretty good collection of the most important Bronte sisters works as it includes Jane Eyre, Wuthering Heights and Agnes Grey. Jane Eyre is of course the best book in here, and I really enjoyed revisiting it after having studied it in my university days. The other two I had not read before, and while both were decently enjoyable, they simply do not read with the same clarity nor do they contain the same character development. Still, all three books are written in the same style, with Wuthering Heights being somewhat more difficult to follow. For people looking for an introduction, I definitely recommend checking out Jane Eyre at least.
