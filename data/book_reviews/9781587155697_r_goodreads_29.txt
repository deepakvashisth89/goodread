Good friends, I will tell you of this extraordinary book. It is a first hand account of the many exploits of the famed Baron Munchausen. I must admit, many adventures surprised me, and I felt that they may have been embellished. Usually, though, when these doubts were raised the Baron himself made vigorous claims to their veracity with-in the text. It was like he read my mind hundreds of years later.--This book is split into 2 parts. The first part is a collection of somewhat related stories, the second is a larger tale of African government and wild goose chases. Part I is excellent. Part II did not hold my interest as much, but had its moments. If you are in a hurry a recommend reading just Part I-- but completionists won't be disappointed by the second half-- its just not as good as the first.
