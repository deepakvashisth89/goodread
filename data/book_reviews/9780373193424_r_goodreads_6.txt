Not particularly enjoyable on the whole. It was an okay story but I found myself uninterested in the proceedings of the story and frankly, I was irritated by the makeover route that this book took. While makeover themes are not uncommon in romance books, this was poorly executed.Cindy was not a relatable heroine and at times, I felt like smacking her. However, I liked her reluctance to go through that whole makeover route even if she did give in. The hero is not too great. Rather forgettable
