1.5/2 stars, dropped down from 3 because of the terribly unsatisfying ending.

This book was deliciously angsty with an absolutely evil OW who gets all the heroine's closest people -- her husband, father, and godfather -- to betray her. Excellent writing and I enjoyed reading and learning about the publishing/writing industry.

However, what the husband did was unforgivable in my opinion. He not only cheated with the heroine's best friend for months, but he continued seeing the OW when the heroine threw him out -- and we get to read their sex scenes. I am happy to say thought that the heroine finds her own lover too and does not remain celibate!

What really brought my rating down was how fast the hero and heroine's reconciliation was and the author's VERY cliche resolution.
I swear this plot-line is in 90% of cheating hero stories. I'm over it. It's lazy writing. But what was lazier was that this reveal was accompanied by basically an instant switch in the heroine's mindset and she actually is the one to fight to get the hero back, post-divorce and while he is living with another woman.

In the end, the hero's cheating is blamed on the heroine and a 'both sides' argument is used. This one line from the book pretty much sums up how terribly the cheating was justified by the author via the heroine's mother:





Like, no. First of all, it wasn't just a one-time drunken sex, thing. It was a months-long affair with her best friend. Second of all, the the specific instance that triggered the cheating is the heroine going on a business trip and forgetting to tell the hero that she's delaying her return, so he goes to pick her up at the airport and she's not there. Literally, that's it. The fact that the author and the heroine's mother is trying to equalize both their transgressions is ridiculous and insulting.

Anyways...everything is wrapped up in like the last 10 pages of the book and we never dig deep into the cheating, whether it could happen again, etc...Absolutely zero grovel. It very much sounds like if the heroine does anything to piss the hero off, it's fair grounds for him to cheat....
