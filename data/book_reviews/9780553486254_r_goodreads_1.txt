I really like The Saddle Club books, and this one was really good too. I find myself cringing every time Stevie does something bad, or gets in trouble. This book switches between Stevie's class trip to New York City for extra school credit, which she desperately needs, and Carole and Lisa, who are dealing with snobby Veronica diAngelo at Pine Hollow. Veronica's father got a TV segment called Genius Kids to feature her about riding, even thought Carole would be much better. Lisa and Carole, to save Pine Hollow's reputation, keep covering for Veronica, and she is nasty to them. Stevie keeps sneaking away from her class in the museums they visit to go to the carousel in Central Park, and she gets in a lot of trouble, but eventually saves her class using her horse intuition.
