This book started off well for me and then nose-dived. It should have been called View of Delft – the title of Vermeers’s famous townscape. The whole book was more or less orientated towards describing this painting, and it was wrong that its title suggests a broader agenda.




Whilst it was a pleasure, as always, to see prints of Vermeer’s interiors - which constitute the bulk of his work - I have to ask what they were doing here. Except for one, which threw an interesting light on , the rest where pretty well irrelevant to the text of the book.

Okay, whinges aside, I did learn a something about Vermeer and his work, and some of it was very interesting.
