Ah, so Kafka was the first to start the discourse of "would you still love me if I was a worm" (cockroach in this case). And the resounding answer from his family? NO.I was never assigned this short story in an English class, and I thought it was about time I finally picked it up! It was well written and I enjoyed the themes of alienation and capitalism— although I'll admit that I'm grateful I never had to write an essay on this one in high school or college. I'm equally grateful for every day that I don't wake up metamorphosed into a giant cockroach. RIP Gregor Samsa!
