This is not a book to read. It's a book to sing with! I don't use the large print edition, I don't think; it's smaller. It's got a whole lot of "singalong" song lyrics--you'll know at least half of them, if you're a boomer like me. They supply the guitar chords and suggest that we go to YouTube to listen to the melody. I have in fact found the songs I looked up, in that way.This song got me through the pandemic. And as a person who lives alone, I use singing as a way to boost my spirits. I couldn't do it without Rise Up Singing.Lots of Beatles and other 70s hits, many, many labor and activist songs. Full of traditional tunes too.I bought the book on Alibris, and amazingly, the sellers had only one copy at that time--their own. They assured me they could get more and sent me theirs. Charmed.
