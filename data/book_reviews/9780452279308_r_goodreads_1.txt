I saw the movie back in the day and the play is just as good, one of the best second wave AIDS-era plays. I need to do a bit of research, the set design doesn't quite sit in my head (summer house, the lake, the garden, etc.) so it might be more difficult. The dialogue is just electric even if some of the references skew a little 90s dated. 7M.A Perfect Ganesh (the other play in this collection) doesn't speak as much too me, but it's still very readable with some moments of real wisdom. It floats between a bunch of locations, but the magical style of the narrative lends to a more impressionist approach anyway. 2M/2F
