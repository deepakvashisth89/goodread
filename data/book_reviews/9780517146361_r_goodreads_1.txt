This probably should have been a DNF but it was a gift, and I really wanted to love it. But just no. Why is Arthur's round table so romanticized? It is full of adultery and witchcraft. Lancelot's devotion to Guinevere is not called chivalry, it's called an affair. Granted, the book was "cleaned up" for kids, but to the point where this was praised. Apparently, it's ok to have an affair if you're faithful to the person with whom you're having the affair. Then the witchcraft combined with medieval Roman Catholic superstition just wasn't awesome. In the effort to glamorize Lancelot, the book also skipped over the fact that Galahad was the illegitimate son of Lancelot. There were also just quite a few depressing stories that were the result of bad choices. Not legends I'm going to encourage my kids to study.
