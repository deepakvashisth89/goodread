Just like the previous two books in James Alan Gardner's "League of Peoples" series, "Vigilant" is a very good book with an interesting writing style, characters, and concept. Even though it's very well done and easy to read, there are just a couple of small things which prevent me from giving it a full five star review. First, the interplay between the main character, Faye Smallwood, and Festina Ramos (from the original book) is a bit adolescent. Nothing horrible, but it just doesn't suit their backgrounds (especially Ramos'). Secondly, the repeated behavior of "Mouth" and "Muscle" is pretty darn silly. Heck, right at the end, Gardner even has Faye describe exactly what was wrong with their behavior. Yet, we're not offered a plausible excuse for it. These are pretty small issues, so, once again, it would be nice if I could rate the book at four and a half stars. But, since I can't, I'll go with the merely Very Good 4 stars out of 5.Also, as a side note about the Kindle edition, this book has quite a few scanning/formatting errors.
