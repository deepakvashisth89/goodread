This is not merely for parents. It is for teachers, youth leaders, ministers, or anyone who has earned the trust of a potentially vulnerable young woman--and all are potentially vulnerable to abusive relationships. Young women tell their stories, both the bad relationships and how they ended them.These dangerous relationships are not always easy to spot. Crompton shows the way--how to spot a bad relationship, what to say and how to say it, other ways to intervene. The lessons should be taken absolutely seriously. This is a very readable and important book.
