There is a lot about this book that is fairly gruesome. Part of it is just the medical focus of the book which deals with the physical aspects of crime detection.

I have read several of these books and this one is by far the least satisfactory. It is interesting because once again, the primary character, the woman, medical examiner, is directly involved at the end with a confrontation with the main criminal.

If you are interested in this series I suggest an earlier book for starters.
