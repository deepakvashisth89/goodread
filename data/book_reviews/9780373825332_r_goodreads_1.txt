What a lovely book this was, quite an unusual plot and it was exactly what I was looking for, what a touching love story between the Reverend and the pregnant failed actress that he had been hopelessly and secretly in love with for most his life. What a wonderful man he was, I'm half in love with him myself, and Eva was a good character also that had good development and learned from her mistakes and became worthy of a man as wonderful as Howard.
That's all i really wanted to say, I recommend this book.
