The Kelladys are two of my favorite characters in the Newford books by Mr. de Lint, so it was a real joy for me to delve into Cerin's past, and the past of his magical harp, Telynros. I hope, somewhere, there is more about how he met Meran. That, I would love to read.I sometime buy used books, when new ones are beyond my meager budget, and this one came to me from the entire other side of the country, from Scotts Valley High School library, in California. I'm sure it made a few stops on the way. I was saddened to see that it had only been checked out once in its original home. It will be lovingly placed on my shelves, and reread and treasured, until I take my own spot in That Place Where All Things Are Known.
