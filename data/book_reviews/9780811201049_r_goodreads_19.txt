Though at times, this book was a bit over my head,Zen and the Birds of Appetiteis a beautiful and worthwhile comparison between Christianity and Zen. While they are indisputably different in almost every way, they have one very important thing in common: paradise is found in emptying oneself to return to a state of innocence, purity, and fullness, a process which entails shedding the ego to make room simply for what is.This was a short, yet meaningful and accessible read that I found highly relevant to my life and current faith journey.☆☆☆/☆☆☆☆☆
