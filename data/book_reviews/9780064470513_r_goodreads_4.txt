After reading through some of the list of awards that Bruce Brooks has won, I had expected this book to be... well, better. The plot sounded interesting and catching and the setting in a somewhat futuristic society where huge percentages of adults are alcoholics and children are trained to deal with alcoholism from a young age seemed interesting. However, I felt that book was very impersonal, creating no connection between the reader and the major characters let alone the protagonist. In hindsight I see a little more what the book might have been after, with changing relationships between mother and son, but it doesn't seem to have come together very well. One of the things that I really regretted was the lack of information about the world in the background where society has adapted to the high saturation of alcoholics. Instead, it's sort of an underlying set piece that never explains how and why it occurred, although a little bit of how it is handled is discussed.
