I think Alice Gray's purpose of writing this book was to open teenagers eyes to other peoples feelings, and to write stories that poeple can relate too. One story may make you laugh and one may make you cry, but they are all great stories.I think the theme of this book was kind of all over the place. One story would be energizing and then another would bring tears to you eyes. I think the all around theme of this book, though, would be relatable because with the wide variety of stories almost everyone can relate to at least one story.Since this book was a whole bunch of stories there was no definite style of the stories, but there is a style of the overal book. The set up is in sections like famiily, inspiration, and friends. In each section there would be a series of books in each relating to the main topic. After one section would finish with its last story the begining of the next section would have a "title page" telling you the next topic and would have some sort of quote or memory of the authors past or present life.My opinion of this book would be it was great. I really liked the idea of how it wasn't just one plot and one book it was just a whole bunch of different stories put together in one book. It made it eiser to read as well because unlike a regular book where its in chapters, each story was around a page or two so instead of trying to read a whole chapter with a little bit of time to spare and having stop in the middle of the chapter, if you are waiting at the end of class or something you can just pick up the book and read a story before the bell rings. I would suggest this book because like I said the stories are short which makes it eaiser to read but also because if you are wanting to read a book but don't have a lot of time, instead of reading a full book and wanting to finish it to know what happens at the end but you don't have the time to, you can read this book so you read a story or two a day or however you plan because it only takes a minitue or two to read each story. Also some people may not want to read this because real books have a better plot and more suspense or details and things like that, but even though these stories are short they are really good. Just because a story is short doesn't mean you cant get anything out of it.
