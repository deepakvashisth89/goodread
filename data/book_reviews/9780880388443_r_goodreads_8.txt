A 2nd edition supplement that introduces various real world religions (Norse, Ancient Egyptians, Ancient Greek, Celtic, Aztec, Native American), real world legends (King Arthur and the Knights of the Roudn Table), and some classic fantasy (Fritz Lieber's Nehwon) to D&D.

In this book, the avatars of the gods have stats, information is given to create clerics, druids and paladins of the deities listed, you have new spells, new magical items, new monsters, and many stated NPCs like King Arthur, Merlin, Fafhrd and the Grey Mouser.

We had a lot of fun reading this book, as the real world was "stated" in Dungeons and Dragons terms but we didn't use it much in game as it wasn't terribly well balanced.
