Disappointing and well below the quality of Beast In View. The idea of someone acting so firmly based on a dream, is hard to take. The father figure is the most convincing character in the book. This is the sort of book where it feels like paragraphs or chapters have gone missing. It contains a number of thinly drawn caricatures rather than substantial characters.Characters speak out theories for why the main character had the dream, but this doesn’t feel like a closing of the mystery.
