I was a little disappointed in this book; I expected more, I guess. So often you seldom really learn about the crew. I want to know the men who gave their lives, as well as the technical stuff. I thought there was too much repetition and too much speculation. My Dad lost a buddy on the Thresher and worked for the investigation team. He usually won't talk about it, but in the right conditions he will, and he once told me that the Thresher almost made it to the surface; I don't know what data that conclusion is based on. I do think it interesting that in several books now there has been no love given to Rickover.
