I have been enjoying the books of Mary Higgins Clark for some years although I will admit I treat them rather like a palette cleanser between big reads.This is another such break - it is part of the Alvirah and Willy series which I will be the first one to put my hand up to and say I am reading totally out of order. I have read a few of them, however at the start of this book there are references to some of their previous adventures so I recognise and others i do not (maybe I should sit down and work out the timeline of this series once and for all).however reading out of order aside (as it does not really affect the storyline or the enjoyment of this book in the slightest). The story pretty much flows as you would expect (and how you would expect having read the cover of the book or previews).However on reflection its now down to the surprises and twists to the story its more about the gentle ride you take with the heroes of the story as the slowly circle in on the villains and fix the world in the process. If ever there is such a think as a feel good mystery this book (and the series) most certainly would fit the description.So for me it was good fun and great break in the trends I have been reading recently - shame it was such a short book but then again there is never a shortage of Mary Higgins Clark books to choose from.
