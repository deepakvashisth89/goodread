I'm not sure about this book. On the one hand, like most MBS books, which I've read a ton of because they get passed down to me (MBS stands for Mind, Body, Spirit) this is a very pretty book, beautifully written, full of lovely imagery -- shiny treasure chests full of glittering jewellery; the sunrise filling you with light; stormy seas turning calm, and lots of it is actually quite sensible if you have the time to read it in depth.It looks like my dog's had it; it's bent, has pages missing, and is underlined and highlighted to within an inch of its life. I've been lucky because my life has been basically crisis-free, and wonder how this book would hold up if I had a real problem.On the other hand, if there really was someone who could get rid of all pain/problems in our lives, surely (s)he would have done it, or made sure there were none in the first place. If you're ever not feeling your best, this is not the best book to dip into (this is why some pages are missing).It's nice to think, or perhaps imagine, that everyone will be nice to you if you're nice to them, and that you're never alone, but the world doesn't work that way. If I went to give one of my flatmates a big hug, and said “drink of my pool of love, my sibling!” I'd probably end up being sectioned!If you take this book too literally it could cause you MORE problems, because you'll end up overwrought, exhausted, a bit vague and generally away with the faeries (sorry! nature angels!) ; or you'll be a complete and utter, shall we say, not very nice person; the angels love you so it doesn't matter.There's a sequel 'Messages From Your Angels' but like this book, it depends how you take it.
