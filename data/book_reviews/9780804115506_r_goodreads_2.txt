To my mild surprise, I really liked this mystery set in modern India and linked to a massive environmental disaster - or what would be massive in any other context but in India is just another in a long list of horrible tragedies involving incomprehensible numbers of people dying.
This is the most recent in a series and now I will have to back & read the first ones. Very well written, well paced and never dull. Although I think George could have taken 2 minutes to phone Annie at some point, surely ?
