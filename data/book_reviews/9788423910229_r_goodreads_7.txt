As shattered legendary Troy fell the fires engulfs the edifices and smoke flows, the shocked survivors flee dodging lances, swords, stepping over a plethora of rubble, dismembered bodies but where to go? Aeneas a member of the royal family seeing his family butchered, gathers a few Trojans, quickly escaping to the nearby coast picking up stragglers there, if only they find boats. Thus we begins the definitive Roman epic , yes indeed influenced by Homer's The Iliad and The Odyssey, not in their class not many are to be honest and having read now all three. War and numerous wanderings from both previous poems are in Virgil's single book, the surprise it's excellent. Asia Minor, is modern day Turkey, the isles of Greece , North Africa, the sandy shores of Libya, to the spectacular harbor in mighty Carthage, then unknown Sicily, finally, the inevitable, barbaric future Rome. And the struggles start, where the blood red drenched grounds appear. Heads chopped off, stomachs pierced, dislodged, vomit eruptions, from full dead mouths, spraying the horses and the innocent, still carnage prevails the heroics must show. The glory their friends and families expect, cowards will not be tolerated. Death is cruel yet better than lack of bravery all must fight.... or be despised.. for the young , healthy. Strange though, reputation valued ahead of your own precious, dear skin. Women weep, fathers stunned, wives mourn, nothing changes, the slaughter continues.No quarter given vicious battles occur, again and again, to the victor the supposed great spoils, they are the price of a new civilization?. If your heart beats faster as swords relentlessly slash through the air, the two battle leaders Aeneas for the Trojans, Turnus the Latins, the reader looks forward to the final match. The ancients writers knew how to entertain, not too interested in historical accuracy, since the myth of Rome founded on April 21, 753 B.C. is wrong and the fabled Trojan War happened circa 1200 B.C. Nearly a vast 500 years apart, nevertheless a very good story for anyone interested in what the few people who could read then found quite thrilling. In conclusion for those that seek knowledge even in quite fictional tales the subject will reveal aspects of truth not of history but their selves. Which to my state of mind gives flavor and understanding, the ambience of the author's trying sadly to communicate duty above well-being.
