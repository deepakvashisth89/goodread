Third Eye- Lois DuncanA blast from the past!Growing up, I read a ton of Duncan. Struggling with dyslexia, I really didn't read a lot. We had A.R points to get and somehow in 7th grade, stressing in the library for a book that I could actually read and get my points in order to pass, I stumbled apon Duncan. She wrote ' I know what you did last summer' (yes, what the moves based on). Her titles and covered just drew my 13 year old attention. So i gambled and checked out Killing Mr. Griffin. I was hooked, I checked out all her books and tornadoed through them.Anywho, tangent, so went to the library on tuesday, I saw some of her books and instantly zoned back to my ,no less awkward, 13 year old self and had to get one. Thank God they have a few there because I read this in a day. Work was slow, what can I say.I never relised how much adult things were in her books at 13. Or maybe I don't remember them, who knows. There's a lot of underlining sex stuff. Nothing crazy or abscene, but it's there. I thought it was funny how such a straight edge, no nonsense small town school would have books that even mention sex at all in them.Th stories about a girl who randomly has physic powers when a little boy she's baby sitting goes missing. After he's found the young rookie cop on the case asks for some help on another case, but it uncovers a baby stealing ring. I know, I made it sound stupid and I really didn't explain it greatly. It's a good book, just go check it out.D.Deckard
