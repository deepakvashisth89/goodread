Honestly more of a 4.5 star book. I really enjoyed the plot and the characters. It was great to see a hero who had his limitations and manage to rise above them in a not overly corny way (in other words he had to acknowledge his weaknesses and find strength within himself to compensate for that and have to struggle through and work through the process rather than coming to a miraculous realization). I also like the emphasis on the fact that people deserve a second chance and should not blame themselves for mistakes they have made in the past. It also reminds us not to blame others for their errors especially since we have not lived in their shoes. Back to the story however. I really liked Airella a lot. I thought she showed a great deal of courage and gumption and in the end admitted her errors when she made them. She also gave a second chance to one of her friends who may or may not have deserved it. It had a great plot, memorable and relatable characters, and a lot of humor. Especially liked the two elders and their efforts to become involved in learning to defend the castle. They made me giggle more than once.
