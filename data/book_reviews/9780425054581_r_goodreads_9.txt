My first impressions, made without any great thought were that, while some science fiction rides very well through time, this book does not. There are elements in it ofStar Trektechno-babble but at other times something charmingly old school slips in, as when:"[Virgil Samms] again shut off his atomics and started the sixteen-cylinder Diesel engine which would do its best to replace them."Best of luck with that, mate. You're hoping to hurtle through the cosmos in a fucking spaceship. And then, towards the end, I had to wonder which side of the Un-American Activities Committee the author was on. There were so many John Waynes among the Lensmen I felt bewildered, even submerged, by the level of testosterone. It was not my favourite science fiction novel.
