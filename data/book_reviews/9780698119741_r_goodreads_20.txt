I was pleasantly surprised by this novel which has quickly became my favourite Morgana related novel.
I've read "I am Mordred" many years ago and I fear I don't remember it much, so I am sure this novel can be read quite indipendently from it.
"I am Morgan le Fay" is a novel that tells us the story of Morgana but all in the years before Arthur's kingdom, in fact the novel starts with Gorlois being killed and ends with Arthur taking the sword from the stone and Morgana travelling to see him for the first time since he was a baby.
What I liked about this novel is how it explains us Morgana's ambiguity. Among all the Morgana novels I've read or the novels with Morgana I have never been completely convinced by Morgana as an enemy and then as the one helping Arthur to Avalon. Instead in Nancy Springer's novel not only Morgana becoming the enemy of Arthur is convincing but it's also sensible to see a future where they can be brother and sister again.
The story of Morgana is here heartbreaking, scary even, with the theme of love, power, desire for power and how dangerous love and power can be.
I was also very touched by the story of Igraine. I don't like her much as a character, I'll admit it, but here she felt like a real person, a real character in a novel, for the first time, to me.
The same for the religious themes, which did not annoy me at all, but were very well researched (at least from what I know from the Mabinogion and the Welsh triads) and connected beautifully with Morgana's story in the novel.
I don't think there was a single thing I disliked!
