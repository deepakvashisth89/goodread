I am so overdue on reviewing this book—I read it weeks ago and life got in the way. Mason wrote this book in the early 1980s and the only reason I picked it up was the author has the same name as I do. Silly, right?This book of short stories was VERY well written but each story had a similar theme—women living in western Kentucky (or from western Kentucky as in two of the stories) who were restless and unhappy in their marriages. “Mork & Mindy” was mentioned several times as a popular show. It was very strange—I didn’t have to read the whole book to get a good taste of how well Bobbie Ann Mason writes, though. I just wish the stories had a bit more variety. So the writing was a 4 but the story “plots” were more like 3s. I am going to shy away from short story collections for a while.
