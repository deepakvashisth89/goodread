The Martian Chronicles, sort of, in Pohl style.Definitely not your typical martian story, the martians in this book are purposefully not important of themselves, just evolutionary flimflam.You get a series of interesting, although pulpy at times, vignettes that often have very little to do with the ovearching storyline presente din the first few chapters/storylines. Hit and miss, some are pretty poignant. A satircal tone is strong throughout - theres a particularly biting one about crackpot dictatorships.Fun, but not great.
