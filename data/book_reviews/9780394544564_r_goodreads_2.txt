This cookbook put together by the legendary Judith Jones along with her husband Evan is excellent, for it's recipes, many of them historic old New England family recipes. Many of which I see still used today but a different way of putting them together, ingredients a tad different, but in 33 years things change, one particular stands out as I see this floating around the Internet today, Jones states that our ancestors used it to make their own convenience Baking Powder Mix, same one out their today just reduced in size, I use it myself, who would have suspected it is that old. Well worth the read, the headers to recipes and highlights are priceless.
