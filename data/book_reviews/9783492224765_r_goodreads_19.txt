Oof. This is a standard cat and mouse action novel. It's loaded with testosterone, which basically means there are lots of guns and ammo, careful descriptions of illegal/psychotic/bloody actions from everyone, and women who either need rescuing or are ball busters.It's got some racial slurs (apparently to demonstrate the badness of the bad guys, but that's doable without being a racist so...).There are some redeeming scenes - mostly the cat and mouse stuff inside the building. It's too little of the book to warrant more than 2 stars though.
