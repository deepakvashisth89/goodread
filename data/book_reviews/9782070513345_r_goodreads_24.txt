This book is one of my favorite books! This book is funny, sweet and charming. Even though there are at least 4 or 5 "Nicholas books" in the series (so far) this one is my favorite. I like this one because Nicholas' mother is manipulative of Nicholas' father. I can read this book over and over again without ever getting bored. Nicholas is a fabulous read on a cold winter night when you can just curl up on the couch with a blanket. This book will make you feel warm. I highly recommend this book!
