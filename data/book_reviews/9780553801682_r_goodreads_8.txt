This book is a perfect part of this series. Inspector Ian Rutledge continues to discover the real mystery behind the crime he is to solve. Even the past does not prevent him from a committed search—to reveal the real from the scapegoated, to reveal the one who relentlessly persecutes an innocent woman, and why.In the process, the Inspector reconnects with family who live in Scotland. He also connects with the land and the people that he came to know during the war—came to know through the stories of one of the Scots soldiers he led. The internal process of laying to rest his internal 'stress' from the war is furthered. And the reader can carry the hope forward that this piece of his past will not get the best of him. He will be living to solve many a mystery.
