I can't really say that I liked it, but it is definitely worthwhile! Dr. Jensen's goal for the book is to make people think about their bowels to improve their health. I first read about him in the Green Smoothie Girl's book. She talks about this book as a formative part of her learning. Dr. Jensen attributes most of the ills people suffer today to toxic bowels. He may be right, I don't know. I do know that we are better off making our own foods because the garbage on the grocery store shelves is just that--garbage! I can see how eating it would lead to toxic bowels. This book is NOT for the faint of heart as it has color photos of various bowels and their contents.
