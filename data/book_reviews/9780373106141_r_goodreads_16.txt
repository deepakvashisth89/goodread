3 Stars ~ Alexa is heartbroken when her fiance falls in love with her best friend and he wants to marry her instead. When Mark and Elaine want to marry in Alexa's hometown and they invite her to the wedding, she feels that in order to avoid everyone from feeling pity for her, she has to go. To bolster her courage she drinks quite heavily and attracts the attention of Mark's older brother, Scott. They've never met before so Scott doesn't realize she's the jilted ex. The resemblance between the brothers is very strong, and Alexa flirts boldly pretending in her mind that he's Mark. When Scott insists that he see her home, she invites him in. It's easy to fantasize that it's Mark she's kissing, and when Scott begins to make love to her, the fantasy continues. In the throes of passion, Alexa calls out Mark's name, and Scott realizes he's being used. He's furious and lets her know just how disgusting she is. Fleeing into the bathroom, Alexa is distraught and she reaches for anything she can find to end it all. Scott breaks down the door and catches her swallowing the bottle of aspirin. As Alexa becomes aware of how low she has sunk, Scott refuses to leave her alone, telling her that if she kills herself she'll ruin any chance his brother will have for a happy marriage. The only way forward is for Alexa to go back with him to Brazil, but the location where he works is isolated and the company has rules that men may only bring their wives. A deep depression has set over Alexa and she doesn't fight Scott nor the sham of a marriage he suggests. Once in Brazil, she slowly learns to live again, and somewhere along the way she finds herself thinking more about Scott than Mark.Throughout the first half of the book, Scott keeps telling Alexa to find some courage and to grow up. I wholeheartedly agreed with him as her rants were just a tad over the top. Once in Brazil, she does start to realize she's too full of herself and needs to open her eyes to others around her. With her obvious distress over Mark, it did seem rather odd that she realizes she never truly loved him when she realizes she's in love with Scott. As to the hero, Scott is clearly besotted but he's not prepared to let Alexa know it. This is one of those stories that would have benefitted some of the hero's point of view.
