While I am not very deeply knowledgeable of the literary history of any King Arthur stories, I have always been a fan of what I have seen in most media. I know this book was written based off much older texts, I found it very hard to enjoy. This version does not read well at all. However, I can respect these stories for being a precursor to much of our current fantasy genre.

I do also have a copy of Sir Thomas Malory's Le Morte d'Arthur and have heard that is a much better version. I am certainly looking forward to getting around to that one and comparing these two. Especially as a fan of fantasy as a genre, I would love to spend the time experiencing some of its literary history.
