OMG! This is like the best book. I didn't know it was a series, even though it said A Roxy Girl series.
Now lets skip the part about the plane ride and talk about Kanani. She is awesome! Now who thinks that when she first met James they were meant to be. ME! I rooted for Kanani and James. I swear they were the ultimate soulmates. Then Rae comes along, with James. Honestly I wanted to punch her in the face just Kanani wanted to when she was jealous. The moment when Rae said that she and James went on some kind of like kayak date...

*Dies* (I came back to life though)

I felt like I took a knife to the heart. Not really. I was like "Ok they were just hanging out as friends." and then they start going on some more dates. Not to mention they meet a guy named Francisco. Never ever would I think that Kanani and francisco would like each other in the end. I was so surprised. To be honest I felt kind of dumb not seeing that coming.

That's it for my review. Sorry if i kinda spoiled it for you. KIDDING!! You should not of read it dummies.
