What a book - what a writer. So well written, warts and all, history of the catholic church.
I imagine many catholics will hate this book, unless you are tired of the backwards power driven focus of the curia and what to see the church bought up to date.
So much of the church history is caught up in power plays and not humanity. So much of the teachings of Jesus seem to have been left behind in this focus on 'Do what I say and not what I do' attitude within the church.
Reading this as a book, not as research, it is also highly entertaining. If only more historians could write like this.
This book takes you from Jesus, the founding of the catholic church, the popes and many deeds done in their name based on forgeries, the split with the Eastern churches, treatment of the Jewish by the Catholics, the inquisition, renaissance, reformation and counter reformation, the scientific shift and the age of reason and secularity, before finally getting to the Pope John II and his backward facing ideas (mostly).
This is an amazing book of scope of such an institution in just over 200 pages. With recommendations for the future of the church which in the view of the author, is what it will need to survive.
I would recommend this book to Catholics (to know where you have come from) and everyone else, who would be foolish to believe the Catholic church has no bearing on your life - it has fingers everywhere.
