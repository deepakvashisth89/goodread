“After eighteen months Bosshog was released from the grip of San Quentin and from dependence on me for tobacco and Being Peace. Before he walked off the tier, he stood in front of my cell and together we recited what had become Boss's mantra— something he'd learned to say whenever he was about to blow his top. We always started off chanting in unison with the words“Man, man," and then, "If we are peaceful, if we are happy, we can smile, and everyone in our family, our entire society will benefit from our peace’” (121).
