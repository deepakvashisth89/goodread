Mr X, a Lovecraftian figure who believes he has been created by gods (or aliens) and put on earth to bring forth the apocalypse. To do this he must sire a son, kill him thus ensuring fiery hell will reign down on all mankind. Star Dunstan is the unfortunate mother, and when she realises something is not quite right with the father, she hides her son (Ned) in the foster system. On her death bed Star gives Ned the name of his father and so begins a quest of who finds who first.

Fun Fact : The Dunstan’s are a weird family, they have abilities like Telekinesis, levitation, telepathy, time travel (called time eating), and they can do this thing where they can turn people into Swarfiga with their minds. However, they only really use these abilities for cheating at cards and stealing staplers and televisions. They love chicken livers, and all they really want in life is cable tv.

This was hard to follow at first, but Straub does a really good job of pulling you into a story and feeding you titillating moments that make you want to carry on. I think this would have been much better if there had been more Mr X in it, he was completely insane wish a dash of camp. Also wasn't totally bonkers about the ending, I mean who the hell was Mousie, did I miss something? (I think I probably did).

4 Stars. Usual thing with Peter Straub, started off not knowing what the hell was going on, but stuck with it and was hugely entertained.
