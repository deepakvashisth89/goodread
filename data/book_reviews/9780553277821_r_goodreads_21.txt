This is a very mixed book.

It is very obvious that the author is intelligent, but does he write a compelling story?

The open of this book is fairly grand, there is an action set piece that introduces you to the alien threat. After that, the book gets bogged down for quite a while.

Much of this book, in my opinion, is what would/could/should be background information in the start of the series, not the actual first book. There are some very important events that happen to set up the actual war with the worms in the books political climate, but it is ever so boring getting here.

This story has at least 5 characters who love to fly off on monologues that are entirely too long. Instead of characters having their own identity, many start to blend together as the author’s mouthpiece. I do not even necessarily disagree with what the message is, it’s just a bit hamfisted for me.

I read two-thirds of this book in 2019 and the monologues, speeches, and far too long winded conversations made me put it down. However, I did return now in early 2022 to finish the story.

It has promise. Most scenes that actually involve the alien race are interesting. The action pieces are good. I just hope the next book has less verbosity.

I understand the series is not finished and may never be, but I thought the concept of the series sounded fun. I own all the existing books. I won’t jump on #2 immediately, but I will get to it.

I have to give it 2 starts for how much it drags, but if you are willing to drudge through the points I mentioned, there are some bright spots in the muck.
