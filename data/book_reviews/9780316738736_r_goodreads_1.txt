I think this book is a great book for the classroom. I love the use of the primary colors. I think it is also a super easy read for students, this is because every page has simple sentences. On top of this is has a fairly simple plot and story line. Otto doesn't want to sleep, but then learns the good things about sleep and decides he does like sleeping. The illustrations look like they are from a coloring book and I think that is just a very different style you don't see as often.
