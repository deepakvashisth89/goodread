This was good, but odd... it was very little to do with crowns, silver or otherwise, and involved a lot of traveling on foot by a 10 and 8 year old. For all that, it was very direct, not a lot of whimsy or ambiance, but certainly had a style befitting juvenile lit from the 60's. I both liked and didn't like the sci-fi twist at the end, but that was certainly a theme for that time period and this fits well within that theme. I also appreciated the alternate ending for the UK version more than the one for the US, so if you read it, tell me which one you liked more and why. Overall, I recommend this as a near-classic. It had excellent pacing from the first line, none of this faffing around setting up moods, frictions, and troubled backgrounds, etc. It just jumped right in! And it was clean, unless you, too, consider "belly" to be crude and vulgar. :)
