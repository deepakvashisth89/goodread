Exposing myself. I have been a fan of Mr. Rivera’s ever since I was glued to the TV set watching him host the show about opening Al Capone’s vault in Chicago. Yes, the show may have been a flop, but he was mesmerizing. I couldn't take my eyes off of him. The irony of this book is that's exactly where this book starts. It's not a great book, but it's not too bad either. It walks a fine line between interesting reading and a trashy tell-all.
