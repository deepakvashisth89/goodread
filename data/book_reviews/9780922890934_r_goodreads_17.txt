4-Star ReviewI enjoyed The Con Man more than the earlier entries in the 87th Precinct series. By this fourth book, the characters are being fleshed out, and you can see the groundwork McBain is laying as they grow with each instalment. I particularly loved the Carellas—Teddy played a key role in the case, which added real tension and excitement to the resolution. Her dynamic with Steve is becoming a highlight for me. The pacing was generally fine, though a few parts dragged. A well-written, enjoyable quick read that’s perfect for switching off.
