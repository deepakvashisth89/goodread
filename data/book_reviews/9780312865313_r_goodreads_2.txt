The Visitant is a great story idea, as we have a centuries-old murder mystery set amidst the Anasazi People coupled with the modern-day excavations of the deceased.The author makes a good attempt at inserting humour and maintaining a quick pace. However, the circa 1050 A.D. language (translated into English) does not come across as believable. Also, every character in the present-day story has a heavy, tragic past. Too many characters are gifted with other-worldly, spiritual talents for this reader.
