I really enjoyed this book. There's something of a hook at the beginning, but otherwise the first third or so seems to really just be setting the stage for the rest. Most of the action occurs fairly spaced out until near the end, but despite that, the character-driven writing made it an engaging read an hard to put down.I was surprised when I finished to learn it was released twenty years ago; Benford did a fine job writing for the future (most of the book takes place last year, in 2018), and it feels like it was written much more recently.The ending nicely left me in a place where I could imagine what came next for the characters lives, and I was happy to see there was a sequel as well. It was a good balance where I'm excited to pick up the next book, but if there hadn't been one, I'd have been satisfied and pleased with the closing of the first book.Five stars because it's an engaging read, it does an incredibly believable job of addressing topics no one has experienced first-hand, and explains the science behind most everything while still somehow maintaining a note of mystery and awe.
