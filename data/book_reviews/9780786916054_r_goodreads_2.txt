Book two of five in this series.Cadderly our scholarly cleric is back along with Ivan and Pikel Bouldershoulder, Danica and all of the supremely skilled elves on a desperate, suicidal mission to thwart a seemingly insurmountable invasion until the very trees themselves join our heroes.A good, fun story.Salvatore is known for his fight scenes but there were just too many in this book - I would've liked a little more characters, a little more plot and a little less fighting.Still, it was good, it was fun, it was Salvatore.Looking forward to the next book of Cadderly's adventures.
