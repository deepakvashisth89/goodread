This is a book from the Mystery box I picked up at a library over a year ago. I had to rearrange my bookcase and found it.It is both funny and heartbreaking. There’s quite a bit of repetition throughout, which is the only reason it’s not a four star read for me.But this gem of a sentence from page 10:“He had a thick Scottish accent of the slow, whimsical brand that got less whimsical when it was someone else doing the whimsy.”
