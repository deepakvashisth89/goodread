I gave this book three stars because of the detail of what goes into making perfume, which sparked an interest in the subject, and I intend to learn more about it.

As for the story, while I liked the character of Jacinth (Lorenz not so much at first, he had to grow on me), the story itself was kind of insipid. There didn't seem to be enough emotion there, everything and everyone (even the villains in the story) just fell flat, at least that's the way it was for me.

It's not a bad story, just that I've read plenty that were better. If, however, you have an interest in perfume and what goes into preparing it, then I recommend you read this.
