It did give me the creeps every time John referred to her as "little girl" or similar. Eeek. But overall, it didn't leave much of an impression past me wondering if there was a postpartum depression undertone. Also, let's not lock women in rooms alone to "recuperate". Yikes.As for the other short stories, I was kind of bored by most of them... surprisingly I likedThe Boys and the Butterthe best, but I got the feminist gist, and I'd say Gilman was way ahead of her time in the "man-hating" department.A girl after my own heart...The shining star for me wasHerland.
