3.5 stars. 1/2 star for Larry McMurtry

I wanted to love this book. I was prepared to love it unconditionally. I loved the movie years ago, I love the author, I read all the 5 star reviews, I was so ready. But then I met Aurora Greenway and despised her. She was an over-the-top narcissistic attention hound, and we have enough of those on the news everyday. I never understood the men who fell all over themselves trying to attract her. I never understood why her daughter took all her verbal abuse and came back for more. I like quirkiness in characters, but this went way beyond that. What made the movie so affecting took up a grand total of 20 pages at the end. Granted, they were the best 20 pages of the book, but it wasn't enough.

Before I start getting hate mail, I'll just go ahead and admit that my timing on this book was way off. Another place and time might have had me agreeing with all the 5 star reviews for this one. We'll never know, I couldn't take another exposure to Aurora Greenway. My final thought is that this is one where the movie was better.
