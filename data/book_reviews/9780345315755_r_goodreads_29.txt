Every single adventure story you've read or B-movie plot you've seen is within the pages of this book. As much as I enjoyed the pulp fiction of the Legend of Tarzan, the Return of Tarzan gets more and more fantastical. By now I have worked out that Edgar Rice Burroughs is writing about a superhero, not a real man by any means. The plots get sillier and sillier and, boy, it seems Tarzan gets from one scrape right into another. King of the Apes...check, chief of black savages...yep, French diplomat...you betcha, discoverer of ancient civilizations...check again, champion swimmer...absolutely. Everyone wants to kill him or dies trying. At some points I found myself skimming through the long winded narrative when our author gets carried away with yet another tall tale of Tarzan's heroism. It became predictable and the ridiculousness of it all had me rolling my eyes.
