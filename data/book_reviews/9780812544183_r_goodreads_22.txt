I looked it up and this was originally a gigantic 900 page book broken into two volumes and published separately. I don't think this was a great idea seeing as how the pacing and events of this one play out like half a book. The first 100 pages are pretty fast paced plot wise, but the rest is them hanging out adjusting to their new lives and planning the future. The future I'm assuming will be the next book. I did enjoy the it, but that's because the characters are interesting, but seeing as how this is essentially half a story, there was zero conflict. Just a small conversation about the plans for the next one.They should have been published together.
