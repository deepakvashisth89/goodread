John Brown was dedicated absolutely and radically to two things: (1) his faith in Jesus Christ, and (2) the abolition of slavery through any means necessary. I knew before this book that he and several of his sons incited violent insurrections and murdered for their cause; I'd heard but didn't believe how exceptionally pious he was, and I didn't know how tender he was toward his family and animals, how fond they were of him.I see now why W.E.B. DuBois is revered for his writing. I have to admit, though, that the first quarter was my favorite, because it was an intimate biography of the man of John Brown, whereas the rest was (rightfully) saturated with the language of war that makes my eyes glaze over. This man with this many others went to this town, but THIS man with THIS many militia went to THIS town four miles south to thwart them. Just not my cup of tea.Old John Brown is not uncomplicated, but I like him more for having read this.- Black authors for Black History Month #6 -
