See, this book is much better than theKanji Starterbooks. For one, the pictures for the most part remain true to their meaning and do not venture off into more abstract areas to explain the kanji. If a kanji is made of dog and man, the picture will be of a dog and a man and have a little phrase or sentence explaining the meaning. Another reason this is better than theKanji Startersis that it gives both thekunand theonreadings while KS gives only thekunand then later expects you to know theon. I like the way the book is arranged and I like the story element.I still can't see these pictures working for me.. well some of them might, but mostly this doesn't work on my brain.However, my brother used this book to learn kana and it worked extremely well for him.
