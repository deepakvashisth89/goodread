Published in 1982, this book is an edited collection of Andy Rooney's remarks and reflections in the closing three of four minute segment of the 60 Minutes TV show in the 1960s and 1970s.The ultimate "dip in" book, I kept a copy in the car to pass the time when my wife ran into a shop on a brief errand or two. Although timely in their day, Mr. Rooney's comments are naturally dated three or four decades, which is hardly Mr. Rooney's fault.If you only have time for one essay, go to page 211 and read his two page remembrance of D-Day 1944 written 35 years after Rooney went ashore on D-Day +4.
