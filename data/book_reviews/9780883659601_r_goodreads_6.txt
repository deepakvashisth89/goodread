This was a loaned book that I quickly picked up only to put it down. I read the introduction, which set the tone for a lackluster read. Byrne sets himself up as a quotable author and wrote about how well he had chosen the quotes - not one could have been omitted. His own words that he included in the book were, as he admitted, self indulgent. The running bit with the "Plato is a bore" was the deal breaker for me. I'm uncertain if I shall pass this book along or return it to the lender.
