So, what did I think about this book? A very good question.Did I enjoy it? Yes. It was —for the most part— what I expected. As i mentioned in my notes for book one, I know the animated series way too well. Both the original broadcast and the later remaster. Obviously this book is based on the original.In my previous review for book one I stated the book follows the series quite closely. In many respects it is a good substitute for watching the series. The same is not true of this book. It vastly condenses whole sections of story and omits others. Entire sections of dialogue are not as they were.This is not to say it is bad. Just different.If that is a problem you can not reconcile, give this a pass. Otherwise if you like Robotech and are okay with it being slightly different, give it a go.
