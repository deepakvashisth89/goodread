First, let's remember that this was written in the early 1980s, as a set of novellas.Publication year 1983 is 35 years ago.Also, it's perhaps/probably aimed at a somewhat younger audience. It follows a young man through Starschool (and no, he doesn't meet a young Jim Kirk or Ender Bean).Our hero is a bit dim, but that actually improves the story. One of his classmates is annoyingly capable, but it's tolerable.In the end, this is a straightforward series of action adventures, capped by a section that actually adds some depth as the team visits a set of nested spheres that allow for multiple civilizations. This is vaguely similar to Jack Chalker's Well of Souls setup, and allows the authors a lot of room - as long as they don't have to explain the logistics of building such a thing.If you've just read a Hannu Rajaniemi or a China Miéville, this would be a good light relief.
