This was such an intense book and an intricate mystery! Well done, Charles Todd! Please keep up the good work! (I say, 16 or so books behind the current release in the series.) Several times, I had to put the Kindle down in shock and amazement and walk away for a moment to deal with the revelations.Note: Either England and Scotland combined form a very small, tightly-knit universe, or there were a lot of converging coincidences.I sometimes wonder if this series has something supernatural going on in Hamish's existence, and I love that it can be taken as straight historical fiction with (fictional?) psychology AND a story where our protagonist is saddled with the presence of a talkative ghost simultaneously, depending on how the reader is feeling. (I know, he says Hamish isn't a ghost, but sometimes I have to wonder). I am LOVING this series. More, please. Also, what an ending!
