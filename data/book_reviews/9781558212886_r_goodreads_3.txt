I ordered this book and its opposite number from "Outdoor Life" magzine in an effort to track down a story I read over 50 years ago in one of these magazines. Sadly the story is still out there, waiting for me to track it down. I'll continue the quest.In the meanwhile, let me say I enjoyed the wonderful stories and writing in this collection. Getting to read tales from Zane Grey, William G. Tapply, Havilah Babcock, Archibald Rutledge, Ted Trueblood, Frank Dufresne, Warrem Page, Gene Hill and Robert Ruark - tales that, for the most part, I read 50 years ago as a youngster, carried me back to the days of "What Might Be." And that's never a bad thing.
