One of the big men's adventure series from the 70's than ran an impressive 145 books. The series while an adventure/action story is also full of satire toward much of the mainstream fads and icons of the time. An interesting main character and the sarcastic mentor makes this a funny action/adventure read. Adolph Kluge is the leader of the secret organization known as IV, a part of the resurgent Fourth Reich. Living in the mountains of Argentina, he has yet another plan for taking over the world and, more importantly, he has the means of funding it. Recommended
