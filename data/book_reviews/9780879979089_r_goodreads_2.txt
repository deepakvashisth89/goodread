This DAW book from 1984 is the 29th novel in Tubb's Dumarest series, which had debuted from Ace in 1967. This one has a serviceable but uninspired cover by Ken W. Kelley, who chose to paint a guy wearing a red robe hopping out of a flying car rather than any of the intriguing and imaginatively described alien creatures or settings that Tubb so ably described. The series is the continuing saga of Earl Dumarest, who roams the spaceways searching for his lost home planet, Earth. He's pursued by the evil Cyclan, a group of computer-minded former humans who want a secret that only he possesses that will enable them to take over the galaxy. This one is pretty atypical in several ways. Earl has to fight in the arena, but loses, for example, and he encounters the Church Brotherhood but pretty much dismisses them as Pacifist losers rather than working with them. The title of many of the earlier books was the name of a female character with whom Earl has a relationship, but the Angado of this story is a gay man with whom Earl travels and befriends. Earl does meet a woman near the end of the book with whom he has a brief but passionate romance, but it's really Angado's story that's the focus. The ending is something of a cliffhanger, as Earl is still on Angado's planet but is headed off to find the man who may have information about Earth's location. It's one of the best of the later books in the series that I've read.
