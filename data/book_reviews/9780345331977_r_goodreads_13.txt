“Evil is a point of view. We are immortal. And what we have before us are the rich feasts that conscience cannot appreciate and mortal men cannot know without regret. God kills, and so shall we; indiscriminately He takes the richest and the poorest, and so shall we; for no creatures under God are as we are, none so like Him as ourselves, dark angels not confined to the stinking limits of hell but wandering His earth and all its kingdoms.”Interview with the Vampire by Anne Rice is a mesmerizing and haunting exploration of immortality, morality, and the complexities of the human condition. Set in 18th-century New Orleans, the novel follows the charismatic vampire Louis de Pointe du Lac as he recounts his extraordinary life to a curious journalist.Rice's lush prose and richly detailed world-building immerse readers in a dark and atmospheric tale that transcends the typical conventions of the vampire genre. Through Louis' introspective narrative, Rice delves deep into themes of loneliness, desire, and the eternal struggle between good and evil.The character of Lestat, Louis' enigmatic and unpredictable maker, adds an electrifying dynamic to the story, while the exploration of their complex relationship provides a gripping emotional core.Interview with the Vampire is a timeless classic that continues to captivate readers with its intricate plot, vivid imagery, and profound exploration of the human psyche. My favourite of the genre.My Highest Recommendation.
