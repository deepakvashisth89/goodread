3,5 starsTroublemakeris the third case of Dave Branstetter and though I liked the first two books more than the third one, I can assure you - it was not the writing that influenced my rating.The writingof Joseph Hansen is masterful and one of his biggest talent.His books are less suited for the reading in-between.You have to forget everyday life around you to fully concentrate on his wording to detect the small jewels that are hidden on every page.The inconspicuous things like:Something had offended her and failed to apologize. Not lately-long ago. Life, probably.or:Cement trucks climbed dirt trails, tanks turning, turning, like pregnant iron girls in sleep.His sense of perfectionism made him to re-write some of the passages sometimes over 30 times and you feel his great attention to detail in every line.The mysteryinTroublemakerwas actually good, but not as good as in the previous books. I think that my biggest problem was the number of the murder suspects. Toward the end of the book it became hard to follow the story and I had to page back to understand who was going and what was happening, whom Dave was talking with and why he suspected this person at all.My next personal problem becamesex scenes. Or better to say totally absence of them. In spite of the fact, thatTroublemakeris the mostgaycase of Dave Brandstetter until now- he tries to find a murderer of a gay bar owner Rick Wendell - it is also the moststerilestory until now. I know,Dave Brandstetter's Mysteryis in the first place a gaymystery. And I knew what I was getting into. I am not complaining about almost non-existent romance part. But I needat leastone BJ or FUCK or ASS or COCK or DICK, SOMETHING, that brings a little, tiny, small, invisible - I don't ask for more, you see!- joy, satisfaction for my naughty part. You won't find it here. And though I said already in my previous reviews that I was very surprised how up-to date and modern the stories appeared, it is the point where I consider the series a bit old-fashioned. It is like in the old black and white movies: The bedroom doors would be closed in front of your nose, and all you have is your own imagination. InTroublemakerJoseph Hansen didn't let us to come even close to the bedroom door. And I need SEX in my MM books. I miss it here.I have to pause for something hot, silly, less sophisticated and overloaded with sex and then I'll be back to Dave.I hope that my tiny problems will be solved in some way in his further cases.But the writing and the mystery is totally worth it to try.Still HIGHLY recommended.
