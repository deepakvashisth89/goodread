This was the first published book I read (i.e., not slash or a free story I found online) that was gay erotica. Some of the stories were a bit odd to me ~ coming from a slash background, it seemed beyond me why authors would write stories containing sex and little else. But a few gems stood out in the collection. One in particular I remember (I don't recall the title) about nameless lovers who wake in bed and share an intimate moment together. Overall, a good selection of stories featuring some of the big names in gay erotica.
