This book was similar to another book I’ve read not so long ago called “Shadowmaker”. Both have the same feel. The main character is a high school girl. There is a lot of talk about boys and babies. The suspense part of the story was okay but the interrelationships between characters and dialogue drove me crazy. I do not like reading about teenage romance - it makes me cringe. I also did not like that in this story, there is a notion that a girl is mostly defined by the boy she is going out with. I prefer seeing girls with strong personalities who make something with their lives and who are passionate about something other than boys. I did finish this book just to find out how it all resolves although Karen’s mom’s attitude to life completely infuriated me. The ending was so cringingly sweet, I had goosebumps.
