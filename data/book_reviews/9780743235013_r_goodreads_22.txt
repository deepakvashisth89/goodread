A three-part perspective book that shows us the very different takes on occurrences within a dysfunctional doctor's family. First we see things through the eyes of Nina, the widowed daughter. The last section gives us Andrew, the single son's point of view. Most interesting for me was the middle section - seen through the mother/wife's eyes.A slow read at times, but generally good. Structurally, I would have put the mom's section last, as it tied things together - merging the past and present. I understand the point the author was trying to get across by not including a section for the Doctor's view, but I think the story suffered for its absence.
