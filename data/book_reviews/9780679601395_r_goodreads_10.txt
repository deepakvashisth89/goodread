Perhaps I would have enjoyed this more if I had not read The Human Stain first.

In Invisible Man, there is an unnamed narrator who is a member of the Black community. He initially attends a Black college and then moves to New York.

First, I just don’t enjoy unnamed main characters. I find it confusing and difficult to refer to The Unnamed in my notes.

Second, this felt a bit too long, and almost all of the characters were undeveloped with the exception of Norton and Bledsoe. This is probably why I enjoyed the first half of the book more than the second half.
