I love the teachings of Omraam Mikhael Aivanhov. I began studying his teachings in the late 80s, and followed many of his spiritual suggestions such attending the sunrise each morning.(He writes about the power of early morning rays!) He calls many of his teachings, "The Living Book of Nature" and makes connections between us and the life around us.Omraam's teachings were oral and many of his students made certain his words would not be forgotten. Although I've never been one for gurus, I consider Omraam a teacher and a fine one at that. He has many books available and most are timeless and very worthwhile as a read. Sometimes, his teachings may appear "out there" for some...such as bees being from Venus. It works for me.This book was no different. Reading Omraam helps one connect with the spiritual world and how our thoughts and their powers for good or bad affect us and others. Great inspirational read!
