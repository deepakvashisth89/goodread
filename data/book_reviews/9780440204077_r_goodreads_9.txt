No doubt the book contains a number of old-fashioned, literary, and even obsolete words that have not only fallen out of use and favour, but which even native speakers of English might not understand: I find it well worth the time and effort for anyone who loves words.The book contains a fine selection of recherché words with precise meanings that have long been forgotten, yet are still useful today. Readers weary of clichés and banal expressions will find the book’s presentation of synonyms a delightful way to overcome the tedium that pervades much of today’s novels and non-fiction writing.
