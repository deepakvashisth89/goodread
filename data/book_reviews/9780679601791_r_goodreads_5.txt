I love A Christmas Carol. Every film adaption from the black and white one to the muppets.But I have never read the story. So this year I vowed to finally read the original.Along with a few of Dickens’ other Christmas writings I really enjoyed it.I won’t bother with a synopsis, as everyone knows the story more or less. It was wonderful to read and be reminded of what makes this such a powerful tale of kindness and generosity to our fellow human at this time of the year.
