Started of as a fun light read, but I feel it covered the really dark aspects of the war really well. It didn't shy away from the absolute cruelty of the invading Japanese, but also kept the elements of belonging and found family, and how war corrupts all and there is no true evil.Hearing about the story it was based on was quite sad, because in reality the American POWs never did escape the prison.I think it's a 3 1/2 for me, but I bumped it up to 4.
