This was written in the 1970s by a dude who went to Eton & you can tell. He's not big on naming + dating his sources, so it sometimes reads more like high fantasy than biography. According toErnst Badian's reviewit's rife with mistakes from a scholarship perspective; as someone who knew almost 0 about Alexander going in, accuracy would've been lost on me either way. The descriptions (of military campaigns, battles, geography, and broader Greek society and philosophy at the time) were absolutely fascinating. How they waged war during a time where they hadn't even invented stirrups was crazy. Even for minute events, there's analysis of numerous sources/plausible timelines, from the respected to obscure, and what Fox believes are their biases/limitations as well. He kinda shits on other historians which is funny
