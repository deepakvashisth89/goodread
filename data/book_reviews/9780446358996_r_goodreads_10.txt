In the long run, Macdonald's reputation will rest on his late novels -- though many readers probably will never get this far. It's a pity.This book, Macdonald's penultimate, is flawless. For most of the novel, Archer is operating in his increasingly familiar role of 'therapist with a P.I. license'. But in the meantime, Macdonald has been weaving what ultimtely turns out to be one of his best and tightest mystery plots. The writing, plotting, and character handling are all superb.
