Although this is an older text and people in photos are in 1970s clothes, it is a truly remarkable tale of how a gorilla who signed with his caregiver and asked for a cat for Christmas. He is introduced to a kitten and clearly loves and cares for the kitten, but the kitten is hit by a car and dies. It takes a while until a replacement kitten is found. Just fascinating glimpse into a gorilla's mind, emotions and connections.Could be part of a text set on animals for 4th grade. I would include it in a classroom library for 2nd through 6th grade.
