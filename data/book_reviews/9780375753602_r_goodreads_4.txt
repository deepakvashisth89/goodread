Reading this book was interesting with the given perspective. It took familiar events that I was moderately aware of and provided a unique point of view from the eyes of Richard Holbrooke. The book covered diplomacy and political strategy that you just could not read elsewhere. Holbrooke spoke of his strategical mission in which was a risk that may have redefined the entire Clinton presidency. There was no saying if his mission would be a success. The book was not the easiest read but had value nonetheless.
