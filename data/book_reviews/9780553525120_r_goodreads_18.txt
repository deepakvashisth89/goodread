One of the most tiring books I have ever read. Will not read again, I certainly wouldn't recommend it to someone else.The points that anoyed me with this book where the following:1. Random historical names were popping up like daisies, with some vague connection to the story.2. Backround themes (such as secret meanings) that weren't properely explained.3. Generic, unintresting heroine that somehow managed to be extremely talented, clever and beautiful (as described by the book) despite her lack of character.4. Sex (no reason, no backstory, no relationship, just sex).5. Wrongfull usage of ancient greek words (some of them, with as much reserve as I can muster, I consider made up).SPOILER ALERT!!!6. Random family members and sexual relationships between them are uncovered in numbers (more than 10 new relatives and/or family connections between existing characters, as well as close to 20 sexual relationships with absolutely no depth or meaning and certainly without doing some service to the plotline).7. Bullshit (in my opinion) twist(s).END SPOILER ALERT!!!To sum up, the magic circle isn't a book I enjoyed in anyway (even by trying to laugh at it). So many hours lost. Would not recommend.
