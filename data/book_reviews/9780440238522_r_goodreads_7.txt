Read this book when I was a kid and searched for it again to confirm it wasn't a fever dream. Made me realize younger me didn't realize how explicit this book actually was. Is it a quick and fun read? I can't lie, it was. Something about it makes you so eager to see what comes next.It's hard to discover what the author's intentions were. Was this written in a way to expose the over sexualization of girls based on the bad influences around them or was it simply written in a "my sexy neighbor wants me" way. I want to give the author the benefit of the doubt and say it's meant to give some sort of lesson but I don't know...
