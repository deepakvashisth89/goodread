The story begins with Laurie Kenyon as a young child of 4, who was told not to go near the street because one of the teenager neighbors had a habit of driving down that same street way too fast. However, one day there was a parade and when the babysitter was on the phone, her curiosity got the better of her. Laurie sat on the curve watching the parade until Bic and Opal came and swept her up and placed her in-between them in the car. Laurie had her music box that was playing “All around the Town”. Bic and Opal took off and kept Laurie for around 2 years at a house in disrepair in the country, with Bic sexually abusing Laurie. After two years they dropped her off at a school at night knowing she would be found in the morning. When Bic and Opal left her they told her never to talk about where she was or she would be killed, and she kept her promise out of fear.Advance quite a few years and Laurie is starting college. Both of her parents get killed in a car accident and the trauma sets her off. She turns to a professor and he ends up murdered. Laurie starts going to a psychiatrist and we find out she has multiple personalities. Did one of the personalities kill the professor? While all this is going on, the kidnappers, Bic and Opal are back in Laurie’s life, pursuing her because Bic is still obsessed with Laurie.I enjoyed reading this immensely. Yes, many books have been done on multiple personality disorders, but most of the characters in the book are warm and inviting and Bic and Opal seem far too real.
