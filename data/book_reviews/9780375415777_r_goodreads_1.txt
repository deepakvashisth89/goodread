Another reread, these books just flow, one to the next, and reading them is soothing, not because any one book reaches new literary heights, but because the quality of the writing is so high and so consistent, because the characters are familiar enough to feel like friends. An absolute go-to when I want to lose myself for a few hours but don't want to commit mental energy to anything new.This one has a delightful chase and battle involving a pirate, however, and Jack Aubrey outwits his enemies at sea yet again, which, as a fan of the character, always makes me happy.
