The book is a melancholy meditation on the meaning of love at a time when it could often become synonymous with death.The structure of the book is intricate, telling the story of the three main characters over two years. There are two alternating strands in the book. One set originally set in Provincetown in 1994 and another set in Boston about 6 month later. Eventually, as the storylines catch up with each other, the locations of the strands are swapped and the elements of the story poetically merge into one.
