The first book in the Sean King & Michelle Maxwell series, and a good start to a seemingly good series! The book started out kind of slow, and jumped around a lot, and hard to follow, but after about 100 pages it started to pick up, then it got really good!Split Second follows former US Secret Service Agent Sean King who's career ended when a Political Candidate who he was guarding is gunned down right in front of him, and failed to stop the assassin. Now 8 years later, young Secret Service Agent Michelle Maxwell is guarding another Political Candidate, who had vanished without a trace while Maxwell was supposed to be guarding him at all times. Now with Michelle's career on the line, she teams up with Sean King who is now working for a small law firm after his career with the Secret Service ended. Learning of King's past and seeing similarities between the present disappearance of one candidate, and the assassination of another 8 years earlier, King and Maxwell form a Lethal Weapon-type partnership and work to find the missing candidate, and discover the motive of the assassination 8 years earlier, and also learning of a deadly conspiracy.A great first entry to the series, now onto book #2, Hour Game!
