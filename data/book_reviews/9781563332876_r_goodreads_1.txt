I came too late to appreciate Stan Leventhal's books while he was alive. Sadly, he died of HIV in Jan 1995 and this book was published posthumously in March 1995. This is a continuation of his semi-biographical earlier book..."Mountain Climbing on Sheridan Square". This book is a lot deeper as Stan is clearly dealing with mortality and an HIV diagnosis. He writes from the point of view of a "Gay Everyman"..His life is neither glamorous nor does he travel in rarefied circles. That's what makes his writing so special to me..I feel we could have been friends....The book contains a wonderful tribute to his family and to friends whom he lost from AIDS. This book is rare, expensive and out of print, but its warm humanity is a bargain at any price.
