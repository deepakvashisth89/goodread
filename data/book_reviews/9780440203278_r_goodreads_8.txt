The murder mystery in this novel is almost secondary. I was most interested in the meditations on this fascinating period in Britain between the wars. Pacifism, fascism, village politics, class clashes, the end of the era of the landed gentry, the abdication of King Edward, the looming specter of war - all were wrapped up in the story of Sarah’s stay with the Hallams. As a narrator, Sarah was insightful and honest. The glimpses into the future were reassuring, too.
