'Life Before Man' is Margaret Atwood's fourth novel (published in 1979).This time around, Atwood tells us the story of Elizabeth, Nate and Lesje and to a lesser extent Chris (although mainly in absentia).It's a story of collapsing relationships, extra marital affairs, complex relationships and equally complex separations.This is Atwood now really getting into her stride as a great writer, still not at her zenith, but decidedly on her way there. 'Life Before' is extremely well conceived and written - it's told by way of very short chapters, each from the point of view of one of the characters, each one dated and generally sequencially in date.Every chapter, and this is testament to the standard of the writing, feels almost like a standalone short story in it's own right.'Life Before Man' I suspect is often overlooked by readers of Margaret Atwood - which is something that it should most definitely not be.Not classic Atwood - but well on it's way.
