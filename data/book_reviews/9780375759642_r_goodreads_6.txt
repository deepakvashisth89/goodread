Fitzgerald wasn't joking with that title. These people were completely screwed from the moment they hit the page, and it was fascinating to watch it all disintegrate. As I mentioned in the review I just finished forTender is the Night, I found Anthony and Gloria to be some of the more unsympathetic characters I've encountered lately. They are both vain and shallow and utterly useless people in terms of anything practical. I can't imagine being friends with these people. This book worked for me primarily on the level that Fitzgerald managed to keep me hoping that they'd finally get their act together. Of course they don't, but that's not really the point. I kept caring about them even though I didn't particularly like them. I wanted to plead with the judge to give them the money, even though my better sense told me the money really wasn't as much of the problem as they were.I really enjoyed this book - also, an excellent cautionary tale on how not to handle your personal finances.
