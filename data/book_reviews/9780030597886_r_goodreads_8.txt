These strips come from after Reagan's landslide victory over Carter. The title isn't to suggest that Reagan has no brain, but simply that he isn't think or look at the world as normal people might. It's a actually a small part of the over all book, but the provocative title probably made it sell better. Here we have Zonker's competitive tanning, Mike and Joannie's meeting and first date, Honey testifying at the Chinese Gang of Four Showtrial and a lot more.
