Somewhat lyrical... and somewhat tangential.

This is a classic theology piece from the Catholic Reformation period, poetically written by a Spanish friar/priest and mystic. At some points, it was soothing. And at others, it's difficult to focus on. (Particularly when some of the sentences run nearly the length of a page.) x.x

The gist seems to be that those who truly long for and strive to attain some form of union with God here on Earth, suffering is (unfortunately) not optional. And not just suffering, but learning to endure with patient humility. I don't disagree with St. John-of-the-Cross' assessment. Though I think he's a bit overly repetitious in his methodology. But, I suppose one could argue he is simply driving the point home...
