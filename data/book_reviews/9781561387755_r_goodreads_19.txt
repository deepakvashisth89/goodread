Im never really done with this cookbook (or any of the Mollie Katzen cookbooks I have). I got this in 1992, and I have used it regularly since then (2/3rd of my life). The banana bread recipe is still my go to (honestly I don't think I've ever made a different version).. I know the spinach and feta and sundried tomato recipe by heart, the carrot cake helped celebrate my baby's first....
I got this cookbook BEFORE my mom gave me the Joy of Cooking (albeit I used hers at that time), and years before my other go to, How to Cook Everything by Mark Bittman was out.
It's been a constant companion in every kitchen I've ever lived in, the source of winning arguments (homemade tabouli is best), a billion compliments, and it's easy and charming design makes cooking feel comfortable.
I found an older copy in better condition recently at a used bookstore, and bought it, I have a collection of essential cookbooks that I am doubling up on to give to my kid some day. So far I've got two Moosewoods (meesewood?).
