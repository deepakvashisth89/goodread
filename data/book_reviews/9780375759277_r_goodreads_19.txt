2.5. About halfway through this book, I accepted that I just found it extremely dull and I was not enjoying reading it. There's a reason I've never read (or known anyone who has read) Howells before. I own one more short book by him, but other than that, I don't foresee ever reading him again. He was mostly concerned with moralizing and social commentary, and it was just blatantly clear how he invented characters and storylines to give himself an excuse to do so, and they had no greater depth.
Got slightly more interesting at the end.
