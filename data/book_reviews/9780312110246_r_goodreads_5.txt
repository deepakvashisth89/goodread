After twenty years and two aborted attempts, I have finally finished this book.Spoilers: I didn't read everything. While there are some really great pieces like "Lest Levitation Come Upon Us" by Sizette Haden Elgin and John Brunner's "The Things That Are Gods", by and large these are not "masterpieces", and the stories grow tiresome as one approaches the end of the collection. Call me myopic, but quite a lot of the styles represented here went out of fashion a long time ago, and there's a reason for it.
