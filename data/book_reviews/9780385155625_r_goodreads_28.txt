So wildly implausible it was funny. A nice little romp with great characters and so funny too. Loved Professor Svenson, he reminded me of Terry Pratchett's Archchancellor Ridcully of Unseen University. Must be a real-life type wandering about the academic wilds.I read the Amazon Kindle book, and luckily I got it for 1.99 during a deal. It's so badly edited with misspellings and strange punctuations peppered all over the book. If that stuff drives you nuts, find another edition. I would hesitate to get another of this particular publisher's book.
