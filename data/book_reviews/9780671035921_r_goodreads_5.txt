The first half took me a while to get through, and I can't really remember why. I'll attest it to the fact that the setup is both weird and complex and requires a lot of world building, which slows down the pace. However, the second half of the book made it well worth it! I finished the second half within a day.No matter how trashy this book sounds, Shimerman and Scott pull it off with a healthy mix of humour and seriousnes, great characters and absolutely beautiful prose.
