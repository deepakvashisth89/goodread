Back on familiar territory - this was a cozy, charming whodunit!! Loved the Cape Cod setting. Loved the characters! Thank the Lord for female authors who write nuanced and clever female characters (especially in the 1930s) - Prue, Betsey, Dot and Emma all incorporate and yet break the stereotypical female roles. The dialogue was delightful. I’d read the next Asey Mayo in a heartbeat!Reflecting on the golden age of crime fiction … it almost seems impossible in today’s world to write a whodunit where the murderer could actually be apart of the story and also escape detection. Cameras, phones, internet, tracking … all of it measured and tracked and stored. How does one write a good whodunit in light of this reality? Life was so much simpler (but also more difficult) in the 1920s and 30s.
