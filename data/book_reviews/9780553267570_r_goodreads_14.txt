I honestly don't what to say about this book & I struggled to give a rating. I finally settled on 4★

I know I read this one many years ago & absolutely loved it. I think what I liked was that Charlie (Charmian) was the main protagonist. This time I can appreciate what a wonderful writer le Carré is, but I had a few problems with it.

Not le Carré's fault, but now isn't the time I want to be reading about terrorism, spying & innocents suffering. (Yes, I should have realised this before I started the book!) Sadly, some of this almost felt current!



Sound familiar?

The book was very slow moving at the start & some of it was quite misogynistic. Charlie was a sexually free woman of the 80s & was repeatedly (even on the dust jacket by the blurb writer) defined as promiscuous. Not a description given to any of the young men. With this book her sexuality defined Charlie even though she was many other things as well.
