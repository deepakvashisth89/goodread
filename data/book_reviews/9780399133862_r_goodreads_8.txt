A collection of letters from returning Vietnam veterans describing the different ways in which they were received and, indirectly, their feelings about the war and how it affected them. The letters were written in the late 1980s, some 15 to 20 years after their return, but they vividly reflect the poisonous long-term effect the war had on the veterans and our society in general. I strongly suspect that if those letters were to be written now, 50 years after the war's end, they would read much the same. The effects of Vietnam will continue long after the last veteran has passed.
