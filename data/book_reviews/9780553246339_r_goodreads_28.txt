I JUST WROTE AN ABSOLUTELY GIGANTIC REVIEW FOR THIS IN MY SEPARATE DOCUMENT AND IM GONNA TRY NOT TO WRITE SUCH A LONG ONE HERE..OMG! ok so i accidentally skimmed a few reviews while i was reading this book and they mentioned it being misogynistic, and i actually could absolutely see what they meant by that..... but unfortunately i do feel like that is a frequent experience i have reading the especially older star trek books... BUT BESIDES THAT OBSERVATION, I WENT INTO THIS BIASED AS HELLLLLL cause i freaking LOVED Death's Angel, so when i saw this author at the store i GRABBED THIS SO FAST... and even with its many flaws, i have to say, i LOVED THIS......it felt so damn trope-y and peculiar.... in such a good-bad way.. so hard to describe this really.... the psychoanalyzing from mccoy in the beginning as well as his relationship with dr tremain is as entertaining as it is weird, and then the dynamic with SPOCK.. i have to admit, the second half was way too fun for me... i REALLY love kathleen sky's characterization for all the canon characters, ESPECIALLY spock and mccoy, and i also REALLY LOVED chapel being here and having her moment... even though that entire section of the book was the particularly misogynistic part of the book imo.. there's a tone of it throughout the whole book really.. but listen i have to give it a pass because i REALLY LIKE kathleen's star trek books... SHE ONLY HAD TWO :( so i have to cherish this..... even if the romulans are portrayed as extremely comically ridiculous villains (not in a good way, in an embarrassing way), the shitty and used situation that's the source of dr tremain's vulcan racism, or the sort of weird ooc pervert-ness to spock's character a couple of times.... ITS GUILTILY ENJOYABLE...there were several parts i was laughing out loud so hard.. i could mention specific pages with specific parts (there are a few very funny parts i enjoyed as a spones/mcspirk fan.... i always gotta point these moments out at some point cause it always makes me giggle..) but I GUESS I WONT... THIS IS A LONG ENOUGH AND DISJOINTED ENOUGH REVIEW ALREADY....! just know i DO enjoy a cheesy romance with mccoy and spock as the love interests, and i DO enjoy a dying feverish spock being taken care of and mindmelded with, and possibly most importantly, I LOVE THE CRAZY ANT INSECT CREATURES!!!!!!!! IT WAS HALF THE REASON I SPECIFICALLY PICKED THIS OUT AT THE STORE (the other half being kathleen ofc!) AND, ALTHOUGH I AM GENUINELY ANNOYED BY THE FACT THE ARTWORK DOESNT ACCURATELY DEPICT THE DESCRIPTION FOR VIRTUALLY ANYTHING (the arachnians, dr tremain's hair/skin tone, her outfit) IT LOOKS COOL SO IM LIKE FINE!!!!!!!!! this book and kathleen's other book, Death's Angel, both had such enjoyable and descriptively written characters that i always want to DRAW HOW I IMAGINE THEM... AND THATS HOW I KNOW ITS A GOOD BOOK!
