Banger. Jeri Taylor you legend. Read just like an episode of Voyager with some fun flashbacks into Janeway's youth. Really enjoyed knowing where every other chapter would land me - Janeway youth or the current predicament.

A fun read that was exactly an action book from the 90s + fun character analysis. There is nothing subtle about this book but who cares!

Would recommend to anyone who is a fan of the show, and particularly a fan of Captain Janeway.
