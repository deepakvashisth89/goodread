This was such a charming little book! L. Frank Baum is best known for his Wizard of Oz series, and I was surprised to find out he also wrote this origin story of Santa Claus. It's an atmospheric children's book written as Santa Claus's biography. It also has imaginative explanations for the origins of many Christmas traditions such as stockings, Christmas trees, etc.

I haven't read many (or any?) other Christmas children's books, so don't have much to compare it to. But I'm so glad I picked it up! I found it delightful. The little Penguin Christmas Classics edition was pretty too, and had black-and-white illustrations I liked. I'll definitely pick up a few more of these editions to read next Christmas season.
