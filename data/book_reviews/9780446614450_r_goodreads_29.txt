“There’s no greater chaos than when swift, violent death knocks on the door of an unsuspecting crowd.”In my search for new Thriller/Mystery authors Baldacci crossed my path and in a split second I decide that there will be more of him in my reading life.This one is fast paced with characters that entertain and a plot filled with twist and turns.I’m intrigued to see where King and Maxwell will go with their new PI endeavour. Adding the next book in the series to my TBR! Also, I’ll be looking into other works of Baldacci.Just realised there’s a TV adaptation as well… My Baldacci is going to keep me busy.
