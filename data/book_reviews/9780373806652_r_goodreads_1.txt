"Corporate Affair" is the story of Kalinda and Rand.

SO GOOD!

Ah Ms James. You write fantastic books!

The story has our heroine travelling to a remote island. Her plan: to lure and humiliate her married ex to take revenge for his past insults. Instead she meets the hero, a local artist and fisherman, who instantly takes to her. Soon he coerces her already melting resistance, and the heroine ends up in his bed. But while she takes him to be her weekend fling, she soon realizes his real identity and how mistaken she is..

Super obsessed, jealous possessive hero- strong but confused heroine- super hot lovemaking scenes, minimal drama and HEA.

Totally enjoyed it!
