Parisian Encounters: Great Loves and Grand Passions focuses on eight relationships that all have Paris as their common bond: Napoleon and Josephine, George Sand and Frederic Chopin, Gustave Flaubert and Louise Colet, Mary Cassatt and Edgar Degas, Auguste Rodin and Camille Claudel, Marie and Pierre Curie, Man Ray and Lee Miller, Anais Nin and Henry Miller. The book is spiral bound and split in such a way that the reader opens the book to a collage: a sampling of a letter and a sketch of the two persons pulls the pages together. There is also a plan de Paris along with a timeline of each of the lives portrayed.
