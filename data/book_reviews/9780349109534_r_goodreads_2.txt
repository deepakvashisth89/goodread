4.5, rounded up.Having just read Stephen Spender'sThe Temple, I was interested in this, since Spender successfully sued Leavitt for copyright infringement, claiming he had appropriated sections of Spender's autobiography for his own novel. It's a thorny subject, and Leavitt's publisher withdrew the initial print run, Leavitt revised the sections under contestation, and it was subsequently released in this revised format.Despite all the mishegoss over that, the book itself is eminently readable and quite fascinating. Spender objected not only that a portion of his life had been co-opted, but that the book itself was salacious, bordering on pornographic.
