Susan Hauser and I became friends at Bowling Green State Univ. She was an alumna & I was an older "non-traditional" student working towards a degree in Creative writing. I have already reviewed her book "Girl to Woman" and I am happy to review it again. Susan lives in Minnesota with her husband and son. She allows the coldness of Minnesota winters to creep into some of her poems. These poems are in the epilogue at the end of the book where she has witten the poetry of loss, grieving and coming to terms with reality. Susan's 8 year old son was killed in an accident. When my daughter passed, I turned again to Susan's poems and esays. She also writes of protests of the VietNam war and rallies for Womens' Rights.
