As if you didn't know... This is a novelization of a screenplay which was written by Lawrence Kasdan and Leigh Brackett, based on a story by George Lucas. I believe it's the best of the many Star Wars films. The adaptation is fine, is neither adds much to what ended up in the film nor leaves much of anything out. There are a couple of differences (for example, Yoda is blue and Han's "I know" is missing), probably because Glut had an earlier version of the script with which to work. It might have been a good idea to get a writer with a better science fiction background to do it; Alan Dean Foster did the first one well. Sadly, both Brackett (who was one of the best science fiction writers of the time) and her husband Edmond Hamilton (who was another) died before the film was completed. Oh well, it was a long, long time ago... you know.
