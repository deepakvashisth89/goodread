I saw the movie version of this years ago and loved it. A friend recommended to me then that I needed to read the novel because it was so much better. I think I must of forgotten how much the friend and I used to differ on the quality of books. This was a sheer act of will to complete this very long stream of consciousness. It was as though the author fancied himself to be John Steinbeck but unable to decide whether he was writing "Cannery Row" or "Grapes of Wrath." Maybe if I had read the book prior to the movie? I don't know. This was a disappointment to me.
