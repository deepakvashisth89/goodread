A reread from my junior high years of a rousing adventure set amid the War of the Roses.
Young Dick Shelton’s world collapses when black arrows from the forest outlaws expose his guardian, Sir Daniel Brackley, as his father’s murderer. Fleeing for his life, Dick teams up with the disguised heiress Joanna Sedley. Together they join the Black Arrow fellowship and the Yorkist forces under Richard Crookback, racing through ambushes, sea raids, and battles to free Joanna, claim justice, and win their future.
Fast paced, romantic, and full of heart, the novel follows Dick’s transformation from a trusting youth to courageous knight who chooses honor and mercy over revenge. The novel delivers thrills, loyalty, and a satisfying triumph of love and justice.
