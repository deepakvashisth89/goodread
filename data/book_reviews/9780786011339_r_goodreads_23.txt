It's always interesting to read how criminals are caught. Especially those who commit murder. Not just any murder but violet murders. Even more interesting is diving into serial murder cases. I think it's just human nature to be curious about such things.Fred Rosen, in my opinion, did a great job in this book putting you inside the case of Kendall Francois. He also succeeded in doing what many people can't by showing you that prostitutes are still women, still humans, still people with lives, families, hopes and dreams. He also explains very well, the details of how it really happens in real life. The details that classify a serial murderer and how they determine everything in their profiling and prepare and execute trials.If you enjoy true crime, I recommend this book. With the details of the case, Rosen puts you IN the lives of everyone who had to deal with what Francois did, including those who didn't survive his horrible crimes.
