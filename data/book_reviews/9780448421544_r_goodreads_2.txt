In this story Corduroy is getting ready to celebrate Easter but ends up questioning the existence of the Easter bunny! This is a cute book featuring the classic Corduroy I loved growing up!This is super cool to me because I was a big fan of Corduroy when I was younger. I believe this was my 1st time reading this and I enjoyed it! I think most kids go through that questioning stage when it comes to things like the Easter bunny, Santa, and the tooth fairy so it is a book I would like in my classroom!I would discuss different holidays and traditions in class to tie the book into a lesson!
