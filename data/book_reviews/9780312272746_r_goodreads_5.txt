This story is well written and because it is based on real experiences it becomes a heart wrenching tribute to those who died and lived through the holocaust. There were some places where cuss words were used so if you are extra sensitive to that be aware. I felt that in all but one instance it was a natural extension of the real life people's speech given the areas, culture, language and situations and was thus not bothered by it.I picked up my copy at a library sale and am glad that I did. This is a great reminder of what was taken from so many during the holocaust. Like other reviewers have said, it is a great book, full of heartache and trials. I cried. And I learned this book is a keeper.
