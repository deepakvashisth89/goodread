Pretty fun! And I think even better, something that’s instantly reinvested me in the Wild Cards universe enough to want to continue forward.

I’m sure harder core WC people probably don’t like this one because it’s more lower key and doesn’t really feature any “marquee” characters aside from Croyd and Demise (and even they are like co-stars at best in their respective stories), but for my money this probably should be what these should be doing like all the time.

And the concept of the “mosaic novel” too really charmed the hell out of me. Basically it’s just a themed story collection with an ongoing framing device but I was very struck at how well the stories and the framing stuff blended together and all felt like one thing.

Raleigh Jackson, daughter of former b-list Ace Aurora, is trying to make it in the big city as the new cub reporter for ACES! Magazine. Her first assignment? A slice-of-life retrospective on some of the more unsung powered-individuals who were in the orbit of various happenings and pop culture moments across the decades.

From there we get a bunch of really fun and interesting stories about people with crappy powers making their ways through NYC across the decades, the Golden Age of Hollywood, and even the moon landing.

And again, if this sounds kinda disparate, it really isn’t! And ends up making you really care about all these weirdoes (and Raleigh) as they navigate this crazy interesting and just plain crazy world and history.

I will certainly be continuing on past even the one I’ve got (unless that one is a total dog) because this one was just low-key, but textured enough to make me jus want to keep exploring this world and it’s bonkers alternative history and its insanely cool characters (something’s that really stuck out to me when I read the original one finally).
