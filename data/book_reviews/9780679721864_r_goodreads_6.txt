This is not a literary book, but rather a collection of essays, supplements to his novels about the camps. Thus, much drier in style, but all the more profound. Naturally, this grabs you by the throat. Levi's primary intention was apparently to highlight the world's unwillingness after the Second World War to bring the truth about the camps to light. That was a deliberate strategy that had, of course, already been employed by the Nazis. (rating 3.5 stars)
