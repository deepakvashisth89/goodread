Rose and Lila’s parents are doctors and are traveling to Africa for a year, leaving the sisters to stay with Far Nana and her four cats. In this book, the girls Rose and Lila wonder if Far Nana is poor, and go to their new school for the first time.Far Nana is a doll doctor. I really loved how the book tells the way Far Nana repairs them, goes through the process of what materials she is using and why, what she does with it, etc. The educational factor is nice. Also, I appreciate the glossary of terms used and more info in the back about The Great Depression.And on top of that - Far Nana can hear dolls when they speak. In this book, she gets Goldie's story...The story had promise. I usually like children's books that talk about historical events, and I thought this series might be one I'd like. I love the idea of getting a story from the doll’s point of view, which is why I picked up the second book of the series, even though I was iffy with the first book. Goldie's story happened in the Great Depression, which I was hopeful for, but the story was sadly more weird than anything, with some dark themes woven in. (Click here to read the review for the first book:Tatiana Comes To America)As with the first book, I liked the doll's story better than the story of the main characters. And like the first book, I found the way Far Nana communicates with the dolls disturbing because it's almost like the doll possesses her. In this one, there is a mention of a spirit leaving a body and then not knowing what happened to the body. This is a book for young kids and as a Christian, this paranormal stuff bothered me, which is why the low rating. Below are some thoughts that are spoilers:What disturbed me the most in this book was when Eliza sells her doll and the doll's "spirit" left her body and followed Eliza. I almost stopped reading but managed to get to the end, where Goldie's spirit inhabits the new doll Eliza's mom made for her to replace Goldie.This was the point when I made the decision that I was done with this series. I wanted to like it but the spiritual stuff isn't for me.
