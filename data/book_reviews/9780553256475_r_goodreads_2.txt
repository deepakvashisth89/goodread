My interest in this series is flagging. What began as such fun with a Carnaby Street vibe to it has devolved into sort of “sadcap” mystery adventures. I’d managed to acquire about half of these in various used bookshops (and how often is the hunt the best part of it all!), but for now I’ll just read those on hand…Tessa visits her alma mater to serve as a judge in the dramatics presentations during school fete, and a young student she’s just met and liked is soon found dead - it might be suicide, but is it? As no one else seems to care, Tessa is all the more determined to prove that someone so interested in living could not have been desirous of dying. Unfortunately, the grasping, ego-fueled, and unconcerned lot at the school are a major detriment (for me at least) to any enjoyment of the tale. Full marks to Tessa for her convictions, but that’s about the best of this tale; there’s really not much in the way of plot (or resolution), and the denouement with another death occurring and damned little concern about that one either just left a bad taste in my mouth…
