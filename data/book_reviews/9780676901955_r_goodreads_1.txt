“Fodor’s Travel Maine, Vermont & New Hampshire” by Jordan Barry, John Blodgett, Andrew Collins, Alexandra Hall, Kate McCarthy, Mary Ruoff. 2025 edition.I bought this book for my holiday to Maine. Having already visited New Hampshire (before I bought this book) and Vermont is next on the list.Fodor travel books are overall brilliant. However I found this one to be limited in the areas that I was visiting in Maine. Couple of restaurant recommendations and hotels. (Thank god for Google and social media.) The maps were useful. Loved the section about lighthouses!
