I read all the books a long time ago, back in my 20’s, I’m now 53 years young 😜 and I was and still am a fan of Anne Rice. The first book I ever read of Anne’s was ‘The Witching Hour’ which was given to me by a friend, and from then I was hooked onto everything that she did. The book ‘Interview with a Vampire’ was so much better than the film, which I’ve always felt was miscast, because reading the book first, long before the film was released, I would never have imagined Tom Cruise as Lestat 😱 these chronicles are by far the best vampire books I’ve EVER read. RIP Anne Rice 🖤
