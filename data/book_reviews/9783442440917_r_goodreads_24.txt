Thoroughly enjoyed the first four book in this series, 'Dreaming of the Bones' however was a total bore. I wasn't interested in Rupert Brooke or his poetry, even less interest in reading the fictional Lydia's poetry. Lydia and her arrogant, dysfunctional friends were completely unlikable.

As for Vic, it was completely unbelievable how easily Duncan forgave and forgot that she had walked out on him without a word. Also, whatever Gemma pays Hazel for childcare and food isn't nearly enough.

Bottom line, I forced myself the finish this book just to read what changes, if any, there were in Duncan and Gemma's relationship.
