I read all but two of the translations in this version and found it serviceable but somewhat artless and unattractive. Although I know no French, it hardly seemed plausible to me that France's greatest comedian could have written anything comparable to the bland English in this translation. I switched to Richard Wilbur's translation for The Misanthrope and immediately found it more gripping and elegant, so I'll stick to him for Molière in the future.
