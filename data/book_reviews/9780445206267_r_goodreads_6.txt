I'm very glad I picked this book out of a BookCrossing book box, as it was a lot more interesting than I expected. Claudia J Edwards may not be the world's best writer, but once I got into the story I was hooked. An original setting and plot, interesting characters and lots of horses for added interest.Her novels all seem to be out of print, but if I ever come across a second-hand copy I'll definitely be buying it.
