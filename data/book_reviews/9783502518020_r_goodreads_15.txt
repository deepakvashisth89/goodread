Mrs Bantry is woken up by her maid who claims that there is a body in the library, hence the title of the book. While the angry Colonel Bantry telephones the police, Mrs Bantry calls her friend Miss Marple. Who will be able to figure out this mystery first?The thing about Miss Marple is that she can be a background character for quite a while and you don’t really miss her. This can be either a strength or a weakness, depending on how strong the mystery plot and the other characters are. In this story, it really is a strength. The core mystery is quite strong. The layers surrounding the heart of the mystery get pealed like an onion, carefully and deliberately. And the other characters who get the chance to shine are mostly the police investigators. So the story feels more like a detective mystery rather than a cozy mystery. Which I have to admit, I do appreciate.It honestly does feel like a step up from the first Miss Marple novel in my opinion. Even though Miss Marple feels more like a background character for a long time.
