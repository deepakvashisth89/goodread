CAN YOU SUE YOUR PARENTS FOR MALPRACTICE?The author,Paula Danziger,has a good understanding of kids and teenagers.This book was written in the last year of her teaching.Lauren,who is a ninth grade girl has a dream of becoming a lawyer to sue her parents for malpractice due to her wierd mom and crazy and strict dad but gets distracted by some teenage things just like in any teenage life.After all,maybe sueing her parents for malpractice isn't the most important thing.I gave this book 3 stars,almost just right.
