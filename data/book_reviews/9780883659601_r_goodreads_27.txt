So I admit that I have a problem, I like quotes. This book served as an enabler to my problem because it is chalk full of quotes. But not just any quotes. Most, if not all, of the quotes are witty, rude and downright hilarious. The quotes span just about every facet of human existence: love, sex, death, life, children, food, women, men, music, stupidity (and almost anything else you can come up with) etc. There's a little bit of something for everyone!
