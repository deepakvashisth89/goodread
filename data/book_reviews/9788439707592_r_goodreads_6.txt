The Dalai Lama talks about applying ethics to the new millennium in this book. He bases his ethical system on several Buddhist tenets, but it can apply to everyone.The Dalai Lama notices that people of all creeds and walks of life want the same basic things; an avoidance of pain, happiness, and the same for their children. However, in more advanced societies, this isn't easily accomplished. Wealth doesn't ensure happiness or satisfaction in life.In that vein, The Dalai Lama calls for compassion. If we can understand each other and see people as human beings with problems similar to ours, we can change the world.The Dalai Lama is Tibetan, so the book has phrases in that language. English lacks the subtle differences required to express itself fully.
