The convention was obvious (or at least Alison Fraser' style of writing). I need now a break from it. I mean, all those: dramatic "Let me go!" and grasping arms, wrists etc. ;-)

Nonetheless, I admit I wonder how much I have enjoyed these romances.

I think, besides the excitement from the encounters between a hero and a heroine, Fraser's books are nice/enjoyable to read because they are short (less than 200 pages), the language and dialogues are smooth (but not dumb) and that next to the love story there is another story (at least one) in the background.
