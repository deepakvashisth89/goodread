I DID like this book; I’m just not sure how much. I gave a 1-star rating to 4 of the last 8 books I’ve read. I really had to ask my self did I like this book on its own merits, or just in comparison to the crap I’ve read lately.There are a couple of situations that probably wouldn’t/couldn’t have happened; and one scene in a bank that DEFINITELY wouldn’t/couldn’t have happened.I was enjoying the book so much, I was able to put that aside; which I’m not always able to do. The fact that I’d, very much, like to read another Gayle Lynds book tilted the scale up to 4 stars.
