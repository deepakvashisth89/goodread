DNFFine enough book when the MC shows as a tough, independent and adventurous soul. But then emotions creep in and the changes are too much for me to accept - or stomach. A woman is hired to paint a mural in a huge ballroom. She sketches; she starts. The person she's working for is enigmatic and weird - I liked that. Scenery perfect. Dialogue realistic. Then enter an 'old flame' from the past and the book veers down a rather ridiculous path...I wanted to read this author, and so I did. Won't give up on her, will try another. But this one simply did not suit my reading preferences. Sometimes I like a character who goes through a 'dramatic change,' as that often happens to us real, non-fictional beings as well. But I just didn't buy into it. Just didn't.No rating.
