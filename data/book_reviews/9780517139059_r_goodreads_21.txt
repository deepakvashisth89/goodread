My first time with this author. But only part of the reason I chose this book to read. Once a year, maybe twice if I am feeling nostalgic I read a Star Trek novel and a Star Wars novel. These are old friends that I have been associating with for over forty years. I wanted to give this one a rating of 3.5 but of course GoodReads forbids that. So for me it is really a 7/10.

This time around I was looking for an origin story. There really is no definitive origin stories for the original Star Trek crew. After some research this is the closest one I could find for James T. Kirk.

In one of the reboot movies for Star Trek there is a brief scene where Kirk's father is killed on a Starship in battle while James is being born. There is also a scene where he steals a car and is chased by police. This book supports neither of those incidents, instead Kirk's father takes him on a voyage on the original Enterprise and the adventure ensues. So quite divergent options. If you read this book and have seen the movie I am referencing you can choose the one that works best for you. I am guessing that the movie version is the one that Paramount (the owners of the rights to Star Trek) would make gospel.

All that aside, this book was an interesting read. It presents an interesting theory for the young James Kirk. One I dont totally disagree with as it gives a plausible theory of how it all started for one of the most beloved space farers of all time.

This one definitely scratched my itch and it has also allowed me to find other early years books focusing on the other members of the original crew. I have a feeling I will be spending more time on the bridge of the Enterprise than usual this year.
