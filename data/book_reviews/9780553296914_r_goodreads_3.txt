Another old romance novel, this one a bit more bodice rippery than the others. She has SWORN not to marry and he FORCES THE ISSUE so there is much animosity and hostility etc until eventually of course they must admit they are in love..... I do wish the author had done a bit more with the pregnancy drama, that is, had the heroine resign herself to the danger she had feared but have the hero grow increasingly horrendously anxious, so that they would have to sort of balance each other out again..... but overall this was fun.
