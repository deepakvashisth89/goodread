If I had not known I would have said this novel was written in 1950s or 1960s. Perhaps this was but the year of publishing was 1992.The text was old fashioned even though this was probably meant to be modern and funny. It wasn't either, rather clumsy and conservative.The plot wasn't too imaginative either so the whole experience was a bit awkward. I expected much more as Paula Gosling has received The Golden Dagger award which should be some kind of guarantee when it comes to quality. Obviously (or hopefully) it was due some other novel of hers. This wasn't too clever or original.I don't know if the detective lieutenant Jack Stryker is Gosling's basic character... somehow the name rings a bell.The text itself was moderately well written so reading this wasn't especially a torment. You might like it, who knows.Ruumis paratiisissaWSOY 1995
