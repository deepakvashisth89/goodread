I really enjoyed this installment, even though it largely feels like a detour from Dumarest’s main mission. The story focuses on his unlikely friendship with a fellow traveler—young, naïve, and a bit pompous—formed out of mutual desperation. It’s also quite shocking to see Dumarest’s luck finally falter in the arena. Along the way, the story offers a deeper and more intriguing look at the Brotherhood and what motivates them.
