Love this author!Second reading of Sleep Towards Heaven, and it is still one of my favorite books. This is Ward’s debut and she knocked it out of the ball park, IMO. Very moving story of three very different women whose lives converge. The divisive subject of the death penalty is handled with sensitivity and no political bias. ( not an easy thing to carry off with this hot topic ). Wards story telling skills have been proven over and over again, as she continues to write compelling stories. If you haven’t read anything by Ward, please check out this novel, or one of my other favorites: How to be Lost, The Same Sky, Jetsetters ( Reese’s book club), and her latest Lovers and Liars. You won’t be disappointed.Highly Recommended.
