I first read this book as an early teen--I can see now, reading it as an adult, that having read this book (along with all Madeleine L'Engle's other books) at twelve and thirteen clearly had a profound impact on my world view. L'Engle's writing has a depth and profundity that draws on emotions of which most writers only attempt to scratch the surface.I think all developing adolescents should read this book and all its accompanying ones, if only to see that there is more out there than either complete surrender to the inanity of life, or conversely, that fantasy does not necessarily mean being out of touch with reality.I am happy that I left this book on a shelf long enough that coming back to it all these years later meant it was fresh and new while being comfortingly familiar.
