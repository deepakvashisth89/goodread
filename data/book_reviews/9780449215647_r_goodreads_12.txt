2019 Review
Vanessa March quarreled with her husband and now feels dreadful about it. But he's off on a business trip in Stockholm so there is no use fretting to death...
Until she sees a newsreel at the local theater of him at a fire in Austria clutching a very pretty girl. Then all bets are off. Vanessa is going to Austria to find her man.

My biggest complaint with Mary Stewart novels usually centers around the lightning fast romances. I thus appreciated the 'married couple' element of this book. The strong attachment made sense. And it is always delightful to read about happily married couples. (It gives one hope, you know!)
This novel attempts a bit more mystery than the other books by her I have read but still falls heavily in 'suspense.' If something gets mentioned at the beginning of the book, you know it will tie in at the end so not very many surprises there.
But overall, an exciting, adventurous novel with romance and mystery and murder. I especially delighted in the undercurrent of feminist sensibility as Vanessa mentally makes cracks about the men around her asking if she can 'keep up' and then expecting her to anyway. And she can. Because she's tough and while a fellow about helps, she will find a way to rescue herself from the most pressing danger.
