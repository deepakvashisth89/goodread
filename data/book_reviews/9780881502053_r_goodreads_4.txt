I have been working my way through the Asey Mayo books. This is a 1942 collection of three novellas.

What I enjoy most about the books is the description of what Cape Cod was like in the 1930s and the war years. I am not a fan of the rather complicated plots which Taylor enjoyed. She sometimes has multiple suspects who are hard to keep straight and the solution sometimes comes out of nowhere.

Unfortunately, these novellas have less time for local color, and the plots are just as intricate as in the full novels. The gimmick that is used to create the murder in one story is not believable. One of the stories, unusually, is set in 1940s Boston but we don't get much of a sense of the city. A financial motive shows up out of nowhere at the end of a third story.

I would take a pass on this one, unless, like me, you are a completist. On the other hand, I recommend any of the novels. "Cranberry Bog" or "Spring Harrowing" are good places to start.
