An interesting take on parent/child relationships and expectations. While I enjoyed the storyline of a girl who is devoted to her dog, I had a bit of a difficult time relating to the character. Ant seems to be more of a pathological liar, which can be frustrating to read about at times. However, the mishaps that happen when she lies can be entertaining. The book touches on some heavy themes, such as feeling like you don't belong, which are handled well. The character of "Just Carol," the teacher, adds insightful commentary to the story and memorable advice for any readers relating to Ants situation. The audiobook version of the book was very enjoyable and brought the characters to life. I would recommend this book for readers aged 8-12.
