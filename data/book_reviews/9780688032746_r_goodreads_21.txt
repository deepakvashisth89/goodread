I finished this a while ago but forgot to update my goodreads…

I’ve read many books that in one way or another critique the medium of television and its various screen successors, and this is another excellent entry. Because this book is example-heavy, and Mander relies on a lot of experiential evidence, it’s a good starting point for anyone interested learning about this line of thought for the first time.

Mander performs an analysis of the effects of television on the body was that was especially compelling to me. I was particularly struck by the studies on light that Mander uses to supplement his analysis of these bodily effects, the negative impacts of extended exposure to limited spectra. Maybe it’s because I was working under fluorescent light in an office building all summer and felt distinctly depressed because of it…
