I trained originally as an anthropologist, and was hopeful that this would be a rare anthropology- and/or archaeology-based mystery that I actually enjoyed. It's a surprisingly large genre, but unfortunately this book joins the long list of disappointing ones in said genre. The protagonist is so breathtakingly naive, and makes such inexplicably stupid choices, that I couldn't even make it the entire way through the book. I found myself so frustrated with the protagonist's behavior that I put it down halfway through and never picked it up again. Don't waste your time on this one.
