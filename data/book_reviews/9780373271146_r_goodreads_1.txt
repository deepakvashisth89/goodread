I read the other books in this series a while back. This one was a nice story, we have a hero grappling with guilt over the death of a fellow officer who happened to be the heroine's husband and her father's son. The heroine approaches the hero in despair, her eight-year old son has become withdrawn after her husband died. I liked how there were no miracles the moment the hero entered their lives, grief takes its own time. The hero/heroine feel a pull but the heroine doesn't want to let go of her husband and his memories. I liked how the book showed that everyone has their own time and pace of letting things go and moving on. I felt the heroine's fears were portrayed nicely and I enjoyed this.
