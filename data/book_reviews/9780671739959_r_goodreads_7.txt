Frank and Joe go to Maine on a fishing trip to recover from their last case or two, when Joe sees Iola in the forest. Iola is his dead girlfriend. He drops the supplies and rushes to find Frank.To his credit, Frank immediately supports Joe rather than insisting Iola is dead. The two are shot at by two French-Canadian hunters who insist it was an accident.Frank and Joe discover The Lazarus Clinic, hidden in the woods. Joe talks to a girl who appears to be Iola, who knows every private thing the two did.Frank and Joe also discover duplicates of themselves that were put together by the Lazarus Clinic to help the Assassins infiltrate the Network. The goal: replace the Grey Man, and the mysterious head of the Network with duplicates.Yes, it sounds like a plot from Wild Wild West or The Man from U.N.C.L.E.Frank and Joe have several dangerous encounters, and nearly die multiple times. Iola isn't the real Iola, she's a duplicate - programmed with information from the real Iola's diary. Frank and Joe help with the takedown of the Lazarus Clinic.Lots of action, high stakes because Joe is so heartbroken throughout this, and a plot straight out of a 1960s TV spy series - this novel had everything and I really enjoyed it.This entire review has been hidden because of spoilers.Show full review
