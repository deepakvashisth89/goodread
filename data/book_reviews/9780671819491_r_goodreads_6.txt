After unhappily occupying a studio apartment in East Rogers Park, Chicago for a year I was invited in 1979 to move in with the brothers Miley, Tom and Michael, on 1634 W. Chase in the same neighborhood. Another old high school friend, Tom Kosinski, was leaving their three-bedroom to travel to France and they needed a roommate. That situation lasted about a year. Then Tom moved out and James Koehnline took his place.James is an artist, author and appreciator of the offbeat. Among his interests are conspiracy theories in general and the works of Robert Anton Wilson in particular. He got me to read a lot of odd stuff including this overview of conspiratorial Illuminati theories by Neal Wilgus, known as an associate of Wilson.
