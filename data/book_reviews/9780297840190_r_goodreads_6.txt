Wow. Not very like any short stories I have read before.Negative - They didn't "pop".Positive - They make you think. You finish them and you wonder what you supposed to get out of that. If you pause for a little bit and consider, they really give you a lot to think about.Negative - They are mostly depressing. I hear that this is par for the course, though, for Irish authors, a group I have little personal experience with.I liked them all better upon a second reading.What did I learn? A lot about Edna O'Brien's point of view.Worst stories - Brother, Another Time, Dramas.Sure, I know bad stuff happens - people get stuck in incest, people are violent towards minority groups such as gays, etc, but do I want to read about it? Does it help me to combat it in any way?Best stories - Epitaph, Long Distance.
