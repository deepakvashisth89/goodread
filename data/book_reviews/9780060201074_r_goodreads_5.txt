This was a neat children's mystery book. It's an 'I Can Read Book' I would say it would be for an advanced level 2 or a level 3 reader unless an adult was helping them. It 'could' be a bit scary for a younger reader at times but it soon shows that it's not a ghost or whatever appears to be scary. I read this to my 3 year old granddaughter and we both enjoyed it. I'm gonna be looking for other Rollo and Tweedy books.
