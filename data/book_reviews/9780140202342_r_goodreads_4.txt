Assigned reading for a two semester course in English history I took in college. An excellent overview of English history 1307 - 1536, covering the periods starting with the accession of Edward II and ending with Henry VIII's break with Rome. During that time, you have the Hundred Years War, the Back Death, the Wars of the Roses between the Houses of Lancaster and York, culminating with the triumph of the Tudors. There are chapters on government, economic and social developments, religious and educational movements and the arts. The early part of this period was the Age of Chaucer.
