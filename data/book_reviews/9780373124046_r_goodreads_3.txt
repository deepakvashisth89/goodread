Francesca works as a tour operator in Rome and just wants to have a good relationship with her Great Uncle, to marry the cute Italian man she’s engaged to, and have some good friends.

She’s plum out of luck. Which is a shame, because I can really get behind a heroine who has such straightforward and easily identifiable goals. But, can’t always get what you want, and if you can’t, awesome sexy times with a really hot guy who is totally into you, even if you can’t trust him, isn’t a bad deal.

Carlo thinks Francesca is hot. He’s hot too, and casually a billionaire, like it’s no big deal. He has a fantastic ancient villa where you can open double doors out of the house and there’s a stone arch framing a lake. If that was all, and this was just a plot where he lifts her out of what soon becomes a melodrama of broken relationships and lost opportunities there wouldn’t be all that much going on. So, he’s keeping secrets from her. On balance though, the sexy times are fantastic. And you can forgive a guy a lot when he has a lake at the end of his terrace.

Really liked this one.
