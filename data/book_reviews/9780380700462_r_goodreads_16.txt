Between the unlikely romances of a half-hearted law student and aspiring journalist, and the flamboyant caricature of a brilliant radio drama writer, Mario Vargas Llosa sweeps the reader away on a delirious and baroque odyssey, where characters and stories intertwine, complement each other, and transform like a kaleidoscope, seeking to trap or lose the reader along the way.Ultimately, it's a delightful and refreshing farce, brilliantly crafted with a novelistic flair, which I thoroughly enjoyed reading.
