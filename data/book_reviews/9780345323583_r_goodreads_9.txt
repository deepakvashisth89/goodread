Gnome King of Oz by Ruth Plumly Thompson (1927) is the 21st Oz book. The (G)nome King first appeared inOzma of Oz(1908). Although it was a weird choice to leave the G off the Nomes in that first (and subsequent volumes), adding the G back in now is even weirder.Thompson sticks with her boys first agenda and introduces Peter, a kid from Philadelphia who is kidnapped via magical balloon that is more like the balloons sold at fairs and zoos and nothing like the hot air balloon the Wizard flew to Oz. Peter through a series of misadventures ends up on the same island with the former Gnome King.
