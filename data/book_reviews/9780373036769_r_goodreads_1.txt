الحب لا يكذب لكارولين اندرسونYears ago, when I was a teenager, this book was amongst my favorite translated copies, I read it in Arabic, from روايات أحلام and I loved it so much I remember reading it over and over again.Lydia jilted Jake in their wedding day because he never actually said he loved her. Jake thought it was obvious. A year later, she traveled to Australia and worked in many jobs and lost so much weight, she returns home for her sister's wedding. They both fall in love even more if possible, but Jake is so hurt by her that he needs convincing.
