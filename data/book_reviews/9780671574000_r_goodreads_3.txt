2.5 stars rounded up to 3

This was a good Trek novel and I liked the structure.
But man, some of the decisions made with regard to Janeway’s backstory made me roll my eyes so hard.
Apparently massive Daddy issues are the only possible explanation for a successful female Starfleet captain. It was also very notable that there’s not a single close-female friendship in Janeway’s pre-Voyager life - something that I also wish the show would have done better. They included some great male friendships, but female friendships? Nada. Her sister and her mother appear a few times, but especially her mother is an “absent presence” and all we learn about her is that she cooks with real food. I mean, come on. Even for the mid-1990s this is grim.
