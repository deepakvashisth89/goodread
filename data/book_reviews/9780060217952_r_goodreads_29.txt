Sadly we have reached the third and final installment of the Scary Stories trilogy. The child inside me weeps.This will be a collection of stories I will for sure keep forever and read many more times. There's those few items that truly take you back in years to your childhood and this book just reminds me of all the best times. I will eventually pass it to my child when they are old enough.(not me crossing all my fingers and toes that they love books like I do)While the stories no longer chill me to the bones, they seem to touch my heart.
