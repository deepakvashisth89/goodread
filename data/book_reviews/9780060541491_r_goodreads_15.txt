I enjoyed this book, though it had one of the most abrupt endings I have ever seen. I really felt for Darren, who was clearly very confused and uncomfortable in his own skin. The situation with Mr. Tracy left me with just as conflicted feelings as Darren’s, as I know it can be confusing and difficult to react when people make you highly uncomfortable, but don’t actually touch you (making it seem that they may not have actually done anything wrong).
