I finished a book!Finished a couple of days ago, and then worked my way through the Introduction. I knew the story before starting the novel, George Eliot's first, because I have seen the movie.The introduction describes this as "Eliot's most leisurely paced book, the slow unfolding of the story is at the same time the accretory specification of where and how."This is certainly a slow book, and took me months to get through — until it took off and I was gripped.One reason I wanted to read this is because when the Canadian philosophy student Winthrop Bell was imprisoned in Germany during WWI (seeCracking the Nazi Code: The Untold Story of Canada's Greatest Spy) this was a favourite book of his fellow prisoners, to be read and re-read.Now, ontoThe Mill on the Floss— once I've caught my breath, and maybe read something contemporary to reset my palate.
