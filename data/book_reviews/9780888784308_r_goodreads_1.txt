If you've ever taken your kids to Algonquin Park on a canoe trip you need to read this book to them!
Dani and Caitlin set off with their dads for a summer camping trip in Canada's beautiful Algonquin Provincial Park. They go to Canoe Lake, the same lake where Tom Thomson, the Canadian painter died. The girls go canoeing and are visited by Thomson who asks them to help solve the mystery of his murder. This would make a great book for visitors to Canada as it has wonderful descriptions of the park and surrounding areas. Great characters and plot.
