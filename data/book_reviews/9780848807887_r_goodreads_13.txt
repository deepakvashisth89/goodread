Also not a favorite Oz book, but an amusing one nevertheless. One day on a walk home, Dorothy Gale, Toto, and a new and unusual Shaggy Man suddenly find the way strange and the roads changed. They encounter a series of fairy places, whose rulers each ask but one favor: for Dorothy to ask Ozma of Oz to invite them to her birthday party. One of the adventures has the Shaggy Man and a new friend, Button-Bright, being enchanted to have a donkey and a fox head, which they must wash in the Pond of Truth to undo. The four also meet a daughter of the Rainbow named Polychrome.After this series of adventures, the friends sail across the deadly desert to the land of Oz; there, they are soon joyously caught up in the birthday preparations. Notable guests include Santa Claus, his Rhys & Knooks, a wax doll princess, a gingerbread man, a rubber bear, a character who resembles Hope from The Christmas Carol, our old friends the royal family of Ev, the sovereigns of the land of Oz, the Wizard, Jack Pumpkinhead, the Woggle-Bug, the Sawhorse, Billina and her ten chicks (all named Dorothy), the Braided Man from the third book, the Cowardly Lion and the Hungry Tiger, Johnny Dooit, the fox and donkey kings, and several more. At last, Dorothy returns to Kansas.
