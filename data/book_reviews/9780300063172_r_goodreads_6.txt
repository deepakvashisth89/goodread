Very helpful consideration of how to communicate about adoption to children in a variety of adoption situations. I appreciated that it wasn't restricted to the usual focus on domestic or international infant adoption. I found the authors' approach well-versed in child development, but liked even better that they wrote in a calming tone, without hyperbole and dramatic scenarios amped up to appeal to parents' fears or concerns. The many parents' voices presented in the book's second half are also calm and grounded--a welcome change from both the "sky is falling" tone of the anti-adoption folks and the rainbows and unicorns treatment that predominates everywhere else.
