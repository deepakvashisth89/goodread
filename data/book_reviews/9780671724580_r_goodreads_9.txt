I enjoyed this story but the but it just didn't feel like a worthy follow up to The Eagle Has Landed. The book itself is very enjoyable but there are just so many similarities to EHL that it felt almost like a cop out. Of course, the Himmler subplot was a nice shock.The characters, however, do not feel like those we met on scudlyconstable.All in all a fair read, enjoyable but something that felt like a poor sequel. In my humble opinion, I would have enjoyed it more if the characters had been new.
