Midnight by John Russo is about a runaway girl who escapes her creepy police officer stepdad and hitches a ride with two guys on their way to Florida for spring break. The other point of view is about a satanic cult who are looking for women for an Easter ritual, and they believe they are descendants of witches.I love horror books from the 80s because you don't get this type of horror anymore. You can feel the nostalgia, and the horror is much more potent and uncut. I love Russo's writing, and this story kept me on the edge. The ending is for people who like to see scoops of ice cream fall from kids' cones.I give Midnight by John Russo 🎃🎃🎃🎃🎃 out of five jack-o'-lanterns.Keep it spooky! 🎃👍
