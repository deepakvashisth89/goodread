Going without any expectations into this book, it took me longer than anticipated to finish it. For me, the most fascinating parts are the first and last chapters. Probably because I could relate to them the most. Having been to Ireland only once I probably missed a large part of the emotional loathing that the memoir has. In any case I enjoyed getting to know the land through her eyes and experience. Her references to history, poetry and art where delicious. So I will leave some Kierkegaard here quoted by her.Dark is kindlyMaking madonnas out of all their faces,Giving ghosted bricky placesA peculiar pulse of lifeAnd melting youUntil those brown eyesWhisper liesThat the phenomenal heartAfterward dies.
