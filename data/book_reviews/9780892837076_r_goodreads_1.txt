I bought this while on retreat at a Carmelite retreat house, and also while very new to Catholicism. It was my first "saint book" other than Butler's Lives, which were given to me as a gift. This was both inspiring and discouraging. Inspiring to read about all these holy women who lived their lives for God. Discouraging because their devotion sure didn't guarantee that everything was fabulous. I know, I know, that's real life in the world of true faith, but I guess it was just eye-opening that even the mom saints didn't always end up with perfect children, for instance.
