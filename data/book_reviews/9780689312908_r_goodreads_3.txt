I couldn't put this book down.It really should be in our school curriculum, though it isn't for painfully obvious reasons.He argues points that many have tried with perhaps more zeal and less success to argue.I won't spoil it for you, but if you wish to think critically about the relationships between money, capitalism, culture, and what is 'allowed' vs 'disallowed' vs 'not even mentioned, and therefore simply forgotten or unknown', you need this book.
