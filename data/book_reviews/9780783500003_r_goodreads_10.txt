I totally found this very interesting. I enjoy reading the lives of different serial killers, both "famous" and "not-so-famous". Who they were and what they did were very interesting. There are apparently two types of serial killers. One who kills for pleasure and one who kills because they believe they have to because they are being "told" or "forced" to. Psychology had always interested me and learning about these killers gave me a bigger interest in the human mind. Overall, this is a great book to read if you're just interested in learning about real people in our society who happens to be serial killers. This can be a knowledgeable book for grade 7 and up.
