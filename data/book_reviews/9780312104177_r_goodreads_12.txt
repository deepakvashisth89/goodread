Riptide: A Florida Panhandle MysteryIsabel Anders is an artist in NY. She had been fired for two months when she received the letter from St Elmo, Florida stating her Aunt Merriam had been attacked and was incapacitated. She feels the need to return to Florida and check on her aunt. As she spends time with Merriam and the town, she starts discovering all is not as it should be. She believes her aunt was purposely attacked. The rest of the book is her exciting adventure of discovering murder and mayhem. Things get so dangerous she barely escaped with her life. A great read!
