The diaries of an 'alien' - an alien genius, imprisoned in himself.It was a more compelling read than many novels by other authors.The word 'pain' is mentioned many many times in Kafka's diaries. Indeed, his life was suffering and big struggle with himself, his extreme sensitivity, his mood swings, his despair and insecurity, his family, his job, the writing, the circumstances, the women he loved, the whole world. It was the struggle he could not have possibly won.There are a lot of descriptions of dreams and stories or fragments of them - I'm so grateful for that.'The hollow which the work of genius has burned into our surroundings is a good place into which to put one’s little light.
