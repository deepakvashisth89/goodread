Book three in the series. Environmental themes pop in to this one, as well as the usual assortment of goofy clients with pet issues. It's not quite a James Herriott novel, the animal scenes are very brief, but it's still a fun setting. And I love the banter between Sam and the doctor, as they cheekily discuss suspects and murder while calmly spaying and neutering! [See my review of book one [book:Eight Dogs Flying|1380667]
