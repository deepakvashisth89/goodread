The premise of Heinlein's first juvenile scifi novel is, of course, a very silly one: three teenage boys hastily build, with the help of a physicist uncle, the first Moon rocket a fly to the Moon after World War II. If you can live with that, and the fact that the characters are very thin indeed, Rocketship Galileo is mostly an entertaining read. During the first half of the book Heinlein lectures about rockets etc., but if you've read Verne and lived through it, Heinlein's lectures are no big deal.It would have been a three-star novel, but the last quarter or so went to the territory that has been parodied a lot subsequently, and that gave another half a star. I wasn't expecting that, so it was a delight to discover.3.5/5
