I'm really in two minds about this book. I enjoyed the autobiographical parts mostly, but as a sceptic regarding Christian Science and a course in miracles I found those parts a bit hard to take. It was fascinating to see her path to where she is today though. Funny how she has been married 4 (?) times now though...I skimmed through part 2 as it wasn't really anything new to me. Some of it was word for word from her other books.I just think the claim that nothing bad is real is a bit absurd and it's a cop out to avoid getting out in the world and helping improve things. Why can't you meditate and help things in a practical way? That people who are seriously ill caused it themselves it's also to be questioned. A baby born with cancer? Did she think it on herself. A newborn dying from whooping cough?? Come on. Having said all that I still have a measure or respect for Doreen virtue and find her very easy to read.
