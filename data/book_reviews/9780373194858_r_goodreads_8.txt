“The Wedding Lullaby (One Night to Forever Book 2)”

This book was marvelous! A prank,a wedding farce in Vegas,and an unplanned pregnancy made this book what it was,terrific!
Two people joined in a marriage they thought was fake,find out the birth control must have been fake as well,considering the woman was in his office,looking for a job,but he already knew about her pregnancy.She didn’t know he’d been keeping tabs on her,so knew all about it.Then they found out the wedding was real too! He agreed to finding her a job at his company,then taking her in until she found an apartment.
A lot of things happen after that,not the least being she found she couldn’t afford said apartment,so seemed to be stuck with him.
Anyway,this was a powerful book,proving love just might conquer all.
I really liked it and hope you’ll give it a chance!
