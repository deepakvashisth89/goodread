Text-to-Teaching Connection:After reading "Read Leaf, Yellow Leaf," I immediately thought of a good hands-on science experiment for children. What child does not like to get messy and watch things grow? In response to this picture book, children will have the opportunity to plant and take care of a tree seedling. Many science standards could be taught using this experiment such as water cycle, and properties of plant life. Students can also journal about the seedling's sequencing of life as well as their hands-on experience with helping the tree seedling grow. The hands-on experience and the writing connections will help leave a lasting memory that the children certainly will not forget. I think the seedling would be a great addition to the classroom, children have the chance to feel responsible with the seedling's care while strengthening their science and character.
