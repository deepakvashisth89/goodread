Those sections are far too journalistic for it not to have been the case. I found it enjoyable to read about what rural Egypt was like in the late 1970s and how the tourism industry at that time worked (because many important historical sites are now near villages, not cities).That leaves me with mixed feelings about this novel.On the one hand, excellent mystery set up and descriptions of Egypt that make my Egyptologist toes curl in delight they are so accurate. On the other hand -- so totally late 70s low-rent Indiana Jones adventure plot.We'll compromise at 3.5 stars rounded up because good Pharaoh Seti fandom is hard to find!Read in Italian translation for my Foreign Reading Challenge 2026.
