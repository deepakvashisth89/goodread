I can’t think how to categorize Mary Stewart. She deserves a genre all of her own. I suppose I would liken her writing to the charm of D.E. Stevenson with the uncanny eerie atmosphere of Celia Fremlin and Shirley Jackson... but sort of.. all squashed together. Like everything is so quaint and gentle and pretty and colourful, but there is a dark undertone where no one is really as they seem. I love Mary Stewart and am so so glad I have many more of her back catalogue to work through.
