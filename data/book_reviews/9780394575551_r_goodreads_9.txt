4.5/5Rage for Fame stars Clare Boothe Luce, a beautiful and brilliant woman who is also, in turn, proportionally narcissistic. This biography lives along side Mrs. Boothe from adolescence to adulthood as she learns to weaponize her extraordinary brains and beauty to propel her far in life. Starting from her parent's humble origins, the star of the book drastically turns these conditions around through sheer force of will and an insatiable desire for money and power.The writing style of the book made me feel like my literacy rate increased ten-fold every time I picked it up. While at times it can feel dull, it is a biography. Whose life is going to have an action scene going on 100% of the time? Even so, the abundant details really show Mrs. Boothe's dichotomous character and her striking impact on everyone around her.The book is remarkably well researched. At times I often questioned how so many sources, quotes, and such a comprehensive linear pathway of her life came to be and the sheer amount of effort put in was clear.Quotes I enjoyed:"History has never reserved a place for a beautiful woman who did not love, or who was not loved by at least one interesting, powerful, or brilliant man." -Clare Boothe Luce"You are unforgettable ... be afraid of nothing." - Henry Luce to his wife, Clare Boothe Luce."You appear to be everybody's and yet nobody's, wise and yet so childish. Perhaps ... in these things lies your ineffable charm." - Dr. Rosenbluth to Clare Boothe Luce and her enigmatic personality."I have fits of morbidity and melancholy that are exhausting to others, but catharsis to me, leave my spirt shiny, washed, and bouncy. A badly burnt child I am so afraid of happiness, that let a perfect moment begin to unfold like a rose in my hands, and I instantly try to crush it." - Clare Boothe Luce.
