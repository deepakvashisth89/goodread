An excellent read with massive scope over all of known human history that narrows to a focus on the state of war in the nuclear era where the destructive power of our weapons has made the idea of 'battle' quaint. This has led to an era of power politics where we have 'conventional warfare' when we use the weapons that can only kill a lot people rather than all of us - and the major powers never face each other directly. Full of insights throughout and ending with an optimism that perhaps with the threat of killing us all, perhaps we can learn to just put all the weapons down.
