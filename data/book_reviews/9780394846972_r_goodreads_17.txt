They don't write like they did in 1950 anymore. (sigh) And they certainly don't write like they did in 1604, which is probably a good thing. This book makes me want to learn more about the first men and women to come to America. I want to hear more personal stories, and be inspired by their determination and faith. In several places it shares journal entries from those that were there. I would much rather read Landmark Books than Rush Limbaugh's attempt to teach the same material!Update.
