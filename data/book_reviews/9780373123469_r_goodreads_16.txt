Interesting premise where protagonists coparent a four-year-old son. This basically never happens in Harlequin Parents. If a bio kid exists, it's a secret baby (sometimes because the heroine is foolish and sometimes because there was a communication breakdown caused by evil parents, secretaries, and wannabe girlfriends). Unfortunately, the rest of the book didn't live up to the cute family arrangement. It's a shame because I would like to see more HPs with kids and without the "I will make you pay for keeping my child away from me" melodrama. Read via library.
