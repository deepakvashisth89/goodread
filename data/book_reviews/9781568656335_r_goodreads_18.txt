Usual disclaimer: I am not rating the happenings described in these books.

However, this, so far, has been my least favorite Ann Rule collection. I normally enjoy her deep exploration and long-winded explanations but somehow I felt them a bit overblown this time around and struggled to keep myself engaged. I listened to part of the main story on a 3 hour bus journey during which I fell asleep multiple times and I never bothered to rewind because ultimately I only missed a whole lot of ramblings. Alas.
