Great screwball mystery by Craig Rice.Delora Deane was one of the most famous models in the world. In reality, she was the composite of five different women, which was a closely guarded secret.Then one day, the woman with the model's hands disappeared, and the head of the agency received a pair of embalmed hands in the mail...John J. Malone, and his sidekicks, Jake and Helen Justis, have to track down the killer before Delora Deane's other four parts are killed. Things get crazy.A lot of fun.
