5 stars.

Ironically I own the first Baby Blues comic book, and then #10, 11, & 12.
Skipping from Zoe in her infancy to her as a toddler with Hammie as a new baby brother yet again demonstrated just how universal some aspects of parenting are. It was healing to see another toddler scream "no!" and have her parents have to take it in stride (as this is now a daily occurance in my life).
These comics are a breath of fresh air when you feel like you're going to lose it as a parent, and a reminder of why we do all this in the first place.
