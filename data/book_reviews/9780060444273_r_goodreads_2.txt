I was reading this as an Advance Reader Copy which was sent to a professor for his consideration. It's intended as a text for a college course. I picked it up at a book sale at Geneva College's homecoming. The English Club at my alma mater has a book sale in the library.
Geneva College has a fairly unique core curriculum requirement for Humanities courses. Back in the early 1980s, the program was fairly rigorous. We used multiple textbooks, including _Art in Perspective_ by Dorra.
I like the title and the general premise for this text, but I did not feel as engaged as the author(s) probably intended. Rather than motivating me to seek more on topics, I was relieved to be finished with each section I completed. I do not think I would choose this as a college text.
