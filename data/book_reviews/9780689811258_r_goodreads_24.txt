To start with... you can't go wrong with Margaret Peterson Haddix. You just can't it's not possible so buckle up and enjoy this book. Just go to your local library and find her on the shelves and just start reading.
This one in particular, everyone wants to figure out where they fit in, wants to have a friend group they are solid in but when that friend group has a bit of a cult vibe... well you might be better off eating lunch alone.
I loved this book, finished it in one sitting because why not, I didn't have a life I needed to tend to at all, it's cool Margaret.
If you like a bit of coming-of-age-tale, attempting to find your place in school and a bit over zealous religious touch then pick this up and thank me later.
