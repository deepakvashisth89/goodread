A spectacular read of human behaviour and expressions. I was expecting a traditional murder mystery set in an exotic location, but this book surprised me by not following the rules of the genre. Instead of a detective finding clues or interviewing suspects, we have a much more thoughtful handling of the scene, to the extent where the death doesn’t even feel like the main focus of the plot. Posadas cares more about the ramifications on everyone else who remains, not their past interactions with the deceased, and the novel explores this lens with all its ambiguity. The writing is deliciously irreverent and full of dramatic internal dialogues.I was going to give it a four star rating for the slightly slow pace in the first few chapters, but this book deserves a bonus point for the “About the Font” page waiting to be discovered at the end.
