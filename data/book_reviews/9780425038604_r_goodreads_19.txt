Detective Chief Inspector Roderick Alleyn, in New Zealand during World War II to track down spies, both from outside and from within, travels to the countryside where ranchers keep sheep to investigate a year-old murder and potential espionage in Ngaio Marsh'sDied in the Wool. Flossy Rubrick, a member of the New Zealand Parliament, disappeared a year earlier, only to be found rolled up in a bale of hay five weeks later. So the four people who live at her and her late husband Arthur's home in Mount Moon host Alleyn after a year of little success at locating the murderer. Alleyn does not serve in his regular role as homicide detective during the war.
