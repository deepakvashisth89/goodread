Definitely old school SF—but it doesn’t scratch any nostalgia itch for me. The sections don’t really coalesce and I lost interest. It has some decent gladiator style fights and an interesting idea for a final world. It wasn’t enough.If, like me, you think this is going to be about Starschool in any meaningful way—you’d be mistaken. None of the themes or tropes are present. That was disappointing and likely made me enjoy this less.The writing isn’t terrible so if the plot is up your alley, I’d say you could do worse.
