After watching The Rise of Skywalker movie, I was struck by nostalgia for the older Star Wars so badly that I decided to pick this up. I’ve had this since the Episode 1 came out but I never bothered reading it.It was probably a mistake to read this but it’s done now (the first chapter recounts pod racing and ends with the surprise reveal that Anakin is ... 9!! Sigh!). I should have just watched the movie again as this was quite a dull read. The novelization didn’t add much to the story at all and without the music and the visuals it makes for dull reading.Not much more to add here as with the exception of a few more paragraphs, this is basically a transcription of the movie. I am not sure what Mr. Brooks added to the writing here but avoid this and pick up something from his Shannara series instead.
