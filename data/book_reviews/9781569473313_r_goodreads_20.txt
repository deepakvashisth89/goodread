I felt like I was reading a writing class assignment: where did she go, what did she do there, what was she wearing? By the end I could predict the action scenes based on the relative absurdity of the detective's outfits. There didn't seem to be much consideration as to whether the storyline would be believable to the reader. For example, the detective hasgone through a burned apartment plate by plate, sifting piles of debris ... and later in the evening, after no shower or change of clothes, she's concerned about spilling champagne on her friend's rug? That'd be the least of her cleanliness worries!
