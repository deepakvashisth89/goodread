I absolutely loved this book, so much so I just ordered two more by this author, because if this is what her first book is like, I am sure they only have gotten better. There was so much going on in this book you knew it would come together and it did, but you were not going to figure it out and best of all it did not wrap up in a page and a half in a nice little bow, it had a good story to it. It has been on my tar forever, I should never have waited!
