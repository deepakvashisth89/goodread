"It is a long journey, through a country that is sometimes pleasant and sometimes dark and terrible."
Dorothy, a little girl from Kansas and her dog Toto has the most unlikely of adventures with a gang full of colorful characters. Classic American fairytale with powerful message about honesty, self discovery, bravery, friendship and home. Hollywood's interpretation does not hold a candle to the written word, but a close second. Read this for my GG Bookclub. Love the journey of books.
