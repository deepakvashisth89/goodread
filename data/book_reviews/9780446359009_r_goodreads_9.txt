Don't Google where the title of the novel comes from; it will spoil the story.

This is not one of Millar's better efforts. I know he put thought into creating this, but perhaps he put too much thought in. Something you'll find if you read the Archer series is Millar always has some mentally deranged person in the story. Critics call this "psychosexual in nature"; something which is positively true in this story. This actually makes Millar's stories less appealing to me.

I'm not a believer in all this psychobabble. I believe in the ability to overcome both in a physical sense and a psychological sense. I don't believe childhood development determines whether someone will be successful OR that environmental factors in childhood will limit a person as an adult.

I'm not going to discuss anything about the plot or the actors involved. Suffice it to say, there was no mystery here and the psychobabble definitely ruined the story. This story had problems from the get go. The publisher found it too slow and asked for revisions. Millar cut "10,000 good words" out. OMG, can you imagine what this story would be if it was even longer?

I don't recommend this one.
