I was intrigued by the plot initially but as I read the book I began to doubt some of the planning behind the insertion of the team. Why put them through unnecessary marching through hazardous jungle only to end up at a village that Green Berets already use as a base. As for the return journey I don’t think I have ever read so many firefight incidents that this group survived and were able to still march back to camp. I ended up skipping the last 20-25 pages Pretty disappointing book in the end
