Good readIt started slowly and I wasn't sure I was going like it, but as it went on Althea became so real and I had to know what happens to her! Grove Hill and The Summerhouse play a big part in the story but the characters are so well written that you feel like you know them. Miss Silver'srole in the series is becoming much larger, and it took me a couple of books to get used to that, but now it's enjoyable to get to know her better. Frank Abbott is really my favorite. His relationship with Miss Silver is so interesting and fun. I'm on to the next book in the series!
