You need to be fully prepared when going into this anthology that it is nonsensical and you will learn close to nothing. The only thing I learned was just how out of pocket some ideas were. This book will give you a big laugh, but really its only use now is to be a written landmark to how far science and taxonomy has come. The ideas in it are wholly representative of the time and religious influence.If you don't want to read how an animal is either Jesus or the Devil, then skip this one. If you want to read that the beaver got its scientific name from castration, then give it a shot.
