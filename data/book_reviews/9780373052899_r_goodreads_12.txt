"Loveplay" is the story of Cul and Bett.Cul broke Bett's heart years ago. After years of struggle, she's finally in NY when she auditions for a play she has done before, written by Cul..when she realizes hes the director and back in her life.What you have is a heroine crazy in love, and a hero you want to punch through 80% of it.Firstly it annoyed me that while the heroine was so utterly devoted to the hero, the hero slept around.Even when he finally seduced the heroine thinking she was experienced, he avoided using contraception and when a bun enters the oven..shockhe believes the baby isnt his as he's supposed to be sterile!!!! and thats why he writes pregnancy related playsThe heroine is like a lovesick puppy throughout first half, and finally gains some backbone in the last 1/4 for the sake of her baby. The hero is a dbag, and even when he finally realizes the obvious,oh no! the baby is his and suddenly he wants to marry her and claim the babyi remained unconvinced.I know the past shapes who you end up as, but please, I hate when characters use it as an excuse to be an general ass.The last chapter/epilogue-ish makes up for the general frustration I had with this book, but overall.. eh.Unsafe1.75/5
