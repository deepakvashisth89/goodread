I remember my parents reading this to me as young as 4. I remember reading it myself as young as 7 to 10. I remember finding it in my closet as a teen and reading it standing up. I remember when we moved almost 3 years ago and I read it as I was sorting through things. I remember wanting to keep it for sentimental reasons because I must have read it a thousand times at every age, but thinking “this book meant so much to me as a kid and shaped me and another kid deserves to have it and maybe be shaped the same way i was” and giving it away to Goodwill. I remember last year looking up a PDF of the book and reading it, wishing I hadn’t gotten rid of it.

I still get reminded of this book often. I still wish I kept it but I also know I feel more fulfilled at the possibility of a different kid having my loved copy. I still think of the stories and lessons from this book, and I’m not even religious, and this book doesn’t require you to be.

This book is so kind and so real and so important, and I’ll always love it and be grateful for it with my whole heart.
