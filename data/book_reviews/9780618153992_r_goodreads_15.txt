POH-TAY-TOE 😂😂😂

I was so surprised to see how this book was structured, where we follow each group of characters in its own contained story, one after another - rather than using interweaving POVs which is more conventionally standard these days. Definitely an interesting method of storytelling, and I’ll never know how I’d feel about it if I didn’t already know the story from the movie… but coming in with the knowledge I do have, I actually really enjoyed the concentrated stories one after another.

Love the themes of nature with the Ents, and love the mental journey with Frodo, Sam & Gollum.

But this read has just confirmed again that no one really deserves a Samwise Gamgee 🥹
