A nice one for me! Georgie is secretly in love with her best friend's older brother Raphael. They're almost at a point (in the past) where they're dating but some expertly placed events tear them apart. Georgie has a step brother who likes her in a non-platonic way but Georgie is oblivious to that.

And this brother is sort of the OM who causes serious trouble to the extent that Raphael believes him and leaves Georgie and goes away to his homeland!!

Now currently Georgie is off to Bolivia to meet her best friend who's been inviting her for ages. She gets there and her purse gets stolen which had her passport and other essentials. Not knowing to speak the local language and finding out that her friend is actually out of the country, Georgie has no option but to turn to Raphael for help (that is bail her out of prison).

Raphael was one hot, possessive, own-your-soul kind of alpha. He would not let Georgie have any doubts about him or what they shared in the past.

Holy smokes. This book was pretty steamy..as in Raphael was an extremely thorough lover! Georgie was also doing just fine being hurt in her heart, not knowing why he abandoned her so many years ago. She was certainly head over heels with him!!

The end was a bit of a let down. The step brother shows up and unfortunately despite all of Raphael's redemption, Georgie does not side with him and chooses to side with the brother instead! She's also shown to let out doubts on when Raphael suggests marriage in the book.. Like even as a reader "I" could clearly assess how utterly in love Raphael was with Georgie. There was no room at all for questioning his intentions and yet, Georgie was stalling and belittling his proposal.

Eventually Georgie comes about her senses but not before being a total turnoff in terms of refusing Raphael!!!

You have my heart Raphael!! This one's a keeper!!
