This is about two mouse a little mouse and a big mouse. They both had work today but the little mouse decides he was not going to work that day. The big mouse ask him to help plant the seeds and he says no instead he swings. Everything he was asked to do he refused and did what he wanted to. The big mouse was getting frustrated because he was doing all the work. It got very hot and little mouse decides to do something good. He made him a hat and they picked strawberries, ate them and took a nap together.
