I thought that this book would be so fun!
Unfortunately, I was not very interested in the stories and the way that they are presented in this book. So many details about unimportant info that didn’t draw me in.

I tried to come at it several different ways in spurts over the course of several weeks - audio, ebook, and both together - but I am abandoning ship at only 20% in. Maybe I will revisit it later, But for now, I am moving on. Changes in latitudes/changes in attitudes! 🤣

Jimmy Buffett is pretty fascinating! Many Parrotheads might love this book. What a journey! But I couldn’t invest more time trudging through for now. The audiobook is narrated by Don Johnson. I thought that could be great, but it wasn’t great for me.
