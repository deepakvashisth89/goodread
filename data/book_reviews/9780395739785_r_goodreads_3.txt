I let my four-year-old daughter choose her own books in the children's section at the library, because I want her to enjoy reading as much as I always have.Unfortunately, my daughter chose this book, which may be one of worst books ever written.This gem of a book stars a weirdly-adult, nihilistic anthropomorphic cat who enjoys smearing garbage on himself and killing bugs to sabotage "gifts" and the little girl who loves him (and seemingly has no adult supervision). I found it to be as unsettling as a David Lynch film without any of the enjoyment. After reading it with my daughter, I felt physically ill, which is particularly strange if you look at my reading history on this site - I am no stranger to extremely graphic content, but the off-kilter, childish artwork that just seems "wrong" somehow, combined the uncomfortable elements of the story somehow left me feeling nauseous.There is no real "lesson" to the story, although I don't think children's books always need them. There's not much of a story, either, though. If there is a lesson, it's that young girls without parents should remain beholden to whomever is around, no matter how toxic and that antisocial behavior just means that he loves you. This book is the children's literary equivalent of a shock rock album.
