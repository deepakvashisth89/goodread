This book has aged really well. Written by an Englishman who immigrated to America 2 yrs before the American revolution. Payne did a great job speaking both to the timid colonists and the large set of English loyalists about the “common sense” reasons why the only way forward for the colonies was independence. His arguments are very applicable to today’s world, especially the contemporary divisions in our America.

My favorite quotes are:

Society in every state is a blessing, but government even in its best state is but a necessary evil…

For as in absolute governments the King is law, so in free countries the law ought to be king

Common sense will tell us, that the power which hath endeavored to subdue us, is of all others, the most improper to defend us.
