Some very good stuff here.In this novel I think Atwood captured a dwindling art in writing. I adored her lush exploration of the interior and relationships. I’ve seen a lot of people complain that the characters were unlikeable, which they very much are. I found myself frustrated by them and their decisions. I got a headache each time I turned a page.But, perhaps it’s a personal preference but I enjoy when characters are unlikeable. Atwood painted a very human portrait, her protagonists are annoying, selfish, indecisive, but never truly evil or unredeemable. Which I felt reflected real life.We are all in some way assholes, motivated by selfish impulses or an insecurity. The characters got lost in their own melodramas, believing themselves to be more important than they actually are.The prose was gorgeous, and the writing flowed beautifully.However, I do think this was way too long, it dragged on and on for no reason. Also I felt that Atwood sometimes overexplained her characters’ motives or feelings, there should be room for more ambiguity. Also, though the novel is fairly dramatic, there were points that were utterly ridiculous, particularly how suicide is treated. Everyone here talked at some point about wanting to off themselves and it read as cartoonish parody of a shakespearan tragedy.But otherwise, very interesting!
