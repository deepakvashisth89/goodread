This is a good reference book to use as you develop your characters and their traits and backstories. Some great insights, such as the typical Skyjacker has a religious mother and a violent father who is already dead. Who knew? Makes sense.

I use it constantly, if only for the amusement.

I gave it 3 stars rather than 5 since it is a bit outdated in the examples. On the other hand, human nature doesn't change that much in 20 years.
