A highly-literary account of the career—and most emotional or unique cases—of a neurosurgeon. Dr. Flitter is a gifted story-teller and is able to bring to life the drama, joy, and sorrow of seeing some of the worst possible cases a surgeon could imagine from horrible trauma to difficult neoplasms. I've read several books written by neurosurgeons about their lives and careers, and this one is certainly the best ("Working in a Very Small Space" is also very good). The level of empathy Dr. Flitter conveys is what makes this powerful.
