Decades before Patricia Cornwell, Elaine Raco Chase was doing extensive research to put reality and cutting edge science and technology into her intrigues and excellently developed crime drama, with great dialog and story development. I discovered her romance comedies in the public library and ravenously sought every one of her titles. It was tragic to learn her writing was interrupted because of a serious accident and I was distraught to think new titles wouldn't be forthcoming. At that time I wasn't able to purchase the books, but whenever possible I'd re-read library copies whenever they were available. How delighted to discover -30 years later- that the titles have been reprinted, are available for Kindle and are just as delightful as I remember them! I still laugh out loud at the witty quipts and double entendres that flow so exquisitely in Elaine Raco Chase's books. Imagine combining Judith Kent and Patricia Cornwell; that's Elaine Raco Chase writing style. Make it a goal to own each of her books! I do!
