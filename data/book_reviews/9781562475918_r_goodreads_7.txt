My choice to reread this when I did was absolutely timely. My mother passed in November, and looking for something light and easy to read during the chaotic aftermath I picked these up, dusted them off, and embraced them like the old friend that they are.I had completely forgotten that a key thread through this series is how the heroine's family is dealing with everyday life a year after the death of Josefina's own mother.Josefina's stories remind us how hope, new life, new love, and joy can be found in the every day living and family life after such a loss. They teach us that life will not, can not, and should not halt in its tracks after the death of a loved one, but that it must continue to carry on, to thrive, to grow, even to change. But it also doesn't ignore the difficulties, highlighting them as each is faced and overcome with often bittersweet triumph. And at the end, these stories prove that even the most heartaching memories can evolve into promising new love and happiness, if we just take the care and patience to weave old remembrances into new happinesses.I'm sometimes glad my mind tends to forget details of books so I can read them again almost like new. This is one of those times. What a comfort these were, even if I didn't think I needed it!
