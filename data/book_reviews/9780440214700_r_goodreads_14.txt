Starting out, this book was a considerable letdown after my high praise for Dark Dance. Where Dark Dance was very moody, following a single POV, very gothic and lush writing, this book has comparatively pedestrian prose, follows multiple POV, and is much plottier.

However, we lost power over the weekend, which made picking this up and reading it rather more motivating, and I finished the second half in a single long session, and enjoyed it quite a bit more than the first, which felt a bit meandering.
