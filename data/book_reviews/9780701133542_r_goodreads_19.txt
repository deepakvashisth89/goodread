There I was, hungry in the nest, when mommy bird Carter flies home with good grub. Finally! Suffice it to say my mouth was wide open. If I weren’t a baby bird in this metaphor, I’d say I’d be patting my belly and licking my fingers clean, but baby birds do not have fingers, so I won’t. Instead, I chirped and went to sleep or something. Right? I don’t know the goings-on of baby birds.But boy do I know the goings-on of the Hazard and Chance family! Transition. Dora and Leonora Chance are twins cast off by their famed actor of a father (Melchior Hazard), yet they share a love of the stage nonetheless. Must be a genetic thing. Their paths with him and the seemingly never ending buds of the family seed intertwine in five glorious acts of family comedy and tragedy à la William Shakespeare. I’ll steal from Elle Magazine now and say that “Carter’s storytelling constantly performs aMidsummer Night’s Dreamof the heart,” and if that isn’t the truest thing I’ve read, slap me silly and log out of Goodreads. I’d go so far as to say each sentence does the same. After 3 books, it’s glaringly obvious the woman crafts down to the details, and if someone could point me to similar authors, I’d be so gracious. Besides maybe…George Saunders, I don’t know many who devote themselves to silly. Who find the silliest way to write even the smallest sentiment. Meanwhile Carter’s here cranking the silly knob on her word machine. Woah there! It’s too much fun!!!! I can’t take it!! Humor, allusions, and philosophy blend in a way that makes you jump when you realize one’s been sneaking up on you. How does she do it? How can I ponder on father and child relationships while feeling like I’m watching the chorus line? She entertains like it’s show business. With flair and glitz and sex and vulgarity! This is carnival, it’s magical realism, it’s just about everything I want in a book! I’m cheering long after the curtain closes. Huge Angela Carter fan over here, I’ve got a foam finger with her name on it.I will say brushing up Shakespeare may make for a better experience. I figured I knew enough when really I was lazy. Like I am now. I may return to the review to gush about things I’m too lazy about writing. Idk.Now comes the part where I don’t feel like wrapping it up in a bow cause I can’t do that in either the metaphorical or literal sense! Here’s a haiku I wrote to finish us off instead:Angela Carter!Shall I compare thee to amotherfucking beast?
