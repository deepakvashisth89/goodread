A fun little adventure. Not as exciting as some of the other ones, like Mesozoic. Also, it begins in the middle, without a proper introduction of the characters or the origin of the marsupalami. That’s because it’s actually the 5th Spirou book written by Franquin, though the first published by Cinebook. (For some reason they decided to start the series with 4 volumes from the later & lesser Tome & Janry run)The story has two parts: first, a mystery. Finding the Marsupiilami. Then, a jailbreak, freeing him from captivity. There are many hijinks, though the tale never reaches a satisfying climax, remaining low key throughout.Narrative is mostly real-time, which can get tedious, but captions are also employed several times, compressing and clarifying events. Captions explain how the Marsupilami becomes panicked by the laughing crowd, for instance.
