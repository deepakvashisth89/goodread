Excellent biography of John Muir, upon finishing it I feel like I lost a dear soul friend...Wikipedia, and all the superlatives I could find, fail miserably to convey the greatness of this giant of a man. A superb naturalist, outdoorsman, inventor, scientist, craftsman, writer, family man, friend, and on and on... A truly brilliant spirit, he described nature as "a conductor of divinity," and I believe he indeed was himself also.
