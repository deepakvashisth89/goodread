Rick Wendell, part owner of an LA gay bar, lay naked and dead near his desk at the horse ranch he shared with his mother outside LA. In the next room, his mother walked in to find Larry Johns, naked and with a gun. Clearly, Larry had killed the well-liked bar owner, but insurance death claims investigator, Dave Brandstetter, is not so sure.His investigation takes him from gay bars along the beach, to a horse ranch, and to the home of an architect to movie creators. It also takes him to his own home where he and his lover, Doug, have just moved and Doug has opened an art gallery while caring for his aging mother.I enjoyed this third book in the Dave Brandstetter mystery series though, because there were so many twists and turns in the plot that I could not keep all the possible suspects and motives straight, the ending felt a little unsatisfying. Perhaps a second reading of the book, or some notetaking, would have helped me see where the clues were pointing. Still, the book was fun especially because of Hansen’s skill at creating his 1970s Los Angeles setting. Hansen is a worthy successor to the great LA noir authors who preceded him.A recommended book.
