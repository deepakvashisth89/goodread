Popper is a pretty persuasive writer, a real scholar. I was a bit skeptical when I started this book, since I had always held Plato in pretty high esteem. But Popper brought together a lot of what I already knew in a way that made me rethink some of my opinions.I would like to read the other volume of The Open Society and Its Enemies, on Hegel and Marx, but don't yet have it.
