Get Shortyis a great, fun read. I'm not certain why this is the firstElmore Leonardwork I've ever read, but I'm already looking forward to reading more!Leonard gives equal send-ups of the tough guys and the movie development players. The tough guys quickly experience unabashed excitement as they start considering casting or producing movies. And the movie-making players take them seriously as they want fresh ideas and angles.These movie folks include the would-be producers, stars, agents, and studio execs, but not the semi-invisible writers. Somehow, the writers are always referred to but not seen. Pitch perfect (except for the "Shylock" references and the casual misogyny toward actresses, but neither organized crime nor Hollywood can be accused of leading the way toward enlightenment).In readingGet Shorty, there were definitely moments when scenes from Robert Altman's "The Player" would flash into my consciousness. This was a positive bit of synchronicity.
