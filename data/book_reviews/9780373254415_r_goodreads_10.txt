Loved this book. I have always been sucker for decent character build up by an author. Ms. Krentz have a way of doing t that is seriously off the charts. The lady can pack a lot of story in few little pages.Letty is one independent gal. Believe me you can describe her as naive and sweet but she is strong. Xavier, well he is a good main lead. Letty teaches him a good lesson without being overly harsh and still stay true to herself.I mean today's books are so damn focused on explosive chemistry and what not that they end up describing a minor scene in ten pages and delegate pertinent content to just a few lines.Jayne Ann Krentz is now my official fall back romance author. Sometimes you just a need a break from all the bad writing out there.
