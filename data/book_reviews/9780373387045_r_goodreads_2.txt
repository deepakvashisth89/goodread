This book introduces the series. Meredith Colton is planning a major party for her husband Joe Colton's 60th birthday. And some other deadly surprises. .......

The major love interest in this book is Sophie Colton and a foster child still living on the ranch, River James. They grew up together, until Sophie graduated from high-school and moved away for college & a job. She then was engaged, until a vicious mugging brought Joe to help her and she broke it off with her fiancée. She also moved back to the ranch where she learned some creepy things about her mother, uncle, and that River was still in love with her. River and Sophia were set to announce their engagement but Joe was shot at during the first toast .........

Unbeknownst to the characters, but the reader is privy to: Louise is having problems with her memory. Her therapist tries hypnosis and some memories resurface. She had episodes and feelings based on the events happening to other characters.



I wanted to find out what happens with the stolen identity aspect of this series. I think: Patsy and Meredith were twins. Patsy was committed, had a girl that was kidnapped (Jewel), and then assumed Meredith's identity so as to find Jewel. She dropped a child off and then had an affair while "Meredith", breaking Joe's heart. Louise is the real Meredith, but has had amnesia which prevents her from confronting her twin and reclaiming her happy family.
