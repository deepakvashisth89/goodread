Dinosaurs SAD" and ends with "Dinosaurs looking right at YOU to say GOODBYE because we're through." It was interactive in the end in a way, with the dinosaurs saying goodbye to the reader and waving. I thought this was a fun book and I would read this to a toddler or smaller child.
