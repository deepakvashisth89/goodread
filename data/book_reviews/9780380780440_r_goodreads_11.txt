This actually had enough errors to be noticeable—but thankfully none of them were in the plot or character development. This American civil-war era romance sets a pickpocket against a soldier, and as one would expect, both hilarity and hotness ensue.I loved Devon and Cole every step of the way, and while I could absolutely understand Cole's frustration with Devon's determination to head off on her own, the twists in his story made him adorable!
