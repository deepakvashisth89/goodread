Three stars on reader error (?): definitely more history than theory that I was hoping for here. The first chapter is full of setting up the ways in which definitions and collective thinking have shaped the patriarchy and oppressed women for over two millennia, which I think could have cultivated in a fascinating intersection of communication studies (particularly the Sapir-Whorf hypothesis), women’s studies, and political theory, especially with the emphasis of definitions being the cornerstone of developing a feminist consciousness. However, the historical background provided in this book vital as foundation for what can be explored next.
