This book was just "bwah??" Exceedingly bizarre, and quite refreshing. Napolean becomes a street kid, by the way, so that is what most of it's about. A writing style unlike anything I've ever seen before, what with the words "sez" and "skool" being common, while "and" and "walk" don't appear at all. This one blew by, and I quite enjoyed it.

I was quite surprised when the author himself stopped by my webpage (due to a vanity search) and graciously answered some questions I had about the book. It made the experience that much more meaningful.
