This Le Carre George Smiley novel is oddly organized. Without giving away too much the novel involves a series of lengthy flashbacks to the Cold War era stitched together by Smiley speaking to a room full of awestruck intelligence and spy recruits. My assumption is that Le Carre had a series of story "nubs" or "bits and pieces" set in the Cold War era but didn't know exactly what to do with them once the Cold War was clearly over. So, he conjured his vignettes together by making two of his recurring characters (Smiley and Ned) reminisce about the old days.I was going to give this 3 stars, but Le Carre added a little something extra at the end. The vignettes got stronger and he injected a line of philosophical conjecture: "how and when will we know who won the Cold War?" Thirty years later, he was spot on about the weaknesses of the West and Russia. Hauntingly accurate actually. this bumped it up to 4 stars for me.
