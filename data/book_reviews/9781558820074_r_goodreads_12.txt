3.5 (I seem to be rating a lot of books with 3.5 stars lately) This novel was written in 1939 and considered a zany, screwball mystery. Times do change, and while I knew which lines were supposed to be funny, I didn't have much reaction to them as such. It might be better as a movie.In any case, it was enjoyable, and a pretty complicated mystery. I am planning to continue with the series.
