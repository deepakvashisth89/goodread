I read this book along with classmates in grade 11. I never knew about Afghanistan before picking it up. I was disinterested in reading it with the class but certain moments from the story stuck with me, even after all these years.I saw a few scenes of the animated movie based on the book on Instagram and YouTube and I was reminded of how much this story stuck with me, even superficially. I found a copy at my library and took it home. To read the story in its entirety was an experience. The Breadwinner is full of violence, despair, suffering… and hope. Hope for a better future - one that is still far out of reach for Afghanistan even in 2026. Nevertheless, this story was very touching and endearing.Years later, I still remembered the feeling this story left behind even when I had forgotten parts of the plot itself. I think that says something about how deeply human suffering and hope can imprint themselves on us, even when we’re too young to fully comprehend.
