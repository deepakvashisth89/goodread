This book was really good, but it was ruined by the untimely usage of a time shift in the book. One of the more tension filled spaces in the book is the war, and the aftermath of the war. And it is tense because among other things, we don't know how it would impact into the protagonist. And in the time shift we learn a lot while the main tension knot is not resolved, but a lot of tension is released.Of course, if you read Eon, you'd know he's still alive, but this book is proposed as a prequel, so I read it first.
