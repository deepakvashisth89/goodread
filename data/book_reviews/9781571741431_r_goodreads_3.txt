I’ve initially read this years ago, but only from the chapter “Astral Wildlife” onwards as I was only interested in phenomenons.

Now years later, I reread the entire book and this time, I felt that the earlier chapters held great insights into how one can achieve an OBE. The methods explained were very feasible and simply broken down into layman terms. The theories behind the methods were soundly put, and made a lot of sense. Take for example when Robert explained how mind split could be done, he also explained how this was made possible. The reasonings were well thought out and logical, and seemed to be solidly backed with a lot of his experiences.

One thing I notice though that it can get very theoretical and for readers like me with low attention span, it becomes very tempting to skip paragraphs and chapters altogether. (Probably also the reason why I skipped the first half of the book when I was younger 😝)

Some paragraphs I also find repetitive and becomes a chore to read on.

Personally, I would have loved to read more about what populates the astral planes. It would have been nice to have a seasoned projector create some sort of an encyclopaedia/ glossary of the beings to be encountered there. Would have also loved to read more of his personal encounters with astral beings. The one with that old monk was very entertaining.

I was also very much interested in the Akashic records, and I thought the explanation behind the astral wind/ Akashic pulse and the whole karmic thing was brilliant and sensible.

All in all, this was a really neat book with good coverage on techniques. I’d recommend this to any serious wannabe projectors but for entertainment value more towards the lower side. See this as abit like a textbook
