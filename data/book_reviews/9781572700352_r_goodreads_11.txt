This is the book that started Nero Wolfe on his career. See my comments on "Full House" for review._______________________I decided to take a break and do a re-read of the first book of the Nero Wolfe corpus....the book that started my obsession with that character. It may not be the best of the 40+ books, of which I have read all but four, but it is the beginning sketch of the Wolfe and Archie relationship, one of fiction's greatest duos. It also shows how the household on W35th Street changed over the years with Archie becoming a little more polished and Wolfe mellowing just a bit. What fun!
