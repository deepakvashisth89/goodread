An excellent overview of Magee's philosophical/political/broadcasting journey, he even gives a bit of insight into how this all affected his personal life as well, I like something he said here in the final chapter, 'The basic philosophical problems are presented to us by living, not by books or by the education system.", how true that is...Magee also presents some great reading ideas for those who may be interested in philosophy, some of his early recommendations are Descartes' Discourse on Method and Locke's Essay on Human Understanding, I've read the Discourse its a pretty simple read, the Essay is a bit trying but a good read none the less...
