After all these years, this was my first time reading this one. It was a LOT longer than I imagined it would be from the movie, haha.Broken up into parts, Part 1 was most like the movie, with Louis's interview and a discussion of his relationship with Lestat, his creation, and their early life together. This was also when Claudia was created.Part 2 went more into Claudia, and I loved her so much. Claudia was my favorite character in the entire book. Weirdly, Louis, the narrator, had a minor personality of any of the characters in the book. This section was the slowest in the book and was starting to drag on. I honestly wanted to give up on the book at one point.Luckily I pushed through because Part 3 picked up with leaving America. The new scene provided additional characters and events, and the rest of the novel was a lot faster-paced and interesting, covering the ending events of the movie.Part 4 was almost more of an epilogue. It was also not my favorite section, and Louis was the least interesting.Overall I went back and forth between loving and being bored by this novel. I will be reading the next book to see Lestat's story.3.5 Stars rounded up to 4.
