The memoir part of the book was interesting. Many parallels between our lives, which kept my interest far longer than I normally would have been. When I reached the 'psychic' portion, Part II, I found myself disappointed because it was so very much geared towards 'God' and this does not resonate for me in any way. Will continue to search for what I am looking for... But this book wasn't it. I also found her 'timeline' to be utterly confusing at certain points.
