J.M. Dillard did a wonderful job of staying true to the characters and creating characters with depth. The plot of this book was solid but but towards the end had too many half developed characters/felt rushed. The beginning of the novel was the enterprise helping the planet Aritani which was being attacked by what we find out is Romulan pirates but that goes to the wayside in favor of Spock's amnesia and McCoy trying to solve Spock's problem/falling in love with the specialist sent to help. Poor Dr. McCoy doesn't have much luck in that department.
