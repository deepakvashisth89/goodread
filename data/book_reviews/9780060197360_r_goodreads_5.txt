I would usually rather have a big slice of moist chocolate cake than a slice of pie, whatever the variety. But this book makes you crave pie, makes you (foolishly?) think that even you could make a crust from scratch yourself, and provides dozens of recipes for regionally famous pies (raspberry pear, turtle pie, butterscotch pie, rhubarb custard, etc. etc.) along with a pretty good travel narrative.I don't, however, recommend reading it during the first 12 weeks of pregnancy. It made me so queasy I had to put it away for a few months- but it was definitely me, not the book!
