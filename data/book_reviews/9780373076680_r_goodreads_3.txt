Alicia Scott is the pen name of Lisa Gardner and it is clear from this book what exactly she had to offer the wider world of thriller fiction. This story is book two of the Guiness Gang and tells the story of Mitch Guiness, special operative in the Witness Protection Program. He is supposed to hide Jessica Governee, supermodel, but she is not what she seems and that irks Mitch. Jessica doesn't trust any man, not even Mitch, but there comes a time where she no longer has a choice and no longer wants one. It's a fast-paced story with enough sexual tension to keep the romance strong but you read this for her attention to the littlest details that made her a strong thriller fiction writer.
