I really didn’t know what the hell I was getting myself into when I started this series.. this volume had me crying from how fucked up it was.People that claim there’s no variety in shōjo don’t know what they’re talking about. This series proves that shōjo isn’t just ditzy, dramatic, “feminine” romance. Mars is dark, disturbing, and brutal.I love the deep romantic aspects of this series and can’t look away from the horrifying themes intertwined within it. I’m riding this story out, and I hope it’s worth it in the end.
