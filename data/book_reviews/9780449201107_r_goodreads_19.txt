I like this series and usually listen to one when taking a break from meatier works. My chief complaint with this one centers on the way Charlotte's social standing is handled. On the one hand, there is much discussion about class difference and Charlotte is critical of upperclass snobbery. Yet when she gets invited to her wealthy sister's social functions she quickly accepts a borrowed dress, finds a sitter, and off she goes. Her detective husband doesn't seem to mind - or doesn't know - and is never included in the invitation. All this, of course, is a plot device to get Charlotte involved in investigating the mystery, but seemed contrived. Thomas Pitt played such a minor role that he appeared rather inept in his role as chief investigator of the crime.I have the entire series on my reading list and have hopes that others are better. I very much enjoy the reader.
