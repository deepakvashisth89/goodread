An lucid and clear intellectual biography of Freud's thought. It is interesting to see how Freud's ideas developed over time, with his shifting emphasis picking up and returning to topics with new insight. Of particular interest to me is the extension of psychoanalytic thinking to society.

Wollheim is adept at interrogating each strand of thought and assessing its agreement or contradiction with earlier work. This philosophical bent is what makes this book particularly valuable but also makes it more intellectually challenging than other texts.
