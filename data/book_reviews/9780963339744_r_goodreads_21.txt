DISTURBING. Truly and utterly disturbing. Wow, what did I just read? I am not sure disturbing is the right word. Maybe depraved............ This was my first Jack Ketchum book. I have seen the movie version of this book but don't remember it all that great. I know that it too bothered me. I have also seen The Lost movie based on his book as well and that was really disturbing too. So a couple of Jack Ketchum based movies but no books...............

Many of you know that I dabble with extreme horror now and then and when so many of my extreme horror fans rant about the sickness of this book it really has to make you wonder. I also remember when I first joined Goodreads and was reading horror it really disappointed me. There just wasn't enough horror in the horror books I was reading and a lot of them were more like thrillers. I would ask people that if they had a good horror recommendation to forward it to me. Let me admit that this book is basically what I was looking for. I only had to read like 250 books or so to find it but I am so glad I did. I hope Ketchum can continue to deliver with this disturbing theme. And no, I clearly do not want to read this type of book exclusively but it was a rare find.

So this is based on a true story. Ketchum heard of the abuse of 16 year old girl in the 80's at the hands of her foster parents. What he read re that case stayed with him and after his mother passed he went back to New Jersey where he grew up and the thought of telling this story set in the 50's came to him. He also used Jersey as the backdrop and the neighbourhood he grew up in as the setting just because he was so familiar with it.

I don't know what it is but I so love stories based on young teen boys and the crap they get into. Growing up we lived near a huge wooded area and the things we got into were just nuts and hard to believe. There were several times when I really and honestly believe our lives were in danger.

So in this story Meg our 14 year old star and her sister Susan are orphaned as a result of a car accident. Susan is 8 and reduced to having to use metal crutches to assist her with her walking. The two girls are sadly placed with a relative, Ruth, who raises three boys on her own. The boys range in age from what appears to be 8ish to 14ish.

Now Meg is quite a knockout for a girl her age and befriends a younger boy named David. David is the narrator of the story and he has become a successful investment banker but is clearly haunted as a result of what happened over this one summer and yeah I think you would never really get over what happened.

So the main villain in the story is Ruth herself. She shows glimpses of mental illness early on and by the end she really did flip. She starts on Meg with regard to being a slut and a whore. The verbal attacks keep escalating. When Meg stands up for herself Ruth punishes her sister instead. Ruth thinks this will control Meg's behaviour.

The envelope keeps getting pushed so to speak. Meg is eventually tied up and caged in the basement bomb shelter. She becomes the object of scorn, ridicule and torture. Some of the things done to her include the eating of dog crap, physical and sexual abuse including the carving of the phrase Ruth came up with into her stomach "I FUCK FUCK ME". A searing tire iron is used to put an end to her vagina and the whorish behaviour that Meg's vagina brings out in Meg according to Ruth. Like this book has some seriously sick moments and clearly not everyone would be interested in it.

Yet the story captivates you. You get angry. You get angry at everyone in this book. You get mad at the boys for what they do. You get mad at the main character David as he participates in a lot of the abuse forced on to Meg. You get mad at Meg herself and even her poor sister Susan for not doing more. It is quite a crazy read and yet you just want to keep reading, you have to find out if everything is going to work out, but seriously a burned vagina like how would you recover from that?

In fact you might think that there is no way this could happen but remember it's based on a true story. It also reminded me of an incident in our grade school. I was in grade seven but it involved the grade 8 class when they had a field trip to Niagara Falls (Canadian side). We had one of the most beautiful grade 8 girls that likely ever existed in that class. She was smoking hot with big breasts and a perfect butt. So many of the boys had been to Niagara Falls and were aware of the countless wax museums. Well the boys came up with a plan knowing in that one of them things got dark and isolated. It was planned to grope the girl at this part of the museum. Sadly that plan carried out. We learned quickly of what happened through brothers and friends and many of those involved were so proud of what they accomplished having no regard for that girl at all. This pathetic tale just shows what a group of average middle class kids can come up with. Just nuts, just shameful and just crazy! So this illustration of what 13/14 year old boys can do, how they can think, how they can plan and do something so evil just rang so true to me sadly. In the story many of the neighbourhood kids become involved in Meg's abuse including other girls. The growing depravity just rang so true to me.

So as things escalate for poor Meg her kind of friend David decides that enough is enough. He confronts Ruth and her kids but he too also gets himself tied up and captured. He ends up sharing the bomb shelter with Meg and her sister Susan. As all this continues you just know that someone like David's parents are going to look into matters. They are aware that he is spending a lot of time at Ruth's place and David's mother is worried that such a pretty girl lives next door and doesn't feel David should be over as much. If David does not come home they are going to get involved.

By the end Ruth has completely derailed and I'll leave it at that. The ending is so sad. The story so shocking and sick it leaves one dumbfounded, like just a vicious kick to the groin area. And Ketchum, he is an accomplished author, he can really write and he sets a tempo and a mood that really plays on your mind. This book truly gets into you and in ways unlike many other books. I mean how did he even think of some of these things done to Meg. We're they actually part of the true story behind it?

Again not recommended for everyone but if you want to read something disturbing bordering on how depravity can come into existence and thrive then this book is a great choice. Again I have never read anything that disturbed me like this and due to that and all the feelings that it elicited in me I have to give it an easy five stars. So looking forward to more Ketchum!!
