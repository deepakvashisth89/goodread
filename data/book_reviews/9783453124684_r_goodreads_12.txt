Okay this is a hard one to rate. The ending was great. The reveal of the murderer and to find out who it all happened was so fun and interesting. But the rest of the book was a bit hard to follow. There are so many different people involved and you read from their perspective as well so especially in the beginning it was hard work trying to remember who that person was and how he was involved. But even though I got confused and irritated by this sometimes, I still had a fun time reading it.
