Nice!Who doesn't know this old movie of the eccentric lady and her more an more drunken butler?This book will give me more to think and laugh the next time I happen to see this movie. Even without the connection to the movie it is a nicely written mystery story - but knowing the movie you will find so many big and small portions of it woven into a new story and given its very own background.Why is there a tiger on the floor?What is the reason for the strange ritual of celebration with non-present guests?Funny and entertaining - yet invites you to use your brain for solving the mystery scenes.
