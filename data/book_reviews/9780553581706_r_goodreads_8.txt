Jan 26, 1130am ~~ The final book in the Night trilogy.Deeply moving, but often traumatic and painful to read, this 1961 novel is best summed up by the author himself in the preface he wrote for the 2006 edition of the book."This novel deals with a number of obvious themes, but its true subject remains unspoken: Having survived the cruelest of wars, how does one go on in a hostile or indifferent world?"How does a survivor cope with surviving? Can they ignore the memories, stop seeing what they saw in the death camps? Turn off the pain? The smell?In this book, Wiesel imagines one man's approach to this dilemma. As heartbreaking as the other two titles in the trilogy were, this one hit me even harder.I can't say more right now. I am too full of tears.
