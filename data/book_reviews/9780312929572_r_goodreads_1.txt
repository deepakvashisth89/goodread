I thought I would start reading Christmas books now as I never seem to get them all read before the season is over. The book was o.k. but nothing exciting. It is about a couple that have let the pressures of employment mostly, separate them. They hardly know each other anymore. The wife decides for Christmas she will give her husband a trip back east where there is snow & good memories. (they live in Calif.) She wants to surprise him so when someone offers him tickets to some BIG football game, hotel rooms included, he thinks he will surprise her. What a mess it turns out to be.
