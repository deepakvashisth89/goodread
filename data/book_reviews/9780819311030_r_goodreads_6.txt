As a pro-bicycling commuter this makes me happy to see a kids book showcasing bicycles as transportation.

Overall:
Graphics are nicely done but nothing real special just a typical kids book. There's about four lines per page. The book has some dialogue which will help teach oral reading instruction. There are many quotes with exclamation points so there are plenty of chances for reading with voice inflections. The book has a fun rhythm and the plot fosters problem solving.
