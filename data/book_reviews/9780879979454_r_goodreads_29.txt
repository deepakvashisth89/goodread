Always enjoyable to read science fiction that focuses on a “softer” science, in this case linguistics. The book being about language and written by a linguist added an interesting layer while reading; there were many turns of phrases, perspective choices etc. that would cause me to wonder at the deeper meaning being them. As a result, I found the writing wonderful and the only sticking point to be the content. Some of the plot points didn’t always feel cohesive but I could still see how they served the bigger themes so it never felt glaring.

The creation of a women’s only language being the path to female liberation was an unique concept to me and I’m curious to find out whether this was also the author’s personal view or solely an invention for the book.

A quote that stood out to me:

“Every woman was a prisoner for life; it was not some burden that she bore uniquely. She would have all the company she would ever need.”
