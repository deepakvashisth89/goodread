While this story did a few SB twists that I love, most of this book dragged. Wanting to know the ending kept me invested. At parts, I felt like I was dragging myself through it. I didn't really believe the romance. Gray and Barrie barely had any chemistry. If it wasn't her drive for a good story and his to protect an innocent life, they would probably never even speak to each other. The romance at the end felt a bit flat.

Granted, this story was published around 1996, she has written far better stories since.
