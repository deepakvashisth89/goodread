"Gracious Lady" is the story of Sophie and Maximillian.

Wow. This was an utter mess of a trainwreck.

The book has a simple plot- the widowed hero hires the heroine to be his 16 year old daughter's companion. The execution, however, has too many loopholes.
We have too many things happening at one time- petulant daughter, snarky OW, mysterious assistants, racehorses, unscrupulous journalist friends, a weird forbidden attraction, dramatic past problems leading to suspicion and trust issues, a sudden love confession and the end.

When did they actually fall in love? WTF was this. Not worth anyone's time.

Bleh.
