Ugh. God save me from terrible romances. I downloaded this from my library because the cover looked cool. I admit, I judged too early.The book was dated, from the wardrobe (hello neon leotard) to the lack of cell phones.I had a hard time relating to the main character. Not only was she passive, but her career (academic guidance counselor) was unrealistic. Every academic advisor I had in college was useless, and her constant need to give advise outside of an academic environment was just ridiculous. If anything, the author should have written her career as a psychologist, at least that would give her opinions some backing.
