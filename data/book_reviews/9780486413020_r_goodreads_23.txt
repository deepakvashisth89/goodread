I was not thrilled by this one. The only part I enjoyed was when the Scarecrow, Tinman, Woot, and Polychrome are transformed by the giant and must escape. Including another tin man with a similar experience to Nick Chopper's was also interesting. But mostly the story felt a little dull, and the ending was a letdown, even though things were resolved neatly.My favorite line in the book is in Baum's intro: "my books are intended for all those whose hearts are young, no matter what their ages may be."
