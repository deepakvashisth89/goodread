The level of intensity this play contains with only three female characters is astronomical from start to finish. A heinous crime occurs within the sanctity of a convent and Doctor Martha Livingston is tasked to find an answer - though she ends up with much more than she bargained for as countless secrets come to light. Faced with Agnes' innocent demeanor and Mother Superior's desire to push her out, the Doctor must decide how far she is willing to go to get the answers she wants.There are several intricate relationships explored throughout the play, both on and off the stage, making it a difficult production in content and performance. I would like to see it professionally done at some point because there were a few moments where a new staging action occurred in the middle of the dialogue but wasn't depicted as a separate event and this consistently confused me as to how it was being visually represented.
