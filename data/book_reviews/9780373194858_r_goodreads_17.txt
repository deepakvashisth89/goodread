I truly enjoyed reading this book!
It is an engaging story that started with
an crazy farce that happened every
year. With the roll of the dice, two people
are to get married for one day.
This was finally Brett's year!
He couldn't be more happy, that
his chosen bride is Laurel.
He has always liked her.
But, since they last saw each other,
life has drastically changed for Laurel,
& Brett's life is changing for the better.
I loved everything about the story!
A MUST READ FOR EVERYONE!!
