The first Dirk Pitt story. This is the one that introduced the world Clive Cussler's famous underwater hero (even though it was book #6 released I believe). A very entertaining, fun read. It certainly felt a bit "James Bonds-ish" in most of the story, especially with the villainous monologue near the end of the story, revealing his "evil plans". But that aside, I rather enjoyed the book and plan on reading the entire Dirk Pitt series. 4 action-packed stars for this one. Just my two cents.
