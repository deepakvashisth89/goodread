This was a book I read a long time ago as a teen! It’s a gothic romantic mystery. The first part was a bit slow but picked up the second half of the book! I like the heroin Harriet and she deserved a happy ending . She overcame much adversity and was a strong young woman. Bevil was a decent hero but did disappoint me a bit with his cluelessness at times. He did redeem himself in the end. All in all a good book that brings back my teen years!
