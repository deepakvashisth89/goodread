I’ve had this on my physical “to-read” shelf for years and years. I finally cracked the covers with great anticipation, since I am a product of the subculture Schaap writes about. I found the characters familiar, relatable, and interesting but the storylines seemed a bit forced. I like the story “Mins the Scavenger,” the last one in the book, the best. And I really appreciated the epilogue, where Schaap brings it all home: “joy...comes from two humbling realizations–that our sin is real, and that it is gone.” That’s the good news of the Gospel.
