The world building is alright and the dragons are dragons. Most made-up words are explained, except for “fewmets”. This is quite unfortunate, as it is used almost every page.The thing that pisses me off is the love interest, Akki.Usually I avoid spoilers, but a plot twist must be revealed to understand her stupidityMost of the main characters are bonders, indentured servants who must work until they fill the bag around their necks and become a “master”. It’s disturbing that everyone’s goal is to basically be a slave owner, but whatever. I suppose the whole “being a master” could merely mean being the master of your own fate.Jakkin works at a dragon nursery, as does Akki. I guess they fall in love, but it’s mostly just her dodging questions, laughing at him and calling him stupid (as all poorly written love interest do); and her getting pissed whenever a man tries to pay her. She is determined to fill her bag without a man’s coin. It’s kinda implied this may have something to do with her being born in a brothel (she left when she was 12 and no one knows if she actually “worked”), however, there’s no reason to refuse money just because a MAN is trying to give it too you. He’s not trying to get a lil somethin somethin, he’s trying to be nice, no need to crash out. She gets even more upset when the money is from Master Sarkkahn, the owner of the nursery and her supposed boss.Her irrational behavior is even more irrational when it’s revealed that she isn’t a bonder. Her freedom was bought by Sarkkhan, her FATHER. She’s just pretending to be a bonder. WHY? When Jakkin asks her about it, she just says the same ol’ “no man’s coin will enter my bag.” WOMAN. YOUR DAD LOVES YOU AND FREED YOU FROM SLAVERY WHY ARE YOU DOING THIS? WHAT DO YOU HAVE TO PROVE? That you’re a strong, independent prossy who don’t need no man?I guess Sarkkhan isn’t the best father, judging by how he tells Jakkin that Akki needs a strong master (ie Jakkin).
