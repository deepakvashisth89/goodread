Having lived in Cambodia for 18 months recently, I found this book particularly interesting and enlightening. It clarified many things I didn’t understand about the Vietnam War. Many of the traditions and ways of the Vietnamese are similar to the Cambodian people. This book emphasizes and reveals the power of media. A single picture or a single article can move people profoundly, and this can cause meaningful change. In this case, a war was effected by a single photograph. But this book focused even more on how the lives of those pictured can be impacted. This young girl spent the rest of her life dealing with the impact of this single photograph, first as a puppet or pawn for her communist government, then later as a warrior for peace on the world stage. She is a heroine. But what a life she had to live before achieving freedom to wield the power of that photograph in a way she determined was best.
