Very very depressing.Set initially in Nigeria, then in Paris.Story of a woman, almost an orphan, despite having a mother and paternal relatives alive, who is forced to fend for herself in Paris.Woman centric, but in a negative way.This is one woman, who doesnt like herself, and lets herself be used by men.Phillippe was one of the most negative and disliked ( by me) fictional men I came across in recent past.Her sense of smell isnt used for anything good; moreover towards the end, I felt it could be something akin to schizophrenia.The writing was good, story was pathetic
