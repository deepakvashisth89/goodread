This book is extremely problematic. Ronan is an emotional and sexual abuser, a master manipulator who persuades Lily to marry him, then leaves her one day later after using her - all part of elaborate revenge plan against her brother, Davey by traumatizing her. He then continues this pattern, because he hasn't got what he wanted yet, and apparently can't leave her alone.

There many 'erotic' scenes where Lily very clearly states she does not give consent to his sexual domination, but he continues anyway and it's all ok because her body is so appreciative and 'wants it'

Puh-leeze.

Oh course Walker would have you believe that despite Ronan's established and admitted pattern of betrayal and abuse, he's actually a nice guy, and on the foundation of steamy red-hot sex Lily and Ronan somehow make a working relationship, everything is wonderful, everyone is forgiven. /unicorns and rainbows and everyone lives happily ever after.

No, I don't think so. Ronan's behaviour was twisted and abhorrent. Asides from his 'reformation' and declaration of love (yeah right) there is no proof that he will not fall back into his abusive and manipulating ways if anything goes wrong into the future. People don't change.

If you would like an insight into the mind of a sexual abusive partner and their perspective of domestic rape then this book is for you - everyone else stay well clear.

Oh, epic logic fail: If this is all part of plan to punish Davey and he is consumed by greed for wealth as written, why the hell would he marry Lily, just so she could presumably divorce him, take half his assets and share them with her brother?

I rate this book zero stars - absolute trash and I will be burning it shortly so no-one else has to suffer through it.
