I learned quite a bit about coffee from this book. It made me want to plant a shrub or two of my own, even though it would be way more work than I'd be willing to do. However, it did inspire me to start trying some different varieties of coffee and and some new brewing methods. I had no idea they were so different. Turns out I really like French pressed coffee. And the recipes were quite tasty. Matching the recipes to the different types of coffee was fun...like a wine tasting, but with coffee!
