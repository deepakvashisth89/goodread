I thought the plot for this book was very unique. A young woman finds herself lost for hours and can't remember what happened in that time. It makes for an intense story, which ends in a beautiful romance. Maggie wants to be a doctor, and she's determined to be one. When circumstances bring her out west, she learns all she can from the local expert. Connor owns a Colorado ranch, and he finds himself constantly thinking of the beautiful, red-headed Maggie. He doesn't know that his brief encounter with her in New York will change his life. Thanks to Ebook Discovery for the complimentary book, which I enjoyed a lot. I'm happy to review Forever in My Heart, and I recommend it, and look forward to reading more in the series.
