I loved the start of this book. The pain, loneliness and inadequacy Cass feels about her life and childhood is so real, I felt like she was real. I connected with her character on a deep level. I also love the fact that Melody shows the way an upbringing like that will have you grasping for any crumbs of belonging and affection you can get. And also lead you to make some terrible decisions. Cass is a character you deeply relate to and constantly root for. But the end of the story was lazy. The events on the farm are brushed over and minimized once she leaves and the conclusion is just thrown together. Obviously I'm glad she got a happy ending but it almost took away from the integrity of the story. And how raw and honest Melodys writing was in the beginning.
