As I reach my going home daysThis book tells the story of an elderly couple, together for forty years in India. Tusker and Lucy decided to stay on in India after the British left because it had been their home for so long and also because India is cheaper than England to retire in.It is the most beautiful and heartbreaking story I've read in ages. The couple is always bickering and at cross purposes, mostly because of Tusker's bull-headedness. There is love, devotion, and loyalty there too, but Lucy has to pan for it, like panning for gold. It's hard work. Tears were falling as I came to the end. The author, Paul Scott, is a master at knowing about human emotion and especially of the British of the old school.
