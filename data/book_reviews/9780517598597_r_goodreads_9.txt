I first read this book in August of 2023 and absolutely hated it. The concepts were just too far out for me. Hadn’t thought about the book in a long time but last Sunday felt drawn to revisit it. With a new perspective I now understand the philosophy and actually made a lot of connections. He is definitely onto something, but there are a lot of completely pointless chapters in this book as well. Still skeptical as to whether these ideas alone can restore eyesight. I think he is missing a lot of the physiological factors such as the nervous systems role in eyesight. Also it being written before smartphones makes a lot of the ideas a lot harder to implement in a world where most of life is 5 feet in front of us.
