Months after I read this book and went on to other William Sleator books, I did a report on it by creating the large game board described in the story. I think I still have it in the back of a closet in San Leandro. I do remember than while creating it, I wished the slots I cut, the swirls I painted, and the cards I wrote really would do what they were supposed to do.Interstellar Pig and Singularity are the two best William Sleator books because they fuck with the mind most successfully. At least that's how I remember it.Every time I see a shattered window, I think about how all the lines, if extended, might point to a single location in the distance where space voyagers would perk up their senses with the knowledge that I've spotted them, and come after me.I'll have to go back and read the book again. Maybe I'll dig up my game board and now it'll work.
