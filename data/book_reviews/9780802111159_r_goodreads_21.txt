I have never read anything like this book, and am startled by how easy it was to read, yet the complexity in which lies beneath the comedy.
I realised in the last few pages, when Derek (the husband) is talking about all the women around him going mad, that this book is about the oppression of women, no more and no less, and how women take there power, or do not access it.
I am a little embarrassed that I didn’t notice this earlier, but this single realisation has left me stunned and made me rethink the entire story.
It was fun, easy to read, and just startling enough that it made you think hard about people.
I’m glad I randomly found this book.
