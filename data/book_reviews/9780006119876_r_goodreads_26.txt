My mom & I read a bunch of Victoria Holt novels when I was a teen-ager. I came across this in a Half Price Books, and I thought I'd read it for old time's sake. I expected my adult self to be kind of bored, but I was so wrong about that. I got totally sucked in and I completely enjoyed it.What my adult self did notice was how much she ripped off--er, borrowed fromJane Eyre. It's like she had the outline of that first gothic romance pinned to her wall and she followed not only the same structure but in many ways, used the same ideas. But the similarities didn't bother me. I mean, there's a reasonJane Eyreworked, right?I'm wishing I had another Victoria Holt novel to read right now.
