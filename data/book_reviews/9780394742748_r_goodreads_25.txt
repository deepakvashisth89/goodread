First of all, this not your usual detective story. An old man was killed in a locked room and a bank was robbed. During the robbery, a man was killed. How these separate crimes are connected? To know that, you have to read the book. But beware, this book is slow, horribly, terribly and excruciatingly so. I used this as my bedtime reading, and it gave me guaranteed slumber before I moved on to third or fourth page, everyday.That being said, one would also notice that this is a pleasant reading. The ending was unusual, if not ironical. Most of the characters are simple, normal and just everyday people. Martin Beck, the detective, is not a genius sleuth, he is just focused, thorough and systematic. Rhea, Beck's female interest, is not someone he miserably falls for. They are just comfortable together, just like a chuckle and a wink. This book also has some comedic character and scenes in it, which may be enjoyable. You will also feel that the writers have strong views of the society, politics and administration, and are not sissy to express them. This is unusual again, where other mystery writers mostly avoid this and focus on the crime.At the end, this could be a good read, if you are ready for it. Boring to the brim, yes, but, this is not your usual detective story, like I said before. It talks about various social, political and administrative aspects of 1970's Sweden. Be prepared for a slow but tricky, unusual but so everyday and grim-faced but inquisitive detective story, and maybe, just maybe, you will enjoy it. And at the end, maybe you will also think, why? how? with a smile on your face...
