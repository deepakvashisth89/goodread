There is a dead body followed by a murder investigation in this story, eventually, but there was much more to this story. I really liked it. The story follows Sarah Causeley who has become a governess for a short time to Chloe Hallum. Most of the story takes place in 1936 starting just before Franco starts the civil war in Spain and ends shortly after George VI becomes King of England. World events have a big impact on the Hallam family while Sarah lives with them, but it is Sarah and her insights that story is mostly about.
