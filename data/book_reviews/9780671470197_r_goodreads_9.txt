Promise Me Tomorrow is deliciously bad. Byron is the worst as is Sarah. Actually, there wasn't a character that was good in this book. The plot was misogynistic, but funny enough, entertaining. Nora Roberts hates this book to the point where she will never let it be republished. I managed to get a hold of a physical copy, and I'm glad I did. I knew it was going to be bad, so my expectations were met! It was definitely a time! Will I read it again? No, but I think if you can handle the 70s/80s craziness that is romance, you should definitely give it a go. And Nora Roberts is right. Promise Me Tomorrow is her worst book!
