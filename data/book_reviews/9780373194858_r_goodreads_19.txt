A joke wedding for his best friend will leave lasting memories. Laurel is down on her luck and the only person she can turn to is baby's daddy, Brett. All she wants his a job but Brett wants more a wife and a baby. He will do anything to get Laurel to marry him but Laurel only wants to marry for love.

With many twists and turns do they finally end up as happily ever after couple. Loved how Brett would not give up on Laurel. This is a sweet clean romance that I thoroughly enjoyed.
