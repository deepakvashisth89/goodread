This is almost a perfect mystery. The story itself is spooky (though a little over-the-top), and the Club's detective work isn't cringey or interfering with police investigation or even dangerous. But I can't get over the cop-out at the end. We end chapter 14 with the big reveal... and start chapter 15 with a Club meeting, telling Shannon what happened. They didn't even actively solve the mystery, just figured out what was happening and then watched it play out.
Also, why didn't Mr Rothman, a grown-ass adult, teacher at SMS, and key part of the original event, tell anyone about what was going on and who was doing it? "Will you still love me tomorrow?" The dummy? That's some pretty fucked up shit. Considering that someone died last time, you'd think he'd let someone other than his thirteen year old student know about the whole story. I can't.

Okay, so maybe a over-the-top, but this is a legit spooky story, and perfect to read on Halloween night!
