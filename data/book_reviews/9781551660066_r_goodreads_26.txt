Wonderful!Diana Saxton has to overcome her fears to find that Tennessee Blackthorn is more than just a powerful man with a gentle soul. Finding a way thru the cobwebs of the past to finally feel safe in a man's arms, Diana opens herself up to face life again. The most difficult part is that the very man who helped show her the way, seems to be lost in the past and unable to find himself, or the love they both so desperately need.
