Re Lover's Lies - Daphne Clair does her own version of keepin' it in the family on this one. She couples that favorite Anne Mather trope with a very nice side of heroine revenge.Unlike both Anne Mather and Sally Wentworth, (whose heroine driven revenge books set the bar for HPlandia,) DC actually keeps things pretty mellow. There is no eminent heroine hysterical breakdown and no one was running off to Africa so they wouldn't hurt anymore people.DC also brings back the golden age of HP travelogue in this one, as the H and h meet for the first time in 13 years while they are on a group tour of China.The h is a 25 yr old orphan, she currently owns a small import/export shop in Auckland and is looking forward to a new adventure.She is very unprepared to see the man she considers responsible for her stepsister's death years earlier to be part of the tour group. We learn that when the h was 13, her parents died in a boating accident and she went to stay with her stepsis and stepsis's much older husband.The stepsister was a bit of a drama queen and soon had the h involved in passing letters between the stepsis and her gardener. The h was a bit in love with the gardener guy herself, so it was a bit of vicarious romance for the h. The stepsister claimed that her older husband only saw her as a status trophy and neglected her, so the h was all about helping her stepsister find some happiness.When the gardener guy eventually broke things off, the stepsister drove her car off a bridge and killed herself. The h believes it was because the stepsister wanted to elope with the gardener guy and he dumped her hard, so the stepsis became despondent and ended it all.Now the h is faced with the gardener guy again and she isn't enthused to see that he will be in close confines with her in the tour group. She resolves to avoid and ignore him, but the now business owner millionaire former gardener guy is making that difficult.He doesn't recognize the h, nor does he remember her name, so to him the h is an attractive single lady on vacation and he isn't shy or reserved about making his interest known.Even tho there is another single lady on the tour who is very interested in chasing the H, he targets the h for his attentions - in spite of her very pointed evasions and smackdowns.As these things go in HPlandia, the h and H are forced together due to the H's stalkery insistence and the other tour members are all about pimping out a woman who has clearly made her unwillingness evident.The h does her best to take the high road, but eventually the H's stakerific moves and her own treacherous body syndrome response force her to concoct a spot of comeuppance.The h decides to go with the group flow of hooking her up with the H. Once the H is thoroughly ensorcelled in her wiles, she will dump him hard and let him know what it feels like to be used and discarded.Things go along swimmingly over the next four chapters, we get lovely China travelogue, a bit of food porn with fried insects and the h and H have several roofie kissing moments that lead to the big purple passion moment right at the end of the tour.The last night before everyone departs, the h makes a public farewell to the H, making it clear that he was good for night, but not for anything else. This H is not pleased by that and the h is feeling some pangs of remorse, but the memory of her heartbroken and despondent stepsister dying is enough to hold her to her course.The h returns to Auckland and her shop and has a little mopey moment. Then the H shows up to stalk her and impose himself on her life once more. He does this under the guise of submitting to the h's punishment after she explained her view of things between him and her stepsister.The h gradually begins to understand that maybe her 13 yr old view of things was not strictly how things really were. The H is very hesitant to burst the h's bubble, but when the h learns that her sister's husband was the one that had been financially supporting her during her teen years and provided the money for both her shop and her home out of the kindness of his heart, the h realizes that her stepsister was kinda messed up.The H was only 19 to her stepsister's 25 and to her, he was only a bit of fun. He was also a college student doing a summer job and the h's stepsister was very clear that the H wasn't on her long term agenda for anything other than a stud.The h figures out that the stepsister was also involved with another man and got pregnant in an effort to force him to leave his wife. When the man refused and dumped the sister, the stepsis realized that her sterile husband would probably dump her and she drove off the bridge in a fit of despondency that may or may not have been a suicide attempt.The really sad thing about it was that the stepsister's husband loved the woman anyway, would have accepted the baby if that was what the stepsister wanted and he would have stayed married to her too.The h finally comes to accept that her stepsister was not who she thought she was, and she also realizes that this H has been used and abused by the women in her family twice now, which puts her own moral integrity into a very bad light.The h decides she has to chase the H down to sorta apologize and confess her love, the H plays hard to get at first. But when he realizes the h is sincere, he relents and avows his own true love back. We leave the two happily reunited, planning the wedding and loving it up, for a nicely wrapped up HP HEA.This one was well done on the revenge part and well done on the travelogue. The latter half back in New Zealand kinda dragged a bit, but this H was very attentive to his HP H stalker duties.I would say he was a bit masochistic, but this h was about as mean as baby kitten learning to open it's eyes, so he wasn't really risking much hanging out with her and braving her wrath.The downside of this is that the h is having boudoir moments with a guy who slept with her stepsister, which is rather tacky. The other downside is that even riding the spurs of revenge, this h comes across as a nice person and there just isn't a ton of drama.Everybody is very polite and non-dramatic, even in the middle of being publicly dumped. Which kinda takes all the fun out of it.
