3.5 StarsMy first book by this author and I truly enjoyed it. I love her writing style. I fell into the book from the very start. Of course the naked Judo at the beginning was one reason.The h was young but intelligent and strong. She’s a brown belt in Judo and could throw a man around. She also attempts to see things from a logical POV and does her best not to let her emotions get the best of her. It’s hard when the H enjoys pushing her buttons, but she’s stronger than a lot of other HP h’s.The H is the typical alpha male with commitment hang-ups. He has recently returned to the village where he grew up and is reviled. Apparently there is a disturbing history of him in the village and he is not welcome there by some. This has left him bitter and angry. The h has taken it upon herself to help him overcome his bitterness, all while falling for him.I really enjoyed this author’s writing and am looking forward to reading more of her work.
