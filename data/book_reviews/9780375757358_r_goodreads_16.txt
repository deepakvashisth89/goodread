Written in 1911 this espionage and psychological novel stands up well. Russia is still under a tyrant and the people trapped in a propaganda militaristic regime. The main character Razumov becomes unintentionally involved with revolutionaries. There are idealists, spies, bureaucrats, fantasists and traitors.

Set in Russia and Geneva the story follows Razumov trying to make sense of the quandary he has got himself in too. Others have written excellent reviews which I cannot do justice. I will say I can empathize the frustration Razumov faces in trying to do the right thing and survive.
