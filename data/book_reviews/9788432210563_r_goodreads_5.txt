Like other reviewers I loved the idea of this novel as presented in its synopsis and I had long wanted to read Goytisolo but the reality of reading it was to say the least disappointing. It was enough to make me pause before attempting anything further by him - a pause that continues to today (September 27, 2025).

I will read Goytisolo again, I hope, one day. But my failure to enjoy these 170 pages still hold me back and, were not my failure echoed by so many other GR reviewers I might worry more about it.
