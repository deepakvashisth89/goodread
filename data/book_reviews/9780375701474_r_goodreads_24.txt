whew. should not have read this when i was so fragile. i thought it would be a little drier than it was. jamison weaves between laying down facts on mental illness and suicide and illustrating exactly what that means using actual anecdotes. this is not an easy book to read. yes, her writing is clear, informed, well researched, and even entertaining for science literature, but as someone who has been suicidal for as long as i can remember, i cannot recommend this as 'easy to read' without the appropriate caveats. it felt like i was reading about the hour and manner of my own death.that being said: if you are suicidal, you should read this book. its important to demystify suicide. and i dont want you to stop in the middle of it and decide to kill yourself, i mean you should read the whole thing through and think about what suicide means. it is a difficult, usually impulsive decision made by unquiet, unstable minds under massive and prolonged distress, and theres strong evidence that suicide itself is apart from mental illness. this book does not romantacize mental illness or suicide (for the record i despise that term) when it goes into detail of methods, accounts, studies, and anecdotes.i agree with jamison that portraying suicide as 'a way out' is romantacizing it. i believe that discussing suicide apart from its causes in mental illness and who is most vulnerable to suicide is romantacizing it. but discussing suicide, or talking about being suicidal, itself is NOT romantacizing it.so i want you to approach this book with caution. because there are some stories here that will rip your heart out. that young air force cadet haunts me. youll know if you read it. but apart from that, understanding your own condition is necessary. just make sure you know that this book does not shy on details and will be triggering to those prone to suicidal ideation.sorry if this is rambling, i read this a few weeks ago and im still thinking about it but i desperately want people to read it bc there are so many misconceptions about suicide out there that even i had before reading night falls fast. please think about reading it even if youre not suicidal. especially if youre not.
