Chapter 6, note 51, to the statement of Meyerhold's belief that in opera "Movements and gestures should therefore be rhythmic in character and carefully choreographed, presupposing a certain balletic elegance on the part of the performers.":
Meyerhold quotes approvingly from Der Tanz (Stuttgart, 1906) by the director of the Munich Künstler-Theater, Georg Fuchs: "The German' failure to recognize evn today the beauty of the human body is revealed no more clearly than here, where its absurd distortions are unbearable to watch: Siegfrieds with tight corseted "Bierhuas" bellies; Siegmunds with sausage-shaped legs crammed into tights; Valkyries who appear to spend their leisure in Munich beer halls over plates heaped with steaming offal and steins of foaming beer; Isoldes whose sole ambition is to play the part of fairground fat ladies and exercise their irresistible charms on the imagination of butchers' shop assistants" (Braun [trans. and ed.], , 93f.).
