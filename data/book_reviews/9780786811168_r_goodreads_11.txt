Yes, this is by the same Zindel who wrote The Pigman. No, it's not any good. If it were any other author I'd give it two stars, but with Zindel it deserves one.The son and a daughter of an archeologist discover a family of ravenous plesiosaurs in a lake. The archeologist's boss, a tabloid magnate, wants the beasts captured dead or alive. The children befriend one of the young plesiosaurs. The plesiosaurs eat people.There's not much drama to the book because the dinosaurs get revealed early. There's not much sense to the kids looking out for a baby one, mostly because they both witnessed mama literally dismembering a man. There's no commentary, and none of Zindel's skill in depicting young adults. It's a rather average YA novel written by someone who has done far, far better.
