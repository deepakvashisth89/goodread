The Lay of Leithianis just so fine. This ismasterfulverse: the diction, the descriptive imagery, the rhythm and couplets of the octosyllabic verse form, all perfectly employed to the various layers of the narrative, whether exquisite and enchanting, poignant and grief-filled, or horrific and dreadful. A delight to read.(I haven't (yet!) gotten toThe Lay of the Children of Húrin, but greatly look forward to it.)
