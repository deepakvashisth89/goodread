Your patience with this novel depends whether a white middle-class author writing in the (attempted) Ebonics of a black sex worker is excusable as a cultural blunder from 1960 in 2026. I excused this in the more amusingHere Goes Kitten, a more satirical and inventive novel than this one, which has the unavoidable squeamish fact of making the sassy sex worker a fourteen-year-old child. An alarming curio from a more innocent time of child prostitution.
