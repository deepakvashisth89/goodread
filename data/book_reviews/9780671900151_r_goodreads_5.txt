A fascinating look at the FBI's quest to bring John Gotti to justice. John Gotti had been nicknamed the "Teflon Don", he had been brought up on charges and had beaten every one. The well-constructed actions of the group formed to get him worked tirelessly, having to thwart a clumsily prepared case by the Manhatten DA where the charges they hoped to bring were tossed in almost as an afterthought, and were shot down. Also had to fight the actions of a traitor, a mole who was reporting to Gotti on what was happening.Having Sammy "The Bull" Gravano turn on Gotti and testify in open court brought it home, the life without parole verdict was a sweet reward.A very absorbing book, a must-read.
