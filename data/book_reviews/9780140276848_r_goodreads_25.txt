I respect the fact that the author has chosen to discuss her mother's Bi-Polar Disorder. My mother suffered from that mental illness as have I. The thing that has me stymied is the fact that the author is all over the place with her own life, her mother's, her sisters' and her grandmother's. It is very confusing to follow along with her diatribe. A few times, I nearly gave up, but felt it would be beneficial to finish the book in order to discover her mother' behavior patterns. She saves them until the end of the book. The author writes mostly about her experiences as a correspondent for National Public Radio. The book would have been so much easier to read if she had kept it simple.
