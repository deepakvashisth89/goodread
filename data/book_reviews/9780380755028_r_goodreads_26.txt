The duality between law and chaos and all forces from each comes to a head with our protagonist Merlin, who is a combination of both via his father Corwin and his mother Dara in this 10th and final volume of the epic Amber series. A head spinning travel here and there and back again highlighted by many spells from Merle and his antagonists. The main jist is who gets the throne as many are vying for it and Zelazny delivers a satisfying if not dizzying conclusion.
