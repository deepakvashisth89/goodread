Meet the Frenchiest Frenchman who ever Frenched in the whole of France. This dude is like Pepe le Pew on crack. Ahh ma petite, ma mignonne, ma cherieee. This man is positively bursting at the seams with inuendo, double entendre, lazy eyed looks and smooth mocking lines. He prowled, he growled, he purred and slunk about in his 'Euro chic' collarless black shirts. He was, in short, fricking hilarious.The h was rather dull, she just sort of fell madly for his shit lines from the get-go and spent the whole book melting at his feet and having those oh-so famous 'boneless' knees that romance writers are mad for. Her wardrobe was a screaming case of the 90s, all those peach coloured silk outfits. Peach or salmon... and everything was silk! Tiny peach coloured silk skirts, chocolate coloured silk camisole, and a peach silk jacket... good lord I was trying to picture this woman's outfits in my mind's eye and she was just this slithery mono-flesh coloured twiglet.Worth it for the OTT French stereotypes and terrible 90s clothes. The plot was lame.
