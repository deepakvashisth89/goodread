THE ROTTWEILER (Mystery-London-Cont) – OkayRendell, Ruth - StandaloneArrow Books, 2004 – PaperbackAntique storeowner Inez Ferry, is a young widow who takes in tenants. However, one of them is not as they seem. A serial killer is on the loose.*** There were too many characters, and none of them really stood out. Once I knew the killer, it was hard for me to care about the rest of the book.. It was well plotted, although the ending was anti-climatic. It was okay, but I just wasn't that impressed.
