Letty and Xavier are weeks away from reciting their marriage vows when Letty discovers a document reporting the results of a detailed investigation onher. Suddenly, the ever-so-chaste and proper courtship makes sense. She comes to the realization that her soon-to-be-husbanddoesn't love her at all, only the idea of her as a perfect wife. To put the cherry on top, reading the investigative report makes her realize howboringof a person she is so she decides some radical changes are in order. The first step being, to end the engagement.This was a solid harlequin-type romance. It was quite amusing to read Letty getting so huffed about everything and Xavier trying to smooth her ruffled feathers. Some would say, Xavier was a little manipulative but the delivery was so funny that it could be forgiven. I would have totally given this a 4-star were it not for the ending. I thought there would be a little more growth on Xavier's part but I suppose it works with the time period of male chauvinism.
