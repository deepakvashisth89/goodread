This was the last book in this series. We actually see some not so perfect marriages in this one. Dr. Texas and her husband Glen are actually having some difficult times in this one, and we meet Nicole who has her sights on Glen and tearing his marriage apart. Dr. Texas has to go to California when her dad gets ill and Nicole tries to move in on Glen. Of course the whole town is talking about it, and Glen decides not to tell Dr. Texas instead of just telling her from the beginning. Luckily (of course) they bother realize how stubborn they are being and actually work it out just before the book ends. Overall this was a cute, quick series to read. I would recommend if you are a fan of Debbie Macomber.
