I'll be in my bedroom, making no noise and pretending I'm not there.

Ironically, that is what I did as I read this book cover-to-cover.

As much as I love the start of it all, the second book really holds. Not only as a sequel but also through time.

From third grade until now, I've always looked forward to the second book (especially considering it's still part of the "Happy Arc" as I like to call it (Books 1-4)).


Read by Jim Dale - and he absolutely nailed it. Love the way this man reads.
