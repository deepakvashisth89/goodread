Well, Ms. Leith had an advantage here. After reading Gravity’s Rainbow, I was inclined to like anything that was light and didn't take me four months to plow through. Bonus – I love her imagery; it’s just gorgeous. The story is well-told, well-paced, and full of beautiful images. I love the chapter titles. They’re so much fun. Plus, while I've been meaning to read China Mieville for a while, Leith really kicked that desire into gear, as shall be shown in the near future.All in all, a great reading experience and I’m looking forward to the next installments.
