When I was in high school Dirk Pitt was as big as James Bond to me. I read a lot of his early work and the stories flew by. Great adventures and fast paced with some interesting facts strewn in. I later felt I outgrew Dirk Pitt and stopped reading after Sahara which I enjoyed ( was made into a motion picture, rumor has it Clive Cussler refused to make another movie from his books because his input wasn't taken from the studio). Decent movie and an action packed book but it was the last Cussler novel for me. I had moved on to other authors and began reading a lot of science fiction. 30 years later and I found a stack of Cussler books at a yard sale for $1 each. I couldn't resist and picked up all 15. Pacific Vortex, sounded familiar but I honestly didn't remember the story. About halfway through things came back to me and the nostalgia alone was worth the read. People complain about the treatment of women in his books but I don't agree with that. I find Stuart Woods guilty of that and after a few of his novels I refuse to read him anymore. When I researched Cusslers newer books I saw he had a few new series and I picked one up. I like his writing style, his books are not overwritten and perfect for a 3 hour flight or a poolside read. I will pace them out as like most writers there will be repetition in his characters and plots. I will end this in I highly recommended this author for anyone looking for a good summertime read that is not overly written with lots of adventure. No heavy thinking and you can skim some parts and still enjoy the books. Respectfully, Thomas.
