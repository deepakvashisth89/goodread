This was my first Bentley Little book, so I didn't know what to expect. I liked his writing style, with it being straight forward for the most part. There were a few Chinese words for the vampire thrown in the mix, but nothing too complicated and forced. The story centers around a small town cop, his brother the newspaper editor, a corrupt minister, a Chinese family and the townsfolk. There have been some killings going on in town of both animals and people. They are coming up dry and dehydrated of all their blood.Well, Sue, her grandmother and her family run a Chinese restaurant. Robert, his brother Rich and Sue and her family go on the hunt for the vampire. The vampire is working with the corrupt minister in building a church for the vampire's big sacrificing of people. The townsfolk are on the menu. These people will fight to save their town from the Chinese vampire. Holy water and tradition methods have no effect on these vampire's, so they will have to fight them in a few different ways. This is where Sue's grandmother comes into play. She had the Chinese wisdom and know how to defeat the demon vampire. It won't be easy, for he is old and very strong. The story kept me reading and is kinda like a Salems lot tale. It is definitely worth a read.
