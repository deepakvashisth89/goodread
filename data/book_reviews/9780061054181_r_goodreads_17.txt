Honestly, I have a very limited knowledge of Magic the Gathering. I know the Ninja Turtles cards coming out are expensive and I know someone once described it to me as "D & D with cards", but that's about it 😂

I got through it OK, having read a fair amount of fantasy. It was a very constant book though, a long string of high stakes and battles and injuries. None of that was boring, but having the endless stream of it all made the high stakes just feel neutral. There weren't so many calm bits to elevate the action-packed bits when they come along. Like riding a rollercoaster straight up and then just staying there.

Interesting, fast, very very VERY busy story. Don't know if further knowledge of MTG would change anything, but (for me anyway) it's one of those books I'm quite happy having read once, probably won't read it again
