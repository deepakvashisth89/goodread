The Abominable Manis probably my favorite book in the Martin Beck series so far. To say it is chock full of police corruption, suspense and nonstop twists is putting it mildly. The cliffhanger ending left me in complete shock.Series narrator Tom Weiner could not be a more perfect choice for brining Sjöwall and Wahlöö's writing to life for listeners.Up next:The Locked Room(Martin Beck, #8)
