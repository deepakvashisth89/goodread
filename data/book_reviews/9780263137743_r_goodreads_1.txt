Anastasia SteeleEmily Gainsborough is the fresh-off-college, plucky virgin who has never, ever felt any sensual stirrings until she meetsChristian GreyChristian Malreaux, the mysterious, broody, millionaire with the Terrible, Secret Past that has scarred himemotionallyquite literally on the side of his face.Okay, okay, there was no red room in this magnificentpenthousechateau, but the fresh virgin did turn into a sex siren within minutes of the hero's tutelage, and there were plenty of Great, Big, Terrible Misunderstandings, and OW/OM interference, to drive the lovers temporarily apart.I actually kind of dug the author's writing style, for example her setting of the kind of sinister French Chateau that could have served as the perfect backdrop to a gothic tale.Plus, it's not everyday that a romance book starts with a naked Judo match so that was a hoot too.I just don't really go in for insta love, or for Great, Big, Terrible Misunderstandings that could have been resolved eons ago with two lines of dialogue had it not been for the hero's inexplicable desire to self-flagellate.I will be trying her other books though.
