Jane Urquhart is fabulous. I am so proud that she is Canadian. She is one of the great literary writers of her time.

The Underpainter is not a quick flip through read but rather a strong literary marvel. Something you can really sink your teeth into with a cup of tea on a cold winter day.

What I love about this novel are the varied relationships the underpainter - Austin Fraser has with each person in his life. How these relationships shape his worldview is in my opinion the great tragedy that befalls him in the end. What makes this book fantastic is how Urquhart explores the impact we have on one another; for better or for worse.
