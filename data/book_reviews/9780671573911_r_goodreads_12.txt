Star Trek: First Contactis a blend of science fiction, time-travel adventure, and personal drama, especially for Captain Jean-Luc Picard. It pits the crew of the Enterprise against the Borg at a moment of humanity’s first warp flight and first contact with the Vulcans. The premise is classic Star Trek; they need to protect the timeline and save the future, but there are some dark sections in the book. The Borg are presented, not just as a faceless enemy, but as a traumatic reminder of Picard’s own past assimilation.I watch Star Trek occasionally and The Next Generation has always been my favorite iteration. Picard is also my favorite character and in "First Contact" his arc is the book’s strongest element. He really struggles between duty, vengeance, and self-control. There's plenty of tension aboard the Enterprise as the Borg infiltrate the ship and lots of action as well."First Contact" is a standout Star Trek story and captures the spirit of the franchise at its best.
