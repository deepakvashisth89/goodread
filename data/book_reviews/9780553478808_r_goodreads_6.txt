As stated during status updates, I chose to read others Evans wrote having enjoyedThe Horse Whispererimmensely. A story of loss, redemption and love, its rare a debut novel gets adapted and rarer still when Robert Redford directs and stars in it.Here we're taken to a small town in MT and meet the local color when a wolf kills a favorite dog. Protected by federal law, Helen a young biologist is sent to investigate. The title references a wolf trap used in the old days, but the story is more romance then anything. The alpha male that was responsible is hunted down and killed. Meanwhile Helen and Luke, the teen son of a rancher travel the area setting traps so the rest of the pack will be collared and tracked. As with any plot, the antagonist character is an old timer who could care less about endangered species and does his best to kill the remaining wolves.While its mildly interesting, titling a book this way would have the reader think the focus was the endangered wolves rather than soap between Helen and Luke, her teenage helper..While I don't have a problem with dabs of romance, this wasn't at all what I expected. Enough said.
