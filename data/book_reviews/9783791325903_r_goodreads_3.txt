I love how he translates his subject matter into art: his meticulous techniques, use of color, composition, and every minute detail. Vermeer's paintings have an effect on the viewer's soul. In this brief book, Netta describes in great detail this work--View of Delft, c. 1660-61, oil on canvas, The Hague. It is stunning (I would love to see it in person someday.) Vermeer use of color dots, partly wet on wet, and using the color blue in places where you don't recognize it. He mixed sand into his oils to give a coarse surface to represent bricks, etc. His compositions are peaceful, they are a quiet place where we are can withdraw and comfortably view the world.
