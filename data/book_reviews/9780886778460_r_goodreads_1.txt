Teek was published by DAW in August of 1999, and I notice that it doesn't say "SF" on the spine. It's more of a suspense/thriller/coming-of-age book, though it is about psi-powers. The author is listed as Steven Krane, which was a pseudonym used by Steven Swiniarski, who was always better known as his other pseudonym, S. Andrew Swann. It's a little similar to King's Firestarter, in that a secret group of government-sponsored agents (known as the Agency!), are after the protagonist, Allison Boyle, a seventeen-year-old girl who suffers severe headaches. A boy named Chuck Wilson seems to be stalking her, her mother is no help at all, things go really haywire when Chuck is too near... and the Agency has been at work for years, preparing for...? It's a fun story, probably YA-appropriate, and the ending is all right but a little abrupt.
