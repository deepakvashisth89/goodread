By the way this is tied with Project Hail Mary as my favorite book of all time. I lent it to my ex and after a month when he didn’t give it back after we broke up I ordered a replacement copy because this is just one of those books I can’t live without. Hayduke Lives! is far more akin to a dear, dear friend than merely a book.Thank you Edward Abbey for writing this book for me to experience, and to hold dear for the rest of my life.
