This one is more historical fiction than romance, since Mary is happily married and stays that way, but like its predecessor it's basically a less stressful version of Outlander. All those parts where Claire had to trade sex or got raped or someone else got raped or whatever are replaced with a Bad Guy leering at Mary's breasts while she worries that if she tells her powerful duke uncle about it he will be super, super angry on her behalf and there might be unspecified bad consequences.
