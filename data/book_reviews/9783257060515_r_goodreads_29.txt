My second consecutive Brunetti mystery - I once again enjoyed it but this time the ending left my unsatisfied. These mysteries are a slow burn that I really enjoy as a change of pace from the usual mystery genre where too much action makes many stories unrealistic. However the end of this story leaves too may loose ends and it almost felt like another chapter or two would have brought a better conclusion. With that said, I also understand the choice by the author to leave it as it was and in some ways it might be more realistic to what would happen in real life. Either way, I will continue the series for sure.
