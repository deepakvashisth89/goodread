August 21, 2019.With my deep love for all things environmental and conservational, I figured I had to make book 100 a book about this. This book had some great portions and a wide array of wonderful quotes. Though, it gets pretty dry and meaty in the middle part of the book, I appreciated Helen's ideas of how to combat some of the major issues affecting the earth. As always, the number one thing is that YOU need to decide that a change needs to be made. GO SAVE THE EARTH!
