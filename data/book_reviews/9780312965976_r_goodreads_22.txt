This should have been an enjoyable book. It has a beautiful sea side setting, an old lighthouse, a dishy hero. Unfortunately for the first 3rd of the book almost nothing happens. I was so bored I almost gave up. It’s not helped either by the fact that the heroine is a wishy washy, week willed ditherer with no backbone. Oh and her friends are caricatures.
So nope, not for me this one.
