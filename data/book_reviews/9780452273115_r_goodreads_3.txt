This was Paul Russell's third novel but it is the final one of his, seven, novels that I have read and loved and I am hard put to think of another American author of his generation that I have as high an opinion of and of whose works I believe should, and will be read, a hundred years from now (see my footnote *1 below for amplification).

I have sid many times but will repeat myself and say that the books I really love are almost impossible to review because I can not bring any real perspective to them and 'Sea of Tranquility' is a perfect example. It is not flawless - unlike all of Russell's other novels it has a broader scope in terms of years covered and intention; it is sort-of, Russell's AIDS/state of the nation novel and while not a failure it is clear that his concentration on smaller groups of characters in more limited settings was his metier and one he handled brilliantly. In 'The Sea of Tranquility' the finest parts are those in which the focus is concentrated. When events scatter his characters and he is forced to bring them back together for the novel's resolution it necessitates, for me, a huge suspension of disbelief. Maybe it is my more European outlook and education but I always find tales anchored by the ongoing love and obsession with the boy you loved and lost at 16, 17 or 18 unconvincing. Like everyone I loved and lost many boys in my teens and twenties but by the time I was approaching 30 I would have been horrified if one turned up on my doorstep claiming sanctuary based on our ancient history.

Also at one point in this novel, like in 'The Coming Storm', there is an impassioned rant about pollution, the way the environment is being destroyed by reckless over population, etc. - I take no exception to the sentiments but they stand out as propaganda rather then as an integral part of any of the characters beliefs.

Despite what I have said I adore this novel - there are great and moving scenes that will stay with me and compared to so many authors more favoured with lavish praise Russell is the genuine article.





*1 The only other author that springs to mind is Dennis Cooper who is unique and incomparable but Paul Russell is an author I do not feel insults Cooper by being bracketed with him.
