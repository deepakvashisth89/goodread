A loner cop meets a wary businesswoman in "Simply Sinful"- a Classic Carly Phillips title, which she updated a little before its re-release to include things like cell phones and the internet. Kane McDermott is a detective determined to live life alone, convinced that not caring for others is the only way to keep his edge and do his job. Kayla Luck has inherited a former charm school and turned it into a business teaching business etiquette. It isn't what she wants to do with her life, but she isn't ready to give up her aunt's business so soon after her aunt and uncle died in a car accident. For her, family is everything and men aren't worth the time. But there's chemistry with Kane from the beginning and she's willing to take a chance with him. Too bad he's investigating her and her business for prostitution.

The attraction between Kane and Kayla is there right from the start and never wavers. Both of them feel like they can be themselves, a comfort that usually takes ages instead of minutes. Kayla is smart enough to see this for something special. Kane is ready to run from it. The baggage both have from their pasts have created enough issues to keep a therapist happy for years. I was happy Kayla started to grow and realize that who she was at the beginning of the book and who she was towards the end were two different people and not only did she not have to go back to her old ways, she didn't want to. Poor Kane though, he was sure he could keep things the same and make it work. Thank goodness he had friends to shake him up and get him to see that emotions and caring didn't have to mean disaster! His growth was painful and sad to watch, which meant to me that Carly Phillips hit it perfectly.

Overall, "Simply Sinful" is a lovely book about emotional and personal growth, and the importance of grabbing onto that special someone when we have the chance. A fun and fast read, and a definite classic.
