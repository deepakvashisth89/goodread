This is one of the earliest of Christina Dodd books. She has done a good job on both plot and character development.

Margaret is a butler for a very wealthy older man in Houston Texas. She has been trained to be very proper in her relationship with her employer. But with elderly Mr Donovan, she also has become a friend.

Reid is the grandson of Margaret's employer. He has heard rumblings that his grandfather has had advantage taken. He arrives in order to protect his beloved grandfather and to make the person pay for taking advantage.

This is a romance. There is definite attraction developing between Margaret and Reid. In fact, for me it was almost too instant. Both of them have surprises in their character and their lives.

But, there is also a mystery. Wealthy and elderly people have been threatened with kidnapping in order to extort great amounts of money. Now Mr Donovan has been the most recent victim who is being threatened. Security is heightened on the estate, but still there is the ongoing danger.

Christina Dodd is a very talented writer and this early book shows her talent and creativity. The plot is unusual and interesting as the reader moves along.

Both Margaret and Reid are interesting main characters and the secondary characters are well rounded and all are assets to the story.

This is a quick and easy read - and well worth the short time it takes to read the book. I think you will like this book as much as I did.
