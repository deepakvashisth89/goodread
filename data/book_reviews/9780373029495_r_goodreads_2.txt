Dinah had to spent a year doing civil charitable work according to her great-grandfather's will. She did just that and was about to leave the school she spent a year in in Kakaxia, but because she had doubts about her marriage to Dennis - her childhood fiance - and she wanted to ensure that her favourite student has a fair bargain with a recording company in Nashville, she extends the year by accompanying her student to meet Mitchel Carey, the recording company's owner. She didn't bargain to fall in love with the arrogant man!I loved the story even though it is a vintage. It was obvious both the main characters where bewitched by one another, but they were stubborn and too proud to show their vulnerabilities. A recommended past-time read.
