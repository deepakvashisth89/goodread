I’ve been reading all the Trek movie novelizations in order. I liked this one and enjoyed the book more than the movie. There’s lots of cool detail and the story reads well. I think the best Trek novelization is Wrath of Khan, where the movie is a jumping off point for the book. This one stays close to the screenplay.I think my main issue with this one is Picard. I like it best when he’s a diplomat and reasons his way out of problems. In this one he’s more of an action hero, he definitely has an emotional journey. But the last action sequence seems to ignore Picard’s internal development.My favorite part of the book was learning more about Lilly.
