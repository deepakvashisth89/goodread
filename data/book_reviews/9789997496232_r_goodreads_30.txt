The last of the four spy with no name novels and this one was just as frustrating as the other three. Stilted and often cryptic dialogue and seems more like a travel guide than a thriller. The plot ( or plots) reads like a string of non sequiturs and I found myself often totally confused about what was happening and why whatever it was, had happened. There is a plot about stolen virus ridden chickens eggs and another about a lunatic ex military billionaire who appears to want to destabilise the Soviet Union and communism (in whatever shape or form) with a super computer (the brain). The only saving grace is the Signe - the Finnish assassin - who really does deserve a novel of her own - but not written by Deighton.
