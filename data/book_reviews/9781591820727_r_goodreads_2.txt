This is really heavy—I'm actually prepared for this!This was very challenging. And you thought you'll only feel rainbows and ponies and butterflies in your bellies whenever you read shojo manga, right? Well, treat this one differently. This series is pretty heavy, dark, and thought-provoking. I've been typing this review and constantly taking it down. I can't seem to put my mind to it. I need some alone time. And the cliffhanger about Kira's dark past didn't help to ease this horrible feeling in my chest. That poor girl was ravaged, no wonder she detested men at the start of the story. And she never let Rei touch her intimately.
