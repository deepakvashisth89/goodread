The eighth book in the Swedish detective series, starts with what appears to be the unrelated random vicious slaying of their cab driver, by the two female passengers, and then the apparent heart attack of an IT consultant at a cash machine… which leads to Wallander believing that maybe he has struck on to some sort of global conspiracy. Another great Wallander book, where once again the reader feels like a detective as the detailed procedural process is portrayed so well by Mankell, whilst maintaining the personal theme of Wallander's slide into late middle-age and for him, loneliness. 7 out of 12.2011 read
