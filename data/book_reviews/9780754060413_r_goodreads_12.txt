Benny and his pet pig might be the most annoying creations of Blyton's whole career. I know the pig saved the day but it doesn't make up for the pair of them being such massive sops.It's nice to see the Five interacting with actual military - this probably has the most tech in it of all the Five books. This makes the decision to focus so much of the book on the slow meandering of butterfly collectors even more bizarre! 3 stars.
