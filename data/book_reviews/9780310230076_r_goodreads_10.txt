I'm not sure what I was expecting when I started this book - perhaps a ten step how-to book. What I read was, in a way, much better. Rather then tell you how to have more faith, Cymbala shows you through the stories of real people. He also gives practical advise on how to apply some of the lessons from Scripture. For example, Cymbala points out that many people today focus on the commands of God rather than his promises. It's the promises of God and the work he does in keeping those promises that builds our faith.Fresh Faith, for me, was not a page turner, but it contain many golden nuggets of truth that will help me on my journey for greater faith.
