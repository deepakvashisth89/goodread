7/20/2018 - 6/10The second Spellsinger book didn't hold up really well compared to my long ago teenage remembrances. It's fun enough to keep me reading though. It's a nice simple adventure fantasy story. The book starts off with a lot of travel adventures, then at the end there are a lot of predictable plot twists with the battle. The relationships are pretty shallow and immature with some odd endings (Talea, Pog, and Flores). I seem to remember a lot more detail with the songs and spellsinging too.
