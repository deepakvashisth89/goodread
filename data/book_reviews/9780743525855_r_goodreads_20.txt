Recalling the most horrifying incident in his life, Gary is still frightened and does not want to encounter with The Man in The Black Suit.When he was nine years old, his brother died due to a bee sting. Though he did not had allergy like his brother but the unpleasant incident made him stay terrified when seeing a bee. One day while he was fishing he falls asleep. When he woke up he noticed a bee sitting on his nose. He suddenly heard a clap and the bee flew. Looking around, he found a Man in the Black Suit with burning eyes, stinky smell and a pale skin. He told him horrible stories. He could not believe but still he was scared of him and what he said. Throwing the fish toward the man, he makes his way of escape and finally reaches home to find out his mother is alive and father is at home; opposite of what the man had said. But the event is so dark that shadows his whole.The mystery of evil.
