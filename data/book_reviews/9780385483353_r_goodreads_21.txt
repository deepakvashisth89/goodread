Ok, so I was really looking forward to reading about Julia but this has got to be one of the dullest books I have ever read. The subject, Julia, is a fascinating woman but the authors writing style is terrible. I borrowed it from the library, had to force myself to keep reading because the due date was coming up, renewed it once, tried to renew it again but couldn't because someone else had a request in for it. All that and I had only just gotten to where Julia married Paul and was becoming interested in learning how to cook. I've always had issues with leaving a book unread once I'd started reading it but this is one I am not going to finish. I'll look for another book about Julia's life instead.So my final word on this one - ZZZZzzzzzz
