What is Christmas without Norman Rockwell? I feel like I grew up with Norman Rockwell’s illustrations. I could never get enough of them. And when I think of him now, I think of the last illustration that I ever saw, and that was Thanksgiving dinner. I really like seeing the turkey on the table with all the trimmings, and then a family sitting down ready to eat. This year, I thought that it would be nice to read the story of his life. I don’t remember the author or the title of the book, but when I find it, I will see about adding it to my read list. I might say that this year Christmas and even Thanksgiving will not be as it was in past years, not with our political upheaval . it’s a good time to pick up Christmas stories, to read about Norman Rockwell, and even to look at his illustrations.
