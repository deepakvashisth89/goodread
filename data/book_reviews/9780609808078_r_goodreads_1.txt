This book is a mix of photography and very short interviews/summaries with/of inspirational women from very diverse enviroments. The portraits are ecletic and really show personality, if Im honest the page or so of writing which accompanies these pictures aren't enough to really tell you that much about the women pictured.But by far the best thing about this book is the 'laws' these women say are how they liv there lives, there sometimes funny, often touching and always inspirational.
