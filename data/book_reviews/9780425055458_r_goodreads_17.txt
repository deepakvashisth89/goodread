Old-school detective fiction at its absolute best. My only wish is that Lawrence Sanders had published a companion cookbook featuring all of Edward X. Delaney’s legendary sandwich creations—they sound delicious!This installment offers a refreshing shift from The First Deadly Sin. Instead of knowing the killer's identity from the start, we are taken on a gripping journey through Delaney’s methodical thought process. The atmospheric, intricate twists and turns made this an incredibly engaging and stimulating read.
