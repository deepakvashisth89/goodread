This book, along with his other two (Fresh Wind, Fresh Fire; Fresh Power), that I'm aware of, was another I did not want to put down. The author, Pastor Jim Cymbala, whose famous Brooklyn Tabernacle Choir has influenced a lot of American Christian culture, drew me in with lots of gripping stories of how the Gospel is powerful enough to change people from what seem to be hopeless situations--through faith and prayer.This book is a call to faith and prayer. God has inspired this church to pray 24/7. This is where their power comes from--being connected to the Vine!Hebrews 11:6 says, "Without faith, it is impossible to please God." What a NEEDED reminder!
