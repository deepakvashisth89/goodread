A good read, good characters (Shan is quite Dumbledore like ^-^) in a science-fi fantasy-ish world.But I was sadly kept back in my reading by the weird choice of narrative: we have access to ALL the characters' points of view, frequently in the same sentence, madly turning, leaping from character to another character, even the lesser ones. As a result we sometimes have to make an effort to understand who - for heaven sake ! - is thinking. And, much worse, nothing, absolutely nothing is left to our own appreciation, we have nothing at all to guess, all is meticulously explained before we have a chance to think about it.A shame for readers who love their show don't tell...
