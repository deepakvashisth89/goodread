When Tommy and Tuppence were discussing money, and their lack of it, they decided on a daring scheme in which to make money quickly. Young Adventurers Ltd immediately put a notice in the London Times advertising their willingness to embark on anything – which probably wasn’t too sensible.Their contact sent them to search for an elusive young woman who had been connected to the sinking of theRMS Lusitaniaby a German torpedo during the war – but the danger they would face made young Tommy and Tuppence realise it wasn’t all a game.The Secret Adversaryby Agatha Christie is the first in the Tommy & Tuppence series and I quite enjoyed it. Fun and entertaining with plenty of unforeseeable twists, I have no hesitation in recommending it to fans of both Agatha Christie and the genre.
