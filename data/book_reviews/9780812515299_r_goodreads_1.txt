Overall, the book is just okay. It will keep your attention and it's easy to read, but something about the author's style is off. It feels sort of "half-assed" for lack of a better word. At parts in the story where something was clearly supposed to be of great importance or great revelation to the characters, I found myself confused with understanding only coming 20 pages later. Several times I was left scratching my chin at some line of dialogue that was framed as clever.Also, very anticlimactic. The end especially, but even just throughout. The first quarter gets you so interested and then it's just blah. Happens several times within the plot- you think it's finally getting into an actual A-B-C story and it's just more let down.Very different from the movie. Movie was mom with vengenace on her mind, Book was more Mom becomes voice of reason for victims who used to want revenge.
