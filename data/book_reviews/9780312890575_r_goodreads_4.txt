I also really like short series, and stand alone novels. But nothing quite hits the same, as the endings of 10 book series. Granted that is only true if the ending is good, but that is not a concern here.This is one of the best final books I have ever read. And it is easily my favorite novel of The Black Company. This book is also the longest Black Company book, and it makes use of that page time. It not only manages to complete the stories of all the characters we already know, it introduces a couple brand new characters, and they became some of my favorites in the series. It has everything you could want in a final book. Thematically rich, it has engaging, high stakes battles, an extended, and amazingly written falling action, some brilliant reveals, absolutely incredible tension, and the best tagline in the entire genre.Soldiers live. And wonder why.9.4/10I unfortunately can't talk much more in terms of specifics, because it is book 10. But I loved this.
