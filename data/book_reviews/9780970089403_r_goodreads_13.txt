5/5 Stars for a well composed introduction to readings from the ZoharGet in touch with the soul of kabbalistic thinking with this inspiring and educating selection, edited by Gershom Scholem. Or visitArcane Afternoonfor a closer look at its contents."This small volume will have fulfilled its task if it succeeds in conveying to the reader some notion of the power of contemplative fantasy and creative imagery hidden within the seemingly abstruse thinking of the kabbalists."And indeed, Scholem allows Moses de Leon, the actual author of the Zohar, to speak for himself. The lectures in middrashic character touch on all books of the Torah, though mostly contemplating Genesis and Exodus.We get in touch with the "male and female" man, with the journey of the soul which descended to earth one way, but ascends from it on different ways. We learn of the Tree of Life, of the Sefiroth, the meaning of the Sabbath day and of the court of the King's daughter, his Jewel.Even when you are unfamiliar with biblical texts and kabbalistic symbolism, this is a wonderful text for the spiritually curious, or anyone seeking some beautiful inspiration for art and thought.
