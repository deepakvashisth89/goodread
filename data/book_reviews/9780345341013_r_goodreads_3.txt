The Sword of Bheleu slows down a bit from previous books, and focuses on different characters for a while, which isn't actually a good thing. Still, it has you adrift in a sea of moral ambiguity (which may or may not be to your taste, of course), rolling your eyes at fantasy tropes subverted (the mystical vault full of powerful objects, for example) and there's some epic magic slinging going on, too.What I doreallylike is the feeling that no detail is incidental. I might be wrong about a couple of things, but I really hope they play a part in the final book, The Book of Silence.I love that contains so many typical fantasy tropes, but feels different.
