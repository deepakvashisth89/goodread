Reads like a college term paper. I agree with the other reviews. It needed an introductory chapter to explain what variation of latitude is, instead of the fictional story it begins with that only serves to muddy the waters and confuse the reader. This explanation really only comes at the end of the book when it is too late.
The authors also focus like a laser on the main characters (Scientist) involved in the story, without giving adequate focus on the events and
background of the time to give a flavor of what it was like to live then and peak the reader's interest. The result is a very boring book.
As I said before, reads like a college term paper.
