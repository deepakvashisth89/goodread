Chinese Cinderella And The Secret Dragon Society by Adeline Yen Mah is a very interesting book. It tells the story of CC and how she must deal with an abusive step-mother while aiding a society. The society's purpose is to aid the Allied Powers (USA, France, UK, etc.) and to liberate China from the Japanese during WWII. I really enjoyed this book. The author crafted the characters in a way that let me connect with them and relate with them. The author also changed the genre of the book from historical fiction to historical thriller in the span of a couple of pages. That is a work of art. I really enjoyed this book and I think that everybody should read this, even if they don't like Historical Fiction.
