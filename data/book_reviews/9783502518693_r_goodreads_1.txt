The perfect historical book that didn't bore me for even a second and that I completely devoured page by page,chapter by chapter.The disadvantages of historical books are that they usually are big but slow and that they can info-dump and don't have really lively characters,plot or dialogue,but this book had all this and included witty remarks and speech.I felt like everything was happening right in front of my eyes and I was stunned by the beauty of the whole story and the writing.On another note,I was really saddened and disgusted by the way people treated a passionate genius who gave his soul to Egypt like Carter.I hope that Carter at least died happy and I'm glad that he fulfilled his dream.5/5 stars!!
