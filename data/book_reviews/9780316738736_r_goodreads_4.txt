Whenever I introduce this book to a baby or toddler, it becomes an instant favorite. Actually, all they have to do is see the cover to decide it's a favorite. The pictures may look like they could have been drawn by a kid in elementary school, but they work perfectly with the story, and the bright colors really grab the attention of youngsters. I have literally seen babies and toddlers reach for this book over Caldecott Medal winners and other books with illustrations that adults would consider much better. This simple, bold artwork is more beautiful to youngsters than Caldecott Medal artwork. The story is one that they easily identify with and want to hear over and over again. I've read this book out loud so many times that I know it by heart. If there were an award given by a committee of judges aged newborn to three years, this book would definitely win.
