This is an exceptional Hercule Poirot mystery, probably one of my favorites so far. And, yes, I realize that I say that about half of the Poirot novels I read. But the best part? It has a dog named Bob. And also Captain Hastings — it's the last Hastings book untilCurtain, I think. But mostly I was excited because of Bob. He's a good boy, and I love that Hastings narrates his thoughts.Dog + Hastings + dog thoughts + Hugh Fraser as audiobook narrator = 5 stars. ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️
