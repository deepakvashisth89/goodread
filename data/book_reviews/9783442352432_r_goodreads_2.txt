Set 32 years before the original trilogy, the story follows Jedi Master Qui Gon Jinn and his apprentice, Obi Wan Kenobi, as they are sent to resolve a blockade of the planet Naboo by the Trade Federation. Along the way, they rescue the young Queen Amidala, cross paths with a uniquely gifted slave boy named Anakin Skywalker on Tatooine and face the sudden, terrifying resurgence of an ancient enemy- the Sith.
