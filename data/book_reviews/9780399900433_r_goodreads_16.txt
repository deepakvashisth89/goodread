3.5Two cold war spies and bitter enemies, both the best at what they do, Brandon Scofield from the United States and Vasili Taleniekov from the KGB, must set aside their differences and work together to stop a group of killers, known only as The Materese, from taking over the world.Now, what you'll get: an intricate cat-and-mouse game between Talienkov and Scofieldlots, lots of globe hopping as the story takes you to Scandinavia, Russia, France, Italy, the US, and Corsica, a love story between Scofield and Antonia, an inevitably gorgeous Bond-girl, and then lots and lots of action and espionage type-stuff and dead bodies.Why not more stars? Because it wasn't really a wow for me. I enjoyed it quite a lot but it didn't make me want to read another Ludlum novel.
