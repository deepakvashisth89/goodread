2.5 to 3 Stars

I sacrificed sleep to read the first half of this book, but I had a hard time finishing the second half because I felt the story had gone off track and the characters had lost their way. Also
I realized in the second half that this book does more telling than showing, which was a huge bummer. I thought "Lucifer" was going be a dark and dangerous character, but it seemed like they just talked about how dangerous he was - he never really got around to doing anything impressive. I was also disappointed by how many times the heroine tricked him considering how she'd only been an actress for a few weeks . Plus, I can't remember much of the book even though it's only been a week. I think that's pretty telling by itself.
