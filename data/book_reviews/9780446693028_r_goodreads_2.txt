There has never been nor will there ever be as witty a writer as Donald Westlake. In each of the 12 short stories which make up “Thieves’ Dozen”, his scheme is laid out in a few crisp sentences, such as stealing a champion race horse, Even the asides on names chosen (Abby’s Elbow), and the delectable explanation for breeding the mincing stallion (Dire Straits)
bounce merrily along as the pair make their way through the manure strewn pasture. Each story in this collection shows the inimitable hand of the wittiest of American authors. “Too many Crooks” cannot be topped to show any aspiring writer perfection in crisp dialogue, plot line, scene setting and story telling. Wouldn’t it have been wonderful to sit at the next booth in Armweary’s?
