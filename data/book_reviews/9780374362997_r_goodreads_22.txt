Reading this book was like being swallowed by a pouch of gemstones. Rubies, emeralds, sapphires, opals, and so many diamonds my eyes turned watery nearly every other page; either in wonder or from brilliance. There is nothing dull, nothing lackluster about A Ring of Endless Light. This is one of those rare times I regret borrowing from the library rather than purchasing a copy for my own. Though, with a highlighter in hand, I mightn't have come up for air and reflection very often, and that would have been a loss. This is reflective book. It reveals a heart and a soul, and mirrors yours, in all the ways that makes you hold your breath and try not to blink. I think I must have read every other line twice and even that wasn't enough. I immersed myself the way I always do with a very luminous book.There is a place, where poetry meets the grit of humanity, and marries the two, creating something otherworldly. This is where A Ring Of Endless Light lives.Thank you, Madeleine, truly.
