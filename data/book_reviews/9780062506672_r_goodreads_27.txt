Shakti Woman: is a profound and transformative book that resonated so deeply with the essence of our feminine power. The book dives into the reawakening of women’s spiritual and shamanic traditions, offering women a path to reclaim their inherent strength and wisdom.

The book weaved together mythology, history, and the authors personal experiences to explore the role of women as healers, leaders, and visionaries throughout time. Noble delves into how modern women can reconnect with their shamanic roots by embracing their intuition, menstrual cycles, and bodies as sacred. Her perspective was so unapologetically feminist and empowering, presenting the female body not just as a source of life but also as a conduit of spiritual wisdom.

What sets the book apart is its emphasis on practical spirituality. There is offers of rituals, exercises, and techniques that guide women on how to connect with their inner Shakti, the primordial feminine energy. These practices help to heal not only the individual but also the collective wounds of patriarchal oppression, making the book relevant in today's world.

This book is a must-read for any woman seeking to reconnect with her inner fire, heal herself, and contribute to healing the world. Noble’s writing is insightful, raw, and beautifully empowering. It will leave you feeling inspired, more attuned to the cycles of your life, and ready to embody your true potential as a woman in touch with her spiritual essence.

Highly recommended for those on the path of spiritual awakening, feminine empowerment, and healing.
