One Corpse Too Many, the second book of the Brother Cadfael series, is set during the feud between King Stephen and Empress Maud for the British throne. Brother Cadfael's abbey is caught in the middle of the battle between the two factions. Abbey guest house is filled with nobles who've come to pledge their allegiance to King Stephen. However, one who is evading the King also lives within the Abbey walls, unknown to all, except for Cadfael. And on top of all, when the Abbey was called to carry out the burial of those captured and executed by King Stephen's party, Brother Cadfael finds one corpse too many for burial. Cadfael's duty is torn. But he will do the right and the just by God, if not by man.

There are several things I like about the Brother Cadfael series. The first is the historical setting. That history is little known to me, and I truly enjoy reading about the medieval period. The characters are interesting and likable. Among them, there are one or two strong women with independent minds (in both novels, this has been the case; I hope it'll continue). Then, there is an interesting murder mystery to solve. And there is Brother Cadfael, the defender of justice!
