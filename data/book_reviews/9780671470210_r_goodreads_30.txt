Plodding venture into NorwayFrancis must have really wanted to set a book in Norway, because the setting is much of the content. We know that someone really does not want for the investigation at the heart of the plot to succeed, but there's really not much for the reader to go on about why that would be the case, and the plot just keeps plodding along. Can't say that I found the ending all that convincing either. Not my favorite Dick Francis work.
