I really enjoyed this one! I love this premise -- hero and heroine have known each other since childhood, hero is the town nobleman or whatever (no titles in this one, but he's still the rich boy who lives on the named estate), heroine is the daughter of the lowly shopkeeper. A titch too long and too plot-focused (as opposed to romance-focused), but that's pretty normal for the age of it. Maybe I've just been reading a lot of duds lately, but overall I thought this one was great!
