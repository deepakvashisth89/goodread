Quaintness can be beautiful. I'm glad I took a chance on a first meeting with Phoebe Atwood Taylor, as well as Asey Mayo, her Cape Codder hero. In recent years I've been making up for my knowledge-gap regarding the states of New England, and this story certainly moved that progress along nicely. It's true that this story is old-fashioned and very intricate, and that even for me with my excellent memory it wasn't entirely easy staying abreast of the plethora of characters, but the 1st person narration of 'Miss Prue' helped keep them sorted out. Also, though I won't give away the specifics, this story features one of the most unusual methods of murder I've encountered in any mystery, one which has to be read to be believed. This won't be the last Taylor work I read.
