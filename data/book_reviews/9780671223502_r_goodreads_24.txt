Plaza Hotel, Manhattan, New York: Kay Thompson’s Eloise with drawings by Hilary Knight begins “I am Eloise. I am six.” This 60th edition (white cover with pink stripes with Eloise writing her name using red lipstick on a huge mirror as well as the title of the book - Eloise is underlined with red lipstick too.) comes with a CD on which Bernadette Peters reads and sings this book. Eloise lives on the top floor of the Plaza Hotel with Nanny. Eloise explains what she does during the day at the Plaza. She loves the Plaza, room service, Nanny, the telephone, making things up, lunching at the Palm Court, visiting the Terrace Room, the Persian Room, the Crystal Room, and even the Grand Ballroom. She has even stopped by the Oak Room (which is actually the Oak Bar). Eloise does not stay long at the Oak Room, obviously! Eloise just ❤️❤️❤️❤️ The Plaza! A fantabulous book for children of all ages! The illustrations are brilliant! 5+ stars A book not to be missed by anyone no matter the age!
