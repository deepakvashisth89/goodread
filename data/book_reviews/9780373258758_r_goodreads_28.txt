I liked the main characters, Kane and Kayla. Their chemistry was instantaneous. He's a police detective working undercover, following up on a tip about an illegal business. She's operating a family business that belonged to her recently-deceased aunt, the same business that Kane is investigating. Despite him quickly figuring out that Kayla is innocent of any wrongdoing, an attack on her at the business prompts further investigation. Kane and Kayla are both carrying hurts from their pasts and any future for them seems doubtful. While this story has all the ingredients for a good romance and the mystery surrounding the business is intriguing, the story seemed rushed to me. I felt the plot needed to be fleshed out more. The couple fell in love at warp speed and despite both of them being resolved to stay apart after the case is closed, their reunion afterward happened very abruptly. Hence the three-star rating.
