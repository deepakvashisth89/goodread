Pretty slow read by my standards.I like HST’s gonzo prose and general cynicism, especially towards politicians, but my own general disinterest in the process of political campaign is enough to make this a boring read.The rewarding segments of this read are when Thompson reels off against a candidate or makes up insane rumours purely based on distain, or just the insights into his insane personal experience following the campaign.I’ll read more Thompson, maybe the Hells Angels book, but political literature isn’t my thing, and I knew that from the get go.
