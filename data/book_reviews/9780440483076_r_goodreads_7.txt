This book turns out to be the dream book for me when it comes to a bit of fantasy, historical fiction, adventure all put together to recommend to young readers and for beginners who want to start reading, feel the magic of words printed on papers and spark their imagination on a whole new level!I just wish I read this book while I was in school alongside my English textbooks. I know that this would have been quite the book for me then!But lament not I told myself as I closed the book a few days back as I could still enjoy the inevitable fun and enjoyment while reading this amazing younger middle grade classic.It is the story of a not so popular doctor who later felt that his destiny was to help animals, explore far and wide to help more animals while he could.He learned how to communicate with them from his parrot, Polynesia.The book is really short and it is so easy to read. With the original illustrations here and there, it has been quite a reading experience!
