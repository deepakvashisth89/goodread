Round up to four stars.Ghostwritten by Jahnna Beecham and Malcolm Hillgartner.This could have been another book with a very misleading cover and was about a breath away from making my tearjerker shelf. Part of the beginning could have just been traumatizing for anyone who has witnessed a loved one having a heart attack especially with it having been Father's Day this past Sunday.I lost my dad when I was 18 from cancer but it wasn't any less scary than watching him waste away.Unfortunately, not too long ago, my stepfather also passed away from liver and kidney disease.Snow hits Stoneybrook and the older kids help Watson get his car off and running for work, but Kristy can't help but notice that Watson looks a little winded. Later Kristy comes to give Watson a break from snow shoveling and he has a heart attack on the front porch.Kristy dials 911 and goes inside to yell for both her mother and Nannie as she is on the phone. Once the two adults get downstairs, Kristy tries to keep David Michael and Emily out of the way when the ambulance comes. Shannon witnesses the ambulance and calls after Kristy's mother and grandmother leave for the hospital she then comes over to help after trying to reach Charlie and Sam at a basketball game at the high school.Kristy and her siblings have a long wait to hear anything so Kristy calls Mary Anne to fill her in and she comes over with a freshly back from California Dawn to help out. When Nannie comes back, she tells the Thomas/Brewer family that Watson is okay.Okay in that he is alive but now has to be on a stricter diet for his heart and he needs to ease up on going to work. Being a businessman, Watson has decided that he will quit his job and be a stay-at-home Mr. Mom leaving most of his work to his vice president. It doesn't stop them from calling and faxing the poor man about his company, but Watson handles everything surprisingly well.He can cook, he knows how to clean, Watson even drives Kristy to BSC meetings to give Charlie a break. Kristy's mom still has her own job, and Nannie helps out with Emily too. Everything goes sort of downhill when a situation of poor communication skills has Watson bringing home pizza for dinner with the kids while Nannie has been making a Pasta Primavera dish allowed on the Heart Healthy diet.Older people have their pride, so Nannie is convinced that she isn't needed as housekeeper anymore and decides to move into her own apartment. Emily is NOT happy about that change since she is a young child who has had Nannie around since she was adopted into the family but even Kristy and David Michael are sad to have her go.The minute Nannie moves out, what can go wrong does go wrong.Emily gets the flu, Charlie, Kristy and Sam are run ragged with doing the grocery shopping and getting David Michael to play practice that both get forgotten about. Kristy's mom and Watson handle what they can but then it happens to be the month that Karen and Andrew come to stay at the house. Karen has been in a bad mood because she wants her dad after his health scare.A visit to see Nannie has Kristy almost in tears begging her to come back and it is enough for Nannie to reveal that she was just being stubborn and a little hurt at not feeling needed. So, it is decided by Watson that he will work from home a few hours a day but still help out sparingly for his health. Nannie moves back to the mansion, knowing she is loved and appreciated, and all is well.The B-plot involves baby-sitting client, Mrs. Marshall, taking advantage of the BSC by having them look after not only her two girls, Nina and Eleanor, but the kids of a friend whom she goes to Jazzercise with. Back from California, Dawn is surprised that she will be watching five children on her own.She isn't given any extra pay for the extra children and apparently, it is a rule that there must be two sitters for more than four children. Not just a rule in Mallory Pike's large family household but a BSC rule that Kristy decided.It doesn't seem to be a shared bit of information because the next time that Mrs. Marshall needs a sitter, it is for five kids again. Mallory has to deal with one of the non-Marshall children getting hurt and causing bedlam among the very young children that she has to call Jessi to come and help her calm the storm with popsicles. Only Mallory is paid since she was the sitter assigned to the job.The next call from Mrs. Marshall for a sitter has Stacey as the assigned sitter but Kristy tells her that she is going to send Claudia to go with her in case the other children are there. When Mrs. Marshall refuses to pay for two sitters, the both of them leave her high and dry. Not a completely savvy businesswoman but a competent one, Kristy does call Mrs. Marshall to explain to her the rule and apologize for leaving her in a lurch the other day.Mrs. Marshall decides to take water aerobics instead since her friend doesn't like swimming so there won't be any more extra kids since they couldn't get along anyway. Conveniently solving the problem yet still kind of full of cringe thinking you can leave a thirteen-year-old or even an eleven-year-old girl alone with five kids under the age of six.I'm a grown woman and even my own six-year-old is a handful and I have even been a field trip chaperone.Kristy and Mr. Mom had that afterschool special kind of feel and I'm just glad we didn't have to have a repeat of losing a beloved family member again!In hindsight, there are already serious situations still coming ahead to shake up the world of the BSC...
