This is the 3rd book I’ve read by Laura Esquivel and I can’t recommend her books enough. She has a way of giving ordinary things magical qualities. She also gives you insight in other cultures and their history. This story is about love, family ,trust and how communication is vital to balance and harmony. I loved how she gave you both sides in Jubilo and Lucha relationships. It was very clear for the reader were things got lost in translation and caused a rift between this couple. I highly recommend this quick satisfying read 😊
