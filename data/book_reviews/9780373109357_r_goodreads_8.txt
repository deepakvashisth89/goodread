Love at first sight at war with the H and h’s principles. She loves him, and he loves her.The heroine, Judy, 22 believes in marriage and children. Her parents have been great role models. Will she hold on to her beliefs even if it means losing the man she loves?The hero, Malcolm, 30’s, sees marriage as a trap. His parents marriage was a nightmare. He isn’t opposed to living with women, in fact as long as it is mutually beneficial why not? Once things get a little tiring the live in can move on and he will not have to deal with the messy consequences that come with shared assets etc. However, he has never met anyone quite like Judy. Judy is a pint size Venus with cherubic looks. She is smart. She is not afraid of hard work. She speaks her mind and isn’t impressed with status or money. She can play a mean game of snooker and is loyal to her beliefs. The H doesn’t want to be without her, but he doesn’t want to marry her either.Things I liked:The h, despite her youth, knows what she wants. She stands up for herself. She is able to put others in their place. However, she is human and does make a few missteps in the story which just makes her more relatable as a character. Near the end of the story, she decides that maybe it wouldn’t be so bad to just live with the hero, as she loves him. However, before she can tell him of her decision his very married, very in love friends are over at the house. She sees them. Hears how they talk about their kids, and realizes that she can’t settle for less than a wedding ring. She leaves and I think her anguished pleas and the look on her face tells the hero more than he could ever get from talking it out. He ends up following her. It seems he too has had a change of philosophy and anything less than marriage with the h just won’t do.One of my favorite interactions between them came around page 151, only because it shows the h’s strength even though her heart is breaking and the H was her first lover.The hero:How can you speak of keeping yourself for someone else? You've given yourself to me. You're mine!' he insisted vehemently.The heroine:Tears of desolation filmed her eyes. 'No. I'm not yours. You had first claim, that's all. You didn't think enough of me to take up ownership. You have no property rights on me. No contract was signed,' she said bitterly. 'There's no more to be said. Please excuse me. As far as I'm concerned the matter is closed.'I just really like how the h was like you were my first, but I guess you don’t want to be my last!Both characters were very likable. I didn’t feel that the H was a jerk, his beliefs were just different. In the end, he did the right thing.Something that surprised me: The h didn’t get pregnant her first time out. I kept expecting this to come up as a thing but ED never engaged this possible secret pregnancy trope.Overall, the romance between the 2 takes a little over a month to get to the HEA.
