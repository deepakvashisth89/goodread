This was chilling. I couldn't sleep at times because of how scared I was. I remember reading the first chapter, it was around 1 am, and my first instinct was to put the book down and go to bed. I was petrified. Wendy's death really broke my heart. She was really doing well, and I'd hoped that she'd actually survive. Meredith was annoying at times, but I understand that she was just doing her job. I hated David's boss, the chief, or whatever. He was such a prick. I hoped he'd get his at some point, but I guess not. Overall it was one hell of a ride, and props to the author. I hope that one day I can say that I've read the entire Susan Shader series.
