Reread from when I was first introduced to this book in the 3rd grade during my own parents divorce.It is a well written book, that explains divorce to young children. To help them understand what happens, not only to their parents but to them as well. The glossary of terms in the front was great explaining terms that most children wouldn’t know. It is sad that books like this need to be written, but I am glad we have them as it was a comfort to me trying to navigate a very messy process.
