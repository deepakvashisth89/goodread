In the beginning I nearly stopped reading but by the end this became a book I could not put down.

It had a lot of elements I loved from the start. The gestalt of underdogs desperately clinging to a dying way of life reminded me of "Record of a Spaceborn Few" but without the cozy overtones--these books are anything but cozy. I loved the sacrifices and choices Ubu and Maria had to make to survive and very different ways they coped with what they had become.

And the writing was beautiful. Williams uses language to create the true sense that shooters are a people, distinct and separate from midvillers (people who live on planets). Sometimes the invented language used by sci-fi authors feels contrived but here it was natural. One of my favorite scenes is the one where Ubu and Maria explain shooter forms of address to an uncomfortable company representative from a planet. The situational discomfort that the official who would have previously spit on them is forced to endure is golden.

So why did I nearly stop reading? All the underage incest is why. As much as I loved this book and respect artistic choices, there was no real need for Maria to be 11 and Ubu 13 and have the brother and sister fuck. They were both artificially aged up so there was no practical plot difference if they had been 16 and 20 instead. While the incest added a certain emotional current, the implications weren't really addressed and I think the book would have worked just as well without it.
