I confess I only read up to when the rescue of the main party had been completed (@ pg. 235). Ross Sea party be damned! Haha. But I think I've gotten what I wanted out of it, so I'm marking it as read.

These explorers faced some incredible hardships, obviously. Reading about their fortitude and perseverance as they struggled to survive was pretty amazing. Living on an unprotected iceberg for several months is pretty fucking metal!

The big downside to this book is that it really gets bogged down with nautical and arctic terminology, sailing minutiae, etc. I think I read at least 200 latitude/longitude pairings, none of which meant anything to me at all. The book would surely have benefitted from substantial editing/reworking by a layperson. As it stands, it's an incredible story told in a less-than-interesting way.

Overall: although it was quiiiiite dry, and a tough slog to get through at times, it was not a bad read. 2.5 stars would probably do the trick.
