A little boy and his family live on Mars, where the little boy has a "pet" robot. One day the robot goes berserk and is sent to the junk pile. The little boy receives a utility belt to deal with this crushing blow. The rest of the story is detailed descriptions of the boy using the tools on his belt - ultimately repairing his robot friend. Didn't really care for how mean the sister was to her little brother. Hardly fostering familial harmony.Illustrations are okay.
