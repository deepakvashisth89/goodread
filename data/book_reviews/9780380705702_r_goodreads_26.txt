Unusually for Margery Allingham this is as much an espionage or science fiction story rather than a detective novel. It is set in England in the 1960s.Two of the main characters are schoolboys and this novel strangely echoes a time when the prevalent notions of childcare would be considered extreme negligence in the 21c! At various times the boys are alone and doing their own thing in circumstances alien to the 21c, a time before mobiles invented. Reminds me of my own childhood in the 1960s when it was perfectly normal for a child to spend the day away from home with the parents not knowing where they were.I did not find the story or the main characters particularly engrossing. And frankly found myself surprised and a little shocked at how the parents, teachers and an aunt simply didn't seem to care about or take adequate responsibility for their children. But in fairness to the author, she reflects those times well.I agree with the other Goodreads comments below too! This was a confusing plot and here intelligent women appear to accept the notion of servility to their men!!!
