Be ready for two entirely different books in one. A story about a Dutch family. And a travel/history guide of the Netherlands. The main story takes a break often to follow several youths on their trips to various museums where the story phases into a textbook about the history, famous people, battles/events of the Netherlands.I thought the story was great! Unpredictable and sweet with a wonderful ending and characters to learn from. And if you’re in need of a book about the Dutch, look know further; this can double as your study guide.#NetherlandsAges: 10+Cleanliness: mentions smoking, some alcohol.
