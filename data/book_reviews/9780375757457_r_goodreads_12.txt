TypeeBy Herman Melville (1846-1891)We are in 1842, in Polynesia, on the island of Nuku Hiva, one of the Marquesas.Melville recounts his adventures as a runaway from the whaler “Dolly”. He had been serving as a sailor for six months and could no longer bear the ill-treatment on board. On the day of their land leave, he, Tom together with his friend Toby, climbed the hills at the centre of the island. They had hoped to find plenty of fruit but found none. Progress was steep and barred by vegetation. Exhausted and Tom wounded they suffered. Hesitating they had to decide which of the valleys they would have to descend. One would be to the friendly Hapaas, the other to the Typee, the cannibals. Unknowingly they choose the wrong one. On the way down they had to follow steep waterfalls, arriving exhausted there was no way back. Luckily for them, they were received with good grace and hospitality. They got food and a place to sleep in the house of the chief. All seemed well except that they were not allowed to move to the beach from where they were hoping to find an escape route when they were ready to leave. Only Toby was allowed to go to try and find medical help for Tom. He goes but does not come back. Tom feared the worst, after, one day he discovered accidentally three shrink heads hidden in bags inside the chief's home. During this time Tom fell in love with a beautiful native girl, Fayaway. One day a group of warriors exchanged hostilities with the neighboring tribe. In the end, they brought back three slain enemies from their expedition. Tom is secretly watching a cannibal feast over these victims and feares being the next one in line. What seems like a last-minute miracle, and with the help of his adoptive family he gets word from the beach. A rowboat from a new whaler has come to make an offer for his release. Half running and half being retained he succeeds in boarding the boat, killing one of his fierce followers and getting off, leaving Fayaway crying on the beach.In an afterword, Melville recounts his re-meeting with Toby a year after this adventure. Toby had been hired by another ship and did not have the credibility and means to send help to Tom.
