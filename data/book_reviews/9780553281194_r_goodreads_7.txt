The Five Fingers by Gayle Rivers: I recall I read this book well maybe 40 years ago I couldn't really recall anything about the story other than it was a special forces mission during the Vietnam war far behind enemy lines. It is somewhat like Joseph Conrad Journey into the heart of darkness, a exploration of the deepest depths of madness, darkness, and despair, what happens when you go beyond far beyond the civilized into the animal into kill to survive just becoming as basic and barbaric as one can be. Well it achieves this but it lacks all the deeper ethos of a man in war story. I missed that back in the day and now I'm reading the formula of the story but I think it is missing a real human connection how the main character changed and what the changes brought about to his character. Unfortunately the main character is a little two dimensional and what changes might have resulted were left for another book.
