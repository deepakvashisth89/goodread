I loved the concept but the story didn't grab me. First, the main character is thirteen years old and the book is written at a fourth grade reading level. So, is this a story for third/ fourth graders or sixth-eighth graders? You can have a story for struggling readers without talking down to them; this isn't it. For me, the content doesn't match the writing style/ vocabulary.

I didn't really like the main character, Abby. I felt she was a bit wimpy and too much "woe is me." Did she really lose her identity or did she want to re-invent herself? (Actually, I think she's too timid for the latter.) What seems more appropriate is that she's feeling displaced and missing her old friends, a concept that isn't addressed much in the story.

I might have liked this more if I read it as a child but as an adult, it didn't really work for me. There are too many other wonderful books out there to spend time on this one.
