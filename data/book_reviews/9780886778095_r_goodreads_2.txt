I've read this wonderful book a total of 2 times. Not as much as I've read other books, but it was within 2 years of each other, and I suspect I'll reread it again next year. I honestly wish there was a sequel or a prequel (maybe about Jerlayne's mother and her changes to their world perhaps?) I can not think of anything about this book, or in this book, that turned me off from it in anyway. The world created in this book is detailed enough to paint a nice picture, and made me want to know more of the culture of the elves and the goblins. I'm looking forward to reading more books from Lynn Abbey.
