I loved these old D&D player character books back in high school. I read this one on dwarves many times, and it was nice writing a dwarven character in my own book The Hirelings.This has clear rules on what the race can and can't do for the game, but it also has a lot of cultural aspects that are great for fantasy readers and writers.The pictures in the book are full-page and world-class as well. $20 well spent!
