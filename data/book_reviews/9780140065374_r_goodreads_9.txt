I found "Death of an Old Goat" to be a very effective murder mystery and satire on English and Australian academia and society. The writing was consistently humorous, the characters mostly unique and engaging; the mystery itself, furthermore, kept me guessing, and was not ridiculous or disappointing in the end.The book has a couple flaws that are worth mentioning: due to the short length of the book and the large amount of characters, not all of the characters were as fleshed out as they could have been; moreover, the end of the novel is a bit abrupt which was slightly disappointing, however it does work in the humorous vein of the book.Overall, I was definitely recommend this book to readers who enjoy murder mysteries; I would rank it among Agatha Christie's best works. Furthermore, this book resembles the recent Knives Out films in its mixture of murder mystery and societal satire, so fans of those films will likely also enjoy this novel.
