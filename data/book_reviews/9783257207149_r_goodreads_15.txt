I've tried to enjoy the Maigret books, I really have. However, despite reading seven of them over the last few years I haven't found a great one yet.Simenon's books are enjoyable & have a nice atmosphere & although this one came highly revommended (Thanks, Rhys) I think it's time to give up on trying any more for now.That aside, this 1931 novel is good for a 90 year old book. There's a great opening & plenty of odd happenings throughout the story. As usual Maigret gets out his pipe & goes through a lot of tobacco as he attempts to solve the crime.Perhaps I'll return to the world of Maigret one day, but for now it's time to say au revoir.
