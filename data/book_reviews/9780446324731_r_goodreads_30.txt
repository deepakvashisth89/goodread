I had never even heard of the author Bill Granger until this book came to my attention because of the movie. I now plan to read more of the series.Devereaux(the November Man)was the best operative in Section R. He has retired to try to live a normal life when he is sucked back into the intelligence community. There are twists and turns in the plot that make you wonder who the good guys really are. He is a complex character who is cold and efficient yet loves his woman deeply.He is conflicted between his desire to be with her and his inner desire to be where the action is.
