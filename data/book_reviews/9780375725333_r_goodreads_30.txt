I’ve been a fan of Rich Cohen’s writing since I first discovered him in Vanity Fair and now I enjoy his pieces in AirMail. I devoured his book, The Sun, the Moon and The Rolling Stones and I just finished his memoir Lake Effects. Cohen is a writer’s writer. He makes the words flow effortless across the page and seduces you into thinking you, too, can write that well. Every time I finish one of his books I vow to read them all. Lake Effects is a coming of age story where the everyday shines with relatability and humor.
