Language barriers and personal reinvention sit at the center of English as a Second Language, where Alexandra Brennan's life takes an unexpected turn after she moves abroad following a series of personal disappointments.

As she tries to adapt to a new country and a different way of communicating, Alex also finds herself reassessing relationships she left behind, including complicated ties to family and love interests.

The experience of being an outsider shapes much of her emotional journey, forcing her to confront insecurity and identity in unfamiliar surroundings.

The story blends romance with personal growth, though some plot developments feel predictable within the genre. Still, it’s a reflective and accessible read about starting over in a new environment.

RATING: Four out of five stars. ⭐⭐⭐⭐
