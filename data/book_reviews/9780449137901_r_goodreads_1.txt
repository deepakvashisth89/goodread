I enjoyed reading this book. Make no mistake, it was an uncomfortable read for a woman who is American and the descendant of slaves. However, it was a good story and the moments of discomfort were absolutely appropriate. I generally don't read these kinds of books because of the issue of slavery, to be frank I'd question anyone who didn't feel uncomfortable reading an account of slavery, regardless of their ethnicity. But the cover caught my eye and the plot was unique. I don't know about anyone else but how many times are you going to encounter a former slave who gets appointed to a law enforcement officer? I suggest this book only if you aren't sensitive to racial issues. If you are, don't read it.
