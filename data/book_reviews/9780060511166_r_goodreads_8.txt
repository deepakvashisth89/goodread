Amelia Bedelia's Family Album" is a super funny book by Peggy Parish. Mr. and Mrs. Rogers throw a big party for Amelia's family, and she shows them her family album. There are so many relatives with crazy stories!My favorite cousin Ella makes things from clay. Mr. Rogers asks if she's a Potter like in the Harry Potter books, but she's not! Ella and her husband have a bakery where they make yummy treats.Cousin Susan B is in a fan club, but not the kind with cool air. She's surrounded by fans who love her. Imagine that!Lulu is really funny. She stuffs olives inside herself. It's like she's a big olive herself!The best part is when Amelia's nephew Ollie says he's a catcher. But he doesn't catch balls, he catches everything like measles, mumps, and even colds! Silly, right?This book is full of laughs and surprises, just like the other Amelia Bedelia books.
