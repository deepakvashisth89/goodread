Glorieta PassI gave this book 4 Stars although my enjoyment of it was really only 3 Stars worth! I gave the extra Star because it was really a good story. However, although there were many, & quick, changes of scene & of POV's, the story was very slow moving for me. I personally don't much like stories with so many main characters flipping back & forth. It normally takes several chapters to know & remember who is who & where! Then some just disappear & it bugs me tremendously when that happens! Especially when several were captured but no more is mentioned of them. Several lesser characters just kinda slid off into the sunset as well. I suppose they will show up in a future book.
