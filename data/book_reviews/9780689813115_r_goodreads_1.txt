I read this to see if it was worth passing on to my ten year old grand-daughter. Released by Scolastic, the intended audience is youth aged 8-12 and I believe it should work for that age group. While the two main characters are fictional, many real life characters are mentioned in the story. I found it enjoyable but perhaps a little shorter on details than I would have liked. For example, I know the character Gavin lied about his age to get hired, but no age was included so I was left to guess. I also found the ending was very abrupt and I wanted more. It was a quick easy read.
