Boy, this book came out almost 30 years ago and still - whenever I find the time to reread it it sucks me in …Liam Bartlett died in a blast on the tiny island of San Salustiano, but two years Kayla gets information on a mysterious blond Americano who lives in the jungle with the rebels …She decides to fly to Montana to visit Liam’s brother Cal and talk him into accompanying her to San Salustiano to find Liam … or die trying.Such a great story placed in a civil war-infested island where the locals try everything to make living in dire circumstances livable garnered with white hot attraction between Cal and Kayla who while searching for their brother/friend find each other.👏🏼👏🏼👏🏼
