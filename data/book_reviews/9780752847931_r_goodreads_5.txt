Private investigator Grace Smith is hired by an accountant to find out why his sessions of hypnotic regression show him in a past life, committing a murder. Since Grace doesn't believe in the supernatural, she's sure he must be up to something hinky. But everything he remembers from his "past life" checks out. One of his clients is a famous, aging rock star who has just moved back into an old oast house that he renovated for his second wife--he's up to number five now--and that features in the regressions.
