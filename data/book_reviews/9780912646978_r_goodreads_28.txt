Sometimes you read a book at just the right time for it to be most impactful. This is my time for this book.When Hewey speaks to Eve about them still belonging to the old century, I can understand that now in a way I wouldn't have been able to if I had read this when it came out in 1978. It is much more personal, and much more powerful with the current change in one's surroundings, in one's country, in one's own self.But if I had read it then, I would still have found this to be a great story, written by a great storyteller. Kelton was such a gifted writer. His stories, though fictional, have so much truth in them.Elmer Kelton is becoming one of my favorite authors, with works that I know I will be returning to again and again.
