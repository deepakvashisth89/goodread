The Review

A/ This well-known tale is as much a parable as it is a work of historical fiction.

Yet. It’s as much a work of contemporary fiction as historical. I say this because the letter A is still used on people. In fact, the entire alphabet of the English language is used. For all I know the entire “alphabet” of every language on earth is used against people of that language group. In English, B is used just as A is. So is C,D, E,F, G ……. Employ your imagination to decide what “sins” can be attributed to each letter so that it can be used to hurt and shame individuals.

Hawthorne’s tale highlights the cruelty, hypocrisy and legalism that religion is capable of. And to me, this extends to people who are not typically thought of as religious. In the end the woman targeted, Hester, is more of a “Christian” or “human soul” than those afflicting her.
