I would like to propose a radical truth, if one wants to understand the tradition of American free verse poetry they must first understand it’s origins in Persian poetry, and Persian poetry is rooted in the sufi tradition, which is rooted in Islam. Therefore to understand the unique becoming that is “american poetry/ beat poetry” one must understand Islam. I make this argument because the genealogy of American free verse poetry goes something like this = Hafez -> Emerson -> Whitman -> Ginsberg -> just about everyone.

All too often when people think of the “beat generation” only 3 maybe 4 names come to mind? When in reality we’re speaking about an ocean whose waves are still felt today. One of those waves was the infamous, and controversial, Peter Lamborn Wilson (Hakim Bey), whose work has deeply impacted me over the years.

This text “The Drunken Universe” is a commentary on and introduction to Persian Sufi poetry by Hakim Bey. This may come as a surprise to some, but Islamic civilization is full of a wealth of transgressive literature, poetry and heterodox mystical beliefs/practices. One of the major points of Hakim Beys lifelong work was to uncover and show the complex mosaic that is Islamic civilization, especially in light of current political/social to simplify & reduce everything. The Islamist and Islamaphobe aren’t too different in how they think of Islam after all.

Hakim studied, lived and traveled in the “east” for nearly a decade, and a large portion of which was spent in Tehran aside the likes of Corbin, Chittick & Syed Hossein Nasr - all major names in the field of Islamic mysticism/esotericism/occult/philosophy.

This text is a great diving board into the ocean that is Sufism, that is Islam & ultimately that is the American poetic tradition.
