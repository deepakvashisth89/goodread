This installment in the 87th Precinct series was my favorite so far. It has two stories one being a con man taking unspecting victims money in alot of different ways and another involving women being found floating in a river. The murder story is by far the best and the lead up to the arrest is full of tension. I was basically yelling at the book it had me on the edge of my seat. This is such a fun series can't wait to read the next.
