Blech. This read like a Harlequin from the mid 1980's, where the books were either hidden gems or nothing but men treating women like crap. The whole book the H does nothing but try to manipulate the h. Some things he said to her were quite vile and she just rolled over and said basically "Oh honey, I understand, all is forgiven!". He had a bad first marriage but clearly learned nothing about making sure others didn't suffer like he had. Also, am I the only one that finds it gross that the guy is banging everything that walks where she remains pure as the driven snow in the 8 months between their drunken one night stand (that's on the first page) and his deciding he wants a piece of her again? Book feels older to me than being written in 2002, some very dated themes.
