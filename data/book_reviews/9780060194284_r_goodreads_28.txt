Chierichetti was a young costume designer who became friendly with Head late in her life. He provides a biography, some insight into her designing, and the story of their friendship. I would really like to have known more about her designs and sense of working on different types of films, than the politics of which movies she designed and which she didn't. Lots of good pictures and some interestingly weird insights into bodily quirks of some of the stars that Head had to contend with (for example Bette Davis didn't want to wear underwire bras because she thought they caused cancer so Head had to design around this).
