Oh this made me so FURIOUS! It is classic MacInnes in so many ways - excellent writing, tense, spot-on period and place details. And of course having terrorists poison spies in their apartment lobbies with nerve toxins isn't just for the 1960s and 70s anymore, it's still with us. So yes, read this book for a well-written thriller. Using art as a way of moving money clandestinely certainly happens (I researched this for a book of my own).BUT THE END WILL MAKE YOU FURIOUS. No spoilers, but I knew in my gut that something was coming and I had to keep walking away from the book, getting a drink of water, taking a deep breath, convincing myself that I was overreacting, and then going back to read. So be ready.
