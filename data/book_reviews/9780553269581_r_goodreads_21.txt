The Lord God Made Them All (#7)by James Herriot (1981 1st edition, hardcover), 373 pages.Another really good read by James Herriot. These stories are set after World War II. He has returned home and picks up his Veterinary practice as before. He now has two young children, a boy and a girl who love to attend with him to the various farms. His son later in life does, in fact, turn out to be a Veterinarian himself.In 1961 and 1963, Herriot takes on two different spontaneous jobs, one on a ship and one by a dilapidated cargo plane, transporting cows and sheep to Germany and to Istanbul. His buddy glamorized those two jobs to make them sound like an adventure of a lifetime but turned out to be more trouble than they were worth. He wove these interesting stories in-between his veterinary stories.Now, onto the last book in the series: “Every Living Thing”.THE COMPLETE SERIES* All Creatures Great and Small (#1-2) (1972) (incorporating If Only They Could Talk and It Shouldn't Happen to a Vet)* All Things Bright and Beautiful (#3-4) (1974) (incorporating Let Sleeping Vets Lie and Vet in Harness)* All Things Wise and Wonderful (#5-6) (1977) (incorporating Vets Might Fly and Vet in a Spin)* The Lord God Made Them All (#7) (1981)* Every Living Thing (#8) (1992)
