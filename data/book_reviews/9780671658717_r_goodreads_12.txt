This feels like a predecessor to more recent short story collections I’ve read in the past few years like Homesick for Another World and Heartbreaker where you’re looking at loneliness, love and connection through a less conventional lens either due to the character or the circumstances they’ve found themselves in. Even the writing in this is quite barbed but I really liked it and I’d argue this book out of what I’ve experienced of the mico-genre,(if we’re calling it that), is it at its best. Personal favourites were A Romantic Weekend, Something Nice, Connection, Other Factors and Heaven.
