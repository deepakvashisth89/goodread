This book is based on the famous module from the early history of Dungeon's and Dragon's. Really the only reason to read this is for nostalgia reasons for those familiar with the original adventure, which is why I read it and is a perfectly good reason. The novel itself is not that great by the standards of a D&D novel. It's a little primitive in the writing and character development. It is weirdly paced. The original module dealt with three dungeons around three different giant races. The novel for whatever reason spends a lot of time on the first dungeon and then rushes through the last two, so that was very strange.Still despite all of this it served its purpose of being a light read around nostalgia.
