The second in the three books I picked up from Dani's collection of Van de Wetering's novels -- and, although clever and interesting, it wasn't quite up the quality of the first novel. Two of the main characters leave Amsterdam for Maine in order to bring the commissaris's sister home after the death of her husband which, of course, turns out to be a murder. Interesting cast of characters, but more contrived. Still, the adventures were interesting and the characters first rate. I'm on a roll and will read more.
