I bought this ten or more years ago and don't think I finished it then because I couldn't remember it well and it wasn't rated here on Goodreads, so I thought it was time to give it another go.

The writing was great, characters were interesting, and it definitely tells their lives in a series of snapshots over time, but was it ever a chore to read at times. Those hallucinations, dreams, random streams consciousness and words were all a bit too much. This could have easily been a higher rating if not for that.
