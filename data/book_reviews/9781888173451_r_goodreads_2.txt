This is the second book I have read by Alice Thomas Ellis which is set at Christmas. Neither would I consider jovial or heartwarming, in case that is what you are looking for.

In this book, we meet Eric, who runs the “Inn on the Edge of the World”, somewhere in Scotland, probably in the Hebrides. The inn is not doing well. He decides to run the following advertisement: “ Dreading Christmas? Then get away from it all in a small hotel at the edge of the world..” What a clever premise, in my opinion, as so many people do dread Christmas.

Five people decide this seems perfect so away they go. Do they find what they are looking for? Alice Thomas Ellis does not paint any of her characters in a favourable light. Jessica, the self centred actress; Ronald, the psychoanalyst, who would do well to analyze himself; Jon. A younger actor who is convinced Jessica loves him; Anita, shy and yearning for a husband and Harry, who has come back to an island he grew up on and from which he escaped due to tragic events. Harry was the only character I liked.

Escaping Christmas isn’t all that they expected it to be, because really, they were trying to escape from their lives. The island characters they meet add the humour for which Ellis is known. This was quite a captivating read with some humour, island legends and ghosts. Not a book to put you in a Christmas mood, but I bet we can all relate to wanting to escape Christmas once in a while.

My thanks to Laura, whose review brought me to this book sooner versus later.

Published: 1990
