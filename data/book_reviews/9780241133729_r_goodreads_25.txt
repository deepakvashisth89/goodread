So much happening in this book.Corruption, murder, mystery, V.I. getting hurt, patched up, then right back out there trying to solve the current problem; find missing children!V.I. being wrongly accused of hiding a young girl twice! She keeps facing the adversity, taking the disrespect, and solving crimes/murders/mysteries.
