This book seems to debunk some of the rumors which were flying about what happened in that filthy farmhouse. However, maybe DNA evidence since it was written has helped to fill in some of the questions. I really don't know.This author tries hard and ends up overwriting. For example, he leaves the jail at 11:30 am and arrives at hospital at 1:30 pm. This is described as a trip of "several hours". In fact, it is obviously 2 hours — why say several?The tabloid writing style detracts from the material, which is already over the top. Such material requires no embellishment.
