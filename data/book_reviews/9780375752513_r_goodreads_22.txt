5 stars for Call of the Wild and To Build a Fire.3 stars for White Fang.White Fang is such a repetitive and pale imitation of the lean, atmospheric Call of the Wild that reading it almost makes the latter worse. But much is to be said for its opening scene of the men transporting a corpse by dogsled, which makes in itself a great short story.To Build A Fire is better than either, nearly flawless.
