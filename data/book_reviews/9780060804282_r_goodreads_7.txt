The books in the Nigel Strangeways series are a delight in storytelling. The author, writing as Nicholas Blake, was Poet Laureate C. Day Lewis, who obviously knew how to put words together to evoke images, emotions and more.Nigel Strangeways is an agent of private inquiry in the era following World War I, from a family of means and a former Oxford student. He finds himself working alongside Scotland Yard detectives to solve some of the more intricately plotted murders of that writing period.These are character-driven novels that can be read as standalones, if so desired.
