Fascinating and enjoyable biography of William Haines, box office star of the late silent and early talkie era of Hollywood, who led an open life as a gay man much to the eventual consternation of the studio system at MGM. When his film career was forced to a close, he embarked on his second act as one of the most successful interior designers in California. Mann's prose as always is filled with amazing details (although a little heavy at times with innuendo or third-hand stories) and genuine empathy and compassion for his subjects. "The story of William Haines, then, is not just the story of one man, one movie star. It is the story of the institutionalization of the Hollywood closet, a tradition that has persisted for more than fifty years."
