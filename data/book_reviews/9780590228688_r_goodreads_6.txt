This was good. Poor Luke; he was so scared about the secret he had to keep.
(It was the baby-sitter's boyfriend who caused the fire at Luke's home.) The whole fight against the wealthy industrialist who wanted to tear down a park was fun too. I'm glad Stoneybrook residents for the most part seemed to be pro-park, and I'm especially glad they won that battle. In my experience, few of these grassroots efforts ever actually win against the wealthy developers, so this felt satisfying. The
good twin / evil twin plot was a bit ridiculous but I do like the unusual take in this book that the
evil twin who'd been doing the vandalism is really kinda sorta the good twin who wants to save the park from being torn down for development.
