The action in this rather delightfully illustrated book is set in Dickon Wood where Harry the happy hedgehog sets up his hedgehog band. But the band is not confined just to hedgehogs for too long ...It all began when Harry made a big drum because he loved noise; he banged on it, tum-tum-te-tum. Helen the hedgehog who also lived in the wood heard the drum beat, liked it and went away to make her own drum so that she could join in. Norbet and Billy, both hedgehogs, followed suit and then there were four hedgehogs with drums gathered at Harry's place.Harry's drum went 'Tum-tum-te-tum', Helen's went 'Diddle-diddle-dum', Norbert's went 'Ratta-tat-tat' and Billy's, the biggest drum of all, went 'BOOM!' The whole wood was humming and tumming and drumming but the other animals - the pheasant, the owl, the bee, the mole, the badger, the fox, the crow, the deer. the dove, the frog, the toad, the spider and the dog who was lost in the wood - got together and shouted 'STOP!'The band stopped in astonishment thinking that the other animals were complaining. But they were not, they wanted to play along as well! 'So what can we do?', they said and only Harry, who knew all about noise, knew the answer.He arranged for the others to make their own noise and he arranged humming, hooting, buzzing, whistling, clapping, clicking and popping for them. Each animal had its own noise, I wonder if you can guess which noise each animal made?Anyway they did just that and the band went 'clap, clap, tip tap, click, twang, flip flop, hooooooo, hummmm. tum-tum-te-tum. clack clack, ch-ka, ba-zoooo ba-zoooo, ratta-tat-tat, pop pop, pweee pweee and BOOM'. And the dog who was lost in the wood just danced to the tune!It was a very happy band!Show more
