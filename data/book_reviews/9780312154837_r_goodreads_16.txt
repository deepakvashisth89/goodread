The story is riveting. It builds off both the unknowns surrounding the Second World War and the mystery that followed in its aftermath. The idea of something like what is described in the book actually happening feels unsettlingly possible at times. Having lived in Germany and studied the language and culture, it was shocking to see how some of the political climates in both the United States and Germany in 2026 mirror themes and tensions found in the novel, especially watching the rise of the AFD. The references to networks like Die Spinne make it even more fascinating because parts of that world truly did exist in some form. Overall, it is a fantastic read, and the final ten chapters or so were absolutely perfection.
