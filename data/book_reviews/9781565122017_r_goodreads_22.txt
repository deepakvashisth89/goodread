This is a travelogue of a man hiking through the forbidden areas of China. Not the best written of books but it reminded me of the Jon Krakauer books without all the detail that Krakauer gives. It was a straightforward story without too many flashbacks into the past. At one point in the story it is mentioned that he might be hiking for his father and too me this felt like an afterthought. Perhaps added to try to make the story more meaningful. Aside all that, I enjoyed the book and found the accounts of the people and places very interesting.
