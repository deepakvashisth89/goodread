The ProposalBetty Neels’ *The Proposal* is a heartwarming novella that beautifully encapsulates the essence of her signature “Cinderella” theme against the backdrop of the holiday season. The heroine, Bertha, emerges as a kindhearted yet overlooked young woman, hidden in the shadows cast by her stepfamily. Much like a modern-day Cinderella, Bertha endures the weight of endless household duties, often treated unfairly and with indifference. Yet, beneath her quiet exterior lies a well of resilience and compassion.Her life begins to transform with the arrival of Dr. Oliver Hay-Smythe, a man whose genuine admiration and kindness awaken Bertha's buried dreams and aspirations. As their relationship develops, a subtle but profound shift occurs, bringing warmth and color into her otherwise drab existence. The presence of children in the story adds another layer of emotion; their innocent laughter and hopeful wishes echo the spirit of the season, illuminating Bertha's gradual journey from neglect to recognition.While the title hints at a strong Christmas element, this charm is woven subtly into the fabric of the narrative. It gently underscores Bertha’s passage toward self-love, with the children's hopeful spirits serving as a poignant reminder of the magic of transformation and love. Neels’ gentle storytelling and the slow-blooming romance resonate deeply, inviting readers to witness not just a fairy tale, but an emotional awakening. Fans of understated romance will find solace in this cozy tale, even if the plot leans on familiar tropes.**A Christmas Wish**In *A Christmas Wish*, Neels crafts a more traditional holiday romance that vividly captures the warmth, joy, and magic of Christmas. Olivia Harding, the protagonist, is a young woman yearning to escape her monotonous life. Her encounter with Haso van der Eisler, a visiting surgeon, spurs a delightful change, as he becomes both her rescuer and romantic interest.The emotional depth in this story is accentuated by the experiences of mothers—like Olivia’s own, who embody the sacrifices and unfulfilled dreams of women yearning for something more. Their struggles resonate with Olivia’s journey, adding a layer of understanding and empathy. The children in this narrative play a vital role too, with their wide-eyed wonder and excitement for the holiday season reminding Olivia—and readers—of the magic that still exists if one dares to believe.Neels artfully intertwines the themes of hope and renewal into Olivia’s personal transformation. The children’s laughter fills the air, echoing the sentiments of love that embrace the holidays, while the poignant moments shared with their mothers highlight the legacy of dreams passed down to the next generation. The narrative strikes a balance between emotional vulnerability and the promise of new beginnings, resulting in a heartwarming and uplifting read that stands out more fully during this festive time.Together, these two stories showcase Betty Neels’ enduring appeal: simple yet heartfelt romances, heroines embodying quiet strength, and heroes who provide stability and kindness. While *The Proposal* lightly brushes the Christmas spirit leaning more heavily on the Cinderella trope, *A Christmas Wish* embraces the festivity head-on, filling readers’ hearts with warmth and joy. Fans of Neels will cherish the comforting familiarity of her style, while new readers may find these narratives a gentle introduction to her enchanting world of tender, old-fashioned romance, where love triumphs and dreams can indeed come true.
