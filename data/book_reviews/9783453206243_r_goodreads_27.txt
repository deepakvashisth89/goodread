When men are raised by war, violence follows them. Martin Brosnan’s life has seen him rescuing a war photographer in Vietnam and defending his beliefs all over Europe. Now, he resides in the Alcatraz of France. While an old compatriot and former lover conspire with a British spy to release him, the man they want him to remove from the equation plots his own devious acts. Will any of them come out of this alive?Action-filled and well-crafted, this peek into the lives of spies and politicians definitely entertains.
