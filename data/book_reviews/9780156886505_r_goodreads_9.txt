While not slow going in the beginning, story-wise, I did find it slow to sink in style-wise. Getting the rhythm of the author's cadence in this book and catching on to what was going on nearly led me to abandon the book. Ultimately, it sink in, and while I could see the uncanny antics of Edie being memorable, the title makes her out to be worse than I feel she was. More like a Paddington Bear. The hurricane and her imagining of war and soldiers and enemies were when it got interesting for me. Her siblings Theodore, Hubert, etc., were less interesting. Getting out in the little dory with the two little step-sisters was something to chuckle at. Catching the thief as only she could was the delight to cap it off.
