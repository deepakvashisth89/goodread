This is possibly the worst book I have ever encountered in my life. It starts with a hockey game where the author uses a slew of incorrect terminology, like calling goals 'points'. Then the protagonist, who's 13, inexplicably has no ride home from the game, which sets the stage for him to be kidnapped by another teenager in a limo. Then he somehow ends up on a "professional kids team" in Indiana.One hilarious thing about this book is how so many of the characters are always referred to by their full names: "Kenneth Agard Jr" never becomes Ken. If the book wasn't from 1986, you'd assume it was written by ChatGPT.I am poorer for having read even this far into the book. I regret taking it out of a little free library. Do not, under any circumstances, read this book.
