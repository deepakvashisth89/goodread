Although I enjoyed some parts of this story, I found the pace of the first half quite slow. Once Maggie and Connor had reached their destination, events moved up a pace. At first, I could connect with the main characters, they were two very stubborn people, it did improve once they decided to be honest with each other. I did think the sudden change in one character at the end, a little unbelievable. I received a copy via eBooks Discovery and have voluntarily reviewed it. All thoughts and opinions are my own.
