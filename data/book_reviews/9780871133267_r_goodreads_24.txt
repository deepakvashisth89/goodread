If you're a Highsmith fan (as I am) you'll want to skip 'Found in the Street', a late '80s work that, in character construction and dialogue, as well as overall story, is still stuck in the '50s. Once an excellent observer of human nature, Highsmith failed to make fresh observations in later work, instead using an outdated lens that makes for stilted dialogue and strangely antique responses and attitudes in her characters. Even the plot itself falls flat, never delivering the kind of elegant twist and revelation that made me a fan in the first place. Stick to the early work, which is often brilliant.
