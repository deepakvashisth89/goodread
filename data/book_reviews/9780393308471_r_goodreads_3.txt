There are so many reasons to despise this book. The girl protagonist, Andrea, I swear I would have killed her many times. Not all of its storylines are completed or solved and the ending was tooooooo much. However and despite all of this I have ENJOYED hugely reading this novel. Don't ask me why but I have drowned into these pages and I have the feeling that I'm going to need some time to come out to the surface again.
It has been terribly fantastic.
