When I read the first Marple,The Murder at the Vicarage, I wasn't much impressed. I was reading the Poirot series at the time, so essentially, comparisons were made, and I preferred Poirot. All the same, that I should try another Marple was present on my mind. So, when my Poirot journey ended,The Body in the Librarywas the natural choice to resume Miss Marple since it is the second mystery novel of the series.The murder-mystery of this novel was quite intriguing. I've read many Agatha Christie mysteries, so I can say quite honestly that this one is one of the good mysteries by her. She managed to keep my interest and suspense until the very last chapter. I figured out who'd done it and why, but not how it was done. Only when the facts were fully explained by Miss Marple in detail that I realise the ingenuity of the crime plot woven by our queen of mystery. What a wonderful criminal brain she has had! :)As to our lady detective, Miss Marple, we see only a little of her. But her valuable assistance is felt throughout, strongly. I was a little shocked to realize that I had underestimated the importance of her sleuthing skills when I first read her. I felt that I had not given enough importance and weight to her skills, which are more or less based on her knowledge of life and people in general. But reading this novel made me appreciate Miss Marple and her skills more. Also, I was quite impressed with her motto - not to believe in anything that is said to you but to form one's own opinion of its truth - something which I myself live by. :) And now that I see Miss Marple in a more favourable and appreciative light, I want to read her more.More of my reviews can be found athttp://piyangiejay.com/
