I’m so sad this book has sat on my shelf for years unread. There isn’t much drama to the story, it doesn’t have twists and turns, and it’s never that surprising; that being said, it is one of the most honest books about family I have read.It’s a story of people trying their best, failing, and trying again. For me, it’s nostalgic.it felt like a glimpse of what my life would have been like if I were born one generation earlier in my dad’s family.At its worst, it’s slight, but at its best it’s a vision of how remarkably we look on the ones we love.
