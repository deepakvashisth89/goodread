This is an amazing collection of songs from all cultures and generations. With lyrics and guitar chords for over 1200 song titles, one can't go wrong when in need of a music reference. My brilliant music director told me about this wen we were beginning our new Interfaith Church. It has been an amazing tool that we are using to create our own hymnal. The songs range from popular (The Beatles, Pete Seeger, etc.) to Negro Spirituals to Silly Children's' Songs. The uses for this book are practically unlimited.It is in a compact format, so it is easily portable. Cool illustrations are throughout. Say hello to some old favourites and make some new friends as you experience the blessing of music. Awesome book!!!!
