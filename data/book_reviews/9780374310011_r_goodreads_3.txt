When the buttons off their rotund father’s britches, his three daughters’ fanciful schemes for procuring him new ones end up landing them all husbands.This is a very silly story. Did the father really only have one pair of pants? Why didn’t it occur to anyone in this family to simply buy him new buttons? Is there a 24 hour emergency tailor in town? What is with the wacky schemes? Does no one in this household have any common sense?The text is long for a picture book, making it best suited for older children. The illustrations are a lovely Regency Era England inspired watercolor [?].
