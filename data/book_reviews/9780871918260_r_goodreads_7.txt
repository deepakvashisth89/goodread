In "The Open Boat," one of the finest short stories in the language, Crane relies on tone and imagery to portray the heartless indifference of nature. The famous opening line, "None of them knew the colour of the sky," establishes an immediate bleakness, a world void of the emotional value of one color. The sea is described as grey, and the only green, suggestive of hope, is that of the land that the men cannot reach.
