I've loved the poem 'carry your heart' for years and known I love Cummings because of that. I've been on a bit of a poetry binge so decided to get this book and see if I liked more of his work.I really do enjoy it.The way he used language and syntax, shaping it to his own meaning and expression is beautiful.But it's not easy. There are little intros to the sections which help explain a lot about some of the poems thank god. I like to understand. To work out the layers and meanings and these really helped. I think you could spend hours on these and find more and more, which I haven't.. yet. Hopefully I will appreciate the poems even further then.So many little lines, turns of phrase or just words leap out at you and grab on. I find I don't always fully grasp the whole poem but there are some lines that I keep going back to.Things like:the power of your intense fragilitynobody, not even the rain, has such small handsreaped their sowing and went their came, sun moon stars rainI'd rather learn from one bird how to sing than teach ten thousand stars how not to dance.Now I'm writing this I want to go read it again. It it more confusing than not, but when you work it out the reward is wonderous. You feel like you deserve to understand the beauty and the humour he's portraying after suffering over a passage or a turn of phrase for a while.It's beautiful and this copy is a lovely selection
