A coming-of-age story from the 70's, set in a real Seattle location, and a thinly disguised autobiography of the author. The schools, the locations, the teachers - all ring true for that time.Dan Inagaki is a high school junior in a South Seattle high school. Older brother Brad is the high school big man on campus, athletic, has a pretty north end Caucasian girl friend, accepted to college with the intent of being a doctor. Dan's not athletic, not popular and trying to survive in the shadow of big brother who can do no wrong.It's the '70s. Parents won't talk about the internment camps. There's drugs, threat of draft, hip music, jive talking, Dan's just trying to fit in and find his spot in the world.
