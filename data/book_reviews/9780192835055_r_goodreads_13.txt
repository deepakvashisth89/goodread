The final book I'll be reading for my degree! One of my all-time favourite films, but my brother Ed has said this may not be a book I enjoy....And he was right. I am baffled why this has become a classic. It is, as you would expect of the time, very offensive with the topics it covers, but on top of that I see little value in its quality of writing. The prose makes you feel out of breath, and not in a good way. It is very exposition heavy and the majority of the distinctions of the characters are based on gross stereotypes such as "the noble savage".I now truly appreciate all the major changes that were made to the film adaptation. So, I'm pleased I read this for that reason. Beyond that, I would not recommend this.
