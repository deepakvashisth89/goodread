"The world worked on goodwill, favors and reciprocities, but the system was too crude to keep the exchanges in proportion."Well hell, the Butcher's Boy is back! This is the second book of the series and I am hooked for sure.The Butcher's Boy has been resting in England after running away from some very ugly stuff at home and a trail of dead bodies, when an old foe spots him and forces him back to the States to settle scores once again, of course things get nasty quickly. If you're into fast, entertaining crime thrillers Thomas Perry never disappoints. I'll be catching up with the Butcher's Boy again soon!.
