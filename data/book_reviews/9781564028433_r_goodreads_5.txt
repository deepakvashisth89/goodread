Maisy Pickles was sent to Captain Abdul's Pirate School since her dad thought she needed to toughen up as she wrote poetry and read.She hated the school as they taught them to be lazy and mean, until the she hears Captain Abdul's plan to kidnap the kids and demand ransom. She together with other students hatch a plan and foil the plans and then set out to have a pirate school of their own where she can be the pirate of her own shop
