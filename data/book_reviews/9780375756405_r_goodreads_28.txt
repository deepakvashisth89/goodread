The first half of the book is very enjoyable. Early successes of unclimbed peaks, new ways up these mountains found by Bonatti. Nice little adventures in each chapter that had me on edge for his and others survival. Many thanks to the translator for his efforts to learn Italiano and get us these stories.
The second half of the book deals with the many years of battle the author spent trying to recover the honor of his name. Its devastating and disturbing to have to live with these problems. I couldn't cope with this half of the book.
