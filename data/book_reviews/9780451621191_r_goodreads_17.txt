lowkey best book ever. it gives u such a sense of what the masses of ppl were feeling, the ownership they felt over the revolution and their dedication to it. there’s also just so many little awesome stories within the book that you wouldn’t get from a purely academic/analytical text. i think the most touching part was the funeral for the martyrs and one banner saying ‘martyrs of the beginning of world social revolution’. also the description of workers coming out en masse to defend the revolution against kerensky was rlly moving.
