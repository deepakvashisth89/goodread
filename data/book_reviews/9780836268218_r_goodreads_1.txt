I remember really enjoying the Cathy Comics in the Sunday paper growing up. There was a brief period as an early teen when I thought I could be someone who collects comic strips, so I started cutting Cathy out of the paper. That didn't last long, and Cathy did not age well. I remember her being a strong independent career woman, but her unhealthy obsession with her weight and every fad diet just hit differently now that I am also an adult. It was interesting to remember some of the diets that were "in" when I was growing up, but funny to see Cathy make a doctors appointment to get on Phenphen pre-lawsuit. The simplistic black and white drawing were able to convey a lot of angst in their lines and I did appreciate at least Cathy's consistency in her personal life. I'm still a little confused as to what it is exactly she does though.
