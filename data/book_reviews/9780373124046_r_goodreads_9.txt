"The Passion Bargain" is the story of Francesca and Carlo.
Basically, our naive and maybe rich heroine falls for the bad guy, but on the eve of her engagement announcement, finds the truth..
he is with her for the money and sleeping with her best friend
Then she is whisked away by her hero to security, where he relentlessly seduces her and she gives into his passionate and lust-filled lovemaking. But then he proposes, and she has to refuse. Is he having an ulterior motive too?
Tbh, this had a crazy hero, a super innocent and confused heroine, and oodles and oodles and oodles of sex. This combo usually works for me, but in this one there was tad bit too much drama. The heroine was played by all the men, and I wished that there was more groveling in the end...from both the sides.
However, this did keep me engaged for 1/2 hour.
