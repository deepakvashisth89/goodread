Disappointing, first because of how the female characters are treated. Chalker often seems to have women being forced into some sort of breeding program, but here we’ve got pedophilia happening too and it’s just gross. Also, the villain had to die because she was too powerful (and not a dude, I guess). Secondly, from a plot perspective it didn’t make sense to have this series be one book for each planet - the central guy should have been getting reports from each planet continuously, not in a single dump. I don’t know, I’m a big Chalker fan and I’d been saving this series up, but now I don’t even know if I want to continue with it.
