Another hit from this series, this one feeling the most like a Goosebumps story thus far. The pacing and tone were on point here, and I quite liked the thematic aspect of friendship in this one; it’s a great add-on. There’s some decently unnerving scenes in here and I liked the lore of the Camp Fear Ghouls—er, Girls, that came with it. Characters are solid, the story was engaging, the climax is tense, and it’s an utterly great time if you’re in the mood for a camp/club story. I only hade truly one issue and even then I feel I can forgive it: there’s a moment in here where the villains go back on their word and it felt so unnecessary. Like, what was the point of the past twenty pages? Either way, this was still an awesome read. Overall, 10/10 (rounded). Need I repeat it: this is just another GoFS banger.
