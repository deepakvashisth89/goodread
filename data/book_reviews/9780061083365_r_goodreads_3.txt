I received a copy of this book from eBook Discovery. I voluntarily review this book. This is my honest review. The book starts off with Shylo and her sister Cassie traveling to New York City in search of their mother. Shylo is obsessed with finding her mother because she wants an explanation of why she left and hopes that her mother will be overjoyed to see her and sister. While in New York she meets Dimitri and Ari. After finding out that her mother has married Dimitri's uncle they end up traveling to San Francisco together. Along the way there is a kidnapping, a robbery, a marriage and a reunion that doesn't go as planned. But in the end it all works out. I think this book was full of adventure and was well written. I love Ms. Ihle's inconvenient brides series. This is the second book I have read and I look forward to reading the next.
