Mindshadow seemed to play out like an implausible episode from the 1960’s t.v. show. There ARE a few Star Trek books out there that have contained so much depth, and were drafted so well, that I keep coming back. Regrettably, there are a good many like this one, with lazy love triangles between Kirk, pitifully-pale-legs-ending-in-two-bare-bony-feet McCoy, and some newly arrived mystery doctor.

I suppose the moral aspect this story attempts to explore is whether certain desirable ‘ends’ justify various amoral ‘means’, but I wasn’t pricked by the way it was presented here. After all, it’s all too often the fuel which entices so many mixed-up terrorists in their indiscriminate blastings and mayhem. The trope seems a literary favorite, but I usually don’t like it.
