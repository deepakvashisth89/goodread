I absolutely loved this book! I encourage any driven female who has ever had the strength to push herself beyond the expectations of others to read this! And, even still, any female who is looking to start pushing herself to find her place in this large world, should read this! It can be highly motivating.

The first few chapters of the book almost made me put it down in search for something else to read. After I made it past the first few, I could not put it down. While I have never been in actual sports, I am a very fit and enjoy the challenge of bringing myself to the next level. In many ways, this book spoke to me on similar circumstances. I'm glad Heywood chose to write it!
