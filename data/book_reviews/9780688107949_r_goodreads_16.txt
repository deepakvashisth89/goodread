This dark book explores both the most base and noble aspects of human nature. It tells the story of Donal Cam O'Sullivan's terrible march of escape after the disastrous battle of Kinsale in 1601/02. It is amazing that, in the face of complete despair and the betrayals of their countrymen, the survivors managed to retain enough hope to continue their trek.At times, the PoVs of different characters bleed into each other, which is consistently confusing. But the story is powerful enough to overcome this.
