Really enjoyed this one! It’s very clearly written and easy to understand even though I know nothing about submarines….just enough details are given, and not so many that the story gets bogged down. The courage, creativity, and persistence shown by the men rescued as well as those who risked all to save them (and the submarine itself) is truly inspiring. The book ended up being a sort of mini biography of Mr. Momsen as well, and rightly so - he is a true hero.CC: Just a few uses of strong language, but in context it didn’t seem overdone.
