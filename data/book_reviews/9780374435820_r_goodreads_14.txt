"A wealthy baroness owned a Clousseau painting called The Sleeping Boa Constrictor. One night, it awoke."What if you could paint something so realistic, it came alive? Enter Felix Clousseau, whose paintings bring strange new worlds and animals into France. What had once given him renown, had given chaos to the Hexagon. In return, his fame turned to infamy until he received a stroke of luck. With beautiful artwork that plays with what is real, what isn't, and what's in between, Agee's picture book offers a blurring of lines that will surely make you wonder about your own part in this world.
