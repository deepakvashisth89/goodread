I read this for my English Course and I really enjoyed it. It was very short, a simple adaptation. I had watched the movie a couple years ago and loved it. I liked the strength and determination on Jake's character, the need to win, the need to show everyone the truth. It made me feel nervous at some parts even though I already knew how it ended.I loved all of Jake's speeches, the way he talked and explained himself was incredible.Really good book, easy to read.
