If this were published today, no doubt it would be considered "fan fiction." Robert Asprin created Thieves' World and gathered several science fiction authors to writes stories involving certain characters. Each story is intertwined with the others through the characters. Some were well thought out while others focused on gore rather than plot line. I did note some profound points, however. In the Introduction, Asprin states, "Desperate people were dangerous; they were quick to turn predator at the smallest imagined opposition, which in turn made them vulnerable to the real predators drawn to the town like wolves to a sick animal." This is more pertinent today as it was in 1981. In "The Rhinoceros and the Unicorn," character Enas Yorl says, “The natural tendency of things is towards disorder, my dear. Destruction is easy, as you know. Restoration takes more energy.” Certain people like to create chaos because it's easy. Cleaning up those messes takes hard, dedicated work.
