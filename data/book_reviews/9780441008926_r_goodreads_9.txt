The story is built upon real science as a good science fiction book should be but is then not afraid to leap ahead into a wildly imagined future. The main character as a damaged engram is original and works well, although it can be a bit frustrating the way he has little understanding and no control over the events unfolding. He can hardly be blamed though for as the book starts to accelerate in the second half, the grand scale of the events transpiring are mind boggling. That may be why it has won the Australian Ditmar Award for Best Novel of 2002.Although the ideas in the book could easily have filled a trilogy it is only the first part.
