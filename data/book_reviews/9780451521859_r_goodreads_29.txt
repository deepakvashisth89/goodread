“If I could but know his heart, everything would become easy.”

Sense and Sensibility follows the Dashwood sisters, Elinor (who is very prudent) and Marianne (who is very sensitive). It explores how each sister handles themselves as they go through the hardships of losing their father and their estate, and having their heart broken.

Although this work did not touch me as much as Pride and Prejudice, I think it felt a bit more modern and realistic. Especially in the way it depicts the economic hardships for women without independent wealth, and how marriages are shown more cynically and practically.

I should have known I would have enjoyed this tremendously it was such a delight to read! ❤️
