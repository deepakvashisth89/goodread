Classic Thomas fare. Great setup, double crosses and snags, and then the showdown in the lair. All this, and a weird alternate political history too.One odd thing was I bought the old paperback of this used and a person had written in pencil twice in my copy. Once to note “this is really boring shit!” in a chapter about a minor informant. Now, I totally disagree with that note because it was an entertaining character sketch that lent understanding to the extent of the CIA’s information gathering plus how imperfectly human it all is. The second note: “this is total crap!” was scrawled later in the book and was the morning after scene with maybe the dumbest description of a woman’s breasts I’ve read, so I forgive that one. But those notes felt like a precursor to the Goodreads reviewers who express themselves with such poetic gusto.
