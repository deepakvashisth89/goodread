Is there a negative three stars rating? If this was the last book on earth, and destroying it would mean that all future generations would be illiterate, I would DESTROY IT and save all those unborn children the senseless pain of attempting to take any point away from this author's vulgar display of hoity-toity "I know all about art and greek mythology, so I'm going to talk about it but mix it up just enough for you to buy that I know SO MUCH MORE ABOUT IT THEN YOU that YOU MUST BE STUPID. And it's sad! It's so sad because some of the sentences and thoughts are so beautiful; stunning! But all that literary genius in LOST IN THE FOG OF ENDLESS RUNNING-ON ABOUT NOTHING!

Yes, I'm yelling. This is one of those instances where yelling is warranted. Here is a list of things about this book that I hated:

1. It was written,

2. It had no chapters, so you were forced to just randomly choose a place to stop reading and hope you could remember the two to three hints of plot the author gave you in the last 100 pages,

3. It constantly repeated itself, so if you lost your place there was NO WAY to find it again,

4. It had no resolution, or possiblly, it had no resolution until the last 10 pages, which I didn't read because I was completely disgusted and mentally fatigued from the constant stuggle to find meaning, any meaning, in the rambling self-gratifying nonsense,

5. It had no point, other then to make the author feel superior to his readership, and to make me angry enough to intentionally stop reading with only 10 pages to go because I didn't want the author to have the satisfaction of making me read the WHOLE THING without ever making a point of any kind.

That is all.
