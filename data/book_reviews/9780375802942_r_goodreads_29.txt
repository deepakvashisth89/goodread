Junie B meets up with an old friend: Mrs. Gutzman, the lady who used to deliver snacks to her classroom back when she was in kindergarten. Well, now that Junie B. is a first grader, there are no more cookie deliveries, and Junie B. finds herself engaged in the longstanding debate of brought lunch versus bought lunch.

But meeting up with Mrs. Gutzman opens up a whole new opportunity for Junie B. when she gets the chance to be a lunchroom helper! Junie B. is determined to be the best lunch helper ever, but her first day on the job is full of scary big kids, and the overpowering smell of tuna casserole.

Junie B. is charming as ever as she describes the owlets and eaglets on her baby-birds lunchbox, and shows off the magic power of her plastic mitts at the dinner table. When things go wrong at lunchtime, Mrs. Gutzman seems to overreact to some pretty normal kid behavior, but the scene where things are made right is fun. It did feel a bit like others were solving Junie B.'s problems for her, though.

I read this with the Kiddo, who had fun with Junie B.'s speech patterns and her antics at home.
