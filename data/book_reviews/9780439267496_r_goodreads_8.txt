This book was about a young Japanese high school boy (named Dan) who is just always compared to his older brother named Brad where he is the perfect child and is exactly what their parents want. So Dan was like the bad child and never did any right, all starting with his long hair. Back in the 1970’s Asians in the Seattle area received a lot of racism, mostly from white people. In school, the only history they learned about was about “white” people history, no Asian history, no Black history, just “white” history. Dan noticed this pattern and wanted to know more about whom he was, what Asian history is out there? But the only people who knew about Asian history were the people who lived through those experiences.
