I listened to "Gulliver's Travels" solely on audiobook, which I hardly ever do (not that there's anything wrong with reading that way, I just personally prefer to annotate; I also pay attention better when I have the physical book in front of me)(I also only realized a week or so ago that this is the wrong version of the book, but I don't care enough to find the correct one on Goodreads). That being said, despite my less than full attention, I didn't enjoy this book as much as I had hoped to. There were parts that held my attention, and I appreciate Jonathan Swift's digs at his current government, but the longer the book went on, the less I cared. The final section was downright offensive even. I simply got tired of the nonsense, I think. I'm glad to have checked this classic off my list, but I won't be singing its praises.
