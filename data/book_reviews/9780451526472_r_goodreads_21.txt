I've been wanting to read this book for a long time because I like Stephen Crane. I found the book full of great descriptions and wisdom, but I struggled with him calling Henry "The youth" because it created emotional distance from him. This story is a bit repetitive as well.This book also contains one of the best short stories ever written: "The Open Boat". I love this story, and every time I read it, I love it even more. The other short stories in the book are also good, but "The Open Boat" is a must read five star story.
