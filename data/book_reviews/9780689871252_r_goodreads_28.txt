My rating system1 star- book was so bad I couldn’t even get into it (because why would anyone actually finish a one star or 2 star book? Life is too short)2 stars- Book was good enough I made it to the halfway point but the author did not keep me interested3 stars- I finished the book, but only because I wanted to find out what happened and was not necessarily enjoying the ride.4 stars- I finished and enjoyed the book.5 stars- I finished the book and really felt something. The book impacted my life in some real way, or changed my perspective and/or motivated me to do or change something in my life.The twists and turns!The character growth!The friendships!The bravery, the children being so smart and planning what clever scemes to survive!Every person they met along the way had an impact on me. Although the prose was that of a children’s book—relatively simple and straightforward—it is the way the author builds the story, relationships and characters that knocks this book out of the park.The violence in the beginning bothered me but I had a similar experience with Coraline—the creepy disturbing stuff made me realize later it was all part of what made an amazing story.Jenny was my favorite!! This is the kind of book that hooks kids on reading (if they can handle the violence in the beginning). I, for one, thinks it’s worth it and will read this with my kids around 11-12 years old. I M so impressed.
