I created a Goodreads account for the sole purpose of writing this review.
This book was AWFUL. I read it in one day as fast as I could just so it would be over. I groaned through the whole thing and at the end I threw it.

From the FIRST CHAPTER it's obvious what is happening and I said "oh so the husband did it then and he's back to do it again?". And then the following chapters are just melodramatic and stupid. And oh my god SO COINCIDENTAL! Ridiculously so that I found myself constantly rolling my eyes and asking myself, is this for real?
I found myself starting chapters and saying "and then this will happen..." And. It. Did. Every time. Jesus. Predictable, stupid, STUPID writing. And all the pieces just magically fall into place sooooooo coincidentally.
The day that the children are kidnapped again, an ex-lawyer JUST SO HAPPENS to be reading about the case of the first missing children. And he spots something no one else ever has! Wow!
A doctor with a past with the mother decides that he MUST come forward because he can help!!! And only He can get through to her... with "truth serum"....... Wow!
The only guy keeping her out of jail decides ON THAT DAT that he must visit her to extort her and catches her right as her children have been taken and she's "losing her mind"!!! Wow!!
What are the odds?!?!?!?!?!!?!!! :)))))))))))
Ridiculous. Ridiculous. RIDICULOUS.
Also WHY. Why does her ex-husband stalk her for YEARS and spend all this time watching her, over SEVEN YEARS, and decides to do it that year? Why???????
And why does her current husband not seem to care at all about his children?? Only his ridiculously cliched shocked and fragile wife?????? Jesus Christ.

I could cut this book up, make a jacket out of it's pages and then go stand in the rain and get pneumonia and I would still have a better time with it then than I did reading it.
