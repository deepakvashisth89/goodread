I read this before in paperback. It was just as good reading it on my Kindle. I liked this book because because the Hero is a different kind of hero. He was a drunk with back problems and a lot of pride. The author made him seem real, although why he couldn't guess that the heroine was a woman is beyond me. I liked his sidekick too. I love books with well developed secondary characters. The story had a lot of humor (sometimes too much). And at times the clan seemed a bit unrealistic in their inability to defend themselves. But all and all it is a good read. I give it 5 stars, but not the 5+ stars I reserve for my top tier books.
