I purchased a random lot of Harlequin Historicals off Etsy, and challenged myself to review each one as I go through them. This entry is a medieval romance set in 1399 during Henry Bolingbroke’s usurpation of King Richard II’s throne. Cool premise, poor execution. I got to page 50 and decided not to waste any more of my time. It was originally published in 1991, and I know the sensibilities toward the romance genre were quite different. So maybe in 1991 this was a winner. The FMC and MMC seemed to have one personality trait - asshole. Also kind of a bodice ripper…did they wear bodices in 1399? I’m not keen to read it at any rate. I guess my treasure trove of Etsy finds can’t all be winners.
