Charlie Parker is the accountant for her brother's private investigator business. But when he is gone on vacation and a demanding female client wants help this instant, Charlie takes on the case to find the woman's missing father. Little did Charlie know that this hunt would take her from New Mexico to the mountains of Arizona and back as people were killed, she was chased, and she followed clues to find the missing 84 year old man who was faascinated with prospecting.A interesting cozy with lots of twists, turns, and surprises. Very enjoyable.
