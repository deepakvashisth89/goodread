Great satire to a wide range of notions and devices of silent oppression such as religious organizations, late capitalism and patriarchal, one sided story telling. There is not a structure in narrative and it is not needed for the readers of intertextual chaos. I see before me a very much cleverly written, ahead of its time anti-metanarrative digest. I had to research a lot throughout the pages as I am not familiar with Noah’s ark on a literary level, seeing to it helped recognize the angles from which Winterson criticized the showmen that manufactured the truth throughout the “history”. Humor in this novel is revolutionary.
Salutations to Lyotard, be skeptical.
