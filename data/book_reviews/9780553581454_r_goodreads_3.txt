Not really my sort of book. I actually wonder when it was written given the evidence of heavy smoking by detectives and others, bigotry of the worse kind, misogyny and whatever you do, do not get pregnant when you are unwed. Even the local police boss is only there because the force could not find another position for her, given her indiscretions (financial) of the past.This a particularly long book by usual standards and whilst the storyline is good you could feel you are only reading about a dysfunctional Welsh family who are more mixed up than most not a crime novel. There is a suspicious death but only DCI McKenna believes is such. In the end the most likely suspects are the ones that committed the murder. You find this out after working your way through 95% of the book along with the reasons for the family's fall from grace. You also find out that many of the characters in the book are in fact very unsavory characters and unsavory Welsh characters at that. I have recommended my wife not to read this book or similar. She reads Rhys Dylan, DCI Evan Warlow books which she loves and her great grandmother's was a Davies so she believes mostly everything Welsh, other than Warlow's felons, is good.
