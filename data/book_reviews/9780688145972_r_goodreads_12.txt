PBThis is a beautifully illustrated book. The illustrations are its greatest appeal. The story is engaging though has a melancholy tone. Valerie and Cherubino's friendship is a brief one and too soon they separate but as Valerie is left with the sure sense she will see Cherubino again so we the reader are left with a sense of optimism and the hope of good things to come. I like the story but it is the illustrations the draw me back to the book again and again
