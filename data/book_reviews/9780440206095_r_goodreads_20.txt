The further we go back in time, the more creative license romance writers give themselves to indulge in every barbaric fantasy, it seems. Brenda Joyce’s The Conqueror is set during the Norman conquest of England, featuring Ceirde, the half-sister of Edwin and Morcar, two powerful Saxon lords rebelling against the Normans, and Roife de Warenne, a Norman lord, and one of William the Conqueror’s best fighters. Roife has come to claim Aelfgar, Edwin’s seat, and with it, the bride of Aelfgar, Cerise’s half-sister, Alice.The Conqueror is a fun and proper bodice-ripper. Halfway through reading, I thought this is how I expected reading Bertice Small’s Skye O’Malley would be like. In Joyce’s version, inspired in no small part by Small, even droit du seigneur makes a fateful appearance.The Conqueror doesn’t really have a plot beyond how to get the hero and heroine into vexing scenario #2536, but it sure was a lot of fun.
