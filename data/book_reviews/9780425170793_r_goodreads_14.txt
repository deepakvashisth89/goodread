OK, dare I say I enjoyed this! Straight up yes its still pretty terrible, but it's a solid story. The character of Weed is so very endearing, and we had Niles narration again, this time accompanied by Popeye, a bulge eyed dog.I laughed a lot, I liked how the story came together at the end, and I liked the disparate strands of characters we met throughout. It's much better than Book 1 in the series (not too difficult), not a great work of literature but a fun time, especially if you listen to the audiobook.
