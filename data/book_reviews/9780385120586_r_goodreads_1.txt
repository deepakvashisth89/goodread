A nuclear power plant is planned for congressman Ben Safford's quiet Ohio district. There's local opposition, of course, but it doesn't amount to much--until an AEC inspector is killed after a contentious meeting. Ben's fellow members of congress have their opinions of what he should do as the national networks discover the problem, especially after the charismatic expert Humphrey Kennison appears on the scene. Typically amusing writing style by the pair who also write under the pen name "Emma Lathen."
