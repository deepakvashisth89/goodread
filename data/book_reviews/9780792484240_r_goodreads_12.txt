This could be a five star book.The story is intriguing.The area it takes place is fascinating.The characters - in the field are realistic and credible.The action is very well done.So why only three stars? The love story - which is wholly unnecessary - is horrific. Every time we leave the field and go into the personal life of the hero - Scott Dixon - it is like getting on a plane, opening the air sickness bag, and seeing that it has already been used. To call it ham-fisted is to seriously insult ham-fisted authors. And it is tragic because it is not necessary, adds nothing to the story and is jarringly bad every time it comes up in the book.This could have been a great book - if Coyle had had a decent editor.
