SPOILER ALERT! DON'T READ UNLESS YOU HAVE READ THE BOOK

Eleven year old Andy has found himself in quite the situation. His mother and stepfather are dead and he has now ran away from his aunt because he thinks that she is evil. On top of this, these magical fairy people called Sheehogue are following him everywhere. His aunt told him that his father was alive, even though Andy has been told his whole life that his father died in the war. Andy runs to his father, Vinny. Vinny turns out to a bad father because he smoked and drank all the time, but Andy was determined to stay away from his aunt and tried to change his father’s ways. Later, he realizes Vinny makes promises he can’t keep and that he could never change, so Andy goes back to his aunt.

I finished this book because I wondered if Andy was going to go back to his father at the end. Through the whole time it seemed that Vinny was actually a loving father, but then later on you realize that even though he is loving, he can’t take care of a child.

I picked this book up because my brother read it when he was my age and he said it was interesting.

I don’t know who I would recommend this book to, I didn't really enjoy the book.
