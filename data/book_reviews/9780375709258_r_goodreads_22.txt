These stories are bite-sized marvels of sadness and loneliness. And I loved them. The endings, which I think tend to be the most problematic feature of a short story, were brilliant. The three stories I liked the most were "City Night", "Lunch with Harry", and 'The Break-up". The smallest details were breathtaking. The glove dropping in the puddle was so perfect in "Lunch with Harry"."The Feather in the Toque" was also a favorite. The bird flapping around. Is it a distraction or does it mean something? So much to think about. I intend to read more of Susan Minot's writing.
