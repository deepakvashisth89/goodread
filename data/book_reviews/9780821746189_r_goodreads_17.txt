Good read, kept me interested. The story is sort of a hate to love, marriage of convenience relationship. She stumbles into a House of Ill Repute, drugged, in the wrong place at the wrong time and ends up in bed with a customer. He can't forget the night of passion nor the fact that his money was stolen. To save his ranch he now has to marry the bastard daughter of one of the city's richest men. He realises she is the woman he was with in the bordello, she has no recollection. Together they try to make things work and each achieves their main aim in life.
