I spent the first half of this book marveling over Straley's language, metaphors, originality. I was in awe. The description of fear of flying combined with a collapsed canoe, the story of a dinner table run amok; they were exquisite. And I still feel that way. Unfortunately, at the end, the plot just unraveled. Things had to be explained with the after-the-end conversation. I'm going to read more because I am thoroughly hooked on his way of looking at the world, but this one fell apart for me at the end.
