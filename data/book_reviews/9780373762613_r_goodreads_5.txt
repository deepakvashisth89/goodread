4 Stars! ~ What a wonderful read. It was witty and fun, and the love story of Dan and Shannon was really special. I'll be looking for Ms. Broadrick's precursor to this, Lean, Mean and Lonesome; that tells the tale of Mandy and Rafe who are mentioned in this book.

If anyone has read Ms. Broadrick's Crenshaw of Texas series written four years later, perhaps you can let me know whether Dan is a relation. ... if so I might be tempted to seek out that series.
