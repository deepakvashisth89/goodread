A short, but appropriate children's biography of Pocahontas. It is notably focused on the friendship between John Smith and the young Pocahontas, as said in the notes used information mainly from Smith's own accounts. There is much intrigue going on the uneasy relationships between the tribe and the colonists. I didn't like how the ending was rushed in Pocahontas' capture, marriage and travels to England in just 2-3 pages. It's a little dry, but informative about the short life of this famous Native American woman.
