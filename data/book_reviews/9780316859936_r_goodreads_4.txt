Grant seemed to pack quite a bit into this book. And one of my main reactions was to find it very interesting. I felt there was a good amount of detail and introduction to people and places I haven't encountered before - I felt I was learning as I read. We had professions, families, pasts both near and distant, cultural differences, relationships. However the central thread of the story itself didn't really hold me, which is probably a weakness in a novel.

I didn't enjoy this as much as her 'The Clothes on their Backs', while it clearly shared similarities, but I'd still be keen to try her other books.
