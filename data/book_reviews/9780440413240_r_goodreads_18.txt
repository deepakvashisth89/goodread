I first read this when I was a child, and recall that I really enjoyed it. Well, reading it as an adult was just the same… I loved it. I think if anything, I found it much more of an emotional read, this time round. Maybe due to a child’s naivety? As adults we are aware that there isn’t always a happy ending, and because of this , I was concerned about the ending(as couldn’t remember it).
A lovely story about the bonding of animals and people. This vegan lady sure does love animals so I guess this was always going to be a winner for me.
