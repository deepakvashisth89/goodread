First off, I mean, with that title, how could I NOT want to read it???? Somehow, this was written in the 90's. But the comedy of it still fits in many ways. Because, politicians.... Enough said. Horny single guy running for president (but doesn't sound like he's qualified) has an affair with a naive, simple senator's shrewd wife. Not wanting it to end, he chooses him for his VP. This book isn't anything great, but not horrible either. It's a humorous little story. With likeable villains and a stupid man who maybe isn't. Or is. I suppose, it depends on how you look at it.
