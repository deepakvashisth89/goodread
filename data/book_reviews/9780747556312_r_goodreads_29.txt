Marvin is feeling gloomy, because he's too small to run fast and jump high like everyone else. So he eats more... and gets bigger...until he's really big and can't stop eating. He eats the whole forest, the mountains, and an entire country. But when he's alone on the moon after eating the whole world, he gets lonely and feels stick to his stomach. After chucking up the world, he learns to like himself just the way he is.
