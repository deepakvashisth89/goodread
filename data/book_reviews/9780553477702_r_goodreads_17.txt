(2.5 stars)

I picked up this book as a light, weekend read. While it has all the makings of a light read — predictable plot, unrealistic storyline, cheesy romances, wholesome relationships — I never could get into the writing style. It lacked depth, and that's saying something, considering this book is 500+ pages.
On the whole, it was an easy read, but I'm sure there are better easy reads to pick from.

P.S.: The title is a tad misleading, since the 'wedding' only covers like 20% of the entire book?
