In Riptide, the second installment in the Florida Panhandle Mystery series, this tale in the Florida Keys is so captivating and twisted like a riptide in the ocean. It all started when a diver was killed on the dock and an old lady was knocked unconscious at the same. That's when Isabel Anders receives a phone call that her Aunt Merriam was in the hospital and in a coma. She didn't have a real good family connection with her. So she flies down to Cape St. Elmo in the Florida Keys. Her aunt's place was in bad shape. While she tends to her aunt's needs, she gets down to the root of the mystery of who knocked her aunt down and what happened to her father years ago when he disappeared from her. When her ex-boyfriend from the past, Harry Mercer, resurfaced in the past, that's when she connects both mysteries that her family have been all caught up into a tangled mess. The closer to Isabel closing in on the truth of the buried treasure, the more in peril she would be, when she would discover how sinister evil can be deeply rooted below the surface.
