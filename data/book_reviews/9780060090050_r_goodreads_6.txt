A pretty powerful and sometimes pretty graphic (meaning detailed, disturbing at times) novel written by Mikaelson, who was told this story by a survivor of the Guatemalan genocide that even this summer will be dealt with in the Guatemalan court. The details came from the girl who managed to make it, as a teenager, from her village in Guatemala, to the U.S. Some images will stay with me, but one in particular connects to the title. Gabriela is a tree climber, which is what she does as the Guatemalan army comes in and slaughters her village, her family, friends, all housing. She watches this from a tree, thankful no one ever looks up. Like all survivor stories, it is almost unbelievable how she manages to escape Guatemala, and then survive the escape to the north. Hard to read at times, but beautifully written, too.My third reading of this book, first read in August 2012.
