I would give this one 2 1/2 **

I'm happy this one was a freebee because had I spent actual money for this I would have been disappointed, now all I wasted was my time.

Sherry (h) was the girl from the wrong side of the tracks, her father as an alcoholic who abused both her and her mother. She couldn't believe it when the richest and most popular boy in school was interested in her. When he said he wanted her she thought why not, she loved him and surely he loved her. So she gave him her virginity. When she found herself pregnant and that he didn't really love her, she ran.

J.J. (H) had everything he could ever have wanted in life, except two loving and devoted parents. His parents were more interested in running the family business and running his life, to them, appearances were everything. He knew his mother didn't approve of Sherry but he didn't really care, he wanted her. He just wasn't sure what else he wanted or where his future lied. One nigh he was a bit of an ass to Sherry and the next thing he knows is that she's gone.

Fast forward 15 years and Sherry is back in town, the daughter that she gave up for adoption has sought her out and now what's to meet her birth father. A child he never knew existed.

This book had a few errors that pulled me out of the story, but it was a mediocre story anyway. For those who can't stand second chance stories where the h never moves on, no sex with any other man, then this one isn't for you. Yup, she had sex at 17 and just new he was the only one for her and 15 years later she still feels that way. Not buying it.lol
