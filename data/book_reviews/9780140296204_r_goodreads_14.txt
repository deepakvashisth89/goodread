Believe it or not I enjoyed this even more than "South!". What a slog those fellows had; especially those three that had to put up with the arrogant, two-faced sneak Dr. Marshall-even in that class riven society the man stands out as an utter sh*t.
Note: My opinions about Marshall are based diaries and other sources all published long after this book, "Warts and all" expedition accounts were definitely not a "thing" in 1909. Which is not to say this account is by any means insipid or inaccurate, it is, rather, both thrilling and moving.
