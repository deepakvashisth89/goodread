This mystery is foundational to the character of Bony, the half aborigine Detective-Inspector. Upfield's amazing detective is a product of will, a Jackie Robinson like determination to succeed in an environment where he is surrounded by jealousy and its chief symptom, prejudice. This novel in particular delves into the psyche of this unassuming yet deeply charismatic man who takes command by being the best man in the room (or in the outback).No Footprints in the Bush was a fairly early volume in the series and one gets the feeling that Bony was fully formed in the mind of his creator and is simply being revealed in increasing complexity rather than evolving from book to book. All that said this is what it was meant to be, a fabulous mystery, page turning action, full of insight into aboriginal culture as part of the greater human culture with with its motives for murder that cunningly span all subcultures and all boundaries of human history.
