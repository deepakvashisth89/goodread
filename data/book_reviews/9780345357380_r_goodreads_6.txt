I've finished the first book in this Mobile Suit Gundam trilogy, and so far, I like it a lot! It indeed does feel like a more adult retelling of the classic and reliable Mobile Suit Gundam tale from 1979, without getting too obnoxiously dark. I'm especially fond of the frequent and detailed combat throughout the story, interwoven with a good amount of galactic politics/backstory, and even some personal drama (especially involving Newtypes).

With the grittiness of the One Year War, combined with the heavy emphasis on tactical battle, this makes me think of classic Tom Clancy, if he wrote Mobile Suit Gundam. This isn't just a treat for those who like Gundam, but for anyone that likes military fiction in general.

It still felt a bit weird to me that the notorious Char is called Sha in this book, but at least he hasn't betrayed anyone. :P
