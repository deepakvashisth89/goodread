Re-Read 2025
Yes, I had to read it again when I saw it at my local library. I have read a lot of suspense thrillers in my lifetime, but Mary Higgins Clark's debut into the genre will always be the standard by which I live. The story of Nancy Harmon, who finds her second life disrupted when her children, Michael and Missy, go missing. When the local police discover that Nancy was once charged with the drowning murders of her children, Peter and Lisa, but a missing witness sets her free, they ask, " Nancy, where are the children?

All these years later, I still believe this is one of my absolute favourite suspense thrillers ever. I think I was about 13 or 14 when my mother went on a Mary Higgins Clark buying binge, and this was the second of MHC's books that I read.

The story of a young mother who has the unthinkable happen... her children go missing, but as the police begin their investigation, they realize it isn't the first time.

Let's face it, your children going missing is any parent's nightmare. I always thought Mary Higgins Clark was brilliant in planting little seeds of doubt in the reader, while at the same time giving us so many different character perspectives, including the perpetrator. All I know is that no matter how many times I have read this book, I always hold my breath until we find Michael and Missy.
