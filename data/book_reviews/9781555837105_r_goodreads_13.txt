I am loving the Dave Brandstetter mysteries right now. This is the third of three titles that are being re-released in January, 2022. After this, it's one title a month through October. Joseph Hansen's Dave Brandstetter is a wonderful character—a gay man in the 1960s and 1970s (when the books were originally published) who is genuinely comfortable with his identity, despite the stereotypes and derision he encounters on a daily basis.Of the three mysteries I've read thus far, Troublemaker is the only one in which I've been able to confidently guess who the killer was before the big reveal. Nonetheless, the plot is complex, with plenty of interesting characters with a variety of motivations: the architect with broken legs who's currently bed-bound, his new drifter/lover, his sister and niece, a pair of bar owners, a sleezey lawyer and the drifter's ex-wife he's working for/taking advantage of, a recently reunited husband and wife, and a whole stable full of rescue horses. The mystery takes place as a group of gay bars are putting on their annual Mr. Marvelous competition, which makes for a enjoyable side story.This really is a series to look out for—both for its value as a breakthrough in detective fiction, but even more so because Hansen is a gifted writer whose plots and prose are consistently engrossing.I received a free electronic review copy of this title from the publisher via Edelweiss+; the opinions are my own.
