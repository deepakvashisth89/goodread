Had so much pleasure reading this one!

FMC traveled for the first time to Bolivia to visit her aristocratic reserved friend. Only when she arrived, her friend was abroad and since FMC doesn't know a word of Spanish, she managed to get robbed, mistaken for a prostitute and finally jailed.

Since dire circumstances need dire solutions, she had to call her best friend's older brother to rescue her. MMC who's also her ex-boyfriend that broke up with her four years ago horribly.

The enemies to lovers x second chance romance were vibing so much!
The writing is better than a lot of HQ novels. And the book seems modern despite being written in the 90s.

It was also my great surprise that the book was set in Bolivia and thus I checked the Southern America square in my Bingo Challenge as well!
