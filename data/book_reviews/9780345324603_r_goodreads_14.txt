This was a pretty good military sci-fi story, but the authors have an annoying habit of dumping you into the action without context and explaining what you read after the fact. The result is I often had no idea what was happening. I'm taking off a star for this. They also invented their own curse word for the world. This is a clever way to avoid cursing for real, however, they used it so much, it started getting on my nerves.3.4 out of 5.
