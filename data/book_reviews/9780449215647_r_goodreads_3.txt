A tale that sees married 24 year old Vanessa March espies her husband in a newsreel from Austria (he said he was working in Stockholm) alongside a beautiful young women at a circus fire, where an elephant run loose for awhile! Her nephew, Timothy, a 17 year old supposed mummy's boy, wants to go to Austria to reconnect with his father he hasn't seen in many years. The pair end up going to Austria together, and end up getting caught up in an adventure centred around the circus and Lipizzan horses used by the famous Spanish Riding School in Vienna.

(Lady) Mary Stewart well known in her day for more rounded and intelligent female heroines dropped this wonderful adventure on me out of nowhere, and got me reading this entire book in day. A cracking 1960's take set in 'old' European Austria with a surprisingly assertive and multi dimesional heroine that carries this book with her juggling the line of what is deemed ladylike behaviour and being a smart pragmatic modern woman. The story is weakened when the heroine's husband gets a more prominent role towards the end of the book. 6 out of 12.
