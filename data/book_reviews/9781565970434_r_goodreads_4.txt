The h’s the personal Butler to an aged wealthy man located in Houston, TX. His adult grandson/H comes into town concerned with the older gentleman’s health & rumors of his mishandled financial affairs.

This is an older contemporary romance novel. I hadn’t read it before but I sometimes enjoy a simple romance read between my other genres. There’s a small mystery wrapped into the plot but not much. It’s pretty basic “guy meets girl” with a few sweet moments and a HEA - It’s not a terrific read, but totally okay. I may actually reread it again at some point. I found it on Amazon at a greatly reduced price.
