This a good story, on par with the strength of previous books in the series. I really liked seeing Nita save the day this time. Her story arc from the previous book had a good ending here. And we see more of Kit's family and Kit needing a helping hand.The only problem is Darryl, the autistic wizard. I would love to read the updated/rewritten version of this book just to see Darryl treated more like a... a person. The way that autism is described in this original version of the story is definitely troubling. I didn't think it was terrible, but if there's any book in this series that should have been rewritten - it's this one. I'm glad Duane did!
