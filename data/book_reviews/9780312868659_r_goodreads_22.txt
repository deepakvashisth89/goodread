What if Arthur C. Clarke had told 2001 from the POV of the computer? This book is your answer. It tells the story that Clarke left underdone in his famous book. Although the alien message plot line seems out of place; it just sorta hangs there and has an obtuse impact on the story. And then there’s the brain-scan bit. I still wonder if it could’ve had a clearer purpose. There could’ve been a Colombo-style murder mystery here, but the extra plotlines needlessly pull the story in other directions instead of focusing it. Really, the whole thing needed to be focused on the murder to give those plots a chance to come together. So, even Mr. Sawyer’s version of 2001 feels underdone, but in spite of that I sympathized for why the computer committed murder. Any book that can make you see justification for murder has got to be worth it. On top of all that, it’s from the computer’s point of view. That in and of itself deserves an Aurora.
