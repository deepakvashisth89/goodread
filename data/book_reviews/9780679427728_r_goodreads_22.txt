The best rock bio I’ve ever read. Love the way the author was able to seamlessly incorporate interviews with almost the entire Neil clan, research, first hand experiences, and of course interviews with Shakey himself. I had to roll my eyes at some of the author’s opinions (come on, the first two CSN/CSNY records absolutely rule!!) but really appreciated a writer that wasnt afraid to say what records he thought were crap, and which were masterpieces. Definitely a Neil Young fan for life now!!!
