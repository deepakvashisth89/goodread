I admit that re-reading all these books in chronological order has really revealed how confusing the moral core of these books is. Just in the last book Ozma put a literal kitten on trial and subsequently ordered said kitten's execution - but this book, Ozma refuses to fight what seems to be an objectively evil invading army, because it would be very wrong to harm anyone for any reason. Meanwhile, Dorothy ends up in a land where the people are made of baked goods and before leaving she angrily tells them that they're very selfish not to let her eat some of their children because she is, after all, extremely hungry. It's rather mixed messaging.I mean to be clear, I'm enjoying this re-read, but as an adult reader I have... questions.
