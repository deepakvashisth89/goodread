Story goes back to the Saul and Drew characters from THE BROTHERHOOD OF THE ROSE and THE FRATERNITY OF THE STONE.In this tale both characters are drawn into another mystery when a large number of old men go missing. All appear to have been former Nazis.I wouldn't say this one is as good as the other two, a slight notch down but not enough to dock it a star. To me, a straight three star means average.Good pacing, fast read, likeable characters and some interesting fight scenes and thriller locations.
