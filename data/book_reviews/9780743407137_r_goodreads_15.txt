Ugh. I think my edition is from a book club (blank back cover) and is full of weird typos (i.e. "ban" Helsing versus van Helsing - I mean, seriously??)

Tries to be smart alecky noir but falls completely flat. The disturbing physically abusive relationshiop between the MC and his police captain brother is a) unnecessary and b) extremely off-putting.

For a brief book, it took seemingly forever to finish . . . never a good sign.

Love that the author is attempting to honor Lugosi, but this has seriously squat to do with anything truly vampiric.

Cannot recommend.
