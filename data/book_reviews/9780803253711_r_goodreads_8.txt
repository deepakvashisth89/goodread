I had to read this play for my literature course at university and unfortunately I didn't enjoy it.I'm generally not someone who really enjoys reading plays - But I found this one really awful. I regularly counted how many more pages I had to read to finally finish this piece and although I was getting closer and closer to the end, it felt like it was progressing slower and slower.The characters are 99% unapproachable, albeit “witty”. They seem despicable rather than interesting and there is simply a lack of warmth.What bothered me incredibly about this play is the way women were portrayed. As a woman, some passages felt very uncomfortable. "The Country Wife" seems to disdain women and that is one reason why I will never willingly read any of Wycherley's work again.
