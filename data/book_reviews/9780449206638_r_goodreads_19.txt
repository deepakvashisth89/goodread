This has always been one of my favorite Mrs. Pollifax books. She goes to Switzerland to investigate plutonium thefts, and meets several interesting people - who will show up later in the series. Though other professionals are also on the case, her best helpers turn out to be a ten-year-old boy and a charming cat burglar. Gilman was a writer capable of creating memorable charactersanddevising fascinating plots that keep the story moving. Add in exotic settings and clean stories, and you have a great series that appeals to a wide variety of readers and can be read and reread. I love the audiobooks, too!
