Neill, who illustrated a great chunk of the Oz books, tries his hand at writing one.It's fun, and imaginative, but it's also a mess.Neill creates some memorable and likable characters, especially the two leads, there are a ton of clever ideas and solid dialogue, but Neill seems to have no idea what to do with any of it.Not that most Oz books have intricate or dense plots, but this one is paper thin and just stumbles along and Neill doesn't ever actually resolve any of the plot threads he introduces.They all just kind of peter out to a vague, unsatisfying ending.The art is very pretty and I'd love to see more adventures of Bucky and Davy Jones, just with a stronger writer.
