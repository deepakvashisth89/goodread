Great read. Very easy to conceptualize. I am a therapist, and my only complaint is that there are a few references to God and the Bible and that makes me not want to recommend this book to clients of mine who are atheist or agnostic. It’s not shoved down your throat, but it’s enough to give me pause. I lied, one more complaint is that there’s an emphasis on”self directed” treatment and I’m just not a big fan of the idea of someone treating themselves in general, especially with such a severe condition like OCD. Other than those two things, very insightful and helpful book.
