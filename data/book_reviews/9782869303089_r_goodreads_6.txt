Another derailment on life's railway to heaven.I'm not quite sure how it happened, but I'm a little gone on Sailor & Luna and their doomed love affair.I didn't see it coming, cruising through the cartoonish, hyper-bold dialogue a little bemused and at times underwhelmed. But here I am closing the book 100% charmed and missing them both.I enjoyed the verve and humor, the Tarantino-esque dialogue, the small chapter format, the soul soundtrack, but most of all I love Luna and Sailor's big beating hearts, rat-a-tat-tating in harmony.Good stuff, looking forward to more B. Gifford.
