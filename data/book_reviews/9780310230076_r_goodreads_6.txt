This was a good book. Lots of stories and scriptures really helped the book move along quickly (I read it in one sitting). I was looking for a book that helped define faith Biblically and this book did that while provoking my own faith. I really like what he had to say about unbelief and how the enemy tries to steal away our faith through unbelief, complaining, and seeing our circumstances as larger than God. John 10:10 is the answer and Life to the Full as promised by Jesus is a life of Faith!
