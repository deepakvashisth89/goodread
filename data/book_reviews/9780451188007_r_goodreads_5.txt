An entertaining tale, highly improbable but a lot of fun because the h wasn't standing any nonsense from the H, albeit with extremely bad manners. I would advise Serena Millbanks to go for the throat in future, a much less bloody punch & even more effective. I found the reason for Guy Hawkhurst being in his cups all the time rather risible - not because his brother was dead but the way he died, completely unbelievable. Monroyal comes across better in this one than he has any right to, & I wonder what happened to David Laughton & Tony Dalton in the end - I can't remember if they got their own books, certainly David deserved a happy ending. 3.5★
