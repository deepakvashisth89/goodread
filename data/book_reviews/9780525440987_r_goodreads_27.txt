An absolute amazing story that i doubted from the beginning. Interstellar Pig presented itself as a child’s book to my eyes, but was far from. The book reminded me of the movie “Zathura” if that rings a bell for anyone. I wonder if this book was the referenced material for that movie. I would absolutely recommend this as a light, but interesting read for those who like Sci-Fi with a little bit of horror! I accompanied this story with Alien ASMR videos on youtube in the end portion of the story, and the band Garbage earlier in story and they matched well. Cant wait to read the next installment “Parasite Pig”
