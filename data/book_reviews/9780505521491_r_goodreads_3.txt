I received a copy of this book from ebook discovery in exchange for an honest reviewThis is a lovely story of second chances and 'completing circles' if you want to understand that remark you need to read this book. Both the main characters have gone through heartache and loss, making them reluctant to risk opening themselves to further pain.Having tried out the 'special switch' his partner put in the prototype aircraft Colin finds himself over 50 years in the past but still to late. In trying to help Liesl keep her dead husbands dream alive will he be able to get back home before the window in time closes. How this is resolved makes for a wonderful story
