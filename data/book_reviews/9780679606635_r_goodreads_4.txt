I'm not sure how you review a book of stories in translation from a language you can't read by an author already recognized as one of the world's best. You can't explain why they're good stories, since presumably everyone already knows that, and you can't say whether the translations were good, since you don't know. What I learned: every class of person is corrupt, and most people kid themselves with self-justifications about it. The only happiness in the world is taking care of orphaned children. It makes me want to reread his plays and learn more about him, even though every single story in the volume was sad and bleak.
