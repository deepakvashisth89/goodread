This was a busy little story that had so much happening it was a job to keep up. I liked the characters especially the two leads the intensity between them was well written. The supporting cast were were obviously nasty people only out for themselves.

Although this was quite passionate and emotional, I was left feeling like I’d been cheated out of a proper ending, overall this was okay but it needed fleshing out more as there were a couple of things left unanswered, but of course the author had to adhere to the publishers page count, so maybe she should have not tried to have so many twists and turns in this.
