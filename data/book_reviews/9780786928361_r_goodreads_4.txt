I haven't started playing my D20 Modern (Urban Arcana) game yet, but I found this book well laid-out and easy enough to use when rolling up my character. Personally, I'm not as excited by a modern RPG setting as I am by traditional D&D, but I'm pretty sure nostalgia is the culprit here. The group I'll be playing with is very experienced and creative, so I'm fully expecting to love this campaign. As I said in my review of the fifth editionPlayer's Handbook, what matters are the players, not the rules. So I give this one four stars for now.
