Sisters Betty, Emily and Annie Ruth Lovejoy come together to bury their mother. Their mourning includes a trip down memory lane and many pent up feelings.What I found interesting is the mother's POV from the afterlife. At times it made me laugh and others, it gave me a clearer understanding of the effect Esther had on her family.If I had to choose a favorite character, it would be Betty. She stepped up to the plate for everyone in her family at a tender age, and continued to do so throughout their lives. Now, my least favorite character definitely goes to Esther. You'll have to read the book to understand why.
