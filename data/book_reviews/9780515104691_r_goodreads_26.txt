This one was just flat for me. Very well-researched, but incredibly slow-paced, and torn between being a mystery and being a romance. (I felt like I was reading the backstory for her mystery series sleuth, actually.) The actual plotline is frequently bogged down for pages on how the heroine concocts her balms and unguents, or how the hero takes off his chain mail. There doesn't seem to be much actual organic conflict within or around the romance, and even the outside sources of conflict (oh no, will anyone spot them sneaking around?) are so methodically dealt with that they almost don't register. Things that you might expect to cause conflict - her husband is brutally murdered! he is the investigating officer! - really (no one seems to think she might be the murderer for more than five minutes). By the end I was really slogging. It's all about the history here.
