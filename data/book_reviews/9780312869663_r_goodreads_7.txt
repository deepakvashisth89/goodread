Another great novel in the Catherine LeVendeur series. After Catherine's sister decides to marry a man she has never met the family is summoned because Agnes' new husband has been found dead and Agnes is being blamed for murdering him. Catherine, Edgar, Catherine's father and Catherine and Edgars children all go to Germany to try to save her.I like the way the author takes real history and puts in her story. I liked how she took the story of a Jewish girl who was beaten and left for dead and used it in her story except not the exact same.
