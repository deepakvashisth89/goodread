A Family Affair (The Rex Stout Library)A Family Affair (The Rex Stout Library)by Rex Stout5.0 out of 5 stars The last but NOT the least, August 17, 2013This review is from: A Family Affair (The Rex Stout Library)"A Family Affair" kept me riveted to my seat throughout. A favorite waiter from Rusterman's Restaurant comes to the Brownstone for refuge but from what and from who? Archie provides a room to stay in for the night. Then a bit later an explosion. When all is revealed a death in that room inside the Brownstone.This was indeed the best Nero Wolfe entry to date and sorry to say the last Nero Wolfe by Rex Stout. Please remember when you do read this marvelous story try not to compare all the other N.W. books to it. It is incomparable in my opinion.I listened to A Family Affair on CD narrated/performed brilliantly by Michael Pritchett.BTW...I've read and continue to read several Nero Wolfe entries by Robert Goldsborough who does an admirable job.
