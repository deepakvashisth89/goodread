“Well, let me tell you, gentlemen, the games of the devil are not restricted to those confined to hell. Others can play them.”Oh, I am so happy that I was able to dive intoThe Bourne Ultimatumthis week. These books are just so entertaining and nothing like the movies. Bourne hunting for the Jackal had me going on an interesting adventure. The pace was kind of wild and all over the place (which is saying something when you are listening to the audiobook). Other than that, I'm intrigued to see how the next book and the rest of the series will play out since a different author is stepping in.
