This book is a thematic study and anthology of world literature that discusses the major themes and provides multiple examples (from different cultures) in a clear and structured format. It is rich in content and packed with information. Sadly, it is rather limited in scope, since the author tends to stick with the more traditional hypotheses in the study of mythology. Nevertheless, one of its greatest strengths lies in it being religiously unbiased.I felt that the book's final chapter was rushed. For instance, the parts about forests, caves, and labyrinths were too scanty.
