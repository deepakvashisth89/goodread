This was entertaining (also a bit sad) but flawed (also a bit silly). That being said, I'll explain.It starts with the H and h falling for each other hard and fast, despite their age difference (she's only 18, he's in his 30's). The H is respectful of her innocence, but when he's faced with having to travel to a war zone country, they give in to desire and afterward he promises he'll return ASAP, and they'll discuss their future, even phoning her from the airport so he could hear her voice again before he leaves.That should have told her something, but her youth, insecurities, and the H's jealous bitch of a secretary who's in love with him (or as in love as someone like her can be) all work against things. The country he went to is in turmoil, communication is impossible, weeks go by, and the h discovers she's pregnant. She's naïve enough to trust the OW with a letter for the H, to be sent with other office correspondence, and even more naive (a.k.a. STUPID) to believe the phony telex, where the H claims it was fun, but just a fling after all, no hard feelings, okay?So, she has the baby all alone, and is lucky to have a friend who knows someone who needs a nanny, so she and her baby son are off to France for two years. (This is all offscreen, so to speak, as there are no scenes there, the book starts after she returns and is looking for a job to support herself and her now two-year-old son.)Unlike most h's in this kind of situation, she did at first intend to tell the H about the baby, and even showed up at his office after their son was born to tell him, but when she saw him kissing the OW, she ran off, without them seeing her.BTW: how many couples would give the job of nanny to a 19-year-old girl with no references, no prior experience with children, (other than learning to care for own baby), only the word of a friend that she needs a job? Doesn't seem too likely.But I digress! The h wasn't the only one conned by OW/Bitch! The H was seriously wounded and nearly lost his life and his arm! Luckily, he retained both, but his heart sure took a beating, as he waited for the h to visit him in the hospital when he was sent back home, but she never did. Apparently, she was told where he was but never bothered to show up. (Naturally, she had no idea because OW/B never informed her! Then she left for France.When their story picks up, the h gets a job as nanny to a wealthy man whose fashion model second wife supposedly ran off with a rock star, leaving him and their son, as well as two stepchildren. The h is immediately surrounded by male attention, as her employer takes a platonic liking to her (the man's hopelessly in love with his wayward wife), his cousin/employee flirts with her (but he, too is in love with Mrs. Unfaithful, though she never gave him the time of day) and the boss's 17-year-old son is instantly infatuated with her! Quite a bit to deal with!Then, as rich icing for that overstuffed cake, the H turns up, as he's a friend/business associate of the boss! They had met again recently, at a restaurant, where just seeing him made her leave in a panic, and he pursued a bit while she tried to freeze him out, and he couldn't understand why. (As far as he knew, he was the one who got dumped, and he took the supposed rejection a lot better than she did.) Part of the reason she took this job was to put distance between them, and now here he is! And so is their son, who he doesn't know about!
