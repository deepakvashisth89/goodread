This book displays why this series works so week for me (thus far): despite the core themes being those of cooperation and overcoming prejudice, the presence of some seriously dark content keeps it from become saccharine.

The optimism of the series (and of many characters) is not naive; rather, it is hard-won in the face of evils that might otherwise drive one to become bitter and cynical.

I love when optimism is presented as being the most difficult view to take.

And I continue to love The Thranx.
