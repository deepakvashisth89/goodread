This book was a sweet surprise, got me tight and I couldn't let it down. The storyline was very well written and wisely developed. The characters, oh, I loved the hero, Scott, he was a real hero and so kind, handsome, clever and sexy that I couldn't help, but to fall in love with him. The heroine, Alexa, a heartbroken lonely girl who is deeply depressed and do so many stupid things and is truly a child woman, but all in all her reaction and action were realistic and believable. It wasn't like any other second-chance romances I have read, but I like it even more for it wasn't a quick falling out of love for fist love and fast falling in love with the hero. I really loved this book and recommend it to all.
