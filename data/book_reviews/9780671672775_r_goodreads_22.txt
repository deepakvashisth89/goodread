This is my first experience with Douglas Clegg. This book is a slog despite its short length. The novel offers little in way themes but fails to deliver satisfying pulp. Most of the pages are marital issues and when the scares start up towards the back half they are unfocused. Witches? Sure. Demon babies? You got it captain. Ghosts? Why not.If you're a casual fan of pulp horror then maybe pass. If you're a die hard it's not terrible just a bit dull.
