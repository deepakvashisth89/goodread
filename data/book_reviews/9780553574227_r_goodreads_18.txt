All around terrible. Very unlikeable heroine, who we are repeatedly told is brave & compassionate. Actually she is self involved, stupid, & at times cowardly, worst of all when she humiliates & betrays Malcolm simply because she is too scared to be honest with him. Of course he continues to love her no matter what.Also there is zero historical accuracy. I don't need insanely detailed research from a book like this, but the entire plot depends on a Scotland that never existed, people who never behaved like these people, all with attitudes that ridiculously modern - they build a castle to be pretty & purely ornamental, they never needed to develop defenses because "everyone knows they're peaceful" even though they are very wealthy. Everyone can read & write & they have paper. They are clean, rarely sick, & bathe frequently.All this and Ariella (not a Medieval Scottish name even) actually endangers her clan by putting off finding a husband & male leader because, due to a vision from the clan seer, she'd rather sit and wait for Mr. Perfect to find her & save her. Her inactivity & stupidity repeatedly puts her entire clan in danger.Oh, and to top it off, the story is very slow, boring, and predictable.
