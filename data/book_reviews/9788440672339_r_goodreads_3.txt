Basically being a full version of Goosebumps’ “The Ice Vampire,” this Spooksville is one of the more action oriented adventures of the series, and gets pretty crazy for Spooksville standards. My immediate compliment is that the Sally and Cindy dynamic was toned down; there were books where they were almost unbearable, and here they didn’t argue to the point of frustration, and were actually pretty enjoyable. Glad to see they’re evolving, as I kinda hated them in 2-3. The actual Cold People (Low-Temp Folk, Frigid Fellas, whatever you wanna call them) have some crazy fucking lore that you just have to read for yourself to believe and even rewrite human history (within the book’s universe) if you choose to believe it. Absolutely bonkers lore, and they served as an interesting zombie-esc threat for this volume. There’s some awesome sequences like the whole final chapter and the face off partway through the book that uses some legitimately crazy things for a kids book that accumulated for a great time. The writing is good and some of Pike’s better work for this series, the stakes being higher here than usual was neat, and I enjoyed all the characters. Just a generally crazy and solid entry, with some setbacks. The action was a teeny bit overbearing as I am not the biggest fan of action in literature, so (for my personal account) I didn’t enjoy it at some points. There’s some padding in here like the police station scene and some stuff that could’ve been a tad bit shorter, but nothing too egregious. The climax is kind of a non-climax, the only threat being on dude and just feeling too linear in hindsight. The cold people are a little underused and are more cool as an idea than what we got, and they don’t show up enough to fight our characters to proclaim a high threat level. They’re everywhere in the book, but only fight the group four times total, a couple pretty short lived albeit good. But that’s a lot of nitpicks, so I’m not taking too much off. Overall, 8.5/10. Really good, my third fave of the series (not saying much when I’ve only read five though). Next Spooksville seems to be plot heavy instead of another monster of the week type deal, looking forward to it.
