Helen Keller (Paperback)by Stewart GraffHelen Keller book is about learning about the history how Helen Keller life was and how she changes the perception of disabilities. Helen Keller was a young girl blinded, and deafened by a high fever, Scarlet fever. She was unable to communicate, tell her needs, or even indicate what she wanted. It was her care give who found the magic hidden with in this remarkable child. When she learned to talk, she learned to behave. She became an advocate for children with disabilities and changed countless lives.
