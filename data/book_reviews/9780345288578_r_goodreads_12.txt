Prior to reading Playback I noticed a few negative reviews, some seemed to regard Playback as evidence that Raymond Chandler had lost his mojo. He died within a year of finishing the book and his personal life was in turmoil by the late 1950s.

Despite some foreboding, I was blown away by this book. It's a much more world weary Marlowe in Playback, and lest we forget he was never a beacon of positivity.

is a more unusual case for Marlowe, indeed there isn't even really a case at all. However, Marlowe being Marlowe, he is not content until he has uncovered all the stones and put all the pieces of the puzzle together, if that's not mixing metaphors too much. It's a great story too - surprising and original.

Corruption, cynism, small town big shots, a manipulative woman or two, along with other familiar tropes, are all present and correct.

Magnificent and whilst it's not quite up there with or it's still a worthy finale.

4/5
