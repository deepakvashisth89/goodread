A very gracious 2 star. It was short, quick, and the action was well paced. However, it also felt like nothing happened at all until the final chapters. The main character Jefferson, is highly unlikeable, and it doesn’t really get better. Like he didn’t deserve the promotion he got, knows this, yet freaks out at least like 7 times when people question him in the slightest way. Also the romance in this book was horrible, just came out of nowhere, like their chemistry wasn’t there until both characters just happened to see the other one naked. It wasn’t horrible but the world building could have been done so much better as well as the characters. There are 6 books in the series, I’m willing to give the second a shot, so we’ll see.
