Not a bad read. There were questions about the author being who he said he was regarding another one his books (The Specialist). I knew a quite a few Vietnam Vets from my service days in the early 80's. For those who read it, they were mostly split on the story. They pointed out a few errors that shouldn't have happened since the group were professional soldiers. None of them gave it a glowing recommendation but most enjoyed the story. It was a good read to pass the time while I was deployed. That said, most of my military friends (who did not serve in Vietnam) who read it, loved it.
