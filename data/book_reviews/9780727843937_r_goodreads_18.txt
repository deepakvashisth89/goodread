I remember watching and loving the 1991 film adaptation as a teenager, so when they announced the TV series, I knew I had to also give the book a try. It took me a hot minute, but I finally made some time for it.

I gotta say that the movie was A LOT scarier. As I was reading this I was like "that's it??", there was zero tension. At no point did I fear for the family's life. I mean, okay, I knew what was gonna happen, but still.

Caldy was kind of absent for most of the book, just popping in and out occasionally to remind us he's still there. I feel like he was a more constant presence in the movie? Do correct me if I'm wrong.

The ending was different, and even anticlimactic. I was expecting it to finish with a bang, but the build up just wasn't there.

I don't know if the series will live up to the movie(s), but I'm sure it's better than the book.
