Alternative history is not easy to write, so kudos to any author that seriously attempts it, and especially on a subject as brutal and difficult as Operation Coronet. I will say some of the history is dated, and I felt a bit underwhelmed about the author's telling of the Kyushu invasion for Operation Olympic, but it was a good try.There was a lot left to be desired, a very unnecessary scene towards the end, and I didn't find any of the characters to be particularly intriguing. The scale of the fighting, I think, was done pretty well, and the savagery of the fighting was appropriate.
