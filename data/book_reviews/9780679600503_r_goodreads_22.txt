October 15, 2023: Experienced this incredible bookagaindriving across New Mexico. This time, we listened to it, rather than to me trying to read it, so I know I got more out of it. Wedged between the chapters of the story were a visit to beautifully preservedSan Xavier Del Bacin Tucson, Holy Mass at historicSanta Cruz (Holy Cross) Catholic Churchin Española, New Mexico and finally, our pilgrimage destination nearby, world-famousEl Santuario De Chimayo, or the Holy Sanctuary of Chimayo, the shrine of the "Holy Dirt".These places were mentioned in passing, as well as so many others, but wonderful as they are, they were almost secondary to the 'characters' of the rough landscape, kindly gentle souls, rich smells, hardy plants and endless skies described in Cather's book. I could move to this part of New Mexico; it takes my breath away.More when time permits....June 28, 2006: My favorite by Cather; read this aloud when we did our family Grand Circle trip, especially the part in New Mexico. Such a gentle, quiet story. I know that my children were not touched by it as I am/was, but I'm still glad they know about it. It is a fictionalized account of the real life of the first archbishop of the western territory, a simple, saintly man who lived his faith without fuss or fanfare. The book is actually soothing to read, but I think it takes a certain maturity to fully appreciate it.
