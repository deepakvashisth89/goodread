Surprisingly relatable classics.Poignant family drama uncovering the dark side of an infamous American Dream and toxic upbringing. The characters are precise and recognisable archetypes of a 'father-son conflict' that you can still meet in modern family dramas. Male characters are great depiction of those loser men chasing success but unable to put their lives together, full of envy and living in past illusions.Great and still relatable psychological play about family and society.
