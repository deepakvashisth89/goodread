This was probably more of a 4.5 star for me. I really enjoyed this book. The h is a 25 year old single female who is perceived as a manhater. She is convinced tthat she doesn't want or need men in her life, she doesn't think she hates men, just doesn't need one. In fact she is afraid of men and relationships based on her childhood and on a violent sexual assault when she was 13. She has dealt with her childhood deprivations by building a successful business (exclusive dress shop) and has pretty much insulated herself from men.The H is a 36 year old successful male who is instantly attracted to the h. Since he is a Beta male he is the perfect man to break through her defenses with Gentle Persuasion. Even before he was aware of her prior assault he dealt with her resistance and panic with patience and sensitivity.This was a realistic and appropriate portrayal of an abuse victim and an appropriate response. I'm so tired of HP Alpha males who become angry and even verbally/physically abusive to h's who have issues regarding prior abuse.Gentle Persusasion was a wonderful romance especially if you like Beta Heroes.
