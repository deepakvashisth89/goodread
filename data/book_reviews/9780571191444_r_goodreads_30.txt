My first Golding, so can’t say if this is a decline from his early famous novels or not. I enjoyed it enormously though - beautifully written, interesting flawed 1st person narrator and great secondary characters, brilliantly realised. Interesting to compare this to the Patrick O’Brian novels: this is set in the same time period and similarly focusses on masculine relationships at sea. Golding’s language is richer and more densely literary. Personally I enjoyed the plot, but perhaps it feels a little dated and melodramatic - losing a 4th star from me for that.It’s amusing this years’ reading is taking on something of a seafaring theme.Booker Prize winner 1980.
