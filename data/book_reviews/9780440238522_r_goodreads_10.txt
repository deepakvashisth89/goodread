You know, I really liked and really disliked this book. I think that the author was striving so much for a dysfunctional family for this main girl, that it just wasn't enjoyable to read. I know, I know, it seems like I prefer fantasy over reality, and maybe I do, but still. Is it so bad to have a decent, happy ending when there is so much craziness? Robin is a decent, thriving character for the most part besides her daily emphasis on how fat she is, but she is surrounded by people that are barely getting by: Frankie (early twenties, drug and alcohol addicted, aspiring model/actor man who lives next door and likes to dabble in friends with benefits time with Robin and various other women), China (Frankie's overly sexualized girlfriend that while she has a nice personality, all is described is her boobs and is just completely overly sexualized), Robin's father (who is completely possessive of his third new, younger wife and doesn't seem to know how to keep himself happy), step-mother (heinous person with a boob job who messes around and is NOT a good example for young Robin), Tre (Robin's love interest who seems really awesome at first and probably is at the end but he is struggling with some major daddy/self-identity issues), Robin's mother (who is so obsessed over appearance and finding a man to marry that she makes Robin feel terrible about her weight and looks even though she probably does really love Robin and has some redeemable traits).
