Edward "Ted"Tubb was a very prolific British SF writer in the 195os and 60s. He wrote under a wide variety of pseudonyms and was a major figure in the British scene, though not so well known in the USA. He was so active in the field that on one occasion in order to help a struggling SF magazine he wrote the entire content of an edition - including the letters! He wrote mainly in the space opera style of SF and his magnum opus was the Dumarest Saga. In this, he describes a Milky Way galaxy mainly occupied by humanity, although there are a few alien races. A dispossessed Earl is trying to get back to the planet of his birth which he last saw as a child - Earth. Unfortunately, no-one seems to know where it is and most people regard it as a myth. In no less than 32 novels we follow his quest across the galaxy, all the time on the run from the sinister Cyclans, from whom he has stolen a galaxy-controlling secret. Tubb was very good at inventing alien planets and their cultures, and also at characterisation, and this book is no exception. In this story we meet a wide variety of players, including a beautiful but dangerous woman. Dumarest finds a record of a stellar spectrum that he believes to be of Earth's sun - and his quest continues. For another 17 novels!
