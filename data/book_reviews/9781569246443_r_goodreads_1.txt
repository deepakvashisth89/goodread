Vastu is a detailed primer on the Hindu based way of setting up a room/home/office to enhance the best energies of directional impact, while supplementing options when the best is unavailable. Cox brings in yoga and Ayurvedic principles as part of the way of life.The vast amount of information is somewhat over whelming at times, but the reader can take what they want and leave the rest for another day. Kathleen Cox is clearly a knowledgable and accessible writer!
