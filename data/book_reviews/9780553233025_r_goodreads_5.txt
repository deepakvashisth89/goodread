I like two of Michael Korda’s novels. My favorite is THE FORTUNE, which I have read 5+ times. WORLDLY GOODS is my second favorite; I think I’ve read it twice. I’ve got most or all of Korda’s other novels, but they didn’t really work for me, and I didn’t finish them. WORLDLY GOODS was part of my research for one of my novels which never got finished, and one novel which was finished [I love it, but most readers don’t much care for it—too much sex & violence (or maybe the wrong kind of sex & violence)]. If Korda was trying to top KANE AND ABEL by Jeffrey Archer, he failed; although there is a similarity to the two plots: financial titans at war financially with each other. But Korda’s WORLDLY gets points for weird.If you want to check out Michael Korda’s novels, I recommend THE FORTUNE. If you want more, go straight to WORLDLY GOODS. That’s my advice. @hg47
