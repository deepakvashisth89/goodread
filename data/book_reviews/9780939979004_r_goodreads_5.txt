In this story, Coopie and her Momma are walking to the market, and Coopie needed to use her imagination to keep warm. She asks her Momma, "If I were a moose, do you know what you would do?" Her Momma says that she, of course, would move into the woods so she could be with her. Each page, Coopie asks what her Momma would do if she were different animals or items and her Momma always says that she would be with her no matter what.This is a great reminder for children that their parents will always love them. There are a few things in particular that I think are spectacular about this book. I love that every illustration takes up the whole page. I also love the Foreword that the book includes. It says that parents should build up their children's self-esteem and make sure they are proud of who they are and what they can do. I love that this is included!!I would recommend every parent to read this book to their children.
