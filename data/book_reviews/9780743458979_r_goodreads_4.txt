A lovely and charming book. It has "A Sound of Thunder" and "The Fog Horn," two of my favorite Bradbury stories outside of those set on other planets. There are two other stories, "Besides a Dinosaur, Whatta Ya Wanna Be When You Grow Up," a charming coming of age story, and "Tyrannosaurus Rex," about a guy who works in the movies creating "monsters." There are also a couple of poems.In addition to the stories, the work is illustrated throughout with some "dino"-mite images. These are some of the most gorgeous black and white illustrations that I've seen, by the likes of David Wiesner, William Stout, Steranko, and Gahan Wilson.I really enjoyed this book as a whole experience.
