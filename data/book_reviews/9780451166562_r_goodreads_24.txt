Humorous and dark. Sooo entertaining how it all escalates, slowly but definitely surely. It builds and I was expecting a big climax which I got, so that was satisfying. It gets a bit repetitive in structure, the whole tit-for-tat thing, but I enjoyed Ann’s POVs to mix it up. The part where it is revealed that Barbara just hates Jonathon is amazing. ‘Yes, she wanted him to die’ was so simple and effective. The mutual descent into despondent and murderous chaos is delicious. And the ending with the children continuing the battle over the wealthy possessions…chilling. Must watch the film!
