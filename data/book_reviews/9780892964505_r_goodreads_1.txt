Essays, short humor pieces, and cartoons loosely about crime. Thurber's humor is mostly gentle and absurd; I liked his noir parodies quite a lot, though some of the broader pieces don't really stand the test of time. I wish the editors of this collection had marked clearly which pieces were nonfiction; often it was clear (probably the ones featuring murderous animals are not nonfic!) but not always, and as Thurber himself recognized, real crime is often absurdist.
