A collection of diverse short stories from outstanding authors. The theme of the book is "leaving home".All the stories were good and well worth reading. My favorite was "On A Rainy River" by Tim O'Brien. It really stood out in the collection - such a quiet and touching story.This book was in the "Young Adult" section of my library. I read it as an adult and I highly recommend it for adults, as well as, young adults. It's great for readers who enjoy diverse short stories.
