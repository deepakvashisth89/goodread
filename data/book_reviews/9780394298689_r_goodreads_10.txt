One of the more interesting stories included in the Skeleton Crew anthology. It's about a wild pretty lady named Mrs. Todd that loves taking unusual shortcuts in her car, including pathways through the woods to reach her destinations at record times. She lives around Castle Rock and makes frequent drives through Derry. Both are creepy places where bad supernatural stuff tends to happen. The shortcuts that Mrs. Todd takes seem to be these nightmarish wormholes that defy time, space and reality, odd creatures and bizarre paranormal phenomena lurking just outside the speeding car whenever she's on one of her thrill rides.The story is written from the perspective of someone that once took a ride with her on one of her shortcuts and it was so enthralling that it terrified him to his core and made him fall in love with the intense woman at the same time.It's weird, romantic and creepy all at once. It has some subtle connections to Cujo, The Dark Half and IT.
