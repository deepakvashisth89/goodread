I've read better devotional books but this one was still very good. I read one devotion each evening before bed and each one gave me something to reflect on in my prayer time with our Lord and as I drifted off to sleep. They were short and easy to read and many of them were cute or humorous but very meaningful if you took the time to think about the story. I would definitely recommend this book to anyone who's looking for a good devotional book to read to help them keep their mind focused where it should be: on our Lord and Savior.
