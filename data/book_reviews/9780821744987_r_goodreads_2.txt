Read the whole thing four years ago, decided to re-read my faves and "The Mangler" (2016)My favourite stories from the anthology (no order):1. "Little Black Bags" by Clark Perry2. "An Eye for An Eye a Tooth for a Tooth" by Laurence Schimel. Was a little cheesy, but fun.3. "The Weepin' Tree" by Tia Travis. Might be the best written.4. "Anything for You" by Lorelei Shannon. Was my favourite.5. "Paper Animals" by Christopher A Hall. Even more depressing now, I empathize with the narrator more.6. "Storm Warning" by James C BassettThe other stories were fine, there was just one that I hated. It was the shortest, but managed to be super racist and sexist in just a couple of paragraphs. It's one of those stories that just tries to be shocking and doesn't bring anything else to the table. It just kind of cheapened the rest of the book.
