I recently re-read this entire series, and this is absolutely one of my favourite volumes. The drama is real, but it doesn't feel manufactured or stupid. Normally teen drama bothers me, because it's so trivial, but the drama in Mars is for the most part, pretty serious. Mars overall touches on some heavy topics, like suicide, sociopathy, assault, and this volume is no exception.All of Mars as a whole is exceptional, really, the entire series is just phenomenal, and every time I re-read it, I recall the little details about just how brilliant it is. Not only is the story line compelling and intense at times, but the romance is swoon worthy, you really get a sense of the characters and come to root for them, their development is great, and artistically, Soryo's style is just GORGEOUS. There's so much attention to detail, and not just amongst the characters, but in every single one of the backgrounds and other stuff. This whole series is truly a classic of shojo manga, and volumes 7, 8, 9, and 10 are some of my favourites, because they're so intense, and beautifully emotional.
