"Kwanzaa is like a family day in the park and Thanksgiving and a birthday, all rolled into one! Kwanzaa is a joyous African-American holiday that is seven days long."I really enjoyed this book! It really helped me better understand the significance of the celebration and I thought it was really touching. The Pinkneys usually do work that resonates with me, and this is no exception. The illustrations portray a loving family as they go about each day of Kwanzaa activities and describes the meaning behind each act. The story engaging and educational. I think it would be appreciated by families who celebrate Kwanzaa as well as those just learning about the holiday. As someone unfamiliar with the language used , I appreciated the pronunciation guide. (I do wish they had said which of the African languages was used for naming the various Kwanzaa principles; are they all Swahili? The intro mentions that Kwanza is a Swahili word meaning "first fruits of the harvest" -- the extra "a" was added to make seven letters representing the holiday's seven principles.)Since the book talks so much about "African culture" I would have liked to see a reminder in the Note to Readers section that reminds young readers that Africa is a continent with many countries and diverse cultures and regions. (I think there is an unfortunate tendency (at least in America) where some tend more to think of Africa as one big country rather than a continent full of diverse countries and cultures so it's important to note that Kwanzaa is an African-American celebration that brings together common aspects ofmanyAfrican cultures). Also, as an adult reader, I wanted a bit more information at times. The "Nguzo Saba" are the seven principles of Kwanzaa and "come from the beliefs that are held by families in many parts of Africa" -- I would have loved to know a bit more about how these have been traditionally represented in different parts of Africa. Of course, this is beyond the scope of this picture book's intent and I still think it's a wonderful book and I learned so much. A book such asAfrica Amazing Africa: Country by Countryis a great way to learn more about each country and the diversity in Africa today.
