This is my first ever review so please forgive me if this is terrible. But angel sanctuary! Seriously if you’re thinking about it try it. Ive read all of them and well they are amazing. First off Kaori Yuki is so talented and a pure genius, the plot is so mind blowingly epic it can be complicated at times but it never fails to amaze, the art work is beautiful and so detailed it makes me wonder if Kaori Yuki is actually human. Kaori Yuki portrays Setsuna and Sara’s love so beautifully i constantly forget their actually brother and sister. One down side of reading angel sanctuary is that frankly it will ruin all other manga for you, nothing compares to it in the slightest. And if your not yet convinced angel sanctuary is amazing so many characters in it are down right hot!
