This was a favorite book of mine when I was given it at 11 years old and remains one to this day. It's such a sweet little story of continuing on in the face of the disbelief and dismissal of others, even if those others are people you love and who mean well. Three mole sisters live underground, generally having a grand time, and then the youngest little mole goes exploring. On her exploration she hears about "Up Above" and how it is different from where they live and hurries to tell her sister's who dismiss her news as nonsense. Still, Eeny perseveres and is rewarded.

The illustrations are timeless and sweet and remind me of Beatrix Potter. I love the character design for the sisters and the creatures that Eeny meets and all the little details and colors. Very lovely.

It's definitely a worthwhile read and addition to your collection, especially your Jane Yolen collection.
