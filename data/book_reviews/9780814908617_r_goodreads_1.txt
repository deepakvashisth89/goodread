Print Book/Hardcover [Carnegie Library]This wasMUCHbetter than the first one. Other than several words that I had no idea what they meant [both Sister Mary Teresa and Sister Kimberly are scholars and you can really tell when they get into debates], this one was delightful. And much easier to read. I truly love these characters and how they interact with each other and those around them.I had wondered at the killer early on in the book; I am not even sure why.
