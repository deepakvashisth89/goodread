To be honest, I didn't even know WHAT I was reading or what it meant most of this book. All I did know was that whatever she was saying, she felt it literally from the deepest part of her core. This book is just filled with emotion, real life emotion. It's told in "short stories", and I think each one was supposed to have a deeper meaner, but I really couldn't even tell a difference among ANY of the characters. Male or female, the characters just suffered, and it was heartbreaking yet moving as hell by how beautifully it is told.
