This is a very simple book for very young children. Even for two year olds the questions "Who lives in the hen house?" (Answer: Hens) and "Who lives in the pig pen?" (Answer: Pigs) are pretty self-explanatory. My audience of two-year olds and their caregivers was also a bit surprised that Maisy Mouse lived in a little house with flowers, etc. rather than in a mouse hole. However, the flaps added a little bit of suspense, and there are lots of opportunities to make animal sounds, so the book was still a success at my toddler storytime.

Used again 7/26/19 to introduce Maisy Mouse. Very short and simple book that is easy for two-year olds to follow.
