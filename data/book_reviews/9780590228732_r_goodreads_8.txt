The BSC is in turmoil, with Dawn back in California and Shannon and Logan in open rebellion by daring to have extracurricular activities. It’s also never unclear why the BSC needs another full-time member just to sit in Claudia’s room three times a week, since Dawn didn’t even have a real title.

This book serves as the soft launch of future sitter Abby, which gets off to a predictably rough start. Abby is funny, outspoken, and athletic, so she’s a natural threat to the rigid social order Kristy maintains. Nevertheless, she’s inevitably lured into the cult because she’s too new to know better.

Because this is a Kristy book, there is also an incredibly boring plot about the Krushers getting sponsored by a weird and evil cloth diaper company, whose owner seems to be a little too into humiliating the kids.
