This not a translation of The Bible. It is a retelling of some of the Old Testament and New Testament. It is in common vernacular so rather than feeling distant and far away, the people and stories in the OT came to life. I found it moving.The New Testament is the story of Jesus the Christ. Again it’s not a translation, but a retelling. And the way the author brought emotions forward of the disciples, Mary of Magdala and Jesus was inspiring. His words made it so easy to feelthe humanness of Jesus as well as his divinity. The author did an excellent job of making me feel the weight of the world’s sin that rested on Jesus’s shoulders.The parable of the sheep and goats is a good one to read again with what is going on in our country. Are you a sheep or a goat?
