This book probably falls between 3 and 4 stars. It does have some interesting facts, myths and legends in it, but I dislike how several topics are brought up and then summarily dismissed using phrases like, (and i am paraphrasing here) 'of course, scholars don't believe this to be accurate'....All in all, I would call this 'lite' reading, but the reader is introduced to some interesting topics none-the-less.
