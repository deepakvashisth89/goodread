I have not read this book cover to cover (yet) but I have bounced around & read it in sections. I reference it often during my at-home-yoga-practice. The title summarizes its contents: “Mastering the Basics”. There are many simple & clear photos to go along with the written content. The sequences are broken down & include instruction on when to inhale & when to exhale with each movement. I needed that! I appreciate the slower pace of learning yoga from a book vs. watching a video. This book does an amazing job with the basics.
