I decided to read this book because, a couple of mates of mine had recently skimmed through it and recommended to read it as it had a very touching story-line. This novel expresses the difference about diversity and is real cultural based about the Chinese traditions. I don't usually enjoy reading non-fiction stories but i definitely found the Chinese culture interesting. The book was mainly based on a young girl that had grew up in a tough love environment, she had been through so much having been departed from her family and put into boarding school. One thing I will take out of this book is that, we should be very grateful about the life we have today, there are so many kids in this world that aren't getting the love we have.
