I actually enjoyed this book a lot more than I thought I would. It was a spur of the moment buy at a thrift store, and I’m glad I got it. I never read any of the other “Chicken Soup for the Soul” books. I was under the impression they are more so for older ladies who like sappy short stories. Well it turns out they’re also for 30-something ladies who like heart-warming stories, too.I liked that it was organized by different sections, my favorites being “humor” and “overcoming obstacles.” It was easy to turn the pages and keep going through story after story since they were so short, uplifting, and well-written. Despite the title, the majority of them weren’t overly heavy on religion, which I liked. I’ve read novels that were much more heavily religious and were a pain to get through, with all the cliche over-the-top connections that were just much too far fetched to believe, let alone enjoy reading. That wasn’t the case with this book.I will say I didn’t like that the majority of the stories were centered around Christmas time. That became a bit repetitive. Otherwise I really enjoyed it and I’ll think twice before turning up my nose at other Chicken Soup books.
