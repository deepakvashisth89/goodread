Writing about paranormalities can all too easily slide into improbable contrivance. MacGregor assiduously avoids such a facile plot device which, as a skeptic who likes the endless possibilities of the human mind and spirit, I very much appreciate.Without that twist, The Hanged Man would have been just a standard mystery in an attractive setting with nicely drawn characters and a decent plot. And, really, what more does a reader ask of this genre?
