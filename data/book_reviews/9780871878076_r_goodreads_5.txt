I guess most people reviewing this aren't really interested in research in Political Science or HAD to read the book (or parts of it), as opposed to WANTED to read it. I read it because I wanted to understand and solidify Research Methods knowledge and the book worked like a charm. At times it goes somewhat deep with the statistical explanations (your average statistical program will simply run the analysis, you don't have to know the details, just the interpretation) and I felt some Qualitative Methods explanations are lacking, but overall this is a good read if you're already somewhat interested in the topic.
