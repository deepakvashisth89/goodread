“Too Wild To Wed” is the story of Letty and Xavier.Letty is a college professor, happily engaged to the millionaire Xavier and about to be married when she learns he’s had her background investigated.Offended by the same, she decides to break the engagement and go off on a Medieval tour to explore her wild side with other professors, but Xavier has other plans..Ridiculous plot executed with meager enthusiasm. Most of the story is the heroine being offended in one way or another, hero being secretive or insignificant details about various things. The mystery was ridic.Got this because of the cover which I liked- average read.Safe2/5
