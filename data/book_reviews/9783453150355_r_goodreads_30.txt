BALLER ALERT!!This is the second book I’ve read by Maurensig this year and it was so incredibly delicious. His writing makes him a modern master and his narrative layers and thrilling twists and turns made me gobble this up so quickly. The mystery and intrigue of Dostoyevsky without the density, and the insight into human character of Steinbeck but more timeless. (Don’t get me wrong I love those guys but) this is a truly delightful read for both pleasure and insight.I will be writing him a letter.
