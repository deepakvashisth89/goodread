Liza McGrathDr HathawayI really liked this book, It was a fun quick read. I liked all the characters. Liza Colton is a singer on tour when her voice gives out due to exhaustion. She has been worried about Emily who is on the run after someone tried to kill her and someone is looking for her too. Enter Dr Hathaway, he begins treating Liza and offers her a place to stay. His housekeeper Bonnie is over joyed. You have the Meredith imposter trying to kill off Emily and will kill Liza if she is in the way.
