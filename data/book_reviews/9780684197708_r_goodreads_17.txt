Leave the Grave Green (Duncan Kincaid & Gemma James, #3) by Deborah Crombie.I listened to this book on CD.Connor Swann is found face down in the Thames river. Superintendent Kincaid & Sergeant James find the corpse appears to have been strangled. Answering a summons from Scotland Yard Kincaid & James are on the case, but is the first case of this sort? A new world of opera is opened before them as this victim was related to opera singers of world renowned.I enjoyed this story and very much enjoy the team spirit of Kincaid & James.
