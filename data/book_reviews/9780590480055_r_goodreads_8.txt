Having read and thoroughly enjoyed Dorothy Canfield Fisher’s The Home-Maker, I wanted to read more by the author. I chose Understood Betsy. It’s considered an American classic of children’s literature. The author was praised by Eleanor Roosevelt who stated that she was one of the ten most influential women of the nation! A promoter of Montessori teaching methods, the book tells the story of an overprotected young girl who when left with relatives is encouraged to think for herself and share in household chores and responsibilities. The result? She becomes healthier and stronger, happier and more self-reliant.

It’s a lovey story that warms the heart. It’s a good book to put in the hands of a child. It teaches children to help and care for others. Taking responsibilities builds self-confidence. It shows that money and possessions don’t necessarily bring happiness.

The ending is tied up quickly in a happy and sweet way.

The girl is nine. The story fits readers of the same age.

Anne Hancock reads the audiobook. Her narration I have given four stars. She is easy to follow and a young person would enjoy listening to it too. She modulates her tone to fit the person speaking.
