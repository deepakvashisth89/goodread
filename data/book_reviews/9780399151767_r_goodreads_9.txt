A great book. Even though it has a 2004 copyright it is still relevant today. I really appreciated his story about being an advisor with the Vietnamese Marines. They were a great force!A couple of gripes about the format. Not being a professional military man, all the alphabet soup in the book was annoying. I also did not like the italics used so much in the story, my eyes at my age had difficulty reading them.All in all, a good, well paced book. A lot of really "behind the scenes" information we really do not get in the media and other news today about the military. Well done.
