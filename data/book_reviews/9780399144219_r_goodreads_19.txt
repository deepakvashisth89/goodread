I'm writing this review years after I read the book. I wanted to try one of Griffith's military novels and I dived right in with this one, the eighth in his Corps series.Fascinating setting for the story, as the Marines find themselves in the Gobi Desert.I read one other of Griffith's books--and that's it. I enjoyed this book as well as the other one of his I read. I just haven't been able to go much more deeply into any of his series...4 stars.
