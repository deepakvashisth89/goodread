"Pomiane" refers to the author, Edouard de Pomiane and this book was first published in France in the 1930s. It's a cookbook but it has enough narrative in it that it feels like a book with recipes. Pomiane got me in his introduction with "The Duties of a Host". He says that,"...there are three kinds of guests: 1. Those one is fond of. 2. Those with whom one is obliged to mix. 3. Those whom one detests. For these three very different occasions one would prepare, respectfully, an excellent dinner, a banal meal, or nothing at all, since in the latter case one would buy something ready cooked."The whole book is filled with humor and written with simplicity. It is part of the Modern Library Food Series with Ruth Reichl as editor, and has an introduction by Elizabeth David. I'll be adding this one to my personal collection.
