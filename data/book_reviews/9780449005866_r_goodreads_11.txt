I’m never going to be able to give an unbiased opinion on this book, because I just love Jimmy too much. Is it really a 5 star book? Probably not. But for me it is. I read it the first time in my early 20s, and when I heard an audiobook had finally been released, I decided it was time for a reread. It’s a crime that no one recorded Jimmy narrating an audiobook while he was alive. It’s a further crime that after his death they chose Don Johnson to record it. He was just awful. Even so, listening was like having a chance to belly up to a bar on a tropical beach, look beside you and see Jimmy there - barefoot and ready to tell you stories of his life. I always wanted that to happen one day. He just made everything sound so damn fun.
