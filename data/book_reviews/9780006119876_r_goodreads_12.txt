One of my favorite novels.When I get totally depressed, I often read this book.I recently came to know that there is a genre called Gothic romance and Victoria Holt is among the best.She knows how to tell a story-it has everything- romance, suspense,mystery,old world charm, French chateaus..I just love the way she has portrayed the lead lady character.If I ever get a chance to be a book character ,I want to be Dallas Lawson in 'King of the Castle'.I value this book as the best in my collection as I got a copy form my late Uncle's and it has the real vintage look- hard bound and calligraphic script.
