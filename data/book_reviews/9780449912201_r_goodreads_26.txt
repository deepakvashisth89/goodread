I felt like I was waterskiing off the back of this book absolutely clawing on for dear life screaming shrieking about to die the entire time!!!! One of the most neurotic books I’ve ever read but written in a gorgeous and very demanding style that I can understand might not be for everyone but is definitely For Me. The whole time I kept thinking of ayo edebiri’s letterboxd review of beau is afraid but this is actually a huge compliment
