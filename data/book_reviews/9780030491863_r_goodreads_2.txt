Doonesbury mellows out as the seventies limp to the end. Duke defects from the Redskins to the NRA; Duane leaves Washington for Jerry Brown's California and the mastery of Mellowspeak (neat little Jerry and Linda Ronstadt sequence); Boopsie poses for Playboy; Jimmy Thudpucker retires; Jimmy Carter implodes. Nothing earth-shattering, but an appealing picture of an unappealing time.
