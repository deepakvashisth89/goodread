After a bit of a lull in the mid-section of the Gardner novels, SEAFIRE bounces back in a big way. This is the James Bond I recognize, and - as he has aged and run the gamut with promiscuity - it's acceptable that he is now living with his girlfriend spy, Flicka.My only gripe is in the final showdown with the villain; I thought this book was going to be predictable but it threw me for a little loop. As has occasionally been the case with Gardner's 007 novels, the build-up, the detail and the dialogue are quite fine but the ending comes abruptly. Nevertheless, he has presented us a madman villain with equally intriguing henchmen and women, good action and the return of Felix Leiter.3 1/2 stars. #8 of 14 Gardner novels thus far.Show more
