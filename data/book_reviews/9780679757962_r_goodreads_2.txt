Hearon is an amazing writer, and this book is one of her best. Hearon uses everyday themes of marriage and friendship to explore deeper issues such as envy, jealousy, hidden pain, misunderstandings, and society's treatment of women. Whether she's describing listening to a radio talk show, caring for peach trees, or walking with a labrador, Hearon makes her characters and environment come alive. A wise novel worth reading again and again.
