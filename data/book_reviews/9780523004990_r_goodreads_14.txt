This Bolan had a much more military vibe at the beginning, with Bolan being dropped by Grimaldi at an island in Puget sound that had been on his radar. His instincts pay off as he discovers a mob renovation going on, with some sort of massive bunker being built.

From there it’s on to Seattle, to discover what the mobs plans are for this bunker, and this leads to all out war with mobsters and international terrorist types who are importing arms into the US for a mob army.

In between kicking ass, Bolan saves a hot mom and her hotter daughter, has a brief dalliance with the younger and considers one with the older, calls in friends Leon Turrin and Hal Brognola for some backup, and eventually finds time to crush the island hard site.

Another great entry here, and kind of interesting how the mob is as truly feared back in the day, to the point of Bolan referring to them as a shadowy ‘branch’ of the government. The plan for the bunker seemed to be a Fort Knox style place for the mob to store hard currency, with long term plans for investments in oil and mining. Bolan has other plans though, and sets back the Mafia objective by blowing the island compound to hell and back.
