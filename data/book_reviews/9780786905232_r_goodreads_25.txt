This was kind of a weird one. I thoroughly enjoyed theChroniclestrilogy as a kid, but remember being surprised to see that TSR had published a fourth installment to the series (this was a decade after book #3 first came out). Taking place many years after the Chronicles books,Dragons of Summer Flamebrings back the beloved characters from the trilogy and...well, no spoilers, but it gets a little grim at times. Weis & Hickman are still able to tell a fun tale, but this wasn't quite up to the caliber of the first three books.3 stars.
