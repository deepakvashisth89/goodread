I’m not sure why, but my son is fully in a love of bugs and spiders phase and who am I to discourage his interests. He picked this out from the library himself and it was a great continuation to our Carle marathon we are slowly trying to work through. This one wasn’t the most astounding or deep but it held that original feel that the hungry caterpillar also did, that some of his later works just didn’t. Still a great and fun enough little read and I can only imagine how many more times we’ll read through this one before bringing it back to the library.
