Decent Oz outing, but becoming quite dated. There is are recurring themes that beauty is coupled to popularity, beauty is coupled to goodness, and ugly is coupled to wickedness. Plot devices are best suited to the pre-tween crowd when the TV is broken. These later Oz books are rapidly emigrating to the realms of scholarship and nostalgia only, with little relevance for today.Which brings an interesting thought -- what should be the future for Oz the written franchise? Could there be a reinvention, like already happens within graphic novels, movie franchises (ugh) and television? Or does Oz the written canon get put into a glass case in a backwater museum?
