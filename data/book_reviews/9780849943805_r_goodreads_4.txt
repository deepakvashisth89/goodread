Intriguing, inspiring, thrilling, enriching and spiritually enlightening. It was a great way to end the first in the series-“Blessed Child”. Caleb is all grown up, living with his parents in the restored monastery in Ethiopia. However, the Jews feel that the Ark of the Covenant is hidden in that monastery. The reader learns much about the Mount in Jerusalem and the many sects that want the people killed who want to put it on the Mount, and the Ark destroyed.
