Outstanding! It truly reads like riveting fiction, but ... it's all true! Very hard to put down! If you've the slightest interest in true crime and/or Cosa Nostra, I highly recommend it - it will not disappoint.I read this just after it was published, in '93 or '94. For many years after, I often wondered when it would be made into a film, as it seemed inevitable. Many have touched on it and the events have been featured in many a television program, also. None, however, rival Mr. Blum's delivery!It provides insight and detail about the events leading to Gotti's rise to power, as well as glimpses of few other pivotal figures destined for bigger and better things, particularly then U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of New York, Rudy Giuliani. As fascinating as Gotti was, the F.B.I. agent that led the (handpicked) team assigned to take down Gotti and his associates, J.Bruce Mouw, is a very interesting individual too. He'd been on a sub in Vietnam, hunting other ships, and, after the war, when he was looking for similarly challenging work, a friend suggested he join the F.B.I and hunt criminals. Very interesting stuff!Again, highly recommend this one! It's a fantastic read!
