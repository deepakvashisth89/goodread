"Ah didn't want de white folks tuh hear 'bout nothin' like dat. Dey knows too much 'bout us as it is, but dey some things dey ain't tuh know. Dey's some strings on our harp fuh us tuh play on and sing all tuh ourselves."A brief novel about the life of a man, John Pearson. Loosely based upon Hurston's childhood and family life, this book may indulge in the moralistic at times but is ultimately a strong statement about the human struggle to do good when sin lies deep in our breast.
