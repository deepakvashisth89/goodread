When I found this book, I had never heard of the USS Squalus. Despite growing up in Maine and taking numerous trips across the bridge to New Hampshire that is in view of the base the submarine was built. I even have visited the USS Albacore, a submarine mentioned in the epilogue of this book.This book is well written and researched. While not all the techniques and terms used are familiar to me, I was able to understand it. The story of the Squalus and the rescue of much of her crew is epic. How this had escaped me, I have no idea. But it is a tale that ranks up there with all the great military rescues.
