This was a disappointment after I enjoyed the first book in the series so much. I think it was all the extremist women's groups and the shock art neither of which are very interesting to me. One of the characters had a Lolita past too which was very distasteful and so was the reason for a blackmail scheme. Very few of the characters were interesting people - they were self-centered or weak or grasping or unhinged.I don't know that I'll read the next in the series.
