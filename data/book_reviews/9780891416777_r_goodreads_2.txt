Its been a couple or decades since I read this, but recall it covered several espionage investigations the author conducted or oversaw during the alter years of the cold war. Frankly it was shocking how many sevice members (some DoD/Army civilians too) betrayed their country, resulting in varying levels of damage to national security. The book reads like a true crime version of a police procedural; nothing super thrilling, not a shoot em up type of James Bond story, just by the book detective work and legal work resutling in court martials and trials in federal or forieign courts on occaison.A very unique and interesting book. Close to a five star, but it was a little dry. 4/5 stars.
