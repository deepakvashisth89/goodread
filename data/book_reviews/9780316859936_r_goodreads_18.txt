I suppose I would give any book I finished two stars, which indicates that I finished this one. Before I go on, I found Linda Grant's book about her mother "Remind Me Who I am Again" really great. If you do not have experience of dealing with a relative with memory loss, read it. But back to "Still Here". It appears to be fashionable to wend a fictional narrative into a patchwork of real events, and actually to write with more than one voice. I would say that the two voices were indistinguishable from one another - so I had to keep looking to see who was talking, this particularly since both characters kept coming out with some sort of cod philosophy about life. Obviously when recounting actual experiences I knew, but these were unfortunately few and far between. So if you are a lady approaching your middle years, a bit of a feminist, Jewish and come from Liverpool this might be for you. But that being said I note that lots of people really liked it, and it was apparently on a literary prize list, so what do I know?
