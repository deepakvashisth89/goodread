I came across this book back in 2013 and fell in love with it. I had borrowed it from the library so it was rather hard for me to give it back.8 years later and I still couldn't stop thinking about the forbidden romance between the hero and heroine.If you're a lover of forbidden romance with the old writing of smut moments then this for you. We have our female lead having to deal with choosing between her mom and the man she loves.
