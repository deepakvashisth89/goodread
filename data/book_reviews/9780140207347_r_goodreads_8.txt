This book is revolutionary in the way that it normalizes the schizoid (still somewhat healthy) and the schizophrenic (over the cliff to psychotic) mind, and pathologizes the environment that caused the mind to create a false self for its own safety. It teaches us how to have empathy (which means really listening to what people say, a rare thing)! and how to understand the schizoid parts of our own personalities (because there is no one on earth who hasn't created some sort of false self to deal more smoothly or safely with the world).

It seems the worst thing to do is to deny a child's sense of reality, but that's what narcissistic adults do every day. The most eye-opening thing was to read about how a therapist (or other probing person) can cause instant insanity by trying to get inside of a person who feels they will be killed if someone knows their true self. Also, that a schizophrenic person can be cured by seeing a film (a real case history). This substantiates Laing's claim that schizophrenia is created by environment (parents creating a double bind), and thus can be cured by environment (seeing a film, reading a book, or talking to someone, if any of these things provides a solution for the individual as to how they can tolerate living in the world).

I can't recommend this book enough, especially to mental health professionals and people who are anxious, depressed, enraged, emotionally dead, pathologically passive, addicted, suicidal, existentially alienated, or in despair.

On a lighter note, this book offers the narrative of, "It's not me that's crazy—it's everyone else that's crazy!" but in a thoroughly convincing, clinically supported way.
