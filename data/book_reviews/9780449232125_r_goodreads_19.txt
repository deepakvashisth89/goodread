While reading this, what kept going through my mind was "House, MD" meets "The Great Gatsby."" A very intriguing story. Just a heads-up, though: if you are one of those people who can't help but judge the past by today's standards and moral sensibilities, this may not be a good book for you. It will offend you, it will trigger you, and it will compel you to call for the author's head, even though it was written in the 1960s and takes place in 1901. Just skip it and find something else to read. If you are interested in turn-of-the-century medicine and society and are able to choke down a few big grains of salt, it is very interesting and bound to facilitate some stimulating discussions. I personally enjoyed it a lot.
