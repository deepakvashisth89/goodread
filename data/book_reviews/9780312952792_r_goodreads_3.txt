Quite a revealing, detailed yet concise account of the private lives of the current queen and her children (Charles, Anne, Andrew but not Edward because he did not marry until 1999, after this book was written).
Not just a rehash of the tabloid stories that anyone could look up.... Offers the stories behind the story. I won't offer any spoilers here. Well, just one: the courtiers slanted stories about Princess Fergie; apparently they wanted rid of her tho lord knows why.
Lady Colin Campbell exhibits her multiple talents here. Not only can she write, she offers the reader her insights into the behavior and psychology of the various players in the royal family and Palace.
