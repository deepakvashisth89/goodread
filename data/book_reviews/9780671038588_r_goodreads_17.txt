I meant to write something for Dreadnought! when I had read it, but I had procrastinated on that and on reviews for some of what I read before and after. When I had picked up Battlestations!, I had noticed that it had the same two characters on the cover (they are modeled after the author and her husband, which is very cute). It is a direct sequel, so what I have to say about Battlestations! also applies to Dreadnought!.

Unfortunately, Piper is not escaping the Mary Sue allegations, but her character is not insufferable and to be honest, is just fine. Both books read like a self-insert AO3 fanfic. I'm surprised there isn't a scene where the main character ties her hair into a messy bun and gets sold by her mom to Captain Kirk (there is absolutely no one who will read this that will understand that reference). The setup actually makes the books seem ahead of their time and that makes me respect them more. It doesn't actually bother me because it's something different. All these numbered novels and finite amounts of Star Trek plots and you need to do something memorable once in a while, so that makes these books better than the boring ones.

There are so many Star Trek books I have read that I remember absolutely nothing about, so although I don't particularly like Scanner or some of the comedic relief aspects, I do respect what Diane Carey was trying to do and I am disappointed that any sequels seemed to end up in limbo. These books are alright.
