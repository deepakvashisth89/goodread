What started out as an undercover assignment turns out to be anything but in the first book in a new series by Carly Phillips. Detective Kane McDermott is tasked to find out whether there’s an escort service being run from Kayla Luck’s family etiquette business that she just inherited from her Aunt and Uncle. From the moment they met, Kane has a hard time not telling Kayla the truth, as the attraction is instant. Keeping things professional helps Kane get to see that Kayla’s innocent, but something more sinister lurks in the wings.

Hours after their one night together, Kayla finds out who Kane really is and is not amused. Kane steps in to be her protector while trying to get to the bottom of who tried to harm Kayla. To do so, they are living and working together. Both are having a tough time keeping their hands (and other body parts) to themselves.

Once this case is solved, are they just going to walk away from each other? Both of them had crappy childhoods. Can they learn to trust one another? Lean on one another? Be the one for each other? Only the pages of this book will tell.
