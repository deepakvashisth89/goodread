Burke is back in NYC. To be honest I lost it when Flower came into the room on page seven I think. It was so awesome to witness the true bond that this "Family" shares.With that out of the way, Burke has to replenish his bank so he develops an "alternate" identity and gets a job tracking down the killer of a young girl. But as it turns out this young girl's father is a made man and runs a crew for one of the families.Through some late night flashbacks we are re-introduced to some characters from past books. Mostly female. And again when he thinks about Belle a small part of me feels the loss also.The ending, or more to the point the way it ended was classic Burke. Awesome!
