It's fun to compare the different languages, but if I was actually going to travel I probably would just get books for the individual languages that I need. There are several languages in this small book so each language just gets a small section. It also is just mostly the same words for each language, despite the fact that there are different things to do/see/eat in the different parts of Europe.

Finally, this version of the book is very old, so it tells you how to say "I need film for my camera" but not "Where can I charge my phone & get a wi-fi connection".
