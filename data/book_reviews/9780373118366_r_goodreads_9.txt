Maybe 4.5 stars. This was very well-done, with plenty of angst. Very likable characters. I guess the reason it wasn't 5 stars had to do with my own personal preferences -- I just don't like reunion stories where the gap is this long (here I think it was 13 years). It's just so much wasted time, and you lose the fantasy of the absolutely perfect HEA (because the hero can't get back those 13 years of his daughter's childhood). It could have been greatly ameliorated if
the H and h could have had more children, but that option is gone too, which I found a bummer because the h was only 32. Overall though, a very nice read.
