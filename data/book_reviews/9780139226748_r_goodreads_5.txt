Are you getting what you should out of life? Are you giving what you should to life?
This book explains very simply and clearly what you are likely on the cusp of achieving, but somehow not quite there. Often, the most difficult and pertinent problems you face have the simplest solutions. The problem is, despite their simplicity, they require an active, engaged effort.

That is my main takeaway from this book, which shares (and is possibly the precursor to) many ideas from pieces that came after it. If you have had your head down in the weeds for too long and need a breather to look up, you will find the book refreshing.
