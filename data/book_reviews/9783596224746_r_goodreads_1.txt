According to my personal taste, the book doesn't deserve more than 2 stars - the plot did nothing to keep me engaged and it was a bit too brutal in some places. The author hasn't spared anything, including scenes of intimacy and profanity on every second page. Indeed, the novel has its good sides, because it could acquaint you with the reality poorer people live in, but actually it serves best as a learn-how-to-swear-in-German tutor.
