Most likely biased opinion. A great story line and one that I haven’t read before but unfortunately didn’t work for me. The female butler concept was rather strange and took away from the feminity of the h. I also had issues with her helping the elderly man she works for change into his pants and such. But then when she’s a butler, that’s a given.

Strange feels. Not for a reader like me. The H was also very direct. There wasn’t much introduction to their relationship and all of a sudden the H is telling the h that he wants to sleep with her. There wasn’t any pick up of their romance that I saw anywhere in the book.

I was expecting the usual employer/employee bullying/ subjugation/ foreplay chemistry. None of that. Very haphazard writing too. Thumbs down.
