Portions of the Dorset and Devon coast have been declared a World Heritage Site, and this book is a guide to the most scenic and interesting places in this area. I used it to plan walks around Lyme Regis and Lulworth Cove.Filled with historical factoids and notes on the natural history of the area -- including tales of the famous fossil discoveries made by Mary Anning and others. A great resource for anyone with even a passing interest in the area.
