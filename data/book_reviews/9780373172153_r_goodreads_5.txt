Grace Green is a new author to me though this book has been around for a long time. This is a classic reunion story between a couple who'd been young lovers until someone threw a very nasty spanner in the works. Dominic believed Angela had betrayed both himself and her best friend by sleeping with his stepbrother Mike on the eve of his wedding to Patsy.
When they meet again at the Christening of Mike and Patsy's child all Dominic can think of is protecting the happily married couple until Angela leaves.
The revelation half way through the weekend that Angela has a child puts all his calculations out and to salvage the situation there is only one thing he can do. Marry Angela and claim her child as his own.
With this much baggage it's hard to know where they can go but the story is well told and the characters very likeable. It's takes a few revelations from the past to straighten everything out and the results is an enjoyable read with a nice touch of angst.
