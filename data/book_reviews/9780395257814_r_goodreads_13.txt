I went blind into Blind Date, and felt it scattered (even moreso than Steps) as a result. Only upon learning that the novel is quasi-autobiographical did that change my view of the structure. Sort of. It still reads like a bunch of disconnected vignettes because that’s what it is. However the novel’s theme of outcomes of chance works in concert with its structure.This book is at times repulsive, at times banal beyond words that should have been put to paper, and at times astonishingly captivating. I have never studied Kosinski’s actual life, but his writing at top form is incredible and worthy of study. That is evident in this novel.Four stars.If you have any triggers linked to sexual violence I would skip this novel.
