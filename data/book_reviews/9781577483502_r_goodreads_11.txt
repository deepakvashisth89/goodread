awesome book. a nice break from the Victorian collections I read before this one. Season of Love is a four in one anthology set in the present day world(late 1990s) and all stories deal, in their own way, with the repairing of the soul during the holiday season.
A couple of the main characters in the first and third story are a bit whiny and drag the story out or I would have given it five stars. Still, a great way to prepare spiritually for the holiday season that is upon us.
