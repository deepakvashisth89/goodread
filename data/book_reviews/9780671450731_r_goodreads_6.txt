A great rollicking yarn. Matt Shore a loner, divorced and a pilot working as a taxi pilot for wealthy people between race courses in England. He gets in embroiled in a thriller with first a near miss with a bomb and then a rescue of a sabotaged plane. Which the lovely Nancy is flying.Easy to read and a good plot with an absentminded, kindly Duke and his nephew. The reason behind the bomb and sabotage is a fraud.
