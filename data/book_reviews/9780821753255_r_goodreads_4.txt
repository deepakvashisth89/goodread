Have you ever fantasized about getting stranded on a deserted island with a ridiculously hot guy who takes care of you, provides everything you need, and just so happens to be filthy rich? Well, Heather Graham apparently has and put it in this book for us to read.

Not only are you stranded with Hot Rich Island Man, but the island also has a magical vegetable garden, it conveniently rains just enough to keep you hydrated, your survival expert has a lighter in his pocket so fire is never an issue, and—because you’re somehow the most prepared plane crash survivor ever—you brought a suitcase stocked with two bottles of wine, rum, and a cheese-and-cracker platter.

Ridiculous right? And you know what? I ate this book UP.

It’s a quick, easy, predictable read, which is exactly what I was in the mood for. The storyline with the ex-wife dragged on a little longer than it needed to, but overall I had a great time with it.

One piece of advice: maybe don’t read a novel about a plane crash right before getting on a plane yourself… especially if there’s no hot, rich survivalist sitting in your row. I’d recommend enjoying this one from the comfort of your couch instead.
