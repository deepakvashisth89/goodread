Any one of the individual stories in David Leavitt’sArkansas: Three Novellas, all three of them in fact, could have been adapted and interpreted and filmed and released as a super-competent and moving version ofThe Big Chill. Novellas are an awkward length. A writer, even a top-tier award winner swinging from David Leavitt’s rung, can be forced out of the space, time and genre continuum to seek publication. Bravo for making the stretch!
