I did like this--I now want to read up on priest holes--secret rooms for priests to hide in during the reign of Elizabeth I. And about Nicholas Owen, a craftsman famous for his cleverness in building these. But the mystery and the characters I found weak, and oh dear, the women, especially the protagonist's wife--flat and dull in the extreme. Also, in the writing, the aversion to the word "said," which resulted in characters constantly retorting stiffly, asking swiftly, admitting reluctantly, murmuring, contributing, chuckling, agreeing, warning, resuming, stating, mocking, supplying, etc. etc. ad nauseum.
