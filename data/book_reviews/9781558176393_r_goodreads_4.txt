This is probably the hardest review I've written. I'm still not sure how much I liked it. I had no idea what it was about when I opened the pages and began the first chapter...alien abduction...falling in love with her captor...believing her love is his brother...the imposter fooling the grandpa; these were the things I was on the fence about. I step back and think, "This is a work of fiction about aliens. Anything can happen."

The heroine, Jana, was a strong character and I like that (although sometimes clueless). You wanted to say, "Wake up!! It's Varian! Your love." That frustration proves to me it was well written and evoked emotion. I would have given it four stars if I hadn't skipped over some portions because it lost my interest.

Please, take the time to read it and form your own opinion. I've never read this type of book, so possibly I'm still getting my sea legs :)
