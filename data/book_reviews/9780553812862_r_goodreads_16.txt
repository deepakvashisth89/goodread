Extremely long book of 800 pages in small print. Took me well over a week to get through, but very much enjoyed.The four characters are very well fleshed out as the book covers them from childhood, teens and current day where they are planning an overseas holiday together.Brenda was honestly pretty horrible. She was meant to be sympathetic (I think), but just wasn't. Actually quite realistic. I'm sure we've all met someone like her. Paula was a bit spoiled too, but didn't hate her. Jenny and Rachel were probably the most relatable.Really enjoyable story about the relationships of the women and those in their lives.
