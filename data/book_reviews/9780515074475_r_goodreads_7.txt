The Agatha Christie of New Zealand, but still keeping the setting in England.An actor on stage was shot and killed by another actor. The bullets were meant to be fake. Were they tampered with and by who? Just about the whole cast had a motive to kill this most disagreeable victim, yet only one of them got the chance to do it.The police detective work was based on picking apart lies, Christie-style. There was a large cast of characters/suspects. Just when I began to tell them apart, the detective switched from calling them by their formal names to their first names or nicknames, and just like that, I was lost.As I was so bamboozled keeping track of the identities, I did skip to the end about halfway through as I couldn’t piece together who said or did what anymore. I’d say it’s my failing more than the author’s. If I’d used a notepad to keep track it could have been another story.Overall, it was well written enough, the characters were fine, if not a touch too diva-ish. The clues involved really focussing on the stage and comparing dialogue. Not my skill.New Zealand author. I picked it up on my holiday there earlier this month.
