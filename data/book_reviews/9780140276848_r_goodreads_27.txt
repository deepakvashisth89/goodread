This is a beautifully vivid account of mental illness and it's impact on the lives of loved ones. It was when Delores was at her most manic that she was her most creative and vibrant. The bond between mother and daughters remained in spite of some crazy situations they were put in. We do not choose our parents but we are a part of them no matter what may be their eccentricities and life choices. I loved this memoir for its bravery and honesty.
