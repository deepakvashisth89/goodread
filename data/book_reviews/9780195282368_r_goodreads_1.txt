The footnotes and maps in this version are extremely helpful. Many of the more vague and ambiguous passages are clarified in the footnotes and various interpretations of the meanings of the stories are presented in introductions before each book. This is a great study bible for those wishing to read the Bible to be knowledgeable, even if they choose not to be religious. --- After diligently reading a little each week, for two years, I finally finished the entire book. I tired of the footnoted interpretations about half-way through, but they were helpful sometimes for the veiled, vague references to people and places.
