The heroine was sent on a task by her mother to fetch her sister while she tried to elope. Encountering the hero was an unexpected surprise because he was the brother of the man her sister eloped with. When the heroine and her fell madly in love, they married quickly but the villain was trying to get under the heroines skin with lies. Eventually after all sorts of deviousness, deceit and pain the hero and the heroine found each other again. It was a sweet story in some sort of understandable twisted way.
