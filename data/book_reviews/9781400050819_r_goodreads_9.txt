Given that Schirripa smashes the world record for times saying goomba in a 200 page book (which in all honesty is now in the thousands) its an entertaining book. Bobby Bacala isn't a particularly gifted writer, and depends more on spewing out some rather obvious statements mixed in with solid observations.
“You can tell the quality of a restaurant by the items on the menu, too. If they serve a pizza with pineapple and ham on it, get out at once. No goomba would ever, ever eat a pizza with pineapple on it...This is California crap that no self-respecting goomba would touch.”
He's got a part on why they'll never be a goomba president that's really funny, but otherwise it can be difficult to claim this as a humor book. I more so relate to it, but I also relate to Ronny on Jersey Shore, so take it how you may.
