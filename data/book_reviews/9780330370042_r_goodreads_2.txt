Reading this book is similar to smoking cigarettes. The first few times you pick it up, it doesn't leave a good flavour in your mouth. Quickly putting it down, only to pick it up again at a later date. Each time reading a little more than the previous time. Before you know it your chain smoking, turning page after page to see what happens next. It sure is a dirty habit. Gritty and grimey with a hard taste. It's not for those with a weak stomach.I thought I was done and had kicked the habit. Years later I'm still thinking about this book. If street crime thrillers are your marlboro lights, then I would strongly suggest lighting up. If you don't like it after the first 100 pages, crack a beer and keep reading, you will soon be hooked.
