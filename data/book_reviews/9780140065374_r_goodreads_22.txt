Steeped in the academic world of rural Australia, of which Robert Barnard had first-hand experience, his first novel is indicative of what his writing style would become: humorous, satiric, and with a good twist at the end. Probably wise that Inspector Royle wasn't featured in a series, because he's ethically-challenged and not terribly likable. But it was a good start to Barnard's career as a mystery writer.
