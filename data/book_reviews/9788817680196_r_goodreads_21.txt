I love how Maraini writes and I love what she writes about. A tough but moving read, these short stories are apparently based on real cases taken from the news, involving violence and abuse against women and children. Maraini's prose is spare and graceful as always, and her empathy shines through."Bisognerebbe sempre credere ai bambini prima che agli adulti.""E se mentono?""Vale la pena di correre il rischio."
