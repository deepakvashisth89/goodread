This made for a great read, especially having just finished Szasz’s ‘Myth of Mental Illness’; I found them to pair well together. Where in Myth I wanted an explanation of how more ‘serious’ mental health problems may be explained in terms of strategies or reactions (rather than as simply a biochemical process), I found exactly that in this book. It’s the first text I’ve read where the experience of schizophrenia is given some attention (again, rather than simply couching it in terms of symptoms, brain regions, neurotransmitters etc.) I found Laing’s existential approach to schizophrenia to be very humanising and sympathetic, and the diatribe against psychiatric medicalisation at the beginning was thoughtfully reasoned (although anyone who knows me knows I eat that shit up anyway).
