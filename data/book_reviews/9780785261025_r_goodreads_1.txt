I really enjoyed this book. It surprised me that rather than attacking Democrats and elevating Republicans- Savage blasted both sides for standing by and doing nothing while this country is overrun with people determined to censor some religions and not others, teach our children a liberal agenda in school including homosexual education, and on and on and on. He objects because we have stood by and done nothing while the Republic our Forefathers desired for us is desecrated. We started as a Christian nation- those who contributed to the constitution and bill of rights knew we needed moral standards to guide us- personally I would say we need the Word of God- the Holy Scriptures to guide us and our children. I pray for a revival, for us to return to those values that our nation used to pride itself in- the values I am reading about in my grandfather's memoirs.
