Jonah's Gourd Vine follows John Buddy during the Reconstruction years of the South. We follow him from the time he decides to leave home and make a life for himself into his adult years. John is a very flawed character with a penchant for adultery and a bad temper. This book is a little over 200 pages, and at times the time jumps were hard to follow. This was Zora Neale Hurston's debut novel, and was based on her parent's story, which I thought was interesting. Hurston is a master at giving her characters voice and lively dialogue. It really makes them come to life. While I still very much prefer Their Eyes Were Watching God, this one is definitely worth a read!
