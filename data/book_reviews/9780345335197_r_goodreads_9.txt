This book is fairly well written about an incredibly interesting person, but it doesn’t do a good job of engaging with the political aspects of Guthrie’s life, and when it does it seems more interested in falling over itself to bash communism than to engage with why it had a broad base of popular support during the Depression. The choice to publish two page erotic letters that Woody wrote to Marjorie is also sort of baffling - a few quotes would have been fine and felt less voyeuristic.Worth reading, especially the parts about his battle with Huntington’s disease, but also sort of baffling at times
