Attention to Boris Pasternak's work naturally focuses on *Doctor Zhivago*. Yet, this autobiography is a truly overlooked work. Written during the difficult period of the 1930s and 1940s, it was only partially published in 1949 (the first complete edition appearing in 1958, after the fall of Stalin).I read this book in Dutch when I was 17. I only remember the lyrical passages about the endless birch forests. After that, I developed a real passion for the Trans-Siberian Railway.
