An interesting feminist look at women in with regard to careers, marriage, and children, analyzing the choices they make in the light of societal trends that disempower women and mothers. Like many books that focus on the social and gender implications of motherhood, this focuses on middle and upper class educated American women - those whohavechoices between staying home and working. Since I fall into that bracket, I found it an engaging and interesting lighter read, and to be fair, it's not intended to cover all women everywhere.
