It seems to me that Asey Mayo is an acquired taste—one I have not yet mastered. I'm always eager to read a mystery set in a snowstorm where the characters are isolated, and though I enjoyed this story more than the first novel he appears in, the dialect in which Asey speaks got wearisome and even annoying fairly quickly. To be fair, some would just find that part of the charm of the book and the period in which it was written. The mystery itself is a decent one with power-outages and roads closed and people trapped with a murderer among them. So I will continue with this series in time. But first I will sample Taylor's other mystery series, written under the pseudonym of Alice Tilton. I think I might prefer it.
