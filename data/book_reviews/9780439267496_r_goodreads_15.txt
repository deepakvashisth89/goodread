Beacon Hill Boys will change how you look at yourself and the world around you!This book gave me the insight I needed to see my world differently. I used to be ashamed of who I was being Hmong and asian but after reading this book it taught me to see the beauty of my culture and embrace how amazing it is to know my culture and where I am from.I'd recommend this book to boys, girls, and teens who struggle with family, friends, school life, and finding who they are. This book takes place in Seattle's Beacon Hill and the references to the areas locally will remind you of how it feels like to hang out with friends and in and around our area.I would recommend all of Ken Mochizuki's books to anyone and everyone of all ages!
