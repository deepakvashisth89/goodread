I bought this book maybe 6 years ago, after reading and loving High Fidelity and About a Boy. I read 100 pages or so, and put it down. A friend recently said she loved it, so I went back to give it another shot. It is, after all, about moral ambiguity and the search for a good life. That has always been one of my favorite topics. Once I got back into it, though, I remembered the things I disliked about it in the first place, namely:1. I don't like any of the characters, major or minor, and I would not want to spend any time with any of them.2. The major development in the story seems false and unrealistic to me.3. The main character's search for morality is really just an attempt to look like a good person to the people around her.There are some funny parts, but the highlight of the story for me was a cameo by a character from High Fidelity. In other words, it would have been much more enjoyable if I had simply read that again.
