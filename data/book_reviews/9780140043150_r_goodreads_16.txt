I would much rather be out in nature than sit and read about nature. Beston does an exemplary job writing about his Cape Cod beach in the 1920s. He must have found forty different words to describe the surf. But I don't think I could have read 100 more pages about it.Something I found extremely curious was how he elided the fact that he had bought his portion of the beach. In the introduction by Robert Finch we're told that Beston bought 50 acres. That's quite a large bit of beach and dunes and land. The way he describes his presence on Cape Cod is, "Having known and loved this land for many years, it came about that I found myself free to visit there, and so I built myself a house upon the beach." What a bizarre, even magical, erasure of money, commerce, and privilege.
