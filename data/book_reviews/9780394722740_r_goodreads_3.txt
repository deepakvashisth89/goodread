This is absolutely great, for many many reasons, but the main one, for me, is this: it shows the immense capacity for kindness, love, and joy that human heart can have. I will never forget some of the stories from this book; especially the one about the poor peasant couple whose most prized possession was atable, and who would gladly share whatever they could with their fellow human beings – and the wife was just such an extraordinary person; and the train conductor lady, who took upon herself a delivery of a written complaint so that it would reach the Party and the justice would be done.Whenever I see and hear of xenophobic/racist attacks on Chinese people – and nowadays they are almost the norm – I’m reminded of this book.
