An interesting novel looking at a fictional scenario where major investors behind the scenes create incidents to influence world events to achieve their own goals. Whether this is for personal gain, world stability or instability depends on which powerful groups are doing the influencing…and when. For those that end up unknowingly carry out these efforts, it makes for a turbulent life full of life-threatening danger that seems to be around every corner. Who to trust and who to go after can change at any given moment, with split-second decisions needing to be made certainly makes it an exciting ride to follow the main character’s adventures throughout the book.
