If you're fan of more classic-style romance with more sensuality, this will be right up your alley, with Francesca the innocent and naive beauty awakened to her hidden and profuse passions by the dominating, sometimes-cruel, ruthless, all-powerful and impossibly-handsome Carlos. The romance part of this is quite sweet, with both parties swept away by unblockable emotions, watching them fight each other and themselves is quite touching.

The storyline is quite well handled, although I'm not sure I can quite suspend my sense of disbelief as to only two men in Rome being interested in her. I didn't really believe Sonya either, she seemed very undeveloped and unmotivated but I'll let it ride, this is a fantasy right?

The subplot of Francesca's family background tangling with his was well done, and provided extra drama and rationale for the tension point before the fall. Once it begins, it doesn't stop, I had to check I was reading a Sexy imprint, not a Blaze one, because from about half way through the book the leads can't keep their hands off each other and it's seduction after seduction.
