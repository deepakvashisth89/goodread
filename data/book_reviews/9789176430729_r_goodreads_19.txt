Oh what a surprise this novel turned out to be. A pleasant surprise. I'd read about Kerstin Ekman in an article on "Nordic Nor", so was expecting another Wallender style police procedural. Was I wrong! Yes, there is a crime and it resonates through the book, but there is so much more. This is literature of the highest order. The theme of nature runs through this book like a mountain stream. The descriptions of the forests lakes and mountains are outstanding. Poetic and beautiful. The natural world seems at turns hostile, then welcoming.Much of the book seems hypnotic, reminding me of some of John Fowles' writing. Like the Magus with cold weather. Something deep seems to lay just below the surface of the words.I won't give away the story, just read it.
