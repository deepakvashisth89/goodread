Marrying Maddy is a mad, senseless but fun book. You will either like it or you won't. I liked it as I read the 3rd book in the series first and got used to the crazy talks between the grandmother, housekeeper and the siblings. Maddie is about to marry a man she doesn't love , who doesn't love her, when her real love comes back. It's crazy town after that, meddling, hives and what not. Maddie and Joe are two stubborn, immature people who despite loving each other are kind of nuts.I liked the book.
