"Isn't it time we switched from washing our minds with "soaps" to programming our minds with goals?The mind is goal-seeking by design. Successful individuals have game plans and purposes that are clearly defined and to which they constantly refer. They know where they are going every day, every month, and every year. Things don't just happen in their lives. They make life happen for themselves and their loved ones. They know the difference between goal-achieving actions, and activities that are just tension-relieving."Denis Waitley,Seeds of Greatness, Page 124.This book shares 10 Seeds of Greatness by the super achievers that Denis Waitley has studied. They are:1. The Seed of Self-Esteem;2. The Seed of Creativity;3. The Seed of Responsibility;4. The Seed of Wisdom;5. The Seed of Purpose;6. The Seed of Communication;7. The Seed of Faith;8. The Seed of Adaptability;9. The Seed of Perseverance; and10. The Seed of Perspective.I particularly liked the survey and exercise at the back of this book called the wheel of life (p. 213). It shows you how balanced you are in your life. The areas that Denis mentions that are in our life (in no particular order) are: Mental, Social, Spiritual, Physical, Family, Financial, Professional and Community Support.I found that my wheel did not look like a wheel at all, but possibly could lead to a bumpy ride. This exercise helped me identify areas that require more of my focus and improvement.
