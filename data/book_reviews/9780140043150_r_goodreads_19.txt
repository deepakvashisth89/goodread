For such a short book, this seemed to take me an age to get through.I think I was expecting a seaside Thoreau if I'm honest but instead I got a very dry yet at times overly flowery description of living in the sand dunes. I think the writing style here just didn't capture my passion. Don't get me wrong, there were flashes of inspiration in the description of seabirds, insects and the odd storm, but much of the book felt like drudgery for some reason.I'm not sure what went wrong to make this such an overall dull read but yeah, there we are. 2 stars Mr Beston, sorry matey.
