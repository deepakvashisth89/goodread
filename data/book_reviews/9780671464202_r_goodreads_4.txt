Fast-paced and interesting, kinda predictable in the sense of seeing where the conflict was going to collide. There are 3 scenarios introduced fairly quickly: a family protected by the FBI while the father awaits testifying at a mob-related trial, a down & out teenager with a hot-headed abusive boyfriend and the level-headed single sister/aunt of the family under FBI protection. The aunt is taking the only child of the testifier on a camping getaway while Mom & Dad are in court. Meanwhile, the teenager and her boyfriend head out of town, on the run, hauling a... camper. So, quickly I predicted the aunt & nephew will encounter the couple on the run. And I wasn't wrong. What I didn't know was exactly how the rest of the story would play out. I just knew the boyfriend was bad, bad news. The twists & turns of the cat & mouse story are definitely riveting, but in the end, upon reflection, I had to ponder "what was the point of the mob-related trial?" With just a few tweaks, this story EASILY could have played out just as intriguing and gripping without that little pointless sidebar.
