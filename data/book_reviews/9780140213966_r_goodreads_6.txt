I've been wanting to learn more about the so-called Dark Ages in England, between the end of Roman occupation and the Norman invasion. When I got this book, I didn't know that it's more of a scholarly analysis than a general public summary.So, if you like books that explain what the source material is, what the problems are with making sense of the source material, and come to the appropriate provisional conclusions, you'll probably like this one. I wasn't looking for that sort of text, but I'm glad I read it. I expect it to help me read the other books I've gotten that are more clearly targeted at the general public.
