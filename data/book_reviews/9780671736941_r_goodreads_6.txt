This story is a sequel to Mr. Archer's best-seller, Kane and Abel, this one has at its center Florentyna Rosnowski, the daughter of a Polish immigrant, who is on her way of becoming the first female president of the US.Many say that there are quite a lot of resemblances between the main character and at least three ladies which have put their mark on our history, from Indira Gandhi to Golda Meir and Margaret Thatcher, as Florentyna is an iron fist in a velvet glove. But that's only the beginning, the rest is done by Archer's storyteller talents, which are quite amazing...
