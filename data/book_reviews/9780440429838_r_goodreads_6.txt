Now that Tom is back to swindling, life is Adenville is back to being interesting. Even if that does mean J.D. will be swindled the most. But Tom's growing up, and some things eventually change.

There were some sadder moments in this book (like about a boy with diabetes in a time when the disease was not understood), and our family definitely struggled with some of Tom's behavior. But still, this is a series our family has thoroughly enjoyed reading aloud.
