This was a 4 star read in a lot of ways. The first half was excellent military SF. The political/conspiracy thread got stronger & made for a good second half, but there was also a thread of mysticism that ran through it that I didn't care for, although it did help support the philosophical thread to some extent.Overall, the philosophical thread was the main one. We're introduced to it early on in a simplistic format & then it's touched on through out the book. It breaks life into major groups & then assigns motivations to them. It was interesting, although it's a far stretch. Still, that's what SF is about - exploring the far stretches.Dalmas certainly got a lot of the trivia of the military right. As I read about the jumps, I thought he must have done it. He has. He jumped in WWII & later was a smoke jumper. I'm not sure which is more dangerous.I won't be reading it again, but I'm glad I read it once. I'm going to look for the second book in this series, too. It might just be excellent, although no real reviews are on GR.
