Filled with inspirational words of wisdom from the Bible, great works of literature and famous historical figures, this book presents a well-rounded review of how to live your life well, from A to Z. Through short and well-organized chapters, Leroy Brownlow presents a crystal clear picture on the habits, mindsets, and attitudes that are essential for achieving whatever goal you may have, whether spiritual, intellectual, physical, or emotional. Life, and the process of chasing our goals and pursuing our dreams is a great adventure with God at our side, and if your calling and mission are from Him, then you will always be a success no matter where you go or what you do.
