I might be a little biased because this book was written by my aunt and uncle under the pen name John Case. Having previously readThe Genesis Code(which still holds the top spot as my favorite of theirs!), I was so excited to dive into this one, and it did not disappoint. My aunt and uncle remain ahead of their time in medical and techno-thrillers.Reading this book today, in a post-COVID world, is an eerie experience. Written all the way back in 1998, the ideas and pandemic logistics are so shockingly prescient. The level of meticulous research my aunt put into this - at times, it almost felt like she was writing an instruction manual for a global health crisis!*Spoilers Ahead*The story itself is a global puzzle. It masterfully connects a terrifying cover-up in a desolate North Korean village, a radical eco-terrorist cult with a chilling ideology intent on resetting the world, and the dangerous investigation of reporter Frank Daly.It is a fast-paced, and thought-provoking read. I am so incredibly proud of the visionary, brilliant stories my aunt and uncle created. I just wish my aunt was still here so I could tell her myself how much I admire her writing and the intellect involved to write such novels.
