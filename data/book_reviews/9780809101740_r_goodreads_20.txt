I've first read this book when I was like ten. It's been how many years now, and I still think that it's fantastic.This books is about the journey of two caterpillars, Stripe and Yellow, and the obstacles and problems that they faced before they both became butterflies.The story is simple, but beautiful and realistic. It's about learning to give up and let go. How each ending offers a new beginning. It's about taking risks. And the desire to see something more.The illustrations are really wonderful. This may be a book for children's but I'm pretty sure that even adults would enjoy it too.
