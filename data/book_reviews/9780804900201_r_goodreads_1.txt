Stevenson has written an adventure during the time of the War of the Roses. English civil wars, fought by supporters of the rival houses of Lancaster and York, provide the backdrop of bloody encounters and lives of the those serving on both sides.

Richard Shelton is the main character. A young ward of a Lancasterian, Sir Daniel, his life is one of service and sacrifice to Sir Daniel until Richard hears whispered a story involving the death of his father at the hands of the one he most looks up to.

So begins Richard’s quest to find the truth, to be of help when needed, to be brave in dangerous situations, and to be an honorable person.

This is an epic journey during dangerous times—the making of a man.
