Alexa thought that she was gonna marry the man of her dreams. Except that she made the mistake of insisting on getting married in her hometown and introducing her fiancee to her BFF. Her fiancee falls instantly in love with BFF and dumps Alexa.When the book begins, Alexa is attending the wedding of her ex & her BFF and trying to put on a brave face. I have no idea why the heroines in these romances always have to attend these weddings of people who BETRAY her and have to act all gracious as if they are on the political trail. Don't they know that they get these invitations because those a-holes WANT a scene of tears and humiliation? If it was me, I certainly would have obliged them. Instead, Alexa holds up and picks up her ex's older brother, Scott, who looks and sounds just like him, if she's drunk enough. Which she makes sure, she is. She takes him back to her place and they are about to do the nasty when she calls out her ex's name right at the very last minute. Ooops!Then Alexa goes batshyte and locks herself in her bathroom, crying and swallowing a bottle of pills. Scott makes sure she doesnt die because he didn't know any better at the time and tells her that he is taking her to Brazil with him since he can't trust her not to try to commit suicide again and put a damper on his a-hole brother's marriage with her BFF.Alexa is suppose to be twenty-two in the story but she has all the immaturity and manic-obsession of a 11 year old at a Hannah Montana contest giveaway. She has a lot of growing up to do in this story and I am not sure she is believably mature by the end, especially since her obvious drinking problem is never addressed. I was also turned off that all she did thruout the book was laze all day - reading, driving, swimming, eating, gardening, partying - and complaining whenever she is bored. Not unusual to find a heroine with no career goals or direction in an old HP but still disturbing just the same. Having said all that, I felt such sympathy for her betrayal and the story was written in such entertaining, dramatic fashion that I was able to overlook character traits that I would normally find annoying.Scott wasn't bad for an unPC hero, as long as I can overlook the all in the family thing. More than half of the book was set in the Amazonian rainforest and I LOVED the exotic locale.Content warning: rape/forced seductionGrade: B-
