One of Gibson's best. I love his shadowy digital worlds and the characters who inhabit them, often addicts of various stripes, but this one, anchored by the existence of a virtual 'idol'--Idoru--a manufactured female celebrity entertainer, proved him once again the Master of Prescience. I remember being at a party twenty plus years after the 1996 book was published--talking to a friend who is a road manager for various musical acts. I asked her what she was up to and she said she was touring Roy Orbison. "Roy Orbison??? Isn't he dead?" Yes, he was still dead, but it was a holographic concert... IDORU!!! I think I shouted. Gibson had done it again.
