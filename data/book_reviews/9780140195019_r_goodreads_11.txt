This book gives a good overview of various religious orders that developed in 12 and 13th centuries in Europe and Levant. Attention is given to less known orders as well, not just main ones. Of course scope of subject and size of the book limits attention to details but still provides enough of all. Author also provides insight into other fronts of medieval religious wars, Reconquista in Spain and Baltic crusades.Minor thing is that you can easily see author is British as he often provides some detail on England's role in it, or role of Englishmen. It's not a bias except when dealing with Napoleon's conquest of Malta, then under authority of knights Hospitalers.Overall a nice work for person looking for some general information about the subject or a good starting point for further reading. Only minor gripe is that book sometimes uses quotes in French or Latin without providing English translation.
