Ah, the proverbial book of two halves.The first half of the book was fantastic and gripping (I read this in high school but remembered none of it). Usual Stephen King fantastic characters and it was well on its way to being my favourite Bachman novelAnd then it turned. About 200 pages in the main character is visited by an old Italian gangster friend of his and then it goes silly. The Italian gangster turns into Rambo and all intrigue disappears. The ending, involving a pie, was so obvious what was going to happen that I rolled my eyes.Difficult to rate really as the first two thirds were really good before it falls off a cliff. Very disappointing ending.
