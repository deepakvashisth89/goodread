Whoso bringeth a good deed, he will have better than that same; while as for him who bringeth an ill deed, those who do ill deeds will be requited only what they didHaving not read the original Quran in Arabic, I can't comment on how accurate this is to that or how it compares to the original Arabic text in regards to writing style. That being said, I found that this was a pretty good introduction to Islam and never found myself confused by the translated surahs. Along with this, it comes with a selection of notes and an introduction that help guide readers unfamiliar with Islamic history into the context of the surahs. Recommended for anyone who doesn't know Arabic but wants to study Islam.
