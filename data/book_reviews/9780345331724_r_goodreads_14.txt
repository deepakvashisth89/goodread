A sort of new and fresh start in the Sten Saga, this book moves from being a blood-and-gore-and-starships type to a space opera or at least military sci-fi, and that is quite an interesting turn, because imho, if it was a fourth book about some sort of covet-ops or a casual revolution and overthrowing a dictator, it would have lost it's charm. This book is much less gory than previous three, but on the other hand it concentrates more on military tactics and warfare.Don't be unsure about this book though - it is as always funny, a bit quirky, and the cliffhanger ending definitely leaves you hungry for more.
