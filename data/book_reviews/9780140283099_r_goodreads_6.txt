This is a pure, raw collection of stories - or rather, mostly experiences that portray what it means to be a female homosexual in a society that does not value either of those characteristics. It is by no means well-written, nor are these love stories with happy endings; the stories are messy, heartbreaking, hopeful and problematic. It is perhaps a very decent portrayal of what it means to be a lesbian woman in India the 1990s. It is like peeping into the diaries of lesbian women sampled from a country whose population is over a billion; of course the results of that sampling are quite as expected. It is rather heartbreaking though, the number of portrayals of brushed off sexual assaults, unrequited love, and in certain cases, the writers being perpetrators of said sexual assault too. It is not a comfortable read by any measure, just a necessary one.
