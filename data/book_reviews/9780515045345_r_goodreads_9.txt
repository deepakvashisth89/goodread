I love Ngaio Marsh and my book crush is definitely Roderick Alleyn but this (sadly like Death in Ecstasy) left me a bit flat.It plodded on well but lacked the sparkle of some of the other Inspector Alleyn books I'd read, I don't know if it was the setting (New Zealand rather than England) or the plot or the very excessive use of the theatre? I can't put my finger on it but it just lacked.Brilliant writing as always just not as good as I'm use to I guess. Not my favourite!
