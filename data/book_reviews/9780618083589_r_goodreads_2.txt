The seventh book of The History of Middle-earth and the second volume covering the development of The Lord of the Rings.This book continues directly from the previous one, and it’s fascinating to follow J. R. R. Tolkien’s train of thought as he develops ideas related to the plot and each individual character. Much of it is already on a familiar path, but the alternative ideas often provide some of the most interesting insights—things you won’t find in the final text of The Fellowship of the Ring or The Two Towers, yet they clearly linger in the background.Anyone who has read The Lord of the Rings will find it especially engaging to dive into what I’d freely call a higher level of analysis of the most famous work of fantasy literature.
