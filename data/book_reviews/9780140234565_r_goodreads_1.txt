I had trouble getting through this one.Reg - a recovering alcoholic - crashes a party and is found dead in the owners' kitchen of a heroine overdose. The owners of the house and the three couples with them - 8 people in all - try to cover up Reg's death. Janey - Reg's soon to be ex-wife - doesn't believe that Reg would be taking drugs or alcohol. She wants Reg to be clean so that she can collect the insurance money.The general story concept is fine, but the book dragged on too much for me.
