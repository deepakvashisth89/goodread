Marvin wanted more is a fantastic picture book that demonstrates various values throughout. The book tells the story of a small sheep named Marvin; he wants to be bigger just like the other sheep but this soon spirals out of control! It combines amazing pictures a great story line and engaging language. I used this book to during an interview before with a year 2 class. They absolutely loved it and I know of adults who are equal as excited by the book. I would defiantly recommend this for whole class reading with early years. You can follow up the book with some discussion questions and literal questions! It never gets old.
