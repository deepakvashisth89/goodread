Listened to the audiobook with Simon. It's new for him and a comfort read for me. It's tempting to think of this series as cosy mysteries, however the topics covered are anything but! In this volume, two canisters of plutonium are stolen to use as leverage in creating a single world order.Mrs. Pollifax who is accused of lacking a 'sense of adventure' by an unsuspecting friend goes on an undercover mission via the CIA and goes to extraordinary lengths in an effort to keep the world safe. She is aided by a motley band of helpers gathered along the way. One of her team is Hafez, a young boy who is wonderfully described as, "a small intense figure radiating joy," another is a jewel thief. The unlikely setting is a health resort/clinic in Switzerland. I never tire of hearing Barbara Rosenblatt act all the characters' voices.Quotes that made me think:"He who has money can eat sherbet in hell."The sheik talks about enforcing peace on the world. His plan sounds like the opposite of peace. However, Mrs. Pollifax explains: "It's the latest style of peace," she said drily, "It's called waging peace with limited duration reinforced protective reaction strikes, low kill ratios and no incursions."
