This book is a vivid depiction of the lives of the Great Banks fishermen in the 19th century with dangers and their esprit de corps. I fail to see that exposing a spoiled brat son of millionaire to this life would be a way of making him into a responsible adult. The kid had years of social conditioning to shape his sense of entitlement and snobbishness. I liked the description of a fisherman's life but it being a source of redemption is far fetched.
