Another winner from Robert Rankin. The sequel to Armageddon: The Musical, this series is more outlandish and fantastical than the more grounded Brentford novels. Well, for outlandish, read “downright bonkers” at times, although there’s a method in the madness, and a strong moral code. After all, having characters from the future passing judgement on our own lives and times is a solid way of exposing the shortfalls of the age.The world may shatter in the stories here, but the books themselves won’t ever have that effect. But why should they? A good, solid, well-crafted read is always a pleasure, and Rankin hasn’t disappointed me yet.
