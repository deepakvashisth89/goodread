I LOVED it!!!It is a true story of how the Author arrived in Australia in the 1910s and had to go through all of the trials the locals put all the poms through. Such as the bad horse one, every new arrival to Australia was put on a flighty horse and was inevitably thrown off. (It's called breaking the human in, not the horse! - That was a joke by the way).On the boat trip to Australia, Bluey (as they call redheads in Australia - I know, that makes a lot of sense) met three other British men, and one by one they each settle down. Except for one, that is... But you'll have to read the book to find that out.
