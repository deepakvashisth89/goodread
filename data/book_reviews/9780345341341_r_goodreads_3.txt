In book one, the giant starship SDF 1 accidentally jumped all the way to Pluto. Now this ship, which contains an entire city of civilians, has to fight its way back to Earth!This one was quite exciting. The SDF is outmanned and outgunned and can only survive by the skill of its crew. There’s epic battles, villainous traps, and a daring prison break!On the character side of things, Rick Hunter now is in charge of his own wing of Mecha fighters which is an interesting challenge and his new wingmen are memorable. Plus, he begins to have romantic tension with the ice cold Lisa Hayes, while his primary love interest Minmei becomes distant as she seeks fame. Also, the reader gets new insight into the alien Zentraidi mind and culture.It’s a fun space opera. The prison break is especially a highlight.
