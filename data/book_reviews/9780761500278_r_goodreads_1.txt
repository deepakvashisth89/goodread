This book has one of our family's favorite soup recipes: Barley Cannellini Bean stew with broccoli and tomatoes. Delicious, hearty and pretty, great on a cold day, served with garlic bread and a salad. I've only made one other recipe from this book, however when I consider giving it to the library I just can't do it as there are many recipes which sound good and several are labeled "almost instant" meaning you can make them in 30 minutes or less. There are no pictures, however nutritional information is included which is nice. Ingredient lists are not too long, instructions are clear.
