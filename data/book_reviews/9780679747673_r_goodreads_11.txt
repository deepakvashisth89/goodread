It'd be easy to read Gilbert's only occasional poetic output as diffidence or reclusive distance, but all you need to do is peek inside the cover and read a line or two to know that he's one of those rare creatures that at one and the same time celebrate and darkly understate our mortality. Much of the themes here are taken up with love, death, and a eros unstifled by either. The guy's poems are just so alive, so frank, and subtle, that you can't help but read one, sit back, blink a few times and wonder where the hell you've been all along.
