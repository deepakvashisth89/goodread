An atmospheric story Kal tells the tale of two families making a life in the red dust gold town of Kalgoorlie. The Giovanni’s escaped Italy to get rich down under after a violent altercation leaves one of the brothers crippled and full of dangerous rage. While the Brearley’s have made themselves a success in Kal through Maudie’s hard work with her pub and a little con artistry from Harry. An unequal partnership between the two families creates a violent feud that carries through the generations as the families try to battle the beast of Kal that can make or destroy you.Sweeping the Gold Rush to the First World War Judy Nunn does a fantastic job of mixing fact with fiction to create super readable Australian history novels. Somehow desolate and beautiful at the same time, lovers of the outback I think you’ll find yourself completely transported to another time and place.
