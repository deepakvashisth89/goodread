Some of the stories in this collection were incandescent, like the title one that kicks off the collection and a beautiful story about a German woman choosing freitod, free-death, in Baja. Others were perfectly fine, but nothing to write home about. I couldn't read the last one, about preparing to put a beloved dog down, and there were a few others that were just this side of banal. Her writing is gorgeous but only a few really gave me chills and feels.
