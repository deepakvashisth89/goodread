"§246. It can’t be said of me at all (except perhaps as a joke) that I know I’m in pain. What is it supposed to mean a except perhaps that I am in pain?"

I was running a writing exercise with my students and one of the prompts I gave was, very loosely in the tradition of ekphrastic poems, to write a poem based on another work - a book, a film, a video game, a song - and I always like to do these with them, it's fun, and helps me test my prompts to make sure they're actually generative. In twenty minutes I wrote two lines, haha...



If I were a bur, I would not know that, I simply would be.
A propagatory castaway, riding on sleeves.
