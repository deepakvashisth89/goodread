Somewhat uneven book. The prehistoric and early historic sections are given over to abstract ideas of some interest, but make for dry reading as they lack narrative structure. In the later history, and especially the account of the nuclear era, Dyer shows his journalistic instincts and these sections are superb. The final section on the end of war drifts again into abstractions. I don't mean to say the ideas do not have merit but let's just say they're not meant for late night reading, unless one is fighting insomnia.
