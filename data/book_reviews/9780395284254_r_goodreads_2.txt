Beautiful artwork and a great concept that doesn't quite deliver as much as it promises. The interpretations of the motel from the past raises questions about the world that exists in the present. What do they use instead of toilets or plastic, for example? I would have found this to be a positive rather than a negative quality if not for the fact that the humans of the present appear to dress and behave just as the people of the past.

Still, it's a good fun book and, as I said, the artwork is really wonderful.
