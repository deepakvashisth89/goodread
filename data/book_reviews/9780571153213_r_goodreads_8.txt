I don't know how to rate this book. Back when I read it in 1973 it was ground-breaking and I would have rated it 5 stars. It was informative about women's bodies and the things that can wrong in a way that we had not seen before and was very valuable in an era before authoritative sex ed and when too many parents were reticent about discussing these things. I think it might have been the first book of its type that discussed menopause.
It's out of date now, of course, because treatments and testing &c have changed so much, but women still might be better off reading it than some of the rubbish published by the so-called wellness industry these days.
