It took me too long to finish this book , I ended up skimming the last half of the book. I am a fan of Jayne Ann Krentz's books but this one didn't work out for me. I wasn't very wowed by the setting and the atmosphere. I can see the creativity there but I just didn't like it.Overall the plot wasn't very interesting and the characters weren't appealing , it was an okay read but I wouldn't want to read it again.
