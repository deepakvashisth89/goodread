This is a crazy story that is funny to read aloud. The trip to the farm becomes a hectic, silly event all when Jimmy's boa gets loose.Our girls enjoyed this story very much. I loved the backward, roundabout way the little girl tells the story and the illustrations are fun, too.This book was featured as one of the selections for theOctober 2019: Celebrating Steven Kelloggdiscussion at thePicture-Book Clubin the Children's Books group here at Goodreads.
