If you dream of traveling the world -- and discovering places no one has been to --you'll love this book. Through the journal of a man who journeyed into the forbidden Drung valley, you'll discover a place, people, and culture you never knew existed. Feel the author's frustrations as he gets arrested by hostile soldiers again and again. Blister your feet in his hiking shoes. And eat "yak butter and black tea" with the new friends and strangers he meets. After reading this book, you'll be saving your money and packing your bags for Tibet.
