A lot of fun, though mileage will vary by story. Authors as diverse as Woody Allen, Jim Davis (of Garfield fame), PG Wodehouse, and Bret Harte.A few stories spoof the styles of specific authors and works rather than the genre as a whole, and enjoyment of them will require a familiarity with them to reap the maximum laughs (in particular, spoofs of Dick Francis, Dorothy Sayers, and Umberto Eco).Henry Beard's "The Big Recall" is a standout, as well as a prime example of why he was an instrumental force in the rise of The National Lampoon.
