Well, this was fun. A lot happened. The names were many, the places numerous, and it was all kind of confusing.But it was still a good time. I'm glad to have finally read this. The classic 'rob from the rich, give to the poor' wasn't as big of a portion of the story, what it did more of was meting out justice to those oppressors and rulers who were unmerciful, cruel and greedy. You get most of the classical Robin Hood stories, meeting Little John, Friar Tuck, the archery contest, fooling the sheriff, etc.It was just really fun and if you're into Robin Hood it's definitely worth a read.
