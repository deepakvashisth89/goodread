This 1987 novel of a family in western North Carolina (in Asheville, called Mountain City in the book) gradually presents the perspectives the whole family--successful novelist Clare, her mother Lily, Clare's stepfather Ralph Quick, Clare's half-brothers Theo and Rafe, Theo's estranged wife Snow and little son Jason, as well as some close family members. Godwin explores the relationships of parents and children, husbands and wives, siblings, and friends. The family is rocked by a violent tragedy, and most of the book relates how the survivors respond to that tragedy, covering a year of their lives.The device of hearing from these various characters mean there are many repetitions of scenes--an event is described by one character early in the book, then described by a second later, and by a third much later in the book. Some of this seemed unnecessary--that the book could have been a bit shorter than its 536 pages. But it is memorable, and moving, even as much is left unresolved at the end.
