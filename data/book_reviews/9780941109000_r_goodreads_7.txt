The Micheal teaching is a spiritual thing, religion I guess. A friend of mine follows it so I thought I would check it out.

The beliefs themselves mostly invovle a complex personality profile that is supposed to help people understand the particular lessons of their current incarnation in life. As personality profiles go it seems a lot better than the meyers briggs or what hogwarts house you're in. I may even borrow the system to flesh out side characters in my novels quickly.

Of the two books on these teaching I read I found the other one "Michael The Basic Teachings" to be the better introduction and this one to be the better for clarifying the details the other skipped over.
