I just read the Metamorphosis (not the other stories).The Metamorphosis can easily be interpreted in a myriad of ways.I chose to read it as a metaphor for a young man who was a family’s sole provider being stolen away to war, becoming a monster to himself as he did hideous things while his family suffered without him.All I know is that this story was first published in 1915. Kafka wrote this in his home country of Austria. WWI started around the same time he must have written it. This is probably why I chose to interpret the story as I did.It is a deeply uncomfortable story, but intentionally so.I don’t know how to rate this. 3.5 stars ?
