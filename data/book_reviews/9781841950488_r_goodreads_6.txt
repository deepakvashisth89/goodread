Wow, that was one ride. I literally hated all the characters (well maybe not Jake) but loved the book nevertheless. It is a brutal and dark yet at times hilarious depiction of a very dysfuntional family living in Edinburgh, written from the perspective of all four members of the family: father, mother, daughter and son. The Scottish vernacular it is written in was a blast.The ending did disappoint me a bit though, but I'll let that pass, because this piece of literature really made me feel things. Ah dinnae ken, I just thought it was barrie.
