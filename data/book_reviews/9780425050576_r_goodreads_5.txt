A novel with several independent stories occurring simultaneously doesn't always work but that's not the case here. I thought each story could have been a book in and of themselves but Thomas Keneally again worked his magic (as he did inSchindler's List). From a foot weary Confederate soldier fighting beside a man that had a brief affair with his wife, to Union spies, to a philandering Colonel looking for salvation the tales carried me through at a frantic pace. The horrors of the Civil War and the innocence of most of it's warriors are vividly portrayed as men desperately trying to survive and go home. The underlying camaraderie between the Union and Confederate soldiers is depicted several times and will bring tears at their attempts to understand why they are fighting to their gruesome deaths.
