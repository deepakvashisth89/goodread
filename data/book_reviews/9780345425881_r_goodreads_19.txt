This was a nice mystery. Worth the price I paid for it. Again I think it would have been better served being a mystery than a "christian" mystery because the Christian part feels forced and doesn't flow. At times it seemed like a character of his time. At other times it seemed like "okay, now we're going to invoke God because this is a Christian book." and it felt forced and odd. Let the characters be who they are and stop worrying about it. The story will carry things along.The plot was a little slow and the bad guy was only my guess due to the process of elimination rather than anything else. I think I've reached a point where I don't enjoy most mysteries any more. Perhaps my tastes have changed. I've found there are other types of books I don't like. As an example of what I do like I really like Louise Penny.
