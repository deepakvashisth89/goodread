A transatlantic phone call from a mentor and colleague alerts Reese to unspecified nefarious doings, so the seemingly natural death of the colleague that same night arouses Reese's suspicion. Upon returning to the US, he applies his WWII experience as a scout in advance of the US Army and postwar training as an archivist to catch a murderer.The plot is interesting, although the culprit seemed glaringly obvious even to me, when I typically follow every red herring. As well, I would have enjoyed greater emphasis on the archivist. And while it fits that a combat scout would act independently, the situation almost tips into the too-stupid-to-live category.However, already marked second in series to-read.
