I ended up putting this book down for a month as life got busy, so it took me a while to read it.However, this book is awesome. The threat is unique and terrifying. Cadderly's growth is amazing. I loved that he didn't just accept other people explanations, he had to puzzle it out himself. I love the description of Deneir and his religion.Ghost was a really interesting character as he was truly a coward with a reset button that over-inflated his ego. Knowing that no matter what he'd eventually win, and his "experience" was basically the same strategy, so when thing went off the rails he just assume it good because he can reset. Even if failed as he stated he did a couple times, he just had to wait and try again and win eventually. That doesn't make him a good assassin, just a assassin with a cheat code basically.He stated he was a wizard at one point too but never used any other magics, I personally think he was like Boygo as in he wasn't very good at it. That why he change his mind with Boygo, wanting not interfere to put Boygo into his plans. That's all speculative but it feels like the clues were left there to be examined. And if he wasn't very good at being a wizard, his ego would inflating when he assumes he's the best assassin, even to the point of being delusional about his own skill level. Cause the momment someone could counter his cheat code, he had nothing. He was a terrifying character with a real pathetic underbelly, and I really enjoy they way he was written.The only thing I hope for in the future is Danica growth to happen. She has grown and she is very insightful, resilient character, but she harsh sarcasm is unexpected when it comes to Cadderly. I understand her fears and her need to protect him by giving "tough love" buuut sometimes she just needs to chill. The last chapter with Nameless, her words are true and she making a strong point, however Cadderly already came to his own conclusions and didn't need her biting words.
