Well, this was a disappointing read. It started with such promise! I loved the concept, I loved the tone of the first 80 or so pages, and I loved where I THOUGHT the story was going to go. Emphasis on THOUGHT though because I think at about the 100 page mark this story took a turn in a direction I really didn’t enjoy and it just kept…going…and going…and going…and every turn it took made me like where it led even less.

This is by no means a bad book. I think to some people it would be really fun. It’s fast paced, it’s well written enough, and the plot is at least interesting, but I just felt like there was so much promise from its initial premise that wasn’t delivered upon. I didn’t like our main character, and the supporting cast was too flimsy to really get to know. I thought the dialogue was clunky and only got clunkier as the story kept going. And I felt like by the end things were just…happening. I wasn’t sure why. They didn’t tether together super well. They just…occurred. And that was the most unfortunate part.

This is a survival story with some horror and some science fiction thrown in there, but it felt like the actual story arc of this one was pasted together with Elmers glue that didn’t get to every fully dry. It could’ve been better. That’s really the root of it. It could’ve been so much better.

2 Stars for me.
