This book brings the reader into the mind of a psychopath AND those of several more "normal" people who are seriously, destructively obsessed. And it doesn't take us very far into the mind of Adam, Kate or their assistant (here it is Piers). Oh, & the presenting mystery is solved at the end but the murderer won't be brought to justice. Hence I'm not thrilled with this immense tome.Its strength: beautiful descriptions of the city of London, a number of historic buildings (a sense of home or lack thereof is definitely a theme in this story), & some English countryside.I am definitely finished with my P.D.
