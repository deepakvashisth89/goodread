This is Anne Tyler’s second book. I’ve decided it’s time I start reading her works in chronological order, as I wanted to reread the ones I already read and I almost collected all her books, I think I need to find 4-5 books and that’s it. I am going to rate her books only in comparison with her other books.

This one was a little difficult to read because of its first several pages compared to her other books. I am glad I read this after I fell in love with her writing because if this was my first Tyler, I doubt I would continue.

In this book she’s on her way to find her voice. The prose is raw, sometimes difficult to follow. It was the same with her first book. However, I liked this less than her first book. But this doesn’t change my mind about her writing. I said in my review of the first book, this book is a dream come true for a new writer!

And her characters! She’s a natural when it comes to her characters - they are alive, real, ones that you might get friendly with if you knew them.

My copy has praise from Updike stating:
“This writer is not merely good, she’s wickedly good”.

I couldn’t agree more. Some writers are just born knowing how to write.
