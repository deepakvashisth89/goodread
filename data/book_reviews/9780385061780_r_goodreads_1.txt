I don´t have this exact edition - I have the one that looks a bit more modern, with a pinkish cover, but I can only recommend it to anyone with any interest in pre-history, the Greeks, the Romans or the battles and movements of the ancient world. Clear and conscise, accompanied by a breif but informative text, this is a perfect companion or reference work if you like reading history but want to check some details of what went on (great for ancient place names, too). Text is small, though, so glasses on if, like me, you´re the wrong side of 40.
