This is the second book in the Kane & Abel series by author Jeffery Archer and I was a little disappointed following the excellent first book. This sequel fails to deliver on the promise and in my opinion was just an ok read.Prodigal Daughter tells of Abel’s daughter Florentyna Rosnovski who aims for the top just like her father but without his wealth. Her father Abel was a polish immigrant who loved America, but her ambition reaches even higher than his his, she wants to be the first female president.I had big hopes for this book given how much I enjoy the authors work as well as enjoying the previous novel. So much hope it was only going to end up one way. It was still a decent read but lacked that little bit of magic that I am accustomed to when read Archer books.
