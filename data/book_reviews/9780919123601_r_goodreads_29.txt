This was not an easy book to read (which is obvious based on the time it took to finish it – more than 3 months). But there is something very peaceful about it, as if the author, James Hollis, kept telling you: “It’s OK to be feeling those feelings and thinking those thoughts.”

I underlined so many quotes – about passion, parenthood, individuation. If you’re going through a crisis of some sort, try this book, it might give you a sense of ease. “Life is seldom clear and easy; yet choice is what defines and validates a life.”

“One must, amid the disappointment and desolation, begin to take on the responsibility for one’s own satisfaction. There is no one oit there to save us, to take care of us, to heal the hurt. But there is a very fine person within, one we barely know, ready and willing to be our constant companion.”
