I notice that if you are reading this book Goodreads suggests that you might also enjoySara Paretsky's V.I.Warshawski series. And I am not surprised because honestly Kinsey and Vic could be twin sisters. The same brusque personality, the same disregard for personal safety and for other people's feelings - I sometimes have to remind myself which series I am reading:)Which is fine with me because I enjoy both.D is for Deadbeatis not one of Grafton's best but it is an easy read. The murderer was not obvious and the final part of the book was a bit over the top but probably the only possible solution.A fun story, written well and an ideal choice if you want an easy, entertaining mystery novel.
