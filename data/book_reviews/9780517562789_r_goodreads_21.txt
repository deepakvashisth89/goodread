Well, thanks to Stephen King, here's a book with a title that everyone knows how to spell. It's a novel from 1985 about a man with some psychic talents that wreck his life. John Childes had visions that led to the arrest of a killer but also caused his marriage to collapse so he relocated to the rural island of Guernsey to recover. He gets a job as a teacher at a girls' school and gets involved with another teacher named Amy. Then his visions return and get more gruesome, and then bodies start popping up that match the visions. His description of the settings is quite captivating, and it's a very good character study, too. It's a good mix of supernatural and psychological horror, with perhaps a dash of gorier violence and gratuitous sex than was really necessary in 1985. (Some of the speculation about the sexual potential of the young girls seems on the edge of pedophilia and would probably not see publication today.) It's a good horror mystery, with interesting shifts between placid and peaceful periods and intensely horrifying scenes.
