***1/2As philosophical as it is scatological, INFERNAL DESIRE MACHINES OF DOCTOR HOFFMAN is a perverse conglomeration of Jules Verne, Blaise Cendrars' Moravigne, and Leigh Brackett writing barbed fantasy for palm-reared philosophers. It is pretentious, mean-spirited, orgiastic; all combining to thread an oddly playful fantasy of both highest and lowest orders. A hallucinatory treatise on desire as both weapon and wonder, the novel descends into phallic nightmare with nobody appearing safe from physical harm. Rape and cannibalism fill the pages, characters are torn apart and rebuilt, and logic tears off the bone as if torn from a pack of wolf's teeth. So if you're seeking fantasy with soft and rounded corners, you should not read this novel. Angela probably wouldn't give a fuck either way. She lights fires not for comfort and warmth but solely for the dazzle of destruction.Not for the fragile-hearted, a delirium captures the novel with a limitless exhaustion, and what better way to write an anti-novel as a globe-trotting hallucination. Cruel with intent, Carter does let herself indulge in fart jokes, shit jokes, and even goes so far as teasing the old world stereotypes of cannibal chiefs cooking white men in cauldrons, interdimensional pimps seeking revenge via castration, queer drunken sailors lighting up their asses with blue-flamed flatulence, and a posh orgy where the well-endowed Count and our narrator Desederio engage in an orgy with half human half animal fleshpots. And dare I mention, the Acrobats of Desire. Quite brutal.
