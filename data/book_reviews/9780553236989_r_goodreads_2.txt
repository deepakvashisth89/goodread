I'll start by saying I have rarely ever been able to finish reading a western genre novel, I prefer non-fiction when reading about this era, so for me to give a book in this genre a 5 star rating is really out of character. However, Will Henry's "No Survivors" was a notable exception. The story is told from a very unique perspective and gives you a historical peek at the events leading up to and including the Battle fo the Little Big Horn. Its a story of a man ultimatley torn between his adoptive Native American family and who he was born and raised, a white man who served in the Confederate Army, and where his choices ultimately lead him to at the Battle of the Little Big Horn.I now have a collection of Will Henry in my reading stack and have moved on to "Reckoning at Yankee Flats".
