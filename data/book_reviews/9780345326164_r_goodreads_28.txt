3 and a half stars really.Follows the mishap adventures of a mostly down-on-his-luck apprentice wizard who had been taught only a single spell before his master up and died on him. Without a clue, Tobas set out on a mission to earn his fortune with the least effort possible.I remember reading this one when still in high school; it was worth the reread, though it did lose something since I'm far from the teenager I once was!
