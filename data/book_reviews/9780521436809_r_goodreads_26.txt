I bought the 2nd edition of this book from the 11th grade and used it until I graduated university.It's a great book for your self study with practical examples and situations. I highly recommend it for English learners from beginning to intermediate levels.I have just one advice for my Vietnamese fellows: don't buy the bi-lingual version (Vietnamse-English). Because the nature of the book is learning from reading the examples (in use), not from remembering the grammar rules. You cannot learn a lot of English by reading the explanations in Vietnamese.
