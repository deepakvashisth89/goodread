This book has a lot of bonkers stuff in it, but unfortunately I can't say I enjoyed reading. It is by turns dry and cartoonish. The buffoonery of the non-Bum adults is next-level. I give Pike credit for writing his twelve-year-old protagonists as smart (as opposed to Stine, who often makes his kids too dumb to be believable) but they are, perhaps, so smart that it defies plausibility.The Cold People are good monsters and allow Pike to write a zombie story while avoiding gore.
