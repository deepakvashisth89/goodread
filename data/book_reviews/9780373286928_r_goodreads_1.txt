2.5 stars
It could have been an epic romance had someone else written it. Also, I found it to be too superficial, which is probably because of the short format.

The main reasons I didn't rate it higher:
* After saving the heroine from an attempted rape, the hero gets excited by the view of her skin through the ripped bodice and forcefully kisses her. Heroine is shocked, but then of course melts in his arm. There's nothing romantic or heroic about this scene.
* James, the hero, is engaged, yet it doesn't stop them from cheating on his fiancée because she is the villainess. Somehow, that always justifies it.
* Raven, the heroine, was very bland with occasional outbursts that seemed strange for someone in her position, that is an indentured servant.

And last but not least, if slavery is a triggering topic for you, I wouldn't recommend this book as it's set on a plantation in Virginia.
