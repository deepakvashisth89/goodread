Very good. While the structure of the chapters and the book as a whole occasionally seems a little backwards and some relevant topics are given less space than less relevant ones at times, the content itself is, for the most part, excellent.

McGrath does exactly what he sets out to do and presents an overview of major themes, history and controversies of Western* Christian theology that comes off as objective and fairly unbiased, which is what a book like this should be like.

This is not a systematic theology or a thorough discussion of any one specific theological question but an easy-to-read panorama of the Christian theological landscape that provides a great foundation for further exploration.

*) Eastern Christian traditions are also mentioned but clearly not the focus of this work, which is made transparent on the first few pages.
