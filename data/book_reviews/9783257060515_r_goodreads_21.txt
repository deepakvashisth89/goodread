When the corpse of an American soldier is discovered in the canals of Venice, Commissario Brunetti is drawn into the strange world of a nearby military base.Like the first book, here we have the same lovely depiction of Venice, both its beauty and its murkiness. The mystery is an engaging one, and the ending is melancholy. Leon does a good job of showing Brunetti's struggle to be a just man in a corrupt system, and the inevitability of what happens crashes over you like a wave.
