Sally Jessy Raphael has been in show business for over 40 years. In this autobiography, she chronicles her unique rise to fame. With her characteristically quirky sense of humour, she recalls the sometimes crazy, sometimes funny and oftentimes poignant events that have accompanied her on her rocky road to success.I truly did enjoy this book, however I found that sometimes Ms. Raphael's attempts at humour didn't quite come off - at least for me. It was still a very interesting read and I learned quite a lot about her life that I didn't know before. I give this book an A+!
