More from the Man- Kzin Wars universe.Despite the many faults of the first story Briar Patch by Dean Ing, which was a not so great sequel to his Cathouse story, which I liked in the first book, the second story in this The Children’s Hour by Jerry Pournelle and S.M. Stirling was pretty enjoyable.These books are fast easy fun reads with Man and Kzins trying to outwit each other and battling against each other in a never ending war.3 stars. Not mind blowing or anything but definitely good enough to hold my attention. I like these.I’ll read more if these, cause they are entertaining and why the hell not.
