I really enjoyed this. You go through all the different types of weather to, cloud types, air pressure, the water cycle, and many more. It talks about the different systems used to read and understand the weather. You see the history of weather as well from how it was recorded before to who and when Fahrenheit and Celsius scales were invented. It shows you every aspect of weather in-depth and you see the weather with your own eyes through the amazing photos shown throughout the book. It is an in-depth and we’ll rounded book about weather. Recommend the read!
