Can RelateI enjoyed the dialogue in this book, as it did not come off as someone trying to make up the Southern drawl that is usually nothing like us old Floridians actually speak. The story was nicely paced and the actual location became almost a character. As a lifelong Floridian, who has lived in the swamp for the last 30 years, I could feel the sand between my toes, breathe the salty air in, and smell the earthiness of the wetlands.
