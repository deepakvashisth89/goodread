I read this book one Summer when i was bored and had nothing else to do. I picked it up with very low expectations (thinking i would read one chapter and put it down again) but once i read it, i was hooked and i couldnt wait to see what happened and how it ended.I loved the relationship/ romance with Cody and Bree- it was so shy and secretive [even to them:].I would suggest this as a read if you like romantic books.. with a tiny bit of suspense =)
