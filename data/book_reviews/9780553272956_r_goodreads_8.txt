This book is one I didn’t know I needed to read. It covers a topic I didn’t know you could write a 250 page book about. As a hobby actor, the insights, especially in the first half of the book are really valuable.The first half of the book…the 12 guideposts of a successful audition…is where this book shines.Then later half of the book, while well written, is more anecdotal with lots of name dropping by the author.The book was written nearly 50 years ago, so some of the advice is pretty dated, but reading it from an understanding of the time frame it was written helps you glean the good advice found here.Worth the read if you are an actor or aspire to be.
