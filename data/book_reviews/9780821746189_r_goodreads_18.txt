4.25 Stars. This time it’s Mary Margaret “Maggie” turn to be the centered of attention of her father,Jay Mac, as he tries to play matchmaker once again. This time it’s with a business associate’s son, Connor Holiday. Little does her father know or for that fact Maggie know, these two already knows each other in the “biblical sense”.Like with the previous books, the main couple met in an unusual fashion, but this has to be the most extraordinary circumstance. Unlike the other books, this starts right off with some between the sheets action. I found Chapters 1 and 2 to be very cleverly written, explaining different views by the characters involved.There were a few twist and turns that weren’t really unexpected. What was unforeseen, was the most dramatic character change I have ever read about in a book. It totally blew my prediction on how a certain lost object reappearance would be revealed.There is a strong supporting cast representing family loyalty and drama.You do get to catch up some with the sisters, especially Michael. Even though there is less action and mystery than the first two books, this is an entertaining read.
