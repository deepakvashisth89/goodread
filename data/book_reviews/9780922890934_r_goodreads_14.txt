Turns out I had already read The Con Man by Ed McBain (1957) so this was a second time around, not a problem, in fact I enjoyed the re-read. Having said that this 87th Precinct Mystery was pretty standard with two parallel stories. One, a pair of con-men ripping off citizens of Isola, and two, a mail order murder scheme is investigated. One observation, the longer I read the 87th novels the more & more I like the always friendly narrator who always takes you on a tour of Isola and then gently drops you into the story-line. Don't let the 3-Star rating throw you, The Con Man and all the 87th's are worthy of a read. Always entertaining....3 outta 5 Stars...
