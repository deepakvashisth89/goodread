I had a graduate professor who challenged our group to find a contemporary literary novel with a truly believable 'happy ending.' Fowles'Daniel Martindoes just that, but it takes over 600 pages to develop it -- and 'happy ending' doesn't mean a necessarily 'happy journey.' Fowles set out to show that sometimes in life, things do turn out well -- but it takes a lot of hard work, will, and luck. His experiements with changing tenses and point of view make for an interesting read. Adult reading, not for adolescents.
