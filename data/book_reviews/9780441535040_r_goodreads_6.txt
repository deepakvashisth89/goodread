Of all the Robert E. Howard creations, Cormac Mac Art got the short end of the stick.

Howard never published any Cormac tales in his lifetime, so it was up to others to pick up where he left off. Tigers of the Sea was collected in 1974, and Andrew J. Offutt put together a series of stories based on Howard's take on the character.

I absolutely love these. I actually like these 6 books better than most of the Conan stuff. Something about the character just speaks to me and I guess I like Offutt's more straight ahead style a little better than Howard's sometimes purple prose.

I've re-read them a few times and always enjoy the experience.
