I had this book downloaded for a holiday to Morocco a few months ago- I picked another one to be my holiday book, but decided to go for a second anyway.I didn’t love this. I think this is a good example of why child narrators often don’t work- I feel there was quite a lot of inconsistency in how much the child narrator was meant to understand and that meant I didn’t find it compelling. For example “I wanted to tell him I could only count to four which was how old I was.” Bollocks. Has Esther ever met a 4 year old?It made the whole thing read like the first few chapters of a celebrity autobiography where the ghost writer has clearly embellished a few basic memories (that the celeb thinks will give their story an arc) with details that couldn’t possibly be memorable.I also got well over half way through this book before thinking to google whether the author is related to Sigmund Freud. She is. I think that’s Wikipedia rabbit hole was more interesting than the book
