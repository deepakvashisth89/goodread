Amelia Bedelia’s Family Album by Peggy Parish illustrated by Lynn SweetMr. and Mrs. Rodgers want to have a party for Amelia Bedelia, so they ask her to invite her family. Amelia brings out her family photo album for Mr. and Mrs. Rodgers to help her look through so they can decide on who to invite. With every person Amelia Bedelia introduces, she tells Mr. and Mrs. Rodgers what they do, except it is not what you would expect. When Amelia talks about her Cousin Edward, she explains that he races horses, so Mr. Rodgers thinks he is a jockey. Instead, Cousin Edward actually races horses himself and has almost won a race one time. Another time Amelia Bedelia explains that her uncle is a garbage collected, and Mr. and Mrs. Rodgers think that he is a garbage man. The reality is that Uncle Alf actually collects garbage in his own yard. When the Rodgers tell Amelia Bedelia to invite them all to the party, she does but tells them to come the next day. Mr. and Mrs. Rodgers are worried they won’t have enough food for everyone. Amelia Bedelia thinks she was supposed to invite everyone, so she runs out into the neighborhood to invite everyone. The next day everyone comes to the party. I really like this book because I think it is clever with wording and imagination. The illustrations throughout the book help the reader to understand what Amelia’s family members actually do. The colors are vibrant and help to emphasis the books creativity. I would use this book with a group of 3rd grades as it is on the longer side, and they might be more willing to get through the book on their own. I think this book could be a great way to talk about literal meanings and even exploring careers. This is a great way for students to learn what certain jobs do, like bookkeeping and being a bank teller.
