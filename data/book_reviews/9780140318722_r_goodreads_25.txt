I enjoyed following Amy and Axel during the first half of the book, which introduced the city, the filth, the dystopian future, the scarcity, etc. and their journey to find out what exists on the higher levels.The book is also fine for a little bit after Amy and Axel escape, and Amy is experiencing the outside for the first time.But then the vast majority of the second half is like, we run over here, we run over there, and then we run some more, and it really lost my interest. I wanted more world building and more social commentary and less "Do you think (whatever is lurking around) sees us?"Maybe kids like that sort of thing.
