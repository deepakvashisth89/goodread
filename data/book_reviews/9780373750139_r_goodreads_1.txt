It's written okay, but the characters should not have ended up together. Neither has a secure attachment style, and both have many hidden triggers that make them say and do horrible things to each other in the course of the story. They also clearly confuse physical attraction to love, which is of course not uncommon in these types of novellas, but in this case, they decide they love each other after three or four kisses and nothing but drama. I would have liked just a tad more realistic buildup, and better character development that explains more.
