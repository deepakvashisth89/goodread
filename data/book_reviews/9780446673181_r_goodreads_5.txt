I've read the first two in this series already, but I don't remember them so I want to re-read.2020 Update: I really enjoyed this second book in the series. I had forgotten the sad turn of events at the end, but none of the plot points should have been surprising if I was looking for them. (I wasn't looking. I was just enjoying the read and letting it go where it would.)
