Good story line, but…The plot was intriguing but the writing was difficult to read. It’s not that the author didn’t know how to put words together, but that whoever proofread the manuscript knew little, if anything, about punctuation and the correct use of quotation marks. Run-on sentences abounded and I had to stop and reread passages several times to discern what the author was trying to get at. I’ve found that frequently the proofreading for ebooks doesn’t measure up to that for a printed book. The Language Arts teacher in me wanted to correct the proofreading, but life is too short to recopy the book just for my own satisfaction.
