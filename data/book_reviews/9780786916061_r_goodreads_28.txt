This book in the series has one of the better villains in most of the Forgotten Realms books that i have read. The idea of Ghost body hopping is a great one, and could have been used a little more effectively in the story, but did provide some interesting moments.Pikel and Ivan started to get a little annoying - the comedy duo starting to wear a little thin. Yes, Pikel says 'Oi' a lot, and Ivan is the gruff one and they bicker and fight all the time. We got it.Cadderly is going through a crisis of faith, and by the very nature of the journey - it is not a great one to read.Danica has one of the best moments when she is fighting Ghost over her body.
