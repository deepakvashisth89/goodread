You wouldn't think it from reading Vendetta, but, author Steve Shagan has a fairly impressive resume, at least within the world of film. He wrote the Academy Award nominated screenplays for 1973's Save the Tiger and 1976's Voyage of the Damned and was nominated for an Emmy for the script to 1996's Gotti. In his "About the Author" bio at the back of this book, he claims to be working on adapting Vendetta to the screen as well, but I can't say I'm surprised that effort didn't go anywhere. This is a bog standard 80s LA cop on the edge story. Shagan tries to spice things up by including extensive description of pornography and drug use, but it's only so much cheap lipstick on a pig.
