I came across this anthology when visiting Cambridge last year in a bookshop called “Bodies in the Bookshop”. They had the paperback copy there and I my intention was to read it on one of these Campion months of mine. Later on I found a hardback copy online and bought that too as I was rather worried about bending the spine of the paperback.This has been my first foray into the many Allingham short story collections I’ve become aware of and it was quite the good one to begin with. Albert Campion has risen to my favourite literary character very quickly over this year a half of being introduced to him. However, during these stories I feel like I understand Allingham much more as a writer as within these stories some are standalone. There was also a brilliant introduction at the beginning of the book, written by J.E. Morpurgo that was very interesting to read as it told me of Margery Allingham, the person first, and author second.I quite like short stories but I am looking forward to my next collection of Allingham’s more than most.
