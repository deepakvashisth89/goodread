An excellent read - Kirk manages to carefully and clearly explain the different outbreaks of violence that Colombia has had over the past century. Starting with La Violencia, which then led to the forming of several guerrilla groups, then as a response the birth of paramilitaries and mixed among them all were the drug cartels. The links drawn between them all were insightful and easy to follow.

I really enjoyed how the book has at its centre the people that Kirk interviewed and worked with during her time as a human rights worker in Colombia. It really humanised the atrocities and prevented the mass murders of entire villages from becoming statistics in a sea of countless accounts of the atrocities.

Finally, the role that the US has played, particularly in the “war on drugs” that focused on the (foreign) supply rather than investing in demand prevention at home. The vast amount of funding, the failure to enforce human rights conditions with aid and the failed attempts at eradicating the coca plant were all carefully explained. However, it wasn’t as big a focus of the book as I expected from the title and the blurb. It was heavily discussed in the opening and closing chapters but the biggest chunk of the book focused around the violence, hence my rating of 4 stars rather than 5.

All in all, it was a great ready and really informative - definitely a book I will be re reading as I continue to learn about the conflict in Colombia. A must read for understanding how the violence developed and morphed into lots of different beasts.
