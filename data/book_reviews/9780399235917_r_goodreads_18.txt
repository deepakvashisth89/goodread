Notes from a liar and her dog is one of the best books I have read through my reading life. The main character, Antonia has two perfect sisters that she is constantly being compared to, and on top of this her parents don't understand her. Antonia's dog is the only one in her family that understands her and when her dog gets sick, she is devastated. Antonia starts to lie to everyone at school and says that she is adopted and those are not her real parents. However, her art teacher soon realizes that she is lying and she tells her mother. The art teacher realizes that what she did was wrong, so she tries to make it right and help Antonia. The happy ending to this story teaches an important theme that you should always look towards the positives rather than the negatives because things don't always seem to be the way they are. I recommend this book to people who enjoy books with many different emotions and personalities.
