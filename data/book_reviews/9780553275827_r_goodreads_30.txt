A must read for all the military leaders. The book covers every aspect of military leadership and nuances of Platoon level opearations in great detail. The author brings how officer - man relationship gets nurtured in the battlefield like nowhere else.

Author writes about his transformation from a green soldier to a war veteran. He narrates the challenges which US army faced while countering the jungle warfare tactics in Vietnam. He brings out drills and lessons which are imperative today even after so much of technological advancements.

In the end he beautifully writes about how the bravery and sacrifices of an infantryman in battlefield go unnoticed. It is just you who know it and no one else. Everyone just look for political gains.

Must read for all the officers who are preparing themselves for war and veterans also to remember their old times.
