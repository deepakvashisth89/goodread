Picked this up at a lending library having not read the first one, but I don’t really think I needed to thanks to the briefing at the beginning. Pretty typical sci-fi stuff, having to slough through some sexism and military jargon to get to the good stuff. Having each chapter written by a different author made for natural changes in voice, which I enjoyed throughout. I might try to find the next one, hoping to see the disastrous blowout of internalized homophobia and suppressed sexual tension between the two characters in the chapter The Antagonist. (I know I probably won’t get to see it)
