Nobody captures the America of the 1920s and early 30s like F. Scott Fitzgerald - the greed, the snobbery, the decadence, the heartlessness, the rampant alcoholism, the optimism, the potential, and then the reckoning: the dark descent into the Great Depression. (The biggest and most glaring flaw in these stories of Americana is that he doesn’t touch on racial issues, however.)These short stories are a brilliant display from a giant of American literature. So glad I forced myself to pick this book up after possessing it since my long ago college days.
