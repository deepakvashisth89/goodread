This was one of the best books ive ever read in my life. The plot was very interesting. The book is about a man named Maceo Redfield who is in love with his best friend's girlfriend. Later on his best friend dies and the girl turns up missing. For many months he is in search of her. Through this journey he meets a lot of new people.I really enjoyed reading this book. i'm hoping there is a sequel to this book or something similar.
