Richard Peck has long been one of my favorite authors. He mostly wrote for kids and teens and the Blossom Culp series was one I read over and over as a kid. As a teen I loved his two adult novels, Amanda/Miranda and This Family of Women.
Until London Holiday showed up at the library's used book sale, I'd never heard of it. And it's delightful. Three high school friends, now around 40-ish, get together to go on a holiday to a little B&B run by the formidable Mrs. Smith-Porter. It's just dreamy.
Now I want to know just how Richard Peck got inside the minds of women so well? He's really remarkable. I might have to go re-read Amanda/Miranda (a much better Titanic story than the Titanic movie).
