Another hidden jewel of a book I have had on my bookshelf for YEARS but now that I finally have had some reflective time--out of the young kids at home years, no more crazy 13-14 hour days of grading, teaching, dealing with admin craziness--I totally love this book. I rarely give 5 stars but that doesn't mean I can't love a book with four stars! Ha, ha.

If you want to discover authors worth reading or just want short bites of thoughtful reflections, life stories, moments of turning, learning moments then this series is probably one for you. A friend gave it to me as gift, or was it one of my sisters? And I am so thankful!

Lovely stories with historical depth, personal hooks, melancholic in nature it is my book to read at the end of the day or a short snatch of interesting ideas. Still reading since I read so many books at one time but I highly recommend the series because they put one out every year. I almost think of it as a Harper's but just for essays.
