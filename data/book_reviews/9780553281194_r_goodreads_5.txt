Green Beret types sent on top-secret mission all the way into China to assassinate Chinese and North Vietnamese higher-ups during the Vietnam War. The book is written by authors with pseudonyms because, supposedly, this is a true story "that can only be told as a work of fiction," the jacket sleeve informs us. Nixon and crew are mentioned by name. As the team is crossing the border, Washington gets cold feet, orders "abort" on the mission and abandons them to their own devices; able to cite plausible deniability that the mission and its grunts do not exist, etc.Knowing the way the US operates, many elements of the story seem plausible; it also sounds influenced by "Mission Impossible" and "The Dirty Dozen."I fished this out of the trash of a local bookseller, and it sounds like a fun story.
