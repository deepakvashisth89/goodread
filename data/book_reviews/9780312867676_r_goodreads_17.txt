This book is fascinating. We spend the entire book following the life of Cerryl up until he becomes and solidifies his position as the High Mage of Fairhaven, the foreground of this story. Meanwhile, Dorrin is always in the background. This book forces you to contrast life as a Black as opposed to life as a White. Which one do you think is better? Who lives their life as stress free and as righteous as possible. Both are true to themselves, yet I feel that Cerryl is closer to the “better” way of life. Why is that? For me, its about how he lives with Order AND Chaos. He distances himself from the use of pure chaos, using order and getting assistance from Leyladin when he needs it. Something similar to what a “gray” mage would do, which is hinted as what he really is after the first 1/3rd of this book. He also systematically wipes clear all potential rivals in the guild until nothing stands in his way, but he does it with such poise and foresight. I am sure we find out what happens to him in later books. Also, at some point I recall Fairhaven being destroyed by Dorrin so that has to come into play as well (unless the prior book about Dorrin is enough explanation not to have to relive that portion of history). In any event, I have to take a short one month break in rereading theses to read the resurfacing of Dresden after at least 5 years. Ta ta!
