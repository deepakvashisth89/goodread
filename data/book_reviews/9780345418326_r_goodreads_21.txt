"Ned" is a British Intelligence Officer at the end of his career. He asks his old mentor, George Smiley, to give an address to some budding spies at Sarratt, the agent-training school and interrogation centre for the British foreign intelligence service.Smiley's address and conversations spark many memories for Ned and these comprise most of the book, so it's not a continuous story but more episodes from Ned's spying life, dealing with many characters from other Le Carre's other works including Bill Haydon.These tangential memories from Ned's past allow him to reflect on all that has happened in his spying life.
