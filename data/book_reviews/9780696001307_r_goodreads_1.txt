I didn’t know how different this was from the 1969 when I picked it up. It was another book from the bag of books for a quarter sale and I figured if I didn’t pick it up it was going to be trashed. This is the kind of stores most people don’t even realize is still in business because it looks abandoned from the outside (and pretty much from the inside, too) which is probably why they’re going out of business.There are, however, several differences. My favorite recipe from the 1969 book, Perfect Fried Chicken, isn’t in this. And the series of “Action Meals” such as a clambake, barbecue, fish fry, luau, and so on, were also not here. This book focuses more on kinds of dishes, starting with the ingredient (beef, pork, lamb, veal, variety meats, ground meat, frankfurters, and so on) and continuing with kinds of dishes (appetizers, casseroles, salads, sandwiches).It covers many of the same recipes, but with a slightly different focus and organization. The black and white photos are similar in quality but definitely have more of a sense of being vintage about them; the color photos, on the other hand, have that weird sense of almost being a colorized drawing. I don’t see any photos here that were reused in 1969.Something I assumed was a relic of older recipes turns out not to be so. This 1965 book contains the same orange chicken, called Sunday-best Chicken Orange here and Orange-Sauced Chicken in the 1969 version. The 1969 edition does not list salt, pepper, or fat in the ingredients, but calls them out in the instructions. The Sunday-best Chicken lists all three of those ingredients in the ingredients list.I have no sense of how or even if Better Homes & Gardens divided their books into series, or if they just haphazardly came out with new editions of each individual book. I know that I’m mixing several different styles in my collection, and end up mostly just keeping the first book I find that I like, since I’ve become used to where recipes are in it. That’s probably what I’ll do in this case as well, although reading through for this review it does seem like it’s at least as useful and fun as the one I already have.Even if it doesn’t show you how to host a luau.
