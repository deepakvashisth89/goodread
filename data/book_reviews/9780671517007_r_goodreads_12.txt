Somehow - and I thought it was impossible - this book made me love dogs even more. Thomas really gets to the heart of so much dog behavior without over-anthropomorphizing.She lived with a bajillion dogs. And for a while she lived in the snow and observed (and sorta lived with) a bunch of wolves in Northern Canada. And she explains the "whys" of so much of what dogs do - their relationships with each other and their relationships with humans. She love dogs even more than me and it feels like she gets them in a way that I hope to someday even come close to.I don't even know what to say about this book except: love dogs? read this book! (I feel like Reading Rainbow).[tl;dr: doggies, doggies, doggies, wolves, doggies.]
