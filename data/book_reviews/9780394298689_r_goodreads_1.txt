A few Christmases ago, one of my dear friends sent me this story, cut from the anthology, bound into a new little book, saying I would love it. I lost it behind a shelf for all of those months in between and only rediscovered it this Christmas. It felt appropriate to read it on the day she'd intended, even if it wasn't the year.And I did love it. What a delightful tale on driving, time, magic, and regret.
