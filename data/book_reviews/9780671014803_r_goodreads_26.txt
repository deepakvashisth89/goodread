Why so popular?After having finally ticked this off my bucket list, I'm going to say it's because it has some of the most poppin-off-the-pages, stick-to-your-ribs, quotable lines that I've ever read.I didn't much like the characters past their childhoods, I wasn't much wrapped up in the late 1700s-early 1800s soap opera plot, and so I couldn't bring myself to much care about how any of their nonsense wrapped itself up.BUT!"Whatever our souls are made of, his and mine are the same."Getthefuckoutofhere. That one line slapped me right on the ass and left a handprint. And that wasn't the only turn of phrase that woke me up from my nap.So while I may never find Heathcliff to be a romantic bad boy or Cathy worthy to emulate, I can definitely see the appeal of this book.Horrible people need love, too.<--is what I tell my husband when he's sick of my shitAnd while I may always be more of an Austen Girl, I get why this is an enduring classic.Recommended at least once in your life.
