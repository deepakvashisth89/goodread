a fairly interesting overview of the events and dynamics that led up to the original French uprising in 1789 to start off the Revolution. It wasn't the most engaging book, but towards the end there were less discussions of aristocratic politicking and long lists of French Assemblymen, instead shifting more to a focus on the economic forces and social conditions of the French population. I learned less about the general justifications/criticisms of the revolution as the introduction led me to believe that I would, but did get a general explanation for why certain things happened the way they did. The author seems to present the events in a way which suggest that there were multiple steps where the revolution could have been halted. Overall an interesting survey which could probably be dissected much further.
