Mark has loved being in love with his partners, but doesn't seem to get married to any of his partners.
He finds love with all these women, he can't find the right one. He just needs a woman to fall in love with and settle down, especially after leaving 5 brides at the altar. He finds Charlie McPherson, a woman he wants to marry. He knows Charlie is the one!
I really enjoyed this, and even I thought he wasn't going to marry Charlie, but to flee from the altar instead - but he didn't! Enjoyable reading. 4☆
