While I see the point that some people make about it providing 'stock' characters, I do not think that is the author's intention. She presents several aspects of people: traits, relationships, etc. It is up to the writer to use the book as a resource to create a complex character. It could add amazing depth and back history and helps to give clues on how people respond to grief, love, good/poor family relationships. I read it cover to cover, and I found it interesting. I look forward to using it as a resource.
