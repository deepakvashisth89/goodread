I had some high hopes for a book with such a funny title/cover.But! It felt really outdated (Pub 1993, with some articles from 1986). Lots of simplistic rants and random stories with not a real point or laugh to be found. But flawed logic and ridiculous theories abound. There's a few good points and bits of good advice, but they are not novel.Some parts that annoyed:"If you want to have a baby on your own, you have to fight your very nature...You must overcome every primitive instinct.""If she doesn't sleep with you after the fifth the fifth date she's either actually still in high school, born again, or not interested, no matter what she says.""Wearing make-up is asking for approval...an apology for our actual faces...means 'I am a sexual object of prey. Come after me.'"Maybe I'm too young to tolerate the dated-ness that's soaked into such a feminist book, but I don't think so.
