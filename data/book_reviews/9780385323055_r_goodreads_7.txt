A lot of moms come into the store to get this for their daughters who saw the movie, so I need to state up front that THE BOOK IS NOT LIKE THE MOVIE.

The book is wrong on so many levels that I don't know where to start. Unlike in the film, main character and narrator Vivian is a calculating and hypersexualized 16-year-old girl. She is mean-spirited and nasty to others, and frankly, I didn't find much about her to like for a good deal of the book. Her character is not alone in that, as with the exception of Aiden, the human boy Vivian wants to bed, every character in the novel behaves like a rapaciously sex-starved porn star locked permanently in his/her 40's. Vivian seems to be intended as the young conscience of the book, alone and confused and trying to find her way, but she fails miserably as, when the chips are down, she behaves just as greedily and selfishly as the rest of them. Annette Curtis Klause seems to hold some seriously unhealthy views of what love is and the appropriateness of certain relationships.

The book takes a few interesting turns, but for the most part, I found it to be nothing more than a story of instant gratification run violently and horribly amok. Not to mention the incredibly inappropriate relationship between the adolescent Vivian and Gabriel, the adult head of her werewolf pack, who spends most of the book bedding both Vivian's mother and another woman in the pack, while literally sniffing after Vivian, until with the book's resolution, he finally makes her his permanent property – with her happy acceptance. As a reader over the age of 18 and with some life experience, it really creeped me out, and I can't tell you how shocked I was to google Klause this morning and find out the woman is not only a librarian who "specializes" in young adult fiction, but is actually head of children's services at a Maryland community library. I would not want this woman recommending books to my children. Nor will I recommend her books to my customers. Blood and Chocolate is one of the worst-written, most misogynistic and inappropriate books I've ever had the misfortune to read.
