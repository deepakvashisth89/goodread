I always enjoy seeing Egypt through the eyes of Christian Jacq.I've said before how much I wish I could read his works in their native French. A translation can easily detract from the feel of a novel, and there were a few times in this book where I paused; thrown by a word choice or sentence structure. I maintain that the translation style of Mary Feeney in Jacq'sRamsesseries was superior, despite a few anachronistic idioms here and there, but The Tutankhamun Affair read well overall.To be fair, it's hard to go wrong when you put me and Egypt together.
