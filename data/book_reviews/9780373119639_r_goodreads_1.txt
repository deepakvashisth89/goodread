Re Ultimate Temptation - Sara Craven's contribution to theNanny Wanted seriesis a remarkably nematode H coupled with the most martyrish of HP h's.If you like your hero's mean and your heroine's doormats with a tiny hint of a spine, this is a vintage angst fest just for you. We get not one, but two nematode slime swilling Evil OW and combine that with the sewage slurping relative trope, so there is a lot of angsty vintage goodness in this one to go around.The story starts with our recently jilted h taking a dubious house-sharing trip to Italy. She doesn't know the women she will be sharing the house with very well, they are the hard partying and out to score type, but she has time off from her ad agency job and is getting over being dumped by her long-time fiance for a woman who has more money and style than she will ever have.Yes, our h is a nice, modest good girl at heart and only decided to join the other women as a last minute cancellation left the group with an opening and the h desperately needed an escape from the pitying looks of her friends and family.So one drunken plane trip with the rest of the group later, the sober h is a bit perturbed that the villa they are staying at is clearly a luxury, antique filled showplace and the villa renter is demanding payment in cash.Still the h does the usual HP h tourist things and there is a dramatic purse snatching moment where the h is rescued by a tall, dark and handsome Italian.Then the rest of the ladies in the group decide to throw a party and the h gets hit on by tons and tons of very unsavory types. The h is very uncomfortable and trying to escape, when she runs straight into her rescuer tall, dark and handsome Italian.He looks at her like he wants to grope her up too and instantly makes certain tart harlot assumptions based on the dress the h is wearing that she borrowed from one of the other women.The h is astounded to learn that her handsome sorta rescuer is the REAL owner of the villa, the guy who rented the place to them is the nephew of the housekeeper. Naturally the H throws everyone but the h out and she is soon shanghaied into being a nanny for the H's sister.Ostensibly this arrangement is to pay the H back for the damage the furniture suffered during the party the other women held, but we all know it is because the H is just waiting to get his hands all over the innocent h.So the h reluctantly agrees to nanny the H's sister's children. The complications come in when it becomes obvious that the children's mother is a useless twit and the H's stepmother and her protegee are viperous scum gulpers.The little daughter of the sister is made a scapegoat for abuse by the H's villainous step-mother and the H refuses to do anything about it. The h steps up as a defender for the little girl, in between getting groped by the H and attacked by the OW.SC ups the drama stakes by having the second OW show up, with the h's ex fiance in tow. Which of course leads to more confrontations and H tribulations. In fact there are multiple wanna be OM in this and every single one of them is a gropey, grabby attacker of the h.The H uses these scenes with OM that he walks in on to heap ever increasing amounts of tart shaming upon the h, cause of course she MUST be encouraging all of this. Even as the H makes a point of flaunting the stepmother's protegee OW, (who is engaged to the h's ex,) right in front of the h's face.There is a ton of bad behavior from everyone but the h and the little girl, when it is time for the big final explosion. The big finale is both OW conspire to make it look like the h stole a family heirloom ring from the evil step mother. The h is afraid that the little girl took it and hid it her room as an act of spiteful retaliation against her bad treatment by the rest of the family, so she doesn't defend herself from the accusations.The h is thrown out by the evil stepmother and after seeing the H with the other protegee evil OW, goes to board her plane back to England. She is heartbroken and hurt, because the H's unique way of tart shaming and gropey hands moves has completely won her heart in two days or so.The H shows up at the very last minute to declare that he knows the whole accusation of theft was a big set up. He has banished the evil OW, both the protege and the step mother, his sister's husband has returned to sort out his family and he loves the h and wants to marry her for the big HEA.The h is soon won over by his roofie kissing moves and the ex-fiance was dumped and sent back to the HP mists by the evil OW - or not as the OW seems to have lost out on the H and may call him up for a rematch.Either way the H and h have made their true love avowals forever, the h will give up her career and become the full time handmaid and broodmare wife of the H, with time set aside to cater to the sister and nanny her children, along with her own.SC calls this a win and we leave the two of them getting ready to lurve it up in purple passion bliss for another interesting SC day at the HP office.So the love story wasn't unusual, but I liked all the old skool vintage touches in this one.
