This might be my least favorite of Herriot's books, yet I still enjoyed it a good deal!There's plenty to like. Lots of retread of the usual heartwarming animal-and-owner stories. These contain the Herriot homespun narrative style readers have come to know and love. He may not have been the most elegant of wordsmiths, but the man knew how to spin ripping yarns about saving the lives of pigs, goats, cows, cats and dogs.However, so-so anecdotal sketches about Herriot's trips abroad, traveling on ships and planes in the care of transported herds, fill out the chapters between the good stuff. These don't usually pack the plot punch of the farm stories he's famous for. They tend to be little more than light reminiscences, like narrated photo albums of family vacations.Still, this is a good read for anyone who's read the previous books and is hankering for more.
