When I first started this book, I was like, 'Oh wow, I am not following.' it just felt very hard to read and understand. The first chapter was a doozy. But as it went on, wow it became beautiful. Chastity and Friendship were my favorites, I think.

I learned a lot, as well. Any 'natural love' that tries to become a god in our lives quickly turns into a demon. That's what he says. I couldn't agree more, but I'd also never heard it said like that before. Very impactful. And I think that can be said about anything that becomes an idol in our lives and tries to take God's place, of course.

I'd encourage anyone to pick this up, especially if you enjoy Lewis's writings. I still think the first chapter was rough to get through, but after that it's really beautiful. And ultimately points to the Ultimate Love found only in Jesus Christ. And that is what every heart is seeking.
