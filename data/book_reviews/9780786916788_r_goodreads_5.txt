This one lost the magic that had me glued to the first book (Doom Brigade). There is no grand adventure like in the first book and the funny bickering dwarves are replaced by a band of female draconians as they get their chops, which unfortunately, were not nearly as entertaining. It reads a lot like a slice-of-life/side adventure that one would expect to read in a series of many such adventures, but this being the second and final book in the Kang's Regiment series feels pretty underwhelming.It still retains a lot of the light-hearted charm from the first entry, but a lot of the sense of wonder and journey is lost, as the band is holed up in a fortress under siege for the majority of the book. The antagonist ends up being who you think it will be (I was desperately hoping for a bad guy reveal bait-and-switch near the end), while some other characters are just plainly sacrificed once they serve their plot purposes.If you enjoyed Doom Brigade and want more, then this won't ruin your day, but it just feels like a missed opportunity considering everything that the first book contained.
