3/4 DNF… having been raised on the Bible, I found the theories presented here very interesting. Friedman has a well thought out hypothesis and backs it up with a lot of plausible examples. The book held my interest, but I had to return it before I could complete the last few chapters. This is a decent book for those who take the Bible seriously as it does not attack or try to disprove the Bible in anyway, just adds some new perspectives.
