The Sound and the Furyshould be read attentively, step by step – one should crack every sentence like a nutshell to get to a sweet kernel – only then the novel will be enjoyable. Otherwise it will remain just “a tale told by an idiot, full of sound and fury, signifying nothing.”Some days fly… Some days crawl… Some days are sunny… Some days are rainy…Clocks slay time... time is dead as long as it is being clicked off by little wheels; only when the clock stops does time come to life.Time doesn’t make us move. We make time move.
