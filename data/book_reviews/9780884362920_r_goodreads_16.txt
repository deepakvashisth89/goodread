Although the title indicates this is probably a murder mystery, the novel is more noir. As per the title, Signora Giulia disappears. Her husband is 20 years older than she is and he's more interested in his law practice than his wife. Apparently he has lost interest in any amorous activities. Everyone, for at least 3 years, believes she has run off with a lover. And then sort of creepy things begin to happen.This is a very short novel and is the perfect length for the content. Apparently it was first published in a magazine in serial form. Pushkin Vertigo is to be applauded for getting it back in print for twenty-first century audiences. The translation is very good and I very much enjoyed the short time I spent with it. Only because of its brevity am I finding it toward the lower end of my 4-star group.
