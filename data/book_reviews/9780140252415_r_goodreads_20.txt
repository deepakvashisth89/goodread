I love Wallace Stegner BUT his characterization of a woman is sadly lacking. Even really really good writers who are straight white dudes probably shouldn’t try to take on a female pov. You think you know, but guess what? You don’t! And close is almost worse than way off.In other ways this novel got pretty bogged down, lots of hand wringing, not a lot of plot. An MC who was apparently rich and bored, so cheated on her husband and almost immediately regretted it. I got the feeling WS didn’t know what to do with her. There was a moment at the end where she practically throws herself at her best friend’s husband which was absolutely preposterous.I will say I’m relieved to have finished, though I didn’t hate it.
