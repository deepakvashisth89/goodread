Oh man, this was good.I read and enjoyed "Goat Dance" but it didn't hold together as a narrative. The pieces didn't connect as well as they could have this. This one...oh do they ever. The story goes right for the parts of the subconscious that feel fear the most and doesn't let go. I also liked the couple who are central to the story. Their struggle to keep their marriage going felt very real, and I was rooting for them from beginning to end.
