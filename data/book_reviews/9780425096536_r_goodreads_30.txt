I have my personal fascination about Mr J.Paul Getty, which is why my comment on this book will certainly be biased. I am glad that he authored this autobiography just in time, i got to have a glimpse into his life, he thoughts and what really was about all those hypes and rumors.I don't think we have man like this in this new age. People are different now, gentlemen of the old times are simply fascinating.Apart from looking at his wealth, it's worth to take a good look at how much he had sacrificed, how hard he had worked to build his business empire. I was amazed by his open-mindedness, the willingness to compromise in the Neutral Zone deal, the willingness to accept the counter-party's difference of culture, language and so on. I do think it takes a big heart to own a big business. Wealth like this is not meant for the petty-minded. He clearly showed me exactly the virtues and characters that I have been fascinated about him all along.
