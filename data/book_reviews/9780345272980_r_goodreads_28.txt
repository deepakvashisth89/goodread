Deeply personal memoire about the start of the Vietnam war from a young officer's perspective, bookended by his return to Vietnam as a correspondent at the end of the war. Focus on avoiding ambushes and booby traps instead of the standard battles they were trained for.This book, along with a few others published just after the war, is when memoires about Vietnam turned the corner from reporting to literature. Caputo's preface talks about that and more, and is nearly as valuable as the rest of the book.I've seen this as recommended reading for schools now; the topic was a little too close when I was in school. This appeared in many top lists of books on Vietnam, it was apparently filmed as a miniseries in the 1980s. On that, I'll pass - but will highly recommend this book.
