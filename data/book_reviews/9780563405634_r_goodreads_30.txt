The Eight Doctors. The beginning of the BBC EDA Range.I see quite a lot of negativity with this book. People say it's an embarrassment by Terrance Dicks just like Warmonger and "Vampire Science" is the one to really start off the range. Fair enough. I say that with Exodus with the Virgin New Adventures.I may have an unpopular opinion with this book but I disagree with the hate. I think this is a very fun read and a wonderful trip down classic memory lane. It seems people just don't like what it was going for and say it was a rubbish attempt to do a trip down memory lane.Sure this book is far from perfect but all the one star reviews and hate I personally disagree with. It's not as good when compared to the works of Timewyrm: Exodus which is a real cracker but still an easy and fun read.The Doctor gets amnesia (Surprised his brain didn't melt during the EDA's) by a trap set by the master and The 8th Doctor needs to collect his memories back by meeting all his previous incarnations.Sam Jones is introduced and it's nothing to talk home about as it's very little and all the character building and what not is in Vampire Science.The story is essentially set as seven short stories.The 1st Doctor one was rushed and could of done being longer. The 2nd and 3rd Doctor one was good. Liked the Master chase which bridges the gap of The Sea Devils and The Time Monster.The 4th Doctor one was my favourite out of them all and the 5th Doctor one was good to.However, the Sixth Doctor one was pretty onerous to get through. Difficult and tedious to read. The 7th Doctor one was a little silly. Took me two days to read the book. Had a rest because I was getting a little bored the Sixth Doctor story line but I enjoyed this book quite a lot. This book really splits the crowd. If you're looking for an easy, simple, fun and a classic trip down memory lane then I can see you liking it.Overall, an Underrated novel and I don't really see the hate. It's not on the level of bad with Timewyrm: Genesys.8/10.I reviewed this book on YouTube if anyone is interested.In-depth review on this book on YouTube:https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OPnDc...
