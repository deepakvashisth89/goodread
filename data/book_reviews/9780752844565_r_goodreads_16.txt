Filled with factual errors, made up and/or anonymous quotes, this biography is surreal. People who knew Kurt like Jack Endino or Charles Peterson are on record saying they DID meet Christopher Sandford, but that he didn't record anything, and that he fabricated events that never happened. The book works as fiction : it's so bad it becomes strangely entertaining to the scholar type of fan in a strange kind of way. This biography is problematic though, as a lot of its contents is wrong, fake, and because most people in the general public will read just one book on a specific artist and stop there. Coherence isn't the author's forte either : if Sandford's Kurt had been an actual person, he would have been diagnosed with multiple personality disorder. And I'm not one of those who will paint a flawless, angelic picture of Kurt Cobain - it's just that Sandford's Cobain doesn't match the one found in every existing book.It's not coherent, and also very repetitive (the book could have used an editor).

Christopher Sandford went as far as copying Albert Goldman's "Lives of John Lennon" (yes! yes! From one controversial, overwriting scribe to another!). Page 58 (hard cover), Sandford has Kurt heckling "Betty! I wanna suck your pussy!" to a local musician on stage and getting thrown out of a club. That's straight from John Lennon's "lost weekend". In Goldman's book you have the exact same incident - except that in the case of John Lennon, it was real. Here's the actual quote from Goldman's book : "When Annie Peebles came out on the stage,- John was sittin' up on top of the back of the booth. Annie was sort of foxy-lookin', so John starts hollerin', 'Annie! Annie!' He figured she'd recognize him, but with the stage lights in her eyes, he must have sounded like any drunk. So when she doesn't answer him, he starts hollerin', 'Annie! I wanna suck your pussy!' Next thing I remember is these two big arms grabbed me around the shoulders from behind. Picked me up and carried me out. I ended up on the curb, and there was John!" (as told by Harry Nillsson).

That alone should prove that Sandford's bio is not honest.
