This book was pretty good, I thought. It's about this guy, Mage to his friends, who met this girl by a train station. She's upset, crying, and drop dead gorgeous. As a drifter, Mage often followed whims. He talked to this girl, gave her money, took her picture, and when she offered, took the key to the apartment that she wouldn't need anymore. And she takes the next train out. After being trapped in a laundromat with a hired guy with a machine gun, Mage gets out of the next to nothing town he was in. Meeting up with friends from around the country, he battles ancient japanese monsters and tries to find the girl who left him with all his troubles.
