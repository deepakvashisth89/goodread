Thoughtful, detailed, extremely poignant and loving biography of one of my favorite artists. I have always been fascinated and moved by her photography, but I was surprised to find Arbus so relatable, in terms of her struggle between the power of her inner world and outside reality. It was really heartbreaking to realize that this inability to negotiate with her demons is what ultimately caused her death. I really appreciated the author's insights into her childhood, her relationships, and adult conceptions of the world. I feel I will now use this context to view her portraiture in a more thorough way, not just to be shocked by the perversity of the images. It speaks to the time period she lived in that Arbus was devastated to be seen as merely a photographer of "freaks." This was a cheapening and pigeon-holing of what she was trying to accomplish as an artist, and I think her work would've been viewed very differently today -- for better or worse, who's to say.
