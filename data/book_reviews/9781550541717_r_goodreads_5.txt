Well, I guess I gave this book 3 stars because I actually got to finish it. Still, it was hard to do so.I found the character of Lady particularly annoying. She seemed vain, disorientated and to some extent selfish. I do believe her character lacked more empathy and it was really hard to get attached to her. My favorite characters were Mortimer Max and Julius. First Mortimer, who exhibited this kind cynicism that made him somehow interesting. Then there was Max. Although he showed some kind of resemblance to the character of Lady, the idea that he was loved and respected by all stuck with me. Finally, Julius who was, in my opinion, the best-achieved character of them all. His feelings and his battles seemed more relatable to everyday life and therefore one could get attached to his persona.Overall, not one of my favorite books, even if it served me as a refresh to some of the concepts that I had learned in my art classes.
