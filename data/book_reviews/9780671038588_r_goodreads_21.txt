I am about 20% in the book and I have to ask: "Where are you going with this story?" I find that to be very true many Diane Carey novels in the Star Trek universe.

After finished reading it, I found that she started with the very common Star Trek trope: a rogue scientist or group of scientists invent X! - a device that will "change the balance of power in the quadrant." Whenever I hear that phrase spoken in any of the Star Trek franchises, I want to take a shot. Two if it's Picard who says it. Our heroes must stop that scientists by 1) killing them; 2) steal the device back; 3) neutralize the device if #2 is not possible; or 4) all of the above. You can use this trope in any franchise, change out the characters, and it will still work.

However, Carey uses existing original characters from her previous work and this book serves as an unofficial sequel. Kirk and Spock take a backseat but McCoy takes a much more prominent if secondary role.

From a storytelling standing, the pacing is good and the tensions between the characters are natural. Where I have issues is her depiction of some Vulcans in the 23rd century post Vulcan Reformation of the ENT era being okay as weapons officers. Hmmm.... Nevertheless, our heroes - in our case, heroine, win and our X! stays out of enemy hands.
