Oh no, not another Star Trek Novel!Oh yeah, cause I am planning to read as many as I can in this lifetime :))))Written in the precise style of a TNG TV episode, with plot A and plot B happening simultaneously, I was not very hooked on this split because while plot A was really promising, plot B seemed to me to be just a filler using the Prime Directive as a pretext for some common action in the background of plot A from which it's stealing way too much time because plot A is way better and interesting.But this is my just own opinion and the book is still sufficiently satisfying in order to provide a couple of hours of entertaining reading.
