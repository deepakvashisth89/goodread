Zen Catholicism is an interfaith perspective of comparing Zen Buddhism and Eastern religions with Catholicism. The writer tries to compare Catholicism with its rich religious history and compares it with Zen Buddhism. There are lots of points of interest including the references to Yin Yang philosophy which technically isn't actually found in Zen Buddhism but is found in Taoism. Indeed, Taoism is mentioned multiple times throughout the book.The descriptions of Zen teachers and their philosophical insights causes pause for thought by the reader. The book does have many different references throughout the book to the church traditions of Catholicism. And there is a humbleness in understanding different faith backgrounds. There is definitely a good understanding of how Buddhist ideas of the self can shape a less atomistic world view focused on the self.Recommended for those who are interested in interfaith dialogue and discussion.
