Babbitt reminds me of Pink Floyd's Comfortably Numb - we all spin a cocoon of 'becoming' around ourselves and go about our life...but as time goes by the cocoon is not always transformative, but binding. At some point in everyone's life the cocoon of what we wanted to 'become' becomes the web that traps who we 'are'. For most of us 'Comfortably Numb' sneaks up through the decades; only then do we realize our 'butterfly summer' passed by us long ago. Babbitt is the 'everyman' who never ponders this: a Socratic warning to examine your life before the living becomes worthless.
