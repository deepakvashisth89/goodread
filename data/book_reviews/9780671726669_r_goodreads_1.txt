THE BETTY BRODERICK CASE
I read this book years ago before l was on goodreads! Due to an important update l happened to see on the internet on May 8 2026 l decided to reread the book the fast way (reading only the most important passages to refresh my memory) and write this review.

Betty was an American woman of Italian descent who was born in 1947. At 17 she met Dan Broderick at the university of Notre Dame. He was 21 years old and about to attend medical school.
Dan relentlessly pursued beautiful Betty, and the couple married in 1969. Betty returned from her honeymoon pregnant.

Dan completed his Medical Doctor degree and decided to go to law school. Betty was working to support him all these years and the family was living below the poverty line.
He graduated law school in 1973 and the family relocated to San Diego where Dan was hired by a big law firm.
Betty continued working selling Tupperware and Avon cosmetics, while raising the children.
Dan was an absent father and also an absent husband.
Soon Dan started his own law firm and was now making millions of dollars. The couple was now associating with the high society. They became high society themselves in San Diego.

In 1982 Dan got romantically involved with his 21 year old assistant, Linda.
Betty suspected it but Dan denied it over and over again and continued to gaslight her bringing her to the brink of insanity.
A few years later Dan moved out of the house and moved into a multi million stately house he bought for him and Linda, and he filed for a divorce.
Betty had to move to a rental house, and that was when the War Of The Roses began.

Dan was now the president of the San Diego Bar Association. No lawyer wanted to go against him that's why no lawyer would agree to represent Betty in court.
She had to represent herself and she made a mess of it.
Dan knew every legal trick in the book and used it against Betty, from getting what was due to her in assets, to getting custody of the children. Betty ended up owing Dan money.

Dan sold their house without her knowledge or consent. That made Betty mad and in a state of extreme anger she drove her car through his front door. She got arrested and lost custody of the younger children.
Dan drove Betty over the edge and she was fighting back in an erratic manner.
Even Linda was sending her leaflets for weight loss programs and wrinkle creams, mocking Betty's extra kilos and her age.

Dan and Linda got married and the war between the ex couple intensified. They both did their best to make each other miserable. It was a whole lot of crazy!
One night in 1989 Betty stole her daughter's keys, went to Dan and Linda's house and shot them while they were sleeping. She then gave herself up.

Betty was tried but the jury hung unable to reach a unanimous verdict. She was convicted the second time and sentenced to 30 years to life.
Feminist groups supported her from the start. She was an icon to them.

A few days ago l watched a news video on YouTube. Betty Broderick died in prison from natural causes surrounded by her children. She was 78 years old.
I found it curious that all the comments under the video were supportive of her and so very loving 🤷‍♀️

The book is detailed, a thorough account of the case. It is a little biased in favour of Betty, and a little biased against Dan and Linda.
The author considered Betty an emotionally battered woman. Betty was diagnosed with BPD, her character is particularly complex.
Betty always claimed that Dan and Linda drove her to murder.
