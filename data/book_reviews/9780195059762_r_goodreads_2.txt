This is JUST the kind of book on First Ladies I’ve been looking for!! Loved the format; very informational without being dull. The unofficial role of First Lady is so unique and it was cool to read about how each woman approached it. Helen Herron Taft was my favorite hands down, but Dolley Madison, Julia Dent Grant, Edith Wilson, Grace Coolidge, Lady Bird Johnson, and Betty Ford are up there for sure. I wouldn’t want to be a First Lady, but man—to have that kind of platform for good would be amazing. Also, it was thoroughly disappointing to read about how many Presidents were not faithful to their wives, and how many of those wives responded graciously and continued to support, and even defend, their husbands. That was a somewhat consistent theme throughout the book, and I really wish it weren’t. Other than that, LOVED THIS!!!
