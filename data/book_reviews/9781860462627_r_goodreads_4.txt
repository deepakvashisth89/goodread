Monika Fagerholm as her very own prose-style and her very own way of structuring a novel. That can't be said for many authors. And it's beautiful to read, but you have to stop asking yourselves questions all the time and just go with it and experience the book. This is the second book I read of Fagerholm and I do love this style of hers. However, once I'd read the book I found myself wondering what the point was. Where did this book go? Where did it begin and where did it end? Again, I'm not sure I'm supposed to be asking myself these questions, but in some ways there has to be a point to the reading experience and I just can't work out what it was with this book, which made it a little bit dull.
