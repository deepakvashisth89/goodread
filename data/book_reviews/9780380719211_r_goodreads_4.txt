Quiller in the UK, Berlin and Algeria in pursuit of a terrorist plot involving a commercial airliner, with the previous year’s Lockerbie bombing fresh in the public consciousness. Another solid but largely undistinguished Quiller installment, with one exception. The exception is thatHall anticipated the use of jihadi-piloted commercial airliners as guided missiles against high value targets in 1992, nine years before 9/11. That is enough to raise the rating to four stars.
