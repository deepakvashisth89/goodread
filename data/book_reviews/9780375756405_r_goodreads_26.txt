This book succeeded in 2 specific respects. Firstly, it demonstrated the extraordinary lengths to which a human chan chooses to go in pursuit of an ideal (Alpinism). Secondly, it embraces that deeply fundamental emotional response to embracing the natural world in all its glory.

As someone who has embraced the beauty and sense of fulfilment of walking the hills for pleasure, it is so satisfying to hear a more erudite description of the wonder which the natural landscape can elect in a human being.

What made this book such a delight to read was not the technicalities of climbing which are covered at length, but the indomitable spirit if a man driven to test his physical boundaries almost to the very limit, and yet who has the humility to live by his own moral standards and beliefs around the purity of the art of rock climbing.
