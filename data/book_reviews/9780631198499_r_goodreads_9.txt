For context, I read the 7th edition of the book. The book itself provides a brief overview of the differing views surrounding various topics of Christian Theology. The author discusses these views constantly citing the theologians keenly associated with the development of that specific outlook. I appreciate how no effort is made to convince the reader of a specific viewpoint with only the facts being plainly stated. Overall, it was a good read. It didn't provide me with any new insight, but helped me systematically categorize what I had previously known.
