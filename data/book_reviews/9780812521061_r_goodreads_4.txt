3.75 starsI enjoyed this one, certainly a bit shorter and tamer than books I have read from layman in the past but the tension was good and the kills satisfying. Also had quite a unique premise, very reminiscent of the late 90s meta horror craze. Would deffo recommend.Dani is a special effects artist for Hollywood horror movies. An obsessed fan starts stalking her house wanting to be her apprentice so she decides to humour him so the stalking will stop. But tony takes this the wrong way and becomes infatuated. Just how far will he take his obsession? Will Dani be able to survive her ultimate fan?
