The book that made me a reader. When I first read it, it was newly published, recommended to me by my English teacher. Re-reading 32 years later and it is still as good. A biting satire that pulls apart modern religions whilst being hilariously funny. It is also remarkable that although it pokes fun at the absurdity of religion, it is not nasty or insulting. A proper classic from a true master. If anyone wants my views on religion, I can simply point them towards this book. Wonderful.
