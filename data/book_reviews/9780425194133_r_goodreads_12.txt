The Mushroom Man reveals Sophie Powell's potential as a writer: imagination, insight, whimsy, language(!), but the story doesn't quite come together. Think--independent film set in the country, magical pixie children in triplicate, or in the form of smitten adolescent couples. I'm not sure what really happens in this plot--child goes missing, shows up again, goes missing again. These disappearances set the stage for the adults to realize the truth in the people around them and in their relationships. It started to get wobbly for me when the magic pixie children somehow summoned a cadre of other kids to help--seemed a little too Disney there. This writer is one to watch.
