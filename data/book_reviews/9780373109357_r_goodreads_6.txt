"The Impossible Woman" is the story of Judy and MalcolmA faery lookalike but tomboy from heart heroine Judy, who owns a landscape gardening company meets the super rich and successful architect Malcolm Stewart in an identity confusion, and falls for him. But as wild attraction flares between them, does he only want her body, or will she be able to convince him to give in to institution he dreads the most..marriage?Likable, strong heroine, confused hero, loads of passion, supportive family, ample misunderstandings, a promise not to let go and a HEA.I would have rated it higher if the heroine had stuck to her beliefs and not considered giving in to the hero's passionate pleadings.Safe3/5
