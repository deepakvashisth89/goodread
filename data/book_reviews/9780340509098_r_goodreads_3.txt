Well that was a quick romp of a read. After reading ‘Ash’ the authors last work some months back it was time to read another.This story has a great lead character. A paparazzo called Joseph Creed with absolutely no redeeming features. The book starts with him photographing a man who was hanged years ago desecrating a grave. This scene reminded me of a scene in the horror film ‘Phantasm.’The book ends with a Satanic gathering not unlike that in Dennis Wheatley’s ‘To the Devil a Daughter.’ But that said the characters are all rich and the story is humorous as well as scarey. Creed comes across well as a wise cracking pap.
