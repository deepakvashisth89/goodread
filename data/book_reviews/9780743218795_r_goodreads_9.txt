My sister Sue who works in a public library gave me this book about 10 years ago. My wife and I raised our family in Chicago and bought a summer home in Wisconsin. We have since both retired and made Wisconsin our permanent home. I have read this book once a year since she gave it to me! Easy and very humorous book. We have a lot of parallels in our transition to country living. Puts a smile on my face every time I read it! Thanks Sue!
