Laurel and Brett marry as a joke to celebrate a friend's birthday, but an unexpected surprise links them together forever. Down on her luck, Laurel asks Brett for a job, he wants to marry her for real, but she won't marry for anything else than love. Spending time together, going through a few ups and downs, and the closeness brings them together. But a misunderstanding could tear them apart. Will they find a way back to each other and open their hearts to forever?
