My husband was given this book while on a trip. It’s a collection of sweet reminiscences from mainly the late 19th century into the early 20th century of imaginative play in gardens and forests that line the edges of yards. I was unsure what to expect from the book, but I was pleasantly surprised. I even look at some of the plants in my garden a little differently now. It reminded me a lot of my grandmother, combing through my grandmother’s peas in the summer for ripe pods, and listening to her talk about the faces she could see in the petals of pansies.
