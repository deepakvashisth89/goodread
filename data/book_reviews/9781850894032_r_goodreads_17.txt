Two stars ⭐️⭐️

This was a school read and is about Greek mythology. It was written during the late 1800s it felt very dry and hard to follow. Now I’m interested in Greek mythology when it comes to a story like Percy Jackson. But in this case, it felt like I had to force myself to sit down and read it. If you’re very into Greek mythology, I might recommend this to you but personally, I did not enjoy it.
