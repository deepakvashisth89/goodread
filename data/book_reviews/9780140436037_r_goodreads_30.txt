I must have your soul; must have it mine, and mine for ever. (mu hahahahah)Written in 1794, The Monk is a story of forbidden desires, temptation, moral decay, debauchery, bandits, sorcery, evil nuns, good nuns, ghost nuns, religious hypocrisy, love, and lucifer. It’s the far side of completely bonkers, and for the most part it is astonishingly good, especially the ending, the ending is brilliant. Even though some of it feels incredibly modern, I did find some of the prose quite difficult to read, and I think I enjoyed the bits with Raymond & Lorenzo more than I did the manic ramblings of Abromsio the monk and his temptress Matilda. At times it almost felt like reading two completely different books, and it’s probably the reason it took me 7 days to read these 320 pages.I don’t read a lot of classic literature, I find it a tad intimidating, but I plan to change that this year. Any issues I had with this book are entirely down to me. Solid 4
