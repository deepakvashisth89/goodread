So it it went, a step at a time...it came about that I found myself--after the time span of a mere two weeks--in a setup that would be judged, by the people I know, as pathological.

He's moving me, edging me, step by careful step--nothing drunken about it--there goes one limit, another one, limits falling by the wayside. I'm afloat. After three days I've gone beyond my limits. For two months now, I've been out of control.

Wow. This is my 3rd time rereading in 1 month. I was excited to see it free on KU. I originally read it on Scribd, it's also available on archive.org / open-library.

I have to mention this book's writing style is understated, subtle, poetic, and elegant. I love that the author simply told her story without telling readers what to think. It's brave.





































































The first time I read this I finished it in one sitting. I was caught up in the turbulent emotional storm that was this book. The second time I read this was just to make sure this book was real. The third time I was able to think about this a bit more abstractly. There are so many lens of thinking people can read this from. I know this time around I paid attention to the manipulation, but I understand readers who get caught up in the eroticism of this book and the thrill of Ingeborg's relationship. There's also more to think on--I loved Ingeborg's observations about her previous relationships. Her observations about intimacy, adulthood, and her thoughts about her own body. This book is too much! And only around 100 pages long. Very powerful.
