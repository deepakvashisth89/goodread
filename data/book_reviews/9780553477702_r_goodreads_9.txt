Sorry, but this book is so boring. Maybe it's because I don't like this type of books anymore. Simply, I wanted something to relax with, and this was not it. I don't even know why I finished the book... I have read several more books by Steel, but they were not bad. The Kiss was okay really, but this one left me wondering how possibly could she write this... Characters were alright, but the plot was so predictable and uninteresting. Sorry fans, but this was not Danielle at her best at all.
