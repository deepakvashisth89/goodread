The authors have done a good job in attempting to systematize the contents of the elaborately complex channeled Michael teachings. I would rate their effort a four out of five. But as far as rating the potential of readers of average intelligence and comprehension—after having persevered to complete reading their book—to profit from its contents, I give it a one and a half. But the reason for such a low rating must be attributed to the group soul entity Michael itself, not the authors.

In this age of instant answers and short attention spans, the usefulness of esoteric spiritual teachings ought to be measured by their comprehensive simplicity (e.g. “love one another,” which the authors in fact do support) not by formulaic pigeonholing of each person’s attributes and characteristics, as this book does. There are too many factors that play into who each of us are—including the myriads of influences that contribute to mold us from day to day—to justify attempting to categorize us in the arbitrary way that Michael teaches. Yes, it can be interesting, even fascinating, to try but in the end it is as futile as nailing Jello to the wall to represent some kind of meaningful analytical testament to one's true nature.

I believe the adage KISS (keep it simple, stupid) has significance when teaching the meaning of our lives and destinies. However, this book is the cat’s miau for anyone who relishes complicated and complex esoteric theories about every aspect of his/her personal existence.
