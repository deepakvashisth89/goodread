I love science fiction because there's always something new to imagine with every story. That leads me to the positives of this book- interesting to see how humanity's future plays out, how we separate among the stars, how tech develops, and what we do with it.The religious aspects were also something new to me, as I haven't read another space exploration story where it played such a large part. Religion being such a personal thing and contentious topic I worried it would go one way or the other too much, but I have to say it held the line fairly to me.I didn't find the story or action overly gripping, nor were the main characters especially interesting to me.The ending was unique, but I can't say it was very satisfying.Overall, glad I read it for some interesting perspectives and world-building, but I do not feel motivated to continue with the series.
