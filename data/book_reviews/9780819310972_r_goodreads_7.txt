This was my first time reading this book. I picked this book to read because someone in my class brought it in. I liked flipping through the pages. The illustrations reminded me of painting at my grandparent’s house. This is a good children’s book and gift for the holidays. The book Snow Lion is written by David McPhail and is about a lion that lives in the very hot jungle. He is depressed because it is so hot out. He begins to offend his friends because he is too hot to play, or talk, or run. So finally he decides he has to leave the jungle because it just too hot for lions. He takes a journey and goes to the mountains where he finds snow. He tries to bring the snow back to the jungle, but it melts before he gets there.
