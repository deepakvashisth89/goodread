Leave the Grave Green is the 3rd book in the Kincaid/James series. It is a British police procedural. Connor Swann is found floating in the Thames River lock. He is the son-in-law of Sir Gerald and Dame Caroline Asherton. They have ask Scotland Yard to come in to determine if it was an accident, suicide, or murder. The Ashertons are celebrities. Sir Gerald is a operatic conductor and his wife is a famous operatic singer. They suffered a tragedy when they lost a young son many years ago in a drowning accident.Kincaid and James interview the family and locals to discover the truth. Just when they think they might have solved the case, they find the facts do not support their views. I did not know who the committed the act until the end which I liked There were surprises along the way. Even though this is a series book, I think one could start with the book and not be lost with the background and story as the author catches the reader up along with the telling of the story. Looking forward to reading the next book.
