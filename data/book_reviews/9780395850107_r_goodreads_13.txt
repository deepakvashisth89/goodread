A full and informative account of the role gay women played in feminist causes and why. It also takes pains not to claim lesbians who were arguably not. Very carefully contemporary and modern are compared and all the author's calls are fair.The drawback is that it's highly repetitive. By halfway you pretty much know what is going to be said about the next woman considered. We're it not for the cultural climate changing, making lesbian life easier or harder, this would read like a directory.So a good book, making an important point (especially 20 years ago) but more a valuable reference text than a favourite history.
