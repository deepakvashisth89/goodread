For all it was a very well-written example of Americana/Western romance, I couldn't help thinking of it as "dated" under some aspects, mainly due to the heroine's characterisation and the easy lapsing into purple prose especially during love scenes.The storyline was quite engaging (not as much the romance), the focus on the hero's intimate journey from guilt-ridden wretch to stronger and freer man ready to grasp a second chance at life was interesting and rewarding, though the villains were a little too cartoonish and the pace was too slow in the second half.All in all an enjoyable read. I'll probably try this author again, if only for the better than average writing style.
