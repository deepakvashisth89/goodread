got this book because my mother remembered reading it when she was in college. she said at the time of publication (late 1970s) it was revered as something every person should read. so i did.
eh. i got the authors' point pretty early on. i thought this would be a satirical look at how future generations would view our culture, which i guess it kind of did. but mostly it made fun of how archaeologists tend to assume everything and every artifact has a religious purpose. but, it was impractical - if the archaeologists in this book can speak English, why can't they read some of the words on artifacts written in English (ICE?). really? this purposes that future generations are actually more ignorant than current generations rather than more advanced. the parallel it's trying to make (how modern archaeologists view native or Egyptian cultures) isn't accurate because if we could read hieroglyphics perfectly, we could make better inferences.
this book had such great potential to be insightful and had room to ask such big questions, but it missed the mark and deflated into a weak effort to be witty.
