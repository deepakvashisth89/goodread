Characters are excellently voiced, I could picture them as I read the words.

Many reviews mention too many threads and too many characters, perhaps reading those reviews set my expectations a little as I kept waiting for lots of characters to be introduced but really most of them are present from the start and most of the threads are given sufficient time to be fleshed out. Even when time is spent on new characters they mostly interact with the main cast.

Personally I prefer my tie in novels to stick to the settings and characters of the show so I did enjoy this book and time spent with old friends.
