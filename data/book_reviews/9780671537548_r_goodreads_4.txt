1: TAKE IT AS IT COMES (Tom Piccirilli). 2.5/5. I'm sure I've read this one before, or something very similar. About the guy saving the woman from her abusive partner, only to stop at a diner, the partner to find them, and then chaos ensues in the diner. The twist is that the wife gets off on it.2: THE BODY IN THE WINDOW (Ramsey Campbell). 2/5. About the Councillor not wanting his town twinned with Amsterdam, but really I think it's about the guy finding his daughter working there. Not great, in my opinion. Feels half-baked.3: JACKING IN (Brinke Stevens). 2.5/5. About the guy with sick fantasies that buys a interactive sex doll to live out his fantasies, only for the doll to turn around and then live out the fantasies against the guy. Interesting, but only so-so. More fun for the novelty of it - it's very mid-90s.4: END OF THE ROAD (Edo van Belkom). 3.5/5. This one made me feel sick in a good way. About the hitchhiker who takes up the offer to go home to the drivers house, only to discover the man has chopped the limbs off his wife and plans to do the same to him. I liked this one as there was a good deal of dread - I knew something disturbing was going to happen.5: JUST SEX (Michael Garrett) 3/5. Enjoyable. The one about the author friends who decide that they need to have sex to be able to write about it, with the nice twist at the end.6: SKIN DEEP (Christa Faust). 1.5/5.
