Star Trek: New Frontier: No Limits is an anthology celebrating the longest running original Star Trek novel series--in that the series is not based on a pre-existing TV show or movie. I consider this book to be chapter 17 in the overall series because it contains the story of Captains Shelby and Calhoun's honeymoon on Xenex written by Peter David himself, which is worth the price of admission.Will finish these stories after I finish reading the others in the series, as I am told it gets rather dark near the end, and I'd like to go out perhaps on a little lighter note.
