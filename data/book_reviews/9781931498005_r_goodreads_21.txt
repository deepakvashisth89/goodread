As short fiction, this falls completely flat. Basically, it feels like left-wing eco-propaganda molded into a barely passable short story. Granted, the philosophical base beneath all that deeply appeals to me as a socialist, but the writing was so amateurish and utilitarian it fails.I also felt like the point of the whole book was just to advertise the author's coffee cooperative farm in the Dominican Republic. Again, I love co-ops and am about as anti-corporate as they come, but I feel cheated having bought and read this book. I loved the woodcuts, though.
