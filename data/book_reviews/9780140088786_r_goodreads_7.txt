The author / editor needs to look at the dates. He starts off saying that in 388 BC Alexander was a commander of one of his father’s forces against the Athenians and the Thebans and then in Chapter 2 it changes to 338 BC. The correct date is 338 BC. Then again Goodreads has the publication date as 1962 so not sure if the author or editor are still around.Anyway, this is a very bloody jaunt through the history of Alexander the Great. He became King Alexander III of Macedon at the age of 20 after his father, Philip, is assassinated. Did Alexander or his mother have a hand in that? Philip had married a younger woman, Cleopatra, and fathered a child with her. Cleopatra and the child would be removed from the picture also. Brutal times.Alexander would take a huge army through Greece and Persia and onto India. The book goes over his battles as he makes his way towards the Himalayas swiping everything in his path. This is not without many struggles mind. There is only so far an army can go and they had been going for years before they turn back only for Alexander not to make it all the way home.I do enjoy these short history books. Not overly detailed or academic which is perfect for me. I whipped through it pretty quickly taking notes as I followed Alexander on his quite ridiculous journey. It is a travelogue filled with blood, guts and enemies being ripped in half.
