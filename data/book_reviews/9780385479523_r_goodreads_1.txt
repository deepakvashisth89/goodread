Simply remarkable. The beauty and humanity in these stories touches the very core of my being. Native American authors have a way of making me feel unity with the natural world even when that’s not the theme of the story. I also love the veneration of the mothers in their stories, it anchors the experiences and culture in a way that is the essence of being human. Given what Native Americans have endured, and continue to endure, at the hands of people who look like me, I am humbled and filled with gratitude to be given the gift of their words.
