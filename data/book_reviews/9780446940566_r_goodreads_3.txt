I can’t give this book an impartial review because it is a book that I loved when I was younger. My mom inherited 15 or so paperback historical romances published mostly in the 1970s from my grandmother. My mom stored them for some reason underneath her desk. And I took them out from underneath the desk and read them one by one. They were mostly forgettable, but this one was my favourite and I read it many times. A governess has ink poured all over her hair, takes a job where her blue hair is useful and then has some adventures with a circus. Having not read the book for probably 25 years I was wondering how it would hold up. It isn’t anything special in the world of literature, but it was still entertaining!
