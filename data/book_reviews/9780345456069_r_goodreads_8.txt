Regina Burns, turns her life around, but is in danger of loosing her family's home. Her great aunt has a vision that she will travel, where she doesnt want to go, meet a mysterious blue eyed black man, and Regina will rescue a damsel in distress. As long as she can pay back her back mortgage in 60 days, Regina will do what ever she can.I totally enjoyed this book, a lot of it situated in a neighborhood in Atlanta . I'm off to read more of the author.
