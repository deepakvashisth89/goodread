This was the sequel to Yesterday's Son. And while I had read Yesterday's son as a teenager I had not read the sequel. I enjoyed it a lot. Even if it did seem a bit too medieval and Spock's son a bit less Vulcan, and McCoy less Daddy in this one. But it did have a woman POV character and she was really great. There was also an alien race with multiple sexes which led Spock and Kirk to have a great conversation about respecting pronouns even when they didn't fit with the sexes they are traditionally give to. Which was WONDERFUL especially in an 80s book. Star Trek really did have this shit figured out long before, for everyone who claims it's gone woke now.Really enjoying my time back in old star trek books with the Vulcans and the Romulans and going to keep reading them.
