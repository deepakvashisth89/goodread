In my humble opinion, 'The Trial' is a more powerful work than 'The Castle' but it is as difficult as 'The Trial' to describe and define. All these people who live in their village next to the Castle all their lives are motivated and driven by the only desire - to get into the Castle to serve their crazy, comic inhabitants - the officials or at least get closer to them. Only Kafka can create this surreal, dark, tense, somewhat grotesque and mesmerizing (as strange as it may seem) atmospere of the novel where the characters follow their only agenda and twisted logic and keep trying to obtain the unobtainable.Is there a point in existance?
