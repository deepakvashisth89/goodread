As a child nothing could beat the excitement of a Saddle Club Super Edition - they had even more drama, higher stakes, more pages and incredible covers! I loved them.
Despite this being the second of them, it's the first I read, and also one of the earlier Saddle Club books I encountered. It takes place in England and alot is happening: a three-day event, a Pony Club demonstration, an ancient mystery involving lost treasure, a rather epic prank on Veronica, the Italian boys are there, Lisa's friend Lady Tessa is included, Nigel is competing- it's got everything a Saddle Club fan could want!
