This book was reccomended to me by a great friend and roommate of mine. If you've ever been curious about reincarnation you should definately check out this book. The memories from a previous life somehow remain in young children from Lebanon. The doctor follows up with these children years after their original statements to see what they remember and how much of the information has changed. Some of the children remain friends with their old families in their new body. It's an intense read and I suggest you go pick up a copy, you'll have goosebumps!
