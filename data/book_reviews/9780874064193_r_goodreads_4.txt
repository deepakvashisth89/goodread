Very fun and fast read. 11 short stories written in the 80s. I found this very similar to the "scary stories to tell in the dark" books. The stories were a little longer than those books but similar style in creepiness and spook factor. There are no illustrations, only the 11 short stories. I enjoyed this book so much I finished it before bed on a whim. Highly recommended for the 80s and spooky short story lovers. No adult situations so it is appropriate for younger readers but there is some mild gore in a few places.
