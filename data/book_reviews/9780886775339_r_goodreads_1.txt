An OK, fairly good anthology of stories relating to mysteries set in science fictional worlds. The idea is great. Editor Mike Resnick created eighteen scenarios for crimes committed in the future and then set fellow SFF writers to solving them. Unfortunately, Resnick did not write any of the stories. The stories are all written by authors, none of whom are among my favorites--with the exception of Laura Resnick. I did enjoy her "Heaven's Only Daughter."Perhaps my favorite of the collection is "Monkey See" by Roger MacBride Allen, about an alien trying to get a chimpanzee found guilty of murder. Pat Cadigan wrote a story, "True Faces," about aliens, all of whom are congenital liars. And a very interesting and complex story is "Dead Ringer," by Esther Friesner and Walter J. Stutzman. The question to ask in this one is the corpse that of a clone or a natural-born human and is the killer a clone--or a natural-born killer, I mean, human?Most of the 18 stories are at least OK. The worst one--and the one definitely to skip--is "An Incident at the Circus" by Rick Katze.
