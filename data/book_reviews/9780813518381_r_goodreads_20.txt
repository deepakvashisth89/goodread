It's a reasonably good read and, in parts, riveting. The trend of sect-church seems to be a very "human thing" and not just an "American church thing." The authors cast these historical movements in general economic terms, which is interesting, and I really hope they use more of that in this writing.Generally, I agree with their assertions and assessments. But I wish they would offer more than a few pages on possible solutions to the general trend toward secularization and decline.
