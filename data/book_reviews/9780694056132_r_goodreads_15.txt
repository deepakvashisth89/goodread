Holy moly, I feel like I've been reading this book for years. In truth, it has taken me several months. It went on for-e-ver. It didn't help that it's one of the longer Newbery books and the type was small and dense.Jefferson Davis Bussey is excited to join the Union Army and fight for what's right. He learns (I would say quickly, but it took about a 1,000 years for him to get to this lesson) that war is not all it's cracked up to be. He also meets a lot of people from the Confederacy that show him how human both sides are.I feel like after how long it took me to read I should write this long, beautiful review, but I can't. I'm so over this book and ridiculously happy to mark it off my list. Some parts were truly great, but the length diminished the joy any of those parts brought. It's obvious the author put a lot of heart and soul in to writing this book, but it is not the book for me.
