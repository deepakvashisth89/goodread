I don’t want to give this a rating since it is not a literary piece rather a personal diary of a 5th grader. However, reading this alongside Anna Frank definitely lead to some let downs. I think that the main difference in both was that Anna didn’t know her diaries would be read by millions whereas Zlata was writing in hopes of it. I think that this lead to a lot of personal information that would’ve allowed us to connect with her on a deeper level to be withheld. It felt like it was just a book full of updates on the war and occasionally some updates on the people around her (who I didn’t really care about because there wasn’t much about the people to hold on to). Not to talk about the translation… I don’t think anyone who reads this in English would ever be able to perceive the true and genuine feelings of Zlata. Overall, the whole time I kept thinking of this diary as a cash grab. It was still tragic to read about the effects of war on a young girl and her family. Regardless of the way it was written, I feel so much sorrow and anger for what has happened in Bosnia and think that everyone should be informed on it wether it’s through a diary, documentary or any other form of media. I would recommend this book to kids in middle school since it is a good starter on the Bosnian war and there were some informative parts.
