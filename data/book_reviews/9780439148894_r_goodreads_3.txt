Molly Flaherty is a young teenage girl growing up in Boston during the late 1960's. This was such a tumultuous time to live in. People were either for or against our involvement. Molly had questions that a lot of adults could not or would not answer for her. She was labeled a trouble maker in school for even asking. She had a right to know. You can't just accept something on blind faith. She had many questions because her brother Patrick went to Vietnam. She wanted to support her brother, but she also wondered if being in Vietnam was the right thing to do.

When I started the book felt like I had read this before because it seemed a bit familiar. Come to find out the author wrote a book from her brother's perspective, , and that is what I had read. I think reading them one after the other will give the reader a true perspective of the timeframe.
