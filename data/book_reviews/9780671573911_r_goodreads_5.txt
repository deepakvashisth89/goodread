Other than the inconsistency of first contact, this was a well written novelisation of a fantastic Star Trek story. The inconsistency could’ve been fixed by one more pass in the editing process.According to Star Trek, first contact was made on April 5, 2063. When the Enterprise arrives in the past, Data gives the date as being April 6, one day before first contact. But when Picard gives his captains log at the end of the novel, he says that it is April 5, 2063.
