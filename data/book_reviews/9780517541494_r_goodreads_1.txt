It's very dated now. My parents bought this for my brother and I because we were identical twins growing up in the 1970s.

The book begins with some history on twins in literature and art, then moves on to, and the bulk of the book features about maybe 60 sets of grown up twins, mostly identical and some background on them, several athletes, a couple models, and a variety of others. On each set of twins there's maybe 1 or 2 full pages of text and usually a couple photos, so it's almost a picture book with the two authors, both professional photographers, but there is a fair amount of text. Maybe 60% photos, 40% text.
I'd classify it as more of a coffee table book that you'd thumb through rather than a book you'd sit down and read.

For it's time, the book has nice photos and I thumbed through it several times as a youngster. Maybe 4 stars as a coffee table book, but I'm giving it 3 stars for today because it's quite dated.

If somebody is reminiscent of the 70s and likes and remembers twins like Fay and Kay Young or Mario & Aldo Andretti, you might enjoy this one.

The two authors are identical sisters and photographers.
