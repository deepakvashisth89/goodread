This had sat on my shelf because I was waiting for the perfect moment to read it. Thus, as I have been in a state of solitude and somewhat on the brink of a slump I picked up this little wonder. This truly exceeded my expectations; it has hit me in ways that are hard to fully articulate without me crying. It's a wonderful recount of growing up. The wonders and whimsy of adolencense and being filled with drive and adventure which slowly transitions into the sorrows of painful nostalgia. This novel explored friendship, youth, love, grief, and the acceptance of abandonment- the acceptance that one's drive for a life filled with wonder has reached its demise. Reading the last few chapters of this book was like looking through a frosted window of a home which held all of the people and experiences you cherish. Outside you slowly freeze over, but you're okay with it because everyone and everything is else is warm and protected by the burning fire of fir tree wood.
