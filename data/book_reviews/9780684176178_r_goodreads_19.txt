I saw the film as a youngster. They seemed to always show it on TV as a family classic around Thanksgiving weekend.I read the novel as an adult.It is a much more graphic tough read. A heartbreaker.Jodi was a lonely boy growing up in a rough backwoods Florida farm.His mother Ora, endured the grief of all her prior children dying young. This was not unheard of in times before antibiotics and clean drinking water.Jodi's only young friend was a child his age, Fodder-Wing Forrester, who lived on a run-down farm a number of miles away.He was a dreaming crippled little boy who tamed and loved many forest creatures. His brothers were grown men, so he and Jodi cherished each other's friendship.Through a series of events, Jodi adopts an orphaned very tiny little fawn with his fathers permission. Jodi's mother is not happy with this.She knows her young son will soon know what it is to suffer the loss of something you love. She knew this sweet fawn would grow into a large animal that did not belong on a farm.Jodi names his fawn Flag. He brings it to show Fodder-wing. Jodi and his father learn that the little boy has died of fever. Jodi endures his first loss of his dear friend.Jodi and flag are bonded as the deer grows. Flag has grown to a yearling. The deer begins to get into the crop the family has planted. It is backbreaking work.His father wants to use the money to build a well near the house to ease the work of hauling water. Flag becomes very destructive.His father tells Jodi to take the deer as far away as he can and release him. Flag only knows the farm and keeps returning.After trying everything, Jodi's father tells him Flag must be killed. The family's lives depend on their small crop.Jodi can't bring himself to do it, and once more tries to bring Flag into the Florida woods far from the farm.Jodi wakes to a gunshot. FlagIs mortally wounded. Jodi's mother had taken a shotgun presumably to scare him off. Flag has stumbled off into the woods.Jodi's father, who has been long recovering from a serious injury tells Jodi he must find Flag and end his suffering.Jodi finds his deer and sees the horrific wound of a shotgun blast. He begs for Flags forgiveness and finishes him.Jodi hates his parents at that moment. He throws the gun down and runs away into the deep Florida forest. He is lost and starving wandering for days.He is eventually found and returned home, but he learns what hunger and privation is.He realized how much he loved his father and mother. He understood how losing all her young children broke his mother's heart and learned how she walked for days searching for him.A tough story. Heartbreaking in places. I took a lot from the book.How good parents make tough decisions that in the moment you can't understand but do later and are glad of it.How even having only onetrue friend in the world Is a found treasure.Never try to touch or tame a wild animal. They belong in a forest. Its a rough place where life and death goes on unseen by humans.Here is a big one. Never go near a newborn fawn. It's mother is always close by. They are big and can be very protective.A Doe with a young fawn may stomp you into the ground. She can move fast and flatten your head without a second thought.Five Stars🌟🌟🌟🌟🌟
