Capon uses cooking as a metaphor for life. Don’t go for the processes pre-cooked garbage that passes as food because it’s more convenient. The best things in life take time and care and may even give you heartburn. He sees food as something to be savored and enjoyed as a means of seeing God's abundant grace in all of life.Don't read this book for the recipes. Read it to remember what it means to be human!One of my favorite books of 2023.
