One of the few books I feel actually changed my life and perspective. I read almost all of it in one sitting. I picked this one up at a local Church bookstore. It was discounted, and jumped out at me - "intimacy" and "friendship" being the key words, especially because at the time I felt the Lord beginning to build me on that foundation. I rarely buy books without sitting on it for a bit, but this one was different.This book is really just the Holy Spirit and I don't have much else to say. I didn't agree with everything, but that's not really the point because there were things I realized about Jesus himself that I needed to know. Why is he trustworthy? How do I run after Him in obedience?Read this.
