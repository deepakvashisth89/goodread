Dr. Lehman isn't as funny as he thinks he is (or perhaps I don't share his brand of humor), but his assessment of birth order was fascinating and easy to see in my own family and friends. Many reviews criticize this book for keeping birth order generalized, but he does it well and intentionally. Birth order is not a firm mold; it is merely a general pattern that can be worked out differently in each family.I listened to this book on audiobook, but I would like to get my hands on a paper copy.
