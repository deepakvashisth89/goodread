With an incredibly funny and insightful introduction, Waters delivers three complete screenplays for his biggest fans to enjoy. Included in the collection are screenplays for the ultimate midnight movie “Pink Flamingos,” his underrated commercial bomb “Desperate Living,” and the unproduced sequel to “Pink Flamingos” entitled “Forever Flamingos.” Waters encourages audiences to act the films out for friends, recite along with the film, or simply read it and take in the written nuance. Included are also behind-the-scenes photos.

Released in 1972, “Pink Flamingos” stars the legendary Divine as her title of Filthiest Person in the World is challenged by a married couple who think they embody pure filth. “Desperate Living,” released in 1977, stars Mink Stole who is banished to live in a garbage city ruled by a vicious queen who wants to give rabies to all the lesbian citizens. And “Forever Flamingos,” the abandoned sequel to “Pink Flamingos,” sees Divine’s return to Baltimore after 15 years of living in bus station lavatories where her title of Filthiest Person in the World is challenged once more.
