Maybe the fifth or sixth best Ross Thomas book. Very clever international intrigue. I guess a slight markdown because this hero is similar to the one in Missionary Stew, but not as interesting.The thing that made Ross Thomas great was not just his CIA experience; nor his years as a Democrat party bagman; no, rather, it’s the author’s ability to take characters that in many ways have similar back-stories, in different novels; yet make them sound and seem different.Few can do that; fewer still even bother. Ross Thomas mostly succeeds.
