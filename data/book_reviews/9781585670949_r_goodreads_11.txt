Murder in Montparnasse is a historical murder mystery set in 1920s Paris. A femme fatale is stabbed to death in a dark alley. At first, the police believe she is a victim of a serial killer. However, it soon becomes clear that the victim was a drug addict who had been blackmailing everyone she knew for drug money.
As a mystery, Murder in Montparnasse is slow moving and dialog heavy. There are many scenes where drunken Lost Generation characters (some real, some fictional) trade witty dialog without advancing the plot. Engel captures the feeling of literary Paris between the wars but at the expense of excitement. There are long passages that appear to be padding to fill out the thin plot to novel length.
This mystery will be primarily of interest to Hemingway fans. He appears as a thinly disguised suspect and sidekick of the narrator. This novel is n ot to be confused with the identically-titled mystery by Kerry Greenwood.
