This is a spin-off book, not written by the original author (Don Freeman). It misses the mark entirely. Easter is a Christian holiday that celebrates the resurrection of Jesus Christ. This book does not mention this at all. Easter is not just a day to enjoy chocolate and parties.Furthermore, Corduroy’s owner Lisa is not present at all. Corduroy doesn’t have an adventure in this book. They are buying items for a party. Consumerism at its finest.I would not recommend this book to anyone who enjoys the original Corduroy series. It is not worth adding to your collection, despite the title.
