I never read this one as a kid, and I didn't really enjoy this one too much. The story is that you're babysitting a girl who may or may not be haunted by a ghost. This had the potential to be really creepy and atmospheric, but there was no atmosphere whatsoever, just a bunch of mediocre scenes that weren't scary or eerie at all. Many of the endings are vague or unsatisfying, the writing is subpar and the story as a whole is unengaging. Unless you have a nostalgia for babysitting (I didn't) then I'd say skip this one.
