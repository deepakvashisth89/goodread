I'm going to go ahead and DNF this series.I had my concerns about the language in the first book, but it kept getting worse in book two. This is two 15 year olds written by a 60 y/o woman and it shows."Dag, it must feel whack leaving all your homeys behind." is an actual sentence.One of the MC's refers to a teacher as a "barrel-bellied Barney" WTF does that even mean?They say that someone is going to be so angry they're going to "bust a gut". Which is what we'd say back then when someone was laughing too hard.I only read about 80 pages so, there was plenty more to happen I'm sure, but I can't do it any more. I keep getting so enraged by what the kids are saying I can't pay attention to the story. As someone who was within a year or two of the twin's age during the same year, it's glaringly obvious that no one involved in writing or editing this book had even been around a teenager at the time.
