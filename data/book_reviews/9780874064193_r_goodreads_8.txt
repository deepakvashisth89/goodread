I can remember buying this book at a school book fair in elementary school. My teacher decided to read a few of the stories to my class during Down Time, way back when we were allowed to put our heads down for a bit after lunch. I still remember how we loved hearing these stories so much that we skipped our time on the playground just to listen to her finish the book. Revisiting this book is comforting Halloween nostalgia for me. I miss those days.
