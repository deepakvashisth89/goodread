It's been years since I read this, and although I always tell kids it's my favorite book when they ask (I've learned you HAVE to have a favorite book when you write for middle graders because they ALWAYS want to know!), part of me had wondered if I would still feel that way after a reread. The answer is: I feel it more than ever. It's the most perfect book ever written as far as I'm concerned. I finished it in tears, my soul filled.An interesting and unexpected thing about this reread is realizing how many elements from this book unconsciously made their way into THE STARS OF WHISTLING RIDGE, my 2021 book. I really had no idea!
