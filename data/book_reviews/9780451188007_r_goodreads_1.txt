4.15*All for the cracker of a beginning, the sharp and scathing smackdowns by the h, and a dislike-to-friends-to-lovers story between a prickly, snarky and plain h and a handsome, charming but habitually sotted duke.If you can overlook the incongruity of a country bred miss - no looks, no feathers, no connections, no dowry - constantly exchanging barbs and insults with ‘four premier rakes of the realm’ and coming up tops, then go for it.Cuz this one here is a riot!I thoroughly enjoyed this charming and well-paced book. At least it doesn’t flag till the last third of the book when it only changes course to become slow and bittersweet.They actually become friends after the initial skirmishes and everything else is subliminal. He keeps assessing her and finds her lacking in looks and wife-potential even as they spend a lot of time together and he finds her snarky company a bulwark for the haunting pain and guilt of his past. The past that led him to actual alcoholism and escapism through drink and debauchery.And really, all 4 ‘rakes’ do not pursue her. These men are more friendly than amorous - as is the H till the end when he sees the light. I like Monroyal - H of book #10 - as he gives the H some serious competition for the reader’s affections. He’s vastly more cynical and dark than the H, who’s only a charming drunkard.There’s another om at the end that makes the H come back running to mark his territory. His desertion (before this) is hurtful and he lost some shine for me.But the ending is wonderful and sweet.Patricia Oliver is another new favorite of mine. But, again alas, so few ebooks available!
