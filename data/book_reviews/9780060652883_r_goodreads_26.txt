I'm honestly not sure how to word my thoughts on this book... Wow. The way Lewis presents his thoughts in an engaging and easy-to-understand way always astounds me. It's like he's your friend sitting beside you and explaining something, not just a writer stating facts that you should believe because they are what he believes.The whole approach of what it truly means to be a Christian is amazing! The truth is that we are fallen and often the Church and Christians don't act as they should, but that doesn't mean that God isn't real, just that we as humans are fallible.I love Lewis's engaging style, the complex truths that he breaks down in an easy-to-understand way, and the humble tone he uses to deliver his message. This is a beautiful book, enlightening, encouraging, and inspiring. I learned a lot.Overall, I recommend this to anyone looking for a deeper understanding of the Christian faith!
