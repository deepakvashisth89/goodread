A poignant memoir which the author after surviving through scores of atrocities managed to pen. From Jewish exodus of '48 leading to the exile of the Palestinians, the author takes the reader on an emotional and political flashback and shifting western attitude towards the Arab world.Considerably neutral in its core essence primarily focused upon author's on narrative revolving around the highs and lows of a displaced Palestinean in desperate effort of adapting to the culture who wouldn't recognize her and striving for one which haunted her through-out her adolescence the book is a personal perspective of struggle, heartbreaks, lost identity, war, anger, confusion, and hope which the author narrates in a finery that glues the reader till the end. The reader finds himself staring in confusion as the author takes the reader through the complexities of the Arab world and the convoluted structure and struggle of Arabs and Jews.
