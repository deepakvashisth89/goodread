This book is a funny yet engaging book. The main character Antonia "Ant" feel like she dose not fit in with her family. She has two perfect sisters nothing like her, a dad who is always a way on business trips, and a mom who never seems to be happy with Ant. Ant makes up a lot of lies up. One main lie she always brings up and truly believes in it is that she is adopted. Her best friend Harrison, her dog Pistachio, and her art teacher are the three beings who she likes to be around. Great read for anyone who wants a light hearted yet challenging read!
