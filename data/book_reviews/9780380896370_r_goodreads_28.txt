Merlin continues his quest to discover who is behind the attacks on his life.Continued disappointment. The editing is a little better but there are still plenty of painful things to read ("I agree," I agreed.) The story is really going nowhere at this point and there's so much randomly going on it's hard to keep up. The first five books occasionally had a lot of stuff to keep up with but overall it was held tightly together. This feels like a ride where the driver has no clue where he's going. I'll still probably finish it, but I don't think there's any saving this hot mess.
