Art is subject to the whims of fashion, too, as Agee points out in this masterful, and funny, fable. But a true artist survives being a celebrity. Read aloud the names of the already-famous 'artists' at the beginning. (Remember, Agee loves word-play!) Note that posters, street signs, are all in French (for authenticity...). Note that the setting is (at least approximately) what Gauguin and Van Gogh experienced. Highly recommended.
