3.5 stars (reread- originally 2 stars). I HAVE thoughts. I guess I rated this 2 stars when I first joined GR because I couldn't remember how I felt about it. Even while rereading it, I couldn't remember the plot, so it felt like reading a book again for the first time. I was really enjoying it all the way through even though there are issues with pacing. Mayra and her family and friends are incredibly fleshed out and have backstory compared to most Fear Street families. My issue lies withthe fact that Link, her ex, practically rapes her in the woods at one point and the book ends with her getting back together with him and then him blaming his abusive tendencies and scaring her on how much he loved her. GROSS. Yikes and not a good message to have in a YA book. She should've just stayed single. She had two crappy exes and leave it at that. One who tried to assault her in the woods and one who hypnotized her and then tried to drown her. Mayra should've sworn off dating.Full review to come.
