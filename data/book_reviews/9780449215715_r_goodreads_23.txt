Inheritance confusion, evil male cousin, falling in love with the boy next door but not realizing until the end of the book, a giant storm precipitating (lol) the climax. I also didn't like the characters as much as TNTC. Maybe I should choose something other than an inheritance story from Stewart for my next book by her. And re-read TNTC because I think I can get past the psychic weirdness now that I know
she's NOT psycho-sexually communing with her cousin because I just loved the setting and the characters and romance grew on me in a way they did not in .
