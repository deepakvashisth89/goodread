Barely four stars. Full of spoilers. I really wanted to like this book more than I did. We follow Marla who is in the lead for valedictorian but only by a tenth of a point in front of Kenny. She has a lot of pressure from her mom to be the best so she’s totally stressed out. At the start of the book she’s preparing for a debate against Kenny when Clarissa spills her tea on her perfectly planned outfit. As Clarissa is walking away Marla wishes she will fall, and then she does but Clarissa says she was pushed. But no one pushed her. Hmmmm. Gothy juniors Elena and Roxanne come up to her and tell her she has the power and should join them. Marla’s like get away freaks and leaves. At the debate Kenny is amazing and when it’s Marla’s turn she sees the two goth girls and suddenly her throat closes up and she can’t breathe and all she’s able to to is burp so obviously Kenny wins the debate. Marla is able to breathe but the girls are now gone. Next in French class Marla’s test is only an 85 and with Kenny getting a 97 he’s gonna beat her in becoming valedictorian. Later an announcement is over the speaker it’s Marla’s debate well burp. She’s humiliated she sees it was Josie and her friends. Josie is still mad that Marla took her summer job. Get over it Josie. Later Marla makes a hate list, which is basically everyone in the school faculty and lunch ladies included. Marla is becoming unglued. She goes home and her mother is like how’s your day, Marla starts telling her about her day and suddenly her test is a 98. Hmmm. She gets to school the next day and her hate list has been posted. Her life just sucks. So when Elena and Roxanne approach her again she’s like I’ll join you guys. The book becomes a convoluted witchy mess where they summon the dark power and Marla signs with her blood to join them. Then she uses her power to make a teachers teeth come out. Weird. And talks about pushing Josie through a glass door but decides against it. But it happens anyways. Marla is freaked out because she didn’t want to do it. Elena and Roxanne are like we did it we used your power. Then they tell her the dark evil needs blood and she has to kill. So she’s all like I’ll kill Kenny. She gets to his place and is all strangling him until she’s like I can’t do this and like tells him everything and he’s like it’s cool you tried to kill me, oh and my dad has books on witchcraft. These kids are so fucking dumb. Marla and Kenny are gonna fight back the meet up with the goth girls and it’s a fight to the death with Marla killing them both, the whole ending fight was dumb. Then twelve hours later Kenny rushes to Marla, who now has Kenny’s ring (like less than a day after she tried to strangle him to death but okay) and he’s like I deciphered the pact you signed even though it’s a dead language and it’s like if you kill anyone on your evil group with your powers you die twelve hours later and boom the ground opens up and Marla is dead. Okay that makes no sense because in the prolog another girl is killed by I’m assuming Elena and Roxanne and they are alive well after twelve hours. Make it make sense ugh. Also only one senior died. I’m so let down. The last two books better be so death heavy.
