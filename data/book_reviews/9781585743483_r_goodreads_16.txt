I have studied the official records of the investigations of the tragic sinking of the USS Thresher. I am a former nuclear submarine sailor. My boat would often tie up across the pier from the USS Permit SSN 594, one of the same class as the Thresher.The public may never know the full extent of the details surrounding the destruction of the Thresher. At least one point, the four minutes of frequency variation in the non-vital electrical buss, remain unexplained.Mr Pomar created a credible explanation for most of the questions surrounding the incident. His careful research shows thoughtful research in culling hearsay, rumor or opinion from the facts and actual evidence.He points no fingers and blames no one. The Thresher and her crew died because of a tragic concatenation of events that no one could have foreseen. The worst nuclear submarine sinking in world history, yielded one benefit for all US and foreign submarine crews since: better designs of critical systems and thorough testing of all seawater-related system.This is not a book you read for pleasure. It is a book you read to satisfy your urge to know the facts.
