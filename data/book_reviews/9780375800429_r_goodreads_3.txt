This book is divided into two parts. The first half of the book goes through the entire alphabet with upper and lower case numbers. Each page shows a letter and then shows tons of words and picture that begin with that letter. It integrates the sesame street characters into each page and makes them apart of the learning process. The second half of the book introduces numbers. It starts with one and goes all the way to one hundred. One each page it shows the number of an item that the page indicates. For example for the number one there is one broom ect. The pictures are very colorful and teach a lot about letters and numbers.
