Welcome back, Nittle Grasper!After a run-in with Shuichi in his place on a hit music show, Ryuichi decides it's time to reunite Nittle Grasper and tour again. But, his biggest ambition is actually to have Shuichi sing a duet with him, and he feels that the reunion is the best way to acheive this. After all, Shuichi is the ultimate N-G fanboy!And, after many mistakes on Shuichi's part, lots of anger and feelings that never should have surfaced, Ryuichi's dream does come true. But, it's quite the accident. K pushes Shuichi onstage while N-G is singing, and Ryuichi pulls him over front-and-center to sing with him.--------------This is the only volume of this series I actually own. So, I probably won't be reading any more of this series for awhile.
