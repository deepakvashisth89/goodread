I was given this book while helping my parents with a move from my childhood home that they had been in for 60 years. It was in rough shape and I hadn’t heard of it, just thought it was an old book. But as I was researching thinking I might sell it, I started reading it and looking at the illustrations and was drawn into the story. I then realized it not only had my Mom‘s name written in it in 1954, along with her brother’s name on a sticker (she had covered it up with a piece of paper she had made to look like a library log) but a very faded inscription to my Grandpa from his aunt as a gift in Christmas 1917. I was pleasantly surprised at some of the progressive thoughts, though there were also some disappointing moments that you would more expect from the time. But overall can see why it was kept all these years, and I will happily keep it on the shelf.
