Night Life of the Gods had me rolling. Although Thorn Smith died in 1934, his humor has lost none of its edge. Perhaps that's because he focused on those timeless themes that speak to readers across the generations; sex, drinking, and non-stop partying. Highly recommended, as is his better known book Topper, in which two fun-loving ghosts attempt to pull the conservative and straitlaced banker Cosmo Topper into a life of, well… sex, drinking, and non-stop partying.(First read in July of 2011, re-read for the MobileRead Book Club March 14, 2016.)
