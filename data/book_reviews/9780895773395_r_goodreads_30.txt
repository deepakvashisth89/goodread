One more goal for 2024 achieved--another book written by Mark Twain, now my favorite.

Very witty! I was pleasantly surprised by the accurate knowledge Twain had about the Bible and also of the Holy Land and some of his insightful assessments he made about religion. This would help some readers of the 21st century to envision Palestine as Twain observed in 1867. If you've only read HUCK FINN or TOM SAWYER, this is a totally different style.

Listening to this book, I found myself cackling out loud to some of Twain's descriptions, last being the story of climbing a pyramid
