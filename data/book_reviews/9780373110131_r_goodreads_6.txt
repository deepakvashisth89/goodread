This was a follow-up book to "Portrait of Bethany", that for some reason, Ms. Weale felt she had to write. I would rather it had been a completely separate book, as Liz was an interesting character (older and more experienced than the average HP h) and in my opinion, deserved better than to be linked (or should I say stuck) with that pathetic David, still pining for his "niece", who's now married with kids. He goes from place to place, woman to woman, his heart still in pieces, and it feels as genuine as a four-dollar bill colored purple.

After what Liz went through in past relationships (first with immature Barmey, then with horrible Richard), she wanted to find a real love, one that would last, and didn't want to give herself to another man again unless that happened. And that man was David??????

What a waste!!
