I remember experiencing this one as distressing when I was a child. Dandelion dresses up to go to his friend’s party, but she doesn’t recognize him because he’s dressed up and has a fancy hairstyle...so she slams the door in his face. Then he gets caught in a rainstorm, so his hair gets undone and he has to take his fancy clothes off, and then his friends recognize him and invite him in. It was upsetting to me when I was young to see this character’s friends reject him, and as an adult I feel like it sends a troubling message that you should avoid changing if you want to keep your friends.
