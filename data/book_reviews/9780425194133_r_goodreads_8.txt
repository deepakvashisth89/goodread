What a creative, lovely, absolute slice of cake this book was! I adored the author’s writing style, presenting the story almost as a stream of conscience, with just the right amount of punctuation. What a magical story, a TRUE fairy tale that takes the stuffing out of what grown ups believe and reverts the now to a past full of lessons like that fact that believing makes things real. The expressions of love from full on romantic love to self love and courage are all represented here, in the midst of the idyllic Welsh countryside where the worst thing happens to just be the best thing that happens to everyone. LOVE!!!
