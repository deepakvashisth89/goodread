This is one treasury that is indeed, a treasure. We're talking some of the very best stories within the genre, and a sprinkling of some of the very best in any genre. For me, this anthology is a high water mark that—twenty years later—has only rarely been bested. It will continue to wear well and remain a volume to keep on your shelf for those special nights when only a ghost story fits the bill, and only the best will do . . .
