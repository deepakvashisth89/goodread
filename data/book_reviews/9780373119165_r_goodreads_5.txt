A TSTL heroine and a truly unlikeable hero that was too much even for me. Throw in a convoluted story that at times (at least for me) didn't make a whole lot of sense, I found myself wondering "why am I reading this?" I dislike writing negative reviews - and I'm an unabashed lover of cruel-hero stories and at times, I admit, doormat heroines - but I guess we all have our limits.
