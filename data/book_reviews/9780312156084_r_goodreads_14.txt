At first, I was going to rate Night Over Day Over Night by Paul Watkins either a 3 or 4 because of the weird situationship going on between 17-year-old Sebastian Westland and an older woman, Lise Hammacher. That's my only problem with this book. The first half mainly focused on how he was being groomed by this woman who is twice his age since he's been like 13. Only he doesn't view it that way. It wasn't until Westland actually saw her for the last time that the plot got better (in my opinion). When the focus shifted to just combat, I was immediately sucked into the story and felt like I was actually a part of Westland's mind. The last half of the book is what made me highly enjoy Night Over Day Over Night. As Westland experiences his first taste of war, I can't help but pity him because we are so close in age. I imagined myself in his situation, but I can't feel too bad because he willingly joined the Waffen-SS (and also because I'm Jewish, lmfao). Westland could have picked to join the Wehrmacht, but I suppose that was too boring for him. I don't want to go too in-depth because I'll end up spoiling the ending, but if you want to read this book, just know it gets way better halfway through.
