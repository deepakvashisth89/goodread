Though the book is obviously about the adverse effects on the planet from this administration's policies, the repercussions really don't hit you until we hear about the residents from a small Pennsylvania county whose healthy has been ravished by the emissions from a nearby coal plant that should have had to upgrade its equipment to be more carbon efficient. It's like the California town from Erin Brokovich. From there, this raw emotion quickly turns to anger at how this presidency has done its best to trash our environment. While this book does a good job at addressing several issues, often it's too narrative and doesn't give enough of the facts, dates and figures that would help the reader fully understand Bush's terrible actions. Every time I read, "the list of issues is so long I won't go into it here," I kept thinking "Please go into it, that's why I checked out this book." Then again, maybe it's better to just forget about Bush and move on. Interesting note, this book was written in 2004. We could probably make a second volume, just for the second term.
