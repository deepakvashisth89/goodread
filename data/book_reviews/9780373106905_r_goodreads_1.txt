Sweet, if perhaps a bit cheesy story of an emotionally traumatized, English woman and the very understanding, very besotted Dutch architect who brings her out of her shell. In typical Harlequin fashion, the option of psychological therapy is dismissed and the transformative power of love is what heals her :)I LOVED the heroine in this one. Strong, smart, dignified but not a Mary Sue: her fits of temper and stubbornness balanced well against all her positive attributes, making her more human and relatable. I also loved how she told off the OW, who slinked back into the Black Hole where she had crawled out of, never to spoil the story again.I think the hero was too good to be true. True, heroine's main conflict was internal and hero was just there to facilitate her healing. But I like my romances to focus on the conflict between the protagonists rather than on the Great, Big, Traumatic Seekret of the Past.Still, a nice story, with great world-building and well-rounded secondary characters.
