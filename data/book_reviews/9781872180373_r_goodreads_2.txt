I bought this a while back at a used book sale bc it looked cool. I think if I'd read it 25-30 years ago when I was a teenager, I would have been like "so wise, so true" but looking at it today it shows a lot of what is really wrong with publishing. It's elitist, pretentious, and very very white. I'm white and middle aged, but I don't want all my art and writing to be from that same perspective. Maybe I should give this a three star bc I chuckled once or twice, this is an older book, it's not that serious. Eh, nah. It's just okay.
