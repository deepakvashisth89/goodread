Modernized and slightly expanded version of the author's 2000 publication with a new cover. Simply Sinful is the first book in the Simply series and can be read as a standalone book.

Kayla and her sister inherited a business from their Aunt. It was dating etiquette and they are expanding it to international business etiquette. The police have a reliable tip it might be an undercover prostitution business. They send Kane, a detective, to gather information.

Kayla is both smart and beautiful and she has issues with men only being interested in her body. Kane has both trust and guilt issues from his past that keep him from relationships. Yet, they have instant attraction and fall into bed together. Kayla tries to make him realize he loves her. Kane tries to avoid emotional intimacy. This part of the story got tedious.

The mystery of her Aunt and/or Uncle's involvement in what did turn out to be a prostitution service was the background story the second half of the book as both Kane and Kayla tries to bring the facts to light.
