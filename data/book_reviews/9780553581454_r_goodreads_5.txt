I’m not sure what to say about this book. The storyline is pretty good and the outcome not totally obvious but I was convinced that it was set in the 70s/80s because of attitudes and language used and also because of the huge amount of smoking, everywhere and anywhere. I can tolerate all of those things within an appropriate timeframe as being typical of the age but I began to be a little confused when mobile phones came into the equation. Investigation showed that the book was first published in 1998 by which time racism and racist language would not have been quite so overt and smoking was definitely on its way out. It had definitely become less acceptable to light up in someone else’s home but in every home visited in this book, police officers were smoking, admittedly so were many, but not all, of the residents of those homes.These details distracted from the storyline and pulled me out of the world the author was trying to construct
