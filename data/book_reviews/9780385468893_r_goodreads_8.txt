Changed my life by opening my eyes to how racism works, how pervasive and insidious it can be - and also how it hurts people, how it changes people, and how it breaks down sometimes.This book also proves that racism is real which may sound weird if you grew up knowing it was real - but if you grew up in a polite, PC, 90% white neighborhood like mine where no-one was called names it draws back the veil.Thank you very much, Studs Terkel, I miss you.
