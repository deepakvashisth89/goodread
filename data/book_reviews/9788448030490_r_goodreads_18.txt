I read Darkness and Light which was also by these two authors. I won't ever do it again. I took the other book with a grain of salt. I looked at it like AU or fanfic. I guess I loved Sturm enough to wade through that.

Riverwind doesn't have much of the plot in the original series. I was hoping for a book that gave his background lore. Oh I was so looking forward to it.

Had I but looked at the authors.

Riverwind is given a task before he can marry Goldmoon. I was hoping for a story of them together, but okay this could still be good.
When he took to the road with Catchstar, I thought, "oh good a bromance". I'm.a sucker for a bromance. I loved Catchflea's acorns. That is a cool bit of fantasy. They should have leaned into the beginning more.

Nope, instead they end up underground with a dying civilization, a power hungry queen, a blind man who used to be her lover, and barren children who do grudge work. No not barren as in can't have children, but like can never grow up.

It's like they wanted to tell the story of Mors and Di an, but they were forced to write Riverwind, so they just jammed him in that story. Because of the acorns I already new how our heroes stories would play out.

When Riverwind is given the staff I saw how all of this came about. Its my least favorite writing method, writing backwards. They knew how they wanted him to find the old gods and wrote backwards from there. That always feels forced to me.

I started to enjoy it until they threw the story off the proverbial cliff. What kind of ending? Did they have a page limit?
