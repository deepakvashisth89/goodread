Honestly while I did like the Editor's Note in the beginning and end and the content was overall good, I found it hard to finish the last half because it was only the two works of 'Herland' and excerpts from 'Women & Economics'. Not that the subject matter was boring in any way. In fact, I found 'Women & Economics' especially refreshing when it comes to feminist talking points and discourse then vs today and how the discourse and examples used in that conversation hasn't really changed all that much.

I just felt misled a little because Charlotte Perkins Gilman wrote a lot of short stories so to have the last half be two things that are about 100 pages each was a bit of a killer to the momentum I had built up.

As I said, an interesting read but if you find longer works without a break in an anthology hard to get through I would suggest either finding a different book or only reading the first half of this one.
