I made the mistake of getting this in audiobook form. The poor review is more related to the horrible narration than the book itself. The book itself is probably worthy of a three-star rating, while the audiobook narration made me want to make it a one-star... so I'm going for the middle path and giving it a two-star...The content is just as the description suggest... it's a collection of slogans and phrases. They are meant to inspire, explain, and enlighten (or something like that). If that's all it was, it actually probably would've been a lot better. Instead, there is a lot of explanation (and repetition), which would likely lull even the most mindful of monks into a dazed stupor.
