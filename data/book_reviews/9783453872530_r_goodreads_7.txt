Seize the Night is full of mystery & wisdom, but I do prefer Fear Nothing.In typical Koontz fashion, Seize the Night contains references to classic works:T.S. Eliot,The Wind in the Willows,Alice's Adventures in Wonderland / Through the Looking-Glass,Winnie-the-Pooh,Peter Pan.Within the Koontz Universe, I get a kick out of one Dean Koontz title being connected to other Dean Koontz titles. There are of course references to Koontz novels published before the publication of Seize the Night:Watchers,Midnight,Fear Nothing.
