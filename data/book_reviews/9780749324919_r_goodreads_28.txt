I actually read these books in the wrong order. I should have read this one first and then "Strange Conflict".The same main core characters are involved in this tale of supernatural shenanigans and messing with the dark side.I like the way that they aren't all invincible and they have moments of weakness, even the Duke, who is the strongest personality and the most knowledgeable of all things arcane and paranormal.Damien Mocata isn't such a great villain as Doctor Saturday in "Strange Conflict" but he does the job adequately. I would have liked a little more focus on him as a person, rather than only dealing with his gatherings and his rituals.It's an enjoyable read with plenty of suspense.
