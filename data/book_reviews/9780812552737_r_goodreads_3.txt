Another mediocre story in the Solar Queen universe by an author who is not Andre Norton. I glad to be done with the series. I should have stopped after book 4 since all the books after that one were written by other people. The story and the characters lose something when it's done by other people. But I finished the series, so it's done now.
Overall 2.5 out of 5 stars, not nearly as good as the first few books that were actually written by Andre Norton.
