Famous for THE line ….🐴 The Virginian’s pistol came out, and his hand lay on the table, holding it unaimed. With a voice as gentle as ever, a voice that sounded almost like a caress, drawling a very little more than usual so that there was almost a space between each word he issued orders to the man Trampas:“When you call me that, smile!”🐴And he looked at Trampas across the table. Yes, the voice was gentle. But in my ears it seemed as if somewhere the bell of death was ringing; and silence, like a stroke, fell on the large room.[Vintage, a mite slow because it was written in a slower time (1902) before cars and planes and the electrical grid and the hurly burly of our modern world. So, a story to be savored. Take your time.]
