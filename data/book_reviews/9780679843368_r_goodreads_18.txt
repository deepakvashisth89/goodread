I read this out loud to my (about to be )5 and 7 year olds. Some of the stories were a little scary for them, but I still think they got a lot out of it. We read it for a virtual book club hosted by a friend.

From my own perspective, I thought there was a wonderful variety to the stories. Some of them were hilarious, others were really creepy. Some were sad, some were angry, some were tricky, and a lot of them were hopeful.

I highly recommend this book!
