Once upon a time I owned a book titledThe 637 Best Things Anybody Ever Said, compiled by Robert Byrne. I remember lending it to someone who was looking for quotes to spice up a presentation he was preparing. (I remember who borrowed it, the ingrate, but I never saw it again. He probably thinks I have forgotten that he has it, but I haven’t. I can only hope that his presentation went over like a lead balloon.)However, later I discovered a compilation at a bookstore that not only included the original, but three more Byrne books previously published as separate volumes, all in one volume.Book I:The 637 Best Things Anybody Ever Said(originally pub. 1982)The title is misleading in a couple of ways, but the most obvious being that many of the best quotes were not said, but were written.Book II:The Other 637 Best Things Anybody Ever Said(originally pub. 1984)Book III:The Third and Possibly the Best-637 Things Anybody Ever Said(originally pub. 1986)Book IV:The Fourth-and By Far the Most Recent-637 Best Things Anybody Ever Said(originally pub. 1990)If one adds up the numbers they come out, of course, to 2,548.Here is a sample of what is included among the 2,548 Best Things Anybody Ever Said (or wrote):God has always been hard on the poor. – Jean Paul MaratThe difference between genius and stupidity is that genius has limits. – UnknownIf men could get pregnant, abortion would be a sacrament. – Florynce KennedyWhen I was a boy I was told that anybody could become President; I’m beginning to believe it. – Clarence Darrow (Yeah, me too)Henry James would have been vastly improved as a novelist by a few whiffs of the Chicago stockyards. – H.L. MenckenIf you haven’t got anything nice to say about anybody, come sit next to me. – Alice Roosevelt LongworthIllegal aliens have always been a problem in the United States. Ask any Indian. – Robert OrbenWe’ve upped our standards. Up yours. – Campaign slogan by Pat PaulsenI have read every one of the “2,548 Best Things” and I have to take exception to the idea that they are the best things anybody ever said (or wrote). I say this because the following were not included:A man who views the world the same at fifty as he did at twenty has wasted thirty years of his life. – Muhammad AliIf the world were a logical place, men would ride side-saddle. – Gloria SteinemLife is like a dogsled team. If you ain’t ever the lead dog the scenery never changes. – AnonymousAny jackass can kick down a barn, but it takes a carpenter to build one. – UnknownWhen I was a boy of fourteen, my father was so ignorant I could hardly stand to have the old man around. But when I got to be twenty-one, I was astonished at how much he had learned in seven years.—Attributed to Mark Twain, but he probably didn’t say it since he was only eleven-years old when his father died.Don’t buy things you can’t afford with money you don’t have to impress people you don’t like. – Mark TwainPatriotism is supporting your country all the time, and your government when it deserves it. – Mark Twain
