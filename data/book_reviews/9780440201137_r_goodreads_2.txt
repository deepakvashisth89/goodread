The Devereux Hotel on England's sunny south coast was home to elderly residents, the posh sort. Miss Naismith was the concierge and ran the Hotel with a tight fist, keeping everything up market, even the discussions. When one frail elderly resident fell to her death, Mrs Melita Pargeter had only recently arrived. She was younger than the rest, more laid back and casual, much to Miss Naismith's chagrin. An observant lady, one who referenced Mr Pargeter often, she soon noticed some odd, not quite right things about the place. And when another elderly lady died, Melita knew she was onto something. But she also knew the murderer would be aware of her nosiness and was quite convinced she would be next on the list. Would she be able to out think the murderer while discovering who it was?
