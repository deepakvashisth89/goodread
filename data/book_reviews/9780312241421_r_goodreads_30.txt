I have the Fourth Edition, from 2000, so it's out-of-date by now. This review applies to that edition of the book. The book is in wire-bound form, and contains nine sections: The Writing Process; Document Design; Clarity; Grammar, which includes ESL Grammar; Punctuation; Mechanics; Research Guide; and The Basics. It also includes a Glossary of Usage; Answers to Tutorials and Lettered Exercises (included in the book); Index; A Writer's Online Resources; ESL Menu; Revision Symbols (useful for proofreading). This edition if updated with 2001 APA guidelines.

Excellent resource for writers and students.
