I am so glad that I read this classic book to see how it (very much) differs from the movie. I thoroughly enjoyed The Wonderful Wizard of Oz. The second book, The Marvelous Land of Oz, did not hold my attention as much. It was interesting to see where some of the characters come from in Return to Oz, but I was not as invested in the story as the first book. Both are an easy read to escape after a long work day.
