Having spent 6years in saudi arabia during 1978 until 1983 I can relate to this book completely-dear Phyllis you have captured the culture during that era completely. Many of your experiences I too have experienced.After reading the book I felt that you were in fact a friend who had experienced the same adventure I had experienced. I can remember when the tallest building was the roundhouse in Riyadh just 4 stories. I used to walk to work as an engineer and collect letters from Philapino women who were behind gates and holding letters that they were desperate to get posted to loved ones.I have witnessed the terrible punishments that could be undertaken in the square for those who had committed moral crime.You wouldn't park a car near the square for fear of the roof being damaged for spectaters standing on it for a bettet view.I have so many related stories during this period that people have suggested I should write as you have done,even for the sake of history which in future and even now is almost unbelieveable. Thankyou so much for fullfilling my dreams as to what life must exist 'inside those high walls'. Tony
