Worst of her books. Could be my hopeless romantic bias and the situation with Nicole Nelson, the homewrecker. But it wasn't a romance. The happy ending seemed like an afterthought. The entire book as the last one in the Texas series seems an afterthought. I'm starting to realize Macomber's books are a hit or a miss. This one was a miss. Being an author of so many books, to spice it up I can see why she went with a different storyline like this. But I hated it. There was a feeling of frustration, despair and sadness.
