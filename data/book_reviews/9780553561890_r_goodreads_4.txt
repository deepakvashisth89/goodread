Another fine mess for the StarwolfThe Starwolf is crippled, in more ways than one. The Morethans and are tricky and uber paranoid. They'd give the Starwolf a run for their money, if the Starwolf could run. Gerrold plays 3 D chess with his characters, forcing the readers to think along with them. Fun, tho a bit slow in the middle, ergo 4 stars instead of 5.
