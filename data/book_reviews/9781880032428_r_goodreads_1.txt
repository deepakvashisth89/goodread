In this small book, almost pocket size, in simple terms, the author presents what each letter of alphabet stands for to create affluence and fulfillment in life.For example, letter E stands for exulting in the success of others, especially your competitors. Thus when needed, they become your helpers. E also stands for expectancy, meaning always expect the best. Thus, attracting such outcome.The book is divided into two parts. The second part, examines the qualities of the unified field, which is the source of all abundance and affluence in the universe. There are twenty five qualities. In simple terms, traits.One of the fields is of pure awareness. We should be always present in what we’re doing at the time, not drifting with our thoughts.The author recommends practicing one quality per day, and the way to do it is simply think about the word for example awareness. “Don’t analyze the word. Don’t try to define it. Don’t evaluate it. Don’t interpret it. Just have your attention on the idea.”Personally, I prefer such simple ‘manual’ for self-improvement, rather than those full-text books which with many studies and examples try to support the context but often turns out to be a dry read.
