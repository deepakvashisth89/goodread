One of the most depressing book I’ve read and at one that left me infuriated with its main character to a point I had hardly felt towards à literary creation. The fact that it is very well written makes it worse of course…We all have our limitations and reluctance in the way we live our lives but Lewis Percy must be the undisputed champion of limited ambitions and passivity. Whenever he eventually makes a move forward, it’s always very late and time seems to always fly by faster than his train of thoughts leaving him with an ever increasing handicap.Of course, the book wouldn’t have made such an impression on me if I didn’t see a little of myself in Lewis Percy. We all are Lewis Percy! Until, like him, we leave the old Lewis Percy behind and move to Paris. Or America.
