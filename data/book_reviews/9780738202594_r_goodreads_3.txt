This book comes closest to the excitement I feel for mathematics. If you are even mildly interested in mathematics and sciences, you will enjoy reading this. It presents the the interconnections between differing theories of mathematics that you learned in high school. If they thought these things in school, there would be more people who enjoy learning math and sciences.
