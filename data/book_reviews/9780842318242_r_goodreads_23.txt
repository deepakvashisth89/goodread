This is a very thorough theological study of how the Bible says we were created to relate to one another in our marriages. We read a few sections out loud to each other once a week, which was quite the commitment. It may be better to read on your own, but come together to read the questions. The questions were good for us- we were opening up about things we haven't discussed before and for the most part, they were questions designed to focus on your own individual part instead of attacking the other. Not a light read.
