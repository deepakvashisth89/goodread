3.5 rounded to 4This was a really sad read for me. This is a true survivor story of Tami Oldham who survived 41 days alone at sea after her boat was caught up in hurricane Raymond, which not only damaged the boat, but also killed her fiance Richard Sharp.This book deals with her coping up with the shock of Richard's death and being alone in the middle of the endless blue ocean. I think she was really brave considering her age and the emotional situation she was in. This story is heartbreaking. I wish she wrote a bit more about their relationship. From this short book, Richard's character is not portrayed in depth. This is the only book through which people can know and remember about that person. I wish this book had more depth.
