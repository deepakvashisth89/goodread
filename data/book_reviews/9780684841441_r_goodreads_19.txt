There is great writing (an objective parameter); there is favourite writing (a subjective parameter); and then there is a secret third thing — my relationship with anything Mary Gaitskill has ever written. No one comes as close to my heart and mind as she does. I would go as far to say, she’s terribly intelligent. I’m smitten by her ability to hold tight to her mission of an uncomplicated universe of emotion. Before I read her, I thought I was alone in my war with contradiction and ‘that-which-goes-unsaid’.

Reading this collection could inspire the reader to have better conversations with themselves at the least, and completely transform one’s internal world at its best.
