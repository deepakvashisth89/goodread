Wow. The story starts off with a bang and drops the reader into the middle of chaos. The MCs are a husband-wife PI team and their client, a dude with amnesia who appears to be mixed up in something bizarre and creepy. There is a really creepy psycho killer prowling around and some supernatural weirdness. This results in a great story that is part mystery, part horror, part urban fantasy. It has funny moments and tender moments that almost made me cry. When two characters were introduced, I predicted their deaths, and I was right, but there were more deaths I hadn’t expected. There’s a pretty high body count in this one.Writing is solid; it was very intriguing.
