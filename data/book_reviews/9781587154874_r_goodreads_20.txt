I read this book in my android / kindle device.My first thought discovering this sci-fi title was "hmmm... it looks really difficult to handle such a story" and it proves so, with Stith so-and-so successful on the task.A huge alien ship cuts and lifts Manhattan, putting the whole island city in a glass dome, along with a dozen other alien cities. Then a bunch of people is able to penetrate the wall, travel without been discovered to the heart of the huge ship and bomb it, just to discover this was not exactly needed. Then in a Star Trek TNG mode, heroes find a really un-realistic way to fight the ever bigger menace that appears on the sky and everybody is happy heading home.While a whole -and busy as we know it- city is in the centre of the plot, its residents disappear (with the exception of a few comical figures here and there) as the author follows a team lead by a colonel in its attempt to free the city. Mind, it is not a parody in the least.
