The pictures are even more wonderful than the story.

*This* makes it clear why Yolen is so famous. I've not fully appreciated her writing before, but the rhythm and pattern, and characters, plot, and themes here are all simultaneously lovely and exciting.

Still, the text wouldn't be as successful as it is without the wonderful pictures, with all the details. The beetle's suit, the snake curled up on its chair, the bedrooms of the mole sisters, and most especially Eeny's little doll & carriage. I do have to admit that I had to squint to see those details and so now I have a headache, but it was worth it, and younger eyes would probably be ok. I will look for more by the artist.
