I picked this up at a used bookstore, after being drawn by the title and cover. Unfortunately, the title is misleading. This is not a book of ghost stories. At best, it's a collection of legends and folklore from around the United States. While most of the stories were interesting, the one- or two-sentence mention at the end (basically "and if you go there, you might see her ghost!") just wasn't what I was expected when I purchased this book.
