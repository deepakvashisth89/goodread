A rather strange and rather dated book about a middle class woman in her 40’s who experiences a radical change of life when her husband is killed unexpectedly whilst they are on holiday in SE Asia. How she deals with this is a compelling and convincing read. The exploration of her relationship with her brother takes up most of the second part of the book and is well-done. However there is an awful lot of introspection which gets somewhat tedious and it finishes very abruptly.
