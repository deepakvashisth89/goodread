Any book the has great pictures, and an idea or concept that is easily understood, creates discussion or stirs imagination is a good book in my opinion.

This book demonstrates that you don't need to conform to everyone else. If you like a different style you can say something, you can do something and/or you can wear something. Chances are you will not be alone in your differences. Other kids afraid to speak out will feel better about themselves when they hear someone else speak up.

This book is a great guide to show how not to be afraid to do what you like and the next thing you know others who were to timid to speak even though they agreed will be right there or others who also thought that a change get was necessary will support you. Everyone has an opinion and everyone's opinion is different and that's ok.
