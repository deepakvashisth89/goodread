Some reviewers wanted less historical and technical explication and more story.That makes sense to me. Yet I found the story less compelling and satisfying than the research reports that buffer the spurts of story. But that’s a relative comparison.The book started out with some promise, but before I was halfway through I remembered that I’m only interested in heist stories when the characters are vivid and interesting, and the story has zip and momentum.I hung in till the end, but interesting is about the best thing I can say for it, and even then I would have preferred this to be either fiction or non-fiction, but not both.
