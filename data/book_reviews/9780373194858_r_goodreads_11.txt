The Wedding Lullaby is an amusing, fun book centered around a pair of dice. Those dice changed the lives of Brett Matthews and Laurel Worthington forever. It is the story of two people trying to find themselves and denying the chemistry and sparks flying between them. I loved the characters, especially Laurel. She is very brave, strong, feisty, and stubborn. I loved the story and now I need to go back and read the first book in the series! I received an advance copy of this book and I willingly chose to write an honest review.
