I love a good mystery, as everyone knows, and this one is pretty good. Alex and Jilly decide to attend a rave and stumble upon an intriguing mystery. The pair meets their old friend Andy at the rave. He is working for some rather seedy characters projecting holograms to add to the rave. When he turns up dead the next morning, Alex and Jilly are embroiled in following the clues. The deeper they get the danger increases. Read along to see if they can solve the mystery and save themselves in time.
