“The Fires of Jubilee” is very well-written, with wonderful, lyrical prose - until the murders begin. Nat Turner was the antebellum South’s greatest nightmare. By the time he was finished, more than 60 white men, women and children were killed: many were hacked to death in their sleep. Then the retribution killings started, with around 200 slaves and free blacks paying the price. The nation was never the same after this.When people say history is boring, give them this book. I highly recommend it!
