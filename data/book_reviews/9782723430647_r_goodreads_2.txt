The remakable cover caught my attention on a visit to Belgium, and I bought the first book, then without reading it, I bought more volumes of series over the next few years, and completed the collection with the help of Amazon.fr. When I finally got all the books, I decided to read them all at once, and it wasn’t a strain: the stories flow one into the other. The interesting thing is that the first book is in fact the newest one in time, and as the reading progresses, one goes back in time…A volume in a ten-book series of graphic novels, with exquisitely detailed artwork, and a gripping, masterfully-written story spanning decades, about a lost manuscript, and the lives of the people in whose hands it passes.
