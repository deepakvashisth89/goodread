What a haunting, heart-wrenching, beautiful story, told with love and ending with hope. After reading this book I feel ashamed for the times I feel overwhelmed with my piddly little problems. I became immersed in this story and after completing it am still thinking of the people in it. It's a true story, which makes it all the more poignant. It is amazing what the human spirit can endure and still have the ability to love.I think this is my favorite book about the Holocaust because it brought everything into focus and I felt like I was there with the two main characters--living through the horrors of they endured.I also loved it that there was some good news at the end. A miracle really.I highly recommend this book!
