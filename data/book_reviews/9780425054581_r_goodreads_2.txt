Possible Series Read 2023/24And it looks like I am heading into a series read, as I have finished this and am now onto book 3 .Our heroes of the Triplanetary Council do all they legitimately can to ensure that they win the "North American" election, whilst investigating all the wrongdoings of the enemy.Excellent Space Opera and such a fun read.Series Read 2015In this episode Virgil Samms, our square jawed handsome hero is called telepathically to Arisia, the planet that has hitherto been off limits to all the races of the Galaxy.He returns having been judged of suitable character and intelligence by the Arisians to wield a "lens". This gives its wearer unheard of mental powers, but with this power comes great responsibility.In the meantime the Eddorians (boo) have been led to believe that the Arisians (hooray) pose no threat to them whatsoever and so will ignore the Arisians helping humans become the driving force in the Galaxy.This is a great book and a worthy continuation of Triplanetary. A great easy reading Space Opera that got a lot of people through the years into sci fi.This review is part of my effort to catch up and write a review for every book I didn't, on first joining GR.
