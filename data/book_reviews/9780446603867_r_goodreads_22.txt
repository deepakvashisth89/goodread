This book was written in 1966 at a time where T.V. shows and comedians were the hit. It reminded me of one of those shows. Here you have the host dying while on-air live-in front of the whole audience back when it was live T.V. Carella is called in and at the beginning of the book you also get a look at his wife Teddy and their relationship. Carella and Meyer Meyer begin their investigation and they don’t believe that it was suicide.The second investigation deals with Kling and his fight he got into and his issues that are still lingering over from the previous book. The author does a good job separating the two and giving you the reader a good look at what is going on with solving each case. A good book with two really good storylines.
