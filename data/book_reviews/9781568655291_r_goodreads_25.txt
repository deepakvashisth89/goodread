Guilty PleasuresThis is the first Anita Blake novel I've read. Gotta say, pondering the possibilities of vampire civil rights legislation can be more fun than the average person should have in a day.Action packed to the end, there was no wind-down. Once business was done, the book was over. Good thing the next one, The Laughing Corpse is right here in the same volume.The Laughing CorpseThis one was a lot gorier than the first. But still good, a little more scary. Only thing that bugged me was Jean-Claude. Why does he always have to be wearing tight black jeans and a loose shirt that billows openly around his chest? He is such a frigging cliche! But Anita is a little spitfire! Regularly able to beat down even the biggest and scariest foes.Circus of the DamnedWow, this book kept you on the edge of your seat. I know there are more books with Anita Blake, animator/vampire hunter, so when so many crazy things happened to her I wondered if I would read more books with her so drastically changed. I don't want to give anything away, but there were just huge life altering things going on around every corner.Suffice it to say, I'm still looking forward to reading the next book.
