I can’t believe I read this hahahahahah. I bought this at the book fair and smashed through it in a a day to get me through my reading slump. I’ve never read a book with so many exclamation marks!!!!!!! It’s about time I read a classic historical romance like the smut mom I am, and this was everything I expected it to be. I think this author has a niche for Native American Indian romances which was wild. This book had me cackling but go off Cassie Edwards love your work!!!!!
