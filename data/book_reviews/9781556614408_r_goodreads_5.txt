This was the final book in this series and I liked it the least. The author believes in what he called a parentally supervised premarital apprenticeship and pushed it throughout the story. It also had too many letters between Corrie and her intended Christopher. But it was a clean read which I am always thankful for. And I am interested enough to see how Corrie and Christopher’s life turns out to read the next series called The Journals of Corrie and Christopher. So I gave the book three stars instead of 2.
