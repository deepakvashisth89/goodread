3.6 Stars - Well I think the collection is pretty good overall and does demand that the reader have a good grasp of the characters and their histories - even the ship gets a story. I think this is more of a treat for those that REALLY love this series and definitely not an entry point for those slightly interested. Looking forward to reading more from some of these authors here.Loose Ends - 4/5 - The anachronism of Calhoun working for the Maquis for a year when they haven't been established yet on TNG or DS9 put me off at the start but since that was a cover story I let it slide and moved on. The circumstance that brought Calhoun to where he was also irked me as it was a dumb idea but the story kind of addresses that. Anyway, it is a fun story otherwise and very much feels like a New Frontier story.All that Glisters... 5/5. I liked this one as it gives Shelby a great backstory prior to her introduction in The Best of Both Worlds. It shows why she is such an expert on the Borg and also her ambition and her desire to command but also showing that she is good at commanding as well.Waiting for G'Doh - 5/5. Fun Zac Kebron story. As I am just getting started on these it is fun seeing these characters work in an environment outside of the Excalibur and this is a prime one with Kebron being an ensign on his prior assignment...sort of.Lefler's Logs - 3/5 - Didn't care for the medium here as I think I was asked to remember too much of the Lefler storyline to get anything out of it. Necessary for the next story though.Alice, on the Edge of Night - 3/5 - This one was okay but I appreciated it trying to help you resolve Morgan Primus's frustrations with who/what she is and how that affects her family and her future.Revelations - 4/5 - I like Soleta and I appreciate how we find out with her how she discovers she is Romulan even though it is disturbing. Worf, pre-TNG, was a great contrast to see how much his character grew on that show and DS9.Turning Point - 5/5 - Did Shang-Chi steal the plot? Ha ha! As the title suggests this is where we aee Si Cwan make the turning point from moving outside his father's shadow and forming his own path.Q'uandry - 2/5 - A neat idea of bringing all of the characters (sort of) that Suzie Plakson played into one story that just didn't work here. It's a Q story so it's already fighting an uphill battle but the premise was too much to ask of me so I was glad it was short.Oil and Water - 2/5 - Burgoyne 172 is not really a character I care for and it seems like they wrote hir into a box here. The end and how preconceived notions can shape how we treat one another was a good idea, just executed not the best here.Singularity - 4/5 - I like McHenry so it's easy to like this story. Figuring out how his natural abilities could be hurtful to those that try to follow in his footsteps is an interesting lesson and a resolution I could appreciate.The Road to Edos - 4/5 - Funny story and a fun ride. I would rate it higher but I can't imagine how someone so incompetent like Peart got anywhere in Starfleet let alone the Department of Temporal Investigations.A Lady of Xenex - 2/5 - Didn't care for this one. Only really interesting part was the Danteri angle but otherwise judt spun its tires for most of the story where it ended in a quick resolution.Making a Difference - 4/5 - Even the ship gets a backstory. I have forgotten how much Peter David threw in the Excalibur into his other stories prior to New Frontier and I liked seeing where it was during the Battle of Sector 001. My biggest gripe was the levity they showed during such a dire situation and I doubt they would joke around so much. Just my own personal gripe though.Performance Appraisal - 5/5 - Found the story interesting enough and Mueller is an interesting enough character to explore more of her backstory. She is more of a character that does not allow ambition to drive her but seeing a need and filling it. Here we see an example of her doing so and doing it out of a motivation to protect the crew in more ways than one.Redemption - 4/5 - Almost perfect but was not sure if the character in this story was going to become the Martin Luther of the Redeemers or not at the end of the story or just keep propagating their nonsense. Really liked the ending.Out of the Frying Pan - 4/5 - I did not expect a nod to the Vulcan's Heart book here but it would have been quite out of left field had I not read it prior. I forgot hoe Soleta got into the situation at the beginning and I think my biggest pitfall for this entire collection is it expecting me to remember a lot of character backstory that I just don't. It does help with the little intros but some areas I am still lost. It has been 8 years since I read the first New Frontier story so forgive me if I don't remember everything.Through the Looking Glass - 0/5 - Goodreads may not let me rate a book 0 but I can here. Burgoyne at her absolute unlikeable phase and a borderline pedophile to boot - I don't want to read how prepubescent children turn you on. Just gross. Susan Wright right in her element- scraping the bottom of the barrel. What was the point of mentioning Scotty?A Little Getaway - 5/5 - Well at least it ends on a high note. The Shelby/Calhoun interactions have always been one of the best parts of New Frontier and I think Peter David knows it. Fun way to end the collection other than the timeline which was helpful and I should have used more.
