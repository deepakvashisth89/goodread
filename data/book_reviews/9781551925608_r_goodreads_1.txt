This is a cute tale about children who are different, and how lonely they can feel before they find their niche. It struck a chord with me, and I hope my nephew (for whom the book was so graciously signed by the author) enjoys it.Basically, Gulliver Mulligan is a bit of a giant - he's grown quite tall faster than anyone else in his class. His mother, his teacher, all the adults, notice he doesn't have any friends, but big and quiet Gulliver Mulligan never bemoans his state, not even when another classmate is very cruel to him.One day, a little - little - boy shows up, who is as tiny as Gulliver Mulligan is big, and when people stare, and this little boy starts to cry, Gulliver Mulligan notices just how alike they really are - even if they're really different.It's touching, and a pretty straightforward discussion of differences and strengths. I quite enjoyed it, and the illustrations are charming.
