My all-time favorite pirate book! Pickles is sent to Pirate School to learn to become a pirate. Students are praised for cheating, swearing, lying and the like. They attend classes like how to make cannon balls and how to speak pirate. When Pickles finds out the pirate teachers are planning on holding the students for ransom, the students organizes a mutiny and it turns out the pirate students learned more than the pirate teachers realized! The illustrations for this zany book are perfect! I just can't recommend it enough!
