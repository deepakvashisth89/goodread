Three short stories of love and the rocky road to a wedding.Jack and Jill have more than the usual ups and downs. Finding their way together is not a fast path, but well worth the journey.Carrie and Hank might have made it to the wedding, but get to the I Do's is another matter. Sometimes, taking something for granted can cost you, but forgiving can help love in the end.Chase and Natalie are in a race to find love, but the wedding might be for the wrong reasons, and even the wrong man. Dreams can really come true.
