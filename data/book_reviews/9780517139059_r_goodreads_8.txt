It was interesting seeing this version of the Kirk family dynamic. The 2009 film seemed to posit that the absence of his father via death made Jim Kirk a troublemaker. And here, it seems to be due to the absence of his father via work-related duties. Robert April even feels responsible for some of this. And with Jim Kirk being young, I guess it's natural he'd be called Jimmy here. I also quite like what Jimmy had to say about pre-destiny vs destiny. That really resonated with me. There are no things set in stone just because of what day we were born; we make things happen ourselves.
