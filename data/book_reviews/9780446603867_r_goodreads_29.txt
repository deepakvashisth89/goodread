This one mixes 2 stories, both focusing, like McBain often does, on warped kinds of love, and what people do in its name. It's a decent entry, the recovery arc of a major character continues and there's the usual interesting minor characters being interviewed where McBain's dialogue sparkles.The opening scenes for both stories are memorable ones, too, one of the few sequences I've remembered from when I read the books 20+ years ago out of any of the books so far.
