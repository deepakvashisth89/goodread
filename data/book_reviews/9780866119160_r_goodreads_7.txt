A great starter book for the history of a great American Hero .. Clara Barton. A fast read it fills you with hope and need to go out and help! At least it did me. Clara looked up to her big brother, who taught her to be more than an average girl for her time, ride bare back, face your fears and never give up. When he becomes ill after a fall she nurses him back to health and unknowingly starts her journay of a life time of helping others. Its a long road but Clara walks it strong, tall and faithful making a difference everywhere she goes. Starting the American Red Cross might be the larget event we recall in her history, but she did many a great service to many people.
