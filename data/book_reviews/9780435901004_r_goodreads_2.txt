If I have ever forgotten why Chinua Achebe is the blueprint for the quintessential African writer, then these stories have reminded me. Who else can write a story about The Madman and make it so layered with both cultural and emotional meaning? Who else can write a story so filled with a quintessential blandness that you realise that sugar might be a big deal? Who else can tell a story about a society degrading in a time of war...and highlight the stupendous double standard of the men running it?At some points, I had to put down the book and just reflect. Achebe makes me think, he makes me proud of my Igbo roots, and most of all, he writes a damn good story.
