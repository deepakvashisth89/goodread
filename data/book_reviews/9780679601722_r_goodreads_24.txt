I'm so sad to say that I didn't really like this one. I'm getting the impression that I would definitely like it better as a movie (or performed live) though, so it's not the story itself that was the issue, but rather the pacing and writing, and the fact that very few things were actually happening.I recognise its importance, and I'm glad I read it for that reason alone, but I have be honest and say that I was bored. I do tend to struggle with slower paced books in general, so don't let that stop you from givingA Raisin in the Suna try if you've been meaning to.I'd normally give it 2 stars based on how I felt while reading, but I don't think it deserves 2, when I look at it as a whole, so I'm going to give it a slightly higher rating.
