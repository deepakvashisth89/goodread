Well....what a bonkers crazy novel!There is just something about the way Sue writes that makes you want to keep at it and turn the page!I read all the novels of Adrian Mole as and when they came out ( I was 9 when the very first one was released)...and loved them all.So when I saw this one in a charity shop I had to buy it.But wow.....it's just madness!She creates such unusual and diverse characters very much in the style of Victoria Wood....but man my head was all a whirl at the sheer pace and wackiness of it all!...I am glad I read it but I don't think I could read it again....it's a cornucopia of zaniness!!!
