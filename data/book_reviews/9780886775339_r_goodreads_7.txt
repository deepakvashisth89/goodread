I was initially skeptical about the idea of editor Mike Resnick giving each author an "assignment," but this device actually ensured the collection kept a unifying sensibility while allowing everyone to flex their creative muscles.All in all,Whatdunitsis a solid and entertaining collection with only a couple of duds. My favorites were "Dead Ringer," "True Faces," "Murder On-Line," "Monkey See," "Murder Under Glass," and "The Colonel and the Alien."
