I figured because this was written by T.H. White, it would be entertaining; I was wrong. If you want to learn, in excruciating detail, how to build an ark out of a barn and provision it for the presumed second great flood, working with a group of shiftless and untrustworthy rural farmers, this would be the book for you--particularly if unflattering mid-century stereotypes of the Irish amuse you. It did pick up when the actual action started, but this was only during about the last quarter of the book--and it wasn't worth it.
