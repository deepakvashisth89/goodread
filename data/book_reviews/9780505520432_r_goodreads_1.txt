Alena Yor wasn’t eager to board the Moon Courier for a trip to her new job as cartographer on a distant planet. Space vessels made her uncomfortable with their confined space. The only reason she tolerated the voyage was due to handsome officer Garner Rie. When an emergency forced them to land on a habitable planet, she turned to Garner for strength. The captain couldn’t cope with the situation and Garner was the only man whose leadership the survivors could rely on. Caste differences prevented her and Garner from socializing under normal circumstances. But as their small group fought for survival, Alena ceased to care and only wanted comfort in his arms. What would happen when they were rescued and she would have to resume her elite role? Is there a solution for the star-crossed pair? Excellent world building and likeable characters will have the reader turning pages to see how the story will end. "A World Away" is a wondrous tale that will help you escape reality for a few pleasant hours.
