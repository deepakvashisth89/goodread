Another hit for Fern Michaels. This is one of her books that stands by itself and is not one of a series. Lorrie Ryan is taking her nephew, Davey who is a hemophilic, on a camping trip while her sister Sara and brother-in-law Andrew go to Florida where Andrew is scheduled to testify in a murder trial. One of his students was killed and he had witnessed an altercation with an unknown man prior to his student being killed.

Lorrie took Davey to the Philadelphia Zoo and then headed for a campground to spend the night. Davey took a walk with his dog, Duffy before dinner. On his walk, he went by another spot where a couple was camped in a pop-up camper. Duffy started barking and checking out the camper which annoyed the man. He yelled and threw Duffy away from the camper. Davey and Duffy took off for Aunt. Lorrie's RV. Later, they ran across the couple burying a man by the pond. They ran with the man following them. Duffy ran to Aunt Lorrie's RV, but being slower, Davey hid in the man's camper. Before he can sneak out, the man cranked the top down, capturing Davey. The couple then got in the truck and drove off with Davey in the camper. No one knows where Davey is. How will he get free and will he find his way back to Aunt Lorrie?
