We want to think of our own potential as a quality that is always just within our grasp, the sword in the stone that might move by our hand alone. What greatness may lie in the purity of our purpose?I think we love stories of leaders who rise from humble beginnings because, frankly, the only good ones we've ever had have always begun that way. Our true leaders have never started out their lives with silver spoons. They're not meant to have criminal backgrounds or to be geriatric, either.Where there is no direction, the deeds of men run shallow and wander and are wasted.I think we will always have times that feel darker than others, when civilization seems to have run aground, when our future seems insecure.No darkness lasts forever.
