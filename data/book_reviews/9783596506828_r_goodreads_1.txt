After the Funeral is another entertaining Hercule Poirot mystery. I say pretty much the same thing about every Christie novel, but it's twisty and fun and masterfully narrated by the talented Hugh Fraser. I did highly suspect the actual murderer this time around (a rarity for me and this series!) but not the reasoning behind their actions. Timothy Abernethie reminds me somewhat of an adult version of Dudley Dursley from the Harry Potter series, so do be prepared to be annoyed by his character. All in all, though, a delightful read. 4 stars.
