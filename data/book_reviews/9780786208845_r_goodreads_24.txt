This was a good mix of camp and serious. Definitely darker thanThe Bride, but still contained Garwood's wit and humor.Poor Brenna goes through it and a lot of it is the hero's fault. He doesn't actually treat her cruelly himself, but he's very tied up in his vengeance plots against another Laird and leaves her to the crappy in-laws from hell. Also the enemy Laird does a terrible thing that really upset me.He kills her horse and has the remains sent to her and Conner.We also get to see quite a bit of Jamie and Alec, which was very nice.I'm not sure how historically accurate these books are, but they are very entertaining.
