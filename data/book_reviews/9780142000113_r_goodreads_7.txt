This book, starring Episcopal (Anglican) College Chaplain Rosemary Stubbs, offers a complicated mystery with a multitude of believable and complex characters.In fact, I was often confused about the characters because there were so many. However, if this book were the first in a series, the many potential "repeat" characters would add richness rather than bringing confusion to the reader.Unfortunately, I don't think the series ever came to be - if it ever does, though, I will be looking for it. This is a very promising first book.
