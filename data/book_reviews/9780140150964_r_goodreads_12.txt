A good selection of texts from the greatest thinker of the 19th century. I've never read these portable editions before, and they are very solid. Dozens of letters from Marx's life are included, and it reveals a bit more of Marx the man, rather than Marx the theorist - a familiarity in a philosopher's personal life assists greatly in understanding their view of the world. Also included are selections from Kapital, Grundrisse, and the 1848 manuscripts. Probably essential reading for anyone on the left.
