Another book from my sister-in-law's bookshelf, which I brought along home to finish reading. I kept running into hints that there was backstory I'd missed, and sure enough, realized this is the second book in a series (or...Cycle? Never heard of such a thing; what does it mean?) Of course I ordered the rest, as well as a copy of Dicey's Song for our own shelf.

I enjoyed the relationships in this coming-of-age novel, and the way the characters grow and change. There's Mina, who feels like she's too much for the world, and Dicey, who never feels like she's enough. Gram, who can do whatever she needs to do, whether she knows how to do it or not, and Millie, who needs help every step of the way, but is loyal to the end. School-teachers who misunderstand but are willing to change their minds; others who remain antagonistic.

Of course, as in life itself, there is also music.
