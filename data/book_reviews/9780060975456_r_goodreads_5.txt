‘Way Past Cool’, by Jess Mowry grappled with my often fleeting, short attention span in the ‘90’s several years after it was first published. The story was well written and to say it was enough to hold me by my shirt collar until I finished the book is saying something.

Notable as well, although it was years ago, I remember staying engaged and feeling curious about the characters until the end. Ty was one character who stood out to me through my read.

This book followed the lives of young, unsupervised urban youth in Northern California.
These characters knew tragedy, survival and they understood aspects of life that their rugged turf forged upon them.
