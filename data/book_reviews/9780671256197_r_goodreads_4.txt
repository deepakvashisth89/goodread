We got this when we first decided to have Willa Ann. By the time I read the first chapter I was pregnant and started reading preggo books again. Well here she is at 7 months old and I'm picking it up again. I started a few chapters in because I didn't really need to read about "pregnancy and childbirth the second time around." The statistics are alarming and ring pretty true in the little I've read. I'll write more when I finish.

UPDATE:

While I find most books that are about anything having to do with me to be absolutely riveting, a lot of what I've read in this book causes me to utter "bollocks!" under my breath (or out loud). I'm not British, but it just came out--I don't know why. The studies cited just don't fit in with my own experience and that of other "second children" that I know.
It's nice that there have been studies on the topic though. I wonder if there were a new edition with updated data if it would fit better with what I've seen.

It just goes to show you can't really label people according to things like birth order.
Except the part where I learned that second children have especially good social skills...that's dead on. Right? ...Right? ....where did y'all go?
