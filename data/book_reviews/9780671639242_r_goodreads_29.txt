Law enforcement hunts two murderous survivalistsInteresting book regarding a father-son team of survivalists who attempted to kidnap a young woman and fatally shot one her would-be rescuers. After being tracked for months in the wilds of Montana by numerous law enforcement officers, including a determined county sheriff, they are finally brought to justice.
