This was a new author for me and the book was written before I was born. I'll be honest, when I try to read a book that was written before I was born I have the hardest time getting into the book and usually wind up not finishing. I thought this would be the case with this one too, but I was pleasantly surprised at how much I loved this book and the characters. Reading this was a walk back through time when kids actually played made up games. And I loved that the kids were fired up about a time in history. People would probably think that what they were doing was weird, but I'm all for their made up game of pretending they were ancient Egyptians. It was great how the 2 girls welcomed a few other kids into their creative mix. There is a 2nd book in this series and I can't wait to read that one.
