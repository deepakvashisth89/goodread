So this book five of the adventures of Sparhawk and friends -- if you liked the first four, you'll like this one. If you didn't, you're probably not looking at this ranking or reading this review.I confess I have an unexplainable fondness for David Eddings, despite his weaknesses, so maybe others wouldn't enjoy the usual fantasy tropes he rolls out so professionally as much. For me, though, I'm ready for volume six.
