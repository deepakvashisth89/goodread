I like to keep an open mind on this topic, so sometimes I read a little about it. This book is supposed to be an instruction manual on how to accumulate and tap psychic energy for your benefit: the gift of prophecy, enhancing creativity, influencing others, dowsing, proving reincarnation, developing clairvoyance, psychometry, and telepathy. Addressed to novices, it contains far too much jargon to be understandable to somebody new to the field. It also makes a lot of extravagant claims, none of which are substantiated by any references, research, or other evidence. A few famous cases are cited, but these have no science to back them up, and are based on what appears to be subjective wishful thinking. Some of the techniques are pure psychology - for example, when influencing others, it suggests meditating first and then simply being confident. Calming yourself and focusing on your goal in a difficult interaction is elementary communication skills; it's got nothing to do with "psychic energy"! It's a great pity that titles on psychic phenomena almost always tend to make wild jumps in credibility, lending no credence - not even plausibility - to the subject matter. If it was a manual in any other field, it would fail miserably. Still, it was an interesting read. The best thing I can say about it is that it gave some ideas to be researched further.
