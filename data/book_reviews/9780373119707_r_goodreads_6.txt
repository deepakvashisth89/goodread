One thing I like about Daphne Clair's novels is that her heroes are usually decent men, not nasty particularly, not even when the heroine is being nasty herself. Lovers' Lies has a wonderful hero, Joshua, who genuinely loves Felicia. She loves him back but she vowed to herself to make him pay because she thinks he caused her stepsister to commit suicide. Felicia was 13 at the time of the tragedy and knew the sister and Josh had slept together.Felicia leads him on, has a wonderful night together, then publicly dumps him. Since Josh truly loves her, he does his best to get her back; unlike several HP authors' heroes he does not himself pursue revenge on Felicia. In fact he doesn't need revenge because Felicia fell in love with him and is suffering herself.The story is quite good and Ms. Clair spends plenty of time on the Chinese scenery. I would like to give this 4 stars instead of 3 but it doesn't quite reach that depth of emotion. There's something a little too pat about Josh falling for Felicia.Plus Naksed hit the #1 main disgust-o moment: Felicia wants to name her and Josh's firsts daughter after her immoral stepsister. Yep, the step sister she thought killed herself over Josh but who was sleeping with at least one other man (plus her nice guy husband). Would you do that to someone you love?
