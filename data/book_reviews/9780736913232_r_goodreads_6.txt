This story (which I read and enjoyed as a teenager and reread recently) is filled to the brim with a MC who is a Mary Sue and a romantic interest who is almost (if not outright) abusive. It goes about the story with the idea that "she can change him."Aside from that, she is very submissive and doesn't really have any personality aside from later in the story when she has her son.In the end, it romanticizes abusive relationships and the idea that the man can be changed if a woman is kind and gentle enough.Please don't read this book.
