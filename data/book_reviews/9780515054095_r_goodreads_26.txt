A comparison as I’ve read Tied Up in Tinsel by Marsh recently- both take place with a snow storm; and, both involve a gathering arranged by the man who owns a large stately home. The difference with this book is that there is no event (like Christmas) to draw the people to the place. In this instance the man has invited people that he knows dislike each other to some degree to have a party/psychological event. Well what he gets is murder.The first part of the book is not pleasant as the group is truly sniping at each other. However; once the murder happens- the drama is no long just show it’s real now. The mystery wasn’t too hard to whittle down into the who- but the systematic how and how to prove it was very interesting. Marsh does have a great way with language and with describing people and places that — while I didn’t “enjoy” the mystery I enjoyed her writing and telling of events a great deal. She has a great flare for writing characters that are believable and interesting.
