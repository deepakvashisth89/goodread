An entertaining and historical romance. This is book 3 in the Series, The Dennehy Sisters.The setting is in 1880s Colorado.Mary Margaret(Maggie)Dennehy wants to be a doctor. She is waiting for word if she has been accepted to medical school.Connor Holiday is rancher and owner of the double H. Maggie and Connor are from prestigious wealthy families. Their parents have prearranged a marriage for Maggie and Connor.Maggie's only concern is to keep her independence. Connor loves the ranch life but agrees to take Maggie to Colorado.She can't help but be mesmerized by Connor's charms. and her heart has a serious craving that needs satisfying.Circumstances change that once they arrive at their destination.I received this book for free from ebook discovery.
