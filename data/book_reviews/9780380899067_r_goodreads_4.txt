#3 in the Thomas Black series. This entry was a finalist for the 1987 Shamus Award for Best Paperback.Thomas Black series - Thomas Black hits pay dirt when he shadows an alleged philanderer to a tryst. But instead of two illicit lovers, he finds two bodies, an apparent murder-suicide. The dead man's grieving widow doesn't believe her late husband pulled the trigger--on his lover or himself.Everyone--including Black's own sexy client--seems to be hiding something.
