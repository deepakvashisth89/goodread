The only reason I finished this book is because of where I bought it - at a tiny used book store built in to an old victorian apartment down-state where went for a friend's wedding last year. We went exploring off the beaten path and ended up browsing the shop.

This book looks like a romance... is billed as a romance... but reads like a history book. If you want to well-researched picture of how people lived in the 1300's (racism and all) then sure, read this. But if you're hoping for a story, skip it. The writing is dry, the characters two-dimensional.

Also, Jeanne Lancour is a female pen name belonging to a male author. Just, why.
