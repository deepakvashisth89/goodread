If you’re a woman in your 20s or 30s and have gone through the canon event of a friendship breakup, or struggle to understand why some friendships fizzled out, please read this book now! This book is from the 80s but still proves to be just as timely (see: all the friendship breakup videos on tik tok). The book explores the complex psychology of female friendships/bonds, revealing how envy, competition, resentment, miscommunication, and anger can manifest in these relationships. I like how this book did not lean into the misogynist “catty woman” trope but led with research and case studies to advocate for the continuity of healthy female friendships. An insightful read if you’re seeking to be intentional in your current or future friendships!
