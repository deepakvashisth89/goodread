Not nearly as fun as the Marx book of the same kind, in my opinion, but still not a bad introduction to Lenin and the build up leading to the Russian Revolution, or as I learned, series of revolutions. I read it because I liked the style of the Marx book, and it did have a similar style, but it's not nearly as well executed. You can follow the narrative of Lenin reasonably well, but the rest really lost me at times. I realize it's a dense subject matter with many moving parts, but they try to introduce far too many characters, terms, and organizations, and then don't bother to expound on them beyond a blurb. It's schizophrenic mess in parts, to be honest. I learned some interesting things about Lenin, but I don't feel like I gained much else from it. Not all bad, but I wouldn't recommend.
