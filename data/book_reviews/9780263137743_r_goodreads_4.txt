For once, an insta-lust beginning that I believed. In fact, I believed in the H/h from their first meeting to their HEA. They were perfect together even when they inadvertently hurt each other by trying to talk through their differences. Because talk they did.The credit has to go to the bubbly 22 year-old English heroine who is a temporary secretary to the hero at the French chateau and winery that he has just inherited. Hero is a war correspondent who isn’t sure he wants to settle down in the old neighborhood.They meet cute when the heroine comes out of the shower in a bath towel and meets another temporary employee who is in her room by mistake. The employee is leering and obnoxious and the heroine flips him on his back in a judo move. The Hero arrives just as she is flipping the intruder and her towel falls off.The hero gets an eyeful and he wastes no time in firing the temporary employee and moving the heroine to a room close to his. (For her own safety, of course).The heroine is mortified and attracted to the hero and wants to keep everything on a professional level and she tells the hero everything on her mind. Naked body, naked thoughts – she is revealed to the hero and he is smitten. (At least the reader knows that – he keeps his clothes on and his thoughts covered for quite sometime).Once they do have sex, the story isn’t as much fun because they are both troubled.The heroine is troubled because she isn’t sure how the hero feels about her. And she isn’t sure how she feels about leaving after the summer and going to her high-powered job in Paris. Then her pen pal friend tells her what the hero had done to her family five years before. Seems he was engaged to her sister, broke it off, and the sister attempted suicide. When the hero drove her teenaged brother home after a confrontation, he crashed into a tree paralyzing the brother.The heroine has a hunch there is another side to the story, but the hero won’t tell her. He thinks the people he cares about are cursed because of him. He has major survival guilt. A colleague of his is shot doing his overseas assignment. His parents died in a fire on holiday in India when he was young – but left him at home in France. He survived the car crash unscathed, etc. . . He is also troubled that the heroine is unhappy – and that he is the cause. But he can’t quit her.There are also some OW and OM complications. The OW is the housekeeper who makes it look like she was intimate with the H within days of the H/h having sex. (Don’t worry – no cheating) The OW is the fired employee who keeps turning up like a bad penny.They talk all of this out – even clearing the air about the OW and the OM. But the hero is still wallowing in his guilt and superstition of something happening to the heroine if he loves her. But the heroine finally takes action and goes to the pen pal’s family and finds out the real story.The fiancé was two-timing the hero and only attempted suicide after her boyfriend rejected her. The drunk brother grabbed the wheel and caused the accident that paralyzed him. The family finally absolves the hero of his guilt. The truth has set everyone free. HEA.Normally I don’t like when there is too much wallowing in the past – especially if mommy or daddy had done wrong. But in this case, it was the hero’s choices that caused his anguish. All the talk in the world didn’t help until the heroine took action and the hero could finally have his side of the story told. I really liked this story. I liked both H/h. I think I’ll like it better on a re-reading. There’s a lot of nuance in their conversations and now that I know the whole story, I’m sure I’d pick up a lot more clues as to the hero’s true feelings.
