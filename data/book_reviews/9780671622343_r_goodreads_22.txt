Another writer I’ve always delayed reading. This grew on me the longer it went on. 80s college kids. Different narrators, some who are frustratingly unreliable. Tons of sex. Booze. Drugs. Betrayal. People being awful to each other. Sounds like a fun time, right? Hahaha. Well, I’m surprised I liked it because the first third or so didn’t really grab me. I hated everyone in it. But, I was interested. It’s truly dark in parts, darkly funny in others.

Also, if a book had a soundtrack this would be a great one. Lots of my favorite bands were brought up or talked about. Talking Heads, REM, New Order, Roxy Music. Many more were mentioned. I kept putting the book down to listen to a song I hadn’t heard in years.
