My first introduction to James Ellroy, "L.A. Confidential" is a phenomenal novel that I read only after seeing the 1997 film adaptation starring Guy Pierce, Russell Crowe, and Kim Basinger, which I absolutely loved. Imagine my surprise to find that the book was even better. Director Curtis Hanson---simply for time purposes, I'm sure---had to trim a lot of scenes and characters from the book.

The third book in Ellroy's L.A. Quartet series, "L.A. Confidential" has numerous stories going on simultaneously, which is pretty de rigueur for Ellroy. Incredibly fast-paced, ultra-violent, and extremely politically incorrect, the series paints a pretty clear picture of a specific place and time in American history. Hollywood, Ellroy seems to be saying in these books, is a microcosm for the rest of the country.
