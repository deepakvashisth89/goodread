I can remember watching this movie with my grandchildren many years ago. Reading the book, however, did not see very much like the movie, a situation I encounter often. Somehow, reading the book always seems better, more enlightening than watching what others have manipulated to fulfill some bent of imagination. The House with a Clock in Its Walls by John Bellairs, is a fun-filled adventure for most ages - perhaps not for the youngest of readers as they won't be able to follow the action easily. I quite liked the action and the subtle lessons taught. Can Lewis, his uncle Jonathan, and their neighbor Mrs. Zimmerman find the ticking clock in time to prevent a disaster? What obstacles will they encounter along the way? Just how powerful is Jonathan's magic? Will Lewis get himself into trouble yet again? A fun romp through the land of magic and imagination. Recommended for youngsters and oldsters alike!
