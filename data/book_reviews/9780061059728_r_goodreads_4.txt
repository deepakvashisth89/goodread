Usually I just rate books without writing a review. In this case I'm making an exception for this terrible, terrible excuse for a book.If I could, I would sue the writer for emotional damages and time wasted. The writing style is far below par and if there is any worse anti-hero than Scott I have yet got to encounter him/her.After about 100 pages I couldn't take it anymore and tossed the book away. Even if the rest of the book (and/or trilogy) would be nobel prize quality it just would not make up for the torture of reading this first part. Avoid at all costs, unless you're into self-infliction of psychological pain! Your brain will be very thankful.I'd rather wear a pink tutu and let myself get bombarded with overripe tomatos for a whole day on some marketplace than read one more single word.
