Procedurals and crime novels are among my favorite genres but Murder at the Savoy just wasn't the tea for me.Maybe all the hype and build-up I have read online about this series [right after buying this book blind] raised my expectations way too much. Perhaps I have read the weakest entry in the Martin Beck saga, I really don't know. Maybe I should've started with book 1, but this was the only one available lol...The story is in classic format...straight forward as they come, doesn't hold too many surprises but a relaxing read nonetheless, like a Sunday afternoon made for TV movie. I just can't dodge the feeling that I read a filler episode.The Swedish names for food, places and people's names introduced to me here were fun to get wrong in pronouncing for sure. In any case, Savoy was still a pleasant yet unremarkable that made my weekend more chill like an iced caramel macchiato.
