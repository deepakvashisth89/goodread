Review of my second reading. My first was so long ago that I remember none of the details. I’m wondering if I actually read this one in German?!? My German was good by then (1996), but it might explain my complete lack of retention.Never mind, this one hits closer to home than the previous 2 installments. Quite literally. The murdered body was found in a churchyard in Stoke Poges, which is where my husband’s office (still) is and obviously, we don’t live too far away.Our son is in secondary school, and having a boy in the UK school system (albeit not the private boarding school that is the subject here), makes everything stand out in stark relief. The private school system and the people who populate it seem horribly messed up. Public Grammar school is bad enough, but this sort of thing is at a whole nother level.The story was very twisty, such that it kept you wondering how they would unravel it all. What I do like about these books is that the private lives of the recurring characters continues across the books. I do remember the details of their lives/relationships much better than the murder mystery actually. I wonder what that says about me?
