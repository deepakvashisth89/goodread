“‘And no dream,’ he sighed quietly, ‘is altogether a dream.’”The Kiss(detail), by Gustav Klimt.This pre-Freudian, not-so-erotic novella was the basis for Kubrick’s swan song, Eyes Wide Shut. After a man’s ego is shattered by a sexual confession from his wife, he sets off alone on a nocturnal odyssey through decadent Vienna and falls into an erotic netherworld. The book is very much “style over substance,” but that’s more than excused by its intoxicating atmosphere, especially in a particular scene in the middle—which I’ll leave a mystery for anyone who doesn’t already know it. Recommended, especially if you like the film.
