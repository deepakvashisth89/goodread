Published some thirty years ago, this was Edward Rutherford’s first novel and set his style; a thousand-plus page books about a few families in a particular location from ancient to modern times. I didn’t find his book on New York that compelling but decided to try this one and I’m glad I did. Based around Stone Henge and Salisbury Cathedral, which is only an hour from where I live, it traces the history of England from the end of the last ice age to modern times. I enjoyed it, especially the earlier sections about tribes first coming to settle, the Romans, and the Vikings. Towards the end, the book felt a bit rushed and like he was trying to cram in every major historical event, and with there often being centuries between chapters you just get really into reading about certain characters and then they’re gone. Overall, it was an enjoyable way to learn a lot of history I didn’t know and I am looking forward to reading ‘Russka’, his novel about Russia.
