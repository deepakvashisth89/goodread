Laurel Worthingham and Brett Matthews were married On a lark. They spent one night together than they divorced. For Laurel it was a way to escape her disaster of a life. For Brett it was a way to be part of the rich and powerful elite. After their divorce they parted and went back to their regular lives. Suddenly everything changes Laurel is pregnant. She comes to Brett for a job. Can they became a family? I loved Laurel. Previously she was pampered but now she finds herself abandoned by family and friends. I was rooting for her to succeed. She is off to a rough start, but I loved how this woman is a fighter. She's a potent combination of strength and sweetness. She and Brett make a great couple. Brett grew up without a father so he wants to be a great dad. I loved him for that. However, I wish that he had approached Laurel in a straight forward manner with his hopes. These two make a dynamite couple. Their sweet dialogue is enjoyable and their chemistry makes me swoon. I fell in love with The Wedding Lullaby. Melissa McClone wrote a sweet romance that made me feel good. I loved the characters, the romance and the HEA. The One Night to Forever is a charming series.
