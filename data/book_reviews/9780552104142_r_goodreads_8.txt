This book kept me turning the pages. Whenever I had to put it down, it stayed in my mind. It was very well-written, and the main character, Sarah, was sympathetic and likable. So many bad things happened to her, though, that I wondered how she could keep going.There were a lot of side characters and everyone had problems. The problems were interesting, but occasionally stressful to read about.This is not asatisfyingbook in that hardly any of the characters ever get anything they want. As a reader, I like to identify with certain characters and enter into the spirit of their desires and hopes. In this book, most of them desired and hoped in vain, and I, the reader, did so along with them. So, no, it wasn't satisfying, but it also wasn't predictable, and ultimately, I think that will make it more memorable.Although I noticed the frequent POV changes common to older novels, they weren't jarring. The author describes settings very well. The story flows and carries you with it.I recommend this book.
