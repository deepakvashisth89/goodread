This book is the third in the Wexford series and Rendell is still developing the characters of Wexford and his sidekick Burden. Anita Margolis a wealthy young, beautiful and a bit of a swinger who has disappeared after a party. The brother who she lives with in a cottage is an eccentric artist and hopeless about day to day life as well as self obsessed.A new detective Drayton is assigned also to the case. He falls in love with Linda Grover a pretty dodgy corner shop owner’s daughter. The investigation uncovers a mechanic with strange habits, char women or cleaners that are obsessive in one case and a bit lackadaisical in another.I was fooled with the red herrings but enjoyed the twist at the end. The clues are there to solve it but I am afraid I am more Pink Panther than Poirot or Holmes. I do like that the stories are set in Sussex where I currently live. Not one of her best stories and set in the swinging 1960s but I still found it entertaining.
