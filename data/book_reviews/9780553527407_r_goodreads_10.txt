“You want to remember that while you're judging the book, the book is also judging you.”― Stephen King, Night Shift"Night Shift" remains the best collection of short stories by King, IMHO.I haven’t read “You like it Darker” though, and that one got really good reviews.Even though the quality of the stories is uneven, as a whole the collection offers quite a lot to digest and think about!Some of the stories are weaker and look more like outlines for novels and novellas to come, some feel rushed and too short (I suppose King really needed the money at the time so he tried to maximise his income), some are totally bizarre, while others are calmer in tone and emotionally rich.Overall, it is a great read by the young King who was still developing his skills at the time and didn’t have the luxury of writing out of sheer pleasure, the way he could once he became famous.We often recall his own words from numerous interviews - his wife Tabatha asking him to invent a new monster every time they needed money to support the growing family.Here is the list of my favourite stories from the collection - in printing order in the book:Jerusalem’s LotI am the DoorwayThe BoogeymanTrucksSometimes they come backThe LedgeQuitters Inc.Children of the CornThe Last Rung on the LadderOne for the RoadThere is really something for every profile of Constant Reader in this collection.Pure horror, suspense, malice and dread, twilight zone like atmosphere, totally bizarre gore, which craft and voodoo stuff… you name it - you got it!I’ve been returning to this collection every couple of years and always enjoying it immensely. It has been 20 years now and there is always a bit different feeling about each and every story.Amazing stuff!
