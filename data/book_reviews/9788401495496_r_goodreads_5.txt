The Bad Place starts out following our two leads Bobby and Julie Dakota who run a private investigations company when a prospective client Frank approaches them seeking help for an unusual problem. Frank has amnesia and only knows his name and that someone is after him.Strange events start occurring to Frank. As the Dakota's attempt to figure out Frank's past and who is after him their world is turned upside down and everything they knew to be true in life comes shattering down around them.Frank and the Dakota's are in serious trouble and so is the life of everyone close to them.The Bad Place is jam packed full of supernatural phenomena, gritty grotesque violence, alien creatures, terrible sadness, overwhelming fear and one psychotic madman on the loose. The character development is top notch as always by Koontz even the side characters who are fully developed and engaging never making a dull moment when the words are not on the action.Whilst it may not be as highly praised as some of his works The Bad Place is a horror novel which will stay with you and delivers real scares with the tension, violence and action rocketed up to 100 in the final quarter of the book.
