"My parents died in an automobile accident......they crashed head on into a semi hauling cattle. My father was killed instantly in the wreck, decapitated by the hood of his car." These are the opening lines to Scott Smith's "A Simple Plan". It is later explained that the hypothesis for the cause of the accident is suicide (which obviously makes it not an accident at all). Does this prepare you for the mindset of the story to come? I think yes.Hank and Jacob are the surviving children. They both have different beliefs and understandings of their parents death. But both are certainly shaped by the rearing of their childhood and their parents fall into bankruptcy and alcoholism.Their father placed a clause in his will that they swear to visit his grave every year on his birthday (dec 31st), without fail. It was the only thing that bonded the two brothers together. Otherwise they never make an attempt at socializing. You see Hank went to collage and got married and is expecting a baby. Jacob dropped out of high school is unemployed and probably is an alcoholic.On this particular December 31st (number 8 of the annual visits), Jacob is to pick up his brother after an ice fishing day with his only friend Lou (who is an equal loser to Jacob). They're supposed to drop Lou off before the visit. But on the way the very unexpected happens. And it will change their entire lives. It will pit family verses friendship. Morality verses greed. And no one is immune.This is a very disturbing tale. It is a page turner. It will have you on the edge of your seat yelling "Don't do it!! What are you thinking?!" And it will have you reflecting on every mistake you ever made. At least take some self umbrage.4 stars (should have ended sooner for 5. I'll say no more).
