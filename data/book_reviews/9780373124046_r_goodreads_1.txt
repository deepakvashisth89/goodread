Half English/half Italian heroine is engaged to a cheater who cheats (with her roommate). Hero is a wealthy Italian businessman who was smitten with her at first sight. Heroine finds out on the night of her engagement party about her roommate and fiance and the hero whisks her away.

There are further complications: Is she an heiress or not? Is the hero seducing her for revenge?

Poor muddled heroine isn't sure of the hero or her fickle self as she falls for him the second half of the story. The hero is sardonic and cryptic and impatient and ardent. No wonder the
poor heroine didn't know whether she was coming or going. The OM makes a last minute appeal for the black moment - but the heroine has finally wised up.

A fun little story with the heroine giving some good set downs to her roommate and the OM.
