She thought he married her because her father promised him the keys to the kingdom a.k.a. the local, small town newspaper that was founded by her ancestors. He thought she married him because she was daddy's little girl who would obey his every command.

Their Great Big Terrible Misunderstandings do eventually clear up naturally, but not without some gut-wrenching confessions and confrontations from both protagonists and their assorted family members, including hero's tragic but redeemed mother, and heroine's absolutely awful, narcissistic, demaguogue, gutless father.

Overall, it was terribly sad and depressing. I didn't really get the feeling the heroine knew what real love is about, cause how can you love someone you feel capable of such ruthless, cold, greedy manipulation? It was more of a case of lust coupled with her desperate, compulsive need to obtain the approval of her father.

If they are to have a true HEA, they need to move out of the family mansion pronto!
