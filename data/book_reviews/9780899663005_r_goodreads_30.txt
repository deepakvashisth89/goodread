I read this novel over 25 years ago, and remembered liking it for its striking and sweeping images of colonial India. I was also obsessed with period movies and novels, so that had something to do with my reading this book.

I've revisited it some odd years later, and now recognize it as a seminal work of British Modernism.

The book is a poignant and beautiful narrative that brims with the unease of British and Indian cultures during the early part of the 20th century, as Britain's Empire is coming to an end.

The famous clashing of Aziz and Fielding vs Mrs. Moore Ms. Quested, and Ronny Heaslop remain some of the most interesting interactions and voices that live on in Modernist literature. While reading the novel is so much fun, it is yet filled with so much sadness at the same time. Imperialistic themes were never so poetic.
