If you have seen the movie, you will not be surprised by this novel at all. In fact, this novel was adapted FROM the movie so again, do not go into this book expecting much difference from the movie.With that being said, if you are a fan of the movie (or the series in general) I think that it's worth the read. Not only did this book remind me of the less obvious things that occurred in the movie, it also gave some additional details that left more of a mark coming from a different perspective.Personally, I really liked this adaptation and think it's a great addition to the movie. I'm looking forward to continuing the novelized adaptions for each movie in this saga.
