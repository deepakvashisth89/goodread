Not bad. A teenager runs away from her abusive father and finds herself swept up in some unusual predicaments-she meets a couple of frat boy shoplifters and witnesses some reckless police corruption-but the sh*t abruptly hits the fan when she is kidnapped and caged by some devil worshippers. Religious jargon often devours various pages in this brief read, but Russo’s writing style is horrific and fetching.The overall tone is a bit pulpy, but their are some graphic moments filled with sinister torture. Worth checking out.
