3 1/2 Stars ~ For two years there'd been a rift between Meredith and her parents because Meredith had gone against them by dropping out of university to help Piers start up a new clothing chain. The day that Meredith and Piers were to marry, he'd been called away urgently to solve a company crisis in Paris, and he died in a plane crash. Wanting to rebuild their relationship with their daughter, Meredith's parents invited her home, and while she decides what to do with her future, she can help in her father's legal firm. When her car breaks down just outside of her hometown, she's forced to walk to an Inn nearby, where she overhears the landlord and a patron talking about her.'They tell me the Paxton girl's coming back here to live,' she heard the landlord say. 'How does that grab you?''It doesn't,' said another male voice. 'Secretary, and more, to a self-made millionaire who got himself killed in his private plane! Sad they both ran out of luck.' His tone was disparaging, completely lacking in sympathy.The landlord laughed. 'The newspapers made a meal of it. But I suppose you had inside information.''None. The Paxtons are fine people. I only hope their spoilt only daughter will appreciate the fatted calf.'Her eyes were smarting at the unwarranted attack and anger was a stabbing pain. How dared two total strangers talk about her like that, as if she had committed a sin! What right had they to pass judgment on someone they had never met? It was the two little words 'and more' that really infuriated her, implying a wealth of meaning for which there was no justification. It was unfair, unforgivable!The man having the conversation with the landlord is Joss, her father's new law partner and the man she'd been asked to work for. Meredith can't understand what she did to earn his animosity and how he had come to have such a low opinion of her, but she isn't the sort to let him get away with insulting her. Her first day in the office she calls him on it, and Joss is perplexed as to why she seems to bring out the worst in him. He'd been Pier's stepmother's lawyer in Pier's father's divorce, and had jaded opinions about the whole family, except for the stepmother, Corinne. They'd set into a routine at work and steered clear of each other outside it, but when a crisis called Joss' sister to her wounded husband stationed in Arabia, Meredith steps in to help out with her kids. Corinne arrives to find Meredith in Joss' house with Ellen's kids, and insists on taking over. Corinne and Joss have a history, and she's determined to marry him.This was a fun read with loads of bickering and sparks of attraction. Joss has a habit of jumping to conclusions about Meredith, obviously feed by lies from Corinne, and Meredith isn't one to let him get away with it. With each spar, their attraction seems to heat up, but Meredith doesn't chase after men who belong to other women. Antics from the kids balance out the angst and help to bring depth to Meredith and Joss, seeing them in other lights. Pamela Pope wrote only three books for Harlequin Mills & Boon, which is rather sad, as I found the writing in the book very engaging and I'm sure I'd have enjoyed more from her.
