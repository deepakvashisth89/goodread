A shrewd CEO is accused by his ex-wife, an opera diva, of endangering their child, whom she left with him shortly after the baby's birth. He fights her claim on the baby, only to become emmeshed in her shallowness and manipulations...again.A good book for the work commute; but, I often wonder about shrewd men who have such shabby personal lives...oh, the choices we make, and why we make them (if we ever think when we are deciding!)
