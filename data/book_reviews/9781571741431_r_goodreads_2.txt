Quote by Bruce: "It is my considered opinion that when you have an OBE, a dream or a lucid dream, you are not actually moving outside the boundaries of your own mind, because your mind has no boundaries or limits (apart from your own beliefs)."

FYI about this review: I'm an experienced lucid dreamer who had hundreds of lucid dreams, hundreds of false awakenings, sleep paralysis and dozens of OBE's. So this review is based on how much I recognize things from my own experiences. There is a big difference between lucid dreaming and OBE's and this book is one of the few out there which explains it in much detail.

I read this book again 9 years later and I still believe it's the best book about OBE's out there. This is the only (!!) book which explains the mind-split effect, experiences I had in which I was observing space from 2 locations at the same time. For years I searched in books and online, no answer was given... besides this book. The same goes for astral wind, the magnetic pull, blurry vision and more errors you may find during your experiences.

As far as critique goes:
This book is so detailed, it can probably feel like he's dragging you along from time to time, I know I had this feeling with the chapters about energy work and chakra's, especially because I already know this stuff. His techniques are very detailed and might be repeated from time to time.
Sometimes he writes things as if they are the norm for everyone. Like afternoon OBE's, I never had them and so far I simply can't no matter how hard I try - while in his opinion those are the easiest to achieve. He describes places and things as if these are collective creations which really exist for everyone, but besides a few I never experienced those. Do they really exist? I'm not able to tell you.

But I deeply respect the fact he decided to share his own experiences and put them in a 'manual', even if you won't get to see what he's seen, the information is here and you can use it to navigate if you ever find yourself in those places. And you will, if you truly believe these are collective levels.

Even with the critique, I'm changing my 4 star rating to a 5, after all those years and many resources later this book is still the best and most detailed book out there.
