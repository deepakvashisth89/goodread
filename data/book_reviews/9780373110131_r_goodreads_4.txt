This is the second in the duet and is the story of the man who missed out on Bethany in Portrait of Bethany. He is an artist and even in the first book he was a bit of a bohemian manwhore.
The heroine is experienced which was still fairly unusual in 1987 but not unknown. This author writes more liberal scenarios than many others of her era.
The story is about the affair and the shadow Bethany casts on the future of the relationship. Well written but not really my cup of tea.
