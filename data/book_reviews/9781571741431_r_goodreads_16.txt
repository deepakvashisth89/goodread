Robert Bruce does a great job bringing these timeless concepts of astral projection and lucid dreaming into modern scientific language. He outlines his vast experience in the field, that experience being a lot of practice in traveling the astral world and studying it like a scientist. It somewhat reminded me of the work of early theosophists who put these concepts into science; however, those works are over 100 years old so the language is dated. Bruce's language is very modern and up to date.

In addition to several summarizations of his experiences and some in depth work in trying to categorize, classify, and bring the mysteries of the astral world into modern consciousness, he also presents several techniques to get into the astral plane.

Also, he discusses the nuances between being in the astral plane experiencing the physical reality versus traveling in a projection only loosely based on physical reality. For example, traveling in the physical world using your astral body, it is possible to validate/verify this by doing things such as reading playing cards or things that actually exist in the physical world, then checking back when awake. Wheras in a projection, it's not the real world, but a simulation likely created by your own mind

Overall, while there are some more practical books for learning techniques (such as The Phase), Bruce's personal experiences are quite fascinating and i love the way he gets into the nitty gritty of the science (to the best of his ability, considering that it's almost impossible to prove non-physical science in the physical world). His insights are enjoyable and relevant for anyone stuyding the phenomenon. I think he'd also be a cool person to meet!
