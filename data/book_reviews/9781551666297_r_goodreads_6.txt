Dallas is a Texas Ranger helping out a lady. Angel is disguised as a man so he doesn't suspect her appearance much, but she doesn't recognize the modern conveniences we have come to take for granted. She is from a time just after the civil war is over. She needs to go back to her time and they access the past through a cave where he and Angel accessed the portal to the past. She wants to go back to see the trial of the robber that made her sister go bad. The end up running back to the future for their lives, and along the way Dallas questions his commitment to never falling in love.
