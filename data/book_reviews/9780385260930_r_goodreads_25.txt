A Book of Stillness, Strength, and Deep Personal MeaningThe Three Pillars of Zen by Philip Kapleau was more than just a book for me—it arrived at the exact time I needed it most. I read it during one of the hardest weeks of my life, after the loss of my beloved cat, Purple, and while it did not remove the grief, it offered something equally valuable: steadiness, perspective, and moments of peace.The book is a profound guide to Zen practice, meditation, discipline, and the path toward greater awareness and equanimity. It explores different methods of meditation and the inner work required to pursue clarity and peace. What especially stayed with me were the personal accounts from practitioners who devoted themselves to this journey and shared their struggles, breakthroughs, and moments of awakening.This is not just a book to read—it is one to sit with. Deeply meaningful, calming, and transformative. It will always remain special to me.
