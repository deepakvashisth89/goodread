2.5This story was a decent read but I felt as though it could have been better. I didn’t really care for the whole “mystery” as the book mostly contained the main character freaking out the whole time and no one takes her seriously. It is full of cliches and there wasn’t any great evidence to support our main character so it just felt random and kind of annoying. This book wasn’t terrible but I didn’t have as much fun with it as I thought I would.
