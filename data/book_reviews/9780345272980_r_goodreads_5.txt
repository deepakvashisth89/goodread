Sometimes the books we favor most are the ones to which we might have some sort of personal attachment. For me, this is one of those books.I served in Vietnam as an army infantryman in 1970 and 1971. My experiences, although somewhat pale in comparison to what happened in this book, were quite similar and relatable.I knew I was destined to give this book 5 stars just by reading the prologue. Written with clarity and simplicity of style, "A Rumor of War" is a work of literary art.Second Lieutenant Philip Caputo served 16 months active duty in country with the first Marine combat detachment sent to Danang, Vietnam in March of 1965. He returned going full circle in April of 1975 as a correspondent to cover the collapse of Saigon for the Chicago Tribune.His book lets you feel the heat, the monsoons and mosquitoes. You will experience the snipers, booby traps and ambushes along with the anguish and pressure of constant combat.If you plan on reading anything about the war in Vietnam, put this memoir at the top of your list. An experience you will not soon forget.
