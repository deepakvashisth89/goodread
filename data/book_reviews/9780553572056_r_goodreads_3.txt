This was really fun. You start out with a lackluster main character and get thrown into a plot twisting exciting mystery. I loved the Alaska archipelago setting, the details of nautical navigation and small bush plane knowledge that made the adventure more entertaining. Of course the plot and it's twists were sometimes outrageous, but that was part of it. The 90s references comically dated the story and setting. I picked this book up from a free book exchange at the airport in paperback.
