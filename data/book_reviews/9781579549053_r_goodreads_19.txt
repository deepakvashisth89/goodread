I deconstructed from christianity and became an atheist Buddhist in 2023. I have also been in recovery for over 15 years and have worked the N.A. steps multiple times. It was only after I left the notion that a “god” exists, that I struggled with the recovery literature which uses god every time it can.I found this book to be a refreshing new perspective on the archaic wording of recovery literature. I know have the framework to understand how I can apply the steps without needing a “god.”
