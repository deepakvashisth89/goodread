Reading this book made me feel nauseous, but it redeems itself. A book that you hate that you like... This is like my kind of horror story!

There's a reason John Updike is not popular these days, it is just too triggering to watch people emotionally destroy each other in slow-motion.

If Rabbit Run was about why Rabbit Ran, this is about why he came back, and that makes it sound like redemption but it's clear that Rabbit is getting a whole lot worse and muddying my earlier view of this series as a man trying to save his breaking family.

It's a bit like Breaking Bad except Rabbit isn't admirable or strong in almost any way, and Updike takes no liberties at torturing characters. I think Updike excels at writing about the unpleasantries about sex in an unstable relationship, but whether it's enjoyable or educational enough to prioritize it on your reading list is hard to say. Need a break from reading "BroLit", bad men narratives are trying.
