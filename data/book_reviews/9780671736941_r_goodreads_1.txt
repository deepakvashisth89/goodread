Definitely not as great as the first book, but a decent read.Some parts of the past seemed pointless. It could have been easily more concise without the repetitions from Kane and Abel. Also, the last 100 pages got pretty boring with too many irrelevant details.Another major disappointment was Richard not being alive to become the First Gentleman. I loved reading about him more than any other character and it got depressing after his unexpected death.Although I have enjoyed these 2 books, I don't think I'll read the next one.
