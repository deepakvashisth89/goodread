Attorney Billy Halleck seriously enjoys living his life of upper-class excess. He’s got it all he could ever want. An expensive home in Connecticut, a loving family and about fifty extra pounds that his doctor repeatedly warns will be the death of him. Then, in a moment of carelessness, Halleck commits vehicular manslaughter when he strikes a jaywalking old woman crossing the street. But Halleck has some powerful local connections and gets off with a slap on the wrist. This does not please the woman’s father, who in a blind guy exacts revenge on Halleck with the use of a spell by uttering a single word: “Thinner.”Now a terrified Halleck finds the weight once so difficult to shed dropping effortlessly—and rapidly—by the week. Soon there will be nothing left of Billy Halleck. That is, unless he can somehow locate the source of his living nightmare and reverse what’s happened to him before he utterly wastes away.Leave it to Stephen King/Richard Bachman to take an outrageous story of a man becoming thinner every day after being cursed by a mysterious old man for killing his daughter and seeking a way to break the curse before it kills him, and somehow makes it an interesting read. Definitely not his best story, another case of King/Bachman over illustrating what’s going around the characters and seeing the ending coming from a mile away, but an enjoyable read nonetheless. Most of what I found enjoyable was wondering how Halleck was going to lift his curse and just following his desperate journey to find answers. Praise to Joe Mantegna narrating this audiobook as his voice made you feel Halleck desperation throughout the book. Another great narration in the audiobook catalog.In the end, I’d give this book a solid 3.5 stars ⭐️ for the reasons I’ve given above. Give this one a try if you’re looking for something new to read.Happy reading everyone! 🫶
