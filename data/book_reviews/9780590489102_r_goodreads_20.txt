I read this to my daughter for homeschool as part of her curriculum. I personally found this book the be a fun and informative book about early explorers. many explorer's quirky links to eachother were brought up, as well as distinctive parts of their personality and the some horrors that met them at the end of their adventures. many historical details are provided and with maps of their routes in the front. even some fun art work is smattered here and there.That said, it didn't capture my daughters attention or interest much.
