Aka Stephen King Vs Richard Bachman

This is probably a 4.5 but I've rounded up as it wasn't that far away from being a 5.

This was old school Stephen King. It was extremely creepy, extremely thrilling and extremely violent (there are so many murders that are described as violently detailed as possible). It is well written and again has characters that you root for. The book is genius and I also loved the ending. I was worried it would run out of steam but I found the ending extremely satisfying (I'm also aware I've used the word 'extremely' far too often here lol)

It was nice to read about Alan Pangborn again, as well as a few other characters from 'Needful Things' but I do wish I'd read this first (as I should have). George Stark is such an unusual and deranged character and I had such a good time reading about him.

Not a perfect novel but not far at all. I can't think of anything I really disliked and I raced through this in a weekend.

Highly recommended
