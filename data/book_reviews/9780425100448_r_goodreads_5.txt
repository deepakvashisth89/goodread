This is a novelization of the screenplay by Janice Fisher & James Jeremias and Jeffrey Boam, which was based on a story by Fischer & Jeremias. Chances are that if you loved the movie, you'll love this adaptation and the expansions it includes, but, if not, you can skip it. It adds a bit of backstory for Star and more characterization and has an interesting epilogue that's cute in prose but wouldn't have worked on film. There are some inconsistencies in the text, and it has a kind of rushed feel, but I thought it was still a lot of fun. Frog brothers forever!
