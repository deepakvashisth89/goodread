I love reading Hawthorne even though I don't always like him & yes, even I find he can be dull at times, but there is much in there, so I've been reading & re-reading his stories most of my life. This collection is perhaps the best, good intro essay & generally Hawthorne at his best, with a few of the dull ones. "The May-Pole of Merry Mount" is worth reading to show how Hawthorne didn't cherish either the Puritans or his family's leadership in early Massachusetts.
