I enjoyed this book about class differences, life in an Ivy League school in the 8os, and the daily travails of the lunch cart industry. Danny, the main character, is a likeable guy who takes us on a first-person account of his college years, with several hilarious anecdotes along the way.I didn't like many references to books (most "classics") that I hadn't read - while the author is being true to the 1st person narrative of our English major main character, I just felt uncultured - and set the book down to Google references to books I've never read.
