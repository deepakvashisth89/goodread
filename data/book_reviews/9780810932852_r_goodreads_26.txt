This book is full of information that helps readers engage more fully in Austin's fiction. The book begins with a short biography of Austin, then treatment of all kinds of details about the world of England during Austin's lifetime, including fashion, food, transport, international doings, and others. There is a heavy emphasis on descriptions of the places Austin knew best. The last portion of the book takes the novels one by one, in publication order, telling when and how they were published and giving background information (heavy on descriptions of the places of the settings) which might best help the reader understand the nuances of Austin's stories. The book includes copious contemporary portraits (of real people who might match Austin's character's appearances), illustrations of real buildings that might match Austin's descriptions, maps of places contemporary to the stories, etc. It is a valuable resource for any Austin fan.
