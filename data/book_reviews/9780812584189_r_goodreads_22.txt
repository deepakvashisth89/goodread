I love Star Wars, but I have never had the slightest interest in Star Wars books. But when I saw Zahn had written this non-Star Wars tome, I was confident it would be good because surely Lucasfilm knows how to pick authors.I was right. This non-Star Wars novel is a lot of fun and very competently written. Characters are interesting, complicated, and sympathetic (except for one over-the-top villain) The plot moves along, and the premise is interesting.One criticism - does the Empire not have an executive? This random high senator is just ordering people around on a planet he doesn't even represent? Where are the rest of the senators? What if they gave contradictory orders? Everyone bows and scrapes for this guy until just once they tell him to eff off. It was a little odd.Other than confusion about Senator Forsyth's role, I did enjoy this novel quite a lot. The premise is truly unique, as far as I can tell - a difficult feat in space opera. Great fun. I'll be looking for more Zahn non Star Wars novels.
