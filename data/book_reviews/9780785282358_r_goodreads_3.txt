An excellent Bible commentary though small and portable it packs a lot of information between the covers. This is a wonderfully written and highly convenient commentary,Warren Wiersbe shows his expereince as an acclaimed Bible teacher where he takes the reader through the Bible chapter by chaper. With clearly laid out passages and the clear explanation. It helps the reader shine a light on the meaning of the passages they are reading from the Bible.I can highly recommend this commentary to all who want to learn more from their Scripture reading.
