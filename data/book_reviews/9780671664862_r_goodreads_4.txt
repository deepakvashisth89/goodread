Just finished this superb novel and what a thrilling read it was. A weird women (Mary Terror) with ghosts of the past disturbing her mind is kidnapping a baby. Together with the baby she wants to reunite with "Lord Jack" a cult leader of a group of radical protesters. Laura, the baby's mother, leaves nothing untouched to get her David back. Robert McCammon probably created his ultimate masterpiece her. The message is clear: it's the end of 60s flower power movement ending in chaos, disappointment and political terror ("Storm Front"). Misguided former cult members, tragedy, the music of the Doors, the fascinating charisma of Jim Morrison, a generation lost. The book read like a roadmovie at parts ("going to California"), reminded on Sam Peckinpah's The Wild Bunch in its relentless slow motion showdown. The characters were absolutely outstanding. The pace and narration excellent. What can I say, this is one of the ultimate classics from the past you shouldn't miss. A true highlight. You won't find anything better. What a gripping page turner. A must read!
