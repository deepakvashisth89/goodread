This is a well written book which starts with the reentry into society of a man who has spent the past 25 years in prison for a crime which he may or may not have committed. His old neighborhood has fallen on hard times. Drugs, robberies, crime in general.The characters are all broken. I read to the end, hopng that they could find a kind peace together. I guess they did in a way, but it was a pretty grim journey. Not sure I would recommend this to friends.
