4.5 stars

The re-release of this book is still a great read and the characters and plot remain fun and intriguing. Detective Kane McDermott is put on an assignment to see if the local dating agency is running more than it says. Kayla Luck inherited Charmed! from her great aunt and wants to change up the business to offer other services as opposed to the dating help it currently offers. Kane propositions Kayla, but what happens from there is purely attraction. When he realizes that Kayla is not what he thought she was, he wants to get to know her. Then danger keeps popping up and Kane makes it his duty to protect her. I really loved this story and the characters. There is so much confusion and crossed signals that you will be cheering for them to work it out and find what they both need with each other.
