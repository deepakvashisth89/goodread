This book was fantastic and absolutely hilarious. If you don't like it, you're probably Australian. Barnard is relentless with his skewering of Australia and Australians. The humour is dry, subtle, and very very funny, and it's a good little mystery as well.One very important thing: if you're in the habit of peeking at the last page of a book, you MUST restrain yourself. The very last sentence is a stunner.
