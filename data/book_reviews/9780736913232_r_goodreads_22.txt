*4.75 starsThis one is by far my favourite in the series so far. I was thinking all day about it while I was at work until I could get back and finish it. I was so emotionally invested throughout the story.I really liked that this one looked less at the initial romance, and more at the issues that arose during their marriage, and how with patience and love they were able to slowly overcome it.I’m also really loving reading Christian romances again. They are such easy reads as I don’t feel like I’m on edge from watching out for open door romance scenes, and any excessive swearing. It’s also just good to read about characters who has similar beliefs to me and can help reinforce those beliefs through their actions in the story.
