*trigger warning* for attempted rape of a child.Heroine has been messed up by her erratic upbringing. Her mother had bad taste in men and she and her half-sister paid the price. When her mother was hospitalized, the latest boyfriend attacked heroine and that has put her off men and any kind of intimacy for years.The reader doesn't know any of this until half-way through the story. Heroine is a successful dress shop owner, beautiful and remote. She meets the hero when his cousin knocks up the heroine's half-sister. Heroine mistakes him for his cousin and he is treated to a long angry speech before she flounces out.The pregnancy ends in miscarriage. But heroine meets the hero again at a Christmas house party of one of her wealthy clients. The hero begins his pursuit and it is indeed a gentle one. He's too good to be true, but heroine is so damaged she can't see it.This was well-written, but kind of depressing. Hero is a sweetie, but heroine must have been really something for him to put up with all of her trauma behaviors for so long.
