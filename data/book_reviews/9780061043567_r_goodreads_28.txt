A mixed bag of Sayers’ short stories - whodunnits and mysteries, a mixture of her novels, and of the more chilling works of Patricia Highsmith and Raoul Dahl. Most enjoyable, if disconcerting!The GR blurb:‘All that was left of the garage was a heap of charred and smouldering beams. In the driving seat of the burnt-out car were the remains of a body...An accident, said the police.An accident, said the widow. She had been warning her husband about the danger of the car for months.Murder, said the famous detective Lord Peter Wimsey--and proceeded to track down the killer.This is vintage Sayers, a collection of her finest crime and detection stories.’
