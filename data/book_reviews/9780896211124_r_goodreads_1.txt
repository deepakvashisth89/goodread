Elizabeth Lamb Worthington, now 12, coaxes her grandmother's new suitor into inviting her and her cousin Persis on a trip to Maine on his luxurious yacht with his ill-assorted guests. Persis immediately comes down with German measles, and various guests suffer from allergic reactions and bad colds. Elizabeth Lamb is supposed to be a companion for Vittorio Vincentia's sixteen year old granddaughter, the beautiful, mentally handicapped and malicious Viola. When the yacht's engines and communications systems break down shortly before the ship drops the cousins off at their grandmother's summer place, the travelers become desperate to get help for a sailor with a broken leg, Vittorio, who has a bad heart, and Viola, who has broken an arm in a freak accident. But when Viola dies, Elizabeth Lamb starts putting together things that she has noticed, and realized that someone has been lying--and worse.
