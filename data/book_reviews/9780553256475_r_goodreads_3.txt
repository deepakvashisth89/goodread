Tessa Crichton, young TV actress married to a Scotland Yard detective and amateur sleuth, is invited to judge the performances of the last-year students at the theatre and dance school she had attended a decade earlier. So she renews her acquaintance with the formidable owner, Mrs. Bland, her amiable husband Dr. Bland, the school matron, and one of her own classmates, now a teacher. She also meets some of the students, among them Hattie, a girl with no performing arts talents but a wicked knack for caricature. When Hattie is found dead, and some of her sketches are missing, Tessa does not buy into the suicide theory. Sure enough, the girl was poisoned -who, in the small circle of students and staff could have been responsible?I found it a rather weak story. Too many premonitions, lucky accidents, convenient convergences and the like. I think that some of the dialogue was supposed to be snappy repartee but it felt like grumpy back-and-forth to me.
