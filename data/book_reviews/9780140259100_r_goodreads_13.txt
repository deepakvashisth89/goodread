I am not sure what to make of it. Often times throughout the book I found the characters and plot to be a little too unbelievable. But clearly the interplay between what is reality and what is not, is a big theme of the book."The Bombs are Real!". Is one crazy for caring about trivial domestic duties while mercurial politicians control massive nuclear arsenals? Or is it sane to force your family into a home-made bunker to protect them from such risks? I think I know how most reasonable people feel, but O'Brien forces us to think a little harder about this choice we face.
