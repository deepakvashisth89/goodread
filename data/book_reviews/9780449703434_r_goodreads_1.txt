A bit of teen trauma fantasy to start the new year!

Joanne is pretty but has a big nose she’s self conscious about on top of a gorgeous sister who she constantly compares herself with. After an embarrassing date with her crush (who thought he was going out with her sis,) their car crashes and Joanne gets hurt badly. She *has* to get facial reconstruction surgery, including rhinoplasty.

She comes out gorgeous of course, realizes her full potential with her new confidence, and recognizes the few friends she had before the accident are the most important.

This honestly read like a fanfic I would have written about myself in middle school and I kinda enjoyed it just because of that.
