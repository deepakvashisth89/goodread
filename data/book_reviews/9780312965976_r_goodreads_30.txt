Nice romantic story set in Cape Cod, USA. Maddie spends summers at the family summer-house with her daughter, mother and family. A nearby lighthouse is suddenly rented by a former sweetheart whom the family believe was responsible for the devastation of the family many years ago. Maddie's ex husband makes life difficult for her and things become complicated with the ensuing entangled relationships.
Maddie's father's murder becomes the focus for the story and the twists whilst detecting his murderer bring a nice twist.
A nice holiday read.
