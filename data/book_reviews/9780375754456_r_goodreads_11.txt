There are times when you read a book and while you're reading you just feel the NEED to tell everyone about it. To just push it into their hands because it's IMPORTANT.This is one of those books.A well written overview of the history of lynching from reconstruction to the civil Rights era, it goes into the unique history of this American darkness. It is unflinching in details, but never lurid in descriptions. It is simply what happened, something that I find personally much more impactful. It is stark, unrelenting, but so is lynching.Just. Everyone should read this.
