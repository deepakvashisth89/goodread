These were my favorite books growing up. And by favorite I mean I read these more times than Harry Potter-- I know, you're shocked right?There are plenty of things wrong with this series, that if I had read them as an adult I would probably have ripped it to pieces. But after years of not reading it and finally picking it up again, it gave me a warm fuzzy feeling of being young, in my own bed with a cat and not having any real responsibilities. I loved reading about my beloved characters again--like old friends!A great beginers fantasy series!
