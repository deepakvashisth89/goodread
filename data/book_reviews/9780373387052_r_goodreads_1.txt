Rebecca is a foster daughter of Joe and Meredith. Her childhood was perilous with an alcoholic mother who didn't care about her surroundings as long as she had a drink. Drink was supplied in various ways, and Rebecca ran away at 14 when mom's "friend" trapped her in the bathroom. Then, she was almost raped at a shelter. She somehow got to the Ranch, a refuge for children, which Joe and Meredith ran. She received counseling and a new family, but was never able to be intimate with any man she dated because of panic and flashbacks.Travis is a Personal Investigator and was called in by Joe to see if he could find the shooter (Joe was shot at during his 60th birthday party). During his investigation, Rebecca helps him out and they develop a friendship and an attraction. Travis shows Rebecca the respect anyone deserves, in contrast to a co-worker of hers. Travis is called away to help with a friend in dire straits, giving his last report to Joe - for now. He also has a heart-to-heart talk with Rebecca and they get engaged.All through the book, the reader is shown Patsy, playing Meredith. The reader is given her motivations, her goals, her likes/dislikes, and her fears. She was arrested for killing her daughter's kidnapper, and blames Meredith for not speaking up regarding the reasons. This led to her incarceration as well as her commitment in an insane asylum. She has hired different P.I.s to find her daughter as well as "Patsy", Meredith's long lost sister. In this book, she also hires a man to murder the adoptive daughter Emily - who was in the accident and saw "two Mommys"......The reader is also shown "Louise". Louise believes she is Patsy, though her memories come through, very slowly. She tried dating, but remembered that she had a husband - though she cannot remember anything else.
