I had a free copy of this book, but this is my voluntary and honest opinion. During a friend’s birthday party, Laurel Worthington and Brett Matthews were married as a joke. Their wedding night resulted in an annulment, but she ended up pregnant, in addition, her family is in turmoil over being bankrupt. With no money and no job skills, Laurel travels from Chicago to Portland to look up Brett hoping for his help. He does give her a job, and offers to marry her, but she refuses to begin with. A heartwarming book about love, standing on one’s own feet, and commitment.
