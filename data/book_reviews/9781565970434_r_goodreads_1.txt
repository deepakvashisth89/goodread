"Lady in Black" is the story of Margaret and Reid.

S O G O O D.

A pleasantly funny love story between a billionaire and a lady butler. When the hero visits his 90+ year old grandfather whose life is being threatened, he never expects that his personal bodyguard and carer would be a sexy lady butler! Initially cautious yet wildly attracted to her, he questions her intentions, until he starts falling head over heels for her. As he realizes she is a widowed single mother, he starts knitting dreams of having pets and kids 2.0 with her. Ofcourse the humor is provided by the hero, the grandfather and the heroine's daughter- but I could not help *swoon* over how giddy and romantic the he was, and how he totally gave into making the heroine his wife as soon as he realized his love for her. His methods were unconventionally hilarious, and while the heroine was skeptical, he won her heart. Oh and it's SUPER sexy too!

Totally loved it! Mature, well rounded characters.
