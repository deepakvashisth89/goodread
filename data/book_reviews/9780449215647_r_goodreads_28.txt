A romantic suspense story set in Austria, the plot is exciting enough but is almost secondary to the lyrical descriptions of the beautiful and brilliant horses of the Spanish riding school in Vienna, and the lovingly detailed descriptions of their dancing. The heroine, Vanessa, finds herself reluctantly accompanying an aloof teenage boy, Tim, to Vienna, while hoping to find her husband, Lewis. She discovers Tim has a passion for horses, and wants to be one of the haute ecole riders at the Spanish riding school. She and Tim find their way to a circus, where Vanessa, who just happens to be a vet, is able to help an injured old horse, who turns out to be no ordinary horse. As Lewis turns out to be no ordinary husband. The adventures of Vanessa and Tim are exciting, but it is the horse who is the real stars of this story.
