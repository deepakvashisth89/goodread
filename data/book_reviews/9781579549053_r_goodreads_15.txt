This book lives up to the subtitle's promise, skillfully integrating Buddhism and the Twelve Steps. It's written with clarity and candor. I appreciate the author's transparency about his own failings, sharing hard-won wisdom so that readers don't necessarily have to struggle in the same ways. To me, the intersection between Buddhism and the Twelve Steps isn't obvious... so it's a testament to Griffin's skill and years of experience as a spiritual practitioner that he manages to pull them into an integrated path.
