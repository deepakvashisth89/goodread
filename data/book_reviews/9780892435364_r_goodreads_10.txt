So this one made its way into my cart by way of a few recommendations on some of my favorite forums, and was clinched when I found out the local church uses this as part of its initiation classes.

Lukefahr has a way with words! You could just read the entire Catechism the hard way (it's quite a slog), or you could read this, which delivers the same concepts in a much more approachable way.

Basically, the entire book is broken down by subject. This belief, this rite, is referenced in this part of the catechism, and this is why, and here's the biblical/liturgical authority for that info.

It's short and to the point without being light on detail; highly recommended!
