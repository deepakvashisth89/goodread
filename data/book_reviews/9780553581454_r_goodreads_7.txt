The Glamorgan Killings: Alison TaylorThis book was so like the preceding two, about the Welsh people and Police procedures. It was very perambulating at times, hard to grasp people could have such fixed ideas about life and responsibility. The killing of Uncle Ned was the idea out of which came so many weird and bad things happening. The end just about explained everything but it never the less kept me reading until the end. I wonder if McKenna will ever be able to be free of his ex Wife and find happiness again.
