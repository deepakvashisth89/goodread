A collection of poems and nursery rhymes. As with any compilation like this, there are some better and some I don’t care for as much, but this is a fairly good collection, in my opinion.

Honestly, I would have only given a 3-star rating, but for the beautiful artwork that is included in this collection. Interspersed with the poems are classic beautiful, sometimes full-page paintings that help children to envision what the poem is talking about.

It’s a delight to read these poems along with the captivating art!
