This book has everything that you would need to make it an exciting and interesting read. All the races on the brink of war either with each other or amongst themselves. All needing to settle their differences and band together, or face total destruction at the hands of an enraged god Chaos.This is a next generation story with just a few of the original heroes managing to help out here and there. I absolutely enjoyed reading this one and particularly enjoyed the involvement of the Kendar, Tass who saves the day more than one with The Kendar Spoon Of Turning. Frankly. If I had the choice to have a Knight or a Kendar in my party, I would choose Tass.Read and enjoy.
