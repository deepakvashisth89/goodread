Great find picked up at a library sale. A quick, light read about a pair of city slickers who buy a weekend house in the Catskills and their rocky adjustment to the country life told through a series of comical anecdotes. The events take place in the late 80s and early 90s, so it was interesting to read the descriptions of the NYC of that time -- many elements still ring true, but enough has changed that this book is very much grounded in the past.
