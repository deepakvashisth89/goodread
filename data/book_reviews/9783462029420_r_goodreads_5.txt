‘I sometimes think that the only things really worth talking about are the things people absolutely refuse to discuss.’Under the Skin is a must read for fans of science fiction, horror, and philosophical musings. Faber takes readers on a spine tingling journey that explores the boundaries of humanity, identity, and our place in the universe.The novel follows protagonist Isserley, who preys on unsuspecting men hitchhiking in the desolate Scottish Highlands. The novel's tension builds steadily as Isserley's true nature is gradually revealed. Faber masterfully creates a sense of dread and unease, keeping readers on the edge of their seats throughout the entire narrative.The ending, both haunting and enigmatic, leaves a lasting impact and provokes a deep contemplation of the themes explored throughout the novel. Under the Skin is not for the faint of heart. It is a challenging and unsettling read that will stay with you long after you finish the last page.Faber's thought-provoking exploration of humanity and the unknown makes this a novel that deserves a place on the shelf of any discerning reader.Highly Recommend.4.5
