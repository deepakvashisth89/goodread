Our youngest brought this book home from her second grade class to practice her reading. While it is an interesting tale, I encouraged her to seek out any chapter books that might challenge her a bit more.The narrative byPauline Cartwrightis short, perfect for beginning readers and the repetition helps to foster reading ability, comprehension, and confidence. The illustrations byMarie Loware colorful and complement the story nicely.Overall, this is an interesting retelling of the classic story byJacob Ludwig Karl GrimmandWilhelm Karl Grimm. We enjoyed reading this book together.
