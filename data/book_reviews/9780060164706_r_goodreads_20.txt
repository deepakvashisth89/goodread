A bland and shallow encyclopedia of random “lowbrow” things from American culture. The Sterns do a great job of saying nothing about a lot. The encyclopedia features a random assortment of people, objects, hobbies, and phenomena from the likes of Dolly Parton and Jayne Mansfield to cool whip and Elvisana.Bad taste has often been associated with “the common man” or “lower classes,” but in the words of John Waters, “There is such a thing as good bad taste and bad bad taste.” The Sterns fail to deliver any sort of nuanced analysis on why certain things resonate with the masses, and why certain things are labeled as “low class.” Instead they kinda only deliver smug, biting commentary on the white-washed history of the things that they have deemed to be low class.
