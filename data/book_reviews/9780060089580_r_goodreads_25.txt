Set in Detroit (affectionately known as "the D" to us natives), this classic Elmore Leonard novel was familiar and gritty, like the city itself. Selected as the March book for my library's Mystery Book Discussion, it was unexpected in many ways. I listened to an audiobook version and, unfortunately, the narrator mispronounced common street names that will make any local cringe. But the story itself held me until the very end; and what an ending!
