My experience reading these “Longer Stories from the Last Decade” was revelatory. I'd known that Chekhov is revered for his short stories but had never read a word of them, so I came to these pieces fresh. Soon into the first story, as Emily Dickinson would say, I felt “as if the top of my head were taken off.” He’s so good he’s almost uncomfortable to read – you know, that rare mixture of joy and pain one sometimes gets in the presence of true genius? One keeps wanting to stop, put the book down, and think about how the hell he’s doing what he’s doing. There are moments of such truth, it’s almost mystical. Pathetic, sad, funny, flawed…his characters are nearly four dimensional, so alive are they. Read this book and prepare to meet yourself along the way. Literature's supreme specialist in disappointment, Chekhov the Good Doctor offers no solutions, but his bittersweet diagnoses somehow feel like a cure.
