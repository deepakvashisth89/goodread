This was an excellent read, with interesting characters and plot, and sustained tension.It also provides a look at the ugly prejudice against blacks that apparently still exists in the deep South, including among some in law enforcement, and the hypocrisy with which people try to hide but not deal with or change their feelings.Iles has at least two instances of unexpected help coming from unexpected places, a device that often gets to me. He also has his main character (author Penn Cage) reflect on how sometimes writing blocks can be solved by just stepping away from the page and letting the words sort themselves out in your subconscious. Obvious, I guess, but still a useful reminder.
