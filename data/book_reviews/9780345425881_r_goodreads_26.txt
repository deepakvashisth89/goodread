The plot was good. The main character is an archivist/rare books specialist which is why I chose the book. But that didn't really come into the mystery part at all, although a not-so-old book did figure prominently in the plot. I was a bit put off by the narration being rather didactic and religious, but apparently that was its target market. I also was thrown by periodic shifts between third person and first person narration that I couldn't figure the reason for.
