I bought my first Gies books at university 20 years ago, and I still love them to give me simple, basic primary source-based information.

In turn, that makes them a great resource. In this case, it doesn't matter that they don't give you interpretations, but rather tell you 'this person did this, as shown in this source, make of that what you will'. That also makes the books rather timeless.

Here, we follow different women (a queen, a lady, a merchants' wife, a peasant, an abbess, a cloth-worker) through their lifes and learn how that looked like, following conventions and restrictions. It's an easy read.
