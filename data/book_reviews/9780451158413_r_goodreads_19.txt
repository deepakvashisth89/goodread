One of the best McBain's I've read, Dick Hill even did a good job reading. This short novel starts out with a mass shooting at a bookstore with a very personal connection to one of the regular detectives. It's a police procedural, like all of the 87th Precinct books and it's quite interesting to see how the squad figures this one out.I'm working my way through the Oeuvre but there are a number of these old books I can't find as audiobooks, which is about all I'm "reading" these days. I've still got 20+ to go even without all of them, which gives me plenty of time to find the others.
