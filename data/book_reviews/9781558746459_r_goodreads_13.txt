I started this book after I read "Looking for Alaska."I have not finished it yet but I knew that this would be a good book to choose because these books generally have inspirational poems or songs that move the reader. I have read a lot of these books and I am always moved by poems people have written so I knew this would fit the criteria needed for the Reading Portfolio well. I am still in the process of reading it but pick it up every now and then for a few quick stories. These books are always a fast read because the stories always pull me in. I also really enjoy the variety of stories. When I want a good cry I can find a sad story here or if I want a good laugh or just to read an uplifting story you can always find one of those in these books.
