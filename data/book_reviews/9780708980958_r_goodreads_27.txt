Looking back (2 days) it was probably a mistake to listen to the second in the series so quickly after listening to the first. (Or maybe reverse the order) Oh well.There are good things about this book. The beginning is ok and the Hemlock character is solid. But, as opposed to the Eiger Sanction, the plot seems to be muddled and confused. The story bogs down in the middle and is retrieved only by an explosive ending.The humor is dialed back, the satire appears occasionally, but it's not as biting as Eiger. The ending is more explosive and more in the nature of a thriller, but not as clever as Eiger.Had I read this one first or more time had passed since I read Eiger I might have a different rating. It's good enough, just not another Eiger.
