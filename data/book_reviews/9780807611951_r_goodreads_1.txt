MagicalHokusai: Thirty-six Views of Mount Fiji is an art book. Originally, 36 prints were ordered and an additional 10 were ordered after the initial success of the book.The prints all show different views of Mount Fiji in the 1830’s. In many of the images, the local populace is enjoying the views of the Mountain or busy working.The first print in the set is the inspiration for the cover of Tomorrow and Tomorrow and Tomorrow.I found it interesting to try to look up the places in the prints and compare and contrast to what the region is currently. For reference, Henry David Thoreau went to Walden Pond in 1845, just 15 years after Hokusai.The book itself was a joy. The prints are not traditionally bound but attached accordion-style to the front and back covers.
