This is Childers' only novel, written as propaganda to urge the British government to develop defenses against a possible German invasion pre-World War I, or at least mandatory naval service. Yachtsman Davies has persuaded his Oxford acquaintance Carruthers, not entirely forthrightly, to accompany him as mate on a meandering journey through the North Sea waters and sands of the German East Frisian Islands, at high tide and low, on the cramped Dulcibella. We soon find out that Davies needs Carruthers' help to figure out the intentions of the mysterious seafaring Herr Dollmann.
