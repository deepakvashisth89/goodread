I would not recommend this book for beginners in information science. The book itself is poorly laid out (the irony!) and assumes you already have beyond a basic understanding of the subject. As others note, it's extremely dry and academic. The index is not thorough, the information is clearly outdated (it has an unhealthy obsession with Yahoo Directory, for instance), and there are too few examples to ground theory in real world applications. Knowledge organization is a nebulous topic, you have to use examples. An updated version is supposed to be coming out soon, but as it stands the 3rd edition is of little use to anyone.
