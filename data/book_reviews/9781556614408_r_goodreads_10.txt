The eighth book is not the end by the way. I really enjoyed the final chapter in the joining of Christopher and Corrie. It was again quite a ride. Although this was my second time through this series, I saw it much differently as a more mature Christian. But I still cried at the wedding scene regardless. Once again, kudos to the author for historical authenticity, Christian value, and growth, as well as bringing a series to a satisfying close even if you did tease us with more to come.
