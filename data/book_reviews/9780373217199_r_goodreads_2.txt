The first story, "Like Daughter," got a little mired in angst that didn't propel the story well, but overall, an enjoyable read. I really liked Gettin' Lucky. I appreciated the humor and the oddball antics--which made the black moment all the more touching---and the characters were well-drawn and compelling. I thought Lucky was endearing and it was fun to see her get her happy ending. (It's a romance; I didn't spoil it, did I?)
