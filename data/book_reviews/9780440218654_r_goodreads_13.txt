Here is my review form 2020 when I didn't finish the book."I usually enjoy Ruth Rendell's book immensely but I could not even begin to like this one..........didn't like the characters or the story. Or maybe I should say the "beginning of the story" since I did not finish the book. I seldom do that, and certainly not one by Rendell but I have too many good books waiting to be read. I am probably in the minority on this story but so be it."I gave it another try and can now say that I that enjoyed it but still was a little disappointed. It was an odd tale about a young girl raised by a mother who could be classified as "strange", although very loving. It follows that life of the these two main characters and is told by the daughter, in a style that resembles1001 Nights.An unusual plot but, IMO, not one of Rendell's best. Others will disagree.
