A solid entry in a solid series, but it falls prey to a fault of the modern series mystery — an abrupt ending that leaves a lot of threads dangling. Will I ever discover what happened to the wrongly accused victim, so cruelly slandered by the real killer? What about her child, stripped from her by the authorities? Will our detective hero be able to come to terms to the voice in his head? This mystery keeps its secrets well, but does not reveal them all at the end, like it is supposed to. (I’m still not clear why everyone KNOWS who the victim is — when all they find is a stack of bones. I am also puzzled by the absolute awfulness of the local police inquiry. They seem absolutely uninterested in the source of the poison pen letters that set the whole drama up.)I think you are supposed to stay tuned until next time, but my wife did not buy me series entry #5. Alas.So, for me, this a book that succeeds more on an emotional novelistic level than on the basic, chock full of holes and assumptions mystery plot.
