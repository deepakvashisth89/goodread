Originally published in 1931, it starts out that Prudence Whitsby and her niece Betsey have a cottage down at Cape Cod, Massachusetts. They invite two friends down to keep them company - Emma Manton - the widow of a clergyman from Boston, and Dorothy "Dot" Cram, a former classmate of Betsey's from New York. A small cottage on the property is rented to a Dale Sanborn an author who publishes exposes and is a really disagreeable character. One night, Prudence goes out to bring in her cat, Ginger, and she follows him over to Sanborn's cottage and finds him murdered. The local sheriff, Slough Sullivan and his medical examiner, Dr. Reynolds, pin the blame on Bill Porter, an old friend of the Whitby's, whose family owns a thriving business, and who had called on Sanborn earlier that day. Soon after, Bill's handyman and friend Asey Mayo takes the lead in investigating the murder and the story goes from there. This book is the first of a number of mysteries written by the author with Asey Mayo as the detective.
