Irene was named after her great aunt and is now inheriting her Irish estate. She takes her twins - rather they take her - to Ireland one step ahead of her controlling ex. The estate is nice. The disinherited relatives aren't.
Shay is charming. Why did her aunt suddenly turn against him?
Who is telling people where to find her? She ends up being followed, getting involved with a death, fighting her growing regard for Shay and resisting the pull to stay on her new queendom.
This is a romance, but very low key. It is much more a very busy mystery. The one 'flaw' is how wishy washy Irene is. The book is fast, easy reading.
