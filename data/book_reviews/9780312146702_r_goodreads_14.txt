Among the other pleasures of this installment of the series is the inclusion of a piano playing aristocrat who hails Mary as "Mrs Sherlock." This is, of course,Dorothy L. Sayersmaster creation Lord Peter Wimsey. Unfortunately the Sayers estate apparently forbade King from using him again, but it's a funny cameo.There is also a line here about a batch of hairs found at a crime scene being a "red herring," and there are precisely 5 hairs. One cannot help but wondering if Laurie R King has inserted a Sayers "easter egg"here, harking back to Wimsey's adventureThe Five Red Herrings!
