I did not finish this book. The research was decades old. The author seemed to blame depression - an actual brain chemical imbalance - on the person that is depressed. He was baffled as to why his father, after suffering a debilitating stroke, felt helpless. And don't get me started on the cruelty to the dogs he and his partner subjected the animals to in order to support their theory.

From what I read, the author was more interested in showing the reader that he was superior to other researchers. There was no discussion of neuroplasticity which current research has shown that we can change how we look at things by creating new neural pathways in our brain.

The questionnaire was greatly flawed, having no room for modern interpersonal responses.
If my partner says that they wish to have some time away from the relationship and I do not continue to pursue them, according to this questionnaire I am a pessimist. Current thought is that I would give my partner the time that they need because this is the right thing to do.

I actually threw this book in the garbage so that at least my copy wouldn't be read by anyone else.
