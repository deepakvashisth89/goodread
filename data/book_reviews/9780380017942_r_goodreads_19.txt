Michael Moorcock takes us on a trip, from the end of time to the nineteenth century, to the end of time again which might just be the beginning of time. It is the trip to end all trips.Themes of love collide with nods to vintage science fiction ( Moorcock dares to feature H G Wells as a major character), and the humour is rich throughout with its fish out of water scenario, as Jherek Carnelian tries to make sense of 1896 England.This is fabulous science fiction, rich in fantasy and imagination, and thought provoking and psychedelic. I loved every page of it.
