Some books have lofty goals - to introduce you to a unique culture or character, to put forthcompelling ideas and perspectives, or to inspire us and stretch our imaginations. Then there is this book, which simply seeks to entertain and amuse you and your grandkids as you bond over a silly, funny story. And maybe that is a far loftier goal than you thought initially.This is a good read that captivates younger kids and keeps everyone laughing as 4 young boys are involved in a contest involving the main character eating a fried worm every day for a month. The humor flows naturally from this premise that we all can relate to from our youth: “I bet you can’t (fill in the blank)”.I liked it but my grandsons loved it so the target audience would give it 5 stars. Enjoy - there’s even some tasty recipes for fried worms at the end of the book (which I am not tempted to try).
