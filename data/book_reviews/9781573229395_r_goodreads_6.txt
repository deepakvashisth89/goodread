A bittersweet story, as I expected it to be, told in a series of vignettes. For me, the more interesting scenes were Joseph's recollections of life in Europe during WWII as a Jew, hiding from the Nazis. The character of Kitty was secondary, oddly, as it is, I assume, Gwen Edelman's persona. The "details," such as Joseph's little tics (storage of food, the way he eats, etc.) added to the flavor. I'd be interested to know who was Gwen's "Joseph" in real life. I enjoyed the book.:-)
