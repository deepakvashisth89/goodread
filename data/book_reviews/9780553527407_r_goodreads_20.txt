Whenever there is an opportunity to read a collection of horror short stories by Stephen King, you must jump on it. Night Shift captures many of King’s greatest short stories and puts it all together in a must-have collection that you’ll enjoy reading from start to finish. One of the biggest reasons why I wanted to read Night Shift was to read some follow-up stories directly following the events of ‘Salem’s Lot.Both “Jerusalem’s Lot” and “One for the Road” were absolutely fantastic! I won’t ruin anything for you but wow, it was great seeing ‘Salem’s Lot in such interesting ways. Considering this was King’s first-ever collection of short stories to be released, there’s a bit of everything here for everyone. Some of my other favorite short stories here were “I Am the Doorway”, “The Lawnmower Man”, “Children of the Corn”, and “The Man Who Loved Flowers” as they each had their own horrific, creepy, and chilling vibes to them.I give Night Shift a 5/5 as I genuinely enjoyed reading all these stories as they were very intriguing and unique in their own way. The majority of stories here really hit that spot for me that you can only truly find in King’s work and considering this has 20 short stories altogether, makes it a great deal. I would recommend this to anyone that loves horror or wants to read more of King’s earlier work to see why he’s the greatest horror author of all time!
