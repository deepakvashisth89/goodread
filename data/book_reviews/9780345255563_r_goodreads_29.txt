A great look at 1970s through the lens of science fictionI loved this book. Ben Bova tells a great story about the Cold War and about colonizing the moon. Indeed, the major emphasis of the book is the politics of the 1970s. Although some of the material is dated (this book first appeared in 1976 and this is a re-release), this doesn’t change how good the story is. The story is well paced with some great action scenes. The characters are well developed and their interactions well written. I recommend this book for anyone interested in science fiction, politics and history.Disclosure: I received this book free via Netgalley in exchange for an honest review.
