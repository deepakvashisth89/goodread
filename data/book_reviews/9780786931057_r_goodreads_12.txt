I love Dungeons and Dragons. I love Forgotten Realms. This book had a strong enough set up to keep me reading it.Nonetheless, little things in the book screamed "I was written by a dungeon master!" and yanked me out of the story and setting, such as using character classes as pronouns (e.g. "The cleric" instead of "him"). Third person omniscient narration did not help either.I rage-quit reading this book after the line, "The sunlight was absorbed by elegant strands of Spanish moss..."
