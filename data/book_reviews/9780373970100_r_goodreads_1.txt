Sad, depressing and full of triggers for those of you who have issues with fat-shaming, food and/or body issues :(

Summer's Awakening begins with heroine overhearing hero call her a "fat pig" and ends with heroine thanking hero for giving her the needed wake-up call to slim down and change her life for the better, even if it hurt her immensely. In between, we have an infomercial for Weight Watchers, a lot, and I mean a lot of food porn, design porn (jewelry, architecture, interior design), and travelogues from the English countryside to London, Florida, New York, the Swiss Alps, Nantucket and finally ending at Mustique, at Les Jolies Eaux, the private retreat of Princess Margaret. There are also many mentions of the Royal Family and the usual Princess Diana worship.

The story was not all bad. The way the author gets inside the body and mind of a self-loathing young woman who eats for emotional comfort, and is in the throes of a food addiction as dire as any meth or heroine addict, was very believable and moving. I was happy for her that she achieved her goal if anything because she conquered her demons but sad that she still displayed an obsessive need to weigh herself daily and agonize over gourmet meals.

As for the romance, forget it! The so-called hero was disgusting. His arrogance never took a dip. He was callous, arrogant, cold, and aloof and I never saw him grow up. His background sad story in no way justifies the way he treated the heroine. So he was the product of his mother's affair with another man while married to her husband. So his big first love affair dumped him, breaking his heart. How in the hell does that justify him talking about the heroine, or any human being, the way he did. How does it justify him sexually harassing her from time to time as if for some form of amusement, while he had a full-time mistress. How in the hell does it explain his insulting marriage of convenience proposal which wasn't even put in the form of a question but as kind of a nonchalant statement of: "We should just adjust our professional relationship from live-in nanny to my orphan niece to my wife." And in the end, for her to have to make the first move towards him, ask him to marry her and tell him she loves him first and basically let him off scot-free for everything was absolutely nauseating. Unfortunately, one of those "be happy the heroine is happy" kind of story.
