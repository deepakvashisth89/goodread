I've read this entry, and this series, multiple times, reliving the action/adventure themes of this and other writers, enjoying the nuances of those times. In this entry, Bolan has moved to Mexico, on the trail of a summit meeting between the current king of the crime scene and some potential invading Mafioso from the States.Great action scenes with the scenery of the tropics as the canvas. Some of the players: Bobby Cass, a paper tiger; John Royal, former movie star; Martha Canada, a beautiful girl Friday; Max Spielke, local bad guy; Too Bad Paul, head bodyguard.
