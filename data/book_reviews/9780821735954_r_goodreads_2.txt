This book was set in Brazil and the Portuguese language was so refreshing and welcomed. Sebastian is a modern day Robin hood. When his friend, Padre Osmundo, encouraged Tory to visit Brazil and donate her time and services to teach the poor peasant children, sebastian knew he was in for a lot of trouble from this woman. Tory is an american fighting for women rights. After she was fired from her teaching job, she decided to head to brazil to teach. But from the moment tory and sebastian met, they cannot tolerate each other. They are always bickering over something or the other. But the attraction is so strong between them, they cannot ignore it.I enjoyed this story. Set in the late 1800s when Brazil was uncivilized, it portrays the hardship of life during that time. Rich landowners taking advantage of poor peasants using them as servants. It also depicts the catholic culture of brazil. Christianity plays a big part in this book. Sebastian is a strong fearless landowner and bandit champion of the poor. He steals from the rich to give to the poor. Tory is pioneer woman fighting for women rights, wearing men's clothing, riding bicycles and horses. The secondary characters were strong and lent a solid backup. It makes for an enjoyable read because you have two strong primary characters navigate and overcome their obstacles in order to find their HEA.However I must mention that the cover is deceiving and so is the title of the story. Tory was never a tropical captive by sebastian. There was no beach scene in the book as depicted on the cover. So I'm a little disappointed with this. The cover does not match any scene in the story.
