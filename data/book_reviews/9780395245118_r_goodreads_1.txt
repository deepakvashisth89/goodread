In the glorious days of the Algonquin round table, Cornelia Otis Skinner was a delightful writer who honed her sharp but loving wit at The New Yorker magazine writing about theater, etc. This is a biography of Howard Lindsay and Russell Crouse, two American originals, who conquered the Great White Way with such shows as LIFE WITH FATHER, ARSENIC AND OLD LACE, CALL ME MADAM, SOUND OF MUSIC, and many more. Their early years growing up in turn of the century America provides a fascinating insight into the good old days.

These two men lived life with wit and verve, which Ms. Otis splendidly showcases. If you are all interested in American Theater, this is a classic.
