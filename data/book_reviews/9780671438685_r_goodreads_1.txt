I read this decades ago, when I was maybe 7 years old. And I adored it. Heller was everything I wanted to be: smart, sassy, struggling to figure out where she fit in between her childhood and the seemingly worldly lives of her elder sister and friends. I'm sure I still have my original copy packed in a box somewhere - I have always taken exceptional care of my books - but because I never go long without thinking about this story (oh, the train trestle! How that scene scared and thrilled me!), I've actually ordered an old, used copy so I can read it again soon (my freshened memory will allow for a better review!), and perhaps pass it along to a friend who has a little girl, one who may someday love Heller and her world as much as I did.
