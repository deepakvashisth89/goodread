It's a world where you have to accept being overwhelmed. It's not always easy, but you get caught up in the game, the mix of people, places, and eras. It is a pleasant reading to approach under the angle of poetry, dreamlike. I liked this reading, even if it sometimes required effort: one does not enter this universe unexpectedly and probably does not leave it unscathed; it is a look at people, life, and feelings. And at war, alas. A look at yourself, too, at each other's humanity. I wonder how they brought this novel to the screen; it must have been a tremendous work to respect the spirit.
