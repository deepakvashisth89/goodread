This is a light read and yet you get a lot of rich images and great characterization. Personally, this will be a favorite of mine to return to at times when I need something to lift my spirits. Comedy, romance, and even a bit of mystery are all set in the enriching backdrop of a French chateau. The writing itself is quick and witty; cloaked with blithesome sarcasm and stereotypical comments. There isn't one character that I disliked; in fact, they all work together in a truly enjoyable way. I enjoy Mayle's craft for the dialogue between the characters and even the way the point of view shifts among them. It's a well-rounded read and I would recommend to anyone looking for something that sheds a bit of warmth on the winter ahead.
