3.5 out of 5 stars.A well-researched and compelling account of one of California’s most notorious serial killers. The investigation, trial, and sheer scope of Randy Kraft’s crimes make for an engaging and disturbing read, especially for anyone interested in true crime or Southern California history.My only reservation is that I’ve read a lot of true crime over the years, so the book didn’t quite have the impact on me that some of the genre’s classics did like In Cold Blood. Still, it held my interest throughout and raised some intriguing unanswered questions, particularly about whether Kraft may have had help in at least some of the murders or whether law-enforcement has done forensic genealogy on samples from a couple of the crime scenes that were neither the victim nor Randy Kraft. Not an all-time favorite, but a solid and worthwhile true-crime read.
