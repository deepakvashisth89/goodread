I very much enjoyed reading this book. I found it in a "Little Free Library." I've been to Paris three times and have walked many of the streets Mike Ward walked on and stayed in small hotels on the left bank. I've also read some of the novels, seen some of the paintings, and read about some of the people that Mike Ward meets or mentions. All the name dropping was great fun and with Google, I was able to find out about who some of the people were I had never heard of, or look up the French phrases that are used. I don't speak French, so someone who does would find this book fun to read.

The period after WWI interests me so this was another reason I enjoyed the book. I don't like Hemingway's writing, but after reading the book I might pickup "The Sun Also Rises" so see what all the fuss was about.
