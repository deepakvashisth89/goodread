Although, Dolly, the first person narrator is not as psychotic as many of Jim Thompson's narrators, he is a whack-a-doodle and Thompson hangs him out to dry. And this is really Thompson's forte, taking on the voice of a character he wants to skewer and doing them in with their own voice. Thompson's ability to throw his narrative voice is vastly underrated and it is on full display in this novel. One thing I loved is the way he showed Dolly scrambling, making mistakes and then trying to cover them up, all the while he has a dialog going in his head thinking he is smarter than everyone else.The ending! Wow, I had forgotten how he had layered the alternate readings into one flowing narrative. That is five-star all by itself!
