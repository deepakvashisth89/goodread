An important book this, and one that has been poorly translated in the past, so I advise you not to read the free versions out there.As always with Norton, there are some great essays and other contextual docs here which provide much needed background and additional detail for those of us not too familiar with the Mexican Revolution.The novel (or, really, novella) is well written and punchy - comparisons to the short sentences of Hemingway are not misplaced.
