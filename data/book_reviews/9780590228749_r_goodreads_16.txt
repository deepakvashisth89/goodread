I liked this book. Abby is baby sitting and had an asthma attack. Kristy takes over the job while Abby is rushed to emerg, but Kristy is skeptical of whether Abby could handle babysittingHowever, most of the book is focused on the Stoneybrook fair to raise money for the arts. The BSC have a booth where they are selling cut outs of music and art images with a pin on them. Other baby sitting charges have fortune telling, a booth for selling crafts, and taking pictures with Elvira the goat and buying cookies shaped like goats. Abbys family is running a booth for decorating cupcakes, as well as selling flat cakes shaped like a ballet slipper, a piano, and one that says “support art”
