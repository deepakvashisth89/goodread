What a mess. Much of the book takes place in dreamlike settings because characters are moving back and forth between 3 or 4 planes of existence. Much of the action is propelled without an engine, it just sort of happens, characters just know things, when its convenient they magically communicate across miles and dimensions, sometimes as vague feelings and sometimes as full conversations. Some steampunks will probably like it anyhow, as it has inexplicable steampunk elements.The Zelazny co-authored books I've read have not been very good. He was so very good flying solo. I wonder if any of his friends told him, "stop diluting your talent with co-authors" while he was alive.Also, terrible book jack design. Who leaves a back jacket completely blank? The marketing manager should have been fired.
