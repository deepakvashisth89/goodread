Being a lifelong fan of baseball and having seen Tim play there were no surprises in this book. There weer not even any revelations on the game or people I had watched play for years. I hate to say it was old news but for the seasoned baseball viewing of my generation all the information was previously known. Sure, there were small personal interactions here and there that we wouldn't know about. For the younger person or newer fan there is information here that is worth a read, most about the grind of "the show".Bottom line, an enjoyable and easy read but I wouldn't have felt like Imissed anything if I didn't read it.
