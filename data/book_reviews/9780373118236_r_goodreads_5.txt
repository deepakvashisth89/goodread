I believe, that with the right writer, any story can be told in an entertaining manner. The idea of falling for your dead fiancé‘s brother was intriguing to me. But OMG this was such a flop to me.It may be that the story doesn’t stand the test of time. From the 70s to the 90s, the girl Boss was promoted softly in films, but was reduced to a regular vulnerable girl when the right man walks into the room and wants her. And it was OK for him to take her so long as she finds him attractive. This book unfortunately does the same thing and turns it up a notch by having her be literally weak and fainting.Major Spoiler: And I’m sorry, nearly being raped by your new love interest is not love inducing especially afterwards when you establish that you were nearly raped by your deceased lover and didn’t like that!I know that this author can write better than this. There are more things that upset me about this book in regards to pacing, adjectives, and general storytelling.TLDR: I do not recommend this.
