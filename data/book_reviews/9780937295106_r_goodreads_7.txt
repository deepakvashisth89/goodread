I read this aloud to my 1st grader. We really enjoyed this second book in the Kirsten series. It did give me some good talking points on honesty.

Personally, I didn’t like that Kirsten sneaks around her parents to meet with her friend. I get that during that time they probably would have said no. We talked about that. BUT I also know many kids read these books alone, and dishonesty is not a character trait we should be parading as ok. I wish there had been a conversation between Kirsten and her parents about that.

I did like how Kirsten’s view of her teacher changed. Miss Winston was a bit over the top but it was made clear she really did want her students to succeed. No kid wants to work harder by nature, but I was glad her teacher wasn’t excessively cruel and Kirsten didn’t give up.

Content to consider/discuss for parents: dishonesty, hiding things from parents, friend moving away
