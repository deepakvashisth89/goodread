The wise person does not read William Sleator novels before going to bed. Well, maybe Oddballs, but that is not his usual fare - and nor is it the book in question here. Anyways. This was a disturbing book with an interesting premise. Nothing, really, to distinguish it from other Sleator books. I've been trying to find some sinister twist in the ending, but either I'm tired or just not seeing it. A decent book, quick to read, but not as good asHouse of Stairs.
