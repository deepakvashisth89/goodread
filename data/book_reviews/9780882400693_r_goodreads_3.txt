At first this was an excellent book. It painted a realistic picture of what daily life was like some 100 years ago. I was fully invested in these people and could really imagine their existence. Because my interest was so piqued I searched into a few events that Clara mentioned. I found that her account didn't hold up with what other people had said, which was disappointing. Also, from looking into her and her children's lives I found a few major life events that she glossed over or which weren't even mentioned in the books. I understand that there are a lot of reasons that someone might minimize traumatic events. However, her focus on acquiring furniture and painting rooms just didn't resonate with me as much as a continued focus on her and her children's lives might have done. Overall it was a decent read, but given the time and place, it seemed like it could've been much more.
