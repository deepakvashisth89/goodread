Maybe a little bit of the Maltese falcon as well. I don’t remember if this was written completely by SM Stirling, or if it was a collaboration with either Jerry Parnell or Larry Nevin himself.But he is in fact playing in N-Space, where “madness has its place”… I don’t remember if that story is also included in this compilation.The children’s hour was later expanded into a full book and it was one of the best reads I have ever had it kept me completely engrossed from start to finish.
