This one was worth searching for. One of the bloodiest, craziest, YA horrors I’ve read in a while. Reminds me of Christopher Pike a bit, as the writing felt a little more mature than, say, a Point Horror. We do get some cussing (lol, like a dozen uses of the word “bitch”) and the characters are on the hornier side.The plot is a bit predictable but it’s still a fun and wild read, promise. The ending goes all out.Could’ve done without the author’s descriptions of teen girls in swimsuits though. It was a bit much.
