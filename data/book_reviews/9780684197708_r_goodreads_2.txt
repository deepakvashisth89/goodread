3.5/5Aaaaalright, so this was also quite a trip. I’m kinda upset that it took me quite a while to finish it, mainly because if I take too long on a book it kinda affects my rating in a way? Not sure how to explain it.Moving on to a quick summary with my own words.Julia’s brother, Matthew, drowned by accident when they were both just kids. Time passed by and now, Julia’s husband showed up death and by coincidence, he also drowned?! So now it is up to Gemma & Duncan to figure if these are correlated, or just…. Indeed, a coincidence. And if so, then who was responsible for it?I managed to find the plot just, enjoyable. Mainly because I guess it wasn’t keeping me intrigued at all like the previous book and because I was getting a bit confused with all the bonds with each character, the multiple alibis, it was complex for me but then again, I might have found it confusing because I had too many pauses to finish the book.However, as expected, the 2 main characters are still super likeable. I love their dynamic, their friendship… the type of romantic tension they seem to have (which yes, I am absolutely rooting for because slow burn is the best), etc.I think there were at least some characters or scenarios that didn’t brought up anything to the plot at all and just seemed kinda… extra. They were just there.. but I guess those are necessary when reading this genre.As for the plot, I found it alright. I think it was quite slow for me, and I wasn’t truly intrigued on what was going to happen next or I wasn’t shocked at all with the plot twists (except for the very last one).Apart from everything else, it was a good read (get it? Cause “Goodreads”. That was such a dad joke, sorry). So I’d recommend it if you’d like to read something to entertain yourself for a little.
