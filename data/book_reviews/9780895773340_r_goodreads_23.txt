This novel depicts a clash of cultures, a clash of sensibilities between the British and the Indians. The novel takes place in the early 1920’s at a time when India was under British rule. There is a contempt and revulsion of the Indian people by the Brits. Forster depicts their snobbery and elitist attitudes throughout. He actually by the end of the novel foretold that change was coming.

Mrs. Moore arrives from England to visit her son, Ronny. With her comes Miss Quested, whom Ronny hopes to marry. Mrs Moore comes off as an open minded woman and Miss Quested as a woman hoping to learn and embrace the Indian culture.

An event occurs involving Miss Quested which pits the English against the Indians. Forster shows well how the event is viewed from both sides. I did appreciate how he ended the book- He seemed to want us to embrace cultural differences and to be open to others.

Published: 1924
