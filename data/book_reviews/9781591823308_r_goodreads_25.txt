This volume really gets into Ryo's past and explains his parents' murder, as well as gives us the background around why Ryo decided to become a police officer. It also reintroduces us to Diana Spacey, who showed up in a previous volume and is absolutely amazing. I wish she was around more.But, I think, most importantly, this volume shows that Ryo isn't as calm and unaffected by things as he tries to appear to be. And it was nice to see Dee show up in a role where he wasn't the hardhead for once.
