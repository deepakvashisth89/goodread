I was taking a walk in my neighborhood and came across a little give-and-take library. I picked this one because it looked the most used.I really enjoyed this book. It was hard to get used to the language at first (referring to people’s ages with sun cycles, etc). Once I got through the first chapter, I was hooked. I do see where folks are coming from saying that the two main characters being “god-like, skinny, adonis, etc” is a bit cheesy and outdated. With this, I felt that the author was constantly painting a picture of the characters, and I was really able to visualize them.It had many confusing aspects that seemed to all come together at the end. It had you guessing but you still didn’t expect what was coming. I will also say I did feel triggered by some mentions of SA, but it was still a good read.
