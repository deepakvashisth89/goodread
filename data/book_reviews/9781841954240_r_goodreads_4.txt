This book wasNiccolò Ammaniti's English language translation debut in which, during a sweltering hot Italian summer, a group of children go off together, when one of them finds what he believes to be a corpse! This discovery leads to something altogether possibly worse(!) as Ammaniti explores the vagaries of childhood with its shifting friendship alliances sometimes caused by casual betrayals, with the mix of helplessness and earnest audacity with which children confront adult situations. A nice idea, that could have been so much more compelling and 'deeper' than it is here. 5 out of 12.2004 read
