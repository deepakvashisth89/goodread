Not too much to say about this tome - but all of it is positive. The book deals with a range of potential relationships including parent-child not just relationships which some will find helpful.The book focusses strongly on the specific strategies of abusers and more on the cognitive level of how to reclaim your safety, not so much a practical guide, although they do touch on safe ways to confront a person if that is warranted.Will take a break from my 'toxic' books for a while!
