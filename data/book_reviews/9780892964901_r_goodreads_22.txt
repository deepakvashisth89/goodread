The second installment in the Hap and Leonard series takes place not too long after the first one, with Leonard still recovering from his wounds. One day Leonard finds out his uncle Chester passed away. Now, Chester assumed the role of a father figure for Leonard growing up, at least until Leonard 'came out' and Chester basically disowned him. Nonetheless, Leonard still has a soft spot for his old uncle. Turns out his uncle had a soft spot for Leonard also, leaving him about 100 grand and his house a few towns away.So, this starts off with Hap and Leonard moving into uncle Chester's house; pretty run down, Leonard figures he can fix it up and sell it. Their next door neighbors run a crack house, which is not pleasant, but hey, the house is in 'nigger town' and the cops do not really care. Chester also left some odd items in a safe deposit box; was Chester just losing it at the end? Who knows? While this starts off pretty normal, things quickly get interesting when Leonard finds a dead body beneath the floorboards of the house, packed in a chest to which his uncle left him the key for in the safe deposit box. What was his uncle up to? Turns out for the last several years small boys have been going 'missing' around the hood. Leonard cannot imagine his uncle as a child killer, but if not him, who has been doing the deed?Overall, the mystery behind the story did not really impress; no real twists and turns here and you pretty much know the guilty party right off the bat. Nonetheless, Lansdale can tell a story and this one had me laughing out loud in parts. The acerbic social commentary along the way also added some value, as did the fun characters. Fun story-- not too deep, but engaging and vivid. 3.5 stars, rounding up for GR!!
