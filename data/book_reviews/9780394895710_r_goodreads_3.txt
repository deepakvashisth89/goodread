This is a beginner reading book. It's rather funny. A boy keeps making things and is scolded for making so much noise. On the day of the chief's birthday, the boy fashions a new thing (a wheel). It, like all the other gifts, is discarded since the chief (and the boy) can't figure out what it does.The boy sees the wheel rolling down the hill. He goes back to his cave to re-develop . . . he ends up making a tricycle. He presents this to the chief, who becomes pleased. The boy was able to make something no one else could: the chief smile.
