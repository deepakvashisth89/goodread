Review:The Very Busy Day is a cute story a big and little mouse. The big mouse is doing all the work to plant flowers and the little mouse is too busy to help. I would read this story in small groups before planting flower seeds. This is also a good story about friendship. Is it nice to help your friends? Do you help your mommy or daddy? Have you ever planted a garden? What was in your garden? This story is great for a discussion starter.Learning Experience:We would create a life cycle of a flower on chart paper. I would ask the children what it takes to make a flower? Then we would discuss cause and effect. What causes the flower to grow? What would happen if we didn't water the plant? Then the next day I would have the children plant their own plant and let the children be responsible for watering thier own flower. I would go over the steps it takes to plant a flower.
