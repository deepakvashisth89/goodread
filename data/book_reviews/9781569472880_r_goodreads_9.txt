It was a captivating book up until the first half, then you stop feeling sorry for Leela and just begin to hate her for being so weak. She lets herself be degraded by every character in the book and all you see is revolt but no clear action to follow it. It could have been a great book but i find the ending lacking in substance and the omnipresent force that the other chapters had. I always though that she's open her own restaurant and make good use of her sense of smell but i was left wanting.I guess i understand part of the ending, and the conquer of fear but i am still disappointed because the book ends just like it starts - with no clear definition of who Leela is.
