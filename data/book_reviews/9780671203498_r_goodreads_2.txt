This collection is classic Wodehouse. I particularly enjoyed The Drones Club stories, written, I assume, before his comedic brain gave birth to Bertie Wooster; the Empress of Blandings story; the Oldest Member stories (especiallyThe Coming of Gowf); and the novel at the end,Quick Service. The latter would make a terrific period comedy; if we can have 10 iterations of every Jane Austen novel (and I say this as a devout Austen fan), why can’t “they” — the BBC or other filmmakers — give us something a bit different. Wodehouse, Heyer, and other writers from that era could quite successful, I think.Anyway, when life has me down, a bit of Wodehouse is always the cure for what ails!
