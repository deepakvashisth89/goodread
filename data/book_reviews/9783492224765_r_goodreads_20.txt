Dave comes in early to the office and his boss tries to kill him. A real thrill ride.I really like the character. It turns out Dave is not only good at turning the hunters into the hunted, but he gets a real charge out of it.I would give it 5 stars but there is some stuff about torture, heads on poles, and some bad stuff from his Vietnam days, and the ending had some odd spots, but it is still one of my favorite books.
