A little too convoluted for it's own good but in the end a pretty good Bond novel. The first half of this book is also VERY slow and is mostly setup. Once things start rolling about 170 pages in though I was glued to my seat. I also enjoyed the Hong Kong setting in the later half and I find it very interesting that the original title of this novel was Tomorrow Always Comes which almost kind of makes this novel a possible inspiration for Tomorrow Never Dies? Regardless, the 2nd half of the novel more than made up for it's mediocre first half and earns it a solid 3.6 stars rounded up.
