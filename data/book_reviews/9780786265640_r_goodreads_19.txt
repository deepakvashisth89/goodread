Washington state, Arizona and the Caribbean Island of Santa Inez ~Hannah Jessett becomes involved with ruthless and experienced ‘business shark’ Gideon Cage after he threatens to destroy her younger brother Nick’s software company. Hannah and Gideon Cage begin as enemies, can anything more ever exist between them?Comments ~TWIST OF FATE is a standalone novel published by Krentz in 1991. I have read and enjoyed Krentz’s novels for decades and years ago I read TOF. After rereading it, however, I felt that TOF is a novel which hasn’t held up well over time. The conflict consists of the two main characters facing off against each other with the accompanying emotional drama, arguments, etc. The story felt old & dated to me and I nearly didn’t finish it. There’s a considerable side dish of feminism tossed in as well. In addition, the conflict device of plotting one MC against the other is one I don’t particularly enjoy. I prefer novels in which the main characters, each with their own unique strengths, join together against a shared common enemy so the format in TOF just didn’t work for me. I also didn’t care for the excessive blasphemy nor the utter lack of humor (the first is not usually prevalent in Krentz novels nor the latter lacking). I won’t bother to reread this one again. I still enjoy other titles, both Oldies and new, by Krentz. Absolutely Positively, an Oldie title pub in 1996 is a fun Krentz novel plus others under her Jayne Castle and Amanda Quick names.
