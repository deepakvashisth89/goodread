is an episodic novel read not for its plot but for its elegant economy; for the sucking of oranges, or the eating of peas with a two-pronged fork; for the sparing use of candles, or the chasing of sunbeams on a new carpet with sheets of old newspaper; for an incident with lace (hilarious), or a cow in grey flannel; for the fascination of a sea-green turban, or the evils of green tea.

Some books seem to have been written to be read aloud and this happens to be one of them. I could listen to the audiobook again and again, always picturing the cast of the miniseries—Judi Dench, Eileen Atkins, Imelda Staunton—as I do so. Comforting is exactly the right word. Gentle people, gentle manners, gentle humor.
