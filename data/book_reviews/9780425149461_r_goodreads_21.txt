Ketchum is a new author to me & while this is not horror as such it's certainly an horrific tale - that of an evil man who inflicts the worse kind of pain & torture on his family.This harrowing tale - despite making me shudder at times & at others making me feel sick to my stomach - had me gripped, as disgusted & horrified as I felt at times it was really hard to put down. A really good read - although "enjoyed " seems an inappropriate word to use about it!
