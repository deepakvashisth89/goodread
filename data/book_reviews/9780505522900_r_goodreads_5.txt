Not really sure where the author was going with this book according to the blurb. First of all, there were 3 love interests. Even though Steven did some horrible things, I still wanted Melanie and him together, idk why. I didn't even like Simon that much. What I really didn't like is that all the men kinda forced or cajoled Melanie into sleeping with them. She passively accepted. They chose her, rather than she choosing them.The first half of the book is compelling and hard to put down for some reason.
