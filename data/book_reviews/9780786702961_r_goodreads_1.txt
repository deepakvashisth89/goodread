'Erotic Verse' claims to be 'a shameless celebration of sexual poetry, with less of the boudoir and more of the bedroom'... Well, it surely is very colourful! It clearly leans towards the steamy and funny side of things. Not that there isn't some beautiful poems on lovemaking; it's just that these poems quickly seem to be outweighed by a display of under-the-belt type of wit which might not be to everyone's taste! It might be playful away (slightly!) from the gaudy, but it has personally left me with an after-taste of the funnier more than the sensual, which I found disappointing. A dashing read for the non-squeamish, it will delight adepts of a blunt and brash sense of humour. As for me personally... nope! I prefer something more sensual, lascivious and passionate -like ;-)
