Following on from the events of Armageddon, Rex is pulled into the past and teams up with Jack and Elvis to take down the antichrist.This is packed with running jokes and throwbacks to the first book, with several interconnected plot lines, even OMalley ans Pooley make an appearance. I found this to be more fun than Armageddon, which had a much more bleak setting.Overall great fun, well worth a read.
