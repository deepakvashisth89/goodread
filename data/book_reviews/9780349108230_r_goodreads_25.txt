It's terrific that Jane Gardam's novels are being reissued in the US because it gives a whole new audience the opportunity to experience her language, plots, and unique perspective on well, just about everything. This book is no exception. It takes a village to raise a child, even if that village is filled with people who are not especially likable. Gardam specializes in skewering those who need it- some of her characters are caricatures, and as long as you accept that, you'll get a good laugh. I'm curious how this reissue with sit with readers; the world has changed but people remain the same. Thanks to Netgalley for the ARC. Keep in mind that Faith Fox is less the subject than the novel of this entertaining novel.
