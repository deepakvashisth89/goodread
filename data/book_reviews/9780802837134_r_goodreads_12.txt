The small town description was a bit too drawn out, lasting at least ten chapters ish near the beginning. Then things picked up. Some of the 50+ chapters are absolute fire. Excellent sharp critiques of evangelicalism. Feels Machen-like at some points. Some chapters are wonderful about the role and call of pastors. I would consider extracting these portions for elder training. 4 stars because of unnecessary length imho. Still an excellent book.
