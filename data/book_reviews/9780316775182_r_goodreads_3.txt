I really loved this book! The family in the book loves each other and loves Judaism. The basic story is that the children put on a Purim spiel for their family, but since cousins are not coming to help with the acting, and older, Ashkenazi Jewish woman from the neighborhood, who used to be an actress, comes to help play Haman. At the end, Frannie makes friends with her! I love intergenerational Jewish friendship! I love this book so much! :)
