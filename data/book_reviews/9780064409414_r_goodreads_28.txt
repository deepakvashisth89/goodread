“Our world is going to have an end some day. Perhaps this one won’t.” 🦄🌏⚔️

what an extraordinary end to an extraordinary series! i feel as though every reader should read this series, especially if you’re a fantasy reader. it really is such an important one.

the plot!!
in this book, you will be seeing what the final days of narnia is like. sure, it is sad, but it is also very beautiful. we also get to see our friends of narnia from the previous books! this really is such a special book of the series.

the characters!!
eustace and jill are back for another adventure! this time, it’s with a king named tirian. there’s even a unicorn involved! with these characters and the characters from the past books, this book is certain to make you happy.

the ending!!
not going to lie, the ending is pretty dark, but also very beautiful indeed. i definitely didn’t expect to see an ending like that one, but i guess it makes sense that it ended that way. that’s all i’m going to say without spoiling it for you.

i can’t believe this series is over! it was such a fun ride. i definitely enjoyed it! i hope there will be even more movie remakes in the future.

would i recommend this series? UH, YEAH!
would i read it again? OF COURSE!
