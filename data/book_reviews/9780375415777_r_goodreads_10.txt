This and the next two books form an arc; they are in some ways all the same book, split up. This one ends very abruptly at what feels like the end of a chapter, after one of the fiercest, most vivid of all O'Brian's battle sequences.Before that we get the tedium of blockade, an attempted ship entertainment, and some very lively characters. Steven also gets to slip into France for some derring-do.The writing as always is terrific, but with great swathes of somewhat repeated minutae about shipboard life. Jack's frstration mounts, particularly when he comes within inches of ship action in a vivid, truly unexpected scene with untoward consequences.It's the next book that I find myself rereading the least of all of them save 19 and 20, which I pretend don't exist.
