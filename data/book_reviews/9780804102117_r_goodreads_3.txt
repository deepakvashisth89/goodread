As usual, a well written William Martin Novel.In the late 1800's, a man named Jack Tracy stops three men who are plotting to kill Queen Victoria. Two of the conspirators are killed after Jack is Shot, but he does not kill them, the third was Jack's nephew and he, on the advice of his uncle, fled. Jack was tried, convicted and hanged. His wife, and two sons fled to America.We move ahead a few years, Jack's oldest son Tom is involved in Boston politics, his younger brother Danny, is growing up on the streets as a typical 15 year old.When they are visited by their cousin (the third conspirator)they all end up going back to Ireland to revenge Jack Tracy and to start the Irish uprising to throw off the tyranny of John Bull.On it's own, it is a great William Martin book. As written, you will be hard pressed to put it down to see the final outcome of the "Rising of the Moon."
