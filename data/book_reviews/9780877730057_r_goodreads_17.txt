This book was recommended to me by my friend Francis.I am cognizant that in giving the most famous Buddhist sutras a three-star review, I might seem like a heretic and an ignorant. However, I experienced them as somewhat impenetrable. Certainly, the riddle in the Sutra of Hui-Neng is worth pondering and sitting with.However, there are other books that I would recommend more highly on awareness and meditation, including Joseph Goldstein's The Path of Insight and Eckhart's The Path to Power of Now.
