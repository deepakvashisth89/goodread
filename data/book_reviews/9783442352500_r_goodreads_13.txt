Return of the Jedi was my least favorite movie of the original trilogy, while there are some very good moments and some of my favorite too, they were overshadowed by bad things. But I have come to love it over time, but this novelization puts every bad thing on a high pedestal and also I just cannot stand the Ewoks. If you think watching them was bad, reading of them and about them was a lot worse.Finally, even though some good moments shine through, sadly they cannot overshadow the bad things. But I heard the prequel novelizations are a lot better. Now, that the novelizations are over I can finally dive into the extended universe books and hope that they fare better than this.
