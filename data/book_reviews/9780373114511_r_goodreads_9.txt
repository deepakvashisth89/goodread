Helen Foster heads to her childhood home after learning that her father is considering selling it to a rich businessman living on the estate. Helen's first interaction with Caleb Jones is nearly hitting his baby nephew with her car! She's not impressed with Caleb, or his parenting skills - he has custody of his nephew. She proceeds to be a complete bitch to him for the entirety of the book, but can't deny she finds him irresistibly attractive.I'm used to the hero being the asshole in Mills & Boon books, not the heroine! Caleb Jones comes across as nothing other than a nice, ordinary, laidback guy, but you'd think he was a child killer the way Helen acts around him! Bloody hell! She doesn't have a single nice thing to say to him until the obligatory happy ending! Cal isn't even a beta hero. He's a solid, stand-up guy who handles the obstacles in his life with calm rationality. He's in the wrong genre!Helen was insufferable. His mistreatment of Cal continues far beyond what is believable or necessary. It got very tiring. I'm never terribly convinced by a romance in which one of the characters is being an outright, endless asshole to the other. Two stars for Cal, who's my kind of guy, and his cute nephew, Sam. At least they got their happy ending. If only it had been with someone other than Helen!
