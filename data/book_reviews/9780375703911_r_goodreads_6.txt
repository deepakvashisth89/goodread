This is the haunting story of the Marsh family: Former politician Clement Marsh moves his daughter Pella, and sons David and Raymond to the Planet of the Archbuilders after his wife Caitlin suddenly dies in Brooklyn.Pella is a precocious 13 year old who becomes entangled with Efrem Nugent, a mysterious and shadowy figure reminiscent of John Wayne's character from the film "The Searchers". Inhabitants include names like Lonely Dumptruck and Hiding Kneel- a mysterious civilization that often frightens the visitors from Earth; often everyone is feeling solitude and isolation in this mysterious word.Everyone collides in this makeshift area where devastation is often felt. Efrem and Pella are attracted to each other with a sexual tension that is deeply realized in this offbeat and bizarre retelling of the classic frontier western.
