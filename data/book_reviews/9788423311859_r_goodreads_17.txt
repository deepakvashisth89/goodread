Merlin and Company, as narrated by Felipe; Merlin's page boy, was a colourful read. From the start, everything was described so vividly as if you were really there in Miranda.After Arthur's death, Merlin and Guinevere moved to Miranda with other characters in a single house. With Merlin as his master, Felipe recalled a series of happenings that arrived on Merlin's doorstep. Strange and wonderful cases from all over Europe asking for the wise magician's help. Dwarves, mermaids, princesses, curses; it was beautifully documented.
