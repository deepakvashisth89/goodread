Elizabeth Sheridan daughter of the editor-in-chief of the New York
Courier News, is a pampered but determined to try to be a reporter on more than just the society page. Her love interest turns out to be Max Cassiday the gritty reporter for the True Crime Gazette. When an unexpected meeting, he enters her carriage while trying to outrun a couple of thugs. When her brother Ross scheming for riches associates himself with an old prospector Dooley with the promise of riches from a silver mine he owns in Colorado, they start on the adventure. Unknown to them, Elizabeth follows. Unknown to them all, Max follows them all.
A decent read.
