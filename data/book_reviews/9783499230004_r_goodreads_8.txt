I had high hopes for this based on the description, but the summary was way better than the actual book. Too many foot notes, too much rambling science, too much bizarre philosophy, too many coincidences, too many unbelievable storylines. I mean, sci-fi is supposed to be about science and unusual space-based storylines, but it just didn't work here. Also, all the narrator chapters rambled on and broke up the pace of the story.I would have a hard time recommending this to anyone.
