So, when you're Donald Westlake and you stage a murder mystery weekend, you play along with other writers like Stephen King and Peter Straub, which sounds pretty epic. This book isn't a novel, but the script of one of those weekends, kind of a glimpse into the Olympics of murder mystery weekends, if you will. While interesting, it's plainly one of those 'you had to be there' kind of things to really get the max enjoyment out of it.
