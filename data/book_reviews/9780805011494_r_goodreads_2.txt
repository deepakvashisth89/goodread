Reads like a rush job. Nothing is quite complete; the premise is shaky, to say the least, and the entanglements are sketchy, so at the final outcome the reader is left wondering how he got there! Henry Tibbit and his wife Emmy, are visited in London by an old (really old: 90-something!) friend, Lucy, from the Caribbean Island of Tampico. She implores them to come to the island to help figure out what's going on with all the corrupt government officials. All impediments are swept away in a vague way and they end up vacationing at the top resort. There are plenty of characters who show up supposedly involved in the drug business. There is also a murder. It is much easier to pick out the murderer than it is to keep track of who the good guys are vs the bad guys.Emmy Tibbit is a much more active participant than usual in this series ( and, man, does she know how to sail!) It's a thrown together plot without much concern for details, but makes for a quick, easy read.
