I found this while cleaning out an old garage and loft. Having some interest in obscure cults I took it home and, despite its failings, finished it in a couple of hours.Author Shah assembles a hodgepodge of 'secret societies', most of them 'religious', a few political. There is no method discernable, though a few accounts include reference to his personal experiences with some groups.If there's any merit to this book it may be in its being oriented to the East, many of the groups discussed being referred back to Islamic sects and even older pre-Islamic sects. These supposed transmissions through the centuries are often, in my opinion, quite dubious and Shah's evidences, being usually unfootnoted, are suspect.
