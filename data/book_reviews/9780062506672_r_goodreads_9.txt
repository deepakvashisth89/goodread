Well-written and easy to read, but nowhere shallow. The author shares personal stories as easily as scientifically supported information on the history of Goddess worship, astrology and of course shamanism. She is clearly a feminist (creator of the Motherpeace tarot and books) and focuses less on male virtues, yet she makes clear form the start that she seeks to empower women and as such, her writings don't feel out of balance. I enjoyed every bit of it.
