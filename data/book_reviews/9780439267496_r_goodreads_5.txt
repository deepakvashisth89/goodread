The book I read was called “Beacon Hill Boys”, the author is Ken Mochizuki. The genre of this book is fiction, and this book takes place in Seattle, Washington 1972. “Beacon Hill Boys” is about an American-Japanese boy who is trying to get through his high school with his group of friends. I recommend you read this book because it really teaches you, to try and not care of what people think of you.This book is about Dan Inagaki going to high school and having to face racism. Dan was just a confused teenager trying to learn about his past. Facing racism, being compared with his 4.0 gpa older brother, and trying to get the girl he wants, was a real challenge for Dan. He wanted to escape reality, so he and his friends decide to try to smoke and drink. That wasn’t a good idea if he wanted to prove his family wrong. Through the book, Dan learns a little about his families past, and why his father is so secretive and becomes moody when the topic is brought up. Also, Dan has this one person that doesn’t judge him, and accepts him for who he is, but when his family makes a decision that it is time for his grandma to go to a nursing home, it breaks his heart. She was the person who understands him, as for the other people around him who he thinks are against him.I recommend you read this book, because it teaches you, that you will not get all of your answer at once. You must have patience because there might be a valuable reason on why you must wait. I also recommend you read this because, it teaches you to be independent, Dan did not have a 4.0 gpa like his older brother, but he was still smart if he tried. After reading this book I have thought a lot about asking my family about the times when they were younger and what did they have to go through. I want to compare the past and now, just like Dan wanted to do.
