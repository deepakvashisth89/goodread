A book lets face it about bums in post ww2 traveling across America, usually getting out of town just as both the angry law and public want to strangle the beat generation folks. Mr. Sal Paradise (Jack Kerouac, the author) his soon best friend Dean Moriarty (Neal Cassady) a chronic car thief and proud of his ability. However frequently incarcerated and all habitual lushes. Wine, women and song primarily jazz, in little, loud, dinky clubs full of thick smoke and fights, ladies of the night, callous pimps, conmen getting high with their countless and illegal substances, the drunk and wasted musicians vigorously blowing horns. The boys avoid cops mostly but constantly begging for more funds, Sal's aunt ( in reality the mother) saving his rather worthless existence. This classic published back in 1957, the reaction was quite instant, a surprisingly immediate bestseller, reviews a mix bag of good and bad in numerous major newspapers, readers loved, one night an obscure Kerouac went to bed the next day he was famous, but that's years later.. However for now, skipping from town to town visiting friends poor as Sal and Dean, just a tiny , little bit tipsy, flying to nowhere. They can run but hiding impossible , their deficits clearly displayed. His other famous or infamous companions were Carlo Marx (Allen Ginsberg) and Old Bull Lee (William S. Burroughs) revealed not in a very good light. Saying it mildly, lowlife conditions, none will be chosen citizen of the year. Dean a maniac behind wheels speeding down roads at 110 miles an hour, weaving from lane to lane, just missing trucks, cars , structures, Sal shuts his eyes.. Nevertheless Dean has time to marry different women for a spell then abandons , divorce and repeats it, a fanatical chaser yet easily bored, Sal almost the same. Back and forth, east to west at dizzy speeds, their only gain are the two new dubious friends, they picked up, interesting hitchhikers, arrive at the desired destination earlier than thought possible. Dated but very fascinating slice of the underground from 75 years ago. The crimes of shoplifting having sticky fingers, treating loose women badly, stealing to get intoxicated until they fall on their face will not endear many to the boys, call them anti-heroes but never take away the bottles and drugs, then they resolve to fight. One final trip to fabled Mexico, land of hot tequila, hotter wild women, beautiful but shy senoritas, and vast, debilitating, endless poverty, a kind, generous people lacking amenities, which gives life comfort to the living. Where a supposed true - blue friend will leave you to your fate, loyalty what's that?
