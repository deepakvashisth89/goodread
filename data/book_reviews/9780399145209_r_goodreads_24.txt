Really liked it. I am slow to hook up to the Prey series by Sandford. Just starting to read again and not sure it's good for me as I tend to read at 1am when I get up to pee and with thrillers it's hard to get back to sleep! I really like the Lucas Davenport character. Trying to remember now all that I have read in this series. Rules of Prey was first.I've read Silent Prey, Bekker in NYC. Then Winter Prey where he meets Winter. Mind Prey which had the villain John Mail and the victim Andi Manette.According to my notes I've read Hidden Prey but I don't recall the characters.This one, Certain Prey, had the aggressive good lookin lawyer Carmel Loan and then the female assassin Clara Rinker, both really good characters.Sometimes it bothers me that there's so much blood and gore and violence in these books and I wonder why I'm using my remaining time on earth reading about this crap but then I want to know how Sandford is going to solve the problem, how the characters are going to develop, what kind of plot holes I see in the book, and so forth. Sometimes it gets really exciting like you want a thriller to be. For me I thought this was one of the best of the Preys. Also liked Winter very much. Wish I had gotten in on the ground floor so I could see the development of the characters. Seemed to have missed when Lucas first met Officer Marcy Sherrill. Which book was that?Trying to mix reading thrillers with reading American history and progressive politics.
