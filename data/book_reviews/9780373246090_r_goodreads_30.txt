This book is apparently number 3 of a series called Million Dollar Destinies. It was fine as a stand alone story but now I want to read about the rest of the family. There's two brothers and a sister raised by their aunt after their parents are killed in an accident. The aunt's matchmaking is involved with the first three books and from what I can tell the aunt gave up a lover to raise the children and the last book is more than likely about her and this lover years later.
