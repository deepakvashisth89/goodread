The rise of Cerryl the Great, as he is promoted through the various positions in the Guild. First serving as Gate Mage against external threats (customs inspector), then as Patrol Mage against internal threats (police), as Arms Mage for military operations (including covert assassinations), as Military Governor of a newly conquered province, to finally High Mage.It is revealed that Anya had been plotting against him all his life, from the death of his uncle and aunt to the assassins during his apprenticeship in the sewers, because of her foresight that he would one day become High Mage.
