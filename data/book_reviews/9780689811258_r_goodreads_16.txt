I liked this book because I feel like it was very original and creative. You probably won't find many books that revolve around a teenage girl joining and leaving a religious cult. Which is why I like Margaret Peterson Haddix's books. Though it was a little slow in the beginning, and she only really starts "leaving" the Fishers towards the end of the book. She also doesn't really seem to have a mind of her own, blindly following every command the Fishers give her. That may just be a part of her personality, but I personally can't relate to that. I would be doubting them even after they apparently "brainwashed me". But overall, it was definitely an interesting read.
I would recommend this book to people interested in cults or people who like other books by Margaret Peterson Haddix, like myself. I probably wouldn't recommend it to people who are constantly doubting everything that is told to them, as they may be frustrated with how Dory accepts everything the Fisher's feed to her.
