Sea Fighter is the third book in the series and this is much better than the second book of the series. Each book in the series so far can be read independently, however, having read the previous books will help.In this story, Amanda found herself in the middle of a UN mission in Africa. Instead of her usual stealth destroyer, she is now heading the Task Force consisting of a bunch of Sea Fighters. She is also heading the entire US Navy deployment in this UN mission.While her job was to observe and impose UN sanctions, she found an effective way to ensure the end to the war by staying within UN imposed limits.
