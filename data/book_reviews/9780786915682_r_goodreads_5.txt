For a long time (since the book was published in 2000) I have dreaded reading this anthology. But I thought the 4 books connected to it were really good. Finally cracked this open and read the 1st story, which was very tasty as is all other stories in this book. This wound up being one of my favorite books of all Forgotten Realms & non-Forgotten Realms. As I have rated it....it is/was Amazing.
