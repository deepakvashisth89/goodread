ALMOST A PERFECT BOOK!PREMISE:THOUGHTS: I really loved this book and have many pro's for it. However, there was a con and for me it was big enough to deduct .5 stars. Anne, George & Timmy are without Julian and Dick for the first third of this book - this to me was an absolute pro as we've never seen less than the 5 at the beginning (nor has it lasted 1/3rd of the way through!) However, without spoiling, while camping Anne & George stumble across something that puzzles them but it was something I worked out RIGHT AWAY!Julian and Dick come in at the 1/3rd mark and work out that thing very quickly!The con therefore was Enid Blyton making the girls Anne & George look stupid which we know they are not.Aside from that however, this book had it all! Fun, Friends, Adventure and yes another SECRET WAY!!! Aside from the earlier mentioned perceived stupidity of George and Anne (I again blame Enid for that) an amazing book!4.5 Stars!xoxo.
