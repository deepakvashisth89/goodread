How I loved this book as a teenager. I got rid of my original copy (so stupid!) but recently purchased another one. In fact so recently this new copy only arrived today. I probably wouldn't rate it 5 stars if it didn't bring so many memories back, but actually it is pretty interesting and well-written. It seems slightly dated that some of the characters are talking of getting married at 18 though! On the other hand, this was one of the few books I had as a teen that featured a black character. I really like Tonya, although I do prefer Jessica overall.I wish Angel had her own book in which we saw her develop as a character.And I still have no intention of learning to ski. Me and winter and sport...no.
