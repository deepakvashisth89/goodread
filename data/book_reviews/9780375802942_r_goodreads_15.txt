1) This book is about Junie B, as a first grader wanting to help in the cafeteria at school so bad that she cannot stand it. She really wants to help after she realizes Mrs. Gutzman works in there, the lady who would bring her cookies in Kindergarten. She begs her parents, and then she finally gets to experience working in the cafeteria with Mrs. Gutzman. Anything that needs to be done, she is willing to do, and is so proud to be working alongside the other lunch ladies. However, Junie B runs into a couple complications throughout her duty as a lunch lady and eventually gets sent back to her classroom, Room One. Junie is wanting to leave school after feeling unsuccessful as a lunch helper until Mrs. Gutzman surprises Junie B in her classroom to help her pass out cookies. Therefore, everyone was happy in the end, Mrs. Gutzman had a helper, and Junie B got to be a helper while also getting to enjoy her cookies.
2) This is a chapter fiction book with a reading level of Grades 1-3
3) Throughout this Junie B. Jones’ book and all of the others, she is always learning valuable life lessons. Therefore, this book could be used to teach in the classroom tolerance and acceptance, conflict resolution, problem solving, character education, and the way Junie B. reacts to each situation.
4) Girls who need a good character to look up to, and someone to lead them through the “struggles and trials” of elementary school
5) This book could be used as a part of small group use in building character skills, along with the whole series. The whole series has different character building skills and the children could read one book one week, and another the next in different small groups discussing as a group what they learned.
6) As whole class use, this book could be read aloud if there is a character skill you believe your class may be struggling with or if you think they need a laugh, because everyone can relate to Junie B. Jones.
7) All of the other Junie B. Jones chapter books
8) There is a kindle version of this book, an audible version of the whole Junie B. Jones’ series, and occasionally some theaters have Junie B. Jones shows to attend
