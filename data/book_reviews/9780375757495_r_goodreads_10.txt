I read Silas Marner when I was a freshman at Michigan State University for one of my American Thought and Language classes. I loved the book until I discovered the last 30 pages were missing in my copy of the book, and I no longer had the store receipt. Also, I didn't have the time or money to go buy another copy of the book. Somehow, I managed to write a book review without knowing what happened in the last 30 pages.

For the last 56 years, I've been wondering what happened in the last 30 pages of Silas Marner. For the last 56 years, I've been planning on rereading Silas Marner. (Don't ever say I procrastinate!)
