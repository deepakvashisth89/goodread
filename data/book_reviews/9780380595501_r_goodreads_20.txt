This was the author's 2nd book, published around 1983 if I remember correctly. She apparently lived in the small town where I live now, and the book at that time was quite scandalous. Townspeople tried to figure out who of the characters were their neighbors! Lots of sex (in and outside of relationships) and of course, babies - and we're introduced to girls and women for whom babies are front and center - but each one has a different take. This is a pretty amazing story about teenage moms, grandparents, delusions, and love, once you have all of the characters sorted.
