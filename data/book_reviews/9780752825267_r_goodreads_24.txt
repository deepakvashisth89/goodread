This book was gifted to me, something that I ordinarily would not have looked at twice.In truth it shouldn't really be my cup of tea, but, I have to say I quite enjoyed it.A chase, of sorts, with two leading protagonists, NOT, the bad guy, plus a host of shady do-gooders and ne're-do-wells with blurred lines making it hard to categorise them.3 1/2 stars and a recommendation from me.
