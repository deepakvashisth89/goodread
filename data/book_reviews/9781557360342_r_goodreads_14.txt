2 stars. I’ve wanted to read this for a long time, as it’s something of a classic. I was deeply disappointed. The cupboard and key things confuses me. The boys shut toys up in it and take them out alive. They put them back in to undo the “spell,” if you would. It is made plain that the toys become real people who have time travelled into Omri’s time. The medic was a real man in WWI and Little Bear is a real Indian from the French-Indian war. That just makes me uncomfortable, right there, with it’s mix of magic and time-travel. But that wasn’t even the clincher for me.

To start off, the cupboard magic thing required an awful lot of sneaking round and lying. I don’t need to explain why that’s a bad thing.

Second, the adults were all pretty much presented as stupid or mean, except Omri’s mother. The portrayal of the teachers and headmaster, in particular, disgusted me. The whole school chapter was too much. The attitude towards school, learning, and authority was just plain bad.

Third, Omri and his brother had an awful relationship, always fighting and saying nasty things to each other and doing unkind things.

Fourth, Patrick & Omri’s relationship/friendship was bad. Omri treated Patrick like he was stupid and Patrick had no respect for Omri’s feelings.

And fifth, even Little Bear & Boone were nasty—rude, insulting, mean, and revengeful. And when they became friends, they were still unkind!

In fact, was nice in this book—except, again, Omri’s mother, who sounds like a dreadfully exhausted woman with a terrible household to run.

On top of all that, the language in this book took me aback. This is a book FOR kids—and ABOUT kids—and the main character is always swearing?? Oh, and also, is it necessary to talk about how though Omri was a kid, he knew how dangerous it was to have two guys and one girl, and what if babies came???

The one thing I liked about this book, and the only reason I give it 2 stars instead of 1, was how it tackled the way Indians were treated, both in real life and in films. It was well done. Read for a much better, in-depth explanation of how great that part was.

Besides that, the writing style was really superb. I mean, it was I don’t say that lightly, and I would love to read more of this author, if she has any decent works. But this book shocked and horrified me, and turned me off for good on the entire series.
