What took me so long to read this book?!

This was my very first read by Mary Higgins Clark, and it certainly won’t be my last. I have always heard of this book, and it had been on my TBR list for what seems like forever, so I finally read it. This famous mystery book was written in 1975, and I must say that it is written better than half of the books that I read today (no disrespect to current authors). This book has stood the test of time, and I can see why it has been so popular. This was also the very first mystery/suspense novel that Mary Higgins Clark wrote, and because of this book she went on to become the “Queen of Suspense”. If this is true, I’m looking forward to reading many more of her suspense novels.

In this story, we have a young woman who has left California, and has settled in Cape Cod to escape her past. This young attractive woman, Nancy, has heard that the people in the Cape kept to themselves and didn’t interact much with strangers. This was just what Nancy wanted after her two children from a previous marriage went missing and were found murdered.

Now Nancy has a new life on the Cape. Nancy has a wonderful new husband, Ray Eldredge, along with two young children, Michael and Missy. On Nancy’s 32nd birthday, both her children disappear again. How can this be happening? As Nancy is once again being accused of the disappearance of her children, a frantic search begins to find them. Will the children be found alive? If so, who could do such a horrible thing to Nancy and her family?

I couldn’t stop reading this as I had to find out how this would all end. I enjoyed the suspense (especially given the time it was written), and I’m looking forward to reading the sequel to this book, “Where Are the Children Now?, which came out in 2023, and was co-authored with Alafair Burke.
