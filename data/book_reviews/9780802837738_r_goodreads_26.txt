The second of Wells' four volume trilogy. (Yeah, yeah, I know, four volumes does not a trilogy make.) All four volumes are excellent and necessary. I've realized something, however. While there is no doubt a difference between each of the four books--i.e. I'm sure he's laying out a different thesis in each one--I realize I can't really tell them apart. They're all good, but they all seem to be explications of the same thing.Nonetheless, it's a message we desperately need to hear in this day and age.
