I was forced to read this for a college class and I only made it a third of the way into it before I realized my eternal soul was not worth a grade. This book is rampant with sex, profanity and every offensive word I know (except one racial slur), and graphic animal cruelty. Totally disgusting. The worst part is, Nichols is a good author who creates believable characters and engaging situations--if he'd quit telling how each and every character likes to have sex, how often, and with who, along with chopping dogs to bits with an ax, it would be an excellent book. Oh, and quit swearing every paragraph.
