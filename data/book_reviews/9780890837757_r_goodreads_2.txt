The Sanction is one of Johnstone's most obscure books and was one of his earliest ones. I guess it's a suspense/thriller but seems to want to be something of a romance, too. It's about a man named James Edward Prince, an orphan who gets a forged birth certificate so he can join the army when he's fifteen years old. He goes to Vietnam and is put into special forces and black ops training and becomes an accomplished assassin. He's forced to retire, becomes a famous best-selling novelist, and has an affair with a married woman (which is okay, because the woman's husband plays for the other team), and the army decides it's time to retire him permanently... Lots of action/adventure, but it would have been a much better book if it had had a hundred fewer pages of anti-liberal and homophobic philosophy.
