One of the joys of being a book reader isn’t just reading different books for new information. It’s about discovering new interpretations of works you thought you already understood. Ever wonder why your true love would give you six laying geese or 11 pipers or 12 lords jumping all over the place? Here are the answers.

With its extraordinary insight into a common Christmas carol, this slim book outlines the religious significance that lies behind each of those weird gifts we’re supposed to receive on the 12 days between Christmas and the Epiphany. The song was crafted as a sort of hidden code for people forced to hide their religion. When the ban was lifted, the meaning behind each of these odd presents faded into obscurity.

So this book is a fascinating look behind an old song. The text is also accompanied by glorious watercolors, each almost shining from the page. It’s the perfect stocking stuffer, especially for those who like the notion of keeping Christ in Christmas. But it’s a grand present for any bibliophile regardless of denomination.
