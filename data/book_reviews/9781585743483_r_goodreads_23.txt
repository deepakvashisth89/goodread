I read this book as a scanned copy on the Internet Archive. The USS Thresher was a nuclear powered attack submarine that in 1963 sank with all hands. When it sank, the submarine was undergoing sea trials off the US east coast following an extensive overhaul. This book covers the sub's history prior to the accident, the search for the ship after the accident, the discovery of the wreck in water 8400 feet deep, and the Navy inquiry that followed. The author also covers deep sea rescue vehicles that were developed after the sinking and provides a survey of other accidents involving nuclear subs. Four out of five stars.
