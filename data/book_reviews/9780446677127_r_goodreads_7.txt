This is the 2nd book in the Kindle County Thriller series by Scott Turow featuring defence attorney Alejandro Stern. I enjoy Scott Turow’s writing and was very impressed with the opening novel in this series ‘Presumed Innocent’ so I had high hopes for this one. If I am really honest I didn’t enjoy it as much as the first one which was an excellent courtroom drama but I did find this one which was more about Stern’s family troubles entertaining.When Stern returns home he discovers that his wife of 30 years has committed suicide, leaving behind a web of mystery, money and guilt. Stern feels the need to look for answers while he is caught up in the threatened Federal prosecution of his most powerful and troublesome client, his own brother-in-law. What made his wife feel that her only escape was to take her own life? and what troubles has she left behind for the rest of the family to face. Although full of grief for the woman he loved Stern is desperate for answers to the many questions. What was the prescriptions filled in for his wife shortly before her death and what did she have to do with the financial manoeuvres of her brother Dixon?This is quite a long read and I definitely felt that although I was enjoying the novel it was still dragging a little in places. A rather complex plot that twists and turns and requires a fair amount of concentration along the way with all the characters and deceptions that take place.A good read but quite a lengthy one. I much prefer the courtroom drama to the family issues.
