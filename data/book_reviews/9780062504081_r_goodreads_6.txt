This book is really like a calendar of sorts with a short one to two paragraphs of Emmet Fox pieces of wisdom for the day. Not a book but a series of short takes for each day. Contains 365 separate pages for each day of the year.Each day has its won pearl of wisdom to help get you through the day. Similar to a quote of the day or cartoon of the day only this is for spiritual. If you enjoy Emmet Fox you will enjoy an easy way to get a daily dose of his insight.
