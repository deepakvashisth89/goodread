I believe this is an important but disturbing book because it is an absolutely honest look into the world of children and how disturbing that world is because children are not innocent, they are ignorant, and now-a-days then are less innocent than ever because superficially nothing is unknown but so much is not understood or misunderstood. It is a difficult book because what it is looking at is disturbing - but be clear there is nothing sexual or titillating about this novella, nor is it 'transgressive' writing - transgressive writing is the claptrap that middle class white kids from the suburbs produce while, or after, spending a sabbatical in vanilla slumming which they like to think is a walk on the wild side. A Transgressive is trying to shock by insisting on taking off their clothes when everyone would pay for them to keep them on. It is the equivalent of gesture politics without substance, cause, following or affect.My apologies for the lengthy digression but nothing in this novella is said or described without reason. You can not have this story without every word as presented. It is not a nice or easy read but it is an important one.
