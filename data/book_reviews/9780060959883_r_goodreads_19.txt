⭐️⭐️⭐️ (3/5 stars)This one was part of my “12 books from 12 friends” challenge this year and definitely not my usual read.I’ve seen the movie, so I already knew the general storyline going in, but what really stood out here was the way the story was told. I chose to listen to the audiobook based on recommendations, and I’m really glad I did. It felt like sitting around a fire listening to someone tell a story, which added a lot to the overall experience.Some thoughts:- The storytelling itself was the highlight. It had a very immersive, almost oral storytelling feel that worked especially well in audio format.- The book is very metaphorical and philosophical. While it’s centered around golf, the internal struggles the main character faces can easily be applied to life in general.- This isn’t something I would normally pick up, but I can appreciate what it was trying to do.Overall, if you’ve seen and enjoyed the movie, I think the book is worth checking out, especially in audiobook form.
