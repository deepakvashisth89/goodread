In the latest instalment of Cadderley's adventures, he is joined by his friends on a mission to destroy the evil wizards once and for all, stopping off along the way to get rid of the artefact he took from The Ghost.This is fast-paced and bounces around a bit from viewpoint to viewpoint, but not enough that you can't follow the action. Lots of fighting and casual violence, especially against non-human characters - even though Cadderley's reluctance to kill is mentioned A LOT.I would have liked the shade of The Ghost to have more of a role - I'm not actually sure what the author was aiming for when he brought him back - after all, he didn't really do anything other than being the reason that Druzil gets stranded. Maybe he just needed to fill some chapters before the heroes finally got to the Fortress.The final showdown was good and a nice, fittingly horrific end for those that deserve it.
