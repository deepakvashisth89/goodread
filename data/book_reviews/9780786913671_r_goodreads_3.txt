This was a fun read. It's tie-in fiction for the Star*Drive RPG setting, but I'm only vaguely familiar with the setting and I still found it enjoyable. The author does a good job of giving you enough info so you don't feel lost, and most of it didn't feel like it was info-dumping. It has a very "how convenient" twist at the beginning to jump-start a major section of the plot, but I've read a lot worse in my time, and it's pretty standard in the genre anyway.If you're looking for a light sci-fi read with action, alien mysteries, a gratuitous sex scene, and cyborg laser fights you could do worse than giving this a read.
