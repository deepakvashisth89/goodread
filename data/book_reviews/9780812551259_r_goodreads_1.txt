The Lagrangists is the second novel in Reynolds' Lagrange series that features private eye Rex Bader. (L-5 is an orbital position where objects maintain a stable position above the planet due to gravitational forces from the Moon and Earth offsetting.) He wrote several books in the 1960s and '70s with that setting and introduced Rex in 1979 in the novel Lagrange Five from Bantam. This one was published by Tor in 1983, almost a year after Reynolds had died. It says "Edited by Dean Ing" on the cover and title page, though it isn't listed as a collaboration as several of the future novels would be, including the last book in this series, Trojan Orbit, which Baen released in 1985. Other than being set in space, this one is more of a spy thriller novel of intrigue and espionage than a traditional sf book. Rex is hired to protect the head of the L-5 colony, but he's not sure who's trying to kill him, the Russians, the Mafia, or the nutjob political opponents. Reynolds was almost always a fun read, though sometimes he tended to let his politics and philosophy go a little too far to the fore.
