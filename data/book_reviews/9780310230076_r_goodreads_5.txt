Jim has an annoying habit of writing books that completely wreck my life and cut so deeply into my soul that I feel like I’ve just undergone open heart surgery. This was just another of those. Trying to pick a favorite Quote from it is like trying to choose which one of my children is my favorite. This book will encourage and challenge you in incredibly deep ways and if you let it, it will reignite a passion for true faith and humbling to ask God to do a mighty work in your life to restore everything and anything the enemy has stolen. I can’t recommend this or Jim’s other books enough! If you want more of Jesus and his mighty power at work in your life, this book is an excellent place to start!Favorite Quote: “As Jesus said to two blind men “According to your faith will it be done to you” (Mat 9:29) This means that my life or yours has only as much of God as our faith permits.”
