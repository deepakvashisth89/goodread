A bit difficult to wade through in parts, but a thought-provoking discussion of Christian history, especially its negative impact on our society. The author concludes the book as follows:"As we have ignored the horror of Christian history, so we have ignored scrutiny of Christianbeliefs… efforts to convince us that God demands our fear and unquestioning submission are infact efforts to control us and to contain our spirituality…. [additionally] the belief in a singularsupremacy lies at the root of chauvinism, racism and totalitarianism."
