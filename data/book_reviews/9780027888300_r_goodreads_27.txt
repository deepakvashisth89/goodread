Interesting tale for a quick read. Tan has the ability to paint vivid pictures which I enjoy.This short story begins with a little girl who is dressed for Moon Day in a yellow with black highlights outfit. She doesn't like it and she wants it gone. She smears fish guts and scales on it so her nanny gets angry and is sure the mother will dismiss her and punish the little girl. She tells the little girl to "stay put" on the party boat while she goes to find a remedy. The little girl ends up going overboard and.... rather than spill the beans I'll let you read the rest.
