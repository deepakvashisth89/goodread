Published in 1987, LONG beforeMurder as a Fine Art(2013), Morrell was just writing thrillers. (Apparently he was the original creator of Rambo!) Lots of violence and gore, a few nice people with hair-breadth escapes from three sets of bad guys including a cult within the Vatican, old Jews torturing Nazi death camp leaders, second-generation Jews blackmailing to fund Israeli armaments, bouncing all over the map. None of the delicacy and thoughtful intricacies of the twisted life of Thomas de Quincy. Recommendation--read the later stuff. Not the early. Unless you like thrillers.
