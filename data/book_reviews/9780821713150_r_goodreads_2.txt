This is my favorite Romance novel. It really is more of a tragic love story. A story of loss, letting go and violence. The violence against Lisa may put some off, but it shows her strength of character and the true love Eric has for her. The couple goes through many obstacles which, for me, made it more of a page turner because I wanted to know how they'd come out of them.
The story spans from the midwest, opening with immigrants traveling west on a wagon train, then ends in California. The villans in the story are vile and conniving and well written. The hero and heroine are easy to fall in love with and with each chapter you can feel their love for one another and just want them to find happiness, but it does not come easy.
