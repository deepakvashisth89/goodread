So if you’ve even glanced through my recent reviews, you’ll find books that left me mildly unimpressed. That’s not a good way to start a new year; seems I could use a little behavioral/attitude change here. A dose of optimism is called for, and forgive the passive voice above.This book is motivating and inspirational. Anderson’s writing style is compelling, and while there’s a lot of pom-pom waving, (remember to smile; no one else can do that for you), it has a lot to offer if you’re someone who needs to reframe your attitude and shed shyness or just be able to tell a better story.I confess I didn’t actually pause the book and do the exercises; it probably would have been better if I had. But a quick read through it still gave me ample information and plenty to implement and think about in the days ahead.
