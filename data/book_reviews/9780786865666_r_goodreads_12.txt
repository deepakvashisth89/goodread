The idea behind this book has something going for it. I read it because a friend recommended it to me. The answers that are given in snapshots, one answer for each 2 pages, are broadly applicable and sometimes will give you just the right inspiration to continue during difficult parts of the day. Therefore, it might even be interesting to flip through the book at the start of each day, at a moment when you are not full of questions yet. Right now I am full of questions and I wish I had this book near me. Luckily I can ask someone near the book to send me some inspiration.
