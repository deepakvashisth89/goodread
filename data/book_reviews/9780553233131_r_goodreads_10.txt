Popsugar Reading Challenge 2020: A Western
Although I read this for the challenge I actually enjoy reading westerns occasionally. I once read that they are romances written for men by men and that seems like a reasonable description of those I have read. This one wasn't my favorite. It read almost like a Shakespearean tragedy with practically everybody being killed, don't worry though our hero squeaked through, as well as a very few good cronies and of course his girl.
