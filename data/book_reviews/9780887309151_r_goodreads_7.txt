Content:
One of the most interesting aspects about this book was how cleverly Michael Dell predicated the dominance of Amazon.com in the e-commerce sector and the growing importance of the internet in the then upcoming 21st century economy. His emphasis on why certain business methods will work in the future for companies with greater efficiency as opposed to traditional business models was fairly accurate to say the least. His understanding of what it takes to grow companies in the new millennium was brilliant and is arguably one of the many reasons why Dell is still successfull today.

This book gave me a definitive understanding of why Dell remains one of the largest PC companies in the world simply because of Michell Dell's futuristic thinking of the direct model.

If we could get a 25 year update on how Dell is doing today that would be awesome!!!
