I normally enjoy first hand accounts of historical events, but I couldn't finish this. It's not because Reed was overwhelmingly biased towards the Bolsheviks (he was, but I knew that before I started). It's just that this book is incredibly boring. Reed seems to have had an absolute obsession with the wording of Committee resolutions. It takes a perverse skill to turn the Russian Revolution into something this turgid, but this author manages it. I struggled through to about half way but couldn't persevere any further.

Looking at the other reviews, most people seem to have enjoyed this more than I did. Oh well...tastes differ.

I haven't given the book a rating because I didn't finish it. I don't think it's fair to rate a book that I didn't complete.
