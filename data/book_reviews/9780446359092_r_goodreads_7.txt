Published in 1931, "Beggar's Choice" begins as Car Fairfax struggles to find a job in an England on the brink of the Depression. He has been trying to work for three years since his uncle/guardian kicked him out, has lost weight, and his clothes are shabby. To make matters worse, he runs into Isobel, the love of his life. Then, an advertiser passing out ads hands him a paper asking, "Do you want to earn $500?"The story deals with illegal drugs that I thought were "modern" problems. It also describes a slightly more rural London than it is today. It encourages a willing suspension of disbelief, but you hardly notice until you try to write a review.
