Though the first book I read by Kathleen Harrington is one of my top favorite historical romance novels, this sequel fell incredibly far from DreamCatcher.I didn't get the range of emotions that I did from DreamCatcher and found too many weaknesses to recommend this book.The premise was what I was seeking in bringing together my two favorite historical types of characters, first nation and highlander. What a heady mix of action and romance this promised. But alas, even the romance rang hollow.I think I will let some time pass before I try another Harrington romance.
