This is one of those nonfiction books for children that really have wider appeal for adults.I read this aloud to my 6 year old daughter, (she just turned 6) and she's not quite at the point where she really understood that 100 people were representing all the people. She enjoyed listening to it anyways.Parts of the book are a bit dark, but, that's how the world is and it doesn't make too much sense to hide the disparity in circumstances from children.I hope to revisit the book with her in a couple years when it will make more sense.
