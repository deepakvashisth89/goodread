In this book three, Aunt Destiny is up to her matchmaking ways again. When the youngest brother, Ben the reclusive artist, meets up with art gallery owner, Kathleen, his walls finally crumble, but not without prodding from his entire family and some pretty tasty baked goods. Sherryl once again pens a beautiful story of conquering fear to find true love. This character driven series is too good to miss. The dialogue is mature, loving and spectacular. I look forward to the final book regarding "Miss Matchmaker" herself , Aunt Destiny!
