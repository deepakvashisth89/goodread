I spent 6 days reading this 447 page book, and only made it to page 185. This is one of those extremely rare times that I've given up on a book, as it makes me feel guilty. I have *loved* the 3 previous Julian Kestrel Mysteries, but couldn't warm up to this one.
It began with the subject of opera (not a fan) and was based in Italy. There was a parade of characters that I found unlikeable, and when they were all rounded up and herded to the Villa to solve a 4-and-a-1/2-year-old murder, my patience started to wear thin. I threw up my hands and gave up at the point when the second (unlikely) person suspicious for the murder came onto the scene. It was too late in the game to hold my interest.
