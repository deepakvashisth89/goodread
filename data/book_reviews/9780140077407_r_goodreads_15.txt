This is a new series to me, one that I had seen read by others on Goodreads. Set in Ontario, Canada it is a story in a similar vein as Mickey Spillane or Philip Marlow, here he is called Benny Cooperman. Not such a strong character but a determined Private Investigator who follows up the supposed suicide of a local businessman and finds the inevitable "can of worms". Some violence but not gorry, some attractive women but no sex or heavy drinking. Sounds sanitised but it is not just a good days read on a cold wet day and not being able to get out or meet anyone due to the Covid restrictions.I'll make this a four-star as I did enjoy the book.
