“Suicide is a death like no other, and those who are left behind to struggle with it must confront a pain like no other”.This book told it all straight up and it widened my eyes and healed a little bit of my heart. The final line says “oh mother, mother, why did you do it?” Leaving the reader to understand that the author who has decades of research can’t fully make sense of this act and those left behind can’t either. The hardest part for me is the debilitating confusion that follows suicide and hearing this helped me not get so stuck on the why and find peace in that I will never fully understand the why.“You’d never be the same, but the world went on and you went with it. It was not mother only that died… the child in your heart died then, the bairn that believed the hills were made for its play, every road set fair with its warning post, hands ready to snatch you back from the brink of danger when the play grew over rough. That died, the quiet corpse of your childhood”Words do not begin to express the hope the knowledge of the plan of salvation brings me in a situation that feels so dark and hopeless. I’ve had so many sacred experiences where I know Lauren is watching over me and guiding me on my path. Lo I love ya you’re the bestest friend I’ve ever had and keep sending me sighs that you’re around ok?
