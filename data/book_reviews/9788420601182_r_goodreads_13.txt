I think I would enjoy Hoffman more if I knew an awful lot about the technical aspects of music and music theory, because he obviously did and so many of his stories divert into that subject providing a major distraction for someone like me.I bought this volume in order to get my hands on "The Sandman" which is in here but not identified in the table of contents in the Kindle edition for some reason.I feel HP Lovecraft is more assessable to the average modern reader, though Hoffman offers pre-Freudian psychology and plenty of allusions to music and very old 17th - 19th century European composers (most of whom are well footnoted in this Kindle edition) for those readers interested in this.
