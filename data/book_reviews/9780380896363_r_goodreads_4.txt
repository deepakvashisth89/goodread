Merle, aka Merlin, the son of Corwin, in this second book of the second series of the Amber Chronicles sees him trapped in the Crystal cave continuing from the last novel. He eventually escapes and through many travels and dangers he makes a pact with Luke to rescue his mother Jasra from the masked sorcerer making his life miserable. We end the book with a bizarre cliffhanger in which Merle pulled is trumped into the mad tea party by Luke along with the Hatter, Hare, Cheshire Cat and Humpty Dumpty.
