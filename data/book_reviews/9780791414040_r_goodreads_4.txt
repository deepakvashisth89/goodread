I deeply appreciate this as a feminist reconstruction of Buddhism, as someone who loves Buddhist teachings and beliefs. I think this is especially valuable as Buddhism is often idealized in the West as a religion without the problems (racism, sexism, violence) so often attached to Christianity.
However, I wish Gross would've made an effort to make her deconstruction more intersectional. I understand it was written in the 90's but it's almost humorous how much Gross avoids race and sexuality. Additionally, it's a little dense but what else can you expect from an academic text, I guess.
