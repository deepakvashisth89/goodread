I really like Laura Esquivel’s writing, andSwift as Desiredelivered.I enjoyed her fanciful descriptions of communications in different modes, particularly the excitement of telegraphs, the newest and fastest technology at the time.The story was lively, but with sadness through it too. I found it a wonderful reminder of how technology shifts and changes, and skills that were once so valued can become outdated and irrelevant.While it didn’t reach the heights ofLike Water for Chocolate, the author’s love of her characters, the place and the time really shone through.
