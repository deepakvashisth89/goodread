This was a fascinating, wonderful, absorbing, funny read. I can see (from reading my own review, not because I remember) that I had some trouble with a few of the essays inFlight of the Iguanabecause some of the data in them has since been discounted and the theories have been updated. However, I didn't find that with this book. Some of the storiesarea bit dated, but it didn't throw me as much. And there were some particularly evocative passages, particularly about the importance of water.I highly recommend this book for nature enthusiasts, science geeks, and anyone aspiring to be a science writer.
