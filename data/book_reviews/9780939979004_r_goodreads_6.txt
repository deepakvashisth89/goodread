Mommy's review from 9/30/11 -2.5 - Coopie? What kind of name is Coopie? (Anyone out there with any kids names Coopie sorry but it's just way out there.) Coopie.The entire time we were reading this, save for the first time the name is mentioned, we called the kid Poopie. It just seemed more.... fitting.So anyway, besides the Coopie/Poopie drama the rhymes are cute. This is along the same lines as the usual 'Mommy loves her baby so very much' books which make up some of my favorite children's books. This loses just because I think the name Coopie is stupid but it's a decent book.A little more writing than I expected which I liked and the illustrations are on par with my expectations. Coopie. Huh.
