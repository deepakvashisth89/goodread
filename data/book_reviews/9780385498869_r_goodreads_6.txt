I feel conflicted. On the one hand this book gave me a lot of think about and reflected issues I am currently wrestling with. I think the question of women's changing roles is an important one and the book really covered a good range of women and life choices. However, I also feel like much of Orenstein's biases and judgment came across at certain points. On top of that, some of her conclusions (or maybe observations is a better word?) seemed simplistic and possibly predetermined by her own feelings on the issues. I am curious for more information on what her sample size was and whether she has a beak down on the ages, races, socio economic backgrounds of the women she spoke with. Ok, now that I write this I realize that I wanted a lot more out of this book than it gave me. I think I wanted it to be more sociological (in that I wanted methodology and some statistical data) than journalistic. Certain issues really hit a nerve and made me angry with the women in the book and with the author. I really think that book casts too wide a net and comes up simplistic, with broad strokes rather than presenting anything earth shattering and profound.
