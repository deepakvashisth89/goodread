I had never heard of this author or series before. I picked it up randomly and decided to try it because I like mysteries and I thought I’d try a new author. It was an enjoyable read.Professor Ben Reese gets to England to begin his sabbatical, researching old coinage and authors. He get a 2 am call from his dear friend, head of the English Department, Richard West. He begins to give Ben a strange message, when the call is disconnected. Ben receives a telegram from his assistant that Professor West had died, apparently of a heart attack. Ben returns to Ohio for the funeral. Within a couple days time, an alcoholic professor is found dead, of an apparent overdose of sleeping pills and alcohol, a suicide attempt. Ben realizes they have been murdered when his own life is in danger. Ben decides he must find out who and why.
