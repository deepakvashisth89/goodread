The writer Elke Linda Buchholz’’s and Beate Zimmerman’s illustrated biography of Pablo Picasso was published in 1999. The book is a beautiful introduction to the “Life and Work” of Pablo Picasso. “Life and Work” is the book's subtitle. The book is part of the Art in Focus series. The book was translated from German into English by Christine Shuttleworth with the help of the firm named Goodfellow & Egan. Michelangelo lived between 1881 to 1973. Each of this short book's ten chapters begins with a timeline of events in Picasso's life and events in the World’s history during his lifetime. The book is only 91 pages, but the book covers a great deal of ground. The book does an excellent job of describing why Picasso’s work is significant. Picasso had a long, long artistic career covering many different genres. Picasso was a public artist from 1896 until his death (Buchholz, Zimmerman, & Shuttlework 10). The book discusses Picasso’s troubled relationship with his “Wives and Lovers” (Buchholz, Zimmerman, & Shuttlework 86-87). The book's audience is a general reader, so the book probably portrays Picasso’s relationship with women in a too positive light (Terol 2022). The book includes a glossary of terms used in the text. The book has large print text and is very readable.
