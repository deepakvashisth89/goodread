A Yule gift from a Santa who likes the naughty boys better, this book is quite the steamy bit of erotica, with some remarkably cross-the-spectrum variations of man-man eros.

The tales tackle most genres of erotica, from light to pretty heavy and/or dark, and various authors have strikingly different voices. Some are warm and snuggly, some make you wonder if leather worn that way wouldn't chafe, and some will make you never look at a motorcycle the same way again.

My friend gave me an Aragorn bookmark with this one. Can you say synergy?
