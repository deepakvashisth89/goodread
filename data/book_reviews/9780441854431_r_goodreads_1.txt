This is another fine volume in the Dozois & Dann-edited exclamation point series. It contains fifteen stories originally published from the early 1950s into the early '80s, as well as an excerpt from White's The Once and Future King which appeared in 1939. I especially liked Theodore Sturgeon's The Silken-Swift (which came from his collection E Pluribus Unicorn), Larry Niven's The Flight of the Horse, and Harlan Ellison's On the Downhill Side, but my favorite was Roger Zelazny's novelette, Unicorn Variation.
