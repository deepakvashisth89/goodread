Copyright 1994. This is the 3rd in the Dennehy Sisters series. This is Maggie's story, the second youngest.This is a reread for me. I had to buy my paperback used, cause it was out of print by the time I wanted to read it.The first 2 chapters is the same scene from Connor's & then Maggie's POV. Half the book is set in New York City & a series of coincidence (fate?) leads them to each other. The other half is in Colorado on a remote ranch which Connor calls home.The story is convoluted, & sometimes angsty. There's plenty of drama & the story kept me interested. JG is a talented author & has a great imagination.The HEA is satisfying. I liked this story sooo much better than the 2nd book!
