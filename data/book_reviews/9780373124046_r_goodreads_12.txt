i was wavering between a 3.5 and 3.75 stars but I think the final answer is 3.75 stars. Review to come...

So I kind of read through this fast. The story is that the h is engaged to another man but the H is absolutely smitten with her and does all that he can to get her – and of course protects her as he suspects her fiancé is a fortune hunter.

What I like:
1. Yay for an absolutely lovestruck, smitten, “I will have her no matter what” attitude H! And the usual Hot, Sexy, Controlling, Alpha an dall that.
2. Lots and lots of lovin’ and boiling hot!
3. The drama

What I didn’t like:
1. The h was a tad annoying. I mean talk about clueless and naïve! And stop wishy washy-ing! Go get him, he B sexaay!! (talking about the H of course)
2. I thought the drama of the h’s family history was a little confusing, or I don’t know, not really that developed.
3. Even though I do love all those love scenes after scene, for awhile there I was like when will it end, LOL! I wasn’t sure when the story/drama/conflict was going to continue/get resolved. Also, what, did h’s “friend” really had honest to goodness sincere feelings for the h – like please I still want to be your friend? Or was that all for show?? And 2nd, did the ex-fiance really develop sincere feelings in the end or was that still a trick??
4. I felt there were some skips/edits. I mean in the end when the h was sick – uhhh… those were the first signs of being pregnant right? Right?! Or was she just really honest to goodness ill??? But if she actually was pregnant, I was hoping ya know that whole cheesy – you are pregnant with my child? Yes! I love you blah blah blah :P

Final Thoughts: I think that’s the reason I wavered between the 3.50 and 3.75 stars. I’m leaning to the 3.75 stars because of the wonderful H. He was sexxxxyy, he knew what he wanted, he went after it, he protected her, and even though the h was hee hawing all over the place the H stood his ground and was persistent and he didn’t slink off to drink his sorrows and all that. I’m sure I will re-read this book again the future!
