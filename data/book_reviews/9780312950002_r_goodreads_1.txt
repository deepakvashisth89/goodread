A young boy is kidnapped from Atlanta and taken to a hovel of a house in Pennsylvania, where he meets another child who has been kidnapped. Together they try to escape from a disoriented Vietnam Vet out for revenge. He has lied and abused both of them and made them totally dependent on him. They are both terrified, but don’t know the extent of his madness. It’s up to rookie FBI agent, Kira Thomasian, to try to put the pieces of the puzzle together before it’s too late for the two kidnapped children. I thought the book had a great plot, was a good story, was well written and I liked all of the characters (except for Bull, the kidnapper, of course). I really liked the strong characters of the kidnapped boy, ten-year-old Jonathan Grady, and the strong female FBI agent, Kira Thomasian. What I was really impressed with was that after the end of the story, the author included statistics on missing children, how to get in touch with the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children and a list of rules to teach children how to protect themselves and tips on how we can all prevent kidnapping and abuse of our children.
