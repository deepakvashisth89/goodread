I loved Don Freeman books when I was really young (Dandelion, Corduroy, etc)- this one especially with its mostly pink and gold color palate (colors I ended up loving together most of my life, maybe because of this) and its cute be-yourself story.I cannot count how many times I checked this book out on the trips my dad took my sisters and me on to the library. Why it never got old to me, I couldn't tell you!About 15 years later, my sister gave me a little paperback edition to treasure and giggle about... and I do!
