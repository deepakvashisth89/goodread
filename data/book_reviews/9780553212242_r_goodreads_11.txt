If I love immersing myself in a classic, a novel that has survived more than two centuries of reading can't be bad. With Notre-Dame de Paris, this little theory has been confirmed once again.
Victor Hugo's most famous novel is fascinating. Magnificent, beautiful, and tragic at the same time, there is a power in the writing that captivated me. The timeless story revolves around Esmeralda, whose beauty alone will change the destinies of four men: the knight, the archdeacon, the poet, and the hunchback. These are four very different men madly in love with this young woman.
Victor Hugo also offers a very captivating immersion in medieval Paris: the descriptions of the city, the daily life of Parisians, and the vocabulary used. Thanks to a vibrant, detailed universe, everything teems with life and screams truth.
Notre-Dame de Paris is a relatively demanding read that requires complete dedication to appreciate it fully. But as long as you enjoy classics and hardbacks, you'll undoubtedly enjoy it.
