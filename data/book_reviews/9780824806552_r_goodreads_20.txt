A sad, tragic, realist story of Xiangzi, a tall, honest, serious country boy who works as a rickshaw puller in Beijing. Xiangzi’s ambition is to own his own rickshaw, which he estimates will take him three years to save for. Xiangzi is a hard , conscientious worker. Things do not go to plan.A memorable, interesting, thought provoking, somewhat pessimistic novel about the life of poor uneducated members of society.A very worthwhile reading experience.This book was first published in China in 1937.
