It could be because of the storytelling language. Because I’m not unfamiar with sci fi and space travelling. Although I’m talking about series/movies and informative books. Not sci fi books. This might be my very first one, to be honest. Which could explain it. Anyhow. It was definately not a bad story, I just stuggle to visualize everything.
