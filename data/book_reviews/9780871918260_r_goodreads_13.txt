Quote added 18 November 2024

It is wonderful to discover a new author, in this case Stephen Crane, well known for The Red Badge of Courage, but one I have not yet read. So The Open Boat had the benefit of being fresh and it turned out to be immensely satisfying, a story of courage, a story of quiet competence, skilled seamanship, teamwork and above all, restraint in the face of the likely prospect of drowning within sight of the shore.

The open boat of the title is a ten foot dinghy.






This is a great adventure story and it reminds me that the best sea stories come from the veterans of sail and steam like Melville, Conrad, London, Conan Doyle and Mark Twain, who have real experience of river, sea and ocean.
