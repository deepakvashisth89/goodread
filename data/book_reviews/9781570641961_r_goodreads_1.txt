Mark Twain'sThe Prince and the Pauperhas gotten the celluloid treatment several times; there was even aVeggieTalesepisode based on it. So, of course it would get aWishboneversion as well. If you've read any of the other novels featuring that literature-loving Jack Russell terrier, you know what to expect...and that's what you get here. For fans like me, this is a treat; the only problem I had was that the copy I read today, which I purchased secondhand from eBay, had coloring in it. Then again, as someone who regularly buys books used, it's nothing I haven't seen already.
