⛸️ What would be cooler than having all your canals freeze over so that you could skate for miles and miles without going in circles? That’s Han’s World and that’s how it was in the past - the 1600s, 1700s, 1800s, even 1900s. Now apparently the temperatures do not drop low enough for skating every year. The last time was 2012/13. So enjoy this story about a different time in Holland when there was lots of ice.A classic YA tale.
