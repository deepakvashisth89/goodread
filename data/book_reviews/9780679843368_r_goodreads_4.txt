My kids and I listened to The People Could Fly together. I was surprised by how much my kids enjoyed the stories. Two kids gave it five stars. The other kid gave it four stars, because some of the ghost stories were scary.

We talked about colloquialisms and how languages evolve. This book tied in nicely to our history lessons about enslaved people. We were able to imagine these stories being told at night when no other entertainment was available.

The book explains common motifs found in African American folklore. One thing I learned is how witches were used in stories as a way to control the environment. I’m glad we were introduced to the trickster Bruh Rabbit and the concept of Tar Baby.

Each story ends with a little explanation about the story. We all looked forward to explanation as much as the story.

I would highly recommend listening to audiobook while following along in the book to see the pictures.
