The author captures the dynamic between the regular residents and the seasonal folks in this small village of Waggs Neck Harbor reminiscent of places like Sag Harbor Long Island. One of the powerful women from a long established family is founder & chair of their Annual Literary Arts Symposium. The Symposium is focusing on feminist authors and it's not long before the rivalries of authors and the rivalries between members of the organization are at each other and bodies begin to appear.
