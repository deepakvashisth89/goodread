It's kind of like a mix between Gone With The Wind (or maybe more accurately, Scarlett) and Danielle Steel's The Ring. Sweeping, somewhat historical romance that spans a century of women in the same family. The very start was confusing and had me saying to myself that this was not a book I was going to like if I could'n't even make sense of the first sentence, but it improved rapidly and kept me up too late nights finishing it.
