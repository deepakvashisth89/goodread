The way I wanted to throw this book into a volcano.

I loved it but damn, it was such a frustrating read. My only critique is how deeply (and uncharacteristically) stupid everyone was. Maybe it was because the twist was revealed to the reader very early on, so you’re just along for the ride for SO long, and watching in horror as it all unfolds but MY GOD. Jim and especially Bones were especially pissing me off.

I loved the Spock whump though. Top tier stuff. Very heart wrenching. Also, JM Dillard’s writing is fantastically accessible and flows so nicely that I read this book in just two days.

Also there’s some really nice Spirk moments sprinkled throughout if you’re into that.

Highly recommend but you *will* be yelling into the pages many many MANY times.
