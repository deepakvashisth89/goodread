These books are braindead rare. Wish I could read these more often, but alas, I was able to snatch a copy of this one last month. Goblin kids’ horror is pretty rare as well so it’s nice to see some more additions to that niche. This was a pretty good volume from Are You Afraid of the Dark (I will refer to it as Dark going forward). Dr. Vink was present in this one which was nice as it further that connection to the television series, and he was a fun character. The characterization in general is good and the writers (plural since it was a pair) have good style and prose. The ending is good, the actual story is fine with some decent morale, and there’s some nice horror usage with the goblins. Lore is great, too; these goblins 999 millions years off from Scientology contracts (you NEED context). However, this book fails hard in pace and its underwhelming climax. Most, shit I’d argue EVERY Dark book I’ve read has had pacing issues, and this is no exception as it’s 5MPH until the final chapter where it shoots up to 20MPH. And the climax is pretty brief without any actual payoff to some early foreshadowing that I thought was gonna turn this into a Labyrinth reimagining, which I would’ve enjoyed. Things are resolved too quickly and I felt shortchanged of what turbulence the deadly diary in question could’ve caused had the story not been poorly paced. Overall, 7/10. It’s pretty good but falls short at the end and should’ve had more going on/been better paced in general or rather tighter. The deadly diary isn’t even deadly btw so one-out-of-ten unreadable.
