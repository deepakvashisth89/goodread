ECHOES OF EARTH is the first book in a science fiction trilogy (ORPHANS OF EARTH and HEIRS OF EARTH to follow). It is a worthy first step in a richly-imagined odyssey. The main protagonist, Peter Alander, is a flawed, space-traveling engram ... essentially an avatar of the original and equally-flawed human Peter Alander who remained on earth when the star survey began 100 years prior. Alander is likable and you root for him to succeed. The other supporting characters are, for the most part, an unlikable collection of AI intelligences, other engrams, and tech-enhanced/assisted human also about the mission, and alien intervenors--both of the positive and negative varieties. There is a lot of adventure in this book, a lot of speculative fiction, but it all fits together nicely. I'm looking forward to reading the other two installments of this space opera. Highly recommended for all fans of hardcore science fiction.
