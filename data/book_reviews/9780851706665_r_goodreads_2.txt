This is a collection of essays first published in 1978. This is an updated edition including essay updates and a new essay on more recent neo-noir.
I wanted to like this a lot more than I did. Most of the writing was very academic, viewing the subject from a professorial lecture taking the very soul out of film noir for me. How can writers make feminist thought, and film noir so dull?
One essay had thirty footnotes for a short essay, yikes! Also all the footnotes at the end of each essay were in 5 point type unreadable for me, even with a magnifier.

I did enjoy the Janey Place essay, for me the most approachable.
The book is a trade paperback. I found the film shots included were very low quality.
