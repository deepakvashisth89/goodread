One word: MASTERPIECE. Periodt.

I have no idea why I dropped this book the first time around. I must have hit a massive reading slump and lost my momentum, because reading it this time completely blew me away. I should have picked this up sooner! If you haven’t read the book or watched the movies yet, honestly, what are you even doing? Go read it, ASAP. This is high fantasy at its absolute peak. It is a sweeping epic and the foundational blueprint for all modern myth or fantasy making. To think Consider me officially converted- I am now one of on the planet.

Tolkien didn't just build a world but unearthed a history. Every ruin the travelers pass has a name, a poem and a tragedy attached to it. The depth of Middle-earth really felt limitless because Tolkien approached it as a philologist and mythologist. The cultures of the Hobbits, Elves, Dwarves and Men feel distinctly recognizable through their architecture, mannerisms and languages.



The brilliance of the Fellowship is in its vituoso pacing and shifting scale. It begins in the Shire, an idyllic, pastoral paradise filled with comfort, pipe-weed, and birthday parties. This cozy introduction basically grounds us and establishes exactly what is at stake. However, the story quickly shifts into a gripping psychological thriller and survival horror. As Frodo Baggins inherits the Ring, the cozy atmosphere evaporates, replaced by the terrifying, faceless threat of the Black Riders. The journey, from a localized escape, evolves into a grand, multi-cultural odyssey.

I am still completely astonished by Tolkien’s genius. What was his brain even made of? Where did he even get these ideas? Literally every fantasy book, game, or movie we enjoy today has taken inspiration from his work, either directly or indirectly. He didn't just write a story; he birthed an entire genre.

The Lord of the Rings.

After that 'what-the f' cliffhanger, I am on my way to grab the second book.
