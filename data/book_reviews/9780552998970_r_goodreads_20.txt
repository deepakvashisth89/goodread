It took me a while to read this book. It wasn’t gripping or exciting but it was thought provoking and thoroughly enjoyable. It was interesting the different relationships between all the characters. All very believable. Also, beautiful descriptions of cities in Spain. You could see, hear and smell the sights and sounds.Although I didn’t rush to read the next chapter, when I did pick the book up I enjoyed it and sort of sank into the characters and the settings. It was like a cosy, all encompassing, comfortable cushion.But just one point - what a grumpy, touchy, miserable, self opinionated, short tempered woman Frances was at the beginning. She was lucky to remain in this book… I would have knocked her block off right away!!!!
