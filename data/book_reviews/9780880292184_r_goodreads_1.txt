Not a BAD read but I had a lot of difficulty following this book. At first I thought it was because I read most of it gradually over 2+ months on my computer at work as my secondary book, but reading the last 1/3 to 1/2 of the book in the last couple weeks led to the same results.I'm not sure that I can pinpoint why, but I've noticed that with fiction and non-fiction, I often have trouble following longer works that are made up of shorter stories. Like "fix-up" novels for example and books like this one. When the people/characters/stories are changing every chapter or two, it's difficult for me to invest in the overall work.Still not a bad read, but in terms of aviation history, not my favorite!
