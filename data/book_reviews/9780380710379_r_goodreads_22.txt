A Tragic TaleA shocking case of child abuse and murder, "Beyond Obsession" is a well written page turner. I felt so sorry for Karin, having to live with a monster for a mother. The adults who saw the abuse did nothing, or if they did report it, Joyce talked her way out of it. The father was really at fault for abandoning his daughter to a mother he knew was not only physically, but emotionally abusing the child. Joyce was also a pathological liar, but nobody caught on to her many delusions. It's heartbreaking to think of children who have to suffer in situations such as this book describes. I don't condone what Denny Coleman did, he did end Karin's suffering.
