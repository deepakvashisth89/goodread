I'm going to have to savor the third book after this. I don't want to part with the storyline.It is bewildering to me that the Dancers at the End of Time series isn't more well-known in this day and age.I don't want to spoil a single thing, just read 'An Alien Heat', and then read this.If you're one of those people that doesn't get into sci-fi, but really got into the "Hitchhiker's Guide" series for the existential circus, and humor of it all, this is exactly the kind of sci-fi that you'll want to read.Just...read the whole series. I wish it were longer.
