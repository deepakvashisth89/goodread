I pretty much feel as if I killed a bunch of time reading this book.I had read it when it first came out as it was given to me as a gift. I thought I had liked it at the time once I got into it but maybe my memory is wrong. I really struggled getting through this book as the characters and plot were just so jumbled. Way to few details and changes in characters to keep them all straight.Also, having grown up only a half hour from St. Cloud I find it hard to believe that anyone who LIVED in that town wouldn't be able to remember if it was Sauk Rapids or Sauk Center that was nearby. Sauk Rapids basically abuts St. Cloud while Sauk Centre (real spelling) is 45 minutes away. And there are only three high schools so to not know that Cathedral was the private/Catholic school would be rather unlikely.And, the baby only weighed 6 lbs at the end of the book??Petty maybe, but I find these kinds of "errors" annoying.
