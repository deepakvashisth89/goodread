I cannot rate this story. I have read many Carole Mortimer but this one I kept having to check the cover that she had written it. Max was arrogant and angry and irritating. I couldn’t stand him. Sophie was her own mess. Back and forth emotions. The ending was lousy, everything tied up neatly in a bow, and Sophie motioning to Jennie to vacate the room so she and Max could be alone. Stopping myself from going any further. This book was a no for me and sadly wasted time in my life. And that’s my opinion only.
