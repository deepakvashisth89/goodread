X Wonderful title but not as good as later novelsBy Charles van Buren on March 13, 2018Format: Kindle Edition|Verified PurchaseThis, the first of Rex Stout's Nero Wolfe mysteries, is not the best. If you are trying the series for the first time, I recommend choosing a different book. This is not a series which has to be read in order to make sense.The Nero Wolfe in this novel is not as likeable as he becomes in later books. SPOILERS ahead. He essentially extorts $10,000 from the district attorney. The DA is a very unlikable character but I don't think the later Nero Wolfe would have done it. There is always a touch of arrogance about Wolfe but I do not recall him playing God in the other books. As brilliant as he is, he is not qualified for that job.
