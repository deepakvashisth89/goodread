While this book has many insights to offer first-time Youth Pastors and Ministers and church youth workers, most of this is common sense. In fact, many of these same themes and topics we should all be applying to our spiritual lives regardless of our age or demographic. My biggest qualm with this book was with Dunn's use of the term "Pacing." Besides simply being a really annoying term for "walking alongside the student while setting the standard," it seems woefully inadequate for the sort of ministry that we as Youth Pastors (etc) actually engage in with our students. If we need to "pace" with our students, what are the parents doing? What are the schools doing? And (more importantly) what are our church communities doing together as a whole? These are just a few of the questions that the book left me with.
