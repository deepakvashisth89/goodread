This book has a lot of good information for first time poodle owners. It covered all the basics as far as what to look for in a puppy, what one would need before bringing said puppy home, etc. The only real issue I had with this book was during grooming, it never mentioned plucking the poodle's ears. There is a brief sentence mentioned in the end section about medical care but since this is something most groomers do, I feel like this needed to be brought into more detail in the grooming section. Overall I would recommend as a first read for first time poodle owners, but definitely follow up with a more thorough book.
