Three "short" stories by Thompson.
"Manhunting in Montana" Photographer Cleo Griffen is famous for her hunky calendars. Frustrated, she decided to find a long-distance husband when she goes to Montana to do a cowboy calendar shoot. She doesn't count on falling in love and getting pregnant by rancher Tom McBride.
"Bachelor Father". Katherine Seymour takes a 3 day vacation from her NY publishing job in Yellowstone. When ranger Zeke Lonetree pulls her out of a river and they make love, she becomes pregnant. A year later she goes back to introduce him to their daughter and the 3 fall in love all over again, but need to work out the distance/job thing.
"Pure Temptation" is a steamy bedroom scene throughout - very sensuous. Tess Blakely has four big brothers and lives in small town AZ. She's 26, still a virgin and leaving for NYC in the fall to be a school counselor. Her summer project is to lose her virginity so she can better know the kids problems. She asks her best friend "Mac" MacDougal to help her - he eventually volunteers and they realize they've loved each other for years - finally.
