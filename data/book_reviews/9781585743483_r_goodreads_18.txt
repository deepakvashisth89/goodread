Sad tail of this boat. The author is definitely a submarine guy - his easy readable and very understandable explanations of “Navy speak” made this book that much more interesting. All the speculations and testimony lead to the conclusion that the boat sank due to failure in pipelines within the ship or that the reactor went into auto-shut down as a result of the sea water flooding the boat. Of interest is the fact that Adm. Rickover quickly distanced himself from the accident by immediately stating there was no way the nuclear reactor on the boat caused her to sink. The book does segue into a few short chapters of the history of underwater rescue and how the Navy increased its efforts in this field. Mentioned at the end is some information on the other boat death- the Scorpion as well as the myriad of lost Russian subs over the years. Overall a good book for submarine buffs or anyone interested in the “Silent Service”.
