First time reading this book, I'll admit that I was several years younger then I am now. Just having reread it I now have a different view of what I thought I knew.The storyline remained the same of course, and it is some what interesting but the writing now stricks me as unimpressive. Infact it seems a little elementary. The Character development isn't much better. There isn't much actually.It is a good read to get your mind to fly off to fantasy land, but mental stimulation beyond that... is not going to happen. Just takes one sitting of about two hours.
