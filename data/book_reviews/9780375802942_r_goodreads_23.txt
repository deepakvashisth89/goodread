This story is about a little girl who loves to help other people, but always finds herself getting in situations where she is scolded by her teacher or arguing with peers. In this story Junie has a new lunch box that she is very excited about. She can't contain her excitement and she keeps pulling it out in class and getting in trouble. When lunch finally comes around she wants all of her friends to sit with her and eat and talk about her new lunch box, but all of her friends were buying their lunch so she had to eat with someone she wasn't that good of friends with until her friends got back. She noticed that there was a cookie on her friend Herbert's tray, a sugar cookie, she hints that she wants some of it and finally just asks and he gives her half of it. She then explains that she loves cookies because Mrs. Gutzman always gave her an extra in kindergarten. Her friends tell her that Mrs. Gutzman is still working at school and they just saw her in the kitchen, she runs back and finds her and gives her a hug. Junie gets scolded by her teacher because she's not supposed to get out of her seat during lunch, but Mrs. gutzman gives her a permission slip to get signed by her parents so she can help out in the cafeteria. She asks her mom and dad and they finally say yes and sign it, and she helps the next day. Things didn't turn out the way Junie dreamed they would and she got sent back to class embarrassed about how the day went. But Mrs. Gutzman shows up and saves the day with cookies, and it makes it all better.


This is a great book for beginning readers! It's easy to read and will keep the reader engaged and prepare them for harder books. It's also the perfect size for easing the students into reading a chapter book from children's books.

This would be great in the classroom to help teach the children about routine and how to help others as well as providing examples of how to not be of help.
