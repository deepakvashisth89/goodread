The third adventure starring Dave Brandstetter, the openly gay death-claims insurance investigator from L.A., is entertaining and intellectually satisfying. The mystery itself isn't much--something about the murder of the owner of a gay bar and an outstanding claim of $25,000 (more than a hundred thousand bucks in today's money), but I read my mysteries for the characters, the atmosphere and the setting (particularly if the setting is a city), and "Troublemaker" didn't disappoint me in any of these areas.Besides, the prose is spare and elegant--as always--and Hansen give us a rare peek--rare at least as far as the mystery genre is concerned--into gay life in Southern California in the '70's and '80's from the standpoint, not of a young gay activist, but of a man in his fifties who has been living in that culture for more than a quarter of a century.
