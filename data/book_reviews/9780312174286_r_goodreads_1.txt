Invaluable resource for someone interested in the disaster. It's teeming with first-hand accounts of every aspect of the ship's voyage, including transcripts of some of the British Investigation that occurred afterwards. Lots of photos as well, and a lot of background information about Southampton, the ship companies as individual businesses (White Star, etc.) and as an industry, talking about the demand for large luxury liners and the rivalries between the companies. Very thorough, and serves to remind you that the disaster did not end on the 15th April, 1912.
