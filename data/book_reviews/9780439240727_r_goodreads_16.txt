This book does a good job of advancing Alex and Cam's relationship with, not only each other, but with the other people in their lives. Having said that, it falls flat in other areas. Thantos comes off melodramatic and the Coventry island council comes off as weak. They're the ruling body of witches and warlocks and they can't assert their power? The inter-connecting plot threads worked well together, but I think the authors dropped the ball on the plotof Alex's 'father'. It's mentioned briefly at the beginning of the story and then dropped at the end of the book. The authors could have made something into it. An overarching plot for several books.
