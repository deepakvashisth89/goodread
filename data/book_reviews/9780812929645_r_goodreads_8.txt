This is my first Trump book but I knew what I was getting into. It's basically a long book of Trump talking about himself and his accomplishments. He goes on and on about all the famous people he knows and the awards he won for his wonderful work.It is fascinating though. It's a good way to get inside of his head and see how he brought himself out of a low point in his life. It's written like a biography of sorts.This is Trump's version of all the stories that have been told about him in the media and in books. I took it as a way for him to record in print HIS version of the story and sets the record straight.I learned an interesting fact in this book. The Trump HATES handshakes! he's a huge germaphobe!
