I read this book because I guess I was curious about angels--and if they were among us. I believe the book could have been summed up in about 3 chapters...so it got a bit long in some parts. I also found myself having to refer to the bible a lot--as I do not have a lot of biblical knowledge and this book assumed one did. (It gave SEVERAL references to the bible throughout!) Overall, it wasn't bad. Interesting and thought-provoking for me.
