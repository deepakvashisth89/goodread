Medieval history professor, Letty Conroy meets Xavier Augustine when he comes to guest lecture. After a 3 month courtship they are planning a wedding when Letty discovers he has had her investigated and her whole life doesn't fill a one page report. She is indignant and breaks the engagement deciding to live a more exciting and eventful life. What follows is a spritely series of adventures and miscommunications leading eventually to a happy ending. Fun read. Old category romance.
