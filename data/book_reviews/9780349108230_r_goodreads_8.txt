This one was like a Richard Curtis film meets God. So I should have loved it. But it was a bit all over the place and just went on and on in the end. Plus there was one really really ick scene.Still there was a lot I did love. Gardam is so good with strong characterization in just a few brief lines. Great parts on old age and faith too.And there was a lot to unpack about love, disordered and rightly ordered, and about motherhood that I barely glanced the surface of. There's a character named Jocasta for goodness sakes.Not one I think I'll reread, but I'm determined to read everything Jane Gardam wrote.
