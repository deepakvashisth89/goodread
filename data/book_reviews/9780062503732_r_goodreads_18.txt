In my quest to learn about many different types of healing, I picked up this recommended book as a starting point on the study of shamanism. It was a good choice. Examples of students’ first shamanic journeys, coupled with some basic exercises for beginners and background information on shamanistic practices in other parts of the world, provided an interesting and informative introduction.As healing is fundamental to shamans, I would have liked to have learned more about the lives and experiences of well-known North American healers, however only one was mentioned in any detail. Still, there’s plenty of good information for those who are interested in learning about shamanism.
