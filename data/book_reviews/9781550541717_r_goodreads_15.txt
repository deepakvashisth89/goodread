Picked up on holiday in Canada (in translation). I would probably have enjoyed it more if I hadn't found Lady such a dislikeable and annoying character and at times quite unbelievable. Do people really behave like that? And I would like to have known why his friend was always referred to as "Purple". Was that something lost in translation? I also found it just a teensy bit pretentious.I would give more of her books a go though, if I can get them, as she writes well.
