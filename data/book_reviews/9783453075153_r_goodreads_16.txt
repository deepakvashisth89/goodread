The year is nineteen seventy something and Noel Holcroft, a New York City architect, is contacted by the Grande Banque de Geneve, concerning his father's will and testament. Noel, an American, is actually, the son of Heinrich Clausen, a dead Nazi High official. Noel was smuggled to the west along with his mother. According to the testament, Calusen and two of his high rank friends, after finding out about the Holocaust, funneled a vast fortune of money stollen from the third Reich into an account in Zurich. This money, amounting to hundreds of million U.S Dollars should be used by Noel and the descendants of the two other dead Nazi officials, to compensate Holocaust survivors and Holocaust victim's descendants.The terms are clear, Noel Holcroft has to locate and contact the children of the two other Nazi officials and together form a group to distribute the funds and alleviate some of the pain of the Holocaust.However, their are other players in the scene. The children of Projekt Sonnenkinder, the children of Germany's finest, Physically and psychologically, who were smuggled out and spread all over the world by U-boats and airplanes. These children (meanwhile grown up) were raised, provided for and trained as the seed for the Forth Reich and they have plans to use the money for their evil purpose of, and are willing to do anything, in order to restore Nazi Germany and conquer the world.There are other equally nasty groups involved, who want to use the money for their purposes.It is not clear to Noel how to find the other people and in addition, Noel does not know who to trust and who is secretly a member of a rival group. This leads to an action packed book full of espionage, counter espionage and allot of killing and betrayals.The book is not bad, was an interesting read, but somehow felt very out dated to me.
