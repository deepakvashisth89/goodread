This was the second novel by this author that I've read and I've already added another of his to my TBR pile. If anyone tried to explain to me what this was about, I probably would have thought, "Ah. Not my thing." Then I would have moved on to the next one. But I ended up liking this one a lot. It was well written and had a nice flow.I liked the characters. They seemed rooted to reality and they were true to themselves. I always like when the things we have been asked to believe about the characters are supported by the details of the story. This was a solid 4 stars for me.
