I truly can't understand why this book appears to be out of print. I blame it on poor marketing, because I believe the book itself is incredibly compelling and the story well-knitted.A book on King Arthur's reign from Guinevere's perspective, this story offers insight into who the mythic figures right-hand-women was, and how that influenced him. It also delves into religious beliefs common at the time, and how they play into the story. Love, loss, courage and humanity are all part of this story.The two sequels to this story, Queen of the Summer Stars and Guinevere: The Legend in Autumn, are also well worth your time.
