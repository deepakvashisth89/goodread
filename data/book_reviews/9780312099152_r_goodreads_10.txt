King is a good writer and treats this topic with great humor but this book gave me nightmares!!!!!
I kid you not. All the games that have to be played between men and women of ALL ages sent me into a tailspin in my dreams. She makes references to other books written about Southern life, like Gone with the Wind, and uses them as examples for what she is describing and she is spot on.
*Trigger Alert........the use of the word n***** gets used, which gave me a visceral reaction as well as nightmares.
