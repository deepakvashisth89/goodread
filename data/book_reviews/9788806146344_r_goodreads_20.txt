I was really unimpressed with this book. I think similar stories are probably scattered in notebooks of teens and young adults everywhere, stories about people just like them who took the dark aspects of life too far too soon, and that this author was just the one who bound it up in a readable package. I think we stopped just shy of getting to know the characters, and I felt like all the author wanted to do was write sexually "deviant' and sadistic scenes involving tweens and teenagers. I probably would have thought this was edgy when I was younger, but now it feels forced and makes the plot feel flimsy, like the book existed just for the sex scenes gone wrong. When explanations or descriptions were given they seemed lacking, like they didn't really tell me anything despite being well written, and the book is a quick read to begin with.
