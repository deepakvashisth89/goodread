A common sentiment whenever I’m reading a Francis Ray novel is “that woman can write some books”! This novel didn’t disappoint at all. It’s not often that I read Mrs. Ray’s but this novel is clean and with the steam turned on a low simmer.

This was the story of Maddison, Wes, Zach, and several other sub characters. Wes, the spoiled rich only child of his wealthy and socialite parents. He’s married to the beautiful and successful Maddison Reed, an Talk Show Host. Wes is wildly successful in his career as a news reporter, but he is not as financially successful in his career as Maddison. Their marriage is out of sorts and the couple is drifting further apart.

Regrettably, Wes experiences a life threatening crisis that results in a deep dark secret being revealed. How can Maddison deal with the devastating and heart wrenching blow from her husband. What will His elitist parent have to say and how will they react? How will Zach, Wes very best friend come out of this untouched? What will Maddison think when she finds out about Zach’s duplicity?

You just have to read this novel to get answers to these questions and to witness other characters find love right in the midst of Maddison’s life crisis.
