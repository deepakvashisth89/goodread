I love Stephen King, but somehow had never readFirestarterbefore now, probably because I saw the movie as a kid. It's got all the hallmarks of an early King novel: good, straightforward story and several well-developed characters, especially Charlie and her father.Firestarteris not as layered or nuanced as his later stories, but it's still good. If you've never read a Stephen King novel, this wouldn't be my first choice (that would be eitherThe ShiningorThe Stand). But if you're a fan that somehow skipped over this one like I did,Firestarteris worth the time.
