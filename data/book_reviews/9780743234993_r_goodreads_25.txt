I’ve read several well-regarded books on the Civil War. I thought it would be good to read a book that provides a well researched analysis of confederate perspectives. While this book is filled with primary research, the analysis is subjective. I question some of his conclusions and choices in the work. Davis’ affection for the confederates left me with the impression that he is either a confederate apologist or sympathizer. Either way, as someone who expects an academic historian to be objective, I was disappointed.
