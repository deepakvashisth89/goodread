Fantasy for High SchoolersWhen I was in high school, playing D&D regularly with a close group of friends, I enjoyed the Cleric Quintet (and Dragonlance…and Icewind Dale…). Now however, having lived a bit of life, it all rings a bit hollow. The motivations are messy and the relationships are contrived. By the midpoint of the series, Salvatore has made his protagonist vastly overpowered. It does not bode well for future volumes.
