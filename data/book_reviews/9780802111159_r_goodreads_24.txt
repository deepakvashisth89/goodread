4.5 / 5 I don't know why, but the first time i picked this up about 3 weeks ago I got bored after the third page. But then I read it again this week and I loved it from beginning to end (probably had to be in a certain mood to read it!).
It has the typical Townsend tone of voice and the phrases she uses in most of her other books (consumer durables haha), along with some colourful characters. This is must-read for any Townsend fan.
