Who do I need to call to get me my time back?

This book had a promising beginning then quickly annoyed me. The amount of grammatical errors and typos made this a harder read. This released edition seemed more like a first draft. This is my biggest gripe!

There was no need to belabor this story. The “big lie” was totally predictable. TOTALLY. So when it was finally revealed, after so many chapters trying to get you on the edge of your seat, it ended up being a complete let down. Then the ending was rushed. Unrealistic characters, most were under developed. There were more than enough chapters to build this into a touching story.
Love story/ lust story lacking all of the necessary feels.
Glad I thrifted this book. They can have it back.
