Tobas starts off at a low point of his life - and wanders around, hoping to get a better position. He felt like a joe everyman down on his luck - the only thing promising for him being his ability with one spell as a wizard.This is the second book in a series of standalone novels, and is set 200+ years after the first book. This book touches on the lack of a safety net available to Tobas - not that he complains about it.I left feeling that Tobas was just lucky - although his level head did seem to help. It's a fun read, but not as good as the first.2.5/5 stars
