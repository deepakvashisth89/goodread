This is my third attempt to read and finish; read this book through BorrowBox and really needed the time to digest and follow Shirley’s journey. I’m so glad I went back to it. Places so familiar but at a different time, much has changed but some things remain the same. The impact of settlement. Particularly in the period following WW2. The impact of weather, seasonal changes, on the lives of ordinary people. So descriptive and in places could be quite clinical in the telling of experiences that, as the reader, I found utterly heartbreaking. I could appreciate that Shirley divided her life into three distinct ways of being and yet each were inextricably linked.
