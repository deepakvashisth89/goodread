After having read a couple of the Meredith Gentry series I was not sure how the Anita Blake stories were going to play out, but I liked them alot better than I though and cannot wait to continue the series. This is actually and amnibus of the first three books so I am going to summerize each one on its own.Guilty Pleasures:Book one introduces us to Anita Black, she is an animator and a vampire hunter known as The Executioner. Vampires now have rights and people cannot just kill them, but have to get a warrant. There is a master vampire who wants to control Anita. So does the second most powereful vampire who gives her the first and second marks to making her his human services. What follows is both suspenseful and humorous.The Laughing Corpse:This book starts out with Anita being offered a million dollars to raise a zombie that is 300 years old. Anita refuses and the Harold Gaynor (the man who wanted the zombie raised) will stop at nothing to get Anita to agree to his plan. At the same time there is a renegade zombie on the loose and not only do the police (with Anitas help_ have to ind out who raised it they also have to stop it before more innocent people die.Circus of the Damned:After avoiding Jean-Claude as much as she can (for giving her the first 2 marks of a human servant) Anita needs his help in a murder investigation. There is a master vampire and his "crew" killing people. It turns out that this master vampire is actually being ruled over by the oldest vampire Anita has ever met. When Jean-Claude gives Anita the third mark she turns him over to the master vampire, Oliver, to get him killed not realising that Oliver has tricked her. The vampire who is ruled by Ol;iver makes him her servan by force in the bog battle in the end, but she kills him freeing erself of both Jean-Claude and the other vampire.All in all I really liked these books. Anita is 24 and sounds like a 24 year old isntead of an older person trying to sound like a 24 year old. Te books are suspenseful and humorous and the characters are likeable.
