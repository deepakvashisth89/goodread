I really loved The Snow Queen, and I have read The Summer Queen without reading this before, unknowingly reading them out of order. It was nice to read this and learn a bit more about the world of this series, but I would say this book doesn’t have the weight of the other two. It’s about BZ, a character from the first book, who leaves on a quest to find his brothers who are lost in World’s End, which is an area that is a bit of a mystery to most people. Many people go there seeking fortunes and never come back. I will say, I loved the mind-f*** nature of World’s End, especially Fire Lake. The effect it has on the characters is fascinating, and (no spoilers) I did love what the reveal is when I got there. I feel like the first half of the book is a lot of waiting though, very slow paced when the reader is anxious to have questions answered, and it frustrated me a bit. But once I hit the halfway mark I finished the rest of the book in one sitting, however, and the pace picked up a lot. It was a short read, only 280ish pages, and is more of a side story to The Snow Queen’s main plot, but if you liked BZ’s character and wanted to see more of him, you’re in luck! It wasn’t a bad book, but didn’t grip me as hard as the first book did.
