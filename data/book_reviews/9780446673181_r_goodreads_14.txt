A good follow-up to the first Cat Wizardry book. Again, a much more adult book than the Young Wizards series with talk of sex and more violence than you'd expect in the main series.This book handles loss and love and jealousy in a variety of relationships. I don't feel that the book had a real strong thesis and the ending seemed to lack the punch I've come to expect from Duane, but we really enjoyed reading this together.
