I get that this is a children's book, and I'm not the target audience, but I didn't find much in here to really appreciate. There wasn't really a through line for the story, and so the chapters feel like a jumbled mess of different ideas (some of which are really clever, and some of which really aren't).Maybe if I read the previous 4 books I'd get it better, but I was consistently underwhelmed the entire time I read this.
