A classic story retold for the younger readers, L. Frank Baum's Wizard of Oz is a fantastical story about friendship and courage. After a tornado at her aunt's house, Dorothy slips into a coma that takes her on the journey of a lifetime. Teaming up with a cast of unusual characters, Dorothy, the Tin Man, the Lion, and Scarecrow set off together in the land of oz to find the wizard.

This book is for upper elementary students, at a level 4 read.

Wizard of Oz could be integrated into lessons around season changes or weather patterns.
