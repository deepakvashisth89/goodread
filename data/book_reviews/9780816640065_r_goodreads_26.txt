Interesting book - even though I don't live in America I've witnessed a lot of the attitudes described here, especially in online discourse (also Europe is hardly the thoroughly enlightened place the author sometimes makes it out to be, so some of it sounded familiar anyway). It was interesting to read a thorough analysis. Still, the author does ramble a bit at times and seems to occasionally overshoot her goal (for instance, when she claims that the danger posed by pedophiles is thoroughly exaggerated - which is a reasonable position - but then goes on to imply that most of the child porn circulated online barely even involves full nudity, let alone violence, which conflicts pretty directly with various interviews I've seen of people whose job it is to moderate various online communities and delete the material in question). The later, more "practical" chapters were interesting, but sometimes felt like they ought to belong to a different book because the focus shifted so much.
