Thoroughly enjoyable, and God bless Mrs Pollifax for being a spunky, resourceful sexagenarian. She always wanted to become a spy for the CIA, finally gets her chance on a simple assignment, the whole thing goes awry, and she and another spy are kidnapped. Leave it to her to get them out of every jam that comes their way. Like I said, she's resourceful.I might be convinced at some point to read the other books in the series.
