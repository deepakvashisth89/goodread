My second Elizabath Daly mystery, and quite good. Henry Gamadge, the detective, is annoyingly free of faults, but sidesteps being an arrogant know-it-all with his convincing love and concern for friends and family. His desperate quest to solve a murder his wife might be convicted of gives the story an emotional hub most mysteries lack.The mystery is an appealing "impossible" crime that the reader has a very fair opportunity to solve (also unlike most mysteries).
