This one is much better than the third book. This one also brings back Shayleigh and Dorigen. I enjoyed both of them so much in the second book.Also it gets away from the horrible violence of Ghost. There is violence, but it is fantasy fighting, not sadistic.In book two, the elf prince tells Shayleigh that she is in love with Ivan. I cannot help wondering if that is going to happen. Maybe someday I'll find the fifth book.I really liked that Cadderly kept growing in his powers. It was if, he just needed to believe in himself and everything would be at his feet.The scenes with the dragon were pretty amazing. I love how Cadderly can use his powers almost like a Jedi.I enjoyed how the groups got seperated just like in Scooby-Doo..Danica with Cadderly and Shayleigh with the Bouldershoulder brothers. Which of course lead me back to wondering about Shayleigh and Ivan. What? I think it would be cute!
