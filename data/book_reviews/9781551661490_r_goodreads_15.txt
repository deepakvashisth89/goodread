“Diamond Girl” is the story of Kenna and Regan.
Kenna is in love with her boss Denny- who doesn’t notice her. Who does however notice as well as make her mad is his stepbrother Kenna, who decides she needs a makeover to ever attract Denny and get him out of a gold digger’s arms.
What begins is a strange fairy godfather relationship, filled with wit, humor and passion.
I really enjoyed the banter and evolution of feelings between the two.
