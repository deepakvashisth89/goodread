What a fantastic novel! They did call it War and Peace e for 20th century, and i agree ( I love War and Peace, read it twice).

Interesting, well written characters, English translation was despite some unsuitable idioms " shooting breeze"...
The description of Stalinism is shocking even for me, and I grew up in communist Czechoslovakis.
Aksjonov is a master, ,and I will read more of his books. I also want to read the memoir of his mother Yevgenia Ginzburg
