This book is a fantasy book that takes place during our timeline. The author is known for his Shannara series and this trilogy is how that world started. Usually I am not a fan of contemporary fantasy, but this is fantastic. This book is about a battle between good and evil. It is about how today's world is leading to its demise with people's destructive acts and can acts of kindness offset it. I enjoyed the strengths and flaws of the characters. The twists and turns of the plot kept me engrossed in the book and I kept on reading to find out what would happen next. Some of them you can guess but some took me by surprise.
