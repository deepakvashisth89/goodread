This book was so hilarious. I laughed so much.The h is best friends with the H’s sister. The H works in Africa and for the time being the h lives in the H’s house. Although the H comes back from Africa, the h continues to live there. So they live together in his house. They once had a one night stand 6 years before.The h thinks she wants to have a wild romance with a journalist who also works in Africa. The H knows the h finds that journalist amazing.One day the h sees a prize winning contest in a wedding magazine. The prize is two tickets to Africa for newly weds. The h pretends she is going to marry the H to win the tickets, so she can go to Africa to be with the journalist.This book was so enjoyable. I have been reading Harlequin novels for at least 35 years. I can’t remember ever laughing so much reading a Harlequin.The h and H are also very likable. For me that is a wonderful exception. Most of the times I just dislike the h because she is so stupid and naive. This h has a great sense of humour.There are no hot sex scenes in this novel. But it is so crazy and funny. Higly recommend.
