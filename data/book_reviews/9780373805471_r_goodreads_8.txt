⭐️ ⭐️ 4 Anguished Stars ⭐️ ⭐️

Lily meets, falls in love with and marries Ronan all within a couple months. She believes that she has found her soulmate. What she doesn't know is that Ronan has darker reasons for marrying her. And after their wedding night, he walks out on her. When she demands the reason why, he cruelly tells by her to ask her brother.

This is a story about misplaced revenge and the unexpected surprises that fate has in store. There is a lot of pain and angst in both sides. I wanted to hate Ronan for the things he did to Lily, but I couldn't. He has his own deep hurts that explains, if not excuses, his decisions and behavior.

I also wanted to strangle Lily's brother Davey. But he has demons that were so deep and dark that I wasn't surprised by the things that he did.

This was a heartbreaking story from the start, but one that is worth reading!
