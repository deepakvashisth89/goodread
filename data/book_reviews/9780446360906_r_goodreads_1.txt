Reread. Originally read a long time ago. Nice to take a walk back in time and revisit these characters and the high-end world of the book publishing industry. Original review below.


Anyone who knows me well knows I am not as much into romance and chick lit as other genres like Mysteries and Historical Fiction. But occasionally I will delve into the chick lit category. And I am glad I chose this book because it was good! An absolute four star read that really surprised me.


With a terrible name like this, you'd expect a terrible book. Not so. I actually really liked this book about two women trying to succeed in the book publishing industry. I think anyone interested in books about writers, publishing etc may want to give this good book with a terrible title a try!

The book revolves around Hallie and Olivia. They are both editors at one of the biggest book publishing companies in New York. Hallie is the daughter of Amos, one of the most famous writers in the world. Amos is intense and an ego maniac but is decent at heart.

In this book, Hallie finds out her husband is sleeping with Olivia who is also her best friend. That is not the whole focus of the book however. Hallie also wants to go solo and her dream is to open her own publishing company...not an easy thing to accomplish.

I found this to be a cut (ten cuts actually) above most chick lit and really well written.


I did learn so much about the book publishing industry through this book. It is about as cutthroat as it gets. It also reinforced why I dislike corporate America so much.

Wish they had shelved that awful title and given it a better title. But anyway, I really would have given this a 5 except for a few things.
