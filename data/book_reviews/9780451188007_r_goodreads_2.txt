Thank you, Lyuda, for lending me your copy ofAn Unsuitable Matchand, OLT, for your recommendation. The title complemented the plot: a spinster crossed paths with a former-soldier-now-Duke that suffered from bouts of melancholia. Alcohol dulled his emotional pain.Miss Serena Millbanks first metGuy Hawkhurstwhile searching for fish. Unwilling to return to a musty ballroom, she had wandered outside. She made her way to a fountain with a pond.”Where are you, pretty fellow?” she murmured softly, her nose within inches of the water.“I am here,” an amused voice answered from the shadows behind her.Initially using Serena’s body as a prop to hold himself up -he was drunk-, Hawkhurst took a few mild liberties and for that reason alone, Serena punched him in the nose. Call it what you will but kismet decided their fate.I enjoyed this Signet regency. All the elements were familiar but the MCs were appealing, the dialogue *sparkled* and their story moved along at a fast clip. The plot was nicely constructed but the real fun was watching Serena and Guy discover each other’s human frailties. With the exception of her violet eyes and her forthright manners, Guy had written her off as a plain Jane. Serena thought of him as a’fashionable fribble, a Town Beau frittering away his life’. Their tests of will and revelations made it an absorbing read.
