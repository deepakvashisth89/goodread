A 15-year old boy, the reader, is seduced by an older woman; their erotic affair-of-sorts is laid bare by a simplistic sparse storytelling technique. One day she disappears. Years later as a law student he comes across her, on trial, as an agent of the Holocaust!

In just 216 pages, from a darkly erotic coming-of-age through to the relationship power struggles to the shocking reality of the woman's past; to the momentous question she asked re: the Holocaust: "What would you do?" A book that maybe asks why more people didn't try to stop what happened; what would you do? What could you do? So much said in such a short time. An immense work, that takes the Holocaust novel to a place it had maybe feared to tread, and by a German writer! Four Stars 8 out of 12.
