I am writing a Hero Report on a person during the Civil War (a Hero Report is a report where you pick a person because s/he is your hero and you write about her life and say why s/he is your hero) So for my Hero Report I did Clara Barton. I was looking everywhere on the internet and found SOME information, but not a ton. Then I remembered I had this book: Clara Barton and the American Red Cross (Heros of America) that I read a few years back. I brought it down and kind of skimmed over it, read some important parts and this is a GREAT book! After you read it, you really feel like you actually know her. It's GREAT! It is in the context of an actual story, (like people talking and stuff like that) and it has A TON of information and I am LOVING it!!!!!!! It is an amazing story, Clara's life was an amazing story! I would seriously read it for fun! It is not boring at all!!!!
