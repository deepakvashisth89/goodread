Quite a fascinating read.The book explores the lore of the Holy Grail and its connection to Arthurian legend, Celtic myths and legends, and its role in the early Church. It explores the possibility that the Grail might have been the cup of Jesus during the Last Supper, a magical cauldron from Celtic legend, a treasure of the Cathars, or even its connection to Mary Magdalene. It provides a discourse analysis of Chrétien de Troyes's Le Conte du Graal, comparing it with archaeological evidence from the geographical sites the Grail and King Arthur were purported to have existed.
