Did not ultimately enjoy this book. Two story threads that overlap and eventually come together, but the ending was disappointing and quite unsatisfying.I normally read pretty quickly, but this one took a bit longer than normal and then the ending really squashed any enthusiasm for this book. I don't want or expect happy endings, but this one ended in what I felt was a very negative and cynical way that severely lessened any enjoyment I may have accumulated to that point.I won't rush to read other books by this author if this is his style.
