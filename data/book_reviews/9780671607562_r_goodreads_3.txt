Well written and over-all entertaining, but full of little irritations, mostly related to the Mary Sue-ish Saenz. Thought both Kirk and McCoy should have figured some things out about her as soon as I did, and was disappointed they didn't. Also couldn't believe that Kirk would tell high level Star Fleet secrets to McCoy and Spock right after being told their conversation was being monitored. Granted, it’s the Vulcans listening in, but, still...

OTOH, never expected or predicted one of the major plot twists, even though it was obvious in retrospect, which is always nice. Kept me involved enough my mind wasn't working that out, anyhow.
