Reading Cymbala is like reading a trustworthy sage. You can count on straight talk, genuine humility and an unabashedly passionate plea to rely on the Holy Spirit.This book feels like a combination of the writer’s sermons, real life ministry experiences and core beliefs. It deals with how we open up to the Spirit’s moving in our lives. There is a strong emphasis on prayer and applying the Word. It was refreshing as always to listen to his voice.
