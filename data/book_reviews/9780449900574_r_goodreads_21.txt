This man has lived a life I always dreamed about as a kid. Growing up loving nature, the outdoors and being influenced by old western movies, it always made me wonder what it would have been like.The good, the bad, and the ugly, he shares his stories from hunting buffalo with the tribe then to the devastating transition from a free-ranging tribe to the Piegans being controlled and mismanaged by the government officials.I appreciated his candor and writing-style. He shared what doing life with them looked like in all its beauty and horror.
