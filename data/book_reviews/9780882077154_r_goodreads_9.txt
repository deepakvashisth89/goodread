The book was selected for a small Women's Bible Study group at an interdenominational church that I have been attending. I thought it was timely with the release of the movie War Room, which also has to do with prayer. Effective, fervent prayer, I might add.Evelyn's book is great for beginners. It is simple and easy to read of twelve short chapters, and can truly help if the person/group puts their heart and truly desires to see that our prayers can move the hand of God and miracles happen. In our own lives and for those we are praying for.The book is a bit "dated" with reference from the 1960's, cassette tapes, etc.
