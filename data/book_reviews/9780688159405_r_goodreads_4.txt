This story about Cosmo living on Mars with his robot friend Rex really stayed with me. Even though the setting is futuristic, the heart of the book is about friendship, responsibility, and choosing kindness. What touched me most was the way Cosmo refuses to abandon Rex after the accident, even when fixing him seems impossible. The book shows that loyalty is not limited by what someone looks like or whether they're human. It comes from caring deeply about others. Children can see that true friendship means showing up when it matters, that bravery sometimes looks like staying calm and trying your best, and that problem-solving is an important life skill. This book stood out to me because it makes emotional lessons simple and relatable while still giving kids the excitement of a sci-fi adventure.The book uses imagery well, especially in showing the dusty, red Martian landscape and the glowing lights on Rex that slowly fade as he fails. These details make the story feel vivid and immediate. The author also uses pacing well, building suspense as Cosmo works against time to save his robot friend. There is also gentle personification throughout. Rex’s small movements and beeps give him personality and warmth, making readers care about his survival.From an anti-bias perspective, the book does a great job of teaching acceptance through a child-friendly sci-fi lens. It shows that friendship can cross any difference, even between a human and a robot. Cosmo never treats Rex as just a machine. He recognizes his value, listens to him, and shows empathy. This challenges stereotypes about who or what deserves care. The story also highlights inclusion and cooperation within Cosmo’s family, showing that everyone’s skills matter. It encourages readers to understand that every being, no matter how different, deserves kindness and respect.This book is a WOW Book because it takes a simple adventure on Mars and turns it into a powerful message about loyalty, courage, and seeing humanity in others, even those who aren’t human at all. It invites children to imagine new worlds while also thinking about what it means to be a good friend.
