DO NOT READ THIS BOOKAfter reading "Inside the Third Reich" and "Spandau: The Secret Diaries", this book seemed like the natural next step. I expected more of the same that the previously listed books had accustomed me to: intriguing examinations of different members of the Nazi elite and introspective looks at how morally gray man really is. This book provided neither. Instead, its pages are filled with a frustrating attempt by Albert Speer to shift the blame toward Himmler while still posing as an unbiased narrator, when that is clearly not the case.Even more pathetic is the fact that this entire act of innocence has long since been proven wrong. Speer absolutely knew about the Holocaust and may have even aided its progression, making this book not only a bore to read but also historically incorrect.If you are looking for a poorly written and poorly organized book that the author himself was dissatisfied with, this might be the book for you. If not, ignore this book and read "Albert Speer: His Battle with Truth" instead.
