I discovered the work of Diane Arbus many years ago and was drawn to her amazing black and white images. Diane Arbus' journey from a very wealthy NY family to a photographer known for her work on people on the margins of society is captured in this excellent biography. The biography captures the motives for her work, her desire to capture people from a broad spectrum of society and on its margins and the self doubt that followed her life despite her many accomplishments. The biography provides wonderful insights into her early family life, her marriage and children, her development as a photographer trying to navigate between the work that paid her bills and the work that she was passionate about. It is apparent that depression and her challenges with coping with mental struggles were never really far from Diane and it is sad to learn how things spiraled out of control in her final years. Diane Arbus was an amazing photographer and this biography provides a great account of her frenetic yet relatively short work life.
