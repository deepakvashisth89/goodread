This was a very good book for any Catholic or returning Catholic to read, it was very informational. The one problem I had was that I couldn't decide how to go about reading the book. Since the book gives a detailed explanation of why Catholic's worship the way they worship and then references The Cathechism of the Catholic Church and the bible, I didn't know wether to read it through then go back through the Bible and the Cathechism or research the Bible and the Cathechism at the same time. I eventually just ended up reading the whole thing through and now I'm going to go back through and read the Cathechism and Bible at the same time. This book is very informative, put's everything into layman's terms, and has great exercises at the end of each chapter that challange you and help you to reflect on what was just read.
