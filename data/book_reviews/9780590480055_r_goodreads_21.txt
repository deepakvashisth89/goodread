I loved this book as a child and was delighted to find that it is just as wonderful for an adult. I remember loving it for the blossoming resourcefulness of Betsy. As an adult, it reminded me of the wonderful feelings of first discovery and dawning independence. But oh, it does it in such a homey loving way that I’m sure any child would hardly know they are being encouraged to stand on their own two feet. It’s just a beautiful story.
I would recommend this little story to everyone I know.
