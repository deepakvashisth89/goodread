It's sort of as if Tom Clancy started writing cyberpunk. Zoom & enhance trope by the second page. The book exhibits a curious disregard for the killing of cops, federal agents, and military personnel, just reveling in the violence; along with that is a very clear sexist streak that starts with the objectification of women on the very first page. Fairly simple writing, no advanced literature here.This is a sequel to the author's 1989 work "Weapon" which I have not read; what needs to be recapped seems to be minimal and is done fairly decently. I feel no particular need or desire to read the first book at this point.
