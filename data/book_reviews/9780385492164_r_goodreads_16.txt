This one is considerably bleaker thanThe Color Master, but still has that dreamy, dark fairy tale vibe that drew me in so effectively in that collection.The reason why it didn't work so well for me is the fact that the symbolism is much more heavy-handed and clumsy, and the slight absurdity of magical realism feels less natural and smoothly blended in than it does in Aimee Bender's later work.In short, it's an extremely engaging and quite unsettling little treat, written with Bender's usual superlative skill with words, but not necessarily her best creation.
