This is a easy reader for the young reluctant child. My 7 year old Aspie son thinks cave-people are funny so we borrowed this one from the library. He enjoyed the illustrations but found the book way too boring. Nothing really happened and nothing was there to enjoy really. It's not educational in any way. Anyway, it was very easy to read for my 2nd grader and can be finished in about 10 minutes or so. Will we read it again? No thanks.
