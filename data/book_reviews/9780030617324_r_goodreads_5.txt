Another in the series of good not great early 80s Doonesbury collections. Roughly half the book is devoted to Duke's ventures into drug running with the Rusty Nail, which touches on Haitian refugees, the Falkland War, and James Watt's slash and burn approach to protecting wildlife reserves. There are bits on Kissinger's self-serving biography, Reagan's saber-rattling and Mike's younger brother going punk. The short sequence with the greatest long-term impact on the strip is Joanie's announcement to Rick that they're going to have a baby.
