This short story is a brief look into the lives of New Englanders. Yes, it's about Ophelia Todd and her desperate search for the shortest distance between two points, but it's also about the caretaker to her summer home, and the relationship between those that live in a place year round and those that simply vacation there. This is a story King revisits often. A story at which he excels.I am perpetually lost. I am never looking for a shortcut because I am lucky to simply get where I am going, much less shave time off the trip! 'Phelia Todd however, has never met a shortcut she didn't want to know better. Mrs. Todd not only believes you can find the shortest route between two points (as the crow flies), but also thinks it might just be possible to "fold the map" and get there even sooner! Oh, but only if this were so.
