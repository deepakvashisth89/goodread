I've always loved Zahn's Star Wars books but for some reason had never read any of his other stuff. So I picked up a big pile of his stuff from the used book store and am going through them one by one.Angelmass was quite a book!Pros:- Great writing!- Great pacing!- Very interesting deep dive on a subject I had never considered before.Cons:- Too long (but honestly went pretty quick)- The characters never felt fully fleshed out- Unrealistic reactions to huge events (although, that's kind of the point for some of the characters)All in all, this book went down super smooth. Even if it's "only" genre fluff, Zahn never phones it in and gives the reader a great treat.
