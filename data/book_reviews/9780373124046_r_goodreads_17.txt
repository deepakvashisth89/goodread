Francesca was in Italy to meet the last member of her mother’s family. After her mum ran off with an Englishman, she was disinherited from her family. When she dies, Francesca travelled to Italy to find her Italian roots.

While in Italy, she and her best friend, Sonya, become roomies, they get jobs and the same company as tour guides and bff introduces her to the man of her dreams.

She met and fell head over heels in love with Angelo. They were in love, and it didn’t take long before he proposed. In her state of perpetual bliss, she didn’t notice what was going on with her bff until it was thrown in her face on the night of her engagement party.

Carlo is “the” wealthy, powerful bachelor in Italy. Tall, dark and sexy, he see’s Francesca and decided he wants her. He’s there on the night of her engagement party and picks her up when her world seemingly fell apart and takes her away to his lakeside villa. That is where the story really starts and the secrets begin to unravel.

Lord, I loved Carlo. Snarky, arrogant, savage bastard won he over when he pulled up next to Francesca in his red sports car blasting Puccini and making improper advances. While he is all that is arrogance, he is also so caring with Francesca when she falls apart. And she did that a lot. That girl can weep!

Not that I blame her. Her bff is a cow. Her ex-fiancé is a pig and the wolf that dragged her off is an ass. She’s sweet and innocent with an easy trust in people who all had alternate reasons for wanting her in their lives. She just didn’t know it.

And then there’s Uncle Bruno. Smh! I couldn’t believe that he would be so unkind to his last living relative. In spite of the terms of the will, he did not have to be so damned cold to her.

I absolutely enjoyed devouring this book. Michelle. Reid is one of my favorite authors and I love finding random gems from her. Definitely worth reading again.
