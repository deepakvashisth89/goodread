Well I've rated Warhol's Prophecy 3 stars = "I liked it". BUT did I really?? The fact that I read it in a day and a half would indicate there was something about it that I liked but there was SO much about it that annoyed the crap out of me!The constant bickering about the affair got tired so quickly, yes of course resentment and anger would bring something like that up all the time in conversations, but having to read it on so many pages was draining.Adam's behaviour had a red flag on it from the first time he turned up at Hailey's house.The daughter Becky was 5 years old. 5. Yet here is a little girl whose parents take her to a rock concert/promo event thingy where the band playing is an obnoxious bunch of prats whose audience is obviously of a similar vein - wouldn't be taking a 5 year old to that! PLUS, this same 5 year old states to her father that a couple of her friends (remember...5 years old people) want her to get the bands autographs???? Yeah, really? Wouldn't they be more interested in Bananas In Pyjamas or Postman Pat or Makkapakka's autograph that some snotty nosed goose-stepping dorks?Ok, kudo's to the ending though - the last chapter was quite original.
