Riveting!I have always loved reading about the Romanovs and their history, and this book is no exception. The book posits the case that Alexander's untimely death was actually planned and faked by him because he did not want to be Tsar any longer. Feeling deep guilt over his complicity in his father, Tsar Paul's murder, he wanted to spend the rest of his life in peace, solitude and atonement. So another body was substituted, he possibly went to Palestine for a number of years and resurfaced later in Siberia as a starets or itinerant monk. He was so loved and revered in this guise locally that he was later made a saint by the church. While this has never been proved to everyone's satisfaction, the author makes a brilliant case. Only disinterment and DNA study will answer the riddle definitively.
