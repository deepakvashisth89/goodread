I received an advanced reader copy of this book in exchange for an honest review via netgalley and the publishers.

This is a beautifully illustrated book that has been re-illustrated and is being re-released this year.
The illustrations are so adorable and touching and the story is very encouraging for a child who has worries about death or loss or feelings of being alone. The only part I was unsure of was the ending as I felt as though it ended pretty abruptly and it felt like the parent was possibly going to die, but that may just be my interpretation of the ending.
My daughter has recently been asking about death and heaven so I will definitely be sharing this book with her.
