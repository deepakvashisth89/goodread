A pleasure to read this week, successfully distracting me from the awful flu I've caught.Also, I really should have read this right afterAgent of Change.When I started reading the Liaden books and foundthe author's advice about reading order, I decided to start with the Agent of Change "internal arc": Agent of Change, Carpe Diem, Plan B, I Dare. The 1st book was truly standalone, but in the 2nd suddenly Val Con's huge family showed up (it felt like they came out of nowhere) and played a major role. Then in the 3rd and 4th books, some of those extended relations had storyline conclusions which didn't move me as much as they seemed like they were supposed to, again because I didn't really know these people. But NOW, after reading Conflict of Honors (which takes place before Agent of Change), I have a much clearer sense of Shan's ship and crew, and their relatives on Liad, and who they are & why it matters how their stories resolve.I think I might revert to reading the books in the order they were written, instead of trying to follow individual "internal arcs" when they're all so deeply interconnected after all.
