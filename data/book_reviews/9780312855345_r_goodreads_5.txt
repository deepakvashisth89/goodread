Light-speed crawls where thought would leap.A science fiction/fantasy set mostly in this solar system. Published 1994, yet as usual Anderson’s work stands the test of time better than many more recent offerings.The zeroth law of thermodynamics says that everything takes longer and costs more.Events follow two complex plot threads before and after the action reported in Harvest of Stars. To add confusion, if not fun, each thread follows multiple characters whose viewpoint differs greatly from one another. Characters are well developed; motives clearly cross.“I doubt there ever was or ever will be a war that was worth what it cost, when you figure in the costs to everybody concerned, including generations unborn.”Language and specie conventions follow Harvest of Stars. So readers should read that work first, though Anderson explains well enough this can be read alone.“To be exact, I have a bare smidgen more faith in the supernatural than I do in the wisdom and beneficence of governments.”
