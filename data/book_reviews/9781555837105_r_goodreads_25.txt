The third Dave Brandstetter mystery marked my switch from e-books to actual physical version - a beautiful, brand-new print from Syndicate Books. So naturally, first I'm going to marvel at the design of these. The covers might look simplistic at first, but it's done in a style I find quite agreeable, so for me, they're joy to look at. Not to mention the colour scheme, which, frankly, took me by surprise at first, until I put the first 6 books side to side and the revelation hit. Hard. I don't think I can express how much I love the idea behind.Seriously. Marvel:The reprint of all 12 Brandstetter mysteries should have come out by the end of 2022, but the last half of the series got delayed, so I can only hope the publishing house would indeed manage to print them in their entirety. I need the set on my bookshelf.As to the story itself, I was not disappointed.I quite like Hansen's style - there's certain rugged charm in it, in the descriptions of the environment and characters, in thefeelof it all. It has a mild noir vibe, it has a tongue in cheek observations that put a smile on my face and amused huff out of my lungs, it has a good plot that leaves me guessing - not a straightforward investigation nor dark and grim pseudo-psychological thriller, oh no. There's red herrings, there's hunches that might prove to be wrong, and there's a cast of characters whose numbers might be, at times, confusing. The readers get to see something from Dave's private life, not enough to distract from the actual investigation, just enough to realise that there's a depth in this character that we've yet to see.And I am looking forward to reading about in in the next book.
