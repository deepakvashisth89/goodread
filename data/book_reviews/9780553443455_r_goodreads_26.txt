Kay Hooper wrote this in 1994 the last romance, the last Loveswept. I am happy she wrote it and happy I found it all these years later. I have read most if not all books written by Hooper. This story touched my heart. We have a woman looking to hide away in a isolated country cottage. A landlord resting up after an accident that left him broken, Then there is the black cat coming to heal or to keep track of the ghostly visitor. enjoy.
