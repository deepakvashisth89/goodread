I’ve been wanting to learn more about figures associated with the Civil Rights Movement. It’s interesting to me that Nina never originally considered herself part of the movement, especially noting how much of an impact her music made around the world. It was also interesting seeing Nina as a morally gray person. She was very honest about her shortcomings in her life, career, relationships, and parenting. It was cool to see the full perspective of who she was and not just an overly glazed hyped-up version.Overall, this was a very insightful autobiography. I give it 3 stars because it wasn’t exquisitely well written and didn’t flow the best.
