I think myself and Linda Howard are on a break! Its not you its me kind of thing. This is maybe my second or third book by LH and the repetitive writing type does nothing for me. All the talk of Loins does my nut. The thing is I really like how she focuses solely on the romantic relationship between the MC's but its all a bit cheesy and template like for my taste. They loved each secretly for years and pretended they were different personalities to keep each other at bay...nobody like them in bed etc.He would make her his woman and she thought that he would dump her as soon as he was sick of her...Its all over the top stuff...She is poor and weak and he is big and successful and rescues her.It should tick all the boxes but alas it doesn't.
