This is a famously compelling space opera by the screen writer of Star Trek'sThe Trouble with Tribbles.The basic plot, the adventures of a space ship and crew in an interstellar war, is standard fare with interesting variations and strategic discussions.But, the characters really stand out and do what so many humans have trouble with, they actually grow throughout the 4 books. And, their growth is verifiably and realistically due to their life experiences.It is what Star Trek could have, should have been.
