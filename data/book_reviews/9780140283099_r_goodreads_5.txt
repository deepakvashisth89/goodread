My problem with the book was that it seemed to try too hard to be a political statement without actually making a statement at all. Other than homosexuality, there was nothing that binds the book together, not even love, because some stories are not even aboutlove, but were about possession, and there were characters whose behaviour I despised, but I felt like I had to condone it.The writing itself was ordinary, striving more towards a voice rather than any real literary quality. Some of the works were quite good, others quite a bore.
