This hand-lettered and illustrated cookbook is a treasure! I devoured it in a day and can't wait to incorporate some of the recipes into the rotation. The recipes surprisingly age pretty well despite the book being 50 years old.

Pros: Whimsical art, witty writing, and creative and wholesome vegetarian recipes. This book is so unique!

Cons: The use of the word "exotic" is a little cringey. Also, I just don't think any of us need this much cottage cheese, bananas, or raisins. I'll be making some modifications.
