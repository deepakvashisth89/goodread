The premise of this baby name book is interesting, and it has a good index. However, it suffers from irritating accuracy errors and could have used a good fact-checker before it went to print. Errors were especially likely to occur in entries that had anything to do with the fantasy genre. For a particularly egregious example, look up the name "Edmund" in this book: the character Edmund Pevensie from "The Chronicles of Narnia" is confused with another (Eustace Clarence Scrubb) and given the wrong and, additionally, misspelled last name (Scrubbs), and the quote that is provided from one of the books (it is from the opening line of "The Voyage of the Dawn Treader," no less) is inaccurately worded. "The Literary Baby Name Book" also could have benefitted from being longer and including a wider variety of examples of famous books for most post-Medieval character listings.
