I also remember when this crime made national news. I worked in the mountains of N. Idaho and ran into several characters like the Nichols who dropped out of society and lived on the land. Some were scary. This book does a good job of explaining how they managed to escape capture for so long and also the dedication and sacrifice of all the law enforcement officers who relentlessly pursued them, especially the Sheriff. It was sad that not everyone understood that at the time and they were criticized. Hunting skilled survivalists in the rugged mountains is a daunting and dangerous task. I really enjoyed the book and it kept me on the edge of my seat.
