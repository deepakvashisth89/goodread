Wicked from the Fear Street Seinor series is a book I was not looking g forward to reading ,but I decided to knock it out.I've only got two more in the series after this and I'm done.This story follows Marla Newman.She is getting ready for a debate today for valedictorian.She ends up bumping into a girl named Clarissa and she spills tea all over her sweater staining it.Clarissa apologizes but Marla wants none of it.She focuses her anger on Clarissa and she falls but claims she was pushed.Two girls end up talking to her,Elena and Roxanne are sophomores and they tell Marla she has the power.Marla has no idea what they are talking about but they want her to join them.She brushes them off and goes on with her buisness.At the speach thing Marla ends up not being able to talk,and ends up burping and the whole school laughs at her.The next day she goes to class,but agian people are laughing at her.She is in homeroom when they play a full recording of her burping in front of the class.Marla gets mad and makes a list of people that made her angry.She runs into the girls agian and they talk her into getting revenge.She first goes after the French teacher I think and make his teeth fall out in the middle of class.She then goes after Josie.Marla tries to keep things rather calm as she makes her clothes rip,but the two witchy girls have other ideas.They focus their energy and make Josie fly through a glass door.Marla is having second thoughts and wants out,but the girls explain that once you sign the pact it's blood for blood you have to die.That's where I am going to leave this one.I'll be happy to tell you all reading this that we have finally gotten another good Fear Street Seinors book.This one was really fun.I liked how crazy and dark it got.There are two scenes I really wanted to mention.One is where they mention their previous third party who have never found their head and the other is where Marla is trapped with trees surrounding her and one of the evil girls says they are going to cut her heart out and eat it.The climax in this one was also good and had a pretty cute romantic side to the story.I give Wicked a four our of five stars.
