I've had this classic on my bookshelf forever and finally got around to reading it. For some reason I always had the idea that it was an exciting story about a woman who was a criminal mastermind - maybe she dressed up as a man and led a merry gang that robbed stagecoaches or something. I sure wish I had read that book, because this one was SO boring.Petty theft is just not that interesting. How fun is it to read about a woman stealing a bolt of cloth, a piece of lace, or whatever from a shop; or pinching someone’s watch? Not much, really. I guess it is possible that 300 years ago, people found this to be fascinating, because the scenario is described with little variation over and over and over again.A few other things happened to Moll. She was involved with many different men, and frequently married them without worrying about divorcing the previous husband. There was that whole thing about accidentally marrying her brother and having kids with him. But even that was not as interesting as it sounds.I resent every minute that I wasted on this book. I really wonder why it is considered a classic and I recommend it to no one.
