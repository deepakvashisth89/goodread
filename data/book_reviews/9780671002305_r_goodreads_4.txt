I have always been a fan of British History. From The Tudors, then the Stuarts and now the Windsor's. I was surprised to read of Victoria's children and the lives they led. Without the press hounding every move they made, they led lives that were just as bad as today's royals. It seem there was always this idea that royalty was more moral than the common man. To know that several of the children had numerous affairs, had drinking problems and parent issues helps explain some of the problems of the current group of Windsor's.

How it seems that none of the Prince of Wales were given anything to prepare them for the kingship. How these royal children did not know how to give or receive love or even how to function in the real world. No wonder they are all so unhappy.

This book ends with the divorce of Charles and Diana. But the drama never ended. From her tragic death, to Charles finally marrying his mistress to William and his Kate just wanting to live a normal life.

I don't think being born royal and having all that money and prestige is worth the unhappiness they are living.
