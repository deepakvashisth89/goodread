Really enjoyed the first half, with its underground arts cinemas and impassioned cineastes and crazily intellectual film critics. Was less enthused when the mysterious purpose of Max Castle's occult film techniques began to be explained and those pesky Cathars turned up with a Templar or two in tow. I was hoping for something stranger than the usual suspects. Also marked down for the characterisation of Texas Chainsaw Massacre as trashy video nasty. One of the greatest films ever made, actually.
