This follow-up to Turow's popularPresumed Innocentwas a disappointment for me. I thought INNOCENT was a riveting read, and I even viewed the movie, which I thought was quite good before starting this book. I could find no connection to the previous book other than location. The legal case seemed secondary to Sandy Stern's sexual life following his wife's suicide. Toward the end of the book, I began to get more involved in it. I have the next in the series available, but not sure I will try it.
