Witness the recrudescence of Cugel the Clever: confidence man, opportunist, and eternal butt of cosmic justice. His flagitious stratagems—embarked upon with supreme self-assurance—collapse in a sequence of humiliations so inventive that one can only admire the artistry of his downfall. Yet through every reversal, one watches, half-appalled and half-delighted, as a rogue of limitless self-regard stumbles across the Dying Earth, forever plotting, forever failing, and forever plotting again...
