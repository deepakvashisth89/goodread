When I was at University, quite a while ago sadly, they held a little book sale now and then in one of the buildings; just a few tables with boxes full of second hand books to peruse and I saw this and had a flick through it. I was surprised at first as I only knew of Steve Martin as an actor and so I immediately decided I'd buy it, as I was intrigued to find out what his writing was like and so quickly caught a bus back to my hall of residence and began reading...It only took a few pages to know I'd really enjoy this little collection of humourous essays and I immediately warmed to his irreverent light tone and breezy style. Each little piece is pretty short and totally varied in subject matter and I think they were first published as separate pieces in some American newspaper, but they work as a collection very nicely and flow from one to the next seamlessly. Some brought a smile and a few made me chuckle and he does have his own peculiar little brand of humour that I really felt akin to in some way- quite wry and a little dry maybe and sometimes slightly surreal as well in places and it just feels very Steve Martin, which is what you essentially want of course...I think this would be a great reading choice if you feel a bit blue and need a cheer up, as it has that great comfort factor to it, as well as being amusing and almost has a sunny, warm, Los Angeles glow, emanating from the pages...lovely stuff!
