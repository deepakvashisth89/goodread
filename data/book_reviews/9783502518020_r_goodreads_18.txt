Agatha Christie just scratches a particular itch for me. I grew up reading her books--I think I read the first around eleven or twelve--and I have found in my old age that I love the balance of 'sleepy' villages and evil deeds. It's a theme she returns to again and again with Miss Marple, she of the wooly knitting and village comparisons. In this case, the scatter-brained Dolly enlists her help solving the mystery of the dead body that appeared in the library, because as chaotic as Dolly seems, she knows the ways village opinion will end up ruining her husband's life. Is it my favorite? No, not really. But it is decently done.
