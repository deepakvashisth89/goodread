I gave this book 4 stars when I read it years ago. Now I have reread it, and I have two comments along with my 5-star rating. First comment: how could I not have any recollection at all of having read it before, even if it may have been ten years ago? Second comment: few books have events in them that give me a thrill of surprise. This book did that to me. At least once.This book was written before the formulaic cozy mysteries and is a little “meatier” than a cozy. I highly recommend it for those who want the occasional meat in their diet.
