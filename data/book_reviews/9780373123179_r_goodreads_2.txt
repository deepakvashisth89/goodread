Utterly crap title for a really cut above the average HP. This one did not feel like a cookie cutter romance. The two leads were interesting characters with believable back stories. He's a much older - 38 - widowed (effectively divorced) paediatric consultant (cool, reserved, quite dommy but not a bastard, sexually switched on) called Sinclair 'Sinc' (I know) and she's a gamine 23 yr old virgin rock star from a dysfunctional background with a (TW) history of abuse called Tiree (I know). His stepson Kit was in her band when he died in an accident. He thinks the Coroner's court didn't get the full story out of her. He also has a 12 year old daughter Eloise that keeps running away from boarding school.
By any measure these two should be chalk and cheese, and they are, yet something clicks and their sexual chemistry is real.
The dialogue is bold and frank.The tone of this was quite unusual for category. It was sort of modern and wry whilst still having an old school vibe. I thought it was very well done. It's a probable reread.
