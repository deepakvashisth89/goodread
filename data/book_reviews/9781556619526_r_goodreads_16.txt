I love reading Janette Oke's books...When Calls the Heart, Once upon a Summer, Love Comes Softly...all the series that go with those books.I loved following the story of Marty & Clark and then their children and now on to grand children!The Tender Years is a simple but very pleasant read, I loved it so much I read it in 2 1/2 hours. I couldn't stop reading and it had been so long since I've picked up a story that gave a glimpse into Marty & Clark's lives and their family. Looking forward to finding the other books in this series.
