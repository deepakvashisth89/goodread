One of Linda Greenlaw's earlier books about stories she heard over one long night in a bar. A collection of the greatest hits of fisherman tales told over much liquor. She begins by meeting her best friend and former boss Alden. Alden is an old guy of 65, Linda might be in her 40's somewhere. She is increasingly concerned about his health as he is apparently quite overweight and single and doesn't really ever take good care of his eating and drinking habits. Picturing Alden one sees a fairly unkempt and grizzly kind of old fisherman with a red face, whiskers and flannel shirts / tshirt that have all seen better days.They are joined by other fishermen throughout the night in a story fest of "I can top that dangerous or awe inspiring or shocking story" involving boats that are not seaworthy or having mechanical failure. Fish that fail to show up, or show up aplenty.It's a good book, though not my favorite book of Greenlaw's. She has plenty of writing talent and crafting of stories so I will generally read all her books as providing education and entertainment always.
