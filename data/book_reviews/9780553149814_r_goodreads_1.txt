2026 reread:
decided to reread this one in advance of watching its adaptation on the Poirot tv show (the one starring David Suchet), which I've been very slowly making my way through. the plot is as tight and as clever as I remember it being, the identity of the murderer(s) just as surprising. not as great as some of her later books, but still a good one. what stood out to me this time around was the delightfully comedic conversations between Poirot (passive aggressive king) and Hastings (slightly retarded). so wonderfully humorous. both have always been very charming characters, but in their first adventure together, the banter really shines. I laughed out loud many times. Hastings is such a clueless sweetheart; Poirot is so deviously insulting. Love these two together.
