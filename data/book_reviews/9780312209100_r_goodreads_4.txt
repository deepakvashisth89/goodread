My favorite novel from Laura Crum yet, Slickrock blends action, adventure, stunning scenery, accurate and interesting animal elements, and that old keystone, romance. Set in the beautiful Sierra Nevada mountains, protagonist Gail is out for the adventure of a lifetime on a solo horse packing trip that doesn't go quite according to plan. I tore through this book in no time and am intrigued to see what happens to Gail McCarthy next!
