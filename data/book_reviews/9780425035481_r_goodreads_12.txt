Yeah It was pretty fun book. I found it to be an enjoyable post apocalyptic novel. I'd recommend this to any hippie or stoner as there's a lot in this book about weed, love and meditation. It was so clearly written in the early 70s. It was nothing special, kindy pulpy, but the prose was so utterly buttery smooth. I enjoyed the concept of humans becoming so incredibly sensory, that it destroys civilization, and the society they build after the collapse was very interesting, remind me alot of zombie apocalypse societies. The ending kinda fell off imo. It did that thing where everyone gets along and the day is saved, where I woul have liekd to have seen more conflict.
