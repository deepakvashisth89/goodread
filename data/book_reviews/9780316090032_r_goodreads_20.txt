Like the first one, this was a fun book...probably much more fun for someone who likes (or at least understands) cats than someone who's clueless in that regard. Amory has a "stuffy" sort of writing style that has grown on me; I found myself smiling quite a bit at the way he expresses certain things, expecially when he's trying really hard not to admit that Polar Bear pretty much runs Amory and his household.I will read the last book in this triology ("The Best Cat Ever") one of these days and hope it's as enjoyable as this one was.
