Well, this took forever and a day to read, because every time I got going, one of the protagonists (and our occasional narrator), Karin, would do something humiliating to herself to attract the attention of some random dude. And I would be so embarrassed for her that I had to stop reading for a few days or week.Other than that.. I don't know. Ullmann maybe tries a little too hard, at times? The stone metaphor, for example, maybe it goes on a tad too long? Still, the writing is interesting, and I am a sucker for this type of "families beawful" story, so three stars it is.
