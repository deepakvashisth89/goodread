I am a little sad about how long it took me to read this book.
Let it be said--the Bennington stuff only lasts for one chapter. And it mostly a reflection on how people went a little crazy, and she just didn't and that was okay. Most of the book was about her being a young poet and person in New York City in the late 60s, doing some unwise things, being timid about some things, and learning to use her sense of wonder to write good poetry. I liked learning about how poetry has to be both art and craft, and that sometimes you have to be patient with it. Which maybe is self-evident, but I need to hear it a lot. Very good, and fresh and almost upbeat, Kathleen. I learn a lot from you.
