This book was so good! I really couldn't put it down. It's about the Cherokee Indian tribe in the 1830's and how they were forced out of their homes in Georgia and moved west by the American's - a part of history I know little about. It was really interesting!My only criticisms are that it ended rather abruptly and left a few things unresolved in my opinion. Also, there are two pretty descriptive sex scenes that have nothing to do with the story so I would recommend just skimming that part, unless your in to that..
