I read this book at the age of 14 and still regret ever reading it. The book terrified me, and I will probably carry the images and details from the book with me for the rest of my life. I did not only have nighmares, but I also remeber continuosly watching the sky during dayime in fear of a nuclear cloud.I understand what Gudrun Pausewang wants to accomplish and I symphatize with her idea. I still feel that this books contains far too horrible details to be read by sensitive young teenagers already suffering from weltschmertz.
