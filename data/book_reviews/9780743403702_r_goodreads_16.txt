So as a teen I had loved Spock's World by Diane Duane which breaks down the history of Vulcan. I had no idea until I started this that she's also done the same thing for the Romulans! I really enoyed the world building in this. Also I loved the woman spy who had made a home for herself amongst the Romulans. I would have preferred her to have been Vulcan instead of human but she was still awesome. McCoy was pretty great too. Having a mission on his own and seeing him in a very different way to how he ususally gets portrayed. Really enjoyed this one. And will definitely read the other Rihannsu books!
