Nice read. This is the third book in this series. The youngest brother in the family has isolated himself since his girlfriend died in a car wreck driving away from his house. Auntie, who raised the brothers after their parents died, thinks it's time he got over it, so she aims an art dealer at him. He's an artist who paints only for himself. So there's a lot of back and forth, teasing, and getting to know each other. It's a good story. I liked it.
