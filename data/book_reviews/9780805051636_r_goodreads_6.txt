I am not even sure why I picked this book up but I am glad I did. The story is told through letters and diary entries written to or by Tig, the main character. Tig is in her 60s and is on her last not-quite-political endeavor. So much is revealed through the letters written to her husband, Marz, her granddaughter, Hope, her grandson, Ben, as well as her two daughters, Solidarity and Justice. I became nostalgic for letter writing, the act of sitting and writing a letter or finding a letter in a mailbox. Email is so prevalent, FaceBook is an easy way to keep tabs on family and friends. The day of the letter, snail-mail, is fast becoming obsolete. Okay, off my tree stump and back to the review. I have to admit that I was surprised by the ending. I was hoping for happy.
