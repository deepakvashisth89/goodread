I read this book as a kid. I had no idea what I was reading. I think it did get me interested in Greek mythology. I did like it. Good thing I really didn't remember much about the story.After reading it again, as an adult, I enjoyed it. It was a quick read. It got straight to the plot. I need more 200-page books and not 500 page books being published these days.I look forward to reading the rest of my old Christopher Pike books.
