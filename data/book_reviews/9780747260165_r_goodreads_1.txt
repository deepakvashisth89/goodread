I read this book after listening to Katie Puckrik on her podcasts like “We didn’t start the fire” where she shares some insight into her dad’s career rather than her 90s TV era. I assumed the Four Star rating was a fan base 4 - BUT after reading it the high rating is well deserved. Her life on her journey to the big time, and how she tells it, is a real page turner. Really glad I read this book.
