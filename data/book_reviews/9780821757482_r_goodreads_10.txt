RECKLESS SEDUCTION by Jane Feather is another Regency Historical Romance. Creole beauty, Genevieve Latour and privateer, Dominic Delacroix is fill for a rough ride as they battle intrigue, danger, passion and romance. Genevieve knows scandal but when her family's name is threatened she know no bounds. Dominic attempts to blackmail her father. War is raging, seduction is on the horizon as well as danger. Genevieve must trust Dominic both with her life and her heart. Dominic will do anything to protect Genevieve. A little slow in the beginning, but otherwise fast paced and action filled. Ms. Feather is a wonderful storyteller. Well written with engaging characters and an interesting storyline.
