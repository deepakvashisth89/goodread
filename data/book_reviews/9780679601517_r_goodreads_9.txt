This is (unsurprisingly) one of the most powerful books I’ve had the pleasure to read.The way James Baldwin structured these two “letters” was brilliant.The first letter, written to his nephew (also named James), felt intimate and personal which made me feel honored to read it.He wrote about race, brotherhood, family, and his own personal experiences with such honestly and strength, yet his words felt poetic and soft as well. I pictured him embracing his nephew, literally and metaphorically, trying to protect and prepare young James from the reality and brutality of the world.The second letter was equally profound and important. No amount of words or praise will ever do it justice.Once I finished reading, I took several days to reflect on (primarily American) history, Baldwin’s life, and his lasting legacy. Like many of my other favorite authors, it warms my heart to see that although James Baldwin is no longer alive his words are living on. I was reminded of the saying, “The pen is mightier than the sword.” It is books like this one (and author’s like Baldwin) who prove this to be true.This has joined the list of books I think are “required reading” for every human being!
