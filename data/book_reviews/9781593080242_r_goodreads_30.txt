One of the most beautifully written novels ever. It has haunting, simple prose and a spare quality that really fleshes out Antonia and Jim Burden's loneliness living on the Nebraska prairie.Antonia, Lena, and Jim are all well conceived characters that depict the human condition's yearning and of the notion that goodness always is a human emotion that will live on.It's a bit reminiscent of novels on solitude I've read by Jim Harrison, Kent Haruf, Carson McCullers and Cormac McCarthy.I can see why authors have always referred to this wonderful novel as an influence, and it really is a true classic.
