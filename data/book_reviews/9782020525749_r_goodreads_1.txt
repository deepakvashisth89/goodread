When choosing a book, I decide by the title and the first five sentences. Perhaps I just want to listen to someone's voice more than a well-made storyline. As for this book, I didn't need five sentence; the first sentence was good enough for me.
The father asked his son to talk more about it even though it was not a true story. The son tried to do his best telling that story even though his father knew it was a made-up story. They shared in the fiction. I haven't yet found a more gentle way to tell that you are really special.
