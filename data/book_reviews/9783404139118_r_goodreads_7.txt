This year I decided to read fiction from female authors. After finishing my previous book I hankered for an action espionage book, a genre that I liked. (I especially liked the Bourne trilogy). I searched but it was difficult to find a book by a female author. I started 3 different ones and gave up soon after. Finally I saw the "100 Must Reads" list by Thriller Writers. Besides Helen MacInnes, Gayle Lynds is the only other female author. So I persevered until the end of this book.This book lives up to the genre. Lots of action and a twisted plot. But it was written in 1996. It reminds me of Ludlum. But sadly, I think I have outgrown this genre.I read once that an attractive hanging pot should have a thriller, spillers, and fillers. This book has a thrill, spilled too many plot twists too early, and had more fillers than I care for (how many times is this woman going to be captured?)
