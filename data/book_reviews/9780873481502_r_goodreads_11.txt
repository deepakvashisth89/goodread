"In order for the Afro-Americans to control their destiny, they must be able to control and affect the decisions which control their destiny: economic, political, and social. This can only be done through organization." - OAAU (Organization for Afro-American Unity) Charter
"It is impossible for capitalism to survive, primarily because the system of capitalism needs some blood to suck...As the nations of the world free themselves, then capitalism has less victims, less to suck, and it becomes weaker and weaker." - Malcolm via an interview by the Young Socialist
By Any Means Necessary is a deeply insightful compilation of Malcolm's latter (post-Nation of Islam split—1964-65) speeches and article excerpts that portray his ever-evolving revolutionary ideas and increasingly anti-capitalist and internationalist political stance. Malcolm's militancy, eagerness to learn, and uncompromising dedication to bring about liberation for all oppressed peoples should inspire all—black or otherwise—to fight for liberty, equality, and freedom BY ANY MEANS NECESSARY!
