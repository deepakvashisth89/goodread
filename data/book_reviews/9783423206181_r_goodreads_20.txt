I choose this book for my German reading list 2015/16 (we have to read 10 books by Austrian and German authors) and I have to say, this was definitely not an enjoyable read.
The book was written like a report and was without any emotions. Another thing that I found really annoying was the lack of chapters and paragraphs which made the book really hard to read.
I have to say the topic of the story was fascinating, which was why i chose the book in the first place. The characters felt realistic but in a really boring way.
at least many parts of the books encouraged you to think about it.
The end of the book left lots of questions, but not the ones you have if a book ends with an open ending and you can imagine for yourself how the story is going to continue.
