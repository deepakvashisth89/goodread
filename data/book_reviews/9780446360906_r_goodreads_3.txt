The writing was fantastic and this is a 5 star read. However, the ending was not to my satisfaction so I’m going to be petty and take off a star. Characters were seriously flawed and most were abhorrent, especially Olivia, the ow. But the author’s portrayal of her was also realistic and perfectly characterized that she stole the book for me. Argggh! I so wish Olivia got her comeuppance but the book gave her a different ending. Boohoo.

It’s too bad more readers can’t read this as it’s not in a kindle format. If you are a reader of cheater dramas, please grab a physical copy. I keep thinking if Sidney Sheldon and Susan Isaac collaborated on a 90s drama set in the publishing world, this would be it(but Villars is a much better writer).
