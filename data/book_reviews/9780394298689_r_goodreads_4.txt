This story was my February 2016 pick for my full moon reading project. Skeleton Crew has some great stories: Gramma, The Monkey, The Reaper's Image, Uncle Otto's Truck and The Ballad of the Flexible Bullet are all excellent. Mrs. Todd's Shortcut is my favorite though.I like what the story has to say about aging, commitment. romantic attraction and the tendency of "progress" to erase the weird, magical parts of the world from our minds. I'm also a sucker for a frame story. The way this story is structured takes it to the very top level of the form by giving it a narrative voice that's the perfect vehicle for a lush, creepy trip. Ayuh.
