Typee is Melville’s earliest work and is a curious mixture of anthropology, biography, travel and castaway literature. The protagonist, Tommo, jumps ship in the Marquesas Islands and before long finds himself in the land of the Typees. This provides Melville with the opportunity to record the community and customs of pre-colonial Marquesan Islander society, which he witnessed first-hand during the 1840’s. While in some ways utopian, in other ways Typee society was not and warfare between other groups and cannibalism was a regular feature. Typee also gives Melville a platform to criticise western society and the importation of western values into an already contented and successful culture. Perhaps Melville was rather unusual and before his time in this regard. Tommo eventually escapes his golden handcuffs amongst the Typees and returns to European society.
