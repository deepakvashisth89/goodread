When someone lies it hurts for a long time. In this story Marcus's brother lied to both him and Gemma in the beginning. Now years later those lies will finally come out. Marcus and Gemma have a son, but no marriage. They live in England, for now. Marcus must go back to Italy to take up the family business from there and he wants his little family to go with him. But first he wants to marry Gemma. Now this is where the story goes...will he be able to forgive himself for what he thought he had done? And will she be able to accept that he truly does want her? I enjoyed the story by Kathryn Ross. And will be looking forward to more of her works at a later date.
