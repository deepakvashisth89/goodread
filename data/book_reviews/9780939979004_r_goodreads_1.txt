Why do we torment ourselves with these types of books and how can someone have the mind to even put one like this together?The whole book is quite a large book for small hands and the only reason I can find for this book's size is just the fact that the Foreword was so long. Really did it need that much space to explain the idea for the book that you basically want parenting to be where you are the friend of a child and one who encourages creativity whenever their mind runs wild on them? I thought the message could be much sweeter thus the book much more child-friendly in size. And then there was so much wrong.First of all the fact that all the borders were butterflies and leaves even for the wintry scene is just mind-boggling first of all. Although borders aren't needed I do love the authors who normally use the borders to help enhance the scene and not one that just gives the readers a heads-up this will be a trippy book to begin with.Second of all where in tarnation did this child's name come from? The only name I could think Coopie being a nickname for would have been Cooper but this child is suppose to be a girl child so poor child times two if that is the case. And really I know that this was published in the late '90s but I don't feel that was a time when baby naming was getting really creative as it is now.Third and this is up to the reader's opinion but really does this author or even Coopie's mother not know what a moose is? True the book is suppose to be about using your imagination but the only moose with antlers is a bull moose but Coopie's mom is fine with sex changing for the child so they can have tea in the forest with half the forest populace in clothes and the other running wild in their nature-coats.As if this wasn't enough to find wrong with the book there were quite a few scenes going on where the book was quite suggestive if you step back. Although there probably wasn't much thought given to make it quite naughty even my husband was questioning one page and he is the non-reader of the family.Anyway this is one reading trip I wish I hadn't taken and one I wouldn't find myself wanting to recommend to anyone else either.
