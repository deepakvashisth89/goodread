Venters was out in the sage, eyeing the rustlers who were taking Cottonwood's Jane Withersteen's cattle; his shots were true, with one dead and one badly wounded. But the wounded person was a young woman, masked and with a deadly reputation. Venters was devastated to have shot her, and proceeded to try to save her. Meanwhile back at Cottonwood, Lassiter had arrived to see Jane. His quest had lasted eighteen years as he sought his pregnant sister, who had been abducted all those years ago from her husband - Lassiter's grief had been ongoing, but as he spent time with Jane and discovered her traumas - she was being forced to marry a Mormon elder, a person she intensely disliked - his anger and thoughts of revenge lessened. His guns continued to be part of his attire, but his concern was Jane and little four year old Fay.
