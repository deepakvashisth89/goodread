He has some decent thoughts here, but frankly I feel like this is a book about zeal rather than faith. He offers about 15 different definitions of faith (which is very confusing), but all of them ultimately come down to just feel your belief harder. Biblical faith, in my perspective, is an alignment and a trajectory. It is more about holistic allegiance than mental ascent or passionate conviction.All that said, if you take everything he says with a grain of salt, most of his thoughts are good and well meaning.
