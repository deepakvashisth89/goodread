This is not an official review but I must allocate five stars to one of my favourite books. "Ghost Stories Of Manitoba", which I probably procured and read shortly after its 1998 publication, touches on paranormal history and experiences, along with folklore, of my home turf. Most certainly far more fascinating and personal than reading about paranormal stories generally: nothing compares to familiar home city locales that I have and can visit for myself.For example, I happened to work briefly at 'Dalnavert House', after visiting it numerous times as an interested patron. It was built by the son of our first prime minister. I know it extensively, physically and historically. Other places, I stood in, to feel the vibrations of the rooms. The rest, I shall explore at leisure. This is in my shelf entitled "Up To 2012" because it pre-dates my reviewing here. Get to know our region in an unusual light and procure a copy!My favourite ghost story, verified and recorded by a "Winnipeg Free Press" reporter who investigated it at night, is that of the 'Walker Theatre'. It is the site of many special concerts for my spouse & me and is palpably atmospheric. If you look it up, please note that it was renamed for music artist Burton Cummings in recent years.
