From the Lydia Chin / Bill Smith detective series, this one is narrated by Bill. We learn a lot more of his interesting backstory when he comes to the aid of his nephew, a jock whose family recently moved to the father’s childhood town. Much like Bill’s brother-in-law, Scott, it’s a corrupt, football-first town that has secrets to hide.Only the middle part of the novel has the humor and witty banter that I enjoy in the series, but it’s still a solid detective story and we see Lydia and Bill grow increasingly close. If only her mother would approve....
