▶ I fundamentally think that Anne Rice has significantly less of a problem with having sex with teenagers than I do. I think doing BDSM with minors is perhaps bad. I think Anne Rice believes this to be a minor flaw in Marius's otherwise unimpeachable character, and I fear I cannot agree with this.
