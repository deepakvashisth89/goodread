Similar problems to Roselynde in that Geoffrey is a man of his time but this one holds up better than Roselynde I think in that there are fewer problematic things. The history is fascinating - it's all around the lead up to Magna Carta and then King John's death. Joanna was probably always my 4th favourite of the 1st 4 books but that said, it's still very good and recommended with those caveats about some problematic things in the patriarchy described.
