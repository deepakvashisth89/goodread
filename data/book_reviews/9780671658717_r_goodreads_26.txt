4.5

After yeaaars of wanting to read Gaitskill, here I am: having read Bad Behavior and finally becoming one of her readers. I freakin’ loved it.

I enjoyed Gaitskill’s terse, analytical style, and the tone: smooth in feel but hard to the touch.

I like what Leslie Jamison wrote in her review of This is Pleasure (2019): ‘Mary Gaitskill’s fiction is often called cold, or even brutal, but I have always loved it for nearly opposite reasons: its tender attention to the complexities of human emotion, and the compassion it coaxes from clear-eyed perception.’

I totally agree. The reputation for coldness and bleakness does cling to Gaitskill and her work, so I went in expecting that. But while these are absolutely sad, dark stories, it was such a pleasure to be surprised by the amount of intimacy and pathos in them. These fascinating characters are complicated and rendered with depth—the author’s desire not to judge, but to see them, be with them, and understand them is palpable.

One of my favourite reads of the year so far.
