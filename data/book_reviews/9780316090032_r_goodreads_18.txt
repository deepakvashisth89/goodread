A little too much about Amory's thoughts about indoor/outdoor cats, dating (for himself) and astrology for cats, and not enough about Polar Bear, but a few good anecdotes. My favorite is about the animal sanctuary in Texas, Black Beauty Ranch. Amory had mentioned in passing in the first book that he and friends had rescued the wild burros from the Grand Canyon, by air-lifting them out of the canyon. Their new home was the ranch. Among all the famous people littered in the book, George Foreman came to the ranch to adopt four burros. He was told to choose them himself.
