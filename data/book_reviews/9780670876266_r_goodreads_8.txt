Blanche doesn't necessarily solve mysteries as much as she has a lot of responsibilities and her hand in a lot of pots; is highly-opinionated, and nosy! I think I would have enjoyed this book even more had it (1) a shorter roster of characters, (2) a shorter list of murders, (3) a bit less sleaze, (4) fewer ends to tie up at the end, and (5) a smidge less "sassiness" on Blanche's part. A very entertaining read. I readBlanche on the Lamback in the 1990s, but I don't remember her character being as "extra" as she seems to be in this novel. Who knows? MaybeI'mthe one who's changed😂.
