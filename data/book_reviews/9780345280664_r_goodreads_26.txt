Cachalot is a water world inhabited by evolved cetaceans with telepathic abilities. Suddenly, they begin destroying human habitats. Human visitors have with them a new multisensory musical instrument that plays a role in the plot. This 1980 Del Rey novel got a boost from cover art by Darrell Sweet, picturing a whale surfacing near a small futuristic ship.The story is entertaining enough, but it does not rise to the level established by Zelazny’s “The Doors of His Face, The Lamps of His Mouth” (1965).David Brin’s Startide Rising would outdo it again in 1983, and Asher’s 2002 Skinner also does a better job with a similar premise. 3.5
