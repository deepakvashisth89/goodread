Not a book I’d actually read or even pick up! This book is my dads from when he was younger! When he saw I was into murder mystery books, he kept asking me to read one of his! After him asking a few times, I gave in and accepted this one which he recommended me to try first!

After the first couple of chapters, I did feel like this would be a DNF however I battled through them chapters and it did get much better!

I’d say it’s actually a really solid storyline, had good pacing and swift action. The characters are all well done and easily believable. The dialogue for the characters are spot on which makes this such a fun read.

Some clever twists and turns, and especially marvelous use of the early 1940's Los Angeles location to include celebrities in his stories.

I can't say it's great, but it is a fun, and satisfying murder mystery!
