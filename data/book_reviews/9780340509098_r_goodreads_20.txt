Well, this was a quick read! I really got into this story immediately.. First time I read about a freelance photographer and his quirks, but I was instantly sold..We follow Creed as he goes to several jobs, but at one of them he witnesses quite the bizzare scene.. which will be the start of a thrilling ride through a new world .. We meet several interesting characters, once in a while even told from their point of view, but the main focus will be our "hero" ..The story was definitely well told and all was written very detailed.. I'm a fan!I'll be following Mr. Herbert and reading more of his works ;)
