Solid, functional writing, mostly a notch or two above serviceable, with a catchy turn of phrase here and there. As a novel this is probably three stars, but it's a strong source episode, and many of the individual scenes translate well to the page and to Gannett's prose rhythms. Amusingly, the villain's name, as revealed right at the end of the novel, is slightly different from that given in the episode--which really emphasizes its lack of importance. A few other details diverge too, often in really minute ways. More interestingly, the novel contains a few extra scenes not found in the episode. A strong recommendation for die-hard fans & completists. Everyone else, prob a pass.
