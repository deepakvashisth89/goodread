A Gripping TaleThis is a fine account of a terrifying kidnapping, a murder and the ensuing, relentless search for a father and son team in the mountainous forests of Montana. The sheriff, Johnny France, is the chief pursuer almost consumed by his desire to capture and bring to justice Donald Holmes and his son Danny. But this book is more than the story of a crime and a dogged six month pursuit.The book's co-author is the sheriff himself who paints a dramatic account of the psychological makeup of two men who have chosen to live as skilled mountain men isolated from society. Sheriff Johnny in his prolonged musings on the whys and whereabouts of the Nichols men develops a somewhat sympathetic understanding of the harsh life experiences that shaped these two two outlaw/outcasts by choice.We are also given a clear portrait 9f thekidnapping victim a young biathlon athlete training for the olympics whose jogging path intersects with the Nichols in a forest on a Montana summer dayana summer day. She suffers physically and psychologically during her overnight ordeal and is accidentally shot by the younger kidnapper as he runs from her rescuers. Her rescue from a near fatal wound is one of the most gripping incidents in the book.The sheriff turned author shares openly his struggles growing up, his life as a rodeo rider, his family concerns, the difficulties of serving as sheriff for a large geographical area, and provides a painstaking account of the personal toll of six months seeking forest-wisw fugitives. We get an inside look at the logistical and personality diffiiculties that arose during the July- December (1984) manhunt.
