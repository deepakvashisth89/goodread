That... that wasn't very good. Reading it, with it's blow-by-blow descriptions of combat, minimal character development showed how boring some RPG sessions must seem to the casual onlooker. Add to that a shoehorned love story, predictable outcomes and weird prose... let's say it wasn't the best read. Besides, it doesn't really describe many of the things, figuring that someone familiar with D&D will know them.It did have it moments though. It definitely provided a nostalgia trip to the old-style of dungeon crawler RPG adventures. The ending itself wasn't also that bad. I can't recommend it, but I can't say I wasted my time reading it.
