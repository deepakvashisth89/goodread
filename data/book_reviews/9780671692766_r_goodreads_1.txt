Digging through old books looking for a book to lend for a friend I found this book among several other Hardy Boys I received for my tenth birthday. These were fun little quick mystery reads. I only wish that someone would repurpose the Franklin W. Dixon pseudonym and start releasing more of these. This one's a little rough round the edges, but with some polish would make a fine mystery. I enjoyed the anachronistic gee-whiz isn't this tech neat references to...a fax machine. Haha.
