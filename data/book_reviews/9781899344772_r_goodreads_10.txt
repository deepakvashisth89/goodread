Lots of alcohol and brutality. I reached for this book because it has been on my shelf for a long time. I bought it at a book sale. Now I understand why it went on it in first place.The book is about everyday's life of the main character, Mitchell. The first 190 pages could be easily shortened to 15-20, without any damage to the plot. The simplicity of the message contained in this book is staggering. I wondered if the author's writing style was a bit different, a little more colorful, would I like this book more?
