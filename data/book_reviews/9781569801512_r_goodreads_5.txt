Interesting look at the life of a very privileged working actress, who led a very unique life: wife to major star & mother to another. A minor annoyance: there's no complete list of her family members, who show up in some of the many b/w photos and throughout the book. There's a sense that not all siblings, their spouses or children are clearly identified. This could be explained by the fact that the author states she wrote it especially for her grandson Cameron Douglas, who probably does not need to be reminded who his relatives are. If you are looking for psychological insights, they are few. Author admits she doesn't like to share feelings. Yet her evaluation of her marriage to Kirk Douglas sounds astute and she clearly had a terrific marriage to Bill Darrid. Michael Douglas has no flaws.
