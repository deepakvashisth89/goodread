This is a collection of 22 poems that scream for the voices of a vegan youth, published in 2000 when the idea of a vegan society was first coming into fruition.

I loved reading this perspective, the blending of the views on animal rights, the want to care for our environment and protect our planet, and all of this tied to Benjamin Zephaniah's characteristic narrative voice and dialectical choices.

I do find that this is not a collection of poems for me, but to that I will say that I am not a vegan, just someone who does want to put out a little more kindness into the world.

My favourite lines?

We really need this planet
And we want you to be aware
We just don't have one to spare
Not any of us do.
