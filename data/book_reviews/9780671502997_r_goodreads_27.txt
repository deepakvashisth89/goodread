I really enjoyed this book at the beginning, typical love story, good details, but as the story progressed the details were not there, things tended to fast forward quicker than the beginning of the book. The couple ended up getting married, but there was no introduction to the grooms family in advance of the wedding. This was odd to me since Brooke had the need to be a part of the a family. A puppy was thrown into the mix yet was never really mentioned after arrival, especially the first night of receiving it.
