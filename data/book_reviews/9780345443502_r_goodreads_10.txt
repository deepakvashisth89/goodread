I am a long-lapsed Christian. I don't know what moved me to read this book. I had read a review in some magazine, and then saw it at the library. It was short, and it didn't look like the general rah-rah kind of book about Christ. So I gave it a shot. The story is told from Pilate's perspective looking back some 30 years later, and treats Jesus as a historical figure, not the savior of mankind. I found it a quick, interesting, and good read.
