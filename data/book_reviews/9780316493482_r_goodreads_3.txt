A rather disturbing book on many levels. I read the 1984 paperback blurb on the back from reviewers. "Gripping entertainment from start to finish" No, its not entertaining, theres a lot of profound ugly emotional and psychological life issues that surface as the novel continues."His most masterful, comic and moving novel yet" Well, there is some dark dark humour in the characters he portays and in situations it appears sporadically. Mostly its slightly edgy but in an unsettling way with a very grim finish. Good characterisation of the main people though.
