This is a beautiful start (1979 publish) to the "Ghosts" books and calendars to follow. I like the heart-felt writing about the magic of the planes, early in the book. Great pictures, but I wish the captions were on each page instead of a master list in the front of the book.I like the silhouettes in the appendix.I also like the extra "Fundamentals of Air Fighting" from 1942 included in the appendix:"In air combat, the purpose of the fighter pilot, and flexible gunners, is to destroy the enemy quickly with the minimum amount of ammunition."..."The opportunities for accurate shooting are short."..."Suprise may be achieved by:Attacking from directly out of sun,Making use of the bank of haze...In the evening or early morning by attacking from that part of the sky which is darker,"..."Watch your tail.Conserve ammunitionNever fly or dive straight when being attacked by aircraft or anti-aircraft fire"..."Don't EVER fly straight, especially if you are alone. Keep that rubber neck turning continuously and keep a lookout behind"This is a good book. The "Ghost" books that continue to come are GREAT.
