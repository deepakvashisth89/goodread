If you enjoy historical fiction and tales of Native American tribes, this one is for you. At times gruesome, at times mystical, it is essentially a story of one man and his many loves: one main spirited woman with more pluck than most, his love for adventure, dominance, dignity and alas, many more women.Moss, an historian, did a stellar job of bringing the early days of our nation's struggle to vivid life. I was surprised that the native warriors were so vain!
