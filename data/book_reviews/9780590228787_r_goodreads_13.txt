Stacey wants to earn extra money to take Robert to NYC for his birthday, so she takes on a demanding regular sitting job for the Cheplins, a new family in town. Each day, Stacey is left with an increasing list of chores she must complete, on top of her babysitting duties. The mother manipulates Stacey into doing her bidding with cash bonuses, constant criticism, and keeping the promise of permanent employment dangled in front of her.This was the most realistic version of this story that I’ve read in this series. The problem is “Stoneybrook adult abusing child labor” has been a major plot point in at least three other books I can remember just off the top of my head (Dawn and the Impossible Three,Maid Mary Anne,Kristy and Mr. Mom, any more?). At this point, I can’t even feel sorry for them when they get in these situations. They need some sort of written contract or policy the clients need to sign stating they won’t do anything but babysit.
