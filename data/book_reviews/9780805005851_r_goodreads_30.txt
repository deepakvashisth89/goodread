I despise the use of filler in books. My advice to non-fiction authors would be "just get to the damn point!" Much of this book is filler.Still, it has useful information, such that I recommend reading it for anyone who is in a relationship, whether that relationship be a great one or a tense one, or in between those two. It describes what we (unconsciously) look for in a partner and why. It describes written and verbal exercises couples can do together to improve communication. These alone make it a worthwhile read, despite all of the filler.I am going through the exercises with my partner, and I can see that if you're consciously trying to incorporate this book's wisdom into your life, your relationship will improve.But damn, that filler...
