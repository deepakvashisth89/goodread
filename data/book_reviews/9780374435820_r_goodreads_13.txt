This book is about an artist named Felix Clousseau that entered his paintings into a royal contest. At first, no one sees value in his paintings but then they come to life and he wins the contest. After more and more of his paintings come to life everything in Paris becomes chaotic and he is sent to jail. He is released from jail when one of his paintings helps catch a highly sought after burglar that tries to steal the crown. Once he is released, he walks off into one of his paintings. The concept of this book is very abstract which makes it good for older readers to think about. The illustrations are dark and look like watercolors. It would be good to have fifth grade create their own piece of artwork and create a story behind what would happen if it came to life.
