An Eastern woman, coming out West and getting a job working as a cook on a ranch, is an oft-repeated storyline in western fiction. Josephine Whittaker, after a divorce, is looking for a new start. Unfortunately, she can't boil water, much less cook a meal. But that is the only job she can find. Once she convinces J D McCall to hire her, Josephine's problems have just begun.This is a pleasant way to while away some hours reading. I didn't think Ms. Holm added much to create uniqueness to the story but the dialogue was good between the main characters. 3.5 stars
