Set in Waggs Neck Harbor (Sag Harbor, New York) an exclusive summer resort community that has a year round population of a few residents who are eccentric, gossipy and lovable. The Winter Women are those who have found refuge in this place. The characters and their relationships made me remember why I both love and hate small towns. There's a feeling of belonging and closeness, of knowing everything about a place, the smallness of the place makes you warm and comfortable, walking everywhere. There's also the feelings of being an outsider, other, not fitting in, loss of privacy, hurtful and hidden agendas, a class system that goes back to the founding families. There's no way to be anonymous in a small town.

All that is so, and is what makes Kaufelt's The Winter Women Murders a good read and timeless in many ways.
