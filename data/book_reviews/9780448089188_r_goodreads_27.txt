Asked to help with their Dad's smuggling case, Joe and Frank Hardy are eager to commence investigating. Following a break-in and suspicious phone call requesting papers, Frank and Joe are led to the ship Black Parrot but it sails before they can poke around. Fenton Hardy has devised a clever code to communicate with his sons and it's a good thing when he is captured and taken deep into Canada's woods. Setting off on a rescue mission, the boys come upon the two thugs who'd tried to trick them in Bayport and know they are on the right track. Then they learn that the Black Parrot is coming into a port town in Canada. With full support from their father, the two boys gain access by becoming part of the crew who is embarking to a port unknown. As they go about their duties, they learn more and more about the smuggling operation their father was investigating and just how vast it is.
