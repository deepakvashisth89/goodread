What a beautiful example of how books can allow you to look into others lives and experience things you haven’t yet. Terry Tempest Williams allows you to experience grief alongside her and her family and the ugly, horrific scenes of death. Her writing changes you and feels like an intimate and sacred ceremony. I am so grateful to be able to read books like this.“The snow continues to fall. Red apples cling to bare branches. I just returned from Tamra Crocker Pulfer’s funeral. It was a reunion of childhood friends and family. Our neighborhood sat on wooden benches row after row in the chapel, I sat next to mother and wondered how much time we had left together.”
