Well i liked this book, but i really hated in the end to read how Rachel and Slade grew old and how Rachels grandfather is dead and how their children are grown up and have children of their own. Fine let it be 10 years ahead, but 40 is too much. In one moment Rachel is 19 and in the next she is 60 years old. That is the reason for the 3 stars. In the whole book i waited to see them finally together and happy and how they dreams come true, but i really didn't like to read about that, after 40 years of their marriage. I want to read about here and now, not about their future and what they accomplished after so many years and that they still love each other as strongly as they love each other when they were young.
