Very enjoyable mystery set in the Victorian era in Oxford. Unfortunately I liked the side characters rather more than Cribb, so I don't know how much I would enjoy other episodes in the series. The book included a large subplot focused on a book by Jerome K. Jerome, and I believe I have in the past read another mystery that also leaned heavily on Jerome, but I can't remember which! He seems to have been quite the man of the moment, perhaps a Dan Brown situation.
