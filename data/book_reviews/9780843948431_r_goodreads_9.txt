This book is reminiscent of the western movies that I loved so much when I was younger. It has all the elements that kept me entertained. There are good guys and bad guys, sneak attacks and shootouts. Not to mention, the strong, handsome cowboy who saves the prettiest girl in the territory. Pam Crook lassoed me in with just the blurb of this book and then sent me galloping through one adventure after another as I made my way to the end.
