I picked up this book (and a few others in the series) looking for good supplemental readings for my students as we cover these topics in Social Studies. I didn't notice that the target audience is ages 7-11, making this book too juvenile for even my 6th graders. Some kids my still enjoy it; especially my struggling readers.The story starts off with some kids finishing up their Egypt projects when the narrator's kid sister walks in with a magical book that transports the readers to the time/place their looking up.While in their adventure, the reader catches a glimpse of clothing worn, food eaten, religion, geography, and more. This is far easier to read than our text book.
