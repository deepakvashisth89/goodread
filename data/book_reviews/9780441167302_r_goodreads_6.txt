First of all, Kudos for the incredible cover art by Rowena on the Phantasia Press first limited edition...You can be forgiven if you think the cover art is a bit campy, forDream Parkis a campy novel. Written during the height of the Dungeons & Dragons craze, it depicts an amusement park in which the participants role-play in an incredibly technologically advanced environment. There's a murder involved but the fun for the reader is in escaping into this world and wishing such a park actually exists. It may not be great literature, it is certainly a lot of fun. three and a half stars.
