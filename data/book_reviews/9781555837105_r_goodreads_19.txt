3.75 rounded up to 4 starsThat’s it? I could use one more chapter! (At least tell me how the event will affect Dave and Doug’s relationship!)While the murderer was pretty easy to identify, I still thoroughly enjoyed the mystery in this book. Joseph Hansen wrote his mystery in such a stripped-to-the-bare-bone style that spared me the excess of gratuitous deductions and emotional entanglements commonly observed in the M/M mystery genre. The narrative flowed with a sharp focus on the plot and characters, free from melodrama or unnecessary psychological exploration. All of these combined to deliver a refreshing and relaxing reading experience (at least for me), despite the series being set in a period rampant with homophobia.
