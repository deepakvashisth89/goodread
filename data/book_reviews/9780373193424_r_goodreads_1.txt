This was a good book. From the first page, I was sucked in. It was a nice, cute read. The plot is fun and flows well. There's no over-the-top drama, just the perfect mix of anticipation, jealousy, friendship, a little bit of angst, heartache, and sweet moments that make you love this.I really liked Cindy. She is easily one of the most relatable female leads. I totally understand her feelings. I know many women out there who experienced or currently experiencing unrequited love (myself included). So I got this girl. I really did. I adored Kyle. He's so different from most heroes you'd met in books these days. Yes, he's sexy and gorgeous. But what sets him aside? He's such a nice guy. He's not a playboy asshole. He didn't mistreat her or say nasty things to her. And I loved the way he talked about her. It always warms my heart and makes me feel giddy whenever a hero gushes or tells his friends about the heroine and his feelings for her.The chemistry between them was palpable. I loved the scene where they danced. I could almost feel Cindy's giddiness and excitement, the feeling she gets while dancing with the man of her dreams. It is so hard not to relate to her and her situation. I also loved Cindy's friends and the way they ganged up on her! Girl power!Overall, The Boss and The Beauty was such a good read. I enjoyed the story for its likable characters, sweet romance, and no over-the-top drama. I just wished the steamy scenes were steamier because, in all honesty, the "steamy" scenes sucked because they were the fade-to-black type of scenes.
