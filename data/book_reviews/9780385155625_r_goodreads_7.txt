Peter Shandy has another murder on his hands. This time it is a guest that came to dinner and later that night was killed. To make matters worse, his wife is high jacked in a gold and silver robbery that day and a friend of hers from North Dakota has come to move to Balaclava College.This one kept me guessing all through the story. I really think the addition of a wife for Professor Shandy has made the series so much better than the first book. Shandy is a much more likable character than in the first book.Having trouble finding these books because they are so old but they are well worth the read.
