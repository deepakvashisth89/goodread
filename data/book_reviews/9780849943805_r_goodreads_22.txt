I read Blessed Child years ago, and I think it probably had more impact on me than this book. First of all, I did appreciate the setting. I also enjoyed the challenge to how I think about faith and God’s power. There were some good, thought-provoking questions to chew on and a reminder that faith is supposed to be living and evident. On the other hand, it just fell a little flat for me — a bit cheesy and the characters felt off. It’s just not my typical genre, and I appreciate more character development.
