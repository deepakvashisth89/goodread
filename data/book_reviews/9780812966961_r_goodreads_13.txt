This selection of tales is not what you're excepting from a Russian writer. Especially the title giving tale The Enchanted Wanderer is very entertaining. It is a classic picaresque tale and reminded me a lot ofCandide.The story I enjoyed most was Lady Macbeth of Mtsensk. The main character kills both her father in law and her husband to live with her lover. I didn't judge her for that but later for mudering the child and I liked her revenge at the end of the tale.
