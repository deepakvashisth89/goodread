There was something about this book that captured my imagination; although I can't quite put my finger on what it was! The plot wasn't the strongest or most intriguing I've ever read and the 'twist' at the end was a bit much; however, some of the characters were really quirky and interesting and the author created a quaint, yet almost surreal, atmosphere at times that I found quite engaging.My favourite characters would have to be the two men who ran the B&B; I actually wish I could take refuge in the House of Enchantment myself! At times, the main character, Henry, was a bit infuriating and useless; however, I guess the author wished to stress his sense of being at a loss at this time of his life.This book is probably not for everyone but I enjoyed it.
