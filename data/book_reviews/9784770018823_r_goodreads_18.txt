Used this as my textbook for my Japanese class. Overall, I liked the book a lot since there are a lot of exercises and examples. However, the exercises are mainly rote copying of the example given rather than making you think about what words/grammar structures you're choosing and why. I think rote copying can be helpful to a degree, but it gets tedious after awhile. The book also lacks in grammar, but what grammar it does explain, is clear to the reader. I'm also disappointed that the book didn't have any kanji in it all. Even if it didn't teach kanji, it would still be helpful to see it with furigana as you learn.I give it 4 stars for a good beginner book, but if you've been studying Japanese longer than a year, this is probably not a good book for you.
