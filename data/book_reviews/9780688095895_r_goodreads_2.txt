The hand-cut paper illustrations in this book are amazing. This is a great book to check out if only for the illustrations. However, the books also tells a good moral tale about the consequence of distraction from duty that weaves in Viking folklore and legend, like the cause behind the Northern Lights. This was an enjoyable picture book read for our Middle Ages history lesson about the Vikings.In the Author's Note Wisniewski states that he used an X-Acto knife and more than 1,000 very sharp blades to cut the paper illustrations. And because he was attentive to his duty (ahem...moral of the story), he didn't cut himself once. :-)
