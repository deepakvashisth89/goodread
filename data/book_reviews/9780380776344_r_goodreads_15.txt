Slow, Confusing to Start & Then I Enjoyed It...

I had a difficult time getting into this book at the beginning. For whatever reason I found the writing to be difficult and hard to understand. It just didn't flow smoothly and it left me confused. Before too long I finally felt like I understood what was going on and what was being written and it was smooth sailing from there.

I've read a few different stories that jump back and fourth from decade to decade and haven't usually been very big on them. This one was actually quite interesting to me and I enjoyed both love stories. I liked the characters and couldn't wait to see what was going to happen every time the time changed. I got to the point where I wasn't even sure which story I was more interested in. They both had me on the edge of my seat and I really enjoyed the adventure and mystery.

The storyline itself was quite interesting to me. A bit on the depressing side but interesting nonetheless.

If this storyline sounds interesting to you then I'd definitely recommend it. I enjoyed it quite a bit.
