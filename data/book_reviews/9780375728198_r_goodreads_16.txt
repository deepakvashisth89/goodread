I am still unsure if to rate it with 4 or 5 stars, but regardless of that, I must say that it is a great book! A straight-to-the-point novel, good plot, nice characters and nice setting. We have the main character who suffers a lot due to logical reasons (I won't give many details to not spoil anything) and he spends a long time trying to figure everything out and bring some peace to his life, if possible. We have Sarah, who also had a horrible past in sense of relationships, then they merge together in this story with a few other characters involved, all of them really good.
I must say that I really liked how the "narrator" was introduced and how he was slowly clarifying more thing parallel to the story. The only reason I am not sure if to rate it with 5 stars is the end. Even though it was not bad, I guess it could have been a bit better. How? No idea, but that was the only flaw I could see here.

Regardless, completely recommendable and I am very interested about Sparks' other books!
