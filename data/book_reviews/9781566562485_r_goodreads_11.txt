The Gardens of Lightis the second book by Lebanese/French journalist and author Amin Maalouf I have read this year, (the other beingSamarkand) and both are simply amazing works of historical fiction. Both are set primarily in ancient Persia.TGOL tells the imagined story of the 3rd-century prophet Mani, whose philosophy of Manichaeism embraced all faiths, peoples, and cultures, which ended up winning him followers and supporters—as well as powerful enemies.One of the main lessons of the book for me is that those who advocate compassion, tolerance and acceptance in human society will always be bitterly opposed and denounced, if not outright killed. Why? Because such people are an impediment to the exercise of power and control over the many by the few. It’s sad but true, even to the present day.Maalouf is a wonderful writer and storyteller, and I look forward to reading more of his work. 5 stars.
