This is a character-driven story that is equal parts family and legal drama. Alejandro Stern is in some ways as much a mystery to himself as he is to the reader; the two learn about Stern and what makes him tick through the storyline.Stern, an attorney, is both embroiled in the defense of his brother-in-law against the United States government, and dealing with his grief and that of his family's in the aftermath of Stern's wife's death.
