Hans In Luck by Paul Galdone- Children’s Illustrated Colour Picture Book- The book narrates a German Fairy tale about a young man Hans who has worked with his master for seven years. Hans informs him that he has decided to return back to his village to meet his mother. His master gives him a lump of gold the size of his head and sees him on his way. After walking for some time, Hans feels that the lump of gold is too heavy for him to carry. While travelling, Hans meets a man on a horse. Hans informs the horseman about his problem of carrying the lump of gold, so the horseman suggests that he can exchange the gold for his horse. Hans agrees to the exchange. Horse man has taught Hans that if he wants that the horse should gallop, he should speak to him in his ear. Horse moves fast and Hans is not able to control the speed. Hans falls from the horse. Just by chance, a cow trader catches the horse and saves Hans from falling on the ground. Hans wishes to get away with the horse so he trades the horse for the cow. In the hope that Hans will get milk and cheese, he walks with the cow. When Hans feels thirsty, he tries to milk the cow. Instead of providing milk, the cow kicks him. Hans exchanges the cow for a large bird, the large bird is exchange for a piece of round stone with a hole in the centre. Hans places the stone on a well an accidently the stone falls in the well. Thus, every stranger takes advantage of Hans’ simplicity. Hans reaches his mother’s house empty handed. Coloured illustrations help the reader in relating to the story.
