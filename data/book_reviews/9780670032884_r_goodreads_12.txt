“A Hole in the Universe” follows Gordon, a man who went to prison for 25 years when he was 18 for killing a woman. He is freshly out of prison and attempting to reintegrate into society with the help of his younger brother Dennis. Dennis is successful and has a wife and children but he also deals with some personal struggles (such as his affair). Gordon also relies on his friend (and lover?) Delores who he went to high school with and who visited him frequently in prison. Gordon struggles to accept help, to maintain relationships, to find work, etc. and the book follows this journey. Jada, a teenage girl who lives across the street from Gordon, tries to befriend Gordon as she struggles with her mother’s addiction and poverty. Jada presents a threat to Gordon’s freedom when her mother kills Gordon’s neighbour and Gordon is blamed for the murder.Overall the story was very interesting and the author did a great job conveying the emotions and struggles of someone reintegrating into society after being incarcerated for an extended period of time. I thought some of the relationships between the characters were a bit bizarre such as Gordon and Delores’ relationship - Gordon seems to be annoyed by her generosity yet she continues to go out of her way to help him throughout the book.
