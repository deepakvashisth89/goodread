First, you need to learn to play Go well; then you have to become fluent in Basque.Real Go players tell me I'm about second Dan strength, but unfortunately I don't know any Basque at all. One out of two ain't bad, I guess. Anyway, you've probably figured out why I adore this engagingly crazy book.________________________________I thought ofShibumilast week when I was in Japan, and wondered whether I should add something here about the plot.
