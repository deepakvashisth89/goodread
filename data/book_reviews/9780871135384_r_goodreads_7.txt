Witty and irreverent book that explores the complexities of romantic relationships. Heimel's sharp and often hilarious observations offer a refreshing perspective on the ups and downs of love. However, the book's downfall lies in its reliance on gender stereotypes, perpetuating outdated notions that can be off-putting to readers seeking more nuanced portrayals. While the author's humor is entertaining, it is unfortunate that the book fails to challenge these gender assumptions, which limits its overall appeal and makes it less inclusive in its approach.
