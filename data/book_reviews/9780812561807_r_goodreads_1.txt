The final chapter in the Savage family saga.

This books mostly follows Nick and Brook Savage and the dealings of the 1930 - 40's and World War II. Once again the family is centered in Europe, Asia and the US. Compared to the other books, I didn't like this one as much maybe because they discuss a lot of WWII, Hitler and to me that whole time in history is very depressing and I like to read for entertainment. When Nick is an ambassador to the US in Berlin, I found his letters home to his mother painful and frank,but that is history.
