I'm giving 3 stars for the descriptions of the hockey games alone. But the story of Steven Crandell and how he comes to be kidnapped to play with a special team of hockey players is so far-fetched that even with massive suspension of disbelief I had problems with this story. What's worse, is that there was so much opportunity for Steve to grow as a character in the course of this book, that was never taken.So what this becomes then is hockey with only a hint of story - beautiful detailed descriptions of playing the game by someone who obviously loves the game, but has no idea how to really write an engaging story, which is a shame.
