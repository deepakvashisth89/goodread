All he could think about from the moment he met Alex Ladd was her and that was in the beginning of the story.The first woman was a woman who he worked with. He had a casual relationship with her for a year (exclusive, but secret because they worked together). The hero didn't love her. He had already been evaluating his life and breaking it off with her when he went on his "trip" in the beginning of the book. When he met Alex, that sealed the deal for him. He was ready for a woman who was wife material and a family. When he met Alex, she hit him like a ton of bricks. He was instantly smitten. Anyway, when he gets home from his small "trip" (the heroine has disappeared off the face of the earth and he never even knew her name) the woman he works with, shows up at his condo, proceeds to sit on his lap and kiss him. He pushes her away and breaks up with her. At no point did he want her sexually. He knew the whole time seeing her again, he was going to break up with her. He does not kiss her back or touch her intimately.The second woman, Davey, is a very close friend with him. They love each other as FRIENDS and nothing more. Years ago, they had sex - only one time and it was just to do it. It was year ago, before she got married and all. They didn't even kiss in the act. Davey is the one who's husband has been murdered. Her husband was an asshole 200%. She was feeling low one night and comes onto Hammond. She goes up to him and kisses him. He is shocked and doesn't kiss her back. She immediately backs away and they talk about it. She is in no WAY an issue because she doesn't have feelings for him like that, she was just feeling low and wanted a no strings romp. Her story is pretty sad actually.Intimate pasts:Hammond Cross (hero): Not a virgin. Had casual sex in school and college, but seems like an all around good guy. I don't know how long it was for him before he meets Alex, but I would assume not too long since he had that girl he was dating.
