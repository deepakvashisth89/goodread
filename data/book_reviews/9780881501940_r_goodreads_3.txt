Quanomet has been a sleepy little town for a number of years, but their new post office has jettisoned it to the top of the news. Not only is it huge in size but the exterior is red brick, chromium columns, cement elements and pink granite steps. “Early Colonial Modified” is the defined style.The frosting on this strange cake is the mural that is on the walls of the interior. Titled “The History and Customs of Cape Cod,” it portrays various historical figures in a variety of activities. The cherry was the faces were of citizens of Quanomet — and they weren’t complimentary images.When the artist’s wife turns up murdered, things take a serious turn. The woman is the daughter of the Octagon House owner, and doesn’t have the nicest reputation. There is also the matter of a large piece of ambergris, worth fifty thousand dollars, that has gone missing.After a two month absence, Asey Mayo finds this mystery to be his welcome home. Is one of the people who has an unflattering portrait in the mural the murderer? Is the victim somehow involved in the missing ambergris? Who and why and are these two events related?Looks like the “Cape Cod Sherlock” has another mystery to solve.
