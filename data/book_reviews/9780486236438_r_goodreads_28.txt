The biggest stand-out in this children's short story collection is The Glass Dog - quirky and completely Baum but not in some far-distant fantastical world. The best quotable comes from "A Box of Robbers":"It is rather hard to get positions in the gas office," she said, "but you might become politicians.""No!" cried Beni, with sudden fierceness; "we must not abandon our high calling. Bandits we have always been, and bandits we must remain!"A couple of these are clearly products of their time and showcase some cultural ignorance and xenophobia.
