Well, I've been hanging on to this book quite a while - it took me quite a while to pick this up and then frankly, quite a while to get into the book (I guess this is why I don't read historical mysteries very often - they just don't appeal as much to me). I found the first half of this book very slow-going - maybe I am too used to the fast pace of today's modern life but I felt like the characters were moving in slow motion. It picked up some during the last half of the book but I guess Georgian England is not the era for me!
