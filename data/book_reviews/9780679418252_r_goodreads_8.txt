Although I enjoyed this book, I still am undecided about any final conclusion. Throughout the book, it felt like the author had an agenda- he wanted to prove that Lee Harvey Oswald was the sole assassin and presented the evidence with that in mind. That's not to say I didn't think Posner's research was impressive and did blow some conspiracy theories out of the water, because he did. I have heard that Vincent Bugliosi's Reclaiming History is the definitive book out there for the lone gunman theory and I wish I would have read that first.Posner had this very pompous and dismissive way of writing about every single author and even witnesses that don't support his theory, going so far in his footnotes to comment on their privates lives. While that isn't a valid criticism on his theory, I just think it's a crappy thing to do and makes me think less of Posner in general.
