I am convinced after reading this book that a) it should be required reading with homework, pop quizzes and a final test for all those in the process of certifying for Candidacy in the UMC, b) all current UMC clergy should be required to read it, c) it should be presented as a churchwide/small group study in all UMC churches, and d) the Conferences should prepare for not a massive, but a significant exodus of clergy who are not in agreement with much of anything in this book.Not an easy read, but a critically important and massively informative read. I never learned this as a parishioner. We just touched on parts of it over a year of Candidacy mentoring and licensing school. I am glad I read it and already working on a plan to present it churchwide.
