It took me quite some time to read this book, not because it was bad, but because it just felt boring at times.The plot is essentially that in the first 3 books, our intrepid heroes are being chased by a powerful wizard and the armies that he commands to try and take over the world of Faerûn. In the 4th book, the heroes decide to take the fight to him and attack his actual base of operations.Although this is the best book in the series in my opinion, for various reasons I will state soon, I still feel like it was pretty boring overall. It was like 15 or 20 chapters of them running through a castle and fighting the exact same way over and over. However, I understand that this was essentially made in early DnD times when they were not exactly imaginative with the classes. So I try to realize that as well.On the plus side, hearing Cadderly sort of come back to the light, his fight with the red dragon, the magical artifact fight, seeing a bit more clerical magic against the wizard knowledge. Was all much better than the previous entries into the series. There is one more book, and I look forward to seeing how this series is going to wrap up.
