This is a fascinating, but somewhat lengthy tale about a grandmother telling a story about her youth to her grandchildren. I love the way that the story is told from the perspective of a very young girl, with all of the wonder and confusion of seeing something for the first time.The relief of the child and the family at finally being reunited is palpable and the Ying-ying's scary experience serves as a good lesson for both children and caregivers.I think that for the intended audience, the story is a bit wordy and could lose the attention of younger children. But our girls really liked the story and we enjoyed looking at the gorgeously illustrated pictures as we read the tale.
