Very old school, which I often like, but this one didn't particularly stand out for me. The ending and reveal of the secret child was very anticlimactic. Could have been much better, but just average old school for me. Spoilers below:The hero and heroine marry when the heroine is 17 and the hero 22-23. At this point in the hero's life he is just finishing his degree in civil engineering and he has no family connections or wealth. Her parents disapprove and refuse to allow the two to wed. They don't think the hero is good enough for their family, but finally give in under the condition that the hero give up his new job overseas and work for the heroine's father while living in their home.The heroine's family treat the hero like dirt and do everything they can to come between the two and make the hero appear inept and weak.The couple have a huge fight when the hero insists that the heroine choose between him and her parents. He wants her to leave with him, because he's not living with the horrible parents another minute.The heroine says some nasty things, including calling the hero 'impotent'. The hero rapes her to prove his manly non-impotence and then leaves.The heroine quickly realizes that the parents were trying to ruin the marriage and waits 6 weeks or so for the hero to contact her or return for her. In the meantime she learns she's pregnant. The terrible family insists she get a divorce and an abortion. Since she hasn't heard from the hero and refuses to get an abortion or divorce, she leaves the family behind and strikes out on her own. Eventually she becomes a photo journalist and raises her young son alone.7 years later she goes to Greece to try and interview an elusive billionaire and runs into the not-quite-ex-husband (neither filed for divorce). He is now successful and uber-manly, having been hardened and honed by the years into a more typical HP hero.There is some drama where he thinks she's involved with the billionaire interviewee and she thinks he's involved with some topless sunbather.After a rather dramatic forced seduction scene, it is revealed that the hero deliberately sought the heroine out. He knew she would be coming to Greece. He thought maybe they could see if they still had anything between them, even though years before he had tried to contact her and was told she didn't want to see him (interfering parents again).As is typical in HPLandia, the heroine has remained frozen and not dated, while the hero has sown his wild oats. However, he was never quite able to let go of the idea of the marriage and didn't file for divorce. Supposedly he'd look for her whenever he was in England, but as she'd cut off all contact with her family he couldn't find her (until he saw her name on an article). In the 80's HP heroes were not as omnipotent as they are now. He did not have an entourage of security experts and private investigators. Hard to believe since that is stock in trade these days.All misunderstandings are placed aside and HEA with the secret child revealed on the last 2 pages. Very, very abrupt. He only had one second to be furious before the page count ran out and he had to let it go.
