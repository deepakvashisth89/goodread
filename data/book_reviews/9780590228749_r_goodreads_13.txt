The funny thing about reading these books as an adult is that I don’t even have kids and the character I most related to was Abby’s mom. I needed a scene of Mrs. Stevenson smoking a cig with a fellow executive blowing off some steam. She’s already a widowed single mother, who commutes four hours round-trip to Manhattan every day, just so her kids can live in idyllic Stoneybrook. Then she gets guilted into building, stocking, and running a DIY carnival booth, because a bunch of rich and/or stay-at-home parents who rely on child labor to raise their kids refuse to fund the arts. Watson paid for a carnival but he can’t cut a check to the district or pay off a school board member?Unfortunately Abby herself is pretty boring, because she’s an amalgamation of all the other BSC girls and brings nothing new to the dynamic. Instead of being obnoxiously bossy-loud like Kristy, she’s obnoxiously funny-loud. Instead of Stacey’s diabetes, she has asthma and allergies. She’s got a dead dad instead of Mary Anne’s dead mom. She has Mallory’s “ugly” curly hair and an extracurricular that will eventually be a huge issue for the club like Jessi’s ballet. Her one redeeming quality is that she’s not only pretty indifferent to Kristy’s manufactured authority, she finds it entertaining to defy her.Also Abby’s dad went to Woodstock. If this was an adult novel, they absolutely would have found a couple tabs of acid in that envelope.
