This is a reread, but the first one in over a decade. I can't believe I didn't list it here since it is one of my favorite books by Haldeman. It's a coming of age story about a young giant from Spring World sometime in the far future. He's like many other young men & manages to get into a fair amount of trouble as he figures out where he belongs in the world - well, many worlds in his case. He's kind of a college student on a space ship that travels around visiting planets which gives it an interesting curriculum. That makes Carl's problems pretty interesting, not to mention deadly, too.Definitely Haldeman at his best. Highly recommended.
