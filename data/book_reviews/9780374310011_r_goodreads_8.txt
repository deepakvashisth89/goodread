The father has a problem, so of course the beautiful oldest daughter is expected to solve it by getting married. The other daughters try to solve the problem as well, but they just end up getting married. And what leads to the "love" between all three couples? The physical attributes of the girls, of course! Because what else should men judge women by?Other than my problems with the gender dynamics in this book, I also find the story to be ridiculous in itself, and none of the characters are very endearing or likable at all.
