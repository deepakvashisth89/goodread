In my top ten favourite reads ever.
For a historian this is compelling - a mixture of reflection and interaction between those sentenced to imprisonment at Spandau. It contains a wealth of information. As a human I find this a disturbing and altogether heart-wrenching book. Albert Speer was a fascinating man with an unusual way of looking at things. This is easily the most interesting diary account that I have ever read - poignant with dashes of dry humour. I would recommend this to anyone interested in history, the Third Reich or in remarkable figures in history. Outstanding.
