the TLDR is I don't regret reading Vampire Odyssey, but I can't really recommend it given the increased general quality of more contemporary urban fantasy.The Vampire Odyssey reads like a spec script for a lower-tier streaming series. it's a fairly meandering plot with a significant chunk of the book consisting of one long shaggy-dog action sequence that ends up being largely irrelevant to the overall plot.The characters aren't terrible but aren't particularly deep, and the sexy stuff is too graphic to be exciting (at least to me) and is more distracting than exciting. all in all it's a fairly competent if standard take on vampires as a type of monster and some of the action bits are fun. Too many plot digressions kill the pacing, but if you like the old-school less respectable urban fantasy erotica you could do worse.
