This was a tough read for me having lived through the hiv/aids pandemic. But I'm glad that I made it through it - I think I was ready.I outlived all of my chosen family - some by more than 30 years. This book took me to the good times we had instead of all the sadness. And boy did we have some fun over the years that we had together!I know that most of my clan are gone. Either I was there when they went or went to their funerals or I was notified by a friend that they had passed over if we were not in the same town at the time. But there are a couple who simply vanished. Perhaps it was all too much for them or maybe they were poz/sick also and didn't want any attention for their last days or maybe they are out there somewhere wondering if I am still alive. If you are, I am.This is a great book.
