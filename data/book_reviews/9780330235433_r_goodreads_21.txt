Four modes:"I'm OK, your not OK""I'm not OK, your OK""I'm not OK, Your not OK""I'm OK, Your OK".The last one is what he attempts to get us to.Well at least I remember that much! This book seriously needs a reread which I fully intend to do. Highly recommended but it's "OK" if the book is not for you!
