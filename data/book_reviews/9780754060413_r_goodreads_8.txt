The Famous Five off on their holidays as usual, they found another adventure again.I liked the planes and the funny saying 'He runned away again!'Timmy is my favourite because he's a nice dog. I like Julian because he's determined and looks out for everyone. I like George because she has an fabulous Island and a good dog and she's got hair like me. I like Anne because she is in charge of the food and I'm always hungry. I like Dick because he likes a good meal and I'm like him but he's got short hair. I wish I could meet all of the Famous Five.The book made me feel very excited because of the planes. I didn't want to stop reading the book. I think this one is the best so far in this series of books.If you like planes or butterfly farms you should read this book.- AJH (age 7)
