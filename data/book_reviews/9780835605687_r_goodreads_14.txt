This marks the second time I've delved into Hoeller's work, the first being his enlightening book on Gnosticism that I read a few years back. If memory serves me right, I awarded that book a five-star rating. This current book has solidified my belief that this author is truly exceptional, and I find myself deeply connected to his unique writing style. His mystical prose beautifully aligns with my appreciation for Gnostic philosophy. Hoeller is undoubtedly the ideal author to pen a book on Jung and his profound journey into gnosis.The Seven Sermons To The Dead are inspired by Jung's transcendental mystic experiences or his experiential gnosis. The sermons translated by Hoeller not only criticized established religious orthodoxies but also presented a Gnostic alternative that encompassed fundamental psychological principles. These principles, including individuation, the polarity of the psyche, and psyche energy, were integral to Jung's career development.This book offers a superb overview of Jung's fundamental perspective. I strongly recommend this book.
