Nice story of growing up on the North Shore of Chicago suburbs. But Cohen never fills out Jamie enough to make me understand why he is so admired. He often talks about the "great things" Jamie has done, but never describes them. When he does it comes off as nothing more than sophomoric, drunk/drugged hijinks. When he does give us some of Jamie's talk it comes of as simple stoner BS. And I have a real problem w/ white suburban kids so in love w/ Chicago blues, pontificating on how this is the "real music". Ever notice how there are no black people in the blues bars other than relatives of the band and old foggies?Nice color of Chicago and NOLA, but otherwise I'm not convinced Jamie was such an mazing individual in his youth.
