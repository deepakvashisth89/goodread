Usually I write reviews of any book I read to clarify in my own mind what the writer was trying to do. Also, I offer ratings that are generous, meaning that any book worth reading gets a star or two. If it approaches what I thought the author was trying to do by way of literary exploration or communication, it gets three plus.At the start of Chapter 13 in a single paragraph of about nine lines, Ruth Rendell refers to eight different characters. That's about the depth of it. I finished the book, but I wish I hadn't started.
