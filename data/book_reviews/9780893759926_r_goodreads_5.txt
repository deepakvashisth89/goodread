Did you ever wonder what happened after “The Wonderful Wizard of Oz” story? To the characters left behind after Dorothy made her grand exit? The Emerald City is under siege and destruction! There’s a new villain! It’s up to some of our old heroes joined by new ones to rightfully repair Oz to its best and strongest state. Full of feminist undertones, this is definitely a rare piece of writing for its time! Pretty awesome considering L. Frank Baum brought this sequel to life after sharing with one little girl that if he got a thousand letters asking for a sequel, he would make it happen. Lo and behold, a thousand little girls and more all wrote letters begging for more Oz adventures. If it had been my time of existence, I would have been one of those girls.✋The Wizard of Oz was always a favorite childhood memory of mine and such a heartwarming story. I was very excited to learn that there existed an entire book series which unlocks so much more the movie left behind to explore! The sequel far exceeded my expectations, appealing to my creativity and inner spirit. And I loved every minute I spent having adventures in Oz. The writing was very relaxed and easy to follow. A pleasantly quick read. There were new characters introduced and lots of flashbacks and nods to the first book which always made me smile.You even get to experience different areas of Oz itself! I had no idea different parts of Oz were different colors. Though I did have a slight inkling about some of the more unique elements of Oz from watching the film, “Return to Oz” many times in my life. I am slowly learning that it seems that they took random bits and pieces from this sequel and possibly even from other books in the series to make “Return to Oz.” I always felt like it was all over the place 😂, but it always held a special place in my memory as frightening as it was at parts!My favorite character in the sequel was Jack the Pumpkinhead. I first met him in the film Return to Oz and fell in love with his character. So I was head over heels to see him appear in this sequel! I absolutely adore him. He is just so sweet and cute! Always worrying about spoiling! Poor baby! I also loved the flying sofa with the Gump head which also took place precisely as such in the movie. It just brought back a lot of fun memories, and I’m looking forward to venturing on in the series!Important Lessons Learned:✓ “Everything in life is unusual until you get accustomed to it.”✓ “For I consider brains far superior to money, in every way.
