One of the best books I’ve read for a while if not, ever? It felt like a strange sort of reflection of being raised by my Ma. But also a beautiful book about home, being a woman, growing up, how awful the world is, even when you stay good your whole life. The ending was the twist I didn’t see coming. Honestly I really thought Liza would do it but she didn’t and I loved that. And she gets to go to school. I just feel so happy for her.To be honest, it reflects how I feel at the current moment. Being responsible for yourself but for real. Figuring out who you can trust, who you want in your life. Letting go, even when you’re young and just feel like you need someone to hold onto, you can’t. You must choose yourself.I really did connect with Liza’s sense of being alone. I think although I’ve got a large family and always had friends, I loved being alone. I loved reading and playing and imagining and writing for hours on my own. My own world where I got to do and be and make anything.And lastly, I love Liza’s confidence, whether she thinks she learned it from her mother or own her own, or both, I admire it. And will draw from her when I need it most.Ok, now lastly, I can also see why Eve wanted to isolate. The world is so scary and men and capitalism and work outside of just taking care of a home and a child, is hard work!!! And teaching her child every day, that’s devotion even if it was not the most healthy.
