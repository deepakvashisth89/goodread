More like a 3.5. Case study examples provided were all extreme cases and may not resonate or feel invalidating. Religious undertones throughout, with actual scripture used as examples throughout as well. Makes sense with purity culture feeding into OCD/OCPD, but may not be appropriate for all. Overall the book is informative. It could have easily been cut in half. Lots of fluff talking about how great his clinic is. He also repeats himself a lot with his affirmations for relabeling and refocusing, which does make the book feel a little like CBT and could be helpful to clients. The author also does the narration and feels more like a physician lecturing than audiobook. I appreciated the end of chapter recaps and the last chapter being used for conclusion. Some might benefit from reading the book cover to cover but most could read the end of chapter summaries and the last chapter of the book to get the takeaways.
