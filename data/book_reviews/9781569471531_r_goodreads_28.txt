The book ends leaving the reader to finish the story. The ending, in my mind, could go several ways. I like to think good things happened to Vlado. But, you never know.Reading this book, I felt the desperation of its characters. The hardest part of the book was seeing how anyone, even co-workers and neighbors could be against you and your efforts. I get it, faced with starvation and death most people would stop helping anyone but themselves. This book makes you feel the isolation and the threat of living in a war zone and its raw horror in seeing people descend into their worst selves.
