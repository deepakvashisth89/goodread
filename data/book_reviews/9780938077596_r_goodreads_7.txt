This is more of a manual, a sort of "functional" book, rather than something to sit down and read. I've read many of Thich Nhat Hanh's books, almost all of them have been good, but this one is... I guess the word would be "useful." It lays out the fourteen mindfulness trainings (precepts, really) of the Order of Interbeing. It also includes some recitation ceremonies and the Order's charter, which are interesting and useful but mostly repetitive.Good stuff, worth the read. But it's not supposed to end with the read. You've got to put it into practice for it to mean anything.
