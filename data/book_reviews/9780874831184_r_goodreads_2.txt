When I acquired this book, it was with the hope that it contained really classic ghost stories. While entertaining, that's not really what the book is. The stories in the collection are mostly gathered from newspaper, magazine and journal accounts, with a wide range of writing styles and lengths. Some are ghost stories, others are stories of unexplained happenings.As a result, the stories are mostly forgettable, even if entertaining while you are reading them.So, I can only recommend this book to someone who just really, really wants to read an assortment of ghost and supernatural stories taking place all over the United States, for whatever reason.
