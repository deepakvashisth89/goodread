Genre: Modern FantasyGrade Level: 1st - 3rdSuch a sweet book about the normalcy of being afraid of things. Whether that be the dark or bugs/creatures, it's normal to be afraid of something. But I love how in this book, the main characters didn't let their fear control them forever, they faced their fear, were honest and told someone, and they grew out of their fear. Great for young students when talking about fears, doubts, and worries.
