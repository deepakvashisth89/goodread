History mainly through the participants -- in this case the British "Tommy" who did the suffering and fighting in the trenches of WW IIn this volume, the author focuses on the British experience in the first full year of the war, 1915. She provides narrative to link events and then (through copious quotes from journals, papers, diaries) allows the soldiers, leaders, and loved ones in England to tell of what they endured in the various major engagements both in N.W. France. S.W. Belgium, and the Gallipoli peninsula.
