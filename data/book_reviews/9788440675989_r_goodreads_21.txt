"A Simple Plan" is a simple plot: man finds a bag of money in a prop-plane that crashes in the woods. The pilot is dead. What would you do?For a simple plot, Scott Smith's debut 1993 novel is riveting and intense. The man takes the bag of money and hides it at home. He and his young wife decide to sit on it for a while. Then, the man's brother finds out.Nothing about what happens next is simple. In fact, it gets complicated quickly. And, tragically, bloody. It's almost biblical.This book spawned rave reviews from Stephen King and, a few years later, a film adaptation directed by Sam Raimi starring Bill Paxton, Billy Bob Thornton, and Bridget Fonda. Strangely enough, Smith has, to date, written only one other novel.
