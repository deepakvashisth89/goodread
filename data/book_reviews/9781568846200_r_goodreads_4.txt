Series of Tubes: The Book SeriesIf you're looking for an accessible guide to demystify the internet for a non-tech-savvy friend or family member, this book is a great choice. The author, Levine, does a fantastic job breaking down the complexities of the internet and explaining it in a way that's easy to understand.While it may seem like a basic read for more advanced users (like me), it's still a valuable reference to have on hand. And, who knows, you might even learn a thing or two you didn't know before.Overall, I highly recommend this book to anyone who wants to understand the basics of how the internet works. It's a great resource for both novices and experts alike.Not me, as I am a CCNA!3.8/5
