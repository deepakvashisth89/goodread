I really don't like books like this tense 1980's tale of murder and yet it was instantly riveting and I finished it in one night. It was apparent early on to the reader who the killer was. It seems a life in the country would be an idyllic place to raise her daughter after nine years of city life. Carl and Joyce each get a second chance at happiness and move to a remote house to raise their new baby. That first summer there is hectic enough with new baby Adam, and then Carl's daughter comes for the summer, the little neighbor girl, Anita continuously shows up at their house to play and make a pest of herself and the girls make a grisly discovery.I hated the ending of this tense book. The last line was especially unbelievable to me. This is the kind of book you say a prayer for your kids after reading.
