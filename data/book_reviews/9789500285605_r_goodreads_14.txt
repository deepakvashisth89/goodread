While I kind of enjoyed the first book,The Mystery of the Black Jungle, this one was just a such a DRAG to finish. It had basically the same plot as the previous one: a handsome, brave man with a slightly dark side meets a beautiful brave young woman, the two fall in love, and plan to escape from the beautiful young woman's evil captor. Oh my goodness, does the author have ANY creativity at all? No other imagination than this?I really wanted to like Emilio Salgari. I really did. But this staple, unimaginitive plot of his drove me insane with boredom. I was so happy to be done with it!He doesn't write bad, and itispossible to enjoy his books, I guess, but only for an audience of pre-teen boys, really. I never really did fit the "boys" category, am no longer a pre-teen, and I just have a completely different taste in books.The TV series was great, I still like watching them, but I will very likely not read anotherSandokanbook again.
