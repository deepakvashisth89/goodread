And, following the events ofThe Outcast, Tarod and Cyllan find themselves (separately) on the run, pursued by vengeful forces as they make their way to the White Isle, shrine of the Casket that, when opened, will return the Lords of Law to the world so that they can defeat the resurgent forces of Chaos.At least, that's the idea ...A fine conclusion to a fine trilogy that takes a truly unique perspective on the otherwise all-too-familiar conflict between Law and Chaos.
