I bought this series along with Vatta’s War off eBay, in paperback. I’ve just finished a race through reading, now to go back and read it again slowly, as I know I rushed through the introduction of new minor characters and threads the first time.Overall, I’ve enjoyed the whole series. Kate, the Ranger, is a charming addition in this book. I like the resolution for the Nutex women and children.I’m giving this 5 for entertainment and satisfaction. It’s not literature but it is excellent for space opera. Thank you Elizabeth Moon for many many hours of escaping into this universe!
