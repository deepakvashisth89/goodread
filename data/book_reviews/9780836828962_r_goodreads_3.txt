Schnitzel von Krumm, an adorable Dachshund, watches with anticipation as his family packs to go on a vacation. He begins to investigate the numerous boxes and bags that litter the lawn. While trying to be careful, he continues to get underfoot making his humans quite angry.

After counting the children and checking up on Grandma, the family speeds away leaving Schnitzel von Krumm behind. Will the poor Dachshund ever be reunited with his family?

I absolutely adored this book! This story especially touched my heart because I own a differently-abled Dachshund. I can totally relate to Schnitzel von Krumm's mischief making ways. My Dachshund is always underfoot as she is blind and deaf.

Lynley Dodd's illustrations are hilarious and heartwarming. Dog lovers of all ages will fall in love with Schnitzel von Krumm and his adorable facial expressions. This book is a great read and would make a wonderful gift for the Dachshund fanatic in your life.
