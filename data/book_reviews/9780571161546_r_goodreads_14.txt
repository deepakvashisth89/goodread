I read “oldest living confederate widow…” from Gurganis which I enjoyed and spurred me to read more from him. This collection of short stories is subtle. I thought the 1950s suburbia was rather a dull period, but Gurganis finds humanity in the mundane. The best story has to be the art contest. I think this is clearly a manual on “write what you know”.
I’m still not sure what to make of the title. Maybe these are the seedy and/or uncomfortable realities that white socialites don’t want anyone to know and are being revealed here.
