This book was a short novel geared towards 10-11 year old kids, in that sense the book was very easy to understand and to pull what the character was feeling behind the words she was actually saying. I really liked this book, even at my age it makes for a fun read. Ant is a young girl who feels slighted and ignored by her family in many ways. She feels her mother prefers her two light skinned ballerina sisters over her a "nut-brown" skinned daughter who has a smelly dog. Her dad is always quitting his jobs and moving them across the country after repeatedly promising Ant that it was the last time. As for her sisters she already has some animosity towards them because of her belief that her mother loves her sisters more, besides that her and her sisters do not get along and this is not made 100% clear if this is because of Ant's silent animosity towards them or because of their difference in personalities. Especially since this book is geared towards younger audiences the author does an amazing job on discussing the tough topics (mother-daughter relationship, family competition, unstable home life), along with broaching the subject of morals. I recomend this book to Everyone of all age groups.
