A great read.I really enjoyed the fun snappy dialogue.Nick has been disillusioned about marriage blise and when his wife dies he goes a little crazy trying to prove his stud factor to himself. First on his list is his sweet assistant Lucy.Being somewhat drunk he is not sure if that really was the best sex he ever had or was it the drink? He goes onto see if he can find it again with other women.Believing the night is a mistake Lucy tries to forget about it, but she still loves him. Amazingly they are able to work together for months until Lucy starts seeing a guy and Nick gets jealous.Nick starts feeling all these emotions and he starts making plans to overcome them so he can continue on his merry single way.But events happened and Nick can't let her go........You can really feel poor Nick's confusion and pain as he learns to deal with all these new fightening emotions. Lucy is a modern woman and stays strong. Even through she loves him she is not going to be his doormat. The passion between them is sizzling but also sweet.Was a nice sweet read. Well written and passionate. Both characters are likeable and connectable.
