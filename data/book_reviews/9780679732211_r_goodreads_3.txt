This was an enjoyable read. Jack is a short novel that follows a young teen boy dealing with his parent divorce and his father coming out. Although it’s never specified when this is set I would guess late 70’s or early 80’s. I’m not really one for ‘slice of life’ style stories so I don’t think I got as much out of this as someone else may but I did like the writing and the characters so I don’t regret reading it.
