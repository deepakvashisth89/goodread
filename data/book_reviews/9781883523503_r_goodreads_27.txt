Probably a 3.5 starMy first book by McDermid.Val really can write great plot and dialogues and creates colourful characters. The plot here is quite interesting with Val adding her own social and political commentaries to her story line. She has done some good research and that shows.However on the flip side, it is rather unbelievable that a single PI like Kate can do things that the police have no clue about. Some of the plot is also rather illogical like Kate able to follow or staking the bad guys for so long without being discovered.
