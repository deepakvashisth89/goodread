A bit of a mis-sell,Titanic Voices: Memories from the Fateful Voyageactually looks at the ties and impact that the White Star Line and theTitanichad on Southampton, England. It’s an interesting perspective to take on the Titanic disaster, but it becomes apparent rather quickly that the editors are stretching for material; and they end up including a lot of oral histories and other materials that are inane and redundant. Yet, they do offer an eye-opening look at how connected to the town the ship was; from all the merchants that supplied linen, dining ware, furniture, etc., to the labor forces that supplied the crew and other various staff. However, there’s so much minutia inTitanic Voices: Memories from the Fateful Voyagethat it becomes a real chore to read.
