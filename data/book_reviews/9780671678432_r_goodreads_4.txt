The forty-sixth #jackhiggins #martinfallon #hughmarlowe #harrypatterson #henrypatterson #jamesgraham novel #memoirsofadancehallromeo published in 1989. A significant departure for Higgins. The memoirs of a former soldier turned school teacher and his recollections of the women he meets and their sexual encounters together. Each chapter is named after a different woman. The novel shows his growth as a man and the improvement of his skills as a lover. It has some elements of Higgins’ usual tropes - military man, romance and atmospheric rain. But he hasn’t done a fictional memoir like this before. Set in 1949 and full of sex, some comedic, some lovely, some heartbreaking.Limited action/violence which is unusual for Higgins and there are none of his often used tropes: no medicinal brandy and no scene featuring the protagonist beating up a drunk trying to take advantage of a woman. Strangely I enjoyed this change of pace, particularly from the scholarly perspective of reading all of his novels in publication order, it seems there was more to Higgins than just action/adventure. It would have been interesting to see what else he could have written in other genres if his action/adventure novels hadn’t been so popular. I wonder if this might be one his more personal books as the protagonist does share a number of similarities with Higgins, former military man, teacher and novelist. It makes you question how many of the encounters in the book he actually experienced and how many are just extrapolated fantasies of missed opportunities.
