An absolutely uncalled for number of stories about people selling their soul to the devil, stories that read like bible fan fiction, stories where someone gets thee wishes granted by a genie/djin, a fairy, a snail, etc. and does something stupid with it, and vampire/werewolf stories... Not sure what I expected, but not that."Short short fiction" is what people are calling microfiction these days, and maybe 100 microfiction fantasy stories back to back was the first problem. Most stories had a sudden twist ending or ended with a kind of jokey punch line. The biggest sin was that the stories were in alphabetical order with an editorial comment at the beginning that was basically a spoiler.I picked it up because Isaac Asimov was the editor and though I haven't read a lot of his writing, I had high expectations. But now? I have very low expectations, and would hesitate to read something else he edited any time soon. Finishing all 100 stories felt like - surprise - selling my soul to the devil to get another book for my Goodreads challenge.
