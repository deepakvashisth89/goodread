A great novel about the use of sex surrogates in therapy. Dr. Arnold Freeberg is one such doctor in his treatments of patients with sexual problems.Gayle Miller & Paul Brandon are two such surrogates who worked at helping people.Yet, as before in Arizona, people thought them no different from prostitutes. Chet Hunter, a budding journalist and researcher senses a great story int he fact that Dr. Freeberg's clinic uses sex surrogates told to him by Suzy Edwards, its receptionist. Hunter, hoping for a job at the Chronicle, leads him to editor Otto Ferguson, the Rev. Josh Scrafield and Hoyt Lewis, DA.Chet is given money to go to the clinic for treatment of his condition: premature ejaculation. In the course of events, he begins to see Gayle as someone who only wants to help people like himself and Adam Benski, another patient.He finds himself in the middle as Dr. Freeberg and Gayle are charged with prostitution and pandering.As events unfold, Gayle & Paul begin to fall in love and complicate matters further. Their patients have also developed feelings for them. Paul's patient, Nan Whitcomb, is physically unable to have sex due to brutal experience with ex-boyfriend Tony Zecca. Leaving Tony, Nan is followed by him to the clinic and attempts to murder Dr. Freeberg.Adam Benski and Nan Whitcomb develop feelings for each other.Chet of course finds himself unable to go through with his plans. He even brings the DA around but the Reverend is unhappy, who sets to destroy Gayle by pitching poison at her face during a dinner given in Dr. Freeberg's honor. The attempt is thwarted by the Reverend's secretary, Darlene, when she tips his hand and its falls on his face.A happy ending for all. Adam & Nan are sexually compatible; Paul & Gayle are together; doctor recovers from attempted murder; Tony Zecca was shot dead.
