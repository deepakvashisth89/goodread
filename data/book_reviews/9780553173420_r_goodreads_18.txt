These books prove to be better as they go, and i thought book 2 is my favourite only to have this one break me and build me up! The setting, the new side characters and most importantly the endless fight between Carlos and Jason Bourne!It has been 13 years since Bourne lost his memory, and now Carlos got his real identity and he is coming for him! So Jason sends his family to stay with his brother in law and he starts to hunt for Carlos the Jackal! at the same time, the CIA has to deal with Medusa and its new existence!There are different settings in this book and that is probably one of my favourite things, we first start in the US and then head to the Carribbean Islands, and then Paris and we end up in Moscow! The place where the Russians train is so fascinating, the details made me question if there is a place like that for sure!?Mo and Alex are pretty much the best friends anybody could ask for! what they did for Jason and his family is extraordinary! They are his friends, but Kruppi? that man is something else, especially his sense of humour!The action scenes as usual were brilliant, the struggle between David and Jason even better!Every time Carlos escapes I get a headache! I actually never had a thriller make me feel like this before!Favourite scene is when Benjamin was explaining to Jason about the compound and the training, that is the most fascinating thing i ever read in a spy novel!Favourites:Favourite MC: (Alex Conklin)Favourite FC: ( none, not a big fan of Marie and there is no other character)Favourite book: (The Bourne Ultimatum)
