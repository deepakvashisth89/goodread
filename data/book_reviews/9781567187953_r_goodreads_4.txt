I really enjoyed this book and all the great information it gives BUT there was a lot of religious talk which is really a turn off for me. I am still searching to find good books of this nature that don’t quote scripture and talk of their God that created the universe. I found myself skipping pages and rolling my eyes at these parts and also at the somewhat tacky ways that are suggested to make contact with your spirit helpers. These ways are suggested as if all you need to do is get a Ouija board and sit back. This is my personal opinion, but I just don’t believe that some of these suggestions are responsible.Aside from this though, the book truly is a wealth of good information. With so many people being of different spiritual backgrounds and beliefs, we all just need to pick and choose the parts we take in and the parts we skim over and leave behind. This is a wonderful book for the basics on angels and spirit guides and welcoming them into your life.
