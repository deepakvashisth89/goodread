Slightly strange, going back and re-reading these for the first time in probably thirty years, to realise just how much influence they had on ECKO - the hero who's a mistake, the bad guy from the 'real' world, the speech patterns - and they're also a wonderful re-discovery, remembering how much I enjoyed tem.Have they dated? Of course they have. Portal fantasy, quest fantasy, chosen one narratives, anthropomorphised animals, have all descended past cliche. But these books and characters still retain their magic, and still stand high from the mass :)
