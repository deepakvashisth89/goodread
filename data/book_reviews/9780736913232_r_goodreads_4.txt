While there were some positive aspects, overall "Who Brings Forth the Wind" by Lori Wick just wasn't for me.It was painful to read at times. Tanner was an insufferable fool for most of the book. He was obsessive, controlling, and irrational, and I'm not sure that a man with that kind of behavior should be considered safe. This book maybe gives the wrong idea of how a situation like that would play out. I don't know. *shrugs*Stacy was often childlike in her behavior. And Tanner acted like a boorish father in the way he demanded of her.There are my quick thoughts.
