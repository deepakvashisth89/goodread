How did I miss this series when I was a kid? I would have adored it. And, actually, I do now, even coming to it so late. In addition to the fun "summer at the shore" millieu, it also harkens back to the days when child protagonists were not only smart, funny and creative, but very, very independent and resourceful, and had really big adventures, with adults only on the periphery. So much fun! (And now I'm going to go read them all, just as I would have if I had discovered them when I was 10.)
