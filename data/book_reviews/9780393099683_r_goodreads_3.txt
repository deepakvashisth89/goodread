An arduous read. I read 4 pages a day. Very tough going but I finished it. Only great admiration for the author pulled me through. Not recommended if you have not read his other works.Moby-Dick; or, The Whale, of course, but for something lighter tryTypeeandOmoo. Both are South Seas adventure stories. Later, when you're hooked, after the divertingWhite JacketandRedburnand the stories, you may want to move on to the oddments like this and the virtually unreadablePierre: or, The Ambiguities.
