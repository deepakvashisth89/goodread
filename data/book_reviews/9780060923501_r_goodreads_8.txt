I have never used an Ouija board - my life runs on Murphy's Law most of the time, and I've always considered the possible risk too high to chance it - but I'm fascinated with the iconography and the attitude to them in popular culture.This is a short and rather simply written book that goes into all the basics; history, attitudes both pro and against, believers in spiritualism and adherents of psychology, and ends with a practical and (IMO) reasonable set of guidelines for use. The book is actually a lot more even-handed about the subject matter than a lot of the other reviews here would have you believe.
