Why is this series not wildly popular?

I genuinely don't understand.

Leaving aside my obvious bias towards ancient history and Egypt especially (which I admit to and wholeheartedly embrace), this entire series was a rollercoaster. A rollercoaster, but a good one. The plot was plotting, the research was done and the characters seemed real.

I was ugly crying at some point because the book sucker punched me right in my close to non-existent Eastern-European feelings and obviously i didn't know how to cope with that.

It's been years and this is still in my top 5 ancient history/time travel books.

My greatest despair is that i cannot find these in paperback in my part of the continent. While i do esxclusively read ebooks, i admit to having a visceral need to have my golden favs in paperback.

It really doesn't helpt that I only read in English, I hate paying for overinflated shipping and my country has devotedly taken the last place at reading books in all of Europe for decades really.
