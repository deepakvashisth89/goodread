Going through the seasons with Wasserman is a treat. This book is particularly special to me as it describes a town very close to my family compound in Marion, MA. Although Wasserman describes the year round/all season community, a group I rarely have interacted with, I much appreciate the window she provides into her life. "Swimming at Suppertime" honestly divulges Wasserman's financial struggles, yet she is at peace, finding comfort in the rhythm of time.
