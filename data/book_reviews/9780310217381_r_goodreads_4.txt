As the author stated at the start of his book, if you're looking for a great end-times book that is true to most-believed last days event, this book is not it. HOWEVER, if you want a book that helps you understand the concept of dying to self and understanding the love of God, this book is for you. It definitely nurtured my soul once I got over the identity of the two witnesses he puts forth. Look beyond that and you will LOVE this book!
I have not read the first two books before this and didn't feel I missed anything by not having read them first.
