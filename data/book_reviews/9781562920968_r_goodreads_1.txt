Some of the stories and lessons in this little book are true gems. Others seem like poor fiction with a Scripture verse tacked on or a moral lesson added in the last three sentences. While it's enjoyable to read, it's not something I would hand to just anybody because some of the Scripture connections are questionable. For myself, it will probably not be a re-read. I think I prefer devotional books that make me think more. However, I may flip through the pages again sometime to review those few gems it contains.
