I enjoyed this, but I'm not sure it's going to wear well for re-reads. Every other chapter is a run down on Romulan history, and that could get a bit dry at times.I think it'd also bug people who like to see the series characters interact; McCoy is the only one you see much of. I quite like Duane's original character and the story she and McCoy are in is good, but I'm less enthused about the book as a whole.
