2022 bk 114. The start of a fun series that is very entrenched in the world of the 1980's/1990's. Set in a small eastern Canadian town where a love of archery is taught to each Lobelia Falls citizen from almost birth, this story centers around the death by arrow of the head of the town's water department chief. Along the way there are many gadzooks with a bit of lassoing involved before the murderer is uncovered and the victors ride off into the sunset. A fun start to a series.
