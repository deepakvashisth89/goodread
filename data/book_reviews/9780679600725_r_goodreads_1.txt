Absalom, Absalom! is a full of dark secrets and murky mysteries ghost story – the ghosts of the past rise and follow everyone everywhere.
All the human vices rotate around the procreative instinct...
And the male instinct of procreation rotates around woman...
...the other sex is separated into three sharp divisions, separated (two of them) by a chasm which could be crossed but one time and in but one direction – ladies, women, females – the virgins whom gentlemen someday married, the courtesans to whom they went while on sabbaticals to the cities, the slave girls and women upon whom that first caste rested and to whom in certain cases it doubtless owed the very fact of its virginity...

But if man builds his “economic edifice not on the rock of stern morality but on the shifting sands of opportunism and moral brigandage” then sooner or later a day of reckoning will come.
