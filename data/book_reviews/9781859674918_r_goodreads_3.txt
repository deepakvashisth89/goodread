The projects in this book are difficult, and at times there seem to be missing steps, like for instance how to get 14 strips of alternating golden and chocolate gingerbread to meld together so that you can curl them around a sausage of dough. Also to me the basic gingerbread recipes that are used over and over again are too dry and crumbly to roll out easily. Nevertheless once you have surmounted all obstacles the projects are very fun and impressive, and there and well done color photographs of the finished products for every project.
