I was very divided on this book. On the one hand some stories were great, such as "Mansion on the Hill", "Boys" and "Demonology." These shorts didn't drag on and the narrative never seemed forced. It was easy to care about the characters involved, and they all seemed like real people that could have jumped off the page.Others, however, were oddly wordy and formal for the subject matter. I'm not a fan of using obscure, formal wording when the same could be said normally.Difficult to get through the whole collection.
