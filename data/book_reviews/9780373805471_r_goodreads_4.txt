I was so disappointed in this story! I love a good revenge scenario and I thought I was getting the exquisite agony of Savage Destiny by Amanda Browning. From the blurb it sounds like the same set up. The hero does purposely seduce and marry the heroine in order to jilt her the next morning. All that happens. But then . . .

He gives her a really nice house. He leaves and she stays and *gardens*. Then her hapless brother (the reason for the hero's revenge) shows up after five years of no contact and lies about why the hero could possibly be mad at him. There were way too many scenes between heroine and her brother and the angst got more and more diluted. By the time the hero returns to confront the brother all the energy had been bled from the narrative.

The whole thing - even when explained- makes zero sense. Not that any revenge story really makes much sense - but we're suppose to be so overwhelmed by the heroine's angst that we don't notice/or care. *sigh* If you have a yen for this sort of story, try to get your hands on Savage Destiny.
