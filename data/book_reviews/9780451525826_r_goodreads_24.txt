is a dystopian and satirical novel written by Sinclair Lewis, published in 1935. It is an account of a fictional U.S. senator named Berzelius “Buzz” Windrip, who, in 1936, upset Franklin D. Roosevelt, the incumbent president, and captured the Democratic Party’s presidential nomination.

He accomplished this feat by spreading fear and promising that his draconian reforms, coupled with a return of the country to its heyday of tradition and patriotism, would bring back prosperity to a nation mired in the depths of a severe economic depression.

He then proceeded to defeat his Republican opponent in the general election, becoming an unlikely President of the United States.

Windrip quickly and skillfully subjugated the powers of the other branches of the government and soon rose to the position of the nation’s first dictator. He accomplished the concentration of power by establishing fascist policies that were similar to those of Adolf Hitler in Germany and Benito Mussolini in Italy.

Lewis’ model for the Windrip character was obviously the Louisiana populist, Huey Long, who used strong arm tactics to take control of his state’s government. He also had plans, the same as those of Buzz Windrip, to deny FDR the Democratic nomination in 1936. However, Long was assassinated in September 1935.

Critics, then and now, have criticized as being a slapdash affair that lacked literary merit. And there is some truth to that claim. But there are reasons for the book’s weaknesses.

Lewis was trying to hurry and finish and publish the book before the Democratic convention in 1936, hoping that the book might help influence the Democrats to re-nominate FDR. He began writing in May and in a herculean effort he published it in September, a month after Long's assassination.

Despite the criticism of the book’s merits, it is nevertheless his most popular book. Then, when another populist, one who possessed no political experience, won the election in 2016, the book sold out on Amazon.com. And not surprising, in response to the 2024 election it is experiencing another surge.
