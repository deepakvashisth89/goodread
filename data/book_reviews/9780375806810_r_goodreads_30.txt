A modern classicA semi-autobiographical novel of a boy and his pair of Redbone Coonhounds. Growing up in a poor family on a small farm in the Ozarks, neither ten year old Billy nor his family can afford to buy his heart's desire, a pair of coon hounds. He works and saves for two years to raise the $50 to buy the dogs.He trains the pair to hunt coons, that is racoons. Eventually they become two of the finest coon dogs in the country. After many adventures Billy, his pa and granpa take the hounds to a coon hound championship.The story is full of pathos, courage, love, grief, joy, Christian faith and heart warming family and dog adventures. A word of warning to the overly sensitive: This is a bitter sweet tale. The dogs are hunting dogs. There are multiple scenes of hunting and death. And as any dog lover knows, dogs don't live forever.I will admit that at least one tear came to my eye near the end. The same with the fine movie based on the book.
