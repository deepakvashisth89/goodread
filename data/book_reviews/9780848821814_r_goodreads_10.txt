The best of these stories, "The Nine Billion Names of God" and "The Star," are all-time classics that every science fiction fan should read.Another, "The Sentinel," inspired2001, one of the two or three greatest of all science fiction films.Also noteworthy: "The Curse," a somber meditation on nuclear annihilation, and Shakespeare; and the meta-fictional "I Remember Babylon," a cautionary tale about the potential of the communication satellite (a concept which Clarke himself pioneered) to destabilize and destroy civilizations.The worst aren't bad stories, but trivial by comparison to their more scintillating siblings.
