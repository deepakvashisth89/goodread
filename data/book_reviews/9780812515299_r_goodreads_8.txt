I liked this--but it's telling that looking over my shelves to decide what needs to go to liberate shelf space, this was one of the first books I drew down. Holzer is a competent writer--and that's not to be disparaging. It's easy to understand what caught the eye of film-makers in this story of a mother looking for revenge for her murdered daughter. The adaption with Sally Field and Kiefer Sutherland is a good, memorable film, as this is a good memorable book. Not that many books, that decades after reading it, I can call the basic plot to mind. But given that, there's just not enough in terms of either style or character or impact that makes me want to read it again and again--or watch the film again and again. So, bottom line, if this book comes your way, read it. I think you'll be entertained. But a keeper? No.
