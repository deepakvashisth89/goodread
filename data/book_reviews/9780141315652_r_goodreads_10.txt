I love this book so much. I've been attempting to reread some of my childhood books and I loved it then and still love it just as much now, if not more. I honestly think it can be read at any age. I love Hermux's kindness and positive outlook on life and people, I love the food descriptions, his lovely friend Mirinn and his adopted ladybug Terfèle.There is an intrepid and charming pilot to rescue, a mystery to be solved and a courageous little clockmaker driven by his good heart and the thrill of adventure.It gave me warm and fuzzy feelings, which were more than welcome, and oh so needed. Et pour finir, je me dois d'ajouter que la traduction française est d'une grande qualité.
