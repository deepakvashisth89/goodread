I read this for a class I’m taking on Tacitus and now know far more than I ever thought I would about the “Year of the Four Emperors,” AD 69. I read the Penguin Classics version of Tacitus’ The Histories and found the introduction quite helpful. In terms of the rest of the book, I tended to skim the lengthy descriptions of battles, preparations for battles, marches to battles, generals in battles, etc. I’m just not that fond of military history. In contrast, I loved so many of the comments (always snarky) Tacitus made about the various key players in the year. He definitely hated Otho and Vitellian (along with women, slaves, the common people, and so many more). Poor Galba Tacitus just describes him most of the time as old, and at one time comments that “He was lacking in vices rather than endowed with good qualities.” (p. 22).

Book 5 is supposedly devoted to the conquest of Judea, but it seems more a chance for Tacitus to write about every horrifying prejudiced belief he holds about the Jewish people. I ended up skimming much of it as well.
