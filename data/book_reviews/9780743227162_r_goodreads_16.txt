Short stories. I like the way the author writes. Some of the content was not of interest to me, and some was disturbing. Some I maybe just didn't understand. I liked Hotel of the Saints, about Aunt Jocelyn's change after Uncle Lenny's death and her idea for the hotel. The End of All Sadness was frightening, chilling, possibly true, in the way that fiction can tell the truth without worrying about facts. Same with A Woman's Perfume, regarding a girl's coming of age. The author provides insight into people's minds that I find unsettling.
