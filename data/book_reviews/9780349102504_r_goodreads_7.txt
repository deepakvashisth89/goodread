Being brought up with tales of Joan of Arc and of Bluebeard, I had no idea of their connection until reading this novel based on the later life (the Bluebeard part) of Gilles de Rais, one of military men behind Joan's exploits. Nor, for that matter, had I heard of her posthumous imposter and of their connection.Very well written, this book attempts to explore the juxtaposition of good and evil, great goodness and awful evil, in one person.
