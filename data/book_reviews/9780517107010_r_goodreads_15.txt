Picked this up by chance in the library because of the author. I had known that Buckley had written a spy novel but not that he wrote this whole series with the protagonist an alter ego character: Yale, C.I.A. etc. The book starts out OK and the quality of the dialog is not bad. Also the idea to populate the book with characters from real life; Fidel Castro, Nikita Khrushchev, Jack and Bobby Kennedy, who are essential to his plot, is good.
The problem is the plot is absolutely ridiculous, silly and contrived. Also, spy novels are supposed to be technically plausible, but this is filled with nonsense and impossible coincidences.
