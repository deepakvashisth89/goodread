Very good history of Rick Hansen travelling the world making awareness for the disabled. As I read through the book, it had some interesting moments which were pretty eye-catching. This book is great for anybody who is interested in learning about how disabled people overcome hard times in life, and I thought it was pretty interesting. There were some nice moments and hurdles along the way, which some may be pretty wild at times. I very much enjoyed reading about the adventure of a worldwide tour of a disabled young man whose goal is to raise money and awareness for spinal cord research and the disabled. It has a happy ending with big events going on, and overall, it was a wonderful story.
