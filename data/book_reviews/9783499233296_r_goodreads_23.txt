I have just discoveredJanwillem van de Weteringby reading multiple praises from Goodreads groups. So I decided to venture onto the first of his Grijpstra and de Gier series -Outsider in Amsterdam.I found both detectives charming, clever and realistic. They both have their faults and their feelings correct the wrongs of the world.This novel was a breath of fresh air. Instead of violent shoot-outs, door-busting bad-tempered men filled with testosterone, the reader follows these policemen and their superiors while they methodically try to solve a murder staged to look like a suicide. Even the multiple suspects are charming and civilized.There was a bit of sexism and racism in the book. But considering the book was written in the 1970s, a reader needs to pause before judging. those areas in the book made me squirm, but I quickly moved on to the actual story-line.
