DNF. Hard pass on more Riverworld. I liked the first two books just fine. They were roaring pulpy adventures, all good.This one has an introduction where Farmer informs us that this trilogy will not have an ending, and more to come in the fourth book. What?Why is this book longer than the first two put together and still not have an ending? There isn’t enough to these stories to get me to read another adventure in this world. A world that barely makes sense.I don’t care enough about this concept to continue reading about it, especially when I know I won’t get any answers. It was fun while it lasted.
