Parks Jones is a baseball player whose team is headed to the playoffs. And Parks is having an exceptional year. Which has just earned him a contract doing commercials for de Marco menswear.Brooke Gordon is a self-made woman, who after spending her youth in foster homes is now working as a successful director. She isn't too happy to be working with a jock on the latest commercial series she's shooting.Luckily Parks turns out to be a natural with charm that easily comes across in the ads. He works well with Brooke.Another pleasant book in the Language of Love series. I didn't think I had read this Roberts' story before, but a fire right at the end of the book seemed vaguely familiar.
