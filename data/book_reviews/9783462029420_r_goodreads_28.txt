Both the title and the story might seem to direct us into thinking this is an allegory, and several reviewers have alluded to that, but the writer has a much deeper purpose than that. The story of Isserley, a woman who scours the A9 in Scotland looking for male hitchhikers, is one in which the writer leads you cleverly in one direction to the point where you feel you know what the story is about and what is going to happen, and then veers off onto a new path and in different direction. There was a moment about two thirds through where I suddenly became so gripped that I had to continue reading right to the devastating end. Haunting book!
