The synopsis sounded promising, but Fowler succeeded a little too well at creating an absolutely ordinary protagonist: the guy is so boring I couldn't bring myself to care what happened to him. Something is not working in a book when you've just read a gruesome murder scene and left the protagonist enthralled by a stunning, mysterious woman - and then forget to read on and the book gathers dust for over a week.For readers who have the ambition to read everything by their favourite author, everyone else should stick to his Bryant & May books - that's where Fowler shines.
