Started off good, but what little there is of a plot is drowned in countless expositions through conversations. Everybody talks as if they have never talked about a subject before. It felt cheap. It also didn't help that the characters are flat and uninteresting. I only liked Eilan, but it kept switching away from her POV. Gaius was a blank slate, not really a character but a plot vehicle. The ending is very disappointing, but I vaguely recall Mists of Avalon having an ending that fizzled out too. Still, that novel is (Roman) leagues ahead of this prequel. You can tell The Forest House wasn't written fully by Zimmer Bradley.
