I really enjoyed reading this book about a man's experience studying wolves in the far north of Canada. I believe I read this book many years ago, possibly when I was a teenager. I've always loved reading books like this that tells of a person's adventure out in the wilderness, with wildlife, whether it's based on real life or in fictional stories. I think everyone should read this so they will understand that real wolves are not a threat to humans (although some dogs can be). It's just an amazing story based on reality. It's even more amazing that the Eskimo man he knew could even understand the wolves' language and knew what the message they were howling meant - and proved it a few times! I also found parts of the book humorous, especially a few bits in the first half: it made me chuckle. Like that his radio picked up someone in Chile (but could not get a single sole in Canada). Or how he was watching the wolves, wondering where they were, only to realize they were watching him! It's sad to think these beautiful animals are in danger.

This is the first book I've chosen to read in 2020.
