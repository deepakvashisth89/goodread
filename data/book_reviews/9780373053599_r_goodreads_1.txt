Another nice read by the author. The hero is an FBI agent who is thinking of hanging up his badge and buying a farm. His boss knowing about his intentions gives him an assignment to help decide; he is supposed to go undercover as a farm-hand at the heroine's grandmother's farm and find out if the former air-hostess heroine is a mob assassin. The premise was hilarious and I loved the execution. From the start it is obvious that the heroine can handle herself and she is quick to discover and point out the hero's ineptitude at the farm. These two also share a sizzling chemistry but the heroine has been burnt before and the hero thinks she is some femme fatale. He soon discover she is not of course! This one was fun, the romance moves really fast but it was believable. I liked both the h/H and even her grandma.
