Matilda by Roald DahlExhuberantly narrated by Kate Winslet with original music and sound by Pinewood Film Studios4 hrs 18 minutes at 1.xGenres: Children, Classics, Fantasy and Magic, Humorous FictionDespite 531 editions of this book with translations in several languages, I wasn’t aware of this story until it was mentioned in the last book that I read, as THE book which helped a character discover (as a child) and rediscover (as an adult) her love of reading. So, I decided to give it a quick listen…Matilda Wormwood is a precocious 5 year old somehow born to really stupid parents.Miss Trunchbull is her terrifying headmistress at school who seems to really hate children, especially children who can outwit her, like Matilda.Fortunately, Matilda will come across a few adults who will recognize and nurture her genius-a librarian and her first grade school teacher, Miss Honey.At first, the story almost seems like related vignettes illustrating both Matilda’s pranks and intelligence, but eventually it becomes cohesive as the fairy tale-like story reaches its conclusion.I did not realize that Kate Winslet was also a highly acclaimed voice artist in addition to being an award-winning film Actress but that became quite evident as she brought to life all of the characters with many distinct voices.Pleased to have made your acquaintance, Matilda!Thank You to the Chandler Public library for the loan of this FUN Audiobook!
