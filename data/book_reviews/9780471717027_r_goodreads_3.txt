I loved this story and it was good to see Mrs. Dove-Lyon makes another great match in this enemy to lovers marriage. I loved the unique twist they put on their wedding vows which made me laugh out loud. I also loved the strong woman that Olivia has become even though she has not always had things easy. I felt for Thomas and the secrets he has kept that is slowly destroying him. The story is well written and very entertaining. It is full of compassion and at times you just want to cry. It’s a wonderful story and I was glad for them to have their HEA but the road was not always easy. It’s definitely worth reading and I would highly recommend it.
