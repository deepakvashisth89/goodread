Yuki is nisei: first generation born in the United States and is an American citizen. Her parents are Issei, immigrants from Japan. They would become American citizens but are not allowed to do so. Then the Japanese bomb Pearl Harbor. Yuki's family couldn't even believe it at first. But Papa was taken off by the FBI then the family was sent to makeshift barracks until they could be sent off to some place called Topaz in Utah. The story is adequately written but never came to life for me. What did come through was the Japanese insistence on their loyalty to the United States and eagerness to prove their loyalty. While this book is essential to tell children what happened, I do not feel that the injustice to them was really given enough space. Even the elderly man killed because they were too close to the perimeter just didn't seem real. Recommended until better fiction on the subject is written. Teachers might want to give this to children trying to understand why their POC friends are afraid or just gone because they are "foreigners". We Americans do not seem to learn from our history!
