I am rereading the wonderful Wexford series. Wolf to the Slaughter is the intriguing third book in this series and just what I needed, a British old police procedural mystery.Rendell honed her craft on this series that went on to be a tv series. There is something for everyone who loves a good police mystery whodunnit.Crime, murder, mystery, thriller of the old kind is just what is needed to escape for a while.
