I always enjoy reading the books that came along in parallel with the successful series. Rising of the Moon is an excellent read with a number of engaging characters and a stream of action that makes it hard to stop reading. I believe that this came after Back Bay, but before the rest of the Fallon stories. Its depiction of South Boston in 1916 is vivid as are the scenes that take place in Ireland. A lot of complexities make this a satisfying read and the hopelessness of never-ending rounds of revenge are highlighted by the actions of one man who decides after great personal reflection not to play that game. This is a message that has extreme relevance today. The importance of the Irish in Boston and of the Boston Irish to Ireland is very clearly delineated, without ever being dry or overly emotional. Martin strikes a nice balance, which is essential for one walking this tightrope.
