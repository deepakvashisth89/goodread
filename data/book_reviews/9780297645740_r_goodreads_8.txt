This is a sad tale of woe on multiple levels. Excellent information gathering, I found that it was often not presented in a manner that followed a logical timeline - the fuzzy timelines made the first 3/4 of the book a bit difficult to follow. This book primarily tells two tales, the first being the exploitation of the the riches of the East (mainly Burman/Myanmar) by first the Chinese, and later by British colonizers stealing and looting what the Chinese had taken from Burma. The second tale covers the devastating situation in the jadeite mines of Myanmar in the very late 20th/very early 21st centuries that resulted in a full-blown AIDs crisis for exploited miners and others barely surviving in the jungle mining towns. Just so very sad all the way around. No wonder it took me so long to finish this book. It is worth reading but is not uplifting in any way.
