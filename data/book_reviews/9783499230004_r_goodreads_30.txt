The birth of a self aware computer -- always an interesting topic. But, let's hope that when the machines do become aware they are nothing like the self-centred, pompous, prig that this uber-intelligence turned out to be. I can't imagine having to spend quality time with that puffed up personality! I completely disliked it by the time the story was over.The other players in the story were all equally interesting, and the distopian near future certainly gave me pause to think -- I could imagine humans behaving this way (badly -- needless to say).My only quibble was with the "humanizing" on the main characters -- they seemed like the machines and the machines seemed "human". That may be one of the points that Kerr was trying to get across, but it didn't ring quite true for me. An interesting read -- just pronounce "bleep" over most of the conceited computer text.
