This ended up on my to-read list one way or another, so I thought I'd give it a shot. But without a serious investment in learning the culture and literary background of the time, and teaching myself how to read the unfamiliar style, I felt that I wasn't doing myself much of a favor slogging through it, and at my age I recognize that I would rather invest in other pursuits. But it wasn't a total loss, as at the very least I got to discover such gems as:"Already this hot cocke, in bush and tree, in field and tent oreflutters his next hen""...whom Dildoes, Bedstaves, and her Velvet Glasse would be as loath to touch as Joseph was"and of course"But these do mee no harme, nor they which useTo out-swive Dildoes..."So at the least I added the word "swive" to my vocabulary.
