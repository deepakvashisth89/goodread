Peter Ustinov goes to the top of the class for originality with this one. The concept on which this book is based is that the Old Man is God and Mr Smith is the Devil and they come back to earth so that God can check on his creation handiwork and how it has evolved. Unfortunately in New York the run foul of the FBI who keep pursuing them no matter where they go in the world. This results in escapades in London, Moscow, Israel, China, Japan and India. All very entertaining encounters with the local citizenry and as a result the book becomes very hard to put down.
