I became fascinated with J. Paul Getty when I visited the Getty museum in California on a girls' trip. (Five stars for the museum, by the way). I wanted to know more about how the art was acquired and what made a man like Getty donate it to the public for their education and enjoyment. I wanted to know more about how the museum was built. That part of the book, came late and was very short, but it turns out it was enough.This was a decent memoir that seemed like it really was written by the author himself. He had a stately, turn-of-the-century air about him. He led a dignified and interesting life, and of course, he was filthy rich, and who doesn't like to read about the filthy rich?He shares his views on politics, business, relationships, civilization, morals, movie stars (many of whom were his friends) and money. An interesting read, but not a necessary one.Don't miss the museum though!!
