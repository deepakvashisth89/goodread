This is the first volume of a trilogy, but it can be read as a standalone. I read it as a part of the monthly reading for February 2026 atThe Evolution of Science Fictiongroup. The novel was nominated for the Hugo Award in 2003 and won, the only Hugo for the best novel among the author’s trophies. I am unsure, but probably it was the last Hugo-winning novel that was originally serialized in a SF magazine. The second volume,Humans, was nominated as well but lost toPaladin of Soulsin 2004. The last Hugo nomination for a novel the author received in 2010 but he continues his writing to the present; his latest novel,The Downloaded, was published in 2024.The story starts at a neutrino-detection center in Canada, which suddenly starts to experience a massive inflow of neutrinos (funny, just recently I finished another book,Нові Темні Віки. Колонія, which also starts at a neutrino-detection center in 2140 Antarctica, and also a spike is registered). Turns out that someone was in heavy water, pools of which are used for detection. People working at the center save a strange man…Ponter Boddit, who works on a quantum computer experiment, and whose world, from his routine description, while native to him, is not ours. The experiment went wrong and it was Ponter who almost drowned. And what distinguishes him from people from our world – he is a Neanderthal.The rest of the story goes parallel in both universes, ‘ours’, where Ponter acclimatizes to all concepts strange for him, while in ‘his’ universe, his partner is prosecuted for the likely murder of Ponter.Overall, an interesting concept of ‘other Earth’ where Neanderthals never went through an agricultural phase, but created a society, which sometimes uses higher tech than we do.
