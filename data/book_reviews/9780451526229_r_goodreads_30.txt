There’s a gentle sadness running beneath A Little Princess that makes Sara Crewe’s story so memorable. Sara begins life at Miss Minchin’s boarding school as a wealthy, imaginative child adored for her kindness and intelligence, but everything changes after the sudden death of her father, Captain Crewe, leaves her penniless.Forced into hardship and treated cruelly by Miss Minchin, Sara clings to her sense of dignity and imagination while forming quiet friendships with characters like Becky and Ermengarde. The story moves slowly by modern standards, but Sara’s resilience gives it emotional warmth throughout. It can feel sentimental at times, yet the kindness woven through the book makes it an enduring and comforting classic.RATING: four out of five stars ⭐⭐⭐⭐.
