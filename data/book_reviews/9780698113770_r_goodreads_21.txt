My kids and I read this book as part of our homeschool course on ancient history. The book is well written, age appropriate, and gives a good look into the lives of early humans. I appreciated the variety of prehistoric animals and plants that were mentioned. This book also prompted a good discussion about the value of each human life, regardless of whether humans agree on its value or not.For one child and me, the extremely detailed descriptions about painting were tedious. Two other children didn’t mind. The ending was unexpected and climactic. I would recommend this book to other homeschool families.
