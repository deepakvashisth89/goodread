This book is very useful for its intended purpose. There are lots of interesting facts about the origins of words and phrases, as well as words that I didn’t know before reading this book. The author also had the good will to tell the reader when he doesn’t know the origin of a word. Shame about the style.The author was a columnist, and therefore writes his word origins and definitions in a repetitive style. First, there is a funny first paragraph where he makes jokes and sometimes gives a false story about the origin of a word. Most of those made me roll my eyes. And then he explains where the word comes from, or explains various theories where a word might come from, or admits he doesn’t know.The writing style might work for a column in the newspaper or on the Internet, but it is boring and very repetitive in print. However, it is a little interesting. I would have preferred the sections in chronological rather than alphabetical order, just to see how the author’s style evolved. Even if it is unfunny and condescending at times. Too much “complaining about things in the nineties” humor.Thankfully, I did learn a lot about words, so this book wasn’t completely useless. Though it is the type of book that I read in a week, all the knowledge slips out of my brain, and I have to reread. Except I don’t really want to reread this book, so all of my new knowledge of words might be forgotten. Oh well.
