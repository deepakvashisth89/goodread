So yes, the factual information presented by Jerry Pallotta in alphabet format (from A to Z) in his 1989The Yucky Reptile Alphabet Bookis certainly sufficiently interesting and also seems scientifically accurate enough to provide a decent albeit also just basic introduction to reptiles in general (but with there being no included bibliographic whatsoever materials to be found inThe Yucky Reptile Alphabet Book, there is sadly and unfortunately also no way of actually easily verifying this, and indeed, this definitely seriously lessens the teaching and learning and in particular totally erases any supplemental research value forThe Yucky Reptile Alphabet Book).However and indeed this is a very massive and heavy duty however, I am to be perfectly honest also and equally quite simply much too angrily annoyed and furious that similar to Jerry Pallotta’s series of picture books on insects (on bugs), which his book titles label as supposedly being icky, with his alphabet book on “yucky” reptiles, Pallotta obviously and once again is using a silly and ridiculous gimmick by somehow and in my opinion for no real and justifiable reason using a book title that is actually at best insulting and denigrating to all reptiles, for me to consider more than one star forThe Yucky Reptile Alphabet Book. And no, not even the fact that I do aesthetically really enjoy Ralph Masiello’s accompanying artwork is going to change my ranking from one to two stars this time, as I am definitely majorly fed up and massively furious. For indeed, reptiles are obviously not even remotely and inherently yucky and for Jerry Pallotta’s book title ofThe Yucky Reptile Alphabet Bookto in my humble opinion blatantly assume this, that is not only silly and ridiculous, it also does nothing to render reptiles as animals to be appreciated and not to be inherently feared and/or despised. And while I do think thatThe Yucky Reptile Alphabet Bookactually features much of interest about reptiles, and in fact does not seem to be at all textually negative, sorry, but there is NO WAY I am going to be recommendingThe Yucky Reptile Alphabet Book(for in my opinion, Jerry Pallotta’s penchant for using in my opinion inappropriate and misleading book titles really is something that needs to be strongly criticised and condemned).
