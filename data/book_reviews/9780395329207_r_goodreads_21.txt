As someone who did a mini-thesis on the subject of castles, this was as interesting as expected. The author explains (with pretty drawings!) all the process to carry out a construction of a castle and the town attached to it, as well as all the defense mechanisms the people would use at the time through the story of a fictional town set in late 1200’s.

There’s not much more to say than, if you are interested in castles at all, then this is a very quick read that paints a clear picture of the construction of these fascinating buildings.
