I love the writing of Rebecca Goldstein but this is certainly a strange example.The book tells the story of a 47-year-old German-American philosophy professor at a small college in upstate New York who has sealed off her emotional life. She lives only for her study of Plato and Spinoza, until she falls in love, or at least in heat, with a handsome young student.Some parts of the book are a bit too dense for my taste, with long passages about philosophy, German history, the character's strange relationship with her dead father, etc. Plus, there are too many arcane and foreign words.
