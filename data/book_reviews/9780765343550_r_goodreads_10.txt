This novel just worked for me, even better than the first. I do think the whole "alien species evolved under the influence of an ancient alien artifact" angle is interesting. And not like the 2001 Monolith, this is an unintended side-effect. Worlder's shared reality fits really nicely into the universe Kress is developing here. There's something about ancient alien archaeology that scratches my brain- rediscovering what was once plain. This book gave me a similar feeling to Rendezvous with Rama and I can't pay a higher compliment.

But the characters are even better! I loved watching Kaufman balance the tempers of his crew, make difficult choices, handle diplomacy within the crew, the worlders, and the navy. It's all so well done, and the resulting climax is exceptional.

My only critique might be the Worlders. They're fairly interesting, but reading Enli chapters was the least exciting portion of the book. The worlders aren't really justly considered by the humans, who care more about their own survival and the science behind the artifact. So, while the worlders feelings are interesting, they really don't matter to the story being told.

I liked the first book, I loved this one, and I'm excited to see how Kress ties it all together in the finale.
