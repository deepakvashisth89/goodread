This book really wants you to know it was written in America by American for Americans. Between chapters on American presidents, American sports and American TV stars, it was really difficult to find topics of interest. I enjoyed the chapter on Pepsi vs Coca Cola, Marilyn Monroe conspiracies and a few other shorter chapters. I just think there is way better books to enjoy these out there. Perhaps I should've only read this book in the Bathroom in short session to enhance the experience.
