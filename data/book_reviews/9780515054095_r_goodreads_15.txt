I loved this book!A country house, a weekend party filled with guests who really hate each other, a snow storm that traps them all together and a serious of attempts on various guests lives!That plus a murder and Inspector Alleyn what's not to love and what else more do you need?Well!! There is also the references to the "boomps-a-daisy" dance that made me laugh! That along with a Footman who is caught doing the dance whilst the murder is taking place...A perfect read if you want a Country house mystery!
