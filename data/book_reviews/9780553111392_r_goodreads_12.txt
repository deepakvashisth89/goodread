I thought on picking this up it would be a bit of froth and I'd pass it to a charity shop when done. WRONG! It was at turns tense, at turns educational and also very easy and riveting to read. The cover is by Gerald Grace who did a lot S-F covers at that time but is also known for his western art!The story is about world food shortages leading to a very novel solution - shrink man! The story tells of a the journey taken by the so-called Micronauts of this new terrain. The journey educates us as we encounter insects from down where they are! Some are terrifying; some benevolent, but all are interesting. A great read
