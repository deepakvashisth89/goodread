Although I found some of this book interesting (being a dog lover) most of it had me horrified and pissed off.She let her dogs run loose through busy streets and highways, let them breed indiscriminately (including letting one of her dogs kill another dogs puppies) and threw them all in a outdoor pen when they became inconvenient to her lifestyle.I don't agree with most of the ways she treated and raised her dogs either.I don't recommend this book.
