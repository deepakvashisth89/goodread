Another highly entertaining early Jack Ryan novel.Compared to the other books so far in the series, this adventure is set a little closer to home as the U.S. President starts a war on drugs with the Colombian cartel.It's been years since I'd seen the subsequent movie adaptation, so I came into this rather fresh.I must admit that I preferred the Cold War setting of the earlier books, though Clancy's detailed descriptions of the high tech military procedures alongside plenty of action certainly made this another worthwhile experience.
