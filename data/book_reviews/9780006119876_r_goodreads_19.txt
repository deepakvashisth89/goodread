When she's good, it's so good!My heart was pounding as I finished this book because I know Holt can pull a fast one at the very end and my heart was gonna be broken if she did.But she didn't.She let me fall in love with the enigmatic lord of the mansion who was giving big Mr. Rochester vibes if Rochester was a flirt and possibly a murderer. The way Holt can write a mysterious man always leaves me breathless. The way her men are ready to sacrifice themselves for the woman they love is so refreshingly redeeming and I am a sucker for it every time. Every time.But if Holt weren't such a consummate writer, her men would come off as two dimensional. It's because she can take her reader into the world she's creating that her characters come to life. I can see, smell, and hear her settings. And when she sets up an untrustworthy supporting cast, the story is better than any movie or tv show I've ever seen.I finished this book with a cliche sigh of satisfaction. I'll run back to Holt's books every time current authors disappoint me. She's such a glorious safe space.
