Another Lyons den success.

Thomas and Olivia are complex characters linked by one other Cynthia.

Thomas loved Cynthia but lost her and during child birth. Olivia thinks Thomas killed Cynthia and wants to take revenge. Book starts out with both of them being forced into a marriage.

Lots iof hard discussions later, Thomas and Olivia learn to leave their pasts behind and move together for a better future.

Really lovely read with depth of emotions
