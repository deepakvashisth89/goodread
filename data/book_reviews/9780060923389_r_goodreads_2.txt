It's always nice when an appalling character is eliminated. Not there yet, but am anticipating the awful and narcissistic Mrs. Graham getting popped off. Since it's a Miss Silver book, Miss Marple's secret adversary, it will be bloodless and discrete but that's enough for me.REVIEWAll in all a good mystery. Wentworth gives Agatha Christie a run for her money with her Miss Silver mysteries. Not quite as groundbreaking but equally prolific and equally veddy British.One issue I have is that other than Miss Silver, her female characters tend to be wet blankets and lacking in any sense.
