Another fun installment in the 87th Precinct series with once again Carrela carrying the lead. As usual, McBain keeps a couple of plot threads going here, one (the case Carrela is on) concerns some 'floaters' that keep turning up on the river banks; all are women. The second, headed up by Brown, looks into some confidence men; the first reported case is of a young women being swindled out of 5 bucks, the next a Boston business man getting tagged for a few hundred...Also as usual in this series, the plot takes second place to the interpersonal dialogue and McBain's reflections on life in the big city. He really plays with cons here-- from advertising, used car salesmen, 'I love you' (e.g., I want to get in your pants), politicians promises, etc. It seems life is all one big con! These books are like candy but also short and sweet-- you can read them in just a few hours. Interestingly enough, my copy has an inside stamp from "Army Libraries-- the community recreation division, Panama branch." Never knew the army kept libraries on its bases. 3.5 light stars, rounding up!
