Much like the previous book, while I enjoyed the story (and this one is a stronger story than the previous), I find the explanation for why Dhamon has become what he is to be very weak and not believable. I'm also not fond of most of the characters, Rikali in particular.

Also similar to the first (and the previous trilogy), the narrator is good, but the mispronounciation drives me crazy.

All in all, I do recommend this book to Dragonlance fans, though it is far from Jean Rabe's strongest entry.
