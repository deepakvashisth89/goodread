During the days of my probation, I had somehow become infatuated with Colin Dexter's creation and his investigations. Why? I don't know. Perhaps Morse was, in my opinion, perfectly placed to provide us with a view of Oxford that's known, yet unknown. The mysteries were strangely complex, with living humans turning out to be far more interesting than the deceased. And most importantly, there always would be a deep sense of pathos and compassion, that put a different hue to the red and black of the plot.This particular novel wouldn’t be amongst the top stuff. But still, it’s very good indeed.Recommended.
