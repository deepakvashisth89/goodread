It's amazing to see how Kafka lets you feel the world around him through his writing. A world that is depressing, absurd, and worthless that even an optimistic person would think otherwise. Rare to find, to say the least. Somewhat similar to Dostoevsky and Dazai, this series of Kafka's writings showed a hauntingly beautiful depiction of absurdism and existentialism yet his writing showed a hint of his feelings.

I loved every story, but my most favorite one is Metamorphosis, you can never go wrong with that; a picture of how the world spuns away from you as soon you became a worthless as an insect is the cruel reality. One thing I love about reading such deep and dark stories from Kafka to Dostoevsky is that it shows you what the human condition is; what we're capable of when no one is looking, prompting you to ask the question who you are. Challenging reads like this are my forte. Would definitely recommend buying the whole version of Kafka rather than his best selling Metamorphosis only.
