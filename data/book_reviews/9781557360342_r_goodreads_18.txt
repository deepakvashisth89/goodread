“Little Bear looked at him steadily and nodded. Omri opened the flap and Indian and horse stepped out into the morning sunlight.”
― Lynne Reid Banks, The Indian in the Cupboard


Reread. I did not love it honestly. I'm way too tired to do a proper review right now. I liked the beginning, got bored in the middle and was skimming by the end.

My feeling is I just would have preferred it as a short story or a novella.

The thing is the magic of the idea wears off for me. I get the premise and I've read many many books on Native culture but maybe that's the problem.

Most of those reads were in childhood. My all-time favorite is "The light in the forest." It seemed to me this was trying to be both a very serious story AND kind of whimsical. Kind of difficult to be both.

That does not mean I didn't like the characters or wholly enjoy the premise. But it got tiresome. Also it doesn't really feel like a children's book. If that makes sense.

The adventure parts didn't do it for me because it isn't the type of genre that I really get into. And I kind of knew how it would end before it ended especially being that it IS a children's book.

I still feel this is one of the best titles for a book ever. Just the title evokes such imagery. And it was well written. It just really isn't my type of book. I think it really would have been if it was a bit shorter.

I think at the end of the day, I'd have preferred "whimsical" over adventure but that's just me.
