Here's a wonderful encyclopedia of country-by-country background info and statistics. Each new edition of the book gives a listing of key historical events (including a historical background), brief demographics data (births, deaths, education level), economic data (major industries and economic sectors), natural resources and energy resources, current political leaders, and much more. Few resources -- yes, even online ones -- package country information like this book does. And it delivers a highly objective international perspective (not a U.S. publisher!) difficult to find anywhere else. Yet another vital reference tool for quick facts and background info.
