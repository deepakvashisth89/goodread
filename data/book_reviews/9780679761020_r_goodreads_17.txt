Maxwell was born in the first decade of the 20th century, grew up in a small Illinois hamlet, and ultimately made his way to New York, where he was an editor at literary publications for decades.
These stories were written over various decades of his life, and explore themes of childhood experiences, small town families, the nature of memory, the nature of success and failure, among others. A few are set in urban NY, but most are explorations of his small-town Illinois roots. I enjoyed many of these, although I liked his memoir/novella So Long, See You Tomorrow better. This edition also includes several brief modern-day fables collected at the end of the book.
