Edward Talbot is not a likeable character and as one other reviewer said a bit of a nob. The story told from two perspectives is well written and there are twists. I enjoyed the novel and the other main character Colley the parson was also a bit of a nob and naive.The heavy storyline is also lightened with many amusing passages. Not sure I would like to be a passenger from England to Australia in the Napoleonic era and glad to have avoided the badger bag.
