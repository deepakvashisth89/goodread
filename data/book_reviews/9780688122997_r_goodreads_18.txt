A long, dense book, though the writing flowed with plenty of live scenes, e.g., in restaurants, with storytelling and dialog.As an aside... I came of age during the women's movement of the '60s but *never* blamed men. Instead, I saw our toxic culture oppressing both women and men. I always hoped for a men's movement where men could bond in a safe place and become their authentic selves. A men's movement was started some time ago (a few of my friends were involved), though the groups have drawn only a small percentage so far. Perhaps this book will help spread the word.I especially liked the chapters on Vietnam, the Promise Keepers, and, surprisingly, Sylvester Stallone. My own family fit right in, with my silent veteran dad and my unfathered brother. So the stories were eerily familiar.A long slog, but worth it. Recommended.
