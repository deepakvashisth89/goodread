This book is one of my favorite if not for a long time it was my favorite of more than 40 books about health that I own.

David Wolfe's book dicusses how you can create massive changes in your health using the raw food principles.

I've listened to a couple of his audios and attended some events and workshops in Amsterdam and I find his message to be fresh and mind opening when it comes to nutrition. Though I will warn you, that for some people it might be too "healthy extreme", especially if you think that chickenbreast with some salad is a healthy meal.

David's main argument comes from Dr. Edward Howell which is the Enzyme doctor that prooved that coocked foods is dead foods and raw food contain more "live value" or the enzymes, nutrients, and energy that we need to regenerate, rejuvenate, and re-energize.

The new Sunfood Diet Success System will benefit raw food beginners and experienced raw foodists. It is a "success system" rather than a book about raw food book because it is packed with great insight and philosophy about raw foods and life in general, which most of it is mental than physical. It's a great starting point for whoever want to take control of his health.

One thing I didn't like is the poetry part into it, but that's only a personal preference of mine.
