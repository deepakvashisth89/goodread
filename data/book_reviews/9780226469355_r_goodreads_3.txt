I am Canadian born, but have read very little Canadian literature, other than the legendary Margaret Atwood. I'm very glad to have now read Margaret Laurence, who is also a well regarded figure in Canada.This is the life story of Morag Gunn, a realistically flawed person. So I certainly don't agree with all her choicesMorag's daughter Pique never meeting her step grandfather was unforgivable & Morag knows thatbut this feels like a very authentic journey through a late twentieth century life. It does have such a 1970s feel - life before money became so all important. The detail is awesome, so you really feel like you are in Morag's head.Laurence's books aren't easy to get hold of it NZ, but I will certainly keep an eye out for them.
