I really liked Savage Season a lot, the first book in Lansdale's Hap & Leonard series, but Mucho Mojo was even better. Lansdale locks into a groove here, hitting his stride with his characterizations, his stellar dialogue, and the portrayal of this friendship. He's also able to balance the dark nature of his stories, and their heavy themes with legitimately hilarious banter between his characters, and a keen sense of place. Despite being able to easily pick out who the villains were, the book did not feel hampered at all. His authorial voice is quickly becoming one of my very favourites. Looking forward to volume three in the not too distant future!
