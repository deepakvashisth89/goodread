Anthony Price... what a talent. Here we have espionage done in such a gentle way... Cottage gardens, tractors trundling down country roads, green fields and small villages with their church and graveyard, yew trees and dappled sun. Everything is so civilised as we drink a pint in the local, listening to the country folk chatting...Anthony Price's world of spies and assassination is so civilised... so... well, it could be happening right now, in that tea room over a cup of Earl Grey and scones, or eating a packet of crisps and supping a dark ale whilst you watch the local hunt get ready...I love the world Anthony Price has created.
