Okay, THIS is more like it! The setting, plot, writing and characters all seemed to finally gel for me with this series and I had a blast with this one. Elvis only gets more and more likable and Pike only gets more and more badass. A quick aside about Pike, I really don’t get the hate he gets sometimes. The character’s whole charm is that you don’t ever know what’s going on in the dude’s head other than he’s Cole’s buddy to the death and will put a motherfucker on ice with the greatest of ease. What else do you need? Angst? That would only ruin the fun of these books. I did find it funny that even Cole has no idea as to how Pike can see in the dark with his ever-present sunglasses on.
