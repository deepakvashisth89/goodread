Fascinating study of admittedly spotty evidence for the existence of a Briton war-leader that could've given rise to the iconic series of legends. Alcock marshals what evidence exists, making inferences where warranted, to arrive at a "more likely than not" conclusion for the existence of a historical Arthur.Some of the discussion is fairly technical and is hampered by poorly-executed illustrations (particularly the maps) but the care taken with the evidence is evident throughout. Warning: this is a fairly dry topic, but a fascinating look into the difficulties caused by lack of physical evidence from post-Roman Britain.
