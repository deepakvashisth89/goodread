Although I haven't read any MZB in a long time, her The Mists of Avalon and the Darkover series remain some of the best works of fantasy. She is, simply, an amazing author. The Colors of Space, an early work of hers written in 1963, displays many of the author's amazing qualities of writing style and messaging. The best scifi tells not just a story of the fantastic, but also relates something about ourselves in the here and now. MZB was always very good at this, and here she speaks to prejudice and war. works well for a young adult audience, although parts of it haven't aged very well, particularly the descriptions of spacecraft computing. Overall, very nice.
