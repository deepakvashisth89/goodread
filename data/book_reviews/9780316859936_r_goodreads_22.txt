I hadn't read anything by Linda Grant for a long time and had forgotten what a perceptive writer she is. In this novel set in Liverpool, she shows understanding of being a woman, ageing, being Jewish and the inheritance from parents and grandparents. The novel focuses on Alix and Joseph but along the way we understand Sam, her brother, his wife, Joseph's wife as a separate entity from him, which maybe he is not ready to see himself. Grant is unique in that she can make the seemingly trivial aspects of how a woman dresses and looks central to her self esteem. I will definitely be reading more of her books soon.
