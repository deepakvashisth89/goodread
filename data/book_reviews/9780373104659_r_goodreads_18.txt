Well that felt a bit drawn out. Cusp of the 60s sensibility but written by someone much older would be my guess. Matt, the artist, is very taken with 18 year old pretty childish Amy. There's misunderstanding after easily explained misunderstanding and lots of jealousy with the odd you deserve a slap. Oh yes, it's one of those but not as bad as they can be. Lots of passion but too much washing the dishes and deciding what to wear.
