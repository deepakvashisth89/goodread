Spotted this book at a charity shop in Keighley, UK, last year, and I couldn’t stop myself from buying it when I knew that it was priced at £0.10! 🪙I’ve read some books about poetry before (such as Why Poetry by Matthew Zapruder), but this one is more targeted towards younger readers who are interested in starting to write their own poems. As such, the content covered is appropriately more rudimentary, but still interesting for me to read. ✍️I wanted to know how the author would simplify the art of poetry writing to younger readers—which I believe is not as easy as it seems, and I think he did a rather reasonable job of making it less daunting. He also teaches it with an encouraging tone, which is nice! 👍My rating: 4/5
