Koontz again thrilled me with his ability to set a scene and create a true sense of dread. His gift is in putting his characters in situations where they (and you as the reader) feel helpless in the midst of unsettling and spooky circumstances. In this case, the circumstances all revolve around occult happenings in and around Fort Wyvern, the abandoned military base that the main character, Christopher Snow, frequents.Aside from that, the book is a solid mystery about the disappearance of several children. Over the course of the book, we find out why the children were taken and how it connects up with a serial killer and the strange past of Fort Wyvern. The book gets exciting right out of the gate, with dark wanderings in old buildings, troupes of killer monkeys, strange deaths, and the a fight in the night. From there on out, the book remains intense and lets up only rarely. It finally builds to a climax that really stands up there with the best I have read from Koontz.Thanks for reading.
