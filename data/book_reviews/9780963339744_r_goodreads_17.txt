So I actually finished this last night but there were two short stories at the end and it was getting late but this is the review I wrote right after I finished it :

That was probably one of the most upsetting, uncomfortable, brutal and depressing books I’ve ever read. I don’t know what I was expecting when I started this book but it definitely wasn’t that.

I felt so gross and icky the entire time and I have never felt so bad for a character before, and Ruth is an underrated villain cause she’s truely awful ( and that’s an understatement).

And books usually never make me cry but this one got me. Maybe it’s the way Jack Ketchum writes his books that makes them so easy for me to picture but it’s truly great writing.

It’s very effective and he doesn’t really beat around the bush too much. He just kinda says stuff as it is and it works.
