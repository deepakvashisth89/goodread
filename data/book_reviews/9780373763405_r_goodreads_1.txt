In this book, we have the story of the rich, gorgeous, hot, and sexy trauma doctor turned cosmetic surgeon, Dr. Justin Webb. He's been in love with his friend Winona, who always turned him down every time he asked her to marry him. And when Justin sees an opportunity to get Winona to the altar he, grabs it.Winona is a foster child turned cop and usually deals with troubled kids. Everyone in Royal loves her. Maybe that's why she found a baby left on her doorstep. She wanted to keep the baby but knew that being single, she didn't stand a chance unless she accepted Justin's marriage proposal.Aside from the blossoming relationship between Justin and Winona, I also enjoyed the investigative works, diplomatic relations, history, and mystery elements of the story. It gave the story exciting twists and turns that will surely have your head spinning. I also loved both Justin and Winona; they seemed real. It's always fun to read a book with characters you can really relate to.And I think that being the first book in the new set of Texas Cattlemans Series, it bears the responsibility to set the framework. Because of that, it feels crowded introducing many characters that will play a major part in the next books in the series. I admit I had a hard time remembering who's who. But I still enjoyed reading it as much as I enjoyed the other books.
