First of all the title, cover, and description of the book doesn't represent the plot line of the book at all?! The cover should have been dark with a pic of a gothic castle on the front or something. I was so surprised by the story but I liked it. It reminded me so much of a Nancy Drew novel from back in the day.

After the death of her great aunt, Cora, Schyleur returns to her gothic mansion in hopes to follow Cora's will and "help put the ghosts to rest". She meets Cora's lawyer, Trace, and they begin the process of figuring out what is going on in the castle. Mystery noises, flickering lights, ghostly figures, secret passageways, windows without rooms, rooms without windows, a long ago tragedy in the garden, odd characters showing up around every corner and building suspense will keep you from putting down the book.
I would have rated the book higher but the ending was so quick that it was hard to catch up with all that was happening. If you are/were a fan of Nancy Drew you'll love this. And follow Schyleur/Nancy and Trace/Ned through their suspenseful yet romantic adventure.
