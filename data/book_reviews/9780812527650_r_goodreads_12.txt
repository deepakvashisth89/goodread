I should have taken notes while I was reading this since I had so many complaints, but by the time the novel puddled to its anticlimax I seem to have lost my coherent complaints. I remember thinking the language was unnecessarily convoluted—that the characters were meant by the author to bridge into some profound truth about life and death and absence that haunts every living creature—but the characters are so banal and the events so muddlingly rendered you can’t get anything out of the book; you just can’t wait to get out of it.
