What would have happened if Queen Elizabeth I had an illegitimate son and he lived to tell the tale?This book gives an answer of sorts.The book follows the queen's son's adventures and it also follows the story of Elizabeth and Robert Dudley's relationship.I liked the vivid descriptions about horses,english towns,country life and battles.Although,I found the relationship between Robert and the Queen plausible I felt I couldn't say the same for the story of Arthur Dudley.His exploits seemed far fetched at points but this aspect didn't ruin the book for me as I just kept in mind I was reading fiction and went with the story.If you like loosing yourself in the Tudor era you may want to read this.
