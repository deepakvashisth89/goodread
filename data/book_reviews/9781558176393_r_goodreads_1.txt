So good!

Stardust & Shadows by #JanelleTaylor is a throwback from the 1992 & is book 2 of the series. I read this book way back when (it was my mom’s book!) & I have been thinking about it for weeks. So I decided to get the E-book to read it again! Dual POV.

The last thing Dr Jana Greyson, PHD remembers is falling asleep with Commander Varian Saar, her new fiancé, after being rescued from being kidnapped. She wakes up & Ryker (Varian’s half brother) is there telling her she has been deathly ill, the wedding had happened to each other & Varian was engaged to Cass (the woman who had kidnapped her.) Jana is convinced it is Varian pretending to be Ryker for some assignment as Varian is notorious for doing whatever it takes for an assignment, but then he’ll say something that makes her think it’s Ryker. 🤔 They make love (Jana is convinced it is Varian). Then the next morning she see a “live” news interview Varian talking about his upcoming wedding to Cass. Ryker continues to confuse Jana - is he or is he not Varian? Ryker & Jana leave on Varian’s starship to try to save Earth. Jana finally believes that Varian is not impersonating Ryker. She starts to believe in him, and starts to like him, though she is still in love with Varian. Ryker saves Earth. They head back home, then are kidnapped by a neighboring galaxy ruler. Ryker is able to get them to an escape pod & they land on a nearby planet. Ryker finally tells Jana the truth… he is Varian. Jana is furious with Varian & tells him there is no chance of them getting back together. They are rescued by Varian’s team. Jana agrees to help Varian finish the assignment. Jana finds out she is pregnant! She gives Varian 2 weeks to settle everything so that they can marry without the baby’s paternity coming into question. While waiting for Varian to get back, Jana is kidnapped (again?!?!) by Cass. Varian rescues her. They marry a short time later. Eek! Such a great book!
