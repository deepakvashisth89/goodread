The only thing I have to comment on for the Prince/Pauper part is skepticism about their families being human, while Wishbone imagines himself as Edward/Tom but still as a dog... a little weird? Why wouldn't the imagined King Henry and John Canty, etc. be dogs as well? I'd understand in a show (that's a LOT of dogs to train!), but this is a book! I don't know.The "Joe as coach" part is... okay, I know I don't know much aboutLittle League, but if the players are *FIVE YEARS OLD* and are regularly NOT PLAYING THE GAME [doing cartwheels, braiding each other's hair], do they really belong in Little League? Further, I'm not even remotely involved in sports, and I *really hate* the parents of players who get mad when their children's team loses or even starts losing. THEY ARE FIVE, and I question if they're even interested in playing T-ball, if they're acting the way they are in the game! Give me the dad inLittle Teammate: Let's Play Baseball, who knows how to support his child, *win OR lose.* Heck, I was hopingJoe's team would lose and teach a lesson about good sportsmanship, but nope—gotta defuse the angry dad who doesn't know how to handle his FIVE-YEAR-OLD losing a game. Who's REALLY the child here?Okay read for Wishbone/sports fans, but I don't know that I would read it again.
