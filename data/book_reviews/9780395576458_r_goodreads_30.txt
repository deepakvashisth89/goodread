Awesome read!!!!!!!

Farmer Giles of Ham by J.R.R. Tolkien is just fantastic! I can't say enough good things about this author, he’s my absolute favorite! I recently grabbed this book at a bookstore, and it was so much fun!!😍😊
The story is packed with laughs and adventure, making it super entertaining from start to finish. The characters are great, especially the clever Farmer Giles. Tolkien mixes fantasy and charm in a way that really pulls you in.

If you want a fun and exciting story, this is the one! I totally recommend it!!!👍✌️
