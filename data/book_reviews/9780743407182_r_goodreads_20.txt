4 to 4.5 stars. "Sailing to Byzantium" by Robert Silverberg includes five novella. What I found most captivating was the range of these stories, each one excellent, but very different. I also enjoyed the brief introductions to each story by the author. If you wanted an introduction to his work, this might be a good place to start.This is my first encounter with Robert Silverberg, one of the old masters of sci-fi. I'll be on the look-out for more of his work.
