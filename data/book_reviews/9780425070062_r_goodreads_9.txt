A solid 3 star read, maybe would give it 3.5 if I could. Jake Pickett, Uncle Jake to his niece Amanda, is a man in his 70s that discovers he has a strange ability. Not only does he have some telekinetic powers, popping off bottle caps, cleaning his car in the blink of an eye, etc. but he can also communicate telepathically with his physically disabled niece, Amanda.

Uncle Jake has lived in his home for over 50 years and doesn't plan on leaving. That is until he learns that he's been living next to a toxic waste dump and that the chemical company responsible is out to capture anyone who has been affected by the toxic chemicals.

There weren't too many surprisingly plot twists in this one, though there was a nice one in the last 5 pages.
