Nabb's books precede those ofDonna Leon, but I am not sure that I will love Marshal Guarnaccia the way I do Guido Brunetti, or love Florence the way I have come to love Venice. Guarnaccia spends most of this book in bed with the flu, and a good deal of the focus is on a pair of British policemen and a young man who is still in police training. That doesn't really set things up well for a series where Guarnaccia is supposed to be the main character! I will try the next book, but am not sure if I will get much further in this series.
