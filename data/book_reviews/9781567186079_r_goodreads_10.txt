Saunders recounts her experience as, first, a cruise hostess and later, as a cruise director. I have always loved the TV show, The Love Boat, and this book that inspired it has been on my TBR list for years. Sadly the book has not aged well. I tried to read it with an eye to the decade this came out in, but it was such a disappointment. The author was the first female cruise director and some of her stories were funny, but mostly she came across as crass, vulgur, not at all PC and really pretentious (is braggy a real word?). Her descriptions of people who were unattractive were very out-of-date and the descriptions of the crew had such racial stereotyping and hyperbole that I was skeptical of every story. At least toward the end she gave some quaint locale descriptions. It was interesting to read how much the cruise industry and vacations have changed through the years. I can't believe I'm saying this, but the book was not better.
