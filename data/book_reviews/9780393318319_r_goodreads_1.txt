The long-running Harpur & Iles series by Bill James (a Welsh pseudonym,notthe baseball analyst!) stylistically combines elements of gritty police procedural and comedy of manners. Cynical plotting and calculation, by both "bad" and "good" guys, is very strongly portrayed. Desmond Iles is one of the memorable "monsters" of modern fiction. Like many series, the early entries are somewhat better: Iles comes into his twisted element in the early/middle books. This is the first H & I novel I read, the third in the series, and one of the very best. Only qualifier is that the language/dialogue isveryBritish, which could be difficult for some.
