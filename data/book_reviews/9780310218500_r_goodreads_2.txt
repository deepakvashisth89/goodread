I thoroughly enjoyed this story! It starts out as a missing persons story and then it also includes the very serious subject of spousal abuse, although it's handled very well. I love the main character - Merry Kramer. She's diligent, caring and fearless in her quest to right wrongs as an investigative reporter! Her co-worker's husband suddenly vanishes which leads to her then writing about a home for battered women and then she gets tangled up in so many other things! Merry has a knack for that, it seems! There's even humor thrown in now and then with her knack for getting into crazy situations! Her sweetheart, Curt, is an artist and he loves her just the way she is but that doesn't stop Merry from being jealous of the woman who sponsors Curt's art. Some situations were downright hilarious to me! However, Merry gets herself into some terrifying situation also when the people who are responsible for the missing person catch on to her figuring out what's going on!Great story!!!!
