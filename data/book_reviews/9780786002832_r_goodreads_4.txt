I don't know that I've ever had such a negative reaction to a book as I did with this one. It's been about 15 years since I read it, yet I still feel annoyed at the time I wasted reading it. I can overlook many flaws if I like the characters, but I didn't like or respect them, especially the stupid heroine. I don't throw books away. If I don't want to keep a book I either donate it or turn it in at the UBS for credit. Except for this one, which I happily stuffed in the trash. Wendy Corsi Staub has some fine books in her backlist. This one, however, is not one of them.
