How much of this is fiction, I’m not sure. What I will say is even if half of this is true, it just illustrates clearly that Dylan Thomas was an addict, and spent much of his life in pain and anguish. How he didn’t get the professional help he so obviously needed is beyond me. Everyone seems to have brandished him a “bit of a laugh/prick/mad genius”. I fear the truth is much more sad.Speaking of truth. There’s definitely a shade of guilt in John Malcolm Brinnin’s writing here that I just can’t shake. Obviously, it being from his own perspective, we must take every detail, big or small, with a pinch of salt. One feels he’s tried very hard to make himself look a charitable saint. Maybe he was. Unless he was wearing a wire, or has totally perfect hearing recall, I find it very hard to believe that he was able to remember and then write down entire monologues by Dylan.
