I didn't know whether to give this book three or five stars since it is set in medieval times, a period of history that I truly hate since there was so little respect for life. It is a brutal time. The first third of the book reflects that brutality and filled me with horror. The next third was a bit better, and then it goes back to the ugliness of the times. A very difficult book to read, so I took it slowly and finally decided on five stars because the writing is exemplary. It brought the period to life, and disgusted and horrified me to the point that it was difficult to pick up and continue, proving that it is good writing. I don't think I will re-read this again, however.
