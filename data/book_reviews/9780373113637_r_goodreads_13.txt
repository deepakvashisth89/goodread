Pretty decent, with my main issue being that the h carried the farce on waaaaaaay longer than she should have, AND that her internal reasoning/maturity level felt more on the lines of the free-spirited teenager she was pretending to be than a successful, established late 20's London surgeon.

Some good points:

* The H, unlike many, many HQL heroes, does NOT like the idea that he's attracted to an 18 year old, is squicked out by it, and fights it as hard as he can. He is NOT down for cradle-robbing. This is a fresh of breath air, to say the least. (LOL)

* The main actual issue between the two is that the h is a career woman or more a vocational woman and the H thinks women can't be both wife/mothers AND fully devoted to their career, so he would expect a wife to give hers up. Miraculously, the H ends up capitulating, the h does not give up her career, and they are already working out plans on how to compromise and work things out together. It helps that he has a very efficient staff to run his home of course.

So, to the summary: this is along the lines of by the same author, but slightly less OTT and farcical.

The h is a talented surgeon of some type whose boss has insisted on her taking a 6-month leave of absence from anything related to medicine, so she decides to stay at her godfather's country home which is next door to a large estate (not um council estate, more like big ancestral estate) that's been passed from the late owner to his computer-genius nephew. Nephew has converted it to being half-live in laboratory for his "think tank" and most computery employees, half living quarters for himself.

Anyhow, vie shenanigans involving h's godfather's dog sneaking next door, the H finds the h on his grounds hunting said dog and decides she's come for a part-time housekeeping staff position.

She gets offended that he mistook her for a teenager, so instead of clearing things right up she decides to take him up on his offer and act like an apathetic, listless teen... anyway she falls for him, but there's an OW, his idk personal secretary who runs the entire house/estate but who isn't a housekeeper per se., and h keeps finding excuses to keep up her pretense, she gets accused of stealing a couple of things due to H's prejudices, and of course instead of just telling the H she's actually a very good surgeon and not an aimless teen drifter with no ambition, the OW manages to fall down some stairs and gets major stab wounds all over her body, which the h has to emergency-operate on.

Anyhow, some miscommunication and personal growth later, HEA.
