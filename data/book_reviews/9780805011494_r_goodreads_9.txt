A 90 year old woman undertakes a dangerous mission, a trip from her drug dealer infested Caribbean island in the middle of December. Why? To warn representatives of Her Majesty’s Government of deteriorating conditions there. What’s the answer? Well, not 007, because Patricia Moyes writes Agatha Christie influenced cozies.So Scotland Yard sends her husband and wife detective team, who has a very nice vacation, even though one takes a bullet to her leg, and the other one keeps getting in trouble with authorities.Order is restored, even if the US DEA isn’t much help. The result is a reminder of why cozy writers should stick to bodies in libraries. The mix of 80s problems does not meld well with the 50s style.
