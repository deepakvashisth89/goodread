This is a wonderful series, and Winter and Night may be the best of the first eight. As the author continues to alternate the main focus between the two PIs. This one is mostly about Bill Smith. It pulls you in from the start and continues to engage the reader wonderfully until the end. It also shines a light on the abuses of High School Football.Some Mystery-Thrillers are more thriller than mystery. Not this one, the reader has a nice ride until the end. S.J. Rozan knows how to make you feel you are actually there as the story unfolds.
