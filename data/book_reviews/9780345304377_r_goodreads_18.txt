3.5/5 stars rounded up to 4 stars for Goodreads!This book is a fun, unholy time (if you can stomach the misogyny, homophobia, and the fact that the protagonist's boyfriend is an absolute asshole. Ah, the 70's.)A young woman burdened by a dark past, an apartment listing too good to be true, strange neighbors (including...*clutches pearls*... LESBIANS!), a blind priest, and the gates of Hell, all wrapped up in 200+ pages of an occult horror classic.And yet, my favorite part of this book is the birthday party for the neighbor's cat.
