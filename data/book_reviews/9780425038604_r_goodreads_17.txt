B-:Marsh offers yet another distinctly different unfurling of mystery here, conjuring up a retrospective portrait of a formidable woman from the views of those who knew (and loathed) her most. It makes for an interesting whodunit, although I wasn't as enthralled as I have been in the past (the characters in this one are particularly annoying). I rather like that her wartime books signal a departure from normality for Alleyn, and that his role as an Antipodean hunter of espionage demonstrates the need for flexibility when fighting a greater enemy.
