Will Sackett, of the well known Sackett family, finds more than he bargained for in this adventure.

As the story starts out, he and a friend are caught and fighting for their lives in deadly Apache territory. His friend is severely wounded by the Apaches and IS going to die… the question is when? Mind you, NOT IF, when? Sure death is imminent, but will he lay on the ground helpless in the sweltering sun to suffer further brutality by Apache assault? His friend begs, insists, pleads for Sackett to shoot him in the head so he can die more quickly and end the terrible painful and suffering at the hands of the Apaches. As much as Will doesn’t want to shoot his friend, he doesn’t want him to suffer either. Mercifully, he shoots the man according to his final wishes and insistent pleas.

Now safely back to town and out of Apache territory, he soon meets a woman claiming to be his sister-in-law (being married to one of Will’s brothers). She tells him her very young son (so Will’s nephew) has been stolen by the Apaches and implores his help to get him back. (His brother is a lawyer, not a fighter and couldn’t do this besides getting himself surely killed.)

Being a “Sackett” that is fiercely loyal to their family and name (even if they haven’t seen or spoke to each other for years), Will is willing to venture into that extremely dangerous Apache land again to retrieve the boy… but the mission is viewed as suicide by almost everyone…except 2 men (who are also loners) who volunteer to go with him.

And there is where the true adventure and the real danger starts! Against impossible odds, there will be battles seen and unseen, battles known and unknown with complete surprises in the finale.

For fans of the western genre and Louis L’Amour, it’s an enjoyable and worthwhile read!
