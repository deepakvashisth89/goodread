Have you ever had an acquaintance whose life seems to be forever on the dung heap? Years pass, and every single time you try to spend time with her, she treats you to a litany of her miseries-- and never seems to have anything to say about having tried anything new to get herself out of that monumental funk?That's the friend that this book is all about. Stegner describes his heroine's surroundings in gorgeous prose, but bottom line is: she's whiny, horrifically selfdestructive and just a bummer to be around.
