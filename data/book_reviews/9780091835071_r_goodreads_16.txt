I really loved this book for a few reasons, but mostly because Judy Nunn skillfully crafts a setting that becomes the real star of the show. Of course the tale itself wending its way from Italy to Australia through Boston, Galipoli and the Somme following the fortunes of the Brearleys and the Giannis keeps the reader involved and the number of coincidences, whilst far fetched actually added to the pleasure of the saga.I would recommend this book as a good read.
