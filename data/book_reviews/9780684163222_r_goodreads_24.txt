The wolf has been invented. There is no wolf. There is only a figure moving like fog through the twilight. Science has only drawn a circle and named it Wolf. Propaganda has turned it into a monster, from the early church to the beef industry. Sacred stories from the Eskimo to medieval England to the Middle East and beyond have gotten it a little closer, by never fencing the wolf in completely, and Barry Lopez has written a book on nature like none I’ve read. By breaking each of these circles and more and looping them into a spiral he examines the idea of Wolf from many different angles while never deigning at exhausting its Wolfness.
