The priest has been Kincaid’s mentor since he was 12 years old.The priest becomes a suspect in the murder of a female member of the congregation and reaches out to Ben for help. Before that murder can be solved, another female member is murdered during a freak blackout at the church and the priest is found near the body.The priest declares his innocence after being charged. Ben an his team work feverishly to try to build a solid defense, only to find more obstacles in their way
