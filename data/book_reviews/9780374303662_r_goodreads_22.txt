I loved this and really wish I had read it sooner! Annie on My Mind is the first sapphic YA romance (with a happy ending) ever published and it's a classic for a reason. It follows a high school senior who slowly falls in love with a girl and realizes she's gay. But of course there are challenges, many of them centered on the private school she attends. The writing is simple but evocative and the slow unfolding of their relationship is really beautiful. While things are different in some ways today, I think this still really stands the test of time. The version I read included an interview with the author many years later and it was cool to hear what the book has done and how things have changed. Plus her own story of growing up in the 50's & 60's and falling for the woman who would one day become her wife (after being together for many years). Loved this.
