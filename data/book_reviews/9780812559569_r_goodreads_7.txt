The short is that this was fine. I’m glad it is only 80 pages because it didn’t excite me and the storytelling was bumpy. Though every now and then I thought a concept or angle was interesting. Basically Vymes is on a space station and he and his staff interact with The Golden, humans with the capability to travel further in the galaxy than other humans. Also side question, is this one of the earliest trad pubs SFF with poly and bi rep? It’s kind of fascinating, it’s so queer-normative and it was written a long time ago, but I guess it was during the Free Love period in the US so I shouldn’t be surprised. Anyhoo, glad I finally finished a Delany.
