This is my third James Baldwin novel and, for reasons given later, I truly wished for it to be a 5-star review. As with the other two (Giovanni’s Room and Another Country) I’m really impressed with the writing, the non-linear storytelling, use of flashbacks and the narrative told from different voices but, like the others, there’s just something that holds me back from giving a full rating. Maybe the heavy-laden religious elements are a factor? Maybe it’s something indescribable? Maybe I’m not fully understanding the themes?In any case, it’s a worthwhile read and I should leave it at that.On a personal note, my US Goodreads friend, Josh, took the trouble to post me a couple of Baldwin’s books (extras he found on his shelf) as a gift. This touched me greatly. There’s nothing like the joy of arriving home with a surprise package in the shape of books waiting for you!I wish I could have given that extra star by way of appreciation, but thanks again, Josh. I’ll get to Baldwin’s Collected Essays sometime soon.
