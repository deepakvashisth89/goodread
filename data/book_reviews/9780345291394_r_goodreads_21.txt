4 Stars! Enjoyable and quirky and fun. I love Simak’s imagination and unique pastoral writing. The plot was a little chaotic at times but overall good. Lots of great characters and a variety of aliens. I wish maybe Decker’s storyline turned out a bit more positive as he was really becoming one of my favs. Overall I thought it was a really good read. Enjoy!
