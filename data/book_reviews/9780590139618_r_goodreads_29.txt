Actual Rating 4.5

I read this book when I was younger and loved it. Rereading it many years later, it still an excellent read and I feel like I got much more out of it this time.

The world building is wonderful. Pullman created such an interesting world, parallel in many ways to ours, but full of fantastical beings and ideas. The originality of the world and setting of this book are most of what makes this read so compelling. There’s certainly some info-dumping present throughout, but I didn’t find it bogged down the read as much as it could have.

Reading this as an adult, I appreciated and understood much more of the philosophical, scientific, and theological themes that are incorporated and explored during this read. I also quite liked the inclusion of daemons, and some of the details surrounding their existence. Pullman excels at writing emotions as well as well-rounded characters, and I quite enjoyed the growth that Lyra underwent throughout the read.
