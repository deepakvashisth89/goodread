3.5 stars. A tragic, unpleasant, sad, disturbing, powerful, memorable short read about young Colombian hit men murdering with or without cause. The novel is semi autobiographical and set in Medellin. It is narrated by an old gay man named Fernando. Fernando describes a number of brutal random killings by his young teenage lover and others. The author vividly describes the city, it’s churches, stone monuments, the ineptness of the police and the blind efficiency of hospital staff.In the 1980s Medellin was considered the most dangerous city in the world.This book was first published in Spanish in 1994.
