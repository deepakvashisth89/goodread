Only has a story to tell and its unique and heartwarming. If it doesn't make you look at your own dog in a new way, I would be surprised!This book starts off with George and Alice, a rather non-descript couple and there life and how Only comes to be in it and what he means to them. As time goes by, you see more and more of what Only thinks about, longs for, dreams for, not unlike anybody else. We follow his adventures and his return home and finally the end, which was the biggest surprise of all and completely not what I was expecting.A brilliant writer and a highly reccomended book.
