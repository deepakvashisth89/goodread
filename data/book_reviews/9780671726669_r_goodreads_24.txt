I just finished this book on the Betty Broderick case “Until The Twelfth Of Never”. What a fantastic book. So full of information I’d never heard before. Wonderfully written.
My only criticism is that the Author Bella Stumbo was somewhat biased to the point of it being a bit annoying. Over the many months of interviewing Betty for this book Stumbo & Betty formed somewhat of a friendship and from the start it is obvious of this author’s dislike of Dan Broderick. Apart from that it’s a brilliant book
