This is not Ashes in the Wind. What we have is a perfect man and a perfect woman who fall in love with each other’s perfect bodies, don’t think it’s for anything else. Woodiwiss does this fairly often and it makes for a boring story. At least Alainna of AITW had to grow as a character to see her hero as more than just a Yankee. She has moments of humiliation, agony and self loathing. Lierin and Ashton are just boring. I remember being unimpressed by this book as a teen and upon rereading it I know why. I couldn’t even finish it.
The mystery was a good one, Woodiwiss can weave a good tale but the mcs were just too sickeningly sweet.
The language was the usual purple prose. I think Woodiwiss is the queen of that. She has prisms of light “chasing, frolicking and gamboling after each other”. And that’s just inanimate objects.
Her side characters all lust after the MCs or are obnoxiously loving people who defend the MCs to the death. No one is just indifferent to their plight. Villains twirl mustachios, OTHER WOMEN scheme for ways to ruin the beauty’s face and EVERYONE says things like, “Why must I be cast aside for yonder vixen whom you have formed a tendre for?” I kid you not. I’m all for historically accurate speech but not histrionically ott speech.
And Woodiwiss conveniently glosses over historical information that isn’t hot enough. I’m not even talking about the Leeeesiana plantation, I don’t expect her to have her hero be the cruel or indifferent master that many were. I’m talking about bosoms spilling out of gowns that didn’t exist then. Paintings, fashion plates and writings of the time clearly show wide necks that cover a woman’s chest. There was no way to spill out of anything, especially if you looked at how the corsets were constructed then. And the hair… none of it makes sense.
Anyway, not one of her best.
