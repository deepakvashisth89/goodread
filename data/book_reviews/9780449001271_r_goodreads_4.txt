Read: 8/5/26Setting: Victorian, AustraliaTrope: governess, second chance, bluestocking, older MCAug. BOTM / 3.5 starsAnother well written CP book!Plot: Amanda is stuck in Australia after refusing to leave her dying employer. Now, she needs to find work so she can return to England. The only position available is for an ENGLISH governess, an occupation she has never held before. The woman is hiring for her brother who resides in the rustic Outback. Amanda has to agree to stay a year or pay the cost back to town. She reluctantly agrees to the terms. It doesn't take Amanda long to discover why so many governess have left abruptly and the remote location has nothing to do with it. These hellion children are determined to see her gone and their father doesn't help when he disregards her stricter rules. Still, she can't flee. She just has to make it a year, then she can return to civilization in England. Nothing is going to stand in her way and that includes her growing feelings for her employer.Patrick avoids all English ladies. Both his mother and wife left their husbands because that hated the Outback. These type of women can't survive the harsh climate, so he is furious when his sister sends for another ENGLISH governess. This one looks as uptight as the last one. Still, he wants his children educated, so she'll have to do. It doesn't take him long to notice that the new governess is beautiful underneath her stiff exterior, too bad she takes herself way too seriously. That slowly changes over the year and Patrick starts to fall hard for Amanda. Still, he must let her go. It would be folly to tie another woman to a land they hate. Though, it might not matter if this drought doesn't end soon.******This was such a well written story with an interesting backdrop. The beautiful, graphic descriptions of the setting were impressive! So why only 3.5 stars? Well, this might be more of a me problem than a story problem. I'm not a fan of the governess trope, so I was already reluctant going into this story. Still, it would have worked if Amanda had been more adaptable. She's so prissy and uptight that I wanted to shake her sometimes. She needed more realistic priorities, not every rule must be followed. I get that her past shaped her that way, but did her stuck-up personality have to last the whole book? TBF, I could relate to some of her struggles. All that heat and poisonous creatures would have been tough for me too. Still, I would have at least dressed appropriately for the weather. Then there were her interactions with the children. Yikes! Yes, the two eldest were hellions, but she made the situation worse by overreacting. I'm probably being hard on poor Amanda, but she kept me from loving this book.I also wish the children had been better developed. The two eldest never really change or improve that much. They did finally stop torturing Amanda near the end, that's it! Personally, I think Liam is a future serial killer. No kidding, he enjoyed hurting other people way too much. That's not misunderstood, is psychotic! TG for sweet 6 year old Missy!Conclusion: even if I didn't like aspects of the plot, I'd still highly recommend this book! Again, most of the issues are more of a me problem and most readers will probably enjoy this story. TBH, this author doesn't write bad books. I just prefer some of her other books more.
