This book took me way too long to read. It’s pretty dense stuff and I got a bit bored of it at times. Honestly I saw some of myself in most of the main characters, in ways that kind of scared me. Most of these stories are depressing, but the last one is about how Sin enters the world and the state of man with and without it. I don’t think Dostoyevsky is very cynical, I just think he lives in a cynical world. I’d definitely read more of his stuff.Also people keep saying notes from the underground is some of his best work. It definitely has its moments and I should reread it some time but the guy needs an editor there.
