Bends the arc of human history through the prism of armed conflict, and delivers some remarkably persuasive theories and oodles of interesting factoids. The notes of cautious optimism in the 2016 postscript ring hollow in 2025’s dystopian idiocracy, and the conclusion that we shall all perish in nuclear hellfire (or winter) seems inescapable, but then that does seem to be Dyer’s point, however softened.
