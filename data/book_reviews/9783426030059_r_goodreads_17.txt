Read this book in 2009, and its a standalone book about the history of England.Set between the years 7500 BC until 1985, this book will tell the tale about the establishment of England from the Ice Age towards the middle 1980s.It tells the fortunes and failures of five families throughout the history, and these families are the Wilsons, the Masons, the Porteuses, the Shockleys and the Godfreys.What is to follow is an marvellous retelling of these families happenings throughout this history of England and their fortunes and misfortunes.Highly recommended, for this is a splendid standalone book about England, and that's why I like to call this story: "An Epic Tale Of England"!
