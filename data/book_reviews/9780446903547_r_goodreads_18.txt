I enjoyed the storyline and the conclusion to the plot begun in Spellsinger; there are some really lovely lines and ideas that stayed with me for quite some time after I'd read them. I also appreciate what I interpret as the larger messages within the novel; however, I think some of the character development is a little flimsy. I don't do spoilers, but there were some arcs that I didn't find surprising necessarily, but believe could have been worked in better than they were. Overall, I think I enjoyed this volume more than the first (although evidently, I rated it lower), but I still feel that there are improvements that could be made.
