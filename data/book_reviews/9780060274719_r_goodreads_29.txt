This book made me cry in a “there is still so much good in humanity” kind of way. I loved it.

It’s super short. I read it in one sitting in 2 hrs or less. Each chapter is told from a different character’s point of view, and you never circle back to a POV you’ve already read. So it’s 13 or so mini stories that are interconnected to make a whole. A cool stylistic choice.

It’s about a garden in a vacant city lot. So, plants —topic I already love— and how they heal people in a wide variety of ways, which I deeply believe. If the book The Well-Gardened Mind were to be written as a middle grade novel with characters’ stories representing the points of the book instead of scientific data, minus the weird Freud chapter, then you’d have Seedfolk. It’s lovely. Go read it!
