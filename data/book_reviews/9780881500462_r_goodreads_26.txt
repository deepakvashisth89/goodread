There are a few popular plot mechanisms that I absolutely hate and one of them is the bumbling, stupid cop who accuses and abuses the innocent. I get that it's supposed to be funny but to me it's just not...in fact, it's the opposite of funny. I have been fully cognizant of my dislike for this plot mechanism ever since Nora Roberts fooled me into thinking that Eve Dallas is a smart cop. I wanted to give up The Cape Cod Mystery somewhere around page 100 but I persisted. It wasn't as bad as I thought it would be but that was only because the aforementioned bumbling cop, Slough Sullivan, played a very minor role.On the positive side, there is some sarcastic humor from Asey that I enjoyed, especially the first few lines of Chapter 12:Asey picked up the pin. "Where'd you get that?""What? That? Oh, yes. That pin, you mean?""Yup," said Asey. "That pin I'm holdin' in my hand. Not two other pins in Chicago. This here pin now."
