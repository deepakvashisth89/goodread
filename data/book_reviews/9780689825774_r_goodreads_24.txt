This is one of the most amazing memoirs I have ever read. I laughed, cried, and felt almost every emotion I can think of. Livia has lived through more hard times, and struggled more than anyone should ever have to, all because she is Jewish. This story starts where here last book 'I Have Lived A Thousand Years', ends, with Livia and her mother returning home from Auschwitz. She is an amazing woman, and her stories filled my heart with faith in humanity. I hope her stories will help to make this world a more peaceful, and loving place.
Pretty much a perfect book.
