After almost two decades, Vance once again returned to the Dying Earth and gives us another Cugel the Clever adventure. After Cugel failed in his revenge against Incounu, the Laughing Magician, in the last installment, Incounu sends Cugel back to where his epic journey began once again, far, far away. No quest this time, but also no money, no magic thingie that turns anything into edible food (albeit not the taste!); Cugel is bereft of anything! He does, however, still have a strong motivation to get even with Incounu, but first he has to return!

Cugel. What needs to be said about this iconic anti-hero? A hustler, a thief, lacking morality, an egoist for the ages, but perhaps not quite as clever as he would like to think. For inexplicable reasons, however, I started to feel some sympathy for Cugel. Each chapter recounts one outrageous adventure after another as he makes his journey, always short of money and food, but never short of blag. I love Vance's imagination here as each of the 'traps' Cugel falls into remains novel, unique, and oh so cleverly constructed. This reminded me a bit of his Demon Princes novels, with all the elaborate ruses and faints. 4.5 LOL stars, rounding down as I felt the ending a bit abrupt.
