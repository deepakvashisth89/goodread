This book.....is completely amazing and I give it 5 stars because I adored the story and this book is nothing like I have ever read before. The storyline was perfect in my opinion! I would talk about it but I would rather not ruin anything for anyone ;)

The only issues I had with this book is that it was from a third person's point of view, and I'm use to reading from first person. Even though that bothered me at first, halfway through I got use to reading it. Plus that style of writing did not change my mind about giving this book 5 stars.

Honestly, (at least in my opinion) I feel like the Twilight series kind of copied this book. I know that a lot of books are similar, or have similar plots, or say similar things, but certain things in this book that are mentioned, you kind of see it in Twilight. Since this book was published years before Twilight, I feel as though Stephenie Meyer has read this book and took ideas from it!!!! lol.. I can be completely wrong, just my opinion! XD

But yes, I give this book 5 stars<3
