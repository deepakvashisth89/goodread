I'm being a little generous giving this book a two star rating. I've read other Irving Wallace novels and this book isn't of that quality. Overall premise is o.k. Sex therapist setting up a practice in Southern California, and how the lives of two of his sex surrogates and patients intertwine. Meanwhile a televangelist and an overzealous D.A. try to shut the practice down. The author did research in the field. I found that the character development was very weak. Most very one dimensional, or stereotyped.
