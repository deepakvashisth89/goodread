The H was treated like crap by the woman who married and promised to love him. She died in a car crash with her lover du jour.The book starts with him returning to his office on the night of the funeral and finding the h, his secretary, there. In an attempt to comfort him, she has sex with him and then regrets happen on both sides.Eight months later, he learns she is dating and the guy proposed. So he makes her take a “working” trip to the Caribbean and they end up in bed together during a hurricane.They have a torrid affair, she gets preggers, he accuses her of the stupidest things. She leaves, he had her followed and even the PI thinks he’s an idjit, and I agree. Wholeheartedly!This was an ok book. I didn’t fall in love with the H. And I understand why the h took him back but dammit, she should have given him hell first. She deserved a bigger, better grovel for the vileness he spewed at her! So disappointed in her. Smh
