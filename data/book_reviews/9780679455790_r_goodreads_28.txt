I genuinely do not understand why this isn't the first book handed to everyone who claims to want to be a screenwriter. It should be.After "how to write a screenplay" guidebooks turned screenwriting into a solo and technical pursuit that involves words on a page in certain order, this book busts up any illusions that this is the central talent of screenwriting. Instead, this book illuminates the subtle reality that successful screenwriting involves 1) relationships and 2) meetings and verbal discussions.And that it's a game of insiders.
