Feels strange rating a translation of the Quran but on my journey towards understanding the meaning of the verses I recite daily, I chose Pickthall's version out of convenience as I found an old copy in a secondhand bookstore.I am so in awe and can't even begin to describe the feeling this translation has over me. Pickthall wrote this translation in an effort to make the Quran more accessible to the educated elite, as there weren't many eloquent and grammatically supreme translations of the Quran in the English language available during his time. The writing flows beautifully and elegantly. Although it is hard to encapsulate the beauty, aura, and miraculous nature of the Quran in any other language, this translation does come very close to instilling that sense of deep reverence which is deserving of the book of Allah. I do occasionally consult other simpler translations from time to time (Sahih International being the preferred choice) but Pickthall's version remains the most esteemed and eloquent in my opinion.
