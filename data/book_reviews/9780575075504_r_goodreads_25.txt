While not as good asAltered Carbon, this still has enough going on to be interesting. It's more a creepy ghost story than a mystery, and for a while there's some excellent suspense - Who might be sabotaging the crew from within? Who might come out from the other side of that Martian gate? And what will those crazy nanobots think of next? Be forwarned, though, that herein lies theabsolute worstsex scene that has ever been put to paper. The fact that is takes place in virtual reality is no excuse.
