I read Heidi after retrieving my old teddy bear from a trunk and reminiscing on my days in elementary school near Houston,Texas. We piled onto a couple of school buses that took us to an auditorium in the big city. Kids from all over the Houston area were also there. We, to our surprise, were shown a film called "Heidi" whom I had heard about but knew little. I remember how we enjoyed that film and being with each other in such a place.Johanna Spyri, the author, was Swiss-German, of which I learned many, many years later my family line was also from 6 or 7 generations back--from Langnau in Bern Canton.Johanna's mother was also an author relatively well known then in the country. Johanna had most of the settings of her stories in the alpine regions of Switzerland. The novel, Heidi, is about a little girl's experience in the mountains in which she lived as an orphan. She experienced several difficulties, but her bright, happy attitude overcame many obstacles. So, it is a moral tale laced with some religious insights and views on interpersonal relationships--most appropriate for a nine-year old from Texas who had never been to the mountains and had seen very little snow.
