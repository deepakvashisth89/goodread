Well, to start off, any book with a teacher student romance will attract me. This one did, the cover had every element of what I was hoping for, but I was failed. The student teacher romance in this book is the underlying subject, this book revolves more around the animal rights topic(and if you're like me you don't care for it). The teacher and student romance isn't stressed as forbidden, there isn't a huge connection and you can't(I couldn't really) relate aswell as you would want to. Besides that I found myself skipping over some parts that dealt with animal rights acts and the ending wasen't at all what you were expecting, and not in a good way. The ending made the whole story almost pointless and was handled unrealistically.
