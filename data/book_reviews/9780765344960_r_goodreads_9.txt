“Dark Light” is a novel by Ken McLeod and the second book in his Engines of Light Trilogy. It picks up where the first book, “Cosmonaut Keep,” left off.The human starship, Bright Star, has just navigated interstellar space to the world Croatan which is inhabited by various human civilizations including one based on pre-Columbian Native American culture. This civilization has interesting ideas about gender roles. The other human civilization seems to have been derived from European colonials. The arrival of the Bright Star precipitates significant changes in both, and leads to the overthrow of established order.Meanwhile, the long-lived Cosmonauts have their own plans. They intend to talk to the advanced and enigmatic alien organisms that live in the asteroids, beings known as The Gods. What they will learn will change everything.It’s an interesting story, with a variety of sentient species, three of which are hominids. There are also intelligent giant squids commonly known as Krakens, and intelligent and long lived dinosaur-derived bipeds simply known as Saurs. All of these species originated from Earth, and were removed from there and transported to other worlds by The Gods. All except for the human Cosmonauts, who traveled to the stars themselves.This is a complex story, and sets the stage for the final book in the trilogy, “Engine City.” I’ve put it on my “want to read” list!
