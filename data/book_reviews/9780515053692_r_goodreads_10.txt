A Grave Mistake (Roderick Alleyn, #30) by Ngaio Marsh.What a joy! A splendid addition to my library and to the Golden Age of Mysteries. Chief Inspector Alleyn is a delight as he uncovers the truth that lies beneath the pretense. The characters and their relationships added depth to this wonderful mystery.Open this book and delve deep into this mystery written the way they were meant to be written.
