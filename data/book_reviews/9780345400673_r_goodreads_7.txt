The P.I. Thomas Black has an interesting assortment of friends. He is helping one who is accused of murder and gets involved in a prior case the man had worked. Black's wife, Kathy, is an attorney and is defending the man. Along the way they meet lots of people all eventually connected. There are several more murders and the main killer commits suicide in the end. His life was ruined by his father-in-law and when he finally realizes it does him in.
