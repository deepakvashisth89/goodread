The reason bluewater yachtsmen carry life rafts but don't ever want to have to use them...

Our sailboat Quest came with a life raft similar to the one in the book. Ours came in a suitcase too big and tender to store on deck but it was so heavy to lift out of the berth in which we stored it, we knew we could never use it in an emergency. Yet another reason we were so careful and always waited for weather. Since we never had to use it, my husband David never wrote about it in the four books of the Quest series so all we have are photos, one of which I just added to my profile which I'll move here if someone can tell me how!
