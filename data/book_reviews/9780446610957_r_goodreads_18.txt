I am glad I didn't skip this book because of the negative reviews I read because I actually enjoyed it.The previous book I read had fantastic reviews and yet I found it wasn't that interesting to me.What I liked about this story was the concept that all information on the internet should be considered sceptically as we can't really be certain what is true.Also the fact that the wealthy elite secretly run the world seems closer to fact than fiction.I didn't really like the predictable storyline of the handsome average intelligence psychiatrist being irresistible to the perfect genetically enhanced woman named Larissa. But I find many books written by men with similar storylines. (Don't get me wrong, female authors often have their own versions too, which usually depict some big strong man falling for the average lady who is the main character).I liked the ending.The writing style reminded me a bit of John Wyndham and the plot is something I think he could have come up with.I wouldn't say this is one of my favourite ever books, but I'm definitely glad I read it.
