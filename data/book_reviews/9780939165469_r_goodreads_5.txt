I think I would be less angry about this book if I hadn't just had a client today treating her dog for mineral deficiencies because of similar bad advice she got from a book like this. Just because the author's pets haven't had a problem so far, doesn't make the advice safe. This book has a very casual relationship with words like "authority" "expert" and "fact". This book is not very well researched and brings little true expertise to the table.Frankly I think some of the advice within is dangerous. I highly recommend instead checking out Feed your Pet Right by Marion Nestle if you are looking at an outsider's but more scientifically based point of view.
