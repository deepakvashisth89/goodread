My review of these weird tales will become a weird tale as I tell it.In a nightmare I encountered a being of pure evil, it was beat formless and it did not move for it was crushed between the walls of a stairway. I was alerted by another horrible creature that I had to leave, it said to me “He is like a Milton’s Poem, He will suck out your eyes and this will be much worse than death” I gravely felt these words and remembered them as I woke up.In the day that followed this nightmare, I read “The Sandman” and was much surprised to find that I had dreamt of eyeless Sandman from the tale before I knew he existed. Sandman, in Hoffmans´s version, is an oneiric creature that steal people´s eyes to feed them to his owl-children in the moon. I wondered about the affinity between reading and dreaming and again found the repetition of my own thoughts inside the tale, I saw my life echoed in fiction in a reverse time flux: a premonition?The nature of this mind-consuming entity is discussed by idiotic Nathanael and pretty Clara and the tale adverts the reader that one and one’s phantoms are just the same. The in-dream reference to Milton’s blindness certainly suggests this, for Milton was always my favorite poet, could the other horrible creature be me? It would be boring to make a simple connection between the unconscious and fantastic literature, for truth lies in what we can not know. There are many aspects of the reader's experience that can not be accounted by any kind of analysis and end up becoming literature itself.
