Queen of Sorcery is definitely still my favorite. There's so much humor in this series. The characters are ridiculous and funny.One good thing about reading this volume as a whole is that Pawn of Prophecy is a tad bit light on substance, and Queen of Sorcery is high on humor, but by the time you get into Magician's Gambit you're actually getting much more into the darker aspects of the series. It's no longer light-hearted. There are still plenty of laughs, though.But, oh god. I wanted to kill Eddings when a line comes up something like this "Garion instantly knew that the city had been designed by a woman. Men think in straight lines and women in circles." I actually just had to close my eyes and shake my head a few times at that one.
