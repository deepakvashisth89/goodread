T.H. White has written a delightful middle-grade book about a young orphan girl, Maria, who also happens to be an heiress. And she is being mistreated by her court-appointed governess and "the Vicar." Of course, the two villains want to steal her inheritance from her. But behind all this story is the exciting tale of what Maria finds on the small island, known as "Mistress Masham's Repose," in the back yard. I will not tell you what it is -- but you will be surprised! And that's when the excitement comes in!

I enjoyed this book immensely, however, though it is supposed to be a middle-grade level book, I don't know many kids that age that would be able to understand all of this one without a little help! Some of it was over head!! ;) But it was still a lot of fun, and the characters were so funny!

I read T.H. White in high school ( - 1938, and - 1958). This book was written in 1946 -- maybe he needed to take a break from his King Arthur and Merlin characters! Somehow this book surprised me with itself playfulness and comedy. It was just fun! I hope you enjoy it, too ;)
