Quick review. Fox is one of the more imminent experts on Alexander. He goes into huge detail and analysis in this book that be quite useful for the scholar, yet the weekend warrior historian's fire for Alexander knowledge will be swiftly quenched by the weight of detail.Nothing against Fox, he's a good historian, but if you are after a more digestible, and dare I say more enjoyable, read then see the more recent work of Phillip Freeman. If you want the weeds then by all means jump in here.The 3 star rating reflects good content but unenjoyable prose, depth and analysis that may ruin the sparkle of the story.
