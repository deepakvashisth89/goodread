I say its closer to a solid 3.5. Its a compelling story with plenty of action. The characters are pretty well formed with distinct personalities. Writing style is basically non existent, it's basically a running gun battle for most of the book, one direction or the other, and that's hard to keep interesting. They do a decent job at trying to get you interested in the characters so you don't get tired of the same, oh we shot a bunch of guys and we got shot up but we still going, scenario over and over again. The story is basically a crack commando unit is formed to go waltzing through North Vietnam into China to take out leadership of both while they are at a meeting. Well they get the abort signal while set up in China to complete their mission, and now it seems everybody is looking for them, wanting to kill them while they struggle to get back to somewhere safe.Recommended somewhat, I did enjoy it for the most part but its really longer then it needs to be by like a 100 pages. If you are a big fan of Vietnam era squad based novels then you could definitely do worse then this one.Show more
