The best thing about this Oz book is that it has all the main characters adventuring together - the entire huge ensemble of them - as they search for Ozma.ETA 2018: The above point still stands - it is nice to see the stable of Oz characters undertake an adventure, instead of being the deus ex machina in someone else's story. While it seems to me we all could have been better off if Ozma had stayed lost, this was an enjoyable read. I didn't care for the Magic Belt being the last-minute answer to everything, but ah well - the journey was fun.
