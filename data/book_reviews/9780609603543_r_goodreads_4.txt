It is a really helpful book. I am often guided by emotions in my actions and I used to think and believe that I was right because as they 'listen to your heart', and I thought I was doing that. This book showed me how what my heart sometimes wanted were fantasies and that I was actually pushing myself in a sad state. You know how it sometime happens that you want to do something and you are excited about it and how there is one voice inside you which keeps pricking but you cant understand why is that happening cause you think that's what you really want and that that is the right thing to do. And then you end up doing something but it doesn't turn out the way you expected it to be. I can be anything going over to your boyfriend's place after you have broken up, telling your mom the truth about your brother 'for his good', ignoring the flaws in people around you cause 'hey! I am happy, and focusing on the flaw will just ruin it', etc. etc. This book helped me understand what that voice is, that how often I acted on my fantasies. That voice was the realist inside me who was trying to bring the reality into picture. But I couldn't figure out cause I didn't know the real difference between reality and fantasy.

Though at a lot of points the author had explained things in an American way and I couldn't make much sense of what she was saying. But I skipped those parts completely instead of analysing and finding my own meaning. But the books was so good and helpful that I am thinking of reading it again to understand better. And this would be the first book, if I read, which I would read for a second time.
