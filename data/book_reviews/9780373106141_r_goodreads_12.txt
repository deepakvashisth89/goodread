Well that was certainly different. I didn't really like the heroine at all. She needed to put on her big girl panties and get over herself. And the hero needed to cut her loose and get a real woman. I never saw a single thing that would make him love her. She was a whinny brat most of the story. She does eventually get over his brother and fall for him. Their relationship in the second half of the book was well done. They spent time together and he was super to her and why wouldn't she fall in love with him? But it did seem quite the about face after the over the top dieing for love thing she had for his brother in the first half of the book.There were a lot of travelogue elements here. Lots of descriptions of Brazil etc. Most of it now quite out of date but still interesting.
