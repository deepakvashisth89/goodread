this book was a journey with Vicki herself. it’s far from perfect but it’s real. i’m in awe of how ahead of her time the author was. This book was a mini initiation to my own shamanic qualities.. i did experience alot of unexplained sensations whilst reading this book. I love how articulate, determined and aware the author is about herself! she accepts her shortcomings openly and in a very compassionate way.. this book was published in 1991 but much of what the author talks about where humanity was/is headed is more relevant now more than ever. The author has done a remarkable job highlighting goddess cultures ( although utopian in modern sense but still so fascinating) , activism and being on the ride side of the history, references to other authors, her own imperfect journey with shamanism and life as a mere human, her humbleness & grounding to earth mother!! i enjoyed it thoroughly and had a privilege to watch life through vicki’s mystic yet anchored in truth lenses. she undoubtedly has great wisdom & is a great mother herself.
