This book is a must-read for all Vampire players and for most folks who like Vampire fiction in general. Written in the first-person perspective with the vampire as a protagonist, Agyar manages to be as compelling and sensual as the Anne Rice novels, but with far fewer "filler" paragraphs.The vampirism is portrayed subtly, and I find that it gives this book a much classier air than your average Vampire story. Steven Brust is a phenomenal writer and uses a lot of short, witty language that includes a lot of very powerful subtext - simple on the surface and very rich between the lines.
