Re-reading this trilogy after many, many years, and I have to admit that although the POV switches to a different set of main characters, it was done fairly smoothly by Mr. Coe and the occasional glimpses of the main characters from the first novel help with this transition.I also want to mention that I am rarely a fan of combining modern technology with medieval-style fantasy settings. If I wanted to read contemporary or sci-fi novels, I would do so. That being said, I have to admit that Mr. Coe presented the two aspects in a smart way and managed to keep me interested and reading on. He also managed to impress upon me the reluctance of the inhabitants from the "medieval land" to open their minds and borders to the more advanced technology. Very well done.
