I'm a big fan of Gail Godwin's books but I wasn't crazy about this one, mainly because a lot of the characters were unlikable. But I appreciate that there is truth in that for if we all had books written honestly about ourselves, our lives, and our experiences we might not all come out looking good. There was also presence of sadness throughout the book that I found hard to stomach."I come from people who were glad to have somebody between them and the bottom." Page 158
