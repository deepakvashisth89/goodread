A wild, imaginative ride from start to finish! The Hour of the Gate is a worthy sequel that expands the world of Spellsinger with even more danger, magic, and wit. Jon-Tom’s journey through strange lands filled with bizarre creatures and unexpected allies is both thrilling and hilarious. Foster’s blend of humor, high fantasy, and clever commentary makes this series something truly unique.What really shines here is the heart—beneath the talking animals, musical magic, and zany peril is a story about courage, growth, and friendship. If you loved Spellsinger, this book dives deeper into the world and raises the stakes. I couldn't turn the pages fast enough. Foster proves again why he’s one of the greats of speculative fiction.
