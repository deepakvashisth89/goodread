A thin book, but one which defies a quick read. Each sentence, each line, is densely packed with ideas and images that carry you along from farm and field to church naves and public houses, city tenements, diaspora and notalgia and longing and searching. There is enough personal, and enough universal, to make this more than a mere oblique memoir. It is only one life, and only one time, but a valid place holder for so many others born and raised there, died there or sailed elsewhere.Hour after hour I can think of Ireland; I can imagine without going far wrong what is happening in any one of the little towns by day or by night; can see the tillage and the walled garden, see the spilt porter foam along the counters; I can hear argument and ballads, hear the elevation bell and the prayers for the dead.
