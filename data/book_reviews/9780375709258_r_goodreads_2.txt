Oh goodness. Oh my. These stories were, they were, just,gutting. I started these stories at an airport terminal. I read them in the back of a cab. I finished them in a hotel room. And I felt empty and broken and faraway.Each story ripped a hole in my soul just a little bit with the dull aching beautiful loneliness of Minot's words. Her style punches you in the chest. These (girl) characters are silly and searching and losing.The body. The heart. The ache. Wonderful stories.
