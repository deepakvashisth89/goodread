Having written five sequels to the original Wizard of Oz, writerL. Frank Baummade the wise decision in his seventh Oz tale,The Patchwork Girl of Oz, to avoid the long boring reunions of the original Oz characters and introduce a story from the point of view of all new characters. Ojo the Unlucky, the Patchwork Girl, and Unc Nunkie are brilliant inventions. When they finally meet up with Dorothy, Ozma, and the original Oz crew, there is more variety and less boring pageantry.
