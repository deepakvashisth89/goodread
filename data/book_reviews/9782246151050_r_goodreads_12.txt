Love is blind.Once upon a time there lived in Berlin, an expert art critic called Albinus. He lived happily in a big house with his wife and little daughter. He was rich. He was well respected and loved. He had everything.But he wanted more. He wanted to have a mistress; someone young and lively.One day, he found his dream lover and he loved her passionately. He lavished her with gifts and money in return for her care and affection.Death is often the point of life's joke.Once upon a time, a dupe called Albinus fell in love with a woman, who didn’t love him in return; who mocked him behind his back; who used him to fulfill her own dreams.
