The heroine thought the hero was responsible for her mothers break down when she was a child. Obviously part of that was true, however not all of it. For the hero once meeting the heroine again had fallen deeply in love with her and would do anything to convince hr of that fact. The heroine was instantly attracted to the hero and once a certain time had past eagerly gave up her virginity. When the hero wanted to marry her, the heroine said yes but there was one big problem, the heroines mother..I thought the angst and passion were pretty good in this book despite the fact the storyline was unbelievably simple. I wished for a bit more drama.
