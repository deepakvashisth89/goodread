Kind of went in to this anticipating more of Nancy talking about her life and career rather than giving detailed accounts of the making of the show, an area that has been covered so much. The personal stuff is by far the highlight; her time training with Daws Butler, her experiences with the guest stars and a heartbreaking story about her meeting a young cancer patient. Nancy is clearly a gem who has not taken her responsibility and honour of playing one of the most iconic fictional characters of all time for granted.
