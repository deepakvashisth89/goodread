Camp Fear Girls had an interesting concept using the summer camp setting in a slightly different way. The main character felt very average, and even the side characters didn’t stand out much.The atmosphere of the camp was good, but the plot itself never fully gripped me, and it's predictable, Some scenes were exciting I can’t deny that, but overall it didn’t feel as strong as it could have been.The general idea was fine, just not remarkable. The ending also left me underwhelmed. In the end, it was a very average read that didn’t leave much of a lasting impression.*******************************************Verdict(6/10)
