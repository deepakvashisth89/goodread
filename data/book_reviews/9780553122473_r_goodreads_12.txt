"To all those who lead monotonous lives in the hope that they experience at second hand the delights and dangers of adventure."Christie's early novel (Jan 1922) (and not mastered her craft as of yet) and the first in the Tommy and Tuppence detective series. It starts off in 1915 during the sinking of the Lusitania. A man hands a pack of sensitive political documents to an American woman named Jane Finn. He believes a woman has a better chance of being loaded in the rescue boats than himself. Jane survives the disaster but subsequently vanishes without a trace.Thomas and Prudence are childhood friends recently released from service. Tuppence is something else, very quick on her feet, her mind is always on. She is completely broke! She loves money and excitement. She plots how her and Tommy (who is completely loyal) could post an ad stating they are willing to do anything, no unreasonable offered denied. Tommy is the stabilizer of this duo, but distractions are infused in every corner creating lots of suspense.Tommy? Hard to deceive. Tuppence? Intuitive.The first assignment? An international conspiracy.British Intelligence high ranking Mr Carter.A group of conspirators.Kidnapping.The hunt for the missing and key character Jane Finn.Julius a millionaire cousin to Jane and forms the trio.The secret treaty.‘new and deadly significance’Master criminal Mr Brown who wants to unite all of England’s enemies!Tommy and Tuppence: out of their league? Bluffing their way through?Thrilling fun and swirling activity after WWI. Adventures not as a duo as well as elements of mystery, adventure, and romance between them! This is a focus on international intrigue rather than a traditional murder mystery. I really found our fond pair very likable and cannot wait to read more in the series!
