Picked this up on a recommendation from a friend on Facebook.It's a light read, and I can see why my friend Clint recommended it after I posted notes on a Robert Parker book (Jesse Stone series). The heroes were totally different, but there was a certain common sub-theme that both authors add. That is, the story is not at ALL a romance, but the author puts in some male-female relationship chemistry (how itcanbe) which is contrasted with other male-female emptiness. I'll probably pick up another of Emerson's books just for that.
