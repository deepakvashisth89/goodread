It's funny; a lot of the lower-star reviewers say this book wasn't their "cup of tea." I guess, then, that this kind of bookismy cup of tea. BecauseA Girl Becomes a Comma Like Thatis about women and girls, many want to classify it as "chick lit." Not that "chick lit" is an insult (it's a genre in its own right), but this doesn't qualify. The writing is insightful and literary, if at times overwrought. Also, I'm not sure these interconnected short stories quite pull together to make a cohesive novel, but still--I enjoyed it.
