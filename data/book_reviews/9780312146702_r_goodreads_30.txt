This book concludes the Mary Russell and Sherlock Holmes trilogy and feels like a lovely final goodbye to these two characters.After they got married, I was excited to see them working together as husband and wife.While they remain the same incredibly rational (almost emotionally reserved) people they've always been, I would have loved a few more tender moments between them. Nothing over the top , just small gestures to remind us of the feelings they confessed in the previous book.I enjoyed this mystery slightly less than the first two because it wasn't as personal for Mary and Sherlock.
