I really enjoyed this book of shorts. As I continue to read through the Forgotten Realms Cannon, I am attempting to read the stories in order that they happen in the Realms timeline, not publishing date, which sometimes causes issues. All these stories take place prior to the attack on Waterdeep, but I think there is some base knowledge that might have made this group of stories a little difficult for someone that is not super familiar with FR...not a lot, just a little. And they would still probably be enjoyable.My favorites were the ones by Lisa Smedman and Mel Odom (both have written on this race and topic in the past), but there really wasn't a story I didn't enjoy.Another FR book done.
