A good way to read stories from the Bible without all the hype and especially for younger people, it tells the stories in a way that gets you interested and also puts a more personal feel into them, so that you can relate to the characters as individual and important players...especially some of the (otherwise) lesser known characters.A good introduction to the Bible, which can be read as a collection of short stories.I have always been of the opinion that the Bible should be written as a historical novel with a timeline of events.*I have also found this book to be a good ready reference book ;-)
