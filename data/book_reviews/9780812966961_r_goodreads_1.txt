Nikolai Leskovwas a unique writer, he was one of the grandest connoisseurs and maestros of language and it’s a great pity that his magnanimous and juicy handling of words was unavoidably lost in translation.But all the sameThe Enchanted Wandereris an enchanting reading. The tale possesses almost proverbial qualities.Kneel, first of all. Man’s knees are the first instrument: as soon as you kneel, your soul at once soars up, and there, being thus elevated, you must bow down to the ground, as many times as you can, till you are exhausted, and wear yourself out with fasting, to mortify yourself, and when the devil sees you striving for a great deed, he will not endure it and will run away at once, for fear that with such a man his machinations will drive him still more directly to Christ…Nikolai Leskovwasn’t a writer of body or mind… He was a writer of soul.
