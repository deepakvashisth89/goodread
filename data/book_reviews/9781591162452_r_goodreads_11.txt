This is a Japanese comic book (manga). It has forbidden and taboo themes that may be unusual and strange to other readers not familiar with Japanese manga and culture. I gave it four stars because I do enjoy the Forbidden Love theme - yes even taboos lol and the story has enough to keep me riveted and entertained. This is an old manga so it might seem dated to the modern manga reader (1995) but since I'm a manga fan I was ok with it. I've always known about Angel Sanctuary manga but at the time the manga was not completed yet /poor/don't want cliffhangers. So I never got to reading it /read manga summaries instead and satisfied myself with the really short and incomplete animation OVA.Despite the mature theme it might seem a tad juvenile (we're dealing with highschool kids) but I'm just rolling with it. We got angels, demons, reincarnation, angst, etc. Etc. The animation is also really pretty. The first volume tried to include some lightheartedness but I'm not sure it really jives well coz everything does seem pretty tragic for our main characters and even the demons and angels. It was a pretty fast read since it's a comic and now I'm off to volume 2.
