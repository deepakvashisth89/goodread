Great book about one of my favorite actors Jack Lemmon! Only thing is it was written in 1975 and only goes up to his career ending with his movie The Prisoner of Second Avenue. But the book talks about all of the films he did before and includes any television and theatrical work he did. Also it gives incite into his two marriages and the failure of his first marriage to Cynthia Stone. Also his alcoholism is discussed at length. Very interesting actor and man!
