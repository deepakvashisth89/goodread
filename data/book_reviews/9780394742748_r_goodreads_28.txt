This was my follow up read to ‘The Laughing Policeman’ by this author, which is one of the best police procedurals that I have read. This book was a DNF letdown. The interesting opening pages bank robbery devolved into slapstick cops pursuing two robbers in an alternate side story. There was way too much exposition and diatribes about life in Swedish cities c. 1971. The authors really had it in for the government and law enforcement of Sweden.I skimmed over the many pages of politics to get back to the story, but found that it didn’t hold my interest any longer. I just didn’t like any of the characters. They were almost all depressed, coarse, or foolish. So goes Sweden! I wish this had been a tighter and better written locked room mystery.
