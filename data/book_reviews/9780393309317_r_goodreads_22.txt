I smirk here and there but I do love his mystic intensity: your beloved is blocking your view of love; your churches clutter your companionship with Death, and “Oh how completely an angel would stamp out their markets of solace.” And your so-called life? A marionette moves with more essence. “Rilke,” wrote Tsvetaeva, “this man who is distant among the distant, lofty among the lofty, lonely among the lonely.”
