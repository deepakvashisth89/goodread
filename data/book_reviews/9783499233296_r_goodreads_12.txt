Not awesome, but quirky. No wonder the series had a certain appeal for the flower-power folks.The detective duo are worldly-wise and do not mind breaking into lengthy philosophical discussions. When they reach the crime scene for the first time they enter a Samuel Beckett kind of argument about who should ring the doorbell. It makes perfect sense when one of them convinces the other saying: "You have an elegant index finger!"It is actually an amusing read, not overt or melodramatic. I'd check more books in the series if I were bestowed with infinite chronons of time in my life.
