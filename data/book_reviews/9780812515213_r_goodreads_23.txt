Hi,I know this is not part of his regular series and it is a far cry from Brust's normal story, but I loved it! I was at a sci fi convention that he was doing a book signing. Of course I did not have my books with me and I could not get to them. He had just put this one out, so I bought it there and had him sign it. I went home and read it in one sitting. I just could not put it down.I know that this book gets very little attention, but it deserves way more. Read this book! Even if you are not a fan of the Vlad series. Totally worth it.Jenn
