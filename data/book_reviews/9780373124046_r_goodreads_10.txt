A passionate read.

Francesa is one of those unawakened innocent woman looking for true love that she believes she has found it in her golden man Angelo. Who makes her feel loved, protected, and cherished.....trying to never question the way the sexy Carlo makes her feel....because she loves Angelo?????

Sexy Carlo can't believe his luck, the woman he wants and desires in love with another! He can't resist the attraction he feels for her and when the chance araises to claim her as his own he takes it.

Francesa reeling from the betrayal of 2 she loved most gives into the attraction between her and Carlo only to learn he is harboring some secrets.

The sexual tension and passion between Francesa and Carlo is off the charts hot and steamy.....so so sexy! It was such a tender moment when Carlo realizes she is past her breaking point and is crying, that you discover his eyes are wet too and his throat is tight....that maybe he is not as heartless or cruel as he has portayed but that he might just care more about her than he has let on.

A wildly romantic and so passionate read.
