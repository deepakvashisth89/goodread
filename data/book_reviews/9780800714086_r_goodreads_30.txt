Pretty quickly into this audiobook I decided I didn't want to spend my limited time listening to the whole thing. I was intrigued after hearing some friends talk about it, so I skipped around and I ended up listening to most of it. It was interesting, and I certainly do see myself in the classic middle child. But there are just so many exceptions, and he used the same person as an example of a first born child (because they usurped the older brother) and a middle child, which seemed a little sneaky or something, like just changing the rules to prove a point.
