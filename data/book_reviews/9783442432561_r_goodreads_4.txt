“The Sins of the Angels” was given to me by my 88-year-old landlady when I told her I was trying to read more in German—I put it off for a while honestly but figured I’d read it before moving out at the end of this month. Charlotte Link is the queen of the German thriller, she’s written 30+ books since the 80s. This one takes place in 1995 across Hamburg, London, and Southern France. I had fun reading, even if it was slow to start and predictable in the end (in any thriller with twin main characters……they’re going to have swapped places at some point), story relied heavily on the suspense surrounding some psychosexual drama that I’m sure was much crazier thirty years ago, but a relative page-turner nonetheless, with a big enough cast of characters/storylines to keep things moving
