A straight up terrifying horror?
The loveliest of romances?
An endearing coming-of-age?

Stephen King can do it all.

I loved this little story. A quick read, and it went in a completely different direction from what I had expected. Okay, maybe I shouldn’t have been totally shocked, this is Stephen King.

I loved the group of friends, and I loved the messaging of this book. There’s something special about a group of friends, but there can also be something destructive about it as well. This story felt personal to me, like we were reading something about Stephen King rather than a story he had made up.
