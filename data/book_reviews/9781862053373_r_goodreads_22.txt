This is sort of like a visual companion to the movie The Labryinth. Goblinland and it's inhabitants are laid out for you in true Brian Froud and Terry Jones style. It's fully enjoyable and reminds you why the joining up of Froud, Jim Hensen and David Bowie for the movie was so magical in the first place.The art is superb, the layout is amazing, much like a field journal. It all comes together in a volume that any Froud, Hensen or Labryinth enthusiast will want to have.
