I haven't read The Help, but I have a feeling it would make me feel how this one did. While you can't help but love Katish, the author's failure to address the elephants in the room (Katish's origin story, her life outside service to the author's upper class California household, her feelings generally) just stood in the way of any ability for me to enjoy this. It felt, as they say, "cringe." The recipes didn't do much for me either unfortunately.
