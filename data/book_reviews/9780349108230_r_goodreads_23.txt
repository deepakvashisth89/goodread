Jane Gardam has done it again. She brought together a strange group of characters bound together by a newborn baby who hardly makes an appearance until the last chapter. Faith Fox is the daughter of Holly and Andrew. Holly died in childbirth and Andrew is a doctor who is totally consumed by his work at the hospital. Holly's mother, Thomasina, can't look after Faith because she has gone off to Egypt with an elderly general she met at a retreat before Faith was born. Holly's friend, Pammie, took on Faith's care for a short time until Andrew could take Faith to The Priory where his brother Jack had a commune. Even there Faith's care fell to one of the aged Tibetans staying at the commune. Andrew's parents, Dolly and Toots, lived nearby but they were too old and decrepit to even come to see Faith let alone look after her.The cast of characters is finely drawn by Gardam so that the reader feels like they come alive off the page. You keep wondering when we will actually meet Faith and who will look after her.Loved this story and these characters.
