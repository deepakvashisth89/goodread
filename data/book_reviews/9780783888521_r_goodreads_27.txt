So much better than the first book, I liked this one a lot. Great story line, I liked the new characters and it was not very predictable. The one thing that's bothering me with Johansen's books are those dialogues. A lot of dialogues between the characters and short descriptions. I would like something deeper in the background, more detailed profile of the killer, something that happened in the past or whatever (like in Gerritsen's books for example).I would recommend this book to mystery lovers.
