My first, and probably last, Father Brown collection. I rather liked the unassuming, mystery-solving priest. But the stories got more and more improbable as I progressed through the book, until I had to force myself to keep reading.I understand that this probably says more about me than about Chesterton's writing. These are classic old-school British mystery stories, after all, with clever thieves and murderers and a cleverer amateur detective. I just found the crimes and solutions more and more ridiculous from story to story. I won't reveal any spoilers by going into more detail.So four stars for the admirable title character and two stars for the plots, for a generous average of three.
