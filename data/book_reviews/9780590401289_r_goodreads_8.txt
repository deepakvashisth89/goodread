I bought the three books in one. I was at a workshop and the presenter began the workshop by starting to read one of the mysteries. Before she was finished, everyone (all adults) were listening to her finish the story and asking her to read it again so we could solve it. She smiled and said, "I have just shown you the easiest way to get your students' attention." She was right! I have used this book over and over and shown others the "magic" of it. When I was at a private high school, students would love to hear one each day and compete for the sticker that the first one to guess the solution got. Some of the terms are out-dated, but that is a great way to bring vocabulary and history into some of the stories. Give it a try!I'm setting reading it as today because I use it so much.
