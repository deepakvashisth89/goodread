This is one that had been on my list for many years, since enjoying The Terminal Experiment and vowing to read all of Sawyer's stuff.Of course I wanted to start with this, his first novel but it took a while to finally stumble across it. Golden Fleece is pretty good, and comparisons to 2001: A Space Odyssey are unavoidable with the main character being JASON, a HAL-ish computer with an agenda.It's a quick read, although I did find myself getting bogged with the mathematics of space travel and prime number encryption. Other than that, a pretty good read and I did enjoy Sawyer's pop culture referencesfrom our time being used by these year 2177 travelers.
