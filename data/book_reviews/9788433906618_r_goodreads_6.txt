Horribly pretentious, badly written and utterly confusing book that I actually had a hard time reading all the long and tedious way to the end.The story is interesting enough, but it's stuffed too much with pseudo-intellectual sounding passages that have no relevance to anything whatsoever. Norfolk doesn't write too complicated as such, maybe if he didn't find it so necessary to riddle his text with countless important-sounding and long words this would be a better read.Nice try, but that's just trying too hard!
