Widow Lucia Newell is always calling Asey Mayo for help with one problem or another, so he doesn't take her current plea very seriously. Besides, he has several things to worry about--for one, somebody seems to be shooting at him, and even worse, the local women have all obtained guns and want to train to repel enemy invaders in the dark days of 1940. But Newell really was in danger, and now Asey has a murder case and a lot of suspects clamoring for his attention.
