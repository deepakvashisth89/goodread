I enjoyed the second novel in the Nigel Strangeways series much better than the first. In the debutA Question of Proof, author Nicholas Blake makes amateur sleuth Strangeways such a colorful eccentric that Strangeways emerged as a few degrees away from being a buffoon. Here Blake tamps down his attempts to exoticize Nigel Strangeways, and the result is much better.Here Strangeways is sent to ensure that an enemy doesn’t do in a decorated World War I pilot. Unfortunately, Strangeways fails in his task. Readers will find the novel’s ending shocking, and I look forward to this newer, more normal Strangeways in the next novel.
