GREAT BOOK! However I give this 5 star with a slight caveat: the very ending was terrible/not the ending I expected. Kind of felt like the author got tired of writing and just ended the story. Maybe it was not “terrible”, but the book definitely deserved a better, more complete ending. But then again it’s his story and that’s what we got, so you can’t judge a whole novel on a few pages. And justice is not always served and some stories are not meant for “good to trump evil”.
