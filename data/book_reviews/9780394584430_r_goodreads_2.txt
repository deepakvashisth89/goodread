An excellent book from 1993, which provides a more or less framework of issues that Paul Kennedy predicted, based on heavy data, to be worrisome in the 21st century. He was correct. Biotech, aging/growing population, environmental damage, automation and industrialization, globalization and big corporations, financialization of the economy. All of these topics are heavily debated and poses a real challenge in our modern days.It is incredible how much accuracy he had from such a long distance (he aims at trends up until 2025, more or less) but a great perspective nevertheless. It was one of the most interesting and complete books I’ve ever read.The only reason I took away one star in my rating is that Kennedy is not able to place some personal input along with the reasonably neutral and data-oriented trends for the future of the world (especially when talking about Europe and the US). And although I agreed with most of his personal input, it just felt too much like a rant for me. Anyways, besides that vacillation, it is after all an incredibly well researched and open-minded view of the current century.I certainly recommend to everyone so that they can get a brief (although super interesting) history of certain regions, how they were in the 1990s, and the prediction then of how they would turn out to be today. It is shockingly precise.
