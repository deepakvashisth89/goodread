An easy read for when you don't want to engage with anything taxing.George and Molly Palmer-Jones don't quite carry quite enough punch to make this enthralling. After creating Vera and Jimmy Perez it seems that Ann Cleeves is settling down with more staid detectives, and she doesn't really pull it off.This was a pretty good mystery but when the culprit is finally unmasked, they were so unlikely and non-sensical as to make the whole thing lose any potency. Very disappointing.
