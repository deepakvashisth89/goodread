With shades of future Colonel Phoenix, this Bolan tale takes a surprising left hand turn when Striker stumbles on a plan to kidnap the president and his family.The mob has teamed up with an ex-military mercenary named Harrelson, and his professional army of killers to snatch the prez for a cool billion dollar ransom.Those plans go awry when the big guy comes on the scene, blitzing across the snow swept range of the Rockies to smash the mercenary army and save the president.Concise and action packed, with a bunch of government conspiracy stuff thrown into the mix makes this an excellent Executioner.
