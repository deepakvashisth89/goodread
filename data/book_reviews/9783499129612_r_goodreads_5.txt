Wanna travel somewhere where everything works out for the best?want lots of drinking,smoking,ambling about a beautiful location with viral men,pretty naive girls,jovial,industrious servants? crack open the nearest Rosamunde Pilcher book. Could it be him,afterall? so off she goes to his beautiful island home where life is even more languid and discovers a part of her own mind.
