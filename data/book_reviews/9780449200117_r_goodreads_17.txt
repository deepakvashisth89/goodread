A nostalgia read, Norma Klein was one of my favorite authors as a teenager in the 1980s. It's a very progressive and forward thinking novel about two teenage girlfriends and their new romantic and sexual relationships. Themes include premarital sex, abortion, suicide, friendship, and coming of age. It's mostly dialogue which is at times stilted, but I think it's just a different era. No computers or smart phones so it seems like it's a much simpler time, though the social issues remain the same.
