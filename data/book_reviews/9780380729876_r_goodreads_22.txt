I ploughed my way through this novel which is very detailed story about a dysfunctional southern family raging with personal grudges, self destruction, and family denial.The author artfully weaves both class, gender, and racial tension throughout the story line and manages to lobb a few jokes along the way as well.The characters are believable based on my exposure to various aspects and elements of southern society.I do fault Godwin with long windedness--the story could have been preserved within a 300 pp. format.
