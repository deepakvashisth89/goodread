I liked this book very much. Solid 3.5 stars.Characters were so well developed with an engaging story line. There are many violent battles so if you are squeamish don’t read this one. There are also behaviors involving involving rape, child rape and incest so if these are triggers for you please do not read!So, how could I like this book? It is the story of human life in balance and what happens when life is out of balance where evil rules the current day. Good vs evil. Evil is ugly and monstrous so naturally the behaviors of those choosing evil will also be ugly and monstrous!This book is about the journey to right the wrongs and put life back into harmonious balance. There is so much beauty in the journey! Isn’t seeing the beauty in the journey what it is all about?
