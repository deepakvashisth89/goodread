This is the fourteenth volume in the Wingman series, Mack Maloney's ongoing chronicles of the adventures of Hawk Hunter and his allies in a never-ending pulp-magazine-inspired battle for American democracy against foreign fascism and tyranny. It's an entertaining story for folks like me who enjoy such things, but I do think this volume was beginning to be a little repetitive and edged more towards 1950's science fiction adventure and away from the Men's Adventure sub-genre that's listed on the spine. It's well written and engaging, but I don't think it covers any new ground.
