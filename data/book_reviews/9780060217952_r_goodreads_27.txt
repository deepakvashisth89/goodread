Alvin Schwartz excellently maintains the quality of his scary stories in this third installment of the series.Every one of the books contains funny stories, folklore songs, and some narratives that are more intriguing than frightening, but there are also one or two or three tales in each book that make my blood run cold. InScary Stories 3: More Tales to Chill Your Bones, "Harold" and "The Red Spot" give me chills, but it is the haunting "Maybe You Will Remember" that is perhaps the scariest story of all three Scary Stories collections.Stephen Gammell keeps up an exceptional level of skill in his eerie illustrations, which are the perfect compliment to Alvin Schwartz's storytelling.
