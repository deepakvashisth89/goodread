Jacki Lyden is as poignant and important in prose as she is on the front lines of broadcast journalism's war-torn daily headlines. She is a writer who bares her soul and twists a braided pigtail of wit and wisdom into a crowning glory of beauty. She takes her readers on a personal adventure of love and bewilderment. This memoir is a solicitous inquiry into the soul of a mother-daughter relationship bequeathed with the sagacity of hindsight and benevolence. It's brave and memorable and durable.
