This entire book is a quiz to see how much you know about Wicca (and associated belief systems). It seems like a pretty silly idea--and in a way it is, because who cares what ranking you get--but actually, it's got an interesting little roadmap for what you might still need to learn if you want to have an overview understanding of Wicca. Some of the questions are just trivia, but some depend on your knowledge of what goes with what (in certain traditions), what elemental correspondences are, some things about deities, and bits about the Sabbats and Esbats. You do get a fair amount of stuff on Wiccan history, as well, and if you're not really a people person on the history you might not swing this very well. Still, when you grade yourself, you get to see what your areas of best knowledge are and where you might want to do some research. I took the quiz and got the second-highest level of expertise, which is good because I would have been surprised if I'd gotten the top tier; I never claimed to be an expert, so if I'd tested as one, I'd be disappointed in the book.
