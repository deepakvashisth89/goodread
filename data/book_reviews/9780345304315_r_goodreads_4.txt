A lively and well told western novel.Sisters of the Wyoming Mountains is a well told western adventure story; all your emotions will be brought to the surface as this story progresses.It is a lively experience of two sisters. As a reader, I went the full gambit of thrilling to the main characters life struggles, and how they are forced to survive in the Wild West in mid-1800. There are many twists in this story.I would certainly recommend the reading of this story, the characters are all believable, and you will love the heroes or hate the villains with gusto.
