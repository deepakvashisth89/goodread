I had read The Scarlet Letter before and loved it. My focus was The House of the Seven Gables. A great tale with themes of the sins of the father being visited onto the children of a family. The house itself, built on the uneasy foundation of bloodshed and dishonesty is seemingly cursed by the wrongdoing of the patriarch of a New England family during the witch hunt, is a great metaphor of the Pyncheon family; itself teetering on its' faulty beginnings. "God will give you blood to drink"- an unforgettable line and brilliant curse, stolen by Hawthorne from the curse spewed by the real victim of the Salem witch hunt, Sarah Goode. No ordinary ghost story, the living characters in this book haunt more than the dead. A bit verbose for my taste, it is nonetheless a great example of American classical literature.
