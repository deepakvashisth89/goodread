Rating:5/5

{“Things never happen the same way twice, dear one.” }

Returning to Narnia in this second book was an absolute dream, but it was so bittersweet to see how much time had passed! And to think that I had read the first book years ago and never read the remaining books!!
The story completely swept me away. Watching the Pevensies navigate a world that had forgotten its own magic, while Caspian fought against the tyrannical Telmarines to bring back the talking beasts and trees, was pure perfection. The pacing was wonderful, building up to that epic final battle! It's an incredibly rich, nostalgic, and thrilling fantasy adventure which I absolutely adored!!
