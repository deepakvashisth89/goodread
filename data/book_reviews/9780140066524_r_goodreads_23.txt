A dead man in a presumably stolen car is discovered in the garage of the enigmatic Carl Anderson. He is brought into Inspector Maigret's police station for what ends up being a marathon 17 hour interrogation. He remains staunch in his plea of innocence. After he is released from custody, Maigret and his sergeant Lucas go to the scene of the crime, a remote crossroads with only 3 "homes" in the area. Each of the residents have a story to tell and thus begins the most interesting investigation of thewholestory.Simenon sets the stage with a lot of atmosphere and interesting characters. Thoroughly enjoyed the 7th installation in the Maigret series.
