If a green giant crashed
your New Year's Eve party
shouting hey everybody
let’s play a little game
one of you take this axe
and chop off my head
and in a year's time
we'll meet at my green chapel
so I can chop off yours—
would you accept the challenge?
I don’t know about you
but I would in a second
I’ve always dreamed of
having a severed head
think of the things you could do
with one of those!
You could go to work
and keep your head at home
You could go shopping
without running into
annoying neighbours
you could scare children
on Halloween night without
having to buy a costume
You could walk around
the block naked incognito
You could cheat on
your spouse and say
Honey it was nothing
I just lost my head
You could easily get
a seat on a busy bus
—seat otherwise reserved
for the wounded at war—
You could become
your country’s first headless
head of state (actually
you wouldn't be the first)
You could become head-
(less)master at your school
You could use your head
in all sorts of situations
to hold the door open on a windy day
in lieu of a ball when playing rugby
as candle holder guard dog or scarecrow
You wouldn't need a pillow nor a hat
You would no longer get headaches
You’d be spared the tyranny of mirrors
You'd never have a bad hair day again
In other words if a green giant
chopped off my head
I really would get a-
head in life
