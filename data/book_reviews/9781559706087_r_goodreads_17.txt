Its been a couple of years since I read this but I remember being utterly fascinated. Ever since reading War and Peace and biographies about the Romanov 's I found myself searching for other unique Russian tales. This fit the bill.A Russian royal (who didnt like the limelight) suddenly "dies " but his coffin is empty. Enter : religious zealot wanderer with no past. Are they one and the same? The author had really done his digging and gives the reader much food for thought.Bottom line: No one knows what really happened but this book REALLY MAKES YOU WONDER! Well told!
