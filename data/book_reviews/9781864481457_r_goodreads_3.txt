Set in Carlton in the early 1960s this story is told from the point of view of a young girl, who is growing up in a situation of poverty and domestic violence. It is well told from the child's viewpoint, school, best friend, playing, loving mum and alcoholic father. Perhaps I'm still coming down from my last read, or perhaps I am a little played out with the domestic violence scene, as, despite the fact that the book is a Vogel Award nominee, I found myself skipping passages to get to the end.
