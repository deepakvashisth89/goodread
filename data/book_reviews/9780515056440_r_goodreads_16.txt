Book 2 of the series revolved around the start of the Korean War and followed four or five military officers in their military and personal escapades. I enjoyed reading about the insider’s view of military life. While there is some combat scenes in Korea, much of the book revolves around these officers’ personal lives.The book was good enough. To be honest, this is my “bathroom book.” I keep it in a drawer in the bathroom on only pull it out when it is business time. The book is perfect for that usage. Interesting enough but not so interesting that I care about reading it all the time. Three stars. Soon Book 3 in the series will become the bathroom book.
