Almost thirty years ago, when I was a child, I read this book for the first time in my grandma's library. I still remember how Sicily smelled in those summers, like dry wheat and wind. I could feel all the same feelings while going through this story one more time, I feel so blessed to be able to relieve those same emotions so many years later. It really makes you think how much the books we read as children shape who we are as adults. For me, this will forever be a masterpiece, and indeed anything this author has written; am I being objective? Probably not. But I'm writing this review with my heart and not with my brain <3
