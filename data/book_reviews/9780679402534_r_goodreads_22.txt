Peter Mayle's "A Year in Provence" is one of my favorite books. So, I hoped that "Toujours Provence" would have the same magic.The book is written with the same understated humor as A Year in Provence, but it is not really a story. There are 20 chapters, or vignettes, covering a wide variety of topics about life and food in Provence, and nothing ties them together. There are some attractive simple drawings at the end of each story. The lack of a unifying story means that the book just ends; despite the fact that the final chapter provides a glimpse of the changes in Mayle's life after settling there.The stories are pleasant, like a light appetizer. I wish I had read this before "A Year in Provence".Show more
