Very australian book and I suspect it gives quite a real glimpse into a small period of time in Australia. I enjoyed the book and it is quite well written the ending was powerful. There was a small bit of bad language and people were gambling, getting drunk , fist fighting and smoking, and it was often a bit crude. Also a few beginnings of romances, and talk of pushing over girls. But it gave a great image of the bush, and of the lowest kind of honest australian life, in the bush and small towns.
