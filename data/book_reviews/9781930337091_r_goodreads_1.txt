This brief book of poetry is very earnest. Lovely black and white photographs by Jane English. I particularly like the one of geese chasing a rabbit opposite the poem "CRAZY LEADERS."

This is the poem I flipped to when I picked this up while browsing:

FAILURE

After victory
failure
may be the next
best thing

In a bad performance it is easy to tell
what went wrong
In a good one
it is often hard even to find the seams

The primary lesson of failure
is to welcome being alive
then the deep learning you need
comes by itself
