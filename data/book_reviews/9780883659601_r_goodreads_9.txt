I have loved quotation books for years, but this one is lame on a number of fronts.Of course there are many good quotations in here. One could assemble a small book of about 24 pages that would contain all the really good ones that are in this book. But over 600 pages (guessing) with only 5 or 6 on a page???? What kind of book design is this?Other negatives: There are NO PAGE NUMBERS to the book. Huh? What??? Yes, you read that right. So if you want to write down page numbers to mark a favorite quote, you can't do that because THERE AREN'T ANY. And, also, there is no index of subjects. None. You DO have an index of authors which refers us to the quotation number, but not a page number (because there aren't any, remember?).Even worse, Robert Byrne who selected and compiled this book includes lots and lots of padding with quotations BY HIMSELF that almost always are forgettable. (Is he up there with Bernard Shaw, H.L. Mencken and Oscar Wilde? Really?) (Here's one example by the 'inimitable' Robert Byrne: "Love will find a lay." I'm sure you'll be jotting that one down!)And this monumentally-oversized book takes up so much space on my shelf (whereas most quotation books outside of Bartlett's takes minimal space) that I feel like just chucking it. What I'll likely do is write down my favorites from this one I don't already know (maybe two dozen of its choice bonbons) and then gift the book to someone with lots of empty space on their shelves.For a quotation book, this is a real loser.PS: I gave this book 2-stars to be kind because I did find a number of really good ones I hadn't heard before. As I said, they could make up a small book, maybe even a pamphlet.
