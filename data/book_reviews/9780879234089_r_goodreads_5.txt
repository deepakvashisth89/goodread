I bought this after reading that Stephen King recommended this book, and it's easy to see why, but getting hold of it took months. For some reason there are lots of copies of this on ebay for extortionate prices, it took me ages to track down one at a reasonable cost.It was worth every penny. Balzic is a fantastic detective character and the murders he solves are puzzling but utterly believable. I really enjoyed reading it and had to slow myself down to savour the story.
