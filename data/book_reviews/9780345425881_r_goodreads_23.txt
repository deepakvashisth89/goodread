Recommended by Dorothy L which I trust. But this was written by a conservative--snide remards about Kennedy, Roosevelt, pro-death penalty, religious, really strange to me, because I don't think I've read any other mysteries like this; either you know the author's politics and they agree more or less with yours, or you don't know at all. What was interesting to me was the overlap: liking Dorothy Sayers and C.S. Lewis, horses, sympathy for animals,for example. Food for thought. Aside from that, the book was readable, except for disorienting shifts in pov and person.
