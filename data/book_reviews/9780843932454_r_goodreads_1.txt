Part 1 of the Shitty Romance Novel Book Club:This is a pretty strong start. The main characters are absolutely insufferable and Raven cannot make up her mind to save her life. Pretty much all of the men in this book threaten to rape her, including the guy who is supposed to be her love interest, which is incredibly weird. She's also very classist and judgmental towards Jeffrey, her love interest, because she assumes that he was born poor and only stops judging him when she finds out that he was actually born to a prestigious family. The writing style and story are repetitive, predictable, and little actually happens in the 366 pages of this novel. It leaves you feeling like there was no reason to read this at all. The only reason that this gets a 2-star rating is because the writing style is very easy to follow and it's not hard to read, so finishing this isn't absolute torture.
