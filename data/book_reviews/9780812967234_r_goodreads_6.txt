Wonderful! I've been a Rockwell fan since I first crawled over to a Saturday Evening Post and stared at one of his covers which have been etched in my mind forever. Norman Rockwell is Mr. Americana. But he was also a human being. I wanted to know the man behind the Man. And this book gave me what I wanted to know plus a lot of behind the scenes work that went into his paintings. Highly recommended to Rockwell groupies.
