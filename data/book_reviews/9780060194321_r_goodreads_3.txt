If someone had told me what this book was about before I read it I probably would have passed it by. I'm so glad that didn't happen though as it turned out to be a wonderfully heartwarming story. Of course it takes place in a small mountain town so right off the bat I loved the setting, but the characters were so well written and the story so timeless that I was instantly pulled into their world. Michael C. White is a wonderful author and I look forward to reading more of his work.
