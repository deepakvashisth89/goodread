Not over all a terrible book but read something better with your time. I didn't think it was that great.

Man her husband is a bit of a jerk. This girl is what 19 when they get married? He never explains anything and expects her to blindly follow his orders without any explanation. I get it but then he doesn't speak to her or leaves her alone because his 'pride' is hurt. What kind of love is that? Yes the Abbie makes some foolish decisions but if her husband would see her as a helpmate and let her in on the decisions and processes of their ranch maybe things wouldn't be so difficult. One example he buys her a horse as a wedding gift and tells her she can't ride it. He thinks to himself it's not safe till the horse knows it's way home. But he never tells her this just expects her to listen to him. She is not a child and can understand what he is talking about. He says it's her house and to do what she would like but doesn't tell her his housekeeper will get offended if she cooks in his kitchen. It's all sorts of little things like this where he brings her into his house and just expects her to understand the nuances of his household.
