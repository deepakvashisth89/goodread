This book is all about Junie B. and her experiences with lunch during first grade. At the beginning of the school year, she gets a new lunch box and is so excited that she keeps opening it in the classroom, getting her into trouble. When they go into the lunchroom, many of Junie B.'s classmates are buying their lunches, instead of bringing a packed lunch. This causes Junie B. to wonder if a bought lunch is better than a brought lunch. While debating this, she spies a cookie on Herb's tray, making her think about getting cookies in Kindergarten, so she goes to find Mrs. Gutzman. Junie B. becomes a lunch helper, and learns about being a listener and not a leader. This book would be very useful in the classroom to teach children about how things change as you get older and that you have to adapt to those changes. I would also be able to use this book in the classroom to talk about respecting others and sharing with our peers. This book also has illustrations periodically. They are pencil drawings, that help students visualize Junie B. in different situations throughout the book. I would recommend this book for emergent readers or readers around the ages of 9 to 11.
