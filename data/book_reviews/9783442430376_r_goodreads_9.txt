Nice read and was intriguing enough to keep engaged. I cared about the characters and what happened to them but I lost patience towards the end. This woman has had three intense lover affairs each one making her feel “a way I’ve never felt with anyone before” and seemingly falls desperately in love with every man she meets. Felt the last few chapters were rushed in an effort to tie everything upIn a pretty bow.
