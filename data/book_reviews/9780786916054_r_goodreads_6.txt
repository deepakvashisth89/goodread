This is a good book and a good series. I like the 5-book format, and I'm one of those readers that likes to see Salvatore boxed-in like that. I want a conclusion, dang it, and I don't want long, open-ended series where you can wander, like happened with some other stuff.Anyways, this is a good series that I've read once and enjoyed and think is a good addition to the Forgotten Realms universe. And how 'bout those Snowflake Mountains! Nice place.
