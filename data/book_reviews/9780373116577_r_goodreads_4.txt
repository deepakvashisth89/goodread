One of Caroles classics...and a delightful one.


Gosh,reading this book was like watching a romantic comedy film.The POV of the frank heroine Sophie Gordon was so hilarious,and i just really enjoyed her thoughts and emotions.She was a coulerful and lovable heroine,and no wonder the head-strong Maximilian Grant fell in love with her.

The characters and the plot were a charming one,and i just LOVE the hot romance between Sophie and Maximilian.Their kissing-scenes were SMOKING HOT!

The title to this book is because of a beautiful race horse with the name "Gracious Lady".
