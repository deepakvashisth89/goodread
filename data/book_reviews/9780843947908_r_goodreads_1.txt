Susan Edwards’ White Dreams is a hauntingly beautiful tale of love, loss, and redemption set against the vast, untamed plains of the Nebraska Territory, 1857.At its center is Star Dreamer, a woman gifted and burdened with the Sight. Once she saw visions as divine guidance, but after tragedy stole her husband, those same visions feel like chains of guilt. Seeking peace, she escapes into the white man’s world, where destiny leads her to Grady O’Brien a widower whose own heart is buried beneath grief.Their love blossoms amid prejudice and peril, tested further when they risk everything to save a free Black woman from slavers. Their act of compassion sparks vengeance from a man called the Dragon a predator who will stop at nothing to reclaim what he believes is his.Edwards weaves an epic love story of courage and spirit, filled with the mysticism of Native prophecy, the danger of the frontier, and the timeless hope of two souls daring to heal. Her prose is lush and cinematic every page alive with heartache, tension, and the wild beauty of a land both cruel and redemptive.Fans of Linda Howard, Catherine Anderson, and Beverly Jenkins will be swept away by White Dreams a story that reminds us that even in a world divided by fear, love remains the bravest act of all.
