If anyone reads this book, I didn't do my job. That should say it all. This book is not worth reading. The storyline is about a little girl runing away from her Aunts house to go become a fairy with the mushroom man in the forest when on vacation, leaving the family searching for her for three days. Yeah, you heard that right. All implausable and it sounds just as bad when you read it. I finished it thinking, I'll just give it another chance and then feeling, "let the torture be over." Sadly, I hastily chose this book at the library in one of those "cute cover" moments.
Problems:
1. The setting is Wales, Great Britain. No child is going to stay up in a tree in the rain without shoes on, regardless of whether they think they have transformed into a fairy.
2. Basic needs not met. No child would stay in a forest playing make believe for days, not eating and sleeping in a tree unless psychotic.
3. Search dogs, a helicopter, and police personal combed the area and never looked up? Are you kidding? A child didn't make a noise with all of those people calling her name?
