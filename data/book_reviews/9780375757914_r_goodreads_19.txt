It's one of the oddest madcap stories I have ever read...I feel like the author really wanted to get his point across, but I had to do follow up googling and reading to figure out what that point could be. I usually do not like stories that go through chaos only to prove a simple point (I almost quit at chapter 12 because of this). I guessed Sunday was the recruiter very early on.... so the craze and pursuit felt to me like I was just impatiently trying to get to the end of the big reveal, which also ended in another one of my loathed tropes of it's all a dream. There is much to be said about the odd conversations they all have and the intensity of the quirky observations and reveals.
