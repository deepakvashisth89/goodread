I am spoiling myself with my literary discoveries!! I enjoyed George Eliot's Middlemarch again, which is nearly 1,000 pages long. It is a fantastic story about a small village in England where several locals' destinies meet, and we embark on a grand adventure from the very first page!The novel focuses on several couples: Dorothea Brooke and M.Casaubon, a boring clergyman, followed by Dorothea and Will Ladislaw, whom we follow throughout history; the unhappy marriage of Tertius Lydgate, an ambitious but touching doctor, with Rosamond Vincy, a vulgar young woman wishing to arouse the admiration of all her neighbors; finally, the couple Fred Vincy / Mary Garth, whom I most appreciated.Besides, the characters are all more interesting than the others, offering a variety of roles for the reader to meet. I preferred Dorothea Brooke's style, which is endearing through his choices, awkward moments in his life, his generosity to Doctor Lydgate, and, finally, his access to happiness at the end of the novel. I also liked all the male characters, including M. Lydgate, Will Ladislaw, and Fred Vincy.Finally, George Eliot depicts the society of his time down to the smallest detail, allowing us to participate in animated discussions and scandals that upset the village and its surroundings.So I loved this excellent novel by George Eliot, one of the greatest of English literature, despite some flaws (though very rare).
