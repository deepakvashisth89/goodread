George Schuyler was ahead of his time when he wrote this novel, he essentially argues what we know today that race is a social construct.Black No Morestarts off as a story about one Black man who wants to be white but morphs into a larger story about what whiteness becomes when there is no Blackness. I especially enjoyed the social commentary Schuyler addresses when he calls out the different social leaders in the Black and white communities, some of whom are based on real people from the time. This classic novel does not read like your typical dry classic novel from the time, it's accessible and really good. It translates even today, minus the dated references. A funny, thought-provoking book about race, that I believe is still relevant in this current moment 93 years later.
