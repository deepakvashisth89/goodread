I love Rita Rudner. I love her humour and having it in a book is such a gift. I came across this book with luck and didn't put it down until I was done with it.

A very easy read, Ms. Rudner gives us multiple essays dealing with random things from lifeskills, marriage, lifestyle, and more.

Written in the early 90s but very timeless, Naked Beneath My Clothes is definitely worth reading over and over.
