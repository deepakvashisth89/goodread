Quite a delightful book with stories covering a wide gamut of everyday life in America. The stories are well written and very descriptive which is like the Best Meal in the World, and the Worst Meal in the World i.e. both the same. His descriptions whilst laying out the whole story became at times interminable. I was engaged with most of the stories, some very much so, some a little less. The one thing that really grated with me was his endless descriptions of the 1950's style parties. Maybe he found these boring, or false, or both but to me they just seemed to go on like Kraftwerk's "Trans Europe Express" Perhaps he was just as bored going to these, who knows? Butgenerally a great read and I recommend it to all. (Five stars except for the "Party" stories.
