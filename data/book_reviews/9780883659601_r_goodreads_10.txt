I didn't really have a good category for this book of quotes, but since some of them were well-known, I shoe-horned it into literary fiction. Oh well...From time to time I like to read bits and pieces of wisdom, smart-aleckness and memorable short observations. This book is a decent collection of all of the above.I found a lot of the quotes to be cynical regarding human behavior, and the funniest ones the most memorable. This says something about me that may or may not be flattering.Again, oh well...These types of books make for nice breaks from longer novels and can be easily picked up and put down per the situation, so handy and interesting to pick through. Recommended.
