One of the better of the Plumly Thompsons.More plot than usual, and she makes nice use of some bits of Oz history.Nice to see Dorothy back in action, the villains are kind of sweet in their friendship, I love the resolution of the meeting with the Black Witch.Bitty Bit the Seer and Piddy Penny, the much put upon prime minister, may be two of my favorite characters from Plumly-Thompson's run.The ending is rushed, as usual, but I like how we weren't't beaten over the head with the lesson of the book and aside from the painful poetry, the humor actually raised some chuckles.Loses a star, as I got a cheap edition, that included no illustrations and some issues with crowded text.
