Reviewed in the United States on June 14, 2020When the president of the United States declares that drug smuggling has become a clear and present danger against the country, a covert military campaign is launched in Columbia against the drug cartel. The rules regarding these covert campaigns are a bit murky, and when they get uncovered and it all begins to unravel, the response is to abandon the mission and the men who were part of the mission. Enter Jack Ryan in the picture, who won’t allow this to happen. Ryan starts a rescue mission deep in the Columbian danger zone to get the men out of there.This is one of the better thought out Clancy novels. He tends to get a little outlandish, not only in his premise but also his execution of the stories. Here, the premise is good, and the execution is well developed. The conclusion was satisfying and compelling. There were some definite plot holes and spots that are hard to get through, but this remains a solid read.Carl Alves – author of Blood Street
