I read this first in 6th grade and loved it. Upon re-read, I see that I still enjoy it. I wonder at the realism (but hey, Nancy was the best part of the story) as well as the use of the term "girlfriend" with a book set in the 1700s...but that may be too picky.
At times, the plot was slow, helped not at all by the fact that there was so little dialogue. In my mind, these are not critiques but facts. I would have my child read this book someday.
