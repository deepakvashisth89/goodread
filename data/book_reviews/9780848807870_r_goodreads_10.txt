Another cute Oz story, but not at all my favorite. The plot was thin (thinner than usual - the "main plot" was Dorothy and Trot going on a quest to find a birthday present for Ozma.) The subplot had more potential - a selfish Munchkin boy who gets ahold of a magic word of transformation, and he reluctantly teams up with the Nome King on a quest to conquer the Emerald City. While that plot had more potential (and had some really clever and suspenseful moments), the Munchkin and Nome were vanquished almost accidentally, cheapening the whole thing.Again, Baum's cleverness with inventing fun creatures and new magical ideas is strong in this book, but with such a flimsy plot, it's not an Oz tale that I would re-read.
