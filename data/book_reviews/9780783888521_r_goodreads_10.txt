I rounded up to 3 stars. How anyone can give this 5 stars is beyond me. I picked up the Eve Duncan series because so many people raved about it. The first book I found somewhat believable and enjoyed it for what it was.This book you literally have to suspend your rational thoughts and believe that the writer knows about police procedure. NOT. Joe ( Eve's protector and the relationship is totally bizarre) is somehow an Ex navy seal, police detective, big brother and now he miraculously becomes Eve's lover? He comes across as more of a street thug than law enforcement. Let's suspend our intelligence and believe the FBI profilers who are trying to find a serial killer are working the case, ( Only three agents it seems, never mention any fbi procedure. They just let Eve Duncan escort an orphaned girl around because hey, maybe that's what they do?The ending was so unbelievable and over the top, ( I don't give spoilers), I was literally laughing through the last 50 pages. I retired from federal law enforcement and you have to understand when pursuing a serial killer a task force is set up with a command center. Not one agent and 2 assistants, LMFAO. The beginning of the book was interesting but midway through i could tell Iris was just tossing ideas around and it was all about Eve, the young orphaned girl was interesting but forget about her, let's hear Eve talking to the ghost of her daughter some more ( that is getting old and this is book #2). I actually was thinking of giving 2 stars but being i finished the book in 3 hours by my pool, taking the occasional dive, I was mildly entertained but mostly laughing at the unrealistic story. People giving this book 5 stars? Did we read the same book? Have anyone of you read a Jeffery Deaver book? At least he makes law enforcement seem somewhat competent. Iris seems to have cashed in on a mediocre series, ( I have been told the books do not get better and just skip to the final books to find out what happened to Eve's precious child Bonnie). If you know nothing about police procedure and like fast paced action with a little love interest then by all means enjoy the book. I will wait a few months and try one of her standalone novels. I read The Ugly Duckling by this author first and that was a more believable story.... Congrats to Iris for cashing in. I was always lazy and retired recently but now I may have to write something because the absurdity that these crime thrillers are such bestsellers is amazing...
