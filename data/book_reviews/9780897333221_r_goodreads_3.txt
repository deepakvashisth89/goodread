Book 2 of another amusing series by Joyce Porter. Book 1 is hard to get/expensive for some reason (?). Anyhow this one finds the Hon Con (Honorable Constance) investigating the death of an au pair in a house somewhere below her in the town. She must chat up the neighbors (with whom she has frosty relations) to pursue her investigative hobby. She trips into a cheerful girl cab driver with whom Hon Con flirts shamelessly (poor old Ms. what's her name, the house sharer/cleaning lady at HonCon's house). Finally it is all worked out - though mostly for good clean Porter fun.
