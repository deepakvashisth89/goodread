I really enjoyed learning about the Western part of the world with Sariana as she fled there to escape after failing to be accepted at the University in the East. Since the two sides had only recently been re-introduced, she isn't familiar with a lot of the customs. This is a problem when a Shield takes an interest in her.It wasn't right of Gryph to marry her without her knowing that sex would mean marriage in his clan and as such he deserved every insult she threw at him later.Learning about Shields and where they actually came from was interesting as was the hunt for a new crystal ship. Gryph and Sariana were a great match.This was a re-read
