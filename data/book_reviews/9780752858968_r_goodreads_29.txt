7 out of 5 stars. Complex novel about the friends of the man who gave Siobhan a black eye and the ex-SAS soldier who shoots 3 teenagers at an expensive private school, killing 2 of them and himself. Siobhan has been giving Rebus CDs of heavy metal music to add to his collection of classic rock and jazz. C.O.D.Y. (Come On Die Young) is a message a dead man sends her and a metal song. Rebus reacquaints himself with his cousin's family because his boy is one of the dead kids. Rebus has somehow gotten 2nd degree burns on his hands (he says he plunged them into hot dishwater or a hot bathtub but nobody believes him). He was also the last person seen leaving a bar with a young thug who burned to death in his kitchen when the chip pan caught fire later on that evening. Rebus learns that 'The Wind in the Willows' unlocks the kid in everyone, especially when one kid tells him 'Toad was an arse, wasn't he?'. The Army sends out 2 investigators to see what the SAS mass shooter had on his water skiing boat and yacht. Drugs? Guns? And are they there to investigate or cover something up? - Covid's over (only 100 people a day dying in the US). Trump's a Winner (Iowa, not in court). Most of the US, Europe and China is freezing to death so 2023 being the hottest year Ever goes down the Memory Hole.
