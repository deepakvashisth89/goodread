I really do enjoy reading this book to young EYFS children who are developing a sense of self and place in the world. It is a story that affirms and assures, with beautiful illustrations and a very warm tone. Harry lives in the city; his granddad in the country. Both are rather settled where they are, but each tries the life of the other. A little about other types of home and different types of transport sympathetically added along the way, and it sends a message about reconciling with difference and trying new things.

I read it today to a group of Nursery children: the story at the end of the day that is intended to settle and send children on their way feeling a sense of warmth and belonging. A relatively long story by Nursery standards, but the vast majority remained engaged throughout and were responsive to my invitations to engage and share personal anecdotes related to the theme. A success every time.
