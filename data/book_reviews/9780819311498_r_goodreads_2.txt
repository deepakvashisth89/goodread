Sherlock Chick is called in to find a missing little mouse who got lost after being scared by a cat. Can Sherlock Chick find where a little mouse would hide?Sherlock Chick checks all sorts of potential animal homes with only eyes showing one one spread before the animal is revealed in the next spread. This gives little readers plenty of opportunities to guess what kind of animal may live in that kind of spot before the reveal each time. The mystery is very simple, and the text is quick and easy for beginning readers.
