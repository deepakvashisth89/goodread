Bertrice Small was an amazing and super talented author! To this day I have not read any book like her.This is a strong storyline, rich in love, adventure and attention to details. I have read this book so many times and never tire of it. Miranda and Jared are the main characters and a strong and in love couple.This book is on Audible and free with my plan and the narrators performance was amazing!The book is more detailed, the Audible cuts out some parts. But, it’s such a beautiful and sad story of love and perseverance.
