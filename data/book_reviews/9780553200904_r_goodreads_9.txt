If I was to recommend one series for everyone to read at least once in their lifetime, it wouldn't be Narnia, though those have so much good in them, and it wouldn't be Little House, though those are rich with life, it would be Little Britches.

From honesty, to hard work, to life and family and getting back up on the horse, this series is full of life and goodness and of becoming a person who knows how to steward what they have been given, especially the first 3-4 books.

If you haven't read these yet, do. If you haven't ever read these aloud to your kids, once the oldest is around 9, they should be old enough, and it will be good for all of them, from oldest to youngest, no matter the age, I would be willing to wager. There's so much in these, and they are more than worth the read.
