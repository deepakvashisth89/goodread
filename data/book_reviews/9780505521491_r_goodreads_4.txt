A beautiful and emotional story most of which takes place in 1946. Colin Castle felt he was a failure and kept trying to prove otherwise. Liesl was grief stricken and lost without her love, a young widow. I loved this story and I want to read more by this author absolutely. Colin and Liesl’s journey is full of emotion, pain, sweetness and desire.~ I received this book for free from eBook Discovery. I voluntarily review this book. This is my honest review.~
