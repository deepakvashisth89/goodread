The Internet For Dummiesby John R. Levine, Carol Baroudi, Margaret Levine Young (John Wiley & Sons 1993)(004.678). This is part of the popular "----- For Dummies" series. When first published in 1993, this was a widely read and hugely popular and influential reference/guide to the unknown and untamed beast all had heard about but few had yet encountered which was called "The Internet" or "The Worldwide Web." With this guide in hand, many of us tiptoed online for the first time. It is now completely dated, but it remains an important artifact of the early interweb. My rating: 7/10, finished 1996.
