Like many books I read as a child, this one is sparser than my memories have built it up to be. There are whole training montage sequences that I apparently imagined out of whole cloth. It's still an effective page-turner, but I find myself much more uncomfortable with the book's depiction of slavery than I was as a child, and particularly with how often we're told that some people are simply meant to be slaves. I know this is only the first in the series, and I vaguely recall that many truths in the first book are overturned by the third, so that won't keep me from continuing reading, but it does diminish my enjoyment of this particular volume.
