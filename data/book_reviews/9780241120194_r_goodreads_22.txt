Diana Holman-Hunt was the granddaughter of William Holman Hunt, the Victorian artist and co-founder of the Pre-Raphaelite Brotherhood. As a child, while her father Hilary worked abroad, she divided her time between her maternal grandparents and “Grand” – the artist’s second wife - and this memoir reflects on those years.

Her upbringing was somewhat eccentric, particularly the periods spent with Grand, who lived a sparse and frugal life. I found parts of this enjoyable, but at times it felt repetitive, and overall it wasn’t my favourite memoir.
