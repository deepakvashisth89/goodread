I'm seriously ashamed by how long this book took me to read. I'm going to blame the typical cliche: life got in the way. Between planning a wedding & now planning a move, I just had a hard time getting sucked into this one.As with any anthology, I liked some stories while I disliked others. To be fair, I don't think any of these stories were "bad"--I just didn't have ample time to sit down & really read & give them the thought and analysis they deserved. I'd get partway through a story, have to put it down, and then when I'd pick it back up, I'd be lost.This was a great collection of some lesser-known stories from some of the fantasy genre's greats. I just wasn't personally feeling it.
