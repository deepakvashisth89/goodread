This was a fast read for me, as I've been reading about Native Americans for as long as I can remember ( since I started reading) and knew a lot of the story already. I've read a lot about Geronimo and, after him, Cochise. I knew less about Mangas Colorado and Victorio and some of the other Apache leaders so some of the more interesting parts of the book for me were about them.Interestingly, there's a chapter on the Modocs in California and their war against the US government. They put up a good fight, although heavily outnumbered...This book was first published in 1935 but, surprisingly, shows some sympathy toward the Apaches. However, the author still uses words such as "vengeful" and "hate-goaded" to describe Apaches. If your land is being invaded and your people are being killed and your culture is being destroyed, I guess you could become "vengeful."
