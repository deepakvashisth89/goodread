I will kill the lady tonight at 8pm. This cryptic note is delivered anonymously to the 87th Precinct. Is it a crank or a killer wanting to be caught. It’s July 24th and a hot, humid day where everyone is sweating and hoping for a breeze to cool them down or rain.Carella, Hawes and the other detectives are in a race against the clock. There is a chase, gunfight and clues which are tantalizing but keep leading them up blind alleys.Apparently the author wrote this in 20 days snd it definitely could have used a bit more refinement and editing. Still entertaining with the motive getting more money for an expensive woman.
