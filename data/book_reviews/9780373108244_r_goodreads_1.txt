Re A World of Difference - this is a semi sweet story about a 25 year old virgin top fashion model and the 30ish playwright who can't decide if he despises her or loves her or maybe he just likes to irk her a lot. The story opens with the h at a fashionably exclusive NYC society party and she is bored out of her mind.The H comes up and immediately starts attacking her about her choice of career and forcefully grabbing her wrist. He is a famous playwright in New York for a little while to see about his new hit play. They argue a bit about the worthiness of modeling and advertising and the H escorts her home and says he will see her again.The h did not exactly like him but she can't stop thinking about him and then she gets asked to do a photo shoot with him. There is a lot of sexual tension over the next two days and the H asks her out for a date. They meet up again and they agree they have some kind of attraction going on.The H claims he doesn't really do a lot of socializing and the h has been building her career for the past 8 years. The h has to travel for a job but before she goes the H claims he is in love with her. The h eventually returns and they spend a few hours together but then the H has to travel to help his widowed sister and her two kids. He tells the h he will call her, but he never does cause she never gave him her phone number.In the meantime the h has made arrangements to take some time off but when the H doesn't call or write, she decides to go on holiday alone. While on her way out of NYC, she gets into a minor fender bender and then while she is dealing with that, the H shows up - he was chasing after her.He convinces her to accompany him to his rural Canadian home and the h agrees. They have some passionate kissing and some more fighting and he accuses her of using her make up and clothes as a mask to keep him out. They spend a day or so taping maple trees for syrup and the H says he loves her, but she doesn't know what she feels besides a lot of lust. Then the H starts writing his new play and pretty much ignores her for the next 10 days or so.The h decides to go see her family, they live in a small coal mining town and mend some fences with them. The H wants to consummate the relationship but they put off the act because they have no birth control on hand. ( A true first for HPlandia and maybe the ONLY time in HPlandia the H and h postpone physical relations because of the pregger possibility.)The H will miss her but he is engrossed in his new play and the h goes home, mends her familial relationship and decides she loves the H. She doesn't know how they will manage marriage because their lives are truly vastly different. All the things she can do for a living require a city and he needs the countryside to write. She doesn't want a commuter marriage, so she won't agree to marry him.The widowed sis and her kids come to stay with the H and the h returns, there is a fair amount of tension building as the H doesn't want to get physical with the h while his sis is around. The h does a makeover on the sister and the sister suggests that the h should open little salons where regular people can get make up lessons etc. The h likes the idea and when the H gets a minor injury and she is afraid it is serious she realizes that she has to marry him cause no one knows how long they have.The h decides to call her new business "A World of Difference" and thus the title has two meanings - the h and H had huge differences in their lifestyles and the h's new business venture. The H and h finally succumb to their mutual passion and HEA all around.One thing I found interesting in this book is the gradual rehabilitation of fashion modeling as a career for the h in HPlandia. Amanda Carpenter used it in Rage of Passion, Kay Thorpe used it in Double Deception and SF uses it here. In all three books, the ladies are very serious about their careers and work really hard to build them. The model h is being show as a businesswoman. They don't sleep around with all and sundry, and it seems the old HP standard of models being another word for tart is finally fading away.Other HP authors like Robyn Donald and Daphne Claire had used the h as a model career, but the h's always felt vaguely ashamed of using their looks to earn a living. Lindsay Armstrong's Finding Out (which is coming up soon) is a further exploration of the topic of selling products with sex, but that story is much darker and the conclusions are debatable.For now, it is enough that the h who is a model is not instantly cast as a promiscuous woman, and it is very interesting to see how the era of the 80's supermodels not just being pretty faces and arm candy, but actual career women carried over to HPLandia.As for the H and h and HEA in this one, I do have some doubts about the long term feasibility for this pairing, the H is awful fond of provoking fights and the h was actually the more sensible one. They really did not know each other very long and I worried that the H and h were basically caught up in the passion.
