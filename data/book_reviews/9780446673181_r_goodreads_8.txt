Once again, I have mixed feelings. The time travel premise and the historical elements are very cool and interesting. Unfortunately...The plot itself is a mess, the pacing isveryslow and that messes with the sense of stakes throughout the book, the male characters remain constant jerks (except for Huff, who is thoroughly punished for his softness), every female character except for Rhiow gets a "stupid woman who needs the other characters to tell them that they're stupid for being so emotional and who needs to prioritize other people's emotions" treatment (and the one who doesn't becomes a villain out of nowhere, creating a final battle which also comes out of nowhere). I'm still going to read the next book and finish my Diane Duane ebook collection, but I'm remembering why I didn't like this trilogy as a kid.
