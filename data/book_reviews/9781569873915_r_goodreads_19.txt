When Ebba asked me to join her in a reread of this childhood classic, I eagerly agreed. This was my favorite book as a child. I received it as a Christmas gift when I was 8 or 9, and read it many times over. I looked forward to being in the Swiss Alps again with Heidi and the grandfather and the goats.Reading as an adult, were there inconsistencies, miraculous things that were never explained? Of course. Were the good , kind people a little too kind? Of course. Was the ending just a little too perfect? Of course. But none of that mattered. The magic of Heidi is that the cynical old woman reading this was replaced by that 8 year old who believed that miracles do exist, that goodness is repaid by goodness, and that happy endings do exist. What a relief to be in that world for a few days.Thank you Ebba. It was a pleasure reading with you.
