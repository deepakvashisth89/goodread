I enjoyed this book, but it was difficult to follow at times. The story started out really intense, but it didn't maintain the same intensity throughout. There were times, however, that the intensity came back ... but only in snippets. This book took place in France and there was quite a bit of the French language used throughout the book ... I found that having the French language inserted in this way ... it just didn't add any value to the book ... it actually detracted because I wasn't able to get the connection between the inserted French words and the rest of conversation being spoken.
