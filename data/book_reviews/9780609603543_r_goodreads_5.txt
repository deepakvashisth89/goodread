This is an older book, published in 1998, written by radio talk personality, Dr. Joy Browne. I was not familiar with her syndicated radio program, nor her name. I thought the title sounded interesting, so gave it a try,

I may love Joy Browne (now deceased; died in 2016). She addresses callers personal life queries with humor, and no nonsense practical advise, even, "seek therapy." Granted, the book format, and I suspect her radio program, are basic, quick assessments, but still practical advice. She doesn't engender whiners or pity partiers. Shrink wrapped: Feelings happen; feelings are neither good or bad, I may not be in control of what happens to me, but I do control my reactions, responses and am responsible for my behavior!

Lots of light bulb gems tucked into her advise.

Keep in mind this is SELF-HELP, not mental illness diagnosis.
