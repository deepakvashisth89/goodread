I had a girlfriend who spotted a bumper sticker reading "Blow up Your Television." This book tells you why. TV is not only "chewing gum for the mind" and "furniture that talks back to you" but positively pernicious. Anything that is mediatized, from ballet to war, is, by definition, a TV show, not reality. and will absorb you into its world. Don't like the Viet Nam War? Switch over to "I Dream of Jeannie". Per Jerry Mander, we must abolish television before it abolishes us. Take that, Marshall McCullen and Andy Warhol!
