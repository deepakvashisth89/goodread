Dick Francis was a jack of many trades.During World War II,he was a pilot.Then he was a jockey,then a journalist and finally,a bestselling thriller writer.His own experiences find plenty of expression in his books.Here,like the author himself,the protagonist is a pilot,ferrying a bunch of passengers to race courses.Crooked characters get on board.There are bomb blasts and explosions,and the Francis hero finding himself in mortal danger,fights for his very survival.Published in 1970,another cracking,gripping Francis thriller.Sustained tension and excitement,all the way.
