Four people are killed in a fancy Moscow Hotel during the Moscow Film Festival, and Rostnikov and his crew realize that the deaths are linked to a terrorist group that the KGB have had their eyes on. Rostnikov is given the job of tracking down the terrorists but he knows that he will be the fall guy if things go wrong.Good characters, but a couple of wonky plot points and somewhat unsatisfying ending (which one kind of expects given the Russian setting...).This was book #49 on our 2017 Read-alouds List. This was also a "Lutrecia book."
