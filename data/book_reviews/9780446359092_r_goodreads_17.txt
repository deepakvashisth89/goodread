I really like Patricia Wentworth's Miss Silver mysteries. While her plots are perhaps not the greatest, they are the definition of cozy mysteries to me- perfect to curl up with in front of the fire with a cup of hot cocoa. Even though they're mostly about murders, they leave you smiling and feeling good. Beggar's Choice is the second of Wentworth's books that are not part of the Miss Silver series. Unfortunately, both of them have been lackluster for me. Beggar's Choice seemed like it was going to be cute and fun, but it just didn't do anything for me. I actually went back and reread some of it a couple times, thinking maybe I didn't give it a fair enough shot at first. But, it didn't help. A disappointment.
