I liked this book but there were a few time jumps that made it hard to see how the characters’ relationships developed.I liked Amanda and O’Reilly’s relationship but it was a bit quick. Throughout the whole book, Amanda is resisting him then near the end they sleep together and suddenly she’s in love with him.Also, there could have been more scenes showing Amanda getting to know O’Reilly’s kids (Hannah, Liam and Missy) as at first the kids hated her, then Missy was really attached to her and finally Hannah and Liam got over their hate. I wanted to see them interact more.I didn’t like how Amanda still isn’t sure about what her future will look like in Australia or if she can even stay there forever but Amanda and O’Reilly are separated for a month or two and when they see each other again, she suddenly likes Australia.I liked this book but I preferred other books by this author.
