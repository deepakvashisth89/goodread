Some more primo woobie content from Mickey Zucker Reichert. If you are interested in watching a main character suffer, cry, and fail at things, these books are for you. If not, I'm sure it would be, as another reviewer says, "awkward to read." The section dividers in this book are sad birds. There is, admittedly, a lot of crying.But in between that, it's got great action scenes, thrilling dramatic tension (holy shit), top notch funny tabletop banter between the party members (arguing over dog ownership, squabbling over dinner, and making NPCs extremely uncomfortable) and a quasi-gay friendship for the main character. What can I say, Reichert likes what she likes! And so do I. Wish I had spent my adolescence reading these books instead of wretched dnd novels where author self-inserts lick elf ears.
