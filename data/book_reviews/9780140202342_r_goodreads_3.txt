Beautifully written, page-turner account of the medieval period. Although the subject matter has the potential to be dry, I found it utterly compelling. Split into three periods, each is treated with sections on the kings, the broader government, economic and social trends, religion and education, and the arts: the writer demonstrates expertise on all of these areas to provide comprehensive coverage, despite the variety of subjects involved. The account does not dwell on events, concentrating rather on trends, pressures and consequences that provides a picture of all aspects of society of the period. In particular it shows how much more is known about the period than is commonly understood, and gives as much as it can in the space available. Thoroughly enjoyable read
