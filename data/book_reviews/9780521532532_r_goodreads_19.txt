ROMEO: Peace, peace, Mercutio, peace!Thou talk’st of nothing.MERCUTIO: True, I talk of dreams,Which are the children of an idle brain,Begot of nothing but vain fantasy,Which is as thin of substance as the airAnd more inconstant than the wind.Read this one many years ago so it was time for a reread. Witty, sarcastic, and sad, enjoyed it all over again.
