As I've mentioned in other reviews, I'm a huge sudden fiction fan. I write, publish, and teach the form, and I'm pretty certain I've read all of the Shapard and Thomas anthologies. I've read this collection more than once, and my favorite is Buzzati's "The Falling Girl." You will encounter a bevy of other authors whom you may not associate with short-shorts: Gabriel Garcia Marquez, Julio Cortazar, Joyce Carol Oates, Italo Calvino, Margaret Atwood, Doris Lessing, Nadine Gordimer.
