Sometimes you just need a cheesy Zebra vampire novel and if you have such a craving,The Vampire Odysseyfits the bill nicely. The story switches between our two leads- Samantha and her adopted daughter Dani. Samantha found Dani in a dumpster behind her house one morning when she had just become a cop in Tampa. Long story, but she ended up adopting her. 18 years later, Samantha and Dani move to L.A. for Samantha's new job as a private investigator for an insurance company. Dani meets some new friends and lets just say they can be a little long in the tooth...I am not a huge fan of the vampire genre, but Ciencin writes a mean gritty, bloody prose and for the most part, paces the tale well. The vampires here are rather unique, being able to 'feed' on emotions (especially fear) as well as, of course, blood. Ciencin also fills this with lots of pop culture/horror references and since the book was published in 1992, lot of nostalgia for sure; it reminded me of Sean Hutson in that regard. Finally, this had one of the best action sequences I have read in a while-- imagine vampires in a stolen cop car being chased through L.A. and then through a shopping mall. Good Stuff! 3.5 toothsome stars, rounding up for the ending.
