Dr. Vivian Bearing, professor of 17th century poetry and John Dunne scholar, has been diagnosed with stage 4 Ovarian Cancer. What we see is her in her final days dealing with death and seeing her in flashbacks as a child, a student, and an inflexible professor who doesn't show kindness to her students.This one-act play was recommended by my girlfriend. I haven't read a play since my senior year of high school in 1998 (The Crucible). It was easy to read, but the analysis of Donne's work went a little over my head, as I'm unfamiliar with his work. There seemed to be some character growth and it calls attention to how patients can often feel like experiments in hospitals instead of people.
