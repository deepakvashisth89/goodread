This book was an eye-opener for me. I had not been introduced to the concept of left and right brain organization. It helped me understand myself and others.There are some great tips about beginning to clean out clutter and organize your life. Not just tips, but ways to go about doing it.The only drawback, and the reason for the four stars, is that it was written in 1994. Several chapters on organizing your office are outdated due to technology. As we now use mobile phones, some of the filing information, etc. is unusable.
