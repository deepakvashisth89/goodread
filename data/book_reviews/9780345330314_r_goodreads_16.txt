The Naked Sun was Asimov's second novel to feature the unlikely detective pair of robot Daneel Olivaw and human Elijah Baley and is my favorite of all of his novels. It's the ultimate locked-room mystery story, a great science fiction depiction of future civilizations, and a compelling page-turner on all counts. His speculation on how electronic development and advances may affect human interaction seems more prescient now than it did when it first appeared in John W. Campbell's Astounding Science Fiction magazine late in 1956. There are three complex cultures developed and portrayed consistently in the book, the Earth humans, the robots, and the Solarians. It's a classic, and I recommend it for fans of both genres.
