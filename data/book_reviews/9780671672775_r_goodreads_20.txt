Was not sure where Clegg was going with this at first, and his surprisingly lyrical prose was the only thing keeping me from DNF-ing it after the first 150 pages, but GLAD I STUCK WITH IT!A Rosemary’s Baby rip on the surface, but a lot else going on here — haunted house with ghost old ladies who keep fetuses in jars, weird voodoo shit, etc. The climax goes bonkers, with an old man exploding his genitals with a hammer, bag ladies rousing bad husbands with flashbacks of their dad drinking blood, and a talking fetus trying to climb its way into an unoccupied womb. Gonna see if I can’t track down a copy of Clegg’s Goat Dance, ‘cause this left me impressed (even if it is too long).Found at Recycled Books and Records, Denton, TX.
