I have seen many people hating this novel. I ask myself - why? I studied Sons and Lovers during my MA in English Literature and found the novel amazing - yes, a little different if compared to what the usual novels offer. However, the very difference that we find in this novel makes us read it. Paul is a deep-personality who is often in a fix - which way to choose. His relationships are failures because he cannot push himself into any of those entirely. Clara and Miriam are like two distinct poles in the life of Paul but his affinity always lies with Gertrude, his mother.For sons, a mother has been shown as the most important person and the strongest impact on their lives which they cannot resist. The novel is vintage and amazing if you care to read it.
