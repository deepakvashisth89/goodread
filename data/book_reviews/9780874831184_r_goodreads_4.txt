I enjoyed reading "Classic American Ghost Stories" By Deborah L. Downer especially because it will give me some ideas on what stories to tell when I am camping next time. It was just a fun book full of ghosts, Monsters, one was even bigfoot I believe. I would really like to take this book on a trip to spook some people out but I don't believe I am allowed to check books out over the summer. I liked it very much because I always like watching scary movies or reading scary books although I regret it later because they freak me out at night.If you get freaked out easily I would not recommend this book. Some of the stories can be pretty frightening, in one case a man on a horse has been running from the ghost of a man he killed for so long that the horse starts bleeding out of its mouth because it has been breathing so hard. So if you are faint of heart I do not recommend this book.All in all I think this is a very good book if you are not frightened easily. I myself do not get scared easily but this book scared me a little, but that may be because I can make very vivid pictures in my mind about what is going on in the book. Anyway thanks for reading my review. If you read the book I hope you like it.
