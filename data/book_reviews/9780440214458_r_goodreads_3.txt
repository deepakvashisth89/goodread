I liked this book, but did not love it. It was very long and very far fetched. Also - quite old fashioned, as like when I was young. More of a romance novel than I normally like. The story was good and I did enjoy it and wanted to find out what would happen (although as in most romance novels - you know that all will end well....). Just goes to show you really can't judge a book by it's cover - I saw this book years ago in the library and thought it looked great - but didn't check it out as I had many others in my to read pile. Later that week, Bob and I went to a used book sale in Brockport for their sidewalk sale at the Episcopal Church and I found the same book in hardcover and bought it for 50 cents! It had been in my to read pile for awhile. Glad I am finished with it.
