Written as a “business novel” like its predecessors, this book is an easy and fast read, relatively entertaining and presents very little friction for those who have read “The Goal”, “Critical Chain” and other Goldratt novels.Good for perspective and making connections; does little to enable or disseminate any concrete practice. It shows the shift required, and the nature of the continued learning process, but does little to put practical tools or approaches in the reader’s hands.Given the format, I believe that was never the intention.
