This one was a tough book to slog through. The subject matter fascinated me. I anticipated some tell-all about building a new town owned by Disney. I thought we'd get eccentric neighbors, tales of tourists, etc. but a lot of the book was dull. Seriously boring.All the POV switching was obnoxious and there was a lot of repetitive language - and repetitive ideas. I had to skip full chapters at times because I just could not make myself care.This book was alright but could've been significantly pared down. Or padded out with very different details.
