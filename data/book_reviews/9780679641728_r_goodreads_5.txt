This short story is deceptively simple. A wealthy friend, a poor friend, they know each other from childhood. Given their present standings, they may perhaps have never crossed paths in adulthood. Yet, they care for each other. Enough that the wealthy friend graciously obliges when her poor friend asks to borrow a diamond necklace to go to a party… and there it's that all starts to go wrong.For years, this story felt to me as a preachy fable about staying in your line (in this case social class) or risk being punished by Fate itself.This time, I felt it was more a story about being very careful of who you call a friend…A quick and interesting read, nonetheless
