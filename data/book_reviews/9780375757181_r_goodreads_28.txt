46th book of 2022

The Return of the Native by Thomas Hardy is truly magnificent. Like many of Hardy's works, nature and scenery play a role as almost a character in and of themselves, which is something I find incredible about his novels. Everything about this novel is spot on. The characters are memorable, and the story grabs you. Truly a brilliant novel. I have a discussion video posted on my BookTube channel which I will link to down below.
