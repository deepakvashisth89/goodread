A not-bad collection of weird fiction tales from the 30s, all of which appeared in the magazine Weird Tales. Interestingly, even though H. P. Lovecraft is cited in the book's foreword and in the introductions of each story as a major influence, none of Lovecraft's work is included. There are okay fantasy and sci-fi stories by Seabury Quinn, Nictzin Dyalhis, Edmond Hamilton, a Frank Belknap
Long, plus one of Robert E. Howard's best non-Conan stories, and a great horror tale by David H. Keller. The accompanying Virgil Finlay illustrations are unfortunately very badly reproduced. Overall fine but not great.
