In true Dove-Lyon fashion, two souls are brought together to find their purpose in life. The beginning of this story was a bit difficult to dive into as it seemed to be dark and filled with anger. Yet, as it moved along one could begin to find the warmth and beauty in it. Life as we know it is a roller coaster ride with many ups and downs and this author does an amazing job of demonstrating this throughout the entire story. Beautifully written and crafted, a sure inspiring hit.
