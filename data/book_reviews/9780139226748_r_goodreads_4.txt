Yes, certain stories/themes are a bit outdated but if that's all you get out of this book you're not seeing the big picture. Way before "The Secret" took off in popularity Claude Bristol explains that this is not a new concept, it's not a secret or something unheard of. On the contrary, he explains that you've already heard of this theme the problem is that people seldom stop and realize how easy it is to have success in whatever endeavor you are pursuing simply by changing the way you think. I am honestly not a non-fiction reader but I have read this book front to back over 4 times. I always keep it nearby, whenever I falter and loose my focus I come back to TNT and it sets me on the right path. It helps you take responsibility for your own actions and not let that stop you from having success in the future. Essentially success is a mind game and be it that you want to start a new career, invent something or simply travel, TNT is going to help all of those under the same theme and umbrella. Quick read, have a highlighter nearby when reading.
