Tries too hardI love swearing more than most people, but I felt that this book tried too hard to gain a laugh. The overly-American style of humour comes across as extremely try-hard and really not very funny.From an Aussie point of view, I found myself rolling my eyes more than forming a subtle smile through my reading. Perhaps we’re elite in our swearing over here!Although this book was somewhat entertaining, it feels as though it was almost a direct spit-out from ChatGPT. Not a book I would recommend. It’s a quick read, so easy to knock out of the way without wasting too much time. I feel 2 stars is generous, but the concept was great and I really do like swearing, so I think it’s a fair rating.
