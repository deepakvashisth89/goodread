Coming back to this book after so long, and reading the other reviews now slating it, I was happy to find I still really enjoyed it. I think it was the perfect ending to the 4th Age, an tied up all the main novel series. Was I gutted about some of the scenes sure, but they fitted into the narrative well. Tanis has always been my favourite character, and when I was young, him just being killed by a brute upset me, but now as an adult, I can understand why it was done, and for Steel, who didn't kill to get the credit for it, just perfect.
