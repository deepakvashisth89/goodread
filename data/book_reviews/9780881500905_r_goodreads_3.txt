Another entry in Codfish Sherlock series with the Banberry "Cranberry" clan who have made their fortune selling tarts nationwide (reminds me of Table Talk pies). The patriarch brings the family back to the ancestrial town of Weesit, MA on Cape Cod. He plans on fulfilling his deceased uncle desire to help the town. After a few months of civic improvement, some of the citizens are starting showing signs of resentment. When the resentment turns to murder, Asey Mayo is called upon to solve the crime. I enjoyed meeting Dr. Cummings wife for the first time. She seems like a Gracie Allen type character. Missing in this book is the middle aged matron to play Dr. Watson to Asey's Sherlock. The fog could almost be a character in the story. Its a "stayer" and so is Asey Mayo. Some nice action scenes on dark and foggy nights.
