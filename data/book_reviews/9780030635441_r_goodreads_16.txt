Wow I finished this book fast!78/100Devil Wears Prada murder mystery, so many queer undertones and sass, old gay verbiage that rolls off the tongue, and just all around investing tension that builds with every question and new revelation.I found myself invested for the first time in the suspects and thought I was getting somewhere with my prediction but I was fortunately wrong!! The twist at the end got me gagged but at the same time felt way too convenient in retrospect because Henry, the detective, almost took on a role of unreliable narrator. It was inauthentic that his mind puts pieces together that are irrelevant come reveal time, which I guess is the intention of misleading the reveal but at the same time feels like the detective is too smart for his own good, and it kind of fell a tiny bit flat for that reason.
