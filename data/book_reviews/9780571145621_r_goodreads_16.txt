I wonder how I missed reading this book in my childhood. The adventures of Jim Hawkins and his friends, the treasure hunt, and pirates would have been alluring to my young mind. I'm glad that I read it at last.

The book surprised me. I expected a complete children's story. But this was not so. It touched on mature themes such as treachery, mutiny, and murder to please the adult audience and adventure and heroism to please the young audience.

I liked the characters. Jim Hawkins was a likable hero. His friends, the doctor, the captain, and the squire were interesting. I was also taken in by the pirates, especially the cunning and double-dealing John Silver. The story moved slowly until the sea adventure called for action. Once the action started, it became a gripping tale. Overall, their adventure was a fun read.
