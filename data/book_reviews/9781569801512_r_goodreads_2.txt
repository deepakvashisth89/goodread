I enjoyed this. I read it after reading Ragman's Son by K. Douglas. I didn't know that much about him (his earlier life). Then I heard about this book. What a hoot! Diana was from a super wealthy family ("we were so rich it was considered bad taste to talk about money") in Bermuda. I think they were considered the ruling class. She wanted to be an actress and did manage a career. Her first impression of Kirk was when he picked her up for a date and he was wearing his Navy officer uniform ("funny, but I didn't remember him being this short when he stepped out of the elevator.") He also had a huge ego and loved talking about himself. He had shelves and mantles full of his awards and trophies. Their married life was all about him at first. One time Diana won a celebrity tennis tournament in Hollywood and was awarded a small trophy. She brought it home, cleared off a big space on the mantle and placed her trophy there between all his awards, as if to say "I'm a human too." I can see why she divorced him. Gossipy, but not gross stories.
