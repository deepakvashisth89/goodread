⭐️⭐️⭐️½ (3.5/5 Stars)

The Adventures of Reddy Fox was a fun and engaging read that held my interest from beginning to end. Thornton Burgess does a wonderful job bringing the animals and the Green Forest to life, and Reddy's adventures are both entertaining and suspenseful.

My only disappointment was the ending. After all of Reddy's mistakes and close calls, I expected him to learn a lesson in humility. Instead, the story ends rather abruptly, and his character doesn't seem to grow much from his experiences.

Overall, it's a charming classic with memorable characters and enjoyable storytelling, but I was left wishing for a stronger ending and a clearer lesson.
