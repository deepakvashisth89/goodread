Kendra met Daniel three years ago during a hurricane. They spent a few nights together waiting out the storm. At that time he pretended not to understand English, spoke broken, sporatic words, and gave her a fake name.I liked that Kendra was cautious about Daniel and wanted to protect her son. He had lied to her and disappeared from her life. I felt she wad entitled to feel the way she felt.I didn't like Daniel at first or his instance that they knew each other after their brief time together. I'm not a fan of love at first sight, I believe you have to know someone to really love them.The last few chapters were my favorite. I'm glad Kendra gave Daniel a chance.
