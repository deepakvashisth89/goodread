This is a good book for parents to read with children so that they can understand someone close to them dying rather suddenly. I am not sure if I would do it as a guided reading text or not, it would depend on the level of maturity of the children. Copyrighted in 1973, I noticed some interesting vocabulary that you don't hear often which included; hitch-hiking and attention getter.Guided Reading Level S, DRA 40, grade level equivalent 3.2, and lexile 640.
