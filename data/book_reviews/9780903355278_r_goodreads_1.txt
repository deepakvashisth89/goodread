Children’s Lit and Drama strikes again. Not only did I have to get the giant Norton’s Anthology of Children’s Literature for this class, I also had to track down a copy of Read with Me. This wasn’t easy considering it was only published in the UK and therefore had to be ordered from Amazon. It took forever to get here, and I was already sort of fed up with this book at this point.

Liz Waterland’s Read With Me is a great book for elementary students who will be teaching kids to read. However, I am not going to be an elementary teacher or a teacher of any sort so my interest in the topic of discussion is limited. It has really good information about how to teach children a love of books and understand of literature rather than just how to read, and Waterland makes a convincing argument for this type of education. I agree that children should be taught with real books that they are interested in reading.

Recommended for those interested in elementary education and how to teach reading.
