Paco is the top researcher in consumer behaviour. he watches and learns and then writes some of the best work on this subject out there. He other books are most worthy as well. The collapse of the mall is a striking event in the American retailing history. It has been savaged by new trends and behaviour much of which is digitally inspired. I read this to understand the base of the mall generation and grew up in the "Mall rats" period of history. In the 1960's & 1970's there was little else! Highly worthwhile book by a great behavioural author!
