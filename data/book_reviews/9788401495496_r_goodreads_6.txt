The Bad Place is an entertaining chiller, though not among Koontz's best. It is one of his darker books, with some content many readers will find disturbing, and it's a very well-crafted plot, with many intriguing twists and turns. There's no memorable canine character, but the humans are all somewhat annoyingly either all good or bad with no shades of grey. There were a couple of twists that didn't get resolved to my complete satisfaction, but I wasn't engaged enough to fret about it much. Bad Place, good read, move along...
