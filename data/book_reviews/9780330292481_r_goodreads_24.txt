I think reading this one has cured me of my fascination with Lois Duncan books. Over the years, I've discovered when craving something like this, I should probably just watch a movie.Kids keep disappearing in New Mexico, and the only person who can figure out where they are is budding psychic teenager Karen. Her boyfriend is kind of abusive, as is her mother, an overbearing woman determined to keep everyone from knowing her daughter is a freak that has psychic visions. Karen has also developed a somewhat inappropriate working relationship with a neighborhood police officer who keeps bringing up a woman he knows who also uses her psychic abilities to find missing kids -- we later learn that this is his former 10th grade teacher who he's kept in contact with.At one point, Karen, and a whole daycare full of babies gets kidnapped by a Texan couple... I can't even finish. I guess what I found most infuriating about the book, was that something would be happening in one chapter, and then all of a sudden, new chapter, Karen's somewhere completely different in the future with her mother coming up with a fabricated summary of events so that no one suspects she's got a special gift.Stinker. Not even worthy of a Lifetime movie.
