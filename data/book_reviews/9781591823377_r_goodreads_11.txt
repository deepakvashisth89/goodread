Kyaaaaaaaah!!! I love Gravi!! It's one of the BEST manga series out there, if you ask me. Book 5 is the volume in which we introduce K-san and Suguru. It is kind of shocking looking back that it took 5 volumes to finally introduce K. Anyway, volume 5 is one of my favorites. It features some cute Yuki/Shui moments, lots of ridiculousness, and plenty of seriousness as well. This is still at the stage of the manga where things are making some sort of logical sense (in other words, no mecha pandas destroying NYC or anything). Shui and Yuki are the most adorable couple, and Yuki actually starts opening up a little. This is also the volume where the Nittle Grasper/Bad Luck rivalry picks up. I highly recomend this series, but especially this volume. The introduction of a trigger happy American manager was just what this series needed, and we finally get that in K-san.
