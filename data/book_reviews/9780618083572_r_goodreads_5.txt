Definitely my favorite of the 6 volumes of The History of Middle-Earth I've read so far. One plus is that is essentially a lot of writing that Tolkien did when crafting the beginning of The Lord of the Rings, and another plus is that there were very long passages in here, and Christopher Tolkien didn't have to piece together a lot of fragmented material like in previous installments. Seeing Tolkien's process of discovery writing is worth the price of admission!
