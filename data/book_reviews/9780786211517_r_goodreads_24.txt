My second read of this novel, this time in English, twenty-two years later.I had some trepidations getting into it. It's amazing that some images, little details stayed with me all this time. Of course, there were things I'd forgotten, such as the fact that Caravaggio was Hana's family friend, that Almasy, aka the English Patient, was fifteen years older than his love interest, Katherine. I confess, the movie adaptation, which I love, did muddle my reading experience a bit, albeit not as much as I expected it.If I were to put my super critical hat on, I'd say that all the details about the different kinds of bombs and how to dismantle them could have been cut, and the ending it was, arguably, unnecessary.Nevertheless, I loved re-reading this wonderful novel. Now I feel like watching the movie adaptation again.
