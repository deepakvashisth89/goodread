The book, Beacon Hill Boys, written by Ken Mochizuki is a life story about a teenager name Dan Inagaki. He goes to Cleveland High School. He and his friends, Eddie and Jerry, will be going through a lot of high school dramas. From having huge crushes to finding jobs to graduating. Dan Inagaki is a shy student who gets bad grades in school. Eddie is a nice, stylish person who dresses up nicely every day. And last, but not least Jerry. He is outgoing and a troublemaker. They are the best of friends in the book. They don’t get into any nasty trouble, but that does not mean that trouble is not going to come to them. However, besides from these main points, from what I read it is also about Japanese culture. Dan Inagaki has to live up to his family’s expectation, but is unable to comprehend it for it is just not him. Even though it is not him, he still has to do it no matter what anyone says. After finishing this book, I realized that I did not like this it at all because I thought there was not enough drama or action to keep me interested in it. I mean, some parts were interesting such as the fighting scene between Jerry and Dan’s brother, Brad. However, this was the only scene that caught my attention. The other scenes were kind of boring. The book kept on talking about Dan’s family and how he needs to live up to their expectations. It’s informative, but it’s also too much. I think this book should include more action such as more fighting scenes. For example, maybe they could add a fighting scene against Dan and Brad. Brothers fighting for a reason would have really caught my attention. They could have also added a love interest. This could have made it less boring because the people who are reading it could relate to it and want to continue to read it to see what happens to the characters. I like a book that could keep me interested, but this book only caught my attention for only a brief moment. Even if I did not like the book, does not mean other people thought the same thing. They could have thought it was informative because it did talk a lot of about Japanese culture. It talks about how you have to cut your hair short to show respect to people and your family. This book can actually relate to most people in real life because the people who usually read this kind of book are around the same age as the characters in the book. So all in all, this book would be best recommended to people who would want to learn about how Japanese culture works. However, I believe everyone should at least read this book once to understand how it feels to come from a hard life of a teenage guy going through so much trouble to please his family and make it through high school.
