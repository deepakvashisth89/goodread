I absolutely loved this. (I’m slightly shocked still.) It’s got all the classic Mary Stewart gothic suspense elements, except the couple is already married, and I loved them; there’s the added bonus of a coming-of-age angle, and I loved Timothy; the writing is clever, deliberately vague in places, humorous, and beautiful. It’s dated, sure, but in a way that feels like historical fiction. All of it just worked for me.

I’m off to buy it right now, and that almost never happens.
