In this story, Penny (Penelope) is an escapee from what many would feel was an ideal existence. She was raised as a wealthy heiress, set to be the sole heir to her grandfather's millions.When our story opens, she is living in a backwater area harvesting cranberries for a living. She is the inadvertent hostess to a precocious child, who asks her to be a pretend mommy.Meeting the father is an experience in conflict, as he is aggravating and irritating, but obviously loves his daughter.This is a romance which deals with real human issues, in a realistic way. Worth reading.
