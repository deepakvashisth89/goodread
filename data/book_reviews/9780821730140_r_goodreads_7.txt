Great book! So many diabolical villains to entertain me! You have the white girl who was raised Indian and the half-breed male who has turned himself into a white man. There's conflict between their Indian and white beliefs. They're married in an Indian ceremony, but when they go to the white world, are they really married? The heroine has the morals of an Indian and while they aren't wrong, she sees the world in the less complicated way of the Indians.
