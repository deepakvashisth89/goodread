Picked this book up recently at a flea market for 50-cents. I like Rita Rudner, I saw her stand up comedy show in Vegas in 2005 (although i missed the punch line on most of her jokes because she lowers her voice and i couldn't hear it.) I remember wondering if she had a book to read a few years back, but i didn't pursue a purchase. I think she's funny and I know how she speaks, so I thought I could read the book "in her voice", but it's harder to do.

The book was not as funny as she is onstage. However, it got funnier as the book went on. I enjoyed reading her stories and the book had black & white cartoons and photos of her growing up which was a different dimension. On the right side page there was a "flip book" illustration of her diving into water, which was kinda cute. I don't think I encountered that in a book before.

It was a narrow book, about 150 pages. I was able to read it to break up the other sad books I've recently read and to balance things out emotionally. I wouldn't mind reading more funny books, but humor is one of those things that one person laughs at and someone else groans. I find a lot of humor silly or stupid and not worth my time, so it's tricky. In this case, it was worth 50-cents.
