Rereading this childhood favourite was like chicken soup for my soul. I loved it the first time I read it at 10 years old, and I loved it again now at 32. It’s a great beginner mystery for kids, with all the names being fun and weird (I remember reading an interview with the author where he said he would mix Scabble tiles around to make the names), and the adventure being just sinister enough to not be too scary. Like so many other children’s books where rodents are the main characters (Redwall, The Secret of Nihm, etc) there is actually quite a bit of violence in this story. I wasn’t scared by it at 10, but if you have a more sensitive child, know that there are several murders, several attempted murders, and a mention of suicide.(One of the mice straight up has a gun???)It’s hard to get a copy these days but I bet many libraries will have it.I highly recommend it for the more spooky-leaning child who enjoys darker reads like Coraline, Redwall and the like.
