Although I can't recall a single plot detail, I remember being really struck by this one. I read this (along with Eklund's otherTreknovel) at a time in my young life when I was emersed in the Original Series adventures of Kirk, Spock, McCoy, and the rest. What I truly loved about this and the other EklundTreknovel is how they offer a somewhat alternate version of theEnterpriseand Her crew. The tone of these novels doesn't exactly match that of the show, and the characters don't quite speak or behave the way they do on the show, and I remember finding that to be exhilarating!
