This truly feels like a book of hours, with each short poem seeming to represent an act of devotion, whether a lyrical psalm or a vernacular prayer—to God or often simply to life, rendering the words both beautiful and powerful regardless of the reader's faith. There are a few poems (p. 13, p. 29, pp. 41–43) that I particularly loved, and that I think I will turn back to time and again. From what other snippets I've seen online, this truly is a superior translation, simple and lyrical, and I appreciated the preface from Ursula Le Guin.
