The Achilles!Oh, Achilles! Achilles! You steal every scene that you are in. You have made this my favorite poem of all time (and I love others).Your reputation for being stubborn, arrogant and hateful are conveniently brushed aside, precisely because the people that you kill are as misogynistic, brutal and violent as you.But you have your saving graces. You are humorous, but never humble. You are intelligent, but also ignorant and you are majestic as well as manly ( I don’t think of that as a positive).So the poem of a war between Greek and Trojan heroes is finished. Good night, to you.Five fabulous stars. ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️
