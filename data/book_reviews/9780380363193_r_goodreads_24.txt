"Baal" is a great addition to the satanic panic horrors of 1960s and 1970s. Very similar in theme to Rosemary's Baby or The Omen. The books takes us on a globetrotting adventure and is quite fast paced and action filled. A classic tale of good vs evil. Like many of McCammon's books of this period it's quite violent and nasty at times and could be seen as proto-splatterpunk novel. Despite it being his first novel it is actually very well written and is definitely one of the best novels I have read lately. Very enjoyable and well recommended for fans of classic horror novels.
