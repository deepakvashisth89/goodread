An author that I had never come across before, but saw the book in a display at my local library. The initial outline of an ageing PI going off with a 14 year old girl seems a suspicious scenario. The structure is interesting, with both Leo Bloodworth and Serendipity Dahlquist giving their accounts side by side. The focus at the beginning is on Groucho the dog! From there on, the plot becomes increasingly complex as the pair become involved with more and more parties that seem to be intent on the same end, but why is the question. Becomes more enjoyable as it progresses. About 3.5 stars in the end.
