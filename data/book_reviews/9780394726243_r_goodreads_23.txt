The Dain Curse is the second in the Continental Op series of books after Red Harvest and was originally serialized in four installments in the pulp magazine Black Mask. The novel comprises three parts, each concerning different mysteries—Part One, "The Dains"; Part Two, "The Temple"; and Part Three, "Quesada". The plot involves a supposed curse on the Dains that inflicts bloody deaths on those around them. The book is written in the first person singular and is fast-paced, enjoyable, and easy to read.The protagonist is The Continental Op , a private detective who starts out investigating the theft of diamonds from the Leggett family of San Francisco. Edgar Leggett's wife is a Dain, as is his daughter Miss Gabrielle Dain Leggett, a devotee of morphine and religious cults.Gabrielle escapes from the cult and marries her fiancé Eric Collinson, but the 'curse' continues. The Continental Op investigates the reason behind all the events surrounding Gabrielle Leggett, which he eventually uncovers.
