My plan to read this entire series was absolutely brought on by my newfound love for Wicked The Musical. Because I am a nerd and when I get into something I have to do it hardcore.

The Wonderful Wizard of Oz: 3 stars
So I was obviously already familiar with the premise of this story. Although I don't think I even watched the movie, the references are so ubunadant across pop culture that it was entirely inevitable. But i was very interesting to see how this story actually differed from the one most people know, because they actually changed quite a bit for the movie.

I enjoyed the band of characters journeying across Oz and how each of them continuesly proved – witour ever comprehending it – that they already possed all of the characteristics they so deeply wanted the wizard to grant them.

The pacing is so weird. Like everything moves so fast and so slow at the same time, I don't even know how to explain it. I think it is the endless string of destanations and obstacles that makes it feel somewhat long-winded, but everything is also always resolved immediately making us move from one thing to the next so fast.

But it was fun and I'm excited to learn more about Oz.


In a lot of ways this was quite reminiscent of the first book. A band of characters trekking back and forth across Oz to solve some sort of issue. But I liked our new characters way less. Tip is kind of annoying and everyone is kind of bland. Even the scarecrow, who makes a return, is much less likable this time around.

The story also feels kind of progressive and then also very dated at the same time. An army of girls have come to take over the emerald city because the men have been in charge longe enough! (yay! down with the patriarchy) But actually what they really want it just all the emeralds so they can buy pretty dresses (sigh.).

And the ending too just felt a little weird, like let him choose for himself?


This is my favourite book of the volume.

Although for having the name of the book, Ozma really had a quite insignificant part. Like I know she brings everyone together for the "quest" but other that that she doesn't get to do much. Although I disliked her persoality as Tip, I'm kind of sad it now seems like she doesn't have one anymore

But I like the cast otherwise: Having Dorothy back, Billina being a fun new addition, most of the returning characters being less annoying that last I saw them. TikTok is maybe the one exception he also just kind of feels a bit pointless.

But this book had me the most intrigued out of the three, the journeying back and forth is less tedious and the individual obstacles feel more developed.
