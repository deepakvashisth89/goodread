I'm less than satisfied with the credibility of Mr. Webster's information, as well as the credibility of his personal experiences. His information on spirit guides contradicts other sources; for ex. he says that spirit guides are loved ones who have passed on. Other sources state that spirit guides are not loved ones who have passed on. He suggests techniques for reaching spirit guides such as ouija board use, which is not recommended by other sources. He suggests that anyone can become a medium, while some would argue that mediumship is bestowed. Beyond the content, his writing style is not compelling.
