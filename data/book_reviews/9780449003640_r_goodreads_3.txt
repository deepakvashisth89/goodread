It was a delight to find a book by Dorothy Gilman that I hadn't read. I picked it up early this morning and was so delighted with the world I found that I didn't put it down until it was finished. Gilman creates characters I care about. She builds suspense and drama. She includes touches of deep wisdom. And she does all of this while reminding me that even in our busy modern world, kindness trumps all and has it's own magic.Unfortunately Gilman died in 2012, of Alzhiemers. I will miss her.
