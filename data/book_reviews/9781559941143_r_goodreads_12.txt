This is a short story that shows that even the 19th-century New Englanders knew how to go viral.

Reverend Hooper, a man who presumably had a perfectly pleasant face, decides one Sunday to don a black veil, transforming himself into the talk of the town and a master of psychological warfare. You'd think it was a new fashion statement, but no, it's something far more sinister... it's a symbol. The symbolism is still in an endless debate.

Hooper, a man who seemingly valued a good mystery over good social graces, keeps his motivations shrouded, much like his face. The story is a masterclass in awkward social interactions, proving that a single accessory can derail an entire community's peace of mind.

It's a quick read, but the questions it raises will haunt you:)
