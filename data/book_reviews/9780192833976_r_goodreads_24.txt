On the one hand Machiavelli's 'instructions' cynically promoting practicality over morality in the field of politics seem archaic for the 21st century. On the other hand, many modern politicians continue to apply some of them. Some are the proponents of political pragmatism and realpolitik. Others wage actual wars and try to invade a neighbouring country. As a rule, public discourse emphasizes ethics and transparency.
