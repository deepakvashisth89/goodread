Hello my beautiful goodreads family! It´s Foxyreading (@Foxyfangirling on twitter) with my full review of Man and Wife!

4 stars
This was a pleasant surprise! It was not what I expected AT ALL! The plot was amazing, the story was really well written and I really liked the psychology aspects! It was really interesting to read about the psychologist feelings (and his life and family) and his patients feelings and the things he went through. There were a lot of plot twists in this story, but I did see the biggest plot twist coming but it still was a really good story. Even though I think the writer emphisized a bit too much on the psychologists thoughts.

I definitely recommend this if you're into psychology or psychologic books!
