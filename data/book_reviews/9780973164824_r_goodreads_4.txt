Remember the third party in the creation myth of western civilization, the serpent that tempted Eve to eat the forbidden fruit.
This Genesis story reflects the universal myths of the cosmic, primordial serpent, often linked to worship of the Great Goddess among diverse cultures around the world, from prehistoric times to the current era.
In Goddess in the Grass, Linda Foubister skillfully blends these stories with insights into the more positive portrayals of the skin-shedding serpent as a symbol of healing, fertility and immortality.
To many, a slithering snake is a fearful creature, harkening
back to our original sin and fall from grace. But to some men, according to this book, this anxiety may represent a fear of women's sexuality.
Perhaps that explains why many cults of the serpent and the goddess have been suppressed throughout the ages.
