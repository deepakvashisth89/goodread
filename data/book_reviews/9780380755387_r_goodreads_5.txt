There is skulduggery afoot in Lobelia Falls once again! In a bid to win a collection of theatrical memorabilia to display in the back bedroom of the Architrave Museum, Dittany Henbit Monk and the rest of The Grub-And-Stakers Gardening and Roving Club and putting on a play written Dittany’s beloved husband, Osbert. But a stink bomb, a cobra and a rat trap, not to mention the bullet that should have been a blank, are wreaking havoc with the play and causing an uproar in town. Looks like Dittany and Osbert, along with the rest of the eccentric residents of Lobelia Falls, will have to get to the bottom of things again.
