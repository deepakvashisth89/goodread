I hate this novel. I do not recommend it at all.Crashis different facets of the same erotic fantasy described in vivid detail for over 220 pages. It is intriguing in the beginning ("This obsession with the sexual possibilities of everything around me had been jerked loose from my mind by the crash"). Then it reaches a point where you cannot take it seriously anymore ("As Catherine and I drove from the coroner's inquest the flyovers overlaid one another like copulating giants, immense legs straddling each other's backs"). It starts to get a little gross ("A dank odour rose from his body, an amalgam of rectal mucus and engine coolant"). You're tearing your hair out towards the end wishing this would end.But it is so well written, almost as if Ballard had experienced or fantasized about each one of the sexual desires of the characters. And maybe he did : (http://www.dailymail.co.uk/news/artic...).Ballard sure knew a lot about cars, its ancillary parts and the human body. But without the introduction by him at the beginning of the book, I wouldn't have a clue about what he was trying to say with all this. Harry Crews'Carwas published a year beforeCrash.
