Detective Haydon decides to take over the murder investigation of a photographer named Powell. Haydon soon learns that Powell works for the Langer Media hired by Langer to work on something personally for him. He also soon learns that a friend of Powell's named Toy Rick, who is a cameraman and will risk everything to obtain the most violent footage possible. He also learns that The Langer Media Corporation is owned by a rich man named Josef Roeg, who pressures everyone that works for him and will do anything to keep with mouths shut once they become no use for him... What could have possibly got Powell killed? The tapes he obtained?
