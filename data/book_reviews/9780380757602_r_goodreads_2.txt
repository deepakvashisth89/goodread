Holy crap! Can't believe I got this sequel! Used on Amazon but in very good condition. :)

The book is great. Lots of journeying around and Quadro befriending or becuting his foes into submission. I don't know if she needed to fill it out, but there's a whole ridiculous fungi and mushroom element. She thanks some sort of Encyclopedia of Mushrooms in the end, so... :) I like how everything talks.

The plot is a good continuation of the first story. I don't know why she didn't write more books, I love her style! :)
