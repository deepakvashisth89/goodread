Quite an anthology of stories collected from a large variety of places and people. Certainly the research that went into this project is admirable. I enjoyed reading the stories, though I found them a little disjointed and lacking continuity. This collection would be a treasure for people who have roots in this region. Being from the Midwest I have only visited Shenandoah National Park one time for only a couple days. This book would be good to read prior to a visit; now I wish I could return.
