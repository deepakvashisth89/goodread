I had so much fun reading this book. I had it on my shelf for some time, but thought it was an autobiography, and I've grown to shy away from those of celebrities because they burst my idol-bubble. No, this is stand-up comedy in a book. My sides would be hurting from laughing if I were in the room with Rita. From clothing to cooking to cars to relationships to parents to moving to bikinis and more. I'd love to quote her, but I'd have to quote the entire book. As a bonus, there are early pictures of Rita growing up. As another bonus, there is a flip cartoon on the side of the page (don't buy any other edition--I think this is #10 (originally published in 1992).
Do yourself a favor--read this book. It will make everything better for you.
