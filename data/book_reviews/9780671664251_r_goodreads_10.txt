This book took a long time to get going. Like, a really long time. And it's not a short book. Fortunately for me, I love Clegg's lazily elegant style. He creates a town like Steven King.And once the horror starts kicking in, it just keeps ramping up, like a combo of Dean Koontz's Phantoms with a, large side of the film In The Mouth of Madness. Not for the squeamish, that's for sure.
