I didn’t think I would ever finish this book.The densest book I have ever read front to back. Was written pretty well and goes so in depth into many different topics. The depth makes it very had to read for long though as you need a break after 10 pages if you are really trying to absorb the material. No dilly dallying either which is good lot of the books I’ve read recently could have been half as long not this one.Would read again but need atleast 5 years before that would happen, I am tired.
