I really like Brust's books, but this one was a bit of a disappointment. It is a conventional type of story that is told in an unconventional way. It does not take long to realize that the main character of the novelis a vampire, and he has been summoned to a Midwestern city by the woman that created him, Kellem. Kellem has decided that Agyar must take the blame for a recent murder that she committed.The main conflict is revealed very early in the book, but it takes forever for this plot to move forward. Much of the plot is taken up by the day-to-day musings of Agyar as he juggles his relationships with two young women. While this was intriguing at first, it dragged on too long before there was any significant progress with the main conflict. Not nearly as engaging as his Vlad Taltos books.
