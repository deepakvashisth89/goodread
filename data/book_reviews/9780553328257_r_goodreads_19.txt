The just recently concluded third season of the hit BBC seriesSherlockwas pretty much the impetus for my reread of the entire Sherlock Holmes canon this month. The show's writers were pretty much Conan Doyle inspired and the cases Benedict Cumberbatch's detective solved and episode titles were lifted from the original stories. I figured a reread of ACD's entire Holmes work was in order as I could barely remember the particulars of the original stories the show drew its inspiration.I've read another edition of Conan Doyle's entire Holmes material but this was the first time I read it in an eBook format. It was an entirely different reading experience, almost like encountering the stories for the first time again. It was a perfect match for me current reading habits as I was heavily sourcing my reading to an iPad.Yet, I couldn't bring myself to give this edition of the book five stars. It touted itself as "The Complete Sherlock Holmes" but the maps and other graphics are missing. The stories were still readable and enjoyable without them but it could hardly call itself a complete edition without those.The entire Sherlock Holmes canon would definitely merit five stars. Just not this edition.
