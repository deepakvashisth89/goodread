4.25 Stars

I enjoyed the story, though I knew one of the antagonists (individuals fighting against our crew) very early. Called it. Not sure IF ofthers picked up on it, but I felt sketchy about this character from the jump.

Watching the storyline of Spock plummet and be gaslit. Did NOT like that aspect of the story at all. When a person knows that they can do better, but is being put in a position that what they are thinking isn't true, AND it is...really pisses me off (sorry, not sorry).

Thank goodness for A1 friends like Captain Kirk and Dr. McCoy (Bones).

Loved the character of T'Pala and her struggles. Great backstory and similarities with Spock...which made you draw closer to her, but also be wary of her.

Loved the background characters and THEIR moments. I see you Lt. Uhura, Scotty, Sulu and Checkov, and even Nurse Chapel. Yes.

Loved the involvement of Sarek and Amanda (Spock Da and Ma). It gave me the feels from Star Trel IV: The Voyage Home with both of these characters...which I just recently watched. Great movie.

A little bit of the Babel episode in TOS...so the complications of bringing a planet into the Federation.

Good story, indeed.
