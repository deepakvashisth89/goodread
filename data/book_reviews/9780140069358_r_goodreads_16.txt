This was a very fast and easy read. I would give it 3.5/4 stars. I enjoyed the atmosphere of Florence in Summer and the style of writing, which was quick and interesting. Additionally the dialouges and charcters were very well written. The pace of action was very fitting. I loved the little twists and turns, eventhough some investigations and results remained rather vague.Also, Gino's death really hit me...All in all - a nice and easy summer read.
