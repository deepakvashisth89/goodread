Just the the rest in this series...GREAT READThe books in this series are always fun and entertaining to read. Our Hero travels away from Nashville Tennessee and gets into an adventure along the way. He is on his way to witness the birth of his Daughter when he gets robbed, meets a girl and in general has lots of problems. Once he arrives, his Girl friend's, Aunt's Friends, have a job for him. He ends up being an unwilling inside man / Spy at a brothel in an attempt to discover how is laundering Money and how. While there , there is a Murder and he is the #1 suspect. No fun at all for him. Naturally our Hero gets out of trouble , proves how the Murderer is and who is the Money Launderer ... all the while awaiting the birth of his Daughter !
