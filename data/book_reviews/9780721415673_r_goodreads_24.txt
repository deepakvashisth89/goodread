This 1983 version of Rupelstiltskin has some interesting back and white drawings. The drawings were done with pencil on Herculean Mylar.
The story line is true to the original story. Instead of the little man disappearing into the ground after he stomps his feet, he stomp one feet into the ground and he then tears himself into two by pulling his other leg. I was a bit gruesome. This was not one of my favorite versions.
