I have really enjoyed reading Linda Grant's novels this year and this tale of two mature adults who meet in the decaying port of Liverpool is no exception. Grant presents the reader with a fascinating social history of Liverpool, especially the story of migrant populations. As usual she is great on clothes and food and Alix the female protagonist even met George Harrison of Beatle fame.

Alix is a feminist irreligious Jewess who finds herself both chidless and partnerless in the middle of her life. She travels the world researching and reinstating old Jewish places of worship!. Resigned to live the single life though consumed by bright flares of lust she meets an American architect when she returns to Liverpool for her mother's funeral. This chance meeting is the catalyst for a series of changes that lead to revealations from the past and aspirations for the future.

It is also blackly funny. Highly recommended
