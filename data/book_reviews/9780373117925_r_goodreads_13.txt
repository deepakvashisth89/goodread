Another nice middle of the road LG. I thought she did a good job making the hero seem to really be an aristo from South America. The heroine was a bit of a wimp but not too too bad. The hero did do the abrupt about face when he found out she was a virgin and no a 'ho. But that's okay because if I didn't like that kind of thing I wouldn't read HPs would I?

My biggest peeve about this book was the overwrought way that the heroine reacted to the fight at the end. I did enjoy though that the hero was determined to beat up the guy who wronged them all those years ago though.
