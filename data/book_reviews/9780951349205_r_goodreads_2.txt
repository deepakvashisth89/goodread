I read this book when I was in primary school. I recall I begged my parents to purchase it for me after a performance in front of the school where someone read from the book.

At the time of reading, I remember I found it really profound. I don’t feel that the book ended in a way that was final and recall there was some sort of cliffhanger. It was one of the books that really inspired me to read as a young child , and I am probably going to try and hunt it down so I can reread it now but I am in my 40s!
