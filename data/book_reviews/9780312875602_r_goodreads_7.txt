Anne Harris is wonderfully entertaining. I recommend this one frequently. It's about a dyke couple growing up in a working class community of laborers in future Detroit. They're living in the aftermath of a vicious class war/union organizing campaign, surviving in one of the most dangerous industries around: vat diving. They don scuba gear and dive into genetically engineered growth pools. The material is highly toxic, and they all suffer from severe health problems. This follows one of their kids, a dyke punk kid, and her new mysterious friend with four arms. Quickly they are drawn into a corporate conspiracy involving the synthetic manufacture of women.It's not the best written thing in the world; you have to have some love of genre fiction. But the writing is workable, and its rich themes of queers and workers battling corporate genetic manufacturers is absolutely delightful. I grew to really adore the protagonist.
