This was my first ever classic and I really enjoyed it. I sometimes had a hard time understanding certain comments and parts, because it was written so long ago. So I listened to the audiobook mostly and that really helped. I liked the aspect from both books, the way that a tamed dog is turned into a wild animal and the story of a wild wolf becoming a tames wolf. I sometimes had a hard time with the cruelty portrayed against these dogs since I’m a big animal lover, but I kept telling myself that this was 1903 and times were different. Overal really good book and definitely worth reading.
