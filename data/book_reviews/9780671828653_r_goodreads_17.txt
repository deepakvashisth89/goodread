OK, this, the seventh book in the Grijpstra & de Gier series, is really a Commissaris & de Gier novel as this installment finds the commissaris having to travel to the United States after finding out that his brother-in-law has died.

Although the commissaris insists he does not need any assistance, de Gier is "temporarily transferred" (without the commissaris' knowledge) to work under the sheriff of the small town where the commissaris is going to. This will allow de Gier to be there to protect the commissaris.

But, upon arriving in the US, the duo are quickly swept into a mystery as they find out there have been multiple deaths and that there is a whole slew of suspects and shady characters. Seeing this as an opportunity, the two of course, offer to help solve the mystery.

I actually found this to be my favourite book so far in the series, even though it does not center around the Grijpstra & de Gier partnership.
