This was, according to the back of the book, Patricia Lake's first novel. And you can tell, if only for the inordinate amount of coffee the heroine drinks. It's like, anytime the author couldn't decide where the story was going, Amy was drinking 3-4 cups of strong, black coffee. Except the one time where she had "cream, no sugar".Angst was high here, so I liked that, but the end seemed forced. He doesn't declare his feelings until she says she's running off again. If she didn't decide to run away to West Germany (:D), would he have kept throwing the secretary he was using to make her jealous in her face? Probably. Chicken shit.Seriously, if you're reading my review and you're gonna read this for the first time, please count how many times she drinks coffee and lemme know.
