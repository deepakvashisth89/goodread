The subtitle of 'Faithful Listening' is 'Discernment in Everyday Life.'

We need to discern so many things in our life to make small and big decisions. We discern to find out God's Will for us, and to do His Will. Discernment is not an easy job at all, because we can be easily misled if we are not awake mentally and spiritually. What looks good is not always good; the beginning, the middle, and the end of the enterprises should be good.

Joan Mueller in the book shows us very clearly in details with a lot of episodes what discernment is and how and why we have to discern. I learned a lot more about discernment from this book. Joan Mueller invites us to discern many cases introduced in the book and think of advises we can give to the people in dilemma. I believe we can train ourselves to learn how to communally discern in a group using this book.

The book also shows what true consolation and desolation are. The author recommends a lot of related books for further study.

Muller says in the epilogue, "'Falling in Love with the Holy Spirit,' discernment is a loving vigilance toward God, others and the world that seeks to do the truth in love. And if one desires discernment, one must practice a discerning lifestyle and beg God fervently and humbly for this gift."

I highly recommend to read this book. I will read this book again and again.
