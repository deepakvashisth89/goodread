I’ve got to give this one 5 stars because there were times when I was literally laughing out loud because it was so funny! There were two scenes in particular that were my favorite. One was the scene where Alice was looking through National Geographic magazines for pictures of naked men. Two, the scene in which Patrick and Alice were passing notes about their boring history teacher back and forth, complete with drawings of their teacher spraying saliva all over his students. I think these scenes were so funny to me because it reminded me a lot of what me and my friends did when I was that age.Can’t wait to read the next one in the series!
