I still find Miss Seeton to be a gentile proper polite spinster who is almost oblivious to the evil around her while managing to shine floodlights onto things which evildoers would prefer to remain hidden in the dark. Scotland Yard believes Miss Seeton to be a catalyst - I cannot disagree. I take inordinate delight in reading/observing her trip over or fall into the most inconvenient situations for criminals. I almost feel sorry for the criminals!
