DNF - extremely childish, irresponsible and ignorant 20 year old heroine. Implausible setting that feels incomplete. For example, the setting is a vast ranch in Wyoming with a very large house, aptly named Big House. The heroine seems to be one of only two women on the ranch. The other women is a part time cook, who only cooks occasionally. There are no servants or housekeepers to maintain this Big House and all its elegant accoutrements. No one to wash the heroine's gorgeous and straight-from-Paris clothes or to help her with her extensive toilette and elaborate coiffures. She didn't behave like a ranching woman or seem to have the competence for the authority and position she craved.The book also included a lot of flashbacks to give the hero's back story. It was kind of hard to keep up with that.
