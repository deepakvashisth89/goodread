My rather meek remonstrance to the effect that wolves had been preying on caribou, without decimating the herds, for some tens of thousands of years before the white men came to Brochet, either fell on deaf ears or roused my listeners to fury at my partisanship.

Aspects of this book’s legacy are dipped in infamy. There are hints and whispers of falsehood and plagiarism. Apparently by any metric the author’s characterization of the Canadian government is unfair and exaggerated. It seems a Dickensian whimsy but I digress. This same federal agency sent the author to northern Manitoba to investigate the apparent appalling numbers of caribou which were being destroyed by the evil wolf population. The field biology begins with sufficient missteps to recall Lucky Jim but the subsequent treatment of the wolves is beautiful. I feel like I need to read a few more books on the subject, luckily we have two more here at home.
