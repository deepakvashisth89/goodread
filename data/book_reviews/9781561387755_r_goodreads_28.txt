I had to give this 5 stars for the artwork and funny commentary alone! I’ve made several of the recipes and they’ve mostly been really good. The mushroom barley soup and apple krisp were personal favorites.

There are A LOT of eggs used in this cookbook and I both hate eggs and don’t want to support the industry. That was a big con for me. The other con was that the recipes make enormous portions, even when halved.
