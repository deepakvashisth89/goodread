After the explosion in the last Volume, our duo is stationed in the Bronx. They are called to a murder that reminds Ryo of how his parents died. One favourite FBI agent Diane is called in and we learn more about Leo. As Ryo and Dee investigate this case they are eventually removed from it. Due to Ryo's parent's death being connected he is too closely involved. That is not going to stop him so Dee is watching him closely. We learn more about Leo and how he was involved in Ryo's parent's death. When we learned the truth it was a sad story indeed. I felt so sorry that Ryo had to hear that and I can understand why he acted as he did. I'm just glad that Dee is there for him and you can see how much he loves him. We have a brief case involving our ever sweet JJ and I felt for him, he's such a great guy. His love for Dee is ever present but we do see a different side of him here. As usual we also have a short Bikky and Cal bit too.
