other words, you'd be surprised how much this story from 1000 years ago has in common withclockwork princess.DAY 3: THE MILLER'S TALEoh thank god it's a short one.reading yesterday's in several-page there's any competing with yesterday's, but this one is at least even shorter.DAY 5: THE COOK'S TALEmercifully this one is like 2 pages long. thank god, because any discussion of what passed for food 8: THE FRIAR'S TALEthis seems like it's going to be very exciting (about thievery) but it is not (mostly just two guys talking to each other about how bad they are and then becoming good guys).DAY 9: THE SOMPNOUR'S TALEstep one: google what the hell a sompnour even is.at one point in this a man proves he isn't drunk by killing a child with a bow and arrow. at another point a guy tells a friar he
