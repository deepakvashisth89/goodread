The Black novels are all over complicated and for this one it’s as if Emerson just started writing without an outline or a plan until he decided to stop. Much of it is chaotic and actively irritating- the character of Snake doesn’t wear well nor does fhe whole space alien thing which is the starting point. The characters, intended to be colorful or wacky, just grate. The sexual banter between Kathy and Black is tiresome. I don’t like to give one star ratings but this came close.Also, ‘a million to one odds’ are bad, not good as Kathy seems to think.Emerson must be a Jacques Anquetil fan because he gives an incidental character named Raymond Poulidor a sad death in this one.
