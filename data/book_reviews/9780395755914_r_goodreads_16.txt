"Shapechangers in winter" felt so familliar to me... like a dream you can't remember but know the feeling of, like a longing for someplace you've never been to and yet, it feels like home. There is only this kind of pain in your chest and throat, a moment of not remembering how to breathe...That's how this poem felt to me.
