A supercomputer, DEUS, gains the power to improve itself and create life. After interactions with a genetic laboratory computer, DEUS creates a plethora of mutant new life forms that swarm the earth. It is Riley’s task to make sure the computer’s powers cannot be used by the wrong people. Together with his girlfriend, he needs to find a way to stop the spread of these mutants and keep the computer from falling into the wrong hands.Asking the computer to design a virus that will kill them all seems the solution.
