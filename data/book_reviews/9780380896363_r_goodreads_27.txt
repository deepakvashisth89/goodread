Okay, Merlin has completely won me over. I'm not saying I like him better than Corwin--I could never--but I love him equally. Except I refuse to call him Merle, even in my mind. Merlin is a much better name (no offense to anyone who goes by Merle).I'm not sure what happened at the end of this book. It's so strange and bizarre, and it literally involves the Mad Hatter, but it somehow makes sense for this narrator who is a child of Amber and a prince of Chaos at the same time. I am still wondering who Mask is, and I'm really, really hoping that it's not Corwin. The only thing that could be strong enough to completely justify all the suspenseandthe only thing that would make me want to throwThe Great Book of Amberat Zelazny's head is if Corwin is Merlin's antagonist.But we shall see.
