The King of Torts is a legal thriller by John Grisham. The story follows Clay Carter, who in the beginning is a struggling public defender in Washington, DC. Who stumbles upon what appears to be a routine (for the slums of DC) murder case involving a homeless man. That case leads Clay to a massive pharmaceutical scandal involving a dangerous drug and thousands of victims. Guided by a mysterious and shadowy mentor, he leaves public defense and becomes a plaintiffs attorney quickly rising to extraordinary wealth and power. Along the way theres a kind of weak love story and semi intriguing moral paradox.3 to 3 1/2 stars
