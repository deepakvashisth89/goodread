This book is a compilation of many of the Holocaust survivor's stories. It goes from the time before the Holocaust and goes until after it all ends. It heart breaking but very informative and well written. It's very personal as it uses many personal experiences. This book describes thing in detail and due to that I would wait until you are at least 15 years or older to read.I wouldn't offer this book in my classroom unless I was teaching older kids due to the detail it goes into. It's a great book that is written in a way that is very informative and intriguing. For this reason I gave this book 4 stars.
