Not only does Burns recount the story of the Maccabees, she includes detailed information on modern Hanukkah traditions, including the lighting of the menorah, the eating of potato latkes, the plaing of dreidel, and the exchanging of gifts. This section also has several craft and recipe ideas, with detailed instructions. Burns also explains how the Jewish calendar differs from the Gregorian calendar. Finally, Burns discusses how different Jewish people feel about the celebration of Christmas (some celebrate it; some don't) and offers four of the most common arguments, pro and con, on the subject.
