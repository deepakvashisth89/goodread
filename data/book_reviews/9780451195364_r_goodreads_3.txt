A sweet romance, but not particularly deep in the relationship development department.Our heroine Joyce and her mother have been living with Joyce's Uncle until he is killed in a curricle race accident. His will provides that Joyce will inherit only a small some of money unless she marries the next lord of the manor, who succeeds the uncle. Turns out to be a long lost cousin, Gavin Sinclair, whom Joyce had just met.One of those books where it felt like there was more backstory than action. I liked both of the characters - Joyce was a strong woman affected by a deep secret. Gavin was a solicitous hero also battling his own personal demons.There's a bit of a murder mystery regarding the Uncle and a slightly crazy plan by Joyce to solve it.It's an enjoyable easy read, but may leave you wishing there had been more depth particularly with development of characters relationship.(Rated on my five star romance scale)
