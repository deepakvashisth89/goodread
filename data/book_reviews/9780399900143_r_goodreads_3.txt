3.5 stars. Rounding up because some are so timeless that I can use them in a presentation for work.
Black humor, New Yorker magazine style cartoons, in the vein of Charles Addams although with a unique style. Happy to add it to my collection. Excellent art work for the genre.
There were a lot with someone about to be murdered which were somewhat disturbing to me.
The less homicidal ones were great and I laughed out loud several times.

Definitely the best way to spend an evening after meeting with H.R. regarding a Toxic Manager.
