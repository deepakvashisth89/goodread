Despite the title, it isn’t just the brass section of the Wagstaffe Symphony Orchestra that’s troubled. In this fourth book in the series featuring Detective Inspector Madoc Rhys of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, Rhys’ famous father, renowned conductor Sir Emlyn Rhys, has become worried about strange goings-on in the orchestra. But cruel pranks give way to murder when a French horn player is poisoned; soon thereafter, other musicians suffer as well, although I don’t want to spoil it for readers with too many details.Charlotte MacLeod, writing as Alisa Craig, has penned a charming cozy with plenty of suspense and humor. The only downside is knowing that the late Ms. MacLeod left only one more book in this fun series.
