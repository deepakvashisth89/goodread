This book is amazing. Clara Barton is truly a hero to be respected. I admire the fact that she didn't care whether the solider was fighting for the north or south. She took care of them no matter the uniform they wore. We need more people like her in the world. I recommend it. I find it has a lot of interesting facts and I tend to be a little bit obsessive when I talk about her because she is my hero, role model and a person to be respected.
