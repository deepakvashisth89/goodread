Well, I guess it wasn't too bad that I stuck it out this far with this series. How this gets 5-star ratings is beyond me, but that's beside the point.In this volume, we find out more about Ryo's parents and their mysterious death. In all respects, this volume and the previous one have been the best/most interesting ones so far. Still, I guess my journey with FAKE ends here because I've been reading them with KU up until now, and for some ungodly reason, the last two volumes are for purchase only, and I refuse to spend 10$ CAD on each just so that I can rate it 2 or 3 stars.Sorry Ryo and Dee, this is where it ends for us. Good luck with your lives, I hope you finally get together (properly).
