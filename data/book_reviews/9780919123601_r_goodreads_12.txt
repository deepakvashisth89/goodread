Lucid, scholarly, and easy to comprehend this influential book by Hollis answers the question: What is our task in mid-life? In his words, "The act of consciousness is central; otherwise we are overrun by the complexes. The hero in each of us is required to answer the call of individuation. We must turn away from the cacaphony of the outer world to hear the inner voice. When we can dare to live its promptings, then we achieve personhood. We may become strangers to those who thought they knew us, but at least we are no longer strangers to ourselves...Recall Jung's central question. 'Are we related to something infinite or not?'"
