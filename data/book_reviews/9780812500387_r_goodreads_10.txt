Reads more like a history book than a tale. Not that it is not well done, just not quite what I expected from this novel.

Would have been more interesting to me if it had followed the story of the main character more and the progression of the war Alitalia less.

That said it is quite visionary for 1981 and gets the current perceived threat, Islam, correct, although it allies that threat with China and India.

Not a bad read for early 80's thought on possible WW III scenarios, but to dry for me.
