"Hidden" between the great rulers Peter the Great and Catherine II, these five women (four tsarinas and a regent) are not well known but they deserve to be. Starting by Peter's wife Catherine I, followed by his niece Anna Ivanovna, his grandniece Anna Leopoldovna and ending by his daughter Elizabeth I, these women ruled Russia for 36 years and it was not a piece of cake. It was a time of wars and upheaval. Some where loved, other despised and almost each one was criticized by their sexual behavior.
This book is not so big, but it goes to the point and allows us to take a good look at how it was to live and rule in 18th century Russia.
