This felt like reading a Star Trek movie or tv series - in the best possible way! It had everything. Action, diplomacy, a paranoia conspiracy theory and the classic characters we know and love. Yes, it is called Sarek and the emotional journey is all his, but actually, I was surprised at how much story there was without him. Peter Kirk being a secondary character was awesome and then OG Enterprise team had a lot more to do than I anticipated. At some points, I had concern we were straying too far from Sarek, but it always came back round and actually, once you realise the parallels between all the stories, it makes total sense. A.C. Crispin is an absolute genius. This is the second book of hers I’ve read and I would now like to read many more!
