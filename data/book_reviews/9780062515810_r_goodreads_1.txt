This book is deceptively short. Not a quick and easy read,nor pages to be blown through. While I have technically finished the book, I am far from done with it. Or rather it isn't done with me.
The author's line by line expansion of St.Francis's prayer includes his personal stories and reflections. Whether you are a devoutly spiritual person or a devout secularist, there is much to be learned from this little book.
I have followed this author for a while and enjoyed his work. I believe this is my favorite.
