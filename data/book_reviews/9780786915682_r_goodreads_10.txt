Mixed bag of short stories, some decent, so not so much. Better stories are by well-known authors like Troy Denning. This book actually has to be read between Books 2 & 3 of the Threat from the Sea trilogy as it contains a short story by trilogy author Mel Odom that is a significant part of the overall trilogy. The first time I read this trilogy, I skipped this anthology until after Book 3 and was a bit confused about how some events had happened. Overall, this anthology is okay.
