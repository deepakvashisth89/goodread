Wishbone was watching Tom play his baseball game. Tom's team lost and Tom said it was because their coach wasn't coaching them right. The coach asked if Tom wanted to coach his own team. Tom said yes and this reminded Wishbone of the Prince and the Pauper. All of his life the Pauper wanted to see prince Edward. Not only did the Pauper see Edward he met him too. The Pauper told the prince things that he had never heard of before. The servants mistook the Pauper for prince and the pauper moved into the palace. Life was different for both of them. Life was hard for the prince and now the pauper had everything handed to him. Not only was the prince gone but now his father had died too. Now the Pauper was going to become king. The prince met a man named Miles. Miles helped the prince get back to the palace in time for the coronation. The Prince and the Pauper learned that they had two very different lives. The team Tom coached ended up winning the game. It was hard for Tom to coach that team. He learned the same thing that the prince and the Pauper learned.I thought "Wishbone: the Prince and the Pauper" was an okay book. I didn't really like that he imagined himself as the Prince and Pauper. I liked that Tom coached kids younger than him and not kids his own age. The story was based on a real book so that made it different. I think it was odd though that the prince was a dog and his dad was a human. I really didn't understand why dogs could talk to people in the Prince and The Pauper part, but not in Wishbones real life. I would recommend this book to people who really like dogs.
