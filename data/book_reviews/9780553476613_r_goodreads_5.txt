This was hilarious.Harold Parkette hires a strange man from Pastoral Greenery and Outdoor Services Inc. to cut his lawn. The strange man indeed does a great job cutting his lawn, but in a horrifyingly silly way that makes everyone involved extremely uncomfortable and ends in bloody disastor. Let's just say the title Lawnmower Man is very literal. The mower ain't the only thing mowing around here.Not scary and not that good of a story, but still pretty damn entertaining and funny just for how ridiculous it gets.
