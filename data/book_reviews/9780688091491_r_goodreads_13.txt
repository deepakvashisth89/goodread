This book was nowhere near as good as I'm with the Band. Her stories were less engaging and more about her failing marriage and difficulties raising her son (with her not really accepting his obvious signs of psychosis, which was frustrating). However, I really enjoyed the portions of the book where she wrote about the public reception to I'm with the Band. She was treated so poorly by the men she wrote about in her first memoir that it was nice to see a different group of aging musicians desperately try to sleep with her in case she ever writes another memoir. All in all I would call this book fine.
