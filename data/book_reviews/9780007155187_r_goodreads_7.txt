Welcome to Percy's Park!

Percy the park keeper works hard looking after the park and his animal friends who live there. But Percy still likes to find time for some fun and games. And, of course, in Percy's Park, there's always time for a story...

It was a double bubble find of Percy the Park Keeper today. This book is one of the earlier ones and features all of his animal friends. When Percy decides to clear out the old maze, you know trouble is sure to ensue.

This newer edition includes a bonus poster of the maze, which adds to the fun. 3 3/4 stars.
