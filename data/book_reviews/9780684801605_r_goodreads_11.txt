Being a woman has its curses, and also its gifts. The title of this book evokes that Biblical curse and all the beauty and wonder that goes along with being a woman, and the book elaborates through the lifelong story of four siblings, 3 sisters and a brother, over the 70 year-ish span of their lifetimes. It tells their stories relative to their parents, spouses and offspring, as well as each other, in shorter vignettes woven together.
