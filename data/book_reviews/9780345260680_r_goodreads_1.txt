Rocket Ship Galileo is a Heinlein classic, good, straight up science fiction, and a fun story with some genuine twists.True, it is not ageless, the 1947 publication date speaks volumes about what the reader will encounter, but reading between the lines, the reader can also find the beginnings of RAH’s budding libertarianism.Not too far removed from his time in the Navy, a crisp, starched, straight postured militarism is still noticeably present. Good fun.** 2019 addendum - it is a testament to great literature that a reader recalls the work years later and this is a book about which I frequently think. I need to re-read Heinlein's juvenile works, campy and corny as they can be, they're also a lot of fun.
