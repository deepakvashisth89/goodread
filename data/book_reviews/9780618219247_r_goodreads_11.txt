I found this last week at Pages on Pine (for those of you Spartanburg readers who aren’t aware or PoP, I highly recommend it) and had no idea that Steve Earle had written books too. So of course I bought it. I’ve gotten really into short story collections over the past year, and I very much enjoyed it. I wouldn’t say that every story was an absolute home run, but some of them were very good and captivating. Stories about vagrants, vagabonds, drifters, travelers, junkies, musicians, and the like. In a way very classic Steve Earle. It’s not always easy for a legendary songwriter/music storyteller to translate one medium to the other, but Earle does a pretty solid job at it here. The best story was the penultimate one, The Witness, about a man witnessing a public execution with a wild twist at the end. If you’re a Steve Earle fan, I would recommend giving it a shot.
