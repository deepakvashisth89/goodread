There's something that's just a little off about Karen, even though she's recently acquired a boyfriend that helped her shoot to the top of popularity. But when a babysitting gig goes bad, Karen realizes that she's more than a little different. She has the ability to find missing children. But is it a gift or a curse? Karen must decide if she will use it to help, or simply hide it away.Very good! Suspenseful and a great mystery. Very well written, Lois Duncan is considered a master for a reason. You can relate to the characters too. I recommend it!
