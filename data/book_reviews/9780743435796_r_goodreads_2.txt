A mystical connection between Edgar Allen Poe, Annie, and former soldier Edgar Perry leads to a mission against a group of bad guys through various real and surreal Poe tales in this co-written novel by Saberhagen and Zelazny. I’m guessing the scenes written with food and drink were written by Zelazny. Entertaining enough read as the heroes move from place to place on land and over seas meeting characters and becoming involved in situations related to many Poe stories.
