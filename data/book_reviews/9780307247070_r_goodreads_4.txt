Three books in (well four actually, but I read them out of order) and the Fear Street Seniors series is certainly consistent. The books generally tend to have the same strengths and the same flaws.The plot moves so quickly inThe Thirstthat I can accurately describe it as a page-turner, even if it doesn't always make sense. There's a lot of nice atmosphere--shadowy figures melting out of the fog, characters alone and vulnerable in the woods. The red herrings are pretty obvious from the jump, though, and the new-student character Anita is pretty inconsistent, so far as she's developed at all. But the vampire's demise is fun, in an 80's drive-in movie sort of way.
