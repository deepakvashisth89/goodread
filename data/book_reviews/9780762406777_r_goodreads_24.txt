Poignant account of life between blacks and whites in a small Georgia town during the 1920's Jim Crow era.When published in 1944 the topic of a white man impregnating a black woman was considered controversial. Once banned in Boston and Detroit for its lewdness and sexual promiscuity.Was a favorite of World War II Troops and was requested for inclusion as an Armed Services Edition paperback for distribution to soldiers, sailors, airman, and Marines.
