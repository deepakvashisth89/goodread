I read this on the recommendation of my neighbor, who knew Diana growing up. We live in Bermuda, and I was looking forward to reading about the author's Bermuda background.It was a good read. I've read better autobiographies, but this one was still enjoyable. I liked the Bermuda mentions and references to landmarks I see. Her breezy descriptions of travel destinations made me wistful, and her long career in the arts was presented jn a straightforward non-gossipy manner. She seemed like a class act.It's a shame she passed recently, but from her story it's clear she lived and loved a full life. I'm happy that her son is rebuilding the Ariel Sands property, which is a landmark here.
