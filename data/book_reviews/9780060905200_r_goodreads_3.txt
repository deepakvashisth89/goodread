This is the story of what can happen when humans realized there is more to this world than what our five physical senses can tell us. This is a beautiful book about a very special place that teaches us to work with spirit in all that we do. When we choose to wake up and acknowledged the Devas and nature spirits, and give them the love and respect that is rightfully theirs, when we choose to work with them as equal partners, miracles can and will happen!We are all individuals but we are also part of the Whole. After reading this book I have gained a better understanding of my own purpose here on this planet. I am not a gifted sensitive who can openly communicate with the nature spirits, but you dont need to be. Acknowledging them and showing them love and respect is enough to let them know you wish to work with them. A way of communicating will always be found. Love is the key.I highly re comment this book to everyone. Gardeners and non-gardeners, all who are on a spiritual path. It gives one hope and the encouragement to work with those in the Unseen. Imagine the miracles that would happen if we all started working with the Devas and nature spirits!
