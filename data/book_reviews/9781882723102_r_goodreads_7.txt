"I know that these stories take people out of their comfort zone.." This line from the closing of the book, summed it up for me. This book was very well done and interesting, but also very sad and disturbing. I love her accepting and loving nature and example.I volunteered at the School With No Name my first year of college, 1990-1991, about an hour a week. I wish I remembered Stacey Bess and my experience there better, but I do remember being impressed with her and her story - pregnant and married at 16, but still went to college and became a teacher.
