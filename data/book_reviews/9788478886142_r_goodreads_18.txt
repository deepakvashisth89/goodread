Third Read Review

What a nostalgic read! It’s my 3rd reread and it still feels magical. It’s such a wonder how a story like this one could exist. It’s original and funny! I always come back to this series no matter how old I am.

Book 1-3 are the “cheery” plots before it takes a dark turn which I love. Honestly there’s nothing I can say about this book. My rating already sums it up and my old reviews already describe what I feel for this book.
