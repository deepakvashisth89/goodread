This is one of my favorite Leslie Kelly books. I read it when Temptation first released it, and saw it on Amazon for Kindle for some ridiculously low price so grabbed it up to read again.Once I got over the silliness of Chloe never once slipping and calling Trent, Troy, even in the heat of passion, I was able to fall in love with this sweet, passionate story. Complete with eccentrics on both sides of the families, both Trent and Chloe were down to earth, nice people, and I wanted to see them together. The imagery is wonderful. I Could completely see Trent in the rain and envision their courtship played out in a display window. The date they had in the store was so romantic.I also loved that Trent could have believed the worst of Chloe but instead chose immediately to believe her explanation of a sityation he'd misconstrued.Two nice, fun people in a passionate but incredibly sweet and romantic relationship. Love this one!
