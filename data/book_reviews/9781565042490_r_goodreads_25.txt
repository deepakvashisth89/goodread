Simply one of the greatest RPG’s ever written. Stylistically fabulous with an unbeatable storyline; the Clans and their intra-Kindred maneuverings perfection. Only the mechanics (the nuts and bolts of the game) could have used some work (most of which were vastly improved in Vampire: The Requiem; unfortunate at the cost of much of the style and feel).It only gets a four instead of a five because I needed to leave room for Mage: the Assertion to score higher as I thought it was the absolute best RPG I have ever played/run.
