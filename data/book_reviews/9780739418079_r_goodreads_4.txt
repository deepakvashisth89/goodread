Its not really my type of book, I just found it on my shelf so in my very biased opinion, I didn't like it very much. For me, it was just a little too schmutzy. But that's what I get for choosing a book with "The Affair" right in the title. The main character, I found, was really annoying and the whole book is through her perspective which gets monotonous at times. Being super busy, it was a good book to not have to really think about and I did enjoy finding out what how everything turned out in the end.
I wouldn't recommend, but it was a book to just read, not think about.
