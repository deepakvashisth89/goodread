The Submissive SonGeorg Bendemann has a dominant, invasive father who leaves no room to let him Be. In fact, Georg is fully manipulated by his old man and… period!On the other hand, Georg is also a happily engaged man who devotes plenty of time to his fiancé. Hence, he has a way out, a salvation gate out of the toxic relation with his prepotent father. At his core, Georg wasn’t just a blank blackboard where his father could write anything he pleased. Nope!…Georg had another option, a chance of choosing a healthy breakup!So why the heck didn’t he do it?!Why didn’t he embrace that path if it was a matter of life and death?! A matter of survivance of his own, precious self?!Yes! WHY?!…Although I understand the point of the author, I think this tale could have been more focused in the doorway to freedom instead of the accusing tone. Parents should be more cautious with what they say to their kids, but… kids also have the option of confronting them. Georg didn’t use that option when he perfectly could and should!
