An omnibus edition of two of Allen's oldest works. A bit dated but overall not bad. Not too fond of the write out of one of the key bits of tech from the first book to the second, "oh hey, it mysteriously melted down, so we're not going to use it anymore" just comes across as silly, every bit as bad as the Treknobabble explanation of the week for why they couldn't solve this particular episode with the transporter. Other than that minor issue and some oddities of the bio-tech in the second book, it's a decent bit of military SF.
