Haven't stumbled upon a book with an actual happy ending for ages now. That's probably one of the very few reasons why I liked this book -some of which are not even related to the latter, from a literary point of view. Frankly, I wouldn't have given it the time if it wasn't given to me as a gift by someone who's dear to my heart. But to be fair, it was worth my while anyway. To quote the book, it was an amalgam of 'soap opera of the first order' which is not something I'm attuned to read. Plot-wise, it took me a while to find my way to where the title started to have a meaning, because afterall how can a whole book solely revolve around 'The Wedding'? It did, somehow. There were no major plot twists in this sense albeit a few tiny ones that were sort of expected, but it gave me a respite still to see things happen the way I hoped they will. So I guess, afterall, this book makes one good emotional break, but I'd rather plunge back into 'tragedies' now.
