Never managed to get all the way through this until I just gave up and skimmed through as best I could - I wanted to know what happened, but I couldn't endure battling through all the sex and violence to get there.I'm not sure I'd say it was badly written per se, but it reads like pulp fiction & that's not a compliment. I likely would never have picked this up had I not inherited it, along with a few hundred other books, from my mother, who usually had excellent taste in reading material.If anyone reading this wants to try it, please let me know. I'd happily pass it on (I have plenty of other books I've inherited from Mum, I won't miss this one!). I'm likely going to register it on BookCrossing & give it up through BookMooch as a controlled release. I need the points...
