This really is the last of the Serrano series, and what a good series it has been. This one too.It’s not a fast read, it’s like some of the old political novels Tolstoy, Trollope etc. Full of detail and conspiracies, and wonderfully complicated. Very well written too, so being a slow read is good.Because it's the last one I wanted all the loose ends to be tied up, unfortunately that didn’t happen, it just finished, as if she got tired of it. What finally happened to the mutinous fleet faction? They lost I suppose, which they thoroughly deserved, but we never got an explanation of how. Never mind I recommend this series highly.
