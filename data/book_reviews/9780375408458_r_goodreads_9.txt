This is a well-written, entertaining, fast paced crime thriller. It has an intelligent, strong and capable female protagonist, murder, suspense, wit, a touch of humor, workplace and family drama, and a satisfying conclusion. This novel can be read and enjoyed as a stand alone, but it is better when read in order for continuity. I listened to the audio version of this novel, and the narrator, Ms. Kate Reading, does an outstanding job voicing the characters.
