I really liked this book. The advice is 99% system agnostic. There's copious examples both written for this book, and references to popular media. Although the references are now a bit dated, they come with enough explanation that you can get the gist anyway.There's a lot of good advice on making a memorable, sensible villain, as well as their henchmen and power networks. I say sensible because the book encourages villains that are internally consistent, but not morally relative 'well he was just misunderstood!' Villains your players can feel good about trouncing, but that aren't cartoon cutouts. Definitely recommend if you can snag a copy, or a pdf.
