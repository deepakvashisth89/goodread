⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️I have no idea why this book sat on my ebook shelf for as long as it did. What a moving, heart-warming, wonderful story. Three main characters - Trevor, a twelve year old boy, his single mother Arlene with her own set of problems, and Reuben St. Claire, a middle school social studies teacher. In fact, as I was reading, I had this book pegged for a different prompt, different challenge - a book that sparks joy. Alas, before it was finished, I had shed too many tears to place it into that slot. If you’re looking for something different, that really touches on our community and humanity, you should pick this up and read it soon.ATY Goodreads Challenge - 2022Prompt #6- a book with an image of a source of light on the cover
