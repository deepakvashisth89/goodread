Death Lights a Candle is the second in Phoebe Atwood Taylor's Asey Mayo series. It's set in a snowed in summer house on Cape Cod and has a locked room murder to start. It's every thing I enjoy in a mystery with one exception. For some reason I had trouble seperating some of the characters. This isn't a problem I experience with her books as rule and I will continue to read the series. In this instance it was a minor annoyance as the story was very well written otherwise.
