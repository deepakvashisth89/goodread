4,5 stars.Lots of drama and it’s certainly not boring. Their lovemaking in the middle of the jungle is 5 stars.The h is spoilt and annoying in the beginning of the book (therefore it’s 4,5 stars and not 5), but later on she gets better.I especially like the ending of the book. She doesn’t forgive him immediately for what he had done. She lets him grovel a bit.I hate it when a H does cruel things and he is immediately forgiven by the h, as happens in so many HP’s. Most h’s are doormats. This is a h with a backbone, so that’s a relief.Oh, and what I also liked: the h didn’t get pregnant after they had (unprotected) sex in the jungle. Most HP h’s get pregnant when the H only looks at them.And he said ‘I love you’ a couple of days before they had sex, so that’s unusual too in a HP.
