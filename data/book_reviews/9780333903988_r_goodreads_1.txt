This is one of our favorite board books. We love the tabs on the side with the different baby faces, and it's a great educational too for beginner learners on emotions and how to express themselves. Both my 3 yr old twins and my 5 yr old love it, and every time we read it, I make them all make the same faces. Making sad and angry and silly faces are their favorite, and usually I join in, because honestly, how often do you get to be a kid and make a goofy face and cross your eyes? There's just something about it that makes you feel young again. This book never gets old for us.
