Here's the thing; one of my all time favorite books isLove, Cherish Me. It's simply perfect. When I read it, I knew I had to read all thatRebecca Brandewynehas ever written. Well, I've been working on her list, slowly but surely...and I feel so utterly dejected.This was by no means BAD, it just merely fell short. The story is about Slave Maverick (the wussiest gunslinger I've ever read) and Rachel Wilder. Rachel is, according to this story, a spinster at the ripe ole' age of 18. We are told she is this because "most men find her plain," and she was "too outspoken." OK, whatever. But, here comes handsome mustachioed Slade, gunslingin' and spittin wads of to-baccy like it's goin' out of style. He has come to reclaim his nieces and nephews that are practically orphaned (long story) from Rachel who has been watching after all 342 tykes (or 8? I lose track). She's a hellcat and he's enamored. Why he's enamored? I haven't a clue. Rachel's a grade A idiot and a B word at that. Not that he's a gem. Slade is possesive, arrogant and a jerk...normally my kind of historical romance hero. He has all the makings of an Alpha Male, but for some reason, about half of the way into the book, right when their (unrequited) sexual tension is at a peak...he becomes a pussy cat.Meh.
