Honestly, I don't remember the book that preceded this one plot wise (with, spoiler alert, Claudia becoming Queen of the 7th Grade) so I spent this one a little confused. Honestly, for a Claudia book that actually has ramifications for the ~plot~ of the series this one was pretty weak. It was highly telegraphed what choice Claudia would make, and the fact that we're still pretending this could all happen in one school year is pretty silly.That said, it did have some cute points. Not the worst BSC book but definitely not the best.
