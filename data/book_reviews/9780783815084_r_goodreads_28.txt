This is the first of Dick Francis's books I've read, but I think it won't be the last. It was intriguing and I finished it in less than two days.A 9-year-old leukaemia patient discovered her pony with a foot cut off--and it's not the only one. Who could be doing such a brutal thing, and why? The girl's mother hires ex-jockey Sid Halley to investigate, but nobody likes what he turns up.
