Another cracker of a novella from Mr King. Unusually this is one where I have not seen the film. But I now need to. Story of four friends setting out to find the body of a boy hit by a train. King always seems to drop a reference to one of his other stories in his works. In this one I found a reference to a dog called Cujo who would eventually become rabid. Probably other references I missed. But note to self - not read nor seen Cujo.

As I read this story I thought perhaps it was based on an event in the Author’s old days as the main protagonist is an author in later life. Really enjoying my immersion back with this author who never fails to deliver.
