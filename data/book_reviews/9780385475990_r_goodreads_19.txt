2⭐️ I DNF this book about halfway through but then decided to skip a lot and somewhat read through the last few chapters. This book was extremely slow for me as I could not truly get into the writing style and what Lena was experiencing in the beginning. I decided to at least skim to where her love interest Herman finally came in and I really wish he as introduced earlier. Even skimming through the parts with him, it just seemed she was obsessed with him and fell in love very fast and just talked about how they did everything together. At the end he had to disappear and the book finished off with her memories of him and him always being there as a spirit.
