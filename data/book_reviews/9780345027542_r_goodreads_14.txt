Arthur Clarke has published several collections of short stories. This particular book was originally published in 1957---so contains some of his earlier works.All of the stories are well written; Clarke has a clear prose style and some good ideas. However, this was his early work and none of the story is really memorable. This is the type of book where most Sf readers will read it, enjoy it, and not remember the stories two months later.However, if you have read Clarke's later works, it is interesting to see how his style of writing has developed from thes stories in the 1950's to his later, better known tales.Enjoyable, classic SF tales from an acknowledged leader in the field.Recommend for SF fans, especially if you like classic SF; fans of Arthru C. Clarke; fans of short stories.
