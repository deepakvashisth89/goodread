Rough. Very rough. This is the first collection of material from his daily comic strip and he hasn't gotten into his stride in both art and story. It mostly revolves around a group of college students and the eternally horny Mike Doonesbury striking out. Most of the main characters are introduced here, but there is none of the political satire, except in very small doses. It will be awhile until he comes into his full stride, but this is interesting if you are interested in the roots and development of a comic strip.
