Assey is once more out to save Weesit from itself. Mr.Banbury of Banbury Bakery fame has decided to return to the home of his ancestor and fulfill the dream of helping the town. Banbury's intentions are good and he does well at not stepping on toes until he bakes his famous tarts. Someone has used arsenic in the tarts and made the town folk very sick. Was it Banbury or someone setting him up?
