Genre/Quick Summary (No Spoilers): Seven men are sent into the jungles of eastern Asia to ambush and assassinate high level Chinese and North Vietnamese officers during the Vietnam War. They are a part of the top secret mission, “Operation Five Fingers”.Quote: “We were in a hell of a state… seven guys, half carrying, half pushing one another alone… parts of us hanging off… a lot of blood… a lot of bone.”Quote Explanation: This quote really shows just how torn up these soldiers got. They knew if they had taken a quick rest to tend to the wounded, the Viet Kong would be all over them in a few seconds. It shows how the soldiers were very persistent and strong as a group of brothers. The theme of the book also comes out with the quote; gritty, violent, and exciting.Main Message/Theme: The book is meant to tell the story of operation Five Fingers and the brave men who were a part of it. It shows how war is hell, and what soldiers must go through every day to keep us free. It is a very intense military thriller.Recommendation/Rating/Why: I highly recommend this book, 5/5 stars. The book will keep you on the edge of your seat with some actual adrenaline pumping moments. There is quite a lot of violence, but if you liked Saving Private Ryan or Lone Survivor, you will love this book.
