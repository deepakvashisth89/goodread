I wasn't all that impressed with this book. I had heard a lot of good things about Cleveland Amory, and his white cat, Polar Bear, so, I was expecting a more interesting book. In fact, the first part of the book seemed to drag, but it picked up (only a teensy bit) in the latter parts.Also, this may be unique to my copy, but there was an error in the pages. Page 201 was printed twice, and page 202 was left out.3 Stars = Just 'okay'. It's definitely not a page-turner.
