I was initially annoyed that the book didn't give me the heroine's name up front, but the reason for this became more understandable as I read on. This would actually be one of the first romance novels I read, and it was one of the only ones to remain in my memory over a decade later.

The story itself is exceptional, as I'm not particularly a fan of historicals. This one won me over with it's imagery and emotional depth. I'm not sure what I could say about the plot without giving it away, so I won't risk it. This is just one of those you shouldn't judge by it's mid-eighties cover. ^_^
