This was a fun easy read.Letty Conroy had led a quiet life as an intelligent and unassuming professor of medieval history at a college in Tipton Cove when she met Xavier Augustine. He was looking for a wife and after he had Letty investigated he found she was exactly the wife he was after. She was fine with that until she found the written report on his investigation of her, then she was outraged, especially when she read the one page document of her life and realized how boring her life looked. So she broke off their engagement (at least she thought she did) and went away for a weekend with a group called The Order of the Medieval Revelers, that was okay until Xavier got himself invited to it as well.
