I quite liked this latest instalment in the Oz series, and I think that Baum did a pretty good job of reusing existing characters without it feeling old and stale. I even liked the fact that there was a romance element to this story, in part because it basically centred around the tin woodman feeling as though he ought to marry someone that he loved back when he had a heart.It’s also potentially the first book in the whole series that isn’t mistitled, so there’s that.
