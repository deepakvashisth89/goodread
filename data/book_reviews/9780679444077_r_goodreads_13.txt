Another great book from Dr. Nuland. He does such a great job of explaining complicated science to the general reader. I'm not willing to take the time to understand it completely. But even just reading it once, you get both the gist of the inner workings of the human body as well as some important details. His descriptions of some of the medial procedures - the heart transplant in particular - give you a sense of the awesomeness of the people who perform this work.
