DNF. Oh man! I feel so let down. One of my favorite western story lines... rough and tough gunslinger arrives to help sweet, caring lady care for a passel of orphans.Let me break down all of the disappointments...📖The Prologue- A very steamy prologue of the hero and heroine in the future, already married, totally going at it on their front lawn, looking up at their nice cabin that they had built filled with all of their kids, etc. I don't remember ever starting a book by reading a spoiler prologue like that. I hated it, I felt like that I had cheated and skipped to the ending. I also did not want to read about them having sex and all of their loving feelings so soon, because I did not know them yet! I like to start a book and get to know the characters before I get that thrown at me.🤠The Hero- The hero was actually a nice masculine, rough, don't-mess-with-me type of guy for 10 minutes... until he met the heroine. Then he became a 5-year old. Normally if I dislike an MC, it’s usually for a heroine being too bitchy or what not, but this hero was a total putz and a half.🪑The Chair- I hated how rude the hero was to the Swedish guy, Gus. Gus had a crush on the heroine and was nothing but polite and courteous and the rudeness from the hero was uncomfortable to read. This hero gunslinger shows up, and immediately throws himself into a childish competition against Gus for the heroine’s favor.One particularly ridiculous scene, both the hero Slade and Gus were fighting over who would hold the chair for the heroine so she could sit and eat. They ended up snapping the chair in half due to the struggle. (dumb). So Gus felt bad and whittled away a new chair for her. When he arrived to deliver it to the heroine, Slade walked up to him and told him it was an ugly chair. (dumber). Really? Are we 5 years old? So they rolled around in the dust and beat the crap out of each other.The more I read, the less of a hero he appeared, and more of an asshole he seemed. So I gave up...━ ━ ━ ━ ━•❃°•°❀°•°❃•━ ━ ━ ━ ━
