As a fellow person of Judaism I found this book to be very informative and insightful. It covers basics of what Judaism stands for and the different aspects from different types of Judaism from Orthodox, to reform, conservative, and reconstructionist. From this book my mind as a reform jew has changed drastically and philosophically has made me more of a open minded person, not that I wasn’t before but in the way that as a jew it has been enlightened to study and find more about the people and culture that has been practiced for centuries.
