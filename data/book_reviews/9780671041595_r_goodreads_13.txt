I have read this book several times and given copies to friends, who for the most part really enjoyed it too. This journey of discovery about the author's search for his roots is captivating, and very well written. A surprise near the end really rocked me, and still does every time I read it. Strong emphasis on the evolvement of the Sicilian culture, and supportive at the same time as exposing faults. Let me know if you like it too!
