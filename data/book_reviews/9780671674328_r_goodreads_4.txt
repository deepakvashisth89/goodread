I grew up listening to audiobooks of Waitley while being introduced to him by my gymnastic’s coach. I’ve tried to share some of his videos and books with my own children and with students or athletes I’ve coached. I never tire of his stories, or the principals behind them.Seeds of Greatness begins with a story of his Grandma instilling in him hard work and teaching him both literally and metaphorically “how to plant the seeds” of growth and greatness. I reflect on my own great grandma and her happiness, hard work and dedication to others.Whether you use his tools in the back of the book, Wheels of Fortune, Balanced Living, or the Dimension Grid (or just skim the book for a few moments of attitude adjustment and gratitude reflection) you may find this book as rewarding as I do.
