This book did not disappoint me.The book talks about writing, art, music, movies, choreography, titles. Another thing I learned is that the meaning of what it means when something is first published is not as straight forward as I would have thought. For instance showing a film to the public is not mean the film is published. I hope I have that right.One of my favorite things about the book is the resources section at the end of some of the chapters where you can get public domain materials.
