I like the better legal thrillers, and I think this is one of them.Dismas Hardy, attorney, arrives home to find out his wife is in jail. She refused to answer a grand jury question about her friend Ron, who is suspected of killing his wife. She made a promise and she does not go back on promises.It looks like she is going to stay in jail unless Dismas can find out who actually killed Ron's wife, even if it is Ron. Thus begins the attorney's journey to find out the truth, not only about the murder but about this friendship his wife has that he knew nothing about.It is fortunate that Hardy is friends with Lieutenant Abe Glitsky, chief of San Francisco's homicide unit. Glitsky managed to keep Hardy's actions quiet and to bring his considerable power to bear on the case himself. But the two were not to be easily satisfied.The investigation ranged far afield, including into the dead woman's work life and her recent conversion to an opponent of Big Oil. Getting answers proved to be difficult.As we might hope, we get drama in the courtroom and we know that Lescroart knows the law. Enough to stretch it sometimes.
