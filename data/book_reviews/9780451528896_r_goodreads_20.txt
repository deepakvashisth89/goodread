I disagree with Paine on some issues, mainly his optimistic Lockean view of human nature. However, it is astonishing to me and admirable to me the degree of modernism with which he writes. His views are so shocking in line with what we believe today and unlike what was common then that it is as if you took someone from the 21st century and placed them in the 1700s. His views of international trade, economics, the absurdity of inheritable government, slavery and religion are spookily modern.

I think Paine would be overjoyed at seeing the world today which operates now in many of the assumptions he and some who he agreed with had. He would probably have room to disagree with the operation of our world. However I think he would be ecstatic to see the state of Europe and the creation of the EU as well as the continued operation of the US. He mentions that in his time a "political spring" had begun and I certainly think he was right in predicting that as we (atleast many of us) now get to live in the "political summer" with functional institutions, representative governments, and commitments to human rights.
