I finally clicked my heels together three times (wearing my Hey Dudes instead of ruby slippers, of course), whispered "There's no place like home," and just like magic, I found myself in the Land of Oz!

Metaphorically, speaking of course. I actually just started reading the book, though that's a much more boring way of saying it.

I've been intending to read about Dorothy and her adventures in Oz for years; I just couldn't find the right inroad to starting the 14 part adventure. I happened to stumble upon this collection that is broken into five volumes and unabridged. So my sojourning begins with Volume I which includes THE WONDERFUL WIZARD OF OZ, THE MARVELOUS LAND OF OZ, and OZMA OF OZ.

These are true modern fairy tales crafted with children in mind. They're full of whimsy, the ridiculous, and tongue-in-cheek humor. They're stories that feel fully made up on the spot and not thought out more than one page at a time; but there in lies the charm. It's written the way a child would make up a story.

What wonderful fun Frank L. Baum must have had letting his imagination go wild putting pen to paper with these wonderful characters and their tales of adventure. It's a pleasant and easy read, even for adults.
