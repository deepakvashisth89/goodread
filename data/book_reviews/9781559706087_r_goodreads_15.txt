Alexander I became Tsar upon the assassination of his father Paul I. While not a party to the conspiracy, he did know about it but understood they simply wanted him to abdicate in favour of his son.In 1825 Alexander I died under mysterious circumstances. No one was allowed to see the body. At roughly the same time, a "starets", a ascetic penitent, named Feodor Kuzmich appeared in Siberia, where he lived on charity. A monk without a monastery.Rumours abounded from the time of his death that Alexander I faked it to escape the guilt he felt for his father's murder and that Kuzmich was indeed Alexander.The book examines the evidence, mostly circumstantial and concludes that there was a very great likelihood this was indeed the case. A fascinating read.
