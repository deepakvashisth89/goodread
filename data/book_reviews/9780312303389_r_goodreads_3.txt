I wonder if anyone has read this book & continued to eat meat regardless of the ideas provided in this book.

I have always wanted to go vegetarian since I was a teenager so I personally like the idea of going for a meatless life. However, I've found difficulty with my family & relatives, especially my mother because she's the cook at home. In an Asian household, it can be quite annoying when you need to keep convincing other that I don't eat meat & yes, I'm not crazy (although I can understand that is debatable). Currently, I am a pescetarian, mostly because I still eat sushi occasionally.
I've found that even when the number of vegetarians & vegans have increased in my country (or at least in the city where I live), our current society is still promoting meat eating lifestyle. Crois-moi, I live in a "culinary tourism" area, where food is abundant & creatively created just for the sake of foodism. I am trying hard not to fall prey, but to be honest, it's never been my goal just to enjoy food while sacrificing my health (and my wallet for sure). I believe in a year or two, I can be a vegan. But first, please get me out of here.
