This was a fun listen! My husband and I did it together whenever we had longer car rides. We laughed quite a bit, enjoyed discussing some of the life lessons Father passes down to Ralph, and sharing the sentimental moments together.

A great family read-aloud but it’s also a good audio!

Ages: 8+

Content Considerations: “d*mn” and “h*ll” are used a decent amount. “G*dd*mn” might have been used once or twice. The children don’t always behave the best (sneak, lie, half truths etc.) and don’t always get caught/punished when they have done wrong. (Mother and Father don’t always agree on the behavior and/or whether it should have been punished either). Mentions drinking and alcohol. Ralph is given rum when he was cold/sick and he later pretends that he’s still sick so he can have more.
