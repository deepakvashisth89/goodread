Two and a half stars for this book.
Edward Packard has one last time crafted a significant Space Hawks science fiction thriller, showing more depth in the plot than most Choose Your Own Adventure books while following the suspenseful adventures of the highly trained Space Hawks as you desperately try to free the planet earth from the imminent destruction of the horrible enemy known only as Nemesis, a pulsating alien race of such evil that not even the most formidable allied aliens of the Earth Federation are willing to provide their succor.
My favorite of these Space Hawks books is still probably # 5, "The Fiber People", but this book does well, too. It is a good end to an excellent series of gamebooks, and I actually wish that there were more!
