Only Tolkien could write a 20 page info dump about hobbit lore as a prologue and make me enjoy it. Genuinely the best info dump ever.The way this book goes from high stakes adventure to “hobbits love mushrooms” in the next sentence is something that needs to be studied. It’s whimsical, it’s cozy but also eerie in many moments.Sam & Frodo / Legolas & Gimli are maybe my 2 favourite fictional friendships ever.I am so in love with this world.
