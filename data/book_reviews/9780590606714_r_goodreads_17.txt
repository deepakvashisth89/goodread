This was in interesting book. I’ve never read a person having to deal with a broken bone in their point of view. Some of the practical jokes made me kinda irritated and angry. Harmless things like the joy buzzer and the water squirting is fine, but pulling peoples chairs out from under them, and hiding from your baby sitters is not funny. I tried to imagine what I would do if I were in their shoes, they managed to act better than I would, but in all, I love this book, and can’t wait to continue down the Baby sitters club reading journey.
