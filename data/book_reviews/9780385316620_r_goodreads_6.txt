A fun, fresh, inspiring book with an author who gushes her belief and encouragement like your best friend.There were a few ideas that differed to my view of them and a chapter that was kind of boring but all in all its a good 'Go for your dreams!' guide.It's set up that you're supposed to be doing lots of exercises and activities Right then and there, don't turn the page til you've done them kind of style. I kind of did them mentally and I plan to do the exercises that sounded useful properly soon. But sometimes these kind of books begin to feel like homework or there's just too many things and instructions that bog it down. They are fun though, just a lot of them.
