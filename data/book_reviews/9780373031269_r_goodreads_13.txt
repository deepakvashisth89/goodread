This is on my favorites list. Lots of lol moments. Snarky banter. Thane, the big boss, has just fired a secretary for falling in love with him. He wanted a male secretary, which he didn't get. When Josslyn falls asleep with her head on his shoulder and he doesn't push her away, you know he's smitten already. When pretty much every guy who sees her asks her on a date, that alpha male comes shining through. Some spice. If you are a fan of Jessica Steele's, the spicy scenes are all the same and easy to skip. I also loved the Egyptian travelogue. Definitely a keeper.
