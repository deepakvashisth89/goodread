The drama continues!This volume of the series was more character driven than plot driven. Rei reveals more about his past, revealing that his and Sei's father wasn't actually their father. That revelation is ultimately what drove Sei to jump from the roof. There was a lot of crying of both Rei and Kira in this volume as well. Kira worries about Rei wanting more of a physical side to their relationship and her just not being ready. Adorably, Kira plays nurse maid when Rei gets a cold. And Rei FINALLY shuts down Saori for good.Oh, and Kira's artwork is finding some popularity out in the world.So not too much plot but more backstory development and exploration of the Rei/Kira dynamics were instead explored.
