The story line on this gets 5+ stars. LOVED it! Excellently done. And her character cast is also 5 stars. Very real, true, moving characters. Great depth of writing. The only thing that moves this down is the book felt a little wooden. Since I rate by how much I enjoy the book, I give it 3 stars. Everything but the writing itself was fabulous. And the writing was very good, it just felt a bit wooden. Still, highly recommend the book and will read others by her.
