The lead character is a former senior bureaucrat who is a genius with "a slight flaw in my character" - he's a trickster and schemer. And he's very, very good at it.This book has it all - Chinese history, adventure, romance, bad jokes and puns, wonderfully inventive scams and schemes, and heroes who just don't give up no matter what.Be warned - there are two other Hughart books set in this world, and they are hard to find. But you will want them. Try abebooks.com; used paperbacks can be had affordably.I re-read all three of these books every few years, and I've given away several copies as gifts.
