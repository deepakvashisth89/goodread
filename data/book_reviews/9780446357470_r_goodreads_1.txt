I couldn't finish this one, because I was only interested in one of the sisters, and I knew right away where her story was headed, though I wished it would have been different. It's one of those cases where the H isn't necessarily the HEA, and the OM may be the H in disguise, but the h is so besotted by the H (or by who she thinks he is) that she can't see the forest for the trees.

This isn't a bad thing, except when years go by, children are born, the h and OM are both married (but not to each other), and the h seems to be living "crappily ever after" with an emotionally troubled H with mental health issues, who uses them as a passive/aggressive weapon against the h.

If she stays married to him, it hardly seems worth it. If she ends up with the OM, it's too little too late. Either way, I decided it wasn't worth hanging around for.

Didn't live up to its promise.

DNF
