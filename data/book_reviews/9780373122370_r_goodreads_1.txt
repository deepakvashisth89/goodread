I was expecting great things as marriage on rocks is one of my favorite tropes.And it had a good ‘rocky’ beginning. I loved all the simmering discontent between the two. Impatience and irritation from his side; and anger, growing rebellion from hers. Theirswasa whirlwind marriage with both falling hard for each. Love is not a problem. They say those words to each often enough. So this is not the story where the three magical words suddenly bring on a hea.**Spoilerish**They met two years ago when she was 20 and he 35 (I think). They get married, are happy enough only for the h to realize - over the years - that she is imprisoned in a gilded cage, with the H not allowing her to have a life outside his own. He discourages her socializing with her friends, dislikes her ‘wasting’ time on social work, and she dare not even mention her dream of continuing her education and working. He comes across as cold controlling kind, and she as an unassertive young wife. Add to that a devious ow, his ex-gf who brings the thing to a head by insinuating an affair with H. The h leaves home bent on making a life for herself.Now by the 4th chapter, the issue of ow is resolved as the H exposes her for the manipulative liar she is. As a reader, I was a bit baffled as this is not very usual and so what now??But, here the h decides to battle on for her rights. So she keeps living on in a dingy bedsit with a job at the corner shop. All well and good. But it seems a silly rebellion after a while as the H keeps visitingandthey keep having sex! But I was still hopeful for a big conflagration or maybe an epiphany for the interesting climactic end.Two chapters to go when I realized that she never really got down to getting the education for which this whole uprising was staged. Trust me, apart from a cursory mention now and then, she doesn't spare it much thought. To cut it short, shegoes back to himbut all her reservations still stand. We are at semi-hea/somewhat HFN stage.Color me confused!And I really hated her when she finds herself pregnant (after trying vainly for 2 years and many visits to the doctor) and wishes she had not as things are still not fully resolved and what about all herplansfor (mythical) independence? See, we are still in the hea-is-fluid state. The H, on the other hand, is ecstatic and gets all teary eyed.Btw, I am totally in love with this H by now as he shows himself to be really caring, generous and deeply in love with his wife. Bitch! (Sorry but!) It’s only at the very end (4 pages to go) when he reveals howhe(she never gets around to it) has asked around on her behalf and she could start as a trainee asap that she goes all melty and teary and finally hea-fied.Great, they are happy but I'm in the bemused, not-so-hea state here.This was potentially a 5 star book. I could throw in an extra ½ for the H too but the h's idiocy ruined it. Do these authors do it deliberately? Make us hate the h?
