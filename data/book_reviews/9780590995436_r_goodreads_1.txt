1. Summary: This humorous books is about a fly who decides to disturb the sleeping animals. A young boy is asked the clean his room, but he replies by saying "I can't clean the room, the animals are sleeping" So the young boy is able to wait until the animals wake up. A fly comes in and wakes up all the animals and they go nuts trying to catch this pesky fly. In order to figure out what happens to the room you'll have to read!2. grade level: k-23.
