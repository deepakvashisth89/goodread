What a great collection of tales of the melancholy and macabre!I don't believe there was a single story that I didn't like, but there were a handful that stood out above the rest, such as the Crowd, the Small Asassin, and the Scythe (the last being my personal favorite).Not including the mandatory Farenheit 451° read I was assigned in school, this is my first real read by him. Since 451° was an assigned read for the class, I never really put my heart into reading it, so I will for sure have to read it again.Other than that, I've only seen the animated "the Halloween Tree" and 1983 live-action adaptation of "Something Wicked This Way Comes", both of which were so great, I imagine, because Bradbury himself was attached to them.But after this read, those will for sure not be my last works of his.Highly recommended!
