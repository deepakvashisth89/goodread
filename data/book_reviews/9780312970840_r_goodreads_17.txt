A satisfying mystery from the late 1960s (but in style very much like earlier Golden Age mysteries). I liked the dual time structure, with each group of chapters being preceded by a short chapter where Alleyn sums up the case to that point to a later class of trainees. I actually figured out who the murderer was (which I rarely do in this kind of book), though I needed Alleyn to explain some of the details before I could get there. Entertaining and just what I needed as I plough through end-0f-term grading.
