I think it is interesting that most of the GoodReads members who rated this book chose not to add a review. It was tempting to follow suit.I am working through a group of three Moyes mysteries that I borrowed from a friend whose taste parallels mine. If I didn't have these books sitting in a stack in my reading room, I am not sure I would bring one home from a library or bookstore. Inspector Tibbett and his wife are interesting characters and the two Moyes books that I've read so far are challenging puzzles that somehow lack charm. I can't put my finger on it, but there is a clinical or somewhat plodding technical aspect to the mysteries that detracts from the escapist fun that I like a mystery novel to provide.
