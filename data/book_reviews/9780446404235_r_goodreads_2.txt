Bette Davis' husband hires Toby to protect her from Axis spies who are after the military secrets he works on.Of course, Bette gets kidnapped right out from under Toby. Three times. Killers, femme fatales, bad character actor rejects with guns, a sleazy PI whom Toby knows, and a sleazy, deadly talent agent. Oh, and a record of Bette cheating on her first husband, which the spies are using as blackmail.A fun romp with all the supporting cast in play.
