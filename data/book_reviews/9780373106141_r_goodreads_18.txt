Well written classic of its type. Really enjoyed the local Brazilian colour, almost a travelogue. It's old-fashioned but gives a real sense of place. Took a while to warm to the h, who initially was engaged to the younger brother before the eponymous jilting. The focus was very much on the relationship with the H rather than third parties and all the more satisfying for that. I barely came up for air in what felt like a proper early 70s era story (albeit dated 1983 in my open library copy). I queued for many weeks and enjoyed it.
