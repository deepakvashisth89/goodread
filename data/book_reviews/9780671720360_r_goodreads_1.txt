This is the second volume of a long-running anthology series that presents stories based on Larry Niven's Kzinti, a race of war-prone felines, and set, of course, in Niven's universe of Known Space. Think Klingons except with panthers or lions. This second volume contains two novellas, Briar Patch by Dean Ing and The Children's Hour, a collaboration by Jerry Pournelle and S.M. Stirling. Briar Patch is reprinted from an issue of the Baen paperback-magazine anthology series New Destinies and is a continuation of the story from the first volume featuring Locklear. The Children's Hour, which they expanded to novel length a couple of years later, is also a good military-style adventure, and they're both well worth a look for Niven's fans. Once again, the Stephen Hickman cover presents the Kzinti as looking much more like Tony the Tiger than I'd pictured them, but this time wearing very shiny high-tech body armor. A fun series!
