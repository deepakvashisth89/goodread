This was my first encounter with Leonidas Witherall, and I am hooked. Very complicated--strange happenings on a train, strange characters coming and going while doing strange things. Things don't really improve when "Bill Shakespeare" (so-called because he looks like Shakespeare with his beard and all) get to his new home. Not only is there a dead body in a brand new car in the garage (conveniently isolated from the rest of the house), hideously murdered with a pickax, but it seems like everyone in town has a key to this house and think nothing of walking in and making themselves at home. Why didn't they think of changing the locks earlier?! The murderer was totally unsuspected, by me, though it was pretty clear which of the many characters didn't do it. However, eliminating those, there were still a lot of people to choose from.I only have one criticism, and that is people (Cassie? Could I possibly be thinking of Cassie?) talk too much, and often not to the point! It made me very nervous as it seemed time was a-wasting, and I wanted them all to get on with solving the crisis at hand.
