Great compilation of unicorn(!) stories featuring some of my favorite authors. Funnily enough I think the weakest story is the first one (a kind of rambling pseudo essay), the rest of the work in here is quite strong, I thoroughly enjoyed reading every other story. My favorite stories in this book would probably be Mythological Beast by Steven R. Donaldson, Elfleda by Vonda N. McIntyre, and Unicorn Variations by Roger Zelazny. I’d definitely like to read others in this “exclamation mark” series.
