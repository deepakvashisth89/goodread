This non-fiction book explains what Kwanza is about and what the seven candles lit on Kwanza represent. It teaches the traditions and cultural significance of the holiday in an interesting and informative way. I think that this is a great story for students closer to third grade but may have too much information for younger grades to retain. I learned an immense amount from this book and think everyone who is not sure what the significance of Kwanza is should read it. The texture of the illustrations is very interesting as well. I think that it is important to have books like this because it explains a culture, non-religious holiday that not many people have taken the time to understand the meaning for.
