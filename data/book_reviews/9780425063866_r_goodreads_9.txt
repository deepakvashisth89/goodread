Somewhere between Piers Anthony's batshit ORN series and Clarke's straightforward RAMA novels, TITAN is an entertaining mess. The snark quotient in the dialogue between Cirocco 'Rocky' Jones and her polyamorous crew mates may drive more traditionalist SF readers to guffaw and eye-roll. All the boundless allusions to Greek and Roman myths may feel like a cop-out to some (who knew Centaurs had two penises and one vagina), and some of the more fine-tuned scholarly pedigree may equate the science in Varley's Gaea to one getting their proper intake of Vitamin C by drinking an Orange Fanta. Sprinkle in more sex, drugs, centaurs vs. emo angels, lesbian incest, crumbling crystal palaces, giant tentacles dismantling space wheels, TV transmissions lost in space, and a memorable scene where our heroes battle a giant mudskipper, this is 300 pages of bewildering candy.

No bones about it, this is not high SF literature, but a way to escape the doldrums of the everyday grind, and in that, it succeeds like taking the right pill. Of course there's quite a shocking scene that comes from leftfield and seems like a cruel afterthought -- Deliverance along Saturn's rim with an unhealthy dose of misogyny? And then once things ratchet up as Cirocco and her astronomer Gaby ascend miles and miles of city-sized cables (complete with forests growing on them), some may feel the climactic moment dissolves like a Wizard of Oz parody - who knew that the Overlords of the Universe were really Jewish grandmothers wearing a mumu house dresses and cutting lines of cocaine). Strangely, I read this and kept thinking about Laverne and Shirley in Space - just replace Milwaukee with a BDO, Big Dumb Object planetoid. Take that for all it's worth.
