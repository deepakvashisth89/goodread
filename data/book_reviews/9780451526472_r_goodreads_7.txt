Crane's a grim fellow, ain't he?Red Badge of Courage is about a soldier who goes AWOL, but then returns and finds his courage to fight the good fight. It's a story about a boy becoming a man. And about that man walking into a slaughterhouse with his head held high.Upturned Face is about two soliders burying their fallen comrade. It compliments Red Badge of Courage in that it too is about the horrors of war.The first is about a small group of people stranded on a small boat trying to return to land, while the second is about a small group of people fighting with each other. Both stories end with the death of one person. Both stories are about man fighting something bigger than themselves. Open Boat is more in-your-face about this, being the ocean and all that, while Blue Hotel is about how little decisions and indecisions lead to a fight that ends up with one man dead.In both stories, I got the sense I was reading one of Hemingway's first drafts. Which is to say they have interesting themes, but way too many adjectives.The Bride Comes to Yellow Sky is about a bride and her husband returning to the Wild West, where a cowboy goes to fight her husband but backs off when he learns that he's married. It's about the death of the Wild West.Crane isn't a bad writer. He died young, and his youth shows with his raw, unpolished talent.
