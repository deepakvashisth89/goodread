Published in 1939, Johnny Got His Gun is about a WWI soldier who wakes in a hospital and is forced to come to terms with his horrific injuries.

The book is heavy, claustrophobic, disturbing, joyous at times, manic at others, and all-around soul crushing.

I can't believe it took me so long to finally crack this book, and I ended up listening to the audiobook....which I highly recommend. I'll definitely read the physical copy in the near future, but the narrator fully commits to the protagonist's feelings and gives a spectacular performance.
