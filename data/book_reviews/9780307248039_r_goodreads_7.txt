The full, ranting review posted over atmy blog.Nostalgia disappoints yet again. While the vampires in this book don't sparkle, they don't do much of anything until the very end. Low on action, horror and characters to care about, this book doesn't have much going for it. It's a Mary-Sue fantasy that was seemingly penned by a 15-year-old-girl but was actually written by a grown man (named Brandon Alexander, not R.L. Stine). I definitely won't be re-reading this one ever again.
