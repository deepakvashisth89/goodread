". . . a distinguished-looking man of about fifty with a pale face whose most striking feature was a nose as sharply triangular as the jib of a sailing boat. There was a faintly exotic air about him, as though he were Greek or Levantine. His expression was cynical, suave and aloof, and a distant smile flickered on his lips. But it was his eyes that compelled attention. They were gray with glints of blue, and held sinister stillness which made Veronese shiver. A cold fish, this one, he thought."I never get a complete picture of the hero, policeman Dottore Aurelio Zen and I think this is a good thing. It kept me paying attention and trying to figure it out. The mystery is equally obscured. The hints dropped were also easy to miss. Good intellectual exercise for my mind.
