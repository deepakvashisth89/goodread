Just the absolute best.Beverly Cleary wrote Ramona with such clarity and specificity that you absolutely fall in love with her and root for her through every mishap.This was a great back-to-school read and I felt so much for Ramona, who likes to write her Q's with cat ears, is bursting with creativity, and is struggling to transition to 1st grade with a teacher who doesn't always see her for who she is.I can't recommend it enough.
