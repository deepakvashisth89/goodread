Audio is my least favourite favourite way of reading, but I had read this as a dead tree book before, so I gave it a shot. I was also able to read the first 10% on Amazon (that was essential to get all the characters straight in my head) & I tried to 'read along' with a copy on Open Library, but Open Library stopped cooperating for me.

& it proved not to be necessary. Hugh Fraser read beautifully but a bit slowly so I amped the speed up to 1.2.

Almost as soon as I started I remembered virtually everything about this Poirot murder mystery, but it didn't matter. Christie as usual played fair with her readers, so all the clues were there to discover. My eyes did glaze over with Christie using her characters as mouthpieces in her longing for the past (I wonder if older servants really did feel like that, I think I would be more envious of the younger generation & the choices they had) but I'm not going to mark the book down for that.

One of Christie's best.
