A hidden gem 💎 dystopian that surprised me with its multilayered philosophy and themes but also frustrated me as intended with its depiction of a restrictive patriarchy.

In a far future everything has advanced including many contacts with aliens across the galaxy but society has regressed in 1 big way: women’s rights have been revoked and are seen as a minor regardless of age. The story focuses on 5 family lines known as Linguoes who train their children from birth in humanoid Alien languages to be able to act as interpreters for the galaxy and so far no one else has been able to do so. We follow 3 POVs- a government division trying to replicate the Lingoes language acquisition with human infants, Michaela- a bred wife for a mid-tier human male whose perfect wifing skills are put to test by her crafty and brutal revenge arc for the death of her infant, and finally Nazareth, a woman born into one of the top Lingoe families whose plight gives us the inside scoop to how the Lingoes live and work especially the secret life of the Lingoe women who are developing a langauage only to be used by women to allow everyone to communicate they way they have been denied.

This story is not one of raging feminism but rather unjust survival and quiet revolution. It was compelling narrative and is reminiscent of Handmaid’s Tale despite having been published several years before atwood’s story.

This is one that should NOT be forgotten and is an incredible tale with many themes. Very much enjoyed this 5 ⭐️ book.
