**4.5 stars**We are approaching the end.I mean we all know or can guess what’s going to happen in the end. But the quotient of anxiety is so well-balanced here with the wondrous, fantasy quotient that, unlike other Stephen King books, you won’t actually hurry through the pages to find out what will happen next or so. Brilliant as usual, but a bit emotionally bland compared to the sixth part, maybe? I get it. All the parts can’t be equally good even if they all are terrific.I’m just trying not to get biased here.
