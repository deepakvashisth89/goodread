I first read this book in fall of 1983- my first year of college. At the time, I had no idea it was a trilogy. Fast forward 40+ years later, I decided to read all three novels. I basically came here to say this: read them in this order…(1) Dawn Of Desire, (2) Winged Priestess, then (3) Desert Fires. Why? The twins are born in book one, in book two…they are small children, and in the last book they are in early adulthood.
