Amanda Davenport has missed her ride home, and is struggling to feed herself as she awaits another ship bound for England. As time passes her funds run low and she is forced to take the job as a governess for three wild children who want nothing to do with another governess. Amanda's hate for Australia doesn't help matters any, especially since she soon finds herself falling in love with her employer, one Mr. O'Riley who is far from the man she thought she would one day find and love.This is a wonderful book. It is refreshing in the fact that is a historical romance that is set in Australia which is a place that I have seldom encountered in novels. I was amazed at Ms. Procter's ability to describe the landscape well enough for me to picture it, and of course her romances are never lacking excitement, strong personal conflict or passion.
