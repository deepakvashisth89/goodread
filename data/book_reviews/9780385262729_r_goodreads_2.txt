As a Lexington resident, I found The Bluegrass Conspiracy to be fascinating! I highly recommend for anyone who lives in Kentucky or has an interest in true crime/political corruption. This tells of corruption that reaches from the Lexington Police Department all the way to the governors office including agencies such as DEA and lists activities such as drug smuggling, murder and links to Vegas crime, this book has it all. I had heard of this book and various aspects of what went on for years but had never read the whole story. My biggest criticism is that the sheer number of people involved can be difficult to keep straight and the timeline feels disjointed, jumping between characters and events that requires close attention. While this story took place in the late 1970's and early 1980's, it's easy to see patterns that continue today.
