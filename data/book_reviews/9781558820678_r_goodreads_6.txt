Another good one from Craig Rice, the alcoholic mistress of the screwball mystery. This is #3 in this series, wherein a woman bets Jake Justus that she can commit a murder in public, with lots of witnesses, of someone no-one will mourn, and get away with it. The very next day, the newspaper reports that just that has happened. Justus, along with his new wife Helene and his lawyer friend John J. Malone, try to prove that she did it.Many laughs on the way to finding the guilty party, and a sudden twist at the very end make this an enjoyable read.It takes place in 1940 Chicago.
