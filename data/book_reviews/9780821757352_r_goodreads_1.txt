This is the kind of book you don’t enjoy, yet somehow keep reading, hoping it will get better.The hero is a boring, selfish jerk, and I genuinely hated him. The way he treats the heroine is frustrating, and there’s no real chase or effort on his part. I kept waiting for character growth—for him to finally step up and become a true hero—but it never happens. He never truly stands up for the heroine, nor does he ever fully trust her.The heroine herself is decent, but when it comes to the hero, she’s far too forgiving and comes across as pathetic. Her tolerance of his behavior only made the dynamic worse.There was no romance, no chemistry, and no emotional payoff. I stayed invested, expecting a turning point, only to end up disappointed.Overall, it felt like a complete waste of time.
