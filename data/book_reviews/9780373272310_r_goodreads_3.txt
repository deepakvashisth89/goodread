This is by far one of my favorite romance novels! I can read it over and over again. I get tied up in the pure emotion and good will of a man who... well I don't want to give it away. If you have not read this book you should, when I read it I felt like I could really see things happen before my eyes, I could almost smell the freshly brewed coffee in the morning, and was tempted to purchase a yellow robe to remind me of the good read. Unfortunately I have to buy the book again because I lent it to someone, who never returned it to me.
