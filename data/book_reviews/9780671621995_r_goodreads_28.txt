This version of Guinevere portrays her as a bright sunshiney person who has some maturity but also a desire to be cherished romantically. This first book ends before she meets Lancelot but you can get a sense of where it's going. There's very little complain about with Arthur. He's going to be a strong, focused, flexible insightful leader but he seems to view Guinevere at a distance. He's thrilled with the strengths she brings to her new position as High Queen but blind to her yearning to be special to him.There are stretches of this book that move pretty slowly. I think the characters are very well drawn particularly the women and there are lots of women in this story. Morgan was particularly interesting with her quicksilver temper and her blindness to others. Igraine is a queenly ideal, warm but also remote. Nimue is a strange combination of maturity and innocence.
