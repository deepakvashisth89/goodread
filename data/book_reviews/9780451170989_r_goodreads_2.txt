I would rate this a two except for the OMG ending. I kind of hated reading it, but it did keep my attention.
The Mayor, married and responsible, sure he is, fall "in love" with a 17 year old girl. First of all, let's get this clear, what he calls love is actually an obsession and lust. And the girl's love is more genuine but she is in love with an image of herself that she sees in his eyes and the man she thinks he is. She is missing a lot of information and is gullible and naive.
The Mayor goes on a major crime spree in the pursuit of 17 year old Sheila, but no one knows this. Detective suspects it but is outwitted. Wife figures everything out, but she is still in love with her husband, whatever that means at this point, and believe me it is nothing good. Wifey makes a plan, hubby makes a plan, Sheila sits in the middle of these scary adults like a tasty meal.
The ending? Wait for it...!!!
