Strozier's interviews are both frightening, and enlightening. I wish it had been written ten years later--because the same study on the same people (New York fundamentalists) would have yielded even stranger results in a post-9/11 world.To sum it up: "The apocalyptic tends to undermine personal efficacy and a commitment to human purposes . . . something important is lost as fundamentalists shift the Christian emphases from the Sermon on the Mount to the book of Revelation." (pg. 252)My FAVORITE lines: "Augusta Almeida . . . claimed that Jesus was an extraterrestrial and the commander of a large force he has already brought with him to earth, many of whom still remain. Almeida claims that the earth is to be 'evacuated' between 1993 and 1997 so it can be repaired; then Jesus will return. She names this process of repair the 'Grand Lift' and says it will begin in the Phillipines." (pg. 237)
