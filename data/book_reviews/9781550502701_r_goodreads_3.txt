I'm really enjoying this series and am looking forward to starting book 4 while handing this one off to my grandchildren. Each novel in the series helps teach a different aspect of canadian history with a focus on Moose Jaw. Andrea and her brother Tony are forced to go back in time and the tunnels again to rescue their friends Kami & Eddie. Kami and Eddie aren't very interested in their genealogical roots as Canadians born with Chinese heritage but in the tunnels, they are given a very clear picture of the difficulties that early Chinese immigrants to Canada faced. The story re-introduced favorite characters from the earlier novels plus some new characters, both good and bad.

I would definitely recommend these books!
