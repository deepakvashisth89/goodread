Space Search is a rather short YA adventure book that Dell published under their Emerald imprint a year or two after Reynolds' death. It's the only one of his posthumous novels that was not published as a collaboration, so it may be the final one that he completed. It's a pretty good story of a boy who's the heir to a large family business, but his father disappears and the business is taken over by his uncle, who forces him to leave school and begin work. He runs away to search for his father on an interplanetary quest with the help of an older mercenary he encounters. It's set within an Indian culture, which I thought was described pretty well. It's a quick and entertaining read.
