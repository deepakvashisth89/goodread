A typical chick-lit story I grabbed off the shelf at the library.LOVED that it is based in Australia! There were lots of little Aussie references and recognisable Brisbane landmarks. It's not often we get to read a book and know the shopping centre mentioned!The down-side was I did find it a slow-starter and a little boring and skimmed in parts. Also, the dialogue - what was the deal with Rachel speaking in quotation marks like normal, but everyone else's dialogue is initalics - no quotation marks - drove me a little crazy!... or maybe that's just me?**Edited to add** - I just read a few reviews here and ONE other person has mentioned the quotation marks thing! So glad I'm not the only one, but we seem to be in the minority!
