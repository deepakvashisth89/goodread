3.7 stars!

I enjoyed this book, although there are a few aspects that I didn't like. The Wishsong of Shannara follows Brin Ohmsford and Allanon as the go on a journey to save the world from a dark magic. Brin has a powerful magic called wishsong in which she sings and can use her power to do anything. The wishsong has a dark aspect to it and Brin eventually becomes prey to the dark magic. Will she be able to fight the magic and still save the world?

The pacing in the beginning was great, although it was a bit inconsistent in the middle. I really liked the world building, the author spend a great deal of time describing and explaining the world. There were several interesting creatures and people including dwarfs, gnomes, elves, magicians, humans, wraiths and more. I really liked the characters especially Brin, Jair, Slanter, Garret Jax and Kimber. I liked how well rounded Brin was, she went through several challenges the book and made a few mistakes, but she came back the other side stronger. Brin was very brave, strong, loyal, stubborn, intelligent and determined. Brin's character development was great, she learned a great deal about her powers and her self during her journey. Jair, Brin's brother was another great and well-rounded character. Jair is tenacious, courageous, strong, loyal, protective and will do anything for his sister. Jair's character development was excellent, in the beginning he was unsure of himself but during the journey he became more confident in himself. The side characters were great as well, although I wish we learned more about their backstory and understood their point of view more. The writing was detailed and immersive, but at times there was too much detail about the setting. I'm glad that the story was resolved at the end, but I'm sad about some aspects of it.
