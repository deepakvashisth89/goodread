2012 Grade A-.Although the book has 560 physical pages, the story is only 531 pages. The rest is mostly a preview to another book. Quite interesting, good characters and plot, both of which, as a reader, I cared about. Reads easily and can be read in short segments, for example 30-60 minutes. I had no problem putting it down when necessary and picking it up later, at least until page 295 when the action starts. But it was good enough that I never let a day pass without reading it. I did speed read through some dull political parts.
