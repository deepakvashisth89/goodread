"Memories Of The Past" is the story of Helen and Caleb.In this book, our h returns back home to prevent her father from selling the house she grew up in Cherry Trees. She blames his new neighbor and friend Cal for it, who is a businessman staying in the country to take care of his nephew Sam. She develops instant antagonism for him, baits him at every turn, but as she sees him lovingly take care of Sam, she starts to fall for both. However, she is obstinate and hell bent on resisting him. Furthermore, a douchy ex tries to blackmail her. What happens with all this drama forms the story.Honestly, the heroine in this one was so damn annoying. She was pretentious and judgmental, and though she came on strong and independent in the start- when she starts letting herself be blackmailed by the stupidest ex, she later becomes super doormat-ty. I did not like her.The hero in contrast was sweet and caring- we could see how smitten he was by the heroine and how gently he pursued her. The super adorable baby Sam, and his scenes with Cal- especially Cal's fight to keep him makes you just go Aww!Average at the best.Safe2.5/5
