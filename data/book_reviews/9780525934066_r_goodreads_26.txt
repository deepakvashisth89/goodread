I bought this for my friend from a thrift store for $1 as a semi-joke and we both ended up loving it. Absolutely not a book that will shake up your worldview but I laughed out loud at least once per chapter (which I almost never do while reading). Very easy and fast read. Vaguely misogynistic and racist in the way only white gay men in the 90s can get away with, but my god, I loved every inch of the ridiculous world the author created!! Dogs name is Carmen De fleur. Loved it so much.
