French is said to be the language of Molière, such is the author's fame. I thoroughly enjoyed this book of his clever plays, it easily passes the test of time. There were many sections when dialog made me laugh aloud. Any one of these plays would make the book worthwhile but the combination is outstanding. The translation of prose and verse is enjoyable and easy to read.For others like me who read plays infrequently I'll suggest that reading an entire play, or at least an entire act, makes remembering characters easier. They're not that long.
