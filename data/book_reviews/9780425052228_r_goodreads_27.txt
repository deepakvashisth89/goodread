"A lonely wife cheats. A brutal husband gets revenge. A not-so-innocent stranger hears a cellar door scrape shut - and begins twenty years of indescribable horror, chained in total darkness, feeding on live rats and human flesh, becoming himself the nightmare creature that lurks within us all..."This was an excellent book for anyone looking for a unique creature feature. This would have been a five star read for me, but I felt like the ending was a little underwhelming.
