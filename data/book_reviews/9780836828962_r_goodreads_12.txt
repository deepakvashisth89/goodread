Granny has had loads of dachshunds (Hector, Boris and Poppy for the record) so she was thrilled to discover the Schnitzel Von Krumm books and read them regularly with my son when he was aged 2, 3 and 4 and maybe even later. The dog with the very low tum was very popular in our house although he was always announced with a German accent. In this story, Schnitzel's family are going camping and true to form of all dachies, he's keen to stick his nose in everywhere, even if it's not wanted. The illustrations are wonderful and hours were spent pointing out the canoes, tomatoes, dog bed, etc and imagining what their holiday was going to be like. Lovely book from Lynley Dodd and a must-read for all dachshund owners.
