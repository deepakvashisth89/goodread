In a world devastated by at least half a dozen different plagues which have killed 90% of the population, remains of bodies are turning up all over the U.S. The military and government are visibly denying the rumours that have arisen that alien creatures are doing the killings - strange reports of red or purple worms metres long and voracious. Jim McCarthy gets drafted into a Special Forces crew by mistake and finds that the worms - called Chtorr, after the sound they make when attacking - definitely exist and are definitely spectacularly dangerous. Napalm flame-throwers are employed but they violate a number of post-plague military agreements so they are used in secret. When a captured worm runs amok at a conference killing representatives of countries that feel the Chtorr are harmless. This turns out to have been planned and the true battle against the Chtorr begins. David Gerrold spends an inordinate amount of time setting up the politics and premises and includes what is to me a superfluous subplot, but since it is the first book of a tetralogy I guess the unanswered questions will be answered anon. An easy enough read but could have been edited more judiciously.
