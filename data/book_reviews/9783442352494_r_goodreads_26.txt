The Empire Strikes Back - do I really need to say anything? It’s Star Wars! The character development is fantastic, and the story is both action-packed and surprisingly deep. It’s a little strange reading about Leia and Luke together before knowing they’re siblings (yes, I watched the movies, so I know!), but it works in the story. Full of twists, creativity, and brilliant storytelling, this book perfectly captures the epic struggle between good and evil, with lessons that are timeless and relevant. A must-read for any Star Wars fan!
