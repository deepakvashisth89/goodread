This one wasn't as exciting as the first book, but it was still an interesting case.I guess it feels weird that it was written in 2001, but feels more like something that would have been published in the 1980s with the social view of things like sex hotlines. I was well versed in what these were in 2001, and I was in high school, so this felt off with the timeline of the book.The killer was the best-written character up until the climactic point. Everyone else felt a bit too out of touch for me.I am sad that there aren't more in this series, even if the connection was tenuous at best.3.5 stars rounded up.
