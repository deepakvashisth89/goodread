I finished this mammoth book in 3 days which shows how much I was involved in it. It's a typical early Stephen King novel with a small town with fantastic characters and a battle of good Vs evil.

There were a few negatives when reading this. The final confrontation, when it arrives about 40 pages from the end, is over very quickly. I did like how neighbours were pitted against each other but that ultimately was 95% of the novel. It was a massive build up to a very short confrontation. But the character of Mr Gaunt was a fantastic, evil, charismatic character and I loved how he weaved his spell.

The good is the same as always with Stephen King. No-one writes small town characters like he does. Every time I read his novels I know what I'm in for (character wise) so I'm just hoping the story grips me. You really love so many of these characters and are rooting for them (I thought one character was going to die and genuinely felt terrible until it turns out they survive)

I really enjoyed this but I did feel a little like it was Stephen King in 3rd gear (which is still better than most). Compared to his other epic novels it's probably on a par with 'Salem's Lot but not as good as The Stand or It
