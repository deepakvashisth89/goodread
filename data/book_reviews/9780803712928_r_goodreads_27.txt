Andrea Davis Pinkney's Seven Candles for Kwanzaa is an interesting, informative book about the African American celebration of Kwanzaa. Pinkney explains the origin of this holiday and describes how it is celebrated during each of the seven nights. Throughout the book, she includes vocabulary for different African words, including but not limited to, each of the principles and the kinara (candle holder). While this book is perfect for teaching children about this holiday, it lacks in terms of plot. Brian Pinkney's beautiful illustrations display the African colors and symbols as he portrays a family throughout each night of the Kwanzaa celebration. Beyond the illustrations, however, there is not a strong connection between the illustrated characters and the text. While this book is very clear and informative, I think it would be more engaging for younger children if the characters were more than simply illustrated. For that reason, I recommend this for slightly older children, perhaps ages 7 and up.
