Another book I read back in my teenage years - I had T.E. Lawrence stage - I even owned one of the early editions (long lost, sold, given away) - it is amazing because Lawrence is amazing - the more we know the more interesting he is - particularly compared to the legend he was made or the legend he created. The more flaws and imperfections he has the easier it is to love him. That the real reasons for his problems are more interesting is only the tip of the iceberg of what fascinates.This is a marvellous book about war or writing on war - how many other officers memoirs from any war are interesting as literature - on top of it how many English officers open their memoirs of war time activities start by proclaiming they had sex with the natives they led?!The Seven Pillars of Wisdom is unique and anything this unique should be read.
