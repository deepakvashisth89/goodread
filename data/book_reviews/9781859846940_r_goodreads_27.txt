This book was very enlightening, and I learned a different history of the Palestinian/Israeli conflict. I do not totally agree with all the author wrote, but reading a view from the other side was mind blowing. The history of how Palestine was decimated and reborn as Israel is something every person should know. However, my belief is NOT politically correct, and I will probably be vilified for stating as much. Should not all people know both sides of this tragic history because there is plenty of blame to go around?
