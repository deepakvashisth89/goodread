A series of short stories that feature A.I. run tanks called Bolos. Brief History is a little dry and Courier is more an introduction to Retief series rather than fitting in this collection but just when you think there won't be a Bolo it is there eventually. Not really featured though. Still a fun little story.A very good collection. All fun to read except the Brief History. They're all still short stories so if you want more out of it you'll be looking elsewhere. Also none of them are really linked together except by featuring one of these tanks.Doesn't take too long to rip through so even if you're only slightly interested it won't cost you that much time. Solid 1960s sci-fi.
