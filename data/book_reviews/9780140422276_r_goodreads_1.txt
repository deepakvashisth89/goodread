Liberty is lever.Wyatt is a master who introduced the Renaissance sonnet, among other things. He is a poet of wit and originality. You can read more about him here:http://www.the-tls.co.uk/tls/public/a...Here is his Sonnet XXXI (from p. 87)Farewell, Love, and all thy laws forever.Thy baited hooks shall tangle me no more.Senec and Plato call me from thy loreTo perfect wealth my wit for to endeavour.In blind error when I did persevere,Thy sharp repulse that pricketh ay so soreHath taught me to set in trifles no storeAnd scape forth since liberty is lever.Therefore farewell. Go trouble younger heartsAnd in me claim no more authority.With idle youth go use thy propertyAnd thereon spend thy many brittle darts:For hitherto though I have lost all my time,Me lusteth no longer rotten boughs to climb.
