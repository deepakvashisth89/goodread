Maybe this was 3 stars. It wasn't as good as some of the others in the series, and there were 2 stories I thought were too long to be included, though they were both very good, especially "The Boss in the Wall," one of my favorites in the book. The very best story, however, was "Adventures in Further Education" or something like that; at only 2 pages long it just beat the shit out of everything else, unbelievably cool. The Neil Gaiman story was great, as they usually are, Harlan Ellison a little under his standard, Peter Straub a little over his standard. A great book to keep on the nightstand and read before going to sleep.
