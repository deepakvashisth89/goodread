A re-read, (I believe it's my third), this time via audio. Frank Muller is a wonderful narrator and really brought home this tale that was a mash up of fantasy/horror/bildungsroman. It's a story of a boy that loves his mother and will do whatever he can to save her. It's the story of friendships...maybe even a bromance. (Does anyone write bromances better than King? Red and Andy Dufresne? Jack Sawyer and Richard Sloat?)
It's also the story of the Territories. It's the story of a giant black hotel. (This world's dark tower?)

There are other worlds than these and I enjoy it when Stephen King takes us to them.
(In this case he did it with Peter Straub an extraordinary writer in his own right, may he rest in peace.)

My highest recommendation!
Be on the lookout for a Netflix show of this book, (hopefully this time it's really happening.)
