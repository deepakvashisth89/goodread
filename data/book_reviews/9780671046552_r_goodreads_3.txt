Money-making hack jobs like this usually aren't worth your time, but I found this cheap and gave it a go. Blood spurted, evil plans were explained, Buffy agonized over Angel, and it was all pretty bad, if readable.Credit to being spot on with the characters and their dialogue-felt like it was actually an episode of Buffy. I also appreciated the descriptions of Sunnydale--it gave me the feeling that the town was real unlike the TV show.
