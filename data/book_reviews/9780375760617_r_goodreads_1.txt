The endings of Mason’s stories tend to disappoint. Their abruptness is part of the problem, but more troubling is their lack of significance. An effective short story ending, even when unresolved, should reflect upon or deepen the story’s central tension. Mason’s conclusions, however, often feel muted and purposeless, leaving me questioning not only their meaning but the reason for having read the story at all.
