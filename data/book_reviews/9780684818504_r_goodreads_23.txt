I finally did it. I finally read the whole thing.I reached the end unceremoniously, about a week ago. It's taken that long for me to come to terms with the fact that yes, I'm really done. I've been reading this book for what, two years now? More? Some chapters I read seven times. There wasn't one single chapter that I skimmed.Everything about the whole entire world makes so much more sense now. This has been my anchor in the political wreckage that surrounds us right now. This is easily the most useful book on Donald Trump I've read. I understand Donald Trump now, or at least, I understand the cultural processes that created him. I understand the intermittent recurrence of Russophobia in our society. I understand politics. I know why presidents "run" for office. I have a clearer vision of the true underpinnings of bureaucracy, of property laws, of the lyrics of Tori Amos. I have superpowers! I can see through time!H. P. Lovecraft was right. This book is sort of magic.
