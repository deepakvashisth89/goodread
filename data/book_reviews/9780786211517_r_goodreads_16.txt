"She puts her cupped palms out of the window and combs the rain into her hair."Beauty oozes from its pages. This book is like a woman who is satisfied and scented with sex. She stretches as she wakes. Remembering. Smiling to herself.For pages upon pages, I smiled to myself. It was as if the author were my lover and his writing were his manhood whose praises I had to call out loud. Then, I got to the end. Now, I wanted to shout something else. Not his name in my ecstasies. My hurt. I felt cheated and used. I gave my time as I would my heart. But Ondaatje reminds me of the man who tells you in bed that he is seeing someone else. I now doubt his intentions all along.Still, it stands. A part of me almost.Beautiful.Beautiful.Beautiful.But that ending took away a star. No wonder the filmmakers left the author's ending out.
