It will make you think differently about your child's "misbehavior". The main theme of the book is that kids learn (and their brains develop) through exploration and play. Kids that seem like they are acting out or being bad when they hit or get into things are usually just exploring their bodies and their surroundings. They are developing ideas about what they are capable of. Kids need guidance with rules, but they can be taught good behaviors without punishment and actually turn out better developmentally--not spoiled.I am still reading it and as I am reading I am also seeing my own discipline style changing and growing to fit the needs of my child better and he is responding really well! We are both learning to trust each other more and that is helping him to follow rules when I ask him to do something.
