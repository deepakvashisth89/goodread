Totally enjoyed this collection of stories by Ramsey Campbell, only a couple of which I'd read before. My favorite was probably "Heading Home" which still makes me laugh in ghoulish delight at odd moments.There is a vague theme to the piece in that everyone does have some sort of dark companion, but it's a very loose theme, nothing that makes all the stories alike by any means. I'm definitely going to look for more RC in the future.
