This is not just a book about a wolf named White Fang. It is a universal story about life, loyalty, cruelty, kindness, and, most importantly, love.Since the story is written in the perspective of third person limited, the reader is able to see the world as White Fang sees it. You feel what he feels. You hear what he thinks. You understand his natural impulses in a way that feels unique and familiar all at the same time.As I watched White Fang grow up and explore the wild world around him, I was reminded of my own youthful curiosity.When he faced his adversaries, fellow predators, and metaphorical beasts, I could feel his emotional torment and anger. My experiences of being bullied as a child weren’t to the death, as they were for White Fang, but they left a lasting impression, just as they did for him.Finally, when things began to change for White Fang, when the chains were broken… my heart and my tears poured out onto the pages. By the end of the story, White Fang felt like my own dear companion. I grew attached to him in a way I didn’t expect. I know he isn’t real. I know he’s just a wolf made up of words, but I’ll never forget him.This is one of those precious stories that I don’t think I’ll ever stop thinking about.Once again, this is not just a book about a wolf named White Fang. It is a story about me, and you, and all of humanity. I think we all have a little wolf within us. The question is: are you a wolf who obeys “the gods,” are you a wolf who fights to the death to survive, or are you a wolf who loves and protects?
