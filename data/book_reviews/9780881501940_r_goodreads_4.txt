I've read several Asey Mayo mysteries now. I enjoyed this one from beginning to end. Ms. Taylor does a great job of spinning out characters, and with it being a mystery, all the characters appear one way in the beginning and as Asey investigates, of course, he learns that each one is more complicated than first assumed. There are several characters that appear to be suspects, but with varying shades of alibis that stand or fail scrutiny right up to the end.Oh yes.... A central maguffin is a treasure hunt of sorts for a big ball of ambergris, found at the beginning of the story, and once it goes MIA, the whereabouts play a critical role in solving the crime(s)
