Short book for children helping them to develop a greater understanding of what goes on in the world. By comparing the world to a village of 100 people, it helps us understand what percentage of people have what experiences. Very socially-oriented, with lack of clean water, hunger, education, etc being top priorities.Occasionally the comparison leads to slightly funny scenarios, e.g. in the education section they mention that one child would be a child soldier, but having just that one in the village is weird (no adult soldiers are mentioned). But overall the gimmick works well.I'm ambivalent about the drawings - they didn't feel to me like they added a lot to the story. A bit of visual interest, that's all.
