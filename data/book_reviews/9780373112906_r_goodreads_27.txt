Rating: 3.5 stars, bordering on 4 stars? (Because of fond memories)Reread: August 13 2015Charlotte Lamb really takes me back to the days when I didn't necessarily know about harlequin, but I knew about the 'weird' romance books with the cheesy blush worthy covers!I first stumbled across this when I was 9, in my mums hospital ward she worked at in patient lounge, where I was taken because I hated being left home alone. I didn't discoverer Harlequin until I was 11, thanks to harlequin comics (Idol Dreams by Charlotte Lamb) being displayed at my school's library (I know, weird right). But, I knew about array of books (with scandalous covers) scattered across the Gynaecology in patient lounge rooms my mum would put me in, while she worked, and I would stay with patients (70+ years old). Can't say I was into this when I first read it, but I think I might just bypass all my to be read list, and read this instead! I have fond memories of the hospital ward lounge and the lovely OAP ladies I chatted with and kept me company. At the time I felt like such a bad kid for looking through the book with such scandalous covers! Even to this day, I'm embarrassed to purchase romance books with 'scandalous' covers, because of my mum. Enough of my reminiscing, let's get on with the review! Well.. After I reread this! XDReview:The heroine is very different from many heroines I typically read and she's especially different compared to the heroine's you get during this period of Harlequin romance. She was extremely headstrong and independent and took no for an answer. A type of heroine I get annoyed and frustrated by, but nevertheless respect for not being a doormat and not easily submitting to the hero like a dog tempted by a treat. However, what really irked me about her was that she felt sorry for the secretary. The woman who practically tried pulling her husband away from her, the gall!At first, Francesca was the bored but contented housewife, but did a complete 360 when she started becoming suspicious of her husband's actions. From there on, we get the woman that I described above.The hero, Oliver was a typical businessman, a workaholic who spends too much time at work and business trips, as well as his scheming secretary. Additionally, Oliver had neglected his home life due to him feeling inadequate and neglected by his wife. But soon realised what a big mistake a made, after his wife leaves him.This book was an entertaining read, however I didn't feel invested into the characters. Maybe it was my mood, and perhaps it was because I was distracted by other books and media that I couldn't fully immerse myself into the book. Regardless, Charlotte Lambs work are well written and a great read to pass time.
