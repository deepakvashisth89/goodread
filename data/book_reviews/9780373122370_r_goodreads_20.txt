Great read, enjoyed it.It's a marriage in trouble trope but I find the blurb to be misleading. There was a small OW drama but that's resolved fast by Hero. The rest of the book deals with their real issues.Please bear in mind that this is HP. You'll have to take the grovel and solution as is and just enjoy the angsty ride!However, I liked how the heroine stood up for what she wanted. Although I expected Liliana to have a little more spine and not fall into bed with Zeke at every available chance even though they are separated, but she did not bend to his will right after or go back to him.
