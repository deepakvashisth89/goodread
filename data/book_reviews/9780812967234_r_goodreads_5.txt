This one was a little hard to get into. Too much detail at first, with too much family history and side information. However, once I got into it, I really enjoyed knowing the background while learning about Rockwell's life. Not a book for the uncommitted. It took me a while to read but I was glad I finished it. Rockwell was an amazing, intelligent, dedicated man. He had his flaws, but they just made him more interesting. This book will make my viewing of his pictures more meaningful as I will have associations with what was going on in his life when he painted them, and how he dealt with his life triumphs and trials through his art.
