Arthurian legends and retelling are one of my comfort reads. I'd read the second book of this trilogy, years ago, but neither the first nor last. Now I have read them all, and while they kind of work as standalones, if you want the FULL story, you've got to read all three.Some good world-building here, and this novel, set in Guinevere's childhood and adolescence, offers us a strong, powerful girl who loves to ride horses. Her cousin Elaine comes to live with the family and they become friends, for now, although Elaine is obsessed with what they hear about the young High King who would (eventually) become Guinevere's husband.There definitely ARE shades of "The Once and Future King" and "The Mists of Avalon" in this, although in most places it's a different take from them both. It's hard to bring details into a story that the protagonist isn't personally involved in, but it's done well here. Plus lots of horseback riding.
