Read this book for a class on Leading Organizational Change, and I thoroughly enjoyed it. It’s far more interesting than most textbooks I’ve read, and far more applicable. I’m going to be thinking about the various metaphors of organization in this book all the time: brains, organisms, machines, power struggles, politics, culture, dominions, holograms, and psychic prisons. There’s a reason that people are still reading this particular business book decades after it was written.
