An early Byron and Win book in the series. Great funny dialogue between the characters. In this outing Myron is protecting Brenda Slaughter the daughter of Horace who he used to be coached by him. They drifted apart.Aunt Mabel the sister of Horace is an interesting character along with the Bradford brothers. When Horace disappears it leads to Myron trying to find Anita Slaughter, Brenda’s mother. She disappeared 20 years ago. A rival mafia run sports agency also wants to sign up Brenda a star basketball player.Lots of red herrings and also clues. Still a good twist at the end.
