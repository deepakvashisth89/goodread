Since this Jayne Ann Krentz was published in 1982, I was not expecting anything great, but I was pleasantly surprised that the plot and characters (Kalinda Brady, president of a data processing firm and Rand Alastair, corporate acquisition shark) were better developed than expected. This is the fifth time I have read this book, so I am now reading less for content than for structure. All in all, a good re-read, however.
