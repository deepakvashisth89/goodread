The feel of this book is very similar to the Three Body Problem. The plots is different overall, but some of the elements are retained with a vastly superior alien force technologically but not in a position of pure dominance physically. It was also similar in that I would rate the character development as very low and the plot development as exceptional. I wonder if Cixin read these? The argument that all of humanity’s atrocities and infinite religions aren’t natural and must have come about from a sinister alien collective is comforting but I can’t quite put that much faith in humanity to not just be doing these things to ourselves.

If you love hard sci-fi plot and don’t care become attached to any character, then this is a great book.
