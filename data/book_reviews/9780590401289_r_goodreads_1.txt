This was a fun read for me as an adult, but I don’t think kids will really enjoy this. Most of the cases are too difficult and require knowledge beyond a child’s years – knowledge of wine, bullet trajectory, trains, army drill team marches, and other not so common knowledge, even for adults.These mini mysteries would be fun read-alouds on a multi-generational road trip as the older generations will offer a different perspective and just might have the knowledge to solve these cases.
