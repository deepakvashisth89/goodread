I chose this book thinking it was another book titled Sea of Tranquility. There seem to be quite a few with this title. Nevertheless, it is a good book and I would read more by this author. The story is about a family with an astronaut father and a gay son, and a mother who is loving but who has many issues. The plot and the characters are well written. I finally was tired of the dysfunctionality of all of the characters though and did not read the last 20 pages. Please note: There is quite a bit of graphic gay sex in this book.
