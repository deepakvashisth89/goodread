The first entry in the Deveraoux series and one of the best. Full of twists and surprises. This entry keeps you going right up until the end. The only weak spot is when the author attempts to inject some romance mixed with character insight. Best if he had just left that alone and focused on the plot. It would have made the book some twenty pages shorter, but it wouldn't have been missed.I believe that he was trying to be more like John le Carré and it doesn't feel right. However the book still manages to moves along and the story will keep you involved since you justknowyou won't get the whole story until it's all over.Recommended.
