Simply put, this book is a how to on catching the bus: overview of the effectivity of the methods, logistics and other practical considerations.Published decades ago, many of the information are now dated. For instance, many of the self-deliverance drugs are no longer available in the market.Also, as of present, there are now more effective and more painless methods available, but they were not mentioned in this book; however, these information are easily accessible on the internet.That being said, I think this book is a good introduction on this topic.
