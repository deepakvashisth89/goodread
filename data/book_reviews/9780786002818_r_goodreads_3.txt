Sissy is running away from problems long past that she wishes to keep in the past. When she arrives at Two Sisters, Minnessota, she is immediatly bonbarded by curiosity. Joe Willie Everett is the sheriff of the town and is wondering why Sissy would come to their quiet town unless she was running from something. The last thing Willie needs is to have more trouble on his hands because of Sissy because the town is full of brothels and gambling clubs. Willie knows that everyone has secrets but he can't stay away from Sissy he feels an unstoppable attraction for Sissy. Sissy and Willie overcome their differences and try to get past the fact that they come from different sides of the law. Their passion erupts when Willie finds out her past and he tells her of his love for her. But eventually Sissy's past catches up with her and Willie. Willie doesn't want to let her go so he fights for her, even though it seems immpossible. They eventually triumph and she learns to live with her past mistakes and Willie learns to except her for who she is not what she has done.
