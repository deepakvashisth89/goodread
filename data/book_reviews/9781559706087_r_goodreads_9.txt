Though the subject of this book is quite fascinating - Tsar Alexander I faked his death & lived out the next 40 years of his life as a hermit - I found it quite boring & redundant at times. I feel like this mystery could easily be solved nowadays by comparing the remains in each of the coffins. DNA tests could determine age at death & whether either body descended from the Romanov line. The clues mentioned were from second & third hand sources, & most documentation from that time period has been destroyed. Overall, an interesting theory on the death of a tsar, but poorly written.
