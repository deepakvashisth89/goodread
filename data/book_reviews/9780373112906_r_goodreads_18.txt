"Runaway Wife" is the story of Francesca and Oliver.When Oliver doesn't show up to his tenth wedding anniversary because of a work emergency, Francesca realizes what her life has actually become and moves out.She then takes a secretarial job in his company, with the help of his business partner Matt- and how she finds her own footing while Oliver realizes her worth is the story.I liked the gist of the story- however it was just disconcerting the way she encouraged Matt on every occasion; on the other hand Oliver was an idiot when it came to his vicious secretary Janice and should have fired her ages ago.Typical misunderstandings trope in a HQN book.Technically safe, but with minor exceptionsin a way she spends most of the book with Matt, and Janice forcefully kisses Oliver3/5Ps. It was weird how the book ended with Fran wanting a baby- because literally ten minutes ago Oliver had told her her baby obsession was the reason they drifted apart. *shaking my head*
