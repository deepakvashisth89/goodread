What?! All this time I was feeling sorry for him and it turns out Ichabod Crane was a douchebag!Ok, this is my first time hearing theactualshort story. So, I've been going off of movies and cartoons as my reference to whatThe Legend Of Sleepy Hollowwas all about. And if you haven't read it, you may have some of the same preconceived notions that I did. If a character is outgoing and generally well-liked, they must be the villain.THAT'S SO WRONG!I mean, yeah. We all root for the underdog who is trying to break out of a bad situation that isn't their fault, right?But sometimes (and youknowthis is true) the supposed underdog is a really annoying dick who brings down shit on their own head and then whines about it.
