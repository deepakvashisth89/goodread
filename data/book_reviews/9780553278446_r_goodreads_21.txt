I read this yet more years after the second book, and after re-reading the UN-abridged version of the first. Well, I may have well just stayed with the first. This book delved deeply and at great length about what it meant to be "human" in this books' universe, and it was all rather new agey confusing. Almost no revelations about the Chtorr, and those that were there were just as confusing as the new agey stuff, as it's all supposition on the characters parts. They never come to any real conclusions and leave even more questions. These quesiton are left in the "Oh, GET ON WITH IT" kind of way, and not so much the "ahaaaaa!" kind of way. It really was an effort to finish this book.
