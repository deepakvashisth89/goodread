It's always fascinating when a bad book is excellently written. Sky is a very good storyteller, but the story she's telling is awful.The main point of the book is about how a scientist, Tremaine, visiting the Enterprise is racist against Vulcans and hates Spock because of it. The entire first half of the book is how her presence on the Enterprise disrupts everyone. It was mind boggling to spend so much time with Tremaine, because what Trek fan is going to enjoy a character that irrationally hates Spock? At no point is Tremaine not doomed to fail as a likeable character.The second half is classic Trek adventure, with a planetary exploration and threat from a Romulan ship. This half is mostly fun, thanks to being very familiar territory.I thought this was all leading somewhere. Ultimately, though, no lesson is imparted. No big moral is taught. No philosophy is put forth about the stupidity of racism. The entire point of the book is just unfulfilled. If anything, the many plot elements about Starfleet create a stronger case to be made that this book is a cautionary tale about blindly fulfilling duty to your government.Disappointing.
