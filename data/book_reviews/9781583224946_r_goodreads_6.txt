Chang's text, written shortly after the 9/11 attacks, provides an eye-opening perspective as to how our civil liberties, particularly our freedom of speech and first amendment rights, have been thoroughly and unequivocally decayed by the PATRIOT ACT.

To date, there are a notable number of outdated references now that some of the act's statutes have since expired or been repealed, but the book nevertheless speaks harrowingly to the overarching implications of exchanging our liberties of a false sense of security.

The text extends beyond a brutal excoriation of the Patriot Act and explains using clear examples how the anti-terrorism measures of the past continue to haunt us today.
