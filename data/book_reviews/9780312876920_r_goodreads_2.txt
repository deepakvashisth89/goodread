This is worth reading for the intriguing depiction of an advanced neanderthal society, but it's not a very well-written novel.The narrative was more readable when it focused on the neanderthals, perhaps because the alienness of the society kept my attention, but as soon as it returned to the homo sapiens the prose became clunky enough to pull me out of the story. The characterizations are poor, particularly that of Mary, one of the main protagonists.And the author almost ruined my enjoyment of the neanderthals by using them to sermonize about our abuse of the environment, unfair judicial system and foolish belief in God. I would have appreciated a more honest attempt at contrasting the ethics of the parallel humanities.
