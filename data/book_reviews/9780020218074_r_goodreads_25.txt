Kafka’s writing perfectly describes these feelings of helplessness, worthlessness, monstrousness, uselessness, of being separate from society, of being misunderstood, all while being actually funny, sad, frightening, gross. Every story feels like a real dream that I really had, that I just woke up from, that somehow Kafka wrote down over 100 years ago.I picked up this collection to reread the metamorphosis and to jog my memory about why I liked it so much when I read it in high school. I know it isn’t breaking new ground to say that Franz Kafka’s writing rocked my world, but it did, and I think he’s an author that everyone could love because everyone brings their own weird psyche to the work and gets something different out of it.
