Very short read, almost too short, but the simplicity of it makes it easy to remember and put into practice.

The main things I'm taking away from this book are:
1. To stop fighting my feelings! It's crazy how we're taught to deny our own experiences and repress our feelings from an early age (ie. "don't cry" "don't be angry" "calm down" etc). Actually accepting my emotions and feeling them stops me from being at war with myself, and also greatly lessens the power those feelings have over me.
2. Love myself where I'm at now. Love myself for feeling the way I'm feeling.

Also interesting insights into why we learn that we are unlovable and how western culture was built upon a negative view of the self.
