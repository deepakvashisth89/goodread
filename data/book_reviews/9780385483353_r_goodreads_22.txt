I enjoyed this book very much because Julia Child herself is such a fascinating subject who led a fascinating life full of family, friends, travel, and of course, food. The only part of the book I will criticize is the amount of detail; it's mind-boggling. I can understand mentioning names of main characters, but this lady seems to have named and explained everyone Julia ever met; it got pretty tiresome. With less of that yearbooky, who-can-forget-so-and-so stuff, it would have been a shorter and more enjoyable biography. As it is, though, I'm glad I read it; it certainly represents a great deal of work of Fitch's part and a great deal of living and working on Julia's.
