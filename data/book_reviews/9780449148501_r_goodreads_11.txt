I was not familiar with this series by Cleeves. Probably 3 1/2 stars. It was the first novel series of Ann Cleeves’ career as an author. She had definitely grown and found her place as an author with the Vera and Shetland series, but I liked the Molly and George characters and the plot was sustainably interesting. I listened to this as an audio book which was enhanced my an excellent narrator. So I will read/ listen to others in this early series.
