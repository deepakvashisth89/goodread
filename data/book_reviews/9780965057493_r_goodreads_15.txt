It’s easy to see how Boz (Dickens’ nickname) was the pre-eminent novelist of the 19th century, plus is one of my wife’s favorite albums. Dickens once again provides a heady mixture of comedy and pathos and even though the ending was spoiled by accounts of American readers sinking British vessels because they didn’t have the last serialized installment of this book and didn’t know what the hell happened with Lil’ Nell, I dropped a man tear anyway because I’m a deep and sensitive dude.

The humor: For a guy who’s been dead for helluva long time, Charles Dickens has a remarkable gift for seeing the humor in the way human beings function and interact, which is, for the most part, still prescient today. It’s more a-smile-and-damn-I-wish-I-had-written-that, than laugh out loud funny, even though there are a few of those. Still, jolly good times.
