I became concerned for Gus when I saw him pacing, pacing, neurotically pacing in the Central Park Zoo. I was right to be concerned. He was not a happy bear. The Zoo has done a lot to solve Gus’s problem and help him adjusted to the zoo environment. Imagine my delight if finding this book, which, thorough the magic of Photoshop, puts Gus literally on the couch to work out the stress of being a bear in the middle of a major met area. The book is fun in itself and has extra meaning for those of us who love Gus.
