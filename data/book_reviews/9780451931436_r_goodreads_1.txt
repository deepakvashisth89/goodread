The world may end in many forms, but here people die, and some forms of animal life, but basically you have everything else left. What really matters? I read this book 20 years after it was first written. No cell phones. Very little if any t.v. or new movies. Appreciate what you have. Those left behind in any disaster will represent all walks of life, all different morals and different ethics. Yes it is a story of good vs. evil, but for me it was man vs man and man vs himself that have kept this novel "still standing" 20 years post writing.
