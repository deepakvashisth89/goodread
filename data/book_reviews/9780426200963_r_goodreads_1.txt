Certainly one of the weaker Dalek stories of the classic series and whilst the premise of a stalemate between them and the Movellans is an interesting one - it doesn't quite have the impact that The Doctor's fearsome enemies deserves.

Dicks does a decent job of turning this serial into a quick brisk read.
All the humour of scripit editor Douglas Adams is present - most notably Romana's regeneration, though Dalek creator Terry Nations last script for the show is still flawed.
