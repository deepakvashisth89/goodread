I like the titles of Betjeman's poems more than the poems themselves. If this was a book solely of his poem titles I'd have given it five stars. Here are a few of my favourite titles:"Distant View of a Provincial Town""Suicide on Junction Road Station after Abstention from Evening Communion in North London""On Seeing an Old Poet in the Café Royal""Oxford: Sudden Illness at the Bus-stop""On a Portrait of a Deaf Man""Original Sin on the Sussex Coast""Monody on the Death of a Platonist Bank Clerk""Meditation on the A30"
