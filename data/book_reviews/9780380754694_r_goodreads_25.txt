This book will not end. I have disliked it from the very beginning, and it keeps getting worse! Flor (the main character) is an idiot! I just want to shake her. And why does it take 40 pages to say what should take one paragraph?The entire story is way too wordy - not poetic, just repetitive and slow. We get it. You don't have to go on about it. I don't like any of the characters - they are all either "good" or "bad" or somehow moralistic. The book seems to preach that poverty and disregard for other people is to be respected. It admires the lowlife gamblers and berates those who make something of themselves! And as for the plot...well, that seems to be beside the point. If we go on enough about casinos and whores, we don't really need a plot now, do we?Those of you who say there is a "strong woman character" in this book - well what book did you read? I read a female character that defines her life by the men in it. Oh, and she happens to cook. She "loves" the man who beats her, steals her money and constantly cheats on her. She repeatedly goes back to him. And this is okay? This is not a strong woman. This is a person trapped in a terrible abusive relationship. She doesn't even express "feelings" - she cannot seem to differentiate love from lust and has no control over herself. She works because of her fear of poverty - fear driven by her first husband who sucked her dry of every bit of money to gamble it away.And this book is called "sexy?" Just because it uses coarse slang terms for body parts and mentions how good sex is with the abusive husband? Not hot. Not sexy at all.This book is obviously not for me. I will finish it. But I have not liked a single minute of reading it. I picked it up because I leave for a couple of weeks in Brazil soon - I hope I like the country much better than I liked this book!!
