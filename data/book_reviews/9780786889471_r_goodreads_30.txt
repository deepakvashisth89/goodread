I've having a hard time rating this book.The writing wasn't very great, the nonlinear narrative made it hard to follow (even for a fan), and some of the stories seemed out of place.At the same time, I really loved this book. I'm giving it four stars, but I'll offer the following modifications:1) Add one STAR to my rating if you are a Van Halen or David Lee Roth fan.2) Subtract one STAR from my rating if you've never heard of either, or dislike either.With that out of the way: this book is what a rock and roll autobiography should be. Go read it.
