An account of the Vietnam war that goes between the wide-angled lens of the war and its aftermath and the story of “the girl in the picture” who was exploited by the Vietnamese government and suffered as an icon of war. The story tells of her and her family and what they endured, but also admits that many had it worse, but considering her fame, she should have received some compensation from the government and some care for her family, since they were using her and forcing her to go on speaking tours rather than studying or working for money. It’s depressing but ends on a high note for the family. This was written more than 20 years ago, and I would be curious to know what they are up to now, but at the same time I hope they finally get to live normal quiet lives.
