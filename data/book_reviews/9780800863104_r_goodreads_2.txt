This book is a study in brief of several people who pioneered investigations into the metaphysical & occult. These include Sir Oliver Lodge, Charles Richet, Baron von Schrenck-Notzing, Cesare Lomborso and Raphael Schermann. These men were not so much psychics (although Schermann was, as portrayed here, a man with the ability to predict things by looking at signatures), but rather men who investigated occult happenings (including mediumship, poltergeist activity, etc) from scientific ends. The author does manage to do a fair job here in his brief look at these people, trying to be balanced in his presentation. However, what really would have been nice would be some footnotes . I don't know why people don't use them; they are very helpful.Overall, a good read and intro to the field of psychic research; recommended if you like that sort of thing. You may wish to read other works on early investigations before you do this one to familiarize yourself with the names & circumstances. But if not, you can branch out from here.
