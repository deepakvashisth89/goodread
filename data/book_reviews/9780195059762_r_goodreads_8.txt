A friend borrowed my copy of this book about 15 years ago, and he's still borrowing it. I found this particular copy in a Friends of the Library used book sale, and snatched it up for a buck. I'm a sort of First Lady-aphile; I love First Lady stories and histories. Although this one is not as good asCarl Sferrazza Anthony's well written and well researched books on the same subjects, this is an easy and fun place to start in boning up on First Lady histories. Boller does a great job of bringing out something unique and interesting about each Lady, even some of the more obscure ones. I definitely have my favorite first ladies; I hope by the time you are done with this book, you will too.
