I would have liked this better if the H thought he had a reason to seek revenge on the h for something she had apparently done herself rather than make her a pawn in his game of vengeance against her trainwreck of a brother. And, despite her young age, the H's sister showed a real lack of judgement getting involved with an addict.

And it annoyed me that the h's weak, self-centered brother is later made to seem a sympathetic character, so sorry for what happened, he was led astray, didn't mean to let his sister down, etc., and everyone (including the H) suddenly forgives him. BALONEY!!! This guy needed to grow a pair, put on his big boy boxers and man up a long time before he did, and stop blaming the world for his bad choices and stupidity. And the h needed to stop enabling him!

The H needed to realize long before he actually did that his revenge scheme was crummy, the h didn't deserve that, he needed to rid himself of his demons and when the truth of what really did occur finally came out, he should have been a lot more regretful.

This story had too many weak points.
