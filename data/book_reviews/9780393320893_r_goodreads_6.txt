One of those unforgettable books of which I can always rememeber the name of to recommend to others.
The beautiful writing grants forgiveness for the haunting themes. Perfectly achieves that which I imagine the author wanted, because it cannot be faulted as a psychological, disturbing thriller.

Loved it. Ties up all the strings that fetter around you as you read it, with the strings all coming to the bomb that the ending is.
