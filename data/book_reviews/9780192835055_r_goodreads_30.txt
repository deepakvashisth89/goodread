I first read this many years ago and while reading The Greater Journey by David McCullough, was surprised that this book created a great sensation in Paris so decided to reread. The novel does have a horrible massacre of the English during the French and Indian war which could account for some of the French excitement about the book. Cooper does write very expressive prose and battle scenes are vivid.Cooper has an annoying habit of having poems before each chapter rankled me as did his absurd notion of having a man in a bear suit that goes undetected. His description of people who can discern who is riding a horse by day old hoofprints but not a man in a bear's suit is ludicrous.Overall, a fun read with many Western themes that we still see today with the bad guys getting killed at the end.
