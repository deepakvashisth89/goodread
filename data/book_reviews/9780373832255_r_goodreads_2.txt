To continue my little romance kick, I chose this gem. It contains three short stories by three different authors. The first takes place in Victorian London and deals with a couple who had once been engaged to be married, and now must decide whether they want to try again. The second is staged in Berwyn, PA during the American Revolution, where a single mother falls in love with an American soldier. The last one's backdrop is San Diego, CA in 1889, in which a spoiled rich girl loses a bet and must marry the town's favorite ladies man. All three of these stories were well written and made it difficult to put the book down. The first story is endearing, the second one is exciting, and the third one is bittersweet - all with happy endings.
