Unsatisfying -- for the reader as well as all the characters in the book. But I was mesmerized by Highsmith's writing and barely noticed that I wasn't enjoying it.Jack and Ralph are brought together by accident (a little like Guy and Bruno). Jack is a cultivated Manhattanite who says "vahse" instead of "vase". Ralph wanders the streets sticking his big nose where it doesn't belong. They both think the other one is capable of murder.
