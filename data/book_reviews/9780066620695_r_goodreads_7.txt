This books intended audience was business managers but I enjoyed reading about how he offers solutions in dealing with disruptive technologies that start out in niche markets and later overtake long established ones. An example is how the steamboat started out as the favorite of the small river market and as it evolved replaced sailing ships on the seas. The innovator's dilemma is huge companies rely on huge markets for their products and can't make money in small emerging ones. The solution is for large corporations to create smaller subsidiary companies or adapt larger ones to sell to smaller markets. What I liked best was the evolution of many companies that start out small and later displace more established ones. This process is very fascinating to me as is the dynamics of business. What the writer does not go into is how predicting what technologies will become disruptive is unpredictive in a world in a complicated world where randomness rules.
