I don't know about the rest of the readers, but I couldn't get into this book, it sounded phony and stilted from the start, as if the author had wanted to write a Regency novel, changed her mind but had modern people sounding like they were from Regency times. And the descriptions of anything and everything were BORING; this is a novel not an auction catalog! The description of the h in particular (as an apparent paragon of perfection) was just plain ridiculous, the H's introspective thoughts and comments made him sound like he was AI and the chemistry between the H and h was ZERO, which also the rate of my interest in the story, so it was a DNF for me!
