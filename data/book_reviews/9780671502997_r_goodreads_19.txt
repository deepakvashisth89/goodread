Rounded up to 4Nora Roberts is a new to me author (yes. I know…) but one that’s been on my list for a while. She’s a staple in contemporary romance. I bought this one because it is a novella and (according to the ‘zon) was published in 2021.It didn’t take long into the story to figure out that was, in fact, NOT written in 2021. Try 1984. The MMC looked up the FMC’s number in the actual phone book, there was no googling to learn more about each other. And the FMC drives a Datsun (and not ironically).All that said, this was a sweet story. And as my significant other said, “what’s the big deal? Sex is sex.” So there’s that.I really loved the baseball content. We’re a baseball house and so getting that wrong would have ruined the story for me.The ping pong nature of the banter was sometimes too short - not in tone but in context. Just one-word answers and not enough threads to carry through a scene.You get a full-size story in this small-size package. I definitely need to read something from Roberts that’s more recent.Show more
