I didn’t dig for deep and veiled meanings while reading this story. I just let myself be carried along on Jyoti’s/Jasmine’s/Jase’s/Jane’s journey from the fate of being a child bride in India on a horrific underground journey to America. Maybe the idea that by chance she is rescued by benevolence and ends up assimilating with what seems like ease is a bit contrived, I did not mind.For me, the language and writing made this a terrific read.
