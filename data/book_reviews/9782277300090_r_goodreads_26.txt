My first reaction when I finished reading the play was to wonder why it took me so long to read this beautiful Shakespearean play, having it with me all this time. Perhaps I thought I didn't need to read it since I knew the story from the movie adaptations. How foolish! I had no idea what I had missed for so long.

I have never enjoyed Shakespeare's writing as much as I did in this play. It was passionate, lyrical, and humorous. It was amazing to find all these in a tragedy! Only a great playwright can accomplish that feat. The story is both romantic and tragic; and coupled with lyricism, the play is a "beautiful" tragedy.
