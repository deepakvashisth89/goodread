If you push past the painfully self-conscious "look, I'm writing literature!" moments, this is an interesting biography/autobiography. Delman retells her own life story (thus far; she is young), as a Bene Israel descendant with roots in Israel, India and the US, interspersing it with that of her maternal grandmother, who faced much graver challenges in a far different time. It's difficult to say how much insight one gains into India's ancient Bene Israel Jews, as opposed to into this particular life story. In contrast, much of what she shares about being Jewish-plus -- Jewish as well as another minority -- is important reading for synagogue leaders committed to keeping the tent of welcome wide open.As the non-Indian spouse in a HinJew marriage, I was excited to find this book on our temple library shelves.
