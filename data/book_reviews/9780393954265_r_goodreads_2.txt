Good. Bless. W.W. Norton!This editions not only combines many of Hawthorne's shorts that are rarely anthologized, but also provides several of Hawthorne's own prefaces--a useful and entertaining addition. And, of course, the critical materials are wonderful. I appreciate the inclusion of Hawthorne's historical letters and reviews from the hand of Poe and James almost as much as Crew's contemporary article "The Logic of Compulsion."
