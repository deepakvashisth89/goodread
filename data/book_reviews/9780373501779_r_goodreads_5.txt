I really enjoy reading about the Fortunes of Texas and this book was as good as the rest of them. I could not put it down.Caroline is the Vice President of Marketing at Fortune Cosmetics and Dr Nick Valkov is the chemist who is designing the new Fabulous Face cosmetics for Fortune Cosmetics. It seems that the INS is threatening to revoke his green card so that he can't stay and finish the project.His boss, masterminds the wedding so that he does not have to leave. If you think this sounds intriguing then you should read this book. I really enjoyed this and can't wait to see what happens next.
