This was and still remains my favorite childhood read. I love everything about it, from the austere grandpa to the fantastical elements the children experience.That's not to say it's perfect. It's neither the best-written nor the finest narrative ever. No, but this one holds a special place in the charred remains of my black heart. From his little deathbed, my sickly inner child rasps out a request to have it read to them one more time before they depart forever.And, yes, I do pick it up and give it a read occasionally with my face frozen in a queer smirk and a tear in my eye as I recall how I felt reading it the first fifty or so times as a child. But, you know, things happen and, of course, it could be worse.
