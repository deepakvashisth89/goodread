Deadman Switch is a good stand-alone novel with a complex and engaging story. It's a hard-science fiction first-contact novel yet has a strong spiritual content and zombis. Solitaire is an area rich in heavy metals, and it's discovered that it can only be navigated by a recently deceased pilot. The galaxy is in need of that material, and there's major money involved. So, they start putting a pair of death-row felons on each ship, one to get them in and one to get them out, but what happens when it's discovered that one of the intended zombi pilots is actually innocent? There's a religious order called the Watchers that examines the question, but Zahn doesn't get overly moralistic or preachy. It's a good, thought-provoking story that is also a fast-paced adventure.
