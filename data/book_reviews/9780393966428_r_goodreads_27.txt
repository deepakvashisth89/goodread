Long novels are a struggle. So is love. I struggled. Now, I love this book.

Here is my advice to Tolstoy virgins.

Pleasurable and frustrating in turns, Tolstoy demands our patience and trust. Give him both. Don't worry if you fear you can't go on. Let him pull you where he wants. Give yourself up to him. Answer to the names he calls you whether it is Anna or Lewin. Stay open to all that he gives. Join his rhythm so you move together as one. When you finish, you will want to do it all again.

Second times can be even better. All those pages you pounded like rails for a train will fly like waves once you stop being afraid.
