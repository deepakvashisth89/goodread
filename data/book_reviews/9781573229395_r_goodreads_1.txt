The structure of this story is quite similar to that in Train to Warsaw. In this case, it is the impressionistic memories of a Holocaust survivor recounted to his much younger lover. For no accountable reason, I liked Train to Warsaw much better...maybe it was the fact that the couple is confronting modern Warsaw in the context of their wartime memories of the Ghetto. Who knows? Basically, though, I quite like Edelman's style and this one is worth the read as well.
