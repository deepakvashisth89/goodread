Was great to read the screenplays for Pink Flamingos and Desperate Living, I think those movies are absolutely fantastic pieces of satire. Found myself torn when it came to Flamingos Forever; I can't tell if I'm extremely happy or extremely annoyed that it was never made, the script isn't quite as sharp as the other two.
Sidenote: really interesting to see that Waters formats his scripts like plays rather than in conventional screenplay format.
