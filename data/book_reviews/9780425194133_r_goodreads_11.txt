I read this since I was considering taking a writing class with the author. The story is told from (too) many characters' viewpoints so it feels more like interlinking essays than a novel. Despite this structure, the characters were not fleshed out well enough for me to like or care about any of them. The plot is vague enough that when the book stops (there is no ending...ugh), I was left feeling very dissatisfied and confused. Overall, it has a very sinister feel to it which was quite disturbing since the main character is a little girl. I would classify the book as horror or surreal fiction, not fantasy.
