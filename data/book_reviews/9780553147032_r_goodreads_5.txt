Describing someone as being as “helpless as a five-year old with Down’s syndrome”. I beg your finest pardon? That and calling the feline ambassador Neko (Cat, really?!!!) from a planet called gyuunyuu (milk…be so fr right now) absolutely finished me off. Because Japan is just so ✨foreign and exotic✨The mary-sue female character wasn’t much better. Lady, we get it, you want to write a self-insert and make out with Kirk. Heck, I love that stuff but I don’t get it published. Was terribly written. Giving it one star for being a hoot before it got old and then it just became painful to read.
