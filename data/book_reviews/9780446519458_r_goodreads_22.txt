The book was written on the year I was born. Reading it the first time after 24 years of coexisting gave me the indescribable feelings of interconnection as well as heart-to-heart. In my opinion, it was better than any romantic movie or fiction, aka “ngôn tình”, out there. The content was first spoiled by my Mom, but it didn’t reduce any bit of my intent interest in savoring the story. I can’t even count how many times my tears profusely shed during the reading. The author’s story-telling is excellent, but his skill of conveying didactic messages about unconditional love, immortality, forgiveness, justice, etc. is way more to be revered. And I strongly believe this book is indispensable to anyone who are humbly and considerately walking on their spiritual quests. Love, is all there is. <3
