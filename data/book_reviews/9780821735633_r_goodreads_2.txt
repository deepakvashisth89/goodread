While living in McAllen, Texas at the Jackson Square apartments, a man in a suit would occasionally pass by while I would be tanning on my 2nd floor apartment balcony. One day he stopped from down below and introduced himself as Fred and we became friends, but nothing more (I had a boyfriend from back home). I was just out of college and teaching my first year in the neighboring town of Weslaco. Soon I found out that Fred was going through a divorce and was distraught and depressed over the fact that he did not have full custody of his children, Michael and Jane. His children would visit every other weekend and sometimes I would accompany them and play with them at the apartment or pool, and once we went out to eat at Luby's. His kids were absolutely adorable and were very friendly and happy. I enjoyed spending time with them. Fred claimed that he didn't have custody because when he found out about his wife's affair (he found her diary with details about the affair) he took off. Cassidy and the courts claimed this was abandonment which devastated Fred, and he felt was unfair. When the school year ended I moved back to WI to be close to my family and friends and substitute taught. Fred kept in touch with letters, and a few others from the complex would write to me as well. After sometime Fred stopped writing, and another friend sent me a letter and said that one day Fred just picked up and left, with no notice that he was moving. About a year later or so, I was shopping at the mall in Madison, WI and entered a book store. To my surprise, Cassidy's book, "Where Are My Children" was on a spinning shelf in the middle of the store, loaded with all her books! Of course I bought her book and read it immediately. Nothing like reading a book about people I knew and wondered what had happened to them! Like other readers had mentioned, I found the book to be captivating and very well written. It's unbelievable what Cassidy went through to get her children back. I was on the edge of my seat often while reading the book and I couldn't put it down. I finished it in two days! This book was so good I think it should be made into a movie! I would love to find Cassidy somehow and ask if there's any possibility of that. I returned to Texas after a year in WI and I currently live in the Austin area. It's been many years since I read the book but I'm going to look for it and read it again!
