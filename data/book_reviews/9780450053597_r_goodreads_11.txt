I’m a fan of the 1982 movie adaptation ofMidnight, directed by the author, John Russo. It’s a low-budget but charming occult slasher filmed in the Pittsburgh area. I was curious how Russo’s original novel compared to the film. And look at that gorgeous paperback artwork. How could anyone resist that? The book itself is pretty straightforward, the writing style isn’t spectacular but nevertheless perfectly readable in a pulpy sort of way. The story comes across a little flat on paper, but it’s also a bit darker than the film, which surprised me. It’s a quick, painless read, not a bad way to pass an evening. But I still think I prefer the film.
