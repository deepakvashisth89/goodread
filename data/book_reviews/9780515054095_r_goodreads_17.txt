The book was written in 1941 and Marsh predicts the war's eventual futility through Alleyn's eyes at the end of the book; "I believe that in a year's time we shall look back on these frozen weeks as on a strangely unreal period. Does it seem odd to you, Fox, that we should be here, so solemnly tracking down one squalid little murderer, so laboriously using our methods to peer into two deaths while over our heads are stretched legions of guns? It's as if we stood on the edge of a cracking landslide, swatting flies."
