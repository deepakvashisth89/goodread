Another espionage masterpiece from Morrell. Combining characters from his The Brotherhood of the Rose and The Fraternity of the Stone, he creates a complex story with many threads that neatly come together in the end. Have to admit it does have a lot of moving parts and at times I was losing the thread of the story but it does come together so well without any force feeding to dumb it down that you feel satisfied with it.Highly recommended, it can be read alone but read Brotherhood and Fraternity first. I believe it adds so much to know the characters before you start this one. Also going backwards wouldn't be near as satisfying.
