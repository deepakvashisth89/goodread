this would have been 4 stars except the author INSISTED on using multiple nonsensical analogies.
a good analogy should bring understanding by comparing an unknown concept to a known concept. this does NOT work if you compare an unknown concept to something that doesn't exist or make sense! the Author also had a tendency to wax poetic about what certain scientists may have been doing or thinking but I'll give them that as artistic freedom in a piece that clearly is not meant to be read as science literature.

Otherwise, I did enjoy it. I liked how it was sort of written like a travel book but through history and science instead of a physical place.
