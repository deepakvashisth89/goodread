Surprise!It is odd. I have been reading Science Fiction and Fantasy all my life and I had never heard of Clifford Simak. It took my wife, who read it behind the Iron Curtain during the cold war, to introduce me to an American Science Fiction author! I am glad she did. These are traditional Science Fiction stories with robots seeking their humanity, odd planets, and quirky aliens. I particularly likedAll the Traps of Earth, the first story in this collection, for its sense of the sublime and questioning what life is really about. I believe it won the Hugo. Easy to read, I recommend this book, and I will look for other Simak novels/stories to read.
