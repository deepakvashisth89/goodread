Either my first or second Star Trek book ever, this is phenomenal. A fantastic book on the origins of an offshoot race (Romulans from the Vulcans), I found the backstory chapters (interspersed with the 'modern time' chapters) far more interesting. Its also when I ran into the difference between "canon" and "non-canon", as well as my first introduction into the mind-bogglingly lame and obnoxious Trekker tendency to worship TV canon (to the point of needing to actively disparage book stories). That, and how trek fandom went off the deep end (see also the hella lame "call us Trekk-ERs" movement; if you're too sensitive to proudly call yourself a Trekkie and reclaim the word from the naysayers, get the F out) is what eventually killed my interest in most things Trek (that and Next Generation).Ahem. Anyway, the book is awesome. So there.
