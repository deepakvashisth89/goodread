Lenin for Beginners is a terrific guide to Lenin, his philosophy, the history of the Russian Revolution, and the preceding Russian revolutionary movement for, well, beginners. It brilliantly puts Lenin in the context of his times and explains his actions, making sure to demonstrate that Lenin was never a mere plotter or power-hungry upstart, but a committed thinker who tried to change the world in a very real way. It's a rousing story of victories and setbacks, of allies and enemies, and for me it accomplished what I'm sure was its main goal: to interest me more in the life and times of Vladimir Lenin.
