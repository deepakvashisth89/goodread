The stories focus literal and figurative journeys. Fittingly, I read most of it while traveling to my cousin's wedding in VA.Many of the authors are very well known: Amy Tan, Sandra Cisneros, Tim O'Brien. The very short opening piece by Cisneros is one of my favorites. Speaking of her home, she provides a starting point to this journey.O'Brien's piece describes the physical, intellectual and emotional journey he took, fleeing to Canada when drafted into the Vietnam war, but unable to summon what he calls the bravery to make that choice.While the theme provides a framework for the work, each piece has a unique voice. The stories aren't held to any narrow definition of travel or journey, aren't forced to fit an overall narrative, but somehow do work together. While some of the stories can stand on their own, the sum is greater than the parts.
