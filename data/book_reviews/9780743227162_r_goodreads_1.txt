The short biography in the back of this book says that Ursula Hegi lives in New York. But when I first started reading her, she was living in Spokane, Washington. These stories are all set in Spokane or Oregon with two set in Cabo San Lucas. That last location might seem strange to those of you who live elsewhere, but Cabo is a favorite vacation spot for west coasters as much as the Bahamas are for east coasters. (I don't know the equivalent for those who live in other parts of the world.)Most of the stories are fewer than 20 pages, a few fewer than 10 pages. Only the final story is of any length and that at 40 pages. It's hard to write about such stories without spoilers, and I pretty much try to avoid spoilers in my reviews. Instead, I'll tell you that one of the stories reminded me of a woman I knew years ago. She drank pretty much every day. She had been known to be so drunk as to fall off the bar stool. When her husband died she stopped drinking - just like that, one day a drinker the next day she was not. She said she didn't need to any more.These stories are all about life. Some of the people were familiar as the woman in that story was to me. Others were people I know fill this world but who I have not encountered. For the most part, the stories were sadder than I'd want as a steady diet. I was glad I read one or two and then read a novel or a story by another author. While there is not a truly bum story in the collection, neither is there one I can rave about. I admit to have been hoping for more from this author.Show more
