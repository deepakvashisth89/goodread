This book is the perfect book for a parent to share with their child. Coopie and Mama have a very positive relationship, as every time Coopie asks her what she would be if he were something other than himself, his Mama simply replies she would transform as well to be right there with him. This book has an excellent child/parent relationship, and you can tell the author put time and effort into writing the mother as a very supporting, loving character towards her son.The illustrations and use of color in this book are fantastic, and will easily capture the attention of any child. On each page, Coopie and Mama are drawn completely different, from them becoming dinosaurs, rainbows, suns, moose, kites, etc., but the detail put into their faces each time helps with the continuity of the story because the facial characteristics are the same, no matter what new thing they are. Overall, this is a great story about a child's imagination and a parent's loving support, and reassurance an acceptance for being whoever you are.
