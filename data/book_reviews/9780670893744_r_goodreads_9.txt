Very interesting account of Marion Woodman's experience with cancer, and how she relates to the journey by continually trying to remain open to soul.Marion Woodman is a difficult one for me. She always strikes me as a bit too woo, and a bit too twee, but she still has some core of something tangible underneath it all. This book made me 'get' her viewpoint more, because it was so completely vulnerable. I still find her a little too naive regarding the darker aspects of life, but I admired her fierce protectiveness of her own experience, her refusal to live under anyone else's expectations, and her total authenticity in her pursuit of life. There are two cancer diagnoses here, and how she approaches each of them is different and shows discrimination, rather than blind following of unconscious images.
