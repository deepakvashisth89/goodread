Kind of old school romantic insta love, really INSTA LOVE, between a charming and smart h and her not very charming but doting Scottish H. Bare hairy knees can be a good thing. Poor H, he doesn't know he dotes on her but can't help himself.What can else can I say that others haven't? Banter, the h continuously getting one over on the H in a loving but funny way, and a nice suspense plot tossed in make this 4 stars.As Ivy H said, the fate of poor Gilly was horrible and points off for that.
