Tanith Lee gets back on my good side with this bodice ripping gore fest. After an atmospheric start in North Africa I was concerned we were entering cliché territory with the extremely Dracula inspired return trip to England. Luckily Tanith takes a whole new direction with a Bronte style romance providing the backdrop to frequent blood baths. In other hands it could all be a bit naff but Lee writes so damn well that it all works like a charm. The later scenes with our love triangle sitting in the snow strewn mansion waiting for the inevitable are vividly realised. Hell, it's all vividly realised, it's Tanith Lee! A BBC Sunday night series (late Sunday night) would no doubt break the naff scale but I'd still like to see it.
