This book will not give you the be-all and end-all stories of each crimes they report upon, so don't go into it thinking you'll get expansive explanations. It's an interesting read if you're into CSI stuff, but I wouldn't recommend it for the faint of heart. There are some gory pics, but unfortunately I've seen worse! It's an easy read -- it took me a couple days probably of just poring through the pages. Don't read late at night home by yourself!!
