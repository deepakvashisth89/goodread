I enjoyed this much more than the better known Diary of Dixie (Mary Chestnut). It is not nearly as well written but covers the aspects of the war that I am particularly interested in. I am trying to learn more about the alteration to domestic life during the war and just afterwards; the way it effected normal households. Ann Frobel's diary is all about that. Some of my ancestors lived within a few miles of Ann, so this is especially interesting to me.
