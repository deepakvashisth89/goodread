Also read for research, also super super formative. I think this was the first short story I read andliked, and hence realized I could like the form (The Gift of the Magiwasn’t just a weird little exception). Tolkien is one of the few authors I’ve read (and was definitely the first) who takes the sublimely wonderful route with his depictions of fae/fairies/elves and it works. Is, in fact, sublime and wonderful rather than cloying.This story never loses its power for me. It’s a perfect companion to “On Fairy-Stories,” showing in his own fiction what he means by all that about the fairies and the Perilous Realm and the power of fantasy and sub-creation in Secondary Worlds.
