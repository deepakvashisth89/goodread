I was happy with this book. Zilpha Keatley Snyder shows herself to be a writer of the first rank, meting out humor, suspense, and some genuine drama at a nicely maintained pace.The Egypt Gamerings with kid-friendly dialogue and characters, effectively camouflaging the author's presence. A good story often seems as though it wasn't written at all, but actually happened, magically appearing on the page as the events occurred.The Egypt Gameis one such book.I enjoyed this novel, and would recommend it with a solid two and a half stars.
