Closer to a 3.5, all of the Pendleton written ones are good, and this one is really good though the ending was quite abrupt. This one has a large paramilitary force in Colorado partnered up with the mafia who kind of tricked Bolan to come there as a test. Way to large of an operation for just Bolan, who needs to find out what else they are for while trying not to be killed.Recommended for fans of the series, it is a solid tale, just again the ending seemed a little rushed though I do understand the concept of it.
