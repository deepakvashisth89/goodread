Alright alright- you know I am a Flynn fan by now. But this is by far my favorite in the series. The story is keen on plot and deep on characters as we learn more and more about them all. Mitch Rapp continues as a force to be wary of but now with this new closeness with Kennedy as she takes the reins from Stansfield.. and how the trust builds between them--Flynn gives a glimpse of the intelligence world as well--This espionage thriller never slows down and neither does your finger as you turn the pages as fact as you can--If you are a Mitch Rapp series follower-- DO NOT MISS THIS ONE--
