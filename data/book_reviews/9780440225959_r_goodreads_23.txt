"King Suckerman" is the second installment of Pelecanos's 'DC Quartet' series. It finds close friends Dimitri Karras and Marcus Clay just before the bicentennial, Clay just getting started with his chain of record stores and Karras living the bachelor high-life, seeling marijuana to make ends meet. Just when Clay thinks that his hell-raising years are behind him, he finds himself at Karras's side during a drug deal gone wrong. When they remember the bag of cash that Clay snagged during their hurried escape, Clay and Karras realize that coming after them is Wilton Cooper and his crew who are out for blood as much as they are out for money.On the surface, "King Suckerman" is a fast-paced and effective crime thriller. But deeper down its an intensely detailed study of Washington D.C. in the circa-1976. The intersectionality of culture, the explosion of African-American culture in film and music, and the visceral reality of life on the streets. It's at once a fully-realized cultural study and a can't-put-it-down thriller.
