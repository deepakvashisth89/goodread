Not Chalker's best series, but I believe the last one he wrote. Humanity has colonized the starts, but the worm hole network for some reason breaks down, stranding hundreds of poorly developed colony worlds. Religion has made a huge comeback, and the story centers on a ship bound group traveling to lost colonies and giving them the Good Word, and trading a little while at it. They arrive at a backwater planet, but something is not quite right...

Over all, this has a rather melancholy feel to it, and the clever plotting and body transformations so characteristic of his work are not really here. Still a fun read, but clearly not his best.
