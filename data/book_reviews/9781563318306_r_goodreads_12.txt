In 1996, Goran Kropp sets out on a bike while pulling a trailer carrying 240 pounds of food and gear. He will use everything he is towing to bike from Sweden (his homeland) to Nepal and once there, he will climb to the top of Mount Everest. If that isn't enough of a challenge already, he will climb it without the assistance of Sherpas, Porters or Oxygen. He will be on this quest for a total year, including biking back to Sweden.On his first attempt to actually peak Everest, he is forced to turn back 350 feet from the top. Ironically, he is attempting Everest at the same time (April 1996) as the tragic expeditions of well- respected moutaineer guides Rob Hall and Scott Fischer and their clients, all of which are paying $65,000 each to get to stand on the top of Everest. He is recovering and regrouping at Base Camp for a second attempt when he starts hearing about their plight, which results in eight less people surviving and one other losing several toes and fingers and part of one of his arms.Kropp does a good job of keeping the reader updated on many of his thoughts with all of the above happening. His girlfriend Renata is with him at Base Campas his support through this mental and physical roller coaster. (She also later bikes with him back from Nepal to Sweden.) There is a lot that happens in his year adventure to keep the reader engaged.
