I don't know why, but I had to start this story over several times before I was able to give it enough time to listen all the way through. I would get several chapters in, but then I'd take a break and have to refamiliarize myself and basically start from the beginning.I think part of the issue is that this is book #6 in the series and I jumped straight to this book because I wanted to get a taste of this author's stories, but this was what was available online from our library. Having missed out on the drama, intrigue, and familial connections of the first five books, I am sometimes a bit lost remembering who's whoStill, I really enjoyed listening to the audiobook edition.
