Decided to start the year by re-reading most, if not all, of my Agatha Christie's. This is the first novel to feature Miss Marple who, as a character, is a lot less arrogant and smug than Poirot.

I love the Miss Marple novels as they always feel quaint being set in a very small village. Lots of gossiping and tea parties and vicars and (obviously) murder

A very easy read with lots of clever twists. Not perfect by any means but extremely enjoyable and a real page-turner.
