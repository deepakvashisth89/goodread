Bryan Magee is simply my guy. I read the whole thing with his particularly British enunciation and cadence in mind. He had an obvious talent for clarifying philosophical ideas in terms that are easy to follow. Provided a good overview of the landscape of the field in the western tradition. My preexisting predilection for the dude made the personal sections discussing his journey and views on the different figures a private joy. I’m definitely the target audience for what he set out to accomplish here. Thoughtful and enjoyable.
