After challenging myself to revisit the books I loved as a child and young teenager - and then review them from memory - where better to begin than at Jacqueline Wilson? I loved the 'Girls' series, I think I must have read Girls In Love a dozen times over the years.As told by Ellie, an artistic pre-teen, I instantly warmed to her. Her insecurities are typical of a girl of her age. How Wilson manages to so expertly define the thoughts of a young girl throughout the generations is nothing short of genius.Ellie lives in the shadow of her two gorgeous best friends, Nadine and Magda. Dealing with family and relationship issues is Wilson's forte; it is only as an adult I am able to look back and note how Wilson managed to tackle sensitive topics from a child's perspective. Again, this is nothing short of genius. I cannot recall any other books I read as a youngster that did the same thing so well.Todays generation of young girls should enjoy the Girls series, although may identify the absence of social media in the characters actions! What thirteen year old picks up the actual house phone to call her friend nowadays?!
