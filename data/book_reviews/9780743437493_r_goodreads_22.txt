The Shining is a masterclass in psychological horror. King expertly weaves tension, isolation, and supernatural dread into a story that keeps readers on edge from start to finish. The Overlook Hotel becomes a character in itself, its eerie, haunted presence heightening the suspense. Jack Torrance's descent into madness is both heartbreaking and terrifying, while Danny's psychic abilities add a thrilling layer of mystery. King's writing is captivating, drawing you into a chilling world where fear lurks in every corner.
