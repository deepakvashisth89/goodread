There have been times during Kira and Harumi's friendship that I didn’t think they were very supportive of each other, especially when it comes to talking about Kira’s relationship with Rei. To Harumi, having sex is no big deal and she was constantly trying to push Kira into taking that step with Rei, saying that he would leave her soon if she didn’t. The idea of Harumi trying to force Kira to do something like that when she clearly wasn’t ready to take that step rubbed me the wrong way.This volume was very emotional. We learned more about Kira’s background and what happened that resulted in her mom splitting up with her step-father. She did the right thing in the aftermath of finding out that the man raped her daughter, but I don’t agree with inviting him back now. I get that Kira’s mom has been struggling lately with being a single parent, but there are other ways to get by without bringing her daughter’s rapist back into the picture. I don’t blame Rei at all for being upset and not liking the situation.
