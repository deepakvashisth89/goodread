Read this in Swedish (the author is a Swedish-speaking Finn). The stream-of-consciousness writing and the chronological jumps demand some concentration but paid off in the end. I liked the observations done from a child's perspective- light and humorous on the surface, but hinting at deeper issues between the adults.One thing I didn't like was that the ending switches to dealing with the POW of a different character from the rest of the book. I suppose there was a point to that, but it felt tacked on for the sake of a dramatic ending.
