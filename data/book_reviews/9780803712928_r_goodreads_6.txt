Seven Candles for Kwanzaais a children's picture book written by Andrea Davis Pinkney and illustrated by Brian Pinkney. It is a book about that explained Kwanzaa and the seven principles associated with each candle.The text is rather simplistic and straightforward. The narrative is rather informative about Kwanzaa, the seven principles that represent each candle, and why it is colored accordingly. The illustrations are done and accentuate the text rather well.The premise of the book is rather straightforward. It is an informative book about Kwanzaa and why the seven candles representing the seven principles of Kwanzaa are so important. The seven principles of Kwanzaa are outlined rather well in the book as well with the history.All in all,Seven Candles for Kwanzaais a wonderful book explaining the seven principles of Kwanzaa, which relates to each seven candles.
