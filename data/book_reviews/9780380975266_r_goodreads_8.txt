The fifth instalment in The Pendragon Cycle sees Stephen Lawhead lean even more heavily into Christian themes than in the previous four books. This interpretation of King Arthur presents him as feeble and indecisive, lacking much of the legendary heroism traditionally associated with the once and future king. However, Morgian remains the most compelling aspect of the series, and it was welcome to see her given a more prominent role. Overall, it is a decent story, even if it may not fully satisfy those seeking a more traditionally heroic Arthur.
