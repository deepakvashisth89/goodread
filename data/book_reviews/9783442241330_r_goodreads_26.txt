This is at least the second time I have read this book, possibly the third.I've never read the entire Shannara universe, and I've never read the entire universe in order. So, when I found the entire collection but one at a garage sale for 25 dollars I snapped them up. I am kind of wondering why I did that now.Sheer nostalgia, that's why.I may not have read the entire universe in order, but I have read certain parts of it over and over and over and over again. Elf Queen of Shannara captured my imagination when I was twelve and has never let go. The others published at the time were pretty good too. Watching the first episode of the MTV show tore my heart out. How dare they trample all over my nostalgia?!Now my nostalgia is bucking right up against reality and I'm wondering if I dare to finish the whole series. It's not that they're bad books. They're not. They're just not as awesome as I remember them. How is it that I can read something from Robin McKinley every year for twenty years and still find something new and enjoyable in it, and then I pick this up after twenty years and am like...meh. Well, I take it back, it couldn't have been 20 years, it was only published in 2007, so 15 or so.
