Published in 2003, 'Hurricane Aces 1941-45' is number 57 in the popular 'Osprey Aircraft of the Aces' series of aviation titles. This is a fine companion to the previous publication 'Hurricane Aces 1939-40' and does what it says on the cover - looking at the aces that used the Hawker Hurricane during the remainder of WW2 from the busy times of 1941-42 through the next three years during the twilight of its active use. All of this makes very interesting reading, particularly considering the range of theatres in which the Hurricane was used. This is achieved quickly and efficiently, which is just as well given the narrowness of the volume. As usual, a good selection of supporting photos, colour artwork profiles and 1/72 scale plans are provided, with the aviation modeller clearly in mind.
