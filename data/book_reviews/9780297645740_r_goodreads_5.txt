"The Imperial Green, also known as jadeite, is rare; worth more, carat for carat, than diamonds, or rubies. Not to be confused with common opaque jade, jadeite has an astonishing green hue, more brilliant and translucent than emeralds." The source of the stone in Burma was kept secret for hundreds of years. Chinese emperors coveted and collected the stone. Almost all the collection was lost when the Forbidden City was sacked, first by the relieving troops following the Boxer Rebellion, then by the European Diplomatic Corps, then by the Chinese.
