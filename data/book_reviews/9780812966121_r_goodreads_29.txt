Well this was the first time I'd read a Western, and it was nothing like I expected. As a kid I'd watch westerns on TV and so I imagined that's how the book would roll. No. Not at all. Its a quiet story, an adventure, with a bit of romance, a bit of derring-do, and just a hint of cowboy-ness.Overall a decent enough story, just not high in action. It surprised me as it got quite interesting near the middle, building up to the denouement, which comes upon you really quickly.Apparently there's a sequel. I will look for it.Read on my Kobo Touch e-reader.
