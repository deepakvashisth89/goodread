Timbuktu is an Islamic city in the Sahara desert… Mr. Bones is a dog… Willy is a tramp – a vagrant bard – a schizo…
Mr. Bones knew that Willy wasn’t long for this world. The cough had been inside him for over six months, and by now there wasn’t a chance in hell that he would ever get rid of it.

The story is flowery… The dog is a raconteur…
Willy’s sidekick was a hodgepodge of genetic strains – part collie, part Labrador, part spaniel, part canine puzzle…

Willy is in search of his former teacher… He wants to leave her all his writings… It all started a long time ago…

After a drugged existence at the end of the druggy sixties his mind blows up… And he becomes Santa Claus’s viceroy on earth…

Now all this weird paradise is left in the past… The end is near… And of the novel is a hypothetical hereafter…

Even the dying ones have some ambitions.
