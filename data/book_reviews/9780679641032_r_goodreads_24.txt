There's a James Baldwin quote that goes: "You read something which you thought only happened to you, and you discover that it happened 100 years ago to Dostoyevsky. This is a very great liberation..." and that's about how I feel about the admittedly often mundane descriptions of Franklin's life. It's good to read someone famous for eloquence and ingenuity chide themselves for their adolescent screw-ups and things they wish they'd done but had been too lazy or imperfect to do, and to get a look at the way they think and approach life.
