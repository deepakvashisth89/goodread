When Tish’s English teacher Mrs. Dunphrey tells students she won’t read their journals the sixteen-year-old begins opening up about home difficulties. As things at home escalate, Mrs. Dunphrey might be the only one who can help.From sexual harassment to domestic violence Tish is in way over her head, trying to protect her younger brother from life’s reality. She’s a sympathetic character who comes by her mistrust of adults through years of experience.DON’T YOU DARE READ THIS, MRS. DUNPHREY was my first Margaret Peterson Haddix book. At only 136 pages, it can easily be read in one sitting. Appropriate for tweens and teens the message of talking to adults when problems are too overwhelming or dangerous is an important one.ETA: Audiobook
