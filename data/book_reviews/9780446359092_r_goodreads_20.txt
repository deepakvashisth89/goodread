I don't know what it is about Patricia Wentworth. She has an absolutely appalling attitude to women. She seems to believe that we're all silly, illogical children who faint at the drop of a hat and need a man to 'look after' us. It annoys me horribly, and yet I keep coming back for more because her stories are just so readable and fun despite the characters' shortcomings. Guess that's the mark of a great author. And these are 'Golden Age' mysteries, so I guess we have to cut her a bit of slack. These things are as much social history as they are entertainment these days. It doesn't hurt women, or men, come to that, to remind ourselves where we've come from and what things used to be like.
