I wanted to like this book, I really did.I couldn't tell if I was reading a self help book, a Sex In The City monologue or a spirituality book and somehow the author managed to make it all three. I felt that this was a really vague interpretation of spirituality, self realization and was so 'bite sized' and common sense based that it wasn't any help to me.The mention broadly of 'god' or ' the great beyond' left me truly confused to the authors intended audience or if she was just trying to apply her belief system without labeling. But I will say, labeling can be a good thing because this book was just too eclectic for me. There was not enough information on where the life lessons came from, not enough mention of meditations or practices that weren't painfully simple or found on any meditation app.I felt somewhat cheated by the end, hoping for something that never came and I think that Andrews failed to make her point about love and power being connected in her writing, it didn't leave an impression and is sadly forgettable.The one plus is the little activities/questions she poses to the readers which are good for basic self inflection.
