First Jonathan Carroll book. I thought it was enjoyable enough and can see why he has a cult following. I was on the fence about whether it was a 3 or 4 star book. To me it felt a little dated and that newer authors after him have progressed modern magical realism further.

Murakami definitely comes to mind as an author in the same realm that has progressed this genre and has a similar cult following. While I think Murakami has a different style and feel to his work, he also uses magical realism and explores many of the same themes/objects/triggers across his works.

In Outside the Dog Museum, I think the flaws are in not exploring the tower of babel, the middle eastern magic/cultural aspects, or progressing the character's relationships amongst each other further. It left me a little flat at the conclusion, and yet I enjoyed reading it. It was a fun diversion and I intend to read some more of Carroll's works.
