"A Journey to... The Heart of America" is a true story about a guy at age 50 planning on riding a bike from the Pacific ocean to the Atlantic ocean. Mike Trout, the guy decides to let his son in law Brian come along with him on his bike trip. He also works at the Focus on the Family radio which is a christian station. There are several key reasons for him taking this cross country bike trip across America. One, he was frustrated about gaining weight and also wanted to do it because his uncle did it to at age 62. Another reason is he wanted something new to talk about his life.You can't just take off and pedal 3,200 miles so they started training for the trip which includes eating healthy too. They got special bikes for long distance and got proper bike gears and clothing. Mike and Brian also decided to bring a van with them to make the trip safer. They lived in Colorado so they took the van down to Santa Monica, California. May 10, 1997 they started their trip out by dipping their back tire into the Pacific ocean. Now Mike and Brian were off to the Atlantic ocean with many people praying for them.On their exciting adventurous trip they faced the blazing hot sun, the windy and rainy storms, and gigantic mountains. Brian and Mike passed beautiful lands and along the way many searched for Mike to support him. They even had bicycle teams join their ride part of the trip. The highest elevation they faced was the Wolf Creek Pass at 10,857 feet high. That is just ridiculous! Mike also took pictures to show that they accomplished their goal.Each night they stay at a motel or at a friends house. They got food either from restaurants or from strangers supporting them. Many churches had also fed and welcomed them too. From time to time he got interview calls from focus on the family radio. They went to this one restaurant called Lambert's Cafe, "Only Home of Throwed Rolls." who throws rolls to customers who want one at the tables! Through Kansas they did a lot of drafting behind their van due to the heavy wind.Getting closer to dipping their front tire in the Atlantic ocean they had a few bike escorts to help guide them. There were many people watching cheering them as they go into the ocean! When Brian and Mike accomplished their goal Mike especially felt emotional. He couldn't find words for awhile for the news. Mike claimed he learned "There's nothing like perseverance. The Bible talks a lot about persevering when you think you can't finish, when you're sure that the situation is going to be tougher than you can handle." So don't think you can't do something just because of your age. You got to have perseverance like Mike did, he pushed it through.I recommend this book to anyone who loves to ride bikes or to people who like true stories. While reading this book I felt like I was right there with Mike and Brian on their big adventure. It talks about the faith America has in living, many places they visited, and the people they met. I think you'll enjoy their adventurous bike ride across America. I also encourage you guys to go on adventure trips because it's so much fun getting to learn new things and it gives you something to talk about. Don't forget to persevere on what you're going through, never give up!
