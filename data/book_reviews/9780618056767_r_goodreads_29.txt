I don’t think that I will ever conflate monkeys and chimps again, now that I’ve read this book.I do wish Jane Goodall had explained how on earth she at such a young age with so little experience managed to live in the jungle for all that time. I know she had help, but still. She described sleeping alone some nights with the chimps in the heart of the jungle, or walking back to camp in the dead of night through the long grass where there were leopards, snakes, etc.… I scratch my head about how she practically managed it. Alas, that’s not what the book is about! It is about chimpanzees.She does a great job telling you all about wild chimpanzees via personal anecdotes and eyewitness accounts. Through her observations, you get a good sense of how looks, mannerisms, and talent vary among chimpanzees, and the many ways that they share traits of our own.
