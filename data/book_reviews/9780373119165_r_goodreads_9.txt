Neither the characters, their motivation or the plot make any sense. This is one of the worst books I have ever tried reading and I didn’t quite finish it. I was curious if it could get worse, it did to a point I stopped reading.The h who is a model is defamed and wrongly accused and ends up suing the newspaper for libel but ends up losing the case. When she turns to her husband for support, he not only accuses her but ends up asking her for a divorce and flaunting his affair with a rival model in their bedroom.The h is an idiot who is still in love with a man who has shown through so many action his contempt for her and if she can’t rely on him for support during a time in life where she needs help why then ask for help? The whole plot about her finding her father and a mother who didn’t care for her and abandoned her too not just over the top melodramatic but really flimsy.
