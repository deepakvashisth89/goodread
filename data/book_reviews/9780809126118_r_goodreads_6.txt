Fascinating, scholarly examination of seven early churches in the New Testament period after the death of the apostles. How did they function and survive? How did authority work in them? What did each do well and poorly? What did each believe about Jesus and how did they act on those beliefs?Many Christians in America today are asking why our churches are so broken or have seemingly ceased being a church altogether. Brown’s voice from the past (he died in 1998) offers a robust series of answers for the challenges we face. A must read.
