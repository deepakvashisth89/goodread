Thomas K has a way with words. He gives us the world of Kate who lives with her husband, a wealthy property developer, and two children. Tragedy strikes and Kate flees to northern NSW where she meets people who value friendship and community over money. There are no over the top characterisations of the Ozzie outback battler. The people are real, some are good, some are selfish and some just vicious.The Catholic Church takes a hammering, and the book turns into a mystery, a story of revenge and finally a happy ending.The book is written like a spoken tale, with a narrator speaking to the reader - or bookbuyer as the case is in this story.
