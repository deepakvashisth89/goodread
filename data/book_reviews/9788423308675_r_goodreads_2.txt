Although this is dated (written in the early 1950s), I was totally fascinated by the intrepid early archaeologists both amateur & professional and their amazing finds from the 18th C through the early 20thC. Ceram can be overly effusive in his storytelling about various expeditions and digs, but it just made it more enjoyable to read. There are a few chapters that the history and plethora of names through the dynasties had my head spinning. Ceram divides the book into section and then within the section highlights a particular archaeologist and/or excavation.
Part I: The Book of Statues: Covering Pompeii, Troy, Mycenae & Crete
Part II: The Book of Pyramids
Part III: The Book of Towers: Assyria, Babylon & Sumeria
Part IV: The Book of Temples: Toltecs, Aztecs & Mayans
Part V: New searches for Old Empires

It definitely makes me want to read a more current book on the subject that discusses conclusions from these earlier finds and what we have added to our knowledge with new finds.
