I have to give major applause to the author in how he brought the Land of Oz back to life. This was like Reichenbach Falls (Sherlock Holmes reference) for Dorothy Gale. And how does the Land of Oz come back, even though we were cut off from Oz in the last book, with brand new stories for readers?... Through the clever device of... the telegraph. (Mind blown.)So I was basically ready to give the book 5 stars just because of how the author wheedled his way out of that tight fix he put himself in.Oh, and secondly because there may just be love in the air for the beloved Scarecrow. (This strangely excites me to no end.)Anyways, on the main gist of the book. Here we are along for the ride of another interesting trek through Oz. This time it is quest that a Munchkin boy named Ojo is on, to collect some very vital ingredients for the Crooked Magician. The reader gets to meet a variety of intriguing individuals, make some new friends in Oz, and overall have a wonderful time.
