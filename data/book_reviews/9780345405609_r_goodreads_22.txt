An interesting (or horrifying) alternate history novel about World War 1. Set after the events ofHow Few Remain, this book tells the tale of a house divided during the Great War. The beginning explains how the CSA has won secession from the Union with the help from the United Kingdom. What remains of the USA allies itself with Kaiser's Germany in World War 1 in an effort to destabilize the British Empire and reclaim the southern states. The book carries on with a host of characters that have to deal with the horrors of trench warfare as the US and the CS duke it out in another great war.Read if you likeHow Few Remain.
