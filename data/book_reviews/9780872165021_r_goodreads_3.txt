I thought this would be good at first, as it reminded me in the beginning of an old Clark Gable/Yvonne DeCarlo movie, where a southern belle heiress is on the brink of inheriting the family estate when it's revealed that she's part black and she's sold in to slavery instead. In the movie, Yvonne's bought by Clark and the story plays out well.

In this book, however, far from it! The H, Kirk, buys the h, Lorinda, and never ceases to remind her that she's his property. She claims to despise him, but naturally she can't resist him in bed, and soon has dreams of the two of them having a HEA somewhere, since she's so light skinned (only 1/16th black) she can easily pass for all white. However, charming Kirk wants to marry a proper, respectable girl he met in Texas, and tells Lorinda all about her while they're in bed, then right afterward asks her for a blow job! (I'd have bitten him hard!!)

It's not surprising she'd want to escape Mr. Wonderful, but her actions from then on are a real train wreck!
