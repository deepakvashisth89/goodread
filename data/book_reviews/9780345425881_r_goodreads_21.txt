A promising start to a series. The author seems to have a good understanding of people- her characters came alive on the page. I enjoyed reading about people who are obviously Christians, without the author getting all preachy and ruining the story (by being too heavy-handed and sermonizing). Rather, she portrayed them living the life: trying to love others and do justice, failing sometimes and having to seek forgiveness, doing hard things and speaking the truth even though it might hurt them at the time.The editing was a little funny (characters' thoughts are usually italicized, and weren't in this- weird, and hard to track).
