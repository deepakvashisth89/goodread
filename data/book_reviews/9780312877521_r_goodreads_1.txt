This is a western book for adults. The main character Adam Dawson was a gritty war veteran who traveled by himself. He made an oath to not kill anyone unless his life depended on it. Adam is faced with a tough decision to save a women who is abused by her husband. I think it's interesting how Adam has to make a choice to kill the women's husband because her husband helps Adam at the beginning of the book. I would recommend this book to adults who enjoy western books.
