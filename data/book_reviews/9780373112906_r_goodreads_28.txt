3 1/2 Stars ~ When Oliver missed their 10th wedding anniversary because of a business crisis, Francesca decided she'd had enough of their marriage of separate lives. So she packed up her clothes; left their Sussex home behind; and drove to London, the hub of Oliver's attentions. Needing work but with rusty skills, she goes to Oliver's partner, Matt, and asks for a job. Matt is the inventor and creative brains, while Oliver is the charmer and number cruncher, of their ever growing electronics company. Oliver is shocked that Francesca could leave him and finding her working for Matt enrages him. Being in Oliver's environment, reminds Francesca that she'd been a part of their company from the start, and she'd missed so much over the years. Every company has a grape vine and so Francesca hears the rumours that Oliver's secretary Janice has the hots for her boss. Janice makes it obvious that she's out to get him, and uses every opportunity to belittle Francesca and to drive home how close she is to Oliver. While Oliver is determined to have Francesca home again, she insists that she's on to his little office affair and that she wants nothing more to do with him.This is a charming reunion story of two people who slowly drift apart only to wake up one day to find they're married to a stranger. Oliver doesn't recognize the vital spitfire that Francesca has become. She fights his every attempt to get close to her. Francesca wants to shake up Oliver's world, and tumble that cold wall he'd built around himself. With the added tension of the evil OW, and some rather charming scenes with the bumbling OM, Oliver and Francesca begin to see themselves from the other's eyes and discover that their marriage is worth fighting for. Ms. Lamb shakes up both their worlds, and gives these two a passionately explosive journey to a second HEA.
