Peak Stainless Steel Rat.Very funny and beautifully written (hard to do both), our lovable anti-hero is sent to the Fascistic planet of Cliaand to try and work out how they are successfully invading other systems - which is not supposed to be economically viable.We open open with a bank heist ('Slippery' Jim not above the odd side hustle to stay in form) followed by a shotgun wedding - literally - as his pregnant partner in crime forces him into what he cannot quite bring himself to do.I laughed out loud reading this book as a kid, and had the same experience again recently. Harrison's style and wit were inspiration for my debut novel Jubilee.Here is Jim passing through customs on Cliaand and being questioned about his belongings:'Is this religious symbol?' another asked, holding the photograph in his fingertips at arms length.'It is a picture of my wife.''Only religious symbols permitted.''She is like an angel to me.'They puzzled over this one for a while, then reluctantly admitted the picture. Not that I would be able to have anything as deadly as the original. It was whisked away and a photographic copy returned.
