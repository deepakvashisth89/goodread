Now this was a Dawn book I liked as a kid that I still like today! I always appreciated the character of Whitney, because I knew someone with Down's when I was a kid and it was just so refreshing and nice to see them not only in a book, but to see them represented in a normal way. I liked that, and I still like it now. I mean obviously some of the terminology and ideals are dated, but what do you expect from a late 80s/early 90s BSC book? Most of it held up pretty well over time, and I love that.Except for the B-Plot. The B-plot can suck it.
