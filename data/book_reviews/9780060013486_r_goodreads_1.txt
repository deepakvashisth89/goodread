Escape from Five Shadowsis an average Elmore Leonard western that is unique in its plot. Rather than the typical cowboys and Indians, the novel focuses on a prison gang that has been commandeered from the Yuma State Prison to build a road through the mountainous deserts of southern Arizona. One of the gang, the main character of the novel, is a wrongly convicted man who wants to gain his freedom through escape rather than serve out a six-year sentence.Like most Leonard novels, this one is driven by its characters, but unfortunately, the characters are fairly unexciting. The wrongly convicted man is a good guy that would never shoot to kill. The bad guys do not have an ounce of goodness in them. The women characters are the bright spot in that they are shown to be keenly aware of their surroundings, and they are smarter than any of the men that populate their lives. Unfortunately, Leonard relegated these women to supporting roles.The result is a novel that just sits there when completed. Leonard gives hints of a amazing ending mid-way through the novel: "I could leave here - and you’ll still be thinking about it when it happens." But this ending never materializes. While the novel indeed ends, it is nowhere near amazing and I’m certainly not still thinking about it.
