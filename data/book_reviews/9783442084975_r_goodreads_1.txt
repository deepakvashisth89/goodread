Ad Hoc Read January 2023

Having a month away from any "must" reads, I picked a few favourite authors to read, and Clive Cussler is one when it comes to Adventure Thrillers. I hadn't remembered I had read this 3 years ago, but that wouldn't have changed my choice. A real rip-roaring adventure that is only the start of a great series of books.
Yes they are a bit unbelievable but when you want a no nonsense hero (or heroine) these hit the spot. As the series progresses it gets a lot better, and the latter books are much less unbelievable, much less misogynistic , and far more , with Dirk coming to rely and realise the equality of his women friends.
As with a lot of books (read the early James Bonds !) these early ones are of there time, but are still worth a read , and still enjoyable.



Great escapism, more thoughts to follow

Although this wasn't the first Dirk Pitt novel released, it was the first written and is the first in the series "adventure wise". Having read a number of Dirk Pitt novels, I know they get better than this, but that said this was a good escapist novel, and is a great introduction to Dirk and his colleagues.

Relaxing on a Hawaiian beach, Dirk spots a emergency capsule floating out at sea. It turns out to be from the US Navy's latest submarine that went missing during sea trials.
A number of adventures for Dirk ensue, and comprise golden-eyed giants, car chases down mountains, beautiful girls, handsome naval officers, a brave Italian friend, explosions, underwater caves, sinkings, crash landings and ultimately a bruised and battered body.
All in a days work for NUMA's Major Dirk Pitt. Yes it is unbelievable, but hey in these times we all deserve a bit of escapism, and to be there right alongside Dirk as he battles yet another baddie, because we know he always wins.
