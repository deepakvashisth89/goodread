This is one of the few Gail Carson Levine books I hadn't read yet and I really enjoyed it!Although my favorite will always be her fantasy, I've loved both historical fiction I've read by her. This one was rich with history, characters, and relationships I was cheering on. I loved Dave's growth throughout the book and was especially touched by his forgiveness of his brother.Overall, I thought it was a great middle grade read for kids and adults.
