Boomer humor, rescued from the discard box at the library where I vacationed this week. I've enjoyed Barry's columns in the past, and this seemed like a breezy end-of-summer read, so I fished it out. It was indeed breezy and amusing, although his tactics for generating laughs become clear and predictable when you ingest more than 20 pages in a sitting. It's still funny, but less so when the mechanics are on display. The humor also deflects from true thought and feeling, except for one strikingly serious passage describing his mother's path after the death of her husband. Barry is a smart, entertaining writer, but there's not much depth here, and that's not his intent, anyway.Show more
