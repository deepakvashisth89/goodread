A real winnerA gripping blend if historical romance and suspense that kept me completely on edge from beginning to end. From the very first chapters, Patricia Potter creates an atmosphere thick with tension, danger, and emotional intensity, making it nearly impossible to put the book down. The pacing is excellent, fast enough to keep the adrenaline going, but balanced with meaningful character development that makes you truly cate about what happens. If you enjoy historical romance with a strong suspense element, The Black Knave is absolutely worth the read. It will keep you turning pages late into the night.
