Incredible book. Can’t believe more people haven’t read this gem. Perfect for anyone deconstructing any part of their life, upbringing, beliefs, faith, etc. A great book to read BEFORE you have a midlife crisis (ideally so you can avoid the crisis part).

🍆 3 BIG TAKEAWAYS:
1. The deconstruction happening in my life the past few years is normal. People will think I’m crazy. “You’ve changed.” This is normal and healthy. This is me becoming the real me.
2. What are the things in life I’m clinging to out of fear or fear of loneliness, and I need to let go of? Career, job, clients, friends, identities, beliefs, habits.
3. It’s super important to allow my spouse/friends the space (and encouragement) to find their real self.
