"We cannot afford to be sentimental, Mr Carnelian.""We cannot afford not to be, I think."The love story of Mrs Amelia Underwood, prim wife and missionary from 1896, and Jherek Carnelian, experimenter in the emotion of love from the decadent End of the World, continues.With epigraph--and title--from Ernest Dowson, whose fin de siècle poetry I was infatuated with in the first anthology of English verse I got my hands on.
