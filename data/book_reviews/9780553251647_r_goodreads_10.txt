I was well into the book before I realized who Peter Bogdanovich was. A Hollywood power broker, thoroughly marinated in his self worth. He has a beef with Hugh Hefner. Ms. Stratten beguiled many men. Many were obsessed with her and obsession is not healthy. He was not her direct killer, but his steadfast denial of his own culpability shows it was always more about him than her.His protestations of love are as hollow as he is.Unfortunately, she is not alone. Many other famous beauties have fallen prey to these delusional, obsessives: Jane Mansfield, Maralyn Monroe, Anna Nicole Smith. The list goes on and on.These young women are all of the things their abusers claim: beautiful, intelligent, enlightened ... but they are all also very young and those who wish to abuse them use this against them.
