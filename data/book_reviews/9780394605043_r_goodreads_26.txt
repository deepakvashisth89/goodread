This is the sort of book where I lingered and reread certain parts, the writing is subtle and clever. The characters are mostly awful except poor Portia Quayne, 16yo orphan sent to live with her older brother Thomas and his wife, Anna. Not a lot really happens in the novel, it’s more a character study about love, and home and definitely class and the superficial and cynical relationships in the upper classes. The ending is rather abrupt, it seemed to be building to a more powerful event but unfortunately I’m left to wonder what will happen to Portia.
