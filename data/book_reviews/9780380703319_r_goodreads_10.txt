The women of Lobelia Falls are, among other things, ardent conservationists and skilled archers. Unmarried Dittany Henbitt is visiting the local nature preserve when she finds a new town employee doing perk tests on the protected space, almost as shocking as finding the stodgy head of the water department skewered by an arrow. As Dittany tries to figure out what's going on, hindered by the local practice of demanding her help with a variety of problems, she finds that she suddenly has two suitors, and many, many suspicions,. A delightful introduction to an unusual cozy series.
