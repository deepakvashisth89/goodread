2.6 stars rounded up.The writing is not a clear as one would wish for an RPG rules supplement, but that is not the reason why I am dissappointed with this book. It has some cool things like wild magic and cooperative magic, however, the new spells and magical objects are not that interesing or innovative compared to what is already in the core rules. Additionally, some of the quests spells and magical objects require too much bookkeeping on the part of the DM, the kind that makes game sessions drag forever and impede action scenes. In summary, since I already bought it I am keeping it, but it's not my favorite of the AD&D 2nd Edition books.
