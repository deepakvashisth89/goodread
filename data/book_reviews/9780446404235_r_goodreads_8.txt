Mr. Kaminsky wrote books in four different series. Prior to reading this I had read and enjoyed books from each of the other three. This one was a big disappointment. Set in WWII era Hollywood the series seems to be about problems Tobey Peters solves for a variety of celebrities. I found the concept to be a bit too cute for my taste. In addition the story was choppy and improbable. How many times can one person walk into a potentially dangerous situation without taking even basic precautions. Being kidnapped three or four times over a period of just a few days was really stretching things. I don't think I will bother with any more of this particular series.
