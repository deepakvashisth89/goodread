A good anthology of Canadian fiction. I read three of the stories long ago (by Hopkinson, Doctorow and Ryman) and am excluding them from my comments. Of the ones I just read I especially enjoyed Eric Choi's 'Divisions,' an alt-history with a mature view of law, politics and the administration of science; and Karl Schroeder's 'Halo.' Of the more comic stories, Jean Pierre April's Rève Canadien (translated by Howard Scott) was the best.
