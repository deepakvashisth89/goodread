Coming of age story set in 1898 in Vermont. A 10 yo boy "renounces" God so he can do whatever he wants. He nearly gets a man hung because of his lies, but at the last minute does the right thing. And "rediscovers" his faith. Also of note, his older brother is a special needs child whom he learns much from.I enjoyed reading this book.
