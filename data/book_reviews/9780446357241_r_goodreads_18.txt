This sequel to Sleeping Dog has the same entertaining characters that represent the "Odd Couple" of private investigators. I loved the initial book and although the characters continue to sparkle in this book, the story itself isn't as strong.As a reader who is primarily interested in character development and sense of place, I would definitely seek out another book by Lochte because he excels in both. His scene at a debauched Playboy-type estate were well worth reading and his ingénue friend (and aspiring sleuth), Serendipity, is bright, brash and loads of fun.
