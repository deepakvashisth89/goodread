Oh god this is the worst book ever. The audio version read by the author is even more of a trainwreck. The characters are one-dimensional, the plot is full of holes, and the pacing is preposterous.This book does excel at one thing: being unintentionally funny through Carr's mindless use of cliches. However, this is only amusing for the first third, after which you just want to throw the discs out the car window and continue on your road trip in silence.Seriously, don't read this book.
