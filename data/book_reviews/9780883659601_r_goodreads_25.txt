This is nothing more than a book of quotes, and in my opinion, it is excellent. The quotes are little lines, all related to each other in a long chain of similar topics. The quotes are witty, and just knowing a few of them to spout at your friends when they do something related to it is entertaining. I only keep books that I really liked on my one bookshelf, and this has absolutely become one of them. Its like Mao Zedong's Little Red Book, but amusing and lighthearted. I would carry this with me everywhere, but it is rather large. However, it has to be long, considering that there are exactly 2,548 little humorous lines in there. The lines range from proverbs/sayings, to Greek humor, and to 20th century musicians.
