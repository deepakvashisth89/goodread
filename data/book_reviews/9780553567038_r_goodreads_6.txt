Fuggit, I'm straight up giving this book 5 stars. Here's why:1) A harder-edged YA Thriller/Horror than what I've been accustomed to, and that's a good thing 😜 Liberal uses of the word "BITCH" (gasp!), cringey teen sexuality, and OMG THAT ENDING (more on this later) 😱2) Can we please talk about the proliferation of awesome 80's/90's pop culture references in this book? Ohh the days of "The sultry sounds of Babyface" and Ace of Base emanating from a boombox on the beach, John Sencio as the MTV VJ on tv, and a super radical 80's vampire double feature playing at the local cinema, NEAR DARK and THE HUNGER. Dream date!! 😈Also a show of hands if you've ever experienced what our protag's brother has gone through: "There was a burning sensation in his belly, as thought he had one too many chimichangas at El Torito" 🤣🌯🌯🌯3) THE ENDING. Hands down unlike any other 80's/90's YA ending I've read, it literally made my jaw drop several times in it's balls-to-the-wall, no holds barred brutality, vengeance and violence. A wild freaking ride.
