All Fisherman are Liars by Linda GreenlawDry dock bar is located in Portland, Maine. This book is about tales from others and her own from this bar and others along the coast.We learn of her fishing career with Captain Alden and how over time they are BFF's. Life lessons are learned with nothing but open sea around you.Excuses are listed at the beginning of each chapter as the subject changes.Many trips and what happened are told and thoughts they did the best they could with what they had..
