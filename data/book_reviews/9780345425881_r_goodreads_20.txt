#1 in the Ben Reese series. This 1997 debut introduces a unique mystery series lead. Ben Reese is an archivist at a small Ohio university. He was a much injured, behind the lines soldier in Europe during WWII. This series entry takes place in 1960. There is a lot of backstory and a plethora of characters; this makes the story slow to get fully into. Tenacity is rewarded with an excellent novel. I anticipate enjoying the next in the series even more because the learning curve will be shortened.Ben Reese series - The death of a wise professor and good friend to Ben Reese in a small college town puzzles Reese. He digs until his own life in threatened before finding the truth.
