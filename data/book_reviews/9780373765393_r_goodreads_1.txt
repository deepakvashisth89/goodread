"Tempting the Tycoon" is the story of Rachel and Nate.Basically Rachel is a wedding planner who organizes her best friend's wedding. She ends up meeting the best man Nate, who cannot stop flirting with her.Heavily cautious due to her past, she does not have time or faith for relationships.. that is until Nate starts relentlessly pursuing her.The first 1/2 is about a sweet chase in which a charming and determined hero wooes a reluctant heroine. The story somewhat dwindles after they get together with her issues intruding at the worst moments. I give kudos to the hero for sticking with her.That being said, sweet ending.Average read.2.5/5
