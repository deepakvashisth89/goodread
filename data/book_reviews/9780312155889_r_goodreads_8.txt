Meg Eliot’s life is turned upside down when her husband dies. In addition to coping with the loss, her financial situation is precarious and she has to turn from a life of voluntary work to paid employment. In the process she falls out with her closest friends but her relationship with her younger brother, who is gay and has also lost a loved one, becomes stronger. The two of them help each other come to an understanding of their roles in life.This is a very introspective book, at times not the easiest read. But it doesn’t pull its punches. It’s a honest portrayal of how two people, especially Meg, come to terms with what must be done to live a fulfilling life, as far as that is possible.
