I listened to the audiobook version of this very dark and twisted tale, which Tom Baker narrates himself with gruesome relish. Being of the age to fondly remember Baker's post-Doctor Who stints on British children's television with The Book Tower and Jackanory meant that any semblance of objectivity flew out of the window as soon as he began reading.To summarise: I thoroughly enjoyed this.The story itself would have been a solid four stars, but I do feel that Baker's narration of the audiobook edition certainly deserves that extra star.
