This book helped me in truly understanding :''that you are what you think''. That thought is energy swelling up from the absolute field of consciousness is something that the mind can conceive and mind eye can see. If you can develop power over thought emerging from the depths of yourself than you can have control on what your mind entertains and what you want to manifest in your life. The tone of the book can be somewhat patronizing but in the end you will certainly enjoy the read.
