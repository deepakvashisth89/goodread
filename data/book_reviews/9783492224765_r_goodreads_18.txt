This was a great page turner. There were times when I felt like the flashbacks got in the way, a bit. But it was mostly very well written and revealed just enough to keep me wanting to find out what happened next. I liked the ending, as well.The premise is that normal business exec goes to work one day and his boss tries to kill him. He then finds out that pretty much everyone else he knows wants him dead, as well.I originally was going to give it five stars, which I do not do very often. I would give it 4.5, if I could. Very good escapist material.
