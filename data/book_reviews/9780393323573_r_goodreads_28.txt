Servants of the Map is a collection of short stories bookended by two novellas, all of which are suspenseful and fascinating. The settings range from the Himalayas to the Adirondacks. Although they're considered loosely connected, for a couple I struggled to find it. In other cases, it was rather thrilling to come upon a descendent of the main character from an earlier story, to follow up on that family's adventures. Andrea Barrett often uses letters written by her characters to describe their thoughts and feelings, and she does so quite effectively in this book. I've read a few of Andrea Barrett's novels now, and liked them all very much, especially Ship Fever. I haven't yet read The Voyage of the Narwhal because it just seemed too freezing cold. In Barrett's afterword she tells of linking all three together, and I noted references to the Narwhal novel in this book.
