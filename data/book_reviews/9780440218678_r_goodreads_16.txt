This isn’t horror, it’s horrible.It’s hard not to compare this novel to Stephen King’s ‘Salem’s Lot’ and find jt inferior in nearly every way.Only the language, the clear imagery and regional dialogue hold any worth in reading this novel.Which is a shame, as it starts with so much promise, taking the time to set up a whole host of intriguing characters and storylines... only to discard it all in callously poor storytelling.And then the ending arrives... and it’s worse than all that came before, taking the last shred of hope that there was anything to salvage from the experience and trashing it.
