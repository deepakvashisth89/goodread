The second book I picked up secondhand in Japan. I am a sucker for anti-heros and while the Othello references were a little on the nose, Vicent lived up to his reputation as the Iago of Hollywood.Vicent's passion for making good films throughout the novel and his attention to production detail added a sense of taste to the sleazy underworld figures you would expect in a book about a mobster moving to Malibu.The book ended too suddenly in a tragic fashion not quite deserved. Like all mobsters, only the FBI knocking on the door finally brought the movie studio mogel down.I was so engrossed I didn't notice the cover falling off. Read in one sitting!
