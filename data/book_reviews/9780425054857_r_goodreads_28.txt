This is a wonderful conclusion to the whole series.
I really liked the evolution between the books, starting from Book 1, clearly a children's book, with importance of magic and animals talking, to this final volume where there's a lot about society and political models. WWII is important in the background.
Animals are still present, like a loop with book 1, mostly with ants and geese.
This volume was actually published posthumously in 1977.
