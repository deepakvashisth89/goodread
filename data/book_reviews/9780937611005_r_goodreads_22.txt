“Heal Your Body: The Mental Causes for Physical Illness and the Metaphysical Way to Overcome Them,” by Louise Hay, is a concise self-help tool.When I received my copy of “Heal Your Body,” the small size of the book and my quick flip took me aback. However, the proverb about not judging a book is fitting here because when I sat down to read it, I was happy with the content.“Heal Your Body” discusses how we talk to ourselves and what we tell ourselves is important, but how important? If you are new to self-talk concepts, this book would be a helpful addition to larger research. If you are familiar with self-talk and positive affirmations, then this book is a great tool. Its compact size makes it easy to carry, and Louise has filled it with affirmations related to many alignments.“Heal Your Body: The Mental Causes for Physical Illness and the Metaphysical Way to Overcome Them,” by Louise Hay, is a self-help tool for personal development and growth.
