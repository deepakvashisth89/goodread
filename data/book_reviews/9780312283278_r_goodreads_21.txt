Tom Perrotta's novel Joe College is an interesting examination of how adolescents and young adults enact different social roles. Danny is the son of a blue collar lunch truck driver from New Jersey. Danny also happens to attend Yale where he works in a university dining hall. These two environments help to show the contrast in the world of Yale. These three environments are what set us up to understand the differences in the culture of Danny's home in New Jersey, the people who work in the dining hall, and Danny's friends who are students.In this novel, everyone is trying to negotiate the boundaries of their worlds. Danny is struggling with the belief that he doesn't belong at Yale because he didn't attend a fancy prep school, and his parents aren't wealthy republicans. Meanwhile, his friends are also struggling to deal with their new place in the world.
