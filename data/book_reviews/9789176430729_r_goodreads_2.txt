Wonderfully subtle and lowkey, this strange novel develops to a psychological firework that requires slowreading first and then the same kind of slow thinking afterwards.Things that happen by the water have a blurry surface and a cold, deep, dark secret...Recommended to all of you who suffer from prolonged reader's block. This novel will let you move at your pace, will coax you when you feel tired, and will lead you to the last page with a sense of happy grief or sad fulfilling.My first Kerstin Ekman won't stay the last!
