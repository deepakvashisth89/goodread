On the recommendation of Anne, Toby’s ex-wife, he is hired by Howard Hughes to look into the guests he had over for dinner a few days ago. Hughes is certain that one of them went through the blueprints for airplane designs on his desk in his study. Hughes is already rude, unsocial and a germaphobe.

Toby interviews the guests one by one. They include Basil Rathbone and Bugsy Siegel amongst others. He gets lucky with the wife of one the guests. He is attacked several times which result in several head injuries.

There are Japanese and German spies. At one point he meets Bertolt Brecht.

He wraps up the case and makes it home to hear the news report of the attack of Pearl Harbor.

He is an unhealthy eater: hot dogs, burgers and shredded wheat or cornflakes. When he dreams it is of his life in Cincinnati, a place he has never been to. Guenther, the dwarf from an earlier case, has become a permanent fixture.
