Elizabeth Conway is a passionate astronomer and the eldest of a number of sisters she cares for. One day, the heir to their estate, Lord Clanross, comes back to take his inheritance.Wow, this was so unique. The writing is incredibly clever. I loved Eizabeth so very much and also Miss Bluestone, the girls’ governess who’s a passionate botanist of her own and Bevis, a young, dashing suitor who’s been courting Elizabeth forever.This book is about Elizabeth having to make the right choice to preserve her passion and talent for astronomy. I think the writing really elevated it so much - the dialog especially is sparkling and witty while being beautifully economical - there’s not a word out of place here. Really good, it wasn’t funny ha ha hence the four stars but I highly recommend it.
