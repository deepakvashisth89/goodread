I have read this book a gazillion times over the years, and I never get tired of it. I like the fast pace, the horses, the circus, the mystery (even though that part is admittedly not all that mysterious), the horses, the relationship between Vanessa and her husband, the way young Timothy becomes more of who he is supposed to be, and the horses.

The chase across the top of the castle never fails to leave me breathless, but I must admit that I can never picture things like 'the northwest corner' or 'the southern turret'. Left, right, forwards, backwards.....these words make sense to me, but compass directions in such situations leave me lost and confused.

But oh, those horses!
