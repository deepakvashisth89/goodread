There is some overlap among Pond Life and other Golden Guides, but there is still plenty in this series entry to make it worthwhile. Golden Guides were written primarily for students and hobbyists, and some of them are now showing their age, but I’ve been having a good time revisiting some of them recently. Not only do they provide a good science/nature refresher course, they take me back to my grade school and junior high days, when I had quite a collection of these little books.
