3.5 stars.
Continuing finishing these country/continent area-centric vegan cookbooks from this author, this one, like others has a number of recipes (120+) with introduction to each recipe groups, no photos, and a list of storecupboard ingredients at the start.

I found many good recipes in this book, though no doubt if I could eat nuts, I would be able to use many more (including in the desserts). The author prefers her carrots scraped, not peeled. For pizza dough, plain flour is used, no mention of the 00 flour. I think at the time of its release (2001), this book was one of the few vegan ones, but it’s still a nice adding, though short.


Mushroom with sun-dried tomatoes, Tomato vegetable and rice soup, Borlotti bean and lentil soup, Pea and mushroom risotto, Vegetable and chickpea stew with soft polenta, Broccoli and mushroom Florentine, Savoury rice-filled cabbage leave with tomato sauce, Spaghetti with fried courgettes, Pasta and vegetable Romana, Broccoli with garlic and lemon dressing, Potato and spinach gnocchi, Cauliflower in mushroom sauce, Green lentil pasta and pepper salad, Onion and sage focaccia, Sun-dried tomato and oregano rolls, Pane cioccolata.
