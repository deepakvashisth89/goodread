Well.....,
The action part of the book actually got me hooked for a fair bit: different characters with their individual storylines all gradually converging.
A one-dimensional super-villain out to "rule the world", a bit of waiting to see who was going to side with him or at least be indifferent as opposed to actually opposing.
Other than that, interesting ideas about the separate cultures, the legend of the sentient kelp and the hybtanks in orbit, but no real conclusion to any of that. Presumably it is in book 3 (or 4 depending on when you believe the series started) Ascension Factor - but I don't own it and doubt I will bother looking for it. Seems like Frank Herbert wasn't involved in it and there are poor reviews regarding it.
