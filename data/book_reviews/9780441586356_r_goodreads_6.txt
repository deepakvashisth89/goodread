Pleasantly entertaining adventures, especially for youth. The Asimovs take you to interesting worlds -- a mud world where musical slugs dominate everything, a water world inhabited by flying-and-swimming squid-like beings, the inside of a forming planet, and more. The plots are okay, but in one of the stories, the princess was rescued by a never-justified bit of alien working (i.e., magic), and in the other, a murderous plant had a change of heart so complete as to beggar belief.
