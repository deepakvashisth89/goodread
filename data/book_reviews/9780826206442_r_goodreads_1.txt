Henry Shaw lived a privileged life that grew from his ability to accomplish the task set before him. In immigrating from England he was simply finishing the work his father started. His practicality, fastidiousness, and kindness, however, blossomed into a legacy that endures to this day. While not without unpleasant details and flaws, Mr. Shaw by and large nurtured plans, plants, and people to our great pleasure today.Note: As a Tower Grove resident in the neighborhood (and on the street) named after this man, I’m biased. I romanticize my city and community frequently. Plus, I find myself at a unique point in my personal and professional life that I found this biography inspiring.
