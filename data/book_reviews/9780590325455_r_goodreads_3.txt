Students end up stuck at the old Graydon Place, and it’s quite obviously haunted. To help the ghost, our heroine must resolve her story, and sort out her own feelings about her future.
It’s hard to imagine how long ago I read this! School book fair, I was 10, and if anyone wondered, the mystery bug never left. I remember having to look through the publisher’s list in a huge binder at the special order desk at WH Smith for the rest of the Windswept titles later on.
