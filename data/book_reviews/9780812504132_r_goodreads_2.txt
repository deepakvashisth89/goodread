Five years after getting kicked out of the CIA, Kirk McGarvey is pulled back into the spy business in secret when an outsider with inside knowledge and McGarvey's skill set is needed to help uncover a spy with influence in the highest echelons of the US government.Took me a bit to get into this book, but I soon found myself quite thoroughly hooked. People who like their spy thrillers to be all action and little else might get bored, but I rather prefer mine to offer extra helpings of intrigue, betrayals and doublecrosses instead - and on that front, this one delivered in full. I expect this series will be keeping me busy for quite some time...
