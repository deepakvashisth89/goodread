Dawn was my favorite sitter as a kid, and I know now that I was definitely in the minority about that. (And truthfully, I see her in a different light now that I’m an adult.) But, due to her being my favorite, I re-read this book obsessively growing up.Unfortunately, this wasn’t as good as I remembered. I LOVE the description of Sunny and her hippie parents, and I loved the fire chapter, but overall it was a lot more lackluster than I remember it being.Also, why on earth did she get a “B” for presentation? I’m pretty sure that’s the lowest grade any of the girls got on this “assignment”. Maybe California schools grade harder than Connecticut?
