Love, Sara by Mary Beth Lundgren is a book that at first I didn't think I was going to like. The first 100 pages are very slow and boring. Although once you get past it, you start wanting to know what happens next. This is a good book, but I wish that the pace was a little faster. This book at times can be a little tough to process all the information given at once. Love, Sara although wouldn't be my first choice it was a good read. I would recommend this book to teenage girls.
