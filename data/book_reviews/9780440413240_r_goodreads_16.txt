A very sweet story of two dogs, Luath and Bodger, and a Siamese cat, Tao, who are making their way home over the rough terrain of Canada. They have some interesting adventures along the way.

This is primarily a children’s book, but the author does not talk down to her audience, so I enjoyed it. I liked that she handled it without trying to get inside the minds of the animals and anthropomorphize them.
