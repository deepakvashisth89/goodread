Rereading this one because it’s been years and it made such a huge impression on me then! Let’s see if it’s still as brilliant as I remember 😁********This book actually gave me my very first (and only) experience with existential dread. Maybe I was just in a weird mood that day. I don’t think I can get too specific about it without spoilers, so I won’t.I’m big on novelty, so anything new is always going to score major points with me. I’ve never read anything like this, and the ideas presented were fascinating. I thought the story structure was unusual, too.I was surprised by how neutral the whole book stayed regarding its own content. Humanity transforms irrevocably and there’s no underlying vibe that it’s a good or a bad thing. It’s just a thing that happened. This is so rare in fiction. It did make the book seem a bit impersonal to me though.The ending was vaguely sad and left me disturbed, which isn’t necessarily a bad thing. I’m more impressed by it now than I was the first time around.
