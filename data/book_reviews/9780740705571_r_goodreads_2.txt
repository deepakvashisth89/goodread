I have been reading Baby Blues for years and recently decided to re-read all the books of the collection, and I have been so enjoying it! This book is one of the earlier in the series. In it, the children are much younger and some of the drawing is still evolving. But it is not dated at all, and the laughs begin on page one and continue throughout! Among the funnier moments are when Zoe brings home the class hamster and when Daryl coaches her pee-wee soccer team. Any fan of Baby Blues will love this book and if you started following the strip years after it began this book can give you some insightful background!
