This, the second adventure of "Mongo the Magnificent"--private eye, doctor of criminology, karate black belt, former circus acrobat, and dwarf extraordinaire--is better than the first in the series.In this novel, written in 1977, Mongo tangles with the SAVAK, Iran's secret police, who are trying to suppress the coming revolution. The plot is exciting, but the problem is that the revolution Chesbro assumes is coming is of the democratic socialist variety, and he makes very few mentions of Islam and says nothing whatever of Islamic fundamentalism. I'm sure that back in '77, many people made the same mistake he does, but still it makes this otherwise enjoyable detective/spy novel appear slightly quaint and wrongheaded.
