An American artist who painted around the turn of the 20th century, was especially drawn to the Northern shore of Lake Superior. In that area, he meets the best friends of his life but never fully is able to tap into the richness of those connections. His paintings also seem to be underpaintings of his true expression even though he becomes a successful artist who sells his work in NYC. Close friends who fought in WW ! were scarred by that and come to a sad demise. This book won a Canadian literary award but I found it to be slow, plodding, and depressing.
