This is a TOTALLY excellent book. Even if you, like I, have never worn or been interested in perfume, you will find this book a delightful introduction to chemistry, told through the lens of the protaganist's obsession with and amazing facility for the composition of perfumes.Although it will never happen in this lifetime, this book came THIS CLOSE to making me want to study chemistry. (An effect it shares with Alan Bradley's FLAVIA DE LUCE mysteryies for young adults, starting with THE SWEETNESS AT THE BOTTOM OF THE PIE. But I digress.)The author has chronicled the story of a young man who always had a knack for analyzing the structure of perfume. This young man pursued that interest right into the secret labs where France's most famous (and most secret) perfumes are constructed. The blend of chemistry, mystery, politics, and business practices in this story is unique and irresistable. I highly recommend it!
