4/5

For what this book is, I thought it a solid, quick read. It does not work as a thorough biography on Woodrow Wilson, nor does it claim to be. What it is, rather, is an overview of the man’s life and presidency. I thought there were some odd omissions such as any discussion of the passage of the 19th Amendment or, shall we say, Woodrow’s beyond questionable taste in films that he showed at the White House. All in all, it was a good introduction to the Woodrow Wilson and I would recommend it.
