Aside from hair raising scraps from Jamaica Kincaid and Bessie Head and hilarious cynicism from Margaret Atwood this is a collection of gnomic pebbles from a distant clogged beach. I am a short story fan but these very short stories are jokes without punchlines and strange directions without a map. There’s a reason songs are three minutes long. The shortest song to get to number one was Stay by Maurice Williams and the Zodiacs – 1:38.
