I liked this book. It ended up being longer than I thought it would be.We start the story out with two sisters trying to infiltrate high society in hopes of locating their mother whom abandoned them when they were smaller. It ends up taking them from Boston to California. We see a lot of lies and stories in between. Both sisters end up falling in love. Some of the stuff that happened kept me shaking my head at it but it was well written and very descriptive.I received this book from eBook Discovery. I voluntarily reviewed this book. This is my honest review.
