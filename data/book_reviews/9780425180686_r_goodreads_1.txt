After I finished reading the Bible (on my accelerated three year plan by doubling up my daily readings), I wanted to jump start my brutal endeavor of reading the "Oxford Dictionary" in a similar fashion. I was only 1/3 of the way through at the time, so I calculated if I double my reading from one page a day to two pages a day, I would complete it by year end. I have dotted all the words I don't know, never heard of, lack confidence in using, or the tricky ones I know in context but have a hard time explaining the precise definition. Now, when I have to look up a word while reading, I will know if I need to focus on the word a little harder. A great sense of accomplishment, but not as rewarding as finishing the Bible or "Atlas Shrugged" as I did early this year. I am happy it is done. Yeah!
