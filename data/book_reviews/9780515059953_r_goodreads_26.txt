A decent mystery, quite well-supplied with appropriate levels of fair clues and false trails, and some rather good descriptive passages capturing Westland in New Zealand, and some nice local flavour cameos - I quite liked Bert, the chauffeur, with his unmistakeable (and unending) flow of kiwiisms - right on the nose, and just the right side of caricature. Also, the NZ detective inspector and his 'statusquow'.But on the whole, I think for future silly holiday reading, I'll be going back to Georgette Heyer. Silly throwawayness in romance is far more satisfying than silly throwawayness in crime, at least for me. Probably because romanceisreally just that silly. Or alternatively, maybe crime novels would be just as awesome if there were more cravats involved.
