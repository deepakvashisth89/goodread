Long and drawn out

I am not much of a fan of this one at all.
As a Black woman reading this story it was impossible to accept seeing African Americans called "the black" as if we're not human beings. I could not ignore the racism and stereotypical rhetoric used throughout the book.

I will say that I appreciated that Ashton treated Black people with respect and had earned great loyalty and respect from them without using fear, threats, or pain in any way

A lot of things don't make sense in the plot. People's reasons for doing things or allowing things to happen are completely ridiculous.

The ending was terrible and left me feeling unfulfilled after already suffering through the racism aspect.

I'm not sure if I'll read any more K.W. books after this. I'm on the fence about that. I know when it was written there wasn't a lot of care for racial sensitivity and maybe the author didn't mean any disrespect but instead tried to write in a way that would reflect how we're taught to look at that time in history.

All I can say for sure is I wouldn't recommend this book to anyone. It wasn't good and it's insulting in a lot of ways.
