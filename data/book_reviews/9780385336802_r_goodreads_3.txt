Blue Poppiesis the story of two unlikely lovers who get caught in the turmoil of China’s invasion of Tibet in 1950. Puton is a young widow who came from Lhasa to a very remote town with her tax collector husband, stayed after he died, and struggles to get along in Jyeko when all the other townspeople think she’s bad luck.
