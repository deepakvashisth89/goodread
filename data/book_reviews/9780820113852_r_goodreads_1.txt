This "book" was originally a series of letters written over the course of 5 months in 1906 during an extended vacation in Egypt, Italy, and France. The letters were meant to be passed among family and friends. There was such an interest in them that L. Frank had a small number of fancy, leather bound copies made so that each member of the family circle might have his/her own copy. The letters were not intended to be read one after another as a book.
It is an interesting travelog if you read 1 or 2 letters at a sitting. I would not recommend trying to read it as a normal book.
