I also read “The Siege of Isfahan (Sauver Ispahan)” by Jean-Christophe Rufin. It is a sequel to “The Abyssinian” which launched the literary career of this “doctor without borders” who became a member of the Académie Française. The book offers a discovery of 18th century Persia as seen by Jean-Baptise and Alix, a French couple who retired in Isfahan after their Ethiopian adventures. I liked the book almost as much as the “The Abyssinian”. The French critique is very positive, the English reception was more mixed.To read more:http://www.travelreadings.org/2015/11...
