I’ve begun picking up books I demolished when I was young (i.e., read till the covers came off, the binding broke, and I had to create several tape-bound parts). This is one of those, and if all you need is to see song titles to feel good (which I did for years), this will give you plenty of that. A good half of it is just lists with no commentary (e.g., “10 Songs About Cars”), which isn’t all that gripping, especially in the age of the Internet. The rest are fun lists of greats and awfuls in and associated with rock music through 1980, with the most opinionated and weirdest the most fun. I suggest you skim—you’ll find something! Do not read too closely or you’ll see how terribly it’s edited.
