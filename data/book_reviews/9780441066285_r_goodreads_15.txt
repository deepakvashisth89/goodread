I knew even before opening the book that it would be four stars, so I am likely very prejudiced by enjoyment and not being very objective.In this particular novel, Kaminsky’s work as a professor of film studies comes through very strongly as the setting for the novel is an international film festival in Moscow. This background really works for the novel and I think that Kaminsky does a great job with it.The pacing in the novel was spot-on and the writing is very well done. The novel, which on the surface is just a little mystery thriller, is actually a bit more significant when read as a film theory. The fact that I enjoyed this and picked up on a lot of this speaks to how skillfully this was all done! I definitely recommend this to readers and I do intend to read more in Kaminsky’s series. Also, there is a pet cat in the novel.
