This book is completely underrated and often goes unmentioned when reading lots of best Star Wars books.Though it's a slow burn until about the last 100, pages, this book has it all. Luke coming to terms with Anakin's death, Han and Leia still coming to terms with their relationship as Leia reconciles her heritage with herself, a pre-Mara Jade love interest, a crumbling Empire still unaware of the Emperor's death.I am so happy I picked this book up on a whim.
