What people don't absolutely love about this book is that, if you don't already know the tunes of these songs, the book won't teach them to you. Suggested guitar or autoharp chords are given (in autoharp notation style), but actual melodies, in musical notation, appear only in the "Rounds" chapter. This was due to copyright laws and to the fact that in 1988 you could always find all the recordings of each song--most songs were recorded by more than one artist.If you don't know most of the tunes to these songs, chances are you're not a U.S. baby-boomer.
