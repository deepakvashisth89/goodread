This book came a little too late for me in life, but my goodness id like to go back and give my younger self a copy and tell her to pay attention instead of learning the hard way.Although I feel if youre looking for a *how to heal* this isnt it…..The book more explains your typology through the trauma and youre left to identify yourself in the categories…Still, very useful tool.This is a must read if youre the daughter of an alcoholic. So many things are throughly explained, correlations are made.the book did focus on different ages when you become aware of the chaos, or maybe when the addiction started and changed you, but it didnt really give a ton of insight when youre *born* into it. Maybe at the end of the day that doesn’t matter, but I believe it does…We know no different…So I would like to hear more insight on that.
