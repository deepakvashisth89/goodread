This book was a roller coaster. There are several places where you think the penultimate climax – the one where Bourne and The Jackal finally have it out – only to have the book continue further. There are plot twists and hopes dashed by disappointments along the way, but it is the ultimate Bourne book. (Although, unlike many other Ludlum fans, I don’t think the Bourne books are his best works, but that’s not to say I disliked any of the original Bourne trilogy.)I could have done with a little less of the hold-onto-David-Webb and make-Jason-Bourne-go-away stuff throughout the book; it does get a bit tiresome, but it’s also Marie that makes Bourne into Webb, and defending Marie and his kids is what makes Webb turn back to Bourne.
