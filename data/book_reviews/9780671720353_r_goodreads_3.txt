This is the first volume of a long-running anthology series that presents stories based on Larry Niven's Kzinti, a race of war-prone felines, and set, of course, in Niven's universe of Known Space. Think Klingons except with panthers or lions. This first volume is actually a reprint anthology. It includes Niven's first Kzinti story, The Warriors, which was first printed in the February 1966 issue of If magazine, edited by Frederik Pohl. The next story is a short novel by Poul Anderson, Iron, which was serialized in Baen paperback magazines in 1986 and 1987; the last issue of Far Frontiers and the first issue of New Destinies, which led to considerable confusion for bibliographers. The Anderson story has the best hard-sf content but struck me as the least similar to Niven's work in style. The last story is another novella, Cathouse by Dean Ing, which also first appeared in Baen's New Destinies. It's the funniest of the lot. They're all good military-style adventures, well worth a look for Niven's fans. The Stephen Hickman cover presents the Kzinti as looking much more like Tony the Tiger than I'd pictured them.
