A Children's Book Gone WrongEloise is a six-year old girl living at The Plaza, a very swanky hotel in New York. Eloise engages in all kinds of obnoxious and selfish behavior that would be unbecoming for even a four-year old. The only funny thing about this book was the nanny who had to have not one, not two, but three adult beverages after dealing with Eloise all day.
