The series is good. It alternates from present day archaeology to fictional representation/explanation of the various situations. It follows different pre-historic First Americans from 15000 years ago/Ice Age on. The current book presents a story of the Mississippian era where corn was king and drought the big killer (circa AD 700 to 1500). The stories ring true as pictures of time, place, beliefs and culture. You can identify with the characters as the struggle to survive continues.
