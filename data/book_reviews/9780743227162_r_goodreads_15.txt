I enjoyed these stories very much. The stories are all very different but all feature characters who you keep in mind for a while. Two memorable ones for me were "Stolen Chocolates" and "Lower Crossing." In the former a woman dreams of her once handsome high school crush, who is now obese, and dreams of enjoying pastries and cakes with him, items she has denied herself in order to stay thin. In "Lower Crossing" we meet an 84 year old women friend who says, out of the blue, "I am a great masturbator," and later. "But no one talks about it," Gloria said.
