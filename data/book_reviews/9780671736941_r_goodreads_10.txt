The Prodigal Daughter is undoubtedly very well written. But the reader must note that the story is but a continuation of his very popular Kane and Abel. So about half of the book dwells with the early life of Florentyna daughter of Abel and Richard Kane's son and also kind of retells the story of Kane and Abel. So overall it helps to fill in the gaps of the firs volume and the second half starts off from where Kane and Abel ended.I didnt like the ending though. It was rather sudden.
