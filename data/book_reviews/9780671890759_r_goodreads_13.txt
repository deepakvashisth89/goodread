I remember very little of this book but I came across the cover and vividly remember how much I loved it as a young kid. Bill Wallace was the first author whose work I really truly loved that I wasn't eased into by a parent.I remember feeling like this was a great mystery book. There were all these characters who came in and out all the time, and they had big personalities. I was especially fond of the Irish Setter?Maybe I will reread these books sometime soon
