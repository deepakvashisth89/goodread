Pendleton is always trying to find a different approach to his shoot-them-up formula for the Executioner series. His hero, Mack Bolan, was a sniper in Vietnam who came home to discover that his family had been destroyed by the Mafia and he decides to get even by violently eliminating the entire criminal organization. He's been at it for a lot of books now and Pendleton works hard to keep it from becoming too rote. In St. Louis, he is trying to kill off the young Turks in the organization to allow less ambitious and violent old men to keep the area from getting more robust. It's an effort on Pendleton's part to show Bolan beginning to realize that despite having easily killed a couple of thousand mobsters since the beginning of the series, the situation is not getting any better on a national scale. It's enjoyable.
