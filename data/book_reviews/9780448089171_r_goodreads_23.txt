Frank and Joe run into a most intriguing fellow, a diver, Mr. Perry, who tells them of such exciting adventures. When a mishap with the Hardy's boat causes Perry to tumble overboard they take him home to get a fresh suit and a bit of lunch. Unbeknownst to the boys, their father has some important papers in the suit's jacket and Mr. Perry is called away before the boys can reconnect with him. Chasing after the fellow in a time-crunched effort to get their father's papers, Frank and Joe experience every obstacle known to man. Finally, successful in their endeavor, they decide to stay and watch Mr. Perry's latest job. Invited to join in diving themselves they help Mr. Perry and the owners of the salvage company with raising a luxury yacht and with searching for treasure on an old ocean liner. The fellows that caused the mishap with the Hardy's boat are working for the competition, a less-than-honorable salvager with questionable business dealings. The two thugs dog Frank and Joe's steps the entire way and try to bungle and hurt them at every step. Still, the boys trick and maneuver their way into uncovering the unscrupulous man's behavior and the liner's secrets.
