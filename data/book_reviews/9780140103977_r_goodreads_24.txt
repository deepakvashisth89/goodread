Natalie is the sort of woman that other women either envy (she’s very pretty and men are attracted to her) or want to wise up because, in Natalie’s world, feminism happened to other people.Heart of the Country, by Fay Weldon, opens on the day Natalie’s husband leaves her for a younger woman. Harry left behind a bankrupt company and too many bills for unemployed Natalie to handle. Within days, her comfortable, boring life falls apart. On its own, this wasn’t enough to tempt me into reading this book.
