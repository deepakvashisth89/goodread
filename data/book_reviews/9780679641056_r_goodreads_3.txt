I read some of the tales and letters in this collection, including Bartleby the Scrivener, which I'd never read before. I do, however, own the movie adaptation starring Crispin Glover. Which meant that I pictured him in the role of Bartleby. It didn't diminish the story.I like reading Melville because his prose forces me to relax and just go wherever it is going, like taking a mental carriage ride. It's slow (the reading requires more attention than usual) but interesting. And often funny.
