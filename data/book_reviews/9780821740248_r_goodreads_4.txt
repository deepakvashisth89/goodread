Romance has never been my top genre. Historical Romance then would be worse. I decided to take a chance with his one because the cover was enticing(silly me). It was a frustrating read to be honest. I was very glad when I finished it yesterday. It was worth celebrating.There are so many issues I have with this book to be honest. The major being that it was just too long. Unnecessarily long. According to someone, it was oddly paced and I fully agree with that statement. The pace of this novel was not really normal. It could have done pretty alright without alot of characters. I may even go as far as saying it would have alright without some chapters. It was all just too much and too long (yes I will continue to state that fact). I would have given it a star but there were moments I just could not help and some twists I appreciated.
