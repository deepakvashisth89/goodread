I loved this book. I read it a few years ago and gave it to my teenage son who liked it (but not as much as I did), then I reread it recently and still loved it. Just writing this review makes me want to go read it again. (It's a short book, wouldn't take long.)I love all her books. She has such compassion for her characters and she tells their stories with a dignified sort of gravity. The people and situations and individual lines of text resonate with me long after I'm done reading them.Nick's character, and the gap between how he tells his story and how the reader reads it, particularly moved me. If you liked The Curious Incident of the Dog in the Nighttime, you'll probably like this, too.
