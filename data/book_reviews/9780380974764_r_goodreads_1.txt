I read this book years ago after it had sat on my shelf for many many years. I received it as a gift when I was in early high school from someone who told me that I would definitely enjoy it. When I did get around to reading it I did find it enjoyable but it had to work really hard to get my attention. It is one of those science-fiction books that has a different name for everything and bizzare character and place names. That sort of thing grates on my nerves pretty quick. Once I realized that this was basically another retelling of Hamlet I got a little more interested just because I was curious to see how it would play out. I don't remember much else other than to say that I didn't dislike the book. However, I am unlikely to give it a second read.
