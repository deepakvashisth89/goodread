Mme. D'Auvray, a wealthy widow, has a maid and a chauffeur and then adds a young companion, Celie Harland, to her household. During her vacation at the Villa Rose, Mme. D'Auvray is murdered, and Inspector Hanaud comes from Paris to investigate the crime. Suspects include her maid and her companion. The detective, M. Hanaud, is a compelling character. The reader is allowed to see everything that the detective sees but must interpret the clues alone, as the detective does not explain his methods until after the crime has been investigated. Instructive narrative about human nature and also entertaining.
