Another book about Amelia Bedelia, but this time she brought her family. The Rodgers family wants to throw Amelia a party, and want to invite her family. She begins to tell them all about her family, tat just so happens to be as quirky as her.Amelia Bedelia is a fun and quirky character. Her exceptionality is that she does the literal of everything that is said or asked of her. She shows readers that even though you may be different than people, that does not mean you should be treated any different.
