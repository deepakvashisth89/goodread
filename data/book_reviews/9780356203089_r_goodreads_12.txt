This book could stand alone reasonably well, but the ending may be a bit unsatisfying without reading book 1.

This book is a prequel to book 1. It's nice to have the backstory, and I enjoyed it better than the first. But knowing how it ended, due to book 1, kind of soured the end for me. That makes it kind of hard to decide which to read first! Ultimately not good enough on it's own to keep.
