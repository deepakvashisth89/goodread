I have been a fan of Steve's music for many years and have seen him perform live twice, and this was the second collection of his stories that I've read. He is a hell of a storyteller, which almost goes without saying for a successful cou try music star, but he is an accomplished wordsmith too. These are stories about underdogs, down-and-outers, junkies, killers, and folks you just want to pull for that they finally get a damned break. If you could cross Jonny Cash with Bill Burrroughs, you might get a Steve Earle.
