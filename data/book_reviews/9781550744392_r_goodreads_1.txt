Electric Gadgets and Gizmos is a non-fiction book about all the gadgets that can be easily be built. This book is a how to build where it shows how to build one of the gadgets step by step with pictures and words that describe what the picture is showing. One of the gadgets you can learn how to build is hand fan that uses a battery, and used toilet paper roll, some electrical wire, and popsicle sticks. This one example of the several gadgets you can build.This book does a great job of picking gadgets you can build very easily. The pictures used are also well labeled and how the words describe them, makes clear sense. There is also numbers for each step so you do not get lost on the page when you're building a gadget.I would use this book in my classroom, because some students have a building mind and some would really enjoy trying to build some of the gadgets at home. There are gadgets that all ages could build and I think kids would really love this book.
