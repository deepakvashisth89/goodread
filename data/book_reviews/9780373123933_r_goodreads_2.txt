I liked this much better than the first book about Cesare and Megan.

Gio and Teresa were fabulous characters and their story was so real and authentic that I was engaged from the very beginning.

Gio is a widower with a little boy and he and Teresa have a night of passion which shocks Gio because he,is been celibate since his wife Lucia died two years earlier. Teresa is so compassionate (after her initial misunderstanding), about Gio’s situation and she behaves in such an adult way - I loved her.

This is a story of second chances , overcoming grief and guilt, and love conquering all. The only thing stopping 5 stars being awarded in the incredibly sudden denouement and no epilogue.

If ever a story deserved a better ending - this one did.
