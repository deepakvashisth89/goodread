One of those books that started as a strong 5 stars, slowly dropped down to 4, and ended at a 3.The main problem is that, the longer the books goes on, the more you realize how repetitive it is. The Buddha meets a doubter, convinces him of the truth of Buddhism, and then the person becomes a monk/nun. Repeat this for about six hundred pages and you will have a good idea of what to expect here.One good thing, though, is that Hanh is really good at speaking through the Buddha. Whenever the Buddha gives a teaching, he does it in a way that is easy for the reader to quickly grasp.
