I stumbled upon this book and was wowed by the information presented here. This is quite an old book on witchcraft, which I appreciate over the more modern books on it. The only reason I didn’t give 5 stars is due to the sections on controlling others. I don’t think that white magick is inclusive of controlling others’ free will, but I have to keep in mind that this was written in a different era. Additionally, the author does a decent job at giving disclaimers to look inward first prior to doing spellwork on other people.
