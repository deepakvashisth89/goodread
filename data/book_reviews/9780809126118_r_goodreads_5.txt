The late Fr. Raymond Brown S.S. has written a nice little book; which provides a look at the early Christian Church, based on Scriptural evidence. Based on this evidence, he shows that immediately after the Apostles died, unique ecclesial communities came into existence. Eventually, these communities would evolve into the early Church.This is a book that is suited for the reader who has some familiarity with Scripture studies. Still, a lot of readers might find this book both interesting and informative.
