Once again, here's a book that could have been good, had it been written better. As it was, it's just boring.The h's niece is a real drama queen (and an annoying one at that)! And the h sounds pretty pathetic when she admits that - at 22 - she was jealous of a 16-year-old! She claims that (plus her insecurities) were the reason she believed her niece over the H, more than any loyalty to family ties!And while I can understand the H walking out of her life (who can blame him?) and, when they meet again and she apologies after learning the truth, it's understandable he didn't accept her apology right away, after a time he started to sound like a martyr, and I think he enjoyed that role!They both got on my nerves, so I didn't bother to finish the book. Ms. Lamb goofed when she starts the story three years after the fact, Instead, she should have begun it when they first meet, fall in love (a typical HP whirlwind romance), and start thinking about a future together, so you'd already know them as a couple and the lie/mistrust/betrayal/breakup would have had more of an impact.Instead of stupidity (she was jealous, insecure, didn't believe he really scared, and more of the same nonsense) the h should have been torn between wanting to believe the H but also not wanting to believe her niece would lie about something like that, and her indecision would cause the breakup.Then, instead of letting three years go by (and have Drama Queen fess up on her wedding day, of all days), the h should have found out sooner, found the H and apologized asked for another chance (which he isn't sure he can give, but doesn't act like he's starring in a melodrama) and then the story focuses on how they find their way back to each other.Damn it, I should have written so many of these books! (Feel free to call me conceited!)
