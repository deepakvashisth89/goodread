This is a 1977 hardback reprint in French from Dupuis. Originally serialised between 1956 and 1958 before publication in book form in 1959, this volume contains two stories.

In the first, Spirou and Fantasio return to Africa to report on dwindling gorilla populations but stumble across an illegal mining operation. It’s a straightforward adventure with goodies and baddies, and the portrayal of Africans is a little more sympathetic than in the previous adventure in Africa, but this is still essentially a “white saviour” story. The second story essentially mocks a Middle Eastern oil prince through a plethora of stereotypes. It is notable for the cross-over of a character from another series published in the magazine Spirou, the accident-prone walking disaster Gaston Lagaffe.

Both stories are amusing, full of slapstick humour, and have great graphics, but they make for an uncomfortable read in hindsight.
