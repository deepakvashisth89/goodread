This is the second (and sadly final) Kay Farrow mystery. There are a lot of elements to the story - erotically engraved guns - photography - Aikido - journalism, illegal immigration - crooked cops.Trick of Light, much like the previous book in the series,The Magician's Tale," unfolds slowly, revealing depths in the characters while setting a very realistic backdrop of authentic "San Francisco" flavor.Investigating the death of her mentor, Kay Farrow becomes enmeshed in a number of mysterious and dangerous situations, handling each with style and aplomb, growing even as you share the pages with her, and culminating in a very satisfying ending. There a lot of plot threads and you may believe, at times, that there is no way they can be knit back into a whole story - you will be pleasantly surprised.Highly recommend this book, series, and author.
