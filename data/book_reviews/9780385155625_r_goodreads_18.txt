A nice cozy mystery -- I liked this second book more than the first one.These are set at a fictional agricultural college in New England in the 1970’s. This time around, there’s a stolen prize pig (suspects include a radical group of vegan students), and a robbery which imperils Professor Shandy’s wife, and finally a murder. I was a little saddened by the murder, actually, and the book is so determinedly cozy that it was handled rather too lightly.The book primarily feels dated because the characters are intent on fixing up a single professor with a woman who can keep house for him.
