A funny story about our guy Nataraj, a printer who just wants to work and nothing more and his life is interrupted by Vasu, the man-eater who makes everyone run. There's the poet and the cast of other characters who hang around but man are they ready abandon ship especially after the plot twist at the end.I really love Narayan's style and reading about this far-away land. It was a breath of fresh air in this Western world-focused society I live in. Have to reach out to these books from far away lands so I get to learn about other cultures too.
