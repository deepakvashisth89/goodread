Late review.

My first ever collected book series! I so remembered this.

I was in high school when I came across Francine Pascal's Sweet Valley High. Elizabeth and Jessica were in college. And it was a random book given to me, maybe book 40 or something already. But I got intrigued so I started looking for the rest of the books. Sadly, back home, paperback was expensive and I'm still living off my allowances so I can't collect all the books. But through looking (I remembered I went to the big bookstore thrice that week and search for hours), I found the author started another series of the same MCs - their Jr. High life. This is that book.

At first, I didn't know it was a series. So I read this, enjoyed it, and when I saw in the intro page that this is a series (I think GR doesn't exist during that era), I went back to that same bookstore and started looking for book 1 and so on. I think for a year, I was able to complete until book 20 or something. Sadly, I outgrown Jessica and Elizabeth so I stopped collecting.

But this series is close to my heart. It helped me during my adolescent years. I learned a lot even though they were fictional, and it delve me to embrace reading more books.

I like Elizabeth's character. She's the serious one, responsible, intelligent, leader type. Jessica, on the other hand, is the rebel, courageous, a little daring, somewhat too into her looks and overly cautious with her status, but loves her twin sister dearly.

It's really nice I found this gem during my formative years.
