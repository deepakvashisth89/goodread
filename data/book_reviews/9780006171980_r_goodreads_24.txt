Another rollicking historical adventure for the honourable Richard Sharpe, and once more the Spanish and French are the antagonists to a greater or lesser degree. Religion and the need to steal and hoard wealth and power fuel a colourful cast of characters machinations, although one or two seem to revel in their evil capacity to inflict violence upon their fellow men.You just know that there will be a deadly reckoning for the latter, and that it will be dealt out at the hands of Sharpe to the cheering of the reader. Alongside the enjoyable adventures, the descriptions of the social mores, conventions, and societal interactions between warring enemies are fascinating, and greatly added to my enjoyment of the book.Never a dull moment with our man Sharpe......
