England 1943 three USA airplanes are approaching from the sea to the coast of Sussex, after their mission of bombing fuel depots near the village of Halsvagen in Belgium. The leading plane is damaged and being fired at and then shot down by one flown by Major Albert Markham, the other pilot is Captain Jon-Jacob Anderson. The dead pilot is Lieutenant Dennis O'Connell. The two observers on land, Charles and Molly Gresham are then also shot at.Major Harry Voss JAG heads the investigation with his team.An interesting and well-written historical mystery
