The Enneagram is an ancient system for understanding basic personality types and their development and relationship to one another. Riso's book gives a thorough analysis of the personality types that encompass this system and how they mature and decline. I found this system enlightening and compelling and the analysis of the types using various developmental theories to be profound and deeply satisfying. In finding my own type in the system, and that of my significant others, I experienced deep and valuable insights. I highly recommend this book to any with interests in personality development or who are simply interested in learning more about themselves and their relationships.
