This is a good book, or rather, collection of writings from Mr Schweitzer. I enjoyed reading this and while not every excerpt moved me the majority spoke to me. I think Mr. Schweitzer had a heart to serve and wanted to encourage others to develop the reverence he felt would improve the lives of humans. He speaks of Christianity and shows this as the means to increase that respect for life of all living creatures.
“It has a significance for all future times that the symphony of Christianity began with a tremendous dissonance between faith and thought, which later resolved itself into a harmony.”
