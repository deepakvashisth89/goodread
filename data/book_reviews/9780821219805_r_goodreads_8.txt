Ansel Adams (1902 - 1984) is famous throughout the world for his black and white photography, Ansel Adams is one of the most famous black and white photographers of the 20th century.Ansel Adams was also as skilled working with color film as he was with black and white film. He'd worked far less frequently with color film than he'd worked with black and white film, and he'd also made far less of an effort to display his color photos than he'd spent working to ensure that many of his black and white photos would be displayed.Many people don't realize that Ansel Adams produced an extremely impressive portfolio of color photos in addition to his black and white works. While there have been quite a few collections of Ansel Adams black and white published, this book is the only book that I'm aware of in which the series of color photos that he'd taken are printed in.People who are interested in landscape photography, nature photography, and panorama vistas will thoroughly enjoy reading "Ansel Adams in Color."
