Joanne and Sally were childhood friends until a violent event took Sally's father and they drifted apart. Years later Sally has become a well-known and controversial artist. The local gallery is presenting some of her work and this event becomes the catalyst not only to bring Sally and Joanne back together but also to become the venue of a graphic and brutal series of murders.The plot is weak and vague as is the development of the characters and how the story unfolds. Joanne's loyalties and trust are misplaced and her friend Sally is indifferent and narcissistic. This was not a compelling mystery by any measure.
