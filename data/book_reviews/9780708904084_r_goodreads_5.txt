This is the last of the five books by Margaret Yorke featuring Prof Patrick Grant as the amateur sleuth. I read Mrs Yorks as a teenager in the 80s and it was wonderful to return to some of her books.The series is enjoyable if you like books that evoke times gone by because even though I think they were written in the 70s they feel very much from the golden age of crime writing. There’s mention of a computer in one book and that really jarred as I’d forgotten we weren’t in the 30s or 40s.So would I recommend the books? Yes. They won’t set your reading life alight but if you want some gentle books to sit reading by the fire on a rainy day, they’re perfect. Thats exactly what I did myself and it was wonderful.
