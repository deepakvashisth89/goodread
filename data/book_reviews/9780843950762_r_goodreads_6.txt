This book is in line with the authors, "Blood Crazy." I liked that one better than this, but it still hits a bullseye. A man in a small town that senses when someone is contaminated with a disease and then systematically hacks them or what have you to death brings a stranger into its mist and is chased away. He then joins up with a group of young people and try to survive a bleak world that has lunatics running everywhere.
