This book was published in 1998. In it, Mr. Gingrich tells about his experience as Speaker of the House in the mid 90’s – the lessons learned the hard way. He is really hard on himself, talking about the mistakes he made in his struggles with Bill Clinton and the other liberals.Many aspects of the political world that Gingrich describes have not changed at all, but it was very strange that we almost had a balanced budget during his time. At the end of the book, he assumes that that budget would be balanced nicely in the years to come. How sad.Also very sad: pictures of Gingrich and his second wife, with captions talking about how she was his best friend, etc.
