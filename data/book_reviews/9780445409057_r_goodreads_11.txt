We've got a sort of hard-boiled-type detective hired by Howard Hughes to find the person who has stolen information about one of Hughes's military secret plans. Honestly, Hughes is portrayed here the way we've come to understand him; paranoid.

Toby Peters, the protagonist, lives and works in the Los Angeles area and is familiar with Hollywood and many of its stars. Basil Rathbone invites himself to tag along with Toby on the adventure -- not every stakeout or visit to a suspect for questioning -- but doing a little sleuthing Sherlock style.

This is a quick read, even though it took me almost a week to get through it. Hey, I'm reading more than one book right now. It's just a little jaunt with one of Kaminsky's several recurring characters. Kaminsky was a prolific writer (63 novels and 11 non-fiction works), mostly mysteries with Abe Lieberman, Porfiry Rostnikov, and Toby Peters.

Note: My copy of this book is copyrighted in 1979 and has yellowed pages. I'm not sure why this particular edition would have no cover for Goodreads. I guess that happens.
