This is a book for the "true believer", people who want to find conspiracy to justify their paranoid world views. Written by a theologian (and not a good one at that) this book takes a faith based approach to engineering and facts concerning 9/11. Extremely disjointed views and misunderstandings of basic engineering and physics.While there is a need to show just how incompetent and politically charged the Bush Administration handling of National Security and their attempts to cover this up, the strawmen which David Ray Griffen constructs actually give cover to Bush rather than drives people to investigate their failures.
