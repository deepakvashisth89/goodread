King uses his signature tongue-in-cheek humor at times. Thankfully, it helped lighten the suspenseful mood; and,4. a great read for true-blue King fans, as several allusions to various characters and settings from previous novels are mentioned in this story.Niggles:1. luckily, I have thick skin, because some horrific scenes and graphic violence are definitely NOT for the faint of heart; and,2. a lot of attention (and pages) were paid to John and Alan dealing with the paperwork mess in the Sheriff's office.
