Likely the weakest of Jack McDevitt's work, this one is the story of the uncovering of what might be a sort of multi-planetary teleportation device, and the political fallout thereof. The ending struck me as really awkward, but the story introduces some wonderful plot threads once or twice before it peters out and a few of those story lines just sort of fade away.

That said, McDevitt is one of my favourite SF authors, and this is a good story. It's just not up to his amazing standards, or I read it somewhere in the wrong order of things as I explored his books.
