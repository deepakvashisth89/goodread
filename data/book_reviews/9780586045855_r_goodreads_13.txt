This book is a supplement to the Raj Quartet and is a winner of the Booker-Mann prize for obvious reasons. It concerns a rather minor couple, Lucy and Tusker Smalley, from the Quartet who have "stayed on" in India as they can no longer seem to identify with England and the consider India their home. It begins with Tusker's death and becomes a flashback of their life prior to this tragedy.Most of the friends of their generation are dead or back in England and they are basically along except for their devoted servants. The tables have turned as they are now "guests" rather that "rulers" and although Lucy seems to be able to cope, Tusker cannot.This is an extremely poignant little book which is much different in style from the Quartet and the ending will bring a tear to your eye.
