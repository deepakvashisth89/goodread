3.5 stars. Once again Henry and Emmy Tibbett join the war on drugs in the Caribbean island nation of Tampica, at the behest of old friends Lucy Pontefract-Deacon and Sir Edward Ironmonger, who suspect that current government officials are involved in negotiating a huge drug operation with, most likely, the mafia. But nothing — and no one — is quite what it seems. Since they are there more or less incognito, just who can they trust? Quickly moving plot, excellent story. Recommended.
