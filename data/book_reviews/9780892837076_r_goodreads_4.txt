My boyfriend got me this book for Christmas and I really enjoyed reading it. I’ve been taking RCIA classes and we are at the point where we need to choose a saint name and this book had a lot of great stories of saints throughout the years. It starts with younger saints, then motherly saints, and then moves on to martyrs, and prophetic women saints. The stories are short but detailed and it even gives you examples on how to use in your day to day life along with a prayer at the end.
