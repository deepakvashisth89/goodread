A sweet and sometimes heartbreaking account of a young Chinese-American girl struggling against the constraints of her family's expectations and the prejudice of her peers. Elaine is a fragile, relatable character, full of insecurities and good intentions. Although it may be impossible for readers to accept wholeheartedly the behavior (strict, often cruel) of her parents, it is easy to sympathize with Elaine, caught between two worlds, and struggle to construct an identity despite the negative messages she constantly receives about her ethnicity and her gender.
