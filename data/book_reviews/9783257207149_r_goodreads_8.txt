A prisoner escapes from the death row cells of the Santé prison’s High Surveillance wing.But he escaped with thehelpof Inspector Maigret!This is delightful puzzle of a murder-mystery from the master novelist Georges Simon.All the facts point to one man as the murderer, but he maintains that he is innocent. Inspector Maigret not only believes him, but puts his career on the line by letting the murderer go.Many red herrings are dragged across our path and many of them are true facts.Watch Maigret carefully trick the real killer to reveal himself in a surprise ending.Enjoy!
