Talia fell in love with Alex Jordon three years ago when she was nineteen and working as a secretary. Alex was a buisnessman who visited her boss one day and asked her out for dinner and later continued to go out with her till Talia fell in love with him. When they finally slept together, Alex travelled to South America and sent a telex breaking up with Talia. The worst part was he sent the telex with his secretary who took great pleasure in telling her she was Alex's mistress as well. Talia couldn't bear it especially when she was preganat with his child then.
