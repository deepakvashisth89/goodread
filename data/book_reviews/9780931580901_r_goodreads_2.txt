This is a great in between book to scroll through. Like when you are booting up your computer in the morning or saving a document that takes a while on the network. There are some real gems in here.Probably the best example of TV wisdom is from "Leave It To Beaver" where Ward, his father, tells Beaver, "When you make a mistake, admit it. If you don't, you only make matters worse." So true.Overall, it is a decent book of essential life lessons. My biggest beef is that the binding is cheap, so I have pages that have come loose. But other than that, it is a decent read and was worth the time spent reading it.
