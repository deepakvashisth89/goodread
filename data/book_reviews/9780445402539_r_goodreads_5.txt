The death, in the small village of Duntisbury, of retired 2nd World War General Maxwell and the disappearance of his driver Gunner Kelly. Spymaster David Audley to moves into the manor at the behest of his god daughter and Maxwell's grandaughter Miss Becky. Believing his colleague is on holiday, Jack Butler instead brings in German NATO agent Benedict Schneider to run an eye over the village. As with many of Anthony Price's novels people and events are never quite what they seem. With the village unnecessarily suspicious questions are asked whether Maxwell was the real target and who are they really waiting for and why are the KGB so interested in Kelly.
