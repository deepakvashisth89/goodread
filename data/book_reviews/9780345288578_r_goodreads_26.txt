Well, that's me done with the Philip Marlowe books (I don't have any interest in reading the last one that was finished by a different author) and I had a really great time reading them.

Before reading these, my ideas of hardboiled detective noir came almost entirely from film. Let's just say that now I've read the original blueprints I appreciate the genre all the more.

This book is so short compared to the previous book, The Long Goodbye, it felt more like a short story or an epilogue to the series. That's not to say it isn't any good; I enjoyed it at least as much as the other books in the series I gave four stars. Cracking good stuff!
