Emma Lathen was the pen name of Mary Jane Latsis and Martha Hennisart who wrote twenty four books featuring the detective skills of a Wall Street banker. (Under the name of RB Dominic they also wrote seven books featuring the detective skills of a Congressman.) I first came across their books in 1981 and I read fourteen of the books in this series back then but this is the first time I have read this one.This was first published in 1971 and most of the story takes place in Puerto Rico. There is sabotage in a garment factory there then a murder. More sabotage and a further murder take place before John Thatcher identifies the villain. It's a good solid read told with wit but it is not one of the authors' better books.
