I discovered this author about two years ago when I read Wars of the Heart. I loved it so much I looked up her other books and I thought maybe she had retired. Turns out, Villars, is a pen-name and her real name is Ellen Feldman and she's still writing. With a new book out this Summer 2020!

When I started reading Lipstick On His Collar, I thought it would be about two women fighting over the same man. You know, the wife vs. the other woman. It is but it becomes about so much more than just that. Hallie and Olivia work in the book publishing industry and have been friends for years. Until, Hallie finds out Olivia has been sleeping with Hallie's husband and everything starts rolling from there.

The story is really more about two very driven career women and how they go about doing their job and accomplishing their goals. Hallie takes the high road to get things done. Olivia, not so much. She's spends most of the book trying to one-up Hallie in every single way. She not only wants to take her supposedly best friend's man. She wants everything Hallie has and nothing is beneath her to try to take it.

I don't know how to describe what it is about Villars' writing that I like so much. It has a cleverness to it, a certain bite. Her characters are realistic. I think of this as very smart "chick-lit". Not fluffy in the slightest. The only thing I didn't care for is how it ended but it's a minor thing. Good read.
