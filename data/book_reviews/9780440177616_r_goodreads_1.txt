I liked this book better the first time I read it (25 years ago), but I still very much admire the hero: Robert Frederickson, Phd., doctor of criminology, private detective, karate black belt, former circus acrobat . . and dwarf. "Mongo the Magnificent" (his nom de cirque) encounters bizarre crimes often linked with the occult, and this tale--involving paranormal powers at the United Nations--is no exception.This novel has a lot of action and not much atmosphere--just the reverse of my taste. Still, it pleased me enough that I am sure I will read more Mongo adventures in the future.
