What a story. What world building i experienced while reading this. I tried to take a break from this book and read other books, i couldn't stop thinking about this book; and once i finished it, i still cant stop thinking about it.

Loveable characters, hateable characters, drama, pain, suffering and redemption, straight love, gay love. This book can seem a bit dragged on, but the world building is necessary, when it all comes together it's a beautiful thing. I'll always love this book.
