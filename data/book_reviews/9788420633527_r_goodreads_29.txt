4.5/5"My father is still a giant of a man."Houses don't have a voice. They stand with their heads in mid-air absorbing the voices of their inhabitants in the walls. They all appear calm to a passerby.This story is of one such house which gives off mundane vibes in the beginning but is hiding a cataclysmic scene behind its door.The story catches you off guard evoking a feeling of distress, confusion, anger and sadness in between discourses.If you are versed with Kafka's life, you will find reflections of it in the story. Like it is said, every art is a reflection of its artist.The protagonist is shone in a vivid light with its intensity changing throughout until the end, when it completely drops.Kafka shows the relationship between a father and a son and shows the implications of giving someone power over us.
