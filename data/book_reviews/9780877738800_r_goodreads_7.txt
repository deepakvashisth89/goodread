This book has a few REALLY great statements, such as:"We work on ourselves in order to help others, but also we help others in order to work on ourselves." Oh, wait a second, that's the only one.A major part of her instruction is to teach the reader how to work with 'slogans'. I find these extremely annoying, especially when they are not in a meaningful context for me and she begins every single paragraph with "Another slogan says..."I picked up the book b/c she is said to frame her teachings for the western mind. I found it way more confusing than Thich Nhat Hanh's writing and that's what I'll be going back to.
