Jeanette always has a endearing lure to her writing. She creates entire worlds and realms through her idiosyncratic prose and her peculiar characters.

In this short story collection, Winterson highlights the fragility of life, the desperate urge to be loved, and the everlasting desire to create meaning in existence.

I believe that since I was born I have had this irrational urge to feel something. To be something. To be in a forever state of content, to enjoy life now, instead of waiting for that Friday off work, waiting for the weekend or waiting for summer. I feel like I cannot enjoy my life unless I have something to look forward to.

I think other people are like this. Why do we always desire satisfaction? This voracious hunger for meaning, for lifelong happiness consumes us wholly, and it is brilliantly expressed in this book through the yearning and the longing of the ambiguous narrators.

I think I will always be longing, always hungry for happiness and joy throughout my entire life, but Jeanette’s words remind me to take things slow, enjoy the little moments, enjoy my time with loved ones before they are swept away by the destructive power of time.
