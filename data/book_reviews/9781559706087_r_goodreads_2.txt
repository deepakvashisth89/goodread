This whole book is about something that might or might not have happened. Did the czar of Russia fake his own death to go live a normal life on the Russian east coast? I am still undecided but lean towards saying it didnt happen. I would have at least given this book 4 stars but it had weird time skips. I think the pages of this book got mixed up because the timeline is all over the place. I did learn more about what was going on in Russia during the time of Napoleon so the book is useful just for that reason alone.
