A Fresh Look at the Story of StoriesThis book gives us a fresh view of stories with which we may have become too familiar. Looking at the Bible as the work it actually is, the story of the dealings of God with his people, and the coming of the new and better covenant. While it is important to remember this book itself is not Scripture, it is a fine version of the story of scripture. It brings new life to people, places, and events to which we may have inadvertently grown desensitized.
