Não fosse pelo hubris tantas vezes desajustado e pelo foco excessivo na crítica ao positivismo lógico e à filosofia oxfordiana do século XX, este livro seria uma boa introdução à filosofia, principalmente pós-kantiana. Com 200 páginas a menos seria excelente."Unacceptable doctrines that illuminate are like crosses on maps that show where treasure lies hidden; they tell us where to start digging, in this case because they tell us where we have gone wrong about something important to us. But of course unacceptable doctrines that illuminate can be that only for people who look at reality in light of them; for people who look only at them, and confine themselves to analysing them, there is only their unacceptability to be discovered, and no illumination to be gained."
