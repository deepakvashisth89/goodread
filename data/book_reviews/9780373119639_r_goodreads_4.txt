this was a frustrating book to read... The heroine seemed to fall in love a little too easily(within 48 hours of meeting the hero) and the hero was blowing hot and cold throughout. It was hard to make out whether they actually felt something genuine for each other. there was not enough romance, but several evil plots and not-very-interesting sub characters. This is more of a drama novel than a straightforward Mills&Boon love story. It's also, not my cup of tea!
