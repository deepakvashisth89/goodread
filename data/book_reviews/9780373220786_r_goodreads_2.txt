Insta love on the run. Since this is a very early, if not the first, book Tess wrote in the 80's, there were some things that need to be overlooked: technology changes, social situations, etc. But, overall, it is a very good, solid espionage mystery and I enjoyed it.

A sweet, young thing, Sarah Fontaine, is caught up in a situation beyond her norm when her husband of two months dies in a fire in Berlin. Why was he in Berlin? A strange phone call convinces her that this can't be true and Sarah heads to Europe with Nick O'Hara right after her. Nick is a lone wolf at the U.S. State Department and his inquiry into the Fontaine case raised red flags with the CIA, and others, putting Sarah's life in danger. While a pariah at State, Nick has Don Quixote tendencies and follows Sarah to protect her. Good thing as a contract killer is on her trail also.
