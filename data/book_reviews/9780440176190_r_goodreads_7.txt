An enjoyable, rather gentle Space Opera about not realising what you already have.Interstellar travel is achieved by entering a sort of malleable hyperspace that only certain "specials" -read "social misfits"- can navigate (and if this wasn't an inspiration for Warhammer 40k's Warp I would be very surprised... though, admittedly, there is a decided lack of demons in the SRW version).Gev Carlyle is hopelessly lost in nostalgia, and wants his old star rigger team and ship back together again. Instead he's left with a ship he hardly knows and an alien cat -accompanied by some cute mobile plantlife- that has other ideas about piloting.Colourful, oozing sensuwunda, and ultimately satisfying (even if you do feel like giving Gev a serious talking to at times). I fully intend to read the others of this series.
