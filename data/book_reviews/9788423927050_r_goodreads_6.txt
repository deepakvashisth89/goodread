The 1894 novellaTom Sawyer, Detectiveis Mark Twain’s adventure tale of Tom, Huck and Jim that is the second of two little known sequels to Huck Finn and Tom Sawyer. The first of the sequels,Tom Sawyer Abroad, takes place right after Tom, Huck and Jim returned to St. Petersburg after their experiences inThe Adventures of Huckleberry FinnIt involved the threesome’s adventure in hitching ride on an air balloon as it soars across the United States, over the Atlantic and over the Sahara Desert to the pyramids of Egypt.This story takes place during the following spring and involves Tom, Huck and Jim’s return to Tom’s Uncle Silas’ farm in Arkansas, the location of their final adventure in "Huck Finn." There the three get involved in solving a theft and even a murder. I won’t say more about the plot except that the book could have been called Tom Sawyer Detective/Lawyer as Tom gets involved in both parts of the Law & Order pairing.While not nearly as good as either of the first two famous installments in the series, this book is still a considerable improvement on “Tom Sawyer Abroad.” This one at least deals with some plausible situations and portrays the resulting emotions and reactions more realistically. Thus, some degree of character insight and growth is obtained. The quality of Huck Finn’s narration, while less slapstick than in “Abroad," is still well below that inThe Adventures of Huckleberry Finn. I rate this as 2.7 stars rounded up to 3 stars, in recognition of its improvement on the previous Tom and Huck installment.
