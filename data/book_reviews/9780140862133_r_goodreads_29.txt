“Some birds are not meant to be caged, that’s all. Their feathers are too bright, their songs too sweet and wild. So you let them go, or when you open the cage to feed them they somehow fly out past you. And the part of you that knows it was wrong to imprison them in the first place rejoices, but still, the place where you live is that much more drab and empty for their departure.”Rita Hayworth and Shawshank Redemption was the perfect way to start off my 2026 reading year!! This was the prison story I never knew I needed. 🔑~A True Masterpiece~"It always comes down to just two choices. Get busy living or get busy dying."Stephen King delivers a powerful story about perseverance, hope, and finding your own justice within an unfair and unforgiving system. With a memorable, observant, and deeply human narrator—you are pulled into Shawshank in a way that feels both intimate and immersive. What blew my mind with this novella is how quietly brilliant it is. Nothing is rushed. Every detail matters, and the payoff is so emotionally worth it.I am so ashamed to say I somehow missed watching the 1994 movie adaptation of this during my lifetime (and I am not quite sure at all how this happened when I am such a huge movie lover of so many genres!). After my research I learned this movie is considered to be one of the best movies of all time, so I am over the moon that I am finally watching it for the first time this weekend with my husband!The book was worthy of 10🌟. Andy Dufresne is an unforgettable character. I'll think of him often. And I have now become a strong believer that we should all strive to find the Andy Dufresne within each of us."In spite of the problems he was having, he was going on with his life. There are thousands who don’t or won’t or can’t, and plenty of them aren’t in prison, either."Words of wisdom...If you think you already know this story from its reputation, do yourself a favor and still pick it up. Read it anyway! The journey is far more moving and profound than I ever could have imagined from a simple novella.Stephen King has really caught me in a whirlwind. I am so glad to exist during a timeline where I get to experience his diverse work. I really wish I had started sooner though! I indulged in many of the film adaptations for his books growing up but for some reason never made my way into his books....that was until 2023, 3 solid years into my reading journey! I dabbed my little toe in one of his novellas that year to sample his writing style and storytelling and fell completely into the water!Fast forward to 2026 and I’ve only found myself craving to read more and more and more of his work. Getting through 11.22.63 last year, one of his many grand tomes, gave me just the confidence (and addiction) I needed to continue working my way onward to becoming aconstant reader.And how quickly I’ve already found myself recruiting others just as I myself have been encouraged! King is one hell of a storyteller and one hell of a horror writer. Start somewhere. Start anywhere. Start light. Start heavy. Start with horror. Or start with another genre. Just start!!! Everyone is bound to find something they will connect with amongst his work.And most importantly of all, enjoy the journey!
