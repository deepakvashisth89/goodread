Twain's' biting satire & humor is on full display here. His chapter on a knight trying to sleep outside at night in full armor is hilarious. His chapter on smallpox is an indictment on the cruelty of royalty and the Catholic Church also receives a drubbing.His assignment to rescue damsels in distress who turn out to be hogs is flat out funny. His guide on this misadventure is Sandy, a woman who put the dits in ditsy.
