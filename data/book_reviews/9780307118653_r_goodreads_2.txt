Mommy's review from 11/14/11 -


You know those dated books like this you sometimes find and possibly pick up and they're so old you cringe? That's what I thought would happen when Julia brought me this. I'm not one to ever turn down a book, especially in front of my six-year-old, so we took it on home.
I was pleasantly surprised. It's obviously dated, that much is easily seen.
This basically details what it takes to care for a kitten, just like another reviewer said. Details are given about the kittens like, they stay with their mother for at least six weeks, they should have their own place to sleep, vet visits, exercise, etc.
I don't know that it would be all a parent would need, reading material wise, for a child wanting to get a kitten but it would be fine as an additional book for such a purpose.
