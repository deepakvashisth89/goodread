''Cuando los lagartijos corren''
I read this book more than one month ago, and since then I've been thinking about how to write a good review. This book is amazing, so I wanted to write a good review for it. A review that can express how incredible this book is. And what it made me feel.
The first thing that called my attention was the cover page. A girl who looked my age. Tiger Eyes, I read. I looked at the girl again, those were not tiger eyes, those were sad eyes. It made me think of my grandma. My grandma used to tell me all the time that I have sad eyes. It made me think of . I didn't think twice and I took it. I read the back cover of it. I need to read this book, I thought.
I read it in 13 days. 218 pages. This is a really easy book to read. You can read it in one day. But it took me 13 days. I didn't want to stop reading it, so I just read it for a few minutes a day. I loved the author's writing style. I loved every character. This book's characters are so real. And the way this book is written made me think I was there, in the story, with the protagonist. I felt so identified with the protagonist. So it made me think a lot about my own life, about my family, about love. This book is about love, not about romance, but real love. This book also, surprised me a lot. I wasn't expecting anything that happened. I, honestly, was expecting this book would be a typical book for teenagers. A typical story that never happen to anyone. But this book was real. The story is something that can actually happen to any of us. I was so into the story, into the characters, that I was even scared of reading the end and be disappointed of it. But when I finished the book, I wasn't. I wasn't disappointed at all. I was almost coming to the end and I had so many questions, but just a few more pages. So I read it at once. I cried. I found myself crying a lot of times while I was reading this book. The reason I give this book 5 stars, is because this book is extremely well written, that makes you feel anything the protagonist feels. When I finished this book, I realized I really needed to read this. It made me think a lot about my life, about my family, and my relationship with my dad. This is not a book you just read and then forget about. This book is one of those you read and makes you think about your life. I would recommend this book to anyone who wants to read a beautiful story. Also, to teenagers who wants to read something different from the typical love stories, this is a real love story.
