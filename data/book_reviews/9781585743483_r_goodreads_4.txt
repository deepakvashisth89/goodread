This was good though I wish it had more details about what they thought caused it to sink. It's not that the book doesn't have this but I felt like it could go in to more descriptive detail of each specific possible cause of the sinking and HOW/WHY each option is on the list. I liked the fact it included additional submarine accidents that came after the Thresher, as well as those that came before. I did not know of all those instances because the Thresher is always the most talked about.For those who want to learn about the US submarine history (to a point) and this specific submarine, this is a good book to read. The narration was really good and I was surprised I knew the narrator (as in familiar with his work), really wasn't expecting that. It is a small world, haha.4 stars for the story (I do plan to listen again someday)5 stars for narration
