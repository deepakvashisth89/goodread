I really enjoyed this finale to the Serrano series. Lots of ends were tied up satisfactorily and although everyone didn't live happily ever after, there was a satisfying sense of closure.I will miss Cecelia, Heris, Brun, Esmay and Barin though. The seven books of the series were a wonderful adventure story in a well realised milieu, and I was left wanting more!First read June 2008
