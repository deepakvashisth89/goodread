I borrowed this 3-D sound audiobook from the Dallas Public Library.This was a pretty cool way to experience the story. I have read King's story, seen the movie, and now listened to the 3-D sound version. I love the story all-around. It's a great thrill ride, scarefest, and even a kind of philosophical experience. I especially love the character of Mrs. Carmody, or rather I should say love to hate. She shows how people turn to religion, cults, violence, and scapegoating when they are terrified and insecure.This recording is really meant to be listened to with good headphones on. I actually listened to it in the car with the sound all the way up. Pretty interesting experience! It gave the feel of listening to an old time radio drama, but I'm not sure any of them were like this!
