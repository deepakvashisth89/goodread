BrilliantThe sequel to Yesterday’s Son (so if you have not read that go back and do so). The Guardian is malfunctioning and is causing the rapid aging of stars and destroying planets. Only one being alive can solve the problem and once again Admiral Kirk, Captain Spock, and McCoy must travel to the planet Sarpedian to locate and convince Spock’s son, Zar to return from his time to communicate with the time portal. The novel perfectly sets up the next adventure of the Enterprise and her crew, that being a little training/inspection cruise with a ship full of cadets. A great duology.
