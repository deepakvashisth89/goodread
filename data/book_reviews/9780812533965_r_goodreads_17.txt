The Time Master trilogy was one of my very first fantasy reads back in 1993, and it really ignited my love for the genre. Seeing it peeking out of my bookshelf, I thought it was time to revisit it.I don't know if it's age or something else, but I think I should have left it happily in my teenage memories. The story was too superficial and while the basic backdrop of Order versus Chaos is such an eternal one, the teen angst and sexuality didnt seem to quite fit with the titanic forces at play. I should have left the Tarod and Cyllan love story in my 13 year old brain, my 37 year old one just can't seem to believe in universe-shaping emotions being conjured in three meetings.Anyhow, I learnt my lesson - no more revising my teenage book-crushes (unless its Wheel of Time & then all bets are off).
