The long novellaSt. Mawrrather dwarfs the short story,The Man Who Diedin this slim volume, but both are D. H. Lawrence at his very finest!St. Mawrcontinues with Lawrence's primary theme of a frustrated woman looking for a man who is full of life, but in this case her inspiration turns out to be an unmanageable stallion. InThe Man Who Died, Christ wakes up in his cave still alive and realizes that he has been given a second chance at life.
