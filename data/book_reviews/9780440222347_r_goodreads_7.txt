This was a really enjoyable read. Every character, and I do mean everyone in the cast from the show, had something major to do. That's an impressive feat since the previous books tended to overlook the alien ambassadors, but not this book.

There's going to be a meeting of Centauri and Narn aboard Babylon 5 for a peace conference. This enables G'Kar and Londo to have some terrific scenes, as do their attaches. Naturally, things go wrong. At the top of the list is a group of aliens, about 100, that snuck aboard to try and get someone to listen to their plight, as their world has been taken over by the Narn and their people abused. These aliens are terrorists, trying to sabotage the conference and do harm to all Narn.

This was a terrific read, and it boosts my yearning to read more Bab5 books.
