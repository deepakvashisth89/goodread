I enjoy reading about British Kings and Queens and thought I would enjoy this book about the Queen Mother. What a disappointment. It was like reading a boring textbook in school. Lots of historical dates and facts but the story was told in such a dry, dull and boring manner. I could only read a bit a time and I would put it away and read something else, and go back to it later. I have never taken so long to read a book in my entire life! It should have taken no more than 2 weeks, but I was picking at it for almost 6 months! ☹
