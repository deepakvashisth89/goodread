This book is full of practical knowledge on discerning and hearing God's voice. A lot of the information is backed up with anecdotes from the author's personal lives, stories from ministry, and the lives of the Saints. The author is Catholic, but as someone myself who t0ok a more holistic approach to Christianity and didn't identify with Catholicism at the time I first read this, I still found a lot of the information helpful. The book provides journal prompts and questions for individual and group reflection, so it's a good supplement to your Bible study and potentially a tool for Christian communities to cover.
