Interesting idea for a book series: it follows--from the outside--the development of the nation of Israel. The novel enjoyably paints some famous biblical characters with their ulterior motives for doing what they did. I found myself wanting to re-read the original stories to see who was a real character and who was added for the narative. Interesting plot, decent character development, but the writing was a bit to flowery for my tastes.
