Another entertaining entry into the Grand Tour Universe by Ben Bova. I've said it before, but Ben Bova is incredible with his ability to tell "hard" Sci-Fi stories while still making them different each time.I enjoyed the dynamic of the main character and his dad, which very much reminded me of Faramir-Boromir-Denathor's dynamic from Lord of the Rings. His assignment to go an get his dead brother's remains from Venus is just so engaging.I also really loved the "Villain" of this book, who really surprised me throughout. There are some twists that after they happened made me go "of course, that makes sense".Ben Bova also does a good job of educating the reader about the actual science of Venus, while also manipulating the science a bit for his own gain.The opening sequence is...risque, and I totally get why Bova included it, but man he is in danger of cutting off a lot of readers with that first chapter.The book doesn't hit as well as Ben Bova's best books, but its still a darn fine novel nonetheless, and works well as a standalone.Overall, 8 out of 10. Good job Ben Bova.
