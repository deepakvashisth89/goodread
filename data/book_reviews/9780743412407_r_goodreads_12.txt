It's been a while since I read a Buffy book, and this one caught my attention due to the fact that it reveals backstory about India Cohen, the Slayer directly preceding Buffy in the Slayer line. While the book was good, I just felt like there was a lot going on: too many characters, too many players in a complicated game. I think I struggled with it because I read this coming directly off the masterpiece that is the Realm of the Elderlings. Overall it was decent! Just a little chaotic lol it was cool to see Kendra again, and to learn about India and Kit.
