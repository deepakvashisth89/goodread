A romping space opera - one of my favorite categories. It has breakaway colonies, ways to travel long distances quickly, a black hole with unusual properties, a set of quirky characters who come together (quest-like) and a strange "somethings" referred to as "angels" which seem to be able to influence people. Timothy Zahn doesn't let details about his world-building bog the story down.Does everything work out? Well, read it and find out.
