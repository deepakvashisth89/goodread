This is the first Chopra I have ever read. At first it feels pretty light on but as a result, it leaves a lot of room for reflection.There were definitely some points raised in here I'll work to remember and integrate into my life. They include:- The unknown = infinite possibilities = freedom.- Worrying about money = feeling poor.- Being carefree with your resources = charity and generosity = fulfilment and gratitude.- Attention = manifestation.- Universe literally means One Song.I like Chopra's recommendation to read the whole book through once and then re-read 5 pages per day on repeat thereafter. The idea of having a day per month to reflect on each of the qualities of the unified field also seems like a good way to integrate the learnings into your everyday life.
