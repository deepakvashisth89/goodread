We all carry the seeds of violence…but what factor is it that allows one person to tip over the edge, and another not?Twenty years have passed since Sir Gerald and Dame Caroline Asherton’s son drowned in the river Thames. And now the body of their son in law has turned up floating in the same river.Is his death connected to that of the Ashertons’ son? Or was it an accident? If not, who would want to kill him and why?
