A wonderful contemporary read with unforgettable characters and a beautifully written story that included the right amount of emotion, humor, sweet and tender romance. I really liked the instant attraction and chemistry between Brett and Laurel and got so involved I didn't want this can't-put-down book to end. Ms. McClone never disappoints and I look forward to book three in her One Night to Forever series. I received an advance reader copy of The Wedding Lullaby and this is my voluntary and unbiased review.
