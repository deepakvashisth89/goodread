I have read and enjoyed 5 of Trevanian's novels including the first and only other Jonathan Hemlock novel: "The Eiger Sanction"I usually get annoyed by book purists who complain about what gets lost in an adaptation (movies are a shorter, different visual medium).But I think Clint Eastwood's adaptation of "The Eiger Sanction" relied too much on his cool image to carry the character. I didn't hate the movie. I just thought the book was better (probably something every devoted reader says when a cherished book gets made into a movie).This was never made into a movie and my pleasant memory of reading it is therefore untarnished.But I was always disappointed Trevanian didn't write another sequel. I guess that's a testament to how beloved the two Hemlock books were for many of us
