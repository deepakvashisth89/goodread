Stories which will make your sides split with laughter, or to shudder at harrowing descriptions of combat and gore, an honest discussion of an ordinary Southerner's motives for fighting for the Confederacy, and a likeable, engaging narrator reflecting on his service in the war from a remove of 20 years. All this you will find in Co. Aytch (pronounced "Company H"). Sam Watkins' memoir is a treasure and a must-read for anyone interested in the Civil War.
