From what I have seen of Ms Ashley in films and stage (mid 80's on) I never would have thought she was NOT all the time a Lion of an Actor. Here's the underside of being an actor, particularly a female actor and in a relationship with another actor during the non-feminist 60's-mid 70's.Tony Award winner, a play written for her, Hollywood acclaim, on the Top...then thrown down to the bottom in an all star film--and you really learn that film is good or not because of the Director and Editor...deciding to quit acting and be a good Hollywood Wife but getting drawn back to acting...panic, drugs, never knowing if you're talented enough...then to ride high again because a theatre company Knew she could be Maggie The Cat. Don't read before bedtime or you will really get depressed. But this is such a "scars and all" book.
