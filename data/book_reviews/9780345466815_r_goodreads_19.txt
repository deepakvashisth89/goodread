To begin with. I don't have a problem with old books or old authors, not at all.

Eric Van Lustbader is perhaps an old writer today, and many may see him as old-fashioned, but I can't help but admire his writing. Jian is just as much about love but also about the beginning of a nation's downfall. I think Jian is masterfully written where the action is set in Asia but also in the United States. The book itself is really good but can feel quite long, but that's probably mostly because the chapters are very long. So for me this book is one of the most well-written books from the 1980s and together with the book Ninja an unforgettable reading experience if you love politics, love and Asian culture this book is very good and that's where JIAN ends up on my shelf of classics.
