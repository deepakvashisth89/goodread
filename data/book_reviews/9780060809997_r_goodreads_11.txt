First finished. Book of 2016. I used to enjoy Sgt Cribb on TV in the late seventies, also Peter Lovesey went to school with an aunt of my husbands, so thought I'd give it a try. I enjoyed it very much but felt the ending was a little woolly.
A bare knuckle fighter is washed up on the embankment headless, and Sergeant Cribb sends a young constable undercover to gather information. Poor chap, Henry Jago, is recruited, a champion boxer though not in the banned bare knuckle sport he is able to survive to tell the tale and help Cribb and his DC Edward Thackeray to crack the case.
A nice holiday read, and think there's a couple more in the house.
