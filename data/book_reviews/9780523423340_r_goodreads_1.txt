I'm not going to bash this book over how dated it is (especially the gender stereotypes), but even making allowances for genre conventions, this book is a huge fail. The romance is stale, the protagonists are 2d cardboard characters who are too dumb to live. The action sequences are horrible and the plot is non-existent.

In short this is the result of a author writing about subjects they have no knowledge about. Skip this book, reading the instructions for your washing machine is more entertaining than this book
