One of my favourites in the series. I like how Kollberg and Gunvald Larsson have learned to work together and a solidarity is felt even though they started out disliking each other.The cases are baffling, the solution quite unforeseeable and well thought out. Can't really say more without spoiling.Martin Beck meets Rhea and a new chapter starts in his life.Only two more to go!2025 reread: Still so great. A bleak view of (capitalist) Swedish society and naive adoration of Eastern Bloc socialism, but none of the very dark, very violent and nightmarish storylines of some of today's Scandi crime novelists - looking at you, Arne Dahl!
