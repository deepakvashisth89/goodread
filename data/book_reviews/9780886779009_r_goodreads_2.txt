Awkward to read. The main character remains a rather pathetic, incapable loser until the last few dozen pages of the book, crying and screwing up at least once per chapter. The dialogue is uncomfortable and unrealistic, the characters aren’t believable in the slightest, and the magic is poorly developed, conveniently changed to suit plot lines, and then ends up being almost totally irrelevant. The plot is simple but off in a ‘going around your elbow to get to your ass’ kind of way. Most of the book feels like wasted time and the pace of travel is just bizarre. And if I have to read one more description about “ringlets of X-colored hair falling around her face” I may puke. Still, it’s an easy, quick read that I ended up finishing, so I have to give it 2 stars instead of one!
