A most welcomed addition to any D&D game, no matter the edition, although it is best used with 3.5 and 3rd edition.

This Open Content book, introduces a mishmash of alternative rules and supplemental rules. You get early legacy weapons that grow in power as you gain levels and incantations, rituals any class can perform, as long as they roll the right skills.

The biggest issue with this book is that it isn't focused on any peticular themed and that the ideas presented aren't explored in dept.

Still, a great addition for your table.
