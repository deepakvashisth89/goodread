I listened to the 25-hour "free" audiobook on Audible. It took me an entire year to finish. Here are my thoughts:

Darwin was truly a visionary. His keen observations of the natural world, and of humanity within it, remain impressive even today, nearly 200 years later.

Darwin held a firm anti-slavery stance. He was curious about the people he encountered on the voyage, and at times respectful toward indigenous people. Yet he had his limits: his worldview remained unmistakably rooted in that of a European colonizer.

What strikes me most is a sadness I haven’t anticipated: reading Darwin's vivid descriptions of the natural world makes the loss of biodiversity since his time feel worse.
