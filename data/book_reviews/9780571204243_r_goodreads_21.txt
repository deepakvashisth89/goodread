This is a very "American" novel set in London in the early '60's. "American" because it has as one of its concerns the very American quest for personal transformation. Everyone in this novel is desperately trying to transform themselves. An editor into a TV personality, a reporter into a novelist, a finishing-school (can't get much more transformational than this!) girl into a wife, a housewife into anything but a housewife, a graduate into a press baron.Fans of the play "Noises Off" will be reminded of Frayn's gift for farce, and people who haven't seen "Noises Off" should get off their bums and rectify that situation, I mean, really.Show more
