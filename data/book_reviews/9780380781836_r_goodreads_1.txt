A Little bit drawn out and at times boring,but slightly more informative if less gossipy than the preceding book on the matter Blue Thunder, the most recent book on The Aronow case with new information,but it is more a chronological history. I'd give it 5 stars for the subject and newer information but 1 star for the way it is written and the lack of any conclusion. In all fairness we still do not know who Killed Donald Aronow but most of are sure it was /is Ben Kramer.
