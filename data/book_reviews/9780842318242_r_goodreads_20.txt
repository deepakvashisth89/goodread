I was really looking forward to this book because I liked the idea of Dan Allender, a counselor and Trember Longman,a theologian coming together to write a book on marriage. The idea of the book was good, each section had a "case study" (which I believe were true stores) followed by several chapters discussing the presenting problem/marriage relationship. However, although this book had discussion questions at the end of every chapter the length of the sections made it difficult to facilitate a small group discussion week after week. I would not recommend this book for a small group discussion purpose, but was a great read for a couple to do together.
