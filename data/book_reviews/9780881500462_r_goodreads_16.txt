My Mom used to read this series back in the early 80s, and I'd read them before she returned them to the library. I was about 12. I loved them.I was thrilled to find this one and reread it. (It's been long enough I'd forgotten the whole thing. Lol.)It's "old-fashioned" and quirky, but that's what I love about it.No swearing, no sexual situations, no crudeness, or any of the other things that are so prominent in so many modern books.I like this author and am looking forward to reading more of her books.
