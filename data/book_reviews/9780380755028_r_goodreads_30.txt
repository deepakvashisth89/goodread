And so the Amber series rounds itself out with this tenth installment? What did I think? That's really hard to say. I think my ultimately opinion reflects much of what other people hav esaid; good series, but the Corwin cycle, that is the first 5, are superior to the Merlin Cycle of the last Five. The later are still emminately enjoyable, but they lack alot of the 'oomph' that carried the others through.As I had mentioned before, something that was a tad irritating in this series was the way world-building elements from the first novels were some-times undermined. There is a level of abstraction that diverges from some o fthe thematic elements of the first set of books.All in all though, to paraphrase something that has been said of other others, Roger Zelazny's 'so-so' is far better than many peoples 'best', so I'd still recommend the entire thing.
