A collection of poetry and short stories by the author of Push. At first I was confused by the nature of the collection, I typically have read poetry alongside nonfiction essays, not fictional stories, so when the book opens with several stories telling different takes on CSA I was trying to piece them together to make sense of them all happening to one person. But once I got the hang of the collection I loved it. I think the collection gets stronger as it goes on, my favorite piece being about the murder of Latasha Harlins by convenient store owner Soon Ja Du, who got off with zero jail time despite eyewitnesses and video evidence. Having gotten a taste of Sapphire in this collection I now feel ready to read Push.
