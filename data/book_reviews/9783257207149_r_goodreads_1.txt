Ok, first thing to say isTHIS IS MAIGRET NUMBER 9 NOT NUMBER 5as the Goodies said "Get it Right" !! (Early 1970 British comedy series 😂).This book centred around a convicted murderer who is about to go to the gallows. Maigret had been the investigating officer who had been instrumental in getting the conviction, and is now less certain of his guilt , had he been tricked and misled.The book then focusses onMaigret springing him from Jail, shock horror, and his subsequent reinvestigation of the horrific double murder. This brings in an eclectic cast of characters : rich Americans; psychotic eastern Europeans; seedy bar owners and two relatively inexperienced junior officers supporting Maigret.This makes a fantastic mix in which Maigret is his most ruthless and most inscrutable. Up against an incredibly intelligent criminalMaigret has to dig deep to solve this unexpected and unusual crime.As ever this was a wonderfully written and evocative novel, so atmospheric and just so French. I look forward to the next.
