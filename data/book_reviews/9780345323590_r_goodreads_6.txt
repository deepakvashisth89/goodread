A very cliche Thompson Oz book. We've got a prince on a quest, a character from our world, 2 classic characters (Trot and Scarecrow) and a couple others with strange abilities. Two different groups go questing to solve the prince's quandary and meet up with various weird villages. Very predictable, and the new characters were pretty stock photos.

It does give us backstory on Tattypoo, the Good Witch of the North. That part was interesting. And the part with Quiberon the dragon was a little exciting.
