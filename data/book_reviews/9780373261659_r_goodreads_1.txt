Frank Carver is a enforcement officer for the Forest Service, along with Ginny Trask, also a Forest Service employee though her job isn't clearly defined, he is assigned to help protect Ward Tomasovic. Ward is from the local area, the fictional Coffee Creek, Oregon, and he is giving a speech on how logging companies and environmentalists can work together. After the speech Ward is killed and Frank and Ginny are assigned to investigate his death as the local sheriff is involved with arresting the governors son for a fatal car accident.The book is a bit dated, it was written in 1993. Despite the title the book doesn't deal a lot with environmental issues connected to logging. I wonder if the author was in the Forest Service because there is a lot of information about how the Forest Service is run, there is no authors blurb on my copy of the book. The book ends rather abruptly which was annoying after waiting quite awhile for anything to happen in the story.
