I thought I'd read and loved everything by L. Frank Baum, but I missed the MMM.How could that happen?Anyway, I loved this collection of stories about King Mo and his family who inhabit a world somewhat like OZ, but different.Hilarious characters who do wrong, but make it right. Rivers of milk and trees laden with whatever one needs. Always the ability to undo what's been done.I so wish I'd read this during my Baum period (1958-1962), but so glad to meet and read it at age 66.The illustrations are wonderful, too.Go, Mo!
