Stephen Crane survived a shipwreck and wrote about it as writers do. Experience is the fruit that preserves our names. We pick the ripest, often the darkest, moments from our lives. Those that are ready to burst and bleed our hands are most prized. With words, we distill these berries into wine. This one is red and strong. I drank. The bottle is empty. Here are my thoughts.

Life is an open boat that we share with a few people. For a time, they are our whole world. With luck, we may row well together. Even so, the skies are guaranteed to change. Hopes will swell only to crash around us. The shores which we sought will fade into memory or be lost altogether from our minds. Just being alive will become enough for now. Maybe for the rest of our lives.

I lift my glass to Stephen Crane.
