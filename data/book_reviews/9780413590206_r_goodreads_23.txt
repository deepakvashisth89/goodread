Its not her best work but it’s Winterson so it’s obviously good! It’s a retelling of Noah’s Ark, which I like. I always like things that are in the realm of theology, and last November I went to see Winterson talk about her most recent book that is also a retelling so I kept on thinking about the things she said in the talk while reading Boating For Beginners. It’s also a satire, a satire on so many things, on so many modern things. It’s nice that she took something that isn’t so modern (Noah’s Ark) and used it to satirically talk about modern things (cellulite and freezer foods). But it’s really not her best 😬 might have to re-read The Passion!
