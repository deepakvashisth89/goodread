Good for the actor interviews. That's about it. I appreciate this book included EVERYONE, down to the stage crew. However, it was a bit rambling. Took me half a year to even motivate myself to finish.I'd get it used, but really, it doesn't have anything new that the autobiographies of the actors don't mention. It's just an almost pointless addition to my Star Trek collection.Three stars because the actor interviews were interesting.
