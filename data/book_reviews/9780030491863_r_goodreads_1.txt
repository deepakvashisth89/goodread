A collection of comic strips from the late 70s, on the verge of bursting into the 8os. Included in this collection is a satirical plotline about Elizabeth Taylor's then husband Senator John Warner, which basically states Warner was an empty suit with no real opinions or ability, except for his famous wife. For this the Republican Caucus of Virginia censured the strip and its creator. The interesting thing is that there are also several strips in this book where he basically calls then Democratic Governor Jerry Brown the exact same thing and, in my opinion, was even worse, going onto attack California's known vapidness in general. There were no complaints about that. BTW, the title is a reference to Elizabeth Taylor's appearance at the time.

Along with this, we have Duke advocating for the NRA, jokes about Carter's continuous use of symbolic gestures over practical measures, the rise of the Ayatollah in Iran and the evil repression from that regime, Vietnam's invasion of Cambodia, and so on.
