Another unflinching, honest-to-God kind of book by Linda Grant. She tackles all the big questions here, and does it entertainingly, quite an achievement. What is love, what is desire, what makes a marriage and what a family, how do we grieve, how does war change people, how can we cope with feelings of guilt. Add to that a hefty dose of Liverpool from the fifities until the millenium, a sprinkling of The Beatles (including a cameo appearance of George Harrison) and the result deservedly made it unto the Booker loglist of 2002.
