White wrote some lovely books about Ireland - the Goshawk, The Godstone and the Blackymor, and this, The Elephant and the Kangaroo. While often coming close to caricaturing the Irish peasant, he shows such affection for his characters that you forgive him. And I laughed out loud at his description of the horrible bedroom wallpaper: "represented on it were kangaroos, machine guns and old ladies drinking tea."
