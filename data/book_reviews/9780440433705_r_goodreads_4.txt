This is an easy read, and just a tinge of fear. Cassie is a 13 yr old seventh child of a seventh child, set to become a medium like her mother when she gets older. But unlike her mother, it seems Cassie has a real gift. When teased about her abilities, she raises a specter named Deverill (by mistake. It was supposed to be a young girl) and becomes convinced he’s her personal devil. He tries to convince her of wicked things under the guise of being helpful.

Some of this book is difficult to understand as far as plot. Why does her mother say she’s lost the gift at the end? What sense do her brothers and sisters make? Her mother is kind of nonsensical anyway. I also feel there could have been a lot more done with her gift at the beginning, like when she “saw ghosts” at the beginning.
