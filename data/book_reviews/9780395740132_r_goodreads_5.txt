An excellent companion for your battlefield touring. I just used it for a trip to Shiloh, Chickamauga/Lookout Mountain, and the Petersburg siege (Eastern and Western Fronts, Five Forks Battle). The book includes short summaries of each battle, written by various Civil War historians and authors along with a map(s) overlaid with Union and Confederate force dispositions and movements. I intended to use it on the various battlefield sites but I found the Park Service has excellent markers and maps onsite. I used this book to get the overall layout and summary of what to look for. I have the older version of the book with fewer battles included.
