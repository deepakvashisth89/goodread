At first, I was a bit apprehensive about tackling this Holocaust memoir—these books can be incredibly painful. However, I am so glad that I did. It is almost as if this author takes her reader by the hand and pulls that reader along into a gradually darkening world. Roma’s narrative covers her life from the age of 5 (with her childhood memories of the Ghetto and the detention camps) to her forties (at Spielberg’s premiere of his film “Schindler’s List” to which she was invited). A powerful testimony against the evils of fascism that resonates today more than ever!
