Have you ever known that person who is so long-suffering and so sweet? If not, you will meet her in this book. She reminded me of a friend who said sweetie a lot. Many times I heard disappointment and anger in that dear word. Althea Graham is resigned to being a slave to her dear, sweet mother forever. Then, Frank comes home, and hope becomes part of her vocabulary again. So much joy! Could this be the time of their freedom? Who keeps snooping around the house in the night? Who killed Mum? Miss Silver, please knit this together.
