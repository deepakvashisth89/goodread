This is not a new release for Carly but a remake of an earlier book with updates, however this was my first time reading Simply Sinful and I must say it's a very enjoyable romance with some sinfully delicious characters.

Kane and Kayla meet under the most unusual circumstances and while the chemistry is palpable from the beginning, Kayla is wary of trusting any man. Assigned to investigate her business as a front for a prostitution ring, Kane can't help but feel the attraction from the moment he meets Kayla. Kayla is a beautiful woman and mistrusts men because of their using her in the past. Kayla just wants to run her deceased Aunt's business with some changes but little does she know the business is not what it seems.

After Kane has one date with Kayla under the business umbrella he realizes this woman has nothing to hide and that her business is definitely not a cover for something else. But when she is attacked in the office with threats for wanting something she knows nothing about Kane takes it personal and decides that he will protect her at all costs.

This is a delightful fun, hot story and the chemistry is beyond off the charts just from reading between these characters, you won't want to miss getting this one, even if you read the original I believe you may enjoy the remake more!
