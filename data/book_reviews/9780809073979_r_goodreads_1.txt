A solidly readable account of an under-reported incident in American history, hobbled somewhat by the fact that the victim in this case - Lillie Orleman - left no writings of her own behind, and thus is mediated here almost exclusively by the men around her. We'll never know what she thought or felt about any of this, or about the men who used and abused her, but Barnett makes a fairly airtight case that she was abused, and that the abuse was covered up in order to preserve both social standards and patriarchal control. Poor girl.
