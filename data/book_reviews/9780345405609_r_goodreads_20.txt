This one wasn't as good as How Few Remain. I didn't enjoy it as much and it's worse written. HFR has eight POV characters who make the book feel like a "Where are they now?" of alternate history. This book has 20 - and it makes the whole thing feel rushed, scattered, and disorganised. Nothing has time to sit before you're carted away to the next character.Plot wise, nothing much happens. This book is more character driven whereas HFW wanted to set up the world more, which I'm totally fine with. The problem is, many characters are similar and you quickly lose track of who matters and who doesn't. I hope future books cull the character count back down to a reasonable 10 or so and focus on what matters plotwise.
