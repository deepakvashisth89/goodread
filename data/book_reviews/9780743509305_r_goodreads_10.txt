My first Vince Flynn and I seriously doubt whether this is one his best works. The plot is just run-of-the-mill with US trying to disarm Saddam Hussein of his nuclear weapons, who is on a diabolical plot to attack USA. Enter Mitch Rapp who is assigned the task of foiling the plot. Paralelly a couple of senators are trying to defame the CIA director and remove the POTUS. Eventually good prevails over evil.The action was fast paced and narrative interesting. However I thought I spotted a few minor grammatical errors here and there. Not exactly the best-thriller-I-ever-read kind of book.
