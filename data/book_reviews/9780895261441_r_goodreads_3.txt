Shocking, horrific, but not surprising.This book is a difficult read. The author masterfully lays out the treachery committed in seminaries from 1967-2002. Your heart can’t help but break for seminarians who were tortured, traumatized, and had their lives ruined because they were orthodox men trying to follow the vocation God placed on their heart. While reading this book, it seemed that I had encountered the worst evil imaginable on each page, but then I would turn the page and continue to read about even more deplorable activities.I highly suggest that Catholics read this book. It may explain the liberal nature of your parish, give you a better appreciation for orthodox clergy in your life, and enlighten you on the true value of the vocational program in your diocese. Liberal Catholics or those who promote women clergy, homosexual priests, and married priests should read this book and encounter what happens when those beliefs are put into action.The main point of the book is that our current shortage of priests is entirely “artificial and contrived.” How true that is. May God have mercy on all of the corrupted individuals who intentionally caused this shortage and committed despicable acts in the name of their agenda. I also pray that God can provide healing for all of the orthodox men who have suffered and are currently suffering in American seminaries.
