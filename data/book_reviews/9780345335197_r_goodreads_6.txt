I love Woody Guthrie! Sadly the author of this book somewhat tainted that for me. Joe Klein's liberalism-or-whatever sometimes misrepresents the politics of a deeply political character, and the book really suffers due to this sad fact. Klein describes racist rioters as, I believe, "a proletarian mob." Yuck. He even tries to rationalize someone's involvement in the KKK. In this book there is also an excess inclusion of private, lewd letters which Woody surely would not want to be read by you or me.This book is very flawed and I could only recommend it to staunch fans of folk music. It is very well researched but doesn't take itself too seriously which is refreshing in the genre of the artist-as-genius hagiographies, but again, wholly disappointing.
