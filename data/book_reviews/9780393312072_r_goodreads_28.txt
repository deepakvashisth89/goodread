A fascinating roller coaster ride of obsessive personal relationships, and continual self discovery. Patricia Bosworth neatly outlines the influences of Diane’s early years and ties to her family as her life story unfolds. The New York art world is a key player in the story including the artists and contemporaries who were socially and artistically relevant to her life and work. I found it useful to have a search engine handy to help illustrate the many historical references and images. I was moved by the scope of Diane Arbus' ambition and talent, her overwhelming drive to delve deep into her soul to find her voice and then to continue to live with those demons once they were released.
Sadly Diane struggled up until the end with her depressions, raw emotions toward public opinion, and wavering self confidence.
