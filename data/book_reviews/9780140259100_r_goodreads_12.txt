Wow. I loved this book. I felt like this was kind of similar to bell jar in a sense but I found myself really relating to the main character in a way I just couldn’t with bell jar. (maybe cause it’s a dude in the book not a girl?) I will say the beginning and end were SO engaging but the middle was just so confusing for me. There were a lot of confusing parts throughout the whole book and the whole time I felt I was missing out but the more I think about it the more I realize that’s kind of just the format of the book and it feels intentional. O’Brien would choose when to make it confusing or when to make it clear but getting through those confusing parts were really slow for me.The ending was so good. I loved the way it ended and Williams character development and how the characters grew together and just wow looking back this book was so good. It’s a different kind of good than most other books though- it felt like I was reading a memoir. Like I genuinely feel that William is my boy and there’s a chance I could see him on the street or something and say hi and maybe talk about how we’re both terminally insane.
