This is the most delightful of the series to date. Miss Seeton, for those unfamiliar with the series, has the talent of painting impressions of a person, a scene, a situation, without realizing that she does it. She had come to the attention of the police who have hired her to draw her impressions.
There is a strange new cult in town and it has captured the attention and the treasure of several villagers. The charismatic leader leads the followers on a strange path convincing them to give up their worldly goods and follow his lead without question.
Enter Miss Seeton, who manages to, unwittingly, save the day.
You cannot help but love this sweet old lady with her amazing talent for finding trouble, creating havoc and coming out of the whole thing as a shining star.
