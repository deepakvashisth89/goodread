the real motives which impel a man to take his own life are elsewhere; they belong to the internal world, devious, contradictory, labyrinthine, and mostly out of sight.

death is a kind of pornography, at once exciting and unreal.

all that remains to them is language.

equally deprived; the totalitarianism of the inner world.

since we can’t, apparently, control it on the outside, politically, we can at least try to control it in ourselves, artistically.

a last judgement in the coils and circuits of the brain.

the youth who swallowed the sleeping pills and the man who survived are so utterly different that someone or something must have died.
