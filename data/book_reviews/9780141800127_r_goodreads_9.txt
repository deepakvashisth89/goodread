I have never seen Stand By Me but I will after reading this book. Stephen King does have a way about him which engages the reader to the point of feeling you are in the story, experiencing all the unexpected turns. I do enjoy his writing.
This novel moves slower than others yet the intensity keeps a good pace. He describes four boys setting out on a lengthy journey to see a dead body. Their personalities could not be more different which creates a wonder balance of give and take. The flash forwards to the present day adds spice to this simmering pot of literary soup.
It was an enjoyable ride with a typical unpredictable conclusion which is King’s famous trademark.
