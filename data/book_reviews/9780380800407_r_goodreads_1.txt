I read the enjoyed two books by this author before (MUSTANG ANNIE and AN UNLIKELY LADY), so I eagerly sought out her backlist. I was really disappointed by this book. The heroine was so annoying! She was so guided by superstitions that it seemed she could barely think for herself. Instead, she was constantly jumping at shadows because they might be omens of death and insisting on doing stupid things because of these omens. For instance: shoving the hero into the horse trough to prevent the shadow of a passing hearse from touching him, otherwise he'll die. Or insisting the mail coach turn around because a rabbit ran across its path--and because of it, the mail coach fell in the river. Superstition just goes against the grain with me, and I couldn't be interested in a heroine who was a slave to it. Maybe other people will like it, but I didn't. I guess I should have checked out the book description better before ordering a copy. Oh well.
