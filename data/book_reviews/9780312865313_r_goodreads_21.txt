Chaco Canyon is my favorite place in the world, so of course I was excited to pick up a book about the Anasazi. The plot was enjoyable, though the description of the characters was repetitive. I enjoyed this book but don’t think I’ll read any others from these authors.As a New Mexican, I have set the record straight on two things in this book. It’s does NOT get 108 degrees in Albuquerque or Chaco Canyon. Yes, summers are hot. Yes, we do get temps over 100. But this is not the norm. The hottest temp ON RECORD at Chaco Canyon is 106 a long time ago.Second, there is mention in the book how much New Mexicans love the Mexican food in New Mexico. Sure, we like Mexican food. But the draw is NEW Mexican food. This is quite different, and an important differentiation. You don’t want to mix the two up in front of a Nee Mexican.Thank you for coming to my TED Talk.
