This was a pretty good book. It came across as a bare bones story but still had a full story with beginning middle and end. I didn't find it to be scary but more of and romance mystery.It starts off with the main female character comes is on sabbatical for a year and goes to a remote location to basically research and build a case on a fire that was set that her dad owned from her childhood. Everyone thought he had been the one to set it due to it beinv insured very well. This resulted in her having abandonment issues and unable to let anyone too close in any relationships and no desire to be in a serious one.Upon arrival of the house she is to stay in for a year, she meets her landlord who is drop dead georgus and you can guess what follows in that budding relationship. He is staying in a cottage off to the side of the house also taking a break to heal up from being hit by a car.The house was passed down through his family and is currently his so he decided to rent it out while staying in the cottage. It comes with a mystery concerning the death of one of his ancestors who was a successful writer in which everyone thought he committed suicide due to the inability to write anymore. Low and behold he haunts the mansion appearing to need her help in finding the truth on how he died. Throughout the story a very mysterious black cat enters that is above average intelligence and has his own opinions.All in all I liked the story but felt it could have been a little more fleshed out. I really liked the ambience of the location and the developing relationship. Not inclined to re-read it though.
