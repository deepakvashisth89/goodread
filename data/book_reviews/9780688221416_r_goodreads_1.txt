I read this when I was a teenager and even years later I still remember it very clearly so it was a joy to find it again. Of course, my thoughts on the book are a little bit different today then when I was a teenager, but the two main impressions about the book are the same: 1. the realistic emotional turmoil Damarias goes through trying to fit in to a very different life style than she is use to and her romantic feelings for two different young men; 2. the historical detail of a shaker community.

It is not easy to make a historical fiction book interesting to most teenagers but this one does particularly by being true to a teenager's emotional life without too much exaggeration one way or the other as well as depicting shaker life in a way that makes it live.
