Wow what a great find. I really had no expectations about this one. The amazon discriptions were at time a little much but the story was great and totally different. She was the woman scorned and her "best" friend was a complete bitch. She was just blissfully happy to steel her fiancee. well we love each other and you should be happy for us cause we love each other! Are you for real? if she was a friend this would not have happened however I am happy she ended up with Scott cause he really loved her. he put her best interests first and did not push the physical until she pushed him to end of his rope. I was ok with the whole forced seduction thing as she admitted she wanted it and then lied to him about sleeping with his brother. Mark was a complete ass with his inviting her to wedding ( come and see your love marry another it should be fun for you, cause I want everyone to think I am such a great guy, really asshole?) Scott was a prince and she was a complete mess he really put her back together. I just loved the story. my only problem was the secondary characters Mark and her "BFF" Elaine. They should have had more compassion for Alexa. Alexa felt real to me and that is rare for me now a day when character in books seem two-dimensional. She and Scott were the whole package.
