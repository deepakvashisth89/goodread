I knew that Larry McMurtry would end my three-month-long streak of disappointing fiction. I foundLeaving Cheyennedifficult to put down. Though it is not as plot-driven as my all time favorite,Lonesome Dove, you can definitely see the seeds of Gus and Call here, which I loved. I was really touched by these characters and captivated by the strange and moving love triangle that forms the foundation of this novel. Really beautiful, unpretentious writing.
