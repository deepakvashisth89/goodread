This is a collection of Saberhagen's Berserker stories. The Berserkers are robotic AIs who want to wipe-out all organic life, and were surely an inspiration to the Daleks, Cylons, and Terminators. They had almost all been previously collected in other Saberhagen collections, so I felt somewhat cheated, especially since the bottom of the cover prominently claims, "First Edition." There are several nice full-page drawings to illustrate the stories, but the publisher didn't credit the artist. There is a framing element that tries to link the individual stories together like a fix-up novel, but that didn't really do much. The first story is labeled "Introduction," but is a first-person narrative from a fictional character.
