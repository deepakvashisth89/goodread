I really loved this book.Is it dated? Yes.Would it fail a writing class today? Probably.However, it is a story that doesn't make any pretense. It sets out to be a simple story to get Christians to think. It begs the reader to examine their own life and ask if they are living with "What Would Jesus Do" in mind.It is an imperfect book, that asks the reader to ask with our Perfect Saviour might be asking of them.
