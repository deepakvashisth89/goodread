I first sawThe Legend of Bagger Vancemovie when I was a young boy. Every subsequent viewing has moved me just as much as the first time, if not more. The movie has many notable differences, though it isn't better or worse than the book. Just different. What makes Pressfield's story so great is that Bagger's wisdom can be applied to any profession or vocation. Absolutely anything at all. For me, every word Bagger speaks (allowing for changes in terminology, of course) fits with how I perceive writing. To see it laid before me on paper, in ways better than I could ever hope to articulate, sent goosebumps over my entire body. Not only a wonderful story that even non-golfers (such as myself) can enjoy, but a veritable guidebook for life.
