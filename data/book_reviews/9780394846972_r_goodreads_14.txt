As with many older history books, the reader will need to edit outdated terms on the fly, but it's not onerous, and I think the chance to follow these pilgrims from their early life, troubles in England, life in Holland, and through the extraordinary providence and work it took to plant and sustain Plymouth is worth it. I appreciated the addition of William Bradford's journal, the Mayflower Compact, and some of Miles Standish's journals in the text, too.
