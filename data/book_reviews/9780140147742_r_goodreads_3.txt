[3.6] Reading these stories in the morning over the last few weeks has been a meditative experience. Not that I've ever successfully meditated - but how I imagine meditation might work. Gradually settling me and letting my worries drift away as I focus on Stegner's writing. The early stories, about rural boyhood, resonated with me the most. As the collection progressed, some of the stories fell flat for me and I was tempted to rush through them.
