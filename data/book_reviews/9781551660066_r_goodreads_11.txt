First book I have read from this author. I enjoy a good romance story and having a manly cowboy with charm had appeal. I enjoyed the character of Ten Blackburn immensely. The romance was hot and I liked how Ten took his time, patiently & carefully, as the heat built between him and the damaged Diana. Where I found downfall with the story was the overuse of Ten calling Diana “baby,” that just didn’t work for me, and I was turned off wirh Diana’s need to have Ten’s baby inside her.
