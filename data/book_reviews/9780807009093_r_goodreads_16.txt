This was an interesting story. I'd give this a solid 4 stars and would love to read more by this author!

Angela wasn't necessarily a "likeable" character, but she was written with a lot of depth. I was happy to see how she developed over the course of the novel, especially with her stance on "passing" and representing herself in the world as a Black (or white) woman. Roger really pissed me off on several occasions and I really couldn't stand her dating him!!

I first learned of the author from reading and have since made it my mission to check out her work, since she influenced so many notable Black American writers. This was great over audio and had an interesting concept of different "acts" of the book.
