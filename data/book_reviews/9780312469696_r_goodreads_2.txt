Dunlap has four detectives going now, and this one from the eighties is set in a small, Northern California town, which, predictably, is populated by quirky characters. The main character is Vejay Haskell, a meter reader which makes for an amateur detective most U.S. readers will have never encountered. This is the third book in the series and my library does not have the first two.
