Absolutely fantastic storytelling. If you liked the movie, you will like this. If you, like me, are not fond of horror stories, this is not one. I appreciated the craft of this fiction as the story unfolded.I listened to this novella. The story was so interesting that my mind did not wander at all and I wanted to listen even when not commuting. The narrator was very good, although not Morgan Freeman — but then, they broke that mold and you generally can’t have everything. There are some descriptive explanations about prison sex that some may have a bit of trouble with. With an audio book, you could just fast forward.I always enjoy a first person narrative. Themes developed in the story are hope, perseverance, adaptability and friendship. This is largely a story about friendship. There are some passages about the importance of hope to human life. The novella was obviously well researched, such that an escape could seem plausible. There are some interesting historical tidbits about prisons and the development of concrete.5 stars for storytelling, and prompting me to overcome my squeamish side to take on more by Stephen King.
