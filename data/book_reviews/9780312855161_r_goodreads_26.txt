In contrast to the other two books in The Way series I found this one a hard slog to read and a bit tedious at times. The only connection to the other two books is the Olmy character, here depicted as a youth on his first assignment for the government of the Way, as such the events in this book predate his later adventures in Eon and Eternity. He must follow 4000 revolutionists who have illegally used the way to populate a planet and run it the way that they want. He ends up spending a lifetime on this other planet, before finally finding his way back to the Way and rejuvenation.

By no means a must read, if you are a fan of the other two books, but it does provide some background on this central character.
