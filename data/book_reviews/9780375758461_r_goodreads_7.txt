It is an impossible task to explain to someone who is not a fellow philosophy obsessive why I agonized over reading the words of a 17th century thinker whose greatest contribution is laying the groundwork for what we would now refer to as “common sense”. Had to be done. Could not not read it.Bacon expresses in 200 pages what could be expressed in 50. Induction is a valid means of acquiescing knowledge so long as it is tempered with Reason, but Reason must be checked and cannot always be trusted. Learning is an end unto itself. Religion is where Imagination overtakes Reason. Aristotle, albeit brilliant, is not infallible and should be scrutinized like anyone else. Explosively radical for mid 1600s England.
