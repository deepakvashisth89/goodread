Interesting story! If I give too much away, I will take away some of the interest and fun and enjoyment in reading the book so I shall shut my pie-hole. Except to say that it took place in Padua Italy and there is a luxuriant garden involved as well as a beautiful damsel. I did learn from reading the foreword by Simon Schama that Ralph Waldo Emerson kicked Hawthorne out of his house (The Manse) near Boston for not paying rent. I also learned that he went to Bowdoin College, and one of his friends and classmates was Franklin Pierce who became the 14th President of the United States. And he knew Elizabeth Peabody who founded the kindergarten movement in America, and he married her sister (Sophia).
