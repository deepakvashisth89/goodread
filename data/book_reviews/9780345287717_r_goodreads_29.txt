The third installment of the Giants series tidies up many of the loose ends in the series plot, but it does leave a few. For example, Hogan has fun with time travel paradoxes that go with his version of FTL. At one point near the end, he asks, How could one group of aliens be the descendants of their own descendants.

As Harry Harrison tells us in Bill, the Galactic Hero, they get pretty tricky with the old Bloater Drive.
