Tired from his Oz series, L. Frank Baum tried his hand at a new fantasy story in 1911. It followed a similar pattern to Oz where whimsy reigns, playful dialogue abounds, and plot is an afterthought. I really like Cap’n Bill and Trot though they don’t do much other than watch. They eventually find their way into future Oz books which makes this separate series a part of the Oz universe. Unlike Oz where your attention is kept by the tour of unique and strange inhabitants, this one confines itself to the ocean so that everyone and everything must be aquatic themed. With no plot in sight for the first 100 pages or so, it tires very quickly (even at Oz’s lowest points we had the expectation of making our way to the Emerald City). This could have been a fun departure from Oz if there had been stronger conflict and forward movement of the story. Baum found that out the hard way with poor sales. He tried his hand one more time with Cap’n Bill and Trot before returning to Oz.
