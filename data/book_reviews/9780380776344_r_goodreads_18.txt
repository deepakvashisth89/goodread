Re-read..book is intense in the feelings that come from reading about the treatment given to the 'inmates' of the mental institution. I do realize that white people in these institutions were treated just as badly, but in an earlier time. The treatment of Indians in this country has been nothing to brag about...but at the same time, these things should not be hidden, they need to be exposed, and stopped.

The book is not an 'enjoyable' read, but one that made me think...
