The 14th book in the Famous Five series by Enid Blyton. I'm currently rereading them all, taking a great trip down memory lane. These were among my favourite childhood books.This one sees the Five spending the last three weeks of their holiday at Kirrin. They're looking forward to lots of fun together in the weeks ahead when suddenly their plans are thrown into disarray. A little blonde haired American girl comes to stay with them and she is under threat of kidnap.Of course we all know a big adventure follows!
