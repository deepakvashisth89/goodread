HR’s I will always recommend!I read Joanna when I was 15 and twenty years later I still re-read it and enjoy it the exact same way! Now I can compare to other books by Gellis, Joanna is my still my favourite with the right balance of romance and history. I love the family vibes, I adore Geoffrey and how he compliments Joanna’s feisty personality! And I especially love the portrayal of strong women in this medieval time period.
