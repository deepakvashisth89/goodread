This is a heartfelt farewell to a series that has brought me immense happiness and rekindled my passion for reading, which had been somewhat dormant over the past few years. It feels to me that stories like this have become increasingly rare in our modern times.The final book in this series completed the story perfectly, exceeding my expectations and resonating even more deeply than the earlier installments. It was filled with themes of war, loss, and sorrow, yet amidst the pervasive darkness, there were glimpses of hope, light, and even moments of laughter that provided relief.For me, the characters are what elevate this series to greatness. They are the heart and soul of the story, and I could spend years discussing how much I love them. While I won’t detail my feelings here, I believe these characters will stay with me long into old age, offering comfort and inspiration.Frodo, in particular, stands out as the one protagonist who exemplifies that vulnerability can be a source of strength and that needing assistance is entirely acceptable when faced with insurmountable challenges. My admiration for him grew beyond words; he is unquestionably the most compelling character in the entire series, embodying resilience amid fragility.In conclusion, it is with a mixture of sadness and gratitude that I bid farewell to these characters and this story. I am comforted by the fact that I still have 'The Hobbit' to read, which will allow me to revisit this magical world once more, though this time, I will be missing some of the characters that have become so dear to me.
