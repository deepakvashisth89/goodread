I see how this book might seem outdated for some people, but for someone who's just dipping their toes on this kind of information this book brought revelations. It gets quite preachy at some points but only in order to make you understand it's contents better.

It teaches you about that TNT within you, your fullest potential, that internal power and drive you can make use of to get where you want in life. It can only be unlocked with a positive thinking and the right mental images that will train your subconscious to give you the right premonition which, accompanied by a clever judgement and precautions, will lead you to the right path. It talks about how our mind is influenced by external suggestions and our own fears and worries born from previous experiences, and how that can damage our thinking patterns and make it difficult for us to manifest our needs. But if get rid of those, believe in ourselves, work hard and stay positive we will get far in life.

Not sure if I'm 100% sold, but it taught me that being so negative is doing me more harm than good (as similar things attract each other)
