I was born and raised Catholic so angels have always been a part of my belief system.I found it very interesting to learn about the different tiers of angels and how they can help us in our daily lives. Much of what the late Sylvia Browne has spoken of resonates with my own personal beliefs.After reading this book I have found myself meditating and reaching out to the different phylum of angels and asking for their help with a problem of my own or with a loved ones. I actually found a lot of comfort and peace reading the different chapters and learning how to incorporate an angel's love and protection into my daily life.I feel this is a must read for anyone who is feeling a little lost and needs that extra help to start finding their way. I may not be consciously aware of interacting with an angel but I now understand how I could have and not even known it.
