Mark Richards is surprised when he comes home to his bachelor apartment and finds three kids there. His late brother’s wife (Ardie) left them with him, along with a brief note, and then vanished without a trace. Wanting to find Ardie he hires private investigator Kerri Kincaid. They eventually discover his brother’s death may not have been an accident and the children may be in danger.

This was a very enjoyable read. Kerri was a likable heroine. It was nice how her family took the children into their home while they were trying to find Ardie. And it was fun seeing Mark, who wanted nothing to do with the children, suddenly grow to want to look out for them and protect them. Mark and Kerri made a nice couple. I was drawn in to the suspense plot, curious to see what was going to happen. A good Harlequin Intrigue from the 90’s.
