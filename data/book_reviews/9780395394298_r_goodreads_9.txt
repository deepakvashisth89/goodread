This volume, while largely a (further) re-hash of Simarillion material, nonetheless adds a few interesting tidbits/concepts to the fold, while the style of writing is a nice touch, using [as some astute reviews already noted] verse rather than prose to give it a more funereal [IMO] feel.So, while I'm enjoying (for the most part) this series thus far.... I'm also finding it somewhat wearing on me (akin to Bilbo holding the One Ring for so many years), and I find my interest in Tolkien scholarship actually beginning to slightly diminish as I read on.Maybe it's because there's so much new and compelling literature out there on my plate to get to?So, I'm sticking with the series for now, but maybe a break before diving into Book 4...
