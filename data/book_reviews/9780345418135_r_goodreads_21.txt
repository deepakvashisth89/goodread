Editing is a let downThe story is worth 5 stars if you like a good courtroom drama. But the editing is woeful. Very wordy in parts, the inner thought of every character is included. Constant referral to ‘Terri’ as ‘Tern’ is pathetic, given the existence of spellcheck, and someone needs to lose the italics key. The incessant use of emphasis makes all characters appear to be in a state of high drama, and assumes the reader cannot impose their own inflections.
