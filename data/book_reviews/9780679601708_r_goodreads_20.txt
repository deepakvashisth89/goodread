I have copies of both the print and audiobook versions of this work. I like both, but the audiobook version I especially enjoyed because the narrator was Shelby Foote himself.This work is excerpts from Foote's "The Civil War, Volume 2: Fredericksburg to Meridian". It is a complete recounting of Grant's campaign to capture Vicksburg, including all the attempts that failed, and auxiliary actions to assist this effort. Foote's writing is spare, to the point and gets to the character of the campaigns' participants and the action without encumbering the story with hero worship.
