Wendell Berry’s body of work only has one book categorized under the 121 subsection of the Dewey Decimal System. Epistemology. And this is Berry’s epistemological tour de force. The majority of the book is spent treating Edward O. Wilson’s Consilence on how to reconcile science and religion/the arts. The heart of it comes after this treatment as Berry deviates from Wilson’s essay structure to devote several chapters to Reduction and Religion, Reduction and Art and his own gloss on how the two fields can be reconciled. (Or exist on their own terms) Most potent for me the final chapter “Some Notes in Conclusion”. More approachable than most philosophical works I have read, Berry’s ability to speak to our moment of history in the context of USA’s agriculture is singular in its impact.Show more
