Note: An excellent Covid-19 read.
The Iceberg Hermit by Roth is a YA book from the 1970s that would benefit from a few more pages, just a bit more development, based on my second reading in the 21st Century. Age-wise, my eight-year-old son looked forward to this as a bedtime storybook.

I love this story, based on the actual experience of young Allan Gordon of Aberdeen, Scotland. In 1756, the 17-year-old is on a whaling ship near Greenland, which smashes into, and then onto, an iceberg. Allan is the only survivor.

No spoilers here!
