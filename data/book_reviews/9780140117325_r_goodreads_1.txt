PROTAGONIST: Mac Fontana, fire chiefSETTING: Small town in Washington StateSERIES: #1RATING: 3.5WHY: Mac Fontana is a fireman who had an incident when working back East; he's now serving as the chief in the small town of Staircase, WA. The mayor asks him to temporarily serve as sheriff. In that role, he investigates the disappearance of Seattle fireman Steve Zajax. He has to deal with the politics of the department as wwell as a sexy woman who confuses the heck out of him. There are several deaths, and his own life is threatened. After the investigation, he is more than happy to give up the sheriff job. Firefighting details are well done and could only have been written by someone with deep experience.
