So old- fashioned, it's fashionable. This is the coziest little novel/memoir (?) with a kind of Russian Mary Poppins character. It describes so sweet and clean an era it's naive in its nostalgia (published in 1947 about the 1920s). Changing sparkplugs costs $3. The doctor prescribes port, sherry, and brandy during Prohibition. People lend money and feed strangers.Adorable illustrations. And the recipes all sound good. There are many I want to try and I might even have a dinner party made with Katish's recipes.
