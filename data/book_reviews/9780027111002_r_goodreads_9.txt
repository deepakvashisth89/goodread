I personally did not like this book. To me, it felt like such a negative portrayal of a sibling relationship. I have three children, and although of course they argue, they overall are good friends and appreciate and celebrate one-another. I think I might have liked it better if there was a lesson in this story of how they worked to see the great qualities in each-other. I didn't pre-read this before I read it to my kiddos (4, 7 and 9), but we had some good discussions about this sibling relationship and what they could possibly do to see the good in, help and learn from one-another instead of just constantly being annoyed.
