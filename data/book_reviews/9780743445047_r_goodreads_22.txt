This is an intriguing novel. It seems to be a sequel to the celebrated British TV series of the 1960s rather than a novelisation of that series as the cover and tagline would suggest. It’s beautifully written and very unsettling, but at times quite confusing, which is the intended effect. This is all about scrambling mental equilibrium and breaking down personalities through gaslighting and paranoia.

I enjoyed the read but will probably not revisit. The author provides a few disturbingly accurate predictions of a technological dystopia, describing with great precision how to make deepfakes or how to perceive and manipulate dreams through brain scans. This was published in 1969, but could be describing today!
