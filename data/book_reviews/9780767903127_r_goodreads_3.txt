Professional music critics usually drain the life out of their subjects, but in this case Davis does a good job covering the entire career of the Rolling Stones. Many biographies on this subject tend to peter out after the death of Brian Jones and the Altamont concert, but Davis covers the last few decades very well. With the changes in musical tastes of the 1980's, Mick Jagger wanted a more "modern" sound, while Keith Richards wanted to stay close to the band's roots. It led to solo recordings and disagreement for a while.For a general biography of the band, this one is a good starting point.
