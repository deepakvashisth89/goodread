Angela Benbow, 5 foot tall, a bit tubby, a little flirty and a rich Admiral's widow along with her friend Caledonia Wingate, a gigantic woman with a booming voice who wears nothing but large caftans, and is also very rich Admiral's widow are back in this second mystery by Corinne Holt Sawyer. They are both residents at Camden-sur-Mer, a retirement center located on the California coast. The murder victim this time is a resident that only recently moved into the center and was not well liked by anyone. Angela and Caledonia decide they can be of great help to Inspector Martinez in solving the murder and end up in a lot of trouble. I even smile while writing my review because this series is so much fun. It's a good mystery with wonderful characters.
