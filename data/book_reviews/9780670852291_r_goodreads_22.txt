i was entertained but disappointed. this is my fifth iris murdoch novel. if she set out to create the most irritating male characters in this one, she succeeded. bellamy james and clement graffe are pitiful, and not in an endearing way. a little too magical, not to mention that all loose ends are gratuitously tied up with bows. sefton's story is my favorite, aleph is a symbol rather than a character (and, come to think of it, so is lucas), and moy, despite her telekinetic abilities, is the most realistic! and at 500 pages, it's 200 too long. the angst over the green knight in the final 150 pages is just too much. too many characters. who the hell even is connie? cora? no clue. hopefully the next one i read will be better.
