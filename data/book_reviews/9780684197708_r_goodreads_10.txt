4+ stars.Rounding up because there is quite a bit about this book that should not gave worked--a plot whose key points and conclusions should have been less than satisfying, some protagonist behavior and relationship evolution that could have been cheap and off-putting--and yet this author is so skillful that it effortlessly did all work together.No reader irritation or disappointment here, though I am a prickly reader. I even enjoyed the opera context despite not being a particular fan of opera and carrying prejudice against stage-life novel settings and the stereotypical drama queen characters who tend to inhabit them.This was absorbing, engaging, rewarding, original, and not easy to guess It features a bittersweet ending with credible dramatic promise for future books.Crombie is an understated talent and this novel leaves me wanting more in the best ways.
