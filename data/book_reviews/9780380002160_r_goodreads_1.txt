I read this book long ago, and began reading it again last week. I LOVE this book. I love the writing, the story, the way the author portrays tragic circumstances as something to be overcome, transcended, and, in the end, learned from -- making a person stronger and the heart, larger.

That's the hope.

Hope. Something free to us all, no matter our circumstances or the size of our wallets.

I think it a testament to the writing and story of any book that stays with a person over a lifetime. I have to say this will always be one of my all-time favorite books.

I especially appreciate the skills of authors who can "manipulate" a readers' emotions and make them cry -- both tears of sadness and joy.

Thank you, Suzanne Clauser, for such an amazing novel. My only disappointment is that you're not a Goodreads author, and I can't become a fan.
