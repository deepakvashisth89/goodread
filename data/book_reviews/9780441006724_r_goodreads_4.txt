A little slow to get going, but once it does, this book is full of intrigue and some fascinating different factions playing off each other.

Some characters were really enjoyable, mainly Roche, Maii, and Kajic. Others felt a little flat or underexplored, including some of the main cast.

The ending was a bit lackluster in resolving some plot points of this book, but sets things up nicely for the rest of the series. Still a lot to explore with this setting, and I'm eager to read it.
