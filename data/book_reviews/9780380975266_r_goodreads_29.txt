The book is compelling in every manner. One compelling aspect lies on how the book captures our daily lives as we try to become the Light of the world in a place full of dark temptations. It is a book where we all can make a certain resemblance to as it expresses the common struggle and remedy people who live in the Light has through the main characters of the story.With both good and evil existing in the book and adding on, a thrilling twist, the Grail is a highly recommendable book to everyone who enjoys mysterious, yet adventurous books.
