Okay, Ryo is really beginning to piss me off. He is hypocritical in his dealings (kinda). He tells Dee not to run off seeking his own justice, then does the same thing. All by-the-book, then goes postal (multiple occasions over the series).Mostly, though... we are five books in (out of 7) and this guy can't even admit if he is even remotely interested in his partner. We were at the "make a decision" point 2 books ago.He doesn't need to jump into bed with Ryo (though, at this rate, that will happen long before he even admits he likes men, let alone Dee), but Dee has no clue whether this guy even likes him. Dee has real feelings and Ryo is stringing him along, enjoying the attention, but too scared to commit.
