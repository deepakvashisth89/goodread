I loved this book because it was a quick read that grabbed your attention, regardless of it's predictability. But then again, most crime novels are like that. I, however, enjoyed it. I have a huge weak spot for crime novels. The plot itself was intricate but not to the point where you were aching for what would happen next. It wasn't that suspenseful.The characters were alright. I felt like Marielle was "too perfect" even though she had a rough past. But overall the characters weren't terrible. The love pentagon was a lot to take in.Overall, the book was decent. Definitely nothing to RAVE about but a great beach read/quick read. Keeps you interested.If you pick up this book, happy reading. (:
