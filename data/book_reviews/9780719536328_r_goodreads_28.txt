Thoughts...I was not in the right headspace for this collection. I found it stuffy and a little puerile in places. I had this impression of him as a really solid poet... maybe when you throw the kitchen sink at someone, they'll get a bit put off by the rusting taps and ignore the skillful tiling. What I mean is, this seems like a lot of poems that are perhaps distracting me from the good ones.In the end, I didn't get to the end of this. I read some good. Some bad. Then skimmed a few. Then put this book down and didn't pick it back up again. Maybe I'll pick it up at some point in the future, when I'm ready for it. Until then... a very rare occurrence for me... DNF
