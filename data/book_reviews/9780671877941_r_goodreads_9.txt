This was drudgery to read. While it is clearly of true military nature, the sentences are to overloaded with military jargon and unnecessary descriptions and explanations that the sentences are very difficult to follow. That in turn slowed the pace to a crawl. The plot progresses to two thirds the way through the book then, apparently the book wasn't long enough, so the author added other elements that seemed almost alien to the first part.The characters weren't developed enough for me to empathize with any but the reporter and the boy.I can't recommend the book.
