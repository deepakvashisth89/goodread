The style is superb, and the story is captivating. A man leaves his home and becomes infatuated with a very young, poor girl who leads him to luxury and money. She will be his and will play with him. But, unfortunately, he, too, in love, will be blind to all the perversions and shenanigans.I prefer this one to Lolita for the pacing and plot.For my part, Lolita is too repetitive in its sentences, in its enthusiasm, and in its adoration for its nymphet.
