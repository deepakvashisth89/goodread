The book isn't an easy read; I think it could have benefited from a better editor. It's quite repetitive and includes details that don't seem particularly relevant, especially concerning technologies that were already outdated when the book was published.However, I can see why this book was important in the early 2000s. It introduced some key concepts and provided an in-depth study on innovation, attempting to identify patterns and formulas. The section on the electric car industry was especially interesting.
