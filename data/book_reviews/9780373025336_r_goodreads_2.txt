There have been many Harlequin FMCs in these vintage books who were notable for just how TSTL they were, but this one takes the crown, without question - an immature and lazy spoiled brat who never should have been let out of her nursery. The MMC rescues her on two occasions not even halfway through the book, and she's belligerent, ungrateful and petulant in return. What a charmer!
