Betjemen is not a poet I would have actively sought out but had an awareness of without much background knowledge. I found one of these little pocket poet books on my shelf that I must have picked up from a free library or such like (I didnt buy it) with an inscription on the inside suggesting it was gifted/owned by a Nick McConaghey in 1976 - . Betjemen is what I would describe as wearing his englishness on his sleeve- his work is very centred on English life, social class, architecture and urban/rural living. It is also filled with humour and nostalgia. As an Irish person I cannot relate too much to his upbringing however having lived in London for 22 years and travelled much of the country including coastal towns I can imagine with vividness the images he is portraying. I have also lived and worked in NW6 and familiar with his references to buildings and places he references in North London which has become nostalgia to me for the life I had there.
