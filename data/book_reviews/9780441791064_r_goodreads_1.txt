This is a military-sf anthology featuring tanks in future warfare. It's my favorite of the ones that the amazingly prolific Martin Harry Greenberg produced on that theme. It has a Bolo story by Keith Laumer, and nice offerings from Gene Wolfe, George R.R. Martin, Stephen Leigh, Walter M. Miller, Jr., and Arsen Darnay. David Drake is the master of military sf, and his Hammer's Slammers series is the ultimate look at future-engines-of-war, and his Hangman represents him well here. My favorite in this book, though, is the original version of Roger Zelazny's Damnation Alley, which has my all-time genre favorite last line.
