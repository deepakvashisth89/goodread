I am pleased to report that I have finished this book, a gift from a dear friend of mine, David. I thought Orchid and Kiss and Tell were the best ones, although I do believe that the scene on the airplane was wonderfully done, albeit a hard read. The entire book just reminds me of so many impossible to answer questions that I have had myself in my relationships with family, friends, and romantic partners. The guilt, the happiness, the doubt, the tragic endings, and the injustice for being wronged are all things one has to accept and deal with. All of this novel was brutally raw and totally human. The quotes that this book reminds me of specifically are that “I am a museum of everything I have ever loved”, and “I am a mosaic of every person I have ever loved.” That’s the god honest truth.
