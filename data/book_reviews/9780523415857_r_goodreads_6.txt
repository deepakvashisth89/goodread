Yikes! This was bad. Makes the 1981 film version seem like a competent thriller. The writing is boring, lazy, and confusing — characters speak but there’s no indication of whose dialogue it is! I had trouble following the action that was happening, and I’ve seen the movie. In all honesty, Final Exam might be the weirdest movie in cinematic history to write a tie-in novelization for. Skip this dreadful book and instead watch the (silly but entertaining) movie with a few cold beers ready to go.
