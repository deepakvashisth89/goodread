Both of these children's books are not that well written, but they show children that it pays to be polite and kind to others. Helping friends is very important. Dorothy and her silver slippers defeat bad witches and help her friends. We do have magic within us to help us when it is most needed! The Marvelous Land of Oz is rather silly with silly characters: more bad witches, pumpkins and saw horses which come alive with magic, evil women who do great work, but want to take over the world, and jackdaws who feather their nests with money, which later saves the scarecrow's life, as it were. Again friendship is very important here. Not great, but given the 75th anniversary of the movie, everyone should read the parent and original before seeing the movie again. Not to worry about the flying monkeys. In the book, the monkeys are good!
