I had no idea this book inspired a Hallmark movie and T.V. series, so I had no preconceived ideas before starting this series. I was purely interested in a book featuring a teacher (like me) who helped settle the Canadian West.

The theme of the first installment of this series is ‘time to move on.’ Elizabeth Thatcher’s strong will gets her in a predicament and she soon realizes that she’s leaving a life of luxury and heading to a teaching job in the Canadian Rockies. Her teacherage is full of challenges and she navigates each with her newfound self-reliance.

I appreciated being reminded about the organization and background of our Canadian Mounties and their role in our country’s history. The male main character, Wynn Delaney, is a Mountie.

I learned about Calgary’s former names: Fort Brisebois gave way to Fort Calgary. It was a Gaelic word meaning clear, running water.

Janette Oke’s story about misunderstandings, restitution and forgiveness was a quick, sweet read and one I read free through Amazon’s Kindle Unlimited program
