4 stars.

He is a smitten, passionate, virile, jealous, dominant, mega rich, handsome H. He is everything a woman would want and more. Wow.

The reason that I don’t give this HP 5 stars, but 4 stars is because of the h. She seems to always cry or when she doesn’t cry, she doesn’t feel well. She slaps him in his face out of emotion. She just comes across as mentally unstable.

I hated the scene in which she thinks he is going away from her and she follows him and she throws herself at him and she practically begs him to stay and she cries and cries. I just hate it when the woman throws herself at the man’s feet.

She goes so often to the hairdresser and have manicures that she even needs to borrow money from the H. Wtf? She’s like a superficial Kim Kardashian with nothing to worry about other than her looks and dressing sexy.

Also: having sex with the H on the same night that her engagement to another man would have been announced, is way too quick. She was a virgin until then, but still. She hardly knew the H and she was thinking that she loved the other man.

And she’s the only one in Italy that didn’t know that she was the beneficiary of a family fortune. How come everyone knew and she didn’t. She knew about the rumours. If she had half a brain, she would have tried to find out the truth.

The first half of the book was a bit dreary. Too much about what the h was thinking and feeling made one evening last around 70 pages or so.
