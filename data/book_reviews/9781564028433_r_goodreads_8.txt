I don't consider this an essential picturebook to have in a collection. It wouldn't make a bad storytelling book with all the pirate word play, and there's plenty of action to explore in the illustrations with kids. However, all the pirate students looked the same and that kept irking my eye. There exists more than one nose to plant on a face. Didn't Mr. Potato Head teach us that, if nothing else? Take the kids out and show them yourself how to follow a treasure map, maraud the king's navy and sing a lusty shanty about good Jamaican rum.
