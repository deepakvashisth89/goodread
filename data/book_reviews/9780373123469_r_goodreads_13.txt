I am so angry because it could have been very good, but it is only quite average.The plot is quite usual but with a twist, the hero’s lil brother is friend with the heroine and introduces her to the hero because she wants to interview him and have a job. They fall in love, and have three months of bliss and sex. Younger brother is in love with the heroine but she already told him she doesn’t love him and sees him only as a friend, this before she met the hero.After three months, the hero has to leave for his sister’s wedding and he doesn’t ask the heroine to go with him. The heroine is annoyed, but she goes anyway because she had promised his lil brother that she would go if he introduced her to his older brother. At the wedding the heroine sees the hero with another woman and lil brother tells the heroine that she is his fiancée and that he saw them the very morning while they were making out. The heroine is shattered. She and the hero have a confrontation, he breaks up with her. She leaves and rejects the job he was going to offer, but some weeks later she finds out she’s pregnant. Hero’s lil brother is very angry and upset, he goes out and has a fatal car accident. The heroine tells the hero she’s pregnant, he proposes, she refuses. The twist: the hero doesn’t insist, they co parent.Five years later suddenly the hero decides the heroine has to marry him and they have to be a family. They have sex, all is well, ow makes mischief again, explanations. Basically lil brother lied to both of them, he told the hero he was going to marry the heroine and when the hero told him he was dating the heroine, he told him she was only doing to further her career. The hero believes his lies. He also lied to the heroine because the hero was not engaged with ow, they dated long before he met the heroine but were never suited.So both were deceived.This could have been a good point for a very good story, even with the new and unusual thing of coparenting, but it was never this. The hero was never nasty and neither was the heroine, they were civil and he only was jealous when he saw her with another man , but this was five years too late. Where’s the angst that the heroine should have felt when she understood that he only wanted her for a but of fun on the side while he was engaged? Where is the angst when the hero decided to dump her, believing she only used her for her career? Where’s the angst when she was pregnant and alone and gave birth to their son? The hero seems to be quite a good father, and in the end he also reveals he has been celibate for five years because for him it was always her. So where is his love, his pining, his possessiveness, where is his desire to be with her? They seldom meet and they are always cold. When the heroine thinks he’s going to marry another woman she’s only a bit upset, how could she be that cold if she loved her? And both confess they felt guilty for lil brother accident, so they decided not to pursue a relationship. Really? And where is the angst of this loss, a loss of a love that they apparently both feel but they can’t have because they believe some lies? They both are always cold and controlled. To me it’s a wasted chance. The three stars is because he was celibate but I didn’t feel that great love between them.
