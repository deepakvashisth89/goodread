I bought this book for a laugh, but it is actually really interesting. While it is old and hence a bit out of date, it provides some amazing insight into the The Rock's roots. It is easy to read and hard to put down once you get into it.There are about 50 pages that are about certain legendary WWF matches and are written from the perspective of the "The Rock"'s ring persona. I did not care for these much, but I'm sure any Wrestling fan (I'm just a Rock fan) will enjoy them.
