3.5 stars (rating seen may vary depending on whether the site allows half star ratings)I've read other Chicken Soup books in the past, though not recently. I thought I'd read this before, and yes, a few of the stories did seem familiar, but many did not, so I must have read a different book from the series.There are so many stories (101 according to the cover of my copy) that almost everyone should find at least one story that they like or one story that touches them. I found many of the stories uplifting. Some were sad and some made me laugh.
