The Third Eye is about a girl named Karen Conners and how she has a, "Third Eye." While she is babysitting the Zenner family, Bobby the older son of the Zenners, goes to play with his friends, but doesn't come back for lunch. When Karen asks the nearby families about Bobby, they say he was at his own home, but after the Zenners come back, Karen has a feeling that Bobby is in her boyfriend, Tim's car, when she is right, everyone is happy but the Zenners lose contact with them, because they think that she is a psycho and Karen realizes she has a third eye. When other babies go missing, everyone is counting on her to find them.
