Even though this book made me sad I really enjoyed it. I finally understand the final battles of the American Civil War leading up to Appomattox. Since this is a novel I was hesitant to rely on it as an accurate account of some historical details, ie. Grant was once almost shot by his own Union sentries when he was in a spot he shouldn't have been. But I am reassured after reading reviewers who say the author thoroughly researched this book.
