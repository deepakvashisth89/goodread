Debbie Macomber's Return to Promise was just what I needed after reading so many thrillers.I was looking for something lighter, and this book was the perfect palate cleanser. There was one sentence with an ellipsis, but that isn't something I typically see in Macomber's writing.This love story began with just the right amount of drama to draw me in and keep me interested. It's a charming romance that beautifully portrays the challenges one can face in marriage. I thoroughly enjoyed this quick read!!
