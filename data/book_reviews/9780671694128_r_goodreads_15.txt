Never read this one when I was in school. This was probably one of the best Fear Streets I've read thus far and has me excited even more than I initially was when I first set out to read this series. I think Stine has found his footing with the series at this point, because as I recall, I read the later installments out of series order, and I couldn't get enough of them.Five out of five for a couple reasons. The story line was extremely interesting and I had fun following along with Mayra. Basically, I had so much fun reading this! I felt that the ending was satisfactory while at the same time leaving you wondering about Mrs. Cottler's black cat. I won't say what is revealed at the end, but it makes you wonder if the cat is more than just a cat, if you catch my drift 😉
