The name of the book is Beacon Hill Boys the author is Ken Mochizuki. The genre is friction. This book takes place in Seattle Washington 1972. Beacon Hill Boys is about an American Japanese boy named Dan who is trying to get through his high school life with his group of friends that are also japanese like him. I recommend people read this book because it really teaches you to try and not care of what people think of you and teaches you to stand up for what you bealive in. The title of this book matches the book because the book is about boys growing up in the beacon hill area and people call them the beacon hill boys. This book is about a boy named Dan he has two brothers and a mother and a father. In the book Dan is always being compared to his brother Brad who everyone thinks is a golden kid just because he is good at sports and smart. In the begginning of this book you learn about what Dan goes through with his family and school life. even his life is hard he does not want to smoke to get rid of the stress because he knows even tho he is getting tired of being compared to Brad he is still trying to make his family proud of him. Since he is japanese his family expects alot from him. There is racism in this book because the white people look down on the Japanese people just because of things like pearl harbor and being different. In the beginning you see Dan as a quiet shy kid. Towards the end of the book you see he starts to build confidence as he starts to stand up for things he bealives in. In the end things start to finally go his way. I think people should read this book because the plot is really good. The plot is about a boy who is trying to get through obstacles in life but not just taking the easy way out. This book would be for people who like to read books where the main character wins in the end. The character i like the most is Dan because he slowly starts to change into someone who would stand up for what he wants even if he is alone. My rate for this book out of 5 stars would be 4 stars.
