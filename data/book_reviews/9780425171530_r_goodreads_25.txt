The second book in theYoung Jedi Knightsseries is about exactly as I expected it to be. In other words, it wasn’t essential reading, but I still moderately enjoyed the story, and, unlike a lot of the new Canon stuff, it was somewhat fun and interesting.There’s actually some fairly decent lessons taught inShadow Academyand I feel, now, after 2 books, both Anderson and Modesta have started to get a grip on their characters and know where the story is going to go. Both the Solo twins are starting to come into their own (and I think we can just start to see the beginnings of what’s to befall them in theNew Jedi Orderbooks), though, personally, I’m getting a little tired of hearing about their “brandy-brown” eyes.
