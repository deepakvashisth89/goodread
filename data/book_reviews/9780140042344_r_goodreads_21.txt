Having only read a couple of books by Steinbeck in the past (far past) it's hard for me to compare this story to his other works. It was a compelling true story with a lot of fictitious elements added since not much is known about his early years. Morgan's life is a sad one. Striving to make something of himself and amassing a fortune, once he gets there, he realizes he's really not happy at all.
