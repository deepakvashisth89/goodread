This book was recommended by a friend who is an avid historian when it comes to military things. He runs a small military museum in Toronto.

This book was a fascinating, look into the lives and deaths of a World War Two American bomber group and their actions.

The author eventually made the 50 missions which meant he returned home alive.

It describes first hand the terror of war and the huge losses incurred by this bomber group.

One gains a respect for their losses which meant we continue to live in a free society. A worthy read.
