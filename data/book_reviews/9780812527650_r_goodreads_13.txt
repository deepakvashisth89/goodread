The horror and creepiness was subtle but overall this book had great atmosphere. There’s multiple timelines and POVs but I thought it worked for the story. There are a lot of characters to keep track of but I didn’t have any trouble keeping up with the main ones (Arnaut and Mary, Lynette, Moseman family). Like a lot of these books, the beginning starts out with a lot of momentum, the middle slows down, and then the ending picks up a little. Overall a solid “quiet horror” read.
