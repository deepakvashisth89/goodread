6/5 stars, honestly! I absolutely LOVE this series, Cherryh is a genius of science fiction, suspense, political and social tension, and plot twists that keep you hooked and coming back for more. Book four of the series and I couldn’t be happier that I started this journey into the Foreigner series. She has a wonderful way of starting off mild, building the tension so slowly you don’t know it’s there until all hells broke loose, and somehow wrapping it up in a plot twist and cliffhanger all at the same time. Nothing but praise for the foreigner series!! 🙌
