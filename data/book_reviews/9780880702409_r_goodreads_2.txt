Stephen Cosgrove is best known as the author of the Serendipity series, all illustrated by the late, great Robin James. The Barely There series seems to be the Barely Serendipity series, with the illustrator this time being Wendy Edelson. The two series were written for two different publishers. Still, you gotta wonder how some books wound up in Barely There as opposed to Serendipity.Despite the title, this has nothing to do with the Kentucky Derby or Churchill Downs (the racetrack where the Derby is run.) Just why a rabbit is named Derby Downs is unclear ... which pisses me off to no end, because I want answers to these things, but there you have it.Another answer to a question I have is, "Why are the baby bunnies naked while all of the other bunnies wear clothes?"There is no ending "moral of the story" rhyme like in Serendipity, but the moral here is pretty damn clear --Sit down and shut up.Sleep tight, kids.
