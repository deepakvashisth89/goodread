Despite the controversies surrounding Byron as a person, his poetry is a capsule of the European Romantic movement and I enjoyed his work immensely, in particular his shorter poems rather than winding stories like the Giaour (perhaps simply down to the intricate language used paired with the length, they did not particularily catch my eye).

Although most will know him for his infamous poem 'She Walks in Beauty' , this is, in my opinion, far from his best work with some of my favourites from this collection in particular including 'Maid of Athenes, Ere We Part', 'Stanzas for Music' and 'One Struggle More and I am Free'.

'There's not a joy the world can give like that it takes away.
When the glow of early thought declines in feeling's dull decay.
'Tis not on youth's smooth cheek the blush alone, which fades so fast,
But the tender bloom of heart is gone, ere youth itself be past.'
- from Stanzas for Music
