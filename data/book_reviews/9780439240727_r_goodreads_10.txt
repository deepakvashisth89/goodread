I read this book in middle school and reread two days ago. The book was a fun read but was slowed down by Alex’s disbelief and desire not to rush into busting Helping Hands as Cam wanted to do. The book could have benefited from having 2-3 chapters be told from Beth’s perspective. It would have allowed us to see how Ms. Webb was manipulating her and how her home life was. Also, It would have raised the stakes and could have taken up the space they spent on Cam and Alex going back and forth on Alex’s reservations and Cam’s eagerness to get Helping Hands. Thankfully this book cut down on the voice-y lingo that plagued book 2. Some is fine, but too much is too much.3.5 stars
