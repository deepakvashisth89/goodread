I read the synopsis on the back on the book and expected to find an intelligent, recognisable and fun, light read. Boy was I disappointed... I did finish reading the entire book, but only out of some sort of morbid curiosity, to see if this wreck of a book could possibly get any worse as it dragged on.Though the book was easy to follow and rather fast-paced, the actual writing was mediocre at best. Almost all characters were incredibly shallow and stereotypical. The biggest turn-off for me was the main character, Chloe. For me personally, an intensely unlikeable and implausible young woman.A book like this needs a heroine that's relatable and fun, someone at least moderately intelligent but with some human flaws. someone you'd like to find out more about. Instead, the author presents a young woman who is arrogant, self-centered, incredibly shallow and rather dim-witted. Sure, that's fine as a starting point for character development, but the main character, who states on the first page she's "searching" in life, actually seems to learn NOTHING for the biggest part of the book, nor does she seem willing to really change and learn some lifelessons until approximately page 230 (of a 260 page book, go figure what a rushed ending it had). Which sort of made it feel like there was no point in the entire story this book spins out.In short, besides the two-dimensional, stereo-typical characters in this book, all the scenes, situations and conversations seemed illogical and fantastical, like something written and imagined by a 14-year old. Very disappointing read and a waste of your time and money.
