“𝑳𝒊𝒇𝒆 𝒊𝒔 𝒊𝒏 𝑭𝒂𝒕𝒆'𝒔 𝒉𝒂𝒏𝒅𝒔 𝒏𝒐𝒘. 𝒀𝒐𝒖 𝒎𝒂𝒅𝒆 𝒚𝒐𝒖𝒓 𝒄𝒉𝒐𝒊𝒄𝒆 𝒕𝒐 𝒔𝒕𝒂𝒚... 𝒊𝒕'𝒔 𝒕𝒐𝒐 𝒍𝒂𝒕𝒆 𝒕𝒐 𝒄𝒉𝒂𝒏𝒈𝒆 𝒕𝒉𝒂𝒕, 𝒔𝒐 𝒔𝒕𝒐𝒑 𝒂𝒈𝒐𝒏𝒊𝒛𝒊𝒏𝒈 𝒐𝒗𝒆𝒓 𝒊𝒕.... 𝒀𝒐𝒖'𝒓𝒆 𝒎𝒂𝒌𝒊𝒏𝒈 𝒎𝒚 𝒔𝒄𝒂𝒍𝒆𝒔 𝒊𝒕𝒄𝒉.

Okay guys this was actually so good, what the heck?? 🤯

It had no romance (shocking for me lmao), lots of friendship between the rider and the dragon, and a ton of war and politics, which I absolutely ate up 👀🔥

I know this is an old book, but to be honest, if I had read this when it came out, I totally get the hype. I read it now and it is still worth the hype. The way the relationship between Eragon and his dragon (I don’t wanna spoil anything with the name 👀) was literally one of the funniest and most genuine connections I’ve read in ages!!! I laughed with them, I got scared for them, and I even got annoyed when the dragon got annoyed at Eragon too! 🤣 It’s hilarious how this book genuinely made me fall in love with dragons even more❤️🐉

Besides that (which is mostly why I rated it the way I did 😆), I loved how easy it was to read and how it really took you through Eragon’s journey from how he just found an egg and boom his life changed forever. 🤯✨

I recommend this book to those that love a good journey through someone's POV and won't mind the politics behind the huge war that is underway throughout the book. I will definitely read the rest of the series and the ending of this book was VERRRRRYYY good.
