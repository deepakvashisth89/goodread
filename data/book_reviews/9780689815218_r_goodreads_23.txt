This is one of the books I read on long, delicious afternoons in San Diego in the summers of my childhood. My Bobe and Zade and I would walk to the library and pick out a pile of books, stopping at Thrifty's on the way home for nickel scoops of ice cream (my favorite: rainbow sherbet). I can't even think of this book without feeling a rush of immense love for my grandparents.

One day when my Bobe had first moved to Minnesota (sometime in the late '50s) and she was trying to be a dutiful faculty wife, attending functions, she met Carol Ryrie Brink at some sort of party (I think her husband was also a mathematician at the University). My Bobe's first thought was "What's doing ?" (Bobe missed the sunshine and comparative sophistication of Los Angeles.) Then she began gushing. Embarrassed, she apologized to Ms. Brink, who said, "Do you see anyone else here fawning all over me? Continue!"
