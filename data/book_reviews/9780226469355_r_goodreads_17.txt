The novel showed the tangle of stories with history. I liked how she captured the reality of stories told by people about events that happened, yet were told differently by different people.Also fascinating was a glimpse at social changes in Canada.And, of course, Morag (and a few others) was another great character written by Margaret Laurence.Unfortunately, I occasionally lost attention because my knowledge of Canada's history is limited, and I got confused. Many references were lost for me. So, although it was a great novel, I want to stress that I loved three other books (I have read to this point) of the author more, so I am giving "The Diviners" 4-4.5 stars.
