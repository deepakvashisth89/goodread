Superintendent Peter Diamond is still grieving for his wife who was shot dead in a previous episode when he is drawn into a murder investigation following the discovery of a woman's body on a Sussex beach. Her identity reveals that she was a Bath resident and she had an attachment to the special branch. Peter is on his best behaviour with his female colleague from Sussex but as usual, he is at odds with the hierarchy at the Bramshill Police College and the veil of secrecy over her work. Peter ploughs his own furrow and eventually, this leads him to the murderer of the beach victim and the perpetrator of an even greater magnitude.A good read and a straightforward detective novel. Roll on number 9. A good 3.5 stars.
