I love reading books about an imagined future when you're already in that future and it's not that dramatic. This 1999 book depicts 2025 Edinburgh as a place ruined by climate change and drought, with a dystopian government in place, the city separated off as it's own state run by Guardians and Auxiliaries. Projected onto this we have a murder mystery relating to some poisoned whisky. There is some very 90s writing (the word "organ" should be banned in certain contexts) but this was something a little different with a reasonably satisfying ending.
