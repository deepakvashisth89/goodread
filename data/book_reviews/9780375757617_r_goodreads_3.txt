If I could describe this novel-cookbook combo as anything, it would be "delightful." Katish is a joyful soul whose cheery exuberance is only matched by the American family's adoration for her and her cooking.A great way to learn about people and their cultures is through their food, and this novel offers a great insight into the unique idiosyncrasies that comprise Russian culture. The recipes also paint the accurate picture of how important sour cream and butter are to the overall Russian diet.
