This is one of my favorite books, so to be honest, this was far from my first reading. Good for a few hours of enjoyment, romance, and laughter, each of Carolyn Zane's Brubaker series is outstanding entertainment.
While each of these books are fine as stand-alones, they're even more enjoyable when read consecutively.
One of my favorite aspects of her books are even her heroes are stinkers sometimes. In this one, I'd have liked to give good old Tex a kick in the seat of his pants and tell him to quit being so judgmental. And tell his love interest to get over herself. Yet, Carolyn manages to have you rooting for both of them. (There is a pet pig involved, so this was my horribly sad attempt at a pun.)
i can't recommend this or any of her Brubaker books any higher. Read just one, and you'll be online looking for more, more, and more.
