I love my once or twice a year escape weekends, where I ask my kids to pick me out 2 books each to help me clear off my shelves and I disappear for the weekend at a local hotel and I stay inside and just READ.

My son chose this one and it has been sitting on my shelf for 16 years. SIXTEEN YEARS. Just waiting to be read. Moved from place to place to place only for me to discover that I didn't love it.

This story was not it for me. After the first 50 pages I found myself wondering what the story was about. The pink cover at the time of publication indicated it was to be a "chick lit" style book, so I should be feeling romance while the main character finds herself in her late 20's. This felt a bit more like watching Reality Bites and nothing really happens outside of she dropped everything to move to England for school, she met new people and had a college Masters experience. As we started getting closer to the end of the book, it tried to be more of a relationship/dating storyline, but by then I already didn't care about the characters enough to be invested in it.

This one will be heading to a little free library in my town, and a tiny piece of shelf space has been made!
