Based on a script by Terry Nation this is number 21 in the Target catalogue.

I’ve always really enjoyed this story, Daleks and Davros wrapped in a logic thought experiment. And this is a great adaptation by Terrance. I’m even inclined to put it a nudge ahead of the TV epsiodes for the extra explanations and motivations include in the book.

But nothing is ever perfect, for it includes the mocking of the Daleks for not being able to climb up the chute after the escaping Doctor. Yes, this highlights a design issue with the Daleks, but it also overlooks that they had means around the problem as early as Pertwee in Planet of the Daleks.

The Movellan zombie reference from the TV series is missing which surprised me. I thought that wa an excellent analogy in the TV episodes.

There’s also the Rock/Paper/Scisscors scene which is different in the book and the TV epsiodes. It’s been curtailed in the book. The Doctor never plays against Sharrel beating him every time like in the TV version. I think that illustrated the point more effectively than what is in the book.

The same also applies to the Nellie the elephant. A version of the scene is in the book, but I think the TV version is clearer and superior in making the point.

And the other scene which I think is better in the TV version is the exploding of the Daleks on the way to the Movellan ship. In the TV version the Doctor gets Davros to blow them up be reflex, where as in the book the Doctor simply flicks a switch.

But on the flipside the book offers greater explanation of various details which are glossed over in the TV episodes. On balance I think the book marginally slips ahead of the TV version as the better medium for telling the story.

Besides as I work in IT with AI this statement from the Doctor which only appears in the book truly resonates with me.

A race of robots fighting a race of semi-robots. I knew the Universe was done for the moment they invented the washing machine. (pg 89)
