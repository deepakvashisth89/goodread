This was a required read for one of my college education courses, and I didn't know what to expect.However, here are my final thoughts...I think if you are not interested in teaching/education, this might not be for you. However, if you are, this book can offer insight into the minds of young children as well as gut laughs and giggles every now and then!I truly adore this book! I felt like I was a part of Mrs. Paley's class, yet was also able to receive her perspectives on the lenses through which we look at young children's utterances, actions, stories, and play. Vivian Paley is one of a kind!
