This is a really good book dealing with the Catholic faith...and a great book for the RCIA process. We have a copy of the Catechism of the Catholic Church, which is a rather thick and heavy book which looks more like a dictionary than anything else. This book cross-references that book. I was skeptical about tackling the catechism to learn about the Catholic faith, so this book was perfect in introducing the main points and dispelling my previous misconceptions about the Catholic faith. I also liked that there were questions and activities at the end of each chapter.
