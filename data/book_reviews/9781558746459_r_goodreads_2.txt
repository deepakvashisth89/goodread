I was in need of some real life true love stories that would make me celebrate my relationship with my boyfriend, and I remember coming across this book on one of our first dates, which conveniently was in a bookstore ha! So I picked it up the next time I saw it in MPH Bookstore, and so glad I did! This book is filled with so many wonderful stories of true love, marriages and overcoming life obstacles as a couple. It really did inspire me knowing that no two relationships are the same and we each have our own challenges to face.It also made me think a lot of the marriages of my parents and grandparents and how I have been seeing true love in front of me for years, even if they may seem unconventional.At the end of the day, this is a wonderful book that moved me to tears multiple of times, and I would definitely recommend it if you're looking for a little strength in your own relationship :)
