I picked this book up from the bargain bin because the main character is an artist similar to myself.The positives to this book is that it does justice to the work of a painter, but otherwise I was disappointed.There were sex scenes described that didn't need to be told at all to move the story. One of which was very much sexual assault because she says no several times and it almost had me stop the book altogether. But I skimmed and kept going for the ending.There is no suspense or thriller aspect, but it's a very twisted mystery.
