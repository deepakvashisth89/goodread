Unfortunately, this book was almost unreadable. The prose was messy, bulky, lifeless, and frankly grating. The three main characters were not developed at all, rather assigned attributes (not to mention the buzzard-esque side characters like Jimmy, Mark, and weird-hairline-girl) . The alternating format is usually a favorite of mine, but in this novel it was cumbersome and a wasted opportunity. Sadly, this novel had zero plot (inner or outer) and was flat, thin vignettes blended together to form a dissappointing, bloodless work. From the description on the back, as well as snippets of reviews I had read, this was not what I was expecting from such a seemingly revered work. The main three girls (Emily, Grace, and Jane) were a blur of cliches (in their musings and actions) without distinct voices or even partially-fleshed resonant characteristics. In conclusion, this book was severely lacking in both concrete and abstract merit.
