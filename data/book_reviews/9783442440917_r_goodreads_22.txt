If I wanted to read poetry, I read poetry.
This was not much of a mystery, with half the book all about relationship background.
This seems like the author wanting to work in some literary atmosphere.
Didn't care about the Robert Brooke poems or Lydia's letters to her mother.
Who cares, we never meet the characters. I skipped them and made no difference.
The author decides to kill off Dunan's ex, this ending the chapter in his life. Plus, he founds out he has a son and this whole book seems to exist just to change these parts of his life.

For the series all you need to know is that his ex wife is murdered and he finds out he had a son with her. So you can now skip this book and go onto the next one of the series.
