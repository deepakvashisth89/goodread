This had a great premise and I loved it for most parts. Our h, the correct history professor is shocked to learn that her perfect but distant fiancé had her investigated before popping the question. It’s sweet and funny picturing a woman revolting for being selected on the basis of being nice and suitable and …boring? So she sets out to prove herself unsuitable and interesting. Only her revolt is as radical as her imagination - going on a medieval revellers weekend!The book loses some steam with the shenanigans of the om and others on that weekend even as the H follows her there and tries to woo and win her back. That was a no brainer but the H’s amused, relaxed and pig headed confidence grates. She revolts again but again it was an no brainer. She should have made him work harderandhe should have worked harder - at changing his mindset and for taking her for granted.But still a sweet story with depth in characterisation.
