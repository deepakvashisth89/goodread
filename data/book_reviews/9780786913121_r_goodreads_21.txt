I really enjoyed this one, it felt new and not at all like any other Forgotten Realms novels I've read to date. I enjoyed the sea setting and both the above and below water characters are developed enough to keep you invested in them and discovering their fate. The main characters are interesting enough , but our "hero" is a bit much sometimes. I'm hoping his character will grow more interesting in the next book.I can say it's an easy read that keeps you turning the pages without effort. The story will keep you intrigued enough to enthusiastically move on to book 2.This one gets a 4 out of 5.
