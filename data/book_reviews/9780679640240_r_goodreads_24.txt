I would love to have given this 5 stars and pretend I was clever enough to admire, understand, perchance enjoy it. I long to repay T.S Eliot for the years of joy he has given me through hisThe Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrockby agreeing with his opinion of this book. Alas wanting makes neither of these things possible.I found this tedious in the extreme and ridiculous in parts. If I wanted to waste a couple of days listening to the Jameson lubricated monologues of a verbose Irishman I would have booked a plane ticket to the family in Kildare.
