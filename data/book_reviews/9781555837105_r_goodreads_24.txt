This third Dave Brandstetter mystery is the gayest one yet - with much of the action related to the Mr. Marvelous content out together by a group of coastal gay bars. I read these novels to soak in the atmosphere of 1970s Southern California - a very different time to be an out gay man. I was sometimes lost in the mix of the mystery, but the Hansen’s atmosphere is so strong I’m glad to just go for the ride. The writing is spare and beautiful as always: “Sandpipers hemstitched the wet edge of the sand.”
