Almost 5 stars. Martin, the editor, outlined the universe, very similar to ours except for thealien 'wild card' virus in it. Then he turned some of the best SF/Fantasy writers loose. Wow. History is rewritten in some interesting ways, but filled with familiar figures. Imagine Kennedy, McCarthy, Nixon, Humphrey & all dealing with the wild cards. Comic, tragic, vile & heroic, but all larger than life, these characters bring to life so many of the attitudes & issues we really did face from post WWII through the 70's, but in ways only masters of the genre could tell us.Note: Originally, the book blurb here on GR had a final paragraph that read...Now, thirty years later, the victims face a new nightmare.
