This is the ninth novel ofthe Black Company Chronicles. It was first published in 2000, 16 years after the first book,The Black Company(1984), and I guess it was back then thought by the author as the final book. Overall, I guess that the author initially planned just one book, then the trilogy, and finally the nine-volume series. However, this is his most known series, so this year (2025) a new volume is planned –Lies Weeping.This book can be seen as an attempt to tie up all loose ends or cut them. Even at the start, the Black Company is not what it once was – there are fewer characters from the first volume than fingers on your hands and this number will only decrease. Croaker is back as the company’s annalist (Yay!) and the Company finally reaches its final destination for the last six books. There are more mages, this time with a bit of SF stuff, the daughter of Croaker and Lady, whom they call Booboo and the rest of the world the Daughter of Night, is still planning to bring the Year of Skulls of her spiritual mother, Kina, and she got a potent ally to help her. Two of the Taken from the first book also re-appear…This book is largely a fan service – old characters join together for the final journey. And as such it is perfect. However, if a person starts the series with this book, s/he will throw it away after a few pages.
