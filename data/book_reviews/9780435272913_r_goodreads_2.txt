This is a retelling of one of the Sherlock Holmes classics. It's clearly meant for readers with least expertise but they author has done a very good job of bringing the narrative around nicely.It covers all of the key points of the storyline and should be very easy for someone to follow is there coming up the curve in the language but would still like to enjoy a classic.The illustrations don't actually add a lot for me but may be useful for some readers to get visual clues on the story.Overall, the book serves its purpose well and an easy 4 star.
