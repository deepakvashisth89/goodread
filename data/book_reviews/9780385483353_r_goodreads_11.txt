This is a very thorough span of her life and I immensely enjoy learning about this woman. I'd have to say though, I prefer the heart of My Life in France which probably remains my top recommendation on Julia. This one certainly explains her effect on American culture and eating. I was also amused that she was a fan of Hilary Clinton in the 90s and while not accepting the term feminist, stuck to her guns about advocating for planned parenthood, despite her own tristesse over her own infertility. Such a charming woman in our history.
