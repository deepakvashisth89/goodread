I read this book after promising my brother I would. You see, he was a huge Neil Peart/RUSH fan (and drummer). He had begged me to read it for years, and then when Neil passed away, I felt really bad that I hadn't read it yet.Anyway, I loved Neil's writing style, but as you can see it took me a long time to finish. Some of it felt like a travel guide as he toured around Canada, U.S., and Mexico on his motorcycle. Don't get me wrong, it was interesting and I found myself looking up some of the places he came upon. I loved his letters to his friend, Brutus.Knowing what he went through, the tragedies, and also at that time, knowing he had died, made for somewhat of a sad read. But, I have to give him kudos for picking himself up and finding a way to continue living life.God Bless Him - May he Rest in Peace.
