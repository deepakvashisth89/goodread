The Companyis a history of the transformation of companies and the surprising ways they have changed the world. The authors ambitiously cover 3000 B.C. to 2002 A.D., but focus mainly on business in the United States from the 19th to the 20th century. It was fascinating to see how companies have developed from the East India Company, to the railroad barons of the 19th century, to Enron, and finally to the modern age.In its brevity, the authors don't take the space to explain many business terms in the book, so it was difficult to follow at times. In spite of this, I found it to be an enjoyable and informative read.
