I have no interest in Tolkien but picked up this 1963 edition at the library for Pauline Baynes's illustrations. They are black and white and gray tone, the smaller ones, some in the margins, all monocolor, and the fullpage ones with orange added.None of the poetry really roused me although some of the poems, like "The Man in the Moon Came Down Too Soon," would be good to read to children for their rhythmic quality; sample:He'd have seas of blues, and the living huesof forest green and fen;And he yearned for the mirth of the populous earthand the sanguine blood of men.He coveted song, and laughter long,and viands hot, and wine,Eating pearly cakes of light snowflakesand drinking thin moonshine.
