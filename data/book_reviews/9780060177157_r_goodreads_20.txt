Chris Callaway is a rising star in Hollywood and has gotten divorced, and is in the process of building a new house when she starts getting notes put in her mailbox with stamps. It then escalates to dozens of roses. She notifies the LAPD and is assigned Jon Larsen to help solve the case.So he and her gay hairdresser Danny Devere have to rally around and keep her as safe as possible while they try to find the person who is likely not going to stop he gets control of her or kills her.It turns out not to be anybody they first suspect even though some of the evidence would have pointed to him if they had just known a bit more.Very well crafted mystery story.
