Ted Andrews was rumored to have lived in my area. When he passed away, we lost an affectionate intelligent being that wrote simply, succinctly, and truthfully.

I pulled this book off the shelf of a New Agey store, and like a good bookstore story, I do feel it chose me. My intellectual background swims with the Mysteries Andrews writes about so it me, this book was a natural progression and in some ways a good reminder of where I am on my spiritual path.

I wouldn't read this as a book as its more like a workbook, and as far as workbooks goes, this one is enlightening, serious, and Mysterious...
