This reads like a nature documentary. There is a family and love story with some plot beats, but mostly Bakker uses Raptor Red's journey as a vehicle to present her prehistoric world and life as a predator to the reader. That's kind of cool and the result is an interesting and unique book, for my taste there are to many inavoidable action scenes, though, and while the writing is compelling, even with how short the book is, the pages didn't exactly fly by.
