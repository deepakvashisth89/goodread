Sweet story about a boy named Juno writing letters back and forth to his grandmother, who lives in a small town in Korea outside of Seoul. I liked the way this book talks about the way Juno can understand some of what his grandmother is saying before him being able to read. He uses intuition to read behind the lines of the letter, a photo of her with a cat means she has a new cat, a yellow flower shows she's changed what she's planting in her garden. Juno "writes" back to grandma by drawing pictures. This is a great book to show to parents of pre-literate children showing that you don't have to be able to decipher letters to communicate.
