The New York casting director who writes this book focuses almost entirely on cold-readings including only a section on monologues which says, essentially, 'all the same stuff applies'. However, my friend, the son of a New York Actress informed me that most New York Theatres audition with monologues and not cold-reads.'When was the book written?''The 70s.''Ah, that must be it. I suppose, after they all read the book, they realised how mad the idea really was.'And so the matter was resolved.I enjoyed the light humour that pervaded this book. I don't read much non-fiction cover to cover, but Shurtleff took this beyond the informative into the enjoyable with all his anecdotes and experiences.
