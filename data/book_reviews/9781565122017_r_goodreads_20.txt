To a degree, I admired Brackenbury's (and his travel companions') determination to get way off the beaten track to meet the Drung people, who live in a remote part of Yunnan near the southeastern part of Tibet. The usual hardships and outrunning the cops are well documented in this travelogue. What stayed with me, though, was Brackenbury's epiphany that in his lust for adventure in "forbidden" places, other people, like innocent villagers upon whose hospitality and daily routine he imposed, are left to pay the price with the authorities. Not a new concept, but nice to see that he got it.
