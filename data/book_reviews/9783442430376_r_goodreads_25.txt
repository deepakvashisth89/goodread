My mom's recommendation - Amazing.One of the most beautifully tragic books I've ever read. A shattering display of the brutal, pointless deaths war brings and how love and life continues in the face of tragedy. The perseverance of humanity in spite of cruelty and brutality to breathe a new life into a generation bound to be just as brutalised creates a terribly realistic narrative of the cyclical destruction of human nature. Amazing. The best book I've ever read.
