Many eye roll moments for me in this book. Did you know Marty is the most amazing person ever to be a double agent ever, and is admired and beloved by all? No? You will if you read this book. He's even the best at being shot at least 900 times on one occasion and surviving!! He's a-maz-ing! I get lots of Baron Munchausen-type feelings from this guy, and he's clearly got a right ego on him: "Did I tell you I befriend homeless people because I'm a great guy?"Lots of inconsistencies and a very 'what I did in the school holiday' level of writing means I have to rate it low. Interesting insight to the troubles in NI, but I expect there are better written books on the topic out there.
