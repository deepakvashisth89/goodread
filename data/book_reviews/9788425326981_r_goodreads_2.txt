5 StarsThe Shining Ones is the second book in the Tamuli Series by David Eddings. The Tamuli series is a follow up to The Elenium Series and picks up several years after the events in The Elenium. We again get to catch up with some characters we know and love, as well as meeting new ones.It is an adventure, with some action, battle scenes, and the epic fantasy we expect from Mr. Eddings. The story does lag a little in places, and there is a lot of dialogue- but this didn’t detract too much from my overall enjoyment of the book/series.This is a fun read!The Tamuli Series books are:-Domes of Fire (Book #1)-The Shining Ones (Book #2)-The Hidden City (Book #3)Happy Reading!
