SF Masterworks #47: - This modern classic with the science gone wrong theme is as much a horror book as a sci-fi one. Astoundingly it was written in 1897. Eighteen Ninety Seven!!! It hasn't aged well, for context it was published in the same year as … Dracula.The concept was a H.G. Wells' first; originally serialised in Pearson's Weekly before being published as a novel. The invisible human concept has become a staple in modern culture across all media, and lives on today in the 21st century. I gave this a 6 out of 12, Three Stars for a seminal and innovative mix of proto-sci-fi and horror.2019 and 2009 read
