What a great book about the power of friendship and the human spirit. It is rare to find smart and honest YA books out there about real everyday issues that are often overlooked or not talked about. I loved the mix of humor and truth in this book and found myself really rooting for this rag tag group of young men. TJ is an awesome protagonist and his father is quite possibly one of the best YA fathers I have ever met.I am super sad about TJ's dad dying and wonder if that was 100% necessary to the story (I know, I know it was supposed to be redemption but wasn't he already redeemed??) and I am also not so sure about the whole lost son ending but the rest of the book was so wonderful these things did not stop me from loving this onep.s. thanks to Wendy F for recommending this one!
