"I've been in a good many parts of Oz, but this—this is the worst."I can't be more agree with the Cowardly Lion.Despite being credited to Mr Baum sometimes (not that the author's name matters), by just reading this book I can tell something has changed in Oz.I've never read the word "yawn" as many times as I have in this book. I literally felt myself trapped in that boring Kingdom of Pokes just like Sir Hokus, I was yawning the whole story.I don't know if it should be classified as a poorly imaginative story, or if it should be classified as a too many things for a single book. Dorothy had too many boring/unnecessary adventures, first with the Pokes, then with Fixes.To be the first continued book by Ruth Plumly Thompson, I found it poor in imagination and ideas.
