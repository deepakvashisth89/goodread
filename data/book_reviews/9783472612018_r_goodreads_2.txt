I read Late and Posthumous poems 1968-1974 by Pablo Neruda. I read this book because I loved other Pablo Neruda poems and it was on sale at The Raven bookstore at Harvard (highly recommend visiting if you are ever in the area). I expected this book to primarily focus on Pablo's usual themes of romance but it explored different themes with illness and the environment. I originally discovered Pablo Neruda because one of my mom's first boyfriends sent her the poem “The Lemon.” I don’t know how anyone could not read his poetry and not have at least one of his lines tattooed on their brain. I liked this book especially because the author broke up the book with sections dedicated to ideas like the world ending, yellow fever, and why nature is the way it is. My favorite poem I read in this book was “The Herbalist’s Rose” (37). I would recommend this book to anyone who wants to explore the genre of poetry because they are guaranteed to find at least one poem they like.
