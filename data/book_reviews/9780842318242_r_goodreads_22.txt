Strong theology throughout. This is not a practical marriage book, but it gets you to think about marriage in a fresh way. Every point is supported by scripture. And not just the passages you kind of expect, such as Genesis and Song of Solomon. This one goes deep into the Psalms, Ecclesiastes, Hosea, Ruth, and Esther for a very gospel-centered theology of marriage. A great book for a Christian couple maybe 5-10 years into marriage. Would go in a different direction for a young or engaged couple or one that is maybe not as familiar with Scripture.
