A delightful set of vignettes about life experiences in kitchens, shops, and establishments in France and the U.S. with the wonderfully skilled French chef, Clémentine, and a French-speaking, once-expat American family. As the tales are from the time leading into WWII, some of the recollections of life in France and the personalities of the people of Senlis were tremendously moving. I particularly valued the descriptions of M. Léon Gébaud and his restaurant/café, chez Léon, both of which suffered greatly when Germany invaded France on June 1940.The recipe collection is also lovely to peruse. They lack precision as often happens with people who just know what to do, which reminds me of many recipes I have from my mother and grandmother. I have tried one recipe with some of the abundant zucchini from our garden, but I look forward to trying others as well.
