No review coming for this. I re-read it for background in reviewingThe Horseman, in which the famed rider of Sleepy Hollow is given a new take. The original is a fun read, with Ichabod as a gold-digger of a school-teacher looking to get with the beautiful local heiress, to the chagrin of the Gaston of the town, one Brom Bones, who does his best to scare off the interloper. It speaks to the period, place (there is a lot of local scenic and cultural color) and superstitious bent of area residents. Whether Irving thought this was the case, or made it up of whole cloth I have no idea. God knows there are plenty today who subscribe to all forms of unprovable nonsense. Worth checking this one out if you are planning to readHorseman. Oh, looks like I wrote a tiny review after all. Didn't intend to.
