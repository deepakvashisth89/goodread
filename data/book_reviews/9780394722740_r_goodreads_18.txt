While I was reading this autobiography, I kept thinking it was a literal (Chinese) iteration of George Orwell's ANIMAL FARM. While the writing style can be a bit dry and the detail overwhelming (probably due to both authors academic backgrounds) the material is incredibly rich.Despite the fact it was originally published 40+ years ago, it remains an invaluable first-person account of The Cultural Revolution, and the period preceding and afterwards. It simply has to be read to be believed, and even then, it's hard to comprehend. Families torn apart, physically and emotionally. Everything was shifting sand - virtually no one and nothing could be counted on.There are bright spots of love and kindness, and people still, despite it all, trying to do the right thing. There are far more manifestations of people's base instincts and self-preservation.I had hoped that Liang & Judith's love and their marriage would survive the test of time, but according to the internet, they divorced in 1991.
