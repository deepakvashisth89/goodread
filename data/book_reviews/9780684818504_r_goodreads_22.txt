This book is an enduring riot! An endless source of amazement, amusement, inspiration, and awe. Provides an overview but is also surprisingly detailed. The approach at times can be a little scattershot, which is either a blessing or a curse, depending on one’s magical stance.For writers, this book can be great source for unusual themes and unexpected details; in other words, a wonderful panorama of human creativity.I have the abridged edition in one volume, but still it is almost 1,000 pages.
