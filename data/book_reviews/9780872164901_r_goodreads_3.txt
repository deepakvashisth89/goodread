Pretty good medieval, but when I read romance, I prefer a hero who is faithful to the heroine.Geoffrey already knew and loved Joanna and they were betrothed. He hardly ever spent the night alone and it was mostly 'off the page' but we were reminded constantly. As with the previous two books, the heroines didn't really care about their husbands filling that need, akin to pissing, etc, but that is not something I enjoy reading. He did improve after the marriage ceremony and Joanna supposedly ruined him for other women.3.5/5 stars
