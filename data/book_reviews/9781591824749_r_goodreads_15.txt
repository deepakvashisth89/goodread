That play resonated hard with Arima. He’s the Professor and the android is Yukino. He thinks she’s perfect and since his past haunts him, he thinks he doesn’t deserve “perfection”. I wish he would’ve stayed for the 2nd act….The ending of this volume is sad… Arima feels like he’s losing Yukino. God, the mangaka really did a good job of illustrating how trauma/anxiety/dark feelings creep up on you when you’re feeling down… Some of these panels send a chill down my spine.
