This was not at all what it was advertised to be in my book club literature (it left out that it was an erotic series and made it seem like a straight up horror collection). I was expecting short horror stories and what I got was a mix of horror and sex. I like strange and weird books, but this was way out there (I don't mix horror and sex personally). The stories were well-written and the plots were creative, but unless your thing is erotic horror, I'd avoid it.
