I read this book because it presents information I am not familiar with. Unfortunately, the little info which does overlap my own expertise is wildly inaccurate. Which makes me question the accuracy of the entire book. Which I admit might be unfair, because the author clearly has a particular viewpoint and expertise and perhaps is only inaccurate the way I would be if I tried to stray into this lane, you know? But it left a bad taste in my mouth. As a general rule, I believe teachers should clearly think about, and then communicate, what is their lived experience vs what is a general observation. For example, the path explored in this book is contrasted with theism, however the theism it is contrasted with is a very narrow experience within theism and not the way theism has been expressed historically or globally. I still gave the book 3 stars because it did present interesting information in an organized fashion.
