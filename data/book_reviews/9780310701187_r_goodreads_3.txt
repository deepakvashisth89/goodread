At this time of year it is interesting to find out the story behind songs that we have sung since childhood. The Twelve Days of Christmas sparks imaginations year after year and can be fun to sing. Did you know that there was a time beginning in the sixteenth century when parents in England used this song as a catechism tool? Catechism seems to have a bit of a bad rep these days. Quite simply, catechism is a memory and learning device for biblical truths. Thus in the song, the partridge was the gift of Jesus and the pear tree represented the cross, the two turtledoves represented the offering Mary and Joseph made in the temple after his birth, and so on. The artwork in this book is a wonderful complement to each page and I am thankful to know the fascinating history of this popular Christmas carol.
