This lady is a poet, not a memoirist. Every thought she has is stated, then restated in verbose poetical metaphor. Through the whole thing. Wandering, mish mashy. Every now and then she touches down on something solid, something fascinating...but she doesn't linger there. Off to more twirling, swirling effervescent whirlygigs of beautiful pain described opaquely.

Or maybe I'm just in a mood.
