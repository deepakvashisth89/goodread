Another classic Baum tale. This is not part of his Oz series, although the land of Ix is mentioned in some of the Oz tales. This stand-alone fairy tale has a very distinct plot (unlike some of the Oz books), albeit with unexpected side wanderings common to classic folk and fairy tales. As always, Baum spins a good yarn, full of magic, adventure, a little humor, and a bit of a moral lesson. A fun read for all ages!
