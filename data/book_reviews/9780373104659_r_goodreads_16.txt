I liked this one, but a few things bugged me. To begin with, all the smoking!!! Practically every scene, the H or h is lighting a cigarette! this seems to be the norm for this author, but it reminds me too much of visits to my chain-smoking relatives, where I'd have to wash the tobacco smell out of my hair later on! BLECK!!!!!Also, (again, a thing with this author) there's too much drinking, with everyone ready to pour themselves some whiskey, gin or scotch at the drop of a hat. Feeling nervous? Have a drink! A little depressed? This drink will perk you up! That was a scary moment, but this drink will calm you down!All the characters will end up at AA meetings, if they don't die of lung cancer first!As for the story, it was one where I don't blame the h a bit for running away, as the H wasn't honest with her, not telling her he was married! Given the circumstances of his marriage, he could have been upfront with her, she wouldn't have been pleased, but she'd have understood. Instead, she has to hear it from the snarky wannabe OW, and because the h is only 18, she takes what she says at face value. After all, the H never said he loved her, tells her she's a child one minute (he's 30), makes love to her the next, acts emotionally distant, then does an about-face and can't stay away from her. Too many mixed signals. He was a bit of a jerk, as well as a coward.At least the h didn't take off for parts unknown, covering her tracks so that the H can't find her (real nonsense, especially if the H is wealthy). She left London to live with her widowed grandfather in Cornwall. She needed a change of scenery, he needed the company, so it worked out well.There's a three-year separation until they meet again (he shows up in Cornwall), but in this case, it was good because 21/33 seems better than 18/30.There's the OM, but he'd been friend zoned from the start, and I couldn't help being sorry for him. He's the rare OM that I like, as he's neither patronizing and controlling or a wimpy beta. He accepted her terms and knew there was someone else in her heart, but she should have stopped seeing him when she realized he was falling in love with her. Though she never led him on, she was still using him to try and forget the H, (and later to make him jealous) and that wasn't fair.There's also a fake OW, as the H uses her to make the h jealous, and even though FOW was in on the game and went along with it, it was still silly.
