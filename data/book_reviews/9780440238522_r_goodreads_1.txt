I can't believe I'm saying this, but this is one of the most provocative and disturbing books I've ever read geared for young people, one I would not recommend to any young person. Devoid of any positive portrayal of adults, morality, kids, or their "leanings," while full of clever phrasing and some "good" jokes, I found myself feeling empty at the conclusion. Maybe it's because I'm older (or odder?), but unlike Catcher In The Rye, there is no sense of hope or redemption. It reads like a "gotcha" instead of a worthy YAL entry.
