There is a certain unmatched charm to any of Roald Dahl's books, even the bad ones. Luckily, this is one of the better stories. It perfectly captures the wonder and excitement that is childhood. If you had an amazing dad, this will make you remember him fondly and if you didn't, you will find yourself wishing things were different. This is clearly a love letter in novel form to Dahl's dad and all dads that ever existed. I found the concept that everyone in town had a naughty side adorable. I'd definitely read this one to my kids.
