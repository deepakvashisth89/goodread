The manga that kickstarted Hayao Miyazaki's movie by the same name.
Nausicaä, the protagonist of this story, is a deeply empathetic person that finds herself in a conflict that spans far more than just political unrest, but moreso that of changing climates and an ecosystem in chaos.
The first volume of this manga was, so far, much more fleshed-out than the movie and an easy recommendation for all Ghibli and Miyazaki Fans. At times, it was almost like "reading a movie", with how dynamic, flexible and gorgeous each pages were. I loved how characters that had little to no screentime in the movie had real character arks and amazing scenes that you would have missed if you were only familiar with the movie (which by itself still remains one of my favs, don't get me wrong). I'm interested in how it will evolve from this. The german hardcover version in particular is very awesome and of high quality!!
