fiendish little book, a formidable and disgusting road map, written by a guy as his self insert fantasy about what it must be like to have a buxom, adoring housewife, carpets of cats, close relatives with nicknames, to be tall with curly hair, and to find money on the street and be very rather honest and then everything turn out peachy keen. And, me too!!
right?! might as well.

others in this genre of men writing amusing little lives for their alter egos to enjoy might include every single M*A*S*H book written after the first one, and perhaps everything by Christopher Moore.

vividly appreciated the vitriolic Vermont reference, really delighted to see it as they dont show up often. having left Vermont for the first time in a long time I get these alternating attitudes of the same kind of vitriol, ('vermont aint shit!') and bouts of unfounded anxiety that Noah Kahn fans are moving in while I'm gone.

having visited the Chicago airport once I can say that this book did not contribute anything to my contextual understanding of the city because it all sounded made up anyways.

adored the ravaging cover art by cartoonist Nicole Hollander, however, always so happy to find more of her art and so happy to see the Nutmeg cats illustrated in her distinct cat style. Its the kind of cover art you hang on your wall but refuse to lend the actual book out because although I liked it, it was alright, it was also an unreasonable waste of time that no normal person should have to go through again now that its not the 1990s anymore.
