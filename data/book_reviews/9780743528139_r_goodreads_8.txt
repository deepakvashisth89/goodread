This book was really very action packed from the beginning. I found some of the early parts predictable but not at all in a bad way. When an author knows his subjects (In this case, boats) it's a joy to see the well researched plot develop.I'm not going to write a spoiler review but suffice to say that Carsten Stroud has done his research well and it's made for a compelling read.Contains firefights from boats, firefights on land, scuba diving, knife fighting, submarines, aerial combat and a Morroccan hottie that can hold her own.You won't be dissappointed.
