This book was written twenty years ago, so it’s a little bit out of date. But only in the sense that all the change since it was written makes it more compelling.The boom begins by motivating humanity’s need, individually and collectively, to expand and settle other planets. Then it describes how we could do this. Necessarily high level, the analysis is nonetheless insightful and grounded. Finally it spans to the far future and considers how we might actually travel to other stars and what this means for life, the universe and the Fermi paradox.There aren’t many books they change the way I think and what I care about but this one does.
