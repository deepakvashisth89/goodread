Everything I wanted in a book on this topic - a kind and amiable author (I discovered from sad experience how many expert garden writers can have grating personalities that come through in their books), plant lists that use both Latin *and* common name together, practical, anecdotal advice; and organized brevity - oh, and lovely photos. A few details about ordering seeds, etc. might be outdated, as the book is published in 1992, but most of it is timeless. Keeper!
