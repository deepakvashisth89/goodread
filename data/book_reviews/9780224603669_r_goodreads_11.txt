Double trouble, twice the charm!!!

If you’ve ever wished for a twin just to swap places and dodge your responsibilities—or maybe just to see how the other half lives, Lisa and Lottie would be your childhood fantasy wrapped in a bow of cleverness and heart.

Two girls meet at summer camp and discover they’re identical. Not just “we could be sisters” identical, but actually sisters!!

The twins are delightfully mischievous but never mean-spirited, but “adorable chaos agents.”
The adults are just clueless enough to make the whole scheme plausible, but not so clueless that they would lose faith in humanity.

It is a charming romp with just enough emotional depth to tug at your heartstrings—before tickling your funny bone again. It’s the literary equivalent of sneaking dessert before dinner: sweet, slightly rebellious and totally satisfying:)
