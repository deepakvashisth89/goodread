4.5 🌟!! This has been on my Audible shelf for so long. I don't know why it took me so long to listen to it. I'm glad I finally did though cuz it was a great read! Mara is a fascinating character and does all she needs to in order to gain a better and safer life. Even if it means to lie, cheat, and steal. That may sound bad, but given her circumstances, I won't judge her. Mara is very swift with her tongue and as smart as ever. She definitely knows how to play people and situations to her advantage. I thoroughly enjoyed the ending. It was satisfying, and the romance between her and Sheftu was entertaining and beautiful. In fact, this whole story was entertaining! I couldn't imagine being trapped as a slave. Great book overall! Definitely recommend!
