This was yet another nostalgic William Sleator pick from my early high school days when I had discovered him in the YA section at the Salem Public Library. I remember really enjoying this one but had forgotten about it until I came across it recently and the memory popped back up.

This is a quick, easy read, as it's Sleator's usual fare of sci-fi YA focusing on a teenage male protagonist getting inadvertently involved in a life-changing caper. The main character, Jared, was surprisingly fleshed out given how short this story was, as was the antagonist.

I enjoyed jumping back into one of Sleator's worlds, albeit for a brief time. I'm not sure how this book would land for an adult who doesn't have the associated nostalgia, but for me it's a solid 4/5 because of the good memories and the enjoyable revisit.
