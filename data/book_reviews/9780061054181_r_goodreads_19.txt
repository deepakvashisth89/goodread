No reason to ever read this except if you're a magic-head interested in the early days, but it's totally fine, neither exceptional nor terrible. I think the first Magic novel, Arena, is probably better, or at least more exciting, if you need a point of reference.

2 notes: First, it rules that early magic fiction was all about how the act of playing a game of magic cards is a horrific crime and the in universe equivalent of "players" are evil incarnate. Second, a cameo from my favourite magic card as a child and also now: Sea Serpent. (5/5 for 5{U}: This creature can’t attack unless defending player controls an Island. When you control no Islands, sacrifice this creature.) Genuinely got the biggest smile on my face when he showed up. Look up the art, what a banger.
