After an interesting premise this reader wasn't really interested in the references to the Latin language, nor was he really interested in some of the historical references either and to top it off, he started to get really bored reading about scurrying about an old archeological site in the English countryside. This reader has enjoyed some other Anthony Price books such as 'The Labyrinth Makers', 'Other Paths of Glory' and "Vintage '44" but not this one. For some of the best spy fiction in the business, try: Le Carre, Deighton, Seymour, Ted Allbeury or the contemporary Joe Herron, not this one. In terms of theme, try, Gerald Seymour's, "A Line in the Sand"or "The Outsiders" or try, Ken Follett's,"The Eye of the Needle" for a much better read. This only gets two from me. 2 Stars.
