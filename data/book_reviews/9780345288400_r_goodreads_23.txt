Stars: 3.5
Re-Read: Sure
Recommend for: People who want a glimpse of an early Star Wars book or people who really like Solo.

This book was a pretty fun but very inconsequential Star Wars book. Being one of the very first EU books, it isn't connected to anything else in the EU. The plot also doesn't do much to expand on the existing characters or give you meaningful backstory. It's just a short and fun read.

I don't think the plot was as engaging as the first book in the series, and the ending is terrible.
It has one of the silliest ideas I've seen in Star Wars.


My other main complaint is that Han wasn't competent enough. He had a few moments (
) where his talents were put to use, but too much of the book is him getting schooled.

Daley got a little bit too heavy with his technobabble, but he did describe something that was pretty interesting that I never though about. In the Falcon, you climb either up or down to get to the turrets. Given the shape of the Falcon, you should be facing out toward space when sitting in the turret with your back pretty much directly above the ladder. I never really thought about how this doesn't make sense until Daley did his best to fix it by saying that these two areas have their own specialized gravity so whoever is manning the guns don't have to climb vertically to sit in the seats.

Finally, there is another absurd scene that I think would fit right in with the horrible Disney movies.


It's only about 170 pages, so to me it is a worthwhile little read for Han Solo fans.
