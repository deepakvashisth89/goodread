This is a story about a young girl named Ant, which is short for Antonia. She is not always the most well behaved and is often misunderstood. She lives in a house with 2 sisters who seem to get a lot more attention from their parents because they are easier to handle. Unlike her sisters, Ant is constantly coming up with lies and fantasies. She is convinced her parents do not love her because they are not her real parents, and writes letters to her real parents, hoping they come find her. It takes a caring teacher to start to teach Ant the right way to behave and change her life around.
The cover art for this story is colorful, and gives a hint to parts of the story. The cover only shows a girl and her dog, which is similar to how Ant thinks. She feels that it is just her and her dog against the world. It also shows Ant in casual clothing, which she enjoys wearing unlike her sister.
I this book would be an excellent book to share with students. 5th grade students would probably be the best audience for this story. I am sure that many of them get into trouble and feel as if everyone is against them. I think that allowing them to relate to Ant will show them they are not alone. They may also see how some of the things she does is wrong, and be able to learn from her actions.
