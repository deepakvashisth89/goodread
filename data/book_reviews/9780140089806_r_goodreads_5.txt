I was disappointed by this book. I felt it was a big let down after 'Fat is a Feminist' issue. I felt that the examination was a good attempt at looking at this issue, and yet remained stuck on the surface of it. By the time I read this I'd read a great deal about female psychology from several female Jungian analysts, such as Linda Schierse Leonard, Marie-Louise von Franz, Marion Woodman and Clarissa Pinkola Estes, and others.
