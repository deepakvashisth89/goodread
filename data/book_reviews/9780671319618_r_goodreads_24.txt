Well, aside from one more, relatively brief, offscreen rape victim, this book is one of the more satisfying series conclusions I’ve read. Plot threads are all neatly tied off. Earlier books are echoed and transmuted in interesting ways. Character arcs are satisfyingly concluded, and the audio choir singing chariots of fire at the end managed to bring me to tears.4.5 stars, minus 0.5 for the rape, rounding up for the tears at the end.
