Good book of the tough life in the old west by Pam Crooks.Sonnie is back in Wyoming after her father has a heart attack. He’s never understood her desire to be a helping part on the ranch, so he sent her back East to become a lady.Lance was an orphan who watched his mother die in childbirth, but he was brought to the ranch by Vince Mancuso, Sonnie’s father.Years later they are having trouble with squatters and rustlers when Sonnie comes home. Lance has become the “Boss Man” during Sonnie’s absence but he’s never forgotten how he feels about her.*I received this book as an ARC reader and am leaving my unbiased review.
