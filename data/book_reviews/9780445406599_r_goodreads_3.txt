Book 2 in the Yellowthread Street series and it's just as good as the first!Murder and mayhem abound with some humour dotted about to form together to make a great read.The character development is slow and steady, with 19(?) books in the series you can understand why it's at a steady pace but you still have all the information needed to enjoy the book as it is.I'm enjoying this series and I'm glad I have the 3rd, Gelignite, to jump into soon.*Huge thanks to William Marshall, Prelude Books and NetGalley for this copy in exchange for an honest review*
