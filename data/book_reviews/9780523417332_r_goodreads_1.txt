This is slightly better then the first book, but not by much. This one actually has Raker doing things instead of talking on the phone or driving around in taxis like he did in the first book. It also tones down a bit of the racist elements that were prevalent in the first book. It still has some of the MAGA type of thinking (can’t trust other countries, Americans shouldn’t be spending money in other countries they should only spend it in America, everyone south of the border is untrustworthy and corrupt, and takes a jab at environmental issues claiming that smog doesn’t exist). I also find it funny that Raker once again bears up some teenagers like he did in the first book. The author must have fantasies about kicking the shit out of teenage punks.
