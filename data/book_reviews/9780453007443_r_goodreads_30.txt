Stephen King is my favorite author and I've been trying to read his entire catalogue for the past 7 years or so. It's hard to believe that I'm just now discovering The Langoliers. This one radiates a vibe like it came straight out of a Twilight Zone episode. 10 strangers who have fallen asleep on a plane, wake up mid-flight to find that the rest of the passengers (including the flight crew) have vanished. I'm going to stop there to keep this spoiler-free, but I loved it. You get a dose of mystery, suspense, horror and adventure all wrapped up into one. It's a relatively short novel so you'll go through this one pretty fast. If you've never read The Langoliers, I highly encourage you to click that little green button on your app and mark it "Want to Read" right now....
