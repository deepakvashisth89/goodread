Like most of William Sleator's work, this story tackles an interesting sci-fi concept and then makes it slightly terrifying. Here there main character discovers a device that makes copies of living things, and tries it on himself. Hooray! He can be in two places at once! Except, of course, he is only actually one of those two people. Interesting conflicts ensue, and the story gets very dark very quickly. Would would you do if you found out that your double was trying to kill you?The characterization wasn't amazing, but still a fascinating read.
