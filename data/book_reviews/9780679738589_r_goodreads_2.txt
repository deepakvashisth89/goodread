When I was a child we visited Anna and Harlan Hubbard by boat. They made such an impression on me. I had no idea that such a way of living existed ... no electricity or running water, but creativity and gentility. The lived life “on purpose” (the way they decided to), without trying to influence anyone else to do it their way.Wendell Berry captures them perfectly. I felt as if I was once again pulling up to the shore of their homestead and walking up the hill expectantly.
