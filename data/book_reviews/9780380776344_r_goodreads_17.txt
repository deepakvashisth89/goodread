This is an older reprint, but it's not dated. There are two stories here. One set today (actually in the 70s, when it was written, I guess) and the other in 1933. Central to the story is an Indian insane asylum. This asylum really existed, and the horrendous abuse the inpatients suffered is based on truth. Both stories and both couples were wonderful. I'd give the book 4.5 stars. There's a good deal of Native American history here. The book is a combination of great characters, romance, and history.
