This will be my last Goodreads review because I think I'm switching to Storygraph! Bye Jeff.My first real dive into nonfiction! I saw the musical Suffs at the Bushnell a few months ago, and wanted to read this book after that. There were so many things I didn't know about the Women's Suffrage Movement. It was interesting and inspiring to hear first hand accounts of these women's stories. It makes me feel like I'm not doing enough... It's disheartening that some of the attitudes and ideas that they had to fight so hard against still exist today. Overall, a great read!
