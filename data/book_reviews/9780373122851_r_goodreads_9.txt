Sara was beautiful, secretive, and on the run when she unexpectly lands on Matt Seton's doorstep. Sara takes the job as his nanny to his daughter and sparks start to fly. Matt is intiqued by his unexplained beautiful nanny and vows to help her. A newspaper article appears showing that she a missing married woman of a wealthy powerful man, he feels betrayed but vows to get to the bottom of the haunted look in her eyes. Struggling to break free from her mean vengeful husband puts both their trust and love on the line.....and their lives. ******
Was an okay read. Not as good as her other books. It had a good plot and the writing was great but lack a certain spark to truly make it entertaining leaving some parts kind of dull.
