I am a huge fan of Ann Cleeves. My favorites are Shetland and Vera, but George & Molly Palmer-Jones are joining my list. I love cerebral mysteries ( instead of blood and gore ) and find that Ms. Cleeves writes in the tradition of the golden age authors - Ngaio Marsh, Agatha Christie, P. D. James..... Her protagonists are not of the "cozy" variety. They are well written individuals. I prefer series to stand alones, as when I bond with the detectives I become as interested in their lives as I am with the mysteries. Highly recommend the series and Ann Cleeves. Kristi & Abby Tabby
