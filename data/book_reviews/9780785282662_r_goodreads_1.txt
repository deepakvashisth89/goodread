Spiritual warfare has existed since the time of Genesis, but ever since Frank Peretti'sDarknessduology sold in the millions and made Christian fiction a viable market, bookstores and libraries have been flooded with angels-versus-demons stories, of whichSedona Stormis one. It may owe a lot toThis Present Darkness, butSedona Stormis an enjoyable, if implausible, read. I do have to warn anyone who tries to read this: Due to the subject matter, there are numerous frightening moments, so, this isn't for the easily scared. I'm interested to see how the sequel will be.
