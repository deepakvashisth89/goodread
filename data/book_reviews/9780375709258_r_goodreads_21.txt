I love very short short stories as they can really pack a punch and paint a very vivid picture in just a few pages and some of these stories were just that. While some were ultimately forgettable, others like ‘Lust’, ‘Feather in the Toque’ and ‘Knot’ came together to woo me over. Minot knows how to create a thick and tangible tension in words that practically rises off the page and into the air around you. I love it when a surprising insight or something unexpected or takes place in the last paragraph / sentence and a lot of these stories delivered on that front.Finished ages ago but have been neglecting Goodreads 🥺3.5 stars
