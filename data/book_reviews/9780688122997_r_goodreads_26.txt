Wonderful new preface, and seemingly tireless and exhausting ethnography. There is an excellent chapter, for example, on the aftermath of layoffs at a shipyard and McDonell-Douglas.What marred the book for me was the level of detail about, say, the Cleveland Browns or Sly Stallone, as it seemed tangential at best. And the tone in describing many of these men seemed downright contemptuous, ridiculing men who invited her into their homes and private lives, and were clearly suffering.
