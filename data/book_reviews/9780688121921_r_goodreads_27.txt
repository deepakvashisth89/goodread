After the last several books which have been a parade of psychopaths and sexual sadists it was downright refreshing for Matt to grab a case featuring a murdered young professional with hidden secrets. Secrets that had nothing to do with sexual sadism (Woot! Woot!)Unfortunately the whole thing wraps up to be a big ol' nothing burger. I was really interested in what our murdered man's secrets were, and what they entailed, only to get to the denouement which was about as captivating as the air being let out of a balloon.Also, for the love of God Matt - learn to keep it in your pants.
