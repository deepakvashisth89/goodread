I have no words for this book. I am stunned that I didnt like this.This book is the literal meaning of never meet your heroes. I LOVE Johnny Cash (my favorite song is Chicken in Black bc it’s hilarious) but this book made me see him in a TOTALLY different light and I dont know if it was for better or worse.The beginning of this book was beautiful, it went through his childhood but I could’ve lived with the first 2 parts of this book. Once we got to the part where he had to write I walk the line to keep himself from cheating on his wife I was done with this.This might just be a me thing but I didnt care about the other people that he mentioned, like why are we talking about your 12 famous friends that died in YOUR AUTOBIOGRAPHY and I understand how that can affect him as an artist but we dont need 5 PAGES ON HOW THEY MET AND HOW SAID PERSON DIED. IT WAS GETTING A BIT MORBID.Overall I would NOT read this again nor could I see myself recommending this book but to each his own if someone wants to read this who am I to stop them.ONE MORE BOOK OFF THE PHYSICAL TBR.
