Quite the triumphant comeback for the Parker series! Just picking this up as the next book one would never guess Westlake took a 23 year break from the series and its ageless protagonist. It's as masterfully crafted as the best of them, and feels every bit as authentic. The main ingredients in the the Parker heist formula haven't changed, but of course that doesn't mean it's lacking in suspense or surprises. It also seems that Parker was a bit more brazen here than typical, posing as a phony insurance investigator right under the noses of the cops and a private security chief. Quite amusing!
