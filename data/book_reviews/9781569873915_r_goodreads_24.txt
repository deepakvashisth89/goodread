Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐Genre: Classics + ChildrenMany years ago, I watched the anime TV show “Heidi” and enjoyed it. I had never read this classic before, so this is the first time I've read it. I have completely fallen in love with this charming book. The story follows Heidi, a five-year-old orphan girl who moves to the Swiss Alps to live with her grandfather after the death of her parents.Despite being known as a rigid and isolated man, the grandfather starts loving Heidi, forming a beautiful bond. Due to her cheerful nature and innocence, Heidi becomes the friend of many characters, including Peter and his family, especially his grandmother.One day, Heidi is taken to Frankfurt and becomes the companion of a disabled girl named Clara. Initially, the little girl struggles to live the lifestyle of this wealthy family, but soon, her presence there brings joy to those around her, especially Clara. I will not say more about what happens because you should explore it yourself.The first thing you need to know is that this is a truly heartwarming story. So, if you own the book or decide to read it one day, save it for a time when you really feel down. I’m sure it will lift your spirits. The author has done a terrific job using her words and plots to tug at the strings of readers’ hearts. Another strength of this novel is the beautiful description of the Swiss Alps. The setting is stunning and vivid.There is good character development as well. All the characters undergo changes, revealing multiple facets of their personalities, including Heidi's. The story addresses many significant themes, such as maintaining hope, the importance of staying connected to your roots, resilience, courage, and how nature can heal a person. The ending is beautiful, too, and I loved it.
