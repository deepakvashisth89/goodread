A classic dungeon crawler. Boring.If the first few chapters are good, once the party is inside the dungeon, there is no more interest. It’s just hallway, room, check what’s inside, guards coming, retreat, hide, attack, kill, move to the next room… wash rince repeat.It was excruciating, going through the second half of the book.No final plot twist, everything is linear and predictable.As I said: Boring.I didn’t know what to expect. Now I know and I don’t recommend.
