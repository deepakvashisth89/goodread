Mixed feelings....I actually love Part is Afton Bonds' books!! Her writing includes atmosphere, geography, politics with romance. This is one of her earlier books and contemporary for the yuppie decade.With that in mind, I have mixed feelings about my opinion here. First, I found the first few chapters overfilled with information regarding geographic locations, political groups and characters. It was a bit boring, but I plodded on and found a great storyline filled with intrigue, seduction, motivations, fashion and romance. At this point, I found it very difficult to put the book down. Parris at her best!!! My only criticism is too much description at times.The characters develop well as three stand out....the thief, the insurance investigator and the narcotics detective. As they circle each other with intelligent, interesting dialogue the plot thickens as seduction is the name of the game. Two cats and a mouse ... or is it one cat and two mice??!!Romance between the thief and insurance investigator ensues. Paris does this very well!!! Loved it! Until the end of the book which drops off the cliff like a stone! After a suspense -filled, action packed, thrilling and sexy buildup .... End! Really??!! I'm so disappointed!Recommend to readers of contemporary romance and suspense readers. I'm middle-of -the-road on this one.
