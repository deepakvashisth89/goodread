I've read other mysteries set in British boarding schools, but this is the best because Barnard loved to skewer all such institutions in his books. The teaching staff are an interesting mixture of young and old, progressive and conservative, intelligent and not-so-much. It is stated from the outset that the Burleigh School is not the worst school for boys, but it's far from the best. Which makes one wonder by Dr. Frome would be sending his son, Hillary, there as a day student, since he obviously thinks that Hillary is very special. He is, but not in the way his father thinks: smart but calculating in how he undermines the teachers and uses the other boys for his own ends. So when he is poisoned, there is no lack of possible suspects for his murder, and not much sadness within the school.June 3, 2024: this was just as much a fun read as the first time. Such an interesting mix of characters!
