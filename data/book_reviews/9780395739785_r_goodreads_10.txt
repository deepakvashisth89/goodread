I am unsure of the lesson or theme the young reader is supposed to learn but this book made me sympathetic to Rotten Ralph. It reminded me of how children are expected to hug and kiss, even if they are uncomfortable. Ralph was clearly uncomfortable with the idea of having to kiss whoever wrote the card he picks randomly. When he tries to deter that event from happening by offering his bug candy, Sarah tells him that no one will love him if he isn’t nice. That gives a child extra pressure to do things they don’t want to do because a child doesn’t want to be alone or unloved.
