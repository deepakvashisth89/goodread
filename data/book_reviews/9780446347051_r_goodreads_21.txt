For those reading this book because of the movie: be aware that this is not a stand-alone novel, but the 7th in a series.I enjoyed this book as a spy novel. The writing is very good and the story is largely engaging. One problem I found was that the operation that the story centers on, which is meant to become clearer towards the end, is not spelled out simply for a casual reader. With close attention to this aspect, any reader can find this book enjoyable.
