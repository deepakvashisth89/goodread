My objective was to find a book that we could partially read through to help the kids start classifying pond life into differently categories. Some of the book's classifications were muddled (bacteria included in plant section) and the text was a bit dry. It would not work great as a field guide because it covers the entire US. The opening pages, however, were great and reading them before our first spring hikes great enriched the experience. I'm not going to keep working through the pages as a read aloud but keep it on our shelf for reference.
