What a strange story! Premise quite solid with independently wealthy fellow having the time and money to pretty much do whatever he'd like. His crusade here being to pry into the life of a guy who literally drops dead in front of him. Following the trail of information (clues) has him becoming a bit paranoid about the whole thing, situation gets out of control enough that I was wondering if he'd be locked up at some point? It's all very well plotted, scene-setting effective, characters differentiated (I do wonder what his doormanreallythought of him?), making it fun to follow along. I wasn't that invested in the subplot regarding his ex-wife, though by the end that made a bit more sense. Speaking of the ending, I was disappointed. Didn't go with the story I'd read, but being 40 years ago perhaps it was meant to be ... shocking?Definitely recommended as a fun read- liked it so much I bought a Rubens title new to me for later.
