Chloe has just relocated to Old Dudley, AZ (aka BSB) from NYC. She is working as a Victim Witness for the County Attorney's office. On one of her first calls out, to the scene of a suicide, she recognizes the victim as a friend and lover she knew 20 years ago. The victim known in AZ as Leroy Harris has his daughter visiting from NY. When her mother Roxanne comes to get the daughter she also engages Chloe to find out what led him to suicide or in her opinion who killed him. The book was interesting to read because it was modeled after Bisbee.
