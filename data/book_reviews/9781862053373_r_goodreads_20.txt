(More pictures at parkablogs.com)Here's a pretty fun book on goblins.Brian Froud takes the role as an eminent part-time goblinologist — what? — who discovers forty-three notebooks in a 60-million-year old vase. On these notebooks are stories of goblins and legends. Together with his colleague, Terry Jones, they reconstructed this, The Goblin Companion.Terry Jones is a member of the Monty Python comedy team. He introduces the different interesting goblin characters and his writeup often humorous. The combination of witty commentary and delightful goblin sketches make this a wonderful book.It's a little book that's slightly larger than a DVD cover.
