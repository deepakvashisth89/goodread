An interesting collection of short stories that were originally published during the 1930s, 40s and 50s in magazines such as The Atlantic Monthly, Cosmopolitan, Esquire and Harper's Magazine, among others. The collection was compiled by Wallace Stegner in the last years years of his life. The stories are not presented in chronological order but they all reveal aspects of the life that Stegner experienced - from his childhood in Saskatchewan, high school and university studies in Salt Lake City, to his middle years in California, Vermont and beyond. As Stegner indicates in the book's Foreword:


Though not strictly autobiographical, the current of "lived personal experience" that permeates these stories gives the collection a substance and form that makes it much more than just an anthology of well written but unrelated stories.

I enjoyed most of the stories in this collection and found that Stegner's writing style invariably drew me in and enveloped me in the story - a rare and pleasant experience.
