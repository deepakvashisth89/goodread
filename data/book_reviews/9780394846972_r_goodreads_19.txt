Quick and easy account of the first couple years spent at Plymouth Rock. Written in the 50s, it contains references to "red men" "savages" and the like, but also doesn't shirk away from descriptions of the settlers desecrating native American Graves and stealing their food. Also includes actual quotations from journals. Would love to see source information, but there is no bibliography included.I'm sure there is a modern counterpart to this book that isn't so offensive.
