I have not read a great deal of Margaret Laurence's work, but love her prose style, and the intricate, intimate portraits of Canadian women which she presents.The Diviners, considered to be the final book in Laurence's Manawaka series, sounded exactly like my cup of tea. However, I found myself enjoying it nowhere near as much asThe Stone Angel, which is an exquisite novel. This is certainly a readable book, but due to the way it is structured, it felt a little disjointed, and I was less interested in the protagonist than I anticipated I would be at the outset.
