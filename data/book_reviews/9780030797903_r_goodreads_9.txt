«Shame on me, that I have attempted to describe it by so mean an image – feeling, as I do, that it is one of those symbolic scenes, which lead the mind to the sentiment, though not to the conception, of Omnipotence.»Parables, in a spectacular manner; of a mind that speaks to the power of a young America, or this particular writer it crafted, so effortlessly able to return a favour of Creation, and the myths from which to bring a country forth. A collection of stories and mini-essays, held together by the singular, clean, rich prose of a man that conjures, and being conjured might have told us what new miracles he's witnessed since. Strongly recommended.
