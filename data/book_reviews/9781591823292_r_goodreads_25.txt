In the first story, Dee and Ryo have to catch a bomber out for revenge, for all the wrong reasons, and Ryo realises how much he cares for Dee when faced with the prospect of losing him forever.The second story focuses on Bikky and Carol, and yet another misunderstanding regarding their relationship! It all comes good on Carol's 18th birthday, however, when the promise they made to each other in the first volume is kept.The last story is about Dee catching a cold (I sympathise!) and he doesn't normally get sick. Ryo comes to take care of him though.
