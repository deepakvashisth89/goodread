In the middle of the night after sticking with a quarter of the book, I said "nope" out loud and closed my kindle. I got sick of reading about greed, status, desperation, and deception in the publishing world. An unpleasant novel filled with unpleasant people from what seemed like a "tell all" insider's perspective on real authors, agents, and publishing houses. The opening story about a woman who kills herself after too many rejections sounded much like the story of John Kennedy Toole, author of the magnificentA Confederacy of Dunces. His mother somehow was able to get his manuscript published after his death.
