Well... thin in size and thin on characterisation, but yet, somehow now my second favourite Bachman after The Long Walk. Why? Because it is just a well worked story. It has an interesting premise about the main protagonist being cursed by a 'gypsy', and him thinking 'hey wait one Goddamn minute, I will not take this'. and looking to find ways of fighting back!.In summary - a good story; suspenseful at times; A Bachman jam; but a book indicative of the times it was written in, even for King, with a fair amount of man blaming women for his woes and seeking to punish her when giving the chance - you'll have to read the book to get the context..A recommended Bachman read.... unlike Rage & Roadwork! 7 out of 12..
