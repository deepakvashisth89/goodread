wow wow wow!! I wish this series wasn't over; i've really enjoyed this world so much.i'm still not the biggest robert fallon fan so I didn't love part one but moira saved it from being boring. but part two... james fallon the man you are... I had so much fun being in his pov and seeing the world through his eyes. his wife drusilla and his friend henry were also incredibly entertaining and leonie was a nice continuation from the second book.I was always wary of denmark in book two and my suspicions were confirmed here. as much as I didn't care for that section or jeremiah's death, it did make me interested in the real history here so I might do some research.now for the reason for my rating: edward and the (new) incest. edward was HORRIBLE and I hated reading about him so much. he deserved much worse than what he got. and what happened to robert's daughter elizabeth... awful and that it was revealed to be a fourrier... absolutely not. I also hated that yet another wife I liked reading about died.I do wish james got a chance to reconnect with robert in person but I did enjoy their letters to each other and how they shaped the story.I will remember this series for a long time and hope to pick up the original trade paperbacks one day!
