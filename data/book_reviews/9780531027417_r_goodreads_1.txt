I've had this since I was a kid, so the five stars is clearly biased. But this lovely anthology is both harrowing and hilarious, and full of Ungerer's busy illustrations that make great use of dark tones and hues. I'm particularly fond of Jay Williams's more modern fairy tale Petronella, in which the princess goes off in search of her own prince charming (only to discover he's a complete dullard). My grade 2 class loved this nearly 40 year-old book, proving that the old fairy tales still have some bite.
