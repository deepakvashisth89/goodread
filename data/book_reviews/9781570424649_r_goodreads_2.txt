I decided to read Aftermath after reading an excerpt in the scifi collection "Octavia's Brood" not knowing what to expect from this Star Trek actor and Reading Rainbow host. This book is not the best writing and is full of cliches and plot holes. But, at the same time it brings a diverse set of likeable characters (albeit with some problematic tropes,) an atypical post apocalypse story, lots of good ideas, and lots of nods to radical movements and struggles faced by more than the usual white male protagonists in sci fi. Also, it's interesting as a predictive piece of sci-fi.It was reading rainbow, not writing rainbow, for a reason. But I'm glad Burton wrote this book anyways.Eta:another reviewerpointed out some stuff many years after I read this that make me wonder if I zoned out a bit a missed it being more flawed than I would normally notice.
