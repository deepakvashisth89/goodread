This is a well-researched and moving book about canis lupus. Wolf biology. The Indian tribes mythology in the U.S. The massive killing parties of wolf-hating men. The folklore of the wolf in Europe. Aesop's Fables representations of the wolf. Full of good photos and illustrations, etc. of the beautiful animals. It made me a little more misanthropic, because of the near-extermination of the noble beast. It also helped me better understand Train Dreams, Steppenwolf, and especially The Crossing.
