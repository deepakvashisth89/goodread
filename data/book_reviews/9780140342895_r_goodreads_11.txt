This is an odd little book. A strange jumble of tone and trope and genre, but I kind of love it. I read this first as a young teenager, c. 1996-1998, and I’ve been searching for this book off and on for several years, most intensely in the last few days. After much Googling and even invoking ChatGPT (which was validating but unhelpful) I finally found it in a list of YA/middle grade mid century literature on Goodreads. I had to read more than half the book to be sure, but the strange mix of genre and trope and tone is all here, and my mixed-up memories make sense. It’s an oddly enjoyable read, with a light air of mystery and romance and a little philosophical musing on time and our place in it, and its place in us. Recommended!
