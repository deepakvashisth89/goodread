It's hard to review this without spoiling the story.The writing and characterization are excellent but also of their time. The content can be very dark.It's a story of a man in love with a woman, with philosophy, with writing literature. The main character is passionate as a way of life.It's an interesting picture of past versions of Oakland and San Francisco. The main character is partially a self insert for the author but not fully.Over the course of the book, the main character is influenced by Spencer, Marx, Nietzsche, Darwin and more. George Bernard Shaw and GK Chesterton make a brief appearance within the story as contemporary writers.I was halfway through the book before I was invested in Martin Edens journey. after that, the book grabbed me and didn't let go although I liked Eden less than I had earlier.
