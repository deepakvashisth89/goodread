The first book in the Oz series with Ruth Plumly as writer ( Or royal historian of oz) try to be continuist andfollow clearly the same lines as the original author.In this case, the main stars are Dorothy, the Scarecrow, and the Cowardly lion, who soon will find new friends. Of course other fan favourites characters make also some appeareances, as is usual in every Oz book.The story is not the best ever, but is ok. Without a doubt, the most successful thing in the book is the presentation of the Doubtful dromedary, a new character very much in the vibe of Baum's creations and very, very funny.Recommended for Oz fans
