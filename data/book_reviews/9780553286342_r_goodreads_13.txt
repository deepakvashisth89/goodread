The Duplicate is a book by William Sleator. David finds a mysterious machine that can copy living things, he thinks his problems are over. Now he can be in two places at once, at his grandmother's and out on a date. I liked this book because it was interesting at times, but at other times the book got boring.The author used a first person point of view when telling this story. I think it was effective because if it was in the third person it would have probably get confusing with all the duplicates. “Duplicate A does not run outside. I lie there listening to the bump and scrape of the ladder as he drags it laboriously up the steps”(125).
