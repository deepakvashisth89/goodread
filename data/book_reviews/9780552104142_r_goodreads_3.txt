Disclaimer, I read the reader's digest abbreviated version.Overall I really loved this book, which was a complete surprise. The setting of depression era London was so vivid and shocking, as it's so often glamorised and surrounded with gangsters. Thankfully this is a character driven story with some memorable characters, a strong and intelligent main character who almost seems anachronistic when read under a modern lense and some shocking sections.The pacing is quick, but could be through the RD edit, and the ending cuts short leaving me wanting more, will definitely look out for more from Diane Pearson.
