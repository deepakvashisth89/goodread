I think I read this book in fits and starts. But 17 years? It spent most of those 17 years sitting on a shelf. But this summer I saw it on the shelf and couldn't believe I hadn't finished it yet. Decided to get it done!Excellent memoir. Saw it heavily quoted in Ken Burns' Civil War show on PBS. Drove me to pick it up. I picked up other books from that show, too -Elisha Hunt Rhodes,Mary Boykin Chesnut.Here, we have the memories of a private in a Tennessee regiment. It is well written. A must have for Civil War "buffs".
