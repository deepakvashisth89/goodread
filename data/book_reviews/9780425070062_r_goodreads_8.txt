Alan Dean Foster is an excellent writer of average tales. Which is probably why I don't read too many of his books.

Let me explain. In Slipt, nothing much happens, and the things that do happen, tend to happen over and over (like Jake escaping from his followers through the back window - three times). And yet I kept turning the pages. Yes, in spite of very little plot, or action, Foster was able to keep the tension and the pacing such that I had no problems finishing what was ultimately a light read that I normally might have rated a 2.

Due to my slightly dyslexic brain, I kept reading Slipt as Spilt or Split the entire time I was reading this...

A warning about the digital edition, there were probably ten typos from the OCR digitalization of a physical book.
