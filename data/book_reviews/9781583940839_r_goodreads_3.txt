Wilcock the Reincarnation of Cayce? I’d Say Yes

I don’t remember exactly when I read this book; possibly 10 or more years ago since it was published in 2004 and I remember checking it out of the library when I was living in an area where I still had library privileges. The primary thing I remember about the book is all the documentation and photographs on the similarities and physical likenesses between not only David, but various members of Wilcock’s family and friends as well as Cayce’s.

And while that was mind blowing enough, there was also the photos of a young Cayce compared to a young Wilcock. Even MORE mind blowing.

But what really made me want to read this book – and then what caused me to believe that this claim was likely legitimate – is the comparison of Wilcock’s astrological natal chart with Cayce’s.

I’m an astrologer so it didn’t take much for me to evaluate the comparison of the two natal charts made by the astrologer consulted by Wynn Free to see that – yep, there’s definitely something to say for the probability that Wilcock IS the reincarnation of Edgar Cayce.

IS Wilcock the reincarnation of Cayce? Well, certainly I don’t think anyone can definitively say that beyond a shadow of a doubt. But it sure seems like a probability to me.

And as an astrologer who’s compared natal charts between other individuals claiming or believed to be someone from the past for which there wasn’t NEARLY as much proof, the probability that David Wilcock IS the reincarnation of Edgar Cayce is – in my opinion as an astrologer – pretty high.

Extremely informative. Wynn Free did an excellent job with the gathering of this information.
