I whole-heartedly ADORED THIS BOOK! 💖💖💖 Much likeThe BrideI found myself laughing and smiling throughout this one. Brenna was so funny and Connor was such a lovable jerk. And of course, it was so nice to see Alec and Jaimie again. How cute was their daughter, Grace!?I can't wait to reread this series!Rating:5/5
