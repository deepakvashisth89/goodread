This is an omnibus of Allen's first two novels, Torch of Honor and Rogue Powers. In the brief intro, Allen discusses the changes he made in these for the omnibus, given that they were his first two novels and over a decade later, he was more polished to be sure. Nonetheless, I really enjoyed both books when I read them a while back and still really liked them this time around. Recommended if you like hardish science fiction with some intriguing aliens.
