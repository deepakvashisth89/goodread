An ethical, skilled, big-brained thief in a jam is offered a patriotic part in helping his country. The stakes are high for the thief and England. Things don’t go to plan. Can our man find a way to come out on top?As advertised an old school crime/spy novel. Nothing mind blowing, but not too bad. I believe Royce wrote one of the best audio books I have ever heard- Bones in the Sand. I may read some more of his books, hoping they can reach Bones level, though I have to also give credit to the voice actor/narrator/reader on that one as well as to Royce.
