Wonderfully written and performed by Bonnie Langford (I listened to tbe audiobook).
Throughout, I almost felt as though I were trapped in the Towers alongside the Doctor, Mel, and the inhabitants of the complex. There was a brilliant oppressive, confining feel that really effectively enhanced the tension as it began to ramp up.
The characters and the threats that they faced were grand, and the book also did a great job blending the horror with humour and light-heartedness - the Caretakers and their worship of their rulebook were hilarious, and they were especially good foils for the Seventh Doctor, whose personality and approach to problem-solving made his interactions with the Caretakers a lot of fun. I also really liked the slang used by those in the Towers, particularly the Kangs, and the purposes that these terms served; terms like "build high for happiness" and the meaning that it held for those who used them were really creative.
The way in which all of the peculiarities of the people and set up of the Towers were used to save the day was perfect, and the resolution achieved very satisfying as a result.
5 stars!
