I recall first reading this when it first came out back in 2004 and absolutely falling in love with it. Fast forward fifteen years. First off, where the hell did fifteen years go? Second, since this was a favorite, I was curious how true this novel would ring after so much water under the bridge. Well, guess what...I still fucking LOVE this book. This isn't going to be for everyone, but if you're a 90's kid who grew up loving films like Natural Born Killers, True Romance and so forth, this is going to strike a chord with you. Someone describes this as Bonnie and Clyde via Tom Robbins and that's a pretty accurate description, but I loved and still love the characters in this book. The thug with a high moral code and a penchant for hopeless romance, the midwestern girl searching for god in a backseat, it's all here and all reminds me of a simpler time when life seemed a little more full of mystery and possibility. Anyway, please excuse me, I need to get back to wallowing in nostalgia.
