It’s a great premise, trying to tackle the lack of feminine archetypes in the Jungian thoughtscape. However, limiting the exploration to the pantheon of ancient Greek goddesses doesn’t quite scratch the itch.If we use goddess mythology as the template for developing female universal archetypes, it would have been more interesting to explore the goddesses across world mythologies and hone in on the archetypes that consistently show up to attempt to define what the universal female archetypes are.
