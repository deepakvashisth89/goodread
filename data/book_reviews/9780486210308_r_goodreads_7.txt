I actually want to rate it 3.5 stars.
Generally, it's a really well-researched book about Western costumes, you can almost find everything you need in this book. However, it's really hard to follow the content when there's not enough image to support the description. It's very hard to picture what the costume would look like when you are not a pattern maker, seamstress or those who already have knowledge of outfit design.
A lack of visual support is a huge pity for such a detailed book.
