Better than I expected. I had always ignored this one, thinking “sure, Ted Bundy fed the cops some bullshit that clearly got them nowhere, since Florida fried him in 1989 and the Green River Killer wasn’t caught till 2001.”But Bundy had, maybe not unexpectedly, some keen insights into GRK, even if some of his ideas for catching him were moronic (a slasher film festival? Ted, you jackass), and Bob Keppel was a smart cop who readily admits he learned a lot about interrogating serial killers from Bundy. Keppel includes a lot of information on other cases he consulted on here, some I hadn’t heard of before, and goes into exhaustive detail about the formation of VICAP. It wasn’t the FBI’s idea, and I dig his “fuck the feds” attitude.Also, at the very end, Keppel gets Bundy’s confession, or the closest Ted came to it, three days before he rode the lightning. Bundy fucked up his entire “confess to save my sorry ass” plan; he figured it’d buy him a few more months of life. Reader, it did not. Everyone saw through his scheme, and this is Florida, not some hippie liberal enclave—Bundy was executed while a crowd cheered outside Florida State Prison. The end.
