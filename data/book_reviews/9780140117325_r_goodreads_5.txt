The author is a fireman from the Pacific Northwest and sets his tales in recognizable areas around Seattle and towns to the east.This one lays the groundworks for his character Mac Fontana, who lost his wife and is raising his young son Brendan in the fictional town of Staircase, east of Seattle. He's agreed to be the fire chief and has temporarily been pushed into being the police chief as well when a firefighter is found abused and dead out in a remote part of town.There's the murdered fireman, his girlfriend, a real estate developer, maybe some firebugs cashing in on real estate and fires, the developer's security detail, a crooked fire chief, the town mayor, his predecessor's trained German Shepherd.....
