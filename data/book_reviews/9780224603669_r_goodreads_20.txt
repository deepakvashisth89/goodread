I finally read the source behind both movies which I loved growing up!

What Worked: The Parent Trapis definitely charming in it's own way with an interesting premise. Though the idea of twins being split between parents isn't super realistic, I did find that Kastner was able to beautifully capture the feelings and experiences of children especially those of divorce. The scheming and plotting of Lottie and Luise remained true to the way in which children process problems and attempt to solve them.

I would caution any reader going into this book expecting it to be the same as the movies. After all, the movies did draw from this book as a source. The complexity of the relationship between the mother and father and even Lottie and Luise will feel more fleshed out in the films than in this book. It didn't make the story less enjoyable; however, I did find myself attempting to fill in the gaps because of my previous experience.

Overall, this was a solid read and I'm glad that I finally read it.
