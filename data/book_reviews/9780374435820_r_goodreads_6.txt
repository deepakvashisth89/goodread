Quirky and fun. Set in Paris, this gives kids a glimpse of an artist's world. Of course, every artist has a particular style, and this one is no different. I'll read this with each of the kids because it gives an alternative way of viewing art. With this book in mind, we'll be able to look at a picture and ask, "What if ....?" (I'm trying not to put in any spoilers.)The dark colors, heavy lines, and art spilling off the page gives this a ponderous feeling. The text (of which there isn't much) is a heavy bold serif type. For some reason, I really don't like bold type except in titles. I almost didn't pick up this book because of the type. Petty, I know. Oh, well.
