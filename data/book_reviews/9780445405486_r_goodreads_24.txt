I've read the whole series of these books and I love them. Funny and brutal by turn, they keep you guessing, have good naratives, intelligent, albeit a bit odd, plots and are a welcome bolt hole from the Cozy Crime garbage that is being peddled by our esteemed publishing industry these days, to go with the Woke mediocrity they have been publishing for decades. I also like the setting, Hong Kong, a place which I absolutely love. The frenetic lifestyle coupled with Eastern mysticism is balanced well by an author who also loves the island. If you like mystery with a touch of the absurd, these are the books fir you.
