This is yet another collection of Enid Blyton short stories I have acquired this year; I really love reprintings of classics, and this copy is a beautiful hardcover for the bargain price of $9.99. It contains stories about characters from full books, such as Lottie and Jimmy, as well as new characters and stand alone stories. There are stories about moral behavior, adventures and mysteries (so typical Blyton) As always, I feel a little bit more proper for having read them!

A cute book, and one that stands out among the many Enid Blyton books released/ re released this year. Five stars.
