After a heart attract Thomas Harding & his wife retire to the country in an old manor house.He discovers a secret room that contains a body about 15 years of age.The room is a priest hole from the time of Elizabeth 1 . This was not a good time ( mid 1500s to early 1600s) to be Cathlic in England. Harding sets out who the boy was & why he was murdered & hid in the Priest hole.At the same the local police are searching for Alan Fenny for the murder of his wife.Harding gets evolved in that also.The question is,are the two murders 150 years apart connected.This is the only mystery written by Aird that is a part of her series of inspector Slone & constable Crosby . It is one of her best.If you like mysteries I would recommend this
