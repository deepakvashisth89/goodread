A pretty good read, I was more than surprised. If I had anything critical to add it would be that some of his subjective experiences have been portrayed as the norm, and they might very well be the norm but we have no way of knowing until some well administered, documented and accepted experiments have been performed. It was very refreshing to read about things I was unable to explain that I had experienced as more common than I once believed.

His language was well thought out for such an abnormal subject matter. Most of the things he names have already been named before and he does a good job of using his lucid nomenclature consistently. This book would be equally engaging for a beginner and adept.
