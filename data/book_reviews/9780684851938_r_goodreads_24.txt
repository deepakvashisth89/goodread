I really enjoyed this book it’s very interesting if you’re interested in the topic of reincarnation. The voice of the book is also a skeptic or starts off as a skeptic and makes the insights more fulfilling, interesting and believable. The case studies were really interesting to read and I especially enjoyed when the author included pictures of the “subjects” alongside who they were or believed to be in their past lives. The evidence is really compelling and the reasonings were strong however I found the book to become a bit repetitive after a while which made it a little hard to finish towards the end and keep your mind engaged in the book. I would still recommend as it’s an interesting read
