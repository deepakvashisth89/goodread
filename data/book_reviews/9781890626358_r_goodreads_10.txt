This was a good book. Not exactly what I was hoping for. It provided a great explanation of nihilism and how it's displayed in many types of shows. I would have enjoyed a more movie centric analysis, but it's good to understand some of the major philosophical influences on our society (Nietzsche, Descartes, etc.) I've probably only ever seen a handful of Seinfeld episodes and never had imagined how nihilistic it was. The other complaint I have is that the movies it did discuss, I've never seen (Exorcist, Se7en, L.A. Confidential, Silence of the Lambs, The Killers, and Cape Fear). I hope Hibbs'sArts of Darknessis a little more of what I'm looking for, I'll have to brush up on my film noir for that one.
