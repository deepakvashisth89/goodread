A pretty kick-butt system, by which using the d20 rules set up in Dungeons and Dragons 3rd Edition, you can craft any sort of Modern-era style game.It comes complete with three "mini-setting" campaigns, "Agents of Psi" (a laX-Files), "Urban Arcana" (a laCharmed), and "Shadow Chasters" (a laBuffy, the Vampire Slayer).
