I got the allegory, even as a kid. (It's pretty impossible to miss.) Even then, while I didn't disagree with the 'go your own way' part of the message, I thought the delivery was a Bit Much.It features a HORRIFYING scene where the ambitious caterpillars are literally forming pillars, climbing over each other and crushing each other on the way to the top - where they simply fall from the pinnacle to their splatty deaths.I think it scarred me for life.________Another review just mentioned a bit I didn't remember, so I looked it up.
