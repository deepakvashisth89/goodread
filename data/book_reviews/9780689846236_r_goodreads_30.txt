Update:

This was a reread for my IRL book club tonight. I still loved this one. I loved it even more than the first time.......if that was even possible. We are so eager to grow up but then we find out that growing up hurts. It changes us and there is no going back. We learn to love. We learn to forgive. We learn to grow.

I loved how the setting, both the time and place, was like its own character. The author created depth that elevated the characters in such a way that made them truly unforgettable. She gave life to the little nuances that were incredibly pertinent to the story. Still 5 stars.

=====================================================
This is a young adult Pulitzer Prize winner. I loved this book. I know readers seem to have a love hate relationship with this one, but I loved it. I loved the setting, the language, the characters, the life and the way the relationships were depicted.

Survival is such a strong theme in this book. It has it's tentacles in everything....decisions, choices, relationships and thoughts. I like that the characters were put in situations where they had to make the hard choices even when it was the last thing they would want to do. So 5 stars.
