LOL, as far as I am concerned Koontz can do no wrong. The Face of Fear (written under the pen name Brian Coffey) is a great action packed story from start to finished. The story is compelling and the characters are diverse, likable and unlikable (bad guys).I felt invested from start to finish and found it difficult to put the book down. In Koontz fashion the chapters are small so the reading is fast.Great read and I can't wait to grab my next Koontz book.
