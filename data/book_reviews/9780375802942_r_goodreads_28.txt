Junie B. Jones is taking the elementary school lunchroom by storm. Her brand-new lunchbox has finally arrived, covered in an array of wild birds. She is very excited, because she's been brown bagging it since the beginning of the school year. After all, brought lunch is better than bought lunch. Imagine her shock and delight to realize, the very same day she eats from her brand-new lunchbox, that her favorite lunch lady Mrs. Gutzman is back in the lunchroom. Now Junie B. is eager to help with lunchroom duties, and the lunchroom will never be the same.

Junie B. may not be a role model, but she is realistic. I loved that Junie B's parents immediately tackled what would certainly be a big issue in the lunchroom: an assistant is NOT the boss! Junie B. seems relatable to her age group and fairly benign - she gets in trouble when she acts up, so while her behavior isn't always ideal, the consequences for her actions set a good example. There are lots of teaching moments in the Junie B. series. I can completely picture the stories in these books playing out in real life; well-written, these books often have me laughing out loud. The illustrations included in the book are always cute and appropriate.
