Ok I'm being generous and giving it four stars for nostalgia's sake. I read this as a teen and enjoyed it. Read it as an adult and it was ok. The relationship (even though I like it) goes a little too quickly from "I hate you" to "I forgive everything you've done and you are wonderful". It tells you that she falls in love without really showing how. There were some serious plot holes as well.I mean the Soulenai knew they needed a catomanceur/magus combo to destroy threshold before they even knew what threshold was? And how does a magus convert knowledge of mathematics into power anyways? It wasn't clear how Boaz suddenly had the ability to hurt people. And it was so close! She almost...almost had it. If she'd just said something like "he used a string of sounds to alter the structure of her body" or whatever.Among other holes I've forgotten. Plus, the second half was mediocre. It's like she wanted to create this weirdslave/master/tormentor/saviorrelationship and then just had to finished what she'd started.
