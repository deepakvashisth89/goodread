In 2021, I've been re-reading all the Vonnegut. He is my favorite author and when I had read Vonnegut when I was younger, this was not widely available, if at all. I had never heard of this until this year. I checked out the book for free from Hoopla and read it on my iPad. Random thoughts initiate:1. I guess this is a children's book, though it talks about the king ordering the death of all newborn male children. That would have scared my kid. It took about 20 minutes to read, I guess. It was a kind of charming tale about Baby Jesus being born and what he saw, or thought he saw, during his first day of being alive on earth.2. I would say this is for Vonnegut completionists only. Definitely not essential. The art was pretty simple and basic - nothing special.I gave it two stars as the least essential Vonnegut yet. I like stories about Jesus and I thought it was kind of charming, but didn't have a great pay off or anything.
