The definition of yucky is messy or disgusting. Is the book messy? No. Is it disgusting? Not in a tactile sense. With a title like that, what is the author trying to say? Is he trying to say that reptiles are yucky? Why? That would be the author’s opinion. It’s not a fact, but this opinion along with other opinions within the book are interspersed with facts which could lead a child to believe that the opinions are facts.This could have been an excellent book. The title could have been The Reptile Alphabet Book, but the unfortunate word "yucky" was included. Why yucky? Is the author trying to teach others to discriminate against reptiles?
