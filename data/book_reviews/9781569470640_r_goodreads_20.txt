The Commissaris goes to America to help his sister, Suzanne, deal with the after-math of her husband's death and Grijpstra arranges for de Gier to go on an international police exchange to the same small Maine town, the real reason of which is to protect the frail commissaris. When they get there the Commissaris is outraged that de Gier is there until it turns out that there has indeed been a murder and de Gier can help the local officials as per the official agreement. With the Commissaris thrown in for free. The story is full of the Zen attitudes toward life and especially officialdom that mark the series and has enough twists and turns to satisfy any mystery reader. I cannot recommend this series highly enough though the individual books differ.
