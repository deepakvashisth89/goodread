4-1/2 Stars rounded to 5

I am so thankful to my friend Beth and her Small Home Comforts book box for bringing this book into my life. The story or a young orphan girl who has to leave the home of her aunt to go live with the dreaded Putney cousins in the Vermont countryside. But maybe this will be the best thing that ever happened to timid, mild, scrawny Betsy.

I adored this book and all its coziness. The Putneys were a revelation and a delight. I wish I could move into their farmhouse and bask in the comfort of its warmth and family.

If you like classic children’s stories, don’t pass this one up!
