I first read this book in the 1970s right after it came out in paperback. It really affected me then and, once again, it has affected me, though differently now that I'm older. It reflects the kind of searching and the sudden brutality that became common in book and films during that time. But it also reflects the atmosphere of Southern USA, of small town, of North Carolina small town that I lived in and observed. The characters are all, in some ways, people I knew or watched. I've been to a family reunion like the one described, at a church like the one described. It's a slow book but it is like walking into middle America in 1972.
It is reminiscent, to me, of Steinbeck or Marilynne Robinson.
