I read this one back in the 80's and thought it was a superb realistic novel.McTeague is a novel by Frank Norris, first published in 1899. It tells the story of a couple's courtship and marriage, and their subsequent descent into poverty, violence and finally murder as the result of jealousy and greed. The book was the basis for the films McTeague (1916), Erich von Stroheim'sGreed (1924), and Slow Burn (2000).
