I only readThe Blank Page(which was a requirement for my English 11 class), and I would be outrageously lying if I said that I understood the tale in my first reading. In fact, much that I hate to admit this, I had to read this story of merely 1,000 words or so repeatedly to be able to comprehend what it was all about. No, it wasn't the writing that was particularly hard to understand...it was the prose in its entirety. What was the plot? What really was it all about? What significance did those linen portray? The questions would be endless. But once I was, even a little, beginning to formulate the meaning of everything,The Blank Pageshowed that you really had to think outside the box sometimes. And as readers, we need to read between the lines and try to seek what the author wants to tell us implicitly underneath those string of sentences.
