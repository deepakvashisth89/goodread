A teenage fiction surfing novel set in Florida dealing with teenage surfer girl issues (surf, friends and boys).

I won't even talk about the story line because for the age group it's aimed at, it's exactly right. The mom in me was a bit freaked out at the lack of boundaries these girls had but it's a novel. What DID irritate me to no end was the novel being written entirely in the present tense. I don't need to read a commentary on events - I want to read an account. That was quite off - putting.

Not a book I would read again.
