How do historians write biographies about people who left little or no written record of their own as they are illiterate and without the means to do so? Pernoud has done an excellent job flushing out resources from all available references to Joan of Arc. Granted, she was the talk of the age, so there were many letters, manuscripts and royal accounts which mention her, regardless, it is an impressive undertaking.Granted only half of the book is a narrative of Joan, with the final portion shorter biographies about many participants. Thorough and interesting.
