"Lack of willpower has ruined more lives, destroyed more careers, and brought anguish and tears to more families than the lack of any other asset or quality, even including the lack of wealth and ambition."

At first this book seemed a disappointment, reading someone's 3 year journal isn't my kind of read. After the first few chapters I changed my mind. In this book you have the daily thoughts amd reflections of one of the most successful authors of his time in the world.

It was enlightening to see he wrestled with many of the same things as everyone else despite his massive success. Getting annoyed at stupid things, wondering what will happen with all the bad news events in the world, complaining about national debt and, most consistently, showing that he was always trying to be grateful for what he had.

Og had an incredible path in life starting as a WW2 bomber pilot, the full list of highlights is the last picture on here. Wherever you think you are, you can still be more successful, he didn't receive his first big break until the age of 45.
