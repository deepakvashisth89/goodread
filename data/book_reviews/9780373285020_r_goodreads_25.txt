This is a good/ok book if you are in the mood for fluff. It's cute/interesting enough to keep me going but I'm a little put off by the "bad guy" and "innocent girl" routine. Charmed is the first Nora Roberts book I have read and I am in the middle of Enchanted. She's seems to have a formula she uses for the stories. It's tolerable for two but if they all go on like this I can't take it.
