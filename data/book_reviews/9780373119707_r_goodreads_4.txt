DC is one of my favorite HP authors because she's always game for some expectation twisting. In this case it's Joshua the 19 yr old H to be, whose tender body and pride were pumped and dumped by a bored, selfish, older (26) female lotharia (and stepsis of the h), who soon dies in a suitably HP drive off a cliff kind of way. Felicia, 13 at the time and utilized as an occasional messenger between the two scumbirds, blames Joshua for stepsis's death.Meanwhile the H,left pregnant and kicked to the curb by his censorious parents and small-minded townsfolk, unable to finish school because he's too tired after long days cleaning motel rooms and baby butt, is barely getting bygoes back to school as was his plan all along, starts a farm equipment company with money from family members and is getting by quite nicely. The ramifications and feelings of guilt are just so much less when you're a hot young gardener boy then they'd be for a slutty homewrecking teenage girl.Anyways...years later Joshua steps into the path of the h who he'd like to enjoy a vacation fling with and doesn't recognize her at all. Felicia, who's become successful in her own right, is still carrying alot of emotional clutter and grief related to her feelings about stepsis and H. She wants nothing to do with him but dude won't leave her alone! Even though she is clear about her lack of interest. And so we get some extemporized revenge, and situations which embarrassed me at least, and I'm fairly certain h would have taken the high road except that this is Joshua's kink. He obviously gets off on humiliating verbal slap downs so who am I to judge.I appreciate DC's efforts here but this isn't her best. These characters were actually quite weird and unappealing and revenge plots, even with the relatively low stakes that this one has, make me sad and wonder if perhaps some grief counselling might have been more constructive.
