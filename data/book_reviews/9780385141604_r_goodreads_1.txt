The blurb on the back of this book describes it as a cross betweenCarrieandComawhich is pretty much spot on.The children are creepy and scary as they succumb to their evil sides created by who knows what? Is this a scientific issue? Or does God have a big role to play in this?The book is science versus religion as the characters look to find the answer for this outbreak of terror.It did become bogged down when it came to science as various theories were thought of and discussed in hard science language. It was also spreading from one town to another but I never got a feeling of urgency or a threat to the whole nation.It was chilling as the innocent children went blank and transformed into strong evil killers with no memory of what they had done.An interesting ending but not a great one. An enjoyable late 70’s horror tale.
