I originally read this in middle school and revisited it while I’m experiencing a lot of millennial nostalgia. William Sleator was my favorite author in middle school and I devouredInterstellar Pig,Parasite Pig,House of Stairs,The Duplicate, andSingularity.I don’t have a particularly strong memory of this book, and I can see why I didn’t read it multiple times like other Sleator books. This book focuses so much on how other dimensions physically look that it seemed to replace the central story in parts. There was a lot of focus on “ana” and “kata”, two directions people here in the 3D dimension don’t have.I’m glad I read this so that I can check it off my list, but this isn’t a good book and I wouldn’t revisit it again.
