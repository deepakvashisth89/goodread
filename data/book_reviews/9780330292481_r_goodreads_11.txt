Product DescriptionHigh school senior Karen, who worries that her psychic powers will make her seem different from other people, is frightened at first when a young policeman asks her to use her gift to help the police locate missing children.As stated in other Lois Duncan book reviews: I really enjoyed Lois Duncan's books; she was probably my favorite YA author. I have always enjoyed books with psychic phenomenon in them - ESP, ghosts, clairvoyance, etc - but it's so difficult to find them written well. Lois Duncan is one of those rare authors who manages to write about these topics convincingly.I actually did a book report on this book in high school and as part of it, I wrote to Lois Duncan and asked a few questions. She actually took the time to respond and answer my questions! TERRIFIC!
