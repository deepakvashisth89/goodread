No kidding, when I started this book I was pulled in like a moth to a flame. It had the ingredient of a book I'd normally like; leaving you to piece out most of the story, seeming as if you're just left as an observer in random rooms and listening to conversations.Quickly fell flat during the last few chapters. It had potential but failed to soldier through, as if the author was spurred by a great idea and gotten slowly dispassionate over time.
