PROTAGONIST: Matt Scudder, PISETTING: New YorkSERIES: #11 OF 18RATING: 4.25WHY: Matt Scudder's significant other, Elaine, has an acquaintance named Lisa Holtzmann. She and Matt get together with Lisa and her husband, Glenn. Matt doesn't care for Glenn at all. Shortly thereafter, Glenn is shot to death while making a phone call in a dicey part of town. The main suspect is a homeless man named George Sadecki. Matt is hired by George's brother, Tom, to see if he was innocent, as he believes. The plot was nicely complex. I liked the fact that Block didn't feel the need to resolve threads that weren't all that important. Scudder, always working on his sobriety, faces some challenges. This book marks a defined change in his life.
