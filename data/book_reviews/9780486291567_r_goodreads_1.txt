Found this in a book sale while waiting for my son. I read (a part of )this adventure almost 40 years ago and rereading transported me to my 1st grade self. Back then, Tom Sawyer was a boy, wanting an adventure. Now that I am raising a son myself, this novel is dated yes, but the values and lessons were the ones that spoke to me now... and they are as apt today as they were back then. What a difference can age do to POVs! I also missed the old school writing, the tendency to describe things more as books then, were meant to take you on adventure, to take you to a different place. I'm glad to have had this reread.
