Facing the Mirroris the first book that is based on Indian lesbian experiences. It has more than ninety compositions in the form of verses, articles, short narratives.Ashwini Sukthankaredited and compiled them into a book. The tales don't have a particular theme, some are about the first crush, love, or sexual experiences. While some are about heartbreaks, confusion, and forced marriages.I agreed that lesbians are facing issues, and we need to accept them and things are slowly progressing. It also provides them a platform to showcase their feelings that is commendable.This volume is a one time read, and I left the decision of reading it; or not to you.Read the detailed review here -https://www.bookscharming.com/2020/06...
