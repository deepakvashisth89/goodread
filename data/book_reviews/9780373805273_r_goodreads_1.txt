"Stolen Feelings" is the story of Julie and Cameron.Our h is getting over a breakup, and travels to Pacific Island to help her twin brother out. He has recently got a prestigious job that demands him to be married, and she agrees to masquerade as his synonymous wife. It is there she meets the hero, her brother's boss, who is instantly taken by her, yet is highly suspicious of her identity.I really enjoyed the first half of the book. The hero obviously is aware of the charade, yet tries to coerce the heroine to give the truth up. He is obsessed with the heroine, and jealous of all males who come in contact with her. The book goes downhill for me when he discovers her identity, and OM/ OW get involved while they fight their feelings. Ends abruptly.Overall, an OK read.Safe2.5/5
