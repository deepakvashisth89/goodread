I like Tasi Chih Chung's comic illustrations. Catchy and cute.These illustrations are basically a collection of Zen Koans; each of them is enjoyable by itself but easily forgettable after a short time if one doesn't dwell long enough on it. They are basically riddles or paradoxes that specifically made to make to stop logical thinking and only understood by some new awareness.Personally, I feel that Zen is some love child of Buddhism and Daoism. It creates a refreshing view which can help in our personal struggles but a construct based solely on awareness may be too much to ask with regards to our other struggles...
