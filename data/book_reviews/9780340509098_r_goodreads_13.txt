Written in 1990 the story revolves around Joe Creed a paparazzo. Joe is sleazy, sneaky, underhanded and all in all not a nice person. While taking a secret photo at a cemetery of a movie star’s funeral a supernatural event occurs. We then go on a roller coaster ride with a slow buildup to the finale at an insane asylum.Weird things happen to Joe and he is threatened unless he returns the photo and negatives. Clandestine meetings in Kensington Gardens with some over the top sex scenes all add to the mix. There is humor in the story and you do wonder about Joe’s decision making abilities as he is easily lured into several traps.The paparazzi do not come out of the story glowing. There is even mention of Princess Di and a chase fir a photo of Fergie.
