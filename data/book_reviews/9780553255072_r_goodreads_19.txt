This is a story of William Tell Sackett. In this story, he is employed by someone he believes is family to track down his nephew in the hands of the Apache. Tell goes far south in the mountains of the Sierra Madre in Mexico. By using his knowledge of Native American languages and every clever trick in his mind, he learns that there is no nephew among the Apaches. Laura (the ex-wife of Tell's brother) is the woman who sent him on the pointless expedition among the Apache. Before he can return home, Laura sends assassins to kill him. This book is excellent and horrible. It is, however, one of the best stories of the wiles of Tell Sackett.
