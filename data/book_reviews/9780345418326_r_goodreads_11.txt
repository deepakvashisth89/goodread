‘The Secret Pilgrim’ is a really set of short stories joined together as the reflections of Ned, who after a life of spying now has the role of teaching new recruits. Ned reflects on his life as he listens to Smiley giving a speech to his trainees. I loved the stories, some just simple , gentle, and sad, like the story of Cyril Fruin, the clerk being ‘audited’, and others more powerful like one of a British spy in South East Asia caught during the Vietnam war. Possibly my favorite Smiley so far, but one that is probably best read at the end of the series.
