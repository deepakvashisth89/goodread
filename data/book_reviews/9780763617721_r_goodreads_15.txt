2002 Kate Greenaway Medal
An interesting story with modern-looking fairies of all ages and both genders, even to the detail of operating an ice cream truck for fairies. I loved that Annabelle was able to befriend them, help them, and be a hostess to them, even though they were not able to take her with them to their picnic. A sweet, lovely story and one that boys should not find too "girly" a story.
