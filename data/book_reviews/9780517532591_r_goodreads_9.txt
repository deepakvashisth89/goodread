Sergeant Neil Howie receives an anonymous letter reporting the strange disappearance of a young girl named Rowan Morrison from the private island Summerisle. No-one claims to know her and he soon becomes convinced that true evil is afoot.Religious beliefs appear to be the central issue of this novel, but everything Howie experiences is to be treated as suspect up to the awful climax, since the entire plot hinges on an extremely clever and devious misdirection. Hardy has polished the story from the film script and this is entirely readable without any knowledge of the film.
