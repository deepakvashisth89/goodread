I enjoyed this book. It was an accidental read (I really did not plan on reading it and I'm not planning on keeping the hand-me-down copy of the book either),but it was a comfortable read. It is slow and pondering by today's standards, but it was readable and engaging. The hero was beautifully flawed ( no way he'll live long with all that damage inflicted on his liver and lungs), but at least his head was relatively clear ...
