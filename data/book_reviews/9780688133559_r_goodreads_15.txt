Published in 1914 and based on a play, this is an odd one in the Oz series. World War I was breaking out in Europe, but the U.S. had not yet entered in. The alarums and excursions of war sound throughout the book.The bored and ambitious queen of insignificant Oogaboo decides to conquer Oz, with a gun-tree arming the invasion force. She eventually drafts the clunky Tik-Tok, a clockwork wind-up man, to be the lone private in her top-heavy army. Their troop and others wind up converging on the Nome Kingdom, led by the evil King Ruggedo, where the forces of good prevail.Meanwhile, the heartless Roses of the Rose Kingdom repulse refugees. Ozma declares “The land of Oz is not a refuge for all mortals in distress.” And magic keeps Oz safely isolated.I didn’t find the characters or plot engaging, but I did chuckle when it was discovered that Toto talks, but just prefers dog language.
