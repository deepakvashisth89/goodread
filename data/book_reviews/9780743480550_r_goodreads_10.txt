CSI is a paperback series written by several different authors. It's an interesting read for beginners in crime fiction, but those who have read cult classics might find it a bit simplistic. The plot and flow of the story, featuring characters familiar from the series (I personally started the series because I missed Horatio Caine), weren't bad. More details about crime scene investigation would have been nice. The series is out of print, but I'll read the other books I can find secondhand.
