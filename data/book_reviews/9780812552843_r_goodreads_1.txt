The fourth book of the Lost Swords saga centers on the sword Farslayer which has the magical ability to fly to the foe and slay who the wielder names. Our adventuring team of Mark, Ben, Yambu, and Zoltan in their search for the sword, find themselves involved in a feud between two embittered families. Saberhagen writes more interesting character, drama, and storylines moving forward with the hunt for the missing weapons. A really good book that really ramps up the clash near the ending.
