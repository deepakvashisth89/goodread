While not quite as good as its predecessor, this book was still a great time. It was largely handicapped by splitting up the four friends and therefore removing much of the tension between Jacen and Tenel Ka. I also felt that, more so than the first book, I would have benefited greatly by reading the other stories that it was referencing. For a better experience, it seems like readingThe Courtship of Princess LeiaandI, Jedibeforehand would be advised.
