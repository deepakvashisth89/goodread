I returned to this novel with a sense of wanting to visit an old friend. With Vladimir Nabokov's wonderful guidance on how to read this novel from his Lectures on Literature, I enjoyed the complete delight of Austen's wit and form. Fanny is so true to her heart and her scruples. That this "Cinderella" triumphs in the end makes this fairy tale so satisfying.
