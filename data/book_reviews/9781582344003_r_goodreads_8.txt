Nathalie and David are brother and sister, but they are both adopted and have different biological parents. All their lives they have wondered about where they really come from, and now when they both have partners and children of their own, they deside to find out about their pasts and find their biological mothers. This upsetts everybody. Their own families, their adoptive parents, their biological mothers and their families...Everything feels quite constructed. Every posible problem about adoption and finding your past is included in this book. There are just too many problems and too many touched characters. And none of these charcters get to me. None of them are symphatetic, and many don't even feel like real.The book was an okay read, but I'm definitely sorry for thet I bought it. It's not worth owning.I kind of wonder if Joanna Trollope is really a good writer or a crap writer...
