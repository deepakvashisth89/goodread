Although it is only 70 pages, this is one well-done Young Adult title. There are limitations imposed by the multiple narrators, as each one only speaks once. It can be tough to feel connected to story as a whole. Still, each vignette is a gem, packed with history, social commentary, faith, and on and on.

This is a good example of a book that most teens might not elect on their own, but can appreciate having read. I hope it makes an effective introduction to literature circles in my classroom.
