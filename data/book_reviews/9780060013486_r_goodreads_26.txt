This is not my normal genre. I read this for our library book club pick.Corey Bowen was wrongly convicted of cattle rustling and ended up with a seven year prison term. The story begins with Corey who captures the eye of the daughter of a small town supplier. She believes there is more to him than meets the eye and makes it her mission to help him. Corey is under the head of the prison’s constant surveillance as he is always watching for escape attempts. Renda is a crooked prison officer and doesn’t care about anything but making money off the government for each prisoner.There are more twists and turns and everyone has an angle. Who can you trust and how can he escape his prison sentence?For a western which I don’t normally read, I enjoyed the book. It wasn’t a perfect ending but it was good.
