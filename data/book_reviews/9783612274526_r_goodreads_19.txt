Oh what a true love story this is. I really enjoyed this predictable story yet wonderful romantic story.. It flow effortlessly and ended as it should...Maggie had left her first true love at the Harvest Moon Bar in Canada and returned to Kansas City to graduate school. While there, she meet Joe, married had two children and taught English literature. Twenty five years later, Joe asks for a divorce and Maggie finds the love letters written to her so many years ago.
She now decides to return to Canada hopefully to reunite herself with Robert, the love of her life. Will he still be waiting for her? Is there still a chance for that one true love? Read this heartwarming novel and find out. Will true love win out after all?
