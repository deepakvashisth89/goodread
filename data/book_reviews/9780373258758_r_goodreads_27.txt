Detective Kane has been given a choice and he doesn't care for either. He loves his job so he decides to take on a case that he usually wouldn't even look at. He will be taking classes in business etiquette and foreign language. What he doesn't expect is the woman who will be his teacher and the instant attraction that he feels. Kayla isn't sure what to make of her new client, he's handsome and she can't help but feel a pull. What she soon learns is that her sexy client is a cop under cover. She doesn't care for cops and wants nothing more to do with Kane. She'll do her job but knowing who he really is so she won't let her feelings get in the way. Kane already knows what he wants but worries he won't be able to get Kayla to trust him and believe what he feels for her is real. Follow along as these to do a dance, Kane trying to take steps forward but Kayla always stepping back. Will the two come together or will Kayla let love pass her by.
