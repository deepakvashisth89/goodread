With 60 stories, there are bound to be some here that don't appeal to everyone. Unfortunately, I was still hoping for a few more stand-outs. That said, it's still incredibly hard to give a 'fair' star rating by virtue of it being such a diverse anthology.Maybe the 40+ years since its original publication is the reason why so many of these went over my head and/or didn't land, but aside from Atwood, Holst, Dybek, Wood and Ioannides, I remember little and enjoyed less.I love flash fiction--will advocate for it until my last breath--but the majority of these felt annoyingly insubstantial at best and navel-gazey at worst.
