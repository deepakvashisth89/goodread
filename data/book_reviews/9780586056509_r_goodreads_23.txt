4.5 StarsEric Van Lustbader has created one of my favorite series with the Nicholas Linnear novels. I can't wait to read the next one. The only thing that keeps me from giving The Miko 5 stars is that he seems to get lost on the backstory that goes on for far too long for one of the other characters in the book, I didn't really care about the character to begin with and the backstory didn't help. However, the action and the unique look at the various aspects of training and skill of Linnear-san was fantastic. Highly recommend for any fan of martial arts.
