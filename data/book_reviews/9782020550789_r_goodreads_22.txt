Could not make it past the first 25 pages. The characters were insufferable. Lots of navel gazing and blithe commentary trying to be posh and witty and self important. Just… not at all interesting. Trying to be poetic and striving for creative illusions of grandeur. Also lots of telling the reader what is happening and zero showing us what is happening. Rafael is supposed to be having a juicy gossipy lunch at the Drones with his niece, who he hasn’t seen in 20 years. He doesn’t care about the ambience or the other guests or his niece or her family or even the story she’s trying to tell him about the Murderess who just walked in the restaurant. His niece can’t tell a story very well and Rafael is a pretentious old goat who keeps interrupting or spacing out or criticizing her. So frustrating. I wanted to hear the juicy murder rumors and Rafael was so disaffected and our narrator his niece such a terrible storyteller that I gave up. If this is the author setting the scene for the mystery to follow it does not bode well.
