When Letty finds out her fiancee had her background investigated, she is hurt and even more upset at how dull it is. She breaks off the engagement and decides to put a little more risk into her life. Xavier wants to protect her from herself and continues to claim the wedding is on.What I enjoyed:* I like how Letty grew a backbone, but at the same time stayed a nice person* The interaction of Molly and Hawk* Even though Xavier is pretty manipulative in getting Letty to do what he wants, he still has to jump through some of her hoops to prove his love* Even Sheldon got a happy endingThe book is a bit dated and if you read too much into it, Xavier could come across as too controlling. But I just take things at face value and enjoy the ride.
