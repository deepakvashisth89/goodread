Wow. What a tome! I won't say I enjoyed it, but I will likely never forget it, which is saying a lot. The character development is superb and Murdoch's treatment of the questions of spirituality, good versus evil, reality versus fantasy, love versus obsession, madness versus genius is deep and wide-ranging. This must have been a bear to write!I thought about giving up half-way through because it isn't exactly easy reading. Often her paragraphs are a page long or more and the dialogue can be pretty dense. But if you're philosophically inclined, it's worth a try.
