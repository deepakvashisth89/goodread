After revisiting numerous early Stephen King novels, I had a taste for some of his vintage short fiction. I “discovered” King around the same time I got into horror and started reading Fangoria and all that. It was all part of this huge, big discovery. I don’t know when I first started reading his short stories, as they were mostly published in men’s magazines of the 1970s (and by “men’s magazines” I mean “stroke rags”), but I dare say it was after watching Creepshow. I know the first collection was Night Shift. I read that book over and over. I loved those stories.At this point, nearly every story in Night Shift has been made into a movie. (In the case of Children of the Corn, it’s been made into 11 or 12 movies. I’ve lost count.) The details of some of these stories have faded over time after I’ve become so familiar with their cinematic incarnations.I didn’t want to commit to the full Night Shift audiobook at the moment, but I found this little collection offering The Lawnmower Man, Sometimes They Come Back, Quitters, Inc., The Ledge and The Mangler. Actor John Glover brought them all to vivid life, and it was like a part of my memory had awakened listening to this original tales.However, although these are all 5-star stories, I have to consider the presentation. Through no fault of John Glover, who I could listen to all day, musical intros and outros, and sound effects, were added to the stories. (This audiobook is from 1995, when audiobooks were in their infancy and released on cassette.) At times the music or sound effects totally drown out the storyteller. It is one of the most frustrating things.I will probably give the Night Shift collection audiobook a listen eventually.
