I read the Hardy Boys as a kid (and when I say "I read the Hardy Boys," I mean I read ALL of the Hardy Boys that existed at the time, as well as all the Nancy Drew books -- which I have since figured out were actually written by the same group of people. But anyway....) and decided that with my library's Librarians' Reading Challenge, I would revisit one of them to see how they hit as adults.And hoo boy.... I mean, they're not exactly bad. They're written perfectly for young readers of my generation. Each chapter ends in a cliffhanger that encourages the reader to continue, and there's enough excitement to keep a reader engaged page after page, after page. But as an 8-year-old, I didn't notice that the text, both the exposition and the dialogue, are so corny and cheesy that you wouldn't be surprised to find them as a side dish at Thanksgiving. It's the only place I've heard the word "jalopy" used so regularly, and the exchanges between Frank and Joe Hardy and both their cohorts and their adversaries would be laughable if presented as is on film or video.There are quite a few logical stretches, too. When the brothers find a note that says "Eighty-Six F.H.," they guess it means to kill Fenton Hardy, their father... never mind that one of the Hardy Boys is named Frank Hardy, giving him the same initials as his dad. They pursue their leads to Zurich and Mexico City as if these were small towns where they could just walk around and "check things out" -- but even in 1977 when this book was published, I'm pretty sure both of those were major cities where the information would need to be tracked down, not assumed to rise to the surface on their own. And one chapter ends with the boys staring down the barrel of a shotgun; the next chapter, however, begins with them facing down a rifle. (Sorry, Franklin W. Dixon, but those are two very different, but similar-looking, types of firearms.)Still, it was a fun read. I considered giving it 4 stars because it was actually closer to 3 1/2 stars in my estimation, but I give out a lot of 4-star ratings so I thought I would balance it out a little since it didn't really live up to my childhood memories. Not quite. But kind of.
