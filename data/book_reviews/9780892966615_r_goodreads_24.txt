The coolest thing about Parker's comeback is its lack of fanfare--when it appeared, this was the first Parker novel in 23 years, but that fact is referenced only in the novel's title. Other than that, it's a completely ordinary Parker novel (which is to say, a very good Parker novel) that could just as easily have been published in 1967 as 1997, a few contemporary cultural references notwithstanding.Comebackdrags only in its final act, as Parker novels sometimes do, when it becomes a cat-and-mouse game of who-is-going-to-kill-whom.
