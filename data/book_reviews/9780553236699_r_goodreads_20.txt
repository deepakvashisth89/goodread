Very cleverly done! I figured out the murder was committed before it was revealed, or at least was highly suspicious. But it took awhile, due to the smoke screens.Henry Gamadge's lovely young wife is set up to take the fall unless she decides to lie. She doesn't.This was a very good read for mystery lovers of days gone by, in one of the few good old fashioned mystery series with a back drop here in the United States rather than Britain.
