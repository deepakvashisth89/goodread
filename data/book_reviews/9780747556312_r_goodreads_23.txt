Another entertaining read about a sheep who wants to be bigger and faster than everyone else. His friend molly likes him just the way he is. Marvin keeps on eating and as he keeps eating he wants more and more until he ends up eating the whole earth. He gives himself a bad tummy and ends up throwing everything back up. Marvin realises even though everything isn't quite back to normal he prefers being his old self. A good story to explore in phse about just being yourself.This story reminds me of the dinosaur that poo'd series and you could get children to compare the different stories, characters and themes. You could also link the story to habitats, the food chain and evolution.
