I had missed reading Famous Five 14, 15 and 20 during my school days. They don't have much of an adventure in this book and most of the time they are fighting with this new American girl called Berta.But I want to believe I didn't pick up a good Famous Five book rather than think I'm too old for this series because most of my school days were colourful because I could read an Enid Blyton book. Miss those days ☹!
