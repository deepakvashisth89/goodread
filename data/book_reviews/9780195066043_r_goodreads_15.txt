This was a dense read for me, it covers a massive amount of time and though the authors tries to tack inclusion to the ends of chapters it is still white Euro-North American centric. With that limit in mind, it was a powerful introduction to the history of women under the western patriarchy.One quote summed up the theme that Lerner emphasized again and again throughout her book: “The women of talent existed, they struggled valiantly, they achieved—and they were forgotten. The women coming after them had to start all over again, repeating the process.” This book was a beautiful introduction to connecting with the cyclical struggle that is the origins of our feminist consciousness.
