L. Frank Baum is of course best known for the Oz books. But my mother, who loved Oz books and read them to me before I could read myself, said that "Queen Zixi" was her favorite Baum book. Years later, I've finally gotten around to reading it, and she was right. It's a kids' book but very enjoyable for adults as well.

It's not the Oz world, with a tornado from Kansas and an American con man pretending to be a wizard. It's really more a fairy tale, with a country that has a strange, random way of choosing its new king, who turns out to be a 12 year old boy -- who is a very believable boy. And his sister has a magical cloak that will grant a single wish. Let the adventures begin!

There's lots of humor, and the characters are well drawn. They evolve as the story unfolds, including Queen Zixi herself, who is a witch-queen of a neighboring country. Everyone learns responsibility and the limits to getting whatever you wish, but never in a preachy way.
