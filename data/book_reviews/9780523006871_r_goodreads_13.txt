The Executioner is joined by his old Nam squad and future Stony Man warriors Pol Blancaneles and Gadgets Schwartz, Able Team, in this St. Louis slaughter.

He’s got a different strategy in this one, trying to keep the old guard in charge in St. Louis, while New York mobsters try to horn in on the action in the sleepy Midwest city. They’ve kidnapped the current boss, who Bolan frees and uses to smash the mob.

Good action in this one, with Able helping out all the way, and we learn a bit of interesting background on the boys, as well as see their skills in action, from Pol’s ability to blend in to Gadgets love and use of electronic toys, which are all available in Bolan’s war wag.

Johnny Bolan makes an appearance as well, the kid wanting to join Bolan in his war, but the big guy convinces him to stay with Val and her new husband, Bolan can’t fight a war and look after his little bro at the same time.

A well rounded, later tale from Pendleton, it’s sort of amazing how he can still bring a freshness to this character and his adventures after 20+ books.
