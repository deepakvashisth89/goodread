From the afterword:
“Central to this trilogy, as to most of the science fiction of Suzette Haden Elgin, are two interrelated convictions: ‘The first hypothesis is that language is our best and most powerful resource for bringing about social change; the second is that science fiction is our best and most powerful resource for trying out social changes before we make them, to find out what their consequences might be.’”

This book is everything I want and I can’t wait to read the rest of the trilogy, and then read it over and over again.
