A fun and amusing mystery by a highly proficient writer. For extra fun, the murder is set at a fictionalized version of the American Booksellers Association convention of 1975, which Asimov himself attended as an author and panelist. Indeed, he appears as a minor character here--an overweight, inattentive, food-obsessed, girl-watching character. The narrator-protagonist is an undersized, unprolific, dyspeptic fellow author named Darius Just. The victim: another undeservedly successful author. Fun!
