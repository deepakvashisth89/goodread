This book is short and sweet, but very wise.In simple terms, Judy Blume examines the power of perspective. Brother is convinced his parents love Sister more. Sister believes Brother is the favorite. Both ignore little details, and overstate others, as they jump to wrong conclusions.It's hard to exaggerate the value of this lesson, even for adults.Reading this with my 6- and 8-year-olds (same ages as the brother/sister pair in the book), we had a great discussion about how you can think you understand everything, but you don't. Our opinions are just opinions, no matter how convinced we are. Usually, no one has all the facts. Together, we analyzed the little things Brother & Sister ignored as they chose to focus on the negative. And a week after reading it, we're still talking about perspective, so important in every day life. The best part is, it's broken down in a way that a first and third grader can both understand it.I'm so glad we read this together. I think they'll remember it and keep the lesson for a long time.
