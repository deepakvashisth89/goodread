I adored Hughes' work in high school and rereading this collection only cemented that love. There are poems about race, about everyday life before the civil rights era. There's also charming ditties that seem designed to get a smile out of you.I was surprised how many poems are familiar, how many lines stuck with me over twenty plus years.Come,Let us roam the night togetherSinging.There are others that have much more meaning now than they did in high school.WakeTell all my mournersTo mourn in red—Cause there ain't no senseIn my bein' deadNeedless to say Hughes remains my favorite poet of all time.
