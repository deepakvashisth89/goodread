There is nothing particularly revolutionary about this collection of exercises and reflections on how to quiet one’s mind. However, it was a great tool in examining how I dwell on thoughts that are unhelpful, particularly how burdensome worry is. We can control so very little. Here’s an absolute banger from the end of the book:As someone said to me recently, "The next time you think there is some situation you can control, try doing it when you have diarrhea."
