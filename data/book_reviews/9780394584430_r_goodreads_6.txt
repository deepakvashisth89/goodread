I picked this book up for $1 at bookoff and it ended up being one of my favorites.This book is ~1993 predicting ~2025, and covers major economies and demographics of the world. It really helped me, a person born in the twenty-first century, put my own mechanisms of future predictions in perspective: what signals I've observed in my lifetime are new and what are repeats of historical dynamics (or: already "priced in").Not all the predictions were correct. From my perspective, it failed to significantly imagine the honeymoon period between China and the US. But it was right about a lot of tensions we face today. The whole book revolves around the possible outcomes and the factors of uncertainty, instead of describing in detail the median expected outcome (which would be quite useless in a highly uncertain context).The writing is medium density and I found it easy to read throughout and I was not bored.
