For sure, one of the worst books I've read, a clear candidate for Top5Bleah... The characters are ugly, shabby, unctuous or fat (if not both...), almost nobody seems to be near normality, the atmosphere is depressing.
There are mountains and mountains of small talk, with no value at all, one accident, one killing (I'm very doubtful how you manage to kill someone with a kitchen knife, as you're not a trained murderer) and one suicide. By fortune, not mine...
