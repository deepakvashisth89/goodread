“The Herdmans were absolutely the worst kids in the history of the world. They lied and stole and smoked cigars (even the girls) and talked dirty and hit little kids and cussed their teachers and took the name of the Lord in vain and set fire to Fred Shoemaker’s old broken-down toolhouse.”

If you like a little wry humor with your holiday cheer, look no further than this delightfully narrated audio book by Elaine Stritch of The Best Christmas Pageant Ever!

There’s a simple holiday message hidden under the escapades of the horrible Herdman brood. When these rough and tumble siblings infiltrated the church’s annual Christmas show – to everyone’s horror - no one in the town could have imagined that their holiday tribute would take on a new perspective.

It was an hour and a half of entertainment that eased my work and left me feeling less stressed. And don’t think this is only a book for kids. If you are capable of chuckling over hilarious stunts, then you are the perfect reader for this.
