This easily could’ve been a touching/heart-warming narrative, if, the author actually allowed the child to be a part of the story. The boy was only plot-prop, with one purpose of moving the plot forward but without further importance. There was never a relationship, any tender emotion nor a decent reaction concerning the child from the hero. It was so weird. He was disinterested in the whole thing. Sure, he thought the child was his brother but later when he did find out, it wasn’t the reaction it should be either. Hero has no fatherly instinct whatsoever. Furthermore, heroine was just a disinterested in the matter too. You would think the heroine would react accordingly to father’s & son’s reunion. Yet nothing, besides a fleetingly thought or two. Just too bad...
