We love Lynley Dodd and first enjoyed her stories with Scarface Claw but I have to say that my daughter (5) and I were both giggling over Schnitzel von Krumm....such a little cutie!

In this story the family is packing to go on vacation and poor Scnhitzel was left behind. The story follows how they are reunited and its all done in a delightful and fun rhyme!

The pictures are very cute, Schnitzel is an adorable little dachshund!

Fun book!!
