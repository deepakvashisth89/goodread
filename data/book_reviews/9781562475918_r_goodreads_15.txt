Changes for Josefina is, personally, my least favorite book of the series. Mainly for two reasons.First and foremost, Tia Dolores ends up marrying Josefina's Papa, which was foreshadowed all throughout the book. However, I still don't feel like they should be together. I just wasn't feeling it.Trying to get Papa to propose was entirely what this book was about. It dominated the whole book making it a bit boring in my opinion.Secondly, during the book, Josefina asks her Papa if her sister Ana and her husband Tomas can go back with her grandparents in Tia Dolores's place. This bothered me a lot. The fact that she just wanted to send her sister and her family away just didn't seem right to me. Ana and Tomas were willing to leave their home in order to keep Tia Dolores with Josefina, Clara, Francisca and Papa. Doesn't Josefina think that Ana might want to live with Tia Dolores too?Anyways, just some of my thoughts on the book. Definitely not my favorite of the series.
