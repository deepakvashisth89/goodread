Another good example of how Ed Greenwood can create a magical world and juggle different fractions from across a continent, write a part of intriguing history... but absolutely not stories, especially no novels. Characters are as flat as ever, they talk and act in the way epic tales are narrated but not in a way that would lead the reader to any sort of familiarity or suspension of disbelief that this could actually be a place with living people.Read if you're interested in the clash of the Zhentarim and the Cult and Red Wizards and Harpers and a few new players, not if you think you are getting the last part of Shandril's story because that one does not progress, it just ends.P.S. I still don't understand why they could not simply be teleported to Silverymoon.
