I'm extremely sad that I don't get to know what happened before this one but I was also extremely happy to read this one so glass half empty glass half full situation I guess.

Marla Newman has had a few mentions but this book is all about her once we get past a prologue that is going to show us what we can expect in the scary department and it is gloriously gory.

Marla's deal is that she wants to be the class valedictorian. What is it with these Shadyside kids wanting to be such high achievers? Oh yeah...their parents and in this case, Marla's mom.

Well Marla isn't exactly an angel. She wants to outdo Kenny Klein to win that coveted spot just because I guess despite his being a pretty nice guy. Clarissa Turner trips and gets tea on Marla's blouse and she just freaks out because she was all dressed for success to win at the debate going against Kenny.

Also, way back we were told that Marla took the job that Josie Maxwell wanted for the summer and since Josie is good friends with Clarissa she watches the scene unfold and seem to escalate. Walking away, Clarissa trips and swears Marla pushed her. Josie, Jennifer Fear and Trisha Conrad are also there and seem to agree with her.

Marla didn't push Clarissa...but she did think it. This seems to get the attention of two junior girls, Roxanne and Elena. They wear black, have creepy jewelry that came from this late 1990s version of Hot Topic and Spencer's so clean-cut looking Marla is not impressed when they sit at her table.

Even more not interested when they say that Marla has...the power. You know...witchcraft but a natural witch like in The Craft and they want her to join them.

Marla blows them off and later in the day at the debate, Marla literally chokes from nervousness and it all culminates in a huge blech. The auditorium rings with laughter, Marla is mortified and Kenny beats her. The threshold for humiliation only grows when in French class, a recording of the incident blasts over the intercom for all of those not present to hear.

Marla finds that Josie, Clarissa, Trisha and Jennifer are behind it. Marla just can not catch a break but in Study Hall she vents her anger with writing down a list of people she hates. It's fine but she ends up putting almost all of her teachers and fellow seniors on that list for even the slightest well...slight given.

It comes back to bite her in the butt when it seems Josie gets a hold of it and posts it up for everyone to see. Instead of taking it in stride, Marla decides she's going to steal Josie's clothes during Gym and put them into the dumpster. Another karmic kick in the butt comes when Marla herself falls in and gets trapped.

Accident or on purpose, not sure but it is enough for Marla to now take Roxanne and Elena up on their offer of joining them in practicing The Dark Arts. To get revenge, to gain the spot of valedictorian she so rightly deserves.

It's fun at first and exciting to make people pay for laughing at her but soon Marla discovers that the power of course...has a price. It's even stated on the back of the book...blood.

Blood is the price Marla has to pay because that's what every dark power wants in return...

If Marla doesn't hold up her part of the pact she signed with her own blood in time she may meet a gruesome end...

Oh man there is so much I would love to spoil but I better stop before I say too much for those who have yet to read this entry in the Fear Street Seniors series. Twists, fake-outs, reveals and heart-stopping moments galore that almost had me passing out from holding my breath.

The climax and that ending...oh boy.

So now I am only one book away from finishing this series. Sadly, I'll have to miss out on the next episode but the finale...is just around the corner.
