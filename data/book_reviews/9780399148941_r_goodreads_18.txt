Another outstanding Lucas Davenport book (#13) by John Sandford. This one was particularly good with the return of Clara Rinker, hit woman par exellence. This one grabs you quickly and never lets go. Lucas is away from home chasing Clara as a consultant to the FBI. He, of course, is the one turning up most of the clues/ Clara is able to stay ahead of them for quite a while.My only nit pick was the ending, which was a little bit of a letdown, but as I said, just a nit pick.
