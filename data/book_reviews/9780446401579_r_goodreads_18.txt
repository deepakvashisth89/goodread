I am new to the Suspense/Thriller genre. Read about this author in the back of Stephen King's book, "On Writing."The main character was very interesting and the plot well-planned. The only issue I had with the book was Mr. Abrahams emphasized the "good" and "naive" part of the character well after he had left prison. It was almost too much.If you are a Suspense/Thriller fan, I highly recommend this book. It was written for intelligent people and the plot will not fail you.
