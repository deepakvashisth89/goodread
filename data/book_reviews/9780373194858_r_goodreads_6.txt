A tacky wedding in Reno leads to some interesting consequences for Brett and Laurel. Brett is new money, Laurel comes from old money that has run dry. But they come together for one magical night, then go their separate ways. Laurel goes home to deal with her father's abandonment and her complete lack of funds; Brett goes back to leading a successful company while trying to break into the social circles of the long time wealthy families.

Laurel winds up pregnant, alone, and without a way to support herself, let alone a baby. In desperation, she takes a very long bus ride to find Brett and beg him for a job. He offers her one: his wife. Laurel doesn't want to marry just for the baby, so she insists on just working for Brett's company. Brett is determined to convince her that they should marry to be a family for their baby, so he sets out to make her work life difficult.

Both Brett and Laurel have a lot to learn about love and family, but together they just might make it.

Such a fun book! McClone writes such fun, sweet romance.
