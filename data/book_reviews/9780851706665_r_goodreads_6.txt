I thought this book would be a little more general in terms of the film noir genre instead of its focus on certain movies under it. There were some essays that were genuinely very interesting, particularly the ones about family and the framing of women in the cinematography, but the rest were just hard to understand without having seen the movie in question. The first essay was by far the worst though; it felt like the writer just threw in a million intellectual sounding words in order to sound smart without actually making any clear and concise points.
