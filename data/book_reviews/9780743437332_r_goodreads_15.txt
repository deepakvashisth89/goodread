-- Reread 2016 --This was never a favorite of mine, especially back when I originally read it simply because things like the mafia and gang wars combined with history don't usually interest me. But I feel like I enjoyed it a little bit more this time around and can appreciate how vital it is to the series as a whole.I love Gunny, who is introduced in this book, and I like that this story keeps you guessing of who exactly Saint Dane is taking the form of to cause the tipping of the territory of First Earth into disaster. I also very much enjoyed the glimpse we get of Third Earth and that it shows just how poignant one moment in history can be in affecting the future.
