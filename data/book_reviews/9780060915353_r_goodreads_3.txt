A very good, far ranging survey of WW 2. Limited by author's US-based perspective and occasional, painful-if-amusing writing (to wit: "Hideki Tojo... was born on Dec. 30, 1884, the son of an army officer and the daughter of a Buddhist priest.")If one is to take the time to read it, on the up side, the books limitations make one want to investigate further. On the down side, since that task is daunting, one is left taking some content with a grain of salt (e.g. information in the biographical chapter on Stalin is presented as authoritative, when there is still much debate about many of the facts - should I, do I have the time or enough interest to read a "New Historian" biography of Stalin, as counterpoint?)
