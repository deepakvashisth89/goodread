This is one of my favorite Oz books! I think it's because there's an actual mystery intertwined into the plot... Actually, multiple mysteries!Princess Ozma of Oz is missing one morning, and her faithful friends must scour the land of Oz to find her. They're also trying to find the magical items that went missing on the same night that Ozma disappeared. To top it all off, Toto has lost his growl -- but that somehow has loosed the little terrier's tongue, because my, is he talkative in this volume!Yes, it's another journey that takes the reader through various parts of Oz, to meet new and strange peoples... again. But this time, it just felt different to me. I liked it!A fabulous read for children (and yes, adults).
