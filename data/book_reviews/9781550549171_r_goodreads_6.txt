Purchased in Victoria on my honeymoon in October 2002 at Monro's Books, along with some delicious Rogers Chocolate Covered Ginger Candies. Perused a bit with my wife, and have had it in my classroom for decades now as a little beacon of appreciation for my wife and our life together.
Content wise, super inspiring for students. Could they make a Souvenir of Western PA photo essay book of their things, their parents things, the things of their house?
Coupland's prose is personal, witty, articulate, sharp, informed, nostalgic, and pointed.
As an American, and now for decades a teacher of American Literature, I love the essay on "Them". Used in a one-off year teaching World Literature, because you can excerpt the book, introduce the topic with the photo, use for convergent or divergent activities in a classroom, to teach about geography, culture, linguistics, photography.
The back cover reads:
"I wanted to give people a book that explains what it feels like to be Canadian. And I wanted to find a new way of doing this...and here it is."
Now very much a relic of its time, and in a meta sense, Coupland was aware of time/culture/identity as he crafted it.
