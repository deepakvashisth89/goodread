I really would have given this book a 2.5. Why? Because it's half good. Two awesome stories are included with a just Ok and a story that only makes sense in the writer's mind. The two worth reading are: Magic and Mayhem (about a Irish maid in colonial Philadelphia) and Fairies Make Wishes, Too (which is scarily similiar to The Little Mermaid). I could not put those two stories down. They saved this book from 1 star status. Ever True is a story that I could not believe in (the characters, the plot). I did not get invested into, and it bored me. Although I loved the idea of making perfume to classic Bard works! I hated Fairy Dreams. Should have been named Fairy Nightmares of Confusion. I couldn't follow this story because nothing was explained. Why did Mae fall for a Fairy Prince? Why would a fairy prince care about her? Grrrr.... Totally confusing, badly written, and filled with so many holes I wished the characters would have fell in one.
