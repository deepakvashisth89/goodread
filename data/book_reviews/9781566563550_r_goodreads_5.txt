Beautiful illustrations!

Poetic text about a newly hatched sea turtles journey from getting out of the nest & sand, to the edge of the ocean. He meets perils/dangers along the way- predators like a seagull and crab.
He makes it to the very edge of the ocean and the sea comes in and takes him underwater where he is relieved, happy and excited to begin his life.

This book is especially meaningful to me because I live in Boca Raton, FL and we have sea turtles nesting here on our beaches. I still haven't been lucky enough to actually see a nest hatching out.
