4.25 starsThis book is the third in this series, but works perfectly as a standalone because the main character, George, is visiting a remote Scottish island. He's on his own without his wife in this book and I could be wrong, but it seems like the island residents appear in this book only.George is a birdwatcher and an amateur sleuth and is convinced that the death of a young girl was no accident. He enlists Sarah, a newlywed and newcomer to the island of Kinness to help him in his inquiries. The people of the island don't take well to newcomers and embrace a style of living that seems like something in the past. I enjoyed the investigation and the bittersweet resolution, but there's more to the book. It is as much about Sarah adjusting to her new home and deciding how much of her independent identity she can retain and still fit in on Kinness. I really enjoyed this interesting, leisurely-paced mystery.
