I have always enjoyed the Alfred Hitchcock film The Thirty-Nine Steps so I wanted to read the source material for the film. Even though the plot is different in many ways from the movie, the book is extremely enjoyable.

There is lots of action as Richard Hannay tries to escape being caught by both the police and the bad guys and also decipher the solution to a worldwide intrigue with important consequences. Buchan provides lots of humor and this book is a fast read coming in a little more than 100 pages.

I hope soon to watch the movie again.
