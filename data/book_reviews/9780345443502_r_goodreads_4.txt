This was an interesting and original perspective of the greatest story every told - the view from Pontius Pilate looking back 30 years after the death of Christ. Not only did the author go into Pilate's thoughts, along with Pilate's wife's thoughts and feelings, but he presented wonderful historical detail of the Roman occupation of Israel, explanations of the Sanhedrin vs Pharisees, and the always convoluted history of the rulers of the various territories at the time. This is not a religious story, but an interesting take on Pilate's role in the time of Jesus and early Christianity.
