I give this book 5 stars--because I'm tired of reading about distopias--and this is a utopia I could support! Everyone knows of "Shangri-la" but how many have read the 1933 book by James Hilton ( 1900-1954) ? Many, many years ago I saw the '37 film on TV. It was directed by Frank Capra starring Ronald Colman, Jane Wyatt, and ( most unforgettably for me!) Sam Jaffe as the High Lama. I forgot most of the details of the story in the film, so found the book all the more interesting for that. Four people from the "outside world" find themselves in the valley of the Blue Moon in Tibet and become the guests at the mysterious monastery known as Shangri-la. One man, a war veteran named Conway, finds the place to be a tranquil haven and a place where he could stay. Another and younger man, Mallinson, hates the place and only wants to escape.... Meantime, Shangri-la guards its great secret...
