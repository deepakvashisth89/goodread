This was quite a cute, slightly Dickensian story. I loved Charles’s pranks and his whole personality. The only thing I didn’t like about this book is that it’s very sanctimonious and that the character of Juliette doesn’t even seem like a real human being for a long time. At the end when she insists she won’t leave Charles, she’s the closest to being a three-dimensional character but before that she’s just the saintly, perfect blind girl who bears her burden with humbleness and faith in God … and honestly, that depiction of disabled people annoys me quite a bit.
