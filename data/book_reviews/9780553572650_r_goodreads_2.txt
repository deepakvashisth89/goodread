This was a pretty decent story. It was a unique concept and was executed well. I felt each character brought an interesting perspective and all seemed to contribute to the progression of the plot. I think the author did a good job of having a reader visualize what everything would look like in screen of all the "live" broadcast. Another thing the author did well, if that was their intention, was I was getting quite annoyed with certain characters actions in tandem with the characters themselves. There was a fair bit of fun character dynamics and politics. Although I struggled with the pacing of the story, it felt forcibly drawn out or prolonged. Instead of the characters choices driving the plot, it felt the author wanted a longer story and was forced to extend it and that made the characters make the choices. Also how the last plotline, how the situation resolved felt bizarre and super unrealistic, while the beginning was pretty real this annoyed me. But overall a decent story
