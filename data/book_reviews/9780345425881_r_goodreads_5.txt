Sally Wright's first book in the Benjamin Reeves Series was more than a breezy mystery. The first chapter or two began awkwardly for me. I almost stopped reading the book, which is unusual for me. I decided to stick with it to see if it would life up to my expectations. I'm glad that I did.The story takes place on a college campus where the featured character Ben Reeves is an archivist; hence a "detail" man. Ben, a very likeable main character, believes that his friend and colleague has been murdered. Everyone else assumes it to be a death brought on by natural causes. To prove murder leads to very interesting sleuthing. Lots of twists led to a very compelling story. Using an archivist as an informal detective creates an unusual point of view, which I enjoyed. I enjoyed the story enough to purchase the author's next book, which begins so much more smoothly than her first book. If you're a mystery fan and like a bit more depth and detail in your stories, consider this new series. (I gave it 3 stars because of the slow beginning, but I suspect the next book will be 4 stars.)
