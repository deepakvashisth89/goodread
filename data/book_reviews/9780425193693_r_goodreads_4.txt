A Hot and Sultry Night for Crimeis not the usual MWA annual short story collection; the stories aren’t new and many are okay at best. G. Miki Hayden’s "War Crimes" is what earned the book a fourth star. Although both subject and style are dated, I found the story’s title a prism through which the layers making up both "war" and "crime" are chillingly revealed.Also recommended are Marilyn Wallace’s "Splitting" and Robert Lee Hall’s "No Lie."
