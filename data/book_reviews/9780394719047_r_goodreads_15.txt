Reading Project: This was the thirteenth stop in a series of writers who I haven't read before!General Review: Watts has been on my TBR since the mid 2010's when a former roommate and close friend of mine recommended him. I was in my late teens, early twenties then, and, unfortunately, this book would've meant more to me then.Having grown as a reader over the past ten years, I prefer to encounter the rich ideas expressed in Watts's work within a narrative (preferably fictional) context. Reading essays on what is essentially an unclassifiable philosophical experience is, nowadays, not concrete enough to be compelling to me.Further Reading (?): No, I don't think so. I have what I need of Watts's philosophy and writing style.
