This was a long book with a convoluted, seemingly unrelated subplot. Since I listened to the audiobook, I found the beginning very confusing, especially regarding the oil company and the gubernatorial campaign. Just like any Perry Mason book, the ending chapters with the courtroom scenes stand out as the best part of the story.Because I have not read the beginning books in this series, I don't know whether or not reading this "out of order" affected my understanding in any way.
