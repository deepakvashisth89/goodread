MOONLIGHT MISTRESS - G
Rice, Patricia - Standalone

Orphaned Aelvina Fairfax has survived every challenge of war-torn England—until her faithful nurse and only friend Thyllda falls ill near the castle of the Lady Ravenna, a reclusive widow rumored to be a witch. Frightened and desperate, Aelvina goes to Sir Philippe St. Aubyn, seeking protection for herself and Thyllda in exchange for one night of passion in his arms. But bliss soon turns to despair when Aelvina wakes to find herself alone—and carrying Philippe's child...

Sent to reclaim Dunstan Castle from Ravenna in the name of King Henry II, Philippe is unaware that both he and Aelvina are pawns in Ravenna's dark scheme. And when the ravishing young beauty who fulfilled him like no other vanishes without a trace, he refuses to believe she existed only in a dream. Now, Philippe will not rest until he rescues Aelvina from a sorceress' wrath—and is joined with her in a love that can defy all obstacles...
