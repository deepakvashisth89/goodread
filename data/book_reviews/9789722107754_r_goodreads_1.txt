The short story that the Portuguese writer Mário de Carvalho entitled A Inaudita Guerra da Avenida Gago Coutinho transfers to the reality of the 'daily war' of Lisbon traffic, a retreat that is inspired by the epic Homeric. It is, therefore, about the transposition to a contemporary situation, of 'prolonged conflict', of figures, situations and language marked by the Homeric convention. The evident gap between the modern nature of the case and the aesthetic tone that shapes it contributes to creating a satirical style that is very characteristic of this author, a successful 'chronicler' of today's Portuguese reality.
