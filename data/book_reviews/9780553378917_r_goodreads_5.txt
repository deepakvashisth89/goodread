I read this book because I really enjoyedBlackburnby Bradley Denton.While this was not quite as good, it was still enjoyable. It is a mystery why this author is not widely known in the world of books.Fantasy is generally not my preference when it comes to reading. But, the fantasy elements in this book were used more as a metaphor. The characters and friendships depicted in the story were certainly believable, and true to real life.I'm sure I will read more from this author. But, I will space them out as there are few of them.
