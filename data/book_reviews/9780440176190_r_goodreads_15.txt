Retroactive Review (11 Sep 2021):"Rigging" is a weird method of traveling Flux space (i.e. hyperspace) since pilots need to be "dreamers" and be able to visualize their routes. This was previously the only Star Rigger novel I'd read before, but after readingSeas of Ernatheand then reading this one, I grew really frustrated with the unlikable and frustrating protagonists. (This novel had a fun premise, though--two enemy aliens having to work together to "rig" their ships.)
