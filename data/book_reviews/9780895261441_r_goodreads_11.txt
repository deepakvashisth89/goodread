This is a very important book for persons discerning a vocation as well as parents who have children that may have a priestly vocation. They need to be prepared for some possible roadblocks and know that they may need to be as cunning as a serpent to navigate waters that may be unfriendly to orthodoxy.This book is not appropriate for teens under 16 in my estimation. Also, I suggest following this book up immediately with Gift and Mystery by Pope St. John Paul II. That will combat the funk this book will put you in.I disliked the “liberal v conservative” language in the book. There are orthodox “liberals” as well. The author is definitely a Repubicatholic. I understand the tendency, but it is unfortunate.
