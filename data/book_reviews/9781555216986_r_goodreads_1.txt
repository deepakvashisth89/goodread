Hysterically awful in a "Plan 9 From Outer Space" kind of way. I just kept reading to see where the next typo or wrong "fact" would pop up. And Gary Brodsky wouldn't fail me. For example, he can't tell the difference between a leopard and a cheetah. And every other sentence seems to end with an exclamation mark! To let you know he really, really means it! Oh, the hilarity ensues.There are a bunch of so-so line drawings of cats presumably done by Brodsky -- either that or the real artist was so embarrassed by the drawings he/she had them printed anonymously.And the funniest fact of all is that I paid about 50 cents for this thinking it was ANOTHER book calledThe Mind of the Cat.Or something like that. Yikes.
