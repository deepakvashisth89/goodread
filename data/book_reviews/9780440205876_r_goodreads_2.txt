Robert Barnard is well-known as a literary critic and academic—his forays into mystery writing seem to be largely forgotten today. I read Death of A Mystery Writer and The Bones In The Attic in quick succession and enjoyed both greatly. And then I discovered that I already had 'Death of a Salesperson' on my Kindle, downloaded aeons ago and forgotten.

This is an anthology of short stories with an element of crime or mystery, each one with a twist ending. If you've enjoyed the early short stories of Jeffrey Archer, you'll definitely like these. I think they also have great re-read value.
