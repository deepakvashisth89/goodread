Intense, suspensful and gritty.I live in the mountains so I know how dense, steep and almost impossible they can be and how searching for 2 men would be almost impossible. The two men were so much savage killers as just plain old crazy, demented mountain men. The sheriff was a dedicated no-nonsence guy who was determined to get the men who kidnapped a woman as she ran along a trail in Montana. I would have liked to learn more about the woman but the book really focused on hunting the men down.
