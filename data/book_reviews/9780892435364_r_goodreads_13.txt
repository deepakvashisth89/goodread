This book is the ideal reading for those people who consider themselves strong Catholics because they had 4-8-12 years of education in the Catholic School system and are comfortable in what the allegedly learned to make them knowledgeable and experts in their faith.

Read it and you will realize how much you did not know. For those of you who are either non-Catholic or have had little exposure to the faith, read it to learn a small iota of what it's about.
