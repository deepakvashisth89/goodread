This is the box set for the three Lord of The Rings books, plus The Hobbit.

There's a 15-years gap between publication of The Hobbit and TLOTR - I'm guessing that's how long it took Tolkien to compile his amazing ideas into a readable volume - going from a mesmerizing children's epic to one of the greatest trilogies of all time.

I've rated and reviewed each book individually at 5 stars in every instance.
