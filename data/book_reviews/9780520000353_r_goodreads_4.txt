2.5As much as this book likes to think it's relevant today (see opening of book), it's really not. The reason Arnheim wrote this was to disprove claims at the time that film was not art because it was mechanically imitating reality (or something similarly stupid); which today holds no ground and was something I wasn't even familiar with. Not to bring down Arnheim because he does a good job of disproving these claims very thoroughly. I have to gripe at his indictment of the future of cinema and bemoaning the "death of cinema" or whatever, with the coming of sound and color; his views here seemed kinda pretentious and closed-minded. That being said I appreciate his love and admiration of silent film. I mainly rated this low because I disagree with his predictions/musings on non-silent film and the mechanics in the second part of the book were pretty dull, at least for me.
