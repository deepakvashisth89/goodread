In the late 1950s I remember sitting in class at Glen Avon Public in Willowdale, Ontario. My best friend was Tom Halsey and he would read books hidden in his lap rather than listen to the teacher. He was constantly in trouble for this behavior but it was just that behavior that started me reading something other than the sports pages of the Toronto Telegram. My first book was Scrubs on Skates by Scott Young the writer that I later saw regularly on Hockey Night in Canada between periods on the Hot Stove TV broadcast.Mr Young sure could write about sports and he sure got into a heap of trouble covering the Leafs - this was before Punch Imlach turned them into a contender - they won four Stanley Cups in the Sixties as I recall and Mr Scott Young and I were there through it all. Scott Young got a lot of kids reading recreationally - a lot more than William Shakespeare although I did eventually begin to understand the attraction folks had for the bard and others.When I retired from engineering in 2001, I took up writing novels in the style I learned from Scott Young. Written three so far.Thanks, Scott, wherever you are.Trivial Pursuit input : Neil Young of Rock n Roll fame was one of Scott Young's sons from Omemee Ontario.
