So, this book is on several lists of classics. It's the rough prototype for the Cyberpunk genre, and it's....really, really odd.It has a surreal, skeletal feel that reminds me of Harlan Ellison, or Philip K. Dick. No subtlety, no subtext, just raw ideas (and characters) blasting off the page and squirming into your brain.Its great if you like that sort of thing.If you need more meat and polish, you may want to look elsewhere.
