This book is for everyone who likes a nice, sweet H.The h slaps him in his face in public, she calls him a murderer, she calls him disgusting, she lashes out at him, she says she hates him, she is always angry.He on the other hand is always nice, he takes care of her when she is sick, he says the sweetest things to her, he always tries to protect her, he carries her to the moon and back.In my opinion he is too tame. He didn’t show that raw, vibrant sexual desire for her. He says the right things to the h like “I could eat you alive”, but he doesn act on it.They only kiss a couple of times with their clothes on. They don’t have sex or anything like it. It was a bit dull in that department.But nevertheless, it was well written and a good story. So 4 stars.
