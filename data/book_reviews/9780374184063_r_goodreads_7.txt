This was a book on my mom’s shelf, written early in the 90s.My thoughts were all over the place when I completed the book. Overall enjoyed this unique book.I gave it a 3.5 ⭐️’s: interesting usage of dialogue, intertwined with philosophy. However several chapters were hard to follow. The book was also a love story with protagonist Ava Mueller, in her 40’s who thinks she’s in love with a 20-year-old.
