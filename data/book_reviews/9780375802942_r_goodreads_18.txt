My seven year old granddaughter and I bond further with still another Junie B book. This time little Tillie gave me the book and a quiz sheet with four questions to answer after reading the text. I think I will get 100% correct on the test. I love to do well!!

I had two favorite parts in the book. One was Junie B's mention of her baby brother, Ollie. She says of him, "Ollie is ten months old. He cannot skip or play tag or color. So far, I am not satisfied with him." Since my Tillie has a nine month old baby sister, I really loved this line, though I think Tillie is pretty satisfied with her baby sister!

The other part I liked was Junie B's description of the smell of the tuna noodle casserole she was helping serve in the school cafeteria - stinkle. What a perfect word for bad smelling food and I wonder how many households in America use it thanks to Junie B. Jones!

If you are a first grader (or kindergartner or even second grader), I highly recommend this book.
