Review for A Lonely Place of Dying story line.

I love how this dealt with Batman’s change of behaviour but it wasn’t the sole focus.

The inclusion of Nightwing and that complicated relationship is always a fun read.
Then you have this kid who knows everything thrown in.

I don’t typically love when characters just know things but at least Tim was able to back up his knowledge with some logical thinking.

I also think it was a great decision to have Tim reluctant to become Robin. I can’t wait to read how different his version is (again, and after having read the previous iterations).

The last time I read this, I did not appreciate the ending or what it meant. Moments like that make me feel glad I’m reading comics the way I am.

Reading this again, with the context of the stories that came before, just made everything feel more connected and in sync. It also allowed me to better understand Batman’s state of mind.

The pacing… was interesting. It was a slow start with an explosive middle then an end that only felt quick because they were shorter issues than what we’d just read.

The story is famous for what happens to Jason but honestly, I felt it was a lot more about the relationship between Batman and the Joker.

This also establishes that the events of are canon.

While Jason drew the short straw, it also led to a character that I think is a lot more interesting (almost 20 years later).
