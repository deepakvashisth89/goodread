I joined Goodreads a decade ago next month, having stumbled on it looking for something in relation to Grass. This was the first book I shelved here, and the symmetry between it being the first and then the 1000th was too linear to pass up. My first review holds—I still find this to be Grass’ masterwork and a perfect novel—but this is really nothing more than a preamble to saying thank you to everyone that’s turned me on to so much beauty. Whether directly or unbeknownst, I’ve filched your shelves and brains, making books 2-999 what they never could’ve been without your influence. Foremost, I learned over the last decade the most significant thing, a not always easy process in real-time: how NOT to be the person I have never wanted to be, fell into playing at, and, consequentially, been wildly enriched through the process of unbecoming. I know fuckall—it’s liberating. Fuck persona, fuck the canon, fuck gatekeeping—it’s so much easier just being a person. You’re good. I’m good. It’s only baseball….

I hope I, and all of you, get at least another 1000 books together. Whatever happens, “life was beautiful and nothing hurt.” It’s true, you know.

Be fantastic, every single person that may see this wherever and whenever. It was a time.
