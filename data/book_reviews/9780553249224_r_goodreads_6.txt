Plot – fairly standard.Good guy, minding his own business, is set upon by an outrageous number of the worst and fastest gunmen out there.The heroine, unbelievably beautiful, first irks the hero and then becomes indispensable as the smartest most loyal woman around.Fighting by fist, gun, club, fist again, and end with a shootout.Surprise, the hero loves the heroine.I usually like these books, for just that reason. The good guy will always win. He’s always perfect. The bad guy will always lose. The hero marries the heroine, and they live happily ever after.Unfortunately, this book had more swearing than his other book’s I’ve read, and the heroine’s form was focused on way too much. I would recommend several of his other books instead.
