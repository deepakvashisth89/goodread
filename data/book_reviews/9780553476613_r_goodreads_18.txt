4.5/5The Lawnmower Man is a hoot, I loved it. I love it every time Stephen King makes machines murderous and this time it’s a regular push-lawnmower. He added some mythology into the story: Pan is the owner of the lawnmower company and the serviceman is a satyr-like human who eats grass and has feet that resemble cloven hooves. It’s humorous and absurd and dark, which is what I loved about it.Included in the Night Shift collection.
