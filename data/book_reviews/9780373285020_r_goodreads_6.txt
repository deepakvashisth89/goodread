I love Nora Roberts and her books on magic! She writes wonderful romance stories and although some have a touch of mystery, this one did not. It was #3-4 of the Donovan Legacy and I had already read the other ones. You do not have to read this series in order, they could be read as stand alone and there are no cliff hangers. Ms. Roberts writing is always like poetry in motion and these were definitely “feel good” stories. Love them both!
