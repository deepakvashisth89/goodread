In chronological order it is the first book of a very long saga, even if it is not the first published, and from this I decided to start. The reading is fast and smooth and, after a slightly slow start once again immersing the reader in the universe thought by Brooks, the story "heats up" in the last hundred pages by gluing the reader to the book to find out how it will all end.Despite being a fantasy, the magic is, obviously, present but it is not yet too invasive as, I think, will happen later in the following books of the saga.If, like me, the Shannara saga has always intrigued you, this book is, in my opinion, a great way to throw yourself headlong into the world created by Terry Brooks.Show more
