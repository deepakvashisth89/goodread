Poirot is introduced in this book, so how can you not love it?!



Actually, pretty easily.
This simply isn't Agatha Christie's best work.
And that's mainly due to this being her first book. Yeah, so not only was she still figuring out who was, she was still figuring out this whole mystery writing gig. I mean, for her first stab at it, she did a phenomenal job. But if you go into this thing thinking you're going to get her story, you'll more than likely be disappointed.




For those of you who are already fans, I think you'll find this a fun book because, while he's not fleshed out yet, all the trademarks of Poirot are there. Hastings is still a bit of a well-intentioned dumbass, and Japp shows up and gives the weird little Belgian free rein to solve the case under the table. Plus, the whodunnit was appropriately twisty and turny.



My advice?
If you're new to Poirot - don't start here. Yeah, I know that it's #1, but you don't need to read these books in order. Think of as a prequel that you can go back and read you get hooked on Hercule.
