Having been introduced many years ago to Henry Gamadge by my aunt, who likedNero Wolfe and Agatha Christie, I hunted up a couple more Elizabeth Dalys recently and enjoyed them as period pieces: very white-shoe murder mysteries. I've never seen The Thin Man movies, but gather these would fit right in. This one I'd say merits 3.5 stars but that rounds to a four. I liked better the earlier one in which Henry meets his future wife.
