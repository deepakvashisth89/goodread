The Return of Mr. Campion is a collection of short mystery stories starring gentleman detective Mr. Albert Campion through various adventures in mid-20th-century Britain. This edition, published by Agora Books and released on April 11, 2019, includes The Tall Man, which was not in the original Return of Mr. Campion, as well as an excellent forward by Margery Allingham titled Mystery Writer in the Box that I found extremely interesting about both her work and the genre of mystery fiction in general. Born in 1904, Ms. Allingham wrote British mystery stories, many of which included Mr. Campion, for many many years. And her mysteries are very involved and the solution well hidden.I am pleased to see these novels being re-issued. They are timeless, and an excellent way to calm the world around you if only for a few hours. I look forward to many more, Agora.
