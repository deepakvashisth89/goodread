A random find at a Little Free Library outside the Y. Nothing exceptional in terms of plotting, but Sanders was an excellent writer. This is a pulpy crime novel written with much more care and craft than one might except. Wonderfully redolent of late 70s New York in all its grimy, class-extremes, graffitied glory—with the expected occasional outdated improprieties. But this aged very well, imho, in terms of style and language. And a pretty excellent ending.I didn’t know anything about Sanders before but this made me want to read more from him.
