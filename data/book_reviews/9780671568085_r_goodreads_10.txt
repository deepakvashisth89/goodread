I quite enjoyed both the A plot and the B plot of this book and how they tied them together. I especially enjoyed the interactions between Riker and Deanna in this book. I will definitely be reading it again. It's a fun (though sad) story. I should have expected the ending given the title of the book, but I didn't! It didn't have nearly as much detail about the characters as 'Ghost Ship' does, but I actually enjoyed that. Despite this, I could still hear the characters voices in my head.
