The Breadwinner by Deborah Ellis is a deeply moving and unforgettable book that exposes the harsh reality of life under oppressive rule through the eyes of a child. What struck me most was the overwhelming sense of injustice that runs through every part of the story.Parvana’s life is turned upside down simply because she is a girl, and the rules she has to live under feel arbitrary and deeply unfair. Ordinary things—like going outside, working, or getting an education—become dangerous or forbidden, and that contrast makes the injustice even more powerful.Despite this, Parvana’s resilience is remarkable. Watching her adapt to impossible circumstances and take on responsibilities far beyond her years is both heartbreaking and inspiring. The story forces you to confront how quickly childhood can be stripped away when basic rights are taken.Deborah Ellis writes in a simple but impactful way that makes the emotions and reality of the situation hit even harder. It’s not just a story—it’s an experience that stays with you long after you finish reading.This is a book that leaves you reflecting on fairness, human rights, and the strength it takes to survive in an unjust world. A must-read.
