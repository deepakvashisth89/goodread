The biggest strength in a lot of the Bolan books is not necessarily the action but the supporting characters. By this point Pendleton had amassed a decent amount of supporting characters to interact with Bolan regularly. This book brings 3 of the best in one book and it works really well because of it. Leo, Hal and Jack all have some great interactions with Bolan. It’s a little lighter on the action than some of the earlier books but it does make up for it with some great interplay with the characters.
