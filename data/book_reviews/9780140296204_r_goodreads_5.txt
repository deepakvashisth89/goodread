As this is Shakleton's memoir of his Nimrod expedition, I was excited to read a first hand experience of his lesser known expedition. There were a few slow areas, as was expected, one section being when the Nimrod was trying to get close enough to the ice shelf so the team could unload and disembark. Other misses were all the referenced coordinates and some detailed scientific descriptions, but overall this is an excellent memoir to add to a polar exploration library.

I learned more about the Antarctic, the attempt to reach the South Pole, and also the expedition to the magnetic South Pole by a smaller team that was taken in parallel with Shakleton's expedition.

Lastly, the Appendix on the penguins that was written by the biologist was more enjoyable than expected; it was both informational and comical. I'm glad I didn't pass on that short read.
