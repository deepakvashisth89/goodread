Thriller was never my page of book, yet what lured me in was the brief introduction on the back of the book. I read this on a snail-like pace, I snuck in to my view a page or two in-between classes. And always, always, I have to fight back the urge to turn another page.

The adventure, mystery, and adrenaline was what kept me impatient. I sincerely adore the first-person point of view since I could overlook both the outer and inner part of the main character. Also, my brain went to an imagination overdrive, all thanks to the detailed description this book has to offer (except for the gory part, no thank you).

Overall, I enjoy it. Though I'm not liking the male-centered gaze (since the story was told from Valdiva's perspective) on women character in this book. I would re-read this to cure my boredom (or reading slump).
