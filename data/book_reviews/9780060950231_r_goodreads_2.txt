Being Catholic can be a mystery to the non-believers. But it can be baffling to the truly faithful as well. Along with all the jokes in this book are many eyewitness accounts of miracles, answers to questions about what nuns wear under their habits and tales of hijinks in Catholic schools that are sure to raise a giggle or two. Rules, laws, bylaws, and customs are outlined with all due solemnity while urging us to laugh at the absurdity of it all. As one man stated, if God knows everything, then he knows a few good jokes.
