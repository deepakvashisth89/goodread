Third in the Duncan Kincaid & Gemma James series by Deborah Crombie, I was underwhelmed. The mystery itself was well done, the characters believable and the description made you feel you were there. However the pacing was a little slow, and the main characters are simply not that interesting. Nothing here really held my interest and I continued on to the end more from a sense of duty than to extend my enjoyment / see what happens.There were questionable actions taken by superintendent Kincaid that were out of character, one wildly so. I may try one of these again, but not any time soon. I listened to the audiobook and the reader, Christopher Kay, was very good.
