In Grave Mistake by Ngaio Marsh, former woman of the theater Verity Preston has lived in her original hometown of Upper Quintern and now uses the chance to write plays. Her childhood sometimes-friend, Sybil Foster, whose daughter Prunella is Verity’s goddaughter, accompanies Verity to a private party at the estate of their new neighbor, the rich collector Nicolas Markos. There, Verity is alarmed to see a face from her past, now calling himself Dr. Basil Schramm, and Sybil is drawn at once to the highly magnetic man. Verity can’t settle down to her writing before receiving news that Sybil’s stepson from her late husband’s first marriage, the “egregious Claude,” is about to arrive from Australia.
