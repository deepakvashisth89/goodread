A heavy book of topics that is easy to read.A young woman deals with an abusive past and stumbles into a cult. The story follows her through the stages of her life. You can't help but root for her while simultaneously wanting to shake some sense into her skull.Heavy topics the book hits on: child abuse, death, Christianity to cult beliefs, anxiety, rape, drug addiction (use and selling), alcoholism, adoption/foster care, child loss, and a brief mention of suicide.
