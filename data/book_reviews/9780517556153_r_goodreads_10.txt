A lot good here, but it worked much better as a visual TV series than it does in book form. Dyer is an astute observer and not sentimental. His discussion of the conundrum of the nation-state--that its existence endangers life on earth, but if you don't have one just ask the Palestinians what that looks like--turned out to be sage indeed. How right he is about the extent to which information we've learned about pre-history presages our demise is open to debate, but will keep you up at night. Dyer thinks that the evidence of endemic violence found in the bones of our ancestors implies that given enough time and the right weapons humanity is going to off itself. War bred us, and war will destroy us. Perhaps he is right, but I fervently hope not.
