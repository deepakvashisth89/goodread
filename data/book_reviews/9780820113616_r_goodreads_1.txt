A short early collection of verse by L Frank Baum, between his Mother Goose adaptations and the debut of Oz. The volume is split into themed sections, with rhyming narratives about falling in love, growing old together, being out to sea, cycling, women wearing pants, women wearing pants while cycling (really), and finally a stretch of original nursery rhymes for children. They're all snappy and vivid, with some packing quite a witty punch. It's a very slight and brisk volume, but charming, and worthwhile to track down if you're interested in Baum's development as a storyteller.
