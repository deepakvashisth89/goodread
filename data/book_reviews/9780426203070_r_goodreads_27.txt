The Macra Terror is one of the stories that underlines the difference between the "old" Doctors and the "new" ones ... When an Earth Colony is threatened by the indigenous species, Troughton's Doctor,rather than negotiating a peace, works to see to it that the "rightful inhabitants," the humans, will throw off the yoke of their alien overlords.It's a fun story, and the descriptions of the Doctor's antics are spot on.
