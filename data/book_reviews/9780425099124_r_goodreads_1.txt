This went a long way to affirming my observation of the interpersonal difficulties I have often observed amongst women in an office environment. I don't think it taught me anything really new, but it was sure nice to have women describe what I was observing that contradicted the doe-eyed myth that if only women ran the world the world would be fantastic. Women are people, too, and sex of either gender does not guarantee either the ability to lead or to be compassionate. However I found the writing weaker, in this book, than in Orbach'sFat is a Feminist Issue,but a worth while read nonetheless.
