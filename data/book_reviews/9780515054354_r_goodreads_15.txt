This is one of my favorite of the series. That's a bit surprising since Alleyn doesn't show up till about half way through and Troy isn't in it at all. But the real main character, Peregrine Jay, is wonderfully real and very likable. I love every page spent in his company.Also, this book takes full advantage of Marsh's experience in the theater as Jay writes, hires, and produces a play during this book. The main actors in the play manage to escape typecasting, which is also good.Add in a touch of romance, an abandoned theater, a Gatsby-like millionaire and a mystery revolving around Shakespeare's personal life and you have an excellent story. Even when I remember who did it it's a pleasure to reread.
