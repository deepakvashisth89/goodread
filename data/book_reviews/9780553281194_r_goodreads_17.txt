I read this 40+ years ago when it was loaned to me by a grad school friend. Loved it. Loved it. It is action adventure set in Indochina and involves operators from different services and nations set upon a clandestine mission. The author wove a tight story with reasonably believable details and characters. Dialogue is appropriate for such a tight lipped group. One nice detail is the narrator choosing to carry and use a shotgun as his preferred jungle weapon. The ending left me with mixed feelings and ambivalent desires for more and similar tales.
