Suzanne Brockmann's suspense stories are engaging, but without the suspense we're left with very plain, dull language and plain, dull plots. I nearly gave up on this book until things worked out the way they did in Boston (Simon spends forever trying to get to Frankie to disrupt her dream date and it's so boring and scripted until there's a slight twist and she socks the guy), but except for that little hiccup it was simply an unenjoyable journey from start to finish.
