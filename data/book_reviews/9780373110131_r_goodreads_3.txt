Ok, so this is the second Harlequin book that I've come across that had two parts. The other was a Lynne Graham book. I really enjoyed this book and was happy that David could find a woman who he loved. What I didn't like about this book was how David sort of brushed his love for Bethany off as being banal and not the real thing. If you read the first book, you know how David felt about Bethany.


Thing you may or may not like about this book:

1. Older hero and heroine 40 and 28 respectively
2. These two drank like fish. Not a day went day went by where they didn't drink a bottle or two of wine.
3. The hero is blonde and blue eyed which after the 80's became very rare for Harlequin heros.
4. This is the second part of an earlier book where the hero David plays a major role.
