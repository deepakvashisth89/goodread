This was a reread for me. I read this series in my teenage years and remember loving the series as a whole. But as I started this one again, I began to remember that I didn’t care as much for this particular book. Most of the book is about a troubled marriage and it’s not really a romance. Our main character’s husband is really terrible and his actions could be viewed as jealous, obsessive and controlling (even abusive at times). I really appreciated the faith aspects and how God can change even the hardest of hearts. If you stick it out, the ending is very satisfying.⭐️⭐️⭐️Content: I give this a PG-13 rating.
