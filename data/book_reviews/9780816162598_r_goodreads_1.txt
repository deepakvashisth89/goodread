An ok book but not one of the author’s best. I believe he was just starting out when this was written. Would give it a 2.5 rating if it was possible. Check some of his others out if you want a better representation of his best work. I’d suggest “Canyon passage“, “Alder Gulch”, “ the Earth breakers”, or if you’re looking for a short story “stage to Lordsburg” or “The Border trumpet”.
