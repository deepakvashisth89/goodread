Some great stories in this anthology: short stories by and about women. Mostly well-known writers like Katharine Mansfield, Flannery O'Connor, Alice Walker, Alice Munro, Joyce Carol Oates, Gertrude Stein, Edith Wharton, Doris Lessing, Virginia Woolf and Kate Chopin. New writers that I have only just discovered are going to lead me to their other works; Grace Paley had me hooked straight away with her first line inAn Interest in Life'My husband gave me a broom one Christmas.'I studied Alice Munro's story -The Officeat university, such a long time ago. So, it was especially interesting to revisit and take in the significance of a story well-told and especially how one can empathise with the woman's need, as a writer, to havea room of one's own. However, the landlord in Munro's story is not someone you want to meet.Helen Hagemann
