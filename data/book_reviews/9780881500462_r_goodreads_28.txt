A reissue of an old classic, part of the American Mystery Classic, follows the down-to-earth, no-nonsense Asey Mayo as he attempts to solve a murder mystery. His witty sayings and misquotes infuse the book with humor and charm. (He mangles Bible verses and other sayings.) The book is told from the point of view of Prudence Whitsby, who helps Asey in solving the mystery. Her side remarks add to the humor of the book. As Asey was sayin'“Yup. Well, if I was a feller as understood that, I’d use it. If I understood about finger-prints an’ microscopes an’ things like, I’d use ’em up too. But I don’t. Like the kid in the piece he wrote about two apples an’ a piece of pie,—I ain’t ’temptin’ any flights of fancy but just’s the things that’s in me. A handful of common sense an’ a little imagination is worth all them notions anyways.”
