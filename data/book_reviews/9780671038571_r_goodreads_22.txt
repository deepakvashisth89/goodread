This sequel is a bit of an improvement over its predecessor, but still is pretty corny.We kinda have three plots. A decent universe-ending sci-fi disaster plot, a medieval fantasy plot complete with muddy battlefields and a love story with the enemy's daughter, and a reveal of the Guardian of Forever's originators. All strung together with a healthy dose of time travel paradox.I gotta say Zar sure is a genius, to go from 25 years as a caveman to modernizing an entire culture from what he learned in three weeks living on the Enterprise.ANYWAY! The Spock/Zar relationship is much better and more interesting than in 'Yesterday's Son.' Enterprise crew interactions are mostly good and in character. The plot keeps moving. So like the first book, I'd say, don't put this high on your list, but if you're a geeky completist, go ahead and include it. But do read the two books in order.
