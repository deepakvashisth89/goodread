This was a tricky read since it had enough decent action and narrative complexity to make it a great book but in the end it just felt largely okay? I guess it may have something to do with how unsatisfying the ending was as the book wanted to press the rigors of war and how not all military actions actually matter in the greater scheme of things. We had the Eridani Light Horse still out of their element in a post-Huntress period and you have the Capellan side of things feeling very stereotypically evil and insidious.A lot of interesting mechs though. I'm always game for that.
