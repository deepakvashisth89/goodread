I didn't even know these books existed until I found one while book hauling. I didn't expect too much outside of cheesiness, but I liked how it tied in and explored the Blair Witch universe more. This was a classic tale to scare kids and keep them from going into those spooky woods. A good campfire story.
This isn't a story that has some huge underlying moral or lasting effect, but its quick and interesting.
