As a spy story, it had all the required elements: a beautiful girl that tags along and falls for the protagonist, undercover missions and going into enemy territory, political intrigue, high-tech bio-disguises. As a spy thriller, it worked I guess. But I don't really like spy stories unless it's different than every other spy story.I didn't care at all for any of the characters, but maybe it's just not my thing.I loved several aspects of it.1) the futuristic aspect with the feel of the 1990's. The story was written in the early days of the internet because the spies used internet boards to communicate. It was hilarious and is a good reminder that predicting the future is often tainted with current technology available at the time of writing. This worked for me because it was such a strange combination.2) The world was interesting, all the Asian culture and multiple suns of the planet and how it combined.Toward the end, I skipped a lot of the story, just to see how it finished, which was disappointing as expected. Overall, I'm glad I read it. The experience was interesting.
