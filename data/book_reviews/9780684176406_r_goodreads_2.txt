A solid collection of short fiction from the later nineteenth century, mostly from Britain, with a few from the US. As in any collection, there are a few misses among the hits (one by Anna Katherine Green, for example, is built on some nasty anti-Semitic tropes), but the hits predominate. Most of these aren't great Literature-with-a-capital-L but they do give a good and entertaining sense of how the genre of crime and detective fiction was developing in the later nineteenth century.
