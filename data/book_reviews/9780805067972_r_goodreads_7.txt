Sara is in yet another foster home. And although Carol has been more patient than most, Sara knows it's only a matter of time before she gets sent back. But at least she has a friend in this school - Dulcie. Through emails and journal entries, we begin to understand Sara's life. But Dulcie gets involved with Jon and soon that relationship becomes the center of everyone's life. Jon's family is very rich and very white. Dulcie's family is middle class and Dulcie is an adopted Asian-American. Sara has no one else to talk to so she deals with her troubles through her creative writing assignments. But things escalate with Dulcie and Jon and they draw Sara into their problems.
