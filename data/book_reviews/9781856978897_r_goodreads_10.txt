I purchased this book a long time ago. I am not a science fiction fan, but I think it is important to read a variety of genres to students.There are several good short stories in this anthology. Some of the well-known science fiction authors (Clarke, Asimov, etc.) are in here.I am partial to "The Boy, The Dog, and The Spaceship". I have learned to read this later in the fourth grade school year as students have matured to be able to understand the "twist". Good story!
