In the 1990s, this charming gross-out book was one of the most challenged books from those who feared it promoted disgusting behavior and socially unacceptable activities.The dare...the bet...eating worms for 15 days straight for $50.Not just regular worms...these werenightcrawlers fried in corn meal!Dad had to call Poison Control after day 5 because mom was worried about the stomach growling. Day 8, Billy swished a bit of worm in ketchup, mustard, horseradish sauce, etc. Billy and Tom are friends with Alan and Joe the nemesis/the dirty scoundrels. Chapter 27 is a hoot! There is a cheeky--Tom Sawyer/Huck Finn--fun about this. And no, I have no interest in eating a worm.
