Back when I was young, I got into Star Trek in a big way. Before Pocket Books was publishing Star Trek novels, Bantam Books was. The Bantam line wasn't very big, and I had all of them but one. This was in an era before EBay or Amazon or Abe Books. You had to find them. There was one I simply couldn't find - "Devil World" by Gordon Eklund.By the time EBay and Amazon came around, I had moved past Star Trek novels. The last Star Trek novel I remember buying wasThe Starship Trap, which I think was #64 in the Pocket Books line (and there were unnumbered special ones, and Next Generation Books), so I had read over a hundred Star Trek books from the time I was 11 to 24. That final novel was in 1993. EBay didn't even launch until 1995. So I just never resumed the search.However, last week (in May of 2026), I was in a Half-Priced Books and just happened to see "Devil World" right there. More than 30 years have passed since that book might have been on my mind, yet I knew it for what it was immediately. The one I was missing.And they were selling it for $1. Yep, one dollar. So I bought it. And read it. It was okay. It wasn't a great novel, but I wasn't expecting a great novel. Kirk fell in love too easily, the author didn't quite know how to make the characters feel like the ones on the show as completely as some others, but it was quite passable. If I had read it when I was a young teen, I would have eaten it up. As it is, I'm glad it's finally in my collection (I still have them all - I may not collect them anymore, but they were a big part of growing up for me, so I keep them).So, I am glad I wandered into that book store and glanced down at the bottom shelf. I'm glad I read it. If it wasn't for nostalgia, I imagine I would only give this book two stars - but I'm giving it three because I actually did kind of enjoy reading it, remembering what it was like back then when I was gobbling these up because I just couldn't get enough Star Trek.
