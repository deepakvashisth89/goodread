Could be a good resource for the absolute beginner who's not sure where to start, or if they're even capable of starting. The book dedicates a lot of time addressing misconceptions that may hold someone back from trying, which is nice. Could be condensed quite a bit, I think.

The exercises and assignments are very accessible to the complete beginner, but I don't think they go quite far enough to apply to the intermediate or advanced artist wanting to develop their skills.

There's good stuff in here, I just mostly didn't enjoy the way Mona organizes and conveys the information. Not a bad book, just not for me.
