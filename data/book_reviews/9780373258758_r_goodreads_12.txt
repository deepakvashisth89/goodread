Boring, uneven, lame, disappointing, repetitious – those are just the few adjectives that come to mind. I really expected better after all the glowing things I had read about Carly Phillips. I may try another book, but two is my limit when it comes to sex starved heroines and heroes who constantly have a hard-on – as if that makes a book plot.

Kayla Luck has inherited a business called Charmed! from her aunt. She is also a beautiful, built babe. The plot says she is smart, but I don’t buy that. Kane McDermott is an undercover cop investigating the business as a front for prostitution. He takes Kayla out, decides she is no prostitute, but beds her anyway – she just refuses payment. It was only supposed to be a one night stand, but the next morning when Kayla goes to the office she is attacked. In comes Detective Kane McDermott, not a traveling salesman after all. Kayla doesn’t really care he lied to her, she just wants more sex.
