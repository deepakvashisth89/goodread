Mac Bolan is a breath of fresh air in a world where evil often goes unpunished. But the Executioner from Stony Brook Farm always punches their tickets to hell and I love following him while he does.
Excitement and action from the beginning are a hallmark of Don Pendleton’s work, and it continues nonstop to the last page. So be sure to strap in when you open a Mac Bolan book.
I gave it five stars for constant action and wonderful cheesiness. If you like the Bourne books, the Expendables, Commando and that ilk, you have to love Mac Bolan, The Executioner.
