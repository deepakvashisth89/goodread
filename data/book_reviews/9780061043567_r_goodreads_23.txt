Highly enjoyable, though I am usually not a short story person. Only one story that I quit - the one about the playwright trying to decide if he should let the actor/ producer who made his fortune and ruined his serious reputation die. Quite enjoyed the variety of the rest - some of them quite funny. Including one about a maid entering what seemed like a terribly gothic situation, but it being more of a Northanger Abbey reality. Several quite good twists.It especially struck me that having stories hinge on things such clock weights and the manner of the clock's winding is now out of the understanding of 99.9% of the current population. When I first read her books - and I have probably read this before? - I don't remember thinking of them as being so very irredeemably old-fashioned.
