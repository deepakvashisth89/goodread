(This is for the second edition of the guide.) This is a really good as an encyclopedia of individual battles, along with brief looks at various topics associated with the war (such as battlefield staff rides and the development of map making). The battles are arraigned in chronological order by campaign, sometimes with maps; however, the amount of coverage and the use of maps seems a little haphazard at times. For example, in the 1864 Tennessee campaign section, both Allatoona and Spring Hill each have a map and a few pages, while Franklin and Nashville had only a single page each and neither had a map. And for the 1864 Missouri campaign, Westport (one of the largest battles in the Trans-Mississippi) and Mine Creek had only a half page each without any maps, while Newtonia II (which looked to be smaller than Westport and Mine Creek) did have a map.
