This book is a book about going to the first grade. Instead of children they have animals who are in the classroom. The illustrations are great. The animals have a lot of expression and look like children, only they are many different species. The book is about a Arnie's first day of kindergarten as he tells his mom after school. Everything that Arnie says doesn't necessarily match up with what actually happened during school. This is a great book for children to read who are on their way to either kindergarten or the first grade.

Learning Experience: The children could share their feelings about what they think first grade will be like. They can give each other ideas on how to handle the different situations that they may encounter. This is another great book to use to teach children about differences but being equal and everyone being respectful of each other even if you are very different from one another.
