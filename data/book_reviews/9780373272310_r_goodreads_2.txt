I shouldn't give this four stars. Honestly... I'm not even keeping it, and I keep four and five star books. But... Kathleen Creighton is a TEN star writer who just somehow pumped out a massively BAD book. So she is the redeeming grace in this whole thing.

From moment one, this book pulls out a metal cheese grater and tries to shred your brain. It begins with the most overdone romantic novel literary ploy: heroine stuck in snowdrift outside of hero's home in the middle of a blizzard... (wait for it) OVER X-mas. Let's all roll our eyes in unison, shall we?

But as irritating as THAT is, it gets even better - Creighton decided on page SIXTEEN of this stinker to let us know that she's written LOTS of books with this family in it. We got the whole list of cousins, siblings, aunts, uncles, fourth cousins six times removed AND a blurb about their impossible stories. After all, the Lanagans aren't just Iowa farmers. They're married to superstar rock singers, orangutan herders, secret agents, writers for Newsweek AND former presidents of the United States! Jump back, reality never entered the building!!!

I'm sorry, but if I don't know these people, listing them only tells me that you're not focused on the story - your pitching your book series. Bad form in a novel, there.

Building on all of this fun, we have Eric (our hero), who you THINK is the black sheep in the title, but really that would be Susan, the heroine's sister... although both are actually *CONSIDERED* black sheep when neither of them... really... are. R'something. Eric hasn't been home for christmas in ten years, and now he shows up with a baby that may or may not be his, but that he promised he'd keep away from the mother's family.

And the mother's sister (naturally) - who is the lawyer trying to get the baby away from him (conflict of interest, anyone? ANYONE??) is the hard-nosed cliched ice-queen from L.A. now stranded in Iowa with Studly McStudmuffin, who has to take his shirt off IN DECEMBER IN AN UNHEATED BARN to shovel manure. Gah, someone kill this book with fire, NOW.

And naturally the hardass heroine who thinks he SUCKS and her sister SUCKS and her parents (that she has a polite relationship with, but doesn't remember ANYTHING about, despite going home EVERY holiday) couldn't POSSIBLY be abusive. She'd know for sure if she could recall her childhood or memories from home, but... THAT'S BESIDE THE POINT!!!!

So they fight. And then bicker. And then prickle up. And then rankle each other. And awkward. And then hot... because uber-chick (who can't do much besides make coffee) is SO. DROPDEAD. GORGEOUS, naturally...

Issues. With this book. VAST issues.

But then there's Creighton's writing. She waltzes in and makes you FEEL the crisp air, makes you SMELL the almond cookies, makes you HEAR the scuffing of slippers. She's one. Heckuva. Writer! In those in-between-action/plot moments, when there's description and just *life*... it's beautiful. Seriously wonderful. SO much writing talent, here! SUCH a horrible plot. SUCH good tone, flow, writing...!

So there it is. As far as the story goes I want to hack stars RIGHT off this review. But as far as the author goes... I want to find more of her books. Maybe this series was a very early attempt. Maybe she's left behind some of the cliches and blossomed into something truly magnificent. I don't know. I wouldn't have found her without this erm, book, but at the same time... I can't really... Nope. Gotta dock a star. It's being submitted at three.
