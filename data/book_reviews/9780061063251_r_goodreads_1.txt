I liked Cindy’s tenacity in conditioning Glory in the hopes of keeping him and that she and Heather made mistakes and didn’t automatically know what they were doing or if they were even training him right—like so many horse books would do—but kept trying anyways.I did have some nitpicky problems with the book though. Like I pointed out in my review of Book 11, Wonder’s Sister, wheelchair bound riders wouldn’t be able to jump fences and yet that just keeps popping up in this series and it honestly drives me crazy, and yes I’m going to keep pointing it out every time it shows up. And again I was being reminded of Ian’s full name but thankfully it was dropped after the first few chapters—phew! I did NOT need those endless reminders again! And this is probably just a personal thing but I found it weird when Samantha called Cindy “sweetie”, like I think that would be more of a nickname for parents to their kid or a boyfriend/girlfriend/married couple type endearment, not sisters. I know for sure I would be weirded out if my sister called me that, lol! So that just struck me as odd.Other than my nitpicks this was an okay book and the last from the original author. Guess we’ll see how the ghost writers do from now on.‼️Content‼️Language: heckOther: in the past a horse was abused
