I am enjoying this series but in this book something happens which I can never manage to believe in - a female disguised as a male, in a male dominated setting, and not getting caught - except by our hero of course.

The mystery was good but it was stretched over a few too many pages and became a very slow read. No matter, I will certainly move on to the next book. I trust Brother Cadfael to do the right thing for me.
