I was digging through my old Harleys looking for Dark Illusion (still looking) and came upon this little gem.This one was fun, even though I'd liked to have strangled the heroine at some points. I felt bad for her - I really did - but she was immature and wallowing in self-pity (and sometimes alcohol). She could also be a real self-absorbed and shallow brat. You'll either want to kill her, laugh at her, or both.Still, her actions/reactions seemed genuine/believable (for a teen, which she wasn't anymore at 21-22). I remember having some of the same feelings when my bf broke my heart when I was 16. The girl did need to grow up, but kudos to Wentworth for showing the ugly side of a girl scorned in a believable way. I especially identified with her determination not to let her ex have any reason to believe she was OK - she wanted him to suffer and feel guilty for her pain. He'd be sorry!
