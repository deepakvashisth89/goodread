I love the Canadian setting of this book.Joanne Kilbourn's childhood friend friend, the srtist Sally Love, has an exhibit at greater Mendel Gallery in Saskatoon. Because of the exotic nature of the art there are protesters outside. Joanne attends the opening and reminders of the past come flooding back.Not long after the opening Sally's former business partner, Clea is murdered and Sally is a suspect.What I liked about the book is that Joanne doesn't do any sleuthing and interfering. Things just fall into place making it much more realistic.I did have the murderer figured out pretty early on but that in no way diminished my enjoyment of the book.
