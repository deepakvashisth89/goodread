Detective Kane McDermott is given a choice either take an easy case or time off the job after a terrible case . He is sent to check out Charmed an etiquette school to see if it is legit or a prostitution ring . Kayla Luck owns Charmed ever since her aunt and uncle died . Kane is immediately attracted to Kayla but she is use to guys that are only interested in her body . Kayla and Kane have feelings for each other after a steamy night together . Kane has issues from his past .
She finds out he is a detective and is investigating her business . Kane finds Kayla is in danger and he will do anything to keep her safe . This story is fantastic , well written with great characters .I highly recommend taking the time to read .
