If you are still thinking that America is like Nazi Germany after reading William Shirer’s Rise and Fall of the Third Reich, then there is no hope for you. But if you are a thoughtful, reflective person and sometimes wonder how it was that Germany went astray, then you might read this book. But, warning, you may find it discomfiting.SPOILER ALERTThere is a letter from a German at the end of the book that makes one think. The letter writer, a German who supported the Nazi Party, explains that as between the Communists and the Nazis, he simply chose the lesser of two evils. He fails to acknowledge, as Primo Levi points out, the existence of the Socialist Party, [and also the Catholic Party, but they voted with the Nazis until it was too late] both choices available that were neither Nazi or Communist. But when push comes to shove, and moderates are scared, they will turn right rather than left. Particularly if guided there.
