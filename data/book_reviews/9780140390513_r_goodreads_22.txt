I think your take on Washington will come down to two things: your politics and your race. For me, this book had me literally ready to throw it across the room in anger at the overly simplistic take Washington has on virtually everything. More important than my modern rage is the historical context from which the book came.In Washington's eulogy, W.E.B. Du Bois wrote about how no one ever really knew what Washington thought or believed because he was a master tactician. With that said, it is impossible to see the "tactics" Washington chose as anything less than complete appeasement of white Americans.Unsurprisingly, many of the most positive reviews here and elsewhere are written by either well-meaning but ignorant white people or political conservatives excited to find ammunition to support their bootstrapping and "culture of poverty" nonsense. I think the book is worth reading for its place in the African-American political thought tradition, but I do not think Washington's arguments, if they are taken as his sincerely-held beliefs, are anything short of offensive.
