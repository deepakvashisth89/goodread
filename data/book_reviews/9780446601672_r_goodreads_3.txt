This book is a waste - a waste of time, ideas and paper. The central ideas in this book literally are the stuff of wonders, but the writer is not up to the task of rendering them into words. He's just not good enough. I've never read this writer before, and I never will again; he's a run-of-the-mill sci-fi writer who can handle stories that are bad echoes of "Starship Troopers", yet he happened to stumble upon a brilliant idea. The idea, however, was beyond him in conception and description.Other flaws are that there are no likable or believable characters, and that it takes 70 agonizing pages before the book even approaches the great "idea".It's not worth your time.
