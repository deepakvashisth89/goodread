This book is definitely for married women only or older ladies. I would not recommend this to unmarried young women because there are mature topics in this series. In spite of that I liked this book. It is a Christian book and even Christians have problems they have to deal with.In this book the son of the owner of the Culdee Creek Ranch, running away from home, and looking for his family in Scotland. What he finds in Scotland is love. He brings his new wife home to America. He met his wife and got married quickly and they have many hurdles to overcome in America. They deal with these things with the help of family, church leadership and God. This is a complex story with real life types of problems. They don't get solved overnight and there are repercussions from the past that must be dealt with. Those are some of the things I like about this series. It is not just a feel good Christian romance. The characters grow and seek God and solve real problems. That is encouraging to me.
