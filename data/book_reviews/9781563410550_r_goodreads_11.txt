I really, really enjoyed this book. It was a difficult read, and hit close to home, but was very powerful. The book was easy to read, frank in it’s discussion about Brenner’s privilege as a white middle class lesbian as she navigated the justice system, which I found refreshing. In an age of growing hate, it has made me slightly paranoid about going out into the woods with my girlfriend.One of my biggest critiques is that there were so many moving parts and characters at play that they all became jumbled and lost. I simply lost track of who was who. Many people have critiqued it for “not being the best” in terms of writing, and while I agree, I did not find it distracting, and found it lead credence to the story. Your mileage may vary.
