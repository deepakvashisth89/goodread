This book was more than a little strange at times.
For one, it turns out that our main character, Rela (or Sara), is dating her grandfather, but, like, from the past. Secondly, she is a cyborg from the future. And her grandfather, who is there to kill her, is a robot from the even more distant future. Also, her grandfather is there with his younger self before he is planning on killing Rela. Which, as I know from many sci-fi, time travel shows, books, and movies, is a pretty big no-no.

It was interesting in some ways, but kind of dull in others. I did like most of the characters, of course, although some of them didn't seem to have much purpose. Like Ed. Who sold Rela the VCR and then stuck around for the rest of the book for no good reason. He didn't really have much purpose after the initial sale. Although, he did start dating Rela's friend. So, I guess that explained that.
