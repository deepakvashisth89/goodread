I was looking forward to reading more by these two authors, after I had readThe Siege: 68 Hours Inside the Taj Hotel.This book had problems. The first 1/3 of the book was excellent. Giving the reader tons of information about Imperial Jade and about the ruling Emperor's of China. There was also a lot of information about the destruction and raiding by the Western world, that took place at the Forbidden City.The middle section of the book is the part that was more like a gossip rag: going on and on and on about the millionaires, starlets and socialites that grabbed up jade like it was candy. This is where I blame the Editing. Large sections of this portion of the book could have been taken out and the book would have been more praise worthy.The final part of the book takes the reader into Burma with the two author's as they try to get to the jade mines. This was the thrilling portion of the book and written very well.This was the author's first book, so I will not hold it against them and I will read more by them.
