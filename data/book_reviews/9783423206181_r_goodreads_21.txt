I'm in two minds about this book. I have read several of Glavinic's books before, and I always liked his way of telling the story and his use of the German language.
With this one however, I really had problems to enjoy my read. Although the plot was quite gripping, and I read it in one go, I really struggled with the report style writing which reminded me more of a school essay than anything else.
I did like the subtle media criticism and the original idea, however the style of writing (and the complete absence of paragraphs - where do you actually stop when you need a new cup of coffee??) was quite tiring for me...makes 3 stars altogether.
