Brock Cole always has a way of making his stories sound like fairy tales you heard as a child, andButtonsis no different. Contrary to several other reviewers, I found the story subversively feminist. It wasn't that the daughters' only thoughts revolved around finding husbands (they didn't); it was that one in particular was praised for taking on a traditionally male role, and for the attributes that go along with it.And, ah, their male counterparts were quite eager to be married as well, once they found the right woman. Seems girls aren't the only ones interested in getting hitched. ;-)Beautiful illustrations, a whimsical story, and a ending where you can't help but smile. What more could you ask for?
