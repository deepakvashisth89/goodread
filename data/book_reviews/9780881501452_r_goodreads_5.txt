We have all been to house parties with the host proudly showing off their new home with all it’s finery to friends and family. The characters in “ Death Lights a Candle” are enjoying that same experience in Cape Cod. But the weather in March is very unpredictable, a massive snowstorm has arrived. They are snowed in and completely cut off from town. Their genial host is found dead on the floor of his bedroom in the morning. First published in 1932, this book reads like an old fashioned novel full of description with a clever mystery to delight the reader. Asey Mayo is the book’s main character who is put in charge of solving the mystery. Be forewarned that his dialogue is written in dialect form. There is an over abundance of characters in this book between the servants and houseguests. However, this gives us so many possible murderers! If you want a charming look at life in the past with an unusual mystery, this is the next book for you. It was my first book by Phoebe Atwood Taylor and now I can’t wait to read more. Happy reading!
