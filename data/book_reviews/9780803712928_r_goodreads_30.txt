I liked this book because most people think about Hanukkah and Christmas during the month of December, but it's important to know that other holidays, like Kwanzaa exist and are widely celebrated. This books begins with a short history of Kwanzaa and then explains each day of the holiday and what it symbolizes. It also explains the rituals and the use of illustrations (especially the candles being lit and their colors on each page) make the traditional aspect very easy to follow.I think that this book is great for educating young people about the rich culture that exists in this country. As well, I think this book does a nice job or breaking down stereotypes. (i.e the reminder that Kwanzaa began in America)
