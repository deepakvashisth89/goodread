The Icarus Agenda is a gripping spy novel. I am a Ludlum fan but to be honest other than his Jason Bourne series which I rated 5 stars, his other books in general are not blockbusters.
I never give away spoilers and I read this 20 years ago but after going back to Ludlum for some classic spy thrillers, I remembered exactly what I felt when I first read this book, very slow plot and some of book seems unbelievable. I always recommend Ludlum for a spy thriller and it's a good book but if you read the Jason Bourne books first you may not be so captivated. Respectfully, Thomas.
