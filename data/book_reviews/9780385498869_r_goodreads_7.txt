I dunno. A lot of my friends really loved this book, which is why I decided to read it. But it just didn't do it for me. I thought it was overly simplistic. I didnt' like that there weren't any interviewees who were just plain happy with their lives--surely, there must be some women out there who don't feel that they have had to wade through life, constantly struggling and somewhat disappointed with their choices? I suppose that's not the issue--we all struggle with SOMEthing--but these women just seemed so frustrated, no matter what choices they made. A lot of the stories seemed kind of melodramatic. Also, I was frustrated by the lack of diversity among the women who told their stories--the book felt somewhat elitist. I recognize that the author was interested in a particular subset of women--those who are highly educated and upper income--but I just couldn't relate, despite having a similar educational and professional background.
