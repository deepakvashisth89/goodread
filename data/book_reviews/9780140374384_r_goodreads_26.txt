Oddballs by William Sleator was such a cute book filled with lots of fun stories of a very tight nit an inspirational family. I think this is a great book for children to read as they can relate a lot of it to their own lives. I grew up an only child, so reading this book was an eye opener on what children with siblings life is like! The whole family is very close and that is something that all families should aspire to be like. Teaching this book could be really fun, and it would introduce students to narrative writing.
Overall, this book was cute and fun, and most likely geared to late elementary aged children, and I think they would love it.
