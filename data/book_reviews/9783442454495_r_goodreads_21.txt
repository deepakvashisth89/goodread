This is not your average romance, or even chick-lit experience. Part detective nostalgia, part revenge story (divorcee against cheater husband, a classic), part murder mystery, part mad-cap caper, and part thieving, Fast Women is one satisfying and hilarious read. If you like your romance with a dash of insanity check this out. Crusie is also one of the best dialogue writers I have ever read, in any genre. She is near peerless in her sense for dialogue phrasing and ability to make readers laugh.
