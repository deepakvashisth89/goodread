3.5/5This is a touching true story of two young Jews in love during the Holocaust and their struggle to survive. I love stories in which people have to overcome adversity/struggle and do so with honor. What makes this even more inspiring is that it is true. You can’t help but to think what you would have done in such unimaginable situations. Any story in which one survives through hardship makes you ponder human character-what makes us who we are and why/how we’re able to react in certain ways. I just feel that it’s so telling as to who we are or who we have the potential to be, as well as what moves us (the victims and also sometimes the victimizers). This is also a love story, as the two are separated, but long to “meet again”. I also love how this story brings to life that of the Holocaust, as it should always be remembered.
