For everyone's reference up front, I don't really enjoy cutesy Christmas books (except A Christmas Carol). The sap level it's too high, my eye rolls can't be contained, and I don't like the style.
Now this review will make sense to everyone.
Review: see above. 🤗😂
The autobiography of Santa Claus is a pretty impressive book. It literally covers all of world history since the original idea of Santa was imagined. The book is told first person by "Santa" and talks about history with some cutesy Christmas highlights like books written about Santa, the famous letter to the editor about Santa, the name changes, etc.
It was very slow. It felt a lot like a history textbook with some mall Santa talking about it to me. *Cringe*.
With that kind of title, I expected more fun and jolly stories, inspirational bits, and warm winter fire feelings.
Guys, it's just a fancy history book. I should have just read "A Christmas Carol" again or watched "The Santa Claus".
Hard pass and I don't really recommend, unless you're into Christmas stories and modified histories that include Atila the Hun making toys for Christmas (that's a real thing in this book).
