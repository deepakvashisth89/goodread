This was an interesting read. The characters seemed to be living out "What Would Jesus Do?" in such an extreme way that I didn't know if I should be convicted or just smile and remind myself that this is "just a story." Everything ended perfectly, almost "and they lived happily ever after" perfectly. This book didn't really teach anything about history, but it did have a lot to say about Christian marriage, both preparing for it and then continuing a successful marriage throughout your lives. Therefore, a lot of this book would not be as interesting or appropriate for the younger readers who would have enjoyed the previous books of the series. There was nothing at all explicit or too mature that way, but the depth of the thinking the characters expressed in this book would probably not only be boring to younger readers, but also would be far above their level of comprehension.Show more
