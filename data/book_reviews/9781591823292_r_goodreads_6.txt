3.5*Sigh. This slow burn is killing me. This was more romantic than the previous books and it showed the most restraint. While Dee desperately wants that next step with Ryo, his care and consideration of Ryo is telling.This one dealt with a lot of deeper feelings, but no real progress was made beyond reaching an inkling of an idea about what they may really be feeling for each.It was nice to see Carol and Bikky's story progress. Even though we see their progress happen before we see Ryo and Dee's... technically, Bikky and Carol's relationship was 7 years in the making. That being said, while Carol was 18... Bikky is still only 15. I'm not sure if the author really thought that one out.Anyway... here's hoping for book 5.
