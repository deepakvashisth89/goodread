Exciting thriller, well paced, in which the residents of an isolated Dorset village close ranks following the death by car bomb of the local squire and war hero. Set amid early 1980's anxiety about the IRA and the Soviet threat, it pits the wits of David Audley and German spy, Captain Schneider alias Benedikt alias Thomas Weisehofer, racing against the problem of discovering who set the bomb and who was the intended target before the perpetrators can strike again.
