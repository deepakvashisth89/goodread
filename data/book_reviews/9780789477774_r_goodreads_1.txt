I enjoyed reading this one, the info inside is very bite-sized and good for anyone just thinking about getting started with divination and maybe doesn't know where to start or even what kind of divination they would like to try. The book gives a nice little taster before diving in else whereThere were a few things I didn't agree with, like the idea of telling a pendulum what you want "Yes" and "No" to be, that sound like a lot of unnecessary hard work. I feel it's better and most likely quicker just to ask the pendulum to show you "Yes" and then "No"I did like the illustrations within the book, but sometimes the imagines were placed under text, which made it hard to read in places. I also liked the sample readings within each divination section, it was good to see how the tool(s) can be used
