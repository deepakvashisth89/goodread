This book covered the Civil War in St.Louis more thoroughly than any other book on the subject. I loved the detail in describing the Camp Jackson affair. I was also delighted to see a picture and description of my great, great, great Grandfather Col Franz Hassendeubel who was an officer in the Union’s 3rd Missouri home guard. The book mentioned that he took command of the 3rd when Franz Sigel’s horse fell on his leg and Sigel was sidelined to the back of the column in a carriage! Hassandeubel took part in many battle and died at Vicksburg later in the war due to shrapnel wounds. The book also gives detailed maps on the locations of where all these events took place and the locations of monuments and famous players buried in Bellefontaine Cemetery.
