When you read TH White, you get this feeling that he’s just far too clever, too well read, and well researched to be a children’s writer. But he is and he’s a very good one. This probably isn’t my favourite of his but it’s very entertaining. It has all the classic ingredients - orphan wicked governess, wicked Guardian, dotty Professor reliable Cook. It has a great setting, which must be based on Stowe. And it is hugely intertwined with Jonathan Swift Gullivers travels, Liliput et cetera. It is froth but full of truth.
