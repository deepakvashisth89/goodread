This is the first book with the Luck sisters and it is so good. Kayla & Cat Luck take over their Aunt's Charm School when she passes away and turn it into more than a "Charm and Dance" school like her Aunt & Uncle were doing. Kayla has changed it into a teaching business etiquette school but for some reason, the vice squad thinks that she is a madame so Kane goes undercover to see if she will accept money for services rendered. Chemistry sparks when Kane and Kayla meet which is not a good thing when you are undercover at all. These two have chemistry yes but Kane is one who says has always said he would never be married and Kayla is one who has her sister Cat and will stay with those two.
Sounds like two peas in a pod. And of course, then Kayla finds out that someone is out to hurt her and shock Kane is not who he says he was. Can she forgive him long enough to let him keep her safe and find out what is going on? This book has heat, romance, and yes mystery!
