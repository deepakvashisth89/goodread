The Engines of Light is comprised of 3 books.I must admit that it didn't sound too much fun at the beginning and it's a little confusing when you start reading the first book, but it quickly becomes interesting. In every book there's a clash between different social ideas, individualistic vs colectivistic societies.The story is well told and and the plot isn't easy to predict. I liked the way it simulates societies that have lived centuries with a particular thought on life and the way they develop.In short: It's a nice sci-fi book with aliens, spaceships and war, but told in a non exhausting way.
