Several years ago I purchased this and 2 other volumes in the Lorimer Chronicles series as NOOK books, but only just got around to reading this first volume. Unlike the description provided by the publisher, the novel was neither gripping nor compelling, just historical romance fiction with a strong, independent heroine who suffers from the poor financial decisions made by her wealthy father. I read this to the end, primarily to learn how she fares, but have read summaries of the rest of the series and won't bother reading the novels themselves.
