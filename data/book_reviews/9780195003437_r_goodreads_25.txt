If you are at all interested in medieval literature and its growth toward Elizabethan lit, you should get into this book immediately. I picked this up to help me with Edmund Spenser - following a binge in Ovid. What a discovery! This was serendipity.It is an academic book written at a popular level, so understand that. Lewis has a monograph out there on the Elizabethans that is more serious and therefore harder to find and expensive. I will probably have to get into it after this sample of what his specialty in literature really was.Ovid, Chretien de Troyes, Dante, Ariosto, Spenser... move a bit like this through some specimens of literature to understand the tradition he is describing and extolling.Thanks to him, I have discovered Orlando Furioso and am nuts about it.
