This was my first "official" science book. I had peeked over other books (such as Sagan's Cosmos or Cousteau's Underwater World) a little, but this was the first science book I read as a whole, and the one that got me really interested into science. I think I was around 12yr old at the time. Also, this was the book that got me into Asimov's writings. A gift from my older brother. It's a little outdated (obviously), but I am very fond of it.
