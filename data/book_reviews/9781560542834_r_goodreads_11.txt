It's been awhile since I read a book that made me go "D'oh!" as I close the book, and flip madly back through the volume to figure where I missed "it".An intriguing murder mystery that involves you the reader just as it peaks the interest in the people around the narrator. As I read there was a reoccurring theme, a hypnotic anodyne, that just bugged the heck out of me and it seemed to have a very heavy handed PCness that almost made me want to give up, but I'm glad I didn't because it's all accounted for in the end. I do think the author's device for deception is a little cheap, oh well, if I had spent a bit more time in contemplation I would have been well prepared for the ending.
