4.25*Romantic suspense is a pretty apt description for this delightful 1955 adventure. Stewart paints such an idyllic setting in the south of France, and then drops her heroine in the middle of a dreadful mystery. I was swept in only too easily, imagining Charity racing around in her Riley.It is hard to imagine this was Stewart’s first novel. I’m in awe. Yes, there are some big coincidences but I was having too much fun to mind. The only thing that did annoy me wasnot the insta-love per se but the fact that Richard was abusive and violent at the very beginning, using his greater physical strength to subdue Charity, and her reaction. Mistaken identities, but still.... I still want to read more of Stewart, especially her Arthurian Saga, which I shall start soon.
