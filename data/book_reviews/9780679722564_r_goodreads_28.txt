This is my third Hollinghurst novel, and I think it'll be my last.

His style is stunning, I must say. But I feel he wastes this on insipid, non-narratives which have little other purpose than to make caricatures of gay men. Oh, and what fans would I'm sure see as a subtle (read: nonexistent) critique of racism in the community isn't believable enough to hold water, especially when you remember this isn't the only novel where he fetishises blackness ('The Spell' has the same problem). If I'm being honest, I suspect there's a good deal of Hollinghurst's own prejudices at play here.

Besides this more serious concern, 'The Swimming Pool Library' simply fails to *say* anything, leaving it really quite dull - despite its many attempts to shock with sex.
