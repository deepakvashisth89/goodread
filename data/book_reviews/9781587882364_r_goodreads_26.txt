I had hopes for this book, but at so many turns it went to the ridiculous instead of the believable. What a shame. I actually found it insulting to the reader that the author would present so many fantastic scenarios that could never be true.

Young unwed woman with a toddler girl is thrown out of there family horse ranch. She drives across several state lines and finds a horse farm owned by 2 of the nicest and wealthiest couples ever. She uses her horse skills to successfully contribute to the ranch, leading up to her training and becoming the jockey for a triple crown winner. Not to mention being adopted by the couple and inheriting their hundreds of millions.

And the lunacy continues throughout the book to amuse the reader. Thanks but I returned the next 2 in the series before wasting more of my brain cells.
