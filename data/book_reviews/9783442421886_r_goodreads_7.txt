A somewhat longer book in this series, running to just over 600 pages in mass market paperback. Written in 1990 this one is a sort of push back against the Japanese trade dominance of that decade. All a bit unbelievable but good entertainment in the vein of a James Bond movie. This achieves one of my goals for the year, read the series up to 'Sahara', which I am told is a great book and terrible movie.
