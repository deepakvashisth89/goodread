A terrified old man seeks answers when he believes the walls of his home are breathing. John Hyatt works at the San Francisco Department of Sanitation and has come across much in his career but this is too preposterous to pass up on investigating. He visits the home thinking to find a rat infestation but leaves it utterly terrified and knowing he has come near to the other side.A was expecting thrills and chills but the horrifying elements were only ever briefly on the page. This story involved more dialogue between the characters and their concerns over what the horror might actually be, instead of the reader getting to witness new developments for themselves.
