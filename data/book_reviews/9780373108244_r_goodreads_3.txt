Moira was having strange boring feelings about her life as a top model. For eight long years, she worked hard to be an independent top model and she had become one, yet she was not having the same enthusiasm about her career like she used to be. She meets Patrick Casey, a famous playwright, who seems to despise her career yet attracted to her.
