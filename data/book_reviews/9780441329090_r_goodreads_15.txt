My mother got me The Secret Country and The Hidden Land lo these many years ago, and for a long time—until college!—I did not know there was a third book. (For you young'uns, this is what happens when you don't have an internet where you can look things up.) When you get to the end of this one, think a moment how it feels to have this strange and incomplete ending to the story, with an oddly adult thought of just walking away.
