Nigel Hamilton's book was well-written, though it took some time to get from the less interesting high school years to the more interesting, college and war time years. The emphasis is in the early years is on Joe and Rose Kennedy whom the author considers to be (with some evidence) monsters. Joe Kennedy comes across as one of the most fascinating figures of the century. I may have to pick up the bio about Ol' Joe.At times it seems like Hamilton disliked all members of the Kennedy family but JFK. It appears that the writing and post-publication experience of Mr. Hamilton was quite a nightmare, as authors going back to William Manchester found out. Looked forward to Hamilton's second volume about Kennedy, but that won't be forthcoming. A good read!Recommended.
