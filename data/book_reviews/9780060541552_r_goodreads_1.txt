Critics, teachers and especially parents probably hate a book like this, but as a mildly angsty 15 year old with lots of feelings and hormones I was absolutely floored by the unique, unapologetic, crass style of this book. Themes of sex, drugs, death, domestic violence and homelessness are discussed in a style that toes a razor thin line between frank and flippant. It made me feel a bit punk in my sheltered white bread private school world. Now that it's out of print, this book is really hard to find but I recently scored a copy thanks to the internet. That's a real shame!! I'd love to one day see a reprint that captures the spirit of the original - maybe even a comic book version!
