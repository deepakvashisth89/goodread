Poetry that extracts a, “yes. I think that too,” but only for a moment. When the initial, agreeing appeal fades into poorly constructed melancholic self-pitying drivel it fails to stimulate further than to say, “this is what I think about when I mope around and wish I had things I don’t.” His whiney musings seem as lazy as the supposed brilliant thoughts of someone who was born too attractive for his own good. And that’s to say nothing of his opinions. No wonder this thing wasn’t reprinted.
