The main character in Death and the Dancing Footman is a surrealist playwright, surely not coincidental given the plot of this novel. It’s about an eccentric rich guy who has a house party and invites a bunch of people who all hate each other just to see what happens. What happens is murder, obviously, but not until halfway through. The relationships between the characters were so compelling I actually forgot it was coming. The dancing footman provides an odd piece of whimsy. A really enjoyable slant on the classic house party mystery.
