Can’t even count how many times I have reread this book! It’s zany, outrageous, irreverent, and laugh-out-loud hysterical. I bought myself an old used copy (now barely hanging on due to having been read too often) and can quote my favorite lines at the drop of a hat. To my view, the biggest crime is that although the author is still writing, nothing he’s written in 30 years has come close to the flat-out awesomeness of this one. Jardine is both over-the-top and entirely relatable. He’s a jerk, but as soon as he starts talking to the sky we’re on his side come hell or high water. Sean is both the perfect straight man and well-fleshed-out character himself. Then there’s a whole cast of supporting characters, each more entertaining than the next. We meet the good hearted but clueless model, the good hearted but clueless jock, the egotistical (and clueless) principal, Jardine’s perfectly friendly housewife mom, the kid who is so determined to be edgy and cool that he even has his own language that no one understands, and the crabby, opinionated grandpa from Brooklyn, who morphs into a bad poet and becomes a national star. Every little extra has a spark of personality- even “The bear on a trapeze in the rain” is someone we feel like we’ve gotten to know in the one scene in which he appears. This story is pure gold and written with passion and heart by a talented author and storyteller. Couldn’t ask for more!
