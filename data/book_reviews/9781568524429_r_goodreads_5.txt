Healing Crystals and Gemstones: From Amethyst to Zirconis a great guide to anyone who is interested in the metaphysical properties of crystals and gemstones. If, like me, you are a newcomer to studying stones, then this book is a great beginner's guide for you, as it takes you through more than 100 important stones and details their chemical compositions, where they come from, their histories, their healing and magical properties, and so forth, and what is perhaps the best part is that provided for each stone are beautiful color photographs. My only problems with the book are that (a) it is very exhausting (but, in all fairness, it is essentially a textbook so is probably not really intended to be read cover to cover) and (b) it is poorly edited, as it is practically full of errors, mostly in the form of typos. It is, as previously stated, however, an excellent resource, both for beginners and for seasoned practitioners. It is pretty much impossible to memorize every stone and its properties in the book, so even for people who aren't new at this practice, it is a good resource to have on your bookshelf.
