I've read much of Mr. Sawyer's work over the last couple of decades and really enjoyed the majority of it.Golden Fleececomes in on the enjoy level but I have to say there are a number of his books I liked quite a bit more. Conceptually, this one is a win, both on big picture ideas and making things personal. But the pacing seemed very slow to me a lot of the time as so much of the narrative is, essentially, the narrator (a 10th generation AI) talking to himself or figuring things out. It was an interesting choice which, overall, worked, but there were times I wanted Jason to get to the point a little sooner.
