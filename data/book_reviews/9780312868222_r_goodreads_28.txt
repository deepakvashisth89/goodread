Again a solid three...for the 3rd book in the series.This book tries to flesh out a few of the other rulers.and finally we get some movement on the romance theme...but as always, it suffers from a very cardboard cutout world, where some of the "rules" or how things work are not logical at all and only there to move the plot in a certain predictable direction. ..and getting a little tired of the asthmatic sorceress repetitively hacking away with her lollipops.
