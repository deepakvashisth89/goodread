One thing about Silverstein is his literature never lacks imagination or wonder. I had this book at one point and remember reading it to my kids. I recently purchased a first edition 1996 to replace my missing book. Is it as good as Where the Sidewalk Ends? No. The giving Tree? No. It is a well written book of wildly imaginative poems and drawings for all age groups. I would recommend this for all elementary age readers and any age group seeking imaginative writing.
