I was quite bored for most of this book. I kept waiting for someone to die/disappear and unfortunately that did not happen. None of the characters were that interesting and I was just reading to the end to find out if Jessica really did have a brain tumor. Spoiler she didn’t. So what was that on the MRI that they took a week to analyze. Artifact?It was like the author was trying to reach a certain page count. No way this book needed to be 330+ pages. About 50 pages from the end, all hell broke loose like the author was also tired of writing the book and from that point forward she had quite the one-liners. There was a 23 year old doctor, people were cuddling in the snow, and someone was left to die in the woods.I will leave you with this:“She thought he was the nicest young man she had ever met. They had the same red hair but that wasn’t all they had in common. They both liked to read mysteries and they both liked Elvis Presley. For Cee Cee, that was enough to build a real future together.”
