Incredible telling of the Bible. It connected me in a way that the Bible never did, especially the New Testament.The book is what the title says it is: the Bible as a novel. It tells the story of the Bible as a narrative, starting with Abraham to Jesus's ascension. It's probably the biggest responsibility any author has ever had, in that artistic liberties might be heresy.It's a very easy read and the story just glides. This book still doesn't erase the need for the Bible but I do think it is written in a way that makes the Bible much more accessible, and I'm not just talking in terms of words and sentence structure. The Bible is updated all the time to fit modern language but the format of this story just makes it easy to enter and generate momentum that carries you right to the end of the book.When I tried to read the Bible, I noticed that I was just trying to get through it like I would with a novel. I was missing a lot of it and not learning what was in the Bible. I like to believe that this book entering my life was no coincidence, as it told me the Bible in a way that I was primed to read.This book is precious to me, I would definitely recommend it.
