This was a sweet book! Laurel and Brett were in a fake wedding that ended up changing their lives. I really liked how Laurel decided to step up and change her life for the better when she hits bottom. Laurel goes to give Brett the life changing news and ask for a job when Brett surprises her and also helps her out. Laurel wants to prove she can stand on her own two feet and Brett learns a valuable lesson. Really enjoying this series!
