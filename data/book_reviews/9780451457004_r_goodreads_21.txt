Well... this book was ...It had...Bad writing. Bad, bad writing, no biscuit for you, Author!Aside from the massive quantities of derivative plot (has everyone read Tolkien?) there were words that were used incorrectly, writhing unending sentences, and garbage bags full of overwriting and overexplaining.Why did I keep reading it? Partially, because I was looking for the next BAD bit. Honestly, if this man had retained a decent editor, or someone to quieten down his purple prose, he might have had something.
