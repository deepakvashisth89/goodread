Author Stuart WoodsTitle Dead EyesGenre: Thriller / CrimeDead Eyes was my very first time reading anything by Stuart Woods, and I honestly didn’t know what to expect. By the time I finished, I was pleasantly surprised and completely entertained. The story centers around a long-unsolved assassination attempt on a U.S. president and the resurfacing of evidence decades later. What follows is a gripping investigation filled with political intrigue, danger, and unexpected twists. Woods does an excellent job building suspense without overcomplicating the plot. The pacing moves steadily, and the tension continues to rise as the truth slowly comes to light. I appreciated how the author blended crime, history, and high-level politics into one engaging storyline. The characters felt confident and capable, especially the lead investigator, whose determination kept the story moving forward. There was always a sense that something bigger was at stake.What stood out to me most was how easy the book was to read while still being exciting. The writing style is clear and direct, but it doesn’t lack depth. Each chapter left me wanting to read just one more, and I found myself fully invested in discovering how everything would unfold. Overall, I thought Dead Eyes was awesome. For my first Stuart Woods novel, I was more than pleased—I was hooked.
