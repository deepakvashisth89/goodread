If you want a book with exact dates and thought processes on albums, go elsewhere. If you want fantastic stories by a true entertainer, with philosophy and history thrown in, this is the book for you. Definitely one of my favorite autobiographies of all time, it not my favorite, and definitely my favorite musician autobiography (which includes Sammy’s “Red” and Keith’s “Life”).Want advice for the music business? Read this. Want advice on climbing mountains? Read this.
