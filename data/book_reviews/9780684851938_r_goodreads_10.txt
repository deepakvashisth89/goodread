An interesting read if you believe in reincarnation or interested in the subject.
In these cases, they consider all aspects. They don't just flat-out believe what the subject is saying or what the family is saying. Just because you have to take into account that there can be some coaching, clues that can be derived from the environment, and so on.
So it's good to be a skeptic.

That being said, I had an issue with these same-family cases, because they mentioned that a child could have overheard a conversation between adults and make a story based on that.
This could be the case, but speaking from personal experience, every family keeps secrets. Things they don't speak about. Things that only come to light when a person dies, and the family goes to their stuff.
For example:
I was 20 years old before I even knew that when my mom married my dad, that was her second marriage. They did not speak of that first marriage, which my mom considered a mistake. She actually got a bid mad at my grandma for mentioning it.

Second example: my mom only found out more about her uncle that died before she was born, after her own dad died. The things she learned was only through letters she found. So there's a lot that she doesn't know. He was never mentioned in conversation;

I get that some families may be very open about their family history. Good on them.
But in my experience, and what I hear about friends, a lot is still kept quiet about. Like even about the younger generation.
