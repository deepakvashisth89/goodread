I have read that Allingham's Campion is a parody of Sayers Lord Peter Wimsey. I didn't see this until I was about halfway through this title. I guess I should do more groundwork for my reading. I didn't especially like the Sayers series, and, though I liked the first in this series, this is my last. I think it's supposed to be humorous. I'm not much of a humor reader, but even if I were, I tend to want mysteries to be straight.Campion is sort of a jerk with an over-inflated ego. (Even though I've stopped reading Poirot who also has an over-inflated ego, I don't think he's a jerk and I don't find that series humorous.) While looking up the humor angle, I found Campion is not his real name - he's some rich and probably titled guy in disguise - and why should I care.In this one, he contracts with a rich American in a protection scheme against Simister. No one really knows who Simister is, not even the people who work for him and it seems almost every criminal of every type in England works for Simister.Well, from the above you can tell I'm an outlier. I can't struggle to find a third star.
