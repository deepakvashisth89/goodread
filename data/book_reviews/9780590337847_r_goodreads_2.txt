This book may have saved my mother's life.This is one of the first children's books to deal with death, and in it ***SPOILER ALERT!!!!***** the main character's best friend dies from bee stings (this book came before movies such as MY GIRL), and when I read this at a young age, I hadn't known that you could be allergic and die from something like a bee sting.Years later when I was 12 or 13, my mother came to me at home and said she had been stung by a bee and was feeling funny. She seemed weak, and the site of the sting was also swelling up. I told her that bee stings were dangerous and it seemed like she was allergic and she should call a doctor right away.
