This novel was written in 1969. Its a bit dated, as far as one twist goes, in the novel, and luckily thoughts have changed since then. But it’s still a good, enjoyable novel that keeps you listening until the end.Weirdly enough, this particular edition shown here on goodreads is the only version of this novel I could find, that wasn’t included with other novels. I’m not sure why this novel hasn’t had all its variations shown here, but this is confusing and annoying.So while this edition shows this novel is a bunch of CDs read by Tony Britton, this is not the edition I found. I listened to the audiobook made by Audible, which has the wonderful Ralph Cosham as the narrator. Hopefully goodreads gets it’s shit together, soon.Ralph Cosham, btw, is wonderful.3.5 stars
