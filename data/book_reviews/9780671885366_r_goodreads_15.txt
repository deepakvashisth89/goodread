Thomas Tryon is one of my favourite authors. Harvest Home is one of my favourite books. This is not quite up there with that sublime tale and writing, but it was a very good read, notheless, and the classic storytelling and descriptions I associate with Tryon are there.
Themes of ambition, and what is costs you run through this. I am not particularly fond of illusions, but love tales of magic/occult. This book blurs the two, almost asking if they are the same and then at times making a distinction between stage magic tricks and "night magic".
I only drop this a star because it was (for me) a slow start. However, the last half picked up and the last third was certainly worth it. Dinner was late, and we missed going to the movies because I just had to finish it!
