A very lightweight and quite dull murder mystery featuring a distinctly odd married couple (Molly and George Palmer-Jones) who are helping the police to find out who killed Molly's aunt, Ursula Ottway, an elderly woman living in Back Rigg Cottage, part of an estate owned by aristocrat and Member of Parliament, Marcus Grenville.There are a number of suspects and some odd and not very nice characters emerge in the story. The final reveal was a bit of a surprise but by the end of the book I'd had enough of it to be bothered. The plot trundles on but it's all a bit trite and predictable.Set in 1950's Rural Britain, I think this series is trying to achieve a cosy crime feel but I thought it fell short compared to other books in this genre, due, in no small part, to the two main characters who I thought were nothing more than interfering busybodies with too much time on their hands rather than any particular detective skills.I'm a fan of a few of Ann Cleeves' series' but I don't think I'll be continuing with this one. Too many good reads out there.
