As far as children's books go, I don't think this was special at all. It's called never spit on your shoes and yet there is no lesson or anything in relation to spitting on one's shoes. I don't like the fact that the kid was practically fat-shaming his mom who could only have one cookie because she was on a diet. One thing I didn't personally mind very much but I could see offending a lot of people on the bathroom page where a girl is kicking a boy out of the restroom and telling him can't you read? Clearly not gender-neutral and would make a lot of trans and trans-friendly individuals annoyed. Either way, this is a pointless read and with thousands of great children's books out there I'd say you can skip this one guilt-free.
