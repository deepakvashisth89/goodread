I cannot rate because I could not finish. We start in 1888 East End London seen through the eyes of Jack the Ripper. His killings are part of a ritual. When in the proper place with the appropriate girl he kills her and utters his incantation while performing his ritual. Jack dematerializes from London to Colony Farms in British Columbia where he is having the woman's womb and kidney for dinner. So that's where I almost stopped. But I enjoy thrillers, mysteries, science fiction and horror stories. This book has it all! There is interesting historic information about how insanity was treated in the early 1900s before we come to a very gruesome murder. Which leads us to the Forensic Psychiatric Hospital at Colony Farm in British Columbia and a particular inhabitant called Ripper for his belief that he time travels and is, in fact, the real Jack the Ripper. The Goth is his only visitor. They discuss science and wormholes and retribution. I could not finish.
