The crew of the Enterprise beam down to aid an agricultural society beset by Romulan pirates. Not only do they fail to keep the pirates out with their forcefield but Spock is injured when he goes off exploring alone and ends up with amnesia. Back on the ship Bones calls in a Vulcan/Human expert, sexy Emma Saenz who causes quite a stir as she flirts with both the Doctor and the Captain.

Someone seems very determined not to let Spock regain his memory, diplomats are attacked and there seems to be a saboteur on board. We then go through a guessing game of who is responsible, Scotty and the new replacement first officer both come under suspicion.

Despite the annoying Saenz I really enjoyed this. Its a great character piece for Kirk and McCoy - love seeing them at loggerheads over the same woman. We also get to visit Vulcan and explore Spock's family and history as he is forced to return home to recuperate.

Characters and world/terminology are spot on - J.M. Dillard is one of the best writers in the field for capturing the Star Trek essence. It really does feel as if you are watching an episode.

Lovely Boris Vallejo cover art as well.

Its a tad obvious who the villain is however, could have done with maybe a few more suspects and a little more action. Still a very good Trek novel, I really enjoyed it.

Solid 3.5
