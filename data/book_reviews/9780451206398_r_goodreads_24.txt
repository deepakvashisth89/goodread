The writing distracted me from this story:On every page of this book one or more of these actions seemed to be repeated: eyes were narrowed, brows were raised or arched, fingers ran through hair, heads were pillowed on arms, hands were fisted, people burst into laughter, characters threw back their heads and laughed, someone wanted to kill someone else, women were called sluts, men were called bastards (didn't realize 21st century profanity was in such common use back then) etc.If repetitive writing like that doesn't bother you, you may enjoy this novel.
