Sad story of a beautiful girl caught in a web of masculine power and greed who paid the ultimate price with her life.The book reads more like a hagiography of Stratton. The author, noted film director and Stratton's lover, paints quite a story. The question I have is where is Dorothy's voice? The book was not at all unbiased. Dorothy wrote some poetry and a brief memoir which were cited in the text but I gained nothing of her from reading those portions.The story however told is tragic.
