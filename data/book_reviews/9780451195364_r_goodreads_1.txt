A strange clunky and obvious plot*, opaque male lead**, and bizarre dovetailing of sex and crime*** make this a rather unpleasant and laughable read.(*The villain is clear immediately but nobody really even talks about the obvious suspect in Miss Carlyle's uncle's murder; like I get that this is a romance but also: without even of a frisson of doubt it's just boring. Also, when a marriage is required by someone's will, I'd almost rather have it take place sooner and then the leads have to deal with the fallout, rather than fall in love and THEN get married halfway through. The tension kinda goes away.)(**He's got a Mysterious Past and he goes around being very Mysterious and she tries to get him to open up to her but her never will. And she reveals HER deepest darkest secrets, but he doesn't, and whatever she learns, she learns entirely by accident. Rather than two people growing into love and vulnerability, it's a hot, funny guy who never has to do anything to win the girl except be hot and funny. Sort of a proto-50 Shades, though, admittedly, far less troubling. I did like, though, that his clothes are described with as much attention and love as hers, if not more.)(***Okay so like: her uncle gets smashed to death in the eponymous curricle, and it's a Mystery, but every time she and the hot guy talk about it or try to investigate it they end up switching right to flirting and making out instead? Which would be funny if it was intentional, but instead it feels like a list is being checked off: we've got that plot development done, time to spend no fewer than 500 words on the romance. I would read the hell out of a Regency-era romance where the leads got turned on by violent crime, but this isn't that story. Can you even imagine though—Pride and Prejudice crossed with David Cronenberg.)
