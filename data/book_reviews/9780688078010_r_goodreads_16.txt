I think when this came out, I probably would have rated it 4 stars. As it stands now, it needs an update. Much of the case law is dated, particularly the section on the 2nd amendment.It would be 4 stars now, even dated, had the foot/endnotes been better structured. There is no link in the text to the notes, so you have to search the endnotes for each chapter in the appendix in order to find relevant material.That said, like their bookThe Right to Privacythis is an entertaining and enlightening look at the workings of law and the courts that most people don't understand.
