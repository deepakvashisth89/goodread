As a sci-fi fan, I've been trying to go back and read some of the classics and it's been... interesting.The book manages to have some very captivating concepts while being quite tedious to read.The book felt dated when mentioning POC and women which, while not surprising, did still take me out of the story at times.Overall I'm glad I read it, at least I can consider that an achievement as I look at all the "top 100 sci-fi books to read in your lifetime" type of lists but, contrary to popular opinion, I don't think it's something youmustread.If you're curious go for it. If you're not feeling it, it's fine to skip it.
