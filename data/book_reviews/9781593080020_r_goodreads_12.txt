The Call of the Wild and White Fang are similar, yet opposite, stories about the nature of the wild beast vs domestication. It makes for an interesting juxtaposition. The Call of the Wild is the most tightly plotted of the two novels. While the titular characters survives their own story, both novellas tend to be rather violent and gory. The Call of the Wild tells the story of a domestic dog kidnapped from his home in California and forced to pull sleds in the Arctic. White Fang is the tale of a wolf-dog cross that eventually ends up in California. Vividly descriptive and compelling.
