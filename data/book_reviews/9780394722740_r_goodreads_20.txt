Stunning and enthralling memoir as told by an ordinary Chinese whose life was dictated by the cruel, all-powerful and constantly changing edicts of the Maoist government.I have read several memoirs of this era and although they all share the same tales of austerity, brutality, and injustice, each story is unique and powerful and should be a part of our education as Westerners.As we slide further from our freedoms and see increased disinformation, nationalism, xenophobia, suppression of the press, government propaganda, disrespect for education and science, and normalization of despotic leadership, Western readers would be wise to learn from Chinese history to understand what freedom really means and what kind of vigilance and hard work needs to be done to constantly protect it.
