Not my best in the series but I did gain a lot from the whole marriage scene. It taught me what to expect and pray for. However, I like the first book more, the character Abby intrigued me so much more and even Hannah than this character Claire. She seemed a little immature, well she just turned adult legally but still glad a lot of growing up, not the least was her husband, Evan.. I was not so much in love with that character too from the unset. Ian on the other hand was too insensitive and selfish and I think it was a bit overboard. Family with so much love won't go too far (well, I know of mine).It really is a lovey read. Great story but you need to beat yourself hard to stay connected with it. I guess I look forward to the 4th book , Chold of Promise; reading the snippet I am somewhat confused and curious, so I'll get it.
