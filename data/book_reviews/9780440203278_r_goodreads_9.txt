Well, I appear to be in the minority here, but I found this book to be a bit of a slog. More a book about class differences, and less a country-house mystery. I had to power through this book, as I came for the mystery, but found the mystery to lack any real substance. I may have been spoiled by literate film mysteries, such as the “Knives Out” series, and classic works of the Queen of Mystery, Agatha Christie, but this just did not work for me.
