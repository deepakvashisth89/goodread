An older book but I found at lot of the business advice was still very current, particularly since I am in a much slower moving industry than Dell.

The amount of import placed in people (employees, customers, suppliers) and the inter relationships between them has really helped me think differently about my own job. Seeking that information rather than waiting for it to come to you is valid in any role and job in any business.

I also found the tone of this very conversational and approachable. There was minimal technical language and the concepts were developed with great story telling and examples.
