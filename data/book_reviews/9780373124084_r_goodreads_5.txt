3.5 Stars.

This book was a pleasant surprise. I enjoyed the characters, and was glad to see that they got to know one another before love was declared. There was insta-lust, but that's totally believable. And I liked that Lucia was able to self-diagnose her own issues and realize how she was able to get past them, without needing anyone else to point it out to her like she's a witless child (which happens sometimes in books).

I hadn’t realized I’d read this book before, but I definitely did not enjoy it as much his time. The ‘hero’ in this book is rather an asshole who made up his mind about the heroine based on secondhand stories from the father of a dead love interest. Not exactly a reliable source of information. And what’s worse is that he continued to cling to that image of Lucia despite all the evidence before him that what he’d been told wasn’t exactly accurate. But he’s supposed to be a successful businessman and...something with stocks, I think, that requires a good bit of intelligence if not luck. But he acts downright stupid for most of this book when it comes to he heroine.
