Alix married Pierce believing that he loved her as much as she did him. After a glorious wedding night, Pierce rejected her in a most brutal, soul-destroying way saying that he only married her for revenge and to get back the (rotting) fleet of ships that Alix's grandfather stole from Pierce's grandfather before either of the grandchildren were born.Fast forward 5 years. Both grandfathers are dead and the publishing company that Alix's father owns is in financial trouble. Alix has worked her way up and is quite excellent on the publishing side, not so much the financial side. In strides Pierce who offers to help the company *if* Alix becomes his wife again, completely.Here's where the spoilers come in:Seriously?! He destroys her as completely as anyone can destroy someone in love- says he never said he loved her, that they only had sex that night so he could "close the loophole", destroys her trust in him and in herself, then forces her to marry him and tries to convince her that he did it because "he loved her" yet had to fulfill his (deathbed) promise to his grandfather?!This was an extremely angsty book, so much so that I had to stay up to see how he would get out of this. I was looking forward to an extreme grovel, but I never got it. All he said was that it hurt him to do this to her, yet he still did it- not only 5 years ago, but in the present time. He was destroying her, because he loved her?! I get that he didn't think she'd believe him if he "just" explained, but to put her through this again?! No, that's not love, and for her to forgive him because "she never stopped loving him" was just too much!So I go back & forth between 2* and 3. The angst was over-the-top good, but the ending was just not there.
