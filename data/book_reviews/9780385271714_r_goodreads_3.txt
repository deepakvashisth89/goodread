Not a happy book -- bad things happen to children -- but then this was not at all a happy event. The book certainly demonstrates the incredible, courageous, and unselfish passion and drive of young people to change their world for the better (think also: French youth and immigrants at the barricades of theJune Rebellion, young Americans burning their draft cards, Greta Thunberg and the kids who brought theclimate change lawsuit, etc.), but it also shows how easily that passion can be co-opted and misused by the unscrupulous and those who want to preserve the current power structure.
