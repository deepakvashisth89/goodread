I'm an avid Dragonlance fan, and had been wondering what happened to Kang and Co. after readingThe Doom Brigade. So I really enjoyed how open the characters were, and how Weis & Perrin humanized the Draconians after being animalistic enemies in the first trilogy. It had a slightly different feel to it than most of the other Dragonlance books that i wasn't used to, but the ending was good, and made a great tie in to the war of the souls.
