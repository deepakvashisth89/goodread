Jack Canfield and Mark Victor Hansen
Health Communications, Inc., 2003, 371 pp., $12.95
ISBN: 0757301177

I have been a big fan of the Chicken soup series ever since I could read words that hard. [I'm kidding:] The stories in this book are written and edited beautifully, giving people a feeling of being with the person who wrote their stories to us.
In this series particularly, I really liked this story about Le Van Vu and his wife and how they were willing to 'pay the price' and now they have become many times more than millionaires. The story was that Le and his wife chose to save money and their determination to survive in Houston. "They kept clean by taking sponge baths for two years in the mall's restrooms. For two years their diet consisted almost entirely of bakery goods" (Canfield 206). They had suffered their part, but they also gained a whole lot more from that pass experience. This gave me the sense that if you're willing to do something, nothing is impossible. Everyone can become successful, no matter how bad of a situation they are currently in. No one is to blame for being unsuccesssful but yourselves if you haven't even tried. This is how much I got from just one story. But there are many more in this book, and they are all amazing.
I would recommend this for high school students and older because at this point of our lives, we should be thinking about what we want to do with our lives. We don't need to start once we're adults. As for the adults, you guys can always replenish your lives and see if you are really doing the most you can with your life.
