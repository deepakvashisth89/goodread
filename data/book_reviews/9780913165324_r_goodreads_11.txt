I had to have some work done on my house this weekend, and there were hours where I had sunlight but no power, so, of course, I passed the time reading. I binged through this short story collection pretty fast. Overall, this selection of stories was really good, with just a couple of duds.Stephen King had three short stories in this collection: "The Reploids", "Sneakers", and "Dedication". I only liked "Sneakers", but I really like it. It is a weird story but also effective, even though this is my second time reading it.There are three short stories from Dan Simmons: "Metastasis", "Vanni Fucci is Alive and Well and Living in Hell", and "Iverson's Pits". I enjoyed all three stories, but "Iverson's Pits" was fantastic. It is one of the best short stories I have ever read. It is a historically set horror story about revenge.Finally, George R.R. Martin offers a novella-length story, the titular "The Skin Trade". It was fantastic. It is a neo-noir horror that could easily be adapted into a movie.
