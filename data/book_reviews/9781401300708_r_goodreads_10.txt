3.5 StarsLinda Greenlaw is a good writer. I found this to be a very entertaining read. Being from southern Maine, I'm familiar with the Dry Dock bar, as well as a couple of the fishermen mentioned in this book. During the 70s, I managed a now obsolete, ship-to-shore radiotelephone service and would sometimes talk with the skippers of these fishing vessels, if they were unable to reach their customary operator based in Camden. It was an interesting time.
