This was an OK read. I would have enjoyed it more if Bryn and Kahlil had communicated better to resolve their misunderstandings. It could have been cleared up in the first chapter if they had trusted each other more. Kahlil was way too arrogant and Alpha for my taste but at the end he finally showed some human sides of him.For those who like their heroes to be brooding, dark, mysterious, and gorgeous Arabic Sheikh's, then this is the story for you. As long as you don't mind they are also bossy, domineering, cold and at times, ruthless.
