An oddly prescient read that is really helpful for explaining the religious contexts that have shaped a lot of our current political issues. This book should have served as a warning to a country that has now been rocked by evangelical and fundamental influences. Honestly, Strozier should probably do another edition to address some of the things that haven’t held up in the last 30 years while also noting how a lot of his research has been borne out in a post-Trump world.
