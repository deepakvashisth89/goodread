If it's real, than it's mind-blowing, if it's fiction, then still is mind-blowing how much a mind can conceive. But it sounds real to me. It is told in a bland way, just presenting the facts in a detached, yet brutal way. The description of the war in Vietnam and a covert plan to take out a big guy in the Vietnam army is fantastic. The things they undergo, show us that soldiers are humans too, they too feel pain, longing, lust, and that these feelings aren't switched off in the middle of war. Definitely recommended.
