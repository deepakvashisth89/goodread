A quietly emotional portrait of love, change, and self-discovery runs through Sarah Whitman as the title character navigates the shifting expectations of her personal and professional life. Sarah Whitman is a woman who appears to have stability on the surface, but beneath it she grapples with dissatisfaction in her relationships and uncertainty about what she truly wants. As circumstances push her toward difficult choices, she begins to question long-held assumptions about duty, happiness, and identity.The story focuses heavily on internal reflection and emotional transitions rather than dramatic external events. While the pacing can feel slow at times, it remains a thoughtful exploration of a woman trying to redefine her life on her own terms.RATING: four out of five stars ⭐⭐⭐⭐
