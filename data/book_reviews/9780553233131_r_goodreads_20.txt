This is one of those books you need a program to keep up with everyone, their motives, as well as who they're for and against. Sheesh! I like Blaine. He has a strong sense of honor and justice. Even after
Neal is killed, he stays true to his word where someone else might up and leave. A good and fast read, but I can't give it a full five stars for the kind of open-ended ending.

Coyer: Read a book that contains all the letters in the word BLUE ~ Utah Blaine (4 points)
