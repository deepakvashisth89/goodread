⭐⭐⭐⭐ A Playful Celebration of Childhood ImaginationA Light in the Atticis a book I remember receiving as a child, long ago, and it has stayed with me ever since. LikeWhere the Sidewalk Ends, it captures a sense of imaginative freedom that feels timeless. The poems are funny, strange, and instantly memorable, and the illustrations add to their charm rather than distracting from them. There is a wide emotional range here, from pure silliness to moments that are surprisingly gentle and thoughtful.Silverstein’s voice remains playful and deeply comforting, inviting readers to see the world sideways and without embarrassment. Beneath the humor is a quiet encouragement toward creativity, empathy, and the freedom to think differently. This is a book for anyone, whether encountering it for the first time or returning to it years later. It is especially the kind of book I would hope to share with my own children one day, passing along the same sense of wonder and warmth that it once gave me.
