An interesting mystery, set in Vienna, with Lipizzan horses being mentioned frequently... so, in many ways, an ideal book for me to read! I've always been drawn to any mention of Lipizzaners, perhaps because they are such an ancient breed, who came close to dying out several times. They aren't as central to the plot as I would have wanted, but I enjoyed the story nonetheless.
Mary Stewart's characters were engaging and fun, and it was nice to follow along. I'll be reading more of her books!
