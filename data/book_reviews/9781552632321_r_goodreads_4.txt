The Mermaid of Paris is about an eccentric inventor (Henry) in the 1900s who is married to a bored housewife (Margaret).Henry works for his father-in-law at his bicycle factory, but would much rather be inventing or looking after his birds. Margaret becomes bored and takes off to Europe with a circus performer. Finding his wife becomes Henry's new obsession.To me, the book is about how obsessions can haunt us and get in the way of living life.A few interesting characters and concepts.
