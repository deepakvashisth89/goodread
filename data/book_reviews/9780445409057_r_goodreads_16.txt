I am enjoying reading about the schlamozel detective, Toby Peters, who tries valiantly to solve the problems for his client and ends up beaten and caught between the bad guys and his brother, the policeman. The use of actual Hollywood characters like Basil Rathbone, in this story adds a familiarity which is old time movie fans enjoy. I am sorry that Mr. Kaminsky has left us, but there are plenty of stories left for me to enjoy.
