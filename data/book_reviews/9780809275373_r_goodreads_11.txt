This is the sixth book in author L. Frank Baum's Oz books. And, with the sole exception of The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, it is the best. That is because The Emerald City of Oz shares with it some worthy villains. So, instead of a mere pageant, there is some suspense as to whether the Nome King and his nefarious allies will succeed in tunneling under the desert that protects the Emerald City and force the residents to become slaves.

To keep up the suspense, there are some chapters where we see the Nome King's plot unfolding, and others in which Dorothy Gale and some of her Oz buds progress through parts of the land to visit some of the stranger residence. In the end (and it's no spoiler to tell you this), the forces of good win again; and the Nome King and his allies are totally befuddled.
