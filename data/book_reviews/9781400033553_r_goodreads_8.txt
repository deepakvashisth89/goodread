450 pages of short articles, but I read this mostly straight through, like a book. That wasn't the best decision and I was ready to be done with it by the end. My favorite articles were in the section titled "Americans abroad" with stories of the race through the Sahara desert, and my least favorite were about groups like the military in Iraq and the tupperware sellers. Very random! It would be better to pick this up and read one article at a time while reading other books.
