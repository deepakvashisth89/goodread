This is an incredible story! The bravery and determination of the Owenses, to follow wildlife in an area where the animals had never before been studied, invoked passion in me. I lived in the Kalahari while I served as a United States Peace Corps volunteer, so reading about Southern Africa and this peace-keeping mission of improving conservation lands in Botswana felt very familiar. Surviving the extreme temperatures, struggle for enough water and available food, and chance encounters from generous strangers all rang a bell from my service days. Highly recommended, and make sure you read their second book from the Luangwa Valley, The Eye of the Elephant. Go siame!
