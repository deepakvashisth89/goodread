I use to be a fan of Dr. Joy Browne’s syndicated radio talk show so when I saw her book at a used book store I picked it up to see what it was about. It’s no more than a written collection of conversations she had with callers on her radio show. She grouped them into nine fantasies people have that fail for them and eight realities.

One fantasy she proposed is that “There is no place like home.’ The truth is that all families have problems and are dysfunctional. No one lives in a perfect family. But we tend to believe others do.
One reality she prosed is that “Selfish is Cool, Mean is not.” People need to take care of themselves before they can take care of anyone else. You can only make yourself happy no one else can do that for you.

I usually read self-help books because it makes me feel good after reading them. This book didn’t give me any such feeling.
