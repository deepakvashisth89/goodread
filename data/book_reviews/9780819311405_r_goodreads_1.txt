Text-to Teaching Connection: What is a response activity that you can do in a classroom with children in response to the book? Be specific (e.g. drama, music, art, writing, math, science, social studies, etc.).I just love this book! It is so cute and fun and is great for word play and imagery. For this books response activity, I would use it in writing and have my students come up with their own jokes. I would have them write it out as best they could, with a little assistance from me or our word wall, and then have them draw a picture depicting the joke they've written. Afterwords, I would have all of my students come to the carpet to display their jokes to the rest of the class. I think it would be a fun and enlightening activity to find out what my students find funny and figure out how they came up with their jokes.
