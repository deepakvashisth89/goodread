I am very partial to academic novels and Barnard is brilliantly witty, so I really enjoyed devouring this. It's also a very interesting, detailed portrait of rural Australia - a setting I had never encountered in my readings - punctuated by rich, unique characters.However, the mystery/detectivesque side of things is surprisingly unoriginal and dull. So much so that it does make a dent in an otherwise great book. Do read it, nevertheless.
