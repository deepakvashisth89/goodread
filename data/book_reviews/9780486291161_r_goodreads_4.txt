Frank Baum in The Wonderful Wizard of OzAlmost a year ago to this day, I was in Chicago and waltzed into Sandmeyer’s Bookstore. Displayed was the most extraordinary copy of The Wonderful Wizard of Oz that I had ever seen. Of course, I had to have it. I pulled out my credit card and tucked away my purchase for a rainy day.After reading several one-star books, my rainy day was here, and I pulled out my previously hidden treasure.At the time, I didn’t even know what I had in my hands. The version that I purchased is by Minalima, a graphic artist team that did most of the props for Harry Potter. The pages are quality – thick in your hands. The book is filled with illustrations and interactive elements that create an immersive experience brimming with wonder and exhilaration.The Wonderful Wizard of Oz was first published in 1900 and has some of the most iconic, memorable characters in literary history. Baum doesn’t waste any time and launches readers into the action in the very first chapter.
