"The Return of Mr. Campion" by Margery Allingham features the titular detective, but only in a few stories. Many of the stories were either "look of love"-themed romances or non-Campion mysteries.The Campion stories are, naturally, the strongest entries in the book, along with an essay about writing. I've always enjoyed Allingham's short Campion fiction more than her full-length novels, perhaps because Campion has less time to dither in the short stories. I find him somewhat irritating if I have to spend a lot of time with him.While I enjoyed the Campion stories, I found most of the rest to be well-written, but of little interest. Maybe if the title had read, "Campion and Others," my expectations would have been better fulfilled. I was prepared for, and wanting, a volume of Campion stories, and got instead a hodgepodge of musings, mysteries, and light romance.Fans of Allingham will probably enjoy this, but other readers should start with "Police at the Funeral" or one of the other short fiction collections.I received a copy from Crime Classics Review Club and NetGalley in exchange for an honest review.
