Agnes of God is a 1979 play that is very of its time, but it also holds up decently well with its portrayal of the complicated nature of psychiatry (which is, again, VERY of its time, but still looks at ethics surrounding psychiatry in ways that remain relevant), Catholicism, unwanted pregnancies, and survivors of trauma. It's interesting, and it's deeply sad. Worth reading for those who know what they're getting into. I listened to the LA Theatre Works production, which delivered great performances all around.
