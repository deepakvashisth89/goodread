This is a really easy, simple, sweet book. I wouldn't say there is anything profound about the story nor amazing in the writing style. However, I so appreciate reading a good, clean story, with religious and spiritual messages (without being preachy or overbearing) so I'll keep reading the series.I do have to say that I pretty well despise Jenny. I fail to see why anyone would want to be her friend. Her selfishness is deplorable - and I almost feel like Victoria is a glutton for punishment, always trying to patch things up with her after a fight. And Jamison should probably realize that he doesn't help that situation. He'd have to be totally blind not to realize Jenny's infatuation with him or to know her vindictive demeanor when she doesn't get her way. Ultimately I'm pleased with the way Victoria has grown and how she's seemed to develop a backbone to let Jenny how she really feels about things. But I sure wish there was some growing on Jenny's part.
