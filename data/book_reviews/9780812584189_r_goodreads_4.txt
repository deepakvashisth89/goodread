This is a long and leisurely blend of hard science and space opera with a YA flavor. It's an easy read, despite the length, and the deceptively simple set-up embraces some nice puzzles and surprises. The beginning is a little long and the ending a little rushed, but overall, I enjoyed it very much. The science is well-presented, too, once you get past the original premise that a miniature black hole is emitting particles that bond with people and improve their personalities. Zahn stays within the confines of his speculation rigorously, and the simplified political situation is engaging, too. It's a fun book.
