Theories are offered to explain the change in the level of homicide rates and violent crimes, based on the common denominator from English homicides. The chapters follow a logical order, as different motivations and relationships are presented in each chapter. Reading the police transcripts gives you a better appreciation of the police dept., as they patiently extract information from witnesses and the accused killer.
The impact of role models on the cultural acceptance of violence did not leave much of an impact due to unfamiliarity with English history. I got the impression that English heroism was equated with being able to shoot a gun. While there were references to different levels of violence and homicides between countries, the five pages devoted to the synopsis of First Blood, the story of Rambo was interesting, but seemed out of place.
