What a book to start the year with. As always rilke's writing is so mindblowing that it makes the book unputdownable. He talks in each elegy about the importance of embracing mortality and the present even if they're impermanent, as well as accepting our humanity as it is, with all its pain and weaknesses and that even if we're caught in the fragility of life yet we still strive for meaning. He also put the light on how we, as humans, no matter how close we try to be from one other, there will always be an invisible barrier between that makes true connection impossible. His words were so sincere that found myself fighting tears all along. Such a masterpiece 🤍
