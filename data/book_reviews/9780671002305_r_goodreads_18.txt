When looking at the book, I automatically thought this book was about Prince Charles and Diana, but when reading, boy was I ever wrong.

It starts off at the beginning of the realm of Queen Victoria, when the House of Windsor first began to 1995. It’s a very interesting read and informative.

I will say at the beginning of the novel, I was very confused in regards to the names of everyone but I did some research in order to follow the book.

The book is a very accurate representation of the coming downfall of the House of Windsor. If there is such a thing as King or Queen in the next century, I’ll be very surprised (not that I would see that).

Very enjoyable read.
