Good book dating back from the 90s. The author's theory should be known by anyone in a position to make strategic decisions. To be honest I was skeptical at first about his ideas, but they quickly became obvious as more and more cases were presented.Taking away one star for being superfluous -- half of the book comprises the same few takeaways that are repeated over and over again in the introduction, conclusion, and in between each case study.Taking away one more star for choosing a misleading title -- a better title would be 'The Corporation's Dilemma'. The book is targeted to successful executives as opposed to ambitious innovators and the author never gives credit to innovators that don't match his narrow definition of 'disruptive', (not even in 2015). Many companies today disrupt through quality execution and/or network effects: Uber, Facebook, Apple, Tesla.Still I feel that for its intended target (corporate executives) this book is a must read.
