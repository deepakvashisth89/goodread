This works if you believe in Fantasy love where you can fall in love with a person within the first meeting and being hauled into an embrace without consent is romantic. But then again this is Harlequin romance.

I wasn’t expecting a deep dive into anybody’s psyche. I just wanted to escape into a romantic fantasy where there is a guaranteed happily ever after and this book gives you that. If anything, I wish that the book was a bit longer to allow there to be more of a connection established between the two potential lovers. This is classic Harlequin.
