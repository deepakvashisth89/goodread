A sad sort of little book, with somewhat trite characters. I did finish it, however, because I needed to know Who Did It.
A few interesting thoughts about dying prematurely: If we expect a lot of ourselves when young, is disappointment inevitable? Was Ophelia mad? Would Dorothy Parker have been happier had she successfully offed herself at an early age?
Altogether, quite English, in the oh-thank-God-I-wasn’t-born-in-a-small-village way.
And I learned a new word: slummocked. So there you go.
