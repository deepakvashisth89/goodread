Super good, had not heard about him until his poems were quoted in "The Hallmarked Man". Some of his imagery and phrasing is fantastic. I very much like how much he could do with some fairly strict forms. The rhythm and rhyming push the meaning.One negative is he was very melancholic - lots of die young, never see home again stuff. Normal for the era but can get a bit depressing if you read too many at once. I now want to buy a copy and work on memorizing some of the poems.
