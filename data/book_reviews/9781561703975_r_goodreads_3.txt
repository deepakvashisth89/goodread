This book was one of the first texts I have read about angels by Doreen Virtue. The first part of the book is channelled healing messages by the angels on many different topics such as anxiety, career, fatigue, friendship and stress. I found these healing messages to be very comprehensive in detail and interesting to read for the first time. This part of the book is great as a reference text when you need help from your angels and can be used as an oracle where you simply open up any page to read guidance the angels may have for you. The angelic messages are written in an older style of writing which is still easy to understand. Doreen explains the channeling process in the preface of the book as the way that the angels like to communicate their messages. The second part of the book discusses the different types of angels and their roles, ways to communicate with your angels and how to develop the habit of asking for the angels help. The book also explains different techniques for creating clearer communication with the angels such as developing new eating habits, meditating and spending time in nature. Overall I think this is a great beginners text about angels and I found it particularly useful in developing my own understanding of the angelic realm. I look forward to future angel reading with Doreen's Healing with the Angels.
