About 7 years after reading Elfshadow and Elfsong I finnaly got around to check up on Arilyn and follow her journey in Silver Shadows. Unfortunately there's very little of Danilo in this book, which is quite a shame. It was nice to check up on Arilyn, and we do learn more about her sword and its powers which was nice. The plot in the first 3/4 is ok, but the last 1/4 felt uneven and has a few problems.All in all, Silver Shadows is ok, but falls a bit in comparison to first two books in the series.First 3/4 - 3.5-3.75/5, last 1/4 - 3.25/53.5/5
