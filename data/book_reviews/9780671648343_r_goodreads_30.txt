I have never read anything by the brilliant Dr. Sacks before and what a treat!! Not that it was easy reading. Erudite and full of neurological terminology, it can be a little tedious at times, but oh so fascinating.My edition was the 1976, first revised copy in which Dr. Sacks has filled the book with copious notes that traverse philosophy, metaphysics, hard science & the arts peppered with many quotes from Donne to Freud in his rumination on medicine in general and the specific cases he writes about, in addition to some updates on the patients. The book brackets the case descriptions with a general introduction about this epidemic that flew under the radar because it surfaced at the same time as the Flu epidemic of 1918, and its effects on those who developed Parkinsonian type symptoms post-encephalitis with a wonderful "Perspective" chapter and Epilogue. The cases themselves are heartbreaking, during their "awakening" with the use of this new drug L-Dopa, almost all expressed what we now describe as a locked in syndrome. They are fully conscious of their world but unable to communicate that in anyway to others. Leonard L. described it in this way "I have no exit. I am trapped in myself. This stupid body is a prison with windows but no doors."Since many reviewers stated that the movie was very good, I am eager to see how this was adapted.
