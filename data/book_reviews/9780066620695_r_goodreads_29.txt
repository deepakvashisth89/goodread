I read this as research for the novel I'm writing. While it didn't end up being particularly relevant to what I was trying to research, I still found it rather fascinating. Throughout these 300 pages, the author goes through a rather thorough defense of his claim that disruptive technologies cannot be weilded by firms with dominant market positions. There were many points throughout the book where I said "wait, what about [concept]? doesn't that contradict what he said?" and then later on he would address my claim. It's a shame he died in 2020, I'd be interested in hearing what he has to say about AI.Although I'm not a manager at a technology company and likely never will be, it was interesting to read, especially the parts which dove into the unexpectedly interesting history of the disk drive industry.
