I have the utmost respect for Laura and the amazing work she does. The book which she co-authored with Ellen Bass "The Courage to Heal" was one of the most helpful books in relation to my own personal journey."I thought we'd never speak again" centres around estrangement and reconciliation and was helpful in many ways, with the personal stories shared only added weight to the conversation. There are still some areas of my life where forgiveness will never happen, and I am okay with that. Sometimes we just need to accept a situation and move on. This book is a wonderful resource though for anyone to begin their own path towards reconciliation. You never know - it might just work out for you.
