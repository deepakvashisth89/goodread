3.5 starsThis is book #12 in the Detective Alleyn series of books. I enjoyed #11 so much that this one was bound to feel like a letdown. None of the characters appealed to me, but the mystery itself was top notch. Colour Scheme takes place in New Zealand during WWII. Alleyn is there investigating wartime espionage. He is mentioned several times but takes forever to make an appearance.One book blogger (Bobonbooks) wrote, “There are really three mysteries in the story: who is the spy, who murdered *****, and where is Alleyn?” You’ll be surprised at all three answers.
