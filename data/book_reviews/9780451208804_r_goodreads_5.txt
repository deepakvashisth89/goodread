2.5 stars. This is a pleasant, if unexceptional, historical mystery with an interesting setting: Georgian England, just as not-yet-mad King George III ascends to the throne, aged 19. I picked this up because I love the Regency romances this author has written under the name Jude Morgan.Our hero Fairfax is a gentleman fallen on hard times who has taken a job as a tutor. He is chaperoning his teenaged student’s first trip to London, where the boy immediately gets himself accused of murdering an actress. Fairfax must unravel the mystery to save the boy, not to mention his job.This is well written, with decent characterizations and good dialog. It’s perhaps a little too formulaic as a murder mystery: as each secondary character is introduced, they are rather obviously being established as a potential suspect. The historical details are well done.
