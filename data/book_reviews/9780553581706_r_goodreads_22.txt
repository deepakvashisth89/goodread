How can a book told from the perspective of a nihilist, someone who is the very definition of survivor's guilt, be so incredibly moving? Day, the final book of the Elie Wiesel's Night series is full of gems. I can't remember ever re-reading so many sentences because they were so profound, so full of the stuff life is made of, which is astounding coming from the narrator.Night was dark, horrific, yet very moving. Dawn raised serious moral questions both on a personal level and a societal level. With Day we find that life continues, even if the survivors of the worst treatment imaginable don't necessarily want it to.And one more thing about all three books, the Prefaces alone are worth their weight in gold.
