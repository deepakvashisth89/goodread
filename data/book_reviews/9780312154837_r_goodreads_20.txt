I loved this book. I've read some books on similar material in the past but this by far has been the best. This book was truly a page turner, and definitely informative as it spoke about a period of history that still remains somewhat secretive and unresolved. The characters were real people, the wonderful ones being the heroes and heroines who did their utmost to save the Tsar's family. The bad ones were real people too and a few were the personification of pure evil.
There were some surprise twists at the end of the story.
