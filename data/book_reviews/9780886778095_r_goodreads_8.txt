This story has some very good moments. It did run a little long, and seemed a little slow at times, but I liked all of the characters and the way Abbey chose to reveal things in the story was very satisfying.

The story follows Jerlayne, an elf of Faerie, through her marriage to Aulaudin, and her interactions with the mortal realm and the other creatures of Faerie. I feel like if I say anything else it would be mildly spoiler-y, so suffice it to say, if you like elves, appreciate a complex plot, like a strong female protagonist, and are ok with a little weirdness, this might be a good read for you!

I gave it three stars because for me:
1 star = I couldn't even finish it it was so awful
2 stars = I finished it but only because I was curious about how it ended
*3 stars = I enjoyed the story but don't intend to ever read it again*
4 stars = I really liked the story, may read again, and definitely recommend
5 stars = I love it and own it and will read it again
