I saw Paco Underhill speak at a symposium at NYPL; looking forward to this book. . . . Quick, interesting read. I picked up some ideas I can use at the library. For example, having baskets available for patrons to use could increase borrowing. Good idea! Another tip, have comfortable seating available for people waiting on a family member who is browsing. We do have comfortable seating, but I might rearrange and make the seating area more welcoming.Even if you are not looking for marketing ideas, this is a good read. A quick sociological/cultural/anthropological tour of American malls and malls abroad. It's also a bit of a history lesson and a glimpse to the future of shopping and malls. Recommended.
