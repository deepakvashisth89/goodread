This is an excellent mystery series that I've read many times. This particular book is one of the New Zealand ones, which are some of my favorites. A vain opera star is being chased by a paparazzi so she retreats to a private island in New Zealand. The famous British portrait painter, Agatha Troy, comes to paint the diva, bringing her husband, the Scotland Yard Detective along. The story is a bit over-the-top for me, much like opera is, but it's still good fun. And any time I can have Alleyn and Troy together, I'm pretty happy.NB - At some point in the series we're told to pronounce the detective's last name as Allen, not Al-Line. FYI
