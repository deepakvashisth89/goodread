The lovely song drew me to read this short book. Although I enjoy the song immensely, the book was unexpectedly trite and, in my opinion, gives very little depth while jumping strangely back and forth between giving life advice and telling one or the other author's story while several times assuming we, as the readers, have similar life stories.This story of how the song came to be cum self help book feels like the two authors were told by their agent and management team to put this together and the two stayed up all night one night riffing this book together with statements and sentences most of us have heard a thousand times.This is not a criticism of the song or of the authors' lived stories.
