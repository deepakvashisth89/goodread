Simple, yet good-to-own cookbook with 130 recipes (and no photos), this book is clear and tasty-reading collection. Like other books of hers, this starts with a pantry list, then recipes in categories with introduction to each.

I noticed how the most common oil is corn, vegan ’cheese’ is used as a vegan alternative, and there’s lots of coriander and nuts (though some can be easily made nut-free by just not putting them on top of the finished dish). I had to look up what chayote was and looked like, it was interesting :)

I found recipes that I would like to try enough to say having this book was worth it for me, using-wise, though of course some recipes are obvious ones. I think in some one could make a shortcut and use what food stores have ready? (I’m not listing recipe names here this time, just because some are those obvious ones, and others… well, I’m just not in the mood for listing them now. But I did list them in my notepaper I keep with books I plan to review.)

If you already have a Mexican vegan cookbook from another author, you might not need this one, but it’s a pretty cheap buy even now, so getting it is fine, in my opinion. And it’s a pretty quick read too :)
