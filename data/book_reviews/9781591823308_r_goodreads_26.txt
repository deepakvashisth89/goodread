An old favorite.The Fake manga is an old favorite of mine. The art is nicely done. The characters are engaging. And the plot usually has an interesting twist. The translations and English adaptation have not aged well though. The dialogue and slang are a bit awkward sometimes. It is easy to see what the writer meant, but the follow through doesn't sound right and in some cases really isn't appropriate.Still an entertaining read, and the extra backstory at the end was insightful into the main character's histories.
