Another winner in the series of mysteries about Scotland Yard Detective Superintendent Duncan Kincaid and Sargeant Gemma James.In this book, Duncan and Gemma are drawn into the investigation of an apparent drowning death of Connor Swann, the son in law of musical giants Gerald and Caroline Asherton and estranged husband of their daughter Julia, an artist.Julia wants a divorce and left Connor to live in the apartment they once shared while she returned to live at home with her parents at Badgers End. After 2 years apart, she would be able to divorce him.Though they appear to be normal on the surface, even enviable, there are dangerous currents running under the surface. Caroline, Julia, and Connor all have secrets. Who was willing to kill Connor over one of those secrets?As Duncan and Gemma investigate, I became ticked off with Duncan for sleeping with one of the suspects- Julia. Julia has exactly 0 appeal or personality. She is cold and devoid of love or feeling for anyone and has been carrying on an affair with a man who is married with children while she herself is married. Why would Duncan just out of the blue decide he must "comfort" her by sleeping with her? Connor's own little tart of a mistress was actually more pitiful.There is also Duncan's growing relationship with Gemma. Why betray that? Why should Gemma ever be able to trust Duncan when he has such poor judgement and loose morals? Keep in mind Gemma's own heart was broken when her husband left her when she had their little son Toby. I am annoyed that Deborah Crombie wrote that scene in. It was uncalled for and out of character and there was no reason for it at all, especially when she has a romance building between Duncan and Gemma.On to the next book in the series!
