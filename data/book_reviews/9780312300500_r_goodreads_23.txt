Wonderful story of love and redemption. Madison an Wes Reed seemed to be a picture perfect power couple. They both were well known television journalist but they had a huge secret - their marriage was a failure. Then the unbelievable happened, Wes was fatally injured in a car accident. The next few weeks were a blur for Madison and she never saw the extreme changes coming.

Ray has out done herself with this work..."trust your heart...it always knows the way." Madison had to work through betrayal of her husband, disrespect from her in-laws, pressure from the media, and getting to know what she really wants. You will run through all types of emotions and won't be able to stop reading. This is an incredible must read novel!
