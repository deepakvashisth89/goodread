Not exactly a unique story. A group of Iranian elite forces land in the USA and attack various locations, including an army base and ultimately the White House.What I liked about this novel is that, unlike other novels of similar plotlines, the author doesn't treat the Iranian team as mindless idiots who have no planning and only suicidal intentions. Their plans are well thought out, and when they face unexpected challenges they quickly adapt.Of course, at the end the US thwrats the final attack, but they are not shown to be invincible.
