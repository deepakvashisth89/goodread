This is three quite unrelated novellas published together. Already not my cup of tea, I was expecting a series with continuity between the three parts.

The first two stories I did not like much at all, they were simply dark and depressing. "Wave Without a Shore" however was an uplifting story that was slow getting started, and a very good read. Standing alone, it would be maybe four stars. The culture portrayed is more than a little psychologically weird, including the main character, but both broke loose from their mental shackles and in the end achieved a quite extraordinary transformation.
