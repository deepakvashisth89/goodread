I remember reading A Time To Die years ago, and thinking it was the best thing I'd ever read. On holidays over the last few weeks I found a battered paperback in my father-in-law's bookshelf and thought I'd give it another go. Unfortunately I don't think it had aged too well. This time around I found Sean Courtney just a little too amazingly brilliant at pretty much everything from rock-climbing to shooting, to guerrilla warfare, to tracking, to fighting off a deadly snake (while rock-climbing), to throwing grenades with unerring accuracy etc etc etc.It's definitely fast-paced, all out action, and I still remembered and enjoyed the character of Matutu as much as ever. But beyond that, this one hasn't stood the test of time.
