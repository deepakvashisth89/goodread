There is a lot of unstated homophobia in this book. Or is it just me?

I think it is just me.

Too much like me in my youth, in fact. Le Guin seems totally wrapped around the axle about it, as was plain to me in Omelas. Back then it was more my own view.

DNF. It’s not for me now.

Now, growing up here in Canada after several uninterrupted years of socialist adjustments to our mindsets, kids are bound to take a wider view than my generation. I am a Christian.

I can see their point of view - angry homophobia is a killer, and the rather desultory, hit-and-miss effect of my neuroleptics have, with painstaking home-care, led me to a newly desultory view of literature and the society in which it is created.

And Diversity is the salt that seasons it all for the kids. Socialism sees sexual diversity as neither inherently good or bad: it just IS.

Jesus made the deep statement that salt made bland by time isn’t worth keeping: He meant, of course, our moral fibre and not our sexuality.

Different, though - face it - is what we are. And our lifetime mission of Swallowing those Differences, and digesting them with our own Christian thoughts, is surely what makes us real and valuable human beings.

Yet our media would turn us solely into happy-faced consumers, and put a thick veneer of glossy Sameness over our Difference.

So sexual diversity thrives unknown within our churches but still we old-style Christians refuse to accept it. For the simple reason that the modern churches, unlike our media, just sidestep it. Purity rules.

Bland salt was unacceptable to Jesus. We must take sides.

Now, I’m straight myself, but I’m not bland. In fact, by accepting diversity more and more easily, after many years, I find my own diversity - being neurodivergent (I’ve been called dumber than sin) - has more savor.

Heinlein’s Stranger in a Strange Land gave us the straight goods: it’s time to accept - with open hearts - the blaring Fact of diversity head-on.

One reason is that we have lost our innocence. The Fall of Adam tells us so. But modern society’s new Bland is Blind to that fact. And the medias' sin is diverse, though blandness makes us myopic. So why continue to be bland about our diversity in church?

Embrace it and love one another warmly as Christians. Swallow that Difference, and live through the purgatorial pain it invokes.

Difference is strong Medicine for modern times, but it works. As does respect.

For we can ALL - and I mean ALL - be saved by Faith. And, by a faith that clearly SEES our diversity painfully -

Through eyes that always try to avoid sin.
