Another interesting story by Grisham, about the corruption in legal practice and greedy lawyers taking advantage of clients. It almost felt like a Mobster film, where the main character starts out from beneath, slowly works his way up making it to the top, then in the end everything goes south and starts to crumble.Clay Carter seems to be getting nowhere in life, a low-paid Public Defender in Washington DC. But one day, Clay is made an offer he can't refuse, after taking the case of a man charged with what is seemingly a random street killing. But as Clay digs deeper, he finds himself up against one of largest pharmaceutical companies in the world. Clay decides to start a new chapter in his life, leaving the life of a Public Defender and working his way up, building his own Law Firm specializing in torts, and making more money a year than he could ever imagine.An insightful story about greed and corruption, some of the legal jargon was a little overplayed, causing the story to drag at times, but other than that, an entertaining read!
