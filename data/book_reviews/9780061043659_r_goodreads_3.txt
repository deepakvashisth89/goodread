I found this book while I was cleaning and thought it looked interesting. I'm usually hesitant to read fictionalized accounts of Scriptural events...but this author took the time to preface her story by acknowledging the book WAS pure fiction because there is no record of the things that she writes. So I took it for what it was: one woman telling the story of how she thought it may have happened. Very fascinating book. It made me think about Mary in a different way. How difficult it must have been--her first child...but He wasn't really hers.
