Well, I must say that Jean Rabe stepped up her game in this book - it's actually a lot better than the first book in this trilogy.
Things are definitely not what they seem in this book - and the betrayal in the title is so much bigger and completely different than I thought it would be. It completely blew me - I did not see that coming!
I like the character twists - you as a reader never fully understands what the characters are and what they are capable of doing before they do it - and the characters themselves don't know anything about their companions and friends for sure.
And poor Dhamon - he came so close ... and he never knew what happened...
