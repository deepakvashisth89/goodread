I really enjoyed the international-ness of the book, with authors from Italy, Wales, Argentina, Austria, and many more places! The stories varied in ratings, with 5 1's, 16 2's, 22 3's, 7 4's, and 9 5's out of 5.My favorite stories were: At the River, Welcoming the Board of Directors, The School, The Shoe Breaker, Looking for a Rain God, Tancredi, On Hope, and The Grass-Eaters!
