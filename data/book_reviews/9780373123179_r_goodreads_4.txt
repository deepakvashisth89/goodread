3.5 stars 4 books in, this author definitely has a formula, some elements a little questionable since they are repeated so often
so far, it’s always an older man/younger woman with a significant power imbalance—even in this one, where the heroine has far greater status/money/etc., she has to be hobbled by circumstance, injury, and past history , but I really like her books nonetheless. Her heroines are always smart and interesting and hold their own in sparky conversation.

I don’t find the rock star thing particularly interesting, but it was fine—a little more about her musical background and future would’ve been nice, though. I need to look into whether this author has written under any other names, because she really doesn’t have that many HP titles and I’m dreading running out.
