The Flight of Solomon


Solomon is a mithycal figure from the African folklore - an enslaved man who conquered his freedom by flying back to his home land…

Abolition of Slavery was not enough. Between 1930 and 1960, for the majority of slaves descendants, freedom was still a small dot in a vast horizon - they were now targets of racism and prisoners of their own poverty. The Flight of Solomon symbolizes their quest for freedom.

In an intelligent, symbolic prose Toni Morrison enchants and magnetizes. More than a brilliant story teller, TM was an alchemist - she recycled the worst of mankind into literary gems
