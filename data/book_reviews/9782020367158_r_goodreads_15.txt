Well written but a bit redundant at times. Storyline is insanely good but I think the concept could’ve been tackled a bit better. Obviously this is a two perspective story and the way it’s written leads to us coming to conclusions which are, for the most part, completely wrong. It’s an interesting reading experience but I did get disappointed by the way the book ended. Seemed a bit far fetched as there really were no indications the book’s mystery could possibly resolve in that way. It’s partly what made me opt for a 3 star review because despite it being good, I didn’t enjoy it as much as I could have, had the plot been used and presented in a different (better) way.I recommend it to beginner level readers trying to get into the mystery/crime genre of novels. It’s good enough to give a person the idea to pursue their readings in that genre.Thank youGorbius
