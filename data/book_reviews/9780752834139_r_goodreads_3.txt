I thought I was going to read a light hearted chick lit. However the casual treatment of the domestic violence appalled me. More care is lavished on the damage to the car than to the woman who isHolding a bloodstained handkerchief to her nose. Her left eye is swollen and closed ... Her lip is cut and enlarged, and blood is streaming over her jaw and dripping onto the pavement... Her other arm is folded around her stomach as if she is in pain.Her partner has tried to drown her.But we ignore all of that and encourage her to go back because she doesn't have anywhere else to go.I am putting this book in the recycling, I don't want anyone else to read it
