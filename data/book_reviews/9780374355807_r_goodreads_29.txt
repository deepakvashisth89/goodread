This book is very hard to rate. I liked a lot of the characters, but the way Deafness was portrayed bothered me. The main character Theo mentioned he wanted to give the gift of hearing to his brother Jeremy. I have a strong feeling many of my deaf friends would be offended by this book. I'm not going to recommend this book to them, but it does have good insights for people who are unfamiliar with the deaf community. This story also takes place before text message, video phone, easy interpreter access,…
Hmmmm. Mixed feelings on this one.
