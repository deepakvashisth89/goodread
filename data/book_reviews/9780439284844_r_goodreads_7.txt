Mommy's review from 6/30/11 -Another Scooby-Doo book we worked our way through. After reading so many in a row we've learned a few things. One is that James Gelsey is, out of the handful of Scooby-Doo authors we've read so far, the best of the bunch. I'll zero in on a Scooby book with his name on it before any other.Another is that Julia much prefers the Collect-the-Clues books to the Mysteries.Scooby-Doo! and the Rowdy Rodeo is from the Mystery series and when we don't have any Collect-the-Clue's laying around these do just fine.The story here is much the same as in any of Gelsey's Scooby books. It's easy to follow along, each to understand and age appropriate.The illustrations are just what I want with a book like this. Enough to keep her engaged should she find a page or two boring but not so much that it's a picture book.I have a funny feeling by the time we're finished (or Julia's reached adulthood) we'll have read every single Scooby-Doo book in the world.
