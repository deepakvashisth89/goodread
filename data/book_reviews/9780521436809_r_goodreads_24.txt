Simply the best English grammar book that I ever read in my entire life.It's extremely informative, simple, great, useful, well-written and lots of other positive adjectives. LOLIf you know English and some grammar then it is your thing. Don't try to read books like this if you are an amateur, because it can make you bored and maybe even mislead you in some way. You need some basic backbone-like knowledge about English before starting something like this.I've found a lot of questions with their simple answers and key to those dilemmas that I had in my mind for a long time in this book and thanked Mr Raymond Murphy in my head during reading this book more than a hundred times.8.2/10
