I've been completely fascinated by the Dakota since I first watched the movie Rosemary's Baby many years ago. I have a collection of books on the Dakota, and this one didn't disappoint. It was written in 1979, which I loved, so you get a real sense of life in the Dakota before the internet age.To be fair, the books starts to wane toward the end, but I still loved every minute of it, the details, the vignettes, and the descriptions are fantastic. This book is a truly important snapshot in time of one of the most iconic and enduring buildings in New York City.
