Cachalotby Alan Dean Foster features an ocean planet sanctuary for cetaceans after humans nearly drove them to extinction. When floating human settlements mysteriously disappear, xenologist Pip Ryder investigates, uncovering the complex intelligence of the planet's marine inhabitants.Foster delivers a fascinating premise with skilled execution in Cachalot. The ocean world setting provides an excellent backdrop for both environmental themes and adventure. The author excels at creating believable cetacean cultures with well-realised dolphin characters that avoid simple anthropomorphism. While human characters could be stronger, the immersive world-building and intriguing mystery more than compensate. A conceptually strong 1980s sci-fi novel that succeeds in both ideas and storytelling.
