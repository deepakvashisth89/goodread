Little Miss Wise is beyond her years. She can also see what others are about and she doesn’t take part which means she is constantly telling people no so she can stay out of trouble. She begins to feel sad and lonely and decides to say yes anyway to whatever wacky thing people around her say. She meets Mr. Mischief and he gives her a present. Instead of saying no, knowing it won’t be good, she accepts the gift and it’s full of sneezing powder and she sneezes the rest of the day.It’s an interesting lesson. I guess the moral is sometimes we have to deal with the inconvenience of people or risk living all alone. That’s what I get from it. This one felt more rushed and less finished compared to other’s I’ve read.Again, the nephew didn’t want to read this. He loved that Mr. Mischief gave her sneezing powder and he laughed at her sneezing. He gave it 2 stars for that scene. He only likes the Mr. Men, but we’re going to finish this series before he gets much older. He might hate them all, but I’m going to finish it.
