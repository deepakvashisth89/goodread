I picked this Hardy Boys book up to read next because it had on the front what I thought was a knight in a suit of armor. I love medieval castle mysteries! Alas, this book was not about castles or knights. It all began with a coincidence- one of the boys found an ancient memoir sort of very old book in a dusty old bookshop. It was written by a pirate long ago. This led the Hardy Boys into the Canadian woods following smugglers and eventually on a pirate ship that docked on a forbidden island where if someone ever sees the island, they must stay forever. What I thought was a knight in armor was a guard on the island.There was a lot of action but the story sort of hopped around and the boys covered a lot of territory.
