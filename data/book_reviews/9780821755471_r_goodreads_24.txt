I have reread this wonderful story over and over. Dani and Shane are wonderfully thought out characters. As you travel from Tibet to the US to the Caribbean to Puget Sound to Washington DC, the adventure follows this pair.Dani is a textile expert, who by touch can tell a material and the age by touch. Shane is a security specialist that has taken a vow of no sex for a year. When he saves Dani and travels with her to get back to the US, he has to try and tame his baser instincts to keep his vow. There is action and love and suspense.All in all this is a great read and wonderful book. I loved the characters and their personalities. I always look forward to books by Elizabeth.
