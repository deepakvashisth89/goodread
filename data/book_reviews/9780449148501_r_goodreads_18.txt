Another George and Molly Palmer Jones mystery. Molly's Aunt Ursula is found dead in her farm house in Westmoreland when Molly and George arrived for a visit. Soon they realised that her death wasn't due to natural causes. There was a poisoned buzzard, two poisoned cats and some poisoned eggs. As well, there's an MP with a lot to loose, a daughter who is on the edge, a disabled child, a man wanting revenge and a Momma's boy. Throw it all together and it feels a bit overcooked.
