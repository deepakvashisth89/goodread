100% engaging. This is one of those books that you feel more human for having read.What the plot may lack in scope, the writing makes up for tenfold with tender and true insights into pain, hope, vanity and prosaic life. It's a true, true, true book, that beats with an honest heart. You get to love the narrator in the very fact that the narrator is open about her love for the characters. this book is a treasure, in all its homely ruggedness and sometimes shocking, but inevitable events. It's not so much the story, but how it is told. full of hearty truths and simple thinking. Smart and substantive. Dang, I just liked it so much!!!!
