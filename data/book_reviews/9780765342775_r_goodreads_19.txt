I read this as a teenager and loved it. Many years later, I have read it again and I didn’t really enjoy it. The story has an interesting premise but the concept of necromancers is incorrect. The characters are pretty much all unlikeable, especially the main character who is absolutely pathetic and either lets herself be abused by her family or her friends. She makes no effort to save herself or stick up for herself. The relationship is gross. There are many details skipped over or not explained well.
