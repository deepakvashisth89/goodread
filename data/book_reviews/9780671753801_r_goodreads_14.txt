4.5 StarsEven though I've only read two of his books (This and Lonesome Dove) I feel that I can confidently say that Larry McMurtry is my favourite author. I don't think I have ever read anyone else that writes characters as well as McMurtry. Everyone feels so real and completely fleshed out. The last twenty pages of this had me on the verge of tears.Going to have to pick up Streets of Laredo soon!
