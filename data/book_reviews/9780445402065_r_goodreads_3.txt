Dick Francis... those who know him and love him are aware that he likes to write crime novels with horse racing as a theme.Did you know he was a steeplechase jockey too?Dick was born in the United Kingdom in 1920 and from the beginning Horses were in his blood.This book, The Sport of Queens, is about his life journey as a jockey.I have a good friend who is a big Dick Francis fan, so I promised to read at least one of his suspense novels just to see if I might like him too.My Dick Francis reading plan took off when I found that he wrote his autobiography.Great place to start, I thought, and I was right.Some of the book is full of racing information. Unfortunately, somewhat dull. But there was so much more that made me smile.. like his learning to ride at the age of 5 on a donkey and his experiences coming to America to run in the International Steeplechase. The chapter 'America' describes this last experience and it was entertaining to read an Englishman's view of horse racing in America.. oh so different than he has in England.So now I have met Dick Francis. My next step is to read one of his many racing crime novels.
