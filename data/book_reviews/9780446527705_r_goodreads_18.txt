Goodfellas was an amazing movie. In the movie Henry Hill didn’t come across as bad as he truly was in the book. He obviously didn’t care about anyone but himself. He subjected his kids to horrible situations & people. I can’t understand how Karen Hill didn’t take those kids & get out of that terrible marriage. I give both Gina & Gregg so much credit for being able to move on and live normal lives. Their childhood was stolen from them. I finished this book in 5 days. Definitely an interesting read.
