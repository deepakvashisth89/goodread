I'm not really sure I know where to start. I found this book unbelievable and unbelievably tedious.

It tries to fit in too many peripheral things and it is then almost like Grant gets bored with them and she drops the idea.

For me the writing sank to the bottom with a description of Alix's underwear. But it was pretty damn close in other "space-fillers" (of which a digression on which countries - the former Axis powers, according to Alix - produce the best fashion).

As for the characters, given the possible interesting back-stories from each of them, they were too flat, wooden, boring, self-absorbed: sorry (in case you haven't got the message!), they just did not work for me. People who made good and are too full of what they are, what they want don't really do it for me.

While I finished the book, I'm not sure why. It was a struggle and not worth the effort.
