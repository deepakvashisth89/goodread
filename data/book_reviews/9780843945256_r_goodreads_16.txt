Wetbones seems to have everything I would want. Well written prose, a range of interesting characters, splatterpunk meets cosmic horror and an uncanny amount of gore and gruesome killings, yet it didn't quite click for me.At times I was thinking, fuck yeah! Others, I'm scratching my head trying to keep straight all the characters that have been introduced and how they all relate to one another and the overall story.Shirley is an author whose prose at times cuts right to the bone, both literally and figuratively, especially when he's writing about drug and alcohol use/abuse and the many layers of living with addiction.There are so many ideas, so many perspectives, and too many competing plot lines that, more often than not, took me out rather than pulled me in.
