Insightful first-hand experiences of life in the Eastern Arctic from the 1930s to late 1970s. You take away an appreciation for the role HBC and the RCMP had in the North during this time. Both the HBC and RCMP still do not receive proper recognition for their stewardship and leadership in the North.

While the first-hand experiences are terrific, I found the writing and manner in which the stories are recollected to be disappointing. At times the writing distracts from the experiences being retold.

Mr Lyall clearly served the people of the North and had a tremendous impact on improving the North. A selfless humble servant who deserves to be remembered.
