This is book two in the Lord Tedric series. Conceptually it has an interesting plot. The Terran Empire has declined in power and the elite rich, mostly personified in the Carey family have been partly the cause as they solidify their own wealth and power. The protagonist have turned space pirates to try and thwart their power.Unfortunately the book is poorly executed. There's not a lot of character development. There are times when the plot is held together by the flimsiest of circumstance and coincidence.
