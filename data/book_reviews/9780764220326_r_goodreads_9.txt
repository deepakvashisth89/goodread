**Update: see what I have written in my update to the first book in the series. This book did not survive round two of the "purge" from my personal collection.**

I still really loved the book for all the reasons that I mentioned in my review of the first in the series.

The reason that I rated this book one star lower is probably partially my fault. I think I read the last half of the book too quickly and because of that I missed connecting details that would make the sudden jumps (or what seemed like sudden jumps to me) actually make sense. The last fifty pages or so moved so quickly, the core of the plot seemed a little bit lost. Additionally, it felt like there were too many villains in this book, villains that had completely separate purposes that just happened to affect Monte and Abbie. At the conclusion of the book, I managed to figure things out.

The previous paragraph aside, I have to repeat that I still love this book, the whole series. There's just so much that Heitzmann crams into such a short book. I wish she had added an extra hundred pages. (Perhaps then a lot of the confusion would have disappeared.)
