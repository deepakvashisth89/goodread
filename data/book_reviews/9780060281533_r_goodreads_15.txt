What a cute little book. This was such a different book by Gail Carson Levine. I adored the buddies of the orphanage. How sweet! I was afraid the orphanage would be horrible. Sure, the superintendent of the orphanage was awful, but the kids were so sweet. I loved Solly and his bird. What great characters. And Irma Lee and her mama were so sweet and caring. As always, Levine has written about some beautiful characters and the ending is quite satisfying.
