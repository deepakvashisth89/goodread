This book is about 3 girls from California that go to Florida to compete in a surfing contest. The book is about the problems they have with boys, the local surfers. The girls all like James which causes all kinds of interesting things. Then a hurricane comes in and James decides to go ride the surf which is dangerous. The girls were good friends to start, but you will have to read the book to find out if they are still friends and who wins the surfing contest.
