STORY TIME!When I was young, maybe first grade, I grabbed this book from the library because of the pretty cover and the crown. I started reading it, but it gave me horrific nightmares, and my parents had to take it away from me, so I never finished it. And now, I finally got to see how it ended. TAKE THAT ROBERT C O'BRIEN!!!I will say this book is intense for children (kidnapping, murder, arson, running away from men with guns), so it's no wonder it scared me so badly. And also, the plot was pretty stupid and didn't make much sense.
