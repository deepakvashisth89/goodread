Three things I pondered reading this magical book as a trinitarian monotheist (who just read 'Living With Other Creatures' by Richard Bauckham).1. I love naturalists. This is the absolute best of what it means to be a scientist - observation and delight over creatures and things.2. Although Goodall and I do not quite share an anthropology (but I think we may share a belief in the immortality of the spirit), I am committed to the idea of being a fellow creature with other created things.3. I am more convinced than ever of the need for Christian ethologies that are not dependent on Aristotelian ideas about the rational faculties belonging to only to humankind.
