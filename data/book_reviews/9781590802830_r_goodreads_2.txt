Crossing the Meadow is the equivalent of walking into the light following death. In this case, the dead who find themselves still on earth are stuck here till they finish whatever business needed closure that they didn't get in their lives. Once that is done, they are able to cross the meadow. I ordinarily like stories like this a lot, but i found this one a little too involved. I think it would have been better as a simple fable. Still, it wasn't a bad story. The characters were good and the ending was satisfying.
