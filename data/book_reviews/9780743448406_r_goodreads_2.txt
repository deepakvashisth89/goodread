As with other books in the Star Trek: Deep Space Nine season 8 series, Unity is too long. I really don't understand why the author and editor would sacrifice the fast pace typical of an action-adeventure book just to make it 300 instead of 200 pages long. The first 60 pages of the novel were just characters catching up to events that the readers already knew from previous books. I guess this is the price to pay when you try to write a long book with little ideas. I mean, reading for the n-th time how every character is feeling (unchanged for the last few books, anyway) was extremely tiresome.
