Discovering unique creative and inspired capacities through imaginationAuthor/healer/teacher of metaphysics/musician Linda Keen is the founder of Mens & Intuitie (People & Intuition) school for energy awareness and intuitive healing in Europe, and having returned to the US she continues to write, teach and appear on national television and radio, encouraging her mission - ‘Integration of all that we know and can accomplish.’ Her books to date - THE DREAM KEEPERS, JOHN LENNON IN HEAVEN, SHOVING OFF FROM THE SHORE, and INTUITION MAGIC.‘Intuition’ – we all know we have it, but what exactly is it? The dictionary defines it as ‘the power or faculty of knowing things without conscious reasoning.’ Yet in this sensitive book, Keen explores the ‘term’ by sharing her awareness experiences on how she developed her intuition and then provides an experiential workshop on how to unveil the spiritual aspects of our world ‘in a simple and playful manner’ – the Intuition Magic Workshop! The personal one-on-one feeling she manages to achieve, sharing how to develop perceptive imagination, visualization and transformation, acknowledge fear and negativity, and even Chakra, opens portals to truly gaining intuition, widening channels of communication with spiritual beings, and enriching our lives. This book makes a difference – read and grow! Very highly recommended
