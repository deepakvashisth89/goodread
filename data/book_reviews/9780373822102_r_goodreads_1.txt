This was a nice sweet read. h/H meet when heroine wins a boat which the hero and his company has built. The hero is in town to find his stepmother niece. h/H spend time together mainly at the hero's behest as the heroine is busy running her cafe and is generally wary of gossip and men after her horrid divorce. This was a sweet romance. The hero feels this instant connection with the heroine and wants to pursue her. He is open about his feelings and even asks her to marry him while she is gun-shy. Even the mystery of the missing niece is resolved. A nice read.
