Zipes makes a lot of interesting and useful points but so much of this is boring. He spends most of his time saying every other scholar of fairy tales is wrong because their perspective isn't historically-based German Marxist enough, and I just really did not care for that.He also did not have a very strong "why do we care?" aspect to his argument, so I didn't. Care, that is. Because what am I supposed to do with an historically-based German Marxist perspective on fairy tales? Not much. I agree heartily that historical context is important in understanding works, especially ones with such long histories, but it isn't the be all end all of looking at a piece of literature.
