This is the most hilarious and society critical Beck book till now. And we get a very interesting insight about how justice really works. Not a pretty one.This time around Sjöwall & Wahlöö are even more sarcastic than usual. Especially about how the police itself is organized and how incompetent most higher management is. For instance in chapter 10, three pages are devoted to this. But all over the book there are satirical remarks about how the police is run.Remarks like:"In this country people only need to be prosecuted and then they are convicted, no matter how innocent they are."speak volumes. This could be the very epitath for the main culprit who gets the blame for everything.Also criticism of society in general is more prevalent than in previous books. Actually it seems they really don't have a high opinion of people in general. But it's done in a matter of fact way. All woven in, in the current circumstances of the story.All this is described with very many understated witty observations.Three story lines:- Beck and the Locked Room mystery.- The special bank robber investigation team, with Einar Rönn, Kollberg, Gunvald Larsson and with a hilarious over the top leader, nicknamed Bulldozer Olsen, public prosecuter.- The bank robbers themselves.The bank robbery investigation is more a Keystone Cops slapstick story than an actual police procedure story. It is the most hilarious of all the books till now. Everything that can go wrong, goes wrong.The Martin Beck story line on the other hand, gives us a good old police procedure. It gets not much space in tbe book, but we are getting to see a more pleasant side of Beck.He meets someone who actually makes him happy!Besides the Beck investigation, which gives the book it's tittle, the bank robbery part gets the most attention.A very entertaining book.written: 1972.Maj Sjöwall: 1935-2000Per Wahlöö: 1926-1975
