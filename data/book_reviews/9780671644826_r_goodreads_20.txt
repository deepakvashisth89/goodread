I decided to read Deviant after watching Netflix’s new Ed Gein special. As a true crime nerd, I was sitting there with my jaw on the floor over some of the wild ‘facts’ they dropped-then I read this book and realized Netflix was basically playing historical fanfic with real victims lives. Harold Schechter absolutely ate with this one. His research is next-level, and the way he tells the story makes it feel both horrifying and fascinating without ever losing that true-crime grit. I came out of it actually understanding who Ed Gein was, not the over-dramatized version we get on TV. Moral of the story? Trust Harold Schechter, not Netflix
