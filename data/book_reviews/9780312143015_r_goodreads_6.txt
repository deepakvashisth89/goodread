Interesting mystery set in Bisbee (called Dudley in the book), Arizona. I recognized many things in the book from being here in Bisbee. Attended a reading by and discussion with the author at the Bisbee library on 4/18/2017. Very interesting. Betsy is considering audio books. I'd like to hear her read some of her mysteries.This the first book she wrote that was published and is part of a "series" of books about Chloe Newcombe. It was a "page turner", which held my attention. I did get a little confused near the end, but things got resolved at the very end. There is actually a prequel to this book/series--"Dream Queen".
