Intriguing murder mysteries, so I'm glad I didn't abandon the book when the beginning was graphically gruesome. The characters are interesting and the writing is excellent, making me feel like I was in the room ... thus the hesitation based on being in the room where the murder had taken place and in the autopsy room. And so, I recommend these novels to all those who don't mind reading gruesome details, and even to those who don't want to read those things, 'cause you can skip ahead and enjoy the detective work.
