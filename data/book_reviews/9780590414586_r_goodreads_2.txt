I really enjoyed this book! It's use of Smokey Mountain English is superb. It has a satisfying and open ending where the writer doesn't wrap up everything to the point of cheesiness but leaves a few points open to the readers imagination. The cover of this book is terrible. The artist obviously didn't read it long enough to know it takes place in the 1920's since the characters are dressed in modern clothing. But I can't complain too much since it was the 1980's and book covers have evolved away from the life-like character illustrations. I liked the book enough to keep it and rate it very high. The characters are fun and come to life and it's a wonderful book for kids!
