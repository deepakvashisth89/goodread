An amazing, memorable book about Jody, a young boy, who over the course of one year, has to grow up and face the harsh realities of life.

This book takes place in Central Florida in the 1870’s. Jody, his father, Penny and his mother, Ora, are making by on their farm, but just. So much happens over the coming year- bear and wolf attacks, flooding, snake bites and animosities and friction with your nearest neighbors. But also, the love and attachment to a fawn.

Imagine living with no other companion but your parents- A boy needs a special friend and Flag, the yearling becomes his. The beauty of this book was not Jody’s bond with the fawn but the love between Jody and his father. Penny was priceless. He loved to tell his tales; he loved to share his love and knowledge of nature with his son.

The survival of the fittest and the ones who plowed ahead despite adversity seems to be a moral here, as well as finding the joy in the beauty around you. The descriptions are so beautifully drawn whether it be of the surroundings or even the food, it had me totally immersed. I am craving some cornbread after reading this book:)

I listened to this book, read by Tom Stechschulte, who was absolutely perfect.

A definite masterpiece!
