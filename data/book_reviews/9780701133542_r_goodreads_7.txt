I'm all for putting new wine in old bottles, especially if the new wine makes the old bottles explode."Angela Carter wrote in 1983.This is her most comic, orgiastic farce, a celebration of life, of theater, Carter's most joyous and playful work and she wrote it knowing she's going to die young and soon. Go figure.The story about legacy, family and loving life just as it is, with all its imperfections. Smart, exuberant and fun fun fun! Never take yourself too seriously. That's why I love her.The last line is: "What a joy it is to dance and sing!"P.S. This book is brimming with references to Shakespeare, his plays, his life. Unfortunately, yours truly knows nothing of his works and life and I suspect in will highly enhance your experience if you know more than me (which is really easy to achieve). On the other hand I'm the proof that this book can be enjoyed by itself.
