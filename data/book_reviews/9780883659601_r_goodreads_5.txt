Well, this flipping ruled.The idea is obvious: a compilation of The 637 Best Things Anybody Ever Said series (in case you were wondering where the 2,548 came from, I don't know where the 637 number was settled on).Sampling:"There are two million interesting people in New York and only seventy-eight in Los Angeles" - Neil Simon"Cats are intended to teach us that not everything in nature has a function" - Garrison Keillor"I don't eat snails, I perfer fast food" - Strange de JimYEAH. This is pretty awesome. Obviously, as with most quotes book especially one of this size you're going to get some misses here and there but you're also going to get some terrific line along the way. And the latter greatly outweight the former here.An index is an added bonus in a book of this size (I can go straight to the Woody Allen quotes!) too.My only problem is that the book would have greatly been improved by sections of quotes on the same subject (marriage, children, jobs). The book tends to lump small section together but not in a straight, liner section by section way.Really ****1/2 but I'll go the full 5-stars on the rating. Buy this. Make your friends think you're witty.
