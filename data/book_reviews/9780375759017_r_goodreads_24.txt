"On the verge of a decision we all tremble: hope pauses with fluttering wings."I know this book was shocking for its time, but I can't help wishing George Elliot had really gone for it with this story. I wanted the shocking elements to stick and have lasting consequences. I wanted the arresting drama to happen where I could see it rather than offstage.
