King Solomon's Mines was published in 1885 in England and is generally recognized as the first great lost civilization adventure novel. Haggard wrote quite a few other novels featuring Allan Quatermain and many, many more that are set on the African continent. He was one of the most popular adventure writers of all time, and also his own time. His attitudes about colonialism and ethnicity reflect the 19th century beliefs that were accepted as truth during Haggard's lifetime. The pacing seems a little slow and the dialog a little odd, but it's a really exciting tale when the reader gets into the rhythm of the text. It's a terrific historical adventure.
