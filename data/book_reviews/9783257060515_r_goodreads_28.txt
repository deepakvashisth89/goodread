An enjoyable police procedural with the added attraction of Venice as its setting. The pace was steady except for the race to the conclusion of the case. I learned how cosseted the US military are when stationed abroad, with all their home comforts provided no matter in which country they are stationed, eg burger joints, coffee shops, etc. I’m not criticising so no backlash, please ;). I’d like to read the next in this series but it seems to be difficult to find unless you’re willing to pay silly money.
