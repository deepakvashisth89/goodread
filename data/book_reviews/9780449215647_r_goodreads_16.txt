I loved this book! Set in Austria, featuring the Spanish Riding School in Vienna, a delightful heroine and her husband who has an air of mystery about him. It's one of my favourite romantic tropes: too married people who find trust in their romance once secrets are unravelled and this is a humorous delight. I loved the relationship between Lewis and Vanessa and I LOVED the innuendo. Stewart excels at closed door scenes expertly placed and this was just wonderful.

Aces, I tell you!

Also, Austrian set romances are my jam.
