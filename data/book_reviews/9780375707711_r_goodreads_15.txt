Definitely one of the better Scarpetta novels. A rich mix of forensic science, gruesome murders and drama, drama, drama. I was reminded how much I hate waiting until the end of the book to meet the killer.Scarpetta and Merino are called to check out a cargo ship container. Sealed inside is a corpse wearing Armani shoes next to the wordsbon voyage, le loup-garou.Back at the morgue, Kay can't seem to find anything. Everything from lunches to equipment to confiscated drugs have been going missing, and she suspects it's someone on her team. Meanwhile, Merino has been busted down to street patrol after new police chief Diane Bray "cleans house." She doesn't like Merino or his close friendship with Scarpetta. Officer Anderson, Bray's snitch, turns up everywhere Kay and Merino go. Killings similar to the shipping container case start cropping up around Richmond. Kay's niece Lucy goes undercover on a DEA case in Miami, but the sting goes sideways and Lucy's girlfriend is badly hurt.The mood of this one is decidedly maudlin, with Scarpetta grieving over the pre-novel death ofWestley Benton. He seems to have been killed in the line of duty while working in England.His absence is constantly felt since Kay, Merino and Lucy always bring him up.
