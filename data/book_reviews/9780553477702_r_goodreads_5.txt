I am not impressed, Ms. Steel. Sorry. This is the first Danielle Steel book I have read, and I guess I had maybe too hight of expectations for such a well-known and prolific author. It was different from some of the other romance books I have read, in that it let you know kind of what each character was feeling or thinking when she wrote about them - even small side characters. She also moved it at a pretty quick pace, but at the same time, you weren't really sure exactly where it was going after about halfway through - after the guy got the girl and got engaged. I mean, you know it's heading for a wedding, because of the title. I guess what made her a little different is it was more of a character study of all the different characters in the book, and not as much about a plot, per say. It was a snapshot of someone's life at a particular moment. No real huge obstacles to overcome (other than emotionally), and the ending was pretty anticlimactic. You finish the last sentence, turn the page and go, "that's it?" So, yeah, not super impressed. And I thought her books had love scenes, too. But, no. Nothing more than a "they made love until morning". That's all you get, folks. Why did her reputation lead me to believe otherwise? Shows what I get for going into a book with expectations. Guess I'll go cleanse my pallet with an author that I love....
