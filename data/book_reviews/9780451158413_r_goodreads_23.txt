Filled with the action, dialogue, and characters that McBain does so well, this is a tough one to read. It's emotionally tough because of the loss of someone loved by one of the detectives.It's politically tough because it's set in the days when we women had even fewer rights than we have now. One of those rights that is currently threatened becomes a focal point in the book. McBain shows very well some of the dangers of losing this one.
