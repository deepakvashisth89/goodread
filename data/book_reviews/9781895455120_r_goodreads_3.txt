This was the first Jean Pare book I bought when I was flying solo. I figured "light" would also translate to cheaper, since it probably didn't have as much meat, and I was correct. I ended up picking up a lot of the Company's Coming books over the years to teach myself how to cook. Like most books by Ms. Paré, this one has a few I go back to time after time: "Carrot Magic," "Gingersnaps," "Chili," "Lean Loaf," "Meat Patties," and "Stir-Fry Meal," all of which are aimed with an eye to the light side of food (and thus a bit cheaper).Fun-fact: Paré is almost always wearing the same colour shirt in the author/cook photo as the colour of the book binding.
