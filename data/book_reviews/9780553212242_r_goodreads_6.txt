The Hunchback of Notre Dame is a timeless masterpiece that weaves together rich, vibrant characters and breathtaking descriptions of Paris. Victor Hugo's storytelling is both poetic and powerful, drawing readers deep into the heart of a tragic yet beautiful tale. The characters, especially Quasimodo and Esmeralda, are unforgettable, evoking both sympathy and admiration. With its mix of romance, mystery, and social commentary, this novel is a truly captivating experience that resonates long after the final page.
