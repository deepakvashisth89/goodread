I know this is a children’s book… But really? Forcing monkeys, who were vibing in their forest & wanted nothing to do with this, to come out of a cake dancing as a birthday gift? And no one thinks that’s unnecessarily elaborate? Or that the love you share matters more than the (ridiculous) extravagance of a gift? I don’t love telling young kids that you need to “beat” your friend’s loved ones by getting them the craziest gift of all.The ending was rather unsatisfying as well, ending one of the (too many) plotlines in an odd way.In this second to last Oz book the ideas were clearly running out (as much as it pains me to say that about these characters whom I’ve grown to care for).
