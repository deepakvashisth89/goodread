So well written, descriptive, and historically richWith just enough historical evidence to work with, this author has developed a charming, intriguing love story between two strong eccentric oft-portrayed legends with detailed, believable descriptions that came to life in the most legitimate characters I have yet discovered. Building an unknown individual out of rumor and supposition into a unique,lovable, courageous and honorable boy to a man of character, intelligence and bravery is accomplished by sharing the trials and tribulations of his harrowing experiences to establish his connections to his royal mother and famous father. Emotionally well done and a detailed description of the wars and intrigue of that time.
