I am not sure if this review should be here because I didnt read the book. This was an audio book.Over Christmas we would be doing many Motorway miles and so I searched for an audio book to help while away the driving hours. This was not the first time that I had done this. The first time was many years ago. Then, we chose an audio book from the library very much at random and that introduced me to Deryn Lake who became a firm favourite and whose entire catalogue both my wife and I later read.This time, I had more time to choose and I picked a title that was already on my shelf to read. What an excellent choice this was.This is my first encounter with this author and I was soon spellbound by his storytelling. Had I been reading this book as usual, I wthink that I would have started a character map to keep track of the people involved, especially as I am not brilliant at remembering foreign names . May be this would have helped me see a few of the twists in the very cleverly worked plot around a subject matter that was handled with sensitivity.Just when you think that the story is over, the author releases another little secret and then finally another.A great story and one that has me seeking out more by this author.
