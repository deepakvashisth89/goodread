Front seat to the end of the world by mother earth.I enjoy James Herbert books because you're always going to be treated to some graphic way of dying and some horrific wounds that make you wince as you read the victim screaming in pain.This story is about how the world is messed up and it's our fault and now we are all dying from it as it transforms itself.I liked the main character Rivers but felt his story with the "badguy of the book" was kinda shoved in between detailing of the world exploding in different places.Nothing incredible but enjoyable enough.
