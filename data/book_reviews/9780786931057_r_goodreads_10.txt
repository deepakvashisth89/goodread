This book was honestly a mess, but I’m hyperfixating hard right now so I’m giving it two stars. The pacing was ok and I liked most of the characters (except for Elminster and Kelemvor). Overall I expected cheap fantasy slop, and I got cheap fantasy slop. But hey, that’s exactly what I ordered. I mean, just look at that abomination of a cover - if it didn’t ward me off this novel, nothing could.Characters:Adon, my beloved. There are only two constants when you’re playing a cleric - abandoning/changing your god and taking war caster as your first feat.Kelemvor, remember when you had suicidal thoughts after the town found out about your curse?
