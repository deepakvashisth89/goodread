The 24th Inspector Maigret book from 1944 still delivers a good puzzle of intrigue and murder as he can somehow see through people to the truth. A country house is the setting Examining Magistrate Brejon sends him to in service of Brejon's married sister. Maigret is not pleased to arrive on the same train as one of his former employees who is now a PI. He finds a young boy to show him the ropes of the town, providing some humour.Penguin Classic paperbackLibrary Loan
