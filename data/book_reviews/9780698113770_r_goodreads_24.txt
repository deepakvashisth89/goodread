I see why teachers would choose this book for character analysis and as a solid classroom read. I feel like Ms. Paver’s “Chronicles of Ancient Darkness”did it better, but that book series was more mystical so maybe not what teachers are looking for with this topic.This story of Tao’s coming of age, his journey to follow his dream of painting caves like his idol Greybeard, and his wolf-dog Ram was very sweet. It is a perfect one for 5th-6th grade readers.
