I read this book shortly after it appeared about a decade ago and to be honest I can't remember much about it beyond: a) Lazarus is clearly a major-league asshole, b) he harbors a bizarre, almost irrational, hatred of Jude Alex Kozinski, and c) he had little respect for the intellectual abilities of Justice Harry Blackmun, for whom Lazarus clerked.Lazarus does deserve credit for being an early proponent of what, in light of Linda Greenhouse's subsequent bookBecoming Justice Blackmun, has come to be the conventional wisdom that Justice Blackmun was a total mediocrity.
