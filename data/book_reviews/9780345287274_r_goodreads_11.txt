Another nearly forgotten gem from Thorne Smith.
Hector Owen is bored, disillusioned and bullied by life as he takes shelter from the rain in a doorway. Without warning, the door opens and he's pulled through into the most unusual department store and into a new life.
A life where inhibitions vanish and dreams come true. Encountering characters reminiscent of a Marx Brothers script, it's a luminous, ribald yet tender tale of an average man who finds a chance to do what he wants for a change.
