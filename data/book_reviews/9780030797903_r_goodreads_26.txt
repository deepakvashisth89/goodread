19th Century diction, and The Romantic Style, in general, are rough on my 20th Century ears, but Hawthorne's themes -- intolerance, persecution, and guilt -- are presented as eternal, and his depictions ring even truer today. I was repeatedly struck by how little we've changed since The Puritans, and easily his tales would translate in a contemporary writer's hands. The best of this collection is immortal, the worst is still interesting, if a tedious and repetitive read.
