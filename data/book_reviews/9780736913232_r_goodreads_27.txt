4.5 starsI found it hard to get into this book initially, but once I saw how the characters related to others from the series, I did enjoy it. There were a lot of ups and downs in this, a lot of twists and turns which I enjoyed. It was tense on times but other times quite funny & lighthearted. I did feel though that it jumped quite a bit, 2 months, then a few years and more towards the end, but overall a nice read. I liked the character developments in this for both Stacey and Tanner. I own this on kindle.
