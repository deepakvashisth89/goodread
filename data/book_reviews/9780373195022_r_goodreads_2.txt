4 Stars! ~ Megan was an O’Bannon by marriage, and next to her seven-year-old daughter, Kara, there was nothing more important to her than being part of that great family. When her husband Brad died, the O’Bannon’s helped her turn a run down Victorian home into a B & B, and this year Megan decided to host the O’Bannon family reunion as her way of saying thank you. Tyler O’Bannon was a very distant cousin; he’d been taken in by the California O’Bannon’s when he became an orphaned teenager, but he’d never felt like he belonged. The first time Tyler had met Megan was at her engagement party to Brad, and he knew instantly that she was marrying the wrong man. That was nine years ago and the last time he’d been to an O’Bannon function. So when he shows up at the reunion, Megan is surprised and very uncomfortable because Tyler can still make her heart race. But Megan believes she’s a failure at marriage, at being a desirable woman and she knows that she’d never be able to hang on to someone like Tyler.

This is one of those feel good stories that doesn’t really hold any surprises. There’s a world of hurt in both Megan and Tyler. Megan coaxes Tyler into the family instead of standing on the outside. And Tyler has to show Megan that she is a woman made for marriage and love. Kara and the rest of the O’Bannon clan are great supporters, and help bring life to this wonderfully sweet romance. This HEA brings a big big smile.
