I recently re-read C.S.Lewis's Mere Christianity, which was originally published in 1952, and before that was a BBC radio broadcast during WWII. Re-reading it again brought back so many great memories of reading it for the first time about 12 years ago when I was still an agnostic. This was the book which led to a gradual conversion to Christianity. I had quite a lot questions, and I certainly was not going to believe in a worldview simply because I was raised in The Church. I was very inquisitive, wanted to know for myself, and was sincerely searching for truth and meaning.The way Lewis brought together history, reason, imagination, faith, and one's own personal experience, was quite unique to me. The Irish literary scholar and lay theologian's writings helped save my sanity, changed my life, led me to many other writers, gave me a passion for writing, literature, and art, helped me understand and appreciate people of different worldviews, and through his influence I have come to have an amazing community, and life changing experiences I marvel at.I thank God that He gave the world this incredible writer and thinker, and I thank Mr. Grant Hudson for giving me the opportunity to write about how this man's writings changed my life in Surprised by Agape."If I find in myself a desire which no experience in this world can satisfy, the most probable explanation is that I was made for another world. If none of my earthly pleasures satisfy it, that does not prove that the universe is a fraud. Probably earthly pleasures were never meant to satisfy it, but only to arouse it, to suggest the real thing. If that is so, I must take care, on the one hand, never to despise, or to be unthankful for, these earthly blessings, and on the other, never to mistake them for the something else of which they are only a kind of copy, or echo, or mirage. I must keep alive in myself the desire for my true country, which I shall not find till after death; I must never let it get snowed under or turned aside; I must make it the main object of life to press on to that country and to help others to do the same.”-C.S.Lewis
