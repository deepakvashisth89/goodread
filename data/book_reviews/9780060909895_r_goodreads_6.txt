I had the honor and privilege of meeting Ms. Brooks at a reading she gave at a local college in 1992. She was the first honest-to-God poet I ever met. She was personable and down-to-Earth and, in her mid-to-late 70's at the time, as sharp as the proverbial knife. And she proceeded to give an hour+ reading which left everybody in that room a true-blue fan of hers.Brooks' poetry has a delightfully strange quality about it: at once gentle and powerful, simultaneously spare and voluminous. She says more in a few lines than many novelists say in doorstop-thick tomes.There are breathing characters and multi-level worlds contained in this verse. If "We Real Cool" is the only poem of Brooks' you know, then treat yourself and give this fine overview of her work a try.
