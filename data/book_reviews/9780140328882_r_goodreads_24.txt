7 short stories/novellas by Roald Dahl.The Boy who talked with Animals. a giant sea turtle is caught and dragged to shore on a Jamaican beach. a crowd of pretentious British tourists soon gathers and the turtle bargained over—its shell claimed by one, its flesh reserved for soup. a young boy arrives and, wrapping his arms around the creature, hysterically demands its release. the father explains that the boy can talk to animals, and offers a generous amount of money for the turtle to be freed. the next day the boy goes missing and a small figure astride a large sea turtle is seen far out at sea....3 stars.The Hitchhiker. a pompous little rat of a man is picked up by the narrator, and soon goads him to test the speed of his car. a policeman quickly pulls them over and leisurely, maliciously writes up an exorbitant ticket and takes down their information in a little notebook. as they drive away, the hitchhiker reveals that he is a masterful pick-pocket...a dislikable man who may get you into trouble, but at least will get you out of it. 4 stars.The Mildenhall Treasure. one of Dahl's few non-fiction tales. a large quantity of Roman silver found in the soil, a greedy farmer who recognizes its value, and the poor tiller who finally ends up with his fair share. 3 stars.The Swan. two bullies, Ernie and Raymond, have just got a rifle and are impatient to use it. they come across their scrawny schoolmate David and bring him along to fetch the birds that they shoot. down across the pond a swan sits on her nest...David, in tears, tries to stop them, but the bullet hits its mark. as a further desecration, Ernie carefully cuts off the bird's wings and ties them to the small boy's arms. he is made to climb a tree, told to fly... terrible, and sad, and beautiful. 5 stars.The Wonderful Story of Henry Sugar. Henry Sugar is a wealthy, single, middle-aged man who likes to bet and isn't afraid to cheat now and then to win. one rainy day he stumbles upon a small book in a friend's library and is intrigued..."The Man Who Sees Without His Eyes". fascinated, Henry Sugar begins to train. in a mere 3 years time, he can see through the backside of a playing card, and he takes his hard-won talent to the gaming tables. after the first night, however, Henry Sugar is left with a feeling of emptiness...he finds he doesn't want the money, and decides to do something with it. 4 stars.
