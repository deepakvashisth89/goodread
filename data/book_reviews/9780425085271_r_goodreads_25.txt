I really want to love The Talisman. So much so that this is my second time trying to read it, thinking that maybe a new set of circumstances in my life might mean I experience the story differently. But, I still don’t like it. I mean, I really don’t like it.

It only feels like King in the standout, weird, ethereal parts. The Straub voice comes through much louder, and quite honestly, I just find it boring. This book is so boring.

I am fascinate by its premise. I want it to enhance my eternal journey to the Dark Tower, but although it feels adjacent, it feels comparatively lifeless. Jack is such a bad character that following him for nearly 1,000 pages is tortuously arduous.

I felt the same way about Black House the first time I read it. I guess this series just isn’t for me, which is unfortunate given the third one is coming out later this year. Who knows? Maybe I’ll try to make a third run at it.
