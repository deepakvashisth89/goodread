Probably my favorite unfortunate event book when I first read them. the Baudelaire orphans are happy for a brief moment with Montgomery Montgomery a brilliant herpetologist. Happy and hard working just trying to help uncle Monty. But of course count Olaf shows up as Stephano kills Monty and tries to take the children to Peru. How will the children escape Olaf this time.

In the Netflix show uncle Monty is played by Aasif Mondvi who did a great job. The conspiracy subplot is very strong in this episode with Monty knowing Stephano is Olaf but thinking he can outsmart Olaf and failing. In the book Monty is also brilliantly oblivious thinking Stephano is herpetological society spy and thinking he will outsmart him. But of course it's hard to outsmart someone who is willing to kill you.
