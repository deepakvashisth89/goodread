As a reading experience, I'd give this 4 stars. We follow the travels of a young hippie mum and her two young daughters traipsing around 1970(-ish) Morocco.The story is told by the four-year-old daughter, which is part of the charm of the story, but is also the book's limitation. We only get the eyewitness account of the girl and never really know what motivates the adults - and of course, the girl never understands their actions or motives either. She is basically a feather, floating along on the wind created by the adult world over which she has no control.Overall, a fun read, and something to bring along on vacation.
