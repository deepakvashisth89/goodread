One of the most bizarre books I've ever read. I started it so many times, but found it difficult to get going as its language is extremely convoluted and the plot pretty abstract at first. It does really pick up about halfway through and the narrative was actually quite gripping for a while. However, I find Conrad's style laborious and difficult to read - certainly a master of his craft but not necessarily accessible or endearing. His work is usually more interesting to study than to read, and I'm sure this is no exception. I'm looking forward to doing more research, but I can't say I enjoyed reading this.
