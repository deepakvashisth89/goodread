Not only is the book a really great surprise baby story, but it’s also got the added twist of a opposites attract tale, too.
Brett is a self made man; he comes from humble beginnings but he’s worked hard to climb to the top of his field. Laurel comes from wealth and privilege, but has had a giant fall from grace, although she’s hiding it when she and Brett first meet and “marry”.
Each approaches their relationship and the coming blessed event differently. Brett doesn’t want his child to have the stigma of an absentee father as he did growing up. Laurel wants to provide for her child on her own; she doesn’t want to be pampered and sheltered, as that hasn’t worked out well in the past.
While I liked their story, I didn’t feel a lot of chemistry between them. Brett was a bit too pushy and didn’t give Laurel enough credit for trying to break out of the poor-little-rich-girl mold...one he labeled her with from the start.
