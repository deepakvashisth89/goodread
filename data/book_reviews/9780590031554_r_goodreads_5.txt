An imaginative, humorous, fact filed book on the ancient Greeks. The book is filled with wonderful facts that are put into context, made relevant and are exciting to learn about.The humour is “corny” yet catching which is a fantastic way for children to remember facts. The book would be suitable for KS2 and would make for a fantastic cross curricular resource in both English and History.The book could be a great tool to be used also in drama where children could use the facts to create role plays, freeze frames and hot seating.If groovy Greeks is anything to go by, I look forward to reading the collection of horrible history.
