Re The Puppet Master - this is an odd little book. PC's first HP takes place on the Andalusian Coast of Spain, in a tiny village between Marbella and Gibraltar - I am guessing around Esteponia as the Sierra Bermeja is mentioned as being the backdrop for the h's sister's bar.I had to reread that part because the Sierra Bermja is also a mountain range in Puerto Rico, and I had to dig out the map to make sure PC hadn't gotten lost on the travelogue. She actually meant the Los Reales de Sierra Bermeja which is a nice big mountain and nature preserve in Spain. People who do marathon trail running and cyclists who do the Vuelta de Andalucia and the Vuelta de España go up and down the mountain like crazy people. It has some nice trails, which means I have to add it to the world hikes I would like to do bucket list.OK geography lesson over, but really the scenery was almost the most exciting part of the book, only surpassed by the great descriptions of paella the h's brother in law cooks up. The h in this one is an early twenty threeish beautiful exterior hiding a very shy interior person who winds up helping out at her sister and brother in law's slightly illegal Mediterranean bar/restaurant.She had a job in Easbourne, East Sussex as an office receptionist, but wasn't very into it. She wanted to be, and was qualified for, a secretary position but her stunning looks made her employers feel that she should be their front desk person. She wasn't happy about that. She is even more unhappy when she finds out her fiance was cheating on her.He blamed it all on HER, (rule #1 again- it is always the h's fault,) for not sleeping with him before marriage. The h was offended by that, she has morals and she thought that waiting until marriage was the right thing to do. She is very sad and unhappy and her older sister tells her to come on over to Spain and help her and her hubby out by working in their bar. The h gets there and settles in nicely, tho there is some concern on her sister's part that the bar they own will be closed cause nobody filled out any paperwork for a bar license or any other kind of permit.There is a lot of starving cats tho, and the h decides they need to be fed - so she wins on points for feeding homeless kitties and they all sit on the lawn and look like garden cat gnomes. The h is also given to a lot of introspection on why she got cheated on, what her values really are and why are all these kitties starving- doesn't anybody care about homeless pets?Then she is tending the bar by herself and a man walks in. He is a very arrogant Spaniard looking for the h's brother in law. He leaves his name and tells the h to have her BIL contact him. She agrees and goes back to wondering about the kitties.Then her sister and BIL return and when the h mentions the visit, her sister has a hissy fit.Seems that the BIL used to work for the Spaniard as his chef for his very exclusive night club. Then he left to start his own place and the Spaniard is not happy about this. The h's sister says he is vile, he always has to have his own way and he thinks people can be owned. The h is surprised, cause she thought he was kinda hot in an arrogant HP H kinda way. The sister is ranting about the Spaniard and the h offers to take in the weekly earnings from the bar to get out of the line of fire.The parking near the local bank is very full and the h is backing into a space when a little red convertible snips in and takes it. The h is really mad, and the Spaniard just smiles and wanders off into the bank. The h finally finds another spot and is preparing to go inside the bank when she is mugged on the steps by a young man and the Spaniard leaps to the rescue with a rugby tackle.He saves the weekly earnings and as he is giving them back to the h, he asks her out. The h, seeing that the immaculate H got his pants all dirty and his shoes scuffed, reluctantly agrees to meet him for lunch. When her sister has another conniption when the h tells her about the date, the h points out that they sorta owe the H for saving the weekly deposit and she felt bad he got all sartorially messed up .The H and h go to lunch and the H tells her he would like to show her his boat. He says it isn't seaworthy at the moment, but he would like her to see it anyway. The h is picturing some charmingly rusticated little fishing sloop and happily contemplating the sweet idyll of lazy drifting while casting a line in the balmy sea. She gets the big disappointment of the afternoon when the H takes to her to full on yacht, complete with multiple decks, exotic artwork and teak fittings and multiple staterooms.She tells the H she admires it, but it isn't really her thing. The H decides he likes her and he asks her out to a party. She says sure and goes home. There the sister is trying to set the h up with an English guest who is very, very nice - but the h just is not into him. She likes a more macho kinda man, her sister disagrees with her taste. Her sister also has big news about having a baby, the h is happy and the sister/hubby team is happy and the h is going to a party.She goes with the H to a party full of wealthy, sophisticated people. One woman in particular is rude to the h and kind of hangs on the H. The h winds up dancing by herself in a corner, having a good time all on her own, and the H offers her a job at his night club as a dancing act.The h says no, she is too shy to dance on stage - the H mentions that his pianist is ill and he needs a Saturday night act. The h can play piano, she does concerts for retirees and children as volunteer work back home in England. She agrees to cover for the missing act. She and the H have some passionate kissing moments, but the h draws back at going to bed with the H. She goes home instead and then feels bad that she might have led the H on a bit.The sister isn't happy about the h working for the h for one night, but the h points out that the H will then owe them a favor if there is problems with authorities over the illegality of the bar. Her sister has worse news tho, their mummy is coming and apparently the mum is a bit of a handful. (Well if she is still called Mummy by her adult children, I think we can all see why.) The mum shows up later and promptly gets herself lost and when the h finds her, the mum is flirting with the H and drinking beer.The h goes off to play the piano and the H gets angry when the barman flirts with her. Then the mean woman from the party and a famous race car driver come in to nightclub and the h winds up having a drink with them.When the race car guy leaves the table for phone call, the woman starts verbally attacking the h. Then the H shows up and it is obvious from the tension between Angry woman and the H that there is serious bad feelings between them. The h wants to leave and gets ready to go, but the H captures her in a passionate kiss and they wind up in bed.Later on that night, the h has to get home so her sister doesn't call up the cavalry or forget to feed the cats. The H is getting ready to take her back but has to do a few things first. Then the maid comes in and tells the h that the H is using her to make Angry woman jealous.He was engaged once to Angry woman and they broke up when the H slept with the maid (she used to work for Angry woman,) again to make Angry woman jealous. The h is hurt beyond words, she slept with him cause she was in love after 2 dates and one piano interlude. She goes home upset and then has to deal with her angry sister and her mummy.The h refuses to talk to the H when he calls and the sister seems intent on forcing the h into connubial bliss with the English visitor. (Really the sister was a big pain, she obviously had some latent jealousy about the h that the h refuses to see and the sister is determined to pair the h off with a man who is a boring nonentity.)There are Ennnnndldddlesssss lectures about the h's bad taste in men, and the h is busy being sad. ( I personally thought she had a good attitude about men, she learned from her mistakes and she didn't dump her baggage from the past on other people, but the sister and the mum subtly contrive to make the h feel she is in the wrong and so the h spends a lot of time thinking she is messing up everyone else's life, when it was their own faults for not doing things correctly in the first place.)The h tactfully excuses herself from a relationship with the English visitor and then she and the sister are fighting because the bar is now on the authorities' radar, and it might be closed down and the sister blames the h. The sister thinks the H is behind the kerfuffle, and she feels that if the h hadn't gone out with him he would have left them alone. ( I wanted to use the skillet at this point, but I put down because at least the sister was feeding the kitties.)The h is moping around town when the H's henchmen kidnap her and dump her on his yacht. She recognizes one of the kidnappers as the guy who mugged her that day she went to the bank. She confronts the H and he happily admits he set the whole thing up to get a date.Then he tells her the maid lied and he kisses her until she agrees with him. He was engaged to Angry woman, it was a match promoted by their respective parents, but Angry woman is more arrogant and controlling than the H is and she dumped him and he refused to take her back and so they still have a lot of bitterness between them.The H swears he loves her and is marrying her and he did not call the authorities about the bar. The h loves him back and the epilogue is the H helps the brother in law get the correct permits he couldn't be bother with by filling out the paper work and the sister likes the H now and the kitties are still being fed and HEA's abound.I still feel the scenery and the paella were more interesting and there really should have been more garden kitty gnome time, but we all made it to the end with no skillet damage, so that does put the book on the tolerably readable scale - tho there really should have been some more romance or at least more H and h in the same actual room together.Instead he sorta does a creepy stalking-H-hiding-in-his-car-while-watching-the-h and that was weird. The sister was really off the wall and I still wondered why on earth they invested their savings in a bar/restaurant that was their only source of financial support and then did not even bother with finding out about the paper work.This one is odd, but readable and the cat bonus plus paella and scenery was about the only thing that saved the book from being abandoned in favor of watching water drips from the tap.This entire review has been hidden because of spoilers.Show full review
