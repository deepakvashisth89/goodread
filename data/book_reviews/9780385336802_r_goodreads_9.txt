I am glad that I recently watched a special on the Himalayas on the Nature channel as I was able to picture so well the physical challenges of the land. I am giving a 5 star rating because I love lyrical writing and this book has that. "The valleys were deep wounds slashed into the Tibetan massif, lined with black rock and scatterings of dwarf rhododendron. There were half-hidden cols and sunless gorges, pockets of iced-over marsh through which the yaks' hoofs smashed into greasy mud, and waterfalls like frozen mare's tails."The story is not predictable, so it is more attuned to "real life." It is 1950 and the Tibetans of a small mountain village face the invasion of the Chinese, but the main character is a radio operator from Scotland.
