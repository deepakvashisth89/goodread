This is a well written and largely sensible account of a commercial manned mission to mars. Although this was written a couple of years before SpaceX was founded, it's impossible to read this without inserting Elon into the role of Axelrod, the Billionaire founder of the Mars consortium.NASA, a competing commercial entity, several disasters, and some Martian life all make an appearance to move the story along. If you like near-future SciFi exploration this is definitely worth a read. My only gripe? It's not very exciting. I guess I'm into more grandiose SciFi Opera.If you like this novel, I would push you strongly into the direction of Kim Stanley Robinson's Red, Blue, and Green Mars. This is the ultimate epic series of Mars colonisation and terraforming novels to read, taking things much further down the line than this novel.
