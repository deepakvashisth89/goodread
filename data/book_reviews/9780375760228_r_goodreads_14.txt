The book, Thomas Carlyle'sThe French Revolutiongets 4.5 stars overall. I reviewed each of its three volumes under their respective names:The French Revolution I, The Bastille,The French Revolution II, The Constitution, andThe French Revolution III, The Guillotine.This kindle version however, I would only rate 3 stars as it does not include the pictures which are in the version I read. They greatly enhance the overall appreciation of the experience of reading this period piece. Also, I found some discrepancies in terms of missing information and incorrect sequencing which mixed me up when I tried to go back and forth between this book and my paper copy.If this is all you have, then read it by all means. But if you can access a better kindle version or a paper copy, then do.
