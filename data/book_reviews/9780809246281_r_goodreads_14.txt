Any dog owner knows, each dog has their own quirks that make them a little neurotic. This book will not make them any less neurotic, nor provide any solutions to dealing with them, but at least it will provide a laugh as you recognize the universal truths of neurotic dogs. This book checked so many of the boxes as far as the behaviors my dogs exhibit goes and the illustrations are hilarious. It may not make it any easier to live with a neurotic dog, but it does make you laugh about the absurdity of the situation (if you aren’t already). A great lighthearted read for dog owners.
