I'm a long-time Jane Yolen fan, and I like stories about smart kids escaping cults. Given that I can personally remember being raised Christian and told that the world was probably going to end in 1987 or 1988.... yeah, a lot of this book tasted familiar.

An enjoyable read, rapidly becoming outdated (not only do cults seem to be less frequent occurrences now, nobody needs a laptop to make a cell phone call). Probably only good for middle-aged folks like me, with either a certain type of childhood or a specific interest in 80s and 90s religion.
