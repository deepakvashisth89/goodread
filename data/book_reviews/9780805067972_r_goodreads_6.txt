I read the book "Love, Sara" by Mary Beth Lundgren and the lexile level is 580. A true story about Sara and Dulcie, Sara struggles with a lot of sexual abuse from her father when she is really young. She also experinces teen pregnancy with a friend and suicide at a very young age. Sara tries to help her friend with her pregnancy and her dealing with depression while she recovers from getting sexually abused by her father and then going into a foster home.
The theme of sadness and dealing with friends and having to care and having a lot of stress while getting older really sets in this book. She really has to think and grow from what happened while one of her best friends is going through something completely different that she has no control over and knows nothing about. I think on page 125 she and her foster mother try to stop Dulcie from committing suicide, this part really propels action because Sara and her where on the phone and not knowing if your best friend committed is really nerve racking.
I really recommend this book to anyone who loves real life stories that talk about hard things and stuff that should be talked about in the world a lot more. I loved this book because it shows what a real friend would go through just for you to stay alive and how ending your life is never the answer because the future could be life changing and you wouldn’t be able to experience it. In some parts of the book it was very confusing because I couldn't picture anything in my head like I did in some other parts of the book but then again it was very interesting and overall beautifully told and written. I loved the ending the way they came together and valued their friendship and didn’t want anything getting in the way again.
