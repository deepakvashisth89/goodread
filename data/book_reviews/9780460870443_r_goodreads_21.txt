The first treatise reminds me of your standard political philosophical diss track from the era. The amount of foundation in the Bible, while interesting, led to a monotonous read.The second treatise is the real juice to squeeze here. Chapters on property, power, and the various forms of tyranny and usurpation of said power were quite memorable. It’s easy to see the foundations this laid for the Founding Fathers.If it weren’t for the old-timey language and the sometimes banal nature of the topic at hand, this was otherwise a great read.
