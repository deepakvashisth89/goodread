Literature broadly experiments with language, but experimental literature experiments with the reader.

📚This is not a narrative about characters nor will it provide deeper insights into the psyche of the characters and the terrors they perpetuate.

📚This novel presents holocaust traumas in a tiered fashion. These are belated postwar effects. The moral dilemmas are layered on top of one another so that there is no right way forward.

📚Where trauma splits the mind into all or nothing categorical thinking, this novel is laying it out in shades of gray. You have to look in the mirror to find the meaning.

This gave me a lot of ick, but also lots to ponder. As uncomfortable as it may be, (especially Helen’s actions!) it took skill for the author to manage this.
4/5🌟
