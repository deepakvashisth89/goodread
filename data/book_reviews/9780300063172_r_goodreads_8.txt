The first half of the book is extremely clinical. The studies are somewhat outdated (as the book was written in 1993) and they are discussed in relation to how they discredit even more outdated studies. The second half is more interesting, as it is a collection of stories from adoptive parents about the conversations they have had with their adopted children about adoption. That was interesting and gave good insight into how children think about it and the questions they may have at different ages.
