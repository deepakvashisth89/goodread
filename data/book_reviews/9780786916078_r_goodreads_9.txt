Another one for the ages. The final showdown between cleric and wizard, the final battle(s) takes most of the second part of the book.This volume is a bit different than the previous ones, scattered fights all the time, running for cover, and running towards their destination - our heroes don't seem to have time for any characters development.Cadderly seems overpowered by far, two of the "big" creatures are overcome too easily, seemingly flukes. And not to mention that one of the biggest monster they will ever likely face - will not spoil - is dealt with in a surprisingly creative manner.
