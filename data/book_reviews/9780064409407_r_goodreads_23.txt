While the horse-trek adventure is a fast-paced change of scenery for the series, I did not much like this one. Howsoever, I plan on reading the rest of the series. <3

Note: I found the depiction of Calormen pretty jarring. The heavy-handed coding of the 'dark-skinned' characters as the primary antagonists felt like a relic of orientalist stereotypes that is hard to overlook.
