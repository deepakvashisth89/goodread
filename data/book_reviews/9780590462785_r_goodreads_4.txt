This book was fun and interesting to me when I read it. I only read this book from the series. I don't think I realized it was a series as a kid, or I probably would have read more of them. As an adult, I find it funny that this is the only Jerry Spineli book I read when he has so many other great ones out there.While I remember reading this book, I remember very, very little about the plot itself. It was an okay read to pass the time, but nothing about it jumped out at me; it isn't memorable almost 20 years later.
