B–:The kind of story that's cleverer in retrospect than at the time of reading it. Far too much time elapses before the murder takes place (I hope this isn't becoming a theme for this series) and there aren't many genuine motives for it, but the solution really is table-turning stuff! The are lots of tolerably neurotic characters, some funny outbursts, and even a cute romance, but Marsh's proclamation that this was her best novel feels indicative of how artists never seem to agree with what their critics champion. More likely, the cultural aspect of this New Zealand-set story made it a very personal story for her, but it didn't fully work for me.
