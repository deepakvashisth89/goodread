This book started out to be a totally out of the hospital,, medical science investigation type reading. Still a nurse overworked once, again with best friend, also a nurse. Best part was the hilarious prank, pulled on the Nurse Manager of the Hospital. The assumption that nurses are totally naïve, is ridiculous. The CEO's have different reasons for running hospitals and its not for helping the masses. Then the characters get carried away with corpses, nanotechnology and, physicians from other third world countries. When the local PD gets involved with its own investigative team, things get a tad more complicated and the book is not for the readers with sensitive stomachs. You have to read it all to find out what happens. ! A good read.
