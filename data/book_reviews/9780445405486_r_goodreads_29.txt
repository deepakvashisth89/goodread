A page turning palate cleanser - if you’re a fan of murder, mayhem, fingers and ears separated from their owners… in a bad district in Hong Kong before the lease expired.(Most of) the police and (some of the) bad guys are likable. A perfect example of why publishers should not have killed the mass market paperback. I’ll look for the others in the series in used book stores and at the library. Great fun but not worth a trade paperback price, unless it’s an omnibus edition.
