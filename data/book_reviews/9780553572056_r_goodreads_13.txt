I had read The Woman Who Married a Bear so decided to give this one a shot. While this time the story seemed disjointed for me at the beginning, it finally seemed to settle down. Maybe i was just tired. But our detective didn't really seem to detect much, was just along for the ride. The story involved a custody battle with neither parent seeming to be fit as physical custody of the child is constantly being wrested from one or the other. Satisfactory ending. Hope the kid has the skills to cope with two such insane parents.
