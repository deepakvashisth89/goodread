Although lengthy and somewhat outdated,Pink Samuraiis comprehensive and can serve as a useful resource. I learned quite a bit, especially from the first three or four hundred pages (covering historical myths and sex in the Heian period up to Edo (Yoshiwara, Shinmachi, etc.) and Meiji–Showa eras). Reading the latter half I got a better sense of how much has changed here for women since the 90s when I moved to Tokyo, and also of changes in the red-light districts, late-night TV, newsstand mags, fetishisms and so forth. Glad I'm finished with it though; roughly 700 pages is a lot of sex.
