Okay, having read the whole of the "second series" of Amber books (#6-10, starting withTrumps of Doom, I can now say conclusively, "Don't bother."In the first series, Zelazny had an awesome hero, an awesome universe, and an awesome plot that spanned five (albeit short, by today's standards) books. In this follow-up series, the hero is--if not dumb, at least ignorant and incapable, the universe is made up as he goes (including none of what was awesome about Amber and Shadow), and the plot is device-driven and unsatisfying.'Nuff said. If you like fantasy at all, definitely read the first series (five books starting withNine Princes in Amber), but leave it there.
