Another biography of my beloved Alexander! I have to admit that I had quite high expectations for this book and that I thought it would be better. However I know that none of the books on Alexander that we have now would’ve been the same without this important historical work! It still definitely holds up, even though several discoveries have already been made since this was first published. Also I feel his youth could’ve been discussed more.“He had the romantic's sharpness and cruel indifference to life; he was also a man of passionate ambitions, who saw the intense adventure of the unknown. He did not believe in impossibility; man could do anything, and he nearly proved it.”
