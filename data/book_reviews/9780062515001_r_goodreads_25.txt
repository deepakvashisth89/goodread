3.5/5A raw and honest account of a man who lived on the edge of heaven and hell. Johnny was a real rebel with a cause and a passion for what his did best. Telling his story in his own voice, with no holds barred. He reveals his highs and lows, his struggles and triumphs, and mainly the people he loved and lost. I love how he always introduces a dear person or a memorable place in his journey, with a witty story or a crazy outlawed adventure, makes you not wanting to put the book down.But Maaaan!.. I've been into Johnny Cash's music from a younger age including his countless gospel songs, but never thought he was this Jesus freak. Although the Nicajack Cave chapter was to me one of the strongly written chapters of this book and I'm sure anyone who's read the book is remembering their eyes tearing up exactly right there, but I could definitely do without all those preaching filler passages later on in the book.
