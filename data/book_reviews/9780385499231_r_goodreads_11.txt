Exceptionally fine collection of stories - having hated Aleksandar Hemon's 'The World and All it Holds' I loved these stories but just how great they are is something I am unsure about. Plenty of GR reviewers are ecstatic in their praise but I can't get over my disappointment over 'The World and All It Holds'. I think I need to read these stories again to appreciate them fully - but I have no hesitation in recommend this collection.
