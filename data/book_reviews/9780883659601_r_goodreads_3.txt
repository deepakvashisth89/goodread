I couldn’t wait to dig into my new book. Armed with highlighter and post-it, I optimistically planned a new blog section titled “Quote of the day”. I changed into something comfortable and snuggled in, ready for all the juicy pearls of wisdom I was going to savor and enjoy.Sadly, things didn’t quite go as expected. After 1000 quotes, sadly I have only marked 20 quotes, and remarkably I had to physically tear out 15 pages just in case my kids got a hold of the book. There were numerous words I felt compelled to blot out with a sharpie... But after 1000 quotes I’ve found that our compiler must see the world’s cup as completely empty and wonder how he figures that these are the “best things ever said”. He must hang around a very depressing circle of people who have seen only the worst of the world.
