My brain hurts. I didn’t think I was gonna learn so much. Haven’t taken in so much since my undergrad. Back to back classes, eyes dry, hungry, very little everywhere. Little money. Little hopes, dreams. Little me. I didn’t expect to learn Greek. I didn’t expect to go back to learning Japanese. Ideas flint left and right. My brain is wired like this. One thought after another. Perfectly pitched on the page as something not so much stream of consciousness, but there’s a running stream. Something is running. Towards something. Away from something. And aren’t we all like that? Aren’t we all some part in the grand scheme of one big chase? A rat race?

Have I said my brain hurts? It continues to hurt. Don’t know where to place this. At once brilliant. But also boring. Compulsive all the way through. And the Kurosawa backdrop just lends to the endless art that backs up other art. Hyperlinks astray, popping off in all directions to no end. When will it end? When is my time up?

What is my last act of goodness? Will the courts know? My friends? Enemies? Who will define what good is? In the end? Does the public decide? What decides the good of the earth? The good of the people. I’ve been such a bad son. My mom knows. She’s tired of me. But she doesn’t know how tired I am of her. I think it shows in my face, in the way I say things, and I don’t talk like that to anybody else, not even my worst enemies. I’m a terrible son. So I make it up in as much good I try to offer. Leave a door a linger longer, strangers rushing in. Accommodating. Giving into the pleasure of helping those in need. I try, I try. Always trying. Doesn’t my mother know? Doesn’t mother always know best? The best of the best is here. DeWitt’s brilliance in how she can shape a story, get away with it, but I’m still a little tired with a lot to think about. And isn’t that just another day in my head, another day on this floating rock, hungry and lonely and wanting to love.
