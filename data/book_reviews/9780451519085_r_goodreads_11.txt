I'm sure there are erudite reviews of this book on this page. Mine won't be. I think I'm fascinated by the decisions men make vis-a-vis the divinely ordered events of our lives. What is it in our hearts and minds that leads us in one direction or another? Hawthorne probes these things. He's masterful with the English vocabulary. The stories are told with stoutness, yet with an undercurrent of personal reflection and pensiveness.
