Not my favorite Morgan Llewellyn book. It got really good towards the end, but I just felt like it was dragging in the middle. However, that may have been the point, as of course, the "refugees" would have felt that way, too--that it was just dragging on and never ending.I did like most of the characters. Donal Cam O'Sullivan was a very human hero. He wasn't perfect, made some mistakes, and got some people killed along the way. That's a realistic hero.I even found some of the villains to be moderately likeable and not too one dimensional.
