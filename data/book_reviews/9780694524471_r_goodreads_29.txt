The moral of this story is absolutely intrinsic!!!!!!!I highly recommend reading this short novel and opening your eyes to more than just the world in front of you. This is the kind of read that leaves you pondering about life and the choices we make, well after you’ve finished reading. It’s the kind of book you read more than once.Written exceptionally well, it’s an effortless and comfortable read that warms you like a cozy blanket. It left me thinking silently for quite awhile after I closed the book. I honestly just lost myself in thought and how we can so easily lose sight of what’s important in life. How we can be quick to assume instead of coming from curious.This isn’t just a novel, it’s a lesson. If you like books that leave you enlightened, this is a good one to read.
