It's been many years since I last read this book, but there were some scenes that I remembered clearly. That's got to be a good sign about Wilson's ability to build suspense and some solid plot points. The background story is a bit confusing--Tom and his friend are staying in British Columbia at a place owned by a woman, who lives with her daughter, grandmother, and boyfriend. The relationships seem a bit strange, and the sudden spelunking trip with hockey coaches adds to that feeling. But it's got some good stories about interesting and strange events from many years ago in the Kootenays. The caves are described well and get the reader's imagination working. Glad I pulled this one out to reread.
