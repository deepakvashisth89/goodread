As a new Australian, I am working my way through the country's popular authors.Kalis my introduction to Judy Nunn. She writes a rollicking good tale that is also a compelling introduction to Kalgoorlie. Western Australia's "largest outback town" (according to tourist information site) is still a center for gold mining. Nunn starts in Italy, brings gold-seekers to the Outback, introduces them to Australians, and sets the stage for a cultural and historical exploration of the impact of three important factors in the country's development: immigration, racism, and resource extraction. In the hands of such a talented writer, the story takes wings.
