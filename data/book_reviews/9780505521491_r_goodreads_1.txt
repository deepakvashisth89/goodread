I received this book free from e Book discovery. I voluntarily review this book. This is my honest review.I've never read any time travel books before the only time travel things I'd seen were films. This book intrigued me and at first I didn't like it at all, the way it went from one time to another, but I stuck with it and I loved it!A heart breaking love story of love lost and people finding closure.It was how someone tried to give back what they'd taken so foolishly thinking he could have the love that was always going to belong to someone else even if they were dead.Colin is practicing for a competition in the plane Jakob had been working on with him for what seemed like forever, but Jakob wouldn't let him he kept making him come down from the sky and he kept talking about windows and not having much time , how he'd have a week and that was all. Colin was trying out a twist where he falls from the sky only to pull himself out of it at the last minute, he needed to win this competition to prove to his dying dad that he could make something of himself and he'd only got a week to get it right, every turn had to be perfect, but something went wrong he couldn't pull the nose back up again. Jakob had put a black box with a button on it next to the throttle without telling him why it was there or what it had to do with flying, but maybe he had and Colin had forgotten. All of a sudden something popped and he ended up pulling back but he didn't recognise anywhere, he flew around to find his bearings , he saw something he thought he recognised and circled around to land, but the airstrip was all grass, how had that happened....he crash landed and someone got him out, he recognised her from a picture Jakob had drawn but it couldn't be that was 50 years ago. Nothing made any sense to either Colin or Liesl, he looked so much like Kurt her heart missed a beat it couldn't be him he'd been killed 2 long years ago, but he looked like him except his hair it was long and his clothes were strange..That is where it all begins to get complicated, Colin needs to get home to win this race, but she wants him to win her race. What can he do apart from win hers saving her airfield, leaving just enough time to get in his plane and go home.I loved the book and couldn't put it down in the end. Hearts are torn, can they really be mended, or will someone always end up paying the price. You think you can second guess everything but this book twists and turns, I cried reading this book in parts.It's one of the best romance books that I've read and I will be reading a lot more from this author. I would recommend it to anyone that loved a romance even if they don't like time travel.
