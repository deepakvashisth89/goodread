This book has been in my TBR pile for quite sometime. It follows the life of Frank, a long time fishing guide in Algonquin Park.I thoroughly enjoyed reading of his numerous fishing adventures and his life with his wife Marie. His love for her along with his care and compassion was immense!It surprised me that so much of the book focused on his alcoholism. I didn’t realize it was such a big part of his life for so many years.
