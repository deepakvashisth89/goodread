An important book in that it reveals a very important piece of history. A piece of history that got me, personally, harassed, harangued and run off the road twice, but it was worth it because there is now a marker there to show for all the hard work. No matter how hard the City of Canton tried to hide it, there is a permanent reminder there now of what they did and who ultimately is to blame for the lives lost at the Hiawatha Asylum.
