Bushranger of the Skies: No Footprints in the Bush by Arthur W. Upfield is the 8th book in the Inspector Napoleon Bonaparte series. A policeman on his way to meet Bony is killed when his car is bombed from the air and after witnessing this, Bony is soon on the case. We learn the culprit very early on and it is a matter of bringing him to justice. In this case, Bony makes particularly good use of his native Aboriginal skills and enlists the help of a local tribal chief Burning Water in his quiest. I loved learning of some of the Aboriginal customs and beliefs and the various skills that they use. Bony faces many dangers on this quest but is outstanding as always. An excellent historical Australian outback adventure.
