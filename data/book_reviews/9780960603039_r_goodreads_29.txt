My first read of 2010. Given the content of this book, one would think it not appropriate to start the New Year with such a book. However, I believe it has given me a fresh perspective to start the year off with a greater appreciation for life.I truly admire Derek Humphry for having the courage to write such a controversial book in order to provide assistance for the dying.This is most definitely NOT a book for those who are closed-minded.Now.... I think I'll pack my bags for Switzerland.
