Shawnee County police officer Sgt. Janna Brill, in a not too distant future, needs to team up with a new partner, imaginative Sgt. “Mama” Maxwell, to solve murders revolving around a shipwrecked colonist spaceship, a presumed gang-related crime extending to a space station, and a murder committed with the help of very unusual accomplices. While she tries to do solid police work, her partner follows his instincts just a bit too often...Nicely set on the borderline between detective stories and SciFi, the three stories in this Omnibus Edition (“Doppelgänger Gambit”, “Spider Play”, and “Dragon’s Teeth”) have some beautiful twists and turns, combined with interesting characters. A very entertaining read (and even a re-read for me).
