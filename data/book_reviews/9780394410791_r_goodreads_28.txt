3.5 StarsSomewhat dated but still an interesting non-fiction about one of the oldest apartment buildings in NYC. Though it never achieved the panache that addresses on the West side has, from its inception the Dakota has had a loyal following.Despite losing money from the first day of operation (which would have horrified the original owner of he had lived to see it), the complex has managed to remain one of the most popular residences is the city.It was interesting reading how the tenants and apartments had changed from the late 19th century to the late 20th century.
