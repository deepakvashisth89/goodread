I'm a fan of short stories and an even bigger fan of short short stories. I like a twist, I like strange concepts hinting of of weird worlds.What I like about this collection is the variety. While most of the stories are more urban fantasy than high fantasy, it's quite a mix of styles and substances. Horror, fairy tales and even some gags. The quality is also quite varied.Having read this over a few months, I can recall only several of the stories. One about voodoo, one about a vampire and one about unicorns, mainly.
