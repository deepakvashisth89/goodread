December 2018 ReadA compelling little read with a clever, albeit hackneyed title. Author Denis Waitley, PhD. (the PhD. is now disputed as he never actually attended University, nonetheless...), shows one how to nurture the greatness within them and offers a system that apparently will allow you to do in months, what takes psychologists years to accomplish.The 10 Seeds are: -1 - Seed of Self-Esteem2 - Seed of Creativity3 - Seed of Responsibility4 - Seed of Wisdom5 - Seed of Purpose6 - Seed of Communication7 - Seed of Faith8 - Seed of Adaptability9 - Seed of Perseverance10 - Seed of PerspectiveAbit dated (circa 1980's), however, still replete with some solid examples of how to overcome feelings of unworthiness and setting higher goals.
