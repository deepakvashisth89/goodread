Nicholas Breakspear, son of a famous British actor, determined early in life that he would not follow his father into the theatre. Instead, he became a marine - a dig at his father who hates guns and fighting.
Now Nick is a sailor who is looking forward to repairing his own boat and sailing to wherever he chooses. Unfortunately, his plans are foiled by a senator's request to care for his adult twin children who are both hooked on cocaine. As part of the deal, he and his crew will be paid handsomely and his boat will be repaired.
A harried journey filled with attempts on his life, kidnapping, and murder make him regret his decision many times over.
A good read to while away the hours.
