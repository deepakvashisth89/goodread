Thinking back, this might be the first time I gave up on a book. All I remember is barely caring about any of the characters to begin with (at an age whereanythingwould suck me in), and then getting worn down by a pseudo-Socratic dialogue on a ship about the nature of evil. It was so out of place, and served no purpose. And that's why this book is 600 pages long. Not because it needs to be.
