I usually get interested in Joanna Trollope's books very quickly but this one took a while. There was WAY too much describing of the scenery etc the first time Frances went to Spain. Boring. I think the synopsis on the back of the book didn't describe the story very well either. It basically said that Lizzie thought that all the things she had in HER life should have been enough for Frances, but I think that what she really thought was that Frances should have all the same things in her life or she wouldn't be happy. I'm glad that, at the end of the story, Luis didn't suddenly change his mind about his relationship with Frances and have a ''happily ever after'' ending. What happened was much more believable. Lizzie was quite unlikable...I think her mother was supposed to be the really unlikable character but I didn't mind her; not really a very nice person but somehow that didn't make me dislike her.
