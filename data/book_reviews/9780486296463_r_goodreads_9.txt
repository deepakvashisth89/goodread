Book number 48, no less, in what can only be described as a series of children's classics by this american author. A bed time story of anthropomorphized forest animals told in 23 bite-sized chapters. Many contain a poem in a-b-a-b rhyme scheme with a moralising message, although not all of them make equal sense. The animals that make up the characters in the story are all mammals.Available on Project Gutenberg. Illustrated for additional entertainment purposes.
