Normally I would have been finished with this Chicken Soup edition quite some time ago. I try to read at least one of the inspirational stories in the series each day. In 2019 I got out of the habit so this one took longer than it should, not because it wasn't good, just because I got out of this routine.

Rating it to others that I've read in the series, I'd give it a 4, not my all-time favorite, but very good.
