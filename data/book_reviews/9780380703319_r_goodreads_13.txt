#1 in Grub and Stakers series, The Grub and Skaters Move a Mountain is a humorous mystery set in a small town where everyone is obsessed with archery, literally every resident knows how to use a bow and arrow so when a man drops dead with an arrow through his heart, it's a real stickler finding out who must be responsible.

I'll be honest, this was definitely not my kind of book. I enjoy a fair share of humorous cozy mysteries ngl but this one just started on the wrong foot for me. I could not connect with any of the characters no matter how much I tried and the main character Dittany Henbit was just a tad bit weird imo.

However if we talk about the mystery itself, there was no amateur sleuthing going on at all. All the MC did was let everyone walk all over like a doormat and the humor was plain self depreciation on her part. I'll have almost surely dnfed it if not for the fact that it was just a matter of few facts and despite everything going on I did still wanted to find out who the murderer was.

I feel like there were a lot of unnecessary plot points in this one that not only did not add anything substantial to the main plot but actually took away it's due attention. I am utterly disappointed and defintely won't be reading any more of this series or the author for that matter.

1.5 stars.
