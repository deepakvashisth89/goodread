What did I think?...I think this book was absolutely wonderful!! It handed to me all the emotional feels with open palms. When this book came to be in my possession, (I don't even know how actually, I think I bought it at a library sale) I didn't realize that it was a sequel. Halfway through I bounced onto Amazon and purchased her first best-sellerand her other book(which I think may just be a different version of Still Waters..but I'll find out when I read it next).I know there is a lot sadness in the world that I haven't fully experienced and when you are living your own life and are so swept up in your own world, you don't really know all the different sadness.Jennifer Lauckdescribes it to me in beautiful detail. I am currently reading Blackbird and it is just as captivating or maybe I should say Still Waters is just as captivating as her first...
