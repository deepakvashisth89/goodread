Very useful guide to all things academic. Thanks Stephanie!! Everything you need to know about publishing, tenure, academic life, etc. Will definitely be revisiting info on how to write a book proposal, turn diss into a book, etc.A couple of chapters are a little, well, creepy, namely the one about women in the academy, which basically says "have no personality, dress frumpy, smile and nod and expect to get treated like total crap until you get tenure." The author softens the blow near the end as says that things have, finally, started to change, although not perhaps quite as much as you'd like to think.I appreciate that the book is honest and doesn't try to overly sugarcoat the internal politics that rage because, as they say, the stakes are so low. Very useful. Although at times it just makes you want to shake your head at how seriously so many with letters behind their name take themselves. Oy.
