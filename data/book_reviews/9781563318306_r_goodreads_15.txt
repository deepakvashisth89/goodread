This is the 3rd book I've read that is about climbing Mt Everest during the tragic 1996 season. Goran Kropp had a different perspective on climbing, and was trying to get back to the roots of it all without the massive expeditions. He decided to go on his own power from Sweden to the top of Everest and back again; on a bike, without supplemental oxygen, without sherpas, etc.In addition to the climbing, you can imagine he has quite a journey just traveling there from Sweden by bike so it is a cool travel book. This book isn't so much about the climbing itself, more about his personal journey and the nature of both climbing and the climbing industry. An enjoyable, easy read...
