A good brief history of law in the United States. The author describes how law has changed over time, how laws have to more or less agree with the society in which they exist. The laws of the Massachusetts Bay Colony were much different than the laws of modern California -- the differences can almost be considered comical. Before the Civil War, there was a well-developed system of laws governing slavery. Now they are all gone. Civil rights laws passed in the past few decades would have been completely impossible in the 19th century.The United States historically had a form of English common law (judge-made), as opposed to civil law (committee-made) which exists in most of the world. The author says that that is changing because of the torrent of Federal regulations coming from Washington.
