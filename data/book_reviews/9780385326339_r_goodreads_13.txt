A decent introduction to the Depression for younger kids, but probably best to combine with something else to get a better idea of it.The story didn’t have the smoothest flow, but it is told from the perspective of a (slightly dramatic) 11yo girl, so it isn’t too jarring. I did like that it’s based on the lives of of the author’s grandparents. Content considerations: rumors of Gypsies kidnapping children, Margo’s best friend’s parents have fights and her father ultimately leaves them to find work. 8+
