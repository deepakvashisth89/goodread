This quick and dirty Bolan sees the man blitzing Acapulco as he has information that the mob is using the resort town to run drugs and girls.It starts off with a bang, literally, as Striker snipes a few mafioso and then messes with the big boss down south by crashing the man’s yacht into shore (Boats N’ Hoes anyone), blowing up his chopper and crashing his plane into the ocean. A great opening half of the novel with Bolan raising all kinds of hell and stirring up the hornets nest down south.The book goes into quasi-mystery territory after that, with the big guy tracking a femme fatale across town, and learning some hard truths, circa 1970s: women are becoming more equal to men in all things: even crime!A quick read for me as the action moves smoothly and the story provided a bit more interest than others with the mysterious woman, the Jewish Mexican mob boss who wants out, and the Mob, who has machinations of its own down in paradise.
