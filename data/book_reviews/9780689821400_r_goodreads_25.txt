I don't know why authors feel they need to "cheat". As if the reader isn't smart enough to see it--in plain sight. When that happens, I don't feel I owe the author any more respect than he/she has shown me. (This might be a spoiler except the reader knows it almost instantly.)If Raven has lived in the same village since an infant--someone is going to figure out he is deaf. Deafness is not unknown--even in a tiny village. And then there is his friend, the priest--who hasn't figured it out in 17 years? The protag is there for 2 days and realizes it--and then the priest won't believe her?At that, I put the book away and I would give it away--except for the lovely cover by Kinuko Craft (a fav artist).
