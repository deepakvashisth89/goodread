How I Came To Read This Book: I believe it was a gift – there’s something twigging in my mind that it has a connection to my Aunt Linda – either she recommended it or also got a copy of it.The Plot: The title is pretty self-explanatory. It’s a book of ‘real’ ghost stories set in Manitoba, many of which are in Winnipeg at famous landmarks like the infamously haunted Hotel Fort Garry.The Good & The Bad: This collection failed to chill me, perhaps because I was too much of a realist by the time I read this book. I don’t find scary books very scary, even when they have a shred of anecdotal truth to them. The fact that not a single story has stuck with me – while other anthologies I read as a child have managed to do so – probably speaks volumes.The Bottom Line: Snoozy, although perhaps of passing interest if you’re a supernatural enthusiast living in Manitoba.Anything Memorable?: I find it interesting that this book exists when a classmate of mine recently published her own collection of Manitoba ghost stories in the last few years. I guess the publisher chose to overlook this collection.50-Book Challenge?: Nope.
