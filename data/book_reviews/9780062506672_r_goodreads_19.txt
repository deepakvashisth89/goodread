To quote Mr. Spock, "Fascinating!" The exclamation point is mine. A treatise on Shamanism, The Goddess, Feminism, and the combination of it all was a powerful read. The book is almost 20 years old and yet is confronting things that are happening now. It is one woman's journey to becoming her true self. Give it a try. I read it in bits and snatches as there is so much to think about.
