I loved all the characters in the book. Our washed-out hero became heroic once again. Our heroine was brave. Both were natural leaders.Ariella needs to find a husband ASAP. Her clan has been attacked, her father murdered and the cur responsible for the destruction of her home is intent on marrying her and obtaining a supposedly magical sword handed down through the decades to the laird of the clan. Legend has it that the sword makes its owner invincible.Ariella fakes her death to avoid her enemy's plans. Posing as a boy, she sets out with two clan members to find the man her seer has predicted will be their new laird. But Ariella is shocked to find that the Black Wolf is a shadow of the man he once was. How will this man, who no longer commands an army, whose body is battered and who is a drunken sot, be able to defend and lead her clan?I dropped one star for the following reasons. *****Spoilers******* It seemed improbable to me that Malcolm would not wonder why everyone was looking to a dirty pre-teen boy for his opinion/consent on every decision.* Ariella's unwillingness to see that Malcolm had become the perfect choice for laird as he had done everything in his power to ensure her clan's keep was impregnable, made a fighting force out of artisans, and forged an alliance with neighbourhood clans so an army would be at her disposal, seemed out of character.* I think the sword should have cured his injuries.
