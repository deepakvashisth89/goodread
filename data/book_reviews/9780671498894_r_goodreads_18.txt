Stories come and stories go, but good language lasts forever.Per Wiki:"Crumley had not read any detective fiction until prompted to by Montana poet Richard Hugo, who recommended the work of Raymond Chandler for the quality of his sentences. Crumley finally picked up a copy of one of Chandler's books in Guadalajara, Mexico. Impressed by Chandler's writing, and that of Ross Macdonald, Crumley began writing his first detective novel,The Wrong Case,which was published in 1975.The titles of many of Crumley's books came from Hugo's poems.Unlike some writers in the mid 1970's, Crumley was never received much acclaim in the U.S., however he achieved a cult following (which I'm now one) mostly abroad.
