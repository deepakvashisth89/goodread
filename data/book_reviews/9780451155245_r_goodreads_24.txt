An interesting, exciting and thoughtful book about loneliness.

A brave woman confined to a loveless marriage on an island ... an ex-fighter pilot removed from the war through tragedy ... a spy cutting his way back to his homeland with information that may win the Nazis the war. They may have been better off before they met. ...

Highly recommended. And not just for espionage or historical fiction afficionados. High 4s, almost 5.
