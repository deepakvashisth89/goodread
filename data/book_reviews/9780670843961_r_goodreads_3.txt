A nice offering from England, the mom in this family of four is due to have her baby. Little sister gets to go along to school with her older brother just this once, creating havoc, of course, on bring your pet day. But all ends well, (I was impressed with the remarkably calm teacher). The wonderful part of this book is how the family is interracial and yet there is no mention of it - I get requests for interracial family books where the subject of race is not mentioned - as one Mom put it - can we just be pictured as a family and not some "subject"?. I usually did out my John Updike children's poem book when pressed, but now I can offer Sarah Garland books. I really love how the book opens and closes with sleeping. Very calming. A lovely family book.
