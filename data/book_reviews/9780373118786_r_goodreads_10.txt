I put off reading this book for several months because I wasn't sure about the whole..... girl sleeping with the same man as her mother had already slept with. (but it wasn't like that at all.....a misunderstanding by a 14 year old girl.....now a 24 year old.....Carolyn)I am glad I finally picked it up and read it. The story is wonderful. What a funny guy Vaughn is. He was so determined to see her, when she dead bolted the doors, that he did his version of a James Bond leap. Face it Carolyn you were never going to get away from him........he loved you too much and you know you loved him too. Loved it.
