In my TBR pile on my computer and Open Library I have the following:Looks like I have a bumpy ride ahead of me if the purple prose lives up to the subtle titles.Review:Very slow but sweet romance caper. Hard to believe this was written in 1985 as it has a Mary Stewart 60s vibe to it with the heroine just living in Venice as she searches for her missing friend. The consequences of loose moral behavior are presented here as well which makes me wonder what a modern girl would think of it.Not a romance to set your hair on fire, but nicely done if you want low level angst.One particularly fine romantic scene is the H and h's night in Venice. For all the romance we read, it's not often that a hero actually goes out of his way to woo the heroine. This hero is quite the European sophisticate from his casual smoking (it was cool back then) to his old world charm.Take your pick...
