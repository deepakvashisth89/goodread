The Lord God Made Them All is the seventh book in the All Creatures Great And Small series and what a fabulous series it has been. Throughout this series, we the reader are taken on a fantastic journey with veterinarian surgeon James Herriot. Some of the journey will have you laughing out loud and for some of the journey you will be reaching for the tissues.Sadly, I now only have one book to read for this series (Every Living Thing) and that will complete the series, so I’ll be holding off on reading that one for quite a while, but then again I might read it sooner than I think. If like me, you like animals or like reading animal stories, then please read this series I promise you won’t regret it. Highly recommended.
