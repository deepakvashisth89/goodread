What an amazing ride Edward Gibbon has been. Who would have guessed that a book written over 200 years ago could still be so relevant and so entertaining. It is enough that I am apt to forgive his comments (while in Parliament) speaking ill of those "upstart colonists" who would dare to make revolution against King and country.If you're going to be a child of the Enlightenment, you absolutely must read this history by THE Historian of the Enlightenment.
