I love Phyllis Whitney books but this one made me frustrated. The heroine knows her husband will keep her daughter by any means necessary but she leaves her daughter alone constantly? It makes it hard to feel bad for her. Very few characters in this were likeable and I did not like the abrupt ending. The husband is ridiculously villainous and I kept wanting Marcia to break his stupid mask he was so in love with. Marcia and Jerome were so focused on themselves it made it annoying to read. The descriptions of the scenery were beautiful but at times they were too lengthy and became tedious for me.
