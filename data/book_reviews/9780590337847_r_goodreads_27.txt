A Taste of Blackberriesby Doris Buchanan Smith (Harper Collins 1973) (Fiction - Children) is one of those tricky books that I remember reading as a middle-schooler that sneaked a hook into this reader that's still there many years later. It's THE book about death and dying for the young;The Bridge to Terabithiapales in comparison. A best friend dies from an allergy to bee stings. What could be more unexpected to a kid? The story teaches in the most gentle way possible that life does go on. This is a masterpiece. My rating: 7.5; finished 1973.
