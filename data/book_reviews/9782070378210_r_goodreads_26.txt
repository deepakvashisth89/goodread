Not as compelling plot-wise, or as complex theme-wise, or as deep character-wise, or as rich language-wise, asSeven Gothic TalesorWinter’s Tales. But then again, those are two of the greatest books I’ve ever read, so take the three star rating with a grain of salt. It’s probably a little unrealistic to expect that she could sustain that level of brilliance throughout her entire career. Anyway, though this was something of a disappointment, I don’t remotely regret reading it, and I still plan to read the rest of Dinesen’s ouevre.
