I’ve enjoyed getting to know Miss Silver and the characters in her world better. I’ve been reading them out of order and figure I’ll line up recurring characters as I learn them. Lord willing, I’ll probably end up re-reading them anyway, so I’m not too concerned about going out of order. This is my third Miss Silver book, I think. In this one, it somewhat tells you who did it earlier in the story. It’s just lining things up to figure out the details, though there’s still plenty of mystery to figure out. I was anxious and highly concerned for one of the characters. Wentworth has killed off a main-ish character in one of the other books, so I don’t fully know her style yet. I was hoping this character would make it, but I didn’t know. I still really enjoyed it, though.Despite the title of this book, it’s not overly summer. If I remember correctly, it mentions fall weather and things different times, so next time I’d probably put it in my fall “pile of possibilities”.
