Wow what a rollercoaster. Almost halfway through the book I was convinced the ML had slept with the FL's mother. I wouldn't touch books with a bargepole where the ML double dips in the family especially the sister trope let alone the mother but the curiosity bug bit me how the author would contrive to bring the leads together with a history like that.Apparently it was a misunderstanding thought the FL's mother did try to come on to the ML in the past. Coming to the leads there wasn't much except both leads panting after each other and FL struggling with her attraction and betraying body syndrome.It got all resolved with HEA but both the ML's antics and the constant conflict of the past made the book draining instead of enjoyable.
