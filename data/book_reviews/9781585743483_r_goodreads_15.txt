An interesting informative history on the Thresher disaster and its effects on submarine safety. It is written in a matter of fact way that focuses on the facts. The book included everything I came here to learn and, in addition, I also learned many facinating facts about other submarine disasters and submarine escapes and rescues that I had not ever known about.One of the craziest aspects of this whole event is that it is essentially an unsolved mystery that will most likely never be fully solved. The lengthy inquiry into the disaster results in a "best guess" as to the cause. The search for the lost Thresher and the review of all evidence highlighted for me just how difficult operating in the undersea domain is. Technologies and operating methods almost seem rudimentary at times and even basic communication underwater is very ineffective.This book is practically a must-read for those in the field of undersea warfare. If you just want to understand the basic history of this disaster and not read the whole book, at least just read the Appendices at the back. I give this book four stars and not five because I feel that the writing lacks the ability to entertain a wider audience other than those already familiar with submarines.
