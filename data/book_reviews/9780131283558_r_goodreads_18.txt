Tomie de Paola is one of Brooklyn's nicest guy authors for kids, which is not something you say about anyone from Brooklyn! His classic book is more to do with learning different words than telling a real story, but it's still a fun picture book to read since the illustrations are excellent, as a small boy tending sheep is reminded by townspeople to get a cloak in case the wind blows cold. So he makes one, out of the wool from his sheepies! Boom. End of story. But I can't dumb it down all the way, cos...the sheepies are so cutely drawn!
