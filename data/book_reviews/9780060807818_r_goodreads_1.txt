The book starts off with the diary of Frank, who addressing the reader tells us that he is going to kill the man who ran over his son. The diary stops at the point where Frank is supposed to kill the person responsible but he fails and the yet the other person turns up dead anyways and Nigel Strangeways is invited over to investigate. The book is well written and intriguing, I haven't really read anything similar but I had a hard time getting into it. I think the writing felt a little dry and the way Nigel solves the mystery seems dubious to me.
