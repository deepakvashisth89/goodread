A self-contained discussion of human morality, written in the language best suited to bring empiricism to ethics: primatology. By the end, Wrangham has taken the reader on a journey to other lives, perceiving, condemning, and forgiving each. The reader learns the scientific method as well as a lifetime of expertise in primatology, and the ruminations on the meaning of life it has sparked.I can't recommend this book enough. It will change the way you view gender relations, forever.
