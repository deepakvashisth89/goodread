Set in the same world, and maybe half a generation earlier than The Thread That Binds The Bones.

It's the story of a lonely teenager Nick, living in mountain lakeside holiday town.
His demanding father owns the local hotel/ grocery store, and when he isn't working or caring for his grandfather, Nick spends his time watching his neighbours and the holiday guests.

Then one day, a new family arrives to stay at one of the remote cabins. They are strange and keep to themselves, and can make people do stuff without remembering why.
Nick is hooked... because he too is hiding an ability to make people do stuff.
