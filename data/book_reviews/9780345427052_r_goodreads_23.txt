I liked this book! It was a very insightful look into the life of this world renowned horse trainer who is known for his join-up methods of horse training, instead of the violent breaking of a horse traditionally used in the years before. There are certainly still loads of people out there who prefer to ‘break’ their horses, but this book shows you how reading and ‘speaking’ horse body language fluently, though it takes more work, can even get you better results long term. Would recommend to anyone interested in horses, horse training, or involved in the equine field in any way. Great to have in your back pocket.
