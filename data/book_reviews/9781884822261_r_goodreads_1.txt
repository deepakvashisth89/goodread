This book was published in 1995 at a time the authors said that the Holocaust was being introduced into school curricula. It was originally issued in 1978. I found this gem at the Half Price Books Flagship store in Dallas. This book is an anthology and should be required reading for High School - at all grades and not just as a one semester class.
The book is broken down into six sections, starting with "What Happened," and progressing to "The Nature of Man," "Victims and Victimizers,""How and Why," "What This Reveals about the Individual and Society,""Aftermath," and concluding with "Could it Happen Again?"
The readings are compiled from more than 100 first hand (primary) accounts and secondary sources.
This book is a serious look at the issues of intolerance, obedience, resistance to authority, conformity, individualism, freedom, repression, good and evil. It is an exploration of morality at all levels.

"...Never before in modern history had one people made the killing of another the fulfillment of an ideology, in whose pursuit means were identical with ends."
When one views todays political landscape and the brutality of mob consensus in social media, the question of "Can it Happen Again" is critically important. History does not repeat itself exactly, but it has exceptionally strong parallels. The Themes in which this book examines and explores are timeless. I strongly suggest this book.
