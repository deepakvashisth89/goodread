A short read to last a lifetime. I was seduced by this novel. Let me tell you how.For my nineteenth birthday, a boy I hardly knew sent me this book signed with his love. Just seeing the cover brings the excitement back to me. An excitement mixed with surprise. We weren't dating. He'd just taken me to a dance where everyone but me was in a slinky dress. Following back surgery, I was in a body cast. The only thing to fit over it was the size of a tent. Yet, he didn't ditch me. He stood by me all night. A few weeks later, this book came in the mail. I read."She was an ocean into which I streamed.""Everything significant and full of fate for me adopted her form."The words jumped off its pages to me, making me blush. Then what took my heart was the scene between Sinclair and Eva (his friend Demian's mother). She comes in out of the rain and there are pearls in her long dark hair.What? No?? There are no pearls in the text??? Hesse writes "Raindrops clung to her black hair." And "I kissed the rain out of her hair." Nope. No pearls, you say.I read the passage again. The pearls shine as bright as ever. That is erotic writing. Hesse suggests more than he says.
