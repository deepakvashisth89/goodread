An engrossing short novella that you will devour in one sitting. I urge everyone who struggles with bitterness after suffering or anger after being treated with abuse to read this book. Two of my many favorite quotes from this book are, "To love like God is to take prejudices and hatreds and hold them in your hands and confess that they are no consequence. Only love counts. Every other emotion is too small." The other quote is "Study makes us smart. Suffering, authentic. Becoming real begins on the far side of pain." Actually there are many more nuggets that I could post. Read the book and find more gems that will lift your heart and challenge you to love Jesus and others (even your enemies) more, more and then some more. Calvin Miller, this book reminded me again of why you are one of my favorite writers.
