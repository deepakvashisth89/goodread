Not perfect, but an entertaining medieval read. Lady shrew Saxon heroine gets tamed by fierce Norman lord.
One thing that seemed odd was during a love scene, the hero shouts out, "God!" twice and then, "God, you're so tight...like a glove around me...", and I had to check that I was reading a medieval and not a contemporary romance. Later on in the book, the hero uses the expression, "Jesu", which is more common in the other medievals I have read. 3.5/5 stars rounded up to 4
