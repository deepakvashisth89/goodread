The cover shown on this page is absolutely horrendous!The book opens with a woman making her way around someone's apartment, searching for something. The chapter ends with her finally checking the pockets of the body on her floor.The story then moves to talent agent Jake Justus whose client Nelle Brown, a young starlet on the rise on radio, is related to the dead body. The pair discover the body, but leave it momentarily, and upon returning it's gone. Brown reveals that the dead man had compromising love letters that could doom her career. Justus contacts lawyer J.J. Malone, who's always got a drink or is pouring the next one. The three go looking for the body and come upon more corpses and clues until the murderer's reveal.This was a very easy mystery to solve after the first few chapters, but I enjoyed reading the book nonetheless. It's an easy read with plenty of clues, characters, and fun lines.
