I learned that trudging across Antarctica back in the early 20th century with a party comprised of stubborn, moody, ill-prepared British men isn't condusive to...well, to staying alive. Without modern technology, it just isn't going to work. The downside to early tundra-travel was that one tended to die in a particularly horrific way (falling into a glacial fissure, starvation, gangrene, respretory failure, exhaustion... hypothermia was far and away the least terrible egress from this mortal coil these men could hope for, to give you an idea).
