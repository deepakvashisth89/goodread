This was a very sweet book about a family whose parents had died. There were two siblings who were near adulthood and 3 younger siblings. Thomas worked all day managing the farm, plowing the fields,and caring for the crops. Angela saw after the three young children, cooked, cleaned,made all of their clothes, and still managed to have a smile on her face each evening. I liked all the family members especially Angela who tried to do everything in her power to raise the little ones to love God and live a good life.
