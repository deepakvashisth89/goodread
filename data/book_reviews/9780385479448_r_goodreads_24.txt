First rate aviation thriller that probably sets the bar for all books of this genre. When it was written in the 1990s, the plot elements were probably considered highly imaginative for the sake of the story. To those of us reading this since 2020 it takes on a whole new meaning. I picture the author shaking his head in an "I told you so" moment when flight restrictions were put into place during the Covid pandemic. Amazing that Nance could foresee just such a situation a few years before it actually took place. Reading the book today is surely a very different experience than reading it when it first came out, and only adds to the sense of drama since we KNOW how close to reality it is.
