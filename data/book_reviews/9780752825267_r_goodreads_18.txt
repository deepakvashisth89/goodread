This is a thriller hinging on people being out of work at the end of the Troubles - on both sides. A bomb on a London bus is the start and a bomb in a London building is the end. In between there are more explosions, terrorists, memories, spying and policing.Two police officers have similar names and it's hard to keep everyone separate. We mainly follow a Northern Irish research scientist whose brother is killed at the start, and he gets taken for a potential bomber and mistreated. While he travels on a false passport at one stage, and he's well versed in tech, it never occurs to him that credit card details can be traced and purchases can leave a trail. I think we all knew that before we even had mobile phones.Some coincidences occur, and it's just not credible that a shocked and injured civilian would be instructed to go into a burning building and search for a bomb.Overall an exciting read, it seems like the author just kept finding new applications for items of tech and adding them to the story.I read a paperback. This is an unbiased review.
