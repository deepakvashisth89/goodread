This book had everyone all up in arms when I was in high school, and when I finally got around to reading it, I thought, WTF? Really, why all the fuss? It doesn't give you tips on how to kill yourself or talk you into doing the deed.Instead, it spends a lot of thought on terminal care, when the patient should choose an artificial end to life, and what procedures one must put into place before getting to that stage. I found it informative but not controversial.
