This is two books: The Torch of Honor and Rogue Powers. In general, I like Allen's writing. I remember liking this one. As I remember, this story starts out simple, but starts getting more and more complicated.

I don't recall details. It starts with couple that either just got married, or are going to get married. They have just completed their training in some space organization. Their honeymoon or first assignment is interrupted by an unknown group of humans.

They later find this group of bad guys have found aliens, of which they have allied. To complicate the story a bit more, it is found that the aliens have their own agenda, which does not include leaving any humans alive in the galaxy.
