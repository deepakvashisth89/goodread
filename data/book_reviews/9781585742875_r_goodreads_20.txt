This book took me a long time to read. Actually, I wondered if I would ever finish it! But it kept staring at me. Regardless, I'm glad that I got though it because it was a very good true story about tug boats and sinking ships.The book is interesting, not because I am a Floridian and deal with a lot of weather events, but because all of these boats were involved in one or more hurricanes (#s VII, VIII, IX) that were tenacious in their relentless search for the same boats!(BTW, hurricanes were not named in the 1950s, they were in Roman numbers.)So, yes, the book was interesting and well-written and often very amusing; but, ladies, there were a few chapters about mechanical stuff that zoomed right over my head.
