Finally finished this book and all I can say is it certainly brought out the demon in me, because I strongly disliked it.While some of the short stories were interesting, many of them were so jumbled and inconsistent that I was unable to make heads of tails of their purpose.Some of the ways in which the stories were written were interesting; for instance one story was written as a track list of songs through years and each list had a column to the left explaining why certain songs were chosen. But the interesting points of view did not make up for the extreme slowness of this book.
