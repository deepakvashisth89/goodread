I had just read an historical fiction book about Child's work during WWIIThe Secret War of Julia Childand followed up withDearie: The Remarkable Life of Julia Childand they were both disappointments. Another reviewer mentioned this book as an alternative, so I borrowed the audiobook from the library's Libby app.Skip all the fluff until Julia's war work during WWII, and then skip ahead to the creation of her first cookbook. So. Much. Extraneous and unnecessary stuff in-between. The creation of the television show that made her a household name is when the book really picked up steam.
