Unsung masterpiece!!! A challenging and worthwhile 5/5 🌟… also i love the bigger easy to read font in this version! Only way to read it is in 1 big chunk IMO.

We follow a handful of scientists from Resune in a future where genetic engineering 🧬 has reached a peak we can ‘write’ dna the same way we write computer code or programs- which result in whole people who are created to act in certain ways, for certain tasks, called Azi. When something goes wrong or someone acts in a way you dont want you can create a real time fix - known as tape- to alter someone’s psychological thoughts, preferences and memory….

In such a world political power and ability to manipulate others to what you want is everything! And the person who is very best at this is Arianne Emory- who plays both the villian and the victim protagonist.

Words are not enough to properly descibe this one and it isnt for everyone. There is a circle a blackmail and complex manipulation that isnt always the most positive vibes… yet its drama you cant look away from.

The story begins in a senate/council chamber with many characters and was honestly complicated, dry and off-putting…. But once u know this is just to set the scene and learn the factions and motivations of what could one of the most impressive stories of manipulation since Game of thrones (maybe better??) its worth sitting thru the beginning and trusting the process…. At least it was for me.

I can honestly say this was one of the most rewarding stories i have read —- if u can make it to the level where you see cherryh’s vision. I didnt love downbelow station but can’t wait to pickup regenesis!!!

Warning ⚠️ This story does have a non descriptive rape of a man by a woman which might be a trigger for some but does serve a purpose in the end.
