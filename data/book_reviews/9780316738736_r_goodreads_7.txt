My daughter thinks this book is "amazing" - the "beach" one and the "bed" one. She named one of her stuffed animal dogs "Otto". I read the one about dads and really like it too. Basically in these books by Todd Parr, I've noticed that the writing is great, the drawings are awesome, the notes in the back of the books are touching, and the colors are a bit bright for me...but I'm sure that's one of the reasons my daughter enjoys them. So there.
