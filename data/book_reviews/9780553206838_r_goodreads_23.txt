I have never read this author, however as I read this book his style is beautifully literate. Descriptively, I could imagine every scene.Initially I remembered the Yangtze river when I was on it a few years ago. But the story evolved describing the history, the beliefs, and traditions. I will not spoil the story but I will plan to read A Bell for Adano and Hiroshima which are only a couple of Hersey's books.
