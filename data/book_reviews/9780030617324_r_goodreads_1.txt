This is a very heavy Duke book, who has become one of the major stars of the strip. Originally based on Hunter S. Thompson, the character has become hos own identity and seems to represent everything that Trudeau is against. Yet, you can tell he loves the character in his own way. The Rusty Nail in question is a boat that Duke buys to smuggle drugs out of Mexico, then smuggle in Haitians, then to take British tourists on a scenic tour of the Falklands war, before finally crashing the boat. Along with that we early 80s problems with Reagan, the deregulation of the EPA, war games, and a brief look at punk.
