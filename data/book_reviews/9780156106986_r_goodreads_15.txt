This was a pretty decent introduction book to learning about Judaism. I felt like it covered quite a bit of the main concepts, which I think can be really helpful for people who want to learn more - it was not as intimidating as it seemed. As someone who did not really grow up being raised with religion, I found the book to be understandable for the most part - I did have to look some things up just because I do lack some basic knowledge when it comes to religion as a whole. I think what stood out for me was the balanced discussion between traditionalists and modernists - I can imagine that it would be tempting to lean a certain way, but I thought that Steinberg did a nice job of having a balanced approach.
