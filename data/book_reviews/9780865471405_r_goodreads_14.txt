TOTEMPOLE is an episodic novel written in eight sections, and one I had been looking forward to reading for a long time. Unfortunately, the last two sections disassociate from the previous six in such a jarring, sudden way that I ended up drifting off, and not enjoying the last 150 pages of the book. I enjoyed the "young" Stephen much more than the 22 year old Stephen. I would have given the book more stars if it had just stayed on the trajectory of the first 200 pages. This is all just a matter of "taste" however, and the brevity of the writing is impressive, even at 400+ pages. The last section "Rats" is incredibly powerful, but the Stephen in "Rats" doesn't feel like the Stephen in any other section of the book. I really wish "Rats" had been its own novel.I highly recommend the Afterword by Peter Cameron. Make sure to read that first to get a better understanding of the novel's function and history.
