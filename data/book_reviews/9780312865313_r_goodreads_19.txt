I'm a huge fan of Prehistoric fiction. Think Jane Auel and Clan of the Cave Bear. Having a background in Southwestern Archeology, though, I prefer the works of Kathleen O'Neal Gear and W. Michael Gear. Their expansive bibliography takes readers to prehistoric time periods across North America. With the added benefit that they are also professional Archeologists. The Visitant is the first in a trilogy of books that combines contemporary Mystery/Thriller fiction with their prehistoric fiction in a dual timeline structure that explores the sometimes violent transition from Chacoan Great House culture to the Puebloan Kachina cult. I really enjoyed what amounts to a detective/serial killer story in the prehistoric southwest. The series has recently been re-released under six title, splitting each of the old books in half. The plot qnd characters are same, so nothing is missed either way. Easy recommendation for readers interested in archeology, ancient culture, and something a little different in their mystery thrillers.
