How wonderful to reread this 30 years after I first read it (as an 11-year-old!) and to love it even more now. I read most of it lying in winter sunshine on the back deck at mum's house, and italmostfelt like I was on a cruise ship on the Nile like the three children. I was in Jordan and Israel only a few years ago and skipped Egypt for some reason, but now a Nile cruise is a must, if only to visit all the amazing archaeological wonders described in this story. The perfect, timeless blend of Ancient Egypt and swashbuckling Indiana Jones-style adventures and problem-solving. LOVED it!
