This is the third book in the "Tunnels of Time" series. By far, this is favorite in the series. The author uses amazing imagery, which helps you to get a feeling of the suspense and mystery in this novel. When a author uses imagery the correct way, it makes you feel like you ar a part of the book and that is exactly how I felt while reading. I recommend this book to people who enjoy mystery and adventure, and if you want an easier read.
