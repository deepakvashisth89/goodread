Another of Majzlik's vegan books, this time of Caribbean recipes. The introduction before the recipes gives some background of food influences and history, then a list of what to have in the pantry/storecupboard, followed by 100+ recipes. No photographs, but the recipes are fairly easy-to-make looking, even if some ingredients are not familiar or easily available to me.

Some of what I liked the look of:
Spiced chickpea & peanut dip, Corn fritters, Peanut- & bean-stuffed tomatoes, Spicy lentil soup, Coconut & ginger soup, Chickpea gumbo, Cornmeal dumplings, Jamaican patties, Macaroni bake, Baked stuffed cabbage leaves, Sweet pepper & garlic rice, Mushroom & chickpea pullau, Red rice, Stir-fried pak choi with almonds, Potato & tomato salad, Nutty bean salad, Mango & ginger chutney, Mango salsa, Peanut & pepper sauce, Pineapple & coconut brulée, Orange & almond pudding, Pineapple & coconut buns, Peanut punch.

This book's recipes brought a great Caribbean feel to me with its fruits, vegetables, spices, hot pepper sauce, and nuts. So much good stuff in here, even without any pictures; well worth it.
