What an interesting concept--this is a collection of quotes simply because the author wanted to collect quotes. (I'm really not sure this would get published these days [this is from '82], unless the quote-collector was Tina Fey or someone.)But it is a neat, quick read. Byrne's foreword and explanation are funny and slightly snarky; I was absolutely on board for his quotes because he seems like an interesting dude. And I wasn't disappointed. The quotes (arranged in an order that makes sense to him, not necessarily one that makes sense to us) are generally dry and biting in their humor, which is right up my alley. This is meant to be read like a book rather than consulted like a dictionary, and I really appreciated that. I found some real gems here.All in all, a great diversion for a lazy afternoon.
