Third read, this time David West’s translation which made it easier to see more of the story nuances.A few months back I read Robert Fagles’ verse translation. This is wonderful in how it brings the scenes to life, where in that second read, I saw a more complex Aeneas, I’m not sure if I like him but I understand better the duty he is bound to and his love for Dido.In some ways the story is fascinating in how it doesn’t quite gel together, and there are pages where I am engrossed in the drama: I could feel Troy burn, Dido’s heart breaking, and the pains and sorrows of war.My least favourite part is how this bristles with propaganda; what continues to puzzle me ishow could there be so many deaths when it was fated? But this won’t stop me from reading this again.
