This marks the start of a series of 24 novels featuring Asey Mayo published between 1931 and 1951. It was the author's first detective story and I think that is evident from the rather simple plot device she uses to "hide" the murderer of an obnoxious author of "modern" books.The setting in Cape Cod is vividly rendered and there are some equally colourful, if overdone, characters.Like many of its US contemporaries, it is over-wordy and just a little too self-consciously smart.Mayo is a likeable, unlikely investigator, perhaps verging on being a caricature of the ordinary guy of extraordinary perceptions and intelligence.Although I found this very easy of solution and the writing at times irritating, I shall try another.3.25 stars
