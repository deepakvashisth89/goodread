Hilarious and informative for all the wrong reasons, this book teaches native as well as foreign users of the English language, the ways of efficient swearing.Of course it was the title of the book that got my attention and I read it out of curiosity. The book is extremely funny and had me laughing many times but reading the book continuously for some time, it tends to get repetitive. So I had to put the book down for a while, but reading it again didn't have the same effect as before.
