Tough to love this book which I read decades after the series aired. At its best it captures the dynamics and dialog of our favorite characters. Xander being the best of the bunch.But the story drags after a while, with an immortal monster of the week who quickly becomes tedious. Then several superfluous characters are added (Terry & Queenie) and it all culminates with a disappointing Boss Battle with a ridiculous deus ex machina twist. I'm sorry, why are there spirits in the woods?The fact that the Scoobies failed to trace Veronique's phone call to her hideout ... that just drove me nuts.I'm not sure who I'd recommend this book to.
