Historical English romance with a (secret) twin to replace the other in an engagement to a Lord.Earl of Linley's engagement to Meg is easily replaced by Maeve because Meg is super shy (social anxiety after the childhood loss of her twin sister?) and no one really knows her except the Vicar Richard who figures out the switch quickly although the Earl is clueless. Maeve and Meg have opposite personalities, one is sarcastic and open while the other is super shy and docile.The usual romance occurs with the Earl for the replacement, Maeve and Meg for the vicar best friend, Richard.96 pages, but felt more like 150 and a kindle freebie2 stars
