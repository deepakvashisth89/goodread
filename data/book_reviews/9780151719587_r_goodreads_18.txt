I found this a tough book to get into.Firstly, the layout. No chapters and paragraphs can last a page, or even more. Personally, I like chapters. For a start, if you have limited reading time, they are a good place to pause. Secondly, often the tone or another aspect of the book will shift, slightly, re-igniting interest and preventing boredom.The second problem I had initially was with the way it was written. Clearly, Boris was a talented writer - no quibble there. But his style is so full of prose and metaphor that the message or main point is often missed. I found my mind wandering as it would take so long to get to the point. I have diagnosed ADD so appreciate that this is not something that everyone will experience.Once I got used to these issues and taught myself to read and re-read and pay a bit more attention, what was written had incredible impact. An overly poetic way of story telling may not be my preferred reading material but, when the points did arrive, as they came out of such flowery language, they had much more impact.If you can get over the structure and style, you will find an account that can be more shocking than my usually preferred factual approach.
