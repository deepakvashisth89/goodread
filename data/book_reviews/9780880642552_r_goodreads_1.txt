What a waste of time this book was, Elvis Costello deserves better. It reads like a cut and paste of various articles from a few different magazines and interviews, slammed together and poorly written. I could get much more from Wikipedia, really do not recommend this "biography". I can't even call it a biography really, it was more a song list with the history of the song with a few personal life items thrown in, not many. Very disappointing book, there must be better biographies of Elvis out there, this is not one of them. Terrible.
