(FROM JACKET)....Central to this haunting (and sometimes haunted) novel are the mothers, a study in contrast in strength and rigidity, Ruby Staples and Eula Smalls, and their children: LaRue Smalls, adventurer, storyteller, and chronicler of his people; his sister Vesta, intimidated by life from an early age, yet determined, valiant even, to hold her disparate family together; and Ouida Staples, a rare beauty who elects, in the face on convention, to spend her life with another woman. Each will face trialsand challenges and sometimes be transformed, shedding like the serpent, and old skin, reborn by the art of invention....
