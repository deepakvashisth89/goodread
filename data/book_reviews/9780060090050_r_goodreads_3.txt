Well written and very touching! Reading the book was like watching a war theme movie. It tells a shocking story through a young girl's eyes to show what wars can bring to human beings. I actually cried when I read about Gabriela's little brother who was found dead painfully yet peacefully during hiding from the soldiers. And it really terrified me by the genocide happened in Guatemala. I was tortured to read through that paticular chapter of massacres, which made me want to throw up.( And all the description of military atrocities constantly reminded me of the Nanking Massacre commited by the Japanese soldiers during WW2.) I also felt so sad to read about the lives of refugees in the camps--scrounging for food, water and blankets and kept waiting for peace while not knowing how long they still needed to wait.It seems to me that I haven't got luck for months. Thanks to this book. I realized AGAIN how lucky I am just to be born and grew up in a country that has no war.It's a great book, but I hope my next reading can be something bright and happy coz I cannot stand continuing reading sad books. I'll be crushed by that...At last, Salute to the real tree gril!
