In this little book, Virginia Woolf's style is revealed through her reflections. We must both let go to follow it and stay focused to fully grasp the scope of what it defends, a considerable range in 1928, when Great Britain was barely out of the Elizabethan shackles.It is dense, and the demonstration is masterful in its depiction of all women's obstacles to exercising their creativity and making a living from their art. Humor and irony add to the subject's relevance, making it delightful to read.Reviewed the masculine preconceived ideas, in particular among the authors, the social and financial shackles, and the attempts to counter any feminine initiative outside the assigned role: I now understand the fundamental place of Virginia Woolf in modern feminism.
