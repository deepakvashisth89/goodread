More like a society book than a murder. It reads with the same feeling of an Evelyn Waugh, but with a murder thrown in almost incidentally. I was interested in the author due to his neurological condition (alexia sine aphasia), but I don't think I'll continue with the series. What was refreshing was that it didn't have profanity, and for the most part, it was clean. Those are both difficult to find in airport/grocery lit. If there were nothing else around, I'd read another, but I don't see myself as motivated to pick up book 2 for any other reason.
