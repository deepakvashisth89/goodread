So I read the second edition of the same book which came out around a decade later and changes were made.

Just from an editing standpoint this book is really well made. It is concise and simple. No doubt having so much time between editions really helped.

As to the material, Robert Bruce puts forward many interesting ideas about astral projection, lucid dreaming, remote viewing, etc.

As to whether or not the techniques work, I do not know, they are common enough to astral projection. Though I must say, from the people who I have heard who have the ability, they never did any practice, it simply occurred.
