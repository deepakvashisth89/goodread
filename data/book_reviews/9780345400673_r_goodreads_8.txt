Seattle P.I. Thomas Black and his attorney wife Kathy have a wild one on their hands. “Snake” Slezak a fellow private investigator and friend has woken them with a panicked call in the middle of the night. There is a dead woman in his bed. Slezak says he didn’t kill her, but he also insists that the dead woman is an alien and not the kind from a foreign country!In THE MILLION DOLLAR TATOO Emerson shows how perspective can be manipulated so that the truth is tough to see. If you want a trip back in time to the 1990’s (people are using floppy discs and the Kingdome is still standing) combined with a complex and at times wacky murder mystery this is the book for you.Show more
