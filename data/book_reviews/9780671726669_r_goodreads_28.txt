First off, the copy of the book I read had 801 pages not 500, and added testimonies from Dan and Linda's friends in the back because the author didn't live long enough to see parole.

The author, Bella Stumbo, wrote the most comprehensive, most detailed and researched book about Betty Broderick and Dan Broderick and their lives up until the divorce and then after the killings and documented the trials meticulously.

Bella did such a good job- I hadn't heard or read a lot of those recorded calls between Betty and her children and when I did, perhaps with a little push from the author's own opinion, I changed my perspective on Betty entirely in terms of being a victim. Her children were the victims, as well as Dan and Linda's families, as well as Betty's family.

I believe that the court house and judges, the attorneys should have seen and noted that they were dealing with a woman who was being irrational and dangerous. Betty was not in the right mind. When she ran her vehicle into Dan's home (her children being inside) they should have then given her 41/40 and had her locked up against her will. They could have established that Betty was not well enough to take care of her affairs and given her a guardianship or even conservatorship in order to ensure she had a proper representation and fair chance in court up until the murders. After the murders, it should have been glaringly obvious that she needed a guardian, that she was out of her mind.

It took two (or perhaps three) for this tango and the truth is Dan and Linda didn't do Betty any favors knowing the state of her mental health. If it was true what people were saying - that they both had explicitly told others they were going to drive Betty crazy from the legal system - they ultimately go what they wanted. I am afraid Betty had started to lose it prior to the court hearings - her identity was being a wife and a mother and when that was taken away, she could no longer recollect any emotional intelligence.

The pain and horror she put her children through during this is unimaginable. I would never dare say those things to my children, it's not their fault any of this happened. And then to say she killed their father for them! She is lucky that any of them visit her and have contact.

I do believe if Dan hadn't been an admirable attorney in San Diego , or an attorney at all, that most of the court harassment wouldn't have been legal or possible at all. It is about who you know, and I think we all can agree to that in our daily lives.

Betty never showed remorse this states- and perhaps she never honestly did, which also proves she has some mental issues here because she still speaks of the dead as if they were alive. Betty cracked when her identity cracked and there was no coming back from that without intense rehabilitation.

I believe Betty and Dan both hold responsibilities in this mess but that didn't condone his murder or Linda's. There were so many other solutions that would have kept everyone alive. The most important being, though Dan was harassing her in court, he should have had her committed for 30 days or longer to a mental institution, though he honestly never believed his ex wife would hurt him, he believed she loved the money when in fact she loved being his wife.

This book is full of information you haven't read anywhere before and I highly recommend it. Bella Stumbo was on top of her game and I wish I could meet her.

I also wonder if Betty, now in her late 70s, has any remorse or fear of meeting Dan and Linda on the other side. I think I'll write her a letter.
