Outside of the crazy amount of typos and an interesting bent on tense, this book was exactly what it said it would be, an introduction to the four largest sects of Buddhism that even an idiot could understand. Personally, I found the entries on Pure Land Buddhism to be the most interesting, also the description of Vajrayana Buddhism. Both contain elements that I've always treasured in my own life, and it's interesting to see a take on concepts I hold dear in my life, but honored by a whole community of believers.
