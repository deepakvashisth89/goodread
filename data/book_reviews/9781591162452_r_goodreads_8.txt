This reviews stands for the whole series. While not perfect I have yet to read a series (particularily an anime/manga) series with as much REAL depth as this one. It may start out simple (and perhaps even slightly amateur) but it quickly grows into an all-encompasisng series that covers every moral and philisophical issue on the spectrum, all without seeming cheap. With a strikingly original fantasy world and "Inferno" level-depth (which is clearly a major inspiration here), this series made me laugh, cringe, gawk, and FEEL. The latter most takes a great deal of effort and the artistry of both the story and the illustrations is almost beyond comprehension. I have no idea how the author juggled all of this and still made it work and without having it fall apart as the series progressed.
