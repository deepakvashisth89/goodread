This was quite good. As the cover blurb stated, the Manciple family was quite eccentric and a force to reckon with. When sudden death (murder?) strikes, it’s up to Chief Superintendent Henry Tibbett of Scotland Yard to make that determination, and if murder, to identify and arrest the killer. Patricia Moyes’ mysteries would never be classified as “cozies” but when set in the countryside as this one is, they cerainly are “English village mysteries.”And huzzah for that, the reading is pleasant and easy, the depictions of village life quite alluring if a bit closed (to outsiders), and the wide variety of characters living there give plenty of scope for weaving a good story. Moyes does just that.
