A thorough account of 1st Minnesota's service in the Civil War. Moe crafts a wonderful narrative around the correspondence of the different soldiers that made up the regiment. He mainly uses primary sources which gives the book a sense of intimacy not found in "grander" war histories. By allowing the soldiers to the tell the story themselves he becomes separate from the role of author and becomes merely an instrument for the reader to directly engage with these wonderful, brave, and real characters. A rare occurrence for most historical texts.

The bravery of these men should never be forgotten and Moe has done his part to make sure that doesn't happen. Every Minnesotan and "yankee" should read this book.
