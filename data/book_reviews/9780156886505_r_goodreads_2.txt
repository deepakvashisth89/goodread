Reading this new edition, October 2010.The consensus? This book is as charming as I remember. Edie and her rather exuberant siblings take up residence in their aunt's seaside house for the summer, and all sort of adventures ensue. Some of the language (mostly sailing terms) is a bit old-fashioned or may go a bit above the heads of younger children, but otherwise Edie is just a regular ten-year-old and this book will still delight modern children.Read aloud to the kids, 2015. They weren't as into as I had hoped. It does use a lot of old-fashioned words, and a lot of nautical terms. I want to find a copy of EDIE ON THE WARPATH, though, and read it to them. I think they'll like her marching in parades with the suffragettes.
