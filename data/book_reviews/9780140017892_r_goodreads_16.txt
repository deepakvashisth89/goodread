At one time in my life I read a lot of Grahame Greene, I don't know precisely when but it must have been in late autumn or winter because my memory of so many is dreary, rain on the window panes, dark, action played out in black and white. An alien mind with a curious if twisted consistency. A feeling of inevitable betrayal and fear of failure inThe Confidential Agent. Relationships here or inThe Quiet Americanas promising a particularly dreary doom. A decaying post war feeling that suggests a world of defeat inBrighton Rockrather than survival and new opportunity.Perhaps if I read them again I'd be surprised at bright colours and flashes of optimism closed to the characters but open to the reader?
