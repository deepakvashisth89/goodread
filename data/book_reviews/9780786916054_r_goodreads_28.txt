I'm giving up on Salvatore.This book features all the things I hate about his writing: the main character is as bland as a slice of white bread while the sidekick is the exciting, over-powered as **** character Salvatore really wanted to write about (remember that Drizzt was originally conceived as Wulfgar's sidekick), there's a laughable attempt at moralizing (Cadderly is traumatized by killing another human, although he and the others kill hundreds of orcs, goblins and ogres, but that'sfine). The plot is particularly straight forward and completely lacking in twists or surprises. Cadderly, Danica and the annoying dwarf brothers have to help elves that are being attacked by thousands of monsters. The language was particularly bad; I dislike authors writing about characters 'shooting' or 'firing' bows (you can't fire bows, you fire guns) but variations on the sentence 'she popped another arrow into the orc' happened repeatedly, and every time I wanted to scream, it's such an awful phrase. And Salvatore cannot do humour. At all.I read a lot of the Drizzt books when I was younger, but I'm not going to force myself through any more of these novels.
