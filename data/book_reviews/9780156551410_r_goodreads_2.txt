Once opened, I couldn't close the book until I finished it, if this is not to say the particular atmosphere that Simenon managed to create in this Maigret.A magnificent opus where Maigret goes to a Vendée village drowned in fog, and that's how its inhabitants and guests would like Maigret to be and stay. This fact is obviously not considered in light of our investigator's good-natured insight.A perfect moment of relaxation!
