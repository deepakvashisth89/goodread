Far and away, the best book on creative thinking that I have ever come across. I don't think anything else compares with it. It's a comprehensive mixture of left-brained and right-brained techniques for thinking creatively, and you will be able to find a dozen useful exercises for expanding your thinking, no matter what you do. There is also a great card deck you can buy that breaks down many of the best techniques in a more digestible form, but I suggest getting a copy of this as well.I'll give one of my favorite examples, just to give you an idea of the type of content it has. The SCAMPER technique is a tool that you can use systematically to change or improve any product, idea, or service. The letters in the SCAMPER acronym stand for Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to another use, Eliminate, and Reverse. The book lays out a method for going through each of these as you are exploring, and it is extremely practical and useful, no matter what field you are in. If you struggle with creative thinking or really want to come up with some new ideas, but this book. Highly Recommended.
