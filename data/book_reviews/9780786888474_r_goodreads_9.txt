"You know how people are--they like to get to the root cause of everything and then trash it." "One's only real regret in life is the failure to act." "The older I get, the more I can't bear hearing about sad things. And, of course, the irony is, the older one gets, the more sad things one hears about." "Actually, I think happiness becomes more of a decision as one gets older. I think at some point you just decide you're going to be happy with what you've got." "A violent act pierces the atmosphere, leaving a hole through which the cold, damp draft of its memory blows forever." "I've come to realize one spends most of one's life eating, sleeping and missing the point!" "A fact is merely an event: it happened, it didn't happen.
