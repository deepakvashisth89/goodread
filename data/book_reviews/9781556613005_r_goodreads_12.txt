As the war between the States escalates into full-scale conflict threatening to tear the nation apart, Corrie Belle Hollister finds herself far from her home and thrust into the center of the battle. She was invited by President Lincoln to visit Washington and help with his reelection campaign and writes regular newspaper articles about her experiences on the front helping the wounded. She unwittingly overhears a plot against General Grant that could change the course of the nation and she risks her life to get to him with the warning.
The story was rather unrealistic -- very light reading!
