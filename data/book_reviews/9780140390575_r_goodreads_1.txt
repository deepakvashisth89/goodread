This was a collection of early American stories by Nathaniel Hawthorne. These were absolutely interesting, thought-provoking, meaningful, and original stories. They focused on old Puritan & Calvinist values, original & generational sin, and witchcraft & dark emotions.

In my opinion, throughout his career Nathaniel Hawthorne was a masterclass storyteller. He delivers a great story filled with rich imagery and a Gothic atmosphere layered with specter evidence and me asking myself "Did that happen the way I read it?" Stories I will reread are:

Young Goodman Brown
My Kinsman, Major Molineux
The Minister's Black Veil
Mr. Higginbotham's Catastrophe
The Birth-mark
The Celestial Rail-road
Rappacini's Daughter

The prose is outdated at times and can be difficult to follow, but its worth the effort in the end. I have read and . The first I thoroughly enjoyed and the latter I did not. Having said that, these were all short stories which were great. I would recommend this anthology to anyone who enjoys early American literature. Thanks!
