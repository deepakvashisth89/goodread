This is a story about a dysfunctional family who gets together to celebrate Chanukah. Two of the characters in the story enter into a shouting match; as a result a latke is launched out the window and begins to circle to planet as a UFO for eight days. This dysfunctional family is forced together when the FBI comes to investigate. This book was one of the dumber books I have read this semester. The pictures are comprised of real photographs and drawings, which would be the only redeeming quality of the book. It was just awful. True I might be able to teach a little something to a class about Chanukah, but it would be a pointless waste of time because it is just that awful.
