A beautifully curated exploration of a nearly forgotten tradition: the personalized bookplate. Tracing its origins and evolution, the book offers not only a visual feast of exquisite designs but also thoughtful commentary on the cultural and historical moments that made various styles fashionable.What sets this volume apart is its balance of scholarship and storytelling. Keenan provides clear, engaging explanations of different nameplates and their stylistic periods, while also weaving in delightful side stories—unexpected glimpses into the lives of lesser-known bibliophiles whose love for books inspired them to mark their libraries in deeply personal ways.As someone who enjoys collecting and discovering bookplates, I found this book both informative and resonant. It reminded me how much a simple mark of ownership can reveal about a person’s identity, taste, and values. Whether etched in intricate detail or stamped in modest type, each bookplate tells a story—not just about the book, but about the reader who claimed it.For collectors, designers, or anyone with a love of books and the culture surrounding them, The Art of the Bookplate is a rewarding and charming read.
