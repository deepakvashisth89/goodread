What I love about McMurtry is how unassuming the writing is. At first glance it's maddeningly simplistic, formatted storytelling. After a while, characters start to come alive and suddenly a description or a piece of dialogue flips you sideways and the book becomes incredibly thoughtful and profound all in a moment's notice. It's vanilla ice cream with tiny pieces of rich, dark chocolate haha. It was exactly what I loved about Lonesome Dove.It's a light, wholesome read about love, life and Texas cowboys. Would recommend for anyone who needs more Yee-haw in their reading routine.
