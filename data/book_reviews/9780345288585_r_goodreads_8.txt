Once you've read more of Mr. Chandler's books, a pattern becomes more than obvious: there is a girl, usually quite pretty , smart or rather poor-minded, who is in some trouble.Someone is blackmailing her and the detective's (by the way, in this story he even has not a name!?!) mission is to stop it. Much drinking, a lot of smoking (we are in the thirties...) even more shooting, many casualties, the hero remains alive and the girl too...
