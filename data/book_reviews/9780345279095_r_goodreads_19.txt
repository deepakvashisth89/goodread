This is the work that made Mary Gordon's name...don't know how I missed it but I was raised 3 little kids when it was published.If you grew up in a Catholic household in the 50's and 60's, you read about familiar situations. I found this book very real and reflective.I especially liked the character of Father Mulcahy...he had a drinking problem but he had a big heart and good theology.Our heroine barely escaped her situation but she did and that's the uplifting message.Highly recommended.
