This is certainly a cute and funny read for children. I grew up reading some of the Amelia Bedelia books when in school.This story is entertaining as far as the quirky miscommunications go, Amelia Bedelia seems to have a state of mind as a child would. Her understanding of certain jobs is an example of how children can hear adults talking without understanding what they are talking about.This is an easy read for children ages 5 to 8. I feel this would be a fun read for that age group depending on their reading level.The artwork and illustrations in the book are very easy to understand with the text and go along with how Amelia Bedelia understands certain things. The artwork is very easy to entertain children while reading the short story.
