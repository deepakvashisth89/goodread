Since  earliest manhood the center of his life has been pleasure with women, the giving and taking of it,  not with weak indulgence, dependency, but with the power and pride of a richly feathered male bird among hens. Branching out from this complete and satisfying center are all the auxiliary  channels of his life, such as his heartiness with men, his appreciation of rough humor, his love of  good drink and food and games, his car, his radio, everything that is his, that bears his emblem of  the gaudy seed-bearer.
