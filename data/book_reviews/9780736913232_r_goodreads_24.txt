My local used bookstore is closing (so sad) and I had almost $200 worth of credit in books saved up. So, I stuffed three bags full of books--and got one that I'd already read.When I started reading this book, I remembered the heroine right away--the rest of the plot not so much. I never really fell for the hero--too dictatorial for me, even if he was a duke--but the heroine was amazing. She sets up the role model for a Christian woman, even before she fully committed to the Lord.So, yes, I recommend this book--just be prepared to be challenged in your own walk.Which is always a good thing.
