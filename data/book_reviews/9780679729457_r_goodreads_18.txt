Caro's research, insight, and writing ability turn what, in other hands, may have been a few moderately interesting introductory chapters into an 800+ page masterpiece.  The Path to Power covers only the first 32 years of Lyndon Johnson's life.  A period during which he grew up in poverty, went to a barely accredited teacher's college, worked as a legislative aide to a Congressman, and was eventually elected to Congress himself.  The book ends with LBJ's first unsuccessful run for the Senate.  Caro looks beyond these not especially momentous events to reveal a fascinating individual who--in whatever setting he finds himself--manages to identify an unused lever of power and to wield it expertly in furtherance of his own ends.  Caro tells this story with a brilliance that lets the reader visualize it better than any movie.  The result is a bona fide page-turner that is as compelling and perceptive as most great novels.
