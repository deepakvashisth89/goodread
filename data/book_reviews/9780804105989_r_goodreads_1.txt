This book was interesting and informative book, although a little dated as it was written in the 1980's. It is focuses on the what goes on at one major New York hospital whose specialty is treating people with neurological issues. It gives insights on the operations from the Doctor's and patient's point of view and is very reader friendly with very little technical language. I learned a lot and would recommend this for the reader who just wants to get basic information on brain surgery and how the brain handles language and other basic functions necessary for communication and for a person to function on a daily basis.
