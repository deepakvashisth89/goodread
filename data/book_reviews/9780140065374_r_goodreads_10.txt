I felt like I had to force myself to speed read this book - I wanted to see who-done-it, so I finished the book. Written in the 1970's, I do not think in this day of the internet the situation could even set-up for the murder. Yes, the author pokes fun at the individuals in academia, large rural Australian landowners and the police. He doesn't play favorites.One would think that in England and Australia the same language is spoken, but this book plays on differences in slang and local jargon. I can only imagine that 50 years ago these language differences were exaggerated and unknown.As I mentioned earlier, the internet has leveled the playing field. Knowledge and information flows much more easily - both ways.The police and others can not get away with what they did in those days, imho.
