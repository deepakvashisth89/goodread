While I enjoyed this Asey Mayo mystery, it was confusing. There were too many characters and it was difficult keeping straight who was who(m).That said, I found the premise to be delightful as far as a mystery/who-dun-it: several people in a rather large house on Cape Cod, trapped by a sudden snow storm, the electricity and phones go out and they are cut off from the outside world. Things get worse when the owner of the house turns up dead the next morning due to arsenic poisoning...and everyone in the house is discovered to have arsenic among their personal effects!I highly recommend these cozy mysteries by Ms. Taylor, and am looking forward to reading all 24 in the series!
