This book is about how Mel Jensen moves to a new school and tries to fit in with the basketball team. He does really well, except for one problem- two of the boys ignore him like no other. They act like he isn't there and that bothers Mel. He is conflicted on whether or not to be kind to them or to do the same thing as they are doing to him and ice them out. In the end, it is the basketball team that will always matter.I liked this book. It is a great book for both boys and girls alike.
