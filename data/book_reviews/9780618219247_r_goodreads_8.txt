Steve Earle plays a mean guitar, he's a great songwriter, he's an admirable man, and his political views are outstanding. I've watched him on cable. I've seen him play live. I mumbled a hello once in person. Still I wondered - does this make him a writer I'd want to read? And the answer was yes.Doghouse Rosesis a collection of short stories. Most of them are good, or at least okay. Earle can write. He's got a style that works. I didn't find that the collection on a whole sustained my interest - but after I read the first story: Doghouse Roses, it didn't matter. I really didn't need to read any more. He could have just published that story and I would have been satisfied. It is a beautiful piece of writing. Maybe that's why the rest of the book left me flat? The first 26 pages was all I needed.
