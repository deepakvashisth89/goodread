Alison's father has been accused of subversive activity against the World Government. This results in the whole family being arrested and taken to a remote rehabilitation camp. At first Alison is hopeful that this will benefit her family and that her parents will now have the time to spend with her and her young brother Gordie. She soon realizes that is not going to happen. In fact, her parents are even more disinterested in the children than they were before. To make matters worse, Gordie is acting and talking strangely too. What will become of her family? Can she do anything to save them? And by the way, where on Earth are they?

Monica Hughes is one of my most favorite authors of science-fiction for children. Her books are so fun and interesting, yet easy to read.
