Top-Notch!! This was a wonderfully romantic story of two people who really needed each other but needed to let go of their pasts first. I fell in love with Laurel and Brett right from the beginning. Their story is thoughtful and sweet and makes you wanted to just stop and pause for a moment. It was thought provoking, sweet, entertaining and fun. This was the first book I read in this series and when I finished it I went and immediately purchased the first book so I could catch up and can't wait for the next story to come out!!
