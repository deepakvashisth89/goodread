I read this Christmas anthology about a 10 days ago, intending to write a review within a few days. With the exception of Elaine Coffman’s Under The Mistletoe, I found the remaining short stories easily forgettable.Under The Mistletoe had a Scrooge-like psychiatrist come up against a thrice-married widow. Naturally, he had never married. When he found out his neighbor had seven daughters, a cat and two well-meaning but slightly cranky caregivers, it was war. The best part of this novella was that it took place over several months rather than a few days. It gave me a chance to get to know this odd family and the bachelor, Stanley Levine.3 1/2 stars
