This book is a huge, monstrous beast -- the good kind. I keep it on the bottom shelf next to my compact OED, OCD (Oxford Classical Dictionary, not Obsessive Compulsive Disorder), Greek English Lexicon (the big Liddell).Where do I even begin to describe the contents of this gigantic resource? It contains 65 chapters organized into 11 sections, including popular ideas about language, the structure of language, the medium of language (speaking and listening), signing and seeing, child language acquisition, "language, brain and disability, languages of the world, language in the world, and language and communication.every conceivable issue connected with language is covered in this book.
