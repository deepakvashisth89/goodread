This version of the book was very fun to read because half of these pages consist of a sentence followed by a page of pictures. You can physically see the lamb following Mary everywhere. This allows the children to see that the words are describing the pictures and the pictures can be in written in sentences. Since there are not many words on each page, this makes a great book for beginning readers.5. a) An in-class use for this book would be for a read aloud.
