This book is a great piece on Stalin and how he came about to his dictatorial rule. From Stalin as a young boy to the age of 50, this book paints a vivid picture on how this tyrant rose from nothing to supreme rule. What sets this book apart from other books is the dive into Stalin's personality rather than just a retelling of history. Detailing the events is simple, but attempting to explain them adds a deeper level of complexity that is executed well.Overall, this book is a great read and I thoroughly enjoyed it.
