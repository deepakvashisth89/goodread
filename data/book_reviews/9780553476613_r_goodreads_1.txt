King certainly has a way with words! I can’t believe I’d not read this sooner. Highly recommend the audiobook version, as I immensely enjoyed this collection of short creepy tales! The audiobook is only about 4 hours long. By far my favorite was the first, The Lawnmower Man. It was both creepy and comical!! Also loved the line in the last story…something along the lines of being shocked as if a priest slapped you right after taking communion.😂
