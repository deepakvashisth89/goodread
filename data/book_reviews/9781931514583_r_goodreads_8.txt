review for the entire manga:

(4.5 stars)

AMAZING, probably the best pure romance manga i’ve read so far. the characters aren’t die-worthy but, i still really liked them, their dynamics, and their trauma and everything. even the side characters were good! sometimes the plot irked me with a lot of drama (especially in the beginning) but, it was just a lot in the beginning and then, it was fine. i really like the exploration of trauma and how… fucked-up they were. the romance started off fast but, it ended up really moving me. so good. the art in this is neat - nothing too much, but nice.
