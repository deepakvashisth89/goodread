Gripping short story about two roaming farm workers, a sort of Asterix & Obelix setup (a short smart man and a huge innocent hulk who can't always handle his strength), but treated in drama rather than comedy. The characters are surprisingly nuanced, given the short form. I found the chapter headings somewhat spoilery, but maybe that's just my particular edition.P.S.: I accidentally picked up a school edition where the language was straightened out, from Steinbeck's vernacular to "proper English". Bummer!
