This very unassuming slim paperback book is so lovely!! It absolutely lives up to its name as having "the best Christmas stories ever"!This nostalgic collection includes:🌟 The Little Match-Girl by Hans Christian Andersen🌟 The Birds' Christmas Carol by Kate Douglas Wiggin🌟 The Gift of the Magi by O. Henry🌟 The Fir-Tree by Hans Christian Andersen🌟 A Christmas Dream and How It Came True by Louisa May AlcottIt's a quick, festive and hopeful book that is perfect to start reading a day or two before Christmas. Since the stories are abridged (sadly), these stories are much more approachable for younger children (I'm sure that was the idea of this collection) to either read or listen to.Despite the strange, thick yellow border of the Apple Classics edition, the colorful illustration on the front is very cozy and cute. I only wish you could get an edition with either red, green or even white border instead of yellow.Highly recommended and so fun to read. I immediately felt uplifted after reading this book. A perfect choice for a vintage Children's book collection!
