Penn Cage is a famous author with his own personal demons, after unsuccessfully trying to move on from the unexpected death of his wife, he takes his young daughter Annie home to Natchez, Mississippi to be with his family. Natchez brings back memories that Penn didn't expect, chief among those is a 30 year old murder motivated by racial tension, politics, and class warfare in 1968. The mystery aspect was interesting, although slightly predictable and not nearly as exciting as all the attempted murder plot points inserted throughout the book.While there was lots of action, and some lovable characters, the book left me wanting more from the story. It dragged on in parts, and could have been cut down significantly. Penn Cage is an engaging character and one I found myself rooting for, however the ending was a little too complex and overly wrought for my liking.
