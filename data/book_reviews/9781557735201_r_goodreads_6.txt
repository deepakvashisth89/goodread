This book was sent to me after entering a Goodreads drawing.

The book was written in short sentences which after a time I found a bit irritating. However the story was interesting and the characters likable so it was a fast read. I especially liked the herbal gathering and making of medicines, teas, and fragrances. Ms. Sholes gives us a clear picture of what life was like in two Indian tribes of the new world. I do have an interest in reading the 2nd book.
