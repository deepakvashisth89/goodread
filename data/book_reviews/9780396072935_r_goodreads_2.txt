gr 4-6 96 pgs1925, Nome, Alaska. When diphtheria breaks out in this small isolated town, the town doctor knows that diphtheria serum is the town's only hope. But how will they get it there? With the dangerous weather conditions, their only hope is for a dog sled team to bring it. The story alternates between the experiences of the doctor and nurses as they care for their patients and the men and dogs participating in the relay dog sled race to bring the serum to Nome.
