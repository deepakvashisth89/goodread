Honestly, I didn't think that it would be that good. I thought it would just show the surface level of how a cult works and give some kind of generic story. It wasn't that deep but it actually gave a good and compelling story about how cults tend to get to vulnerable people and how it can affect them. I just kind of wish it was darker.
Maybe that's why I wasn't that comfortable with the ending. I didn't really feel like a conclusion. It just felt like some things were missing.
I still recommend that book though. It's an interesting read
