With inspiration from Bram Stoker’s classicalDracula, this is the story of a family’s twisted and morbid legacy.At times eerie and macabre, there’s a wonderful sense of foreboding from the beginning. Even though you are well aware of what is happening behind the scenes, you can’t help but be fascinated, waiting for the final moment when you will come face to face with the monster(s).The ending leaves you with an idea of what is to come, and never to conclude for Michael, Count Mihai Vlahuta.It’s a fast and easy read—and thoroughly enjoyable.
