This book worries me about what can happen to an author who takes time off from writing. It's utter garbage.
Skeeve is transformed from an endearing apprentice into a horny junkie. Is this really the direction you want to follow him?
Every. Single. Plot. Point. Is advanced through contrivance and previously-unexplained magic. We can suck the magic out of a treasure map. The villain can cast "dimension lock." The assassin can cast "ziplock mouth." Everyone and anyone can cast the unheard-of spell that is required to advance the story to the next page.

I was binge-reading this (otherwise fantastic) series and this book was that one piece of expired sushi that has permanently put me off this author.
