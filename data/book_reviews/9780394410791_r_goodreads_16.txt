Audible 9 hours 15 min. Narrated by LJ Ganzer(A)This is an interesting look at the history of a 9 story apartment style building located across from Central Park in New York City, some of its more notable residents. On a wider scope ,it is a history of the city itself and a social commentary from 1885 through 1979. I'm not sure why this story attracted my attention, but it was definitely the narrator who kept my attention.
