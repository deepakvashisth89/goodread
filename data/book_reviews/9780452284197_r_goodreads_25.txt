3.5* This short book really packs a punch! A good story leaving you with a bit of a nebulous ending, but well worth it. The one major flaw is that the book is too short - more could have been added on various storylines. Oh well... All in all a good read with great characters (especially Jakob). If you want a MUCH better book by Benioff, readCity of Thieves(one of my all time favorites).
