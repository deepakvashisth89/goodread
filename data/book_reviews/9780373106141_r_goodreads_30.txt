A bit farcical but not bad. Especially the heroine's character.An orphan at 21-22 years of age cannot afford to be as foolish and good-for-nothing as this girl. Its a real miracle that the hero finds her at the right time and pulls her out of her melancholy.Jilted, obviously that's the premise. The hero'es younger brother jilts the heroine and casually marries her best friend. No malice intended, just that he found his partner in the friend and realized that the heroine is not right for him. Cant blame the guy really.Nevertheless, the heroine is attending the ex's wedding. Has one too many to drink to drown her sorrows. Bumps into the hero, who happens to be the ex's elder brother. A little flirting, a bit of dancing and they land up in bed.He is smitten at the word Go. That's his reason for the direct jump into bed. Her reason ? He resembles the ex !! So she lands up calling the hero by his brother's name.What a shame. Enough reason to attempt suicide. So that's exactly what she tries. Hero obviously wont stand and watch. He pulls her out of the suicide episode and whisks her off to exotic Brazil !?!?!? How you may ask ? Get married of course !!!!So they reach Brazil as man and wife. She is being thoroughly bitchy with him, without an ounce of gratitude for saving her life. He is hoping she is learn to 'love' him in due time.Idling in Brazil, some alone time with husband, some cliched amazon jungle experiences. And she is all healed and heart whole once again. This time when they get into bed she is clear its the hero, no confusion. Thank heavens !!!!A small silly misunderstanding follows. Return to safe and normal Britain. A quick confession, clearing of doubts. And HEA.Nothing memorable, just a silly girl and a patient man.
