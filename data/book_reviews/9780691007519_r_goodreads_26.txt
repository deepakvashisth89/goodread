Lefebvre stands as the strongest voice on the French Revolution in the vein of Progressive History - which centered economics and material conditions to tell its story. This type of history fell out of the mainstream in light of McCarthyism and the Red Scare - due to its focus on class conflict. It is an important perspective that you have to seek out, nobody is going to teach it to you these days. As an interesting side-note, this book was banned in Vichy France, and as you read it, ask yourself why Fascists are so threatened by this point of view.
