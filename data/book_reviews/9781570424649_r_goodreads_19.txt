I was really not expecting such a dark story from the Reading Rainbow legend himself. Written in the 90s but set in 2019, this hits a little too close to home with the current climate of America. After the assassination of the first Black man elected President, a civil war erupts and causes a major economic crisis throughout the states. After the war, major cities are in shambles along with the government, leaving the citizens on their own. This story follows four strangers whose fates seem to be mystically linked together. I found the plot to be very compelling. While none of the characters wow'd me, I stayed interested in their story the entire time.I do think this book was written ahead of its time and had it been written in say the last decade, could have truly flourished with some sensitivity readers and maybe another couple rounds of editing to help with some of the rough edges.
