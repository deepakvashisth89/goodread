This was a well-researched and largely coherent account of the aspects of masonic symbolism embedded within the opera, both in the libertto and the musical score, together with aspects of the directed staging.The musical analysis was largely beyond my understanding, however, I found some aspects of his analysis intriguing, although I wished he had devoted more time to his assertion that the opera is not as anti-feminist as it initially presents.The reality is, that the opera is a musical story, and if the story has flaws within the narrative arc, these flaws are bound to be represented in the performance. If you can only understand the narrative with an understanding of the latent symbolism, then in many ways the opera represents a failure of storytelling.I love the opera, and I love music. I want to understand it, and while this book gave rather illuminating insights, I still feel I am only a fraction closer to understanding the opera as I understood it before reading this book.
