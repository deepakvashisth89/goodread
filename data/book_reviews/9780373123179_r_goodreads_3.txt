This is probably closer to a 3 for others, but it checked off boxes for me that made it 4 stars.

Misunderstood rockstar✅
May/December romance✅
Opposites attract✅
Enemies/lovers✅

Ignore the title…I mean when I read the title I was thinking “oh his mistress obviously has one of those 9 month secrets brewing” Not at all. In fact she was never a mistress really and her secret about events was hardly earth shattering. Anyhoo….

Heroine, Tiree, 23 year old lead singer of a rock band. She dons her own version of a Hannah Montana disguise so she can have some sort of private life as she is the object of stalkers and reporters.
She is recently thrust in the limelight when 2 of her band mates are killed. One of the band mates was 19 year old Kit who is the step son to our hero.

Hero, Ewan Sinclair, 38 year old doctor. As another reviewer pointed out, he has a Darcy aura about him.
He isn’t a manwhore, but he does have occasional hookups with like minded women.

He believes the h is hiding something about the accident and he wants to know the truth. After calling her he tells her he is going to pay her a visit. The h freaks out thinking it could be another stalker and falls down the stairs. The Dr. H has to break into her house and takes her to the hospital.

What happens next is the unfolding of a lust/love story between two unlikely characters.

The orphan h has had a tough varied upbringing and has had to develop an outer shell for protection. She has her rock persona for the public, but she is nothing like that. She is attractive, smart, talented, and not promiscuous.

The H fell for an opposite once upon a time and married her(hence his step son). His love/lust for his flamboyant wife wore off quickly and they were at odds for most of their short lived marriage. Once in-between her affairs, they tried to make a go of it and that resulted in Eloise(the H’s 12 year old daughter)They separated for good right after Eloise was born. He wanted custody of the step-son and he would have been the better choice, but since he wasn’t blood related the ex got custody(she often dumped her son on others). After the ex died the step son came to live with his stepfather, but their strong bond had been fractured. Now he meets the h, another opposite. He believes that she is all sex, drugs, and rock and roll. Plus, he feels she was a bad influence on his 19 year old step son. He hurls all sorts of sordid accusations at her, but she doesn’t let it pierce her titanium shell.

Against their better judgement the two feel a pull towards each other. Neither one of them wants to embrace it. He with his stuffed shirts and she with her pixie like qualities. She ends up hiding out at his mansion incognito. Out of her rockstar garb, the H notices how very sweet and attractive she looks. His daughter is also in residence and doesn’t recognize her idol…the H doesn’t want the h to tell her either.

Eloise is initially surly with the h thinking her father is going through a midlife crisis, she knows her father isn’t a monk as she caught one of his female Dr. Friends in the hallway one night. However, she gets over her reservations about the h quickly and they become fast friends. The h is able to bridge the generation gap between daughter and father.

The h and H continue to do a little dance around each other, but eventually succumb to their passion in a quick pairing that leaves the h stunned and the H guilt ridden(he is so guilt ridden he calls in his dr therapist friend Bob to talk to the h).

From here they take things slow. The h is basically the daughter’s au pair until she finishes her term at boarding school. The 3 of them end up attending Dr. Bob’s house for a barbecue. While at the party, the green eyes of jealousy afflicts both of them. The h leaves the H and goes back to her own home. Then, Dr. Bob makes the H confront his feelings for the h. The H goes after the h and she agrees to go live with him. (I guess this is when she becomes the mistress with 12 pages left in the story?)The h insists on keeping it a secret from the daughter, but the daughter walks in on them and the gig is up. Fine by the H he wants to marry the h. The h is still unsure as they are opposites and then she thinks he will want nothing to do with her. He tells her he doesn’t care what she has to say. With just a few pages left she tells him what happened the day his stepson died. It was pretty anti climatic in my opinion, and I wish those few pages would have been spent on an epilogue instead.

The last paragraph leaves us with a wedding so that is nice. There are lots of misconceptions in this story, usually from the Hero but sometimes from the h as well. I enjoyed the supporting characters: Eloise the daughter, Bob the head doctor and his wife, and Rhys the manwhore friend who causes some jealousy.
