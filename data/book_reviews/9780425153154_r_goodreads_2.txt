In this eighth episode a schoolgirl is found murdered in a graveyard. Her father is a prominent figure in the area and pressure is put on D.C.I. Banks to resolve the case quickly. Disappointingly after giving the first seven books in the series five stars each this one only earns three stars from me. I checked the reviews and apparently most other people liked it so why did I not?It may have been to do with the fact that I listened to the audiobook, the first time I have done that in this series. The narrator did a pretty good job of the Yorkshire and Polish accents but it made all the characters sound like badly educated half wits, including the police. Then of course it is hard to skip in an audiobook so I was obliged to listen in full to sections I would have preferred to skim especially a very long chapter where an adult male was detailing his feelings for a twelve year old girl. Just yuk.Even Inspector Banks did not sound the way he does in my head when I read the books. He yelled at interviewees. That's not my Banks. My fault altogether and I will continue the series on paper or kindle. Three stars.I should note that I have enjoyed many books on audio - just not this one.
