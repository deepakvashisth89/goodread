This rewiew is in honor of my friend, Hester, who loves hedgehogs. Harry Hedgehog liked noise, so he built himself a drum. Soon three of his hedgehog friends had built drums and they formed a band - "tum-tum-te-tum, diddle-diddle-dum, ratta-tat-tat, BOOM!" The other animals loved the band so much that they wanted to join, but what could they play? Harry knew all about noise, so he told them that they could hum, hoot, buzz, whistle, clap, click and pop. Soon all the animals were part of the band. Great illustrations of all the animals in a happy, noisy jamboree.
