No doubt a sympathetic introduction to Lenin and the Russian Revolution, but I found it valuable in terms of sketching the spectrum of socialist thought at key points of Revolutionary history, and what animated Lenin to change and grow his initial ideas, and later, his policies when in government.

The narrative really focuses on Lenin and his lionish efforts in bringing about and explaining the revolution, and as a result, the human cost of War Communism is minimised. Having said that, there were interesting details about how internal the Russian economy became during the Civil War, due to blockades and international sanctions.

I don't think I could use this for all my high school students, but some sections would be highly beneficial.
