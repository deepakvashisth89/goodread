I will actually review this series, one day. For now, let me say this:The series is delightful. Not brilliant, it lacks polish and good plotting, and the author has a serious problem with forshadowing/deus ex machina/finishing the story.But... I enjoyed the ride. I love Heris and Esmay and Cecelia as characters, flat as they were.They should rename the series "Old and Cunning Aunties Take Over the Universe" or something. Which is quite unique in sf-land (or fantasy-land, or, well, most books regardless of the genre), the presence of strong *old* female characters.Okay, the last book sucked monkey balls where it came to finishing. I mean, the whole last part of the book plottwist... sjeez, seriously out of the blue redconning. That didn't deter me from tearing up at the end.Flat and wonky they may be... I'll miss them. I'll miss their universe. This ends my Elizabeth Moon-binge, so, I'll miss her as well.
