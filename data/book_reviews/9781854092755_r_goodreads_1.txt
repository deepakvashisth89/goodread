I picked up this trilogy of “Cassell Military Classics” at a used book store and was pleasantly surprised by this first read. Perrett covers eleven military objectives, or coups, under the larger narrative of covering this history of such coups, especially in the age of mass communications. So he begins with the campaign in Romania in 1916 and the prologue ends with a brief discussion of objectives in the Gulf War. It is immensely readable and supported with an index and solid bibliography. Recommended.
