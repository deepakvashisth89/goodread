With a title of O, Pioneers! I anticipated a prairie setting with people and place a major component of the story, but it's more character and vignette oriented rather than prairie-life focused. A disappointment for me, as I really enjoy books set in homesteading times and watching characters succeed through their own hard work and determination.

Unfortunately, as with many classics, this was not a story I particularly enjoyed. I always feel like classics are classics for a reason, yet that reason seems to be out of reach for this pleasure reader. O, Pioneers! is light on action and a bit dull at times, which only intensifies the idea that I should be taking away something of literary merit but it's sure hard to know precisely what that is.

The takeaway for me is that being different is okay. It's not a bad thing to see things from a different perspective, or to have a different personality. In fact, a different perspective can often lead to greater success and happiness. Live life on your own terms and not by a prescribed notion that someone else has set out. Characters like Alexandra, Carl, Emil, Marie, and even Frank show conservative and progressive, positive and negative thinking, and how our viewpoints and thought processes lead us down alternate courses than we may have first imagined, or even now imagine for ourselves.

We should believe in ourself and others, and strive to make something of ourself and be someone to admire. There's even a hint of the thought that you make your own destiny, good and bad. So choose to pursue that which you desire so that you don't end up with that which you don't. And hopefully you've figured out what you want before you've wandered too far down the wrong course.

Now is any of that what the author intended for me to realize? I don't know. All I can do is share what I got out of the writing and hope that my thoughts can be understood and confirmed by other readers.

A monochrome-feeling read that in spite of its self-confidence message left me feeling sedate and melancholic.
