In this story, Spirou and Fantasio solve the mystery of a missing rare animal. Some of the scenes are really well done. For example, I'm not a soccer fan, but there is a soccer scene that helped me feel the appeal and fun of soccer. That's quite an accomplishment. However, there is a problematic scene in which Spirou and Fantasio use magic pills in a circus act to change skin color. It starts off OK as Fantasio changes to blue and green, and then various animal patterns. However, it also includes reface and blackface. The redface is pretty bad because he holds a feather above his head. The blackface is not good either as the audience is shown to be the most shocked at that color change.
