A life-changing book for me. It was hearing about a place in Scotland called Findhorn that set me on the path I still walk today. Though I've never set foot on the actual community, it was this book (and Paul Hawken's "The Magic of Findhorn") that gave me the clear underlying premise of what's made this and other outposts of intentional living both magical *and* practical.Interesting that others have mentioned how it affected their practices of gardening. I recall more Eileen Caddy's stories of receiving spiritual guidance while cleaning the communal outhouse, Dorothy Maclean's vivid account of nature spirits (the devas) and Roc's encounters with Pan in the Edinburgh Gardens that have stayed with me. A wonderful, timeless book.
