I have read many reviews and comments lately that this book is a little outdated. I don’t know why that information is relevant when it is obvious that this book was originally published in 1932, so yes, some information in the book will get to be outdated. The people who say that haven't read the book properly because if the outdated information in the book is all the knowledge they got from the book, then they should change their reading habits. The book was written nearly a century back and I highly doubt that the author knew that his book will survive for so long.

No, the book is not outdated, it is just that you are now more familiar with the concept as there are numerous books and multiple lectures online which prophesize the same thing mentioned in the book.

The content of this book can never get old because the book is not about instances, stories or factual information but it is about an idea, thinking and belief. I didn’t believe in the words that were written inside the book a few years back but then I had to because I was it working for me.

I told others about it but it didn’t work for them. They could never invest that much faith in this book. Or maybe they were not ready for the philosophy of this book. Every person who read this book whose life is changed by this book had one thing in common - he was desperate to change his life and become a better person and a more successful person.

As the author, Claude Bristol wrote this book not as a book but as a pamphlet so that it can be easily distributed to people. It was only later that people came to know the importance of the words written in the pamphlet that they decided to convert it into a book.

The book talks about the power of believing and how much your thinking counts in your life. This is one topic that cannot be stressed more by the authors. Still, people don’t do it so it means either it is a hard process for them or it doesn’t work. Now it took me five years to understand what this book was teaching and how to give my hundred per cent trust to the theory mentioned in the book. So I can vouch that it definitely works. Problem with people is that they are stuck with one thinking and they don't want to change it and they also think that no matter what they think, nothing is in their control. This book, however, talks otherwise about taking control of your life, dream big and go out to achieve anything you want to achieve.

Highly recommended for anyone who thinks that success is accidental as this book will surely explain that success is a mindset and not a luck factor.
