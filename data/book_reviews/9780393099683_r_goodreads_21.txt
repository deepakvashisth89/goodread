For a year in which I’ve already read Waiting for Godot; Absalom, Absalom!; and The Passenger Duology, this book being the wackiest, densest, and most brain-frying of my literature experiences in 2023 speaks volumes. Still processing so much about this puzzling story, but it’s Melville once again being a master at what he does best, which is mounding his tales with interminable layers of meaning and leaving his audience with haunting stops after pages of (albeit philosophizing) hilarity. Comfortable with calling this his best work barring The Whale, but I still have quite a bit to read from Herman (Pierre and Clarel look promising), so who knows?“Something further may follow of this masquerade.”
