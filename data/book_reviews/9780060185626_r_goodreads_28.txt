This is the first book that I've ever read by this author, and I was pleasantly surprised. Ms. Jance is the author of several series, and this is one of the ones in the J.P. Beaumont series. The mystery was good, the players plausible and memorable. It was well-written.Beau, a retired cop, accompanies his octogenarian grandmother, Beverly, and her new husband, Lars, on their honeymoon cruise to Alaska. This alone was interesting to me because 7 years ago my husband and I took the same cruise, at the same time of the year. Aboard ship, Beau's dinner table mates are a group of middle aged friends since college, whom he pegs as snooty and rich. These women cause quite a bit of drama, especially when one disappears, presumably thrown overboard, and only witnessed by an elderly man with Alzheimers. Beau is asked by some undercover FBI agents aboard to look out for a group targeting doctors; the organization thinks that no one should interfere with God's plans. One doctor aboard is the divorced husband of the woman who disappears. Is there a connection? The patient being presented at a seminar is a man whose seizures was cured by this doctor. The FBI suspect that he might also be a target so Beau is asked to watch this man, Marc. On the ship, and off at the various ports, the drama unfolds.This book was enjoyable, and I'll read the others I own that were written by this author.
