Too many plots and none of them interesting.The Pikes are chosen for a televised holiday special about their “old-fashioned” Christmas Mallory dreams up. What makes it old-fashioned? They half-ass some handmade gifts, bake dozens of cross-contaminated cookies, and get a real Christmas tree. Along the way, they learn an early lesson about reality television production, and of course, that money can’t buy happiness.Should children be this invested in the family finances? I’m not a parent, but it seems like the Pike kids worry constantly about money. Did we know Mr. Pike is a lawyer? He has to be some underpaid public defender or something.The B-plot is that Kristy turns into an absolute monster. Once again she conscripts the BSC into doing a whole bunch of unpaid labor. Of course the only time she’s at all interested in Mallory is when she has something to gain from it, which is “publicity” for the club. She demands Mallory host a BSC Christmas party at her house.
