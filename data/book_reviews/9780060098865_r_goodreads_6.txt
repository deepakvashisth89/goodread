Although this book is clearly classified as a children's book, I still loved it. Every moment reading it, I felt like I was being showered with grace and faith that I should be grateful for. I'm Catholic, which means I believe in God. But no matter what they teach us in school or in sunday class, I have my share of doubts. But after reading this really short story about a doubting angel, I felt the connection and bond between me and my God strengthen.

This book may be for children, but I absolutely loved it. The book was filled with enriching and inspiring quotes and saying by the author himself that could've easily passed as a tweet or a typography photo. I loved every minute of reading this, and I'm sure you will too.
