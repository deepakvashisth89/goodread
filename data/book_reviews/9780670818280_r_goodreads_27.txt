"Nobody can be held responsible for the pranks of destiny."

Australian gothic pastoral. I can see why a lot of people find this book to be slow and boring, there really isn't much to the story: three private school girls and one of their teachers disappear in 1900 in Australia at the start of the book, and not much else happens thereafter. There is one significant event around the 50% mark and some rather hurriedly-related and variously tragically or happily wrapped-up loose ends in the finale, but otherwise the meat of Picnic at Hanging Rock is entirely the beautifully written passages by Joan Lindsay.
