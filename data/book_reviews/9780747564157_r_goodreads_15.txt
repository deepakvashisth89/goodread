I want to like this more than I actually did. The premise is good- a 'poverty safari' by a middle class liberal. But as much as she attempts to live the authentic life in the wee flat with the crappy job she is so out of touch it's funny but not in a good way. Buying the Guardian every day when you dont have enough money for food??She admits that she relied on freebies from friends and absolutely did not have a diet typical of so done in the circumstances she was trying to imitate.It's still a good try at gaining an understanding of the class divide in the UK but it could have been sooo much better.Worth reading but take it all with a pinch of salt.
