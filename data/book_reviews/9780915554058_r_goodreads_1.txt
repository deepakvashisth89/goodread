This was an all around good science book with a couple of problems. The first is, the book is long... it took Me 3 days to read it (and i think I'm a fast reader). Second, the book has an interesting assortment of articles about astronomical observation and physics But most of these articles are hopelessly dated. For a less contemporary topic, this might not be a problem But a reader would expect experiments from the 21st century to be included in the topic of astronomical anomalies to current theory. Third, many/most of the articles, while entertaining we accepted by major publications in their time from anonymous sources which generally limits the follow up one can do. In all the length and dated material leaves the reader looking for an Up to Date version of this book.
