It took me a few years to finish this book. I plan to continue "reading" it. I also highlighted all the signs I knew. Some signs were repeated because they can have different meanings. The signs got "easier" towards the end (not sure if it was because I was learning as I progressed or if they were becoming more general). Most of the signs are still in use today even though this book is from 1998. I would recommend to anyone who wants to learn ASL, though nothing compares to a good teacher.
