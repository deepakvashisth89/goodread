I’m not sure a better book exists to read out loud to your kids. 5 stars doesn’t even cover it. I could barely get words out by the end and the last sentence is probably the best LAST sentence of most books I’ve read. We will be moving on to Man of the Family without hesitation. This book is a keeper. Doesn’t get much better! *I did have to substitute a few words for Morgan, but it does not take away from the book. It’s just the way it was back then….
