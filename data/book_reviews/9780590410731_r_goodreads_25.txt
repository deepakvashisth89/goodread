I was a big re-reader as a child. Laura’s Luck was a book I read many times when I was about in sixth grade. I absolutely loved it. I reread it now, more than fifty years later and it brought back so many things to me-pieces of dialogue that I had internalized that just became a part of me. The crazy part is that I never realized that the story took took place in the forties. It was published in 1965, I believe. It puts a different perspective on the whole story.
