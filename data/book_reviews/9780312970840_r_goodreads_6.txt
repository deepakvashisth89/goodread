This is one of my favorite books in an excellent series. I enjoy the way Marsh keeps both Troy and Roderick in the story though they are separated for much of the novel. I also like the frame of Alleyn teaching this to policemen in a class. I think creating a murder during a houseboat vacation is very clever - it's a twist on the usual isolated country house. And all of the characters are well drawn and quite interesting. In addition, the mystery is genuinely mysterious, as are most of Marsh's stories.A home run and highly recommended.NB- Nadia May narrates the audio book. She's usually excellent, but she pronounces his name incorrectly. It's 'Allen', not 'Al line' or 'Al lane'. Just plain 'Allen', as he explains in several of the books.
