Clerics are at their best in the second edition, I think, with the priest spheres and various special abilities providing boatloads of flavour and fluff and making every priest you meet unique. And apart from the mechanical considerations, the superficial but well-written information on the mythologies themselves is sure to get you going.

Alas, this supplement is lacking several of the Deities & Demigods mythologies, most importantly Cthulhu Mythos: I really would have loved to learn the special abilities and spell spheres of an insane cultist-priest of Azathoth. With that and Melnibone gone, Nehwon stands kind of orphaned as the only fully fictional mythology in the entire book. This nixes a star.
