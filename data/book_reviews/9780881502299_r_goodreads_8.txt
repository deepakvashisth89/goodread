Recommended by the Classic Mysteries podcast. An interesting story I might not have otherwise found.
Asey Mayo is a well-known detective on the East Coast (with the "accent" to prove it.) In this book, he comes up on a dead body in the personal train car of some summer folk. But when he then goes into the house, the occupants are acting strangely and a return to the train car reveals that the body has disappeared. His companion, the local doctor, also disappears as well as his new car. He'll need all of his detecting skills to solve this many mysteries all at the same time.
