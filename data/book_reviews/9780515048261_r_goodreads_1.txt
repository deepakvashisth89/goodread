This is the second anthology of stories reprinted from Weird Tales magazine that Margulies edited. There have been dozens of such books printed, but his were among the first; this one appeared initially in 1965. This one contains seven stories from issues that appeared in the 1930's, along with an introduction by Sam Moskowitz and illustrations from Virgil Finlay. Curiously, my edition from September, 1978 has the same cover as their edition of the previous book (which was simply titled Weird Tales), with a different colored background; the art director at Jove must've been really taken with that attractive young lady who's balancing a bat on her head. There are stories from Clark Ashton Smith and Robert E. Howard, two of WT's big three authors (but nothing by H.P. Lovecraft), as well as three other classic writers of the era, Edmond Hamilton (better known as the father of Captain Future), Frank Belknap Long, and David H. Keller, M.D. My favorites are The Sapphire Goddess by Nictzin Dyalhis, a truly terrific early fantasist who's now almost entirely forgotten, and Roads by Seabury Quinn, one of the best Christmas stories ever written.
