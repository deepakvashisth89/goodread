As Alleyn notes, his wife, painter Agatha Troy, has been involved in 4 or his previous cases. Here is the 5th, with Troy's invitation to paint a diva opera singer's portrait at a multimillionaire's estate on a New Zealand island leads to the Alleyns being involved in a Agatha Christie like scenario--only Marsh wrote much better characters than Christie! The plot is good, the whodunnit has some unexpected twists and turns, and Marsh's love for her native country shines through in descriptions of the flora and fauna. My only quibble is that I wish that she had been consistent with time--this is set around 1979 or so, and Alleyn would have been well past retirement age at that point in time!Nonetheless, a fine penultimate book in this series.
