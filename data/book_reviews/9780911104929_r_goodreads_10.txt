Whack Your Porcupine and Other Drawingsby B. Kliban (Workman Publishing Co. 1977)(741.5073). Before Gary Larsen dreamed up the comic "The Far Side", the standard for comic strip lunacy was set by B. Kliban. This is one of his best collections, and it is laugh-out-loud funny! My rating: 7.5/10, finished 1977.
