I was pleasantly surprised by this book. Having read a fair share of romantic novels this year I have to say it’s refreshing stumbling onto a book that doesn’t sugarcoat everything. The relationship between Ivy and Rick seems very realistic, sweet, funny and complicated. They don’t just fall in love and walk into to sunset, they have to work for it when personalities and work related troubles clash.Ivy’s League is the third book in Heather MacAllister’s series of the Hall Sister’s. Not having read the first two books I must say that I enjoyed this book so much that I might just Benjamin Button my way through the series. This book follows the story of the youngest Hall sister in her struggle to balance what she wants in life and what she expects to get from her career.I liked the book, I liked the characters, I liked the story, I hated the little ballet girls (but that just might be my own repressed ballet memories resurfacing). The only problem I had was with the edition, it was backwards, as in having to press the left button instead of the right in order to read, but hopefully that is a simple error the editors can fix.I got this book from NetGalley.
