An excellent accounting of Medgar Ever's life, his work, his cold-blooded murder, and the three decades long pursuit of justice.I know it is a cliche that the book is better than the movie, but this is not even close. It is borderline criminal what was left out about this as story. This book vividly details the hard won fight by citizens to regain their constitutional rights that were stolen away by a vicious and violent segregationist policy of the deep South, and none more violent than Mississippi.4.5 stars.
