An interesting concept that has been completed to a horrible standard. Most aspects of this book, aside from the idea of it, are annoying. Whether that is the writing itself, whereupon everything is drawn out to boring length and anything of any merit is quickly skipped over. whilst reading this book I actually fantasised about rewriting the whole thing and not wasting the idea by having this as its only representation.It lacks sufficient world building to be convincing, is short of any quality of prose to be charming or enjoyable and is constant in its flawed design.I forced myself to consume the descriptions of women and children as satirical ways to highlight the immorality in certain ways of thinking, to be able to read the constant repeated prattle of women being best when stupid and subservient, and how a 9 year old girl should mature until only 13 to be suitable for marriage. I did think that perhaps this is gently disguised fetish fantasy rather than just a reflection of history, because there felt no need for women in the 26th future imaginary utopia to continue being so subservient and willing to sleep with anything that asks them to?Read the book if you want, I nyself did afterall, but if you find it a waste of time, don't be surprised.
