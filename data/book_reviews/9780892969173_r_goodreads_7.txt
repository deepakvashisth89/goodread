LTOM is a collection of short, short stories by the queen of weird. There is certainly also a great deal of misandry thrown in, if only by virtue of the book's title. The first story was a little like a twisted bible story or fable, the rest are mostly forgettable but definitely readable and weird.One of the stories, The Victim, disturbed me more than the rest. In that story, the whole family could have benefited from psychiatric treatment (in my humble opinion). Is there a word like misogyny/misandry for contempt of parents? Nowthatstory is going to stick with me for a long time. I wonder if PH hated men, women, herself or everyone.
