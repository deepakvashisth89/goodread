Petra and Sara were friends since childhood, but had different characters. Petra was sensible and rational whereas Sara was impulsive. When they decided to go to France and work as au pairs to acquire the language, they ended up with a horrible French family. Therefore, when Sara decided one night to leave at midnight with a stranger who picked her up in a bar to Venice and live in Palazzo Berini, Petra simply couldn't change her mind.Sara called later to tell Petra that she was lied to and would come back to Paris. Yet, weeks passed and there was no call or a visit from her. Out of great worry, Petra left to Venice to see Count Carlo Birini, whom Sara said was leaving with. But, Carlo denied any connection to her friend which left Petra no choice but to spy on him!
