Confession: I haven't read every story and essay in this collection.However, I have read the majority, and I must say that LOVE Hawthorne's short stories. His grasp on human psychology is mind-boggling. I have truly come to appreciate his elevated language and old-fashioned writing style, even if it did take me a few stories before I could read him without much difficulty.Ultimately, Hawthorne's stories make me think. Really think. About life, God, religion, community, guilt, nature, family, isolation, identity... the list goes on. He is a brilliant man, a brilliant writer, and his stories are a captivating read.
