I really enjoyed the inaugural collection ofDark Matter: A Century of Speculative Fiction from the African Diaspora— in fact, I'm still mulling over a couple of the stories included in it now, months after I originally read them. Of course, I was eager to read the follow up anthologyReading the Bonesand see how it compared. Most of what I wrote in my review of the first collection applies to this one as well: it's a good (if somewhat dated) compilation of African speculative fiction, it can be a dark and gritty at times, and I cared for some authors and stories over others. I'm curious to see if any of the stories will remain with me for months the way some did from the first collection, but all in all this was an equally compelling read.
