This didn't age well. Or maybe it's just me. Mainly the book consists of an unnamed doctor giving a line of advice, and Joan saying that wasn't what happened on her wedding night, or the only time she got around to pregnancy testing was to find out why she couldn't see her feet, or suggesting that a stiff drink was needed to cope with a facet of pregnancy. The doctor advises calmly against the drink.I'm sure at the time, this kind of book was a breath of fresh air for expectant mothers and those with small kids. Covers from learning of pregnancy up to the child starting first school. Joan puts herself down, but also puts down her husband, relatives, doctors and even the child at times. These are real people. By the end she is praising all of them.I read a paperback. This is an unbiased review.
