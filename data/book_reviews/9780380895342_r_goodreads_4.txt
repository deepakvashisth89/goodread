This was not one of Patricia Hagan's best. I started reading but stopped halfway through, which I've never done before when reading one of her novels. It seemed like she was just going through the motions with this one, as there didn't seem to be any life in the characters, despite all the whining, yelling and complaining (not to mention mild cursing) the heroine does in the first chapters. Even that seemed artificial. The book lacked the spirit of her other novels; my guess is, for whatever reason, her heart just wasn't in it.This is one book Patricia Hagan fans can easily bypass.
