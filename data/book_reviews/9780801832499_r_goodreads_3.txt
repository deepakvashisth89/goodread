This is one of my favorite books about Theodore Roosevelt and his ideology in the world. Though the author credits Roosevelt with errors such as imagining he could counter- balance the imperial ambitions of European Powers careening towards World War, his book is in many ways a great vindication of Roosevelt's accomplishments. Theodore Roosevelt recognized the impending crisis of World War 1 and when he took over from McKlinley, even during his days as Assistant Secretary of the Navy during McKinley's First Term pre-dating his Rough Rider ventures into Cuba, Theodore Roosevelt recognized the greatness that awaited America, and knew that we had to exert ourselves more forcibly in the world. One can argue that Theodore Roosevelt was the President who turned our country from an isolationist trading nation into a Global Superpower. This book is a balanced and impartial view of Roosevelt's policies and their effect on the greater world.
