I've read 300+ books, my 2 favorites are The Historian, and Testimony of Two Men. In Testimony, I loved reading the complexity of human love in several forms, failings, disillusionment, power, jealousy, love, and betrayal. Nothing about any of those feelings was typical in this novel ... there was a surprise with every person. The story was complicated! So many emotions! Complex characters, Jonathan Ferrier fighting to modernize medical practices in his town and fought by the 'old guard', his mother trying to walk a fine line of love for her very different sons, the secrets, complicated crimes.

I think the essence of the book is in this quote, "Someday the town of Hambledon might forget the lies they told about their brilliant young doctor. But they could never forgive the truths he told about them." The novel is a compelling story, with so many twists, of a doctor at war with the world."

Caldwell made early 1900s come to life with architecture, interior design, attitudes, medical enlightenment for science vs tradition. One sad realization was how truly dreadful mankind is during any time period or place on this earth!

[I have loved reading Caldwell books...but I'm quite sure that I would not have liked her.]
