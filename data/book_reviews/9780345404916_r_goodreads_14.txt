There’s plenty to criticize about Jeff Shaara’s work. But what’s worth commending are the small moments where he really captures the “feel” of a historic moment. And he in many ways paved the way for other Civil War novelists by not letting the legacy of *Killer Angels* die. I don’t know of this is as good as Gods and Generals, but it’s still a great read, especially the last part on the race to Appomattox.
