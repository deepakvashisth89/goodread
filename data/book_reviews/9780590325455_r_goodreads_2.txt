I finished reading this last night and absolutely loved it! The gothic haunted house setting was so creepy, and being trapped there in a snow storm with diminishing supplies of food and no way to leave made it even scarier. I found myself on edge while reading about Tracy wandering the house after dark.

Tracy was such a reflective character. I loved reading about her conflict with whether or not she should marry Mac right out of high school (she shouldn't) and what she wanted for her future. Her internal struggle felt so different from a lot of the main characters of other books written for teens in the early 80s. It was such a pleasant surprise to pick this up and find a very thoughtful and smart character.

Also though, they spent way too much time debating whether they should burn the furniture or not. If I'm trapped in an abandoned home in the middle of the worst blizzard in history with no heating and no firewood left, yeah, I'm burning some antiques.
