This book explains very vividly what could happen to a group of friends that have found themselves between a rock and a hard place. Much of this book is based in the States, but soon enough WWII begins and the group of friends enlist to escape the wrath of a angry banker that has turned their basket over and shaken it. This banker is quite mad about his wife and a business deal, you'd have to read the book to learn why. Also the book will skip in the time line very hard to pick up when the book starts off in the middle of the time line, then fills you in as you go.
