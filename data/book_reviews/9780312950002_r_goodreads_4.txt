a roller coaster of good and bad writingDon't Speak to Strangersis like riding a roller coaster - not because of its thrills, but because sometimes it is very poorly written and sometimes it is really quite good.The author does a pretty good job of making you wonder why this boy is singled out by the kidnapper - but when I found out why I didn't really buy it.
