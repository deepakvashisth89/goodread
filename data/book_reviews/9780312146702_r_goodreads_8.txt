This installment of the series was a disappointment. First of all, Mary and Holmes are apart for most of it. Mary’s part of the investigation is almost pointless. There is A LOT of vague action, even when we are supposedly “in the room.”There is so much superfluous action here that the story could have almost been a novella with the first 50 pages and the last 20 forming the entire story.I might have to take a break from the series. When the sleuthing parts are disappointing and the relationship parts aren’t satisfying, the book becomes a bit of a slog.
