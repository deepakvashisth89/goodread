This is the sixteenth Asey Mayo novel. It was published in 1940.It is a wonderful time capsule of Cape Cod in an odd time. England and Germany were at war. America was not, but American materials were key to the British war machine. The Germans were conducting an active submarine war just off the East Coast. They were successful in sinking many British ships.In this book a radio rabble rouser has convinced many Cape Codders that they should be worried about some kind of attack on Cape Cod or about enemy spies on the Cape. A ladies auxiliary gets formed. One of the running gags is that the women are trying to get firearm training, and they keep misfiring the guns. (This is 1940)A flashy society woman is found dead in her lounge chair on the beach. It appears she was poisoned. Asey Mayo is called in. He is an unofficial detective. A wise guy reporter in this story calls him "The Codfish Sherlock", which is pretty good.Asey discovers multiple suspects, including a father and son who both had romantic interests in the deceased. We get chases through the Cape Cod back roads, old time Cape Cod characters and a satisfying ending where a nasty piece of work gets his comeuppance.This is one of the better volumes in a very good series.
