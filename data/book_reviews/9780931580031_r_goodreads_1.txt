Read it with someone you're destined to love--yourself. Takes you through the various stages of a relationship from infatuation to painful breakup via clever, short, and easily accessible poems.
"These are your poems. I only wrote them down," McWilliams says. Honored to have talked to his mother on two occasions after he died and obtained some rare chapbooks he published. I wish I had gotten to speak to him; he died far too soon.
