Not so great. Once upon a time McCarver was my favorite baseball announcer, but this book is filled with too many things that have little to do with the game, namely the author's opinions on a wide range of topics, & includes pages filled with restaurant recommendations from all the cities he's visited. The baseball stuff is great when it's there, especially the details of working with pitchers Bob Gibson & Steve Carlton. I wish there were more recollections of the games & World Series in which he played. An unexpected highlight was the final section of the book, which traces the Mets' (just completed) miraculous 1986 season, told by the man who followed the team & was their regular broadcaster for that season as well as the subpar years leading up to it.
