I loved this book because the setting is NYC and I love the city’s history. No, it didn’t really teach me anything new, but I didn’t expect that. It was about a kid growing up and like most families, not all the kids are perfect, and the author was honest about the fact he was the one with all the issues. He made stupid mistakes, he hung around with the wrong crowd, and he was disrespectful to his mom when he was a teenager. But through it all, he learned a ton, he kept loving his mom and brother, he grew up and found a great profession, and he has done way more with his life than a lot of people he grew up around thought he would or did.
