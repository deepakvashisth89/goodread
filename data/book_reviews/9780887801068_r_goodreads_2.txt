I very much enjoyed this book written by Ernie Lyall, born in 1910 in Labrador. He spent his entire life working in the Canadian Arctic . He has since passed on but his remarkable legacy will live on. Through his life and experiences he has given us a very interesting glimpse into the early development of Canada's far north and his long employment with the Hudson's Bay Company. Mr. Lyall was not Inuit but he married one and learned to speak Inuktitut. Together he and his wife Nipisha raised many children.
