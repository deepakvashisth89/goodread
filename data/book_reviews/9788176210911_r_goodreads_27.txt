I distinctly remember the Union Carbide disaster in India happening when I was in high school but didn't know any of the details. The authors (Lapierre co-wrote with Javier Moro) do an excellent job explaining the history of the corporation starting in Bhopal, describing the complexities of chemical pesticides and events leading up to the disaster, and showing what I think makes India so enigmatic, beautiful, and astonishing. Highly recommend this one if you have any interest in what happened.
