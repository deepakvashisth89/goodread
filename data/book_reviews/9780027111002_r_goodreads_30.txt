We recently readSoupy Saturdays with the Pain and the Great Oneand liked it and saw that this was a picture book about the brother and sister. We just had to read it. I love that the children are 6 and 8 and in first and third grade. That's exactly where our girls are in age and school, so they can really relate to these two characters (even if one of them is a boy.)This is a fun book about siblings. Each has a perspective on life in the family and each one believes that Mom and Dad love the other better. It's a picture book that introduces us to the family and we are looking forward to reading more books in the series.
