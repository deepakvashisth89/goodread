I thought this was a strong end to the Gaea trilogy. It is full of humorous and not so humorous cinematic references more so than its predecessorWizard. I liked Sirocco again and found the epic battle between her and Gaea (with a little help from her friends) to be a fun romp through this most prosaic and strange of worlds imagined by the great John Varley. I think these books are underrated and deserve a little more attention.
