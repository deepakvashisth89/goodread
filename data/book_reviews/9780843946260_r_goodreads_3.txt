This book made the little hairs on the back of my neck stand straight up…a few even fell out. The things Rex does to the cat! He does really awful things to people, too, but the cat really got to me.As often happens with my horror picks, I liked the characters more than the story. So the best way to describe the book is to introduce some of the key players:Norris, Lindsay and Bernie: Cousins. In town for the Lynch family reunion. One is a being blackmailed for having a sexual encounter with a minor. One has a daughter born without eyes. One belongs to a religious commune.Rex: Lethal combination of psychopath and racist. Does not like cats. Founder of the White Eagles, a white supremacy group.Lena: Spent four months with Marla Lynch as a foster child. She’s an unbalanced and tortured soul.Part supernatural, completely sick. Recommend for the strong of heart…and stomach.
