Ricky is a small mouse and he loves going to school but he doesn't like walking to school as gets bullied by bigger mouse. When a mad doctor, Dr. Stinky McNasty builds a robot as his evil plan to destroy Squeakyville, it doesn't go down as the mad doctor wanted and Ricky ends up saving the mighty robot and then the mighty robot ends up helping Ricky and his family.This wasn't bad but it lacked Pilkey's usual sense of humor. Still a good read with good illustrations.3 stars
