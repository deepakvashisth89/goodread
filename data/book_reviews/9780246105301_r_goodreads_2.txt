Is this book non-fiction or historical fiction?That was the question I had as soon as I finished this book.My conclusion is that it is both. The author did a lot of research, including primary research to garner information about the Hindenburg disaster.However, he presents a single plausible reason for the disaster, mildly dramatized for the book. Despite it being a plausible reason, it is not determined to be the true cause and since no other alternatives are presented, I would say is the historical fiction section.In places, the book shows its age, but it was a very interesting read with a fascinating detailed history on the beginnings of airships and rigid structure Zeppelins.
