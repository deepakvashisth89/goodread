I chose to try this series because the fourth book takes place on Cumberland Island, a place to which I am very drawn. I was also intrigued by Ben Reese, the series' protagonist, a scholar with a shadowy past as an intelligence agent in World War II, and the college campus setting with deception and intrigue among the faculty members. One element of the author's style that I found off-putting, however -- she switches from third-person narration to first-person unspoken thoughts with no warning or changes in punctuation. The mystery was not as complex as some, but the story drew me in, and I will go on to the second book, Pride and Predator.
