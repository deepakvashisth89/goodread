This book is a nuts and bolts history. It gives precise details of voting patterns and the make up of the three divisions of French society. It does not make for light reading but it is fascinating.One statement jumps out as a message to our times. The author points out on page 21 that the financial crisis which led to the economic collapse of the monarchical government which led to the Revolution, could have been avoided if only the tax system was reformed so that the richest classes paid equivalently as the poorest. This book was first published in 1939.
