I really like Rosie Thomas as she seems to be able to make characters come alive and describes their thoughts and feelings very effectively.
This book describes two 16 year old girls who run away to London to escape their families. They are taken in by a young man and his mother and gradually find their way in the world.. Mattie eventually becomes a famous actress and Julia marries into the gentry. That makes the book sound rather superficial but their struggles are well described.
Not one of my favourite Thomas books but I was absorbed while reading it .
