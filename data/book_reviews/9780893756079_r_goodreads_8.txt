One of the 19th century classics of English language literature, Kipling called this a boys' story. Hence, twenty first century adults will find this book no chore to read. In fact many will find it quite entertaining even though things move along more rapidly than is strictly believable. However, the details of the setting and physical action are very believable. Kipling did considerable research to get them accurate. The kindle edition is free from Amazon.

This 1896/97 short novel is a sort of forerunner to Jack London's 1904, THE SEAWOLF with nicer people and without the sociopaths. There is also commercial fishing instead of sealing. I like both novels and both authors.
