Louis L'Amour published eighty-nine novels, twenty-seven short-story collections, a memoir, a volume of poetry, and countless other works. If interested, a reader may spend quite a bit of time reading his quirky take on life during the long-ago West. (Yes, I went there).His style of storytelling is crisp, no-nonsense; he is in complete control of the narrative; his cast of characters are always fully realized. His stories are to me like being stuck in traffic and the guy in the next car is hellbent on sharing his playlist with every other driver. I'm frustrated that I can't get to my destination without having to listen to this person's soundtrack, yet I feel compelled to lower my window inch by inch by inch. I don't even know when I start dancing in my seat, forgetting that I'm waiting for the road to clear.During these times of social distancing, hop on down to the West as it was before sliced bread (and political correctness).
