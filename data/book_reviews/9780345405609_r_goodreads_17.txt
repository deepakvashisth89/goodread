An excellent fantasy/science fiction novel at the start of the Great War series. Following the end of the War of Secession, America had been split into two parts, the Northern United States and the Southern Confederate States. By 1914, the North was led by Teddy Roosevelt, backed by Germany and the South by Woodrow Wilson and backed by England and France, as was Canada. War breaks out and becomes bogged down in trench warfare. The story is broken down into several sets of people from various viewpoints, following their paths in the war. Very good characters who all fit well into their situations, and then, underlying the whole story is a steady undercurrent of unrest that calls for a revolution. Although a lengthy book, it romps along at a good pace. Another two volumes. Whatever is to be said about Harry Turtledove, you get a lot of value for whatever price paid. 4stars.
