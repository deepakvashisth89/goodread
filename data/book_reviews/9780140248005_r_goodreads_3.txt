This book is a bit old, but I wanted to give it a try due to a recommendation heard on a favorite radio program. Frequently humorous and at times interesting, this biography did not always entertain. I got through it with some effort. A deeper awareness of all things "British" may have made it more entertaining. I did enjoy a few hearty laughs at the stories about some of the strange defendants Mr. Mortimer represented at the Old Bailey. Indeed, the stories of some of Mortimer's theater friends, such as Sir Laurence Olivier and Sir John Gielgud, were likewise captivating. The repitition finally got to me, though.Show more
