A really fun, quick read - I will definitely be on the look out for more Nudger books. I'll admit I'm a sucker for musicians, and the Big Easy, and a good mystery - with all of these wrapped in one book, it probably would have been hard to strike out with me, but I really, really found myself enjoying it. I am so glad it was the first thing that popped out on the dollar rack when I ran into the used book store on my way to the doctor's office.
