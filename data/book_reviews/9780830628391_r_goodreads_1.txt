Twenty years ago, I went to college for Electrical Engineering. I forgot most of the coursework, but I still remember some select theories and equations. We used Ohm's Law and the Pythagorean Theorem a lot.The Editors of Elementary Electronics compiled 25 Projects to learn electronics. The book is for the hobbyist, but it does have enough detail to learn some theory. The book is from the late 1980s but is still applicable. According to the book, it is the fourth in a series.As with the book on addiction, I bought this from a bookstore on a whim. It sounded interesting, and the price wasn't too bad. The book has four parts. The first covers basic electronics up to cathode-ray tubes. The second part covers electronic circuits for audio equipment. The third part covers circuits for radios. The fourth part covers digital circuits.The book contains equations, along with problems to solve and their solutions. You can do the math without a pencil and paper or a calculator. However, then the audio equipment section rears its head, and you need to deal with capacitance and inductance.Each section has projects to build. The book contains a list of the parts and tools required.I enjoyed the book. Thanks for reading my review, and see you next time.
