Very twisty with lots of plot lines. However, the book itself was quite difficult to follow. There were a ton of characters and it was quite wordy and detailed. All of that together made it a book you really need to pay attention to. In addition to that there was a lot of legal jargon, adding more difficulty. Besides those things, the plot was very well thought out and truly a twisty one. This was a solid 4 star read because I can’t deny it was good, but it wasn’t a perfect book. I will however be trying more of her books.
