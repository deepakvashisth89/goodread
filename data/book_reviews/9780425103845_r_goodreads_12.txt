And since we saw each other every night; since each increment of change was unspectacular in itself; since he made love very, very well; since I was soon crazy about him, not just physically, but especially so, it came about that I found myself – after the time span of a mere two weeks – in a setup that would be judged, by the people I know, as pathological.


Nine and a Half Weeks: A Memoir of a Love Affair (Paperback)
by Elizabeth McNeill


Oh wow. What a book.

This was so ahead of its time. So many do not know of it. It puts 50 shades to shame. It is also a film but the book is way better than both the film version AND the 50 shades books, (which I loathed).

As I said it is much darker..WAY DARKER..then the movie. And gut wrenching. This is not a happy story. It is known to be a true story but it is about a corporate executive who becomes emeshed in a dark and addicting relationship with a complete stranger.

I do not think I should be calling it a relationship but I do not know what else to call it. Yes, the themes of S&M are explored. This book is not for the faint of heart and unlike the movie, which was, in my opinion meant to be erotic (and was very much so) there is NOTHING erotic about the book. It is bitterly sad. I read it y ears ago but for some reason never did an in depth review so here it is.

It really brought me down when I first read it which was many years ago. I had seen the film first and had no idea...none...how dark the book was. It is riveting though.

When fifty shades came out and people were going insane, I really felt confused as this book makes 50 shades look like a Merry Go Round. This is dark, earnest and poignant. It is also as I said not sexy in the slightest so please do not go in thinking it is. It reads very much like a character study and a brooding one at that.

It was one intense read though and I do recommend this book very much.
