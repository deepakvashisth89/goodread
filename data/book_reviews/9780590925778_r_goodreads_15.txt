This series covers a multitude of topics, but this one is something else.

There is no way that eighth-graders today would take a class called Modern Living, "marry" each other, stay "married" for the duration of the class, and have four boys "marry" each other, but two of them have to pretend to be girls.

Even my school in 1992 wasn't that wild, and we are also a small New England town with a lack of diversity.

Also, $2,000/month for a 2-bedroom in 1992!? Even in CT, that's insane. My first apartment in 2001 was $550. One bedroom, upstate NY, but still. $2,000 is more appropriate for twenty years later.

However, the scene at the Pike house with the adopted eggs may be my favorite scene so far in the series. That was great 😆
