I found this one in my TBR and it called to my mood for something light and fun. This is the first book I've read by Ms. Greene and I've discovered that this is the last book she's written. Happily I have two of her previous books in my TBR. If you like some fun and humour, you'll adore this lovestory. Julie is just the breath of fresh air our hero Hunter needs to liven up his life. Her enthusiasm for everything she does and her obvious joy for life shakes up Hunter's very orderly life. Hunter was the boy next door and had dated Julie's older sister. She'd followed Hunter around and loved to get under his skin. The openning of the book grabs you and sucks you in with a surprisingly passionate kiss. I'm looking forward to reading the other two books by Ms. Greene in my TBR.
