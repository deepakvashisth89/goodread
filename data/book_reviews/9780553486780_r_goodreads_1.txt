Since I've always had such a soft spot for Carole, (I don't really have a favourite Saddle Club character, I love them all, but at the same time, she's pretty much my favourite!) it probably goes without saying that I especially enjoyed a book that is entirely from her perspective.
The Inside Story books (there is one for each of the three girls) were so exciting to read when I was a child - they cover the events of multiple books, usually through diary entries, emails, letters etc, while giving you further insight into the girl the book centres around. In Carole's edition, which covers books 34 - 40, we get to read her journal which is supposed to focus on the training she is doing with her horse, Starlight, but is equal parts a regular journal too. Incorporated around the different entries and letters are extracts from the journal Carole kept while her mother had cancer. It's sweet and sad and I always found it incredibly heartfelt.

Also of course, there's lots of horsey scenes, hijinks and general fun.
