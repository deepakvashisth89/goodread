🌵 Published in 1927. Almost 100 years ago. Yet empathetic beyond its time. Priests connect with Mexicans and tribal nations like the Navajo in 19th century New Mexico and for once we get to read about mutual love and respect and warmth. We go with the novel’s characters from youth to old age and death and experience a hundred emotions. Always there is the land and a god known not just to priests but the indigenous as well. This brought a great deal rushing back to me from my years in New Mexico. The writing is round and full and rich and a pleasure to sink into. Recommended. I will turn now to several of her other novels.audiobook ✅
