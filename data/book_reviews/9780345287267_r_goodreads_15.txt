This book I just adored, about the time I was in 8th grade. The humor was 'adult' and it is clever and imaginative and still has much to recommend it. An 8th grader, it seems,is scandalized almost exactly as much by louche behaviour and sexual licentiousness as the anticipated reader in the 30s was, seemingly.Rereading this one was a bit disappointing. It plays a bit like a 30s bedroom comedy--which makes alot of sense--but my tastes have changed long ago, and what seemed daring and smart and clever at 12 seems tired and quaint and dated to my adults eyes.Still, a very clever and funny book, and one I am surprised was never adapted for the screen like Topper and Turnabout. It would have fared very well, and I have imagined which golden age actors might have made a good Poseidon or Bacchus. I can almost see the RKO picture header...a film by George Cuckor or Howard Hawks....
