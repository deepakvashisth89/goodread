Just as many meetings should be emails, this 300pp. book should have been a 20 page essay. Sen’s thesis (the title of the book) varies between being compelling and generally interesting, albeit completely stripped of historical thinking or any interest in anything more than superficial criticisms of the institutions and structures of development. Where this book lost me is that is it atrociously written and was in need of significantly more editing. Attempts to signpost get lost in esoteric jargon, confusing syntax, and mind-numbing repetition. No one should ever let economists write anything.
