Such wonderful things I heard about this book! Preparing for a big birthday (turning 30), all my closest friends were like me, part-time teachers of Transcendental Meditation, very full-time meditators (in our values), and not particularly wealthy.So I asked my friends to chip in to buy me this book. Sweet them, they did.Such wonderful things I'd heard about this book! (And have heard in later years as well.)After unwrapping my not-so-surprise birthday gift, I was delighted by the hefty blue hardcovers; by all those shiny new pages with their promise of wisdom.Unfortunately there was no internet at the time to give me a preview. I had asked for that gift on faith, based on hope and yearning. Soon as I started reading, I knew these teachings were not for me.Much later, I appreciated that channeling is not the same thing as connecting directly with God. At least, not for me. Differently put, astral is not Divine.
