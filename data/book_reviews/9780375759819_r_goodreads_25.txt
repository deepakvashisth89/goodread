Luca Turin is a fascinating man. This book was not. I could only make it about 1/3 through.Most of it was drama surrounding his theory of scent working its way through the scientific political machine, as well as a fair bit of chemistry and physics (my head is not currently in the right place for chemistry and physics).Perhaps if I was still involved in my old pastime of perfume collecting, I'd have more interest. The quotes from Turin were by far the best and most worthwhile parts to read.
