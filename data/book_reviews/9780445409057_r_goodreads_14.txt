Quick Summary: A mystery set in Old Hollywood


My Review: The Howard Hughes Affair is book #4 in the Toby Peters series.

About the Book: Our favorite PI is back it, and he is ready to solve another case.

Someone has stolen plans for an anticipated, classified military plane. With murders and mayhem all around, Toby finds himself in deep. He is a suspect and an investigator.

My Final Say: The draw with these books is the Hollywoodland angle. The story seems to follow a formula. That's fine by me. There is some offensive references sprinkled in this book, just like the last one, and I don't particularly that. In the end, the true criminal is found out, so that's something.
