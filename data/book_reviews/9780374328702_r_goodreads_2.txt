Harry’s home is a story based on the nature of family life and explores the differences between a ‘home & a house’ The story is about a child who is leaving for a trip away from home for the first time and describes the stages of the trip in detail using adjectives, verbs and descriptive language. The story emphasises on the importance between home and a home away from home by incorporating characters which introduce concepts such as nuclear family I.e. the mother and the extended family I.e the grandfather.

Definetely a book I would recommend to key stage one readers.
