Not a "novel" really—see it as an extended erotic poem instead. It’s one pretty cool experience, & fantastically odd; it’s a journey of infatuation into the erotic element inherent in all car crashes. Like a dada experiment with clashing ideas and absurd pop symbols, everything is sensuous, even human defects are seen through a wholly unique filter, in sharp contrast with the immaculate beauty of the automobile. Sex, like driving, has plenty of potential energy that's stored up—the want for a release is, then, the only viable option. A madman wrote this, it’s inexplicable—as if some alien entity tabulated, like the great Marquis himself, acts of perversion and undoubted sin, an tells us these snippits of porn, the hidden truths, in explicit detail. Death is seen as the ultimate goal, as the final & most amazing of all orgasms. “Crash” seems to have been written by one of M. de Sade’s devious, more artistically postmodern, cousins.The danse macabre: all the hallucinations culminate in acid trips, in wreckage, in homosexual liaisons, naturally. Truly a tremendous, astute piece of contempo art. It is the ultimate fetishist novel.
