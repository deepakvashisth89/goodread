Saint Augustine once admonished that we should never use the truth to injure. I believe there are dark and uncertain moments in our lives when it's not wrong for each of us to feel that he wrote those words especially for us.Another outstanding entry in the Robicheaux series that has Dave dealing with the mob and a trio of siblings from his youth. Bootsie's illness and protecting Alafair add fuel to an already explosive situation as Dave tries to sort out his conflicted feelings for the Sonnier family.Just love his writing in this series.8/10
