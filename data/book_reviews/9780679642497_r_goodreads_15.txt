Very short, but good overview of the development of the joint-stock company. It's helpful to have a sense of where these "powers that be" come from.The company has a horrific history, has drastically changed our global society and has potential for a lot of good. Anything that exists simply to create wealth, however, is fundamentally flawed and I don't think it will be too long (maybe 2-300 years) (maybe a lot less) until we dispense of it.Philosophically, I definitely fall into the "stakeholder" camp even though most would argue that maximizing shareholder wealth ends up being good for society. If a company is continually profitable and serving a need for society (providing jobs, providing a good product) it shouldn't need to grow exponentially. Wealth creation is only good in the sense that people are raised from poverty.
