Edge of Your Seat SuspenseOutstanding story telling. You feel like you're sitting right beside the sheriff the whole time! The description of everything from the landscape, the weather, and the conditions to the frustration of the dogged hunt for the fugitives is absolutely spot on and riveting. Definitely not a book you can put down easily.I hope the family of the victim have found peace and I hope the beautiful young lady has found a happy life where she feels safe and secure knowing these two are behind bars.The world can be an ugly place and we owe a debt of gratitude to the men and women who man the thin blue line between those that would do us harm and the safety of ourselves and our loved ones. Thank you for your service!
