If you’re into murder mysteries with a difference this story maybe for you. As are the others in the series, it is set in the Middle Ages with the pivotal character being brother Cadfael a chemist/doctor/detective. The story does capture the era and has a good pace with a few twists. This is an easy cosy read and no one will find the religious setting overbearing.
Having seen along time ago the ‘ancient’ British tv series starring Derek Jacobi, for me the series edges the books.
Sold but not spectacular.
