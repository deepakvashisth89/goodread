Mike is a good goalie, but he plays on the worst hockey team in the region. Each time his team loses, Mike sulks and storms. Mike takes an ax to his mother's maple tree and puts a dent in the family van with his hockey stick. Then, he hits and shoves his sister, Jenna. His parents ground him. They threaten him with goalie camp. Now he's mad at the world and especially at Jenna. Meanwhile, the family’s chow has puppies. Jenna falls in love with the little red female which she names Tan Tan. She knows that Dr. Edmunds, the owner of the puppies' father, has first pick. She, also, knows that her mother will sell the rest. When Dr. Edmunds picks Tan Tan, Jenna's heart sinks. Then, the doctor gives the pup to Jenna. The world is perfect until Tan Tan starts disappearing. One day, after school, Jenna finds the puppy and her mother exploring the edge of the road. Then, a few days later, Tan Tan escapes again and follows the school bus. After a very long day of worrying, Jenna returns home to find Tan Tan still missing.
