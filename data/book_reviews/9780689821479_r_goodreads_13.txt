A gentle and lovely story that touches on the subject of cancer, when that disease is making the life of someone you love difficult. Mrs. K. loves her 3rd grade class and they love her, but when she is out of the room for an extended period of time, they hear from the principal some devastating news...their beloved teacher is sick.Good Luck, Mrs. Kends on a hopeful note as she visits the class on the last day of school, as she tells the kids that she will be back in the fall.
