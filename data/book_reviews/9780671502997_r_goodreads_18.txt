Brooke Gordon bounced around foster homes growing up and has finally found a sense of family with her team at Thorton Productions, working her way up to director. She's happy making commercials and uses her skills from every previous job to be the best director she can. But now, she's having to work with a baseball player who will be modeling high-end clothes. Brooke knows nothing about baseball but, she she watches third baseman Parks Jones, she feels something.Parks is immediately attracted to the redhead watching his game. He immediately wrangles a date but is then offended to find out that she was watching his game for more reasons than to just see him. Luckily, the two figure out how to work together and eventually fall into an HEA.Since this book was originally published in the 80s, Parks is definitely an alphahole but could have been worse.
