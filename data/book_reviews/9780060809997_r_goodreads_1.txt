A headless corpse is found on the banks of the Thames, Sgt Cribb is talked into taking a look at it and notices the corpse's hands or knuckles that appear to show markings of having been used in bare-knuckle fighting, which is illegal in Victorian England. Is there a connection? Sgt Cribb begins an investigation and enlists a few people outside his department to join him in this endeavor. Thackery is near retirement and enjoying not being in the field. He provides some humorous commentary about traipsing around the countryside among other things not to his liking. Young Jago is enthusiastic at first until he becomes the undercover "fighter" and is thrown in among the wolves.
Some of the plot seems contrived, overall a typical "who done it" mystery.
