Very odd book, full of delusion and exaggerated ego, short on sense and honesty. Maybe it's the way things go after a gruesome murder, that the victim becomes a saint and the time spent with that person grows impossibly golden. But the tone Bogdanovich adopts reads increasingly unpleasantly. He blames everyone who "exploited" Dorothy--creepy Hugh Hefner and the sleazy Playboy machine, her vile husband, her unsympathetic step-father, her weak mother--but leaves himself no blame for seducing a much-younger woman desperate to escape the difficult situation she found herself trapped in.Kind of a sickening book, 'biography' at its worst...
