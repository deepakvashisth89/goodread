DNF. In a word: tedious.Definitely not a wallpaper historical...but way too much detail & slow-to-build nuance for my taste. I like fully-fleshed characters, to be sure. But character alone isn't enough. Stuff has to happen onscreen (on-page, that is). Plots must be propelled forward enough that I'm invested. This one...meh. Lots of detail re: the medieval setting & religious vs cultural motivations (i.e. clash of Anglo-Saxon vs Viking, Edward's quest to balance his chastity with duties as ruler, blah blah), all of which is further unsettled by an annoyingly bratty heroine who feeds her wedding feast to the dogs when she feels abandoned by her openly-chaste husband.It's definitely not a bodice ripper. I made it through 130 pages & didn't find any foul language, rape, or bloodspray (though some nuns were attacked off-camera). If you like a tamer (more subtle?) romance, perhaps this will be to your taste -- I didn't have any technical issues with the writing, so there you go. But if you're in the mood for a bodice-shredding good time, look elsewhere.
