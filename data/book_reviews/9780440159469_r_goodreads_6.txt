In this her first Marple novel published in 1930 ( not counting short stories) Jane Marple is not even the main character but undoubtedly the most intelligent and soon talent wins out. The quiet southern English village of St. Mary Mead where nothing ever happens it does when a hideous murder enter the peaceful, fictional settlement. The proper Rev. Leonard (Len) Clement likes a little fun however, lives here with feisty wife Griselda, much younger than he, irrelevant teenage nephew Dennis and incompetent maid Mary. Not quite a cozy family but good enough until unpleasantness strikes. A hated local wealthy magistrate is shot inside the Vicarage while Rev. Clement isn't present...shocking the village. Some people even suspect the vicar...who disliked the victim. Nevertheless the village has too many people who hated the dead man and Inspector Slack just the opposite of his name needs help. Bored Mr. Clement is only too happy to oblige but the road to revelation takes many dips and the police will not take him seriously. Deceit permeates, clues endless, knowledge fleeting. But an ace up his sleeve is dear Miss Marple, she looks frail yet her mind is sharp and the killer or killers will find this out. Did you not know about this conclusion ? Agatha Christie discovered that herself after the novel was read and Miss Marple not the preacher became the star and we are all grateful ...who doesn't like a seemingly harmless old lady with hidden skills. Colonel Melchett did, the Chief Constable of the county was more than happy... still the credit will remain with the police. And everyone important is now....contended...including Miss Marple who enjoys the obscurity until the next homicide occurs, such is the world of the mystery writers. One final word or words, the genre has been around since the days of Poe and Doyle, its last story is in the distant future.
