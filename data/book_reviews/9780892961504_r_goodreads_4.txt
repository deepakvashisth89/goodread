Really do enjoy these. This one was kind of complicated at the end,though its wrap up nicely. Mainly a rich lady is missing, but when its obvious she is dead, her husband is of course the main suspect since all the money would be his. In fact the whole family never liked him and was making sure to get their licks in. However the family were not full of grace either, they all had their problems and only was nice to the lady to get in her will. But everyone knew the husband did it, all but Mendoza, he had a hunch and was going to do anything to see it through.Highly recommend these, fun detective novels.
