Sally Ride:Shooting for the Stars, is a good book. It talks about Sally Ride becoming the first American women to space. The book talks about her childhood, her struggles and challenges, her influences, and her accomplishments. It also talks about her voyage to space and how she dealt with living there. The book is amazing and I will definitely try to model my actions after her because all of the amazing facts I have read about. I will recommend this book to anybody who is into powerful people who have changed the world and love learning about the past.
