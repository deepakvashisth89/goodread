Last night, I fell asleep about 500 pages in and was woken up at 3am by my Ring doorbell. No notification on my phone and nobody outside. The cats were sat on the landing, perturbed. I was half asleep and I'd been dreaming about Ruth going up to people's houses, so I was convinced in my rodent half-dream state that somebody was outside. I ended up going out with a bat and test ringing the doorbell and in the absence of the indoor chime, realised it had disconnected from the Wi-Fi and rang itself. 🤦‍♀️

Great book; It was as fun as the first if not better in my opinion. Gave me a good thrill at 3am and I'm glad I won't be featured on a true crime podcast. 🤣

Adamus was the typically gorgeous man only Tanith Lee can summon, but he was pointedly a wet wipe. Malach is better; I like his kindness to animals, but killing that old man was unnecessary and he seems a bit up his own arse in his ancient wisdom. He said 'I can fix her' when he looked at Ruth and then got his comeuppance. I laughed. Ruth's a hot mess. I don't even know who's to blame for her at this point. Althene was the shining jewel in the crown and I love her, but I'm not sure what she sees in Rachaela, who can't even decide her way out of a paper bag with an arrow pointed at the exit (I do really like emotionally constipated, passive Rachaela for the record. I just don't think she's relationship material). There wasn't as much of an overt build up of chemistry as I would have liked, but I love the way she roasts Rachaela and I'll never say no to more of her.
