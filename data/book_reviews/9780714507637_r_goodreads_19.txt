I remember seeing the film version years ago and loving it. Reading the script was such a delight, as I couldn't remember all of the details going in, but knew it would be an exciting play with excellent exchanges and twisting turns. It's such a brilliantly structured cat-and-mouse game, with some satire and critiques of classicism and nationalism layered in.
