90s manga was something else.

I feel like I would’ve found something to enjoy here if the MC wasn’t such a blank slate. The best way to describe Kira is… spacy? She barely speaks, she just goes along with people she knows want to hurt her and she doesn’t really seem to have anything going on expect art. It makes for the kind of character you often find in books like this where there’s a playboy who’s “icy and unapproachable” but they get protective and emotional over this one “special” girl. These kinds of stories aren’t for me because I can’t get excited reading about female characters with non-existent personalities

Rei’s story is somewhat interesting, but not unique considering most “bad boy” characters have similar backstories. Mother issues and a serious lack of self preservation.

So, there’s nothing novel here and the MC was incredibly boring to read about. Not for me.
