Continuing on my journey reading/re-reading all of SK's books in publication order.

Castle Rock again that's how you know things are about to get bad. I am so glad I decided to read SK's books in order there are so many Easter eggs/spoilers in this book. I took an instant like to Brian - him being in love with his speech therapy teacher and daydreaming about her is so sweet and funny. Although he does call her old at 24 - OUCH!

Castle Rock is a small town with BIG problems, there is something about a small town and the horror genre they just make sense. The plot quickly peaked my interest I think the concept of this book is so incredible. Alan Pangbourne is back in this book and I do like his character, he is pretty black and white and clean cut, but his intrusive thoughts are so funny.

There are a LOT a LOT of sex scenes and references which is never to my liking! Some of the sex scenes were so cringey too I know they were supposed to be but...EW. There is also something in this book that happens that I just hate and can't bare.

Now the real issue I have with this book is that it just didn't need to be this long, it could have easily been half the size and had the same impact. We follow a lot of characters and because of that I think they started to become flat and uninspiring I was struggling to tell people apart at one point because they just didn't have enough personality of their own. Mr Gaunt is a very creepy character but I still would have liked a little more from him.

Overall the concept of this book is a 5 star but the execution felt like a 2.5 star at best, its messy, convoluted and at times boring. I am always gutted to not gel with a Stephen King book and I thought this would be one I would love but you can't win them all!
