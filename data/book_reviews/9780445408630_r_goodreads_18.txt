Not my cup of tea, this light suspense novel was nontheless easy to read with enjoyable characters.I was drawn to the book, but originally thought it was a mystery (though the cover clearly says it's a novel of "Murder and Suspense.")Not the kind of riveting suspense and gory story that have been de rigueur of late, it is a tamer and more proper tale.Perhaps my rating is low, but I rated it based on the type of books I enjoy; your enjoyment and rating may be higher. The rating is not based on the quality of the writing or the structure of the story; I had no quibble with either. The rating was based on taste alone.
