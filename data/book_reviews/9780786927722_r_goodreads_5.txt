While I’m appreciative of Salvatore getting “the band” back together in this story & Wulgar reclaiming Aegis-Fang, there was just almost too much going on. Did Drizzt truly need another morality struggle? There was enough struggle with Wulfgar trying to determine who he wanted to be. There was plenty of action. I also enjoy seeing Regis being less dead weight in this book.
Don’t have much in coherent thought for this review. Maybe I’ll add more later.

Another Drizzt book I read when it came out in 2001, but reread it to refresh my memory. Is still a great book & I enjoyed it very much.
