When Peter and his sister learn they are being redistricted to a new high school without the storied sports tradition of the old one, they and their parents are very upset. Peter assumes he will be the star of the hockey team without any real competition - that is, if he even wants to play with a bunch of misfits. To his surprise, he finds that the new team, while comprised of some non-traditional players, has great potential. If he can control his preconceptions, make friends with boys from different backgrounds, help everyone get along and work together, perhaps the scrubs can become champions!While the the antagonism of Peter's family to recent immigrants seems unpleasant, this is a great hockey story which was a big favorite in my family (written by Neil Young's father, a noted Canadian sportswriter). I have introduced it successfully to many boys who are reluctant readers although it is a bit dated.
