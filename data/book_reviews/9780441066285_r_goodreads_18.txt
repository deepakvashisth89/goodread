4.5 stars. Not as tightly plotted as Death of a Dissident but still expertly done. I am in love with Stuart Kaminsky and his creations: Rostnikov, Tchak, Karpo and the others. All are completely developed with personal lives and psychological depth which make them very easy to root for, even Karpo who is a hard-core, dyed-in-the-wool Leninist. Cannot wait to get to the next one, which I will begin right away.
