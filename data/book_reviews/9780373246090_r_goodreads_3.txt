I really enjoyed Treasured by Sherryl Woods. It is the story of the third Carlton brother, Ben, an artist who chooses to live out in the country away from everyone. However, here comes Kathleen Dugan, an art gallery owner who is determined to introduce the world to Ben's work. This was just a sweet little story about two broken souls who begin to bare their emotions to each other. A bonus with the book was keeping up with the other two brothers and their wives from the previous books. I look forward to reading Destiny's story.

I have been listening to this series on audiobook and they are perfect for driving around and running errands. Also, I listen to them while walking my daughter's dog; dont' have to focus too terribly hard, just enjoy.
