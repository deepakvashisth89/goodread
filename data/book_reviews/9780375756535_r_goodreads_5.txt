OkayConceptually, this book rocks but something was missing. I can’t quite put my finger on it. In some ways it is a bit scattered as if you are only reading a synopsis of a more complete story, and you don’t have the time to really enjoy the characters. And while I think the pace is right for suspense, this book didn’t feel as suspenseful as it did romantic or even just fictional. Good read nonetheless just a little all over the place. The ending was interesting but also pretty underwhelming.
