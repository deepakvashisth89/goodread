When Cyrinda herself openly admits, multiple times throughout the book, that it was written purely for the money and that the publishers wanted all the dark, dirty details of her and Steven Tyler's marriage... well that should give you a good idea about the tone of the book. The first half, where she talks about moving to New York and becoming involved in the Max's Kansas City scene, you can tell she is enjoying recounting those tales. By the end, especially since she discusses Steven and their daughter Mia's relationship, you're left feeling a little dirty. It's unfortunate that she had to write this at a time where her bitterness over their painful relationship was still so strong.
