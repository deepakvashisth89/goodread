'Initial D' is perhaps the most popular car-focused manga with western audiences. I picked the first volume up on a whim after seeing it available on Amazon Prime Reading, and ended up going down a rabbit hole exploring the available English translations of this series.

In terms of the story, this first volume does it's job well of introducing the characters and concept. I know that the art style of this series (in particular, the depiction of the characters) is polarising; personally I found it difficult to differentiate between the male characters as many looked similar. The depiction of the cars and racing is the highlight of course, and specific models of car are recognisable.

I read the 2019 Amazon ebook release of this volume, but out of curiosity compared it to the original Tokyopop 2002 physical release. The text and translation are almost identical (complete with westernised character names and 'Fast and Furious' inspired dialogue), but interestingly the 2019 release has uncensored some previously censored content. For some reason, the 2019 version included some typos/minor editing mistakes that were not present in the earlier translation despite the text being largely unchanged. I did not look at the more recent 2024 omnibus release, but this apparently features an all-new and more accurate English translation so I will seek these releases out if I decide to continue on with the series.
