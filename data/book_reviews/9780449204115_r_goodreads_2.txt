Ad hoc read in May

A good friend of mine that I met in London a few weeks ago , gave me this book. He, like me, is a big fan of Asimov, and found recently that he had two copies of this, so as I didn’t have it, he brought it to London for me when we recently met up.

I have read Asimov mysteries before, like Tales of the Black Widowers, but had never come across this.

So the other day when it was lovely and sunny and we had an hour or so to relax in the garden, i decided, when I realised that even with sun glasses I couldn’t see my kindle, to read this.

An enjoyable book, that focuses on the murder of an author at a book convention, at which of course Asimov himself appears ! A friend of the dead author, also a writer but not as well known, thinks that the death is his fault and so dceides to try and investigate. It’s very humorous at times and a well written mystery, what else did you expect from the master of SF ??

If you like Asimov’s writing and I know not very one does, but if you do , this is a light hearted and enjoyable read.
