The first Sid Halley mysteryJump jockey Sid Halley loses a wife, loses a hand in a racing accident, loses a championship racing career, gains a new career as a private investigator in which he foils a particularly despicable villain and saves a race course. I left out the "minor" things. As usual in a Dick Francis novel, Mr. Halley suffers considerable physical abuse at the hands of the villains but does not fall into their hands through gross carelessness and stupidity as is sometimes the case in a Dick Francis novel. Well done.
