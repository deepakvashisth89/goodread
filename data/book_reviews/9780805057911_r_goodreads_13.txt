Ed annoys the hell outta me a lot of the time but by golly sometimes he can write real good. Autofiction of a dying man who is in self exile in the west and wants to see his verdant hills & home back east again one more time (relatable). Ed said this was his fat masterpiece which is both probably true (it emulates Steinbeck & others in that category and does it in its own way with the alternating timelines, which is admirable) and also an example of one thing I don’t like about him.
