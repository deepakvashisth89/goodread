This is my least favorite Hap and Leonard book so far....but hear me out. It's still 4.5 stars, so it's still an AMAZING novel. I found that most of the book didn't have a lot of Hap and Leonard conversations and interactions. We'll not as much as the others, which has always been my favorite part of this series.

The plot was great but also a little far-fetched at times. I like how Hap is diving into his distane for violence but also understanding its who he is. He wants to see the good in people or as he states, "Look for the Pony." Lasdale is a treasure. The man is one of the most talented authors alive today.

I loved the midget Red and Bob the armadillo in this story as well they were hilarious. So even though I say it's my least favorite, it's still basically perfect. Solid 4.5
