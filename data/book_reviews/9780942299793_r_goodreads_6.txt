Society of the Spectacle is Guy Debord’s critique of:

CONTEMPORARY MEDIA CONSUMERISM.

AND IT SERVES.

ITS DEADLY.

Debord posits that, despite their apparent differences, Capitalist Democracy and a Communist Socialism are BOTH fictions that serve the illusion of participation, and use MEDIA SPECTACLE (PROPAGANDA/ADVERTISING) to DISTRACT attention away from the ELITE and to PACIFY citizens.

Debord asserts that:

IN A SOCIETY of SPECTACLE:

Most of us have bullshit jobs.

We feel FUNDAMENTALLY ALIENATED.

And we are DEEPLY UNFULFILLED.

So we CONSUME to FILL THE (GOD SIZED) VOID.

We CONSUME MEDIA SPECTACLES.

ONE AFTER THE OTHER.

Like last nights “DEBATE”.

We may fell ENTERTAINED.

We may feel DISGUSTED.

We may feel ENRAGED.

We may even feel like we made a DIFFERENCE if we POSTED something (like this review) on SOCIAL MEDA (like GR).

But we are ESSENTIALLY PACIFIED.

And the system remains INVISIBLE and UNCHALLENGED.

Debord published this thing in 1967.

Before SOCIAL MEDIA.

So you might assume it’s IRRELEVANT.

But it’s SPOT ON!

If ANYTHING it’s MORE RELEVANT NOW.

THE KEY CONCEPTS ARE:

1: THE SPECTACLE (of course)

Debord argues that the SPECTACLE is more than MEDA.

According to Debord, the SPECTACLE is the TOTAL MEDIATION AND COMMODIFICATION of all aspects of social LIFE, INCLUDING: DATING, CREATING, and PARTICIPATING.

2: COMMODITY FETISHISM

Debord builds on Marx’s concept of COMMODITY FETISHISM, whereby COMMODITIES are imbued with EXTRA MEANING and POWER beyond their UTILITY.

It’s NOT JUST A CAR/HOUSE/PHONE.

It’s a LIFESTYLE.

It’s a POLITICAL STATEMENT.

It’s a CREATIVE ACT.

WE BECOME SPECIALIST OWNERS OF COOL SHIT.

COMMODITIES DOMINATE OUR SOCIAL RELATIONS.

COMMODITIES DICTATE HUMAN RELATIONSHIPS.

Debord expanded on this IDEA.

Debord observed that in a MEDA CONTROLLED SOCIETY.

The SPECTACLE is the COMMODITY.

The SPECTACLE dominates SOCIAL RELATIONS.

MEDA CONSUMPTION DICTATES HUMAN RELATIONSHIPS.

WE BECOME SPECIALIST CONSUMERS OF THE SPECTACLE.

FULL TIME SPECTATOR/CONSUMERS (📱👀).

3: SEPARATION/ALIENATION

Debord asserts that the SPECTACLE creates a separation between INDIVIDUALS and OUR TRUE DESIRES.

LOVE, MEANING, CONNECTION.

COMMUNITY, FREDOM, ACCOMPLISHMENT.

All that.

As such we feel EMPTY/UNFULFILLED.

4: PSEUDO COMMUNITY

Debord asserts that the SPECTACLE fosters a FALSE sense of COMMUNITY whereby COLLECTIVE EXPERIENCES are MASSIVELY MEDIATED.

As such the SPECTACLE replaces genuine human CONNECTIONS with SUPERFICIAL and UNSATISFACTORY VIRTUAL and/or IMPERSONAL INTERACTIONS.

READIT, FACEBOOK.

TIC TOK, TRUTH.

INSTA, TINDER, GRINDR.

All that.

5: CONTROL

Debord observes.

THE SPECTACLE maintains the DOMINANCE of ELITES.

BEZOS, MUSK.

PAGE, BRIN.

ZUCK.

All those guys.

6: MEDIA

Debord asserts that MEDIA sustains the SPECTACLE by reinforcing CONSUMERISTIC VALUES and IDEOLOGIES.

The SPECTACLE kills CREATIVITY, CRITICAL THINKING and COMMUNITY via CONSUMPTION and MASS DISTRACTION.
