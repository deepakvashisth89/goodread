Quite possibly the first erotica I ever read. I had no idea what it was when I checked it out of the New York Public Library in the mid 80s! The next time I went back to find it again, it was gone -- I wonder why?It was quite eye-opening for a teenager from a sheltered background -- this book included explicit sex, and no one was ashamed of what they were doing, or who they were doing it with. It may very well have been the start of my path into writing erotica. I just ordered a used copy, and I can't wait to read it again!
