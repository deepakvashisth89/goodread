The first Star Trek novel I've read that I don't like.

The beginning isn't bad, the writing is kind of dry. Then they leave the planet and don't return until the very end. The really annoying, very obviously nefarious female love interest is not only boring but also poorly written. The story is abrupt, confusing and riddled with inconsistencies, like for some reason Scotty forgets that he can just call the Enterprise and tell them they are going to be a little late?
Ending is good but I am so aggravated that by the time it finally happened it didn't help my over all feelings for the book
