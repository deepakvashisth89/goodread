"My Antonia" is Antonia, as told by Jim, her best friend and then her lover. Antonia is the conquest of the West, the time of the pioneers; these Europeans came to settle in the Prairie to clear it, cultivate it, live off it, and perhaps even get rich from it—hard times, made of suffering and disillusionment but also simple joys.Antonia is about ten years old when she leaves Bohemia. Jim, a little younger, leaves Mississippi to join his grandparents when his parents die.It becomes a love story when Jim observes Antonia from afar.There's life in the countryside, and then life in the small town. A world separates them, and a hierarchy is established between the inhabitants. And the gaze of others always, especially when it comes to watching young girls.I took a beautiful journey through time, in a wild nature that no longer exists, alongside Jim and especially Antonia.
