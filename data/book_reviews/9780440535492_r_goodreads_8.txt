A tale of godly gifts, unbridled talent, and the poisons of self-destruction.Hellfirefollows Lewis's rocket ride career from the lift off of 'Whole Lotta Shakin Going On' to the downward trajectory of substance abuse and debt. While his cousin, Jimmy Lee Swaggart, followed a self-righteous and hypocritical path to piety, Jerry Lee brought himself into Hell, honestly and wholeheartedly.I know what I am. I'm a rompin', stompin', piano-playing sonofabitch. A mean sonofabitch. But a great sonofabitch. A good person. Never hurt nobody unless they got in my way. I got a mean streak in me.This is a great story.
