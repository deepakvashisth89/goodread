Beloved Wolf
2 Stars

In the aftermath of a vicious mugging, Sophie Colton returns home to her family ranch to recuperate. There, she and her foster brother, River James, rekindle the feelings they have always had for one another.

While the ongoing arc surrounding the changes in Meredith Colton's personality from a wonderful and loving wife and mother into a vicious and cruel woman is interesting, the romance between Sophie and River is not at all appealing.

River is a great hero; he has overcome the trauma of his past with an abusive father and become an honest, hardworking, and compassionate man. Sophie, on the other hand, a spoiled, immature and haughty brat. The way she speaks to and treats River is appalling and makes it impossible to root for their relationship.

Despite my issues with Sophie and River's relationship, I am interested in the other Colton siblings and foster siblings's stories.
