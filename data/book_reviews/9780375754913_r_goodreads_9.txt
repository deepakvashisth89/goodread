Massive compilation of a tormented and tormenting writer who, it must be admitted, was capable of justifying his own bullshit theatrics with brilliance.Not everything herein is good, but much is worthwhile a few times over. I've learned a lot about America from Mailer, and though I think his influence has begun to pass me by personally, he's challenged me as a reader and a potential writer myself.He's daring and always interesting, since he'll pretty much manically surge from one concept and genre to another....usually hitting at least something which is going to stop you dead in your tracks!
