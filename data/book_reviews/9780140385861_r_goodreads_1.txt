Ron and Dallas are battlers. Kind hearted and hard working but life has dealt them just a few too many blows. Felix had it all, money, a big house, servants, way too many brains, but absent parents. Felix latches onto Ron. His only ever friend. He doesn't really mean to hurt Ron, he didn't mean to disclose Dallas' secrets - he just didn't understand that people would react the way they did. And ABR - the Agricultural and Biomedics Research company which employs them all - ABR is just a tad dodgy.
