Originally I had this at 4 stars but, y'know what? I'm giving it 5. Because, really, what more could one ask for in a history of murder? This book covers all the infamous ones past and present, plus some more esoteric ones you may not have heard before. It provides theories and solutions if there are any.Plus, the author digresses into discussions of H P Lovecraft several times, even working in THE CALL OF CTHULHU, despite the fact that Lovecraft's writing is not about murder.Gratuitous Lovecraft. That deserves an extra star.
