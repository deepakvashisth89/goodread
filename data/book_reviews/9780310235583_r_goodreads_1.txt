This was my first book by Gansky. I was hoping to discover another good author to add to my list. This was an uneven book, though. I liked the separate threads and characters through the book. And Gansky crafted some great phrases, too. However, the climax of this book, and it’s interesting premise, fell flat for me. The coming together of the threads was forced/too convenient. And most of the mystery of the book just went away. I was a a few dozen pages from the end thinking, he can’t wrap this up that quickly. But he did - sort of.There was some reasonable discussion of the relationship between faith and science, but nothing groundbreaking. The depiction of Julie and her mental breakdowns/visions and then her creative determination were compelling.
