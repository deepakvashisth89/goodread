Re-read, September 2022:I enjoyed this quite a bit more the second time around. The plot twist was easier to swallow since I'd had time to think about it.Basically, our main man thinks he's dying of stomach cancer, but it turns out he was misdiagnosed by some fancy New York doctor and he only has stomach ULCERS, so he's not dying at all, not even a little bit. I guess when you don't have access to x-ray technology, Mistakes Can Be Made. :-PIt still feels a little cliche to me, but I can appreciate how it pushes the main character to reevaluate his life and get some of that sweet, sweet Character Development. So that's pretty cool.Original review:This book revolved around one of my least-favorite cliches of all time"the doctor told me I was dying so I completely upended my life, only to discover that I'm not actually dying! yay!"and was, objectively speaking, pretty much complete trash from beginning to end. NEVER-THE-LESS, I found myself weirdly invested in the story, and absurdly pleased when it turned out to have a happy ending.
