I liked this book quite a bit, for its unusual beginning, for its unusual heroine (in terms of personality), and for the way the relationship developed with some humour. However, just as things are going along tickety-boo between the protagonists, the author (I guess) decided things weren't dramatic enough, and inserted a drama.The heroine turns up pregnant, and the hero reacts furiously, accusing her basically of running a con on him with the person who "really" impregnated her. Now, this is because he believes he can't have children. However, his reaction is so over the top, incorporating such a range of accusations, it seemed completely off the wall. If he'd just thought she was lying, or that she was unfaithful, I think I could have swallowed it.The scene as a whole, however, really reduced the quality of the book; it went from being quite believable, with appealing characters, to being somewhat believable, with somewhat inconsistent characters. That said, this author is new to me, and I'm still willing to read another of her books.
