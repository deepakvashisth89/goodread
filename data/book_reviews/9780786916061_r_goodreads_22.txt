On par with the first book of the series, but not as good as the second. Cadderly is growing up and he has enemies. Many powerful enemies, and the current one is a wily assassin that has lived many lives. This is even darker than the previous books and the world of Toril is unforgiving as Cadderly and his companions continue to find out. It’s a different kind of action/adventure than the first two, thus maintaining the interest of the reader.The series really focuses on Cadderly to a fault, I think. Some more backstory on the dwarves and Cadderly’s lover is warranted.A decent read which expands the fantasy world of The Forgotten Realms, helping me with my DMing! Also, it does have some more sophisticated themes like coming of age, dealing with the emotional difficulties of killing others, and exploring the repercussions of having immense power.I will continue to read through the quintet.
