A psychic cult reads a few papers on Pavlov's Dogs and uses this knowledge to spread chaos throughout Hollywood. This was my first time reading something deemed "splatterpunk," and I found the excessive depravity, body horror and gore a bit tired by the end. It was so overwhelming that it quickly desensitized me, leaving me impatiently eager for the good horror mystery tidbits underneath. Overall a fine story but not for the squeamish.
