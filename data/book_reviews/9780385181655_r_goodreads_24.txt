I got hold of this book decades ago and it really left an impact. Some of the entries were a lot of fun. Some were quite thought provoking. The most impactful for me was "Displaced Person" by Eric Frank Russell. This is the sort of story to read aloud to a friend, if you're into that sort of thing. Amazing writing and tremendous power in that story. But there were a lot of highlights for me, and I've revisited the book a couple times over the years. Overall a pleasure to own this paperback. - I think it's time to read some of the stories again!
