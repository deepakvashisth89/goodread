I took the opportunity to read Ms. Crooks work, and I truly have to say, I'm so happy I did! She has a unique writing style that captivates the reader. The pace is spot-on, and her characters are exactly what I would imagine someone during that particular era, and in the "territories" at the time, were like. Our hero is rugged, no-nonsense, and yet refined to some extent, while Ms. Sonnie is not only sophisticated, but knowledgeable, born in the wrong century in many ways, yet fitting right in where she is.The tale Ms. Crooks has spun for us is realistic and yet enchanting with just the proper level of romance, in it's own way. Most definitely a book I would recommend to my friends and family! Worth the read if you enjoy a good western, as I do!I was gifted a copy of this book and am pleased to give my honest opinion!
