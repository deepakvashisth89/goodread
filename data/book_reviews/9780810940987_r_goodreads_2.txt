Feels a little like two stories living in the same book because Dudley is introduced as the small dog who feels dwarfed by the world and objects around him. The heart of the book is exercise and healthy living which becomes Dudley's mission for self-improvement and self-esteem building. The healthy living aspects were solid and the story would've been fine if Dudley just modeled behaviors without so much backstory.

Photographs are impressive, incredibly cute, and support the text well. Recommended for PreK-2.
