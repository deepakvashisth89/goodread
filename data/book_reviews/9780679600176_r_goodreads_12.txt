This masterpiece is a classic, a work that I have always wanted to read.And here I am again in the heart of America!An intricate monument surrounded at the start! Rough writing, an upset chronology. Characters with the same first name! A bereaved family, a disabled son, and a son in love with his sister. A distraught mother and a father who died of alcohol.There is a whole family of secrets, servants, and mad humanity.It was a shocking reading.
