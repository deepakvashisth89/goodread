I'm always looking for a new historical series that I can fall into and love. I'm rather particular about my fiction and I know I am often rather difficult to please.I have been able to find and fall in love with a few good historical series including The Captain Lacy Series by Ashley Gardner, The Lady Julia series by Deanna Raybourn, the Julian Kestrel series by Kate Ross and the Ben Weaver trilogy (which I hope will become a series) by David Liss.This is the first Hannah March novel I've read, the author also writes under the pseudonym of Jude Morgan. Overall I thought 'The Complaint of the Dove' was a fairly enjoyable mystery set in London in 1760 before the Prince of Wales was crowned King George III.I enjoyed the setting, especially the mention of Bow Street, Jack Sheppard and the appearance of Justice John Fielding. I thought the relationship between the protagonists, Matthew Hemsley and Robert Fairfax, was interesting but I would have liked a little more character development. I wasn't captivated by either of them and so I will continue looking for my next favorite series.
