#2 in the retired Scotland Yard Superintendent John Sutcliffe mystery series.This is really a Peter Proctor story with Sutcliffe appearing late in very brief bits and contributing very little.Young aristocrat and foreign office worker Timothy Wycliffe was beaten to death, 1956, in Belgravia. It is now 30 years later and remains unsolved. Peter Proctor, once a young man on his way up in the diplomatic service then Member of Parliament and cabinet official who was a friend of Wycliff back then, in an effort to relieve the boredom of retirement is attempting to write his own memoirs. Unfortunately, the effort brings back memories of Wycliffe and Peter becomes distracted by these memories and finds he can't accept the simple a solution put forth at the time, so he begins to probe the past. The effort opens a fascinating window for the reader on British society during the 1950s and its changing/unchanging social mores since.
