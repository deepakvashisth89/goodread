How gorgeous is this cover? Every time I picked up this book to begin reading I had to pause and admire the beauty of it.The plot was a little monotonous. Lilly and Ethan were constantly doubting the other's feelings, which became annoying when there was obviously no reason to doubt or question. Also, what happened to Mari? She's Lilly's best friend but basically just disappeared.I wanted more information and drama! Everything worked out a little too easily.The writing wasn't spectacular but it got the romance across and the really beautiful thing about this novel for me was the verdant and sumptuous setting of Charleston and the countryside of South Carolina during the Revolutionary War. Usually we only get historical Southern novels that take place during the Civil War so I was glad to read about something not as commonly written about.However I am NOT here for the white savior complex that this novel uses. No, no, no.
