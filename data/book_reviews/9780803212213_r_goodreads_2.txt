A series of newspaper columns written by future Oz creator L. Frank Baum between 1890 & 91. They are humorous conversations between a landlady and her tenants. The editor Nancy Tystad Koupal has done a truly awe inspiring job of research, the columns are very topical and very local to Aberdeen, South Dakota and would be totally mystifying today without her introductions. That said however they aren't Baum anywhere near his best, everyone has to start somewhere and although I'm very glad the pieces have been rediscovered and properly published they are still rather dull and I found the book a real slog. However the last few are considerably better in fact the one about the electric farm is great fun, giving more than a little taste of the kind of writing Baum would eventually make his name with.
