The plot was multi-layered and the characters well written. I think it may be a bit too strong for middle schoolers, as the main female character is married at the age of 16 to someone twice her age who has kids all older than her from a first wife. He's also not gentle in introducing her to "married" life. It's not described, but the trauma is made very real through the thoughts and actions of the girl. Very deep, thought provoking book.
