This book was many things. It was written by Bob Greene, a syndicated columnist for "The Chicago Tribune". It was written many years after I had graduated from college, but was about an important part of history that occurred while I was in high school and college. It started with a simple newspaper column about Vietnam. I missed a lot (most) of what went on during that time because I was into the whole high school/college thing, and my parents just watched the evening news, but I never remember any conversations about Vietnam. Bob asked his columnist readers, "Were you spat upon when you returned to the United States?" I do remember that the war was unpopular, and that many demonstrations took place during that time (Kent State). 2.6 million American served in Vietnam; some 56,000 of them died. Bob got well over thousands of replies, and me, not knowing that much about Vietnam felt drawn to the book. While I was single, out in the dating world, I dated several Vietnam vets, but they never said much more than what they did there. One suffered from PTSD terribly. One of my friends drew #1 in the draft pick for Vietnam in the spring. He committed suicide that summer.Truly, many wrote to say that they had been spat upon...literally...in the face, and on their medals and clothing. These people doing this were of all ages, and sometimes parents. Others admitted that they too had been spit upon by words...horrible words. Things that have always remained in the back of their minds.I was horrified to know that many soldiers would ditch their uniforms immediately when they were on their way home, and put on civilian clothes so that they would not be harassed. How could people act that way, when our soldiers were there doing service for their country? How could I have been so unaware of what was really going on in our country? The letters these former soldiers wrote; many of them will put tears in your eyes. At least they did to me.It is a very readable and interesting (and sad) book about a big part of our history. I highly recommend it.
