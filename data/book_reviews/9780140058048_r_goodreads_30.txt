This is an odd book. The famous Sergeant Cribb isn't even mentioned until page 147. We get instead a Proustian description of a not-quite-voyeur trying to get close to a woman. Halfway through the book, no one's dead yet.Don't read the blurb on the back. It gives away details that I'm sure the author would have preferred to withhold.Eventually, we find out how the people all fit together, who's dead, and finally who dun it and why.Slow-paced, but an interesting variation on the theme. And credit for an excellent look at life in Brighton in 1882.Show more
