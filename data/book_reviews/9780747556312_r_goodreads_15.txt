This book would be suitable for FS and KS1, it’s quite short so it could be used as a read aloud class story at the end of the day. It could be linked to thinking of others – Marvin was so greedy that he carried on eating until he ate everything and ended up lonely. It all started because Marvin was small and wanted to be bigger like the other sheep, so this story could be used to talk about acceptance of everyone in PSHE. There are bright, bold illustrations to accompany the story which would appeal to young children.
