As both a health care professional and a migraine-sufferer, I was very impressed by the quality of this publication.
The font was easy to read, the sections were well laid-out, the content was clearly explained, supported, and neither condescending in tone nor too complicated.
The cover design led me to believe that this was more targeted to HCPs and I fear it may have prevented others from giving it a read.
Unfortunately, picked this up several years after printing, meaning that with advances in medical science, not all the contents are the most relevant. Would definitely be on the lookout for an update version.
