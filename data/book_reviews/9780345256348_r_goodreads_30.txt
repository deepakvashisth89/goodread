Four shorts written for the pulps. Not his best work. Pick Up On Noon Street is a, “what the heck happened at the end”. The other three are enjoyable if not exciting.Too many characters in these tales have “smart mouths”. Too many failed attempts to be witty by too many characters. In Chandler’s later novels the hero gets not only the best lines but all the lines. It does not work trying to make multiple characters clever. In addition, many lines just fall flat.
