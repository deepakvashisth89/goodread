I found this craft book very inspiring and will keep it on my shelf for a perusal from time to time and perhaps to teach from it in the future. I read the 5th edition

There are excellent exercises at the ends of chapters, appendixes, a list for further reading, as well as a marvelous variety of poetic examples. I love how the authors analyze the work in an approachable, down-to-earth way that teaches the various moving parts of creating and even editing poetry.

While the book was originally published in the late 1990s, so a bit of the poetry-submission advice is outdated (such as describing sending a SASE), the bulk of information and poetry in this book is motivating, well-written, and helpful for both newer poets and seasoned practitioners of the poetic art.

I heartily recommend this book for poets in various stages of learning their craft, educators, workshops, and readers who enjoy reading an assortment of poetry beyond what one read in high school. This is especially a great work for practicing poets to keep on their bookshelves for those days when grabbing an exercise, rereading examples, and/or enjoying some poetry before writing will get the muse moving. At over 300 pages, there's plenty of examples to choose from.
