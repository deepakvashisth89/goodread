I've been re-reading my FoxTrot collection of books, and I am glad I am! Really enjoying them and for the most part the strips are still very funny and only a few are dated. Come Closer Roger contains one of my favorite series, when Roger and the family head to "SkeeterFalls" on their annual disasterous vaction where they have their very own ranger for a good reason! I spent a few hours reading this book and it brought once again a huge smile and lots of laughs. Any fan of the series will love this volume!
