A bit of a dated story, but a period piece on attitudes about woman and gays, some of which survive to this day. Janet Mandelbaum is the first woman professor in Harvard's English Department. The men are not happy. Woman are not very happy either as she does not espouse the feminist views expected of her. She is set up. Her tea is spiked and she is found passed out naked in a bath tub. A lesbian is called to find her and they are in turn caught together sparking rumors. Janet's former friend and colleague Kate Fansler is called in to find the culprit, but the old boy's network and other obstacles interfere with her investigation. Then a greater rragedy occurs muddying the waters. A great mystery.
