Summary: This book lists different kinds of kisses for all kinds of feelings: friendship kisses, feel better kisses, Valentine kisses, family kisses, etc. It tells you that the best thing to say with a kiss is that you care and that you love someone.
Evaluation: This was a fun little story that would be amusing to small children before they think kissing is gross. It had cute pictures of animals showing all the different kisses. The drawings were done in the naïve style that would be fitting for a very young audience.
