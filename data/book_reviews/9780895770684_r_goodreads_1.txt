There are many interesting facts in this book. Since it was published in 1980, many of those facts are severely outdated, but it still has the ability to enlighten. Having no plot or characters to pull me along in the reading, I could only stick with it in small doses. It's taken me about two years to finish it by just reading it for a quick grab between other more compelling reads. I've enjoyed it, but I'm very glad to be done with it.
