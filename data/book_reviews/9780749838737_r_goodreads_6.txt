To me Little Miss Wise isn't very wise at all. At first it was a case of not living, which she was wise enough to figure out, but then to do the opposite and say yes to everything isn't wise at all. And she learns her lesson ten-fold. Perhaps she is wise but then she definitely isn't sensible about it. Perhaps we need a bit of both. Hopefully by the end of it she has learned that lesson, for though it seems like she may have, we have no indication of that.
