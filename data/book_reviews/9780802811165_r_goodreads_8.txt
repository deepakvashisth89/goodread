This book made my reread list. I love his eloquence. He often presents powerful messages in a gentle way. That is a gift.--------------------------------------------------------I've read different C.S. Lewis books over the years. He is the most often quoted author in Christian religion. This book, however, is my favorite so far. I LOVED this book. It makes me want to be better and to do better. It was eloquently written without the fire and brimstone speech.Sometimes books like this, tend to make the reader point the finger at those around them who aren't living up to their professed standards without looking into their own hearts. With this book, I don't know how one could possibly do that. It was gently stated.
