I love both of these lovely stories. The reader experiences family, love, support, and how one can overcome those who are unkind or abusive. But like a good portion of Betty Neel’s novels, the reader only guesses at the HEA, but rarely see it. Each story ends with the protagonists kissing and a proposal, but no real closure, and the antagonist just slips away.Fun reading, with its simple storyline, but fun romantic tanglings .
