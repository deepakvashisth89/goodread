A collection of short stories by Byatt from the 1980s. They were:Racine and the tableclothRose-coloured teacupsThe July ghostThe next roomThe dried witchLoss of faceOn the day that E.M. Forster diedThe changelingIn the airPrecipice-encurledSugarFor me the collection was variable, with some very weak ones, some pretty awful and a couple that were stronger. The last story, Sugar, is I think autobiographical and was an interesting study of parent child relationships.There seems to be a bit of an obsession in this collection with death and the possibility of an afterlife. There’s some odd stuff going on and I must admit quite a few of the stories just bored me and I am sure that I won’t recall any of them in a month or two!
