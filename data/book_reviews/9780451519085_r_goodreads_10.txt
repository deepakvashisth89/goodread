Interesting look into the life of Puritans, but extremely hard to get into. It took my like 6 weeks to finish this book. It had some great written insights like this one:

"It is to the credit of human nature, that, except where its selfishness is brought into play, it loves more readily than it hates. Hatred, by a gradual and quiet process, will even be transformed to love, unless the change be impeded by a continually new irritation of the original feeling of hostility." (pg. 190)

I say rent the movie, I haven't seen it, but you'd probably get the same effect.
