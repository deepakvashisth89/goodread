Stars: 3There were definitely some things to like about this book, but I never really got into it and found it tedious. The build-up felt really slow, and the reason for this wasn't something I was particularly interested in.I didn't remotely care about the love triangle Sanders created, and that is one of the main reasons the early portion of the book was slow. The bizarre friendship with Dick Fleming that Sanders weirdly had include them having semi-sexual encounters (?) It was a bit confusing to me because it sounded like they weren't having sex and were having tickle fun or some weird description like that. Anyway, so the protagonist also immediately begins having sex with the leader of the bad guys. Then she is going between the two having sex. Then they are just all having sex together and it's supposed to be this Bonnie and Clyde threesome. I just wasn't interested in any of this.So the actual capering was pretty interesting, but this wasn't the focus of enough of the plot.A surprising amount of the book was taken up with them being on the run and making ever more improbable escapes from their pursuers. These super powerful and dangerous villains never seem to think about having a car ready to chase them if they should escape. So the end just drug on for far too long. Plus, their big plan is absurdly stupid. The corporation is apparently feared by everyone, and they plan to go to Miami because one guy "knows people" there. But he is known to be from there. Why go to the obvious place? Even when they have people catching them, they continue along the same path. At most, they travel off the freeway for a day or so and then get back on the freeway. But they just disregard any idea that has them try to disappear anywhere else. It's Miami or bust for no reason at all. Why not drive to Mexico? Whatever. I liked that they got picked off one at a time, but I didn't care at all for the very end when the protagonist is going to confront and kill the bad guy. This seems a bridge to far for her character.Anyway, it's decent but not great. I'd be willing to read another book by this author.
