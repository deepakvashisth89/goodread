A delightful set of 3 novellas (vaguely interlinked) blending academic satire and Poe-ish horror and supernatural. Hynes' delightfully verbose yet descriptive prose adds much atmosphere to each of the stories. In particular he has a good knack of what it's like to be in the midst of the academic upheavals of the 90s when gender studies and postmodernism had taken off. The characters, particularly the secondary figures, come off a like caricatures of academics, but this works to the benefit of the plot and purported style of the writing. One could quibble about some aspects of the plot development, e.g. in the last novella the climactic scene(involving a switcharoo of one page from a manuscript for another)tends to wrap up too implausibly.Really enjoyed the cat character in the first novella - she plays such a crucial role in that one!
