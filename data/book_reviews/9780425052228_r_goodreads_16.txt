I picked this book up on a whim and was immediately told I needed to read it sooner rather than later since I live wedged in the setting of this twisted tale. This is a classic human horror story on level with Jack Ketchum and Ira Levin. I was hooked at the prologue and finished it in one fevered sitting.I love that the story spans so many years and pleased that it felt true to the area.A must read!!!
