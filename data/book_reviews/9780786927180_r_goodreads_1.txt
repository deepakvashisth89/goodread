Rabe's books are so weird. I get into them, and then, they get really boring, and I have to force myself to read, but then, something really cool happens, and I get into them again. This book was slightly less episodic than Downfall, but it didn't pick up again the way Downfall does until the end. The end of Betrayal is strong enough to make me give this book three stars rather than two and a half, like I was anticipating. The ending has a surprising and unfortunate death, a character I really like, and a couple of twists that I was not expecting.

Again, I really enjoy Rabe's creativity, another reason I give this three stars. Because her plots aren't as continuous, it leaves room for her to be extremely inventive with scenes. What happens in her books are events I've never read anything like in any of the other fantasy books I've read, including Weiss and Hickman's own Dragonlance books. Seriously, Rabe is very talented with her imagination in a predetermined fantasy world as famous as Krynn. I'm fascinated by the events she envisions, like an underground world of ships, covered over during the Cataclysm, an ogre cloaking himself as a human, a naga, an old Black Robe sorceress who thinks her reflection in a mirror is her sister, a sivak draconian who stands with humans because he's bent on revenge...and that ending. I have to give it to Rabe with her endings. I have to also give it to Rabe once again for using underappreciated races and types in the Krynn world. No basic elf, dwarf, human, etc. for Rabe. Now, we have Ragh, a wingless sivak, as a semi-main character. I really like that Rabe gives a voice to a diverse cast of characters.

Dhamon isn't entirely unlikable in this book, and Riki wasn't in it much, which was a plus. When she was, though, she was actually tolerable, and a new character that travels around with her makes her even more so. I found a scene with multiple prostitutes to be a little gratuitous, but when you get to the ending, it comes together and actually makes a lot of sense. I have to say that I'm continuing on with the Dhamon Saga in full curiosity of what will happen next to this hero turned thief and miscreant who still has a heart.
