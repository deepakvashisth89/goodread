I was pretty impressed when I read this, back in 1989, when it was just published. It contains great overviews of the great empires in the last 500 years. A few months later the Iron Curtain went down and the Eastern European Communist empire vanished. And for Paul Kennedy, it also was a great shock for his reputation because he hadn't seen it coming. Another illustration of how historians better look at the past and don't risk predicting the future.
