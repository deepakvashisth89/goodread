I really enjoyed this. I read the second book ages ago and wanted to read this one. I have since discovered that there is a third book!! Very excited about that.UDATE - I was mistaken, the third book isn’t actually connected to these two.I loved the writing, it was very poetic and exotic. The romance was good and I felt very connected to the character of Constance, who just wants to be loved. The history was nicely couched in the day-to-day lives of our characters which allowed me to understand the political turmoil of the time more than if the history had been more seperate from the characters. This book had a very nice pace to it and I just flew through it.It put me in the mood to read Shrike: Time of the Dragons and Shrike: Last of the Zinja by Robert S. Shea, which I feel are very similar in theme, although Shrike is fleshed out a lot more in terms of character perspectives, history and has a tad more violence. So if you enjoyed The Crimson Pagoda and/or The Scarlett Princess I recommend picking up the first Shrike novel - Time of the Dragons.4 Stars - A beautifully written romance with historical elements and a focus on the culture of the time period.Show more
