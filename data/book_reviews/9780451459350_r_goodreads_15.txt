This was a good book. If you like Christopher Moore, Neil Gaiman, or Chuck Palahniuk you'll enjoy it. It is a fantasy filled with dark, irreverent humor with just a smattering of clever human insight.I could not give it more then 4 stars because the book dragged at times. It was like the author was not sure where they wanted to take the story. I would chalk this up to experience, but the author has written literally dozens of books. Also, I felt like the climax and conclusion were too simple.Overall a fun read, with some clever humor.
