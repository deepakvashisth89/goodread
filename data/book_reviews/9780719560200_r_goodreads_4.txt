Initially, I thought I would not enjoy this book but surprisingly it turned out to be the un-putdownable type of travel/adventure book! A proud British major, Charles Blackmore, dreamt to be a seasoned explorer inspired by Sir Wilfred Thesiger, Sven Hedin & Stein and the likes. He planned and led the crossing of the Taklamakan Desert in Xinjiang province in China with a team of Brits, Chinese, the native Uyghurs & 30 camels. Taklamakan is best known as the Desert of Death (literally translated as whoever goes in never comes out). The desert also neighbours the Lop Desert of Xinjiang where the Chinese gov conducts nuclear tests. His account of the journey is really mind-boggling and the true grit & mental strength is palpable throughout this episode. I ended up feeling parched and felt as though I was part of the crossing team. A great deal is discussed on the Uyghurs and a little on the Silk Road history which is really interesting. A very rewarding book on adventure travel which borders on extreme craziness.
