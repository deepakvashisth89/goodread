As an Ira Levin fan, I was shocked at how bad this is. I thought once we found out who the building owner/voyeur is it would get interesting, but that's when I totally lost interest. I saw that it was about to turn into an erotic romp between a woman and her stalker and I said, no thank you. The book sounds like it was written by a bad Ira Levin imitator. It has all his earmarks, but they don't work. Plus, horrible characters you don't want to spend time with. It left a film of sleaze on my skin.
