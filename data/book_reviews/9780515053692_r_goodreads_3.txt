This was a late Marsh set in a cosy Kentish village in the 1970s. Alleyn and Fox are called in to investigate a suspicious death at a spa. Was it suicide or murder? The familiar class distinctions are made with the faded aristocracy, nouveau rich, the servants and a doctor and vicar thrown in as well as the black sheep of the family Claude.The victims reluctant friend Verity is drawn into the machinations of everyone. I enjoyed this familiar read of manners and snobbery.SPOILERS AHEADWe all hope it is the fake doctor Basil who surprisingly is in the victims will. I did find it odd Alleyn never prosecuted or charged him with fraud when they discovered he was a fake doctor. Claude the next obvious suspect is a blackmailer and petty criminal. Alas poor Claude blackmails the murderer Bruce the gardener who was also in the Will. The motive a rare stamp and £25000.
