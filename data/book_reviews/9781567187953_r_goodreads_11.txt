I read this book for research. It discusses a wide variety of relationships between living human beings and heavenly bodies, spirits both entirely heavenly (angels) and those who have previously lived on earth (spirit guides). On a personal level I found it difficult to accept entirely the communication techniques described between Earth and spirits, but the information is a good resource and I was able to obtain some useful notes. My biggest problem with the book is that a statement is made followed by an experience that does not really give factual evidence of the statement. For that reason I found descriptions less than convincing.
