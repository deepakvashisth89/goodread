This book seemed fantastical to me. The Owens were constantly stepping on crocodiles, feet away from hungry lions, waking up in sleeping bags next to lions, and facing off with buffalo. Mark's plane was always running out of gas and the engine stall warning came on at least 20 times.After finishing it, I read a New Yorker article. The magazine sent a reporter to Zambia 3 times. NONE of the episodes recounted in the book in which witnesses were present were true.Oh, and Mark went nuts and started killing people (poachers). Which is alluded to in the book when Delia moves out on him. Which was the cool part if you're a Joseph Conrad fan; I mean if you think about it, Mark out- Heart-of-Darknessed both the original Kurtz and the Marlon Brando version!2 stars because I was able to finish it. The chapters written by Delia were more readable than her husband's.
