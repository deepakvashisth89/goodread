Exhilerating story of a single pregnant girl. Brings you to the hardships of 9 months pain.
Chelsea finds out her upcoming baby was from her ex-boyfriend. Everything all of a sudden becomes painful, heartbreaking, and sad. Without her ex-boyfriend's support, she doesn't know what to do. But she's willing to raise this baby and continue her life with problems of keeping together her life, her friends, and her future.
Reccommend it to everyone. perfect book!
