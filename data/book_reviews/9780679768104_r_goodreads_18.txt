You expect a “noir” book to be dark, in the frame of the conventions of the genre - terse dialog, anti-hero with a “code,” arid LA landscapes, whatnot. This, though, with its recurring allusions to Sartre, Dante, Baudelaire and their ilk, is more a noir of the spirit, with nary a healthy soul to be found. An engaging, satisfying, but deeply world weary and sad read.
