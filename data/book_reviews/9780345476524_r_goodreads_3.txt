The reason why I added this novel to the list of my favorite books is because of how much Feline legends and myths were in the novel. This novel went further than what the movie did. I read this last year but from what I can remember it had three stories based on feline myths that were passed down from Ophelia Powers to Patience for her to read about these past Catwomen and how they lived. I felt that was an awesome addition to this book and made it interesting to learn about the different Catwomen. I started reading the last myth but I had skipped over it because it was difficult but I read the rest of the book.If you are looking for a novel that goes further in-depth with the Patience Phillips version of Catwoman gets this book.
