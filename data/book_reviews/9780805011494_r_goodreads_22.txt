Black Girl, White Girl By Patricia Moyes⭐️⭐️Inspector Henry Tibbett is asked to come back to the Caribbean Island of Tampica to solve a potential drug smuggling scheme.A couple of things, Black Girl, White Girl is a book in a series, Inspector Henry Tibbett Mystery, and is the 18th book in the series. Potentially that could be clouding my opinion of the book. However, this just didn’t do it for me. It felt very superficial, very cheesy, basic writing. There is not anything specifically wrong with it but if it wasn’t so short I would have DNF’d the book.
