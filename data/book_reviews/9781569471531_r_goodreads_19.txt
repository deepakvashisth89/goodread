Read this for a geographical challenge, and it was excellent for that purpose. Also well worth it for the knowledge of life in a city under siege, as it’s set during the middle of the siege of Sarajevo.However if what you want is a traditional whodunnit murder mystery, it’s not a good fit. And while it might have as many political factions as a Tom Clancy novel, it is also not a spy thriller. The pace is slow. There is a murder, and it’s a mystery, but investigating it in the middle of wartime, with a newly formed and barely existent government, a lack of basic services (water, electricity, phones, intact roads), and corruption everywhere, is more about navigating those challenges than anything tricky or clever.
