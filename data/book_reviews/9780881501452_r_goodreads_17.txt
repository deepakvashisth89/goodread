This is the second book is the Asey Mayo series, and the second book of Taylor's that I've read. In both cases, I found the reading highly entertaining, the characters are always fun and individual, I love the time, setting, and the set up of the mysteries. But in both books so far, I found the murder mystery a little too complicated (exactly how many people randomly visit someone within a 10 minutes window, or how many close friends shop at the exact same vendor on the same day) and the murder motive lacking. Especially in this novel, the motivation for murder was weak, and the information to uncover the motive was even weaker. However, I still found this book so fun to read and immerse myself in that I immediately picked up book 3! So while I enjoy the writing style, setting, and characterization, the murder plot is still the weakest link in the chain. Still, if you love the golden age mystery genre, this book is a gem!
