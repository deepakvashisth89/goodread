Crushingly disappointing. I'd been looking forward to a fun romp, and I'm a huge fan of department stores (which might one day be a thing of the past, c. 1850 - 2050) ... but while I love a good fantasy, this was just all over the map and didn't hold water for me. Such a business would go out of business instantly if run like that, which immediately made it feel like it didn't exist, wasn't real, and nothing mattered. Customers, staff, management, all equally unbelievable. Behaviours, not normal. Dialogue, improbable.

It felt like a script for a Marx Brothers movie, rightly rejected, and then performed by less charismatic actors.

I stopped at 1/4 way through (I have a limited lifespan, and 4,000 books on my to-read list, which grows faster than I can shrink it).

[Note: 5 star = loved it | 4 star = liked it lots | 3 star = it was fine | 2 star = disappointing | 1 star = hated it]
