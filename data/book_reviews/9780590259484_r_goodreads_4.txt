I'd probably round it up to 2.5 stars.It wasn't awful but it's a retelling of Beauty and The Beast...a tale as old as time.There wasn't anything to stand out from the others I've read except for the girl's father being an inventor and a lobster fisherman as well as the beast having a cyber/VR setup instead of a magic mirror.Belle is called BonnieThe Beast's real name is PeterThere's a Gaston stand in named GaryI don't think there's anything else to tell or spoil. I found this book at the Goodwill and since I like romance and fairy tale retellings I bought it.Short and sweet.
