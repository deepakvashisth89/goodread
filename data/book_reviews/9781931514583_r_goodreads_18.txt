I loved this! For so much of my life, I have been a closed minded douchebag guy when it comes to romance. I thank the heavens that this is no longer the case and that i am now open to romance and romantic comedies. They are so much fun! So titillating!

This manga is from the late 90s and although there are lots of cliches and some stuff hasn't aged that well, I did not give a fuck. The story is a hot, melodramatic mess. I loved this volume for its strengths and its flaws. I'm reading it strictly for hyperbolic emotional entertainment, and it delivers 100%.

On the back of my copy, the manga is described as, "An epic romance that spans 15 volumes, MARS is one of Japan's most popular comics for teen girls ever." This manga has unleashed the teen girl within me, and I am grateful to Fuyumi Soryo for showing me the way.
