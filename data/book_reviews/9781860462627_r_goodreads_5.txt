The impracticalities of stream of conciousness writing for an entire novel aside, this book was at best barely mildly amusing and at worst infuriatingly flighty, stunted and full of useless details. Part III of the book was entirely unnecessary and the story could have ended long before it did. The awkward flow of the language may be an issue of reading this book in translation, however the numerous typos and editing errors grabbed my attention far more readily than any of the characters or plot elements.
