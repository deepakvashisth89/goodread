as somebody not even slightly religious but has had the opportunity of being surrounded by those with different beliefs than me and feeling as though i should mirror them, this book has a special place in my heart. i’ve never been “converted” or anything of those sorts but the overall overwhelming experience of not knowing if there is a God and if there is how i would or should worship them is very familiar to me. this book did an incredible job at depicting that feeling of being completely lost in religious turmoil and having to depend on someone to lead you through the labyrinth that is religion. beautifully done, would recommend to anybody religious or non religious.
