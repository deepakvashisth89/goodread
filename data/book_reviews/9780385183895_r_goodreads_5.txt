Walk in the shoes of an insecure, spoiled man on the day he inherits his work-a-holic father's fortune and newspaper empire.Feel his determination to measure up to his estimation of his late father's expectations. See what lengths he will go to achieve his goal, and feel his ego bloat as he begins to succeed.This book is well written, the characters believable and interesting, the plot excellent. It swept me forward and made it difficult to put the book down.There are a few rather explicit intimate encounters in the book, which will exclude it from the reading list of some.I found this one on the $0.50 shelf at Gatsby Books in Long Beach.
