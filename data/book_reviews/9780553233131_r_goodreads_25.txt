I’ve noticed L’Amour sometimes uses similar words very close together, which would typically be frowned upon in creative writing. “She was excited by the thought. Tom was excited.” Or something like that. It happened multiple times on this book. Doesn’t really bother me, but I do notice it. ANYWAY.

We all know it’s not a L’Amour book until the smaller big guy throws the bigger strong guy with a rolling hip-lock. Utah Blaine does not disappoint in that area. I have to say I was completely shocked by the romantic twist and I did not like it. Poor newspaper fella. The amount of people who survived being shredded to pieces by bullets was a little excessive, but awesome.

Good book. Slightly different story, so that was interesting. Neal devastated me. L’Amour is great always.
