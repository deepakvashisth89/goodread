Pastor Cymbala encourages us to develop a deeper relationship with God. He explains that sometimes what we need is so far beyond our own human ability that we need to pray in dependence of God for Him to move in our lives and draw us closer to him in obedience and faith. These should be deep meaningful words and not just Jesus words that sound good in the right crowd. At the end, faith is focused in the future and on what we cannot see. Pastor Jeffress says faith in the assurance that God will do what he says he will do. Malachi says God loves us. Trust and obey for there is no other way to be happy in Jesus than to trust and obey.
