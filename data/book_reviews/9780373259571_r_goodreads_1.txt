Ah, Operation Honeymoon. Pretty straight forward plot where an inspector and a DEA agent have to go undercover as a married couple on their honeymoon to uncover a drug cartel. Add a lot of sexual attraction and tension, and we've got our story.I liked Blake, but I really liked Ronnie, who was a DEA agent because of her father's pressure, and even though she was good at it, didn't really want to be an agent. Her history was interesting, because of sexism on the workfloor and a relationship with a collegue gone wrong. Still she was competent, and I liked how Blake came to recognize that.Nothing special, but entertaining (and quite hot).
