Book # 2 in David Wells' series.I highly recommend it for church leaders and pastors, or anyone wanting to understand evangelical Christianity in the modern world.The books are now 20+ years old but frankly they are just as relevant. Wells is sometimes a bit cranky, but don't let it get to you. He has a sharp eye for the ills of the church to which she is mostly blind, and we need that. Many powerful and quotable sections worth underlining and returning to.
