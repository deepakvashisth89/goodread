This is the first book in theRicky Ricottaseries byDav Pilkey. I thought it was interesting that he did not illustrate these books himself, although the format of the tale is very similar to hisCaptain Underpantsbooks, without the silliness and toilet humor.I get the impression that this series was meant to appeal to younger readers, be more commercially acceptable, and avoid theBanned book lists. And thus, it lacks a lot of the charm of the Tra-la-la tales.Still, it's an entertaining and quick read and we love reading his stories. We all took turns reading this book and I think it would be a great book to get younger and/or reluctant readers excited about reading. I am sure that we'll read more of the books in this series soon.
