I'm not going to go into plot details or do a lengthy play-by-play because generally I would rather read books than write about them. This is an amazing book. The author's meticulous research and real feeling for the subject matter and time period shine through. I've read a lot of 70s-early 80s historical fiction over the past few years and this one absolutely stands out. It's too bad a lot of these books and authors seem to be forgotten and end up out of print. If you're a fan of historical fiction, especially Western or Native American stories, do yourself a favor and go on eBay or abebooks and buy a copy. You won't be disappointed.
