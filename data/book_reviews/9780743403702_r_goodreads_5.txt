As "Balance of Terror" is one of my favoriteStar Trekepisodes, in my youth I followed Diane Duane's collection of novels avidly as they explored who and what the Romulans really were.I do not "agree" with her version of the Romulans (I have my own "private" Star Trek universe with my own take on these Vulcan offshoots; you can see some of it on my blog:http://spocksbro.blogspot.com/).Ms. Duane is, nevertheless, a pretty good author and I like the character of Ael. I'm not so taken with Duane's rather simplistic view of "good," "evil" and "honor" but this isn't a likely prospect for a new Western Literary Canon, it's just good, solid, escapist fun.
