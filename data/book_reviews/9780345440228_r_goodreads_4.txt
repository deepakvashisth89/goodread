The main character is really lead along the garden path in this book. I won't spoil the plot, but I will say that I figured out most of what was going to happen 50 pages in. It didn't stop me from finishing or liking the book, it just didn't give me that edge-of-the-seat feeling, either.The villain is certainly a jerk, but not the type of villain you can sink your teeth into. I guess what I'm saying is that the characters weren't very fully drawn. And there were a couple of places in the plot where the main character went against all common sense in a way that wasn't justified by his previous actions.
