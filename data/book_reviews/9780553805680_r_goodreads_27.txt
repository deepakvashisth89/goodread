This was a tough rating. In one sense, I loved the strength, courage, and love of this little family going on without their beloved father and mother. Angela works so hard to make a home for her siblings; Thomas is just a wonderful brother; and the struggles they have with rebellious, troubled, and tearful younger siblings are believable and heartwarming.
But...
The plot with Angela and Carter was troubling. Like Christine from Beyond the Gathering Storm, she finds herself swiftly involved with an unsaved man who is pushy, flirtatious, and charming.
I just don't understand how she couldn't see Thane's attraction to her, but Carter's immediately; she wouldn't think of marriage when Thane was around because of needing to raise her siblings, but never thought of them at all when she met Carter. It gave her two different personalities, goals, and value systems that definitely became a problem. In a word, she was no longer believable.
The salvation story that takes place was perfection, though. Loved the way two dear people found their way to the Lord!
