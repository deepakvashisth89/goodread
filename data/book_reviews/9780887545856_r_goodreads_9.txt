Quick and concise, and I mean that in a genuinely great way. Angelique does not hold your hand and gradually acclimatize you to the context of slavery in Canada, but pushes forward to do justice to a historical figure erased by gross Canadian white supremacy that refuses to acknowledge the existence of slavery in Canada. For being so short there's so much to chew on, and the scene between "light and dark" was easily one of my favourites.
