Provided a very good overview of fourteen important land battles over a 130 year period or so. For the "armchair general" who already may know the history of a given battle fairly well, they should not expect much more than a refresher. For me, that was the case for the chapters on Gettysburg, the Battle of the Bulge, Persian Gulf and some others. But for others - such as Dien Bien Phu, Port Arthur, and Stalingrad - it was a solid introduction to the leadership, strategic situation, operational planning/execution, weapons/tactics of both sides.

Ordinarily, I think this would merit four stars, but I have to ding it because it has the most difficult to follow maps that I have ever seen in a military history book.
