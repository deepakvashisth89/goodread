A Marxist jerk goes to China, knowing little about it, praises Mao’s revolution after everything he hears from his translators. Though he arrives in this impoverished village in the early 1960’s, he says nothing about the world’s largest famine having just occurred. He was possibly covering it up, as he is quite clear that he is dedicated to Marxism much more than truth (in the Intro to the 1981 edition, he deludes himself into believing that nothing about Chinese communism has changed, just as the Chinese are half a decade in to chucking most of communism out the window). But much more likely is that Beijing covered up the famine so well that he just did not notice it.
