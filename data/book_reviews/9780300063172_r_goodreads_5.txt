It’s one thing to know that you should be talking about adoption to your kids as soon as possible. It’s a whole other matter to figure out the words to use! This book helps by providing wonderful example conversations between adoptive parents and children as young as two years old, in which the parents explain topics like who was the child’s birthmother and why she placed the child for adoption and answer questions from the child.It’s very revealing to see what the kids say at different ages and how their understanding changes over time and very helpful to have this information as a foundation before beginning to share information with your own kids. The book provides a lot of support for my firm belief that no matter how difficult the information to be shared, it is critical that the parents share it in a way that makes the child comfortable with talking about difficult adoption issues with the parents, thus normalizing the issue in the family.
