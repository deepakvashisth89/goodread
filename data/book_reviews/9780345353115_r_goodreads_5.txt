The word "iconic" is an overused one, but it's absolutely appropriate with Space Cadet. The phrase and concept became standards of popular culture in the 1950s and have remained so, sometimes used affectionately and sometimes in a pejorative manner and sometimes as nostalgia, but it all started with this Heinlein novel from 1948, the second of his juveniles. It's a military training coming-of-age story about duty and responsibility and patriotism and family and friendship. Sure, it could have done with a bit more diversity of characters, but that wouldn't have flown in 1948. Today is July 3rd, it's the Semiquincentennial, and although we seem to be in the midst of what RAH foresaw as "the crazy years," Heinlein was surely the most patriotic person in the sf field. Hats off to Heinlein, and Happy Fourth of July.
