Kindness, friendship, love, imagination, and respect are at the heart of A Little Princess by Frances Hodgson Burnett.This story has one of the most powerful narrative arches I’ve ever read. It explores how we perceive ourselves, how others perceive us, and how to always stay true to who you are despite outside influences. Sara Crewe, experiences—firsthand—how quickly your circumstances can change and, along with it, how you exist in the world.Sara, who at the beginning of the novel is well cared for and well treated, eventually faces unimaginable cruelty. Despite it all, she never loses her self respect nor her loving nature. She was defiant in the face of injustice, showing that nothing and no one could make her feel small. This aspect of her character is nothing if not admirable and inspiring.If you ever feel like you’re just a small piece of a very big and complex puzzle, read A Little Princess. It will show you the beauty of your “small” piece and its effect on the world. With you, the puzzle would be unfinished. We all deserve to be part of the puzzle. We all deserve to be held with a gentle hand and placed where we’re meant to go. That is life, and that is A Little Princess.
