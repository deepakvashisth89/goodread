Dawson takes you through some of her personal life experiences and many scriptures to illustrate how the fear of the Lord is essential to developing a close relationship with God.Some of the more moving aspects of the book deal with restoring relationships with others through forgiveness in order to also restore your relationship with the Lord, as well as evaluating whether you value the opinions of men above the opinion of God.I highly recommend this book for anyone looking to go deeper in their walk with the Lord. There are some amazing testimonies about God's faithfulness in this book.
