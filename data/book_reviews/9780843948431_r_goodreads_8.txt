I enjoyed reading Wyoming Wildflower. Sonnie has been away in Boston and is returning home after many years. Her father sent her away to learn to be a lady, but all Sonnie ever wanted was to be loved by her father. Sonnie is the youngest of 6 daughters and her father always wanted a son. Lance, was taken in by Vince from the Orphan Train and has loved Sonnie since the first time he saw her. Now she's back. Will they get the chance to see where life takes them, with all that is happening around them.I was given a copy of the book by the author for an honest review.
