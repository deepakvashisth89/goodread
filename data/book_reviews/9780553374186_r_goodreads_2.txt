Brings out some very important phenomena among girls' and women's development that are still quite taboo. I gave it 4 stars out of 5 because parts of it are a bit dated (it was written before the Internet and the emergence of GenY!) however I still think it is very relevant for anyone who wants to understand this strange phenomenon with us women - however independent we may have become to the eye, there still seem to be so many barriers to our growth that are hard to put our finger on. That's exactly what this book does: putting the finger on the sore spot.
