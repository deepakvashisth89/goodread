One word: YIKES!

The Book of Merlin was an unceremonious stumble at the finish-line that was published posthumously. Just, no.

I just read all of the "Once and Future King" series, including the timeless classics of The Sword in the Stone, the Ill Made Knight, etc. I'll give those a 5 star review to even this one-star review out. But man, this one should never been published.

Where to start? The entire thing is a long-winded, self-serving tirade where "Merlin" espouses his political philosophy to King Arthur. Merlin turns Arthur into a kid again (why?) and goes on a long, dry rant about Capitalism, Communism, and everything else that doesn't have anything to do with the Knights of the Round Table we know and love.

In the first book, the Sword in the Stone, Merlin was a benevolent teacher that used animals as a tasteful allegory-like nod to human behavior. It was fun, interesting, and didactic. In this book, there is a feeble attempt at wrapping political philosophy in allegory, but just devolves into an outright rant by Merlin about the world, war, humans, etc..

I read another review, and the word "Misanthrope" was used to describe Merlin. Totally agree! In the other books, he's a wise, loving and benevolent teacher. In this book, he's a crochety old man saying how bad the world is.

This manuscript should have never been published posthumously. I'm just going to go back and pretend that this never happened, and that The Candle in the Wind was the conclusion to the series.
