Oh, my heart has found a series that I adore! Although this book did not feature hardly any of the daily rhythms of homesteading as the first one did, the story picked up right where the first book left off. New characters were weaved into Marty and Clarks lives, and this second book had more of a focus on relationships and the daily ins and outs of what relationships in a growing family and community can look like. The faith based elements looked different, but were still there. I smiled, chucked and held my breath in this book from start to finish. Absolutely delightful series. I'll be moving on to the third book at some point very soon!
