Really, I enjoyed this book so much. It's an old skool one, where male chauvinism and prejudices rule. But just for this reason it is sooo hilarious! Our heroine is in jail because she attacked a truck driver who was trying to rape her because he mistook her with a prostitute. In fact she was wearing a mini skirt and this means she's a hooker. Ahahahahahah! Really? Hero saves her and takes her home. They were engaged some years before but he thought she cheated on him with her step-brother (Yuck) Really? Really! Now he plans his revenge that is bedding her because she's a fallen woman (ahahahahahah) and obviously leave her. But unfortunately for him, surprise surprise, she's a virgin sooooo he has to marry her because she could be pregnant. She doesn't want to. He insists. She refuses. He insists. Ok they will marry. He tells her he always loved her (ahahahahahah) and they are happy together. I hadn't had so much fun reading a book in ages!
