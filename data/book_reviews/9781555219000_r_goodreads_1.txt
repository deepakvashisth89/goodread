Even though this was a slim book of short stories, it took me forever to read, since most of them were not too gripping. The majority were pretty bad, especially the older ones. The best ones were:

1) Dracula's Guest by the master Bram Stoker
2) And No Birds Sings by E.F. Benson (known for the satirical Mapp and Lucia novels which I love)
3) Close Behind Him by John Wyndham (the best of the bunch) truly original and scary
4) Vampires LTD (about a vampire car!!!!)
I also liked China Rose, Quiet is the Night and Anne Rice's story, The Master of Rampling Gate.

All in all seven good ones out of 18 stories is not that bad for a compilation.
