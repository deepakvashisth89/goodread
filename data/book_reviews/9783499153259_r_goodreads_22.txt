I loved this. Partly because so much of Klaus Mann’s personality- his literary preferences, his personality queerness and his suicidality - resonated with me. Partly because of how astute he analyses the beginning of the third Reich and how Hitler and the Nazis rose to power. Partly because of the richness of his life (both literally and figuratively) and the way he can write.
In my opinion this is an excellent book, and I cannot wait to read more by and about him.
