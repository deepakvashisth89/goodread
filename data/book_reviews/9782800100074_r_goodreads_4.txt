I have a 1975 hardback reprint in French, from Dupuis. Originally serialised and published as a book in the early 50s, this is a straightforward adventure story.Having discovered a new species, the Marsupilami, in an earlier adventure and brought it back to Belgium to a zoo, Spirou and Fantasio are upset to find that the animal has been abducted, and they endeavour to get it back. That’s it.There’s an interesting gallery of secondary characters, but nothing much else. Fun, but nothing special.
