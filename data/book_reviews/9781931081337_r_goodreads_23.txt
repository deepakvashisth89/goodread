“But don’t you find it hard?” Sarah Jane had asked her once. “Keeping up with it all?”
Aunt Lillian had smiled. “Hard’s being confined to a sickbed, like some my age are,” she’d said. “Hard’s not being able to look after yourself. What I do... it’s just living, girl.”
“But you could buy your food instead of growing it.”
“Sure, I could, except it wouldn’t necessarily be as pleasing to my soul.”
“You find weeding a garden pleasing?”
“You should try it, girl. You might be surprised.”



I picked this book from my public library and I'm glad I did! is a heartfelt gripping book. I enjoyed reading it. It’s a book that talks about sisters, self-discovery, life, and a little bit of love. The writing and plot were amazingly done well, it felt like I’m reading about sisters, all right. Each sister had a unique personality, I quite adored everyone but Ruth and Grace struck me as the fierce sisters who like to get in trouble (i do like them of course). I highly do recommend this for anyone who enjoys a soothing calm book .
