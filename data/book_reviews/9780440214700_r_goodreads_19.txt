Quiet menace. Gorgeous, sensual violence. Senseless, pointless violence. The challenge of remaining passionate while seemingly being immortal. Tanith Lee skillfully wields her immense talent for description to pull the reader deeply into her neogothic fantasy of the Scarabae, a bizarre family of blood drinking sensualists who seem at once to be painfully young and, at the same time, ancient. This one is a slow burn but well worth the read.
