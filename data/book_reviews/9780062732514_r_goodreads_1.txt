Albeit the book was written in the mid-1990s, I was quite surprised with how much of the information about "the recent hard times" resounded with the country's current situation. A lot of the resources listed in the book are pretty outdated, but that didn't stop me from still reading the basic advice and suggestions Dunnan gave. Dunnan's way of writing was quirky yet personable, with many blunt bits of advice about budgeting, finding housing, etc.It's a good book to read for soon-to-be college graduates or recent graduates.
