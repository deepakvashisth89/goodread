In overall this book inspired by the T.V series is sympathetic to read but is too close to the T.V series. It is a reprise from 2 Batman, the Animated Series episodes: "Appointment in Crime alley" and "Robin's reckoning".I wouldn't suggest to fans of the series to read the book, it's exactly the same thing but if you're a die-hard one, it's always nice to have something to read about Batman.
