3.5 stars.Floodis like a police procedural (only with a private eye). It involves the main character's efforts at finding a (truly disgusting) criminal. Where this book shines is its blend of humor and very dark themes, as well as its strong sense of noir.Where this lost some points from me, however, is just how dated it is, and not just in its outdated technology and references. For some reason, everyone loved ninjas and the idea of a romanticized, "mystical" martial arts in the '80s, andFloodis no different. Oftentimes, I felt like I was reading one of Eric Van Lustbader's "ninja" novels rather than a dark and gritty noir. When people were "rising out of lotus position like smoke", bowing left and right, and throwing chops and kicks like they were going out of style, it took me right out of the story with how cheesy it all was.
