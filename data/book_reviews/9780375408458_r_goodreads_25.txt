I am really getting hooked on this series. The author is a great writer, and has mastered the art of flowing the words. You do have to be interested in a medical examiners job, and some of the gruesome things they talk about. There are lots of side stories, and for the most part, she keeps things grounded and authentic. She can drift away on a tangent that feels a little farfetched, but then this is fiction. I would recommend this author.
