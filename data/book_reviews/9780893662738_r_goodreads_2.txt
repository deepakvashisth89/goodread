This is not a novel. It is sort of like the scripts that come with those party game kits you can buy to host a murder mystery. Back in the 1980's tickets to the murder mystery weekends at Mohonk Mountain House (in fact, any of their special weekends) were absolutely impossible to get unless you had the right connections. So this book let us common people get a peek in the door. It's obvious that they had fun getting dressed up in costumes and pretending to be actors and that's the spirit in which it should be taken.

Andrew Benepe, monster maker; Stephen King, and Peter Straub also contributed to the text of this book. If you are a big fan of any of these writers, attended a murder mystery weekend or, like me, were frustrated in your attempts to get tickets, you might enjoy this book.
