The century referred to here is the 19th, and supposedly the pieces by "Anonymous" are from an earlier time when people did not dare put their names on such scandalous filth. There is no editor attributed here, no introduction or information about the contributors. Many of the stories are from the publisher "Masquerade" which also produced this book; in that way, this anthology feels a bit like a catalog promoting the complete works available.

I picked this up because of the selection from Samuel Delaney's memoir. An interesting excerpt, mostly about editing.

Many of the pieces are simply monotonous, focusing on tropes and a narrow range of interests. My low rating reflects how poorly it reads today, as well as the lack of context and background.
