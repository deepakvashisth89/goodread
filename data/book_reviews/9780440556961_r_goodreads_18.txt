I don't often buy books of essays--I find them pretentious and I resent being forced to read them while in college. I expected this to be a bunch of macho bologna, but was captured immediately by both the "voice" and the "world view" of Quammen. When I met him at a reading, way back in the 1990s, he was a serious, personable guy and far from the pompous jerks who usually publish essays.This is not too science-y for the average reader, and is certainly something a nature lover will enjoy.
