It's the second time I'm reading it, just because I was in need for something light after having finished a dreadful book on the ghetto of Lodz. I needed something to cheer me up and I thought Sharpe would do the trick. It did, but not as much as I expected. I guess this is just not one of his best works. Still, all the classic ingredients of Sharpe were present.
Apart from the joke on Desmond Morris' "naked ape", little made me laugh. Too much déjà vu I guess.
