Well written and well assembled reportage on a particular event in time but one with a plethora of preceding problems that led to the act of terrorism that I knew nothing about. This book went a lot deeper into the backstory of Vail politics and Central Colorado history than I expected. I would have liked to know more about the forensics of the arson and the specifics of the investigation because it felt like there was an underlying theory that the arsonists were just as guilty as the locals that hated the corporation were culpable - a sort of “the wrong thing for the right reason” ideology.
