Full review to come. For now, all I can say is THIS IS THE HISTORICAL ROMANTIC SUSPENSE I HAVE BEEN SEARCHING FOR ALL MY LIFE.

I both disliked and felt fierce devotion to the main characters in this book, and if that’s not witchcraft, I don’t know what is. I loved it.

FULL REVIEW:

Historical fiction isn't my usual go-to, but MARA, DAUGHTER OF THE NILE, besides feeding my Ancient Egyptian-obsessed brain, also gave me a romantic suspense where both characters are imperfect, conniving, and kind of scary in their own way . . . and I loved it.

Why? Because the author doesn't care whether you love these characters or not. She presents them in all their flawed, human glory through an omniscient narrator who presents both sides to them but doesn't ask you to love them, doesn't ask you to root for them.

Hence, while I disliked many of the people in this book (besides two side characters who are absolute sweethearts and deserved the world and more), I was captivated by the story and also loved-and-heartily-disliked the main characters, which made for a fun read.

One of my favourite parts of this book was the atmospheric description and world-building. It felt so immersive, and McGraw writes with a poetic lyricism that is both heavy and rich but also light and smooth. It was an absolute delight for my senses.

Anyways, all this to say that, if you're in the mood for a story rich in descriptive, atmospheric detail, flawed characters you want to throttle one moment and hug the next, and don't mind a few scattered moments of eyebrow-raising (this book was written in the 50s and there are times where it shows), go read this book!
