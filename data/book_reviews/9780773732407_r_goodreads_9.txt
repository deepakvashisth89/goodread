Great gentleman.

The first job i had when i left school was in the building trade and he was one of the bosses of the firm i worked for. He used too ask me to clean his car for him. I didn't mind doing it and he always thanked me for doing it. Its only now at 74 years old that I've got round to reading his book and really enjoy it. Its down to men like him that we are a free country. Well done Johnie.
