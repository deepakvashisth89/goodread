Hey, it's Vonnegut! I couldn't give it any less than 4 stars. I just don't think I can give it 5.
I did get caught up in the story of Rudy Waltz of Midland City, Ohio. At the age of twelve, Rudy went to the top of his rich daddy's house ( castle, really) and fired a rifle just because he wanted to do it. As he puts it, " I hadn't aimed at anything. If I thought of the bullet's hitting anything, I don't remember now. ..The bullet was a symbol, and nobody was ever hurt by a symbol. It was a farewell to my childhood and a confirmation of my manhood." It turns out that his bullet killed Eloise Metzger, a pregnant housewife shot dead while running a vacuum cleaner on the second floor of her home eight blocks away. And that's why Rudy went through the rest of his life with the nickname "Deadeye Dick."
