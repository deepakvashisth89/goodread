Rating: 4 starsWhile I loveWrath of Khanwith all my heart, I thinkFirst Contactmay be my favouriteStar Trekmovie. Yes,TNGis my favourite series, (so Picard>Kirk) and that may be having something to do with it, butFirst Contactis just a brilliant movie from start to finish andWrath of Khanhas some... weaker points, I'll say.The movies are very comparable. They are both the second movie in their respective series, both having a lot to make up for after an underwhelming first entry. They both take a beloved villain from their series and add to their mythos, building them up and fleshing them out to be more of a threat than they ever were in their original TV run. Both captains even have a personal connection to the main villain and it elevates the movie.Khan and the Borg are very different villains, however. They both effectively want to achieve the same goal, i.e. domination, but they have polar opposite ways of going about it. Khan has a flair for the dramatic, and the Borg have an inherent lack of it. Looking at it through aDoctor Wholens (because why talk about one sci fi franchise when you can talk about two) Khan is the Master and the Borg are the Cybermen. I personally love both pretty equally (in either franchise), and each serve their purpose brilliantly in their individual movie. But while the movies were even before, I just lovedFirst Contactso freaking much on my recent rewatch that it overtookWrath of Khanin my head ranking. And in reading this novelisation I had the same feeling.But this may be just the mood I'm in. Ask me again three months from now and I'll be back on theWrath of Khanbandwagon and scoffing at myself for thinking any differently. Or I could have an intense whale craving and become obsessed withThe Voyage Home. Who knows. Worse things could happen. I could have a brain aneurysm and think theSection 31movie isn't that bad.AlsoFirst Contacthas Picard shooting up the Borg with a Tommy gun. CanWrath of Khansay that.
