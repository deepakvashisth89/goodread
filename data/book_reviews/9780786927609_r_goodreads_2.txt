Shandril and Narm make a run to get to Silverymoon, trying to stay one step ahead of their growing list of enemies.I disliked this book so much that it made me mad. Shandril is such a cool character, and here she does nothing but moan and feel bad for herself. There is very little linear plot, no character development, and no unique set pieces. Just fight, sleep, repeat. Every battle feels the same, and the conclusion brought no redemption. I've really liked a lot of Greenwood novels, but I feel like he just wasn't sure what to do here.
