I have had this entire series on my shelf for years. Touted as the sequels to Willow. A few weeks ago we watched Willow again, and it seemed like a great time to jump in and get these read. I did not care for any of them much. I couldn't put my finger on it at first. Maybe halfway through the second book it started occurring to me, there may not be anything wrong with these at all. I think they just did not feel like sequels to Willow. As just an epic fantasy series, these may be perfectly fine. Lots of adventures, travels, fights, magic... But it just felt like it was not the same characters. Your mileage may vary.
