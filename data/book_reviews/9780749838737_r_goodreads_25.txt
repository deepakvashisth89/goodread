Little Miss Wise is lauded throughout this book for her wise decisions, saying no to people when she foresees that a proposed activity would have a negative consequence: for example she refuses to accept cake from Little Miss Greedy because she didn't want an upset stomach.And, being such a wise person, Little Miss Wise is aware that saying no all the time may upset people. So one day Little Miss Wise ignores her gut instinct and starts saying yes to everything -- succumbs to peer pressure, if you will -- and immediately suffers negative consequences.Don't listen to peer pressure, kids. Listen to your gut.
