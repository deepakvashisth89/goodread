Realistic Fiction review
ELM 572
2-19-2015

In Barbara Park’s, “Junie B., “First Grader Boss of Lunch”, I enjoyed the realistic fiction about Junie B. being a helper in the cafeteria with her kindergarten sugar cookie friend Mrs. Gutzman because she was her favorite. Junie B. did not realize the responsibilities of being a helper and following the rules was such a big job. But, with the permission and advice from her parents she was ready to help her friend in the cafeteria. Mrs. Gutzman outfitted Junie with her own apron that made her feel like a “princess”, plastic mitts, and a hair net. Junie remember Mrs. Gutzman telling her she wears plastic mitts and a hair net whenever she touch food for good hygiene “cause mitts and a hair net protect our food from dirty germs and hairs (p27).” Mrs. Gutzman didn’t remember saying it the way Junie B. had stated it. But, nevertheless, Mrs. Gutzman was willing to give her friend a chance to be her helper. Junie worked hard at her differ jobs keeping napkins on the countertop, sponging off the counter and greeting the children when they came in the cafeteria for lunch. But, that changed when her classmate May made the remark quoted by Park on page 64 “They’re just letting you pretend. Don’t you know that?” Junie B. knew she was a real lunch maker wearing her mitts and hair net. She forgot her responsibilities when she yells out at May quoted by Park’s on page 64 “ I’LL TELL YOU WHY, SISTER!” I said. “ CAUSE I’M KEEPING HAIR AND GERMS OT OF THE TUNA NOODLE STINKLE! THAT’S WHY!” With that out burst Junie B. lost her job and her classmates lost their appetite on Friday. But, after a long day at school Monday afternoon Mrs. Gutzman knew what would make her friend feel better showing up with sugar cookies, because Julie B. had reminded her to pay the class a visit. Also, Mrs. Gutzman knew she had a friend and helper that needed her and sugar cookies could help. This book is a good read for third, fourth and fifth graders. I would also this book in guided reading in first and second grade.
