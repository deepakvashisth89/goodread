This book not only helps you discover personality traits that could be making you miserable and affecting your relations; it gives techniques to overcome them. The author gives many examples of each baggage and practical alternatives to deal with them. It touches diverse areas of life: relationship with oneself, with family, at work.It's a book to read slowly and digest.I reread this book. It was great to see my growth. I recommend it to anyone who wants to work on having healthier relationships.
