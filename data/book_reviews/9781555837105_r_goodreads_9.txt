Hansen’s third offering in his Dave Brandstetter mystery series has the death claims insurance investigator looking into the gunshot death of Rick Wendell, co-owner of a gay bar. The police have arrested handsome, sweet Larry Johns of the killing as he was found with the gun in his hands, but Brandstetter’s inquiries lead to ‘Mr. Marvelous’, a wealthy architect and several more possible suspects.Hansen includes the riotous California gay sex scene of the time [it was a decade before the advent of HIV] and has the older gay Brandstetter feeling uncomfortable in it. The investigator prefers more traditional, long-term relationships like the twenty-year one he had before his lover died.Enjoy Hansen’s California noir tale.
