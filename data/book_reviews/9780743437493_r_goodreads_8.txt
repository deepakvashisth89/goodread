One of my new top Stephen King books. I loveeee how much this book explores the good and bad in Jack. He struggles with alcohol, anger, and his own personal demons, BUT he wants so desperatly to be good. Then add in a supernatural hotel and Stephen King asks his audience such an interesting question - how much of what happens with Jack's character is because of his personal demons or the hotel taking hold of him? Plus I found Danny's character and his "imaginary" friend and powers SOoooO interesting. Then add in extreme isolation and winter elements!I was really surprised how the characters real life drama, mental states, and dynamics were the most interesting part to me, although the horror was fantastic and chilling as well.
