I always find it fun to read 'future' fiction written long ago... this one was publishing in 1976.Definitely not as good a book as his later 'settling the solar system' books. If fact, one could almost consider this one a practice run.Glad we're still not worrying about the Cold War and 'Star Wars' missile defense, but it always makes me a little sad that in the mid-70s people thought we'd have moon bases by now, and we're not anywhere close.As for the book itself, it's definitely a social commentary with a sci-fi setting more than sci-fi. The characters are a bit unrealistic, and they do some really stupid things to make the plot go, but a fun read none the less.
