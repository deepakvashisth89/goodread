I have come to expect greatness reading Manchester's books and this book exemplifies why I have that expectation. From his magisterial biography of Winston Churchill to the recounting of the life of McArthur this author captures all one hopes to experience in reading great biographies. The same magnificent outcome applies in the Arms of Krupp as applied to a broader scope of history. I will leave the details for the reader to explore and enjoy while being educated in the most wonderful of ways.
