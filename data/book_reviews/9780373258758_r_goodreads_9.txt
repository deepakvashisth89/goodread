Let me start by saying, I really wanted to like this book. The storyline seemed interesting, if not your typical contemporary romance plot of girl meets boy, they fall in love quickly, deny their feelings for one another then come to their senses and live happily ever after.

That aside, the story started off well; the main character, Kayla Luck, inherits Charmed!, an etiquette service for business men. Detective Kane McDermott, however, has reason to believe it's a cover-up for a high class escort service instead, so he goes undercover to try to lure her into revealing the scheme.

Sadly, the book quickly lost steam after the first couple chapters. Kayla is an intellectual blonde with large breasts (go figure), but after a while I got sick of her "woe ith me, nobody takes me seriously" attitude. Kane, on the other hand, is battling his own demons and won't let his guard completely down, and so Kayla does everything she can to make him realize he loves her. I know that most contemporary romance novels have this same underlying plot, but in this case it was poorly executed. Kayla comes off as pathetic and desperate, while Kane seems like a neanderthal who is so stubborn that it's ridiculous. Not to mention that the conflict (if you can call it that) is rapidly skimmed over and resolved in less than two pages.

I didn't this book, but it certainly wasn't my favorite from Phillips, which is a shame since it is part of a series. Then again, who know, perhaps (the second book) is better, and worth a try...
