I first read this book and saw the movie over 30 years ago.Before putting it in my out box I decided to read it again.Both Joanna & Ted are around 30 before they settle down to marriage. Two years later comes their son Billy. Joanna gives up her job to be a full-time mum.Joanna loves her son but she is bored with diaper changing, feeding, reading & playing children's games. She feels she is loosing her self worth, her identity. So when Billy is 4 years old she walks out on her marriage and her son. She divorces Ted and signs over all custody, of Billy, to him.Ted suddenly becomes not only the bread winner but also the ons-on parent. He finds a housekeeper to help with the daily chores. He lives for his son and they develope a strong bond.Nearly 2 years later Joanna decides she wants her son back.The court case nearly ruins Ted, both financially & emotionaly, and "the mother' always has the strongest claim. Justice will provail but is it always fair and what of the 6 year old boy ?
