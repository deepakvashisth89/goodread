The Monster at the End of this Bookuses humor and suspense to distill the idiom "you are your own worst enemy" into a form digestible by small children. That might sound like a modest achievement. I consider it an admirable one. It is all the more effective here for wrapping the lesson in an engaging garb of silliness. Everyone should get a chance to enjoy reading this delightful story aloud to a child. Or better still, to have one read it aloud to you.I suspectThe Monster at the End of this Bookwill endure for as long as kids are learning to read. It deserves to.
