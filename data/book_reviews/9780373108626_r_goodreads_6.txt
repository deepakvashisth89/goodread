This is one of the better Harlequins out there considering the Hero. Usually the lead characters are so contentious with each other one wonders how they even fall in love. The hero in this one matched certain stereo types in that he was a rich, handsome, wealthy and titled. Except for the disagreeable start and the circumstances of how they met, for most part the hero, Carlo, is quite charming to Petra, he helps her with her quest, he takes her out and shows her around. He doesn't make assumptions on their relationship so when another man asks her out and she accepts, he is still nice to her. Although this can be construed as non-interest on his part. Generally a good one and I could actually see them falling in love with each other.
