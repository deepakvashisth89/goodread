Chinua Achebe writes about the contrast of cultures so lovingly and empathically that one can’t help but bemoan colonialism, but feel a deep sadness over all that was lost. The West African humor, tradition, intrinsic sense of the FUN of right now, the presence of song and dance- really couldn’t be more divergent from the stiff upper lip, duty bound (no matter how absurd) Englishman of the time.As only Achebe can do, i was completely drawn in and cared about our unfortunate protagonist from the start. Beautifully and naturally we understand his motivations, and thus his actions and so his heartbreak.No one else (that i’ve found) can so lovingly teach us what is really important & the damage western imperialist cultures have done to it.
