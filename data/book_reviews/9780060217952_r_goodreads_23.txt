This collection of stories is truly the pinnacle of children's folklore and ghost stories!!I didn't grow up on theScary Storiesseries - I was born in the mid-90s, so I missed its peak by a few years. But I've heard lots of great things about them, so when I found this copy in a Little Free Library in a nearby town, I jumped on it for spooky season.I didn't really know what to expect, but these stories were excellent! Well-written for their audience, really compelling and easy to read. Thanks to their length, they also skip out on the drag that sometimes happens when ghost stories are too long. These really are the campfire/sleepover story-telling length (except for the poltergeist one). They don't pull any punches, but they're not much more than kids who are into horror can handle.Kind of want to keep it, but I'm mostly excited to set it free in another Little Free Library to hopefully inspire the love of horror in the next generation!
