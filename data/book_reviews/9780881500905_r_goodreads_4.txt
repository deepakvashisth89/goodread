When Phineas Banbury visits Weesit on vacation, he decides to invest in the Cape Cod village his ancestors lived in. Soon, Weesit is more prosperous. But somebody doesn't like Banbury's involvement, and tries to sabotage his plans, and frame him for murder. Fortunately, Asey Mayo is back on the Cape, and willing to help find a murderer, despite everyone's urge to help him by finding clues. Taylor is able to use all the clues--from a missing watch to a missing sea-clam pie--to find a killer.
