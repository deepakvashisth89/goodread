This book sucks.A collection of rambles from the minds of our two titular characters, i'm pretty sure that this was written in the early days of the show, but even still the humour just doesn't match B&B. I don't think they'd ever write a book, or even come close to doing so, unless it was them lying about how much sex they'd never had. There's definitely slight slithers of their humour in here, but everything is just far too constructed for me to associate it with them, and maybe that's just the book being a bit fantastical just to compensate for its absurdity but i'm a big fan of the show and I just don't get a lot of it here. I don't think an encyclopedia was the right way to go, maybe more of an autobiography would've suited the two dumbasses better. I want to know all about Highland, and Tom Anderson, and Burger World. Instead we get inane drivel about superstitions and myths that weren't even close to what's been documented.Generally speaking, for a skim through this book is completely fine. But when you actually sit down and read through it, it is a bit of a chore. It's something I don't think i'll sit down and read through properly again, it's a below par book for a show that funnily enough deserves a lot better. The show pioneered the internet age, years before it even happened and we have to appreciate it for that alone.Beavis and Butt-Head Ensucklopedia is passable, would I recommend it? Maybe, to certain people. If you're a fan of the show maybe give it a go and see what you think of it.
