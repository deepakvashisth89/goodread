I will be less tedious than some in this famous play because everything has already been said, or almost!I read Romeo and Juliet after seeing two different versions of the play: one in an open-air setting with a refined natural backdrop and the other in a classical theatre, both adorned with drapes and shimmering costumes.Tasty verbal jousts, a ferocious and irremediable hatred, a pure, whole, and heroic love, an unjust sacrificial death, but triumphing over human stupidity, finally reconciling the enemy families on the corpses of their children.This play was a tragically epic, undeniably exhilarating, and utterly timeless work.
