In this Angelina adventure, Angelina and all the other mouselings are ice skating and decide to put on a show. While they are practicing for the show, Sammy and Spike, two boys, are being bullies. Angelina gets them back by stealing their hats. She then invites them to be part of the show. They agree to be a part of the show, which ends up spectacular.
