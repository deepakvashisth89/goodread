The Fisherman & His Wife is a great book for teaching an important lesson about greed. In this book, I read about a poor fisherman that one day catches a magical fish. This fish tells the fisherman that if he lets him go, he will grant the fisherman wishes. The fisherman lets the fish go and goes back to his wife to tell her about this magical fish. The wife tells the fisherman to go back and ask for a wish. Her wishes start small but then she starts to not be satisfied and asks for greedier wishes. Finally at the end the magical fish is angered by this and takes back all the wishes, making the couple poor again. In my opinion, this shows a very likely consequence of greed.The major themes in the book were greed and power. For greed we see this in the book with how the wife is never satisfied with what she has and keeps asking for more. From first asking for a cottage to then later on asking for a castle. For power we this in the book with how at first the wife wishes for a better home to then wishing for the power to "make the sun and the moon rise and set whenever I choose." Her desire for power grew out of control and it had to be stopped.The main genre of this book is Traditional literature. Children reading this book could learn that choices have consequences and that you should be grateful for what you have. This book was a WOW book for me because of the life lessons you can learn from reading it.The author used multiple literary devices and other forms of writing craft in this book. First, the author used the writing craft technique of foreshadowing. An example of this writing craft from this book is when the author gave hints to how having this greed would end badly. Examples such as the fisherman walking slower each time he goes to ask and the water getting harsher and the color of it getting darker. This writing craft technique enhanced the quality of the book by building tension and suspense regarding to what will happen at the end of the book. Second, the author used the writing craft technique of repetition. An example of this writing craft from this book is when the author for each time the fisherman would return to the fish to ask for the wife's growing wishes he would say "Oh, Man of the Sea, Come listen to me, For Ilsa, my wife, The love of my life, Hath sent me to ask a gift Of thee!" This writing craft technique enhanced the quality of the book by showing the extent his wife's greed.The author portrayed anti bias perspectives in this book by showing that you should value character over possessions.
