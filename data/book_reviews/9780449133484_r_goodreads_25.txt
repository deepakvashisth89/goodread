It has been too long since I have read an Ann Cleeves mystery. My favorites are still Vera and Shetland, but George & Molly Palmer-Jones are growing on me. Ms. Cleeves composes great mysteries in the manner of the golden era of Christie, P. D. James, Dorothy Sayers, Margery Allingham and Ngaio Marsh. She does not resort to gore and world destroying plots. As with the golden age rule, all the clues are present for the reader to solve the mystery, but I delight in being fooled. Wonderful read!!!! Kristi & Abby Tabby
