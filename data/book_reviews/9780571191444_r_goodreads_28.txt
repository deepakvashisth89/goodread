Pretty tedious all things considered. It’s narrator, Edmund Talbot is an awful windbag, and while the style of the prose accurately reflects his pomposity and self importance it also makes it a very unengaging 200 odd pages. In fact none of the characters have anything going for them which makes it very difficult for the reader to get sucked into what could have been quite an interesting plot. I enjoyed the vivid descriptions of the conditions aboard the ship, and what life must have been on the long voyages of the era, but other than that there was very little to be enthused by.
