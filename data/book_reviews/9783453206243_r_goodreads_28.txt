Liam Devlin, (almost) retiredA very readable adventure involving Devlin, plus some new characters, years and years after the events of “The Eagle has Landed”. The political events behind the plot — purloined advanced weaponry and KGB mischief — may seem a bit dated, but it’s still the same old Devlin. His principal adversary comes across as a bit too invincible, until the dénouement of course. Perhaps not Higgins’ best work, but certainly worth your time if you are at all a fan of his.
