Leigh Michaels' No Place Like Home was one of those fast paced fun reads that make you wish you were the heroine in the story. Kaye starts out with the wealthy baby food tycoon Graham, but as she house hunts, she meets Brendan and sparks fly.This is a tale of love or money and its good to see the heart winning out. It was easy to identify with Kaye as most of us struggle to make ends meet. Its not always easy to say that we wouldn't be swayed into a relationship because the person offers security. How nice that Kaye found love and security with Brendan and a beautiful house.I read this in one sitting and had a hard time putting it down. As always Leigh's books are on my keeper shelf.
