The first book of a new series featuring struggling theatre actress, Jocelyn O’Roarke, Murder on Cue mixes the dramatic world of the theatre with a good old murder mystery. Finding herself the prime suspect in disliked actress Harriet Weldon’s death, Jocelyn must fight to clear her name.I enjoyed the countless references to theatre and literature. I felt completely immersed in Jocelyn’s world, which made it more enjoyable. I am a big fan of theatre, so many of the references were not lost on me. I’m not sure how a lesser theatre fan would fare, however.Murder on Cue has all the ingredients of an old fashioned murder mystery with a hint of more recent books, slightly reminiscent of the likes of Janet Evanovich, but with a luvvie twist.I give Murder on Cue 4/5 stars.I received an advanced reader copy from the publishers Open Road Integrated Media, in exchange for an honest review.
