As the world's leading angel expert, Doreen Virtue was aware of her psychic abilities as a child, but it would be a long struggle to become recognised for this. Doreen strayed from God for many years, distracting herself with drugs and material concerns to feel happy. She worked as a relationship advisor but her true calling to heal was so strong that she had to answer...and teach others how to heal themselves both physically, mentally and spiritually. 'The Lightworker's Way' is a mix of Virtue's biography with advice on how to heal yourself, others and the planet - it even comes with an entire section of healing prayers, meditations and chakra cleansing techniques to develop your psychic abilities. If you've always wanted to awaken your spiritual powers to serve the greater good then this book is for you.
