Such nostalgia as I remember reading this as a child. This was a library book that we reserved after seeing it being read in sign language on CBeebies bedtime stories.A super sweet book, 18 m/o Bud loves pointing out the names of the animal cuddlies and doing the noises for them. It’s also a lovely repetitive sing song story, which he particularly engaged with. The images are soft and gentle and it is a lovely bedtime read. Perhaps one to buy long term!
