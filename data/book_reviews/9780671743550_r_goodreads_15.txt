One of my favorite Star Trek book.I am glad I reread it... It's one of those timewarp books where Spock goes back to the wild wild West where he meets his great great great times forever grand father. He has lost his memory and doesn't know why he is on earth but he somehow blends in and becomes one of the community. I love the way how the stranger in the strange land story develops and we get to see Spock without the strict "I am a Vulcan therefore I must be logical" face.I also reread the "strangers from the sky" in which another of Spock's ancestor is introduced. I have a feeling the author of that book also read this story and was influenced by it!
