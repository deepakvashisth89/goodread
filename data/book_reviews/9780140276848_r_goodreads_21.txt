Daughter of the Queen of Sheba was a very insightful book into the lives of people who have bipolar or know others with bipolar disorder. Told from the perspective of Jacki Lyden, we learned that the kind of "love" you learn from your parents, influences the love you look for later unless you unlearn it. Reading her memoir, we learn the importance of mental health and the effects it can have on the inflicted person and the people around them as well. It was a bit of a hard read at times, with mentions of abuse and medications and even neglect. Through her lighthearted humor and bizarre metaphors, we really get a grasp on her perspective, confusing as it was at some points. But maybe that was the point. I would recommend it to those who like psychology and learning about how different disorders affect the brain and the behaviors of a person. It really helps you build empathy and changes your perspective on people who have it or grew up with people who had it.
