I didn't particularly enjoy this book.William Sleatorwas my favorite middle school/early high school author and I very much enjoyed a number of his books, includingInterstellar Pig,House of Stairs, andThe Duplicate. I hadn't read this selection before, so I thought I'd try and it out and see if I enjoyed it as much as some of Sleator's other titles.Strange Attractorsfocuses on a time-traveling teenage protagonist who has a short series of adventures. I don't regularly enjoy time travel media, and this book is a good example as to why. The story was hard to follow because of the "time bifurcations" the protagonist is aware of or creates, and the alternate timelines confused me. There's an incredibly small amount of characters, which would have been helpful with the shifting timelines if there weren't two pairs of the same people from alternate timelines interacting with one another. Overall, I got the gist of the story but think it was too complicated for its own good.
