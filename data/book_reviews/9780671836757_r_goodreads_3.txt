Emma Lathen was the pen name of Mary Jane Latsis and Martha Hennisart who wrote twenty four books featuring the detective skills of a Wall Street banker. (Under the name of RB Dominic they also wrote seven books featuring the detective skills of a Congressman.) I first came across their books in 1981 and I read fourteen of the books in this series back then but this is the first time I have read this one.This was first published in 1966 and deals with racism as some people can't handle the possibility of a millionaire joining the New York Stock Exchange because of the colour of his skin. The story starts with a poisoning then a shooting attempt but the story is told with a lot of humour.
