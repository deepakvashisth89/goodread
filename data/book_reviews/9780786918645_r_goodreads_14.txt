As a fan of the D&D adventure, it was a lot of fun to run a party through it as a short novel, with many familiar faces and places. The story itself is harrowing, with the wizard highly limited in magic power, leaving me wondering why such a limited wizard would be on such a crazy mission. The book, however, did not touch on the meat of the adventure module, as the party barely had to fight any elementals at all. Still, it was enjoyable to read.
