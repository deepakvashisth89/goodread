The mystery is secondary to the satire in this tricky little entry from Barnard. It takes place in Australia with much of Barnard's trademark skewering--this time of the Australian social classes in general and the local police force in particular. The coppers are portrayed as stupid, randy, corrupt, lazy buggers who do as little as they can and still draw their pay. The last paragraph is truly a perfect ending; please don't spoil the book for yourself by reading the last page first.
