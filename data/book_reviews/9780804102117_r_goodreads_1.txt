I've had this book for several years and have tried to read it a few times now. I always stopped...not because I didn't like the book, but because I just lost interest in reading all together at those times. I'm glad I decided to pick it up again. It was a great and very interesting read. If you're interested in learning anything about the Easter Rising, or at least the lead up (even in the fictional world), I say give this one a go.
