Probably a good book for those engineering and sciencey types (which I am). First 2 chapters were incredibly interesting, about imagining minds many thousands of years from our own. It really delves into the human psyche well. The latter half was a bit less wonderment and more preachy, but presents rather outdated facts and theories that are a bit too outlandish to be acted upon. Interesting as a thought experiment though.I'd recommend it for anyone interested in experiencing wonder about our world, universe, and mind, but that already has a strong background in physics and science.
