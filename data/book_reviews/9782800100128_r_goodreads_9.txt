Our friends get to go on an assignment to "Incognito City", where the rich and famous vacation in style never fearing the paparazzi because no cameras, videos or other recording equipment is allowed! After stumbling over a gang of bad guys who plan to use a sleeping gas to knock out the whole city and rob it, Fantasio and Spirou come to the rescue!The second story is about car theft and how to discover who is stealing the new cars that just sold.
