This was one of those books from my youth that I remembered with crystal clarity - everything, sadly, but the title! Well, the internet is a place of wonders and miracles: thanks to the whatsthatbook subreddit I was able to track it down. I promptly ordered a copy to ensure that I would never forget it again! It's a lovely, slow-moving little story about the importance of honesty and integrity. The only detail I mis-remembered was that I thought Shan liked to buy sarsaparilla at Mr. Conley's shop, but in fact it was lemon soda pop!
