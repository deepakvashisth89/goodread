Comments on the Story:There is not much of a storyline to this book since it is more informative about the holiday. But overall I think that the organization of the material is done well. What I think does make for a story in this book is the pictures because that makes you feel like you are apart of the celebration with this family.Recommended Uses for the Book:I think that this book would be great for a unit on holidays in a classroom. It is very informative about Kwanzaa and tells the information in away that would be enjoyable to younger children. Also the pictures are great and everyone in the classroom would be able to see most of the details very well. I don’t think that this would be a great book for story time because it is most informational and there are quite a bit of words on each page so this would not keep the attention of younger audiences to well. This book could also be used at home, especially with older preschoolers, kindergartners, and even school-aged children.
