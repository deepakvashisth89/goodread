Alfie gets himself into a bit of a sticky situation as he gets in to the house and the door closes behind him with his mum and baby sister on the other side! A scary situation for a little boy who can't even reach the door handle. This story involves a group of people who try to help Alfie solve the problem without a locksmith.This would be a nice story to discuss with reception, year one or two. What would you do in this situation? Has this happened to you before? How might Alfie/ his mum feel?
