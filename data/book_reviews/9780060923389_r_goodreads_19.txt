Perhaps not Wentworth's best Miss Silver mystery. The murder and appearance of the detective don't take place until 100 pages in. There is a lot of repetition--events happen and then the police inspector or Silver go over it again almost verbatim with another character. Still, a fun read and a fascinating glimpse into 1950's suburban London with its cheap post-war outfits, overly nosey neighbors, and suppressed English malice (at times I had to remind myself I was not reading a Ruth Rendell novel!).
