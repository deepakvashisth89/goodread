This thing smells like paint and Montreal pollution.Sounds appealing right? At it's best moments, the book is nauseating and gorgeous. I've never read descriptions of the artistic process that have made me want to attack a canvas with paint so badly. It goes overboard sometimes- some of the descriptions are dramatic to point that they approach superficiality, and some of the grosser stuff is a little too disgusting to be entirely effective. But taken as a whole there's something really raw and powerful about it, and I feel like it's made a very small, very deep dent in my brain.... so that makes me feel less guilty about not reading it in the original French.
