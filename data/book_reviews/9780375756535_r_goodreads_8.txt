AppeasingTo begin I purchased THE DYING GROUND under the assumption that the title held a totally different meaning. I read the first chapter on three occasions and I finally decided to set aside the time and read it. THE DYING GROUND fooled me in a good way. It wasn't HIP-HOP like I'd read, but instead a mystery littered with question after question. The characters, plot and depiction are so very real making you feel as though you are right in California as the story takes place.THE DYING GROUND is Nichelle D. Tramble's first novel. I found it fascinating and detailed, allowing the reader the perfect entrance. I do hope to read more from this aspiring author soon.A Maceo Redfield Novel (Strivers Row)]]
