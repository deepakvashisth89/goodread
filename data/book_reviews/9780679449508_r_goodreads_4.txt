What's Worrying Gus: The True Story of a Big City Bear by Henry Beard and John Boswell is a picture book about the famous neurotic polar bear, Gus. It tells the story of Gus' adventures in the Big Apple and how he achieves fame. It is actually pretty funny! Gus became the biggest attraction of the New York City Central Park Zoo. I rate this book a 9.5 because even though it was a short book, it still made me laugh and want to keep reading to find out what was going to happen to Gus.
