The author’s writing style was good, but not particularly captivating. The storyline was adequate, but felt lacking, especially in the area of motives. Why was the murder weapon there in the first place? The killer’s emotional baggage was discussed but never fully revealed. A sexual overtone within the story didn’t really work with the general theme - or was it the theme? It was as though the author started in a lot of directions, but when the reader followed a particular thread, it didn’t really lead anywhere.Probably won’t seek out this author’s work.
