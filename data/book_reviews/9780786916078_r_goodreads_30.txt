This is the fourth book in the Clerics Quintet series , following the lives of Cadderley , Danica and the Bouldershoulder Brothers , Ivan and Pikel . The dwarfs add so much entertainment to the books , particularly Pikel , with his cherished ambition to become a druid , instead of the dwarf which he is . Cadderley is still becoming used to his new , increasing powers and the others are getting used to the new Cadderley . Danger is still awaiting them however , not to mention a very large bad tempered red dragon ! Hush I said not to mention . . .
