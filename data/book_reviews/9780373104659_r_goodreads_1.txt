3.5 stars.This is me, while I was reading thispassionate bittersweet, second chance trainwreck.These MC's were each other's one true love, but had been unlucky to meet at a point when the H was still awaiting his divorce decree from his business/marriage of convenience to another woman. I don’t usually enjoy romances where there’s an element of cheating, so I feel like a huge hypocrite for having enjoyed this. But I didn't read this from my usual judgemental perspective, because the H’s marriage to his wife had been a death bed ceremony performed only to appease his dying father. If the H had been in love with the estranged wife or if she’d also cared about him, I probably would’ve seen things from a different perspective. But their decision to divorce was mutual, the wife had moved on immediately to her new lover 3 months after the wedding and the H was happy for her.The H was also so obviously in love with the heroine that the flashback scenes of their initial meeting and their courtship were beautiful.'I think it's you who has the magic,' he said smilingly.'You can make me feel like a boy again, looking at the world through a child's eyes. You're a witch, Amy, a beautiful, untamed witch.'The smile died in his eyes, as his glance slid to her mouth, her lips parted and vulnerable.At the end of the story, when he confessed his love, this is what he said:'I walked into the gallery, and it hit me like a ton of bricks. You were so beautiful, in that yellow dress, so innocent, I had to have you. As I got to know you better, I realised that I wanted to marry you, but I wasn't free.'He kissed her forehead with gentle lips. 'You were very young, and I was thirty—too old for you, and I had to hold myself back all the time for fear of frightening you away.'This is the H, Matt:This is the heroine, Amy:She was his ideal, dream woman and he’d even bought this unique golden gown, years ago in Morocco, that he had promised to keep forthat special womanin his life that he knew he would meet one day. Matt had bought that gown when he was a 20 something year old man and he'd almost given up hope of ever finding the woman to wear it for him. His dream was to paint a portrait of his dream woman wearing that golden dress. I thought that was so romantic, albeit a little bit cheesy, but hey, I love cheese ! The only negative aspect of this story was the H's refusal to tell the heroine about his "situation", because he'd been afraid of losing her. He proposed marriage to her and thought that things would turn out right in the end because his divorce was due to be finalized in a couple of months. That turned out to be his one horrific miscalculation because he didn't consider that a vindictive, scorned wannabe OW, called Celine, would reveal it to Amy.Once again Patricia Lake built a romance novel around MC’s who refused to communicate, except at the sexual level, and this caused all the angst and drama in this story. There were points in this story where I was close to pulling my hair out, in frustration, because these MC’s prefer to engage in childish arguments, jealous tantrums and sniping instead of just sitting down and talking about the main issue that caused all the conflict between them. I know it’s a plot device that’s often used in these vintage HP’s but sometimes authors go a little too far and prolong the agony to ludicrous levels. I mean: just how realistic is it that angry lovers will keep ignoring the ONE BIG ISSUE that’s like the big white elephant in the room ?In reality, it’s the opposite because lovers often talk too much about stuff that bothers them. I’ve concluded that this author LOVES to drag out conflict resolution, to the point where the reader begins to stop caring whether or not the issue becomes resolved. In spite of this, however, I did enjoy this novel because I love besotted H’s and there aren’t enough of them in HPlandia. And Luke had been so besotted/obsessed, that he went off the rails after the heroine dumped him and ran away ! He started searching for her, begging her cousin/roommate for information, drinking himself to sleep, not eating, not sleeping and wallowing in the agony of a jilted swain. I loved it !I’m even gonna get up on my high horse and gloat about the sick, sadistic pleasure I get from storylines where obsessed H’s moon over the heroine and come close to the brink of self destruction.I know it’s wrong to feel this way, but it happens so rarely in HPlandia that I think my sick sadistic glee is well deserved. Too often it’s the heroines who suffer their pangs of depression, in silence, while H's apply for citizenship inManwhoria, as their solution to their post break up angst...This was a fascinating trainwreck, though, because it's a second chance romance that’s not built upon an accidental meeting of the MC’s. The H had been searching for the heroine for the past 3 years because he still loved her and wanted to be with her. He himself had stated that he couldn’t move on with his future unless he got some form of closure – be it positive or negative. The other big problem, for me, had been the author's inability to establish the H's celibate status during their 3 year separation. The heroine had been celibate, though. But, I'm an old fashioned and somewhat naive romantic soul so I decided to believe that Matt had been celibate during these 3 years because he'd been so obsessed with Amy and had been preoccupied with finding her. So, unlike Neo in the movieThe Matrix, I chose to take the blue pill and continue along in my self created bliss:Luke was such an obsessed, jealous and possessive H that he even went so far as to confess:' But in the end I had to find out if I could free myself, by seeing you again. I expected you to be married, and I thought I could exorcise your ghost by seeing you with your husband, and maybe children.'He shrugged gracefully. 'But when I saw you again I knew that I'd been kidding myself—I loved you more than I could have imagined, and if you had have been married I think I would have killed your husband.That comment shocked me !But it didn't bother me; actually, I found it kinda sexy. Yes, I know...I'm sick and terrible...This H is one that fans of obsessed, jealousMine, Mine, Mine !heroes will love. There was one passionate scene just after the MC's initial reunion when Matt had seemed almost deranged with jealousy when he demanded to know how many lovers Amy had been with after him. He kept giving her these punishing sexy kisses until she confessed the truth. In real life, women will most likely run a mile away from a guy like this but in the safe fictional world of HPlandia, it's awesome !Patricia Lake also used another favourite staple of hers: the evil, spiteful wannabe OW who makes trouble just for the sake of malice. This is the 3rd novel I’ve read by this author where an evil OW, who has NEVER even been romantically or sexually involved with the H, destroys the MC’s relationship because she’s the type of woman who believes that if she can’t have the H, then the heroine shouldn’t have him either. However, in this case, the MC’s budding relationship could’ve survived the wannabe evil OW’s interference if they had spent more time communicating verbally rather than just engaging in hot sex. The H failed to tell the heroine this important bit of information and give her the choice to wait a few more months until his divorce became final.Amy, the heroine, was a bit of a childish mess, but I think she would’ve understood the H’s peculiar situation if he’d been honest with her and told her the truth. His failure to communicate meant that the mischief making wannabe OW, Celine, was able to fill Amy’s head with a bunch of exaggerated and sordid lies. I didn’t blame the heroine for being angry and running away when she heard that he was married. She was just 18 years old, in love for the first time with a man who had taken her virginity. It’s understandable that she would feel dirty, used and betrayed when Celine revealed that the H was still married. I really hated Celine because she knew the H didn't want her but still had to meddle and embellish her story with lots of lies.There was a lot of additional OTT drama caused by:1. The heroine's grandfather working in collusion with the H. If the grandfather had told Amy that the H was no longer married, then the MC's reconciliation might've happened earlier.2. The H's stupid plan to make the heroine jealous by letting her think that he was romantically interested in his new secretary. This was taken too far when the heroine misinterpreted the entire situation and thought that he was engaged to the woman ! In reality, the secretary was happily engaged to another man and was willing to help out the H by going on a date with him, to make the heroine jealous.3. The heroine's silly plan to enrage the H's existing jealous tensions by flirting outrageously with a series of men at a party. The poor besotted H was fuming and came close to causing a major public scene.I did enjoy this, even though I hadn't expected to after I'd discovered the H was married. I did wish that the meddling wannabe OW Celine had gotten some comeuppance but her role had been just that of the mischief making woman scorned. These MC's truly loved each other and I was so happy for them when they finally got their HEA.
