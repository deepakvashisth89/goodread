Truly loved this book for its nuanced urban landscape, the way the author handles magical realism (the park in the center of the city that is a portal to the Spirit world}, character and plot development. Really interesting question de Lint poses to the reader: what would YOU do if you woke up one morning and found that your body was not your own and that another had traded places with it?de Lint's background as a musician and poet as seen through the lenses of his characters, infuses this narrative with depth and breadth. Hope it gets optioned for a film, and that they are true to the novel. Plan to read the whold Newford series!
