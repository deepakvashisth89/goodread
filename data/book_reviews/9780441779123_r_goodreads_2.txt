Another entertaining slice of the life of our painfully flawed protagonist Slippery Jim DiGriz. He may have found himself in a long-term relationship but he is anything but settled as he is soon dragged into a mission to thwart a seemingly impossible interstellar invasion.Full of hi-jinks and laugh-out-loud moments, this one was a blast. It does suffer a bit fromReturn of the Kingsyndrome, though, as it ends four or five times.
