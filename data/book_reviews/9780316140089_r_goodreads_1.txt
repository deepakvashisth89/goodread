I read "The Spy on Third Base" by Matt Christopher. I liked this book, although it had a very boring plot, and I didn't get much out of it and it wasn't very interesting.

This kid named T.V has an amazing ability on telling where the ball is going to be hit before it happens. His friends don't believe him and T.V gets disappointed and wants to show them that he can. One game, T.V gets nervous, because he doesn't know if he should say something, or just let it happen and not tell his teammates. He tells his teammates where the ball was going to go, and they end up winning the game! After the game, T.V. gets stopped by a reporter and labels him as a "psychic". Then, T.V. gets a random phone call from someone saying " You better not use your powers next game or else!" then hangs up. T.V ends up using his powers and they win!

I would recommend this book to Middle School people who likes baseball. There is a lot of baseball terms that you will need to know. Male or Female doesn't matter. It overall is an ok book. Very bland though.
