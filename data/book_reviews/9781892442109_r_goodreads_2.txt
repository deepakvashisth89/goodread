First of all, thank you so very much for the ARC..:)This book. Wow. With books that carry any sort of religious nods, it can be pushy and contrite. this book was amazing. And i dont give that lightly. I was pulled in from page one. I wanted Tom to be back with his wife so bad. I felt sad, happy, and angry at times. It covers everything. It was as if i was the journey with them.All the morals are there, Love is the GREATEST; God is always with us, though we dont all know/believe. I really thought that it would go too far into religion and become just that, but it didnt. I felt like i knew these people and i was hoping for a happy ending. I fully recommend this book to anyone. Religious or not, it doesnt matter. You will enjoy this book.I DO think it is absolutely So romantic that someone love you that much to risk literally, his soul.
