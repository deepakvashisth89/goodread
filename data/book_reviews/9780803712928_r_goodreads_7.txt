Summary: This book is all about Kwanzaa and what each day means. It explains the different meanings of there traditions and is very informative. The illustrations are easy to understand as well as the verbiage.Analysis: I loved this book! The language in this book is pretty extensive but creates a great discussion about a holiday that is different than Christmas!How I would use this book in my class: I would use this book for a unit on Holidays celebrated around the world to help my kids understand and appreciate different cultures.
