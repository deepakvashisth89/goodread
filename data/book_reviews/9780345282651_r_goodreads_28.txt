Another Star Trek Log that pretty much coasts by on the strength of Alan Dean Foster's interpretations of cartoon scripts, this particular volume isn't one of the best in the series. The animated series had about the same percentage of clunkers to great episodes as the original series did, and the three episodes in this one (The Survivor, The Lorelei Signal, and The Infinite Vulcan) are all middling shows. Foster does make them rise above the limited level of the original stories, though.The Star Trek completist will want to read these, but any other fan will want to get the books with the really good animated series episode adaptations (like Yesteryear or More Trouble, More Tribbles or the Counter-Clock Incident).
