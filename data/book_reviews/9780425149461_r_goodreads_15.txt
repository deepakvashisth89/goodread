Disturbing and tragic. Ketchum writes with such brutal honesty and sensitivity that leaves even those of us with the toughest hearts in tears. Child abuse is the most horrific act in my opinion. Worse than the child abuse here is how the system continued to fail the child.Before Stranglehold, I had just finished The Girl Next Door by Ketchum. These two stories have left me emotionally depleted. I must pick something positive for my next read.
