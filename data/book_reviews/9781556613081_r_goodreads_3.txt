I liked this book a whole lot more than the last one. Although once again I was a little annoyed with a statement made; "Southerners' hatred of black men was so extreme and irrational, I had to fight against hating the Southerners myself." It seems to me that it's stretching it a bit to say that Southerners as a whole hated black men. There were good Southerners. What about the people on the Underground Railroad, which was mainly in the SOUTH? So this I was unimpressed with.I really liked the part where Corrie went to her hometown in New York.
