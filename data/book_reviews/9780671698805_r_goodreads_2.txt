When I first pulled this out of my huge plastic bin of old books in my garage, I thought it was going to be cheesy based off the cover. However, it was pretty good. I have been in the military for 25 years. You could tell someone did their homework in that regard. It is sci-fi but did not seem outlandish from a military perspective. I also liked the idea of unpacking (i.e., the psychological aspect) in the book too. The story is decent but not revolutionary.
