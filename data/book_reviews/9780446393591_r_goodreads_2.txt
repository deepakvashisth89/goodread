For a book published in 1991, super good and still holds true to today, all centralized around the theme of constantly being outside of one’s comfort zone. When you’re comfortable, you’ve lost. When you get complacent, you’ve lost. If you’re not taking risks, you’re losing. It also hinges itself on another important point which is the emotion known as passion. You should be PASSIONATE in whatever it is that you do, and that success lies in where you can employ your passion unto. It made me realize that these goals that I’m setting for myself are subpar, and that I can be optimizing goals if they are secondary to a dream, because dreams are where passions lie. Supplementing goals as a means to your dreams and a measure of progress towards them is the real way to use goals.
