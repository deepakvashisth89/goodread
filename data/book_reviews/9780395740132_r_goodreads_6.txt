If you are doing any civil war battlefield touring, and plan to visit small sites as well as major ones, this is the first book you should buy. It provides nice brief summaries of almost every civil war battle, and maps explaining the major actions, super-imposed over modern topographical maps. These are invaluable for getting your bearings at each site. If you want to set the battle in its historical context, the first book you should buy is James McPherson's Battle Cry of Freedom, available in a cheap and handy Kindle version for referencing on site.
