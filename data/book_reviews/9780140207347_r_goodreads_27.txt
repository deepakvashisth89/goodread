Seminal psychology from 1960, the work relies on mental security springing from existential delusions of free will and the idea of a self. Obviously the dangerous schizophrenic needs removing from society where the condition can cause harm to others. The thinking is that consistent ideas of personality and an illusory self are required in order to hold down ontological security in relation to others.

Hell is other people as Sartre wrote in No Exit. We are self conscious of self and then our being on view to others. Case study Peter can only relax with a blind girl and dogs to avoid the human gaze. Protecting inner self often involves killing it off.

Traumatised patients without a strong sense of self from their parents go on to attract attention to feel more secure. It may be desirable to be embodied or that may leaving you feeling too attached to the body and traumatised by any harms it experiences. Those who believe in a separate soul may be freed up somewhat in their disdain of bodily sin and death.

Protecting the fragile sense of self through isolation and fantasy. Kafka used his anxiety to function in the real world. Many may find the risk too psychologically taxing.

None of which is unique to the schizoid personality. Indeed we all wear a multitude of masks and personalities depending on our surroundings. What is our true self anyway if not an hallucination? A combination of biochemical states and general behavioural responses to stimuli.

The question remains how to be well adjusted and authentic in a civilization which Freud said is impossible without repression and Krishnamurti rejects as maladjusted?
