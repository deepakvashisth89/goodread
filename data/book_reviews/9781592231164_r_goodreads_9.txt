I first heard of this books a few years ago but in one of my summer class's our professor let me borrow one and I absolutely love these books. They are full of useful and useless knowledge. The funniest thing was we were talking one day in class about The Flash and how he couldn't be real and blah blah blah, and low and behold as I was thumbing through the book there was a section on super heroes and if they were possible and one of the pages was about the flash. I really think this series of book could get students not only to read but to start asking question therefore taking control of their learning. Great books.
