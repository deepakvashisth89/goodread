An entertaining look at American customs and our way of life through the eyes of an Italian. Beppe and his wife move to Washington DC for a year. His story is a reflection on the year he spent in the US including anecdotes re: the differences in culture and customs. As an American, it was interesting to see our country through the eyes of someone from Italy.I did laugh at some of his anecdotes i.e. Americans love ice (I know in Europe they don't use ice the way we do), how many options exist in our stores & shopping malls, and how big a celebration we have for July 4th (in England it is certainly not a holiday!).The book was originally published in 1995 so it is a bit dated. The sections on computer technology, fashion trends, and politics were out of date.I would read another book by this author. I understand he wrote a book re: Italians as seen through the eyes of tourists. I'd like to read it.
