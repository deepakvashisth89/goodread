Beautiful descriptive writing. Excruciatingly stupid plotline.

The premise is all right - a painter is musing over his life, which he spent going back and forth from the city to spending his summers in a tiny town, painting his landlady. He muses for a LONG time about how he would be subtly cruel to her (enjoying her discomfort, posing her for hours and not letting her move, thinking of her as nothing, having sex with her and covering her body entirely with his own so that she couldn't move, etc)
and the story see-saws between how nasty he was and the life of a man in the town, a simple china painter.

This was not done well.
