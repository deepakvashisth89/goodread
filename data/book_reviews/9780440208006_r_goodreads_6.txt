The story was alright and while at times the books did make me laugh it seemed to drag in parts. All in all the writing style just was not to my liking.

I did not care for Annabelle much she had no intention of fighting for what she wanted and just let the men in her life handle everything. I understand for the time period that was the norm but considering how much Annabelle dislike the decisions being made for her one would think she would be little more resistant and not just an obedient young woman.

Ross was a funny character at first but he soon got a bit boring. Just became a cliche character that was around to be a love interest for a girl who had never made a decision on her own.
