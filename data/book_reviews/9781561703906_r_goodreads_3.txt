What I like about The Lightworker's Way is that it teaches through Doreen's personal experience many things healers and those who want to heal their lives should know. This book was one of the first that gave me a language to understand what I already knew through experience. Just as it is difficult to speak to a car mechanic without knowing much about cars, so too is it difficult to learn more about spiritual and emotional healing without knowing the language. Doreen taught me the basics using simple explanations and examples. Ironically, Doreen shares her dream of writing the book, and I often remembered this when writing my own. My book,Transformational Healing: Five Surprisingly Simple Keys Designed to Redirect Your Life Toward Wellness, Purpose and Prosperity, takes the reader deeper into the healing experience, going beyond wellness itself to the core of life and living--Purpose. My book also gives the reader a means to find their own personal emotional connection to physical symptoms. While I recommend this book for any healer's shelf, I advise they use it as a stepping stone for deeper exploration within.
