I hated the first story in this book. The characters were all deplorable and I didn't care what happened to any of them. I'm sure it didn't help that it's been 20 years since I read the first book and there was zero attempt at recap when this story started. A large amount of hyphenated names didn't help in keeping track of the characters either but overall it was a decent story.
