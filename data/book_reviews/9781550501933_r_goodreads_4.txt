This is book 2 in the series Moose Jaw (Saskatchewan) series. Andrea and her brother, Tony, are once again spending some time with their Aunt and Grandparents. The last time Andrea had discovered the tunnel system under the town of Moose Jaw and had a time travel adventure. Meantime, her younger brother had managed to get hold of her diary and read about her adventure, and Tony is all in for another adventure. Will Andrea and Tony go back to the roaring 20's and find another adveture?

This is a well written children's book appropriate for ages 9-12. With twists and turns as Andrea and Tony, along with their grandparents and aunt as children, find themselves in a full-fledged mystery as they determine who is hiding stolen goods in the tunnels. I especially liked the role Tony played as he learns, along with his sister's help, how to deal with his diabetes. It's explained in such a way that children in this age group would understand both what causes it and how it is handled. Overall, an ejoyable and appropriate read for this age group.
