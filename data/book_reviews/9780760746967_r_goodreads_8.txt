I love bookplates and other ways people mark ownership of their books. It might be a bit of a weird thing to love, but I do. I feel like the way someone chooses to mark a book as theirs says a lot about them. Do you just write your name in the front cover? Do you purchase bookplates that you add your name to? Do you have a custom stamp or embosser? Do you design your own bookplates? If you have a custom stamp, embosser, or bookplate, what kind of language does it use? Ex libris, return to, from the library of, this book belongs to…? I know that my mixed method of marking ownership says something about me as well. (If you were curious, I have an Ex Libris [name] embosser with a line drawing of a fox. I also use custom bookplates in books I purchase on vacation that state when and where they were purchased–not just the town, but the name of the independent bookstore I purchased it at as well.) When this book about the history and various design eras of bookplates showed up at the library as a donation, I bought it before it actually hit the shelf in our Friends of the Library bookstore so that I could dig through and read it myself...
