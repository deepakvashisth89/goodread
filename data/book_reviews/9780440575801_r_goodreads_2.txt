It can be dicey reviewing a book you read literally decades ago, but what the hell. What I remember...The Good:-a fun time waster to dip in and out of.-a few interesting lists contributed by musicians (e.g., the songs Carl Perkins would choose to explain to someone what rock n roll is; in an act of admirable restraint, he even chooses a couple that are not Carl Perkins songs).-some of the joke lists are quite funny.The Less Good:-more of the joke lists are just lame.-obviously biased bias. Let's just say that Dave Marsh REALLY REALLY likes Bruce Springsteen, to the point ofget a grip, dude. Not that I'm a hater of Bruce, though I'm somewhat agnostic. I do think it would make a good drinking game to listen to early Springsteen albums and do a shot each time he uses "promised land" as a metaphor, though. Similarly, and also true of most music criticism of the '70s, Marsh shows no love for Led Zeppelin, which is not exactly the way to my heart.-factual errors, e.g., claiming it was John Entwistle who made the crack about Jimmy Page's New Yardbirds band going over like a lead balloon, or zeppelin. Wrong, Dave.
