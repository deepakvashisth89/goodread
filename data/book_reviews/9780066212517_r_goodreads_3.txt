I am a Science teacher so all of the concepts in this book were familiar to me but it was still interesting in the detail given to the personalities involved and some of the detail regarding their thought processes and experimentation.

I not feel that it was necessary for me to know about genes and chromosomes to have found this book a good read, the ideas are not so detailed as to make them either too complicated or over-elaborate. The humour in the book adds a lightness to a weighty subject giving it a good balance.

For anybody interested in science this would be a good read but if you have no interest at all in how biology works then it would not be for you.
