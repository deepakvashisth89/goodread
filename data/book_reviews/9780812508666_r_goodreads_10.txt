i guess this is my first one star read of the year. i read this for class and while the pulp fiction esque vibe can be nice to someone, it was not necessarily my vibe.

i don’t really read science fiction (smth that has got to be changed soon) but this did not make a great impression. the language could be fun but confusing at times and i know that’s just a me thing, but the characters weren’t exactly enjoyable and it was a stream of insane events that were somewhat fun but held little depth to me. the mc is whiny and annoying, the women are almost always written in a corny seductive way that can only be appealing to the main character. i HATE using this term but it was definitely male gazey for sure. i think there was some fun parts to it, like the village god party that definitely pulled me in with the psychedelic atmosphere. that’s one of the highlights of the book, but its stuffed in with many, many lows. it’s a little bit of a shame as i do want to read more latin sci fi.

i don’t know, i just think it’s a mess, it could be someone’s mess, but it’s not my type of mess.
