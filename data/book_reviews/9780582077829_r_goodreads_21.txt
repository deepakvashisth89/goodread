A man who is absolutely not white falls in love with a woman who is almost definitely his half-sister and that’s the most normal thing that happens in the book. A cautionary tale of what a bad idea marrying the only other person you know is, Wuthering Heights is sometimes regarded as an epic romantic tragedy but I think it’s a bunch of abused people who are both in desperate need of therapy to break the pattern of generational trauma they are entrenched in and also deep in the throes of drama-queendom. The heroine dies halfway through because she literally can’t stand to not pull focus for five seconds, and the oft-quoted line “whatever our souls are made of, his and mine are the same” is accurate except “souls” is DNA because those two are RELATED, BRO. Anyway it was great frolicking on the moors with a bunch of emotionally stunted psychopaths.
