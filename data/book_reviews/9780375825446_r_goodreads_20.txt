I first read this book when I was in the sixth grade. I fell in love.I forgot about it for a while. Then I saw it sitting in my high school library and decided to reread it.I fell in love all over again.This book is so magically adorable, emotional, and just plain fantastic.Julianna Baker is one of the best characters I've read about. And the development in both main characters is just extraordinary.I never wanted this book to end. Ever.God, I am so in love with this book.
