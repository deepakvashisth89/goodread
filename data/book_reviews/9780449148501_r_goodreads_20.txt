3.5 stars really! Ursula storms the Grenville family dinner when she discovers poison has been used on the estate, killing a buzzard and her cats. Next morning, Molly discovers her lifeless body and George suspects she too has been poisoned. During the investigation that follows, they work more independently than usual, each uncovering hidden secrets and motives around a broken family for whom privacy and keeping up appearances is all important. The ending is sad but abrupt - I’d like to have learned what happened next to those involved.
