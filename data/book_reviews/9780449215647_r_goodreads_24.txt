Vanessa March gets conned into taking a family friend's young son to Europe to meet his father. The boy hasn't been very truthful about the arrangements with his father, and Vanessa is telling her own lies: despite her husband cabling her from Stockholm, he's seen in a newsreel with his arm around a young blond woman in Austria! LEWIS HOW COULD YOU?

Like all of Stewart's heroines, Vanessa is educated and very capable. A veterinary technician, she is more than up to the challenge when they encounter a circus with an injured horse, a pretty young circus performer who seems to be hiding a secret, a strange and vicious acrobat, and Vanessa's wayward husband.
