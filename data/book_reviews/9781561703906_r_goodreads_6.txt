This isn't actually a "self-help" book it is a memoir about Doreen Virtue's life. Along the way you learn things if you are savvy to what she is talking about. I wouldn't say it is a beginner's book. This being said I have really enjoyed reading her life story. We have both lived in some of the same locations, are both psychotherapists, have both lost custody of our children, so I was able to really empathize with her story which helped me in listening better than I often do. I have been trying some of her concepts which she mentions she tried and I am seeing amazing results. I already knew these concepts but since she made the same mistakes I did, I am taking notes and redirecting my awareness.
