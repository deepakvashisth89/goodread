MARQUES OF GOLD by Roberta Gellis is an interesting Medieval historical romance.A re-print of a 1998 release. It has treason,murder,romance,love,deception,betrayal,rebellion,and conspiracy. “Marques of Gold” is the story of a goldsmith who is found murdered,who made King John’s “privy” seal,and made a fake one also,he and his co-conspirators plan to use against the King. Unknown to his bride,Lissa, of only six weeks,her “bride prize” could be the death of her. When Lissa’s husband is left death,probably tortured on her doorstep,enter Sir Justin,the alderman,who will soon be her protector. A fast paced story of intrigue,treason,passion,desire and betrayal that could change the history of England. A great read for anyone who enjoys suspense,intrigue,romance,Medieval England,historical events and love.Received for an honest review from Net Galley and the publisher.
