Ghostwritten by Peter Lerangis.

I think the only Super Special I ever remember reading was The Baby-Sitters Remember but there was a total of 15 of these. Again, this was one where Abby is a member of the BSC and is a part of the story and it was a little past the time I was reading BSC books.

Dawn (who has by this time permanently moved to back to California for her own series) is visiting Stoneybrook for the summer and her dad, Jack, is supposed to be taking an RV back to a client at his art gallery job. He wants to take Dawn and Jeff with him but needs the okay from Sharon and Richard to let Mary Anne come along and maybe a few of Dawn's other friends.

Watson, Kristy's stepfather, gets RV fever and wants to take his own family on a trip after hearing about this. Kristy's maternal grandmother Nannie will stay behind to watch Emily Michelle and Kristy's older brothers, Charlie and Sam, are already away working as camp counselors for the summer. Knowing that the BSC will be slow with families on vacation, even Jessi and Mallory are allowed to come along.

(I know they go on other vacations in other Super Specials, but I mean they are 11, they do need parental permission.)

Watson, Kristy's mom, her brother David Michael, stepsiblings Karen and Andrew, Mal, Jessi and Abby go in one RV while Kristy goes with Dawn, her dad, Jeff, Claudia, Stacey and Mary Anne in the other to keep things even and each person gets to pick a destination before they all meet up in California. This will take two weeks and 242 pages of time.

The Brewer RV heads south, The Schafer RV heads North.

Kristy wants to hit as many baseball stadiums as possible to watch games or collect souvenir hats.

Mallory wants to head to Virginia to watch wild horses.

Abby wants to visit Graceland as she is a big Elvis fan.

Karen wants to go to the Four Corners while Andrew wants to go to the San Diego Zoo because a panda is supposed to be having a baby. David Michael is interested in seeing a rodeo for the first time.

Watson wants to visit a college friend of his who lives in Oklahoma and Jessi wants to look into a place is Mississippi called Dalton since she discovered it might be where her ancestors came from.

Stacey wants to go to Seattle to meet up with her newest boyfriend, Ethan.

Mary Anne wants to go and visit her maternal grandmother in Iowa.

Jeff wants to go rock climbing in Yellowstone and Dawn wants to visit a ghost town practically anywhere. Claudia wants to visit the Institute of Art in Chicago, so they all head out ASAP to make the schedule work to meet in Palo City at the end of the two weeks.

Each chapter is told by a different character with even a chapter from both Jeff and Karen. Some of these are written as journal entries with Stacey's being letters to Ethan.

Claudia accidentally grabs a book thinking it is her sketch book, but it turns out to be Stacey's diary with her postcards to Ethan inside as well. Stacey catches her putting it back and thinks that Claudia read it, so they don't speak for a while as Mary Anne thinks that Dawn's dad and his sense of humor are making fun of her and making it seem her dad is a snob.

Jessi finds the plantation where her family were kept as slaves and meets an older woman doing her own research. Jessi and the old woman talking about such a heavy-handed subject are not the only times we get some seriousness in-between the wacky hijinks.

When the Brewer group reaches Oklahoma to visit with the Romney family, a tornado hits the town!

(It actually really freaked me out since I lived in Kentucky and I now live in Indiana where we have had some tornadoes before and some very bad ones. There are also all of these people in a tiny bathroom with the littlest kids in tub surrounded by pillows and both Karen and David Michael hanging on to their respective parents in fear.)

Mr. Schafer forgets to put more gas in the RV, and it runs out while they are in the desert of the Badlands. He hitches a ride to go get gas and leaves all of the kids alone for hours at least until it becomes dark. So late and practically out in the middle of nowhere, he comes back with police officers and their emergency tanks of gas. The police happily guide them to a place to eat and park the RV back in civilization.

Since Kristy's mom wants to go and see the Grand Canyon, the Brewers head there and we learn that Abby's dad was going to take her and the rest of their family on the same type of trip but died in a car accident before that could happen. Abby feels some sadness and grief that also made me choke up as I can relate to missing my own dad.

Stacey has to learn the hard way that Seattle is full of coffee shops when she thinks Ethan is running late or she's at the wrong one. It all turns out good as she and Ethan get to share a kiss on their own and it makes Stacey and Claudia friends again sharing the details.

Mary Anne finally clears the air with Dawn's dad, and he admits he has a habit of putting his foot in his mouth. At a baseball game, Kristy sees her estranged father on the screen showing the crowd during the seventh-inning stretch. Her friends go with her, and they find Patrick Thomas and it as just as painfully awkward and sad as you can imagine.

BSC in the USA is a perfect mix of laughs and moments of where you don't know whether you want to cry. Even though the chapters are split a little with more attention towards Abby, Kristy, Stacey, Jessi and Dawn's arcs it is nice to see a little perspective from both Jeff and Karen.

A perfect read for a road trip summer.
