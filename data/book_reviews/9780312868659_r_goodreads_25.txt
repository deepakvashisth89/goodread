There are so many great ideas in this, but the conclusion bringing everything together is very disappointing.It open strong as we are introduced to our narrator who is a murderous HAL-9000 type computer disposing of one of its crew members. It seems like this will be a whydunnit on a starship with an interesting villain. Through the computer's eyes, we watch one of the crew investigate the death and grow suspicious of the computer.There are several asides and diversions, some of which would make their own great books and some of which are annoying and seem pointless, because none of the asides pay off until the last couple of pages in a very quick and offhanded sort of way. If it had not been for the last 25 pages, this would be a 4 star book only brought down by a seeming lack of focus with the asides and poor characterization for our hero.
