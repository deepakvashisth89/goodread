I really wanted to like it, but it ends up being a 3.5 star. It is better than "God Loves Sex" that focused only on the Song of Songs, while this is more of a whole look at marriage with a focus base at Genesis 1-3. The style is very similar, with stories and then some workings through marriage issues and what the Bible has to say about it. It seems good, you may like it, but for me it was just too much writing that took its time to get to the points - points that were there, and that came forth here and there, and that were good. So, there is treasures here, but the book as a whole is not all top notch.
