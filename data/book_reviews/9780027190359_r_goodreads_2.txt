This is the story of cousin winds. The West wind wants to prove that he is more powerful that his cousin in the East so they decide to try and blow a tree frog off of his leaf. Of course, the mighty west wind could not blow the frog of the tree and the east wind could. This story is like the tortoise and the hare with the moral slow and steady wins the race. Because of his calm and non-boastful attitude, the east wind wins the competition and successfully gets the frog off the leaf. This book would be good to teach winds patterns to and about folklore in a different country. It is a sweet story with detailed illustrations that I think children will enjoy. It would also be a really good read aloud book for vocabulary reasons. I will be keeping this book for my future classroom.
