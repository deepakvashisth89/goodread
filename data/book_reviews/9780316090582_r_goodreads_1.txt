As a direct service provider for those affected by violence in the home for over three years, there was little new information provided. However, for those just starting in the field, it provides a clear and concise run down of warning signs and intervention/resolution techniques that are useful for identifying an unhealthy relationship. The first hand case studies lend a feeling of kinship and shatters the shield of invincibility most feel towards this subject. Denial can be the biggest obstacle for parents when it comes understanding and reaching out to their children and vice versa. It reminds us that if you don't think it can happen to you, think again.
