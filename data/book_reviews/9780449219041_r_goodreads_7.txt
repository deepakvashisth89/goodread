I am a fan of Marge Piercy--her poetry and her present-day novels. I read Gone to Soldiers, her WWII opus, and liked it, but didn't care for City of Darkness, City of Light, which is about the French Revolution.Having said that, Summer People is one of my favorites of hers. As a former resident of Massachusetts and visitor to the Cape, where this book is set, I thoroughly enjoy her description of place. The characters, as well, particularly the women, Dinah and Susan, are, I feel, developed thoroughly.
