Well, I'll start off by stating that this was my least favourite book in theGrijpstra & de Gierseries so far. I'm not sure what was going on in Janwillem van de Wetering's life at the time he wrote this, but it seems like there may have been some hallucinogens involved.The first part of this book is rather trippy. The second part is a little better but still unusual. Plus, the names of the characters in this book are unlike any in the other van de Wetering books I have read so far and they were odd, to say the least.The story was just OK, not great. Still entertaining, but lacked something. I am still planning on reading all of the books in theGrijpstra & de Gierseries, so time to start tracking downThe Streetbird.
