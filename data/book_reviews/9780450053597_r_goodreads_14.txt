The children of satan worshipers grow up and kidnap victims to use in a ceremonial sacrifice. It was relatively tame in terms of horror or exploitation, but it has a slimy film of sleaze and nihilism that appeals to me. The book rambled about following various characters, often waiting several chapters before they entered the main narrative. These sections were engaging enough character studies, but I can see how it may strike some readers as padding, especially for a book with this low of a body count.Written by the co-creator of the Night of the Living Dead, Russo directed the film version a couple years later.More reviews atTrash Menace.
