Great weekend read...😍I spent the long weekend reading Ms. Goodmans, Dennenhy series and of the five this one is my favourite. Personalities, anger , love, societies moral judgement from that time period are quite vivid. I did wonder about putting newly weds in a luxury train car with only one three- quarters bed for a two week train trip. Didn't make sense.Herbal medicine was fascinating. Big family dynamic is fun. Poorly Jay Mac was always being blamed for something. Good series! Fun read!
