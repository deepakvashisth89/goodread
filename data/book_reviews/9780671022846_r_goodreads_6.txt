1 1/2 starsThe idea for the story itself was decent... the execution was a miserable failure. The writing style was terrible, and the plot points were ridiculous. Both "mysteries" were completely obvious, and I can't believe it took almost 100 pages before a light bulb went off over one of the main characters heads, and "POOF", the mysteries were resolved. I never would have guessed that this had been penned by a best selling author. I would have thought it had been written by a rank amateur.This was the 2nd novel I have read by Mary Higgins Clark, and I have disliked both of them. This author obviously is not for me.NOTE: I didn't realize this was a series book when I picked it up. It definitely read like one.
