I wish I could remember how this book appeared on my radar, only in the last few months, and it lived up to the hype, and then some. Multiple narrators slowly tell their interweaving story, and spill the secrets no one else knows... complicit, duplicitous, uninhibited. The language is beautiful, and the characters are fully-formed yet flawed (aren't we all?). Wow. Just... Wow.
