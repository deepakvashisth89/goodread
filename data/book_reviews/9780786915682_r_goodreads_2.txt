Maybe it's just me, but I don't find fights underwater all that cool. Sure, there's something intriguing about the idea of battling sea monsters or exploring hidden underwater caves, but after a while, it all starts to feel a bit stale. Unfortunately, "Realms of the Deep" didn't do much to change my mind.Now, don't get me wrong, there were a few stories in this book that managed to hold my interest. But for the most part, I found myself struggling to stay engaged. It's not that the writing was bad per se, but rather that the stories themselves lacked that certain spark of excitement that keeps you turning the pages.All in all, I wouldn't say that "Realms of the Deep" is a terrible book. But if you're looking for a collection of truly thrilling underwater adventures, you might want to look elsewhere. Unless, of course, you're a diehard fan of fights underwater (for whatever reason - aren't they just clumsy slow-motion fights?), in which case this book might just be right up your alley.
