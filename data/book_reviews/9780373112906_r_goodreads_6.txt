* Warning: heavy ranting coming up *Lately I have ‘read’ (or rather: skimmed or DNF’ed) a number of HP’s.I got increasingly annoyed by the same HP story line over and over again with the h having sex with the H the same (!) day she sees him, getting pregnant from that one time, having a secret baby, bla bla bla.In my opinion a HP should be the summum of a fairy tale romance for adults. That’s what makes a HP different from other genres of romance novels. A HP should be a ‘present’ in that it is some sort of romantic escape from reality.But where’s the romance in having a one night stand with a stranger? Where’s the romance in unplanned pregnancies? Where’s the romance in hiding a child from his father without him ever having done anything wrong to that child?Anyway, I reached for this HP from my favourite writer Charlotte Lamb. She made me get addicted with HP’s about 40 years ago.And yes, did Lamb deliver!Lamb doesn’t need explicit sex/porn scenes, she doesn’t need unplanned pregnancies, she doesn’t need a woman’s treacherous body to write a mega HP with chemistry and romance.Lamb describes the actions and attitudes of a jealous, besotted H that you believe him. You believe his passion and rage. And you don’t doubt that he is going crazy with his hunger for her.Moreover, her h’s aren’t so doormatty that you as a reader get the urge to wipe your shoes on them or throw up on them. Her h’s are women, not doormats. They can say no, they can control their body and they don’t have tears in their eyes every other page.I thoroughly enjoyed reading this. This is it. This is a HP.
