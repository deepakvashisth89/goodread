As a big fan of the original, I was surprised I hadn't discovered this sequel yet, though I wish I had left it alone. Barney was never the strongest character, but moving the action to the crab planet, and spending most of the book in captivity exploring the boring-ass crab planet, didn't help his cause. The most interesting character, the parasite in Barney's brain, is wasted on a series of monologues that reveal way too little over way too much time - her thoughts are like a Lost episode, stuff we already knew mixed with intricate ways to not explain anything.Just, bleh. Overall very bland. And they only play one weak-ass game of Interstellar Pig! That's part of what I loved about the original, was the slow dissolution of the enemy alien's disguises around the gameplay. I love gameplay!
