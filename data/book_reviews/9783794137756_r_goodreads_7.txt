I finally got around to reading this classic story. A beginning story, simple and sweet about the love a parent and child have for each other.This will give you warm fuzzies all over. The artwork is cute and the simple story and characters are perfect to deliver this message.The nephew was like, cute bunnies, boring story. Nothing happens. He is a man of action. They spend the book telling each other how much they love each other. He would have loved it a few years ago, but now he is too old for such things. He gave it 2 stars, so he didn’t totally hate it.
