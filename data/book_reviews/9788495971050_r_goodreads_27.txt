McEwan's well thought out and thought provoking morality tale… Molly Lane a lifetime lover of life and men is being buried; in attendance are four men that have had at least affairs, if not more with her. Amsterdam tells the story of how their lives lives proceed after secret pictures that capture one of them, the Foreign Secretary of the UK, in a compromising position. The tale concludes with a surprising and deadly climax in Amsterdam. A good read. 6 out of 12
