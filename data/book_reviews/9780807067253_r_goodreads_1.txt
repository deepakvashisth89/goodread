I think this book is a great read for any woman. With or without kids. And although I have a daughter who is now ten years old, I identified with the things Lazarre speaks about, of the first infancy of her child, of her labor, the pain, and all that comes with those first years of a child's life. Specially that perfect mother ghost which we have all lived through. Motherhood is complex, and the feelings that accompany it are so many and difficult to describe, but this book is a wonderful account of being a creative person, or trying to stay true to who you were before you had a child. But also being present in that incredible life altering moment which is becoming a mother. Lazarre speaks to me, not just as a mother, but also as a creative woman. This book is wonderful and full of love, and intensity. I wish all women would read it, because we need to change that perfect mother image, I also wish every woman could write her own story, since motherhood different every time a woman lives through it.
