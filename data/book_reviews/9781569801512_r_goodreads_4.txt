I've had this book on the tbr list for a long time, having first heard about it when we were in Bermuda (for the second time) in 1996. Diana Douglas Darrid (the ex-wife of Kirk Douglas and mother of Michael Douglas) grew up in Bermuda and maintains a residence there (or did then). I would have liked to read more about growing up in Bermuda, but the book really focuses on Darrid's professional life as an actress.Further, as with many other memoirs and biographies, I was dissappointed by the writing. In addition, Darrid tells her story in a kind of "stream of consciousness" style, jumping around from topic to topic, year to year, but not really delving into anything in depth. She states that she wrote the book for her grandson, Cameron; perhaps it should have stayed as a family document.
