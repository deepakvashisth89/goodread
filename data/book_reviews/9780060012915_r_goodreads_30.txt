Gentle story, beginning in 1950s England, where Celia, eldest daughter of the local doctor, and Lottie, a refugee from WW2 who ended up staying with the family as her own mother doesn't want her back, meet a group of "Bohemians" who have moved into a large Art Deco house on the sea-front called Arcadia. Their behaviour scandalises the uptight residents of the small seaside town, and Celia is sent away to London to work, where she meets Guy, the exotic and handsome only son of an importer of tropical fruit. When she brings her fiancé home to meet the family, he and Lottie secretly fall in love, leading to repercussions for everyone. The story then jumps forward 50 years, to Daisy, an interior designer who has been abandoned by the father of her new baby, and accepts a job restoring Arcadia to be turned into a hotel by Jones, a grumpy entrepreneur. The connections between the characters are gradually revealed as the past comes back into their lives.
I found it rather slow in the beginning, but gradually got into it, especially the modern story, and liked the ending, although was confused by the epilogue.
