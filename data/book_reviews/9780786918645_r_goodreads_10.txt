The Temple of Elemental Stupid.This book features a scene somewhere in the first part that is incredibly dumb. It goes like this: the bad guy sets up an ambush, and the party finds themselves surrounded by bandits and gnolls and what not. The bad guy appears:Bad guy: "Drop your weapons!"The party leader sighs and gives the sign to drop it. Everyone drops their weapon to the ground. The bad guy walks over to the party leader and brutally mauls him down with a single blow.Bad guy: "Now EVERYONE dies!"The party grabs their weapons and proceeds to kill off most of the opposition and survives the ambush. Their leader tearfully dies because his wound is too heavy and can't be healed. The party grabs his body to give him a proper burial and goes out of the tower. A shining knight in armor on a warhorse trots out of the bushes.Knight: "Hail, friends! St. Cuthbert sent me to help you!"DM: *sighs handing a charsheet* "Okay, Jake, here's your second character. Try not to kill him that fast."I've never read this stupid a scene in a book before. The rest is fine though, in that too-much-drama D&D style that half of this series consists of.
