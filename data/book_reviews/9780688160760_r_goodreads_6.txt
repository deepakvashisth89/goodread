I read this book as a tween or teen and I loved it. It reminded me of a Twilight Zone episode, one of my favorite shows. It really got me thinking, and it’s been on my mind vaguely ever since. The physical copy also somehow made its way into my adult stack of books that I still have, which I took as a sign to reread.

That was before I had any understanding of quantum physics or the multiverse.

Now upon rereading I see why I loved it so much! From my current stand point of my 28 year old self and all the topics I love dearly such as the law of assumption, the multiverse and parallel universes, quantum leaping, etc I realize that these themes have become a big part of my identity, which is exactly what Abigail is searching for.

In a way, I feel like I was communicating with my younger self while rereading this book. Something fuzzy about that feeling.
