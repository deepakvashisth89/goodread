Complete and utter bullshit. I expected at least *some* information about kahunas and their work in traditional Hawaiian religion. But the book starts off by saying that none of the kahunas would ever tell the author any of their secrets, then has a *really* thin explanation of how he "discovered" the secrets of their magic by studying the words in the Hawaiian dictionary. After like two pages of that it launches into hundreds of pages of unsupported baloney about spiritual selves and connections and praying to make miracles happen. I scanned through to see if there was any real content and found none. Bleck. Deserves zero stars.
