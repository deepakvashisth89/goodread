This is the second book in a series of fourteen books set in Florence featuring Marshal Guarnaccia of the Carabinieri. I first came across this series in 1994 and read five of the books back then and a sixth book some ten years later.This is a slow moving book in which the Marshal believes a man has been murdered but he has no powers to investigate as he is not a detective. I realised the identity of the killer early on but it did not stop my enjoyment of the book. It's a sad story which reminded me a bit of Georges Simenon's Maigret books.
