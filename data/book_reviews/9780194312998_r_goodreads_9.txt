This is my first proper monolingual English dictionary. I have always wanted one but they were so expensive.. Well, having registered for Cambridge C2 exam which I will be taking this November, I was forced to finally purchase one. I went for the newest 2020 edition and I can't get enough of it. Especially the bonus parts at the end - Oxford Writing Tutor, Speaking Tutor, Visual Vocabulary Builder and the Grammar section are invaluable for me right now, but at the same time just sitting down and opening it at random and getting lost... Dictionaries really are the best books and you never get bored with them.

Update: I finally took my Cambridge C2 Proficiency exam yesterday. After having to postpone the original November date because of being in quarantine at the time, it was quite a relief. I would not normally put a dictionary among my "read" books but I spent so much time with this one at the expense of reading books of my own choosing that I sort of feel like I deserve it. After more than six months of working with this dictionary on a daily basis, everytime I opened it at random I felt like I already read that particular page and had to flip through it for a while to get to an entry that would actually be new and teach me something. Then of course getting distracted by an entry and following a thread to a different entry and so on can easily mean you do not even blink and suddenly you find out you have been poring over the dictionary for two hours. The tutors at the end were particularly appreciated. A great dictionary, one that I will definitely come back to if I ever want to take another exam or am just writing a thesis or something similar. It also has a mobile phone app that complements the real thing quite nicely.
