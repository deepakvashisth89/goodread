In this series of Amsterdam detective mysteries, which centers on the commissaris and de Gier. It takes place in Cape Orca, Maine, USA, where the recently widowed sister of the commissaris lives and plans to return to Amsterdam once the commissaris settles the estate. We find out that the unnamed commissaris's is called Jan by his sister. He suffers from ailments and de Gier follows him to Maine to bring him back safely. The sister revealed that her husband was the sixth resident of the south shore to have died under unusual circumstances.

The commissaris and de Gier begin the investigation, becoming acquainted with a local gang, an island hermit, numerous eccentric residents and taking them to Boston where the pieces start to fall into place. The writing is slow paced imbued with the authors Zen's wit and acute comments on Americans.
