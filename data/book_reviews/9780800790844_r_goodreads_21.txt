The Fear of the Lord hits you in this book, the author speaks from experience with God. She supports everything by the Scriptures. Joy Dawson invites us into friendship with God, revealing the only way to properly walk in friendship with Jesus is to have the fear of the Lord. In essence, He is our Friend yes, but He is God. Dawson hits the fear of the Lord, holiness of the Lord, what true repentance looks like, fearing God in our relationships, the importance of a woman's influence and how to walk in wisdom. MUST READ***
