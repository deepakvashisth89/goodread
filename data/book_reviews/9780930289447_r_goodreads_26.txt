One of the most infamous Batman stories of all time — and still one of the most gut-wrenching.

Death in the Family puts Jason Todd’s fate in the hands of the readers, and the result is brutal: the Joker at his most sadistic, Batman at his most broken. The story is dark, fast-moving, and soaked in consequence. And out of its aftermath, a new Robin rises — Tim Drake, the only one who chose the role and earned it with intellect.

Grim, iconic, and essential reading for anyone exploring the darker corners of the Bat-mythos.
