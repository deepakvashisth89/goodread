As soon as I heard of this book and read the synopsis it just called to me. A seemingly perfectly normal woman goes to the lake with her family for a day of fun and relaxation only she ends up stabbing a man to death. Sign me up!Then I started reading all the mixed reviews. People REALLY disliked this so I became hesitant to grab it. It happened to be available through C/W MARS so I downloaded it and still I debated if I wanted to start it. I finally caved and opened it up. I'm glad I did. I think some of the negative reviews actually helped me enjoy this reading experience. Most people complained that it was incredibly confusing so I prepared myself for this. I think knowing this ahead of time made me concentrate on what I was reading more. And it is confusing because everything this woman says is a lie or is it? So many hidden meanings to her ramblings and I enjoyed trying to figure out the puzzle.I will say that this book had so many icky moments. Like I need a shower now icky. So, ah, be prepared for that. *Shivers*
