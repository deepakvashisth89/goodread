This was a book where I loved and hated it. The main character is so flawed and unlikable for most of the book, yet in the end it is these flaws that pulls this story together.

I like the moments in the book when we learned about the other characters lives. They were so interesting and moving. Then we unfortunately had to go back to the main character.

It is a work of literary skill, and would deserve study and dissection. As a casual reader the literary greatness gets hidden behind the unlikable main character so it loses some of that magic that you see in other parts of the book.

I can see why it is a Canadian literary marvel, I just wished you didn't have to wait to the last hundred pages to see it that way.
