I read this after reading William Buhlman's book Adventures Beyond The Body. Buhlman's book is a better how-to guide but Astral Dynamics explores energetic aspects of astral projection left out of Buhlman's book. I also bought the 90-day Mastering Astral Projection book and did not like it much. Overall this book is OK but gets too tied up in energy work and ignores the fact that astral projection/out of body experiences do not require intense energy work.
