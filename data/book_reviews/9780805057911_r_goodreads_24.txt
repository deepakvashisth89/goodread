I originally read this book shortly after it was published in 1988-89 and I loved the book and it became one of my favorite books and one that I highly recommended to people. So I recently recommended it to my book club and was able to enjoy it a second time and share it with several friends. Obviously I have a different view of the book now that I am the same age as the author was when he died. I love Abbey's humor and writing style and story line.When I first read it I had been working seasonally in Alaska for natural resource agencies and could really relate to only working part time and not wanting to work in an office etc. Basically it was easy for me to relate to his story today and 30 years ago. Excellent book and miss the author.
