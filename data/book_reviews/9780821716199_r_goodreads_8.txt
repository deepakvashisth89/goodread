I’ll start right off by reminding everyone these books (Fu Manchu) were written in the 1930s, almost 100 years ago, so attitudes and language toward cultures not one’s own were what we would call today “problematic.” Having said that, Sax Rohmer can really paint a picture. The descriptions of London Chinatown, the various characters (nefarious and not), and the atmospheric fog were described so vividly I had no trouble losing myself in the story, despite the aged language and outdated views. I came across this 1964 version compliments of Friends of the Library, and paid all of .25. Worth it! I may be reading more of them in the near future.
