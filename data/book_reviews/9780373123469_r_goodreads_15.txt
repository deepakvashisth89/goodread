It’s an enjoyable read with a smitten H who is also a good dad for their son.She believes the lies the OW tells her. He believes the lies his late brother has told him. And of course they both don’t tell the other one what they have heard, so the misunderstandings are only solved in the last few pages.He is an Italian mighty stud, she was a virgin before she met him. I like how persistent he is in inviting her for dinner, in inviting her to go to Italy with him, in his marriage proposal. I like determined, besotted men.
