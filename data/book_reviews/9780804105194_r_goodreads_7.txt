I couldn't decide if this book was filled with offensive stereotypes, or if the stereotypes were so universally applied that they cancelled each other out. The writing is fabulous, though, and it's ultimately a cautionary tale about what happens when know-nothings show up in other people's countries and think they know what's going on. Not surprisingly, they end up making a mess.The main character, Fong, is a Chinese grocer in East Africa who is taken advantage of by everyone he meets--even the American do-gooders who think they're offering him the world, but leave him worse-off than ever. Luckily, fate takes a hand. Because the book is such a farcical romp, we believe anything could happen.
