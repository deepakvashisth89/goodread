I can't help myselfThis review is from: When the Devil Dances (Legacy of the Aldenata Book 3) (Kindle Edition)I like a lot of John Ringo's work. I like the Legacy of the Aldenata series even if it has become slightly formalistic (or do I mean formulistic, formulaic? The dictionary at hand is of no assistance) . This series reminds me of the movie, "The Magnificent Seven". Look past the entertainment and there is a lot of wisdom there.
