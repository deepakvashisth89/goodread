I seem to be either really hot or cold with Koontz books. Unfortunately this book was very hard for me to get into. Which is sad, because I loved the first Christopher Snow book,Fear Nothingwhen i read it several years ago.Maybe I wasn't in the proper frame of mind when I read this, but there were several aspects to this book that annoyed me. Koontz seemed to have fallen into the Stephen King "let's describe every last detail down to the size of every blade of grass." trap. Plus, the surfer lingo used between Chris and Bobby got old really quickly. In any event, I ended up skimming many parts of this book, and was rather happy to see it end.
