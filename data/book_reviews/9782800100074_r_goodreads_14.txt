On this book Marsupliami is still living at the Zoo. It is up to Spirou et Fantasio to get him out, but their plans are shattered when they find out Marsupilami died. They come to claim his body, but it cannot be found. They follow the body thief only to discover it is not dead, Marsupilami is now working at a circus. With the help of Champignac's count they come across a "circus Performance" that lets them be part of the circus. It reminded me of the book "Put me in the Zoo" by Robert Lopshire. Cute and Clever.
