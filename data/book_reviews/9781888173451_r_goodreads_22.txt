I loved the book - but I am a massive fan of her work.
Slightly dated socially now, but unputdownable in terms of her ability to probe the human psyche. Full of entirely believable twists and turns, darkly comic in places (laugh out loud good in others), the book builds pace well and hangs together well. The ending was not predictable but managed to be fun as well as a little shocking.
A great book - well worth digging out.
