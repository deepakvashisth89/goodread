With kids who are first introduced to classical art, they're trained by peers and society really to find art "boring" no matter how excited mom or dad is to teach it to them. This book is a great companion to our story time on days we are learning about classical artists. It helps them engage with whatever artist and painting they're viewing on a deeper, empathetic level that lends itself to emotions they otherwise refuse to formulate when viewing art.Yes, Katie touches paintings that clearly say Do Not Touch. It's an easy enough book to read and stop and discuss, as we should be doing with all books anyways, that perhaps Katie shouldn't have disobeyed. The message to obey signs is written into the story line so, parents, don't fret. Let the book spark your child's imagination and help them to discern between good and bad behaviors and their consequences.
