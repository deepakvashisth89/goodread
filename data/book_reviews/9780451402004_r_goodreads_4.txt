A book about kids with psychic powers that are not the antagonists! It's included in some "creepy kids" lists on here, so I wanted to clarify.Three families of children who have died in tragic accidents start receiving messages that their children may still be alive. The story moves along quickly as the parents try to solve the question of whether their children are still alive, and where they are if true. People are killed to keep the children's whereabouts secret. There is one particularly gory and strange killing to appease the gorehounds out there.This is the sort of book that would have been read by most members of my family, and passed around to friends, too. It fits in with the horror books of the early to mid 80s, which is when most of the books I like were written.
