I had high expectations about this book. I have read many of the authors plays and seen a few of them performed and have also seen a few movies he has written the screenplays for and have without a doubt loved them all. Really interesting characters always a bit offbeat. But this book at just 248 pages was a hard slog. Having no chapters didn't help in breaking up the story that seemed like a very long monologue. I am reading it and wondering why is the author going into so much detail about various things in the past of the characters that didn't seem to have any relevance. But in the end all the loose threads add up and the conclusion is somewhat satisfying but gee it was a hard slog to read this book took nearly 2 weeks for a relatively short book that should only have taken a few days if I felt entranced and captivated by the story.
