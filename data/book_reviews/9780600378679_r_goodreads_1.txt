The book began promisingly, with interesting stories about the history of domesticated cats. Unfortunately, the biggest bulk of the text - the descriptions of different breeds - were tedious in their sameness. A lot has also happened in the world of cats since the book was published in 1982. The pictures were pretty but it bothered me how much emphasis the pedigree cats' appearance got. There must be a million gray tiger striped cats like mine but I never got him because of his looks - I love and appreciate him because of who he is.
