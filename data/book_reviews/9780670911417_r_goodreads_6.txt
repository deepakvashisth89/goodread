Exceptional! A beautifully written story about the last fishing guide in the Algonquin Highlands. Back when fishing guides meant something more important than they do now. Back when fishing guides weren't all about the money and hustling people.Frank Kuiack (1936-2021) was just 8 years old when he started guiding in the Canadian Wilderness. Party story, part fishing, part history. All good!It's been awhile since a fishing story has moved me to tears. I highly recommend.
