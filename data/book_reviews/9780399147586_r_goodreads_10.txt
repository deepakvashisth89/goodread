2nd read - Lucas is pulled into a strange case involving a decomposing body on a hillside. As the bodies pile up, it appears we have a serial killer. A crusty lawman joins Lucas's posse, looking for his lost niece and a few other young, blond ladies who've disappeared.I really enjoyed the police procedure, as our heroes try to tie the ends together. Some readers think this antagonist is one of Sandford's weakest, but in hindsight, he makes few mistakes, leaving Davenport frustrated.I had to re-read the last 20 pages to look at the clues I'd missed. There is a huge twist, and a heart-breaking finale.1st reading - The 12th Lucas Davenport story, with a history art professor who has a devious compulsion.
