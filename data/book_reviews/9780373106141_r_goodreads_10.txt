"Jilted" is the story of Alexa and Scott.WOW, this was one heck of a roller coaster ride!A heartbroken and jilted heroine finds herself with her ex's brother, and when he realizes her identity, she tries to kill herself after a few sharp barbs hit their mark. The hero in turn marries her and whisks her away to Brazil to keep an eye on her. It is there they go through intense drama, with an immature and impulsive heroine who says things just to hurt the hero and is immersed in self pity, and a utterly besotted hero.Honestly, the heroine pissed me off with her lies and self destruction- I understand she was heartbroken but she definitely missed what was infront of her eyes. The hero spent the whole book mooning and brooding.If you don't mind suicide attempts, alcoholism and drugs, almost rape and physical violence, you'd enjoy this story.Meh.Safe2.5
