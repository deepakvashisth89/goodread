Monsters!

The serial killer, Jeffrey Collinson, got hit by a car. He was taken to the hospital.
He died. Dr. Hunt saw something black and gelatinous crawl out of his nose.

Dr. Hunt, Tom and Julie discovered there were more. They hunted them down. They were in danger, but they were determined to kill them.

This book is an easy read. The chapters move fast. I stayed up all night turning pages. The book is that good. The only problem I had with it was that some of the characters were hateful. I wished them dead.

Four stars. ✨✨✨✨
