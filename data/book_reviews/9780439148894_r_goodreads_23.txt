This one was quite educational and I quite enjoyed it. I only wish we got a little more time to get to know some of the crew at the VA hospital Molly decided to volunteer at. I also didn't realize how close together (all things considered) president Kennedy's, Dr. Martin Luther King's, and senator Kennedy's assassinations' were to each other. Heck, I had forgotten senator Kennedy was assassinated, if I'm being honest. (Canadian, please don't hate me)

I think the author did a pretty good job bringing the feeling of what it must have been like to have a family member serving in Vietnam. I also enjoyed to level of sass Molly had and shared (as well as confessing to keeping to herself). I only wish this book was a bit longer so we could get more.



I really enjoyed this book, which surprised me because I usually enjoy historical fiction anything from earlier time periods, but this one was pretty fantastic and had plenty of educational merit.

I think it's appropriate for kids 10+ but do be warned that though it's not graphically describe there are plenty of mentions of injured soldiers with amputated limbs, paralyzations, horrible crush wounds, disfigurement, etc. Molly did volunteer at a VA hospital.
