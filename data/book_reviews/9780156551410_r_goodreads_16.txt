Classic Maigret.As usual it’s all about the people and in this case especially, small town provincial people, when Maigret is asked by a friend unofficially to look into the accidental death of a young man, where rumours circulates that the friends brother in law is responsible.Maigret arrives at the small provincial town at the same time as, Cadaver an old colleague, who’s become a private detective after leaving the police in disgrace, he quickly discovers that no one is interested in finding the truth and that Cadaver who loathes Maigret is somehow involved in the matter.
