John Gardner frequently commits the cardinal sin of writing Bond: he makes the character dull. License to Kill is my least favorite of the Eon Bond films because Timothy Dalton seems to hate everything that makes Bond, Bond. He plays him as a chaste, uncharismatic regular guy. John Gardner probably loved that takeSeafire is the worst example of this approach because Gardner has Bond successfully settle down with one woman. Bond’s sex life works one of two ways: either genuine but doomed or as a serial playboy. Blissful monogamy doesn’t work except as a prelude to tragedy.
