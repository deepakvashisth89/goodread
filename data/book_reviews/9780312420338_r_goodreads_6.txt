Meter, vinyl, petroleum, bodywork, engine, crystal, chrome. Every word in this novel becomes erotic. There is sperm, desire, tooth, areola, curvature, wound, and scar. Each attack of the flesh by the metal signs the man's victory. Rockets, planes, and cars may well melt on us, hoping to reduce us to heaps of crushed guts and disfigured faces. Yet despite the injuries inflicted, they do not have the burning desire that helps man build a consistently successful future.Deviance is not a gratuitous perversity. Here, the gift is offered to those whom progress and speed have deceived without mercy."With the help of scars, we celebrated the rebirth of the road massacred, the death and injuries of all those we had seen dying on a shoulder, the fictitious wounds and the attitudes of the millions who would still die."
