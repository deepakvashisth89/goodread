Hard to mark this, because I know the film so well. Of course the story is great, and there is more back story here, which is nice, and some expanding on the film's action, as well as a couple of new characters. However, despite all that, the writing is simply heavy, overwrought, and just not very good.I can't even quite decide whether it's best to come to the book well versed in The Wicker Man or completely ignorant of it. It's probably best just to consider the book nonexistent and watch the film instead.
