For a full spoiler version of this review, please check out my blog,spoiledbooks.blogspot.comStephen King has a rare talent for taking a simple idea and turning it into a massive, quietly devastating novel.The Dead Zonefollows Johnny Smith, a man who wakes from a long coma with the unsettling ability to see the future by touch — and absolutely no desire to have this power.This book is LONG, but somehow I was completely hooked. So hooked that I only let myself listen to it while working out… which tells you everything you need to know about how compelling it is. 😅It’s less about the supernatural and more aboutmoral responsibility, sacrifice, and missed chances. Thoughtful, tense, and surprisingly emotional.
