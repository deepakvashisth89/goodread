ComplicatedSo much of this is a rip-roaring yarn that it seems a shame to cavil about any part. But there are two things that defy credibility: firstly the over-complicated code dependent on some incomprehensible microbiological bacterial communication used as the gateway code to some computer disks; and secondly, the fatal affair between the MMC and the double agent watching him. Perhaps the author felt that he needed some sex scenes to leaven the ratcheting tension over the suspected outrage, followed by an act of pointless self-sacrifice.Why and how would two ex SAS men have knowledge of some arcane scientific gobbledegook that just happens to be connected to the work of our MMC with a PhD? And why and how would the surviving brother and the conniving double agent believe themselves deeply in love and aiming for marriage on the basis of a couple of meetings? As plot devices these two things made no sense to me and reduced my enjoyment of the overall read.
