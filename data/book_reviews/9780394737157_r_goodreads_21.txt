After reading the “for Beginners” books on Marx and Trotsky, my friend suggested we read the middle book of the series on Lenin. Despite the comic book format, this would not be an easy book for absolute beginners, but I think it does manage to make the complicated factional struggles as understandable as possible without having any background. It’s hard for me to judge, since I have read a lot about the Russian Revolution and the history of the Marxist movement, as well as several of Lenin’s books, so I am not really the intended audience for this. I will have to see how my friend responds to it with less background.
