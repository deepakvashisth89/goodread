Trigger warnings: cancer, death of a loved one, murder(??), grief, cheating oh my God so much cheating.

1.5 stars.

So here's the thing: I've heard SO many people raving about Jodi Picoult's books and how amazing they are. I read My Sister's Keeper last year and mostly just thought that it was emotionally manipulative.

So I figured I'd try another one. We were getting rid of this at work so I figured I may as well make it this one. And I'm really really torn, you guys. Because this was FUCKING TERRIBLE. And yet at the same time, it was incredibly compelling.

So this is basically the story of a small town police chief. He's happily married and he's also a laird despite living in Massachusetts because apparently the Scottish branch of Clan MacDonald doesn't have any responsible adults left??? IDEK. And then his cousin turns up in town. And said cousin has just killed his wife. But it's okay because his wife had terminal cancer and she asked him to kill her. (Do not come at me about spoilers, I swear to fucking God. THAT'S ALL IN THE BLURB.)

So based on all of that, I thought this was going to be a book about ethical dilemmas and whether doing the right thing can be right even if it's illegal etc etc. Instead, this was mostly a book about the aforementioned police chief cheating on his wife. And I did. not. care. at. all.

Don't get me wrong - I cared about the court case and how things were going to play out. But Cam is a garbage human and should be thrown in the trash where he belongs and having so much of the story being about him was...no. Not okay.

So yeah. Really, this would be a 1 star book apart from the fact that it was SO COMPELLING and I had to know how it ended. But if I were basing this book solely on Cam's character? It would be a negative five thousand stars and I'd be trekking to Mordor to throw it into Mount Doom where it belongs.
