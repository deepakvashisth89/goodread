I didn’t expect to like this since I’m not a fan of royalty stories and palace intrigue. Luckily, this princess was only a minor royal who was secretly in love with her older cousin. When he married the hero’s friend, our heroine realized it was time to leave the made-up country and strike out on her own.

Before unrequited princess is scheduled to leave, the hero, a New Zealander, shows up to visit his friend, the new Queen and to do some business. He also wants a spot of revenge on the heroine because she supposedly broke of the heart of an acquaintance, causing his death in Africa.

Heroine is roped into playing tour guide to the hero. There is instant antagonism because they are both attracted to the other – hero makes lots of slut-shaming veiled references to the heroine – to add a bit of spice to this very RD courtship.

H/h end up having sex – outside and he can’t tell she’s a virgin – another RD trope. Heroine claims she isn’t in love with the hero and he’s not in love with her so they avoid each other.

When the new queen announces her pregnancy the heroine realizes she can’t stay a minute longer in the kingdom. Hero misinterprets her distress (he thinks she is jealous of the attention the new queen is getting) and invites her to his station in New Zealand.

Once in NZ, Heroine is tried from the jet lag, but hero figures out she’s pregnant. Heroine vows not to marry since she doesn’t want a loveless relationship. None of that matters when the hero is injured in a plane crash and heroine declares her love. Hero finally realizes this feeling is love and proposes for an HEA.

This has the bones of an RD story, but it is pared down. Hero is kinda storta cruel, heroine isn’t that neurotic. No high drama here, but I’ll take it.
