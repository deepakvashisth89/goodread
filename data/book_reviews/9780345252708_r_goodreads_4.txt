Interesting depiction by Kantor who won a Pulitzer 20 years earlier for ANDERSONVILLE which I have also read. Reread Andersonville for a short magazine feature and this one will serve for the same column come winter.A sort of crazy quilt of disconnected although related vignettes of all sorts of characters, both historical and invented. Zexellency (Washington) is seen through several lenses. The Markee (de Lafayette) is also portrayed as are some Brits. Although 2000 out of 11000 soldiers died of privation and disease, yet there is not a sense of discouragement. The "spirit of liberty" noted by a foreign military person is strong throughout.
