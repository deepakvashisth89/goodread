Our hero wanted revenge on the heroines brother for killing his sister, so he marries the heroine, sleeps with her and then leaves her. The heroine is devastated, when the hero turns up again looking for the heroines brother, he ends up getting her pregnant without knowing. Then upon a sad story in the heroines past, he learns that he was cruel and his revenge was wrong. Now letting her go is the only way that he can feel less guilty but then he notices the baby bump.

Decent story, it wasn't overall anything special but I still enjoyed reading it.
