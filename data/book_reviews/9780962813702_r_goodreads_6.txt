While this book is supposed to teach you ways to actually remember the many complex and varied Kanji, I found that in my case it works better as a reference. I continually remembered the image on the page more than the definition, so I could just flip thru the book and find the kanji I was looking for really easily – a lot easier than with a traditional kanji dictionary.Mnemonics tend to not work with me because I dedicate brain cells to remembering the mnemonic as is instead of what it’s supposed to be helping me remember, but the combination of images, definition and mnemonic that Rowley created works surprisingly well.I’m still a bit lost when it comes to reading kanji, but this book is a good road map to help me get better at it.
