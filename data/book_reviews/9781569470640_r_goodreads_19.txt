A particularly nice installment of the Ansterdam Cops stories, best enjoyed if you've read at least a few of the previous books, especially "The Rattle Rat," which I think directly precedes this one. Former Detective Sergeant Rinus de Gier's longstanding habits of casual misogyny and casual drug use get him in trouble in the United States, and the handsome and fit younger man turns to Grijpstra, his older and tireder former colleague to fly across continents to help him solve the question of whether de Gier himself is an accidental murderer. A nice meditation on the nature of evil, both inside of good people and bad. It has a touch of The Mind Murders' bizarre mysticism in its finale, a touch I didn't appreciate ten years ago but really like now. Sometimes the universe has its own justice, after all.
