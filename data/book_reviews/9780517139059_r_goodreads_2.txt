Apparently it's bring your kid to work day at Star Fleet because Commander George Kirk brings his "snot nosed", disillusioned, hell-raising teenager of a son Jimmy along on what's supposed to be a mundane mission to a remote planet where the remains of an ancient, highly advanced alien race have been uncovered. Cool. But, watch out for those...
space pirates! Young James T Kirk and his father and crew are thrust into a deadly situation. And who knows, maybe Jimmy will change his ways and his life will take on a new direction? Stranger things have happened.
