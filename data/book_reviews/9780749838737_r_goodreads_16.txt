Little Miss WiseRating:3 starsReason behind rating:This book is enjoyable but it isn't really the type of book I want to read now that I'm 19. That isn't to say if that if your child is just learning to read that they won't love it to the moon and back. Just that this is no longer my favourite book now that I can read properly.Younger me though would thoroughly recommend this book to young girls who are learning to read and just want a short story to read before bed.It's funny and relatable with decent drawings that aren't overly complicated but don't look tacky for being simple. It's a good little book!
