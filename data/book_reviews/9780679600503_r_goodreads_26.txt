This is the most perfect 145 pages of writing that I have ever read. On the surface is a story of the glorious US southwest landscape. Underneath, is a meshing of the beauty and level of friendships, the importance of gardening, the vanity of aging, the harmonious Indian/nature culture, and the melding of architecture and nature.I will slowly re-read this just to savor the magical Indian village on the Mesa and to chuckle at the symbiotic relationship between two “donkeys”.Highly recommended.
