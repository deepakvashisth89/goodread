These books are oddly addictive, even though they really have no protagonist. I keep waiting for Thomas to be more developed, but he is the least developed of anyone and Charlotte and Emily seem to be doing most of his job. Mainly the point of these books seem to be rich Victorians behaving badly—trapped in a decadent high society that leaches the humanity out of most of them. The world is so wonderfully detailed that I was drawn in almost in spite of myself. I did like the bits about Charlotte learning to cook, but their marriage feels so cardboard compared to the other characters that Pitt and Charlotte are observing. At some point I just gave up hoping and enjoyed the juicy society scenes.
