Ben is 16 and has cerebral palsy. He lives with his overprotective grandma and with no friends, escapes into movies. One night at the Rialto Theatre Colleen Minou asks him for the cost of admission. She is tattooed, loud and oozing attitude, and Ben tries to avoid her as he knows her from school. When she sits next to him then falls asleep on his shoulder, he enjoys the feeling. They build an unlikely friendship – her the stoner, him the spaz – which they openly admit and label each other.But they are good for each other.I read this book in one sitting. It was like an older version of ‘See ya Simon’ without someone passing away. In fact the main character actually begins to live. Stoner Colleen changes Ben’s life and hers improves for a while with Ben’s influence. But will it stay like that?
