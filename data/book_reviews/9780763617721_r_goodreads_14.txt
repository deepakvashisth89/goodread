I really liked this cute story that had both a different type of family as well as a different type of fairy. I loved that Annabelle's family lives in an apartment. I think sometimes when we read summer stories of kids playing outside, it's easy to overlook the fact that not all families have big back yards.
I also love that the fairies are in a little family unit, with a mom, dad, little boy, baby, and a grandma. It's fun to see a fairy story where the fairies are just like humans, traveling to a party to make hamburgers and ice cream; rather than magical creatures.
