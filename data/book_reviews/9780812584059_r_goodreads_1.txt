Another good read by Richard La Plante. It was completely different to the previous one I had read (Leopard) except that the three main characters mentioned before were still present.The story is based on a theory that minds can be controlled and there's one fella doing time for murder who controls peoples minds through their dreams and represents himself as a bird in the dreams. When the victim dreams of a bird attacking them, they are quite literally frightened to death.A spooky thing happened a couple of nights ago when my husband woke up after a bad dream and mentioned a bird attacking him!! He hadn't read the book and I definately hadn't told him about it!! Coincidence I know and we can joke about it!
