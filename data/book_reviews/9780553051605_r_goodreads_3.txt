This volume of poetry tackles all kinds of issues surrounding not just war in general, but issues specific to the Vietnam War. Readers are given an intimate and sometimes painfully honest look into PTSD, survivor's guilt, the American dream, death, legacy, and the American glorification of war, especially of those who die in war. The poems are occasionally hard to read, not because they aren't good but rather because the raw emotion is strong that it leaps off the page and into your heart.If you like poetry, I recommend. If you don't like the military, then this probably isn't the book for you.
