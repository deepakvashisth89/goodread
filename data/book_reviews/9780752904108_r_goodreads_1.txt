I have no recollection of when or where I bought this book from (or why I bought it?!) but it has been living on my bookshelves unread for a long time because it is not the kind of book I normally pick up. That said, I actually really enjoyed it. I didn't know much about this little bit of history going in but it was fascinating. I'll admit that it took a while to remember who each of the soldiers were but in the end their individual personalities shone through.
