Ms. Ray sure knows how to build a plot. The entire time I was reading I was dying to know what the big secret was. This book was realistic in it's emotions. The impossible situation and the journey from anger to love was heart wrenching for both Madison and me. I must be honest... I don't know if I could have done it. The ease in which this story is told created the perfect flow and made it very hard for me to put down. I only removed one star because the end seemed a bit rushed. People went out of their way to sabotage Madison and I would have like to known how their misfortunes turned out in their entirety. Overall, wonderful read. I was teary eyed and happy by the end.
