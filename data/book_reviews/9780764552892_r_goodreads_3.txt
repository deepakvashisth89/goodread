This is an excellent book for those who are considering getting a dog and are not sure if a Dachshund is got them. This book gives great detail regarding the different types, sizes, etc.From birth to senior age this book shows you how to train your Doxy, especially the Do’s and Dont’s of handling various behaviors.For myself this was a refresher course for me because I need to re-train my 6 year old Doxy and my new 1 year old on potty training and barking.
