I had not been able to understand for a long time what in the world God had done to deserve man.'Day', the third book in Elie Wiesel’s trilogy is a work of fiction tinged with the author’s real-life bitter experiences and struggles.The story is about a broken man who is hit by a cab in New York City while crossing the street with his girl friend.His shattered body is transferred to a hospital where he has to stay for weeks on end to recover.Paradise is when nothing comes between the eye and the tree.But what about his broken soul and his disillusionment with humanity?Could his inner wounds ever be healed? Would he be able to cope with his past and find a way to love and live, despite going through the horrors of the Holocaust.Would he be able to lay his ghosts to rest?
