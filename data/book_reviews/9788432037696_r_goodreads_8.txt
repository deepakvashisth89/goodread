The message on this book is AMAZING, it's like watching and listening to Jesus in real time, his thoughts, conversations and personality are reflected in the book in ways that I never even thought could be possible. We always think of Jesus as a our savior, but almost never think of him as the amazing human being he was. On the other side, reading 1200 pages for a journey that is only 3 days long could be tedious. Details are not a few paragraphs but pages and pages, and sometimes way too graphic, medical and technical. It said a couple of things like Jesus had blood brothers and sisters, which is something that it's not true, that was the only part that annoyed me a little, but the message on the book left me praying and thanking Jesus for dying to save us from our sins, and God for sending his son to this world to evangelize his word.
