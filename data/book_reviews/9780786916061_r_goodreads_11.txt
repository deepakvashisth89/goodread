4/5 This is my favorite of the series so far. Cadderly is still conflicted but not nearly the Mary Sue he was in previous books.The dark priest of Talona, Abalister, has sent a dangerous group of assassins after Cadderly this time around. One particular assassin named Ghost has the ability to take over peoples bodies. This book focuses on Cadderly developing his powers while fighting off the Night Mask assassins.Lots of action and the cast continues to grow on me. More popcorn fantasy but still fun.
