This was a well written and meticulously researched book about one woman's ten-year hunt for her own history, a history that led to Somerset Place. The history of the owners of this plantation and the enslaved people who built it and made it prosper is a fascinating piece of American history. Spruill Redmond is a good writer, unfolding the stories of Somerset and her family. Evidently, she was also responsible for helping the state include reconstructed slave buildings. We visited Somerset Place a few days ago and were privileged to hear the complete story of one of the largest plantations in the upper South. The author's research and the Somerset Homecoming was responsible for bringing light to this history. Recommended.
