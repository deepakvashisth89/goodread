Unfortunately Tunnel Vision follows the path of almost every other book in this series. Vic sticks her nose in where it doesn't belong, she offends everyone she knows, she makes the obligatory pro-abortion editorial comments, fights the cops, commits B & Es and as usual does so badly, gets shot at, gets beaten up, and has her apàrtment broken into while investigating corruption that is way too difficult to follow.On the bright side the Mary Louise Feely character shows signs of developing into something worthwhile. Too bad that's about the only good thing that can be said about this otherwise tortuous read. I realize it's fiction, but V.I. is simply unbelievable. She would have lost her license long ago along with all of her friends.
