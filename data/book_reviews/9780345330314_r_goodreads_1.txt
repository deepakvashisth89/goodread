I really enjoyed this - NY cop, a 'Plainclothesman' from the overpopulated, domed mega cities (The Caves of Steel), of Earth, Elijah Bayley is sent to the far reaches of the known universe to investigate a murder on a planet where murder never happens, where people don't even meet face-2-face, and where there are around 1,000 robots to every person!Not only do we get a stranger in a strange land, we also get a captivating murder investigation on a planet where it is seen as grossly disturbing to meet in person. Asimov delights with not only strong continuity from The Caves of Steel, but gives the reader insight into the 'caves' that mankind and and the off-worlders have got themselves into, as well as using the investigation as a covert spying operation for Earth! Another world class Asimov joint - 8.5 out of 12
