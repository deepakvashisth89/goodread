This is an Original Series (TOS) adventure with the Star Trek crew. I really enjoyed the writing style of Eklund and have ordered more of his books. This takes place on a distant planet that has been quarantined by the Federation. Spock, McCoy and Kirk are all here trying to find out the mystery behind the planet and the strange race of Danon-which resemble the Devil in U.S. culture.Like the TV series, it moves quickly and has action abound. This is the first Star Trek book that I have read and I'm looking forward to reading many more.
