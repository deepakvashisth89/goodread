This book was a real “popper!”.I enjoyed this fast page turning story. The story was written in 1979, and the dialogue was a lot of fun.Colin and Roy’s chemistry was very believable. I’ll admit some of the plot you can see coming a mile away. BUT that’s where you start to wonder where this story is going to take you.I must have thought of 4 different endings when trying to predict what was going to happen and it was a lot of fun doing so. This book kept me turning the page. Which is all I can ask of a book.Anyways check this book out! It’s a real “popper!”.**see what I did there? Lol*to the people that have read the book.
