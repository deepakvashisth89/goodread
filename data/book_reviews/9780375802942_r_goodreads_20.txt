In this book, Junie B. gets an opportunity to help out in the kitchen at school and ends up ruining everyone’s lunch, so all her classmates are mad at her, however, she apologizes later and makes everything okay again with cookies.
This book is important for my database because it teaches that when you are in a place where you have the power to ruin someone's lunch that other people worked hard on, you probably shouldn't do it. Junie B.'s shenanigans are always teaching lessons, even though Barbara Park never planned for it to be that way. These books are always funny and informative, leaving readers wishing that they could meet a real life Junie B.

Park, Barbara. (2009). . Illustrated by Denise Brunkus. New York, NY. Random House Publishing.
