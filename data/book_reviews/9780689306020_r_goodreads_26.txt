This is an anchor text in our second grade reading curriculum, and I must say, I love reading it with my students each year! I grew up loving the Alexander books. Ray Cruz gives such detailed illustrations, and Viorst comes through so well in her Alexander narration. We use this text to teach point of view, and how a writer can tell a story through a character's eyes. We also use it for making inferences with illustrations connecting to the text.My kiddos love the humor in the telling. As we work with this one for a few days, we also use it to help promote reading fluency and reading with expression. Alexander is one of the most expressive characters I can think of in the picture book world!
