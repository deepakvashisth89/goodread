Interesting book that delves into the US Bill of Rights. The authors take the reader through the various amendments using real-world examples and cases intertwined with history of the law that supports each amendment, both within the US legal history, and primarily from British Common Law upon which America's was birthed.Implications of both the law, including complexity and controversies are combined with brief understandings about the broader impact of the legal system, notably at the level of the US Supreme Court and its role in determining various cases.
