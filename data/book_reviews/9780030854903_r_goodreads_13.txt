favorite poemUntitled by A.E. HousmanI to my perilsOf cheat and charmerCame clad in armourBy stars benign.Hope lies to mortalsAnd most believe her,But man’s deceiverWas never mine.The thoughts of othersWere light and fleeting,Of lovers’ meetingOr luck or fame.Mine were of trouble,And mine were steady,So I was readyWhen trouble came.
