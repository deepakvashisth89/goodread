A beautiful book that tells a story of an ERP software company and the way it changes the business scenario with help of Theory Of Constraints. I had heard the three rules of Dr Eliyahu Goldratt, about being paranoid and bring hysterical. This book tells us what is the real meaning of the phrase "being paranoid". With a good software, as a customer you can become equipped enough to get that cutting edge, but it is just a necessary condition. You can get the true benefit out of the software only when you change the underlying rules that were present before the software was installed. Just like the software removes the earlier limitation, the earlier rules that were required for the limitation need to be modified to get the real benefit out of the software. A thought provoking book, though not as beautiful as "The Goal" & "The Critical Chain".Nevertheless, a beautiful read for "Theory of Constraint" fans...
