I was a big fan of John Christopher’s Tripod books when I was younger, but somehow never read this series, which is too bad: at the age of 12 or so I would probably have devoured it. Today, some of the holes show through, but it’s competently written and carefully plotted, and Luke, our hero, is very well done: prickly, ambitious, proud, but just aware enough of his faults to be sympathetic. The way that his character and the circumstances lead to the break between him and his half-brother Peter in particular is executed with precision and subtlety. Good enough to encourage me to read the sequel.
