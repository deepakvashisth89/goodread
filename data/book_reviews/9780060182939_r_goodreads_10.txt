Good book I read while I was taking my Asian American studies class at UCI summer school. Mar discusses the stereotype about being a "model minority" in the U.S. She recalls being bullied on the school yard for looking different than her classmates. Speaking little or no English to defend herself from the taunting, she recounts other difficulties she had to overcome. Growing up with a lack of money and parents being unaware of the American school system, as seen when her mom asks her to explain what Harvard is, are told in an honest and painful manner.
