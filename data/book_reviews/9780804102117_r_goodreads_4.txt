William Martin has done it again for me. This is an old book (published in 1987) but his novels captivate my imagination and it is hard to put them down. This book takes place in 1916 in Boston, with the Irsh-Americans, the politics of the day, and a young man called Tom Tracey, whose mother left Ireland in 1900, with her three sons, shortly after her husband died. The story sweeps across the Atlantic Ocean between Ireland and the United States. The history of Ireland and its struggles with Great Britain, combine with Mayor Curley, Boston's mayor and the politics surrounding him. Into this mix comes a man, who turns the world upside down for the Tracey family.
