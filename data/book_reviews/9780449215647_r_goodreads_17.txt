This is a fun Stewart book that over time has become one of my top favorites. A family friend spots Vanessa's husband in a newsreel, with an attractive young girl in Austria when he's claiming to be in Sweden! Circumstances allow her to travel to Vienna without looking too suspicious, and events spiral out from there. This story includes a circus, Lipizzaner horses, and the gorgeous scenery that Stewart is so good at describing.

Vanessa is a likable 1960s heroine, a product of her times but brave and intelligent and not afraid to be either. I also liked that she's married and they have a strong relationship, even with a few ups and downs.

Young Tim is such a great character, I'm not sure I'd enjoy the book much without him. He really adds liveliness, some humor and feeling. He's a good example of someone in that almost-grown up stage - so good at so many things that sometimes I doubt he's real, then he says or does something awkward, and I go, "Oh, yes, there's that young boy peeking through!"

I should explain that I've never been very fond of horses. We had a golden palomino when I was growing up on the farm, and I probably didn't ride him more than 40 times in my life. They just don't interest me much. But that magical scene in the alpine meadow - I got shivers. Stewart is so good at description, I felt I was sitting there with Vanessa listening to the faraway sounds of the circus.
And I've read this book a few times before. But the power of good writing never lessens for me, and that's really good writing!

Stewart's books were my introduction to Romantic Suspense many decades ago, and they are great fun to reread when I'm in the mood for some adventure and a strong sense of place. I always feel like I've just returned from a vacation when I finish one of her books.
