The forward says that Faulkner "educated himself by reading promiscuously" and honestly same.

I bought this collection at the Faulkner House on my New Orleans trip. While I'm glad I did, and I'm happy to have sampled Faulkner's writing, I don't feel the need to read much more (unless one of his novels is creepy and gothic like A Rose for Emily).

What works about his style REALLY works for me - the sense of dread (when he chooses to write about it), the closeclose perspective, and the intricate dynamics that are only shown partially, yet paint a deep picture. However, he often chooses to write about characters and situations that I have no interest in.

I like challenging myself, and I like reading things outside of my comfort zone. This is definitely both. With a few exceptions, though, I won't be revisiting this collection.
