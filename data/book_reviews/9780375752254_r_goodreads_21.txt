This book has lots of super-practical tips for saving money and living simply. Some of them, like how to handle families who often give very expensive gifts over the holidays, are oh-so-useful. Others, like how to make a wreath out of barbed wire, are a little less so.My major beef with the book, though, is the organization. I would have loved to skip the sections for parents or for car-owners, but the book wasn't organized in any thematic way, which made browsing a little challenging.The other problem is that the book is a bit old, and some of the information is dated. It'd be so cool if another, updated, more modern version were around.
