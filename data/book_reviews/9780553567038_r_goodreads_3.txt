3.5 stars so bumped to 4. While Bikini is a pretty formulaic ya horror book I found it quite fast paced and enjoyable. Although I could tell who the villain was from a mile away I enjoyed the journey it took to get there. We follow Casey through most of the book. She works as a lifeguard at the beach and the death of a drowning victim that she pulls from the water starts off the whole list of red herrings of who the villain is. Because the victim was a friend, Stephanie, there are a few people who could have done it. Was it Tom, Casey’s twin brother and shot down love interest of Stephanie, or possibly Alex, Casey’s new love interest who happens to be Stephanie’s ex? While 85-90 percent of the book is a lot of really false scares the ending is just bat shit crazy. Pretty brutal for a 90’s teen horror too. The ending really made the book even though the villain was super predictable although their whole motive was like really two different motives like Spoilers do you kill for the inheritance or the whole not get caught smuggling drugs. Pick a motive and stick to it lol. I’m hoping Beach Blanket Psycho is at least not so convoluted haha.
