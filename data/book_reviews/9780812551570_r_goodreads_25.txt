Max Trader is a skilled luthier (guitar maker) and nice guy and Johnny Devlin is a mean, lazy user who thinks only of himself. One night they go to sleep and wake the next morning in the other's body. They never met; they have no magic power. But Johnny wished for a better life for himself and somehow his wish was granted, to the detriment of Max Trader. Now Johnny has Max's house, bank account, studio where he works on his instruments, and Max is living in a dumpy apartment.Tanya, Johnny's whiney and pathetic girlfriend, and her friend, Zeffy, go to Johnny's apartment to force him into returning the $300 Tanya loaned him for his rent, because now they need the money for their rent. Much to the women's surprise, Johnny is apologetic and hands over $75, all the money in Johnny's wallet. He promises to get the rest, and the women leave, wondering why Johnny has suddenly become polite.Meanwhile, a teen, Nia, who hangs with Max in his studio, thinks her mother has been taken over by "pod people" and is now afraid to go home. She ends up homeless in Fitzhenry park, but hooks up with Max, although she needs a little convincing that Max has somehow swapped bodies. And of course, they meet "Bones," who travels the spirit world as well as the human world.de Lint also gives a pretty decent description of being homeless.Everybody eventually makes good changes in themselves after their adventure. I started liking Tanya, who is one of those women who believes "better a bad boyfriend than none at all." She really comes out on top when making positive changes. Always happy to spend some time in Newford.
