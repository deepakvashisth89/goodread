Well I guess I am rereading this series! This was my favorite, or at least the one that stuck with me the most. I remember her friendship with the little Indigenous girl, them leaving presents for each other--a green button on a piece of green thread, a tiny basket woven from grass, a little doll with a leaf for a skirt--I lived in the woods growing up and there were no other little girls anywhere near, so for me this was such a beautiful fantasy. I am also someone who growing up loved school and had a teacher for a Mom so this one really spoke to me.
