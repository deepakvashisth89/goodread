Kane and Kayla both have issues from their childhood. I can’t imagine what either went through. Kayla was lucky though to have her sister and an aunt where Kane only had himself. I know Kane was a good cop, but he had shut himself off emotionally for so long I was glad Kayla was able to get to him. Since Kane was trying to find out if Kayla was running a prostitution ring, he hated himself for going to far with Kayla. But he also had the other side where he wanted to protect her and didn’t want to leave her. Their relationship was up and down with lots of emotions on both sides. I encourage you to read the book. I was given a copy and voluntarily wrote a review.
