3 stars because of all the chuckles I got from this Epic Trainwreck :)Seven years before the start of our story, Heroine was engaged to the hero’s younger brother, but he died four weeks before their wedding. After her fiancé’s death, she continued to live with her fiancé’s grieving father and became his personal assistant in his London real estate business. She stayed working for him for the next seven years, until his death. In his will, the kindly old man left her his personal home and half of the shares in his business, with the other half going to his estranged older son that he had not seen or spoken to in the last 10 years of his life.As the older son didn’t bother showing up for his father’s funeral (he never showed up for his younger bro’s funeral either) and he ignored all the heroine’s letters regarding his responsibilities for the inherited business, she finally decides to track him down to one of his hotels in Majorca. Follow weeks of extreme slut-shaming peppered by punishing kisses and rag doll shaking by the hero. He thinks she is a skanky gold digger who carried an affair with both his younger brother and his father. Unfortunately for him, he is lusting after her too.Eventually, hero and heroine fly black to London together, where the hero reluctantly tries to take over the reins of the family business. Hero continues to accuse the heroine of being a tarty, golddigging hall of famer who is now trying it on with the remaining, surviving member of the family in order to get her greedy hands on the whole of the business rather than settling for half. He is disgusted with himself for lusting after her too but can’t keep his hands off.Heroine naturally falls in love with him because I mean, WHO WOULDN’T???!!! She despairs of ever having her love returned since he sees her only as an opportunistic tart. She also believes he is having a long standing affair with his gorgeous P.A.A melodramatic confrontation between the heroine, the hero, and the long-standing family housekeeper finally clears up all the Great, Big, Terrible Misunderstandings or close enough:-Reason for Hero’s ten year estrangement from his family: Hero discovered that his younger brother had embezzled money from one of his father’s construction projects, using cheaper, substandard construction materials and falsifying invoices so he could pocket the difference. The fraud eventually caused the collapse of the building, killing one of the workers. His father covered up for his younger brother rather than prosecuting him. Hero left the family and never returned in ten years because he was so disgusted by his unethical, morally bankrupt sibling and parent.-How the younger brother died suddenly four weeks before his wedding to heroine: Heroine was a naive young girl who wanted to wait for marriage. The hero’s younger brother figured he would marry her if that was the only way to get her into bed. He swept her off her feet and proposed after a whirlwind courtship. Heroine moved into the family home, with her fiance and future father-in-law but kept separate bedrooms. Four weeks before their wedding, the fiance got drunk one night and apparently decided he couldn’t wait anymore. He burst into heroine's room in the middle of the night and tried to rape her. She screamed her head off, waking up her father-in-law, who came to the rescue. Father-in-law dragged his son off heroine but in the ensuing scuffle, the evil rapist fell down the stairs and broke his neck. The heroine, her father-in-law and the housekeeper covered it all up and the death was ruled an accident.-The housekeeper can prove for a fact that the heroine never had more than a quasi-paternal relationship with hero’s father since she, not the heroine, had been the hero’s father’s Seekret Mistress for the past twenty years, up to his death.After the Big, Dramatic Confrontation, heroine hightails it back to Majorca to the hero’s hotel, where she goes into hiding (lol). She contacts her lawyers to sign the hero’s family home away to the housekeeper and all of her business shares to the hero. The hero shows up to grovel but she cuts him off, telling him he had every right to slut-shame her due to the damming circumstantial evidence against her (Double lol).The last Great, Big, Terrible Misunderstanding is cleared up: hero never had an affair with his happily married-to-another-man P.A. However, he had been married to the P.A.’s sister until she died in childbirth four years ago. But it’s all ok anyway because he loves the heroine like no other and wants to marry her.So much random trainwrecky, quasi-incestuous relationships! I couldn’t even get disgusted or outraged because it was so Redonkulous. And since this is Carole Mortimer, it was also wildly entertaining while I read it, only to be forgotten in the next 24 hours.
