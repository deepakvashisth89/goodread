I love books by the author Todd Parr. His books have the primary colors throughout which is great for young ones learning. In Otto Goes to Bed, Otto is a dog who wants to stay up and keep playing. He eventually decides to close his eyes and dream.As a child, I believed days never came to an end. Sleep was for adults who drove cars. And children fell asleep when parents gave them sleepy milk. However, I realized every person needed sleep including me and dreams created success within us.I recommend this book for encouraging kids to dream.
