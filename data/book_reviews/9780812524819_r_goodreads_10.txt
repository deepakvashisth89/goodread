Classic Sci Fi novel, but told with such brilliant vividness that I couldn't put it down until 3am.Legacy follows the Life of Olmy: a member of the Way Defense on a gargantuan ship called Thistledown, until he is assigned to do recon on an Earth-like planet named Lamarckia.Greg bear is thorough in his exploration of human society and what would really happen if a small group of humans were really to start over on a new planet. From the societal factions and shortcomings of people, to mile-long Storm Beasts creating megacytic organisms that control weather, this book keeps you on your toes, deep in thought, and with a fatuous smile on your face.I'd recommend this book to anyone who loves reading and is interested in science fiction, because this book is a Good Read(haha. get it..?
