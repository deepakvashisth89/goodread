This is a 1976 hardback reprint in French from Dupuis. Originally serialised between 1955 and 1956 before publication in book form in 1958, this episode comprised of two stories sees the author acquire a team including a cowriter and a background artist.

Both stories are straightforward police mysteries. The first one is interesting in the use of a soporific gas for burglaries, which chimes with recent real world events in posh French resorts, also involving celebrities. It’s also interesting in its depiction of a gated community for celebrities protected from paparazzi, something that would have struck a chord in the 1950s, but it makes this story quite dated as does some of the James Bond spy kit used by Fantasio for his reporting. The second, much shorter, story concerns car thefts by circus performers, bringing back villains from Les Voleurs Du Marsupilami.

A pleasant read but nothing special at all.
