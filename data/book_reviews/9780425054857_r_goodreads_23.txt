The final part of the Once and Future King.

Merlyn returns.

Following on from 'Candle in the Wind', this book opens as Arthur prepares himself for his final battle against Mordered.

Merlyn is just in time to complete Arthur's education and discover the cause of wars. Merlyn again demonstrates ethics and politics to Arthur by transforming him into animals.

After everything he has learned, Arthur no longer wishes to fight and makes an agreement with Mordred to split England in half. Mordred accepts.

Unfortunately, during the making of this deal, a snake comes upon one of Mordred's soldiers. The soldier draws his sword...and you probably know the rest.
