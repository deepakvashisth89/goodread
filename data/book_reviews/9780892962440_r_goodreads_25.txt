An odd, vicious little book of very short stories about various types of women. We get The Prude, the Perfectionist, The Mobile Bed-Object, etc. Each story portrays a different girl or woman in a very bad light. I found this collection refreshing, as nowadays we're not allowed to create terrible female characters without loaded political meaning. Here, Highsmith pulls no stops. A lot of these types are now obsolete because of how women's roles have changed since the stories were written. We no longer have women comprising solely the limited roles of golddiggers, sluts, wives, mothers, neurotics, and dilettantes. Makes the mid-century seem a very depressing time for women. Highsmith herself stated that she mainly wrote male protagonists because she couldn't imagine women leading active lives; the one story about a female writer describes a literal-minded harpy without talent or imagination. The stories are clever, witty, and incisive, like all of Highsmith's fiction.
