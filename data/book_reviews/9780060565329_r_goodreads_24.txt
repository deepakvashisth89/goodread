A very interesting book. Both an examination of the interesting Mitford family, who were split between fascists and antifascists/communists and the political career and life of British fascist leader Oswald Mosley as well as the relationship of Diana and Mosley.
Diana was a personal friend of the German leader and some of his closest associates just before WW2. She also married, secretly, the leader of the British fascists, Mosley, in Germany with the help of Goebbels. During the war she was "confined" with other British fascists without being convicted yet the conditons were abysmal.
This book was written with Diana's cooperation and there are many glowing statements about Diana that seem dictated. Overall it is reltaively supportive of Diana - one gets the impression that she couldn't behave differently, that Oswald Mosely was just flawed rather than rotten to the core, that both were very talented.
My impression: Diana and her sisters and parent belonged to "if I want it it's right" school of though. They were beautiful and talented and had zero ability to admit mistakes.
Mosley had a deformity and I wonder what his childhood was like to have affected his extremely callous and domineering approach to other people. He was better in many ways- taller, more fit, a better speaker, but then he spread hate and used violence but expected to be elected. He "saw" the fall of the British empire but was he really going to save it?
It's interesting that the Mitfords were close to Churchill's son, less interesting that they were friends of the pro German former King, Edward VIII. They lived their life after the war in Ireland and France but not Germany.
