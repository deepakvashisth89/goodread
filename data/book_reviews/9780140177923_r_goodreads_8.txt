One of the clearest books I have read on the Big Bang and everything since in Physics. Cribbing tends to be repetitive and cuts and pastes between books. I find that this one suffers less from this problem - if you are like me, someone based in the humanities and social sciences - this is a great book to bring you up to date with developments in Physics without the use of mathematics and formulae which will just fox you!Highly recomnmended.
