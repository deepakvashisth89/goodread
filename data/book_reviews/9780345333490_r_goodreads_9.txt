"Star Trek: Log One" was a pleasant surprise! I picked up a few of these in a used book store on a whim. They are adaptations of animated Star Trek episodes. I'd always heard bad things about the cartoon but I've enjoyed Alan Dean Foster in the past so figured "why not?"

He took three episodes, tied them together chronologically and told some fantastic stories! These were as good as any Star Trek Original Series stories I've ever read! I've got Log Two and plan on reading that one soon!
