Considering the page count this was a thorough and enlightening book. what I found most interesting was the the voices against colonization from the 16th through the 19th centuries bravely making themselves heard in the chambers of power throughout the metropolises of empire. Also, a better understanding of the reasoning for empire and its evolution points heavily towards why its downfall was inevitable. I took a star off because I thought his rather dismissive, even smug account of modern Christianity was based more on personal bias than on any deep understanding of the church at its present.
