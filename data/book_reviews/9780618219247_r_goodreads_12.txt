One of my all time favorite songwriters and one hell of a story teller. Every short story in this one is well worth the read."Junkies die down here everyday. Most of the time nobody notices but other junkies, and they perceive only a brief interruption in the food chain. Nobody down here is really capable of mourning in the normal sense.
