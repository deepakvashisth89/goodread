Another Wilkie Collins banger! While it doesn't have the depth and humor of his earlier works, it is really fast-paced, full of twists, and perfectly morbid for this time of year. I highly recommend it if you're hankering for a shorter Collins read.I rate the group reading experience I had with my lovely reading buddies five stars! ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️
