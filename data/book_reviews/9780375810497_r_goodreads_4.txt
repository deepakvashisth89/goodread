SHIPWRECK AT THE BOTTOM OF THE WORLD
By Jennifer Armstrong

Narrated by Taylor Mali

An unforgettable true adventure story for ages 10–14 and up. I listened to the audiobook narrated by Taylor Mali, while my 14-year-old grandson read the book, which includes remarkable glass-plate photographs taken by expedition photographer Frank Hurley. Thanks to Shackleton’s courageous leadership, resourcefulness, the bravery of his crew—and a great deal of luck—every member of the expedition survived.
