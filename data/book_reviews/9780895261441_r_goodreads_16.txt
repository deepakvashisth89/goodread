Even though this book is 17 years old, it is still quite relevant to the state of the Catholic Church today. The sad state of affairs in the current Church are frequent headlines in the news and it's in no small part related to the ideas the author lays out in this book. The push to move the Church farther and farther from orthodoxy is real and it has far-reaching effects. Many of the details in the book are just mind-boggling for anyone who believes in the teachings of the Church. I'd love to see a follow up book done outlining the changes (or lack thereof) in the seminaries since 2002.4.4 out of 5 stars
