I think F. Scott has some of the prettiest writing in the history of the world.That being said the stories themselves are often long and depressing.This collection of short stories was meh. There were a few of them that were awful:, long, boring, weird and depressing. There were a couple that were excellent, the first story and the last were really good and Bernice Bobs her hair is excellent. Those 3 saved the book and that’s coming from a big fan of Fitzgerald.
