I read The Wonderful Wizard of Oz in just 10 days! It was really a fantastic book. It features the original illustrations in black and white and also coloured illustrations. I would advise Reading chapters 8,9 and10 in one day before you read the wonderful Emerald City of Oz or The search for the wicked witch as those two chapters are quite long compared to all the others. I read the first three chapters in one day. All in all all a wonderful adventure story. As for The Marvelous Land of Oz (the sequal L. Frank baum wrote 4 years later)... not a very interesting book. It was a lot of the time very dragged out. I kept waiting for the fun part to come but it didn't. After 5-days of reading it I stopped. If other people want to give it a chance fine. I however would not recommend it. The second book gets only one star. The first book however gets 10 out of 10👍😊. I got this book in the bookshop called chapters in Ireland. They have lots of interesting books.
