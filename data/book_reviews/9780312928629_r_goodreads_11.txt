Eyes of the Hammer is a gripping military thriller packed with covert operations, political intrigue, and strong tactical realism. Right from the beginning, the story throws readers into a dangerous mission where trust quickly becomes uncertain and the deeper conspiracy slowly starts to unfold.

One of the strongest aspects of the novel is the authenticity behind the military elements. The Special Forces operations and mission details feel believable and grounded, which makes the tension and action even more effective. The involvement of the CIA and the growing uncertainty surrounding the true objective added another layer of suspense that kept the story moving at a strong pace.

I also appreciated how the novel balances action with strategy and political manipulation rather than relying only on combat scenes. It creates a more layered thriller experience that keeps readers questioning what’s really happening behind the mission.

A very solid read for fans of military fiction, covert operations, and high-stakes political thrillers.
