More like a 3.5, I enjoyed it. Ive been reading these in order and so far I say this one is in the middle to upper middle overall. Two enjoyable cases. One has Kling investigating an assault on a police officer involving a girl that was the daughter of a victim in another book. One who doesn't like him very well. The main case is a comedian collapses and died on camera doing his live TV show. Carella and Meyer investigates and finds out that most people don't like him but its looking more and more like a suicide. Though for some reason neither detective feels that is right.Recommended, its a very well done book and a good example of what most of the series is to this point.
