This. Is. Absolutely. Wonderful!!! 😍 Why hadn't I read this manga series sooner vice putting it off for years?! Ever started reading a book/ comic/ manga and within a few, short pages into it... you just it's going to be a favorite and read countless times already? Yes, this is that. Precisely!

begins with two high school students meeting happenstance at a park. Kira Aso, an introverted, aspiring artist, is seated on a bench and busily drawing a sketch. She is suddenly interrupted and asked by Rei Kashino, an extroverted playboy and motorcycle racing enthusiast, for directions to the local hospital nearby.

Instead of talking, Kira drafts a map for Rei and then quickly runs off without uttering a word. He nearly discards the map until something captured his attention on the back of it. After that odd encounter, Rei discovers that Kira is a classmate and he is curiously drawn to her. As is she to him. ♡

A fast-paced read with a mixture of: teenage romance, cringeworthy teacher-student scenarios, bullying, awesome Ducati motorcycle artwork, melodrama with so many tears shed by Kira, and much more romance ahead!

I am hooked and I'll probably continue to read this entire series annually. Seriously, I'm a sucker for this type of genre. ♡ My first favorited manga. I cannot wait to read more from .
