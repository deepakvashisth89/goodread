Bullfighting is bullshit, and the passive resistance of Ferdinand is not enough condemnation to make this book enjoyable for me.Even if Ferdinand was deemed a poor example of bullhood and retired from the ring previous to being stabbed by all the banderilleros and picadores, it just means that as Ferdinand is being carted away, down there in the arena in the distance another replacement bull is being trotted into the ring and the bullfight will continue on with the pointless torture and bloodshed when this one is goaded into the proper froth of fear and rage.Ugh.
