The author waited until page 123 (of a 170 page book) to advise that if the reader is depressed, confused, or otherwise desirable of doing away with themselves for other than medical reasons the reader should contact a suicide prevention line. Fair enough.What I took away from this book is that there are no clean or comfortable ways to make a final exit short of building your own Kervorkian machine. But then I fortunately do not find myself in the position of patient or assistant and I hope that will never be the case.
