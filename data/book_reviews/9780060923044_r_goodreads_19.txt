I only read this book to have backup in form of quotes for my claim that a lot of misogyny in medieval-inspired fantasy is applicable to the author, not the Middle Ages.

Yeah, that's right, don't make me mad or I am going to read a book about it! Women in the Middle Ages was the right choice for my niche agenda. I already come to the subject with a solid understanding from taking an entirely unnecessary amount of economic history classes, but still learned a thing or two. Plus, it came through with many relevant quotes to add to my arsenal. Fantasy bros are trembling in their boots, I'm sure.
I especially enjoyed the chapters about ordinary women's lives, the political maneuverings of the elite bore me to death. Women in the Middle Ages doesn't completely steer clear of this type of history, but on the whole it is very engaging and easy to read.

Highly recommend, especially to aspiring fantasy authors. At least make the misogyny historically accurate.
