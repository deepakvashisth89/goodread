You know, when I started this book, I felt it was written with a lack of panache. Yet, this simple story turned into me rooting for Mary and her future. This is a work of historical fiction with the message throughout to be not afraid for the Lord is with you always.“Friends always make the difference” (Acknowledgments).“Nobody knows what tomorrow will bring” (250).“You better do today what needs to be done and not wait for tomorrow. What you put off only comes back to haunt you” (357).I would like to read more by Robert L. Wise.
