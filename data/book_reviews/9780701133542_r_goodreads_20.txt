may 2020:one of the cleverest, most compassionate, most knowing books i've ever read ! gleeful & grinning & slightly glassy-eyed every time i get to the end of it !march 2017:there is so much to love in forty nine bard street, the threadbare glamour and vibrant, warm soul of the building and its women. their world has seen better days, but with every page, dora breathes life back into cold, dusty rooms, old, arthritic joints, long forgotten desires and long hidden secrets. and despite the many conflicts on which the precarious chance/hazard dynasty is balanced, this is a book about living while you're alive, moment by moment, falling in love over and over, feeling, hating, forgiving and starting over.
