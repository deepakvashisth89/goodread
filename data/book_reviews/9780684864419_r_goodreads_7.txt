I read this because I used to always see Helen Ellis lined up right next to Bret Ellis at the bookstore. I took a gamble one day between BEE novels and picked this one up.Wow, what a crappy novel this is. Helen Ellis goes into long and so painfully obvious explanations of her alma mater's sorority traditions. I know we should write what we know, but at some point a line must be drawn in the sand and originality is expected. The characters are not only unlikable but also untenable and uninteresting, and in the latter half of the book they become outright ridiculous in their actions.Just not a good writer, I have to say.
