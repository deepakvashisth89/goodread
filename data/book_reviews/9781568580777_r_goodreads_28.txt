I stumbled upon this book on New Year’s sale on my local bookstore and didn’t expect much of it. But from the moment I started reading I just couldn’t stop. Professor Aczel makes an incredible job in presenting the history of mathematics and laying out some funny qnd very interesting stories from the famous mathematicians and their lives (I specially appreciated the tea he spilled on André Weil and his connection with the Shimura-Taniyama conjecture).

That said, the book does get very technical toward the end and if you haven’t taken a couple years of college-level math classes in number theory the mathematics itself is quite incomprehensible. But I think the efforts to give a hint into the very clever mathematics behind the proof of Fermat’s Last Theorem are quite effective.

Overall, it’s definitely a great read for the mathematically curious.
