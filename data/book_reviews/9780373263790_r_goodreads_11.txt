This is the fifteenth and final book in this excellent police procedural series. I have now read all the books in the series which has seen me through "Lockdown 2020!"
Virginia seems to have an ideal life; a successful and devoted husband, a grown up daughter, a beautiful house and plenty of money. In the middle of the celebration of her daughter Rachel's engagement Virginia disappears. Her body is discovered the following day and she has been murdered.
I liked the way there were tie ins with the ongoing life of Inspector Luke Thanet's family life from bridget age three to Bridget expecting her own baby.
I am now looking for inspiration - what can I read next....
The Killings at Badger's Drift by Caroline Graham is a possibility.
