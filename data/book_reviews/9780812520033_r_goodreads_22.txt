It’s a fun book; nice details and tight writing. It has a darkness, however, that seems to come out of nowhere, and parts of the story feel forced and rushed
especially the part where our hero goes off the deep-end. I could have used some foreshadowing of Mooner’s death, and the fact that the older brother didn’t actually go to Bordertown.
Many elements seemed to come out of nowhere which sometimes made it a little hard to feel invested. It wasn’t great, but the world and the character-building at the front end where strong enough, and the narrative kept up a fast-enough pace, that the story carried even if it didn’t feel as cohesive as it might have.
