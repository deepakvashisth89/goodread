about 300 pages in and am finally starting to enjoy it enough to keep reading.......the writing style is very different and it took time for me to grasp the sudden transport from one scene to the other....i find myself lost and wanting to re-read backward, but i wouldn't dare. i can appreciate the time and place, and the complexities of the characters...somehow i always thought "ruth" was an adult, but only now realize she's one of Josephine's friends.... i find that i am confused about the characters, their ages, and how they relate. i think perhaps the author is giving a very ethereal perspective from the mind of the protagonist, and i find that interesting. it took a little too long to realize this, since the book is in the third person, and would be easier to identify with if it were to be told autobiographically. I like it though. I have finally gotten to a point where I do look forward to turning the next page, however, it took some perserverance.
