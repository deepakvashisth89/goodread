I learned a lot from this book about the reconstruction period. I know it was a lot about African Americans but this focused on that way more than it would consider there were other events that occurred. But it was good to learn. I wish it would have focused more on the presidents of the time because they are just as important to the reconstruction as everything else. I also wish there were footnotes that went with his quoted material. But overall a good book. 3/5 ⭐️
