The Canterbury Tales are not uniformly terrible.  In fact, I quite enjoyed the first two stories (Knight's and Miller's tales).  However, the remaining vignettes were, at best, mediocre.  Further, the longest two tales--those of Melibee and the Parson--are absolutely awful.  Both of these are extended plotless sermons on prudent actions (Melibee's) or the various sins (Parson's).  Getting through this collection was genuinely an act of endurance rather than pleasure.  I could not wait to put this book down and never ever pick it up again.
