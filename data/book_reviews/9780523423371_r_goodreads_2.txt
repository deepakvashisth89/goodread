3.5 Stars

The Hahnemann Paradox starts with an airplane crash where a group of very important scientists die... except one. At the same time a truckdriver gets stuck in a very confusing situation and does not understand what is happening with him. Somewhere else an ambitious female doctor is trying to be one of the bests at her job. This sounds like a good recipe for a thriller and it was indeed very suspenseful.

It is very fast pace, but slows down near the end where I find that the author could have left out 50 pages or so. I took one star of for that. But it did keep me on the edge of my seat. I really wanted to know how this was going to end.

I do have to warn for some heavy medical terms and explanations, if you are not into that maybe pick another book. But this was surprisingly good in my opinion for such an thriller. So although the techniques have changed and is dated in that sense it is !
