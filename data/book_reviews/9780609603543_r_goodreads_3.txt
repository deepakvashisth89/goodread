No matter where you live, you have to acknowledge reality. You have to accept that people aren't perfect, families aren't functional, winning the lottery is terrible, and so on.

However, people still cling to their hopes and dreams, letting them dictate where they go in life. They rationalize and make excuses about their situation, blaming everything except themselves. They hope for a Prince Charming or a Sleeping Beauty to sweep them off their feet and take them away from their troubles.

Dr. Joy Browne was a radio psychologist. I hadn't heard of her. I don't know if she is still practicing, but this book is from 1998, so maybe not. "The Nine Fantasies That Will Ruin Your Life" is a self-help/motivational book. The book takes transcripts from Dr. Browne's radio program and prints them in book form with the names changed to protect the innocent.

Common sense fills the pages of this book. Take the first problem tackled in this book, Dr. Browne calls it, "There's no place like home." Some people think that the past was some idyllic land where dinner was on time, the kids behaved, and everyone had minor problems solvable before a commercial break. Dr. Browne points out that these were television shows. Father Knows Best, My Three Sons, Leave It To Beaver, and many more displayed an unrealistic notion of family life. Take my mother's family, for example. My maternal grandfather was the breadwinner, and my grandmother kept the house in shape. My mother was the middle child of three, and they had a pet dog. Her situation all crashed down when my grandmother started acting strange. She contracted Alzheimer's disease, something they didn't know about at the time. Although I loved my grandfather dearly, I admit that he had a temper. My mother had fights with her siblings over the television, you know, who would watch what programs. There were only three television stations; can you imagine that now?

In any case, Dr. Browne has a refreshing way of speaking. Her no-nonsense attitude towards life is eye-opening. I thoroughly enjoyed the book. Thanks for reading my review, and see you next time.
