The First Minnesota fought effectively throughout the Civil War and played a major starring role at Gettysburg. Moe pulls together an extensive, broad amount of primary source material from the regiment.
One shortcoming of the book is that, in my view, Moe doesn't flow the quotes and narratives smoothly - I struggled to get comfortable with this book.
Another shortcoming is when Moe tries to tie the story of the 1st Minnesota to the broader Civil War. He has a number of misfires throughout. For instance, he claims that the Union and Confederate forces were roughly equal at Antietam, when McClellan had a roughly 2-1 superiority in numbers. Moe also notes that Hooker performance at Chancellorsville went from excellent to poor in a short span - failing to mention that Hooker was hit in the head by a shell fragment and was likely suffering a concussion.
Some really good first person accounts from a remarkable Civil War regiment.
