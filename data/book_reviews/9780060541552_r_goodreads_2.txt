Nirvana's Children is a a rare find that more people will hate than love. The story is thrilling, the language and the imagery alone will blow you out of the water, and it's impossible not to fall in some kind of love with Napoleon as he tumbles out of his comfortable life and into a world of gangs, drugs and sex on the streets of Sydney. At its heart, it's a story of teenager's struggle with his impending adulthood and, above all, the fight for love against the forces of evil, in the form of a grim reaper that follows him around. It's safe to say that there's not another story like it.
