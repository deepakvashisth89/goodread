Reading Wodehouse is unlike reading any other book. It's pure delight from start to finish. It's a journey into a different world. In this case, this world is made up of Mulliners - all kinds of them. Uncles, nephews, brothers, distant relations, all Mulliners, and each with a tale to tell. These are told by one Mulliner at the local pub, the Angler's Rest, where you can also be served my the lady with the picturesque name of Miss Postlethewaite.The stories are elaborately made-up, delightfully twisted and are simply fun to read. Each one is hilarious since the Mulliners have a knack for getting into all kinds of trouble. I, for one, wouldn't mind being a fly on the wall at a Mulliner family party! Or even just spending an evening at Angler's Rest.
