The book Secrets by Danielle Steel is a novel that, despite its numerous run-on sentences beginning with "and" or "but," manages to entertain its readers. It is filled with the familiar tropes that Steel often incorporates into her works, and it follows the pattern of her characters overcoming their personal tragedies and finding the courage to love again. While some may criticize the repetitive nature of Steel's storytelling, her loyal readership appreciates the comfort and predictability that her novels provide.The one issue that stood out to me was the potential disrespect towards the gay community. While considering the publication date, I couldn't help but feel uncomfortable with the portrayal of Zack's homosexuality. It seemed as though the author, Steel, may have intended to depict Zack as "turning straight" or that his sexual orientation was a "mistake." This portrayal left me with mixed feelings and raised concerns about the book's treatment of the gay community.In Secrets, Danielle Steel once again delivers a captivating and dramatic page-turner. While the ending may have been slightly anticlimactic, which is unexpected from Steel, the overall satisfaction and craving for more were ever-present. Love, sex, drama, mystery, and humor are all skillfully woven into the narrative, making Secrets a compelling read.
