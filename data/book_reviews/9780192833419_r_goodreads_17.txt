*as I read other plays within this volume I will update this review and perhaps also its rating.*THE MISANTHROPEI think it's a generally well-done and insightful play in the same vein as Shakespeare's Timon of Athens. Yes, the world is full of vanity, no, you can probably not outrun it, and yes, you are probably engaging in vanity by thinking yourself as one of the few who are "above it all.""But love is never ruled by reason, as you know."I.ii.248
