The first 60% of this is very good, devastating Armand content, and then Anne Rice gets distracted by her confusing Jesus plot. It's interesting how Anne Rice can genuinely write good fiction, but she's always getting distracted. Also this was written when Anne Rice was back in the Catholic church, and it REALLY shows. It is very funny to me that she loves her queer vampires AND Jesus so much that she tries to combine them narratively. She really doesn't care what people will think about her writing and I respect that.

But major warnings for a lot of orientalism / racism in the last half of the book. Like, if you're reading this you'll have read other Anne Rice books, and you'll think you're prepared, but you will NOT be prepared.
