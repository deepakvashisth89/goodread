Mansfield Park is a novel by Jane Austen written in full maturity and somewhat more difficult at first than her other works. Learning the story in which we follow the young and so endearing Fanny, we find with joy such a realistic and uncompromising painting of the English bourgeoisie of the early 19th century. There is only one talent like that of Jane Austen, who can succeed in taking us on board with great pleasure in this so codified environment. Once again, we are drawn into this poignant story with contemporary resonance.
