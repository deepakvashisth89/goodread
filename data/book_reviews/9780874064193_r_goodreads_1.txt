I had this book as a kid and I remembered I loved the stories so much that I read it till it literally fell apart. Now, about 30+ years later, I found it again in a second hand shop and I remember now why I loved it so much. The stories are fantastic and entirely gruesome for a kids book. It’s so nostalgic, no fluff, just bloody, spooky stories that kids and adults alike will love to re read over and over. It’s a rare book so I’ll try not to let it fall apart this time!
