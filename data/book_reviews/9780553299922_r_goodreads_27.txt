Perhaps the most decorated and famous man to fight for the Union in the American Civil War, Joshua Lawrence Chamberlain brings to life one of the most moving moments of American history, the surrender of the Army of Northern Virginia at Appomattox court house. Chamberlain gives a phenomenal first-hand account of the event, and the days and battles leading up to it. This is a book that I recommend all high-school and college students read, as it is a work in the art of literature.
