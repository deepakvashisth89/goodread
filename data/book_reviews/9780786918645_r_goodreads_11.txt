Up until this point, I have enjoyed the books in this series, but other than Against the Giants (which is strangely the only book missing in audio form) I have been frustrated with the overt 90's, male perspective. This book has a glaring lack of that (mostly). Not only that, but the characters are interesting, the story made me want to continue with the book regularly, and I had an invested interest in what happened. Even when a character I thought I didn't like did something I wasn't expecting, I was really excited by it.The book does have some flaws to be sure, but the only major one for me was that it was limited by the D&D adventure it was based on. I'm pocketing this author to dig up more books by them.
