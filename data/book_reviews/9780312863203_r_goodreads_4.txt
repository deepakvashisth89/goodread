This was an interesting book. I expected some kind of Asian fantasy novel what with the title and dragon on the cover, so I was very surprised the first quarter of the book involves an American drifter just sort of going around and eventually becoming involved in a minor mystery. Eventually he meets an Asian-American and the story ramps up by magnitudes. Suddenly there's countless references to Japanese lore, gods, spirits, weaponry and martial arts (so much so that there's an index in the back) and the story takes several unexpected turns and even unveils a semi-unique magic system. All in all, a good read that kept me interested through-out, but ultimately not really my kind of book. Worth checking out if you like Japanese culture, simple magic systems and a solid story by a solid author.
