If Let's Party felt reminiscent of Scream, Wicked has more than a passing resemblance to The Craft. I don't mean that as a criticism. This is one of the more satisfying entries in the series--no fake-out cliffhangers (that I can remember, at least), no mundane explanations undercutting the supernatural elements, no twists to undermine the rest of the story. Also the ending is pretty sadistic.

Like Ty Sullivan in Sweetheart, Evil Heart, Marla Newman is a more dynamic protagonist than you normally see in Stine books precisely because she is kind of unlikable. Unlike Ty, though, she actually does seem to learn a lesson as a result of the events of the book. The fact that she develops some kind of control over dark forces, rather than simply falling victim to them, is interesting. I don't think that will come into play in future books, however.

The only real problem is that the revelation of the last chapter potentially contradicts the implications of the prologue, but I guess you can head-canon that into making sense.
