I read this "thought experiment" (i.e., Veil of Ignorance) which was from The Theory Of Justice (1971, by John Rawls.) While I found it to be rather interesting, it definitely smelled of "Equity" & "Inclusion" wording in order to have a more orderly society. Written long before the Diversity, Equity, & Inclusion (DEI) model that has come upon us by stormfront over the last 10 years. Don't get me wrong, as I am not knocking Mr. Rawls' posited theory, for I believe that you can take good things from any theory and at the same time those same theories can have very bad consequences, dependent upon those who wield the poser over their execution. I, like many others, would love a better society; however, it is not likely to happen while man is in charge! Man has sought the ever-eluding concept of a utopian society. And man is constantly screwing things up. If you truly want to create a better society, YOU absolutely MUST work on yourself and better yourself. Society will only get better when we better ourselves and live our lives according to the Golden Rule! Well worth the time to get acquainted with this ideology and theory.
