Give this author a historical premise of dukes and pirates, and she does a decent job of turning out a sassy romance. But with a simple contemporary story, she struggles to get any life into it !This tale is a lame story of a city girl coming to visit her dad in her country home and falling for the rich guy living next door. All the mandatory criteria are ticked :- he is a financial whiz kid, and so that means crazy rich. He doesn't do a minute's work in the whole story though !- a baby , his nephew is added into the plot. Just to make him look like a saint, and give the lead pair some excuse to meet using the kid as premise.- an ex OM, who neither inspires disgust in the reader, nor spikes our sympathy. He is plain boring !- the girl comes home with a truck load of prejudiced ideas about the 'neighbor'. That he is about to usurp her family home, he got rich by foul means, the kid could be his love child , etc !!!! With no basis at all for her assumptions.There is nothing wrong with the guy per say. And in hind sight , that may be the main problem with the story !!! He is too soft spoken, too fatherly with the kid, too forgiving with the heroine when she insults him. Too sweet to be taken seriously !And every time writers use cliches like 'said huskily', 'blue eyes like the ocean', without infusing the necessary emotion into the scene, it all comes out as fake and make believe.Didn't work for me. But nothing wrong with the tale either ! 2 stars.
