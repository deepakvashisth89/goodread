Reread 2:
With the benefit of more years of experience, I actually fully enjoyed this one, and I can really appreciate the ways it's ahead of its time, rather than focusing on the aspects that are dated (yes, there are a few problematic moments that don't hit).

First off, I think of Crusie as a romance author, but I'd actually term this more of a women's fiction/chick lit mystery. There's romance, yes, and it is relatively central, but actually it follows both Nell and Suze, and they both have a romance, though Nell and Gabe's is more the focal point. It's much more about both of their emotional journeys than it is the mystery or their romances, I'd say. Nor do I think the romances are the strongest element.

What I liked so much about this is the ways in which it's really not like anything you'd read in the genre, especially back when it was published. There's non-judgmental acknowledgment of women being sexually attracted to women. Both Nell and Gabe have sex with other people besides each other before they hook up, and it's never an issue. Gabe gets along with and still loves his ex-wife Chloe, and there's absolutely no drama about this. Nell hates her ex-husband, but she also insists that there was love there once and takes ownership that there were two sides to that story, even though I also hate him. There's so much maturity to certain aspects of this story, and it's just something that is so rare to see, and I really love it.

The mystery is fun and complex and oddly dark honestly. It's well done, aside from the fact that it wraps up with like one page left. The book is also just wildly long for it's genre, taking me over 5 hours to read. I don't get the obsession with china though.

TBH I'd recommend this to fans of Nora's romantic suspense, because the blend of humor, romance, and dark elements hits the way a Nora does.
