3+ Stars for The Howard Hughes Affair
~ by Stuart Kaminsky

This is my second Toby Peters, Private Investigator Mystery. It's an enjoyable, quick (200 pages) diversion from some of my other reading.

I enjoy the Toby Peters character, self deprecating, always humorous thoughts rummaging through his head. And he doesn't have much of a filter to keep from making a sarcastic comment that might be better left unsaid.

Some clever twists and turns, and especially marvelous use of the early 1940's Los Angeles location to include celebrities in his stories.

I had read that Basil Rathbone, perhaps best remembered for playing Sherlock Holmes in several old black and white movies was a chief character in this book. Almost a third of the way through I was wondering when he would show up. Two pages later, there he was. And he was worth the wait.

I can't say it's great, but it is a fun, and satisfying murder mystery.

I've had a bit of a hard time finding a couple of the Kaminsky books I want to read, but I'll be making an effort to locate and read more Toby Peters Mysteries.
