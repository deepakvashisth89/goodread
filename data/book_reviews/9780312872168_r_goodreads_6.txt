Part of Bova’s Grand Tour loosely linked series.Can be read as stand alone. But im not sure I recommend it.We follow the estranged youngest son of a billionaire tycoon who deciding to prove to his father once and for all he is worth something- takes up his father’s challenge to retrieve his brother’s body from the scortched surface of venus.It was readable and parts were fun but the MC was written very immaturely and yet still got his most immature happy ending that didnt feel deserved. I wish he had actually learned soemthing during this journey. I would have been able to write a better ending….One big reservation is women are often seen and act as property to be won in this book andnit just seemed to add to Bova’s immaturity as well as the characters.Meh. 🫤
