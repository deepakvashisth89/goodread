The Jung Cult: Origins of a Charismatic MovementHere is a book I wouldn’t have considered had I not been introduced to the theories of psychoanalysis and psychotherapists through my research. Richard Noll reveals a hard to believe world of mind sciences developed and promoted as answers to human issues. Wonderful as it initially seems it becomes abundantly clear that these theories are really occult philosophies. So how have these become mainstream ideas for training and health?There are not many books that confront the Carl Jung narrative head, in this manner.
