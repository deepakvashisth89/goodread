This is a big serious brick of a book so make sure you're willing to invest the time and brainpower needed before you begin reading it. Having said that it is fairly reader friendly and almost "populist" in tone but it's still a big book with a lot of big ideas and loads of data to digest.The author's main idea (or least as much as i was able to digest of it) is this: to be a great power a nation needs military power, to get military power it needs economic power, to get economic power it needs the right combination of raw materials, economic policy and business friendly government plus an educated population able to participate in a modern economy. If even one of these things is missing the results can be fatal for a nation's power and military performance.Certain historical conclusions follow from this and the author is not shy of pointing them out: both the central powers in WWI and the Axis powers in WWII were doomed from the start having neither the resources nor the economic base from which to wage a sustained war. Also China and Russia have and will continue to fail due to bad economic policy.Having read this book and thought about it a little i'm coming around to the idea that my country (Australia) is also doomed. We have offshored all our manufacturing, we build almost nothing anymore, our education system is basically a visa scam for Indians and Chinese and our entire economy is based on digging up minerals and a real estate ponzi scheme that's well overdue for collapse. Add to this the fact that we're governed by the dregs of our middle class and i see no good future for Australia.Anyway this is a big ideas book and a book to make you think if you're willing to invest the time and effort into it.
