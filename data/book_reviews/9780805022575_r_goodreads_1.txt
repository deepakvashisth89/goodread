This beautiful book features color photographs that capture the essence and function of water on our planet. Each full page spread has a short paragraph documenting the photographic subject matter in simple, yet elegant language.

Even though the language is simple enough for elementary school children, the sparse prose and gorgeous images cause the reader to ponder the incredible element of water.

Beautiful enough to be a coffee table book, educational enough for a life time. Excellent. I would like one for our home.
