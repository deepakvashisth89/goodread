Rancher Corbin Macquarie is injured during a train robbery and left behind for dead. He's nursed back to health by Jessica/Jessie/Jesse/Bessie Watson. The two fall in love and Corbin is eager to get hitched but Jessica/Jessie/Jesse/Bessie holds back because she doesn't know how to break it to him that she was actually a part of the outlaws who were robbing the train.
