To put it plainly...
...a spin0ff of Gone With the Wind and The Thorn Birds.

Reanna is a little annoying in the beginning, but you're able to get over it. At this point, she's still interesting enough to follow through. Her predicament with her husband Andrew makes a good story, even though the roles are really cliché. Reanna is always, always in the right. Andrew is always, always in the wrong. Yawn.

Pride has no personality. He just smiles and bears everything without protest. He's just a perfect little man. It's just another example of how one-sided and tw0-dimensional the characters are. The antagonists are 100% evil. Andrew checks off all the boxes as an abusive person with no "lighter" side to balance his negative qualities. It not only makes him unrealistic as a character, but predictable.

As the story progresses, I found Reanna to be unbearably annoying and stupid. She looks too much like Meggie and acts too much like Scarlet. Some of the events are too similar to those books. Besides the setting and a few things, not much felt original to me. I actually never read the end until a year or two after I started the book. It goes off into Reanna's son's life, and I lost interest.
