In Episode 99 - The Demon Pig: Book 2 in the Quadroped Series by Karen A. Brush, we read a book that was recommended to us on Twitter over a year ago by Twitter user @GeneCurl. The Demon Pig is a clever, fun fantasy novel full of delightful animal characters, but it just narrowly misses the opportunity to be a truly great parody. If you're looking for a spot of cheer with sharp jokes that pokes a bit of fun at the fantasy genre, though, get a copy for yourself!
