5 Stars. If you want to read one Lucas Davenport, this is it. "Consistently Good" is a phrase I could apply to the whole series, but this is one of the ones which stand out. Davenport is challenged to sort through numerous incidents to find the light at the end of the tunnel. And he does. Can you catch the decisive clue? The chair of a large bank in Minnesota, Daniel Kresge, takes his four senior executives hunting in the beautiful lake and forest country in the northern part of the state. When he is shot at close range, each of them becomes a prime suspect. They are all aware that a potential merger was in the offing, and each immediately starts to plot their next moves - as the next chair of a still independent Polaris Bank, or as a very senior exec in the new larger conglomerate. Straightforward? You say one of Wilson McDonald, Terrance Robles, James Bone or Susan O'Dell must have done it? Prove it. Easier said than done. And then someone firebombs Weather Karkinnen's house, clearly in an effort to confuse and distract Davenport - she's his former fiancée, now estranged because of the explosive ending of #8. Get ready for excitement. (May 2022)
