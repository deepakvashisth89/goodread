There's not much information about this series that I could find. It might have only had two volumes. I have no idea. It's published by Pinnacle. THE men's adventure series publisher. It's from the early eighties. Those are two good reasons I picked it up. After a few glances at the cover, it got me thinking. The blurb on the cover states, "The American Hero Who Believes In America First." Then the picture of our hero. A white guy with a bronze tan. A head of orange, blond hair combed over. I thought, oh my, Don Scott created an early version of Trump. Look it up online. It's creepy.

The story goes that Raker, a man not bound by any government agencies can work outside the law to get things done. The word out on the street is that sixty-six pounds of pure heroine is on its way from Tijuana to the States. Raker won't allow that to happen. He goes deep under cover to eliminate the ones responsible. He also gets to see a sex show or two south of the border.

To round out this farce even more, they have an add to buy Raker #1 at the end of this book. To quote the description, it says, "A hardened Vietnam vet with brains, guts and killing power, Raker believes in America the beautiful-right or wrong. Whether it's leftist or terrorists, radical or separatists, blacks or whites, foreigners or freeloaders, libbers or gays-Raker is sworn to annihilate any man woman or collective force that threatens the American way." WOW, too much.
