This is one of the books I inherited when my mother had to move into an eldershome. I think my brother gave it to her. He crossed the Atlantic Ocean a long time ago and lives in Canada. For what other reason could there be a Canadian author on our bookshelves I wonder. We are not used to read Canadian books... I am sorry to say. It is a very interesting story about the difference between people and how they cope with their youth, their experiences and their emotions. For me there were a lot of difficult words in the book, so I often needed tot translate words in Dutch. But well... that is good for my languages. :-) Urquhart playes a lot with time in the book. And sometimes she writes about the same thing in a total different part of the book. That was a bit annoying, because sometimes I forgot where I stopped reading and couldn't find the rigth page again. Often I read pages for the second of third time before I found out where I had to start reading. But all in all I liked it very much. Maybe we Dutch should read more Canadian authors.
