RATING: 3.5The premise of this book was interesting. Beth Ralston is part of an elite team of the future who recover stolen art pieces and return them to the time period in which they belong. This time she is after another member who has traveled back in time and is stealing art treasures and sending them forward in time. I enjoyed the time travel element and laughed at Beth's numerous blunders in attempting to talk like Londoners of the time period. The developing romance between Beth and Lord Knightly could have been an intriguing part of the story but for the repeated comments about their desire to consummate their relationship. I much preferred the humorous romantic attempts by Alexander's tall, twiggy sister, Lady Juno, and her heart's desire, Zander's best friend, Lord Anthony.The regency period was well developed and the author's descriptions of clothing, furniture and places showed obvious research.FAVORITE QUOTES: "Never ever think you may change a man's character. It can't be done." If you cannot love the man exactly as he is, warts and all as they say, then you should wonder if you live the real man or are in love with a dream you've hung about the man like a cloak.""Zander, you weren't raised to fall into despair at the prospect of facing your fences. This is merely another fence...A high one to be sure, but just a fence.""I just didn't know you'd ever give headroom to the notion of marriage......I didn't until the notion took up heart-room!""The fool is the man who can't or won't see beyond his nose."
