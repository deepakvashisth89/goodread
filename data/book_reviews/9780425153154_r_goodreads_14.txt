Author Peter Robinson gives his readers plenty to ponder in Innocent Graves. Beyond the investigation of the murder of a teenage girl, there is the issue of class and its relationship to the justice system; the sensationalism of the media and its efforts to try a suspect in the public eye; and not least of all, the life after trial for a suspect found not guilty.A well-told tale as I've come to expect from Robinson, plenty of suspects from which to choose, and questions to ponder long after the last page has been read.
