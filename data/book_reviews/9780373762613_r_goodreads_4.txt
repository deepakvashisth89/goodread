D1261 Dec99 Crenshaws of Texas #2 and Man of the Month. Dan Crenshaw's business partner framed Dan for the stealing he had done. If it hadn't been for Dan's air tight alibi he would have gone to prison. Dan has checked out and gone to San Padres Island to get away from everyone. His sister Mandy has sent Shannon Doyle to find him and be his housekeeper/cook. Shannon had a crush on Dan when they were in high school, he didn't notice the girl who was 3yrs younger. He notices her now. They are kidnapped by Guardino who takes them out on his ship. They tell him they are engaged and know nothing about the men Guardino is looking for. Shannon and Dan are returned to San Padres Island where her brother show up and takes her home. Dan realizes he wants to marry her, will she? In 2 weeks? Enjoyed this, Shannon was a force to reckoned with.
