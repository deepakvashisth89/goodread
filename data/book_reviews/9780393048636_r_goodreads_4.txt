This memoir was a heart wrenching account of what it was like to live under the Khmer Rouge regime.Him's account starts when she was a child living in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. She goes onto describe the mass "evacuation" of her city and being placed in a labour camp. She also goes onto describe the conditions of the camps, starvation, the loss of loved ones, and the other horrors she faced.If you read this memoir, it can help to put current refugee events into perspective. Also, make sure you have plenty of tissues near by.
