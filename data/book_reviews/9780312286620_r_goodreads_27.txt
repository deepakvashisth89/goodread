I picked this up from a little free library, but was underwhelmed. It read more like a school report than a biography, but I guess that’s the best you can do when all of your sources are secondhand.I read a few stories I’d never heard before, but the author seriously needs a fact checker and a proofreader, or maybe he just needs to read the Harry Potter books. He stated that in the world of Harry Potter, banks are run by owls. Pretty sure the Gringotts goblins would be offended by that. Apparently Harry has a crush on Cho Chan.Then there were the inaccuracies about J.K. Rowling herself, like the fact that he continually called her Joanne Kathleen, as if Kathleen were actually her middle name and not just sort of adopted from her grandmother to create her pen name of J.K. Rowling at the request of her publishers.I get that this was written just after the 4th book and maybe knowledge about the topic wasn’t as prevalent, but, in my opinion, authors lose credibility when I find that many errors in the first few pages.
