Comparable, I imagine, to Lenin for Dummies (if such a book exists). Am not sure I could get through a more detailed history of Lenin and the Bolshevik revolution with its constant swirl of characters, innumerable plots, internal wrestling matches, and the confusing morass of Marxism’s intellectual underpinnings, so this illustrated bio may be as far as I get into the life and crimes of Vladimir Ilyich Ulyanov. That said, this is a sympathetic biography and I may be tempted to attempt something more substantive about Lenin if anyone has any great readable suggestions.
