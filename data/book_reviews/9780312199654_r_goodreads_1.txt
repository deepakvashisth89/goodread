My God, what a horrible book. My stubborness kept me reading because I don't like stopping books no matter how horrible they are. At about page 375, I started to understand what was going on. I'm not sure if it was the poor writing style, the thick Kentucky southern drawal, or the fact that the book had no consistent story line, but this was not enjoyable at all. Some really good components kept me reading, but honestly, this was painful. I struggled through reading it for a whole month and would do other things to avoid having to sit and down and read it. Thank god its done with now.
