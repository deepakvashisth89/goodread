This book has been on my shelf for soooo long. I bought it from the book fair not knowing the author or anything about it but who cares if there's a murder in it, right?Well, I definitely like it. Not like Christie's books of course but I liked Asey Mayo too, even though his way of talking distracted me a bit to be honest. I felt a little bit of a dragging bore, but I like how it was never decided who the murderer was till the very end. I would have liked it if the hints were a bit clearer though, makes one at least look back and say 'A-HA, how did I not see that coming?'Didn't expect the ending, at all. Rated 4 because it was a dear company for about 4-5 nights.
