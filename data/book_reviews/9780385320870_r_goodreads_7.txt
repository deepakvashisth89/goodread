I think this book has a really creative and unexpected premise, teens stealing road signs leading to a woman’s death is such a strange but compelling angle.I originally read this when I was about 11 and only remembered vague pieces of it, so I picked it up again for sentimental reasons and curiosity. Reading it now gives it a completely different vibe.As I’m going through it, I’m noticing how heavily it leans into guilt. At times the repetition comes across as if it's hammering the same emotion instead of letting it evolve.I also remember it being more interesting the first time. That might be nostalgia, but this reread didn’t hit the same way, and I don’t think it’s something I would come back to again.
