An emotional, heartbreaking, and ultimately beautiful book. Two profound love stories, decades apart, that take the reader through the innocence of caring hearts, betrayal of a culture for greed, evil wrought by power grabs, and into love born of finding the one person who sees you, truly hears you, and understands your soul. In Sunrise Song, the ties of family, both blood and chosen, wind through 40 years of history carefully pieced together by research and great storytelling.
