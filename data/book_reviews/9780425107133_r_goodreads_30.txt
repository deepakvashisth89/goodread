Miss Seeton does it again.

Once again, Miss Seeton finds herself embroiled in the midst of a murder investigation and the police have once again roped her in to help by being what she believes to be an Identikit artist.
But the murders are linked to two groups of strange beliefs and right in the middle is Miss Seeton who the idiotic villagers begin to believe must be a witch too. . . Mayhem ensues.

I love how the author weaves multiple points of view without ever once losing the thread or causing confusion. I enjoyed this book thoroughly.

Also, it was very well researcched
