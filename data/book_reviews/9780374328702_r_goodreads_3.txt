Harry grows up in the city and really loves it there. His grandpa lives far away in the countryside. One day, grandpa comes and takes Harry to visit him on his farm for a week. It's strange at first, but Harry soon comes to enjoy the animals and activity on the farm. And when the week is over, he's happy to return home, too. He realizes that everyone's home is different and it's just right for them. Good for preschoolers learning about the concept of "home".
