I didn't realize until I looked this book up on here, that it is part of a series. However, it can definitely be read as a stand alone. Reading almost like a diary, and set in the 60's, the story follows a young girl who is abused and nearly killed by her broken, alcoholic father. Struggling to find her place in the world, and hope in the midst of hardship, she stumbles into a cult. It sounds wonderful to her at first, almost like a promised land, but slowly becomes a prison. This book evokes emotion and is hard to put down!
