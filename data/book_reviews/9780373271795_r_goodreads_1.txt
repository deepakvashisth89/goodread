This was a decent book, nothing great.Gretchen is a CPA and she has played it safe all her life partly because her parents who were much older but now her best friend is dead and when she receives her wishes she decides to follow them at first just to fulfill them and then she gets into the spirit of it, embracing life.Marco is her tenant, a doctor who she has always found attractive so in order to fulfill one of the terms she propositions him but her heart is not in it which Marco notices. When he has a baby to take care of they are thrown together and then strike a friendship while fulfilling Gretchen's need for adventure.Marco is a confirmed bachelor, he feels his job is too demanding for a family though of course we know it isn't.Decent.
