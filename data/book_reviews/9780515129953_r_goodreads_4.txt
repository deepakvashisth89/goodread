This was an odd book, in that I really didn't like the main character (she seemed incredibly immature to me), but I still couldn't seem to stop reading. The best thing about the book has to be David Jones, and it was perhaps his character that kept me reading. However, I agree with other reviewers who felt that he could have done better. The heroine (whose name I can't even remember at this point) was emotionally immature, and her attempts to manipulate whatever-his-name-was that she was dating made me like her even less. Even her mother seemed happy not to have her around for long doses, and I can't say I blame her.However, David Jones was quite enjoyable, and it is for him that I give this the third star.
