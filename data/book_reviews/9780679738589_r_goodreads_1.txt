In reading "Before We Were Yours," I was introduced to shantyboats and shantyboat culture. This, of course, led me down a research hole, which eventually led to a "discovery" of Harlan Hubbard. Once I began reading about Harlan and Anna Hubbard, I realized there was a huge gap in my knowledge of sustainability figures and regional artists/writers. And all of that brought me to Wendell Berry's wonderful book about Hubbard and the revolutionary life he and his wife chose to live.Thank you, Wendell Berry, for again leading me to beauty and wisdom.
