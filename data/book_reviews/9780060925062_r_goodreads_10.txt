Author Kim Chernin tells her mother's story -- a feisty Jewish Communist organizer who went to prison under the Smith Act and was still not deterred from organizing rent strikes, picket lines, and rent strikes -- and in the telling also reveals her grandmother's story, a talented woman who was the victim of a violent husband, and her daughter's story, another talented woman headed to Harvard.
All of the stories are fascinating -- not something typical of an American family. Occasionally a little confusing. Who is the mother speaking? Who is the daughter? But overall a great read.
