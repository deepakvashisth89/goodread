Another enjoyable Palmer-Jones mystery. The death of Molly's aunt brings Molly and George to a small community with secrets going back 35 years. Which secret was behind the murder of Molly's aunt?I love the way Molly switches between being the wise old woman and the dithering old lady. By picking the right persona, secrets are revealed to her. George's law enforcement connections bring in essential clues, but Molly is the center of this series as far as I am concerned.
