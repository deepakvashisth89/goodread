This is the second book in the Inspector Richard Jury collection. Jury finds himself in a small fishing village investigating the murder of an unidentified woman. There are secrets and resentments held by various characters in town. It is up to Jury to discover and unravel these secrets in order to solve the case. Of course, Jury has his friend Melrose Plant, who is more than happy to assist Jury as an amateur detective. We didn’t have Aunt Agatha in this one to add the comic relief.
I didn’t enjoy this story as much as the first one but I will continue with this series.
