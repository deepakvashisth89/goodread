Subtopic: KwanzaaGenre: Historical/InformationalTopics: African American tradition and valuesSynopsis: I loved this book and the illustrations were so colorful! Like many of the Kwanzaa books I've read, this one was extremely informational. The book started out with talking about the meaning of Kwanzaa and its importance. It then focused on how families decorate and prepare for the holiday and celebrations. Kwanzaa lasts for seven days and on each day a candle is lit. The three red candles stand for the struggles that people face and the three green candles stand for what is to come in the future. The book goes through all seven days and what is specifically done on each day of the celebration. This book was extremely informative and taught me so much about the African culture and holiday.Pinkney, A. D. (1993). Seven candles for Kwanzaa. New York: Dial Books for Young Readers.
