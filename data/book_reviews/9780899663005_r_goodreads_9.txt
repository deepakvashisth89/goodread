Rated 3.5 rounded to 4 stars

Mrs Moore and her companion, Adela Questid, arrive in India from England to spend time with Mrs Moore’s son, Ronny, a City Magistrate. This is not current India, but the India of the 1920’s when the British government had quite the hold on the population and its customs.

Dr Aziz, a poor doctor of Muslim faith becomes acquainted with the two ladies and devises a means to properly show them “the real India”. With customs, faiths, and other ignorant beliefs being at odds, a misunderstanding of epic proportions can only be expected.

While this story is compelling, the fact that the telling of it is so verbose, I almost didn’t finish it. It did cause me to watch the 1984 movie version on Hulu once I finished the book.

WARNING: Small minds and racial ignorance (and language).
