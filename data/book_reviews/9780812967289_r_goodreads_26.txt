Typhoon is an exemplary Conrad “at sea” tale featuring a storm at sea that challenges the value system of the crew, and a great bite size example of Conrad’s use of that genre. Falk also features a similar “how the sea changes one” theme. Same with Amy Foster but with different elements. The Secret Sharer is a little bit of a hidden masterpiece from Conrad’s mature age.

Look, either you know Conrad or you don’t. You obviously start with Heart of Darkness. There are novel length masterpieces. Lord Jim and Nostromo. This volume of four stories/brief novellas is a good dipping in point or exploration/returning point for Conrad curious and enthusiasts. It’s a good selection and all four show different aspects of how Conrad explored human nature juxtaposed within the natural and artificial worlds as backdrops. Recommended for enthusiasts of literary modernism.
