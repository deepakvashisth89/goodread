The Outer Limits: The Choice by John Peel was a fairly good. It was an easy to read book that kept me entertained right from the beginning. It starts with a girl named Aggie who is getting bullied by her fellow classmates. Once she had enough time froze and mysterious things began happening. She had no idea what was going on, but once time had unfroze everyone seemed to have gotten beat up in some way. That’s when she met Karen who was a teacher but she also knew what Aggie was going through. This book has very good character development. It’s almost as if I can relate their personalities to people I know in my life. I also never really got bored or discouraged from reading it as it was an interesting book about things we as humans sometimes cannot control specifically our minds. Overall good book. I give it three stars, nothing special but it wasn’t a bad book either.
