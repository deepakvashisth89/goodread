This was an amusing read. I liked that the couple related well with each other and had good banter. They also discovered that they had good chemistry with each other.The heroine is the PA of the Hero, they work well together since she is super efficient though a bit dowdy and plain so the Hero is not distracted by her charms although he likes and respects her.She agrees to help him out for the weekend to act as his girlfriend so to put off the obsessive infatuation of a young family friend. Since the heroine is a bit frumpy, her friend lends her some clothes for a weekend party. The clothes are a bit loud and colorful, not exactly appropriate for a country weekend, but suddenly the Hero gets to see her charms and is immediately and violently attracted. The OW does not stand a chance.Finally they give in to their attraction and have a stunning lovemaking evening. All is well until they have to return to the office and the hero decides that it would be better to go back to their previous working relationship. The heroine is hurt but agrees. When she turns up in the office in a new stunning outfit the Hero is consumed w lust and they do it on her desk. Both realize that this is a bad idea and the hero decides to move the heroine to a different department. However she takes this the wrong way, so there is a bit of misunderstanding between them until they can resolve it in the end for their HEA.I did like this couple together, they had a good intellectual relationship as well as a sexual one. Apparently the heroine was the only one, even before her make over, to be able to pry personal information from the Hero and get him to talk about himself, more than any woman he had ever been with.3.5*
