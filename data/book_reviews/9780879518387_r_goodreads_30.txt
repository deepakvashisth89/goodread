reading the 1848 letters had me shadow boxing angeline goreau all afternoon & half the evening. wept at harriet martineau's account of meeting charlotte the year after; "she cast up at me such a look, - so loving, so appealing, - that, in connexion with her deep mourning dress, and the knowledge that she was the sole survivor of her family, i could with the utmost difficulty return her smile, or keep my composure. i should have been heartily glad to cry."
