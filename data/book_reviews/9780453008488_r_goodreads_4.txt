Stephen King from the start has delivered great novels. Skeleton Crew is in the my top list of his masterpieces. This book contains many chilling short stories that I guarantee Stephen King fans and horror reading fans will love. Each story fluidly introduces characters and greatly takes you on a captivating adventure.Stephen King puts a lot of detail in his story that puts you in the situation and I was surprised how he could have such talent even when writing the smallest stories. Skeleton Crew is an amazing book and I recommend to all young adult readers. If you are a fan of the horror genre, make this book part of your book collection.
