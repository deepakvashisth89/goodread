April goes to live with Grandma, her mum has met someone new and is going away for a bit. April finds is it hard being deserted by her mum but gradually with the help of Caroline her Grandmother and making a new friend of Elizabeth who lives in an apartment in her block she begins to enjoy life and not constantly long go go back to Hollywood.They start a game based on ancient Egypt and soon some others join in. Something bad happens in the neighbourhood and there isn't as much freedom for outdoor play as there was.There are some wonderful characters in this story, April who likes to show off her glamorous Hollywood background and wear false eyelashes is also intelligent, thoughtful, brave and compassionate. How nice to see some boys who are the class's cool jokers join in and find they really like the imaginative play. A wonderful look at children playing outdoors, imaginative play and play that requires a bit of freedom and some risk.A happy and climactic ending. If I had read this when I was young I would have been recruiting members for an Egypt group ASAP!Read on openlibrary
