This book opened my eyes at 22 years old to the "possibilities" my parents, school and others never mentioned to me growing up. Let me tell you how I found this book:After a bad day working for a mean old man who told me my days were numbered, I was heading towards the bus stop feeling really dejected and worthless. My life sucked and I was fresh out of the Military.I dropped inside a book store as I wait for the bus, and meandered around the store. On a back row, back of the store, at the lowest shelf, backwards and behind another book - I grasped at a mystery book. I pulled out the Seeds of Greatness.As I read the book, I discovered information I never knew existed. It opened new doors. It changed my perspective and I found my real life not working for a wretched soul for a wage, but as a motivated entrepreneurial sales guy and unlimited income potential.Almost 30 years later I thank God I found the truth.
