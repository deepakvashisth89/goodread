This is a wonderful collection of poetry, by all different poets, about recollections of you and your relationship with your children that have grown up. It seemed like a lot of the poems could have been written by me! I have even thought of sending a copy to each of my sons so they can read how I feel about them.
"I Am Proud to be Your Parent", "Being Your Parent has Taught Me So Much About Life", "I Will Always Stand by You"...these are some of the poems in this collection. This is a book that all parents will appreciate as they see their children grow up.
