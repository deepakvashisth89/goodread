(2009) My first book on my Kindle... Loved this Austen book--- probably because I could seriously relate to the heroine... Was so similar at 17. It was simply a pleasure to read, complete with several chuckle out loud scenes.

(2025) Currently rereading via an Audible Original narrated by the British Austen Goddess, Emma Thompson. I’m in heaven and the pacing brings with it a degree of detail I’d completely forgotten!

A road trip and a few late night listens equaled a lovely listening experience. I delight to spend a year in the life of Catherine Moreland— from a silly girl to a thoughtful and introspective young lady.

Clearly inspired by early gothic novels, I loved Austen’s use of an involved narrator and gothic-tinged melodrama.
