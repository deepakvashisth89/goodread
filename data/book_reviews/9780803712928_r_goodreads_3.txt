Before reading this picture book I did not know a thing about Kwanzaa. I was under the impression that it was a holiday that some people celebrated in place of Christmas because of their faith. Well, I was wrong. 🤦🏽‍♀️Kwanzaa is actually a holiday created in America and inspired by African traditions. It was created in 1966. It is a beautiful tradition that is observed from December 26th to January 1st and does not take the place of Christmas. It is a time to spend with family, rejoice, and observe the 7 principles of Kwanzaa. I recommend this book to anyone who wants to know more about this holiday.#YesiReads #DiverseAuthors #DiverseBooks #picturebooks
