Still not sure for this book.. It's like.. It was not good. Not bad. Something in between. Just okay, I guess. Since I have already readTwo Past Midnight: Secret Window, Secret Garden, and loved it, I expected something much more byStephen King. But no, it didn't work.To begin with, I want to make clear from now, that I actually liked the story, the main idea, and some of the characters, but it just seemed to me that there were wayyy to many plot holes, or - to be more acurate - unexplained facts. While I was reading the book, I thought that everything would be clear till the ending, but here I am, having finished the book, and still can't make sense. I don't know if I'm just stupid and don't undertsand, or if there are ,indeed, plot holes, but for some reason something didn't feel very right.Here are my unanswered questions, after having finished the book:1.How can this be considered time travel? If they actually had traveled in the past, as they think they did, wouldn't things be different? I mean, wouldn't they meet people of the past, see the towns as they used to be, but still find everythig normal? The fact that they found no one around, that there was no electricity, and that the food was... not tasty, doen't prove that they actually time traveled! To be honest, that's the part that makes absolute no point to me.2.Where did all the other passengers go? I understand that they somehow stopped existing, or something, but then why where their things left behind? And okay, we can accept it till now, but if their things were actually left behind, then why did the find only bags and this stuff, and not their clothes? Did they somehow stop existing along with their owners.. Nah, that doesn't make sense.3.What were the langoliers? Okay, I know that you will unswer that they were just some... strange creatures who destroyed everything, but how did this whole thing worked? And if they actually ate the past like that, the future wouldn't exist either so...4.Why did Nick have to die???? This is not just because I liked the character, but also a technical question too. Couldn't they just regulate the pressure so as to make them sleep, and then return to normal after some minutes? And till then to have the autio pilt turned on? It just seems to me like Nick sacrificed himself for no reason.So, these were the technical questions.. But also I want to point out some things that annoyed me a little.1.How easily Lorel forgot Nick. I know that she said he would be okay with her be happy, and I agree, Nick seemed like a nice person, but from the other hand, isn't it a little bad by her side? I mean, the person she loved just died, and died for them to live, and she just forgets him and goes with Brian (who I really disliked) a second after? No, that's wring...2.How Dinah behaved to Toomey. I must now admit that I am from that minority that liked Toomey. I know that he was bad to others, but he had passed from so many things in his childhood, and also others treated him very badly too. At the beggining, I thought that Dinah acted nicely to him, and was the ony one that understood that he was actually afraid, until I realised that she just wanted to use him, so us to help others (and herself). She throwed him to death, just to comfort her friends... That's bad Dinah, really bad. And you were even the only person who actually understood him. Why did you have to do it?So, yeah, that's my review I guess. Hope you liked it. If you have read the book, you can write your opinion (or your theories) in the comments :)
