A celebrated artist in her own right, a polarising figure, the supposed poster child for avant-garde, and who knows what else. This is basically a book of instructions ranging from a fun day out ("Walk all over the city with an empty / baby carriage") to impossible ("Fly.") to hazardous ("Smoke everything you can. / Including your pubic hair."). Instructions unclear, I am now on fire.Despite that, please trust me when I say there IS some serious artistic insight here (duh, it's Yoko Ono). I just know some serious art school friends who would suck this thing whole, yet also not at all because she presents this truly fascinating dichotomy of the act of living itself being a work of art and yet also something really abstract that can only be understood if you understand nothing at all. And if that doesn't capture the whack-but-profound nature of art itself or what one makes of it, then what have you. Piece out.
