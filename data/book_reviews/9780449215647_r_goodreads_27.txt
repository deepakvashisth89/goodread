Lately I've been reading a few books I adored as an older child or young adult. Now as I re-read them I think, "Wow, my tastes have changed." A span of fifty years will do that to a person. But happily this particular book has passed the test of time.

It is a simple mystery that the reader knows will have a happy ending. The plot involves a new wife searching for her husband who shows up in a news reel from Austria when he's supposed to be in Switzerland. The action includes a circus, castles, the Lipizzaner stallions, a train nearly squashing a person caught in the tracks, and plenty of foot and car chases. And lots of prowling around. What really made me chuckle was the sexy banter between the young couple when they reunited - that part went right over my head when I first read this.

Mary Stewart was a NY Times bestselling author back in the 1960s. In the later part of the decade I read her books every chance I could get. If I remember correctly her series of books about Merlin were fantastic. I think I'll pass re-reading any of these others. This was enough of a nostalgia trip of her works for me.
