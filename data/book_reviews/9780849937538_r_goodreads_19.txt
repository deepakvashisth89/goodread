Short chapters on the theme of 'bruised reeds and smouldering wicks'. The author takes Biblical characters or situations, and puts a slightly different perspective on them. Poetic license undoubtedly, but he's a good writer and it makes interesting reading.Relevant anecdotes from more recent years are included in some places, and the author gently makes the point - many times - that God is with us, and loves us, and wants to heal us inwardly. Shame, guilt and other inner burdens can harm us more than more obvious disabilities.Intended for Christians, and recommended to anyone, particularly if struggling to hear God's voice in difficult situations.Full review here:https://suesbookreviews.blogspot.com/...
