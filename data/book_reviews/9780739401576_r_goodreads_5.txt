3.5 stars,Metaphorosis reviewsSummaryMany races have lived on Demoth, but it's now populated by Oolom - a designed alien race - and humans. Together, they've formed a Vigil whose members can audit any government function. After a plague that kills many Oolom, and a somewhat wild adolescence, Faye Smallwood has married a small group of friends, and decides to join the Vigil if she can. Soon after, she and her partner get involved in investigating a complex series of crimes that change their understand of the entire planet.ReviewThis second* volume in theLeague of Peoplesseries takes a surprising turn away from Festina Ramos, hero ofExpendablein favor of Faye Smallwood, human resident of a planet shared with the Oolom, a designed alien race, and also not the original inhabitants. But not to worry, Festina turns up.I complained aboutExpendablethat non-protagonist characterization was thin, and Gardner does better here. Some supporting characters are just that, and only thinly outlined, but overall, the feeling is of a somewhat more complete cast. I do wish that Gardner had spent more time on two key actors who are identified late, and the rapid, somewhat summary ending undermines the good character work earlier on, but overall this does remain more a character than plot-driven story.There are some nifty alien goodies, but they’re plot supports, not really key elements. The interesting things here are Faye, human relationships with the Oolom, and Festina. Those make for a sufficiently satisfying story, though I do wish the Vigil had been more thoroughly explored.* I don’t really countCommitment Hour,because while it may be in the same universe, it’s otherwise not connected with the series. I’m following the ‘official’ count only because of the cover labeling.
