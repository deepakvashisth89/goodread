Well, what's not to like?- I always enjoyed reading about a women disguised as a men living among the guys and succeeding there.- Imagine fighting guerilla/partisan war having two old mischievous wizards Goblin and One Eye on your team, not to mention the usual Black Company's bag of tricks - it's just pure fun.- Mind blowing revelations about the Plain of the Glittering Stone.- Encounters with semi-gods and demons.- Dungeons'n'Dragons/ horror type adventure in Icy Caverns.And all that spiced up with G.Cook's signature cynical sarcastic dialogues. I had a blast.
