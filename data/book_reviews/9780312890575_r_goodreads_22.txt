What a great end to a great series. I thought the narrator swap would be a bad idea, but honestly, it makes the series. I love that there are several chroniclers and that they each have a different voice. It's genius really, because "fitting the lore of the company" makes a great excuse for how each narrator has a bit of Cook's voice, and each narrator having their own blind spots and preferences (from a cynic point of view) papers over continuity errors and erratic character behavior, honestly it's genius.In this book, after a long hiatus, we are back to my favorite narrator. I don't mind Sleepy, but Sleepy is a better character than chronicler.There is so much to wrap up in this book, but the way it is written, it does such a great job of tying up loose ends. Since so much of the plot is based on lore, again, Cook has the amazing literary tool of just saying "looks like those rumors were false" when he wants to go a different direction, he refuses to be painted into a corner.I'm so torn about there being more books in the series... Trying to decide if I keep reading or give it time.
