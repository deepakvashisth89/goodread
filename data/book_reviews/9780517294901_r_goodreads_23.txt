The Wizard of Oz is one of my favorite books of all time so this review is definitely biased. If you're the type who likes fairy tales or is a fan of the famous MGM film I would say to give Wizard of Oz a read as well as it's sequels. The benefit of this copy of Oz is that it has both the original story that began it all and it's "Sequel", though it actually isn't about Dorothy. More unique is the manga / anime inspired artwork which makes me say that this is the version of Oz to buy.This is a weird gripe but I didn't own any Oz books for years because I never liked the artwork. Not that they were bad but the illustrations of prior iterations were not interesting to me. This one however breaths a life into its drawings. And it's kind of surprising how well the anime like artwork fits with Oz and fairy tales in general. Overall everything is drawn very pretty and nice and clean. Expressions are clear on characters and the designs of each one is very cool. I loved Dorothy's design and it was both a homage to classic interpretations of Dorothy but also it's own thing.My recommendation is to buy this copy of Oz if you ever wish to read the Wizard of Oz books. The only shame is that the rest of the series isn't (yet) released in this same vein, but maybe some day.
