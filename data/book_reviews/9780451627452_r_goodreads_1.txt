This is my personal favorite translation of the Qur'an. Another great one (recommended by Shoghi Effendi I am to understand is the Rodwell translation. Both are very highly regarded by Muslim scholars. This translation is elegant, accessible and regarded as sensitively translated.The Qu'ran itself is a masterwork of religious genius at the very least and from my perspective constitutes one of the sources of the word of God alongside the Bible, the Bahá’í writings and perhaps the Upanishads.
