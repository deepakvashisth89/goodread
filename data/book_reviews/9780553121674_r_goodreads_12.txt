I randomly selected this off my kindle without context having downloaded it from Project Gutenberg a while ago (along with many other old books). The title lead me to believe it was a mystery novel, but I was disabused of this notion fairly quickly, which was fine. I liked the beginning and was interested to see how this boy's life would be altered by the offer of his aunt.What I didn't like was that all of the challenges that he and the other boys faced were so easily resolved without any real effort on anyone's part. Oh, this is a bad boy, and another and another, but they are all just misunderstood. We'll just love them into good behavior and will never have to actually work through any problem. Maybe the audience for this book was meant to be younger children, I don't know, but if it had treated the boy's problems with more seriousness, I think the message would have hit its mark better for me.
