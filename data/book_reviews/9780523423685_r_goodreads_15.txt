Jeff Collinson was brought to an ER after a very bad car accident. Dr. Hunt was the doc that worked on him. Unfortunately Jeff was not a normal patient and after claiming that something is coming even after he dies, Dr. Hunt sees a black snail like creature crawl out of Jeff's nose after he dies and gets loose in the hospital..all hell breaks loose and it's only the beginning.
