I love sagas: The Forsyth Saga, original Upstairs Downstairs, a Woman of Substance et.al.. This book introduces us to the Lorimers, one of the richesy familues in Bristol England. The series was originally written in the 70s and reissued as ebooks a few years ago. I got them after reading a post on a blog I follow.We meet the patriarch of the family:John Junis, and his children:Wiliam, Margaret and Ralph. However, a lot of this book is through the eyes of Margaret. It shows the beliefs and mores of Great Britan of the 1800s, and is greatly inhanced by the hstorical context, almost as if that is a character as well.Well worth tracking down
