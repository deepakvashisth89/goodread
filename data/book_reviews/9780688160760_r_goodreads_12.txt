After moving to a new town with her dad and step mom, Abby Hunter feels as though she's lost who she is. She's thirteen years old and doesn't have any friends. Abby goes out in search of a place where she can buy a new identify. After running into a shop with three older women, Abby has a moving experience where she learns some things about herself. She returns home, then runs a fever for a few days. Upon waking up, she finds out that her experiences were only a dream. As the book continues, she finds that her dream may be telling of her future, and it gives her confidence about what is to come.

I gave this book a three because it was captivating. That's an important quality for books, especially this realistic fiction for younger readers. There was also foreshadowing. This kept me predicting what might happen next, which is a useful comprehension technique. Some of the foreshadowing verged on being obvious, but I think it would be perfect for the intended audience. Lastly, there was clear character development. Abby changed from being insecure and wanting to hide to being confident and somewhat outgoing. The message it contains would be good for young teen girls.
