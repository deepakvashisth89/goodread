What a cute little book that showcases what a stumble into the craft as a teen often realistically looks like, very closely mirroring my own experience! I've known about these for years and have wanted to read them so bad!! I was finally able to get my hands on the first book and I gotta say- it was really cute! Reminds me of a lot of other books I read as a kid from around the same time, rigid cliquey school social structure, fear of deviation from the norm, the importance of friendship, and breaking down the stereotypes of said cliques. I really enjoyed this and plan on seeking out the rest of the series!!

Something I really appreciate about this book that I've just got to mention is that while it revolves around a mishap involving a love spell, meaning that the main character is romantically involved throughout the entire book, he is barely mentioned besides vague sentences like "we talked on the phone all night." Meaning that the main character's friends really fill out the socialization in the novel over and over again (aside from her lovely parents!) making what could have been an eye-roll filled chicklit-lite book into a cute story about finding your true inner power, with the help of some very unlikely friends!
