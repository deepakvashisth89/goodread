Two things about this book.First: the title was really appropriate.The heroine in this book really runs away ecah time she sees the hero, even from afar, to avoid talking to him.Which is not good, since they have been married for 10 years and their marriage has a wagon of issues.The second thing is that it should be angsty but it is anything but.And why this?Because neither the heroine or the hero are in love anymore.Yes, their love ended somewhere sometime during their marriage and my feeling is that even if in the end they declare their undying love I really didn't feel it.The heroine leaves the hero when he doesn't come home for their 10th anniversaire because he had an emergency in his job.The heroine thinks he has an affair with his PA who is always following in his business trips.She really was a neglected wife, her child was at boarding school, she doesn't work and her husband is never at home.They haven't even slept together for two years.She's always alone.This book is her journey to emancipation, not a love story.-She goes to London, alone.-she goes to her husband's company and asks his partner a job (and of course she gets it)-she starts living her life, going to parties, organizing her free time-she finds out she has one third of her husband's company and she starts acting like a partnerHer husband is a peripheral presence in all this.And an unpleasant one.Really, I couldn't find anything interesting about him.The man is a complete as****e.-He knows his PA has a crush on him and still he keeps her : this is very disrespectful to his wife.-He dates his PA, even if he tells his wife it's only business. yeah, right.- His first reaction when his wife left him was being angry and offended. Not worried or hurt, but angry. He didn't tell her he wanted her back, he was only worried that the people working for him would laugh at him or that his partner would be interested in her. Maybe because if she married his partner he would have been the minor partner???-His explanation because he was never home, that is because she wasn't able to get pregnant and he was afraid to be impotent, was ludicruous. Two years celibate??? yeah, right again.-Apparently he didn't have an affair with his PA. This was clear. But he never says he was faithful.-The only angsty scene is when the heroine finds him kissing his PA. Oh, and he says she was kissing him. He was obviously struggling to avoid her, obviously.-I don't know why she loved him, but actually this didn't bother me because i don't think she was in love with him anymore. I think that she was happier without him.Whatever.The book is good, it's fast paced and never boring. If you are looking for a good book without a great love story in it, this could be for you.
