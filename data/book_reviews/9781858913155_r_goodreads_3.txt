This tome is a collection of antiquated, therefore royalty-free horror stories. There are a few that truly chill to the bone. The writing style is as you might imagine from stories written 200 or so years ago.The editor of this work tells of working at a bookstore when young and, during downtime, reading some scary stories that really stuck with him. Presumably, he's found them and managed to collect them all into this book. Many of the stories are forgettable. However, there are a few moments to be found that do stick. Is it worth a read? Yes. If you are a true fan of horror.
