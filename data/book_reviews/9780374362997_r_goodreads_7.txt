I don't know how many times I've read this book. Or parts of this book. Incalculable. This was my heart book in my teen years. I wanted to be Vicky. As an adult, I've wanted to be her mother, Victoria. Or even Caro who is both incidental and integral to the story. There's always a new something. Every time. And it's always beautiful.It will always be an emotional read for me, I can't quite make it an intellectual one. Pretty sure I don't want to.Life death, creation, family, joy, time, whole-ness, hope. Mediation, prayer, self-forgetfulness. All of it. Love.The baby swallows flew.
