Well, it's been too long since I read this to really give it a proper review. I read it for an LDS book club. The discussion we had on it was deeper than what I had prepared for. I felt like the book was just a whimsical story, but I realized (at the book club) some people saw the deeper side.For the record, I don't agree with the character of God, at all. Some of the women had a good take on his "breaking the rules." But I just don't buy into the idea that God has enough time on His hands to play mind games with us in the afterlife.
