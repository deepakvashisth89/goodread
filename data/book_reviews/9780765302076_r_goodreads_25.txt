Note the book is called AFTER The King. Not MORE of the KIng.This eclectic book spans a range of different kinds of fantasy, and a SF story or two.Read as stories about fantastical settings and events, it is a good collection.If you want more of the Tolkien world, and beings therein, go elsewhere, back to Tolkein himself perhaps.Personally I am not a fan of Tolkien and this collection suited me just fine.
