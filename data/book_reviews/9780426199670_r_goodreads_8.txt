Before home video’s the only way to enjoy previous Doctor Who stories was the Target novelisations.So during my rewatch I thought it would be a great way to enjoy the missing stories.The first in chronological order was this one.The TARDIS crew arrive at the Himalayas in 1289, they are picked up by Marco Polo whose traveling to meet the Kublai Kahn.Lucarotti does a great job of condensing down he’s seven episode story into the standard size Target novel.It’s a fun fast paced adventure that faithful to the original story.
