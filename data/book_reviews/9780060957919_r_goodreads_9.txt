This book has some interesting accounts of what it’s like to be a CIA agent. I thought of one of my favorite series of books, the ones about Mrs. Pollifax. The reality is very different from the factionalized version.

A lot of this was challenging to understand. Like the military, a lot of acronyms are used, and there was a lot of information packed into each sentence, paragraph, and story. It was good, but not always easy to follow.

I remember reading Argo some years ago, and this gave me a more complete picture of how that event occurred. Not that it went into more detail, but that the preparation for that event was in the making for a lifetime before it occurred.
