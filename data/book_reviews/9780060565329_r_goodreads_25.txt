I recently read Kick Kennedy: The Charmed Life and Tragic Death of the Favorite Kennedy Daughter and it mentioned the Mitford sisters and how the sisters had very divergent political views from each other. Diana Mosley and her sister, Unity, met Adolf Hitler and supported the Nazi strategies.

Diana Mosley: Mitford Beauty, British Fascist, Hitler's Angel is an interesting book. Mosely married Bryan Guinness, the brewery heir, at age 18. After having two children with Guinness, she left him for Sir Oswald Mosley, a philanderer beyond belief. Mosley was known as "the Leader" in the UK because he created the fascist movement.

I was shocked at the large amount of infidelity by men and women; many of their spouses knew of their indiscretions. Perhaps it was because divorce was scandalous or more likely, it was the character of the people involved. Remorse, morality, self-reflection, and accountability are not present in the people in this book.
