I came to this book not as a ballet fan but as a challenge to myself. I started the book with some trepidation and occasionally wondered where some of the more difficult passages were going, but I grew to understand the book and eventually found it had become a solid companion, and I was sad to find it ending.
You get A LOT of biography for your buck with this book E.g. there is a great deal of history on Dame Margot Fonteyn and other dance icons.
Read it if you can.
