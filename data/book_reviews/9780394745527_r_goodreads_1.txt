Because I like the Van der Valk series, I thought I would try one of the Castang books by the same author. I did enjoy the overall murder mystery, but at some points the story was choppy for me with a lot of repetitive bits of misunderstandings and/or "thinking out loud" riffs. Good resolution, though, hard fought as Castang sticks with it to the end. French/English conflicts amusing.Kindle Unlimited
