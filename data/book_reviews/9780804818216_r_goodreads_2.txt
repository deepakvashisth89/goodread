Helena Paterson is an experienced astrologer and this fact shines through every page of this carefully researched work. The main purpose of the book is to enable the reader to find their moon sign and to read a little of what it stands for. All symbolism is detailed and explained clearly and intelligently. It isn't necessary to have any knowledge of astrology to use the book.
In this book, the lunar cycle has been associated with the Celtic tree calendar, which is a reconstruction of an ancient tradition. For each month a tree has been allocated and further associated with a planet, gemstone, flower, archetypal character and a statement. Every tree month has an original and very beautiful illustration by Margaret Walty and portrays connections and associations in a way that brings the text to life. In addition, the text pages are bordered by yet more beauty that relate directly to the month. The text is itself well written, comprehensive and fascinating.
If you're tired of the same old boring astrology books, Helena Paterson puts a new spin on things with her Celtic astrology resources.
Excellent resource.
