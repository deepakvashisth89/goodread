"Two young adventurers for hire. Willing to do anything, go anywhere. Pay must be good. No unreasonable offer refused."It is after the war and our two main characters, Miss Prudence Cowley (better known as Tuppence) and Lieutenant Thomas Beresford (our beloved Tommy) are strapped for cash. The war has made jobs difficult to get and their resources are slowly dwindling. Tommy and Tuppence, reunited after being childhood friends, decide to start their own organisation: The Young Adventurers.And by sheer luck, being at the right place at the right time and lack of any sense of self-preservation, our main characters find themselves investigating a case of international espionage. They are asked to search for Jane Finn, a survivor of RMS Lusitania's sinking who disappeared along with documents of a secret treaty that may lead to another war.This was such a fun read. Amazing pacing and loveable characters. Tommy and Tuppence had amazing chemistry and their banter was top-tier. Sadly, I solved the identity of the mysterious Mr Brown very quickly and quite easily. Maybe I've read enough of Christie to point out her clues and red herrings.Still, never thought I'd love one of her spy books yet here we are.
