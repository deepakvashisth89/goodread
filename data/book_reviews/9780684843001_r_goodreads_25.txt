What an amazing book, I can’t believe it exists. Read this one day. It’s inspiring beyond belief and a story of staggering devotion. Anybody looking for a book on the “craft of writing” … believe it or not, this book is top dog for that. You got questions on how to make art? The answer is in here. Theo and Vincent were brothers so closely bound they died not far apart. The death of the older sent the younger to his own grave. “Theo ‘was the kind of man who saved even the smallest scrap of paper’, and it is to this trait that the public owes the 663 letters from Vincent.”
