What else can you give Henry iv part 1 than 5 stars. My favourite Shakespeare containing my two favourite characters Falstaff and Prince Hal. Of course this only part of Henriad which are my favourite plays. Prince Hal’s and Falstaff relationship through these plays is witty, intelligent and human. In the end it’s heart breaking but we understand- that’s the glory of Shakespeare and the growth of Prince Hal. This character growth is amazing but of course there are three plays to do that over. Henry IV part 1 contains the most humour and Henry IV part 2 the most touching moments. It’s the humanity that I love. So yes 5 stars. And definitely one of my favourite Shakespeare plays.
