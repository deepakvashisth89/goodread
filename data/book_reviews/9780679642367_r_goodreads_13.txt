The Mysterious Island is the final book in Verne's Captains Grant and Nemo trilogy, and is comprised of three sections itself, Dropped from the Clouds, The Abandoned, and The Secret of the Island. It seems a bit more philosophically based (or perhaps less of an action/adventure nature) than the better-known 20,000 Leagues Under the Sea, but it's still a fine read. There are many different translations available; I believe I first read an abridged Scholastic version. Five men escape from a Civil War prison in a balloon and end up on a strange Pacific island which also houses a mysterious man in a cave. It's classic literature and good adventure.
