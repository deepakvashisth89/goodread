I read this book way back when, not long after it was published. I first heard of it in People Magazine, in an article that asked celebrities what they were reading now. I don't remember which male actor was reading this, but it sounded neat, so I got it from the library. I remember it being science fiction like I'd never read before, with implications of a transcendent power safeguarding its secrets while guiding us along.The fact is, there is a lot I don't remember about the story. Therefore I'm putting it back on my "want to read" shelf. I'm hoping it lives up to my memory.
