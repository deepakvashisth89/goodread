Very satisfying mystery I got it almost correct. The motive was staring me in the face. However, PD James teases us with a few red herrings. So who did it? A doctor, nurse, secretary, janitor? The list of suspects are strong with each having a motive.Like Dalgliesch I tried to make it more complicated than it was. A psychiatric clinic with an administrator who cannot empathize and is disliked by everyone including her cousin who works there. Nagle a painter working there as a porter, his mistress a secretary called Priddy, Doctor Steiner a little bit neurotic and many more in a clinic treating mentally ill people. In the end always look at motive especially with money involved.The closed room mystery was explained and so simple when put in context.What is so good with this story are the clues and of course working out who has the strongest motive. An enjoyable read.
