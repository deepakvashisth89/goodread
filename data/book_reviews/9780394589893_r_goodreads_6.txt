Commander Count Carl Hamilton serves in an intelligence division of the Swedish navy. After participating in a very secret mission against a Russian incursion into Swedish waters, he is one of three people named as a Russian spy. The consequences of this accusation and the subsequent investigation are the basis of the plot, which is really a series of exploits that are almost parodies of those of James Bond. In fact, there are explicit references to Carl being 'Sweden's James Bond' with a tone of mockery. The real interest here is the description of the relationships between Sweden and other countries (Britain, the US, Russia) and organizations (the PLO) and the interplay of representatives of the military, the government, and the press.
