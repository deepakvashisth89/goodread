Published in 1977, 'B-17 Fortress at War' is one of many in the 'at War' series of aviation books from Ian Allan, and in my opinion one of the best in the series. The personal accounts of war in this famous USAAF aircraft are well supported by over 250 photos. Some of them are well known pics, but have the advantage of informative captions not often seen. The writer, Roger A Freeman, has written many aviation books about the USAAF air war over WW2 Europe, so not surprisingly much of the emphasis is on this area because he does it so well. An exciting read from start to finish.
