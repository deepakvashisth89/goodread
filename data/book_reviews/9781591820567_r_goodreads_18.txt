I love Rei and Kira—constantly crying! This volume is so soft and gentle. The art is sparse but beautiful. The details that are shown—how they hold hands or embrace, the facial expression—are so tender.Where are modern manga like this? Re-reading works like Mars and Black Jack (and I know those were thirty years apart), the art, the pacing, everything feels so deliberate. Even when things get goofy. It’s a strong contrast to a lot of modern manga, which often feel loose and rushed. Even sloppy. Nothing wrong with that, but… makes coming back to classics feel like a treat to be savored.Art: ★★★★★Plot: ★★★★★Characters: ★★★★★
