A very good book on fearing God. I had read John Bevere's book, and was looking for another one to keep me focused on the fear of the Lord and found this on my neighborhood digital library.It has several good chapters on what's behind or foundational to fearing the Lord. It covers sin, repentance, holiness and having a clean heart before God.The author quotes many scriptures and uses them well. Her stories definitely support her points and show how good God has been in using her to minister to others. She has an amazing list of the benefits of fearing God in chapter 18. Each one is backed by one scripture. I wish she had put more time into these scriptures and others that show the rewards for fearing God.I was surprised though at how much the author talked about sin, gave examples and scriptures, but never defined what sin is. She has a good list of sins on pages 187-189 of the digital copy. However, she never stated what sin is. Sin is a heart that transgresses or breaks God's laws (1 John 3:4), or chooses to go our own way (Isaiah 66:3, Isaiah 53:6), or turns aside from God (Psalm 53:3), or knows good and doesn't do it (James 4:17).For me, it's been important to face that sin is having a heart that chooses evil things over the ways and things of God (Jeremiah 18:12). I think it's important to know what's going on with our hearts, rather than just listing sins and scriptures. Daniel 12 talks about our hearts being corrupted. In Psalm 51, King David says that as well. We're not good people who do bad things. We're corrupted people who need a merciful God.I did appreciate the author's encouragement to wait on God for conviction of sin, cleansing and release. I hadn't thought about pride being the reason behind not waiting on God, not praying, and so many of the sins that the author listed in chapter 8.I think this author is evangelistic. She stated the truth and used God's word to support her points. However, I think there's a bias here. It's good to encourage people to do the right thing according to scriptures. However, I think she's correct but understated in how important it is for people to be right with God and in good relationship with Him before they consider ministering to others, evangelizing, or doing things for God. Without the fear of God, we could do things without God. Our Lord did what He saw the Father doing (John 5:19).Jesus prepared his whole life before God released Him into His main ministry. He waited on God for timing, like he said to His mother at the wedding. David was a man after God's own heart because he did what God asked (Acts 13:22). He feared God but submitted and aligned himself with God before acting and doing. We need to fear God, get clean and aligned before doing things for God.I think this book is good and worth reading, but for me, it wasn't as clear and convicting as Bevere's book. In his 1999 book, Bevere covers the Fear of the Lord systematically like a Bible study about what the fear of God is. This book seems like a long conversation. It's like a series of personal testimonies or collection of lessons God gave the author. Helpful and inspirational, but not easy to reference or remember or apply.
