If you don't like historical don't read! He does rape her and she does have to be a slave to him. So if those are things you hate don't read this.

He was seriously messed up and so was she. She wanted to trust and love but was so scared to be the loser again that she closed herself off. The whole growing poppies was a great angle.
My fav scene was when he told the evil cousin he was going to kill him because of the pain he placed in the heroines eyes. Omg I loved it.
