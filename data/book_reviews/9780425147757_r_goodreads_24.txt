It isn’t Bond, but it isn’t bad.Gardner’s Bond is unrecognisable in all but name from Fleming’s. He drives a Saab and is in a stable relationship with a woman. Certain things that don’t chime with the original novels. However if you can put those issues aside we have a reasonable tale of espionage.Middle of the road fare which I probably wouldn’t have read had it not been a Bond continuation novel.
