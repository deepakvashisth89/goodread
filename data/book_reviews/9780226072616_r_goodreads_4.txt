Breines delivers an insightful look into the problems faced by young white women in the 50s that led to their eventual participation in the women's movement of the 60s and 70s. While I did think some of her writing could have been a bit...smoother? I found some phrases repeated a lot and she did not introduce the authors she quotes enough, leaving me lost as to the context sometimes. However, this book gave me many things to think about regarding what prompted the women's Lib movement. Definitely something I would recommend to anyone studying that period of American women's history.
