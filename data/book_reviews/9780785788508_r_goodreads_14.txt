Wow, this was a blast from the past. I just discovered that this book was published in 1975. The writing style felt a bit new to me because it actually sounded old and vintage—but I like a classic once in a while.

I liked Nancy. She was a strong woman with a terrible past, someone trying to start over and live for herself. But, well, life doesn’t always go according to plan, and her nightmares are back with a vengeance.

The twists were a bit obvious from the start, but that didn’t take away from the tension when they were revealed halfway through. You would absolutely hate the villain in this tragedy.

Trigger warnings: kidnapping, pedophilia, mental abuse.

Overall, this is an awesome read.
