This was a pretty good story, though it did get a bit tedious at times, to hear the H, Roderick, go on and on about how horrible the h's (Madrigal) father is, who supposedly murdered his father when Roderick was a boy. By order of the king - and his own machinations - he marries her, as a means of getting revenge on her father. Naturally, his marriage doesn't get off to a good start, especially when Madrigal suspects Roderick of preferring her sister Elena's bed to hers. They have a love/hate relationship (though neither will admit to the love part) for quite some time, then Madrigal is kidnapped by Akbar el Kemil (late 70's/early 80's HR books have a thing for the h getting kidnapped to a harem, but at least in this one there was no rape or forced seduction/lust fest), who treats her with respect, though Roderick is all too quick to assume they were lovers. Because of this, he has several affairs and doesn't feel guilty. Meanwhile, Madrigal was ready to succumb to Akbar, because she thought Roderick had slept with Elena. Akbar didn't want her unless she could be sure Roderick was out of her mind and heart, so Roderick's assumptions were wrong.

It's some time before all the misunderstandings are cleared, the accusations stop, and the real feelings are admitted. It's a pretty good story, but it kind of dragged in spots.
