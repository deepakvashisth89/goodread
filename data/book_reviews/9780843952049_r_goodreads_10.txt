Actual rating: 3.5 stars
Well, color me blood-red, red as the blood that splatters this book-Edward Lee CAN write a horror novel without oceans of needless sex! Sure, he does throw a bit in, but much tamer than usual. Bottom line-this is the first of his that I really enjoyed.
Plot-postal workers go postal with knives and guns, due to demonic influence. There is a talisman of the demon, that must be found to stop him. The end.
Hey, do you need a complex plot? Not really. This was good fun. First of all, if you like the gore aspect, Lee throws in a good amount. Also, I actually LIKED the protagonist this time, which was a first. Usually, all his characters are scumbags that you want to get whacked. One of the highlights is the hilarity that Lee throws in at times. The one that had me rolling around laughing was the animated dead guy about to rape a chick, and his dick drops off. This one actually had a few twists and turns in the plot-nothing major that you can't figure out, but it helped keep the story moving. This was a good, fast paced read that keeps you going to find out what happens. Overall, well done.
