That the stories are brilliant, I have no doubt. However, I've read just one: Ethan Brand. I came across it while reading an essay on Ethan Frome which said that EB foreshadows EF. So I, rather sadly and unknowingly, had already read some spoilers. I plodded through the story nevertheless, charmed neither by its wordiness, long sentences, bizarrely dark atmosphere nor the obscure plot. I was watching out for signs of resemblance to Ethan Frome; found very few, and at the end I realised I knew the climax all along; and the whole chilling effect was lost on me. God I hate people who give out spoilers without a warning.
