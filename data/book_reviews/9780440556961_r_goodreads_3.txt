Essays that were originally written for Outside magazine by the author - kind of the non-fiction equivalent of short stories. Although I'm not a fan of short stories, I mostly enjoyed this collection. However, the essays added for the updated edition are much lengthier and honestly less interesting. I wanted to read this because I loved David Quammen'sSpillover: Animal Infections and the Next Human Pandemic, but would only rate this 3.5 stars (rounded down).
