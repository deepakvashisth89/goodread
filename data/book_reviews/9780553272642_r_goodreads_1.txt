Renno, along with his brothers and a sister by marriage are going to North Carolina to visit with Renno's wife. She owns a shipping company and has been taking care of her ailing father. Beth's brother in law has been kidnapped and taken to Jamaica and Renno is going with a ransom to get him back.

This is an ongoing series, but this was my first. I was a little lost in the beginning but caught up and started enjoying the tale. It's quite adventuresome and a lot of action and Renno and his brothers run afoul of some evil people. I will continue with the series from here.
