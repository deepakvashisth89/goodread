One time I decided that I wanted to read the autobiographies of everyone who was on the A-Team. This turned out to be impossible, since Hannibal and Murdock and Amy never wrote books. But Face did! And it was actually pretty good.I would not recommend searching out this book, but if it's sitting right in front of you, go ahead and picked it up.This book was the tipping point to make me a vegetarian. Srsly. There's a LOT of stuff about vegetarianism in here and for some reason it stuck with me. I know it's silly but it's true.
