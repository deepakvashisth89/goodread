This wasn't much of a mystery. Mostly Hemingway and all of his friends drinking tremendous amounts while a serial rapist occasionally murders an artists model. When one of their circle is murdered, the hero, a reporter from Toronto believes that someone has murdered her to match the other murders currently occurring in the Quarter. Set in 1925 Paris. The book does offer a possible solution (although weak, I thought) to what happened to Hem's lost suitcase.
