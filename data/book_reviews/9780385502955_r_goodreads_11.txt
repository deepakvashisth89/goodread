Poetic, rather gentle book. Met the author at the Oxford Conference for the Book and heard him read an excerpt. I identified with the protagonist, having grown up in Alabama and tried unsuccessfully to fit into a certain role. The author has a poet's eye for the unusual in life, which made the story beautiful. The ending was surprising, though a bit trite. Learned more about Harlem.
