I thoroughly enjoyed the Harry Potter books, so when I saw this for a $1 at a local bookstore, I had to buy it.For an unauthorized biography, there seems to be a lot of insider information on how Rowling felt, what she thought, etc. As the sources for the book are interviews, writeups, news and other sources, I think there is enough info to believe that the book is at least mostly true.The book was easy to read and I was able to go through it quicker than most books.Overall, I thought it was a nice way to encapsulate all the books in the series and feel like you've gotten to know Joanne Kathleen Rowling better than you would from other sources. If you liked the Harry Potter books and want an inside look at the author, I think this would be a good source.
