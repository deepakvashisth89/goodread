Nellie Hand was everyone's friend. Although she has a job, she runs errands and looks after her friends. They all tell her things knowing that they will remain private, secret. In a late night phone call from Matthew he admits that he murdered someone long ago. Nellie is in two minds about talking to him again. Her decision is made when he turns up on her doorstep. Later that night Nellie is murdered in her flat. Several of her friends had a motive for killing her but was it one of them or a random attack.
A relatively simple whodunnit but with lots of twists. An easy read.
