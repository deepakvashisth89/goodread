3.5 stars. Fun and quick read, and surprisingly scary (I don't scare easily!). A bunch of these stories were already familiar to me, but a few were new, and pretty creepy for stories geared toward children. Actually, I think these are probably a bit too scary for middle school-aged kids and may be more suitable for young adults. "Terror Trip" and "Trick or Treat" were stand-outs for me. I'd definitely check out the other two books in this series for a short, fun summer read.
