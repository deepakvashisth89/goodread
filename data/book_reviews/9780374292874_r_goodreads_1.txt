I read Pekarkova's novel "Truckstop Rainbows" back when I was in college--before Goodreads even!--and really enjoyed it. A while back I was in a used bookstore looking to use some store credit and ran across this. It's been sitting on my shelf for a while but I finally picked it up this month.

I enjoyed this book, although not quite as much as I remember liking TSR (though, that was almost 20 years ago!). Both have enjoyably "earthy" protagonists, but I think TWIR has even more sex in it. Pekarkova builds a nice ensemble cast of loser Slavs and there are a few sections of vintage palavering. There was one scene that I kind of hastily skimmed over due to the content, but I don't think it ever got too graphic and it wasn't enough to make me put the book down entirely (unlike "Oath of Gold," recently).

I still really want to read her autofiction book "Gimme the Money" about being a cabbie in NYC.
