Not realizing I bought an older edition until I actually got into it, I wasn’t entirely sure what to expect, but was pleasantly surprised. The parenting books I have read so far have been pretty basic in what they cover, and I am excited to get more into specifics now that the baby is here, but despite being just slightly out of date (not nearly as much as I thought) the depth and wide span of topics in this book was pretty cool. I probably wouldn’t read it straight through if I had to do it again, but this is a pretty good resource to just have on the shelf as a new parent, it covers just about every topic you could think of, and while not going into an extreme amount of detail, it gives a springboard to get you started, providing enough comfort to keep anxiety at a minimum while encouraging you to get more information. It doesn’t read like an encyclopedia either, which you might expect from a book in this format. Lots of humor and additional tips. Overall, not a bad one to have on the shelf for coverage on different topics.
