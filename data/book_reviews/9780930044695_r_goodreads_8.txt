This book definitely is not the serene, pleasantly building romantic tension of Forrest’s most iconic novel, “Curious Wine”. Instead, “An Emergence of Green” hurts, often. It’s angering, mostly because of the power Paul Blake wields over Carolyn Blake, his wife, as well as Val Hunter, a character he meets only once before hating her and isn’t related to in anyway. His power is wielded carefully throughout the story, until it bursts like the reader can painfully anticipate the entire time. The relationship between Val & Carolyn is lovely and ultimately prevailing, but the constant theme weaving through the story is the struggle of Paul’s inner struggles and how he uses them to justify his abhorrent power.
