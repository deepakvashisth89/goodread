I really enjoyed this phenomenal & short venture in the history of the Christian faith. I got through it in only a couple days and for what it is - it's as good as it can be. Kung, from the unique perspective of a Catholic Priest banned from teaching Catholic theology, walks us through 2000 years of church history in only 200+ pages. He covers a vast amount of the major events of the Church from Jesus' death to modern day including the Roman Empire, the Schism, Middle Ages, Protestant Revolution, Romanticism, the Enlightenment, Modernity & more.

He is certainly not writing this from a neutral perspective; it could alternatively be called The Catholic Church: A Long Criticism. From someone like Kung who still loves and chooses to hope for the future of the Church in the world, I admire his honesty, perspective, self critique, and realism that is often missing from religious types. He is easy to follow although, coming from someone who is not Catholic, I had to do a lot of cross referencing which was part of the learning fun. I discovered a lot, not just about church history but the way it has constantly been interwoven in with world events, philosophies, and other religious thought.

If your looking for an intro to Christian History (from the Western/Catholic perspective), this is a great place to start. I look forward to reading more of Kung's (often controversial) work.
