Probably the most boring book I have ever read. If it hadn't been for the fact that I read and enjoyed the first four books of the series, I would never have bothered to finish it. It was almost 150 pages into the book before the main murder took place and the constant insertion of letters from Lydia to her mother throughout the book just distracted from the plot. I will try one more book in the series and hopefully it will be back on track.
