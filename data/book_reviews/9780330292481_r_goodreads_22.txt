A mystery novel " The Third Eye" by Lois Duncan is about a eighteen year old girl, a babysitter, who has these feelings when Bobby, the person she's taking care of, goes missing. The feelings which Karen has seemed so true and real to her that which she felt like she was with him. Not even but she could even smell the place where Bobby was at. When she found out she told the police and they found Bobby and since that case the police came to Karen for a favor to find another missing girl. The police knew that Karen was a psychic. But Karen didn't believe him but the officer met people who were like Karen. He has a friend too but she's working on a more difficult case involving many children that's being kidnapped. So he came to Karen for help. After seeing Carla Sanchez, the girl who's been missing and Karen's trying to find, Karen could feel and has nightmares of the girl drowning and could feel it too. After finding Carla Sanchez people starts to believe that Karen is a psychic. She keeps on receiving calls and letters on finding lost children.
