I read this when I was 9 or 10. Loved it for the daring adventure, the evocation of hazy summer meadows and dark star-filled nights. Charley was so brave to me, doing things that I would have loved to do but would have been too scared to do. Every time I encounter grease-proof paper or a piece of sweaty cheese I am reminded of this book. I think even today's sophisticated children would be fascinated by Charley's escapade!Beautifully written.
