a perfect dramatization of the physicality/ objecthood of language."Ever since I can speak I can stand up in an orderly manner; but falling only hurts ever since I can speak; but the pain when I fall is twice as bad ever since I know that one can speak about my falling; but falling doesn’t hurt at all any more ever since I know that I can forget the pain; but the pain never stops at all any more ever since I know that I can feel ashamed of falling."
