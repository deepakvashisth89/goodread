This is a wonderful book on intuition, which is quite different than other books on this topic I have read. The author, Linda Keen, explains how she developed her intuition and in the second part of the book gives practical guidance on how the reader can develop their intuition and perform readings and healings on themselves and others. I was happy to learn that the rose flower, which I love, is central to intuition in the author's teachings. She advises to visualize people as roses and that every detail about the rose has significance. The author indicates that visualization is important. She teaches the reader about chakras, auras and colors and how to read another person's aura.I highly recommend this book for people interested to learn more about intuition and develop spiritually.
