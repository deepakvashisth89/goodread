Finally Merlyn appears in Book 5, The Once and Future King. When King Author was against Mordred at final battle, Camlan, he saw Merlyn at his battalion, in his dream, he set up to with Merlyn who took him to the conference that had Author's childhood companions. And again, King Author became Merlyn's pupil. Learning war, population, communist etc.. Also, Author was transformed into ant and goose to ruminate diverse Philosophies from animals and insects. (This part reminds me of The Sword in the Stone, Young Author was learning knowledge by being turned into animals! It seems that I was back to not just King Author's childhood, also mine.) Especially, Author was transformed into a goose, he experienced love again in his life. He learn how gooses migrate from one place to another, the leadership of gooses, what properties mean to goose. I really praise how T.H White portrayed each scenes from Author was being a goose, traversing upon the vista of plateaus from sky , gooses rest at margin of marsh, and gooses perch on pinnacle of edifices. The reading experience of those portrayed scenes are like watching TV documentaries, that are transmitted images into my brain. T.H White has sublime and visualized writing skill to depict ecosystem of animals.
This book isn't about Merlyn saving Author's kingdoms. In the end, Author tried to negotiate a truce in order to bring peace to the British people however, a unknown wizard or sorcery sabotaged the truce and lead the story to the end. on the context just a few sentences about the man who devastated the truce but doesn't have any clue about the man. Still Author's death is still a myth in The Book Of Merlyn as in other books about king Author's legend.
In summary, King Author is one of legendary heroes in human's histories and I'm rejoiced to read T.H White wrote a excellent version of King Author's story as final installment of The Once and Future King series.
