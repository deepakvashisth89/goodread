This book gets the job doneMy edition promised over 10,000 names and while I didn't count them, that seems about right (many of them are alternate versions of the main, listed names). Along with each name is a brief ( one line) description of origin and/or meaning. The book offers no guarantee of how the kid will turn out, but it surely helps with the task outlined in the title.As for those authors, real or imaginary, who reviewed this book in order to tell us they only use it to name characters, never for anything as mundane as naming a baby; Get a life.
