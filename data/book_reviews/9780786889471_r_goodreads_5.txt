One of the four Moron Gospels of The Holy Dumbshit. Don’t get me wrong, Dave’s no dumbshit, he just talks a lot of dumb shit, ramalama, rock-and-roll-as-Delphi nonsense. It’s glorious. Pot, kung fu, mountain climbing in the Himalayas, Judaism, and growing up in the Cafe Wha? (owned by his uncle) can, and often do, rub tits on the same page if not sentence. Dave’s got the attention span of a toddler on meth and is a fount for more shit than dysentery (also a topic in the book).
