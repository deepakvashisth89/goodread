Multidimensional, flawed and fairly realistic characters in a spooky coming-of-age contemporary fantasy story. This standalone book was something I read many many years ago after having seen an adaptation of it in the British series "Into the Labyrinth", if I am remembering correctly. There were several stories that stuck with me from that show and I am starting to try to track them down and re-read them.

Cassandra is just turning 13 and dealing with the standard anxieties of a new teenager as well as trying to rebel against her domineering mother's ideas of what she should do and be. The story is a little spooky but not over the top, and keeps you guessing as to the motivations of a mysterious stranger who might or might not be an evil spirit.
