All I can say is that this book never failed to put a smile on my face—despite the unwholesome violence at times! I think it's her language; one of those that really gets me. Likereallygets me!The book tells the story of Jasmine, now twenty-four living in Elsa County Iowa—how she grew up in a mud house in Hasnapur, India; her initiatory and berserk encounter with an astrologer at seven; her confrontation with a rabid dog; her finding a husband and falling in love with him; the gruesome deaths of her husband (before her own eyes in a terrorist attack!) and father (gored by a bull); how she fled to the United States as an illegal immigrant after being widowed at fifteen; her numerous relationships with men and the accompanying changes in her personality; a gruesome, painful and extremely sad rape scene with a Half-Face; her murdering this Half-Face; her falling in love with Taylor; her escape, again; her relationship with Bud and his maiming; her escape from Bud with Taylor....It's a long freaking roller coaster full of surprises!
