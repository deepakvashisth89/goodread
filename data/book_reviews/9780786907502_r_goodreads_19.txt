I'm really mixed on my impressions with this book. I truly enjoyed 2/3 of this book, everything from Drizzt & Catti-Brie sailing on the Sea Sprite to meeting Cadderly. It was the last 1/3 with the return of "the missing character" that bothered me the most. Originally when I read this book in 1996 I was excited that Salvatore decided to bring back this character, but maybe I've spent too many years reading comics & unless your name is Uncle Ben or Thomas/Martha Wayne no one stays dead in comics. . . or in fantasy novels.
