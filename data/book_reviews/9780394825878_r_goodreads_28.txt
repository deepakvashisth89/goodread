The title is a little bit misleading. 1939 Edinburgh. Marjorie Malcolm-Scott lives a sheltered life in her uncle's mansion with his housekeeper. Lonely and shy she envies the orphanage children she sees playing together in the park. One of them, Shona, befriends her. But then Marjorie is to be sent to cousins in Canada. In a chance meeting with Shona in the trains station the two make a plan to change places thus changing Marjorie's life forever, in ways she could never imagine. But what will happen when the war ends and the real Shona returns?
