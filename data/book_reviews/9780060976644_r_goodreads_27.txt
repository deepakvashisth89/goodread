Rereading Children of Violence after many years. The last two volumes (Ripple from the Storm and Landlocked) much more engaging to me than the previous volume for reasons I don't understand. But I'll really miss Martha now that she's "gone." Though I know I can follow her to London in Lessing's later books. I feel like I've really gotten to know these people/characters and grieve for them in their - inevitable - misfortune. The characters are so well-developed and real to me. And the exploration of the political times is fascinating.
