I’m hesitant to give this five stars which means I recommend it to readers and non-readers alike because it is among the most intense things I have ever read (and I regularly read memoirs and non-fictions on addiction, abuse, cults, and death).But it is among the best things I have ever read.I picked it up from my friend’s collection while I was visiting in Brooklyn. Jake taught me about writing in books. At first I was aghast, it sounded so sacrilege. Now I love the idea of books I’m currently reading being a diary. He read this when we were in college in 2010. It’s the best to read along and see his thoughts.Because of its intensity, it took me a while to get through. I’ve got cocktail napkins from bars in Brooklyn (and a bagel order ofc) and Chicago enfolded with thoughts. I was reading this when a lil crush walked into a bar and had him sign it. Write in your books, people! Despite the intense nature of the book, it inspired some good journaling from me.Anyway, this author wrote the book that inspired Precious. Intense TW abound, but if any of the description of the book jumps out at you, I recommend.
