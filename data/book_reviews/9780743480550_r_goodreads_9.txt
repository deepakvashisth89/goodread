This is a good book I love the cross over from CSI Vegas to Miami Horatio Caine and his crack team are asked to detain a man for the Las Vegas CSI team what they don't know is when they go to a death, the man they are supposed to detain is gone and likely also dead. As more bodies crop up and things heat up Caine and his team chase down the clues hoping they will be able to stop a killer before he or she can make a Florida Getaway.
