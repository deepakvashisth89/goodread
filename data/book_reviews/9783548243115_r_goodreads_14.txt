I enjoy books of this genre in general. This one struck me as plenty exciting but a little too forced, as if the author was trying to give Bolitho a bunch of experiences in a hurry. At times, it seemed rather Perils-of-Pauline-ish.

This book was written after a number of others which were set later in time, so it was a case of filling in some of the backstory. I take it that Stockdale is a continuing character; this is the book where he is introduced. It is also one in which he pulls off one heroic feat after another, which beggars belief somewhat.

It's a good book, but it's not the best of this type of genre I've read.
