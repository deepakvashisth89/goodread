Peanuts and popcorn went flying every which way."Clara is so bored that she stumbles upon a circus, so her new goal to cure boredom is to join the circus. As things go from bad to worse, will she find a way to fit into the circus? Or will Clara be bored forever?This was an okay story. Parts were funny and parts were iffy.
