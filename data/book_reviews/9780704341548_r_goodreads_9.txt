The book I read had a different - and more arresting - cover than the one shown here. It had a strong woman's face, not smiling, looking directly at the viewer, with a wild-looking horse running in the background. Much stronger, more challenging and active than the cover shown here. I looked back at it many times.This is two novels in one volume: the first a dystopian male-dominated society in which women are slaves and breeders, the second a matriarchal society - or rather two co-existing communities of women - one of which consists of escapees of the patriarchal society.The details of these societies are well imagined and detailed, and draw the reader in. I preferred reading about the matriarchal societies - they were fairer and more compassionate.
