This book makes me want to petition Goodreads for the ability to leave a zero star review. If I didn’t read this as a book club book, there’s no way I would have gotten all the way through. I guess I’ll give it the one star for the apropos title... as I felt the sweet,sweet feeling of mercy when I read the last page and was able to fling this book out of my sight forever. For those debating whether to read or not: no, for the love of God, no. Save yourself.
