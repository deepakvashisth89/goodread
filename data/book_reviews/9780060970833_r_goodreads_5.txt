Just one of the best to do it (imo)

“In the month of May when all leaves open,
I see when I walk how well all things
lean on each other, how the bees work,
the fish make their living the first day.
Monarchs fly high; then I understand
I love you with what in me is unfinished...”

This has been my subway read for a little while now & it’s brought me to earth every time I’ve opened it.
Bly has a way of talking about love that feels so unpretentious but soooo wise.

He makes me want to meet his wife.
