I bought this book based on the excerpt in Bibliomaniac which was very funny. I sort of imagined that Tom Baker, DELIGHTFUL Tom Baker had written a charming kids book about a small boy who goes through his days trying to kick a pig.Oh my god. That’s not what this book is at all!Robert, (pig-kicking boy) is a deviant, and Tom Baker is NOT DELIGHTFUL, he’s a wrongun!I won’t give any spoilers but my 8 year old isn’t old enough for this story. When he’s 10 he’ll love it, but for now it’s not really suitable. My 16 year old will think it’s great. Personally, I love it, but it won’t be for everyone.
