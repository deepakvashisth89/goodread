Simon St. George doesn’t know his family. In fact, he hasn’t seen them since he was two. He calls the Lighthouse School home, although he doesn’t really belong. He is picked on and treated like an outcast, until a strange man shows up claiming to be his father, and more than that, a knight. The last knight. He has come to collect Simon for his safety as they go after the last remaining dragon. It is an adventure Simon is not really ready for.Honestly, this book was weird. Like, one of the weirdest books. The storyline had lots of plot holes and odd choices. Shapeshifter dragons with scales for skin that burrow underground, lay eggs, and also all hate each other. And that is just the start. There were so many parts with weird writing and confusing plot points. So weird. Just weird.
