This was a rich and delightful book. Enrobed and deep African-American spirituality and mysticism, set in the fictional town of Mulberry Georgia in the early 2000s and late 90s, diving into the book was like a swim and murky Creekwater only to come up for fresh crisp air.Our protagonist Lena came from Self made money and lived a stylish life. However, an error made by her mother upon her birth, left her at the whims of malevolent spirits. Unable to sort truth from fiction or in the world of mysticism the concrete from the abstract, the turmoil underneath her stylish clothes and deep in her heart shaped her to later become a people pleaser and do good in her town as an adult.Yet she lacked the one thing that she craved the love of a man. This too was part of the challenge she experienced by being born with avail that was mistreated by her unknowing mother.Eventually, with the help of her best friend, Sister, Lena gets the man of her dreams. But is he more than just a figment of her imagination? That is the question.Overall, the exploration of what love means and how it operates is at the center of this very well crafted, though I would say over worded story. That said I found it truly enjoyable and have recommended it to people that I care about most.I mean, who doesn’t love a good story , black mysticism, and romance?
