The movie was better, extremely different from the book, but not bad.This book was a Libertarian's wet dream of a revenge novel. In a world of libtard judges soft on crime and full of technicalities, crime victims are fed up. Karen Newman, whose daughter has been raped and murdered, wants revenge on the men who killed her daughter and to avenge other victims. Yet, as Karen is seduced into a vigilante group full of victims, not all of Victims Anonymous are good and some are just as evil as the criminals they hunt.The book is filled with plot twists that are at times less than believable, and characters are seemingly killed off once they fulfill their roles in the book to an almost ridiculous degree.
