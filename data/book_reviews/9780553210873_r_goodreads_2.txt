welcome to...THE SEPTEMBER CASE OF DR. JEKYLL AND HYDE.this is part ofproject long classics, a series in which i read long old books in little chunks over the course of a month and make a pun out of it.this isn't really long, and that barely constitutes a pun, but give me a break. i'll read these 10 chapters sometime this september and we'll get through it together.anyway, jekyll isn't special. i contain a monstrous version of myself i may become at any time without warning too.ONE: STORY OF THE DOORwe begin with a long gossipy story, the executive summary of which is that one time a good guy saw a bad guy from a weird house who paid him not to talk about how bad he was using a check with a different nice guy's name on it. you'll never guess who was who.TWO: SEARCH FOR MR. HYDEturns out the lawyer hearing that story represents jekyll, whose will says "give all my stuff to the bad guy, hyde." now he's upset, which means we get to read this amazing line: "'If he be Mr. Hyde,' he had thought, 'I shall be Mr. Seek.'"THREE: DR. JEKYLL WAS QUITE AT EASEa lot of this, so far, has been jekyll being like "if i'm ever not around pleaseeee be nice to hyde and let him use all my stuff...no reason..."FOUR: THE CAREW MURDER CASEokay honestly i did not realize that hyde is committing brutal murders. i thought his evil was of the pushing-toddlers type, like earlier.FIVE: INCIDENT OF THE LETTERjekyll just handed over a letter from hyde that says "jekyll is a good guy and i am bad. goodbye forever" in his own handwriting, told the lawyer he burned exclusively the envelope it came in, and said it was hand-delivered, not mailed. very casual!SIX: INCIDENT OF DR. LANYONjekyll has now retired from public life. my dream date. cute. and he knows that.SEVEN: INCIDENT AT THE WINDOWour lawyer and his cousin were just having a nice chat with jekyll at his window when his face suddenly turned scary and he slammed it it shut. lot of incidents lately.EIGHT: THE LAST NIGHTthe lawyer and jekyll's servant just axed his door down and are like "where the hell is this guy! i see hyde's dead body here in the middle of the floor, but where could that doctor be hiding..."NINE: DR. LANYON'S NARRATIVEif i opened my door at midnight to see a small man crouching against my porch i would slam that sh*t so fast. i don't care if my friend asked me to let him in so he could drink a potion and turn back into his normal self.that instinct is how i would survive where dr. lanyon died of shock or something old-timey like that.TEN: HENRY JEKYLL'S FULL STATEMENT OF THE CASEbasically he invented a potion that made him tiny and bad, and then drank it sometimes.OVERALLin spite of the fact that this small book meets roughly 0 of my criteria for this project, i'm glad i broke all the rules of my own making and read it. it was fun and spooky and i'm ready for halloween now, even if i won't remember it forever.rating: 3.5
