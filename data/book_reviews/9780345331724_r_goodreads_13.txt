Once you get to the 4th book of the Sten series, it doesn't really feel like there's much left to review. The books are all similar. If you liked the previous books, you'll enjoy this one. If you didn't enjoy the other books, you probably should have quit after the first one because the trend is continuing.War has finally broken out, and Sten is once again needed. Now, he's a Commander in the Navy and in charge of a small and dysfunctional fleet. This book feels a little more like the first book since it opens with Sten in flight school and going through the training pipeline for the Navy. Other than that, there's all the normal parts. Impossible situations, impossible escapes, impossible creatures, beautiful women falling over Sten, Alex' story/jokes, and a whole bunch of fun.
