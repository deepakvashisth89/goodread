A nice return for Dave Brandstetter (and for me into the series, after a few months away). The story is tense from the start, with Dave at the center of a deadly web that he must unravel, first around a serial killer targeting gay men with AIDS, and then around a man long trail of broken homes and hearts behind him. Still not keen on how bisexuals are often portrayed in this series, but the drama remains sky high and the characters are all amazing. At this point the series seems so lived in that every twist and turn is riveting and terrifying, and each resolution a sigh of relief and joy. Great stuff.
