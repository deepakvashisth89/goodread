A boy goes to the park with his two moms, and he meets the Zark, who becomes his friend. But he realizes he's lost his parents and asks for help finding them. Unfortunately, he asks the McFinks, who don't believe there should be any kind of families but ones like theirs. The boy has to flee or risk being placed in a different family, but he finds someone who's much more realistic about how diverse families can be and eventually finds his way home.Alright, so this is a little long for a picture book, but I was surprised at how good it is. It's built in poetic meter that wobbles a little at times, but that in itself reminds us not to take it too seriously. It makes a few good points about not wandering away in the park, who to trust, and what it means to be a family. I'm glad this book is available on Internet Archive, as it's well worth the read.
