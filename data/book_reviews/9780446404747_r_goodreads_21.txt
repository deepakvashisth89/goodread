I'm not exactly sure what it was that made this book so hard to read for me. While the premise sounded promising - Falco and Helena are on a roadtrip with a theatre group, trying to find a missing girl while trying to solve the usual murder mystery - the execution was rather ... tiresome.Endless descriptions of yet another city (even though they all felt the same to me), interrogations of actors from the group (who all seemed petty and unlikeable) that never led to any results, Falco stabbing in the dark for ages, moving on to another city, rinse and repeat.When the - admittedly exciting - showdown finally happens, it's a relief. The redeeming qualities of this book are clearly Falco and Helena's relationship and their companion Musa, but all in all it's not quite enough to make me love it. Davis, you can do better than that.
