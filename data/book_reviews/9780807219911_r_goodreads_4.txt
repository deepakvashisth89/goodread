i was nervous going into this reread — which, to be clear, is of my childhood favorite series, my fondness for which continued into adulthood — and i got what i was worried about.

this is a fun and fine book, sure, but...no mysteries here. no codes, no secrets, no spying. no hidden societies or double meanings or any of the things that gives this series its vim and vigor.

and uncle monty is not the guy i remembered him being. sure, he's nice and quirky for all of about 6 pages. then he's right back to the same ignorant, annoying adult archetype that plagues this series.

and some of this 6 pages may just be my fond memories of coconut cake descriptions.

bottom line: fun. but i've read fun-er.

3.5
