I like David Quammen a lot, ever since I readThe Flight of the Iguana A Sidelong View of Science and Naturea good long time ago. He manages to write about the environment without being shrill, repetitive, or completely depressing. Ok his later articles can be pretty depressing but have you looked at environment lately? He's got a clear concise way of writing that really conveys the gravity of say, African rain forest while still leavening it with humor and adventure. His longer pieces are somewhat weaker, especially the book length ones. Maybe I just get tired of the tone he takes after long exposure.
