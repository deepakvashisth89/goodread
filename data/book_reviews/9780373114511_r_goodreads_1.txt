Re Memories of the Past - Carole Mortimer does the bachelor dad H and the troubled h with a meddling matchmaker parent trope in this one.It starts out with a bang, or almost one anyways. The h is a London accountant down to visit her widowed father who wants to sell the family home. The family home is a cottage that was once tied to the big estate next door but is now a freehold. The h doesn't want her father selling the place and her father is hinting that he is thinking of selling off to the estate's owner, a big time wealthy businessman and currently single parent who took in his baby nephew after the death of his brother and sister in law. The h is sure that this new estate owner is unduly influencing her father out of greed and she is determined to stop any sale from going forward. So she rushes down from London to put her dad straight.On the way to her family home, the h almost hits a little toddler tooling about in the road. The h almost crashes her car and runs to get the baby out of the lane and then a guy comes running up. There are words about not watching escape monkeys from their little playpens and the h is concerned and lecturing and the man in question is extremely concerned and apologetic. The h believes the man works for the big estate owner and is contemplating having a word, but then her father tells her that the man she met is the big estate owner himself and the toddler is the nephew he took in.The H comes over to visit the h's dad frequently and the h, after arguing with her dad and realizing that she is going to need more time to convince him not to sell, decides to arrange for several weeks of holiday so she can keep an eye on the house sale situation. The H and the h gradually become closer and the h reluctantly falls in love with the H's nephew.She is reluctant because her mother died a few years earlier in a late life pregnancy from a heart defect when she was delivering the h's brother. Then the baby died soon after from the same heart problem. The h and her father grieved heavily and the h took herself off to London to come to terms with the losses.There are other complications too. The H hires a man from the h's past as his PA/Assistant and the relationship between the OM and h did not end well when he turned out to be a sewer slurper who was practicing dubious business dealings. The h doesn't like him and yet he is at the H's estate all the time making creepy faces and psuedo gropey moves towards the h and trying to bully her about his past activities. The h doesn't want to say anything to the H about this guy cause she is embarrassed by the past association and that she pretended she hadn't really known the OM initially.On the H's side, his in laws, more especially his domineering sister in law's mother, wants custody of the H's nephew. That isn't going to happen as the H's brother and sis in law left legal instruction that the maternal grandparents were not to be given custody at all. The H doesn't want to hurt the in law's feelings, he is a really nice guy, but the mother in law is getting rowdier and more abusive and the h gets drawn into her scenes as well when she helps out with the nephew.The h doesn't really want to get involved with the H, but his roofie kisses make her knees weak and then she finds out that her father never had any intention of selling the family home, he just wanted the h to move back home and he wanted to play matchmaker. The h is kinda sorta mad at her dad, but she has fallen in love with the H. When the sewer slurper OM tries to blackmail the h into his bed or he will make up lies about their past relationship, the H overhears and sends the h off to sort the slime swiller out.The h has a little mopey moment over what she see's as the H's rejection, but then the H shows up and avows true love forever. He just did not want to bruise the h's delicate eye sight with the vision of him beating up the evil slime swiller OM as he sent him on his sewer swimming way. The h is fathoms deep in love by this point, so she swears true love back and we get a little epilogue where the H and h are married, the grandmother in law had a nervous breakdown and can't care for any kids, so she gave up the custody idear, and the H and his nephew are sneaking in to see new baby girl the h just had for a pink sparkly HP HEA.This one is on the sweet side and very readable. Unfortunately , it wasn't my cuppa as I did not see why the h's dad couldn't sell his house if he wanted to and I don't like matchmaking relatives.
