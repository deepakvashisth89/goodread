I loved the first book in this series, but didn't enjoy #2 as much. There was too little communication between Montgomery and Abbie. Abbie was headstrong and not the docile, dainty lady Montgomery had known in the South. However, he knew that coming into this story and yet constantly fought against this. I thought they were both stupid at times. I am annoyed when people who love each other allow misunderstandings to stand in their way for weeks at a time. Communication, people!

That being said, this is still a nice story. It's really helpful to have read the first book, Honor's Pledge, before jumping into this story. I didn't notice it in the first book, but this story seems a Christian Romance, with more talk of trusting God. There is no sex or language and it's an enjoyable read.
