I read (and re-read) this book a long time ago and sold it at a book store when I was strapped for cash, what a mistake! There have been a hundred times when I wanted to read it again only to remember that for 50¢ I sold perhaps one of my favourite all time books which is now quite hard to find, although I know I could purchase it on-line.If you find a copy of it but it and read it, it is very well written despite the very confusing premise. He manages to really work out those details!
