The book contains, in a nutshell, the history of the catholic church. Many of the topics are discussed very briefly. After all, according to the title, it's a 'short story'.

Nonetheless, it still troubled me how some important issues are oversimplified. For instance, even if it's a short history, I think a fundamental issue such as papal supremacy deserved a bit more depth.

Frankly, if you had a reasonable school education, the book won't give you much new information

Still, the book might be useful for those interested in a brief and critical history of the catholic church. Hans Kung discusses, for instance, how the Church promoted anti semitism throughout history (e.g Ghetto di Roma), and the relations between a future pope and nazi germany (Reichskonkordat).
