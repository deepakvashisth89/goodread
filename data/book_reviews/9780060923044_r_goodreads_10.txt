As a non-historian I really enjoy the Gies work for giving me a broad overview of people's day to day life during the middle ages.

Both of the books I've read by them have been compulsively readable and enjoyable.

I think this would be great for someone performing research for a novel, or just someone interested in people's day to day lives at different times. But I would guess there's not much here for someone who already has an in depth knowledge of the era.
