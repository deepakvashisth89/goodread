An intriguing approach to telling a story and very well written, but I have a few quibbles. First --- the character of Eleanor. She is the major driving force in the process of discovering what happened in the past, yet we know nothing about her. We only see her through the mind of the protagonist (Paul). Why does she spend so much time "helping" him?And then there's the ending. I had to read it twice. Was the author really implying what I thought he was implying? Throughout the book it's not always clear what is happening in the "real" world and what is happening in Paul's imagination -- classic Thomas Cook -- and generally I like that about his books, but this ending was a little more vague than usual.
