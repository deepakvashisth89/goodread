Unsure who this book was aiming for. Seems to be an intro to biology somebody may read as a freshman undergrad just getting into the field. At other times reads like it is made to be read by the average joe in a waiting room. So, it ends up getting to be a mix of both, which fits neither for me.

The fruit fly has played a huge role in learning about life and DNA, etc. Unfortunately, a lot of fluff is in this book not related to the subject.

Two stars may be too low, and it is closer to 3. But for me I didnt overly enjoy the book, and did not learn anything major. If you are an aspiring science 'nerd' this book could be for you. Shows how flies have helped make major advances in biology. Sometimes gets too technical for my liking. So it goes.
