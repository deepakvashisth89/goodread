Overall this book was a good read. It took me a little longer than it would’ve liked to finish it, but that was my own fault. (I like to read multiple books at once)

The first story by Larry Niven as well as most of Iron by Poul Anderson felt kind of scrambled, and kept me feeling lost in the plot, but that could also be because I read the first half of this book during nights right before bed.

The second half of “Iron” and all of “Cathouse” by Dean Ing were very good. “Cathouse” especially. As many other readers say, this story shines in the collection. I read the second half of the book mostly on the train and was really pulled into the story.

I look forward to continuing the series.
