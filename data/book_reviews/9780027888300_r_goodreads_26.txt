"So many wishes on a rainy afternoon" - so begins our wonderful story of the Chinese Moon Festival. Nai-Nai is left looking after her three granddaughters and in her wisdom she turns to a story which she shares.When we enter her story we are graced with both a rich narrative and illustrative story of grandmother's life in China. Although the story could work just as well without the illustrations it has to be said that Schields' work is utterly beautiful and worth studying and exploring in and of its own sake. This was a pleasurable and engaging read.
