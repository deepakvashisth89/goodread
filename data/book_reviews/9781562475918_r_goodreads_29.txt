The most romantic American Girl book. The romance between Papa and Tia Dolores in this book plays out like a romance novel, just through the POV of a ten year old. A fabulous conclusion to the series. With most AG series, you can see how the main character grows in maturity over the course of the six books. I love that with Josefina, it is more about her and her family reconciling with the grief they feel over the mother’s death as well as finding peace and happiness.
