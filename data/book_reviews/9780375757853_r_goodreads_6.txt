The problem with mysteries – for me, anyway, is that I don't care who did it. Which is a drawback. SoThe Moonstoneis a very elaborate warning that going cold turkey is a bad idea,you must use the patches.The Moonstoneis often cited as the earliest medical warning story – later examples areDr Jeckyll and Mr Hyde, which concerns self-medication and its dangers, and Henry James'Daisy Miller, which explains to tourists that they must get all their vaccinations.
