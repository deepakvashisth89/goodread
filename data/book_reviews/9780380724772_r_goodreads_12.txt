Really good mystery.

Bodies turning up and Markby thinks he knows who did it? Is he right? Meredith's flat in London is made to feel small as Toby returns home from his posting and as it's his flat, he's staying put. But he's a slob and getting on Meredith's wick, so she travels down to Bamford to stay with an old friend guarding a dig as hippies/travellers are also on the site of the dig.
I'd love this as a tv series. But wouldn't want it ruined though.
These are good books, I'd had them as audio books ages ago but can't remember the who did what. So it's nice to read them in the proper order.
Highly recommended if you enjoy a good mystery.
