The British and the French go to war in the forests of North America. It’s about the exploitation of the native tribes by the two warring nations, setting the natives against each other. It’s definitely an entertaining, adventurous and classic read with some commentary on the modern society at the time this was written. But it does show a bit of a racially stereotypical representation of the natives. It’s not at all bad but I don’t know. It’s definitely a bit dated and the characters didn’t really grab me all that much.
