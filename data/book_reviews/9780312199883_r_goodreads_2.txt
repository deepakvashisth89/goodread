Lucid dreaming is an entirely attainable skill that can be learned and practiced. There's no mystical mumbo-jumbo involved...just a mastery of certain types of perspective-shifting and meditation.And lemme tell ya - once you've "woken up" in your dreams, confronted previously scary dream enemies, chosen to fly through the stratosphere or hurl big chunks of buildings through the air with your mind...you'll wonder why you never did this shit sooner.This book is a relatively straight-forward, pragmatic guide to tapping into your sleeping conciousness and harnessing your own mental powers. Hey - it worked for me!
