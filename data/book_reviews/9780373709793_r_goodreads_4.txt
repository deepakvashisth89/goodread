Although I did like the story line, in general, as I have others from this series, two things really annoyed me about this book. First there was little activity or sustained dialogue in comparison to all the pages and pages of introspective thoughts by the main characters. Boring! In addition, the pages of underlined print were so annoying. What did that indicate? I have no idea, and so I skimmed them just to get through it. The italics for the baby's inner thoughts was alright but that's it. Please no more underlining!!
