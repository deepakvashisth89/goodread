The title is very apt for this book... sadly, it refers to the plot. Honestly, it would probably read better as a stand alone book. Like, if you didn't know Ghost was a potentially sentient doomsday device, and just thought of him as a transportation device, it might not annoy you. Or the fact that Merlin has relatives coming out of the woodwork everywhere, but suddenly knows all about all the court politics even though it seemed clear in the first book he was trying to stay out of all that.Then there's all the politics of the kingdoms around Amber that seemed insignificant and just mentioned in passing, now we are supposed to care about them and who's in charge of them? Surely there were lots of relatives all over the place before. But then, earlier it was mentioned that Amberites don't have alot of kids.. I guess that was wrong.Then, of course, we have Merle totally ignoring good sense and his quest to find his father for.... other stuff. None of which makes alot of sense if you think about it for more than a second.Chaos indeed.
