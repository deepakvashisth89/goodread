Wow! I can't believe I got through this and still have fingernails left - a few!
It's very suspenseful and keeps you guessing. It's very gothicee (Not a word - I know) which I loved. It felt a lot like a Mary Stewart book with a strong, smart Heroine. It had almost two endings - one for the mystery and one for the love interest. They both fooled me! I will probably be searching for more of Salisbury's books :)
