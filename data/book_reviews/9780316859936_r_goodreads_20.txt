I have never lived in Liverpool but this book gives one some understanding of how the people of Liverpool have been forged from a melting pot of cultures.

Grant's architect enables us to understand so much about the place and it's role in the development of the skyscraper. (I put the book down to search the web to see if she had fabricated this element)

For me her story centres on the sadness of real people who have lived real lives as best they can. Some have lived on the doorstep of the promised land and never made it, others have tried other places but have returned. Others were lucky to reach Liverpool rather than stay where they were and die.

This is a brave attempt to deal with big issues which are as current now as they ever were and is very well worth reading.
