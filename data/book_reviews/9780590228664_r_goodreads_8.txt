I absolutely love the scope of the BSC mysteries. The sabotaged baking competition from the last one is treated with the same level of seriousness as
a stalker infiltrating a middle school to terrorize their high school bully.

This was also a great reminder of just how bonkers Stoneybrook is. The middle school decides to bring back a masquerade ball shrouded in tragedy, and Stacey once again goes full investigative journalist to uncover the truth about this cursed event. I mean, they’re going into microfilm and breaking into the school archives to solve this one. And I’m only kind of exaggerating when I say it’s cursed, because we quickly learn that this masquerade ball killed someone. Happy Halloween, Stoneybrook!

The most relatable character in this entire series is Mr. Wetzler, a citizen who rightfully has a lot of questions about the school’s budget. After all, they JUST had a carnival to fund the entire arts program, but sure, let’s bring back an event that produced so much bullying a faculty member died.
