I thoroughly enjoyed this lovely personal history of The Hamlet of Marshfield which covers a ten year period at the end of the nineteenth century. Written by Lura Beam, who was born in 1887 and spent 12 years in Marshfield living with her grandparents, this book feels just so Maine. From the descriptions of the lifestyle and personalities of the inhabitants to some of the hymns sung and games played, it felt real. There were many ways I could relate to the rural life and my understanding of ‘old Maine.’ Interesting to me was the chapter on the migration of the young people...a problem we still experience! A great Maine read.
