Now, for the first time, Amelia Bedelia, the feminist answer to Gilligan from the TV island, introduces the Rogers family, and ours, to her family, whom we never knew existed.... until now! Is our ignorant bliss a blessing or a curse? See for yourself: one member of the Bedelia regalia balances checkbooks...juggler style! Another is a bank teller, telling bank CEOs and patrons where to go...oops! (Townspeople have asked for "early withdrawal" of THAT girl!) One runs a fan club, all sorts of fans from ceiling fans to hand fans making her the "coolest" girl on the block. (LoL) And Amelia's sweet nephew Ollie is a catcher, who catches everything from colds and flu to possibly hypochondria! (He's too shy around the ladies to catch the love bug! Awww!) And Amelia B has a photo album of every member of her family, and is inviting them all to her party for a reunion! The Rogers can now wait with apprehension as to what's next!!! Amelia's story continues to gain new dimension and fans of the Peggy Parrish parish will want to receive the blessings! Amelia B is the Parrish Saint of Jocosity we all need in these dark times. And the Dawn is coming..... to take grease out of the way!Four starsAmelia's family affair is love and not war!!!
