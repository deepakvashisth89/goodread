This was fkn crazy.This was so depressing 😅 I’ve seen the movie and had put off the book because I thought they’d be too similar, but OMG they couldn’t be anymore different!!! Completely different storylines and vibes.This is about Isserley, a strange woman of mysterious origins who picks up male hitchhikers and brings them back to the farm she stays at. She’s in search of strong, relatively fit men who won’t be missed, and knocks them out with a syringe hidden beneath their seat once she’s satisfied they fit the profile. This book is a strange puzzle that slowly pieces itself together along the way and that doesn’t really help the reader figure out the mystery.But MAN was it depressing!!! There’s a strong sense of impending doom throughout, and I thought the way it explored humanity and what it means to be human was very well done and nuanced. I wasn’t expecting this to disturb me the way it did, but yeah it just unexpectedly hits like a truck.Some of the writing in the way it described the world through the MC’s eyes was also very moving and beautiful. I’d describe this as a literary fiction/scifi/horror.I won’t say why or how it made me feel that way because it’s best to go in blind, but this will not be one I forget anytime soon.
