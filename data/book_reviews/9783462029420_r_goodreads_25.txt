I thought I knew what to expect, having seen (and enjoyed) the movie. But the book is entirely different, taking the story of this lone woman who picks up exclusively male hitchhikers in very creepy, bizarre and surprising directions. Faber does a good job at slowly lowering the curtain into Isserley’s life and motivations... but the less said about these, the better: so much of the fun is in the revelation.Under the Skincertainly has its weak points, but overall I found it so strange, unexpected and original, and the grotesque spectacle so compelling, that any faults were easy to overlook, and it was a pleasure to be picked up and taken along for the ride.
