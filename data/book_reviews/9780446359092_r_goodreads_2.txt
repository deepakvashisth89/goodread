I will preface this by saying that I adore Patricia Wentworth's books and am chomping my way through all of them, but...this one was about as hard going as a Wentworth gets. Engaging characters, perfect period details, but the entire first half of the book is a net very slowly closing around a guy as he shrugs everything off and goes "how strange, I guess I'll ignore this completely!" as you slowly tear your hair out.As always, the last 25% of the book is perfection, but...if you're a Wentworth completist, it's worth a read. If you are new to her, do NOT start with this one.
