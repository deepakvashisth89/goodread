British Chief Inspector Roderick Allen accompanies his artist wife Troy to an island in New Zealand so that she can paint a temperamental opera diva, Isabella Sommita.La Sommita, as she’s often called, is the guest of American millionaire Montague Reece, whose compound is on an otherwise uninhabited island. Reece has scheduled a musical composition by Sommita’s latest boytoy, attracting a swath of well-known music critics, musical legends and other dignitaries. Too bad an unexpected storm traps them on that island!To reveal more would ruin the fun, but obviously some bad things happen, or there wouldn’t be a mystery novel. Author Ngaio Marsh doesn’t play fair with her readers, as Agatha Christie and some other Golden Age detective-fiction writers do, but I still liked the novel a lot, and I doubt I would have figured out the mystery even if I’d had all the clues. WhilePhoto Finishis part of a series, it works as a stand-alone.
