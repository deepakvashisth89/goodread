This is a memoir of an immigrant Chinese family told through the eyes of the only daughter. It is a clash of cultures and is very eye-opening. I was surprised at the level of harassment and bullying that Elaine endured at the hands of her classmates. Her parents were struggling, having learned almost no English in the entire time in the US, but she and her brother were able to bridge the gap as is the case in many immigrant families. She had a difficult life. My only cricism is that the book stopped when she went off to college; I would have been interested in learning about her adult life and how she incorporated her Chinese family into her modern life.
