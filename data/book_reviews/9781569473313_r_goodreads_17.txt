Having been to Paris multiple times and having family there, I enjoy reading novels that take place there. To be honest, I had read two previous Cara Black books that take place in Paris and have enjoyed them, but Murder in the Sentier is an exception. This has to be one of the most confusing stories I've fought my way through in a long time, and a book that never takes a breath so that the reader can collect their thoughts. I may try one more book by the author, but I need some time to settle down before going there. I kept wondering just what the point was of the story, and how many more characters would be introduced to the reader and thrown into the jumbled story. But, I did get through it, not being one to give up unless the book is really poorly written. It isn't the writing here, but the complexity, some unnecessary IMHO, that made this so difficult to complete. Cara Black on steroids is the conclusion I've come to, and I hope that whatever book I read next will far less convoluted.
