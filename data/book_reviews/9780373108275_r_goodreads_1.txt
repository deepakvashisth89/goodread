I had to check a few times and make sure this was actually a CL book. Then there was mention of Parma ham and melon balls and the h had a croissant, (no cherry jam tho, as the book was set in Venice not France,) and felt a sigh of relief that I was in the right book after all. The biggest drama in the book takes place in the first 15 pages and the H is nowhere around.The book starts on the wedding day of the h's niece. The h is 26ish and a jazz singer. Three years ago the h fell in love with the H, but the niece (who is a spoilt brat,) said that the H had made inappropriate advances to her 16 year old self. Everybody, including the h, believed her and the H just walked away after the accusation without looking back.After seeing the H on tv and knowing that she would soon be on her honeymoon, the niece finally confesses that she lied to everyone about the whole thing - she did not want to lose face in front of one of her more experienced school friends, so she told everyone that the H was hitting on her.The h is devastated and she feels enormously guilty for believing her niece. ( I actually could understand why she did tho, the H and h loved each other but really hadn't been together all that long and did not know each other well. Plus it isn't unheard of that predators pick women who have dependents of their preferred age to get to their prey. Ironically, the niece's accusations would probably be even MORE believed today, with prosecution to boot. CL also makes a point of saying that as far as the h knew, the niece was not a liar, she embellished things but NEVER flat out lied so the h could see no reason not to believe her.)The h stumbles out of the wedding reception and makes her way to the H's home. She wants to apologize, but is afraid to approach the H. She is slinking in her car when the H appears, he is escorting his father to his car and the h panics. She tries to take off but almost wrecks with another car. The H sees her, pulls her out of the car and takes her into his home. She tries to apologize, but the H rejects her apology and it is pretty obvious he is furiously enraged. Realizing further attempts are hopeless cause the H despises her, the h sadly leaves.Then the H's dad comes to see her nightclub act. He invites the h to sing at his birthday party and the h's riotous mum tags along to flirt with the other 70 year old men there and the H's dad. The h tries to tempt/talk to the H, but he avoids her as much as possible, then the H's dad offers her a contract performing in his new hotel in Venice for a month. The H will be supervising the opening so the h will get a chance to chase her man, encouraged by the H's dad.The h gets to Venice and the H is there to meet her. He tries to throw her back to England, but after a punishing kissing session, he decides he doesn't care what she does unless she wants a quick lurve clubbing, and leaves the h to her own devices. The h is miserable, cause she wants the love back not just the club.CL sorta wrote herself into a corner here, the h is too ashamed and petrified to chase after a man who so obviously hates her and the H is in full despising ignore mode. They are both avoiding each other, so instead we get a guidebook tour of Venice. The h and H have one conversation on a water bus about the h not even questioning the accusations of the niece and the h trying to explain that she was in shock, did not really know him that long or that well, and even tho they were in love at the time, she really had no idea what to believe. The H cuts the conversation short by taking off alone and we are back to the guidebook tour.Things finally culminate after the h sorta mopes and internal angsts, (but not very dramatically I am afraid, CL really needed to borrow some of Penny Jordan's h's internal angsties, they were much more emo and dire than CL manages, her h's are almost always too pragmatic.)It is the Fat Tuesday of the Venetian Carnivale and the h is in full period costume complete with mask. She is wandering the streets of Venice prior to her evening singing engagement and the H shows up in the appropriate masculine period attire. They share a dance and a kiss and then the h runs off to her job, thinking the H did not recognize her.The H shows up for her performance, and she vamps it up by flirting with him during the song and he takes the opportunity to kiss her and then turn her over his knee. The h loses her nerve to continue, and over breakfast the next morning, (with the obligatory CL croissant,) the H calls the h into his office.Thinking he is going to kick her out of his life forever, the h is very trepidatious, but the H throws caution to the wind and confesses he still loves her. The h is overjoyed, cause she loves him too and while the H is commenting that "We should get to know each other better after the wedding," they get a telegram announcing that her mum and his dad have run off to Bermuda and gotten married.The H swears to never speak to the lying niece ever again and the h solicits for a big wedding and HEA's are everwhere and happiness rules once again in HPlandia.Overall this one is relatively mild for CL and yet this book really was a reach for her. The H is not the aggressor in this one, the h is, and CL hasn't figured out how a girl can chase a man and yet remain ladylike. The H was also surprisingly non-domineering in this one. His avoidance tactics for the h were very unlike the usual CL H's straight on til morning and devil take the hindmost trampling of the h that CL usually has her H's do.
