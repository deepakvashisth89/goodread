I like dark comedies, but the foundational misogyny and increasingly dark tone killed my enjoyment for this play (pun intended).It’s a dark satire on country-house murder mysteries with none of the attendant love of the genre that makes satires work. Instead it’s a twisted psychological thriller that pretty much comes down to two men having a p*ssing contest over a woman, who never appears on stage and could be an antique silver platter for all she’s relevant to the plot.Honestly, having them fight over an antique silver platter would have made this one more enjoyable for me. Not recommended.
