I received a digital copy of this book in exchange for honest feedback.I enjoyed this book, but then, I generally like entries in the 'ill-advised, farming adventures of hapless expats' genre.Kerr's writing invites you to slow down to the pace of rural Mallorcan people and hop into the colorful lives of the (often aged) locals who are trying to hang on to a beautifully rich and sometimes riotous traditional lifestyle. "Tranquilo," he is often advised. The same mindset helped me connect with the descriptions of Mallorcan landscape and the cast of interesting neighbors.
