From riches to rags to riches again. Laurel Worthington trust fund child, learned no StreetSmarts, her bother took care of everything then he left.
She sold everything. This is what’s been going on for the last few months of her life. She excepted an invitation to a birthday party where she was the bride and married Brett Matthews for one night only.
There one night marriage in Reno resulted in Bliss for the both of them.
Now she has to face the reality of being broke, being pregnant she moves to Oregon where Brett lives and runs his own investment firm. She asks for a job even after Brett believes they should be married for real as the baby is his. The ups and downs of their relationship results in a wonderful happily ever after story.
