Dean couldn’t help but feel how desperately he needed money as he was sitting in Leonard’s den facing a safe containing $30,000 worth of bearer bonds. While Leonard ran off for TEN minutes, Dean stole the pay to bearer bonds and easily exchanged them for cash – to fulfill his wives desires. The only problem was that Leonard discovered the bonds missing way to early – one crime quickly moving to another – love, larceny leading to a brutal murder…that he almost gets away with!You knew all along who had killed the victim and why – but how it all happened and the anxiety of waiting for the Detectives to put it all together and wondering how it would all fit is ----- just amazing! This one caught my attention from the very first page and held it all the way through. And, even though it was NOT in his plans to commit murder….how he almost got away with the PERFECT ONE was just amazing! Just a GREAT BOOK!
