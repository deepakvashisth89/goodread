A sweet romance bought down by lies.Marcus and Gemma had a passionate affrair years ago which resulted in a son they both adore and manage to share time with.But all that comes to an end when Marcus announces that he is moving back to Italy and wants to take their son. Gemma is willing to work on shared visits but he is not willing to and demands marriage if she wants to see her son.Gemma is not willing to give up her life and put her heart back on the line with Marcus as he had destroyed their love years before. She is so willing to bend over backwards to avoid marriage to him that makes him more determined to reclaim her.They both will need to look honestly at the past and the lies that were told to them......lies told by a beloved dead brother and good friend.Both characters are strong and both feel betrayed and both still love each other. Marcus proves his love by doing all he can to claim his family. Gemma learns to let go and love.A nice sweet romance about reclaimimg a love that was once lost.
