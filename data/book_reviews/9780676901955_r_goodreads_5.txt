And while it's been about 12 years since I was last there, I do believe that Maine, Vermont and New Hampshire have some of the most beautiful fall foliage of any of the 48 contiguous states I've been in! Simply gorgeous! And I'd love to go again. Maybe next fall!From the first pages, which do, indeed, feature photographs of fall foliage, we see the Top Reasons To Go:* Fall Foliage* Regional Food (You haven't eaten until you've eaten fresh caught Maine lobster!)* Outdoor Fun ( Hiking, boating, biking, or simply taking in the views!)* Small Towns (I love the small town greens and small taverns!)* Fantastic Skiing (You'll have to trust the book here!
