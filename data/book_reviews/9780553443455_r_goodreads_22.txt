An truly excellent book by Kay Hooper (and the reader is starting to see more of the paranormal features that we all love in the Bishop books). However, we only see one of the mysteries solved in the book. Hooper has deliberately left us with information about how the second mystery will turn out, but we are hanging with only our imagination to fill in the "why" of the event.Overall, I enjoyed the book and will read it again in the future I'm sure.
