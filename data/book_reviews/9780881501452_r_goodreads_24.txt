This is the second book in theAsey Mayo Cape Cod MysterybyPhoebe Atwood Taylor. I've read a couple books in this series and I love the historical setting in New England, especially since I was raised in Massachusetts.This story was quite contrived and complicated, but it was still very entertaining and a quick read. I like the characters and I am looking forward to reading more of the books in this series.interesting quote:"I guess it's true that Cape Codders don't die. They just dry up and blow away."(p. 73)new words: allonal, flivver
