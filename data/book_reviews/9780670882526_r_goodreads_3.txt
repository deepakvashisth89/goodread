Juno gets a letter written in Korean from his grandmother. Even though he can't read the letter himself, he knows what she's saying to him because of the items she included in the letter. He decides to draw picture to "write" her back.In addition to addressing the topic of communicating with family members living in another country, this book adroitly shows how young children begin to make sense of writing. The message of the book is that the desire to communicate with loved ones is a powerful motivator that transcends language and literacy skills.
