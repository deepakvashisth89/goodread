It's 30 years since I last read this episode of discworld life and it was like visiting an old friend. I've taken to replacing all my paper versions (which my husband inadvertently packed up with a lot of other books we gave to charity - disaster!) However it makes for an excellent excuse to listen again to old favourites.The audio was read really well by Jason Isaacs taking all the main parts along with the usuals Peter Serafinowicz as Death and Bill Nighy doing the footnotes. I guess it speaks to the popularity of Sir TP's work that such magnificent actors are doing the narration.The storyline gives us the Discworld's interpretation of the magic of the silver screen. So off goes Victor, a student wizard, who has discovered something odd going on far away from the Unseen University in a place called Holy Wood. Once there he finds himself starring in the new "clicks" along with the beautiful Theda "Ginger". They're not just setting the slightly wrinkly sheet alive with the smouldering romances they're also beggaring about with the fabric of reality. And once you're inside the cinema you'll need banged grains and something to drink.With a cast of Ankh Morpork regulars including CMOT Dibbler, the wizards of Unseen University and Gaspode the Wonder Dog the question is will the magic of Holy Wood cause a disaster? Will the wizards ever get over the university wall? Amd will the hero save the day just in the nick of time?Wonderful. Loved it. Great narration of another excellent story. Sir TP is much missed.
