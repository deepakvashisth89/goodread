This is Richard Russo's debut novel back in 1986. The characters were well developed and give you a glimpse into small town life in America. Russo does a wonderful job of shedding light on the brilliance of what Belle from "Beauty and the Beast" would call the provincial life. Each word leaves the page in a very gritty and industrial tone that the town Mohawk exemplifies. You can feel the desperation but definitely the hope that shines through. It is definitely an enjoyable read.
