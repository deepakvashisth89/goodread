Goran Kropp is a great guy who stands way apart from the rest of the climbing community. Cycling from Sweden to Nepal undeterred by physical assaults which he faced throughout the journey , carrying all his luggage without the help of Sherpas, summiting in the aftermath of Everest disaster and then cycling back again to home country, this is an extremely impressive and daunting feat.But the writing is terrible, does not do justice to his achievements.
