Hanging Hannah by Evan Marshall was what I would describe as a cozy mystery. Its a very fast read and would be perfect for a snowy day by the fire or a hot sunny day on the beach. This is the first of Marshall's books I've come across and was very pleased by the characters, who to me were full and lifelike. The story drug ( or hanged ) in a few spots but overall I give this story a 5 and hope to run into more of the series.
