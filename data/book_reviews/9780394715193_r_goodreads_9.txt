This book dives deep into the difference between gifts vs commodities. Hyde talks a lot about how true art should ‘sort of’ just exist as gifts, but artists live in a society that requires money to survive (sort of a catch 22). So much of the content is obvious and common sense but Hyde did make me think about some of it in a different way. The parts about Walt Whitman and Ezra Pound were interesting and while they were my favorite parts, I’m not sure how they fit into the point he was trying to make, or if he even had a point. Audiobook. C-
