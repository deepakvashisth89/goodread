The synopsis on the back of my copy of this book is as follows. Since Goodreads doesn’t have one. My paperback says it’s from 1987. This is actually part of an “Under His Spell” series written by different authors.Letter carrier Cassie Larue couldn’t help drooling over the mail Andrew Bennet sent out. A student of handwriting analysis, she was intrigued by his penmanship...and by him. His dotted i’s revealed a loyal nature. His crossbars exposed energy and responsiveness. And his downstrokes....!A man with strong convictions-and a deliciously seductive bent-he is someone Cassie could fall for in a big way...once she figured out how to meet him!Well there you have it. I’ll be honest it’s kinda vague, well of course it is duh, but it is a tad misleading. Especially dealing with how or why Cassie wants to get to know Drew. The handwriting analysis is a part of the book and this was intriguing enough to get me to try this book since I haven’t read a lot of books with that knowledge involved. I was mislead by the title of the book thinking there would be some sort of crime element because of Caper in the title. My bad, no crime or mystery FYI.This was my first full length Vicki Lewis Thompson novel. I had previously read a short story of hers. This book gave me strong Nora Roberts of yore vibes, not her current work.The main characters are Drew (psychologist) and Cassie (mail carrier). While strong side characters are Andrew Bennett and Jo Reynolds. A woman named Ruth (Cassie’s friend) is a weak side character. Cassie comes off kind of childish and very annoying at times with her worrying to much. She is twenty five and didn’t finish college.There is kissing and intercourse (premarital) in this book. It is not overly descriptive. It’s not PG13, more R. This is not an erotic romance so XXX, IMO.I would read more of Thompson’s books after this read.
