Rating 4.5 stars.After finishing Lana Turner’s memoir, I had to read Cheryl Crane’s memoir for comparison and for her perspective. I am so glad I did. This is a well-written story, more vulnerable and honest than Lana’s. Reading the perspective of both mother and daughter was enlightening. The connections and impact of Lana‘s choices on Cheryl were tremendous. I enjoyed seeing Cheryl’s full life, learning, and loves, and not just her early traumas and struggles. This is a great memoir - engaging and enjoyable - that tells of life’s disappointments, challenges, and relationships. Celebrity or not, real life happens to us all.“I think everything happens for a reason, and life is seldom fair… The outrageous privilege and travail my early years strike me now as a distant adventure, a detour that I was able to survive with only the tiniest scars to show for it.“
