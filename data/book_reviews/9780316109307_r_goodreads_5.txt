This is the new lingua franca of an anti-science, anti-intellectual society in descent. Religion, armed to the teeth with dogma and ignorance, with its promise of absolute truth, is a violent assault upon reason and reality. When people act as if they know the absolute or universal truth, there is no limit to the depth of darkness to which they can descend. Absolute belief enables, empowers, and encourages dark nihilistic forces. What we are now facing is power decoupled from reality. This is the key issue with AI. AI can be a great step forward in human ascent, but AI decoupled from reality will lead to human descent.

The inspired ignorant mob is now rolling back public health achievements and taking away healthcare accomplishments as well as banning books, which is the prelude to banning people. With the re-arrival of Trump comes the end to a cultural tradition of tolerance. The U.S. will soon no longer be hospitable to imagination, innovation, cooperation, and progress. What is left of humanism, reason, and basic social sanity, is now in retreat in the face of a lurid circus of coming cruelty and ignorant brutish tyranny. Retrograde fools armed with the clumsy certainty of their monstrous religious beliefs and political ideology, further equipped with the newest tools of technology are in the ascendency. We are lurching toward another humanitarian crisis that the current generation is too stupid to see before its very own eyes!

Jacob Bronowski is of course the consummate humanist, a noble humanitarian, and a personal hero of mine. This book is more relevant now than when it was written in 1973, a year before the author’s untimely death. Relevant not for some of the scientific facts and remarks which are dated in some respects, but for the sheer humanism and unparalleled optimism Bronowski brings to the subject of human development. I think some of the more critical reviewers of this book focus too much on the ostensible and dated scientific claims and miss the deeper timeless nature of Bronowski’s approach which is about the nexus between human imagination, human action and human progress which drives human ascent. This book is an intellectual history which it makes it supremely relevant in an anti-intellectual age. Every era, age and civilization has continuities and turning points and I believe we are at a turning point now. A secular age of reason can give way to a subsequent age of religious violence and superstition. This has happened in the past and is currently underway now. One example, from the book, in 1973 the author could seriously state, without controversy, “The theory of evolution is no longer a battleground.” Need I say more?

From a book written fifty years ago, to wit: “And yet, fifty years from now, if an understanding of man’s origins, his evolution, his history, his progress is not the commonplace of the schoolbooks, we shall not exist.”

One Drawback

This book is essentially the script for the televised series of the same name hosted by Jacob Bronowski. As such, at times the book flows more like a spoken script meant to be paired with missing illustrations and visual demonstrations than it does a narrative or readable text.
