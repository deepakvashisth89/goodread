Caveat: This book is 5 stars ONLY if you like sappy dog-rescue vignettes. Apparently, I love them, and thus I loved this book.My Review:I think I'm becoming a dog-book junkie. This collection of owner's descriptions of dogs is, to me, a fascinating look at the way people view their relationships to their favorite pets. The people interviewed in this book are from all walks - the famous, the ordinary, the unknown (including a homeless man).Yes, this is one of those 'feel good' books that some people hate and some people love. I loved it. (Interestingly, I saw this book at Lucky Dog Used Books in Dallas - wasn't looking for it, but there it was. Ha!)
