I finally got my hands on the first twoCitizen Dogcollections, and I had barely removed the wrapping when I was already fully immersed in the world of dog "I Do Whatever I Want" Fergus, his owner "Why Me?!" Mel, cat "The Unheard Voice of Reason" Cuddles and all the other hilarious beings - animals and humans alike - which populate Mark O'Hare's unfortunately short-lived comic.Deep down, Fergus - though fully anthropomorphised - is still just a dog, obsessed with food, playing (though not exactly your typical canine games) and wrecking Mel's home. Who really is the master here?The very early strips look somewhat clumsy and it took O'Hare a while until he found the characters' final form, but once that had happened, they were a pure delight to look and laugh at. Or rather with. Or often both.It's a shame all three collections are out of print. They should be reissued as quickly as possible.
