Proud but dispossessed ... They were THE SURVIVORSA corrupt English king and a weak French monarch -- between them they controlled half the world. But they were ruled by their favorites -- foppish courtiers who'd use the king's bedchamber to achieve their goals, or wily churchmen who believed that ends justified means.Against this turbulent background, determined, passionate Shanna O'Hara searched for peace in her beloved but troubled Ireland. Meanwhile in England, hot-tempered Brenna Coke fought against a loveless marriage.And Rory O'Hara sought to help both of them survive!
