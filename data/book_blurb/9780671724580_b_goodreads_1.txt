By the end of 1943, all evidence of the abortive German attempt to assassinate Winston Churchill has been carefully buried in an unmarked grave in the Norfolk village of Studley Constable. German intelligence knows that paratroop officer Steiner survived "Operation Eagle", and is now a POW somewhere in London. Reichsfuhrer Himmler wants him back – at any cost – and puts his top espionage agents in charge of the perilous rescue mission.Racing from the nightclubs of Lisbon to Hitler's opulent country retreat to the damp streets of London, Himmler's men get closer to their target – and to the shattering true objective of their mission. . .
