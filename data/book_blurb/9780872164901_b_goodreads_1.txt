In A World Of Deceit And Danger, Love Was The Deadliest Risk Of All.Beautiful, iron-willed heiress to power, Joanna secretly burns with an explosive inner passion as wild and radiant as her flaming read hair. But her deepest emotions are tragically frozen by the cold fear of a man's tender love.Ensnared in the violent lusts and dangerous intrigues of King John's decadent court, she defies every outward peril -- only to come face-to-face with the terror in her own heart. Caught between willful pride and consuming desire, she struggles to avoid surrendering herself to the irresistible fires raging within her.
