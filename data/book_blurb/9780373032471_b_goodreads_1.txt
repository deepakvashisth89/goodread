Sometimes history repeats itself....When Leah Blain was nineteen, she met handsome Vincenzo Petruzzi, and he swept her off her feet. Unfortunately, their marriage was a disaster from the start, and she left him--vowing never to return to him or to Italy.But her promise only lasted five years, until the day she found herself back in Rome, trying to prevent her sister and Vincenzo's nephew from making the same mistake. Trouble was, the two young lovers had gone into hiding, which meant she had to ask Vincenzo for help....
