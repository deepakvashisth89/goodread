Rose Bell and Antonia Ashton were coworkers in the operations room of the 651 Squadron during World War II. Now the war is over, and the women have settled down to domestic life—in Rose’s case, a life of dreary poverty. Britain is gripped by food and clothing shortages, and no one seems as glamorous out of uniform as in.Rose is married to former wing commander Barry Bell, now a civil servant in the Stationary Office. Antonia is married to a wealthy Czech industrialist. Both want to get rid of their husband...Rose because Barry is an abusive philanderer, Antonia because she wants to take off for America with her lover.Antonia, headstrong and practical, formulates a scheme to eliminate the mates. For starters, Barry’s proposed fate involves the London Tubes, and one good shove in the proper spot...
