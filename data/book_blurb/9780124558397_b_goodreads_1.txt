RFCs -- Requests For Comments -- are extremely useful sources of information on the various standards and protocols governing Internet computing. Written by experts in many different subspecialties and evaluated according to strict guidelines of the Internet Engineering Task Force, these documents are the means by which hundreds of thousands of engineers share with one another their insigts to Internetworking theory and practice. Pete Loshin has written several books about the Internet, extranets, TCP/IP networking and network security; has contributed to such magazines as PC World, PC Magazine, and Communications Week; and formerly was an editor at BYTE Magazine.
