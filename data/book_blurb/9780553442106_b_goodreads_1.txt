BRYCE-His Wicked Grin Held a Hundred Sexy Promises...He had a reputation as a fun-loving rogue, but once Bryce LaSalle met the pretty, prickly stranger in town, he suddenly wanted to protect and care for her! A feisty survivor with eyes the color of bluebells and hair like a field of buttercups, Ellis seemed determined to work three jobs and save every cent she could, hinting that she'd left a piece of her heart back home.Playing With Him Was Playing With FireBryce's teasing and flirting made her feel precious, adored--and she trembled with longing to fan the flames of ecstasy with a daredevil sweetheart who would be both friend and lover. He was so much more than she'd ever dreamed of having, but once he'd made her blush with pleasure and blossom in his arms, would she have the courage to risk the little she had for all he offered?
