New Novel Rips Lid Off Terrifying Hidden Agenda in America In Sin in the Camp Nathan Little has created a good life for himself. A respected teacher in a small and friendly community, he finds his world shattered by forces that he cannot see and does not know how to defend himself against. But the real terror is not what happens to the characters of Sin in the Camp. Nor is it that it could happen to anyone, anywhere. The real terror is that it does happen to someone, somewhere. Everyday.
