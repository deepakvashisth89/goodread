Spanning two countries and the turbulent era of the 1960's, the Viet Nam war and social conflict, A Forest Burning is a suspense-filled novel about memory, truth and forgiveness."Carole Giangrande's rich and ambitious new novel, A Forest Burning , is a story of generations of loss, soul baring, and secrets."-- Quill & Quire"In chronicling three generations of war-inflicted loss, in crisscrossing the U.S.-Canadian border during the years of war and protest, Giangrande takes on serious themes and handles them with confidence. And her language is carefully wrought, often epigrammatic."-- The Danforth Review
