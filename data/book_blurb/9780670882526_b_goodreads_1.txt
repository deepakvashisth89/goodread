When Juno receives a letter with little blue and red marks around the envelope, he knows it's from his grandmother in faraway Korea. He also knows that he can't read the inside without help from his parents. After all, it's written in his grandmother's language.But when Mom and Dad are too busy to read it to him, Juno can't wait to open it. What he finds inside the letter turns out to be more than just a piece of paper with peculiar looking shapes. Grandmother has sent a picture and a flower. Juno realizes immediately that he can understand a lot more than he expected to, because the picture and the flower tell him so much.
