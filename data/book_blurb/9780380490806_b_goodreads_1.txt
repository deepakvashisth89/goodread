She has rubies in her teeth, a crimson skirt, and not a shred of skin on her bones. She’s burlesque queen Ruby Redd, missing these past thirty years, and Sarah Kelling has just discovered her sprawled grotesquely in the family vault.Sarah’s nightmare has just begun. As her dear, rich relations hover menacingly close by, she will watch in horror as more skeletons tumble out of the Kelling closet in a macabre montage of murder, greed, and blackmail.
