After more than a decade Vance has returned to the glowing and phatasmagoric future inCugel’s Saga, where once more Cugel, marooned half a world away from home by the nefarious Iuconnu, must use his determination and flamboyant wit to work his way through mud pits and bars, through villages, manor houses and across the seas, drawn by sea worms back to the land of Almery to gain vengeance utter and ironic upon the Laughing Magician.
