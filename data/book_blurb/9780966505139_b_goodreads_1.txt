Adopting a child from a foreign country can be a bewildering challenge, fraught with uncertainty and confounded by exotic cultures and practices. Barbara Birdsey knows this better than most, having been the founder of an international adoption agency, a parent of an adopted child from Central America, and a defender of victims' rights. In this intimate account of her adventures, Birdsey introduces us to the cast of characters who inhabit the labyrinthian world of international adoption and lets us learn from her experiences, including her mistakes.
