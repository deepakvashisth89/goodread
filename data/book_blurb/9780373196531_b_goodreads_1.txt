Perry Square: where love is found...and lasts forever.HE NEEDED A FIANCEE...FAST! With his dark good looks and talent for seeking justice, Elias Donovan thought he had the best recipe for making partner in his law firm. But according to his family-oriented boss, Donovan lacked one crucial ingredient: a wife! So he did what any red-blooded make would do--he claimed he had a fiancee, neighbor Sarah Madison.Practical-minded Sarah accepted Donovan's platonic "proposal" and desparately tried to ignore the way her heart raced in his presence. But as their pretend relationship snowballed into planning an actual wedding, and Sarah glimpsed the romantic Romeo hidden inside her soon-to-be husband, she could no longer deny her very real feelings...Welcome back to Perry Square: where love is found...and lasts forever!
