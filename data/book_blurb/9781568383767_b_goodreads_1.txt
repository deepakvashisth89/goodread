This collection of prayers and inspirational readings was compiled to assist members of all 12 Step Fellowships with their prayer life and spiritual progress.Those who are just beginning their life of recovery will find help with working the Eleventh Step in this book. Those who have time in the Program will find a deepening of their spiritual life. Anyone who has had trouble finding the "right words" to speak with their Higher Power may find exactly what they want to say in one of these prayers.The table of contents will help in selecting a specific topic, and the Guide for Daily Reading offers and easy-to-follow method of using this book.However you may define your Higher Power, may this collection be of assistance to you in your spiritual growth.(The 12 Step Prayer Book is a companion volume to Easy Does It: A Book of Daily 12 Step Meditations.)
