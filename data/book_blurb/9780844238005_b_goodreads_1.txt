This is a complete course in spoken and written Irish. If you have never learned Irish before, or if your Irish needs brushing up,Teach Yourself Irishis for you.Diarmuid O Se and Joseph Sheils have created a practical course that is both fun and easy to work through. They explain everything clearly along the way and give you plenty of opporunities to practice what you learned. The course structure allows you to work at your own pace, arranging your learning to suit your needs.
