From the author of AVENGE THE BELGRANO The future of South Africa ... When a high-ranking South African minister begins talks with the British Goverment, a crack ANC teamlands in England at the same time. When the minister travels north to visit relatives, war-hardened Kate, a British nurse, Elliot Chamille, a rich American and Harry Fuller, ex-lover of Kate, hijack the train. Hunted by British marines and South African agents, they escape into the huge Kielder forest, only to be threatened by a huge forest fire. They soon realise however that the fire is the only escape route.
