With over six million of his books in print worldwide, Stanislaw Lem is perhaps the most popular & most critically acclaimed science fiction writer of our day. In THE STAR DIARIES he recounts the further adventures of the delightfully wacky spaceman Ijon Tichy who whizzes through time & deep space in his interstellar Model T--running out of gas between planets, encountering space gypsies who clean moons for a living, robot theologians who live in caves & renegade potatoes who destroy spaceships. Somewhere out there in the universe he meets his "tomorrow self," quarrels with Plato & reflects on the natural decency of electronic brains.THE STAR DIARIES is a magnificently surrealistic GULLIVER'S TRAVELS in outer space that makes pleasant mockery of our 20th century beliefs & institutions.
