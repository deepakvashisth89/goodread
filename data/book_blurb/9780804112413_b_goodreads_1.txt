Hotshot Chicago attorney Kate Millholland became a lawyer to avoid the gauntlet of parties that -- in her parents' wealthy world -- fill the gap between debutante and bride. To her surprise, she fell in love with the law.Now, at the request of a colleague, Kate agrees to represent Superior Plating & Specialty Chemicals owned by the Cavanaughs, a family as corrosive as the chemicals they produce. She never expects to uncover the sordid, fatal secrets that bind the Cavanaughs together.The least of which is murder....
