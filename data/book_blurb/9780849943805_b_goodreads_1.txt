One man holds the key to finding the ark of the covenant. In this explosive sequel to Blessed Child , Rebecca Soloman leads a team deep into the Ethiopian desert to hunt the one man who may know the final resting place of the Ark of the Covenant. But Islamic fundamentalists fear that the Arks discovery will compel Israel to rebuild Solomans temple on the very site of their own holy mosque in Jerusalem. They immediately dispatch Ismael, their most accomplished assassin, to pursue the same man, but the one in their sights is no ordinary man. His name is Caleb, and he is also on a quest--to find again the love he once embraced as a child. The fate of a million souls rests in the hands of these three.
