A young Viking girl embarks on a quest of peril and adventure when she pledges to save her village against the incursions of the Grey Folk, drowned victims of ancient Norse rituals. Set in the primitive cultures of Iceland and Canada of 1000 AD, this is a tale of withcraft, murder and magic. Tom Henighan is Associate Professor of English Literature at Carleton University, Ottawa, Canada. His previous publications include "Tourists from Algol", a collection of short stories.
