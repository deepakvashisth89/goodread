America's preeminent master of the fantastic brings to life these marvelous beasts of prehistory in this masterful work including: “A Sound of Thunder,” the tale of a deadly time-travel safari; “The Fog Horn,” of a creature from the deep and its strange rendezvous with a lighthouse; “Tyrannosaurus Rex,” about a movie monster-maker’s macabre revenge; a new “Green Town” story written just for this volume; plus a new Bradbury-Gahan Wilson collaboration, an ode to dancing dinosaurs; a Foreword by legendary filmmaker Ray Harryhausen; an Introduction by the author; and over 60 pages of fantastic illustrations by William Stout, Steranko, Moebius, Overton Loyd, Kenneth Smith and David Wiesner.* Besides a Dinosaur, Whatta Ya Wanna Be When You Grow Up?* A Sound of Thunder* Lo, the Dear, Daft Dinosaurs!* The Fog Horn* What If I Said: The Dinosaur's Not Dead* Tyrannosaurus Rex
