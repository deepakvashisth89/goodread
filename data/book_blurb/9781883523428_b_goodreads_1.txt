This novel exposes a lifetime of sorrows that have been held close to the hearts of a group of female friends. Meeting informally over several years, the women, like all women, have shared secrets, heartaches, losses, and emotional pain. When one confesses she has just discovered she is pregnant and that the baby is not her husband's, the confession pushes them to a place of action. Drawing on the passion and love that has bonded them together--and a bit of wine--they reach a decision that turns into a life-altering expression of personal growth and forgiveness. Their bold actions not only change their own lives, but the lives of men and women who hear about their journey and encounter them on the highway. This is a story about love, friendship, and letting go, and also about how one step can change a world. It is an inspiring look at the power of the bonds women forge among themselves. It is a book full of the realities of life and the joys of challenge and change.
