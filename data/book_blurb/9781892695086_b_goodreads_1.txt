It`s Carnaval time in exotic Rio de Janeiro and Tony Kozol`s been invited. Working for Luis Angel, a hot Latin pop singing sensation, Tony Kozol, guitarist and amateur sleuth, packs his bags and heads for Rio. Love is in the air. Samba is in the streets. . . And murder is around the next corner. In THE BODY FROM IPANEMA, Tony and his bass-playing friend, Rock, find themselves surrounded by beauty and intrigue in a land where not all is as it seems. . .
