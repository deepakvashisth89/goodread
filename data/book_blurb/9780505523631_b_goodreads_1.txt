THE LUCKIEST MAN ALIVE?Fearless English earl Thomas Wentworth scoffed at failure, trusting in the legendary Wentworth luck to safeguard his spy mission to Scotland. But a single encounter with saucy Scotswoman Fia MacLean turned Thomas's mission topsy-turvy --- and tested his hereditary luck to the limits.The very embodiment of madcap misfortune, Fia toppled Thomas from a castle window. Saddled him with a mangy mongrel, a knock-kneed nag, and a rotund rabbit he'd sooner roast for supper. Worse, her honeyed lips and kissable curves set him aflame with foolish wanting. And soon she'd all-too-innocently entangled him in an enemy trap! His only escape? To wed the capricious chit! Battered, bewildered, on the brink of disaster, brave Thomas quaked. For his newly betrothed had just begun to bedevil him!
