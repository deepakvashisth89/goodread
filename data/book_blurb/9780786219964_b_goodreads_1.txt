In the bitter cold of a Montreal winter and the gothic darkness of a convent graveyard, Dr. Temperance Brennan is digging for a corpse buried more than one hundred years ago. Tempe thrives on enigmas from the past, but it's contemporary deaths and disappearances that are about to seize her attention and test her courage. People are vanishing and ultimately turning up dead, and Tempe is ideally placed to make a connection among the seemingly unrelated events. Using her forensic skills—from the crime scene to the morgue to the lab—she probes a mystery that begins with horrifying deaths in a Quebec fire, leads to some startling discovering in the Carolinas, and culminates in Montreal in a terrifying showdown.
