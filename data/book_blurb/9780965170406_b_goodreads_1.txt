This unique collection of scandalous activities and pursuits provides a historic reference to the dark side of American history. Covering infamous scandals from the 1630s to the present,The New Encyclopedia of American Scandalfills an important historical niche, tackling lurid subject matter without itself being lurid. Updated throughout, it includes figures and events from politics, business, sports, religion, the arts, high society, and the military. Entries include the Mayflower Madam, Patty Hearst, Warren G. Harding, the Kennedy men, the Kent State Shootings, and Watergate. Deceit, bribery, sex, power, murder, and fame-all of the elements are captured here in this uncensored account of scandals throughout American history.Praise for the previous edition: "...informational as well as entertaining."George Childs has written or edited numerous reference works, includingThe Dictionary of Historic Documents,The Encyclopedia of Plague and Pestilence, andThe Dictionary of Wars(all for Facts On File). He lives in Madison, CT.
