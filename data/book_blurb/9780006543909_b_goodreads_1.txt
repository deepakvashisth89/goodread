Robert, a young pianist, is making his solo début at London's Wigmore Hall. His parents and friends are scattered in the audience, although, Johnny, his lover, is notably missing. As the music fills the hall, his thoughts and theirs recall the events and feelings that bind or fail to bind them to one another and which now form part of what they are - whether loving, evasive, predatory or defensive. Christopher explores for us a mother's love for her son, a father's fantasies and the dramatic world of homosexual encounters.
