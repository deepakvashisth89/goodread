It all begins in Bacon's Corner, a tiny farming community far from the interstate.... An attempted murder, a case of mistaken—or covered up?—identify, and a ruthless lawsuit against a struggling school. Sally Beth Roe, a young loner, finds herself caught in the middle of these bizarre events, fleeing for her life while trying to recall her dark past.Heart-stopping action, a penetrating glimpse into our times, and a vivid portrayal of the power of redemption.
