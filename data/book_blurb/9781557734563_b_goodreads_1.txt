A provocative debut by an exciting new author, Friends and Fortunes delves into the lives of four women who, although their lives grow in different directions, share a bond that will guide them through a roller coaster ride of lies, money, glamor, and success--the seduction that is Hollywood. Four memorable, sharply chiseled women who literally jump off the pages and into your life. I couldn't put it down!--Judith Gould, author of Sins and Never Too Rich.
