A SMALL SOUTHERN TOWN ROCKED BY PASSION, GREED...AND LOVESouthern born and bred, Cara Chastain wasusuallygenteel. But nothing flared her temper more than the hotshot Texan named Lee Powers, who came to "develop" her beloved North Carolina hometown. His plans threatened the island community's most precious gift - the last of the wild Spanish mustangs - and Cara vowed to save these beautiful creatures...Even if her daring struggle turned the town upside-down.Even if Lee Powers fought her every step of the way.Even if she fell in love with him.
