In 1970 Helene Hanff wrote 84, CHARING CROSS ROAD, a record of a postal love affair with England through a twenty-year correspondence with a London bookseller. In both hardcovers and the Equinox paperbound edition, the book found thousands of devoted readers. Their fervent wish -- and hers above all -- was that she go to England. Now at last in THE DUCHESS OF BLOOMSBURY STREET that dream comes true.
