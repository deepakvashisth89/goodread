The author ofHow to Prepare Your Manuscript for a Publishernow presents more essential advice for fiction and nonfiction writers. This reference provides a toolbox of literary stratagems and shortcuts to help writers save time, improve style, and avoid common pitfalls. It includes practical tips for more expressive, colorful writing; using stylistic tricks; improving structure; correcting and rewriting; developing “writer’s logic”; avoiding burnout, boredom, and lack of motivation; dealing with writer's block; and reworking scholarly prose for improved clarity.
