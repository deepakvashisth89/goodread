In this sixth volume of her popular series, Cassandra Eason sheds new light on the ancient technique of dowsing. Her easy-to-follow instructions show you how to read these intuitive signals from your own unconscious mind by holding a crystal pendulum over a runic cloth with option-diagrams and flow charts. Once learned your dowsing skills can be adapted for healing tarot readings and exploring past lives. A genuinely original book on women's self-development!
