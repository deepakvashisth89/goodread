With the tips you get in this breezy, humorous book, you'll organize your kitchen, your time there, and your meal-preparation methods. You'll find the extra minutes - maybe hours! - you used to spend looking for misplaced gadgets. And you'll find those, too.Think how nice it will be when you apply the 80/20 Principle and manage your time better; develop a find-it-every-time, easy-reach storage system; create work centers that save you steps; put all that wasted drawer and cupboard space to good use; plan nutritious menus in minutes, and save hours and dollars; make a master shopping list and play the grocery game to win; ease and speed kitchen clean-up chores; and plan for successful parties and peaceful holidays.
