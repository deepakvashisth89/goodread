Final Exitoffers the terminally ill choices on how & when to end their suffering. It gives instructions to doctors, nurses & families on handling euthanasia requests. In the wake of court cases & legislative mandates, it covers the legality of euthanasia & assisted suicide, examining the personal issues involved. It helps loved ones & supportive doctors remain within the law & keep a person's dying private & dignified. It spells out why a living will may be insufficient to have a person's wishes carried out & what document is a better alternative. It shows where to get proper drugs & how to make a peacefully quick final exit.
