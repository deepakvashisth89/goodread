Of Earth but not from Earth, Penna cherishes her interstellar heritage. Through her disobedience, Penna allows herself to be swept by the Cosmic Storm from Nenmar to Earth, where she lives out her dream of being with her human relatives she has never seen. Penna's nocturnal Nenmaran eyes make her life with humans both awkward and rewarding. She loses her navet but not her innocence. In Penna, the Alien Earthling, you are sure to love the story of this brave and resourceful young alien as she manages life in the world of her human kindred.
