The twelfth and final volume in a series of Star Trek: The Original Series novelizations, written by James Blish and published by Bantam Books, completed by J.A. Lawrence after the death of James Blish.Includes five stories:Patterns of ForceThe Gamesters of TriskellionAnd the Children Shall LeadThe Corbomite ManeuverShore LeaveFrom the back cover:Kirk: SlaveMcCoy: MurderedSpock: Outlaw—in a universe gone mad!A universe where worlds are ruled by neo-Nazi dictators...A universe that immunizes children to the wholesale slaughter of their parents...A universe where the slightest thought unleashes total terror...Captain Kirk, Mr. Spock, Dr. McCoy and the crew of the Starship Enterprise enter the darkest dimensions of existence – on their newest exploration of unknown worlds.
