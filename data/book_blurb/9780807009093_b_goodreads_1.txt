Written at the height of the Harlem Renaissance in 1929, Plum Bun tells the story of Angela Murray, a young black girl from Philadelphia who discovers she can 'pass' for white.After her parents die, she moves to New York to escape the racism she believes is the only obstacle to a life of opportunity. In search of romance and artistic success, she changes her name and lives as a white woman. But she soon discovers that passing is no panacea. When she confronts discrimination as a woman as well, she realizes that love and marriage may not be her best salvation.
