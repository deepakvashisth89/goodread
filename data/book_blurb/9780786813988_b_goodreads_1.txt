Fi is a computer whiz with an obsession for all things weird. Together with her brother, Jack, and their rock star mom, Molly, they crisscross the United States while mom jams with her band. Along the way Fi tries to solve all kinds of supernatural mysteries -- everything from ghosts to UFOs -- even the legend of Bigfoot.So Weird is Disney Channel's new #1 series airing weekly on Friday through Sunday nights starring Mackenzie Phillips from TV's One Day at a Time.Everyone knows the legend of Bigfoot, but they don't really believe in a huge, hairy monster wandering around a forest. But when Fi gets lost in the woods and is rescued by a mysterious creature, she relies on her motto that nothing is too weird to be true.
