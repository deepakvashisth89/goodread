The Nez Perce had believed that their vast and beautiful valley was big enough for both red man and white--and that the United States government would honor all its treaties. THey were mistaken. This is the story of the Nex Perce War and the men who fought it: Chief Joseph, the great leader whose heart held only peace; Toohoolhoolzote, fierce and implacable in his hatred of the white man; One Hand Howard, the ambitious U.S. general who hopes to humble them both; Heyets, the young brave who must find his destiny and his manhood in the midst of war.
