A Healing Heart...Dr. Dan Conway, wounded while saving a Comanche child from brutality, had been brought to a Comanche camp. His healing powers soon became legendary, yet he wanted no payment for curing the Indians of the "white man's disease." He wanted the forbidden: Morning Light, sister to the great Comanche chief.White men brought wrenching sorrow--this Morning Light knew only too well. Yet here was Pale Wolf, a healer, a hero--and a white man. Though he was not Comanche, his touch held the promise of paradise. But how could her heart be joined to a man with the face of an enemy?
