Snyder's second book (afterRiprap), first edition 1960 in stapled wraps by Totem Press, first New Directions and first hardcover edition 1978. The first printing of the New Directions edition has Snyder's name misspelt 'Synder' on both book and jacket.The three sequences in the book—"Logging," "Hunting," "Burning"—show the remarkable cohesiveness in Snyder's writings over the years, for we find the poet absorbed, then as now, with Buddhist and Amerindian lore and other interconnections East and West, but above all with the premedical devotion to the land and work.
