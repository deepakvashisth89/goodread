Little did Ruth McKenna know that love awaited her amid the ruins of an Indian pueblo. Recently widowed, she'd left the comforts of St. Louis for the wild Arizona Territory, thinking she would join her long-lost brother. Upon arriving, she discovered him dead, the victim of illness-or foul play. Could his partner, Gladius Blase, know more than he is telling?Blade had vowed to help Ruth's brother find an ancient treasure. Convinced that the diamonds must be forever buried under the stones of prehistory, Blade had all but given up the search. But driven by Ruth's belief, together they looked for the fabled treasure, only to discover a more precious one in their own hearts.
