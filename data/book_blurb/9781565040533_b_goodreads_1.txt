The Vampire Players Guide Second Edition is an updated version of VTM: The Players Guide for the Vampire: The Masquerade Second Edition rules.A large portion of the book's text is reprinted, updated, and revised from the previous version of the Players Guide to fit the Second Edition's rules and formatting style.Notable among the new content in this edition of the Players Guide is the introduction of two new bloodlines, the Daughters of Cacophony and the Samedi, along with their respective signature Disciplines.
