Each morning, a young girl wakes up to find something in her bed — a purring, furry footwarmer named Pearl. Pearl won’t stay sleepy-eyed for long, though, not when there are paper bags to crawl in, toys to bat about, and all the other adventures that await a lively kitty. Turner’s wonderfully vibrant illustrations not only capture a cat’s every move and mood, they capture the heart of anyone who has ever known and loved a cat.
