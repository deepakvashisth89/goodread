Out in the big, bright world, it is usually the mommy and daddy who announce their baby's arrival. Well, this baby's announcing itself. Now. See, the mommy is keeping things hush-hush, and her cold shoulder has the daddy in a dither. But the mommy has always loved the daddy . . . and, although they were very close the night the baby appeared, the daddy has never uttered the three little words. But babies are all about hope . . . and this baby won't stand for anything but a happy ending.
