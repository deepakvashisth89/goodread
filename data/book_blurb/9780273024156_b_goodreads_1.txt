This is the market leading all-purpose introductory legal text used on a wide variety of courses. It is a comprehensive book that combines text and cases, allowing students to read the facts and decisions of over 500 cases. The book has a considerable reputation for reliability and accuracy, which has been established over 35 years. It covers the English legal system, contract law, tort law, employment and criminal law. It features an update we offer 6-monthly updates via a subscription service for lecturers-this shows how specific passages in the book are affected by legal changes. Additionally, a companion web site gives students and lecturers further access to Update Bulletins and sets of relevant links, as well as other learning materials.
