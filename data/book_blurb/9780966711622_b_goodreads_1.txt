The Clarks and Clarkes are at it again! From Billy and Drab's peanut feud to Mrs. Effie's diet, to Camouflage Clark's latest hunting disguise, you'll howl with laughter at the antics of the folks who live on Heartache and Hunger Creek. Bashin' Bob Clarke is the preacher of the Heartache and Hunger Creek Baptist Church in this imaginary town in Georgia. His simple approach to the townsfolk's problems is at the heart of this collection of short stories that will delight readers of all ages. Written as newspaper columns by the Heartache and Hunger Creek Sentinel's editor, Goat Herder (the only character in the book not named Clark or Clarke), the stories reflect the day to day goings on in a small Southern town, where a major event can be a wedding, a baptising, or a fast running dear in the middle of hunting season. Slapstick humor vies with common sense for center stage. As you meet the folks on Heartache and Humger Creek, you're sure to find someone you already know.
