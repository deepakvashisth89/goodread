Why is it that some superstitions of the past persist in the present? Why do we, in an age of increasing knowledge, still retain some of our fears… the offsprings of ignorance? We can understand the perpetuation of a custom, even when its inner significance has gone, but a living superstition is a different thing. It is the hardest thing in the world to shake off superstitious prejudices; they take fast hold and make lasting impressions, becoming so interwoven with our very constitutions that the strongest sense is required to disengage ourselves from them. On the pages of this most revealing and informative book you will find a comprehensive listing of every superstition and custom known to man.The Origins of Popular Superstitions and Customsis broken down into four sections. Each section contains an abundance of interesting bits of history.Amulets, Astrology, Kissing the Bride, Charms, Crystal Gazing, Divining Rod, Drinking Customs, Dreams, Halloween, Lucky Stars and Months, Occult Powers, Omens, Palmistry, Playing Card Superstitions, Talismans, Toasts, Vampires, and Witches are just a few of the subjects covered in this most unusual book.
