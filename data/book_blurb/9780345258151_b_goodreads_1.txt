The fifth in a series of Star The Animated Series adaptations published by Ballantine Books. - The Ambergris Element (The crew of the Enterprise explore a submerged culture on a water world.)- The Pirates Of Orion (While Spock lies ill, Orion pirates hijack the drug shipment desperately needed to save his life.)- The Jihad (The Vedala, the oldest known space faring race, summon Kirk and Spock to recover the Soul of the Skorr, a stolen religious artifact that has the potential to ignite a holy war across the galaxy.)
