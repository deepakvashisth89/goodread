YOU MAY KISS...THE MAID OF HONOR!When her flighty sister decided she couldn't go through with her wedding, Abby Davis reluctantly agreed to give the groom-to-be the bad news. And when the prospective groom was assigned to active duty, his best man, Pete O'Brian, offered to make his excuses to his comrade's jilted bride. So, of course, Pete greeted Abby with a kiss! Hell, she was almost family, right?Abby was horrified at first, but...talk about your holiday surprises! She had expected to find her sister's spurned fiance. Instead, Pete was looking to her like one groom... with all the trimmings!HOLIDAY HONEYMOONS: Because when you combine weddings with holidays, something magical happens!
