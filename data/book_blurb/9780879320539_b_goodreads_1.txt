With the same drug-addled alacrity & jaundiced wit that made Fear & Loathing in Las Vegas a hilarious hit, Hunter S. Thompson turns his savage eye & gonzo heart to the repellent & seductive race for President. He deconstructs the 1972 campaigns of idealist George McGovern & political hack Richard Nixon, ending up with a political vision that is eerily prophetic.First serialized by Rolling Stone Magazine in 1972, the published as a book by Straight Arrow in 1973.
