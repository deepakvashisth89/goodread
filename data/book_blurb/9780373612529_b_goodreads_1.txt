Check-in: TV news reporter Laurel London has booked a room at the new Twin Oaks B and B, and so has noted travel writer William Byrd. Owners Clint and Maureen Cooper are hoping for a great review!Checkout: Then suddenly, William Byrd vanishes. Policeman Scott Hunter is on the case -- and Laurel is determined to be in on the action. But Scott and Laurel share a painful history. His brother -- her fiance -- died tragically. Can cop and reporter mend their heartbreak, join their hearts...and get to the bottom of Byrd's disappearance?
