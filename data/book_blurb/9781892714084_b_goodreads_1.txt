Dr. James E. Shaw is one of the nation's foremost youth violence experts and is a regular guest on NBC Today, Good Morning America, The O'Reilly Factor, MSNBC Live, CNN TalkBack, FOX News, and Associated Press Radio. He spent 4 years inside state youth prisons interviewing and studying girls and boys incarcerated for committing murder and homicide before they were 16 years of age. The only researcher in the country allowed to go inside prisons and behind bars to get the life stories of children who killed, Dr. Shaw wrote the landmark, nationally-acclaimed book, "Jack and Jill, Why They Kill," as a result of his 48-month study of 103 incarcerated girls and boys. Thirteen of their stories frame this literary tour de force. More importantly, as Dr. Shaw states, "These children used me as a scribe and messenger to tell parents the solutions to student violence in our schools, and they spoke eloquently as they answered the seemingly eternal question of how to rear a vi! olence- and gun-free child." Referred to on CNN TalkBack Live as the "The smart answer for today's troubled times," "Jack and Jill, Why They Kill" is a book that was written specifically for this age of violence. Directed to parents, this book also has a wide readership among school administrators, teachers, social workers, law enforcement officers, districts attorney, probation officers, members of the clergy, psychologists, medical doctors, judges, and students themselves. A favorite of the media, "Jack and Jill, Why They Kill" makes a magnificent contribution to the national effort to rescue our children. They have never been in greater danger except for the horrid era of child labor exploitation in the coal mines and sweatshops of America. As Dr. Shaw notes, "Our country is not presently at war with any other nation; yet our own children have declared war on themselves. They must be rescued, for they are in greater danger than they have ever been in the last 100 years."
