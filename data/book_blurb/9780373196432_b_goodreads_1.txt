All Princess Teresa of Corsaria wanted was a break from the "glamorous" world of society galas and gossip columns. But when her car broke down in a little North Carolina town, she found something far more valuable. She found a place that welcomed a stranger -- a stranger who called herself plain old Tess McDougall -- with open arms.Then she met Dylan Parker, the town's doctor, and she ached to end her innocent masquerade. But what would this honest, upstanding and oh-so-compelling man -- a man so very different from any she had ever known -- do when he learned the truth about the woman in his arms...?
