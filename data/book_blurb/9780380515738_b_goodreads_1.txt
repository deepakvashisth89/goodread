A Classic Treasury of Jewish DramaThe Great Jewish Plays presents five of the most important plays from the rich heritage of the Yiddish theatre. The editor, Joseph C. Landis, has chosen the plays in this remarkable collection to present both the variety and quality of Yiddish drama. The exciting world of Yiddish drama was essentially a phenomenon of the twentieth century. The traditional values have given it the greater concept of “mentshlekhkayt”, of the compassionate, socially responsible, dedicated being, the tragic hero. And the modern art theatres, seething with experimentation in new forms, quickened the Yiddish dramatists to explore the possibilities of realism, of allegory and symbolism. The plays included here are foremost examples of these two currents and are of primary importance in the history of Yiddish theatre.
