They are the incredible women who reach for the stars. From Washington to Paris to Hollywood, they're always where the big decisions - and the big money - are made. And they don't mind taking their men along for the ride....Judith--She rose from poverty to marry one of the wealthiest men in Boston. She would use her hard-won influence to make sure her only son was elected president of the United States.Carlotta--Judith's beautiful young aunt and a successful movie actress, she learned early that danger turned her on, even more than the love that was well within her reach.Francesca--Carlotta's pretty blond sister, she had always settled for second best. But when Carlotta rejected Captain Bill Sheridan, Francesca married him -- and became the First Lady of Florida when her husband became governor.Jade--Carlotta's daughter, she inherited her mother's sensuous beauty,andher secret diaries. It was up to her to untangle the web of intrigue that had tainted her family for generations.
