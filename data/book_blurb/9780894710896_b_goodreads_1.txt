Being a Blank Book with Some Curious Illustrations of Friends and Foes of the Nine Companions by Sam GamgeeA real find for Tolkien aficionados, this "rediscovered" journal of Hobbit Sam Gamgee is enhanced by his whimsical, two-color sketches of many familiar denizens of Middle-earth. It provides ample space for recording personal thoughts, reflections on Tolkien's masterpiece, or fantasies of your own creation.
