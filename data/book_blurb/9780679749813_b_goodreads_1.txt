From the assassination that triggered WW I to the ethnic warfare in Serbia, Bosnia & Croatia, the Balkans have been the crucible of the 20th century, the place where terrorism & genocide 1st became tools of policy. Chosen as one of the Best Books of the Year byThe NY Times, & greeted with critical acclaim as "the most insightful & timely work on the Balkans to date" byThe Boston Globe, Kaplan's prescient, enthralling & often chilling political travelogue is already a modern classic.
