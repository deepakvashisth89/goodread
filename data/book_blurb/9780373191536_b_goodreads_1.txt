MISS JANE DOE OR...Rancher Parker Robinson was having one of those weeks. His cook quit and his rambunctious daughter was trying to get herself expelled from school. And to top it off, he found a gorgeous woman on his ranch who didn't know who she was!ANOTHER MAN'S WIFE?Christine had no memory before the accident-but she was sure she would remember Parker's tender warmth and solid strength for the rest of her life. She was so close to falling in love with the gruff rancher--but how could she love him and his daughter when there was a distinct possibility that she belonged to another man?
