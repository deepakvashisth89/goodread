Dazzling, devious, man-hungry Juliette was anything but popular with the upperclass set into which she’d married. Harried husband Arthur bought her off with a ruinously expensive divorce, and she left the tranquil island on which the family summered—forever, she promised. But trust Juliette to be untrustworthy. A few years later, like a destructive tornado, she turned up out of the blue and began causing trouble. Only this time, it was for herself: Somebody bashed her beautiful head in.Small-town sheriff Shand had more suspects than he needed for this violent act and two grisly encores. Practically everyone on the island had a powerful motive for doing Juliette in. Finding the murderer had the lawman up against the wall—literally, for it was a wall that held the crucial clue to this frightening, fascinating puzzle.
