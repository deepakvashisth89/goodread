On the trail of a murdering cattle rustler named Slattery, Longarm meets his greatest obstacle in the townsfolk.LAWMEN RIDE IN BUT THEY DON'T RIDE OUTBut that doesn't stop Longarm from tracking down a mangy, murderin', cattle-rustlin' lowlife named Slattery, alias Hawkins. He's got the whole town in his pocket. And anyone who stands in his way, won't be standin' for long.But Longarm has other plans for this scurvy reptile: a nice retirement property . . . six feet under. With the help of a landlady who gives great room service, and the lovely hot-blooded, cattle baroness, Rosita, he's set for some hard lovin' and even harder fightin' in Montana Territory.
