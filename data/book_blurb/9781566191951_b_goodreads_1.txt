s/t: The First Forty YearsIn 1947 an American pilot observed a group of strange objects in the sky. He described them as moving "like saucers skipping across the water." From this report, an anonymous journalist invented the term "flying saucer"; later adapted to the more familiar UFO. This book presents the facts, accumulated evidence, & major revelations of the 4 decades & many phases of the UFO mystery. The source: the official case files of the nations of the world.
