Newton Thornburg, author of Cutter and Bone, delivers his most wicked thriller yet with Eve's Men, in which a classic bad seed and his good-guy brother fall in love with the same woman. Wild man Brian demolishes a movie set as revenge against the studio he feels is defaming him. As Brian rampages from Colorado to Seattle with Eve in tow, Charley follows, incredulous to Brian's increasingly violent behavior. To Brian, his actions are simple justice; to Eve and Charley--and the reader--his crusade is a harrowing ride through mayhem and madness. Still, Eve and Charley do manage to find something in the ruins--each other. Eve's Men is a fascinating ride with a wild man and those who love him.
