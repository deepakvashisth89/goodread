At the newly restored Dolphin Theatre, murder takes center stageThe once-dilapidated Dolphin Theater, now restored to its former glory, is open again-and all of London is buzzing about its new play, The Glove, inspired by the discovery of a genuine Shakespearean glove. But on one unfortunate evening, the Dolphin opens its doors to the harshest critic of death. Now Inspector Roderick Alleyn must find out who stole the scene with a most murderous act.
