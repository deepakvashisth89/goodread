World War II. The Battle for Britain rages on. Night after night, despite heavy losses, the dreaded Luftwaffe relentlessly pounds the buildings of London into smoking piles of rubble. But one German air squadron always emerges unscathed. They are the crack flying aces of the Double Cross Squadron, four men and one woman assigned to the most explosive espionage offensive of the infiltrate enemy territory and destroy the Nazi machine from within. They are the cream of Nazi Germany's awesome Air Force, the pride of Reichsmarschall Hermann Goering himself, and the most ruthless double agents ever to fight for the Allied cause.
