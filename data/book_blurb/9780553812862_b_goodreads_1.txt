Four women are at crossroads in their lives, facing difficult decisions and badly in need of a holiday. Paula is successful but can't find love, Brenda is tired of being a housewife, Rachal is filled with suppressed anger towards her father, and Jennifer is struck with a terrible tragedy. When the four women stay in a luxurious villa on a Greek island, they soon realize that they have embarked on more than just a foreign holiday. Amidst rows, arguments, laughter, and fun they come to see each other in a different light.
