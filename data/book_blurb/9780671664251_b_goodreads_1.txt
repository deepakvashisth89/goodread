OUT OF EARTH AND FIRE IT COMES . . .A blasphemous evil one stalked the people of Pontefract, Virginia. It ravaged the living and damned their dreams into eternal darkness. Now in the resurrected body of a young girl, it struggles to return. Surrounded by ravenous minions, wielding unspeakable powers, it seeks the one who will unleash hell on earth.Forever.From the brilliant imagination of Douglas Clegg comes an awesome tale of terror and madness . . . of unholy desires where men are bound by blood and lust . . . and screams are buried in the raging fury of the night . . .
