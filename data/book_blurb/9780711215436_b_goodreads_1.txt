When Daisy goes to nursery, her cat Lupin is always there at the corner to greet her on her way home. But when it comes to the holidays, Daisy doesn't go to nursery and Lupin doesn't realise. They each go on a rescue mission, imagining a whole range of incidents and adventures that might have befallen the other, until both become so worn out that Lupin wanders home to find Daisy asleep in his cat basket. A reassuring story for the very young from a highly successful author/illustrator, with delightful illustrations.
