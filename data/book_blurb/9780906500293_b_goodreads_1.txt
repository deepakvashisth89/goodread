Tales full of humour, high-spirits, social comment, pathos and metaphor made flesh. The landscape changes as you move from one story to the next: medieval Britain, 19th century Jamaica, contemporary southern France, dyke bars in London and the States, respectable Dalesford-on-Sea, a 'Silburian' conference in Vancouver. These stories by lesbian feminist novelists, poets and playwrights rework classic themes and give us fresh departures: the teenage fag hag, Blacklesbian myth and reality, the historical passing woman and, of course, coming and falling in love.
