He woke to death and darkness, unable to recall who or where he was. Around him, unknown comrades lay slain by dragon wrath, while he alone survived to flee these caverns of doom in search of his own identity.But in the world above lurked danger and destiny. For there a wizard would gift him with a name -- Prince Dammon. Now the mortal warrior must ride to Silver Mountain, where his witch sister Princess Devra builds a terrible pathway to earth with the blood of human sacrifice...where the Demons of the Air wait to cross the spell-cursed bridge...where Dammon must battle magic with magic, or watch all mankind fall prey to the evil from above.
