The year is 2001 and the world is on the brink of economic collapse as the Illuminati, a deadly secret organization, succeeds in placing one of their people in the office of the presidency of the United States, gaining control of world events. Have John Elder and Randy Cross read the signs of the times soon enough to expose the Illuminati and thwart their diabolical plan? Or will this organization and its leaders be hailed as the worlds political saviors?
