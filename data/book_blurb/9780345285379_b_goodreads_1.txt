When a man loses his honor in the arms of a forbidden woman, love becomes his impossible dream.John Murrey Eden came home to bury the man and legend of his father -- and to claim a castle and destiny named Eden.Standing amid the beauty of all that would one day be his to rule, was the woman that should never have been his to love -- the Lady of Eden, the mistress of his father...From their tragic, forbidden love, a woman fled into the tormented darkness of endless guilt, and a man wandered around the world searching for honor without sorrow, the future without the past, and love without end.
