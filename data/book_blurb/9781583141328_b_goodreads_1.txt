From one of Arabesque's major authors comes this empowering novel about soft-spoken and shy Elle Ransom, who finally gets asked out by the handsome millionaire she's been secretly in love with for years. When their dating leads to a marriage proposal, Elle feels like the luckiest woman alive, but her joy is dashed when she discovers that her new husband doesn't believe in romance and wanted a wife strictly for companionship. Now Elle is determined to show him a passion he'll never forget - and fire his soul with a love that will join them forever.
