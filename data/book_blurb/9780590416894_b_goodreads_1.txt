Kennedy High actress Lily Roshack is not so sure she'll be able to remain true to her boyfriend, Jonathan, while he's away at college. She knows school plays have a way of creating romance. Then she hears her next co-star will be someone called Buford Wodjovodski. Why worry about a boy with a name like that?But Lily finds out that you can't judge a book by its title when Buford turns out to be great-looking, charming, talented—and interested in her. No matter how much she tries to convince herself to keep her promise to Jonathan, she can't ignore her reading man. Is the curtain falling on Lily and Jonathan?
