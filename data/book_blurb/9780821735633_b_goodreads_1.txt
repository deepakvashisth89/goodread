Six months without her babies was forever for Cassie Kimborough. They'd been kidnapped by their father on November 13,1987 from their McAllen, Texas home and taken thousands of miles away to live in his native Bolivia. Fighting back the pain and guilt for months, Cassie tried to get help anywhere she could. The local police, the FBI, the U.S. State Department, even the Bolivian government itself...no one seemed to care.At her wit's end, she took matters into her own hands. Risking everything she had--including her own life--she took part in a daring rescue mission with hired "recovery experts" to bring her son and daughter back home.
