Life held only promise and pleasure for beautiful young Kate O'Neil: a beachfront apartment, an exciting career, a wonderful loving father, a tender lover all her own... — Then tragedy struck like a storm: the shot that left Patrick O'Neil dead, and the discovery that he was no the man Kate thought he was. He was not her real father.First came the shock, the sorrow. Then the heartbreaking choice to risk the love she still had and search for the truth...to begin the unceasing quest that was to lead Kate into the arms of the only man in the world who could reveal to her the deepest and most hidden secrets of all.
