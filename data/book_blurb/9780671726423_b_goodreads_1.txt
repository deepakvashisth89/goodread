Deeply affected by her mother's abandonment of her for a wealthy husband, Ricky Blair vows revenge on the rich. Now, the top female executive of an insurance company by day, Ricky commands two larcenous sisters - Baby and Gwen - in a jewel thievery ring by night. But when Granger, a cool, handsome, irreverent insurance investigator appears on the scene, Ricky finds herself involved in a delicious game of cat and mouse that makes her lust after more than money. Through a labyrinth of lust and love, danger and desire, the three women all find themselves within reach of life's greatest treasure of all - love. The only question is, who will live to claim it for their own.
