Neither Grace, nor Amelia, nor Hannah ever thought they would live out their final days in the dull, lonely comfort of a boarding house. Still, they have Olive Pruitt's boarding house to thank for bringing them together over three easily digestible meals a day. But there is nothing that says that women of a certain age can't expect the unexpected, and when an opportunity calls, the three ladies surprise themselves with their spunk and set off to create for themselves a place to call home
