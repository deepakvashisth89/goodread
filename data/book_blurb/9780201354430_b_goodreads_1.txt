A beginner's guide to designing, creating, and managing Web pages without the use of HTML codes. Chapters cover Internet and World Wide Web basics; running PageMill; text entry and editing basics; formatting text; adding images and multimedia objects; creating and editing tables; working with links; using frames; creating forms; testing and enhancing pages; using site management tools; and setting preferences. Appendices outline menu and keyboard shortcuts, toolbar functions, and HTML codes. Annotation c. by Book News, Inc., Portland, Or.
