DARING BEAUTYUndaunted by the formidable dangers of the lush Brazilian jungles, spirited Victoria King was determined to help the oppressed peasants, and she wasn't going to let the dangerous and darkly handsome Brazilian landowner Sebastian Black frighten her with his brazen attentions...or his unwanted warnings. Instead, she hoped she would encounter the mysterious Falcon, the daring figure who spirited away the greedy aristocracy's ill-gotten riches and gave them to the struggling poor. Certainly the Falcon possessed more honor than the arrogant Sebastian, whose burning amber gaze and cool insolence never failed to spark her anger. But Tory was stunned to find that his bold intimate caresses and deep searching kisses ignited something far more shocking--a reckless fiery passion wilder than her most dangerous dreams.A PERILOUS DESIRESebastian regarded the upstart Americana with grim amusement. With her storm-colored eyes and delicate curves, he knew she would prove a most desirable diversion. But as the Falcon, he must aid those who had sheltered him in time of need--and at long last wreak vengeance on the cruel enemy who had killed his parents and stolen his lands. Somehow Sebastian would try to keep the indeoendent Miss King well out of harm's way.
