Rebecca Irwin, the daughter of his partner, working in Dr. Harry Jones's Southshore medical practice? It was a big mistake. A father was bound to make allowances for his offspring that he wouldn't for a regular employee. It just wasn't professional, and Harry told Rebecca's father so. Unfortunately, Rebecca overheard the discussion and came out fighting, despite the raging attraction between her and Harry.Would Rebecca and Harry ever manage to get some balance into their roller-coaster relationship, and sort out what they really wanted from each other?
