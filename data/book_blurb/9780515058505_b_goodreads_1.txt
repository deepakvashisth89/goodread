Ashley Welles. That was the name she gave to the millionaire rancher, Grant Copeland, the night he found her stranded in an Arizona blizzard.Her dark beauty stirred him. Her ease and sophistication, even in this rugged setting, told him he had finally found a spirit to match his own. Their lips met...and for one night of boundless passion they tasted the rapture they'd searched for all their lives.Then she was gone forever. Because Ashley Galbraith was bound to a life of elegance and power far beyond Coconino Ranch, pledged to a ruthless business world in which she was destined to be Grant Copeland's bitterest rival. She vowed that she and the man who had touched her soul would never meet again.
