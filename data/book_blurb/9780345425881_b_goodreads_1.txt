It was two in the morning in Oxfordshire, England when the call came through from America - the call that would foreshadow one man's death and place Ben Reese's life on the line. As an expert in rare books, coins, paintings and ancient documents, he works in the heart of mystery. As a former intelligence agent, he lives in the heart of danger. In Publish and Perish, Ben unfolds a story of professional jealousy and irrational enmity that point to murder.
