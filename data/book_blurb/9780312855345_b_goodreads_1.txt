The Stars Are Also Firebegins in the early days of the next century—at the dawn of a new era of space exploration and colonization. Dagney Beynac, descendant of the legendary Anson Guthrie, becomes a heroine of a new Lunarian civilization that, over the course of a lifetime, struggles to break free of the influence of Earth.Centuries later, the entire solar system is dominated by the cybercosm, a vast network of machine intelligences. On Earth and Luna, splintered fragments of humanity search desperately for some way to survive in a system that no longer seems to hold a place for flesh and blood. The only solution lies in the distant past and the forgotten discovery of Dagney Beynac.
