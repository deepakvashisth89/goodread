A realistic, humorous depiction of in-law experiences. Rarely has there been a depiction of family life as vivid and accurate as In-Law Drama. Traci the idealist is experiencing discord with her sarcastic, profane, out-of-control in-laws. She learns that the most difficult relationships offer the greatest opportunity for growth and change. Experience the Jenkins drama as Traci learns lessons about life...and about herself. In-Law Drama is a work of fiction inspired by emotions and responses to in-law issues. Characters should not be construed as real.
