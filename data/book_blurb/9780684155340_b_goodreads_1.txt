From dust jacketPlastic canvas is an exciting new medium that has many advantages:-Does not pull out of shape when worked-Needs no blocking-Can be cut to any shape-Does not fray-Requires no hemmingIt is ideal for such uses as frames, boxes, book covers, rugs, pincushions, place mats, coasters, and baskets as well as for freestanding sculpture such as flowers and ornamental letters.Full directions for 34 such projects are given in this book, as well as general instructions for the use of this innovative and interesting material. Needlepointers will welcome the many ways in which plastic canvas can enlarge their repertoire.
