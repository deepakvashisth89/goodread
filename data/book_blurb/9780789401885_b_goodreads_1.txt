The guide that shows you what other travel books only tell you! Highlighted by more than 750 full-color photographs, DK Eyewitness Travel Ireland is the most informative, entertaining, and enlightening guidebook to the Emerald Isle. Featuring seven regional chapters and one devoted solely to Dublin, this guide showcases Ireland's landscape, wildlife, architecture, culture, and Celtic heritage. Navigate Dublin with street-by-street maps and aerial 3-D views that direct you to all the museums, castles, cathedrals, churches, parks, and key attractions. Experience Trinity College, travel the countryside, the beaches at Ardmore, and visit Londonderry's Tower Museum to the Lakes of Killarney. Get the most of your stay in Ireland with this gleaming gem of a travel reference.
