A New Land, A New LifeThe wilds of New England had bested many a man, but could they tame one wanton woman? Kit Sparhawk had his doubts. Lady Dianna Grey had obviously been brought up a pampered English beauty, but beneath her soft exterior lay a heart as cold as stone.With her gentle world turned upside down by betrayal and accusation, Diana prayed for a new beginning. Yet the Massachusetts landscape was rugged and unforgiving, as were the men. Especially Master Kit Sparhawk, whose disapproving glance revealed a heart as lonely as her own.
