All it takes is one bad apple. When Roy Mortlake drops in from out of nowhere, the sense or tranquility which makes theh Cotswolds' Wychwood House Hotel a well-heeled haven departs. As does Roy, who is soon found fatally wet in the hotel's swimming pool. Death does tend to tarnish a hotel's reputation, as well as the livelihood of its hosts, John and Rose Kempton. When two more guests eternally depart, the Kemptons stare ruin in the face. What stares Detective Superintendent George Thorne and Sergeant Bill Abbot in the face is murder - possibly perfect murder. With hard evidence on vacation, Thorne makes his rounds to the widely different guests and staff - but it is his peek into the past that convinces him who will foot the bill for these murders in residence.
