The Reluctant Knight ReturnsBecoming a knight has changed the Weasel very little. Galen Pathwarden is still reluctant to adventure, still out to save his own skin at virtually any cost. But when his brother Brithelm vanishes mysteriously, Galen sets aside his better judgement and embarks on a quest that leads under the earth, deep into a conspiracy of darkness, and to the end of his courage.Galen returns to imperiled Solamnia in this exciting sequel to the top-selling Weasel's Luck, also by Michael Williams, the bard of the DragonLance Saga.The sixth and last in this series of recovers of classic Dragonlance novel tales. This attractive new re-release of Galen Beknighted showcases the new look commissioned for the Heroes series. This title is a sequel to another recently recovered title in this series, Weasel's Luck, and is by the same author.
