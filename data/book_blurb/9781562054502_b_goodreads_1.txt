A reference for designing, implementing, maintaining, and troubleshooting TCP/IP networks on operating systems such as NetWare, Windows, OS/2, DOS, Windows NT, and UNIX. Half of the book is an overview of the TCP/IP networking protocol. Related topics include frame relay, IPng, ATM, Domain Name Systems, Internet addressing, and security. Contains appendices on current vendors, RFCs, and registering a site, plus a glossary. For advanced users and system administrators of the TCP/IP protocol.
