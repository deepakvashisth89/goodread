The Collected Poemsconstitutes the authorized canon of A. E. Housman’s verse as it was established in 1939, three years after his death. In containsA Shropshire Lad(1896),Last Poems(1922),More Poems(1936), theAdditional Poems, and the three translations from A.W. Pollard’s anthology,Odes from the Greek Dramatists(1890).
