In this heartwarming collection of true-to-life devotional vignettes, bestselling author Carol Kent combines inspirational stories with humor to illustrate the joys and trials of real life.You’ll smile as you hear God’s voice in the funny and poignant situations that make life so worth living.Written for women, Detours, Tow Trucks, and Angels in Disguise is a wonderful book to give as a gift to your friend, mom, or sister.
