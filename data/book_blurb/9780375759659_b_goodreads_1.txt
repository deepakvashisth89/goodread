Pronounced obscene when it was first published in 1915,The Rainbowis the epic story of three generations of the Brangwens, a Midlands family. A visionary novel, considered to be one of Lawrence's finest, it explores the complex sexual and psychological relationships between men and women in an increasingly industrialized world. "Lives are separate, but life is continuous - it continues in the fresh start by the separate life in each generation," wrote F.R. Leavis. "No work, I think, has presented this perception as an imaginatively realized truth more compellingly thanThe Rainbow."
