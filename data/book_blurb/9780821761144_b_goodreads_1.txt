Eden Pembrook had turned down the seven most eligible bachelors in colonial Virginia. Then Sebastian Saber came along, igniting the most intense longing she had ever felt. But he didn't say where his loyalties lay—with the British or with the Revolutionaries. And so Eden, vowing always to put the Patriot cause before her own happiness, was forced to turn him away.Sebastian Saber knew war was hell. Of course he favored liberty, but as a double agent for the Patriots, he had to pose as a British spy. Now his British commander had ordered him to kidnap General Pembrook's daughter. And yet before he became her enemy, he wanted one night to hold Eden in his arms...only to learn that love doesn't always end when morning comes.
