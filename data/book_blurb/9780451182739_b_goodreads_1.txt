Being a private eye in New York City means you never know what kind of case is going to walk in the door. For Blaine Stewart, that's half the fun. But how is she supposed to find a baby that hasn't been born yet, especially when it's the mother who's asking her to look? It's like this, explains socialite Judith Marsden. She has paid Hannah Wyrick, a surrogate mother, a handsome sum to carry her test-tube baby. But now, two months before the delivery date, Hannah has disappeared and Marsden wants her son - the heir to 29 million bucks - back. The name Wyrick makes Blaine stand up and take note. After all, Nikki Wyrick is her housekeeper. That bizarre coincidence leads Blaine straight to the Wyrick family home in New Jersey, where she is stunned to learn that both the Wyrick girls died eight years before. So who is Hannah Wyrick? Who's that woman dusting Blaine's apartment? And who has just decided that Judith Marsden is best dead?
