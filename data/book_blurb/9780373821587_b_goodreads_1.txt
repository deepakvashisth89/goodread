PROTECT ME, DADDY!Widow Georgia Madison wasn't ready to let a new man into her life, let alone the lives of her daughters. But bodyguard Ben Stratton was a tall, broad-shouldered, hazel-eyed temptation with a strong protective streak. And with Ben living in close proximity to protect them from vandals, she was drawn to him like a moth to a hot flame ...While Ben was busy protecting Georgia and her family, he forgot to protect his heart. Suddenly the thought of becoming a husband and father was irresistible, and he vowed to make these three strong-willed Madison women his own!
