She despised rain—and men!And she'd had enough of both in Singapore. But a young and attractive woman alone was fair game in that teeming nineteenth-century seaport. So she devised a daring and impetuous scheme that would take her far from the crowded city—to the New World—and freedom.What she did not anticipate was that the handsome sea captain whom she had chosen to be her deliverer would reverse her plans completely—because of his loving God.
