"I turn to you, my own flesh and blood in far-off America. You are my lifeline."In a desperate letter Yuri Karpeyko begs his unknown American cousin to get his persecuted family out of Russia. As Martin Singer reads the letter in his New York townhouse, a mission begins and cousins touch hearts and hands to plan a daring rescue. Their lives will change profoundly. After a century of silence, a family will be reborn - to survive, to flourish and finally, to prevail.
