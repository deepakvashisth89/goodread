Centering Prayer is a precious part of our Western spiritual heritage re-found and presented in this classic introduction originally published in 1978. Now available in a beautiful, updated 25th anniversary hardcover gift edition.M. Basil Pennington, Thomas Keating, and Thomas E. Clarke introduce the practice of silent prayer and offer both a practical guide to beginning the meditative process and a vision of the deeper life of the soul that Centering Prayer can bring about. Reflections and advice on Centering Prayer's possibilities -- and its pitfalls -- are presented with simplicity and clarity.
