With fiery determination, Boston-bred Breanna Sullivan heads for her California birthplace to confront the man responsible for her father's disappearance. But from the soul-shattering moment she clashes with the dynamic Alexander King, she knows she's met her match. His eyes smolder with unbridled lust, his muscles gleam like burnished steel. She knows he's a man who takes what he wants...and she's powerless to deny him. Fascinated by the flame-haired spitfire who beguiles and confounds him, King cannot allow her to sabotage his secret plans for the California wilderness, even if he has to abduct and marry her. Yet even marriage does not tame her, as King battles the willful Breanna...only to surrender to the unyielding power of a love that is their destiny.
