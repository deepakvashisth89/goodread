This raunchy satire clues the reader in to how real Texans talk. Weaver's hilarious text and R. Crumb's instantly recognizable art shine light on words like "weeble-stick," "wickerbill," and "snakenavel." Included is a section on the nomenclature of Dubya, and readers learn how to pick a fight in a Texas bar: just tell a guy he's "uglier than a cancer-eyed cow."
