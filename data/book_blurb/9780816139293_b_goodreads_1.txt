Chesterton's detective-priest, Father Brown, still delights readers and, from time to time, television viewers. THE SECRET OF FATHER BROWN contains these "The Mirror of the Magistrate," "The Man With Two Beards," "The Song of the Flying Fish," "The Actor and the Alibi," "The Vanishing of Vaudrey," "The Worst Crime in the World," "The Red Moon of Meru," "The Chief Mourner of Marne" and "The Secret of Flambeau." "Another welcome collection of Father Brown mysteries." (B-O-T Editorial Review Board)
